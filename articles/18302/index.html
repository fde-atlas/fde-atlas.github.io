PERSOL Workstyle Consulting Launches New Service "AI Consulting" Providing End-to-End Support for AI Transformation

2026.09.01

PERSOL WORKS SWITCH CONSULTING Co., Ltd. (Headquarters: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; President and CEO: Takamasa Ono; hereinafter "PERSOL WORKS SWITCH CONSULTING"), part of the PERSOL Group whose vision is "Work and Smile," launched its new service "AI Consulting (Switch → AI-Native with AI Consulting)" on August 26, 2026, to elevate corporate AI utilization from "adoption" to "results."

As the core service of the AI transformation support brand "Switch → AI-Native," which also launched on the same day, this service provides specialized services across AI strategy, business transformation, AI agents, organizational/talent transformation, AI governance, and more, bundled flexibly according to each client's challenges. As its delivery model, we have established a "Team-based FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" in which client companies and our experts form a single team, handling everything from AI strategy formulation through business transformation, AI implementation, and adoption/internalization with the same team, without fragmentation.

Background of the Service Launch — AI use remains "individual and isolated," failing to reach company-wide results

AI utilization in society is beginning to shift from generative AI use to business AI use (AI agents, AI integrated with core systems, etc.). While adoption is spreading rapidly, at many companies AI use and proof-of-concept efforts remain confined to "individuals" or "individual tasks," and are not connected to company-wide results. In our "Survey on the Actual State of Generative AI and RPA Adoption 2025," 52% of companies that introduced AI reported benefits such as reduced workload, improved speed, and fewer errors, while the lack of specialized talent ranked among the top challenges for company-wide promotion going forward. In the field as well, we frequently hear voices such as the following.

"We can't decide what to do" — meetings stall when trying to identify target tasks "There's no one in-house to consult with" — assigned as a side duty, you become the most knowledgeable person in the company with no one to turn to "We're implementing it, but it doesn't lead to results" — small efficiency gains occur, but they don't accumulate into company results

To elevate AI utilization toward generating results across the entire business, we believe it is necessary for talent capable of handling the continuous phases from strategy, business transformation, and implementation through to adoption to drive the effort consistently.

Service Overview — Supporting business results creation with a cross-phase team structure that maintains momentum

Our Technology Consulting Division has supported business process reform (BPR) and the introduction and adoption of AI and automation at numerous companies, including major enterprises in finance, manufacturing, and retail. This new "AI Consulting" service is newly launched as a support service specialized in the AI domain, built on this track record and expertise.

The service provides end-to-end support from AI strategy formulation through business transformation, implementation, and adoption/internalization, aiming to transform companies into organizations that can turn AI into organizational capability. It targets companies facing challenges such as: AI use concentrated among certain employees or tasks, inability to move from PoC to production operation, dependence of tasks and decisions on specific individuals, and failure to connect multiple AI and automation products for overall optimization. The delivery format is a consulting service through a "Team-based FDE" execution unit that works alongside clients, designed individually according to the client's challenges, target tasks, and scope of support.

By connecting AI use, which had remained limited to individual use and isolated proof-of-concepts, to company-wide results, and through our support, decision criteria and knowledge accumulate within the organization, enabling clients themselves to sustain operation and improvement.

Key Features of the Service

Feature 1: Client and our company work as "one team," accompanying end-to-end from strategy to internalization Our experts work in the same team as the client company's AI promotion staff, IT/DX departments, and business departments. By supporting "strategy, business transformation, implementation, and adoption" as a continuous flow without switching personnel, we prevent handover losses that would otherwise cause fragmentation.

Feature 2: Seven roles and 217 specialized professionals (cumulative) Experts across seven roles participate in projects as needed, from strategic planning and business design to AI technical design, implementation, adoption and talent development, and industry expertise. Rather than depending on specific individuals, we bundle broad expertise as a team, enabling us to handle large-scale transformations spanning multiple domains. This fiscal year, we aim to develop more than 300 professionals (cumulative).

Feature 3: A multi-vendor structure that combines optimal products starting from client challenges Rather than being driven by specific products, we select and combine optimal solutions from multiple products such as UiPath, Microsoft (Copilot/Copilot Studio), ServiceNow, and Salesforce, starting from the client's business challenges. We also provide feedback to vendors as appropriate, linking it to product updates and thereby optimizing the environment.

Case Studies — Extensive track record of support from strategy to adoption

① Strategy — Corporate Planning: AI-Native Strategy Formulation Support With AI use skewed toward efficiency, we conducted executive-participation workshops aimed at rebuilding AI strategy with the AGI/ASI era in mind. Using backcasting, we organized the AI vision, investment strategy, and promotion structure, supported the formulation of medium- to long-term roadmaps and execution plans, strengthened participants' thinking and transformation-driving capabilities, and built the foundation for strategy execution.

② Business Transformation — Telecommunications: Business Improvement and Citizen Development Adoption Support To achieve employee-driven business improvement, we provided consistent support from task visualization through BPR promotion, RPA introduction, and citizen developer development. By generating 130 improvement proposals and developing 11 citizen developers, we achieved a reduction of over 500 hours per month and built a foundation for continuous business reform.

③ Implementation — Finance: AI and Business Automation Implementation Support We analyzed business challenges in each department and provided accompanying support from target task selection through PoC and business implementation. Using Copilot, Power Automate, RPA, and other tools, we achieved efficiencies such as advanced document search and inquiry handling, automation of monthly report distribution, and creation of stock price simulations, generating an estimated annual reduction of over 5,000 hours.

④ Retention — Finance: AI/Automation Talent Development and Retention Support In response to the difficulty of sustaining business improvement due to a shortage of digital talent, we provided hands-on, side-by-side training using real work as material, QA support, development of learning content, and establishment of a certification system. To date, we have trained over 300 development professionals as well as internal trainers for in-house capability building, establishing an in-house development platform where improvements proceed autonomously.
