A forward deployed engineer (FDE) is a software engineer who works with one customer at a time to get a product running in that customer's real systems: scoping the problem, writing the code and staying until it works in production. In India that product is mostly AI: of the 20 forward deployed engineer jobs open in India on Rezifi's list on 4 November 2026, 17 describe AI work.

Here is what the role is, how it differs from its neighbours, what the postings ask for and how to apply. The jobs themselves are on Rezifi's list of forward deployed engineer jobs in India.

What does a forward deployed engineer do?

Palantir set out the role on its own blog in 2019, in Dev versus Delta. Its software engineers build one part of the platform, and that code ships to many customers. Its forward deployed software engineers sit on a team that supports one customer, deploy and adapt the platform until it solves that customer's problem, and measure success by the customer's goal: “one customer, many capabilities”, against a software engineer's “one capability, many customers”.

It is not consulting, the same post says: a forward deployed engineer does far more engineering than a consultant, and often sends code back into the core product when a customer needs a missing feature.

OpenAI's posting for the role-sf-san-francisco/) reads the same way. Its forward deployed engineers take its models from a first prototype to stable production with its most strategic customers, own every step from scoping to rollout, and still write code when progress depends on it. It asks for five or more years of engineering or deployment experience that includes customer-facing work.

How is it different from a software engineer, a solutions engineer or an AI engineer?

The four titles share skills and differ in who the work is for. The last two follow the postings open in India on Rezifi's list on 4 November 2026.

  • Software engineer: every customer at once. Owns one part of the product and ships it to everyone who uses it.
  • Forward deployed engineer: one customer at a time. Scopes the customer's problem, then builds and deploys the answer in their systems.
  • Solutions engineer: a customer buying or onboarding. In many postings, demos and proofs of concept before a sale, onboarding after it.
  • AI engineer: a product, their own company's or a client's. Builds and deploys AI applications, from LLM apps to agents.

In short, an AI engineer is named for the technology, a forward deployed engineer for where the work happens. They overlap: on 4 November 2026, Databricks listed an “AI Engineer, AI FDE” in Bengaluru.

If the AI side draws you, AI engineer jobs in India are their own list, and Rezifi's AI engineer skills page shows the skills 51 open AI engineer jobs in India asked for, counted on 17 October 2026. Python leads it.

What do forward deployed engineer jobs in India ask for?

The 20 on Rezifi's list on 4 November 2026 all came from employers' own careers sites. Here is what they had in common.

  • Customers. 16 of the 20 describe working directly with customers, clients or partners. Mastercard's lead FDE embeds with clients to build AI prototypes; Adobe's works with its strategic customers in a customer pod.
  • AI. 17 of the 20 describe AI work. Exotel's build voicebots on large language models, Wipro's deploy and manage AI agents, and Handshake's build software for customer deployments for frontier AI labs.
  • Experience. 16 state a minimum, from 2.5 years (Exotel's Forward Deployed Engineer 2) to 12 (Innovaccer's director role), with 5 in the middle.
  • Where. 11 list Bengaluru, the rest Noida, Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai, Chennai and Ahmedabad. 5 are remote.
  • Pay. None of the 20 states it.

So a forward deployed engineer in India is a working engineer first, with large language models and agents on top. The customer half shows up in the duties: scoping a problem, building a prototype, deploying it.

How do you become a forward deployed engineer?

  • Be a strong builder first. The skills in these postings lead with Python and TypeScript, then APIs and the employer's own platform.
  • Ship something on large language models. OpenAI's posting asks for people who have built or deployed systems powered by LLMs. Put one you built on your resume, with who used it.
  • Take customer-facing work when it comes. Palantir lists breaking a customer's high-level problem down to the lines of code that solve it among the skills the role needs. Integrations and client projects build it.
  • Start where the door is open. None of the 16 postings that state a minimum asks for under 2.5 years, so a fresher's way in is a software or AI engineering role first. How to get an AI job as a fresher in India covers that step.

How to tailor your resume for a forward deployed engineer job

Each posting weighs the engineering and the customer work differently. Exotel's asks for prompt architectures and user acceptance testing; Databricks' asks for Spark on its own platform. One resume cannot speak to both in their own words.

  • Lead with a deployment. Your strongest bullet is something you built and put into real users' hands: what it was, who used it and what changed.
  • Use the posting's words. If it says voicebots, LLM APIs or AI agents, and you have done that work, say it in those words.
  • Show both halves. One bullet of engineering depth, one of working with a customer or a team outside engineering.

Questions people ask

What is a forward deployed engineer?

A forward deployed engineer is a software engineer who works with one customer at a time to get a product running in that customer's real systems: scoping the problem, writing the code and deploying it to production. A software engineer on a product team, by contrast, builds one part of the product for every customer.

Is a forward deployed engineer the same as a solutions engineer?

No. Many solutions engineer postings open in India on Rezifi's list on 4 November 2026 describe demos and proofs of concept before a sale, or onboarding after it. A forward deployed engineer writes and deploys production code for one customer.

What skills does a forward deployed engineer need?

Strong software engineering first. In the 20 forward deployed engineer postings open in India on Rezifi's list on 4 November 2026, Python and large language models were each named in 9, TypeScript in 6 and AI agents in 5. The postings also describe working directly with customers, from scoping a problem to production.

Can a fresher become a forward deployed engineer?

Not straight away, judging by the postings. Of the forward deployed engineer jobs open in India on Rezifi's list on 4 November 2026, the 16 that state a minimum ask for 2.5 to 12 years, with 5 in the middle. Start as a software or AI engineer and build toward it.
