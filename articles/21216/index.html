Summary

A Salesforce Japan job posting for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) on a team launched about a year ago. The role leads end-to-end delivery of custom agentic AI solutions built on Agentforce, from requirements through design, implementation, and production deployment, with roughly 25–50% on-site time at Tokyo-area customers. The posting cites strong momentum: Agentforce and Data 360 ARR growing rapidly, over 60% of related bookings from existing customers, production customers up ~50% quarter-over-quarter, and a new metric, Agentic Work Units, recording over 2.4 billion completed tasks. Requirements include 5+ years of full-cycle development, LLM solution experience, and fluent Japanese plus business English. Salary ranges from ¥8M to ¥30M, with one week of training at US headquarters.

Key points

  • Salesforce Japan is hiring FDEs to build custom agentic AI on Agentforce end to end.
  • Agentforce and Data 360 ARR are growing fast; 60%+ of bookings come from existing customers.
  • Production Agentforce customers grew ~50% in Q4; 2.4B+ tasks logged via Agentic Work Units.
  • Requires 5+ years full-cycle development, LLM experience, Japanese fluency, and business English.
  • Salary is ¥8M–¥30M with a week of training at the US headquarters.
