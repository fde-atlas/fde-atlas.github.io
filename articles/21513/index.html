What is an FDE? Forward Deployed Engineer — A New Profession in the AI Era and Enterprise Adoption Strategies

Original title: FDEとは？AI時代の新職種フォワードデプロイドエンジニアの企業向け導入戦略

FDE/エンジニア/ITフリーランス特化のキャリア支援メディア · 2026-10-30 · 3 min read

Summary

This Japanese article targets executives and DX leaders considering adopting FDEs. It explains the Forward Deployed Engineer as a new AI-era role: engineers employed by a vendor who embed themselves in customer sites to identify problems and build and implement AI solutions on the ground. The piece outlines why companies need this role and offers adoption strategies for organizations introducing FDEs.

Key points

  • FDEs are vendor-employed engineers embedded at customer sites.
  • Their scope spans problem discovery through AI implementation.
  • The audience is executives and DX promotion leaders.
  • The article focuses on corporate adoption strategy for FDEs.
