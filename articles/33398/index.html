DXC and Anthropic to train forward deployed Claude engineers

Kernavique · 2026-06-11 · 2 min read

Summary

On June 11, 2026, DXC and Anthropic announced a multi-year global alliance to train tens of thousands of forward deployed, Claude-certified engineers. Candidates will be selected from DXC's existing workforce of over 115,000 people across 70 countries, then certified in 90 days via the Anthropic Partner Academy, with an added DXC curriculum for mission-critical settings. The engineers will be embedded in customer environments such as banks, airlines, insurers, manufacturers and government agencies. DXC also says its OASIS platform, launched in April 2026, is live at more than 50 customers. Kernavique notes the tens-of-thousands figure is a plan, not current headcount, and the route is internal rather than open external hiring.

Key points

  • DXC and Anthropic announced a multi-year alliance on June 11, 2026.
  • Target: tens of thousands of Claude-certified forward deployed engineers, chosen internally.
  • Certification takes 90 days through the Anthropic Partner Academy.
  • Engineers will be embedded at banks, airlines, insurers, manufacturers and governments.
  • Kernavique cautions the scale is a stated plan, not existing headcount.

On June 11, 2026, DXC and Anthropic announced a multi-year global alliance. DXC’s release says the workforce will be “tens of thousands” of forward deployed, Claude-certified engineers and builders, with no exact figure, selected from DXC’s existing engineers. Training and certification take 90 days through the Anthropic Partner Academy. DXC states a workforce of over 115,000 in 70 countries.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: DXC.

What happened

DXC said the engineers will be embedded directly in customer environments, and names banks, airlines, insurers, manufacturers and government agencies. DXC adds its own certification curriculum for mission-critical environments.

The release says DXC OASIS, launched in April 2026, is deployed across more than 50 customers. It quotes Raul Fernandez, President & CEO of DXC Technology, and Paul Smith, Chief Commercial Officer of Anthropic.

“This is a defining moment for DXC and for the industry.”

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

The route described is internal: engineers selected from DXC’s existing staff, then a 90-day certification through the Anthropic Partner Academy. This suggests these roles are not, at first, open hires from outside.

If you are hiring them

Here the vendor, Anthropic, certifies, and the services firm supplies and embeds the people. If you buy or build this capability, the stated scale is a plan of tens of thousands, not a current headcount.
