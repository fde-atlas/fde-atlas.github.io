List of Companies Implementing FDE [2026]: Trends of 7 Overseas and 11 Japanese Companies, and 3 Models

Original title: FDEを導入している企業一覧【2026年】海外7社・日本11社の動きと、3つの型

FDE Consulting · 2026-10-08 · 6 min read

Summary

Based on official announcements and job postings, this article catalogs 7 overseas and 11 Japanese companies adopting Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) as of 2026, grouping them into three types: the Palantir model, where FDEs embed a vendor's own product at customer sites; AI labs building dedicated deployment companies (OpenAI Deployment Company, Ode with Anthropic, Microsoft's Frontier unit with 6,000 resident engineers, AWS's $1 billion push); and large SI/consulting firms scaling FDE headcount, such as Hitachi's plan for 1,000 domestic FDEs and Deloitte's FDE management office. The author warns buyers to verify where responsibility ends, who implements, and where knowledge accumulates.

Key points

  • 18 companies (7 overseas, 11 Japanese) now use FDEs, in three distinct types.
  • Microsoft deployed 6,000 engineers; AWS invested $1 billion in generative AI experts.
  • Hitachi plans 1,000 domestic FDEs; Deloitte opened an FDE management office.
  • The three types differ in whose interests the FDE serves.
  • Buyers should check responsibility endpoints, implementers, and knowledge retention.

Companies that have adopted FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) split into three patterns in 2026: the Palantir pattern of embedding their own products at customer sites, the pattern of AI labs creating dedicated deployment companies, and the pattern of major SI and consulting firms building FDE organizations through headcount.

Here, I list the movements of 7 overseas companies and 11 Japanese companies, as far as can be confirmed from official announcements and job postings. Rumors and "it seems that..." claims are excluded. At the end, I also cover what companies receiving FDE support should look at.

The definition of the FDE role is summarized in "What is an FDE? The Meaning and Work of a Forward Deployed Engineer", and FDE as a business model in "What is the FDE Model?".

3. Outcome-based SI and consultingHitachi, NTT Data Group, Accenture, Deloitte TohmatsuImplement solutions, including third-party AI, tailored to the customer's challenges, taking responsibility for outcomes. A shift away from per-person-month contractingFees for support, with a move toward linking compensation to outcomes rather than person-months

Types 1 and 2 are FDEs aimed at "getting your own product adopted," while type 3 is an FDE aimed at "solving the customer's problems." Even with the same title, FDEs differ in whose interests they serve. Those commissioning the work should pay attention to this point.

We at FDE Consulting are close to type 3. Without a specific product of our own, we start from the customer's operations and KPIs, then select and implement the AI that fits. While an FDE at a product company starts from "how far can we go with this product," we start from "which parts of these operations should be delegated to AI."

The more the name "FDE" spreads, the more you need to verify what's behind it

In Japan in 2026, services and job postings labeled "FDE" have suddenly increased. As early as 2025, Nikkei Cross Tech argued that the American-style FDE is similar to but distinct from Japan's on-site resident engineers (Nikkei Cross Tech). Even when the name is the same, the substance varies by company.

Those receiving FDE support should verify the following three things.

  • Where does responsibility end? At the completion of the PoC, at delivery, or until the business metrics actually move
  • Who does the implementation? Are the ones proposing and the ones building the same people, or separate teams
  • Where does the knowledge accumulate? Does it flow back into the product, stay within the customer's company, or remain in neither

If all three are written into the contract and the organizational chart, you won't be misled by the name. We compared the difference between FDE and SES in detail in "What's the difference between an FDE and SES?"

Companies well suited to receiving FDE support

FDE is not a panacea. If what to build and the specifications are already fixed and all you lack is manpower, it is more rational to rely on SES or contracted development. FDE demonstrates its value for companies in situations like the following.

  • The AI PoC succeeded, but the project never advances to production deployment
  • No one in-house can implement AI. You want not just advice, but people who will do the hands-on work
  • Operations involve many exceptions, and standard tool deployment can't handle them
  • You want to internalize AI development, but no one can create the initial blueprint
