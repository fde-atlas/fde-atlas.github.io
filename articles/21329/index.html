Summary

This is a job posting from Abeam Consulting on OpenWork for a Consultant position in AI realization and solution development (FDE), within its AI & Technology Modernization unit. The role turns client business problems into production-ready AI solutions, covering feasibility validation, design, implementation, evals, deployment, and technology transfer. Example projects include AI agent workflows, RAG and VectorDB knowledge platforms, and LLMOps/MLOps operations. Candidates need 3+ years of digital technology or IT lifecycle experience plus one of three specialties: generative AI application development, machine learning/data science, or AI systems/data infrastructure. Annual salary ranges from 6.9 million to 25 million yen, with offices in Tokyo and Osaka and remote work allowed.
