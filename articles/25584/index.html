Summary

UST promotes its Forward Deployed Engineer service built on its Wanpu enterprise AI engine. Full-stack engineers work on-site at clients, handling requirements, solution design, deployment and ongoing optimization to address the common problem of AI projects failing to deliver business value. Empowered by Wanpu AI, deeply integrated with business scenarios, engineers on the business frontline

Our FDE Forward Deployed Engineer service is an AI deployment "strike force" built on the Wanpu Enterprise AI Engine. Breaking away from the traditional remote delivery model, we dispatch versatile full-stack engineers to work on-site, leading the entire process from requirements discovery and solution design to system deployment and continuous optimization. We deeply integrate Wanpu AI capabilities with customers' business scenarios, quickly bridging the gap between AI and existing systems, and solving the core pain points of AI adoption challenges and hard-to-see returns on investment.

Core Value

Value First, Fast Results

Abandoning the traditional "build the architecture first, talk about value later" approach, we are driven by business pain points, delivering production-ready AI prototypes within 2 weeks and realizing value in core scenarios within 3 months

Deep Integration, Seamless Connection

Leveraging the full-chain integration capabilities of the Wanpu Enterprise AI Engine, we connect core enterprise systems such as ERP, CRM, MES, and BPM with one click, breaking down data silos and enabling intelligent cross-system collaboration

Continuous Evolution, Dynamic Optimization

During on-site engagements, FDE engineers continuously collect business feedback and iteratively refine models and workflows based on Wanpu AI's dynamic learning capabilities, ensuring the AI system evolves in step with business development

Knowledge Accumulation, Capability Reuse

We transform successful on-site experiences into standardized product modules, driving upgrades to the Wanpu Enterprise AI Engine in return, while helping customers accumulate reusable AI assets and best practices

Core Advantages

Native Empowerment by the Wanpu AI Engine, Multiplied Efficiency

All FDE engineers undergo in-depth training and certification on the Wanpu Enterprise AI Engine, fully leveraging its dual-engine advantage of a business rules engine plus an AI large model engine. Key decision accuracy exceeds 95%, and response times improve from hours to seconds, boosting the overall efficiency of AI + business systems by 3-5x

Special Forces-Style Full-Stack Capability, End-to-End Delivery

Our FDE engineers are not mere "installers" but versatile professionals with full-stack development, data governance, business consulting, and project management skills. They can independently handle the entire process from requirements breakdown, model fine-tuning, and system integration to launch and operations, eliminating the need for multi-team coordination and significantly shortening delivery cycles

Deep On-Site Co-Creation, Precise Requirements Matching

FDE engineers are stationed long-term at customer sites, working side by side with business teams to deeply understand customers' unique business processes and pain points. They can quickly translate vague business requirements into actionable AI solutions, avoiding the problem of "technology and business being disconnected"

Full Lifecycle Service, Continuous Value Creation

We provide full lifecycle services from POC validation and official launch to long-term operations. After the system goes live, FDE engineers continue to provide optimization support, conducting regular performance evals and model iterations to ensure the AI system always operates at its best and continuously creates business value for customers

Industry Empowerment

  • Manufacturing: intelligent production quality inspection, predictive equipment maintenance, intelligent supply chain scheduling, process parameter optimization
  • Retail & E-commerce: intelligent recommendations, intelligent inventory management, customer profiling analysis, intelligent marketing campaign optimization
  • Healthcare: intelligent medical record structuring, diagnostic assistance, medical imaging analysis, intelligent hospital operations management
  • Finance: intelligent risk control, anti-fraud, intelligent customer service, credit approval automation, intelligent financial report analysis
