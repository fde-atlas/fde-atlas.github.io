Practice library

AIelsen FDE Academy · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

AIelsen FDE Academy's practice library collects nine public case readings and ten synthetic exercises for forward deployed engineering, each with coursework and acceptance criteria. Cases span expert workflows (AIG × Anthropic), hardware root-cause analysis, Airbus × Palantir's Skywise data platform, Novo Nordisk's NovoScribe, Morgan Stanley and Klarna with OpenAI, Tampa General Hospital × Palantir, and Sensors Data's RaaS model. Content is organized around four stages—discover, design, decide, verify—and readers pick cases close to their work, examine decisions, and produce reproducible coursework. Synthetic exercises cover growth research, sales prospecting, paid media, business reviews, knowledge operations, and store operations.
