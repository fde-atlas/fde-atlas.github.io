Databricks Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-10-30 · 8 min read

Summary

Using an October 24, 2026 snapshot of public job boards, FDE Dispatch analyzes Databricks's Forward Deployed Engineer family postings. Databricks accounts for 122 of 725 FDE-family postings across 121 employers (16.8%): 88 engineer-family, 25 management, and 9 Deployment Strategist roles. The article breaks down titles, locations, travel requirements, and posted US pay ranges, comparing Databricks against all-employer medians. Data comes from public Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and Workday boards; posted ranges are noted as not offers.

Key points

  • Databricks has 122 of 725 FDE-family postings: 88 engineer, 25 management, 9 Deployment Strategist.
  • 95 postings list remote in location, the most of any employer; workplace-type fields are all blank.
  • Travel appears in 81.1% of postings, with a median stated upper bound of 20% versus 30% across all employers.
  • The most common US engineer range is $182,000 to $250,208, matching the all-employer median midpoint of $216,104.
  • No new-grad or internship titles appear among Databricks's postings in this snapshot.

Databricks posts Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) roles, Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer roles, AI Engineer (FDE) roles, Deployment Strategist roles and related Manager, Engagement Manager and Program Manager titles. In FDE Dispatch's October 24, 2026 snapshot of public job boards, Databricks accounts for 122 of 725 FDE-family postings (16.8%) across 121 employers: 88 engineer-family, 25 management and 9 deployment strategist. All 122 leave workplace type blank; 95 of those blank-workplace postings list remote in their location. How much Databricks Forward Deployed roles travel

Travel appears in 99 of 122 Databricks postings (81.1%), and 88 state a percentage; the median stated upper bound is 20%, against 30% across all employers on how much forward deployed engineers travel.

Normalized bands: 20% on 75 postings, 20-25% on 6, 25-50% on 5, 50% on 1, and 20-30% on 1. Databricks's stated travel sits below the all-employer median upper bound of 30% from the same October 24, 2026 snapshot.

Method and data

Population: 122 Databricks postings with in_page_stats among the 725 FDE-family postings from 121 employers counted on the salary, job titles, locations and travel pages. Public Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday boards; snapshot pulled October 24, 2026. Recruiting agencies and duplicates removed.

Title families and vertical labels: keyword match on the posting title. The intern column is false for every Databricks row in this snapshot; early-career counts use title text and find none.

Travel: upper bound = largest number in the stated percentage string; bands normalize en-dash and hyphen variants. Workplace: workplace_type lowercased; missing counted as blank. Workplace-type buckets (onsite, hybrid, remote, blank) sum to 122. Remote-listed is a separate location flag. US pay: US-only location, USD range posted, first range on the posting. Posted ranges are not offers.

These 122 listings were live on the October 24, 2026 snapshot. The ATS posted_or_updated field can show dates earlier for evergreen listings; it is not a posting-date range and should not be read as when the role opened.

Downloads: Databricks JSON, field notes, and the per-posting postings JSON. All published datasets are listed on FDE Dispatch job-posting data. Built by
