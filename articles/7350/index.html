Paid Remote Internship: Community & Campus

FDE Interviews · 2026 · 3 min read

Summary

FDE Interviews is offering a fully remote, paid Community & Campus internship focused on spreading its learning content about forward deployed engineering, AI agents, and deploying software in customer environments. Interns improve explanations, prepare social media posts, run campus and community activities, and track engagement and signups; all contributions go through review. Outcomes are agreed individually, such as active campus discussions or content improvements. The role pays US$25 per week and does not require prior FDE experience—applicants should show clear writing, curiosity about AI, and evidence of organizing or explaining something. Applications require a free account, a short self-introduction, and a view-only résumé link; the site notes this is not a software engineering or customer deployment placement.

Key points

  • Fully remote paid internship focused on community, campus outreach, and content
  • Covers forward deployed engineering, AI agents, and customer deployment topics
  • Pays US$25 per week with individually agreed outcome goals
  • No FDE experience required; writing and initiative matter most
  • Explicitly not a software engineering or deployment placement
