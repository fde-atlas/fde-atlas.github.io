We have systematized the generative AI implementation support we have provided to companies across many industries as "FDE Consulting" and published a service guide summarizing the target audience, process, and approach to costs.

Why we systematized it

As generative AI performance has rapidly become commoditized, simply being able to use excellent models is no longer a differentiator. What determines results is the ability to make AI work within the specific realities of your own operations, data, and constraints, and to root it in your organization. According to a survey published by BCG in 2024, only 22% of companies have generated some value beyond the proof-of-concept stage, and only 4% have generated significant value. To help close this "last mile," we have organized and published the scope and process of our services in a clear form that anyone can consider.

Key points of FDE Consulting

The service targets executives and initiative leaders at companies that want to move generative AI beyond the testing stage into production-ready business use. We define "AI that runs within your company" as the endpoint of our support. One aspect is technical: it runs on your data and can be configured to keep data in-house as required. The other is organizational: we transfer operating procedures and improvement processes to your side, and hand over in a state where improvements continue under your own management even after our support ends.

The process begins with a 30-minute online consultation and proceeds through understanding of your operations, implementation, and adoption. For details, please see the service guide.

Related information

For an explanation of the FDE support model itself, see "What is FDE"; you can also find information on our generative AI training for developing FDE talent.
