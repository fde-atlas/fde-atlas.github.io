Summary

This is a promotional page for FDE Jobs, a Japanese job board dedicated exclusively to Forward Deployed Engineer and related customer-embedded AI implementation roles, including Solutions Engineers, AI Implementation Engineers, Customer Engineers, and Deployment Strategists. The pitch: general boards like Indeed dilute the candidate pool for this niche role, while this site captures intent-driven search traffic, claiming page-one Google rankings for queries like “fde 求人” (Search Console 28-day averages). Posting is free with no click or success fees; paid Featured slots cost JPY 30,000/month (up to 3 jobs) and Top slots JPY 60,000/month (up to 10 jobs, with copy review and monthly consulting). Listings include FDE roles at Palantir, OpenAI, and Anthropic, appear in Google Jobs, and go live same-day after review. Robotics roles are routed to sister sites Humanoid Jobs and Physical AI Jobs.

How our sites divide up

This site covers roles that implement AI at the customer site. Robotics roles are handled by our sister media: humanoid robot roles on Humanoid Jobs (humanoid-jobs.com), and broader physical AI roles such as drones, AMRs and robot arms on Physical AI Jobs (physicalai-jobs.com). We will point you to whichever fits the role you are hiring for.

FAQ

Is there a success fee when we hire?

No. Posting is free, and Featured/Top slots are a flat monthly fee only — no per-hire or success fees. Zero extra cost no matter how many you hire.

Is there a minimum contract period?

No minimum term. Renews monthly and can be canceled anytime. To change or cancel your plan, please contact us.

Are there restrictions on job postings?

We accept roles related to Forward Deployed Engineering, solution engineering, AI implementation and customer engineering. Clearly out-of-scope roles may be declined in review.

Will postings appear in Google Jobs?

Yes. We auto-attach JobPosting structured data so listings qualify for Google Jobs. Indexing usually takes 1–2 weeks.

Do you help write job descriptions?

On the Top slot our specialists review and improve your job copy. Free posting and the Featured slot are self-serve, with templates provided.

Can we use other job boards too?

Of course. Combining with Indeed and others gives you both volume and specialized talent.
