Bringing Experts to the Front Lines of Enterprises: Putuo's AI Training Answers "How to Do It, How to Calculate It, How to Make It Stable" October 23, 2026

Source: Shanghai Putuo

Today, artificial intelligence is accelerating its shift from technological breakthroughs to industrial applications, and enterprises' intelligent transformation has moved from "whether to do it" into the deep waters of "how to do it, how to calculate it, and how to make it stable." How to select scenarios, how to use data, and how to calculate investment remain unavoidable practical questions for many enterprises advancing AI applications. On October 21, the "Smart Putuo · Digital Future" AI FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) training event, hosted by the Putuo District Science and Technology Commission, was held at Puxi Financial Plaza. Representatives of high-tech enterprises in the Changfeng area sat together with a professional FDE training team and an AI security expert team, focusing on practical paths for implementing AI on the enterprise side and engaging in in-depth exchanges.

The training opened with "Restructuring Enterprise Operations in the AI Era" by Zhu Ying, founder of Shanghai Zuoweilai Artificial Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd. From machines replacing repetitive physical labor to AI beginning to replace repetitive knowledge work, the presentation interpreted the trend shifts occurring in enterprise competition: AI is not a decoration for enterprises but an operational productivity tool that can create tangible business value in four directions—increasing revenue, reducing costs, improving efficiency, and controlling risks—providing practical reference for the entrepreneurs present.

Addressing the question "Models keep getting stronger, so why is implementation still hard," Zhang Wei, an FDE expert at Shanghai Zuoweilai Artificial Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd., systematically explained the essence of the FDE model using real cases from China Guangfa Bank and State Grid, broke down the implementation path of AI projects from requirements gathering and small-scenario validation to scaled rollout, and offered pragmatic advice on cost control, providing an actionable methodology for enterprises to "embed" AI into their business workflows. "We used to think of FDE as simply engineers on-site or technical support. Only after this training did we understand that under this model, engineers embed themselves in real scenarios, using real data to rapidly build, validate, and iterate AI applications. For us, this could be an actionable path that lowers the cost of trial and error," said one participating enterprise at the event.

Security and compliance are prerequisites for AI applications. Zhao Guoquan, a digital security expert at 360, focused on the security issues enterprises cannot avoid when applying AI. Drawing on cases such as a contract compliance review AI agent, a government AI assistant, and an enterprise AI knowledge base, he shared 360's product system and industry practices for the AI era, proposing that guardrails be built simultaneously across data boundaries, permission controls, and content security, so that AI can be used with confidence and peace of mind.

AI empowering industrial transformation is a key lever for Putuo District to further enhance its core competitiveness as an urban district. This training focused on the real pain points of enterprises' AI implementation, bringing experts to entrepreneurs' doorsteps and delivering solutions to the front line of demand. Building on this event, the District Science and Technology Commission will continue to collect needs, provide targeted training, and match resources, striving to turn the inspiration from these exchanges into concrete results and drive more AI application scenarios to land on the front lines of enterprises.
