Summary

This page from Japan's Defactory offers a free "FDE diagnosis": a 9-question, 3-minute quiz helping companies decide between ordering work via SES staffing or hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE). It also aggregates supporting resources, including a glossary of 47 easily confused terms, a 10-axis comparison of five engagement models (SES, IT consulting, SIer, full-time hiring, FDE) with TCO estimates, six principles for spotting consultancies mislabeling themselves as FDEs, four KPI design principles, and a case study analyzing why 2 of 9 projects missed their targets. Additional content covers security diagnostics, generative AI leak prevention, and vulnerability management service comparisons, alongside Defactory's results-committed security FDE offering.

Key points

  • A free 9-question, 3-minute quiz compares SES vs. FDE engagement.
  • Five engagement models are compared across 10 axes with TCO estimates.
  • Six principles help identify consultancies falsely branding themselves as FDEs.
  • A case study explains why 2 of 9 real projects missed deliverables.
  • Related guides cover security diagnostics and generative AI leak prevention.
