Accenture FDE Role in India: Forward Deployed Engineer Guide

FDE India · 2026-10-31 · 8 min read

Summary

This guide covers Accenture's Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) hiring in India. In late October 2026, roles were listed in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai and Gurugram, mostly filed internally as 'Custom Software Engineer'. Experience requirements range from 5+ years of Java full stack (Bengaluru, mid-level) to 12–15+ years of .NET full stack (Mumbai and Hyderabad), plus a cloud-infrastructure role in Gurugram. The hiring ties into platform partnerships: a 1,000-person FDE workforce for Google's Gemini Enterprise, a joint programme with Workday, and Claude deployment under the Anthropic partnership. Accenture does not publish FDE pay in India; third-party estimates put market rates at ₹14–28 LPA for 3–6 years up to ₹55–100 LPA for 10+ years, with services firms at the low end. The article closes with preparation advice, including matching the named must-have skill and preparing quantified client outcomes.

Key points

  • Hiring supports Google Gemini Enterprise, Workday and Anthropic partnerships.
  • Accenture publishes no India FDE pay; market estimates span ₹14–100 LPA.

An Accenture Forward Deployed Engineer in India is an experienced engineer who works inside a client’s systems to get AI-assisted software, cloud platforms and AI agents into production. In late October 2026 Accenture had FDE roles listed in Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Mumbai, plus a cloud-infrastructure FDE role in Gurugram. Most are filed under the internal project role “Custom Software Engineer”, so the FDE title sits on top of familiar Accenture delivery work.

Why Accenture is building an FDE bench

Accenture’s FDE hiring follows a run of platform partnerships, each of which needs engineers who can deploy a vendor’s AI inside a client’s estate:

  • Google Cloud: on 8 September 2026 Accenture and Google Cloud formed the Accenture Gemini Enterprise Business Group, which will build a 1,000-person FDE workforce, AI Tech Connect reports. The release does not say where those engineers will sit or how many will be internal reskills.
  • Workday: in October 2026 the two firms launched a joint forward deployed engineering programme to put Workday’s AI agents into HR and finance processes, Analytics India Magazine reported. Team size and clients were not disclosed.
  • Anthropic: under the December 2025 partnership, about 30,000 Accenture professionals are being trained on Claude, and Accenture’s forward deployed engineers, which it calls “reinvention deployed engineers”, embed Claude in client environments, according to Anthropic.

Accenture’s UK posting for a Forward Deployed AI Engineer names Anthropic, OpenAI, Microsoft, Google, Salesforce, SAP and Palantir as platforms its FDEs deploy. It also says the job is “not a consulting role” and that FDEs are measured on time-to-value, adoption and reliability, not delivery milestones.

What the FDE does at Accenture

The India listings describe three different flavours of the job:

  • AI-assisted full-stack builder (Bengaluru, mid level). Build enterprise front-end and back-end components with AI coding tools, integrate pre-built AI services and APIs (including RAG features) and keep that work robust at scale. The listing stresses integration over model training.
  • Engineering lead and architect (Mumbai, Staff; Hyderabad, Principal). Write technical design specs, lead architecture governance and code reviews, mentor developers, and lead cloud, DevOps or AI modernisation programmes. Both ask for working knowledge of LLMs and engineering toolchains.
  • Infrastructure FDE (Gurugram, senior). Embed with a client’s infrastructure team on migrations, Kubernetes, networking, Zero Trust security, database modernisation and AI platform deployment, moving from architecture to production in weeks. It also expects Python for automation and AI agent work, and flexibility for 24/7 support.

The common thread is client-embedded delivery: you own whether the system works in the client’s environment, not just whether your code ships.

Two cautions. First, the Mumbai listing gives 12 years of .NET in one place and 10 years of software development in another, and the Gurugram listing asks for 7.5 years overall but 10 years of infrastructure engineering, so read each description in full. Second, every India listing also asks for 15 years of full-time education, so a three-year degree after Class 12 is the minimum.

Pay

None of the Accenture FDE listings we found in India showed pay, and Accenture does not publish FDE pay. For context, Second Talent’s September 2026 rate card puts FDE fixed pay in India at ₹14–28 LPA for 3–6 years, ₹28–65 LPA for 6–10 years and ₹55–100 LPA for 10+ years, and says domestic services companies sit at the bottom of each band while GCCs and US product companies sit at the top. Treat those as market ranges, not Accenture offers. Check your own band in the FDE Salary Estimator.

How to prepare

  • Match the must-have skill exactly. Accenture screens on one named skill per role (Java full stack, .NET full stack, or cloud design and build). Put it in your headline and show years of production work in it.
  • Show a measured client outcome. The postings ask for business impact a CFO would act on. Prepare one story with numbers: time saved, cost cut, adoption rate or incidents reduced after a deployment you owned.
  • Ship an integration-first AI feature. Build something that adds an LLM or RAG feature to an existing enterprise app through APIs, with authentication and logging. That maps directly to the Bengaluru role.
  • Learn one partner platform deeply. Pick the platform closest to your work (Google Gemini Enterprise, Claude, Workday, Salesforce or SAP) and get hands-on or certified. Accenture’s FDE teams are organised around these partnerships.
  • Internal candidates: search by project role. Look for “Custom Software Engineer” openings tagged Forward Deployed Engineer, tell your supervisor you are interested, and log your AI coding-tool usage and client wins.

Accenture FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Application: Apply through Accenture's careers site or a job board listing. India FDE postings are filed under the project role 'Custom Software Engineer', so search both titles.
  • Screening and technical rounds: Accenture does not publish the FDE interview format for India. Expect questions tied to the listing's must-have skill (Java full stack, .NET full stack or cloud design and build).
  • What is assessed: Accenture's FDE postings stress client-embedded delivery and owning production outcomes. Its UK posting says assessment centres on deployment experience and outcome ownership.
