Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer Salary: Every Band on the Board, and What It Tells a Founder

Hayat Amin · Operator · 2026-10-20 · 8 min read

Summary

Hayat Amin, a former technology CFO, read all 666 live postings on Anthropic's Greenhouse board on 20 October 2026 and extracted every forward deployed engineer salary band. The only US individual FDE role is Staff level, paying $280,000–$320,000 in New York, San Francisco or Seattle; London pays £225,000–£255,000, while Paris and Munich print no band. He compares these with OpenAI's FDE bands ($185K–$300K with equity) and Palantir's New York range of $135,000–$200,000, concluding Anthropic's floor sits near OpenAI's ceiling. His founder takeaway: few companies can justify or fully utilize a $300K FDE, and Anthropic itself admits demand exceeds its team's capacity, so smaller firms should buy this work as short engagements instead.

Key points

  • Anthropic's US Staff FDE pays $280,000–$320,000; London pays £225,000–£255,000.
  • Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering in New York prints $320,000–$405,000, the highest FDE band.
  • Anthropic's Staff floor sits near the top of OpenAI's FDE band and $80,000 above Palantir's New York ceiling.
  • Anthropic's own GTM posting says FDE demand runs well ahead of team capacity.
  • The author advises founders to buy FDE-style work in weeks-long engagements rather than hire at $300K.

The Anthropic forward deployed engineer salary in the US is $280,000 to $320,000 a year. That's the band on the one US role, Staff Forward Deployed Engineer in New York, San Francisco or Seattle, read on 20 October 2026. London pays £225,000 to £255,000. Paris and Munich print no band.

I'm Hayat Amin. I spent twenty years as a technology chief financial officer and sold three companies in that seat, so I've signed off a lot of salary bands, and now I do forward deployed work myself. This morning I read all 666 live postings on Anthropic's own Greenhouse board, then the forward deployed bands at OpenAI and Palantir. Below is every number, what it buys, and what a founder should take from it.

Every forward deployed band on Anthropic's board

Seven of the 666 postings carry Forward Deployed or FDE in the title. Another 23 mention forward deployed engineers in the text, 30 in all. Here are the seven, with the band each one prints:

  • Staff Forward Deployed Engineer, New York City, San Francisco, Seattle: $280,000 to $320,000.
  • Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering, New York City: $320,000 to $405,000.
  • GTM Strategy & Operations, FDE, San Francisco or New York City: $270,000 to $310,000.
  • Forward Deployed Engineer, London: £225,000 to £255,000.
  • Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering, London: £255,000 to £325,000.
  • Forward Deployed Engineer, Paris: no band printed.
  • Forward Deployed Engineer, Munich: no band printed.

A band is a range across levels, not an offer. The Staff posting says that for sales roles the range is on target earnings, base plus the commission target. It doesn't say whether the FDE counts as one, and it doesn't describe an equity grant. If you're weighing an offer, those are the two questions to ask.

What changed since 12 October

Eight days ago I counted this board for Anthropic forward deployed engineer, and the US had no individual FDE job, only a New York manager. Now there's one, last updated on 14 October. It's Staff level, and it reads like it.

The posting asks you to own technical strategy for a key account or vertical, review designs and unblock other FDEs, and lead engagements delivered with partners where you set the quality bar for work you don't build yourself. You'll represent Anthropic to VPs and C-level, and travel about 25% of the time. It says you don't need to have shipped agents to production, but you do need deep experience running production systems and hands-on time with agents. Python is the named language. Former technical founders are told to apply.

London is a different level. The London FDE posting is the individual role, with travel at 25 to 50 percent. It shares its £225,000 to £255,000 band with Anthropic's London Applied AI Engineer roles, so in London the two titles are paid the same.

The roles around it, same board

If you can't get the FDE title, these are the nearest seats, with the US bands Anthropic prints:

  • Applied AI Engineer, Enterprise Tech, San Francisco or New York: $200,000 to $320,000.
  • Applied AI Engineer, Public Sector, Washington DC: $280,000 to $320,000.
  • Applied AI Architect, Startups, San Francisco or New York: $240,000 to $315,000.
  • Technical Deployment Lead, Boston, New York, San Francisco, Seattle: $275,000 to $380,000.
  • Manager, Technical Deployment (Financial Services), New York: $240,000 to $450,000.

The Enterprise Tech band starts $80,000 below the Staff FDE floor and tops out at the same $320,000. That's the level gap in one line. I wrote up how the two jobs differ in forward deployed engineer vs applied AI engineer.

Anthropic next to OpenAI and Palantir

Same morning, the forward deployed bands at the other two big hirers:

  • OpenAI, Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), New York, San Francisco, Seattle, including the Healthcare, Legal and Financial Services roles: $185K to $300K, offers equity.
  • OpenAI, Forward Deployed Software Engineer, New York and Seattle: $153K to $325K. San Francisco: $185K to $325K. Both offer equity.
  • OpenAI, Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering, New York: $302K to $385K, offers equity.
  • OpenAI, Deployment Lead, FDE, New York or San Francisco: $230K to $294K, offers equity.
  • Palantir, Forward Deployed Software Engineer, New York: $135,000 to $200,000, and total pay may also include restricted stock units and a sign-on bonus.

Read it as levels. Anthropic's only US FDE job is Staff, and its floor of $280,000 sits near the top of OpenAI's FDE band. Palantir's New York top of $200,000 is $80,000 under that floor, and its board also lists new grad and internship FDE roles, so it's a different ladder. OpenAI's London FDE prints no band, so of the two AI labs only Anthropic gives London a number. The Palantir side of the role is in what is a forward deployed engineer at Palantir.

What the salary tells a founder

Here's the CFO read. Take the Staff band midpoint, $300,000. Divide by 52 and it's about $5,770 a week in base salary, before payroll tax, benefits or anything else on top. That's what Anthropic pays for one senior person who can walk in with a business problem and leave with a production system.

You aren't competing with that number to hire one. You couldn't keep one busy if you did, unless you have a dozen workflows that need rebuilding. What you need is the work, a few weeks at a time, from someone who has done it before. Anthropic's own GTM Strategy & Operations posting says demand for FDE is well ahead of the team's capacity, and that part of the job is moving more of the work to partners. Even the company paying $320,000 can't hire fast enough to serve everyone itself.

An Anthropic FDE also works on Claude, for Anthropic's most strategic customers. If you run a company of 30 or 300 people in New York, Chicago or London, that FDE isn't coming to connect your CRM to your accounting system.

What is the Anthropic forward deployed engineer salary?

The London Forward Deployed Engineer printed £225,000 to £255,000. The Paris and Munich FDE postings print no band. Each figure is the band on Anthropic's own posting, a range across levels, not an offer.

What is the Anthropic forward deployed engineer salary in London?

£225,000 to £255,000 a year for the Forward Deployed Engineer in London, the same band Anthropic prints for its London Applied AI Engineer roles on Enterprise and Startups. The London Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering prints £255,000 to £325,000.

What is Anthropic FDE pay compared with OpenAI?

OpenAI's US Forward Deployed Engineer postings in New York, San Francisco and Seattle print $185K to $300K and say they offer equity. Its Forward Deployed Software Engineer roles print $153K to $325K in New York and Seattle and $185K to $325K in San Francisco. Anthropic's one US individual FDE role is Staff level, at $280,000 to $320,000, so the fair comparison is with the top of OpenAI's bands.

Does the Anthropic FDE salary include equity?

The Staff FDE posting prints an annual salary band and does not describe an equity grant. It says that for sales roles the range is on target earnings, base plus commission target. The general text mentions optional equity donation matching. If equity matters to you, ask the recruiter, because the posting does not answer it.

How much do Anthropic forward deployed engineer managers make?

Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering in New York City prints $320,000 to $405,000. The same role in London prints £255,000 to £325,000.

Is there an Anthropic forward deployed engineer job in the US?

Yes, as of this month. When I counted on 12 October 2026 there was no individual FDE posting in the US. On 20 October there was one: Staff Forward Deployed Engineer, across New York City, San Francisco and Seattle, last updated 14 October.
