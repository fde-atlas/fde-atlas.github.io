Anthropic, the company behind Claude, has not posted a role called “Forward Deployed Engineer” in India yet. What it hires for in India is the Applied AI Architect: a senior, customer-facing technical role based in Bengaluru (one listing names Mumbai) that takes enterprise customers from discovery to a working deployment of Claude. The real FDE title exists at Anthropic, but only abroad. Today the India openings are few and need 10+ years of experience.

What the FDE does at Anthropic

Anthropic’s own FDE job shows what the company means by the role. Its Forward Deployed Engineer, Applied AI listing, reposted on the Menlo Ventures job board and now closed, describes an engineer who embeds with strategic customers and builds production applications with Claude inside their systems. The work it lists includes MCP servers, sub-agents and agent skills. The FDE also turns repeated deployment work into patterns that Product and Engineering can reuse, and spends 25–50% of the time travelling to customer sites.

The India role overlaps with this but leans earlier in the sales cycle. According to the Bengaluru Applied AI Architect listing on Built In, the architect is a pre-sales technical adviser for large enterprises in India and APAC. The role:

  • runs technical discovery with customers alongside account executives;
  • advises on architecture for the Claude API and Claude for Work;
  • designs evaluation frameworks so customers can measure whether Claude works for their use case;
  • writes technical material for both engineers and executives;
  • passes field feedback back to Anthropic’s Product and Engineering teams.

Scaler Topics (October 2026) makes the same distinction: it calls the Bengaluru architect a pre-sales job that differs from the hands-on FDE. If you want the general pattern first, read our explainer on what a forward deployed engineer is, and our comparison of solution architects and FDEs.

How big is Anthropic’s India hiring?

Small but growing. Anthropic opened its Bengaluru office in February 2026, its second in Asia after Tokyo, with former Microsoft India MD Irina Ghose as Managing Director for India, according to Business Today. The office “will recruit across technical, research and business roles”, the report said. Inc42 added that India is Claude’s second-largest market, that India run-rate revenue had doubled since October 2025, and that Anthropic planned an applied AI offering for business customers. Named Indian customers include Air India, CRED and Razorpay.

By 9 November 2026, FDE Jobs List, which tracks Anthropic’s live postings, counted 66 FDE or FDE-adjacent roles across 19 cities. Only four were in India:

  • Applied AI Architect (Global Customers), Bangalore, posted 4 Nov 2026
  • Applied AI Architect, Partnerships, Bangalore, posted 16 Oct 2026
  • Applied AI Architect, Bangalore, posted 9 Oct 2026
  • Applied AI Architect, Mumbai, posted 21 Sep 2026

Earlier in the year, Digit (April 2026) reported Applied AI Engineer and Architect roles focused on deploying AI systems and fitting LLMs into business workflows, then based only in Bengaluru.

Three points stand out:

  • India’s openings are senior. The Bengaluru architect asks for 10+ years against 4+ for the overseas FDE. Mid-level engineers are not the target yet.
  • Evals are a common thread. Both the India architect and the overseas FDE listings mention evaluation frameworks, so expect to be asked how you measure an LLM system’s quality.
  • Enterprise selling experience counts. The India listing wants people who have handled multi-stakeholder buying cycles, not only strong coders.

Pay

Anthropic does not publish pay for India roles. FDE Jobs List shows no salary for any of the four India postings as of November 2026, and the Built In listing shows only a benefits summary. No credible crowdsourced India figure turned up either.

Overseas listings give a sense of the level, not an Indian number. The closed FDE, Applied AI listing on the Menlo Ventures board showed USD 200,000–300,000 a year, and Scaler Topics reported USD 280,000–320,000 for US FDE roles in September 2026. Indian offers are set locally and will not be a currency conversion of these. To compare senior FDE-style pay at other employers in India, use our FDE salary hub.

How to prepare

  • Check your fit against the India title, not the FDE title. The live India roles are Applied AI Architect positions asking 10+ years of customer-facing technical work. If you have fewer years, look at the OpenAI page and others while you build towards it.
  • Build one evaluated Claude integration. Ship something on the Claude API that connects to a real system — a support desk, a document store or a database — and write a small eval suite that shows quality, cost and latency. Both the India and overseas listings centre on evals.
  • Learn the Claude agent stack. The FDE listing names MCP servers, sub-agents and agent skills as deliverables. Write a simple MCP server for an internal tool and be ready to explain why you designed it that way.
  • Prepare a discovery-to-deployment story from Indian enterprise work. The India listing asks for experience with multi-stakeholder buying cycles in India or APAC. Have one example with a bank, telecom or large IT customer where you shaped the architecture and got it live.
  • Practise role-play and debugging rounds. The one public account of Anthropic’s FDE interview, summarised by Scaler Topics, includes an imaginary-client exercise and Python bug fixing in a notebook. Our FDE interview process guide covers how to handle both.

Anthropic FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Application: Apply through Anthropic's careers site and filter for India; Digit (April 2026) reported that the India applied AI jobs were only available in Bengaluru at the time.
  • Screens and technical rounds: Anthropic has not published an interview format for its India applied AI roles. Scaler Topics cites one anonymous Glassdoor report (March 2026) for the overseas FDE role: recruiter screen, hiring-manager call, an agents coding screen, a customer role-play or ML system-design exercise, and Python bug fixing in a notebook.
  • Final round: The same single Glassdoor report, as summarised by Scaler Topics, ends with a culture-fit and behavioural round. One report is not a confirmed process, so treat it as a guide only.

Frequently asked questions

Does Anthropic hire Forward Deployed Engineers in India?

Not under that title so far. Anthropic's Forward Deployed Engineer postings are in cities such as New York, San Francisco, London, Paris and Munich. In India it hires Applied AI Architects in Bengaluru (and one Mumbai listing), which cover similar customer-facing technical work but lean towards pre-sales.

What is the salary of an Anthropic Applied AI Architect in India?

It is not publicly reported. FDE Jobs List shows no salary for any of Anthropic's India roles as of November 2026. A closed overseas Forward Deployed Engineer, Applied AI listing showed USD 200,000–300,000 a year, but that does not convert into an Indian offer.

How much experience does Anthropic ask for its Bengaluru roles?

The Applied AI Architect listing for Bengaluru on Built In asks for 10+ years in technical customer-facing roles such as Solutions Architect, Sales Engineer or Technical Account Manager. Anthropic's overseas FDE listing asked for 4+ years, so India's current openings are clearly senior.

When did Anthropic open its India office?

Anthropic opened its Bengaluru office in February 2026, its second in Asia after Tokyo, according to Inc42 and Business Today. Irina Ghose, formerly Microsoft India MD, leads it as Managing Director for India.
