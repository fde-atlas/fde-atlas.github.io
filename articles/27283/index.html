How engagements work

  • Share your goals

    Tell us the roles, the tech stack your team uses and what they need to be able to do afterwards.

  • Shape the format

    We match the format to the goal, from a 2-day hands-on workshop to a multi-week programme, several batches or certification preparation, like the programmes listed below.

    Sessions are led by Deepesh Mishra's team and built around hands-on work in your stack, for example building a deployable agentic AI solution from scratch in a 2-day workshop.
