CFDE Foundation Admissions & Scholarship

Artificial Intelligence Uni (AIU.ac) · 2026-10-08 · 6 min read

Summary

Artificial Intelligence Uni (AIU.ac) has opened applications for the December 2026 cohort of its CFDE Foundation course, an 18-week programme starting 14 December 2026 requiring roughly 8 hours per week. The fee is £1,099 with no application charge, and applicants are expected to already know Python, Git, Docker and at least one cloud platform. A limited AIU Access Scholarship reduces fees for nationals of World Bank low-income or lower-middle-income economies and people between jobs: 50% off (£549) for applications from 7–25 October 2026, 30% off (£769) until 30 November. Fees are refunded in full if an application is rejected or cancelled within 14 days; no refunds after the cohort starts. Employer funding is supported, and applicants are screened against UK sanctions lists.

Key points

  • December 2026 CFDE Foundation cohort: 18 weeks, starts 14 December, enrolment closes 30 November.
  • Fee is £1,099, no application fee; applicants must already know Python, Git, Docker and a cloud platform.
  • AIU Access Scholarship: 50% off (£549) until 25 October, 30% off (£769) until 30 November, limited awards.
  • Scholarship targets low/lower-middle-income economy nationals and people between roles.
  • Full refund if rejected or cancelled within 14 days; no refunds after commencement; employer funding available.

Who can apply

The CFDE Foundation course is open to anyone. Working proficiency in Python, Git, Docker and at least one cloud platform is expected; these tools are used throughout and are not taught from scratch. Applications are assessed against the eligibility criteria on this page.

We are unable to accept applications where UK sanctions, export control, or anti-money-laundering law prohibits us from supplying our services. All applicants are screened against the UK OFSI consolidated list and applicable UK regimes.

Fees

Course fee: £1,099. Fees are in pounds sterling and are payable in full on application. Invoices and receipts are issued by Artificial Intelligence Uni Ltd.

There is no application fee. The programme fee is payable on application, after your scholarship band (if applicable) has been confirmed, and is refunded in full if your application is not accepted.

AIU Access Scholarship

A limited number of scholarship places reduce the course fee. The scholarship reduces the fee; it is not full funding and carries no stipend.

You are eligible if you are a national of an economy classified by the World Bank as low income or lower-middle income, or if you are currently between roles. Nationals of eligible economies living elsewhere may apply by certifying current financial hardship or unemployment. The reduction is the same whichever basis applies. Your fee band is confirmed before any payment is requested. As part of a scholarship application you will be asked to describe your circumstances briefly and to sign a declaration; documentary proof may be requested.

Your tier is fixed by the date and time you submit your application (23:59 UK time on the closing day). Eligibility is confirmed before you are asked to pay. Awards are limited and are not guaranteed. The scholarship applies to the course fee only.

How the application works

Step 1

Apply

Complete the application at apply.aiu.ac. Indicate whether you are applying for the AIU Access Scholarship.

Step 2

Fee band confirmed

We confirm your fee band, normally within 2 working days, and send you a secure payment link. The link is valid for 7 days, and payment must in any case be received by 30 November 2026.

Step 3

Pay and hold a provisional place

Pay the fee. You receive a provisional place immediately, with a receipt and your application reference.

Step 4

Verification

We verify your application, including an identity check, within 5 working days.

Step 5

Confirmed

On confirmation you receive your admission letter with your roll number and section. If we are unable to confirm your place, your fee is refunded in full within 5 working days.

Before the cohort

Course access

No course access or materials are provided before the commencement date of 14 December 2026.

Employer-funded places

Many learners have their place funded by an employer. We provide a sponsorship letter and a pro-forma invoice addressed to the employer on request, and the fee can be paid directly by corporate card through the learner’s payment link. The case writes itself: Deloitte alone plans to hire, train and certify forward deployed engineers through fiscal year 2027, and the engineers who can take AI from pilot to production are the scarcest people in the market. To arrange employer funding, email admissions@aiu.ac with the application reference.

Refunds are always issued to the original payer.

If you need to ask your manager, use our employer funding letter.

Refunds

There is no application fee. The programme fee is refunded in full if your application is not accepted, or if you cancel within 14 days of payment. Cancellations after 14 days and before 14 December 2026 are refunded less a £77 administration fee. No refunds are made from the commencement date. In cases of hardship, deferral to a later cohort may be requested by the end of week 4, subject to availability. Full details are in the Refund Policy.

October 2026 cohort (2026-C3)

The October 2026 cohort (2026-C3-CFDE-F) reached capacity and closed to new applications on Monday 5 October 2026; it commences Monday 19 October 2026. Applications received from 7 October are for the December 2026 cohort.

Admissions FAQs

How do I apply?+

Applications are made online at apply.aiu.ac and take about ten minutes. There is no application fee; any scholarship band is confirmed before payment is requested.

Is there an application fee?+

No. The programme fee is payable on application, after your scholarship band (if applicable) has been confirmed, and is refunded in full if your application is not accepted.

Is a scholarship available?+

Yes. The AIU Access Scholarship offers a limited number of fee reductions for nationals of economies classified by the World Bank as low income or lower-middle income, and for applicants currently between roles. Tiers, dates and eligibility are set out on this page.

When does the cohort start?+

The December 2026 cohort of CFDE Foundation (2027-C1-CFDE-F) commences on Monday 14 December 2026. Applications are open now; enrolment closes Monday 30 November 2026. The October 2026 cohort is at capacity and closed to new applications.

What happens if I apply after enrolment closes?+

Applications received too late to be banded and paid by 30 November 2026 are considered for the next cohort in 2027.

Can my employer fund this programme?+

Yes. We issue a sponsorship letter and a pro-forma invoice on request before payment. Employers may pay directly or reimburse the applicant. Contact admissions@aiu.ac.

What is your refund policy?+

Applications that are not accepted are refunded in full. Cancellations within 14 days of payment are refunded in full. Cancellations after 14 days and before the cohort commences are refunded less a £77 administration fee. No refunds are made from the commencement date. In cases of hardship, deferral to a later cohort may be requested by the end of week 4, subject to availability. Full details are in the Refund Policy.

What information do you ask for?+

The application asks for your contact details, country of residence, LinkedIn profile, and professional background. If you apply for the AIU Access Scholarship, you will also be asked for your country of nationality, a brief statement of your circumstances, and a signed declaration; documentary proof may be requested. We do not ask for date of birth, photograph, marital status, religion or health information. See the Privacy Policy for how your data is used and retained.

How are decisions made?+

Applications are assessed against the published eligibility criteria on this page. Any decision to decline an application or revoke a place is made or confirmed by a member of the AIU admissions team. You may ask for a human review and contest a decision by emailing admissions@aiu.ac. Our Non-Discrimination Policy applies to every admissions and scholarship decision.
