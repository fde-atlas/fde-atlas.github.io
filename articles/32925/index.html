Forward deployed engineer interview process by employer

Kernavique · 2026-11-22 · 6 min read

Summary

On November 22, 2026, Kernavique reviewed what 25 employers of forward deployed engineers publish about their own interview processes. None publishes a stage-by-stage process written specifically for forward deployed roles; 11 publish stages for engineering or all roles, which the article lists in each employer's own words with links, using only employer sources rather than interview forums. Notable details include Snowflake's two-to-four-week timeline and OpenAI's final interviews of 4–6 hours with 4–6 interviewers. Anduril, Cursor, Rogo and Snorkel AI publish nothing. The author advises candidates to request stages in writing and hiring teams to define and state their process upfront.

Key points

  • Of 25 employers checked, none publishes an interview process written for forward deployed roles.
  • 11 employers publish stages for engineering or all roles, quoted from their own careers pages.
  • Snowflake is the only employer stating an overall length: two to four weeks.
  • OpenAI's final interviews typically run 4–6 hours with 4–6 people over 1–2 days.
  • Anduril, Cursor, Rogo and Snorkel AI publish no process information at all.

Our reading

What it means

The counts above come from employers’ own ads. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

Read the employer’s own page before the first call, and ask for the stages for your role in writing. Where an employer publishes nothing, that question is the first one to ask.

If you are hiring them

Engineers compare your process with the ones on this page. Decide the number of stages and who is in each before you open the role, and tell every engineer at the first call. A process you can state in four lines is one you can run on time.

Questions people ask

  • Is there a standard forward deployed engineer interview?
  • No employer we checked publishes one. Of 25 employers, 11 publish stages for engineering or all roles. Most begin with a call and end with a set of interviews with several people; what sits between differs by employer.
  • How long does the process take?
  • Snowflake is the only one that gives a length for the whole process: up to two to four weeks for engineering roles. OpenAI says its final interviews are typically 4 to 6 hours with 4 to 6 people over 1 to 2 days. The others give no overall timeline.
  • Why are well-known interview formats missing?
  • Because we list only what the employer itself publishes. Accounts on interview forums and from preparation companies are not used here, since we cannot check them against a source.
  • Will the process for my role match these stages?
  • Not necessarily. Most of these pages say the process varies by role, and none is written for forward deployed roles. Ask the employer’s hiring team for the stages when you are invited to the first call.
  • Which employers publish nothing?
  • We found nothing published by Anduril, Cursor, Rogo or Snorkel AI about their own process on November 22, 2026. That is a statement about what we could find, and it may change.
