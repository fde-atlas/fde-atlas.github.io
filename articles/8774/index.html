Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer Interview (No Offer): The recruiter call was pretty

Taro · 2026-03-10 · 3 min read

Summary

A candidate shares a no-offer interview experience for Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer role in New York. The process included a relaxed recruiter call with behavioral questions, a 30-minute technical screen with a LeetCode medium/hard problem, and an on-site covering data manipulation, decomp/learning, and behavioral rounds; the coding task was the classic trapping-rain-water matrix problem. Aggregated data from 36 interview experiences shows a 14% pass rate, indicating high selectivity, though 56% of candidates rated the process positively. The post also includes employee reviews: fast growth and business impact, but high pressure, occasional overtime, and team quality that varies widely.

Key points

  • Process: recruiter call, 30-min technical screen, on-site rounds
  • Coding question: trapping rain water in an integer matrix
  • 36 reported interviews: only 14% pass rate, 56% positive
  • Employees cite rapid growth and business impact as pros
  • Cons: high pressure, overtime, and uneven team quality
