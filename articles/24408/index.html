First-Hand Overseas Information and Insights on Implementation and Development

Original title: FDEブログ｜海外の一次情報と実装・育成の知見

株式会社本質 · 2026-11-03 · 4 min read

Summary

This is an index page for the FDE blog of Japanese company HONSHITSU (株式会社本質), which summarizes overseas primary sources about AI model releases, forward deployed engineering practice, and engineer training for SES and SIer practitioners. Each post pairs a vendor announcement (Canva Brush, Liquid AI LFM3, Moonshot AI Kimi K3.1 Pro, Mistral Voxtral 3 and Timestral, Meta Muse Wonder, OpenAI GPT-6 Intelligent UI, GitHub ReviewBench, Anthropic Claude Opus 5.5 pricing, Palantir AIP Evals) with a hypothetical business scenario examining how to instruct AI, divide work between AI and human review, and design evals. Other posts cover FDE development topics: a Palantir FDSE interview, Shyam Sankar's 2024 essay, AWS partner FDE programs, and DXC–Anthropic training collaboration.

Key points

  • Blog curates overseas primary sources on FDE work and training for Japanese SES/SIer readers.
  • Each post analyzes a vendor release via a hypothetical business workflow.
  • Recurring themes: splitting tasks between AI instructions and human verification.
  • FDE talent pieces cover Palantir interviews, Shyam Sankar, AWS and DXC–Anthropic programs.
  • Posts are short, roughly 3–6 minutes each, published September–October 2026.
