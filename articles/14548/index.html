Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-10-30 · 8 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch analyzed 50 Salesforce FDE-family job postings from an October 24, 2026 snapshot of public boards, 6.9% of the 725 postings tracked and third behind Databricks and Palantir. The set includes 38 Forward Deployed Engineer postings and 12 Deployment Strategist postings, with no management titles. Levels named include Senior (9), Principal (8), Lead (7) and one Associate. Notably, 84% of the roles sit outside the US across 17 countries, the highest non-US share among large employers, and 10 titles specify a working language such as Italian or Arabic. Travel is mentioned in 43 postings with a median stated upper bound of 25%. US pay ranges span $88,970 to $365,200, labeled base salary only.

Published

Salesforce posts Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) roles from Associate to Principal level, plus Deployment Strategist roles, and some titles name a product line: Data 360, Agentforce, Public Sector or Migration Delivery. In FDE Dispatch's October 24, 2026 snapshot of public job boards, Salesforce accounts for 50 of 725 FDE-family postings (6.9%), third behind Databricks and Palantir: 38 engineer-family and 12 deployment strategist. 42 of the 50 (84.0%) are located outside the US, against 40.1% across all employers, and 10 titles name a language such as Italian or Arabic. 35 postings state a travel percentage, 28 of them at 25%. US engineer postings list four pay ranges between $88,970 and $365,200; posted ranges are not offers. FDE Dispatch is not affiliated with Salesforce; the figures come from Salesforce's public job postings.

What Salesforce calls the role

Salesforce uses two titles for this work: Forward Deployed Engineer (38 postings, sometimes shortened to FDE) and Deployment Strategist (12). It posts no FDE management titles in this snapshot.

Half of the titles name a level. Counting the highest level named: Senior 9, Principal 8, Lead 7 and Associate 1; 25 titles name no level. No title says new grad or intern, so the one Associate Forward Deployed Engineer posting (Public Sector, Missionforce) is the earliest-career seat in this cut.

How much Salesforce Forward Deployed roles travel

Travel appears in 43 of 50 Salesforce postings (86.0%), and 35 state a percentage; the median stated upper bound is 25%, against 30% across all employers on how much forward deployed engineers travel.

Normalized bands: 25% on 28 postings, 25-30% on 3, 25-50% on 3 and 50-75% on 1. The 50-75% posting is the Lead Forward Deployed Engineering role for Data Science & Integration in Herndon, Virginia.

Office, flexible or remote

Salesforce uses its own workplace labels: Office - Flexible on 46 postings, Office Tech-Flexible on 3 and Remote Customer-Based on 1, which sum to 50. 6 postings list at least one remote location, all of them US-located; the remote option does not appear on the 42 postings outside the US. Across the full snapshot, 199 of 725 postings (27.4%) list remote on the locations index.

On the US posting FDE Dispatch hand-checked on October 22, 2026, Salesforce labels its range base salary only (excluding bonus, equity and benefits) and adds a higher range for California, New York and select metros. Method and data

Population: 50 Salesforce postings with in_page_stats among the 725 FDE-family postings from 121 employers counted on the salary, job titles, locations and travel pages. All 50 come from Salesforce's public Workday careers site; snapshot pulled October 24, 2026. Recruiting agencies and duplicates removed.

Level, product line and language: keyword match on the posting title. Level is the highest level named (Principal, then Lead, then Senior, then Associate). US vs outside the US and remote-listed use the published locations-index definitions: a posting is US-located when every listed location is in the US, and remote-listed when any listed location contains Remote.

Travel: upper bound = largest number in the stated percentage string; bands normalize en-dash and hyphen variants. US pay: US-only location, USD range posted, first range on the posting. Posted ranges are not offers. These 50 listings were live on the October 24, 2026 snapshot; counts are open postings on one day.

Downloads: Salesforce JSON, field notes, and the per-posting postings JSON. All published datasets are listed on FDE Dispatch job-posting data. Built by

Questions people ask

What does Salesforce call the Forward Deployed Engineer role?

Salesforce posts Forward Deployed Engineer titles at Associate, Senior, Lead and Principal level, and Deployment Strategist titles. In the October 24, 2026 snapshot, 38 of Salesforce's 50 FDE-family postings are engineer-family and 12 are deployment strategist; none are management titles. By the highest level named in the title: Senior 9, Principal 8, Lead 7, Associate 1, and 25 name no level. Product lines named in titles: Data 360 (6), Public Sector including Missionforce (6), Agentforce (3) and Migration Delivery (3); 32 name none. No title says new grad or intern.

Where are Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineer jobs located?

42 of Salesforce's 50 FDE-family postings (84.0%) are located outside the US and 8 are US-located, against 40.1% outside the US across all 725 postings. That is the highest non-US share among the five employers with 30 or more postings (Accenture 60.0%, OpenAI 50.0%, Databricks 36.1%, Palantir 27.6%). The most common countries are the United Kingdom (6), France (6), Ireland (5), Italy (5) and the United Arab Emirates (4). 10 titles name a language: Italian (5), Arabic (2), and Dutch, German, French, Hebrew and Polish (1 each).

How much do Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineers travel?

Travel is mentioned in 43 of Salesforce's 50 postings (86.0%), and 35 state a percentage. The median stated upper bound is 25%, against 30% across all employers on the travel page. Normalized bands: 28 postings at 25%, 3 at 25-30%, 3 at 25-50% and 1 at 50-75%.

What pay does Salesforce post for Forward Deployed Engineer roles?

Among US-located postings, engineer-family pay appears as $88,970 to $287,910 (4), $172,500 to $260,100 (1), $96,300 to $176,700 (1) and $148,500 to $365,200 (1). The one US deployment strategist posting with pay lists $134,750 to $287,910. On the posting FDE Dispatch hand-checked on October 22, 2026, Salesforce states its range is base salary only. Five postings in Italy list EUR ranges and one in Toronto lists a CAD range. Posted ranges are not offers. Across all employers, the US engineer median midpoint is $216,104.

Are Salesforce Forward Deployed roles remote?

Salesforce labels 46 of its 50 FDE-family postings Office - Flexible, 3 Office Tech-Flexible and 1 Remote Customer-Based. 6 postings list at least one remote location, all of them US-located, against 199 of 725 postings (27.4%) across the full snapshot on the locations index.
