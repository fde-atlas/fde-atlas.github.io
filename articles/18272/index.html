FDE Provider Chaos Map & Excel List | 53 Companies, 7 Categories

Summary

This Japanese resource page from AIエージェントナビ offers a free downloadable chaos map (PDF) and comparison spreadsheet (Excel) covering 53 companies in Japan that provide FDE-style services—engineers who embed at customer sites to implement and operationalize AI. Companies are grouped into seven categories, such as resident implementation, consulting support, own-product-based, enterprise, industry-specific, certified partner programs, and talent matching. The spreadsheet lists each firm's category, characteristics, official URLs, source links, and verification dates, and rates information reliability as high, medium, or low depending on whether claims come from official sources plus third-party reporting, company statements only, or unverified data. It is intended as a practical tool for businesses comparing vendors for AI adoption.

Key points

  • Covers 53 Japanese companies offering FDE-style AI implementation services.
  • Classifies firms into seven categories, e.g., resident implementation and industry-specific.
  • Excel sheet includes features, official URLs, sources, and verification dates.
  • Each company's information reliability is rated high, medium, or low.
  • Free download aimed at helping businesses choose AI implementation partners.

FDE Provider Companies Chaos Map & List Excel

The "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" embeds into customers' operations and handles AI implementation through to adoption and establishment. We are offering a free set of a chaos map (PDF) organizing 53 companies in Japan that provide FDE-type services into 7 categories, and a list (Excel) covering each company's features, information reliability, and official URLs. It can be used directly for comparing and evaluating potential AI implementation partners.

  • Excel list: cross-comparison of category, features, notes, official website URL, information source URL, and verification date
