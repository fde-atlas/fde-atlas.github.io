Not Just Diagnostics: Zept Inc. Officially Launches "External AI Division" to Implement AI Solutions in the Field

Original title: 診断だけでは終わらせない。Zept株式会社、AI活用課題を現場実装する「外部AI事業部」を正式ローンチ

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-09-19 · 7 min read

Summary

Zept K.K., an AI and DX consulting firm based in Himeji, Japan, has launched an "External AI Division" service that goes beyond AI readiness diagnostics to hands-on implementation. Using a Forward Deployed Engineer model, embedded engineers work on-site through a cycle of problem discovery, design, PoC, implementation, improvement, and adoption. The service is offered in three monthly subscription plans: Light at 600,000 yen (40 hours/month), Standard at 1.2 million yen (80 hours), and Premium at 2 million yen (160 hours), plus a custom VIP plan for company-wide AI transformation. Zept argues that splitting work among consultants, vendors, and developers causes knowledge fragmentation and unclear accountability, so it handles everything from workflow audits to tool building and staff training. A sample timeline moves from PoC to production within three months.

Key points

  • Zept launched an "External AI Division" offering end-to-end AI implementation support, not just diagnostics.
  • FDE-style engineers run a cycle of discovery, design, PoC, implementation, improvement, and adoption on-site.
  • Three plans: Light 600k yen/month, Standard 1.2M, Premium 2M, plus a custom VIP plan for company-wide AX.
  • Zept claims a single team avoids the fragmentation and unclear accountability of using multiple vendors.
  • Small PoCs with real business data precede full rollout; a sample case reaches production in three months.

FDE-style hands-on support covering everything from issue identification to PoC, implementation, improvement, and adoption. Offered in 3 plans starting at 600,000 yen per month

Zept Inc. (Headquarters: Himeji, Hyogo Prefecture; President and CEO: Koshi Enoki), which provides AI adoption and DX support, has launched its "External AI Division," providing end-to-end support for corporate AI utilization, from "business inventory analysis" to "on-the-ground implementation and adoption."

Zept provides an "AI Utilization Inventory Report" that breaks down a company's business processes down to the task level and visualizes "where AI should be applied."

However, simply identifying issues does not change what happens on the ground.

"We contracted an AI tool, but only a few employees use it"

"We held training sessions, but it hasn't taken hold in actual work"

"We've decided which processes to automate with AI, but we have no one who can implement it"

To prevent this state of "adopted but unchanged," Zept has systematized the "External AI Division" as an execution team that implements the issues identified through inventory analysis into actual business processes and provides hands-on support until they are used continuously in the field.

AI utilization tends to stall "after the diagnosis"

By conducting a business inventory analysis,

・Which processes can use AI ・What level of effect can be expected ・Which processes should be tackled first

become clear.

However, when trying to move on to actual implementation from there, many companies encounter new barriers.

For example,

・No in-house talent to develop AI agents or systems ・Staff are swamped with daily work and have no time for AI implementation ・AI tools were introduced, but there is no support for adoption in the field ・Even when outsourcing to external development companies, understanding the business takes time ・Prototypes get built, but never reach production operation ・No post-adoption improvements, and the tools stop being used within a few months

These are the kinds of challenges companies face.

To bridge this gap of

"We know what to do. But we can't execute"

Zept officially offers FDE-style hands-on support as a formal service.

FDE: An engineer who embeds in the field

FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer.

One of the biggest causes of failure in AI adoption is jumping straight into large-scale system development from the start.

They embed themselves in the customer's business operations and

repeat the cycle of issue identification → design → PoC → implementation → improvement → adoption

to integrate AI into actual business processes.

1. Issue identification

Together with field staff, they review actual workflows and pain points.

2. Design

They design the approach best suited to the business, drawing on generative AI, AI agents, RAG, OCR, and more.

3. PoC

They first build a small prototype and verify whether it delivers results using actual business data.

4. Implementation

Based on the verification results, they refine it into a form that can actually be used in the field.

5. Improvement

They incorporate user feedback to improve accuracy and usability.

6. Adoption

They provide hands-on support until field employees can use it continuously.

Rather than acting as an external vendor,

supporting with the closeness of "a member of your own AI division"

is the defining feature of the External AI Division.

No large-scale development right away. Start small first

One of the biggest pitfalls in AI adoption is jumping straight into large-scale system development from the start.

The External AI Division starts with a small PoC.

Using actual business data, they verify

"Does it really save time?"

"Can employees actually use it?"

"Is the accuracy sufficient?"

"Is it worth the investment?"

before moving on to full-scale adoption.

This allows companies to verify effectiveness before making a major investment.

The External AI Division offers 3 plans starting at 600,000 yen per month

Three plans are available depending on the company's AI utilization status and the number of processes to be implemented.

Light | 600,000 yen per month

Hours: 40 hours/month

Main team: Consultant + Analyst + PM

For companies that want to start using AI with one department or one process first.

Focused on AI consultation, organizing business challenges, and AI utilization design, it clarifies the direction for implementation.

Designed for companies that say

"We don't know where to start" or "The inventory analysis showed us our priority processes, but we want to start small first."

{{62}} Standard | 1,200,000 yen per month

Standard | ¥1.2 million per month

Hours: 80 hours/month

Main team: Consultant + Engineer + Analyst + PM

For companies that want to advance multiple AI utilization initiatives in parallel.

It goes all the way to actual implementation, including building AI tools, organizing data, and conducting field interviews.

Designed for support involving the construction of one to two AI tools.

Premium | 2,000,000 yen per month

Hours: 160 hours/month

Main team: Consultant + Engineer + Analyst + PM

For companies that want to advance company-wide AI utilization, such as rolling out to multiple departments or building three or more AI tools.

Beyond implementing AI tools, it includes basic and advanced training for employees, advancing AI adoption in tandem with developing in-house AI talent.

Not just AI consultation, but implementation as well

The External AI Division primarily supports the following 8 areas.

・AI consultation and brainstorming ・Organizing business challenges ・AI utilization design ・Prompt creation ・AI tool construction ・Data organization and analysis ・Effect measurement and reporting ・Internal rollout support

Light focuses on consultation, challenge organization, and design.

With Standard and Premium, engineers join the team, increasing the proportion of implementation-focused support such as building AI tools and leveraging data.

A VIP plan for company-wide AX is also available

For companies pursuing AI utilization across multiple departments or aiming for company-wide AX (AI Transformation), a custom-designed VIP plan is also available.

The intended scope includes:

・Assignment of a dedicated AI division head-level manager ・Company-wide AI strategy development ・Governance building ・Security and compliance support ・RAG construction using proprietary data ・Integration with core systems ・Company-wide AI literacy training

and more.

*Pricing is provided via individual quotes.

Progressing from "prototype → improvement → production deployment" in 3 months

For example, through a business inventory,

"Internal FAQ handling"

"Drafting quotation documents"

"Invoice data entry"

assume a company where these three tasks were identified as priority targets for AI utilization.

Month 1

Conduct on-site interviews.

While reviewing actual workflows and data storage conditions, begin building a RAG for internal FAQ handling.

First, we conduct a PoC.

Month 2

Test the internal FAQ PoC in the field and improve it based on feedback.

At the same time, proceed with the design and prototyping of a generative AI tool to support quotation creation.

Month 3

Move the internal FAQ to production operation.

While monitoring usage, deploy the quotation support tool to the field and conduct operational training.

The remaining invoice data entry is organized as the next implementation target.

In this way, starting with the highest-priority tasks, we

prototype → verify → improve → deploy to production

in a short period of time.

*The above is a hypothetical example illustrating the service usage flow.

Why Zept provides "diagnosis to implementation" end-to-end

With typical AI adoption,

consulting firms, training companies, AI tool vendors, system development companies

and so on, there are cases where requests are made individually to multiple companies.

However, when outsourcing each part to separate companies,

・Explanations must be given to each company every time ・Business information becomes fragmented ・Areas of responsibility become ambiguous ・No follow-up support until post-development adoption

such problems arise.

At Zept,

business inventory → AI utilization design → AI training → AI tool development → field implementation → adoption

we provide end-to-end support through all of these stages.

By directly handing over the information obtained from the inventory to the implementation team, we reduce the time needed to understand the field and increase implementation accuracy.

Also, if new issues are discovered during implementation, the same team can continue to address them.

From "introducing AI" to "transforming the company"

What Zept aims for is not merely introducing AI tools.

Know AI.

Use AI.

Work changes.

The company changes.

The business changes.

We support this step-by-step transformation as AX (AI Transformation).

Going forward, we are considering building a service framework that enables more companies to adopt AI in their actual operations, such as organizing FDE teams with industry-specific expertise and establishing mechanisms for multiple small and medium-sized enterprises to jointly receive AI division support.
