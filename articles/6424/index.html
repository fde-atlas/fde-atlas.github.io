About Forward Deployed

Forward Deployed · 2026-01-08 · 1 min read

Summary

Forward Deployed is an applied AI consulting practice led by Basil Chatha, a former Intuit Credit Karma product manager. It helps private-equity portfolios and operating teams move AI from pilot to production, building production agents and internal tools while documenting the process through podcasts, workshops, and field notes. The firm argues most AI projects fail at the last mile—data access, workflow design, and adoption—and that forward deployed engineering keeps technical judgment close to the operating context where those constraints surface.

Forward Deployed helps PE portfolios and operating teams move AI from pilot to production, and documents what that takes alongside the builders and operators doing the work.

What we do

The consulting work turns clear business workflows into production agents and internal tools, along with the automation around them. Engineering and evaluation stay connected to rollout and operator training in one delivery model. Most AI projects break at the last mile, on data access and workflow design, or on adoption and ownership. Forward-deployed engineering keeps technical judgment close to the operating context where those constraints appear.

What we publish and convene

The podcast and fireside chats sit alongside workshops and events, plus written field notes and the Learn AI Engineering hub. All of it studies how builders and operators, with the founders and engineering leaders around them, make AI work inside real organizations. That practitioner access keeps the delivery thesis grounded in current operating reality.

Who leads it

Forward Deployed is led by Basil Chatha. His full background is below.
