Palantir Training & Cohorts | Foundry, Ontology, AIP

PAL4C LABS · 2026-08-01 · 3 min read

Summary

PAL4C Labs, an independent training company, is launching practitioner-led, in-person cohorts covering the Palantir ecosystem: Foundry engineering, Ontology engineering, AIP engineering, Forward Deployed Engineering, and engineering foundations (Python, PySpark, SQL). Classes will run in the Dallas, Texas area, onsite at enterprise locations across the U.S., or as dedicated corporate programs. Each track combines instructor-led sessions, hands-on projects, and practical assessments. First cohort dates, durations, and pricing are not yet announced; interested applicants must register first. The company is not affiliated with Palantir, offers no official certifications, and makes no job placement guarantees.

Key points

  • Five tracks cover Foundry, Ontology, AIP, FDE skills, and engineering foundations.

Training & Cohort Questions

When do cohorts start?

Our first cohorts are being scheduled now. Register your interest and we’ll contact you with dates, durations, and pricing before enrollment opens.

Where are in-person cohorts held?

In-person classroom cohorts are held in the Dallas, Texas area. Onsite corporate cohorts can be delivered at enterprise client locations across the U.S., subject to agreement.

Are these official Palantir certifications?

No. PAL4C Labs is an independent company and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Palantir Technologies Inc. Our programs focus on practical engineering skills and do not award official Palantir certifications.

Who are the programs for?

Engineers and technical professionals who want to build Palantir-focused skills, and enterprises that want to upskill their teams. Each track lists its intended audience and recommended prerequisites.

Can you run a cohort just for our company?

Yes. Dedicated enterprise cohorts are designed around your technical requirements and can be delivered onsite at your locations. Plan an enterprise cohort.

Does completing a track guarantee a job?

No. Our programs are designed to build job-relevant skills and readiness, but we do not promise job placement or employment.
