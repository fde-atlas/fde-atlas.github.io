FDE Field Report Oct 2026: India Hiring, the Real Job, the Hype

NeuraPath Academy · 2026-10-04 · 6 min read

Summary

This October 2026 field report examines India's FDE job market. Major IT services firms have announced large hiring plans — TCS 8,900 this year, Infosys 6,000, Wipro 10,000 within 18 months — yet staffing firm Quess Corp estimates only about 1,300–1,500 narrowly defined FDEs are currently available to hire. The article debunks the widely cited "800% demand growth" figure as small-base TeamLease data, describes the actual job as roughly 25% coding, 50% integration and 25% customer work, notes AI companies like Cursor, OpenAI and Anthropic require 3–6+ years of experience, and warns that Gartner expects ~70% of vendor-led AI engineering efforts to be abandoned by 2028.

Key points

  • India's IT giants plan thousands of FDE hires; supply is far smaller.
  • The '800% growth' stat is real but from a tiny base.
  • The job is mostly integration and customer work, not pure coding.
  • AI-lab postings want 3–6+ years; freshers enter via services firms.
  • Gartner warns many firms use 'forward deployed' as a rebranding label.

In short: India's big IT services firms have announced plans for thousands of Forward Deployed Engineers — TCS 8,900 this year, Infosys 6,000 over the next few years, Wipro 10,000 within 18 months — while a staffing firm estimates only about 1,300–1,500 narrowly defined FDEs are available to hire. The job is mostly integration and customer work, not pure coding; the big AI-company postings want 3+ years; and the fresher door is the services firms' FDE tracks.

Every month we read the FDE job postings, earnings-call quotes and trade press so you don't have to. This is the October edition. Every number links to where it came from, and where a number comes from a staffing firm or a course vendor, we say so.

1. The gap: thousands of seats, a few thousand people

The Economic Times reported on 8 September that TCS wants to hire 8,900 FDEs this year, Infosys up to 6,000, and Wipro 10,000 within 18 months — and that HCLTech plans for 15–20% of its fresher hiring to be FDE roles (source). Quess Corp, a staffing firm, told the paper only "around 1,300-1,500 'narrowly defined' FDEs are available for hire currently."

We are building a team of frontier engineers (FDEs) to support the client work. Our plan is to have 6,000 frontier engineers over the next few years. — Salil Parekh, CEO, Infosys (Moneycontrol, 23 Jul 2026)

Two more data points from the ground, both from ETHRWorld on 30 September (source): CIEL HR, another staffing firm, says FDE hiring grew 130% over the past year with 52 organisations recruiting as of July, and that 3–5 years' experience is the sweet spot; Tredence has about 60 FDEs and expects about 200 within a year.

2. The "800%" number, read honestly

You will see "FDE demand up 800%" everywhere, including on our own pages. It comes from TeamLease Digital, covers the first three quarters of 2025, and TeamLease itself says "this is on a small base." The same data counted 250 to 270 open FDE positions in India at any given time (source). The "729%" headline that followed is US Indeed data, not India.

So the honest reading is: the role is growing very fast from a small number, and the big announced plans are what make 2026 different — not the percentage.

3. What the job actually is

What share of a Forward Deployed Engineer's week is writing code?

Gergely Orosz of The Pragmatic Engineer translated a Google Cloud FDE ad "into plain English" as: "You are a contractor who codes at a customer's office" — roughly 25% coding, 50% integration, 25% customer (source). It is a joke with a point. Here is what the postings themselves say a week looks like:

Find the real problem before writing a line

You sit with the customer's team and turn a vague 'we want AI' into a scoped problem with a success measure.

“Own discovery, technical scoping, system design, build, and production rollout.”

5. Who is hiring FDEs in India right now

  • Cursor posted "Forward Deployed Engineer - India" on 28 September, based in Bengaluru, New Delhi and Mumbai: "This is not a demo role. You are responsible for systems that work in the real world." (posting)
  • Databricks lists FDE roles in Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Pune and remote India, including a Senior FDE where "FDEs are billable" (posting).
  • IT services firms — TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech — are building FDE benches at scale, and this is where fresher hiring into the title is happening.
  • Services and analytics firms such as Tredence, Coforge (20 FDEs, planning 100) and EPAM (about 50 in India, planning 100), per the ETHRWorld and Rediff reports above.

6. The case against the hype

We'd rather you hear this from us. Gartner predicts "roughly 70% of vendor-led AI engineering excursions will be abandoned by 2028." DXC announced plans in June to train tens of thousands of Claude-certified FDEs; on 31 July it had 86. And a Gartner analyst warns that "many providers now use 'forward deployed' as a label for implementation, professional services, solution engineering" — some carefully, "others because it sounds more strategic" (source).

What survives a correction is the skill underneath the title: making AI work inside a real business's systems, and being trusted by the people who use it. That is worth learning whatever the job ad calls it.

Frequently asked questions

How many Forward Deployed Engineers are Indian IT companies hiring?

As reported in September 2026, TCS plans to hire 8,900 FDEs this year, Infosys 6,000 over the next few years and Wipro 10,000 within 18 months, while HCLTech plans 15–20% of its fresher hiring as FDE roles. These are company plans with different time windows, not one combined number.

Can a fresher become a Forward Deployed Engineer?

Yes, but usually not at an AI lab first: the Cursor, OpenAI, Anthropic and Databricks postings we read ask for 3 to 6+ years. The fresher entry point is the FDE tracks at IT services firms. You need software foundations first, then production AI and client skills.

Is FDE mostly a coding job?

No. Coding matters, but most of the work is integrating AI into a customer's existing systems and working with their team. One widely shared reading puts it at roughly a quarter coding, half integration and a quarter customer work.

Is the 800% FDE growth figure real?

It is TeamLease Digital data for the first three quarters of 2025, and TeamLease says it is on a small base: about 250–270 open FDE positions in India at any one time. The large 2026 hiring plans announced by IT services firms are the bigger change.
