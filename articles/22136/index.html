What is FDE

Connecting technology and business, bridging the last mile of AI adoption

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) originates from Palantir's Echo / Delta two-person team model—one Echo who understands the business is deployed to the customer's site, while one Delta who understands the technology quickly embeds AI capabilities into real workflows.

In the era of large language models, with the widespread adoption of AI Coding capabilities, FDEs can both identify business pain points and use Prompt, RAG, Skill, MCP, workflows, and Vibe Coding to get solutions running—making them highly sought-after hybrid talent in today's enterprise AI transformation.

Tencent, Feishu, and Alibaba Cloud have successively launched their own FDE training programs. The QwenWork × DingTalk FDE certification course covers the full chain of "cognition building → requirement identification → solution design → agent development → workflow orchestration → product delivery", using project-based hands-on practice to turn every step into a capability you can take away.
