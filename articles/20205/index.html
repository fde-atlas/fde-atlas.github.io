Forward Deployed Engineering: the new customer success model

Forward Deployed Engineering is the model in which engineers go to the client to implement solutions directly in the operation — replacing the slow cycle of discovery, proposal, and project with direct results delivered in weeks.

Why the traditional CS model is not enough

Traditional Customer Success focuses on adoption and retention. But when the problem is operational — processes that don't work, automations stuck on paper, AI that wasn't configured properly — recommendations don't solve it.

Forward Deployed Engineering came to fill that gap.

What changes with the FDE model in customer success

  • Success is measured by operational results, not NPS or feature adoption
  • The FDE builds alongside the client, not for them
  • Knowledge transfer is part of the scope — the client finishes the project with autonomy
  • Time to value drops dramatically: weeks vs. quarters

Where the FDE model generates the most impact

  • Implementations of operational platforms with multiple processes
  • AI automations that need business-specific customization
  • Companies without an IT team that need fast results
  • Complex operations with multiple systems and integrations

  • SOC 2 certification and enterprise security for critical operations

FAQ

Are FDE and CS different roles? Yes. CS manages the relationship; FDE delivers technical results in the operation. Jestor has partners with both profiles.

Are tech companies adopting FDE? Yes — especially those that sell complex platforms that need customization per client.

Does the FDE model scale? With the right platform, yes. Jestor allows an FDE to configure multiple processes in parallel.
