Recruiting "Forward Deployed Engineers" to take on Anthropic! OpenAI to grow headcount to 8,000 by end of year

Original title: 招募「前線部署工程師」抗 Anthropic！OpenAI 年底前增加員工數至 8,000 人

TechNews 科技新報 · 2026-03-22 · 1 min read

Summary

OpenAI plans to double its headcount from 4,500 to 8,000 by the end of 2026 to compete with Anthropic and Google, recruiting across product, engineering, research, and sales. A key focus is hiring forward deployed engineers who embed with enterprise clients to build custom AI models and drive revenue. Applications chief Fidji Simo is steering staff toward improving Codex and merging it with ChatGPT into one desktop app, while OpenAI negotiates a joint venture with private equity firms. Both OpenAI and Anthropic remain unprofitable but are expected to go public this year, raising pressure to grow revenue and cut costs.

Full text

To counter competition from Anthropic PBC and Alphabet's Google, OpenAI plans to grow its headcount to 8,000 by the end of 2026, mainly recruiting in product development, engineering, research, and sales, and is hiring "Forward Deployed Engineers," hoping to station specialists directly inside enterprises to help enterprise customers build customized AI models and generate more revenue.

According to people familiar with the matter, OpenAI plans to double its headcount by the end of 2026, growing from 4,500 to 8,000 employees, mainly recruiting in product development, engineering, research, and sales. It has also expanded its office space in San Francisco to accommodate its growing workforce, increasing its office area to more than 1 million square feet.

OpenAI's latest funding round valued the company at $840 billion, thanks to roughly $110 billion in massive funding from tech giants and Masayoshi Son's SoftBank Group. The company will therefore adjust its team direction and speed up development to fend off competition from Google's Gemini project as well as Anthropic's momentum with enterprise customers.

OpenAI is accelerating the development of its enterprise-facing business and narrowing the gap with rival Anthropic. Fidji Simo, who heads OpenAI's applications business, has urged employees to set aside "side quests" and focus on improving the capabilities of its code model Codex to win enterprise customers and transform ChatGPT into a productivity tool.

Simo is leading the effort to bundle Codex and ChatGPT into a single desktop application, while OpenAI is in talks with private equity firms to form a joint venture aimed at getting its products into companies backed by private equity investors. Even though both OpenAI and Anthropic are currently operating at a loss—since their spending on training AI models far exceeds their revenue—they continue to push forward with technical progress.

As both OpenAI and Anthropic are expected to go public this year, market expectations for them to cut costs and boost revenue are rising, prompting both AI companies to actively recruit "Forward Deployed Engineers," hoping to station specialists directly inside enterprises to help enterprise customers build customized AI models and generate more revenue.
