Summary

This is a job posting from Acrosstudio, a Tokyo-based firm billing itself as the industry's first generative AI implementation consulting firm. It is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) at ¥7–12 million annual salary to pair with consultants and implement generative AI and AI Agent solutions for enterprise clients in entertainment, finance, telecom, and manufacturing. The role covers the full cycle from rapid PoC prototypes (demos in days to a week) to full-stack production delivery and integration of AI Agents into legacy systems, using a proprietary "AI Agent First BPR" approach. Requirements include 3+ years of development experience, production-grade Python or TypeScript, LLM/RAG/AI Agent application experience, and client-facing skills; familiarity with tools like Claude Code, Cursor, and frameworks like LangGraph is preferred. The ~50-person firm reports annual revenue exceeding ¥1 billion in its fifth year, average pay above ¥10 million, remote work allowed, and standard hours of 10:00–19:00 with 30 hours of included overtime.

Key points

  • Acrosstudio is hiring an FDE in Tokyo at ¥7–12M annual salary, remote work allowed.

Our identity as a venture consulting firm and future outlook ① The origin behind our name "Acrosstudio" Our company name combines Across (crossing borders, going beyond) and studio (a place of creation). It embodies our desire "to be a presence that achieves client growth without being bound by conventional thinking" and our wish "to create an environment where members can autonomously take on challenges such as skill development and new ventures." In fact, the word "studio" also comes from a personal experience of mine. During my student days, when my friends and I crossed America in a camper van, at the suggestion of a hip-hop-loving friend, we used the van as an "impromptu recording studio" and tried making music. In hip-hop culture, it's common to name your studio, and we called our space "Acrosstudio," meaning "creating while crossing (across)." That experience stayed with me strongly, and I later adopted it as the company name. ② What kind of organization we want to be When I talk with people working at major consulting firms, I often hear that while they have aspirations like "I want to hone my skills" or "I'm interested in starting a business," they can't take action due to daily client work or financial circumstances. I myself wanted to change that sense of frustration. That's why at our company, we aim to be a firm where consultants can work on client engagements while also pursuing diverse careers, such as preparing for independence, launching new businesses, or developing products. Over the past year alone, our business has expanded rapidly, including generative AI business and product development and services for freelancers, in addition to IT consulting. The options available to members who join us are steadily widening. ③ A message to job seekers Our company has now grown to about 30 people and is at a stage where we are poised for a major leap forward as a consulting × AI startup. At the same time, our systems and organizational culture are still developing, and the unique appeal of Acrosstudio today is that you can genuinely feel "we are building this company with our own hands." If you want to grow alongside ambitious colleagues and forge a new career, or if you want work that excites you, we'd love to meet you at a casual interview.

  • What drove your move from a major consulting firm to become the venture's first employee? Q. What aspects of the company's philosophy resonated with you? A. The CEO's vision of building an organization that welcomes individuals eventually having their own businesses or going independent, and that encourages synergy within the company. I hadn't known any other venture firm with that stance, and I felt it would be a very attractive organization for someone like me who wants to run my own business someday. Many of my university friends started companies, and those people tend to be interesting. The chance to build friendships in an organization full of such energetic people was appealing, and I've always had a strong inclination to grow by being challenged among people who have what I lack, or who are highly capable and deliver results, so I thought it was the perfect place and joined. Q. What kind of organization do you think this will become? A. Right now it's a company with many events and an open culture, with active interaction among employees. As the headcount grows and people with different backgrounds, philosophies, and generations join, the atmosphere will keep changing, and honestly, where it goes is unknown. In consulting work, some people join the same projects while others have little overlap, so I hope we continue to value encounters and opportunities to connect, and maintain a culture where information sharing and exchange are easy. Q. Who would you recommend this company to? A. People who want to accelerate their growth as consultants. Compared to learning at a firm, more is demanded of your own attitude toward learning, but I believe there's no better opportunity for growth. Also, many members have the drive to build their own careers, and you can be inspired by them and push each other to improve. We look forward to the day when "you," reading this article, inspire us too.
