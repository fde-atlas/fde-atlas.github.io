Tencent Cloud Launches the Industry's First FDE Certification and Starts Recruiting FDE Partners

Original title: 腾讯云推出行业首个FDE工程师认证，同时启动FDE合作伙伴招募

Summary

On September 10, Tencent Cloud launched what it calls the industry's first FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) certification, built on its ADP agent development platform, and opened recruitment for FDE partners. Unlike traditional product-knowledge certifications, it tests three areas: understanding customer scenarios and designing suitable agent solutions; full-cycle delivery skills including data prep, evals, troubleshooting, iteration, training and operations across industries like finance, government, manufacturing, retail, healthcare and education; and security, compliance and responsible AI. Tencent Cloud will also run blended online-offline training for partners and offer benefits such as joint solution promotion and ecosystem support.

On September 10, Tencent Cloud officially launched the Tencent Cloud ADP Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) certification program, and simultaneously began recruiting FDE partners.

Image

(Certificate sample)

As the AI agent industry accelerates, customers' demands for on-site work such as solution design, Demo building, PoC delivery, and post-launch operations keep rising. Partners urgently need a team of professional engineers who can both understand business scenarios and independently complete delivery.

Built on Tencent Cloud's AI agent development platform ADP, this certification is mainly aimed at professional engineers of Tencent Cloud partners as well as individual developers. Unlike traditional product-knowledge certifications, the FDE certification takes a more systematic and comprehensive view of enterprise-grade AI agent deployment, setting several capability requirements for engineers:

First, scenario understanding. Engineers need to understand product positioning and capability boundaries, judge the suitability of AI agent solutions based on customers' real business problems, design sensible combinations of capabilities such as knowledge bases, workflows, and agents, and plan a verifiable implementation path. The certification takes "scenario understanding and solution design" as a core focus, ensuring engineers can not only build applications but also design the right solutions.

Second, delivery and implementation. The certification covers the full project lifecycle, including data preparation, evals, troubleshooting, version iteration, customer training, and launch operations, and examines typical pain points and delivery boundaries in key industries such as finance, government, manufacturing, retail, healthcare, and education, ensuring engineers have the comprehensive ability to independently support the deployment of AI agent projects.

Third, security and compliance. The certification includes a dedicated section on security, compliance, and responsible AI, strengthening engineers' risk awareness and boundary awareness around data permissions, privacy protection, content safety, model hallucinations, unauthorized access, and compliance boundaries, so that AI agent projects can be "deployed and sustained."

In addition to the professional certification, Tencent Cloud will organize FDE-related professional enablement training for partners, combining online and offline formats and centered on real customer scenarios and hands-on projects, helping partner teams continuously improve the delivery efficiency and success rate of enterprise-grade AI agent projects. Furthermore, Tencent Cloud will offer FDE partners benefits such as joint solution promotion and ecosystem event enablement.

Registration for the FDE engineer certification and partner certification is now officially open. Interested partners are welcome to sign up.
