Summary

This article is an introductory explainer on the forward deployed engineer (FDE) role: software engineers who work directly with customers, ship production code, and make products deliver real outcomes within customer systems and workflows, while feeding recurring problems back to product teams. It breaks the work into six phases (discover, frame, build, deploy, measure, scale), traces the role's origins at Palantir, and cites hiring data from a November 13, 2026 snapshot: 587 companies with 1,783 open FDE-type postings, led by Databricks (118), Palantir (94), and OpenAI (40). It also distinguishes FDEs from Special Projects Engineers, who own unclear internal problems rather than a single customer deployment.

Updated

A forward deployed engineer (FDE) is a software engineer who works directly with a customer to make a product deliver a real outcome inside that customer’s systems, data, and workflows. FDEs write and ship production code, and they bring problems that repeat across customers back to the product team. Palantir popularized the role; many AI and data companies now hire for it.

What FDEs do, phase by phase

  • Discover. Observe the customer’s workflow and earn enough context to challenge the initial request.
  • Frame. Negotiate scope with the customer and align the pilot with the product’s actual capabilities.
  • Build. Make the integration work within the customer’s real permissions and operating constraints.
  • Deploy. Train customer operators and make the handoff part of delivery.
  • Measure. Review customer outcomes and use the evidence to decide whether to expand.
  • Scale. Bring repeated field problems to product and reduce the next deployment’s effort.

Where the role came from

Palantir embedded engineers at customer sites to make its platforms work on real problems and to find what the product was missing. Former Palantir FDE Nabeel Qureshi describes the work in Reflections on Palantir , and Bob McGrew explains why AI startups adopted the model in the FDE playbook episode .

Who hires FDEs now

In the November 13, 2026 snapshot, 587 companies had 1783 open FDE-type postings. The most were at Databricks (118), Palantir (94), Wonderful (61), OpenAI (40), Mistral AI (36), HappyRobot (21). See every open role.

How it differs from a Special Projects Engineer

An FDE owns one customer’s deployment; an SPE owns an unclear problem inside the company. Compare the two roles.
