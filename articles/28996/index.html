EXL acquires data and AI consulting market leader Aimpoint Digital to expand forward-deployed AI capabilities and accelerate enterprise transformation

The Manila Times · 2026-11-05 · 5 min read

Summary

EXL, a Nasdaq-listed data and AI company, has acquired Aimpoint Digital, a data and AI consulting and platform engineering firm, for an undisclosed sum. The deal adds roughly 250 AI engineers, platform architects, and strategists, strengthening EXL's forward-deployed AI capabilities and its expertise on Anthropic, Databricks, and Snowflake. EXL aims to offer end-to-end AI transformation, from strategy through deployment and managed operations, addressing the common gap between AI pilots and production-scale value. It follows EXL's earlier acquisition of iMerit.

  • Advances EXL’s strategy to deliver end-to-end enterprise AI transformation, connecting AI development, engineering, deployment, evaluation, and managed operations

NEW YORK, Nov. 05, 2026

(GLOBE NEWSWIRE)--

EXL[NASDAQ: EXLS], a global data and AI company, today announced it has acquired Aimpoint Digital, a data and AI consulting and platform engineering firm known for building enterprise data and AI platforms with deep expertise in market-leading partners including Anthropic, Databricks, and Snowflake. The acquisition strengthens EXL’s ability to support clients across the full AI transformation lifecycle and deepens EXL’s existing relationships with key technology partners. Financial terms of the transaction were not disclosed.

Enterprises are investing heavily in AI, yet many organizations continue to struggle to move AI initiatives from pilot to production-scale value. Aimpoint Digital has built a reputation for moving client workloads onto leading data and AI platforms with speed. By bringing Aimpoint Digital into EXL, the two companies will scale that strength across more workflows and functions, extending AI’s business impact across the enterprise and broadening EXL’s offering for enterprise-wide transformation.

"Clients require a partner who can take them from AI ambition to impact at enterprise scale,” said Rohit Kapoor, chairman and chief executive officer, EXL. "Aimpoint Digital’s forward-deployed strategists and engineers bring the hands-on expertise to turn AI strategy into production reality on the platforms our clients trust most. By bringing their advisory and engineering talent together with EXL’s data foundations, industry expertise, agentic AI solutions, and operational scale, we can reimagine workflows and give clients a single, accountable partner for the entire journey - from strategy and architecture through deployment and managed AI operations.”

"Joining EXL lets us do more breakthrough work on a much larger scale,” said Ben Yopp, chief executive officer and cofounder, Aimpoint Digital. "We bring deep AI strategy and engineering talent and EXL brings industry expertise, global reach and the ability to run AI in operations. Together, we can take clients’ most important AI initiatives into production and keep them performing at the highest levels.”

EXL and Aimpoint Digital will provide clients with greater continuity from the first strategy conversation through architecture, engineering, production deployment, and managed operations. This connected approach is designed to reduce handoffs, establish clearer accountability, and accelerate the path to measurable outcomes.

In a marketplace where demand for strategic data and AI engagements outpaces available talent, the combination adds approximately 250 experienced AI engineers, platform architects, analytics consultants, decision scientists, and AI strategists to EXL's data and AI capabilities. This expands EXL’s forward-deployed talent base, puts senior AI expertise closer to client teams, and adds capacity for complex, multi-year transformations.

Aimpoint Digital also brings deep expertise working with strategic partners, expanding EXL's partner ecosystem footprint and deepening expertise on partners such as Anthropic, Databricks, and Snowflake. This will help clients reimagine core workflows while building on the platforms they trust and helping them get to production sooner.

The acquisition builds on EXL’s recent acquisition of iMerit, a global leader in AI model training, evaluation and reinforcement learning and reinforcement learning. Together, iMerit and Aimpoint Digital extend EXL’s ability to support clients from strategy and architecture through engineering, deployment, and managed AI operations - reflecting EXL’s disciplined strategy of extending its data and AI capabilities through investments in innovation, acquisitions, and talent.

About EXL

EXL (NASDAQ: EXLS) is a global data and AI company that offers services and solutions to reinvent client business models, drive better outcomes and unlock growth with speed. EXL propels enterprises to go beyond AI ambition to impact by combining the power of data, AI, and deep industry context with trusted execution. Our clients include the world's leading corporations across insurance, healthcare, banking and capital markets, retail, communications and media, and energy and infrastructure, among others. EXL was founded in 1999 with the core values of innovation, collaboration, excellence, integrity and respect. We are headquartered in New York and have over 74,000 employees spanning six continents. For more information, visit https://www.exlservice.com.

About Aimpoint Digital

Aimpoint Digital is an AI and data consulting firm that turns AI ambition into production reality, built on a scalable foundation of data and analytics. The company architects, builds, and operationalizes enterprise AI and modern data solutions, helping organizations transform fragmented environments into scalable platforms, intelligent applications, and measurable business outcomes.
