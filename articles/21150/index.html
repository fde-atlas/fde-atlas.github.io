Category: AI Search

18 terms

AEO

Answer Engine Optimization

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is an optimization method targeting "answer engines" such as Google Featured Snippets and AI search. A concept that predates LLMO/GEO, it is now discussed in an integrated manner within the context of AIO/LLMO.

AI Overview

Google AI Overview (formerly Search Generative Experience)

AI Overview is a feature that displays AI-generated answers at the top of Google search results. It launched in the US in May 2024 and expanded to six countries, including Japan, in August of the same year. It causes significant drops in click-through rates and is attracting attention as a force changing the value structure of traditional SEO.

AI Search Volume

AI Search Volume

AI Search Volume is a metric estimating how many times per month a specific keyword is queried on AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. Serving as the AI search equivalent of Google Search Volume, it provides the foundational data for prioritizing keywords in an LLMO strategy.

AIO

AI Optimization

AIO (AI Optimization) is an umbrella term for comprehensive methods of optimizing your content for AI search, AI assistants, and AI recommendation engines. A broader concept that includes LLMO/GEO.

Brand Mention Rate

Brand Mention Rate

Brand Mention Rate is a ratio metric calculated by dividing the number of keywords in which your brand name appears in LLM response text by the total number of measured keywords. It is a foundational KPI for quantifying brand awareness in the AI search era, and a single-axis metric that underpins Share of Voice (AI) and Visibility Score.

ChatGPT Search

ChatGPT Search (SearchGPT)

ChatGPT Search is a generative AI-powered search engine integrated into ChatGPT by OpenAI. It answers user questions in natural language while citing web information in real time. Officially released by OpenAI on October 31, 2024, it is growing rapidly as a force changing traditional Google search behavior.

Citation

Citation (AI Citation)

Citation refers to an AI search engine explicitly showing a site URL or company name as a referenced source when generating an answer. It is the most important KPI in the LLMO/GEO era, as whether you are cited determines brand awareness and traffic.

Copilot Search

Microsoft Copilot (formerly Bing Chat)

A clear explanation of what Copilot Search (Microsoft Copilot, formerly Bing Chat) is. This entry organizes the term's meaning and use cases, covering how it works, differences from other AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity), and concrete Copilot optimization measures such as Bing index registration.

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is an optimization method for getting your content cited and referenced by AI search engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and AI Overview. It is the generative AI version of traditional SEO, and the concept has been spreading rapidly in recent years.

Grounding

Grounding

Grounding is a technique for grounding LLM output in external, trusted information sources. It is an essential method in AI search and enterprise AI for preventing hallucinations and enabling fact-checking. RAG is also a form of Grounding.

Live LLM Query

Live LLM Query / Actual Query Measurement

Live LLM Query is a measurement method that sends actual API requests to the LLMs being measured (ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini/Perplexity) and retrieves their responses. Distinguished from estimation models, simulations, and scraping, it is the most reliable measurement method for AI search KPIs.

LLM Citation Rate

LLM Citation Rate

LLM Citation Rate is a ratio metric calculated by dividing the number of keywords in which your domain appears in the citation sources of LLM responses by the total number of measured keywords. Implemented as the Citation metric in tools like Profound and Answer IO, it is a key LLMO KPI that serves as a prerequisite for gaining actual traffic from AI search.

LLMO

Large Language Model Optimization

LLMO refers to efforts to improve the state in which your company's information is discovered in generative AI answers, accurately described, and referenced as a source when needed. It is measured separately in terms of technical readiness, mentions and citations within answers, and the accuracy of brand information.

llms.txt

llms.txt (Proposed Specification for LLM Guidance Files)

llms.txt is a proposed specification that presents a site's overview and key URLs in Markdown. It is not a crawl-control standard like robots.txt, and Google has officially stated it is unnecessary for inclusion or ranking in Google Search and AI features. At present, it should be treated as an optional, experimental auxiliary file.

Perplexity

Perplexity AI

Perplexity is an AI search service that emerged as an "answer engine." It combines multiple LLMs with the latest web information to answer questions directly, with cited sources. It is especially popular among B2B users, engineers, and researchers, and is a key target for LLMO/GEO optimization.

SGE

Search Generative Experience

SGE (Search Generative Experience) is the former name of the generative AI search feature announced by Google in 2023. It was renamed "AI Overview" in 2024 and is now deployed as an official feature.

Share of Voice (AI)

Share of Voice for AI / AI Share of Voice

Share of Voice (AI) is a ratio metric that compares how often your company is mentioned in LLM response text against competitors. Calculated as your mentions ÷ total mentions × 100%, it is a core LLMO KPI that quantifies market presence in the AI search era.

Visibility Score

Visibility Score / AI Search Visibility Score

Visibility Score is an LLMO metric that scores your company's exposure in AI search results on a scale of 0 to 100. Calculation methods vary by tool: Answer IO combines three axes (Presence 35 / Position 35 / Citation 30), while Otterly implements it as Share of AI Voice.

CategorySEO

29 terms

canonical

canonical URL

Canonical is an HTML tag that indicates the representative URL for duplicate content. It tells search engines "this page is the official version" and prevents the dilution of SEO value.

CLS

Cumulative Layout Shift

CLS is a metric that measures how much a page's layout shifts unexpectedly. It is one of the Core Web Vitals, with 0.1 or below considered good. It can be improved by specifying sizes for images and ads.

Core Web Vitals

Core Web Vitals

Core Web Vitals are website user experience metrics defined by Google based on real-world measurements. They consist of three metrics: LCP (loading speed), INP (responsiveness), and CLS (stability), and significantly affect both SEO rankings and CVR.

CTR

Click Through Rate

CTR (Click Through Rate) is the ratio of clicks to impressions. In SEO, it serves as a combined indicator of ranking and the quality of titles and descriptions; even at the same ranking, a higher CTR can double traffic.

E-E-A-T

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness

E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) refers to the four criteria Google uses to evaluate content quality. Demonstrating experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness improves SEO rankings and citation rates in AI search. It is especially important in YMYL areas.

Featured Snippet

Featured Snippet

A Featured Snippet is a highlighted answer box displayed at the top of Google search results. Also called "position zero," it achieves a higher CTR than the regular first result and remains an important position in the AI Overview era as a cited source.

GA4

Google Analytics 4

GA4 is the current version of Google Analytics. With an event-based measurement model, it enables cross-platform analysis across web and apps. It is provided as the successor to Universal Analytics.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics (GA)

Google Analytics is a free access analysis tool provided by Google. The current version is GA4. It allows detailed tracking of user behavior, traffic channels, and CVR.

hreflang

hreflang attribute

hreflang is meta information that specifies the corresponding languages and countries for multilingual and multi-regional sites. It is essential for making search engines display the correct version.

INP

Interaction to Next Paint

INP is a metric that measures a page's responsiveness to user interactions. It replaced FID on March 12, 2024, becoming a new primary Core Web Vitals metric. 200ms or less is considered good.

LCP

Largest Contentful Paint

LCP is a key Core Web Vitals metric measuring the time until the page's largest content is displayed. 2.5 seconds or less is good, while over 4 seconds requires action. Image optimization and server response improvements are the main remedies.

noindex

noindex tag

noindex is a meta tag that instructs search engines not to index a page. It is used to exclude low-quality pages or members-only content. Be careful, as misconfiguration can cause a serious SEO incident.

PAA

People Also Ask

PAA is the "People Also Ask" box displayed in Google search results. It expands to show additional questions users may want to know, and clicking one displays an excerpt from a website.

robots.txt

robots.txt is a text file placed at a site's root that indicates which paths can and cannot be fetched by each crawler. In AI search, you need to distinguish between bots for search fetching, user fetching, and model training when setting your policy.

Search Console

Google Search Console (GSC)

Google Search Console is Google's official free SEO analysis tool. It lets you check search performance, indexing status, structured data errors, and more. It is essential for anyone doing SEO.

SERP

Search Engine Results Page

SERP (Search Engine Results Page) refers to a search engine's results page. While it used to consist of 10 blue links, the modern SERP is composed of diverse elements such as AI Overviews, Featured Snippets, Map Packs, images, and videos, making it the starting point of any SEO strategy.

YMYL

Your Money or Your Life

YMYL is an area where Google applies the E-E-A-T criteria with particular strictness. It refers to information categories such as healthcare, finance, legal, tax, and government services that can have a serious impact on people's lives and assets.

Impressions

Impressions

Impressions are the number of times a site is displayed in search results or social media ads. A metric separate from clicks, it is a basic KPI for measuring the breadth of awareness.

Organic Search

Organic Search

Organic search refers to natural, non-ad search results. It is the target of SEO/LLMO measures and becomes a long-term traffic asset. Unlike ads, its cost per conversion is low and stable.

Keyword Difficulty

Keyword Difficulty (KD)

Keyword Difficulty (KD) is an SEO metric that indicates how hard it is to rank in the top results on a scale of 0 to 100. This article explains how it is calculated and how to practically use KD figures to choose the keywords you target.

Content SEO

Content SEO

Content SEO is a method of achieving SEO rankings through the creation of high-quality content. It is the central strategy in the E-E-A-T era, and a mass-production system of 1,000 articles per month determines competitive advantage.

Sitemap

XML Sitemap

A sitemap (sitemap.xml) is an XML file that lists all URLs on a site along with their last-modified dates and priorities. It serves as a map for efficient indexing by Google Search and is also an important information source for AI crawlers.

Technical SEO

Technical SEO

Technical SEO is the optimization of a site's technical aspects for SEO. Implementing Core Web Vitals, crawlability, indexability, and structured data maximizes the power of your content.

Black Hat SEO

Black Hat SEO

Black hat SEO refers to SEO techniques that violate Google's guidelines. While they may produce short-term results, there is a high risk of being removed from search results when detected. It should be avoided at all costs.

White Hat SEO

White Hat SEO

White hat SEO refers to legitimate SEO techniques that comply with Google Search Essentials (formerly the Webmaster Guidelines). Results take longer in the short term, but it is sustainable and can be turned into long-term assets.

Long-Tail SEO

Long-Tail SEO

Long-tail SEO is an SEO strategy that targets high rankings for keywords with low search volume but high specificity and low competition. It offers opportunities even for small and medium-sized businesses and is characterized by high conversion rates.

Search Intent

Search Intent

Search intent refers to the "true purpose" behind a user's search query. It is classified into four types: informational, comparison/consideration, purchase-ready, and navigational. It is one of the most important concepts in SEO, and articles that miss the search intent will never rank highly.

Internal Links

Internal Links

Internal links are links that connect pages within the same site. They are a critical element directly tied to the distribution of SEO rankings, user engagement, and AI search's understanding of site structure. They are also the foundation of the topic cluster strategy.

Backlinks

Backlinks (External Links)

Backlinks are links placed on other sites that point to your own site. They are an important ranking factor in Google's algorithm, and natural backlinks from high-quality sites significantly boost search rankings. They remain one of the pillars of SEO today.

CategoryMEO

6 terms

GBP

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business)

GBP (Google Business Profile) is a free tool for managing a store's display on Google Maps and Search. As the foundation of MEO, this article explains, with practical steps, what to set up for registration, optimization, and review management.

Google Maps

Google Maps

Google Maps is a customer acquisition infrastructure for local businesses that integrates store search, route guidance, and reviews. This article clearly explains the mechanisms that drive store visits, including its role as the main battleground of MEO and its relationship with Google Business Profile, the Map Pack, and reviews.

Map Pack

Map Pack (Local Pack)

The Map Pack is a special section showing the top three stores displayed in Google Search for "location + industry" queries. It is said that about 42% of local searches result in a click within the Map Pack, greatly influencing customer acquisition for local businesses.

MEO

Map Engine Optimization

MEO (Map Engine Optimization) refers to measures for optimizing search rankings on Google Maps. By ranking in the top three (Map Pack) for searches containing location names such as "dentist near me," you can significantly increase foot traffic to your physical store. It is an essential strategy for brick-and-mortar businesses.

NAP Information

Name, Address, Phone

NAP (Name, Address, Phone) information refers to the store name, address, and phone number. Complete consistency across all platforms on the web directly affects MEO rankings.

Local SEO

Local SEO

Local SEO is a general term for measures aimed at ranking highly in location-based searches. It includes both MEO (rankings within Google Maps) and regular search SEO for "area + industry" keywords.

CategoryStructured Data

12 terms

Article schema

Article schema is Schema.org structured data for news articles, blog posts, and similar content. Explicitly specifying the author, publication date, update date, and headings improves SEO and the rate of AI citations.

BreadcrumbList

BreadcrumbList schema

BreadcrumbList is a schema that structures breadcrumb navigation using Schema.org. Breadcrumbs are displayed in search results, communicating the site hierarchy to users, search engines, and AI.

FAQPage

FAQPage schema

FAQPage is a schema for structuring FAQs with Schema.org. Although it was discontinued as a Google Search rich result on 2026-05-07, AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overview) continue to use FAQPage as a citation source, so it remains effective from an LLMO perspective.

HowTo schema

HowTo schema is a Schema.org schema for structuring step-by-step and how-to content. Steps are displayed expanded in search results, and it also improves citation rates in AI search.

JSON-LD

JSON for Linking Data

JSON-LD is a method for describing structured data in JSON format. It is the implementation format recommended by Google, and because it can be separated from the HTML body, it is easy to adopt.

LocalBusiness schema

LocalBusiness schema is a Schema.org schema for structuring store and business information. It can explicitly state address, business hours, phone, geographic coordinates, and business type, and is essential for MEO and local SEO.

Organization schema

Organization schema is a fundamental Schema.org schema for structuring company and organization information. It can explicitly state brand name, logo, location, and social media links, and serves as the foundation for Google Knowledge Panel display and AI citations.

Product schema

Product schema is a Schema.org schema for structuring e-commerce product information. By explicitly stating price, stock, brand, and reviews, rich result display becomes possible in Google Search.

Review schema

Review schema is a Schema.org schema for structuring reviews and ratings. Star rating display significantly improves CTR in search results.

Schema.org

Schema.org is a vocabulary standard for structured data jointly developed by major search engines. By implementing it on web pages in JSON-LD format, search engines and AI can accurately understand the content.

Rich Results

Rich Results

Rich results are formats displayed in an expanded way compared to regular listings in Google search results. FAQs, review stars, prices, and more are displayed visually, greatly improving CTR.

Structured Data

Structured Data

Structured data is a method for describing web page content in machine-readable form. It allows search engines and AI to accurately understand page content, leading to rich result display and improved AI citation rates.

Category: AI Technology

46 terms

Adaptive Thinking

Adaptive Thinking — Claude's dynamic reasoning depth adjustment

A feature that allows Claude models to dynamically adjust reasoning depth according to input complexity. Supported by Claude Opus 4.7 / Sonnet 4.6. Extended Thinking performs step-by-step reasoning, while Adaptive Thinking differs in that it balances cost and speed.

AI Agent

AI Agent

An AI agent is an AI system that autonomously plans, executes, and makes decisions when given a goal. Unlike chatbots, it can coordinate multiple tools to complete tasks.

AI Orchestration

AI Orchestration

A design and operational approach that coordinates multiple AI models, agents, external tools, and business workflows for integrated operation toward a single goal. It serves as the foundation for automating complex tasks that a single AI cannot handle.

Anthropic

Anthropic PBC

Anthropic is a US AI company that develops Claude. Founded in 2021 by former OpenAI siblings Amodei, it emphasizes safety through Constitutional AI. This article explains the pronunciation, major models, and the background of FDE hiring.

Batch API

Batch API — LLM asynchronous bulk processing API

An asynchronous processing API offered by Claude, OpenAI, and Gemini. It allows bulk processing of large tasks that don't require real-time response at a 50% discount. Half the standard price for monthly report generation, bulk translation, data analysis, and more.

Claude Agent SDK

Claude Agent SDK — Anthropic's multi-agent development kit

An agent development SDK released by Anthropic on September 29, 2025. It simplifies the design, parallel execution, and tool integration of multiple agents combined with Claude, accelerating the development of business automation systems.

Claude Citations

Claude Citations — Anthropic's official citation feature

A citation feature of the Claude API that shows which sentence in a response came from which source document. A core technology for RAG systems, research work, and hallucination reduction, accelerating AI adoption in research, consulting, and legal work.

Claude Code

Claude Code — Anthropic's official AI coding assistant

Anthropic's official AI coding assistant. With Claude Opus 4.7 as its core engine, it is a development support tool that consistently handles everything from code generation to review, refactoring, and documentation generation through terminal integration, MCP connectivity, sub-agents, and parallel execution.

Claude Managed Agents

Claude Managed Agents — Anthropic's managed agent operation service

A managed agent operations service released by Anthropic in April 2026. Anthropic handles session management, parallel execution, state retention, and billing for Claude agents (sessions at $0.08/h plus tokens), greatly reducing operational burden.

Claude on Bedrock (Claude on AWS Bedrock)

Claude on Amazon Bedrock — Claude via a managed AI service

Claude on Bedrock (Claude on AWS Bedrock) is a mechanism for using Claude through AWS's managed infrastructure. This article explains the criteria for enterprise adoption, including differences in features and pricing from the direct API, IAM authentication and data residency control, and use cases in legal, medical, and manufacturing industries.

Claude Skills

Claude Skills — AI tools specialized for specific tasks

A specialized task feature for Claude released by Anthropic in October 2025. It adds individual skills such as Excel manipulation, PDF processing, and data analysis to Claude, enabling them to serve as alternatives to expert tools.

Claude Vision

Claude Vision — Claude's image understanding capability

A feature that allows Claude models to receive images as input and understand and analyze their content. Supported by all of Opus 4.7 / Sonnet 4.6 / Haiku 4.5. A core technology for business automation, including document OCR, chart analysis, and UI screenshot analysis (the foundation of Computer Use).

Claude

Claude — The large language model family developed by Anthropic

A large language model (LLM) developed by Anthropic. Characterized by safety design based on Constitutional AI and a context window of up to 1 million tokens, it comes in four models — Claude Fable 5 / Opus 4.8 / Sonnet 5 / Haiku 4.5 — available via API, Web UI, and Claude Code.

Computer Use

Computer Use — Claude's screen operation and UI automation feature

A screen operation feature for Claude released by Anthropic in October 2024. Claude looks at the screen and operates the mouse and keyboard, using web and desktop applications just like a human. It is attracting attention as an alternative to RPA.

Constitutional AI

Constitutional AI

An AI training method announced by Anthropic in 2022 that improves safety by having the AI itself reference an explicit list of principles (a constitution) and repeatedly self-critique and refine its outputs. It forms the core of the safety design of all Claude models.

Customer Success Engineer (CSE)

Customer Success Engineer — A role that supports continued product use through technology

An engineer who technically supports customers' continued use of a company's product. Often confused with Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), but CSEs focus on adoption and expansion after product implementation, whereas FDEs differ in that they engage deeply with customer operations in the pre-implementation phase.

Data Residency

Data Residency — The geographic region where AI service data is stored

Data Residency refers to the geographic region where cloud AI stores and processes data. This article explains why it matters for compliance with Japan's APPI and GDPR, and how to choose between Claude API's inference_geo and domestic processing via AWS Bedrock's Tokyo region, with examples from legal and medical fields.

Dify

Dify is an open-source platform for building LLM applications with no-code to low-code. It enables RAG, prompt management, and AI agent development entirely through a GUI, and is widely adopted for internal knowledge Q&A and AI chatbot development.

Embedding

Embedding

Embedding is a technology that converts text and images into numeric vectors of hundreds to thousands of dimensions. It allows AI to handle "meaning" mathematically and serves as the foundation for RAG, recommendations, and classification.

Extended Thinking

Extended Thinking — Claude's step-by-step reasoning mode

A reasoning mode in which Claude models unfold their "thought process" step by step before answering, improving accuracy on complex tasks. Supported by Sonnet 4.6 and Haiku 4.5, it delivers accuracy gains in mathematics, legal interpretation, code debugging, and more.

Fast Mode

Fast Mode — Claude Opus 4.6's high-speed output mode

A high-speed output mode offered by Anthropic for Claude Opus 4.6. Output speed is significantly improved at 6x the standard mode pricing ($30/MTok input, $150/MTok output). It cannot be combined with the Batch API, but can be used with Prompt Caching.

Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)

Forward Deployed Engineer — An organizational model of engineers stationed at customer sites

An organizational model of engineers, established by Palantir Technologies in the 2010s, who are stationed at customer sites to adapt the company's products to business operations. In 2025-2026, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google also adopted it at scale, making it a core role in enterprise AI adoption.

Function Calling

Function Calling

Function Calling is a feature that allows LLMs to call external functions and APIs. It is the foundational technology that enables AI agents to operate real-world systems, and is also known as Tool Use.

Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

Human in the Loop

A design approach in which humans intervene in, supervise, and approve the AI's decision-making process. It is essential for mitigating generative AI hallucinations and clarifying accountability, and it also aligns with the "human-centered" principle of the AI Business Guidelines.

Knowledge Cutoff

Knowledge Cutoff — the training data collection deadline for LLMs

The deadline for information included in an LLM's training data. Claude Opus 4.7 has a cutoff of January 2026, Sonnet 4.6 of August 2025, and Haiku 4.5 of February 2025. Information after the cutoff must be supplemented via Web Search or RAG.

LangChain

LangChain is a Python framework for LLM application development. It standardizes prompt management, RAG, and agent building. While Dify is no-code oriented, LangChain provides a powerful code-based foundation for developers.

LLM

Large Language Model

An LLM is an AI model with billions to trillions of parameters. It can understand and generate natural language, with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini as representative examples. It is the core technology of the modern AI business.

Local-First AI

Local-First AI

Local-First AI is a design philosophy in which AI runs entirely within the corporate network or browser without sending data to external clouds. It is rapidly spreading in industries that prioritize confidentiality, such as healthcare, legal, and finance. Tufe Company's flagship technology.

MCP

Model Context Protocol

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a standardization protocol for AI and tools that Anthropic announced and open-sourced on 2024-11-25. It enables interoperability between multiple AIs, data sources, and tools, and is rapidly spreading as a foundation for enterprise AI integration.

n8n

n8n

n8n is a workflow automation platform that connects 400+ apps. As an open-source alternative to Zapier/Make, its adoption by enterprises is growing. It can be self-hosted, enabling business automation without sending sensitive data outside.

Palantir

Palantir Technologies — a data integration platform company and the birthplace of the FDE model

Palantir is a US data integration company founded in 2003 by Peter Thiel and others. Known for Gotham and Foundry, it has regained attention as the birthplace of the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) model. This article explains how FDE works and why AI companies are adopting it.

Prompt Caching

Prompt Caching

Prompt Caching is a feature that caches system prompts and long documents on the server side and reuses them to reduce token costs and latency. This article explains Claude's cache_control implementation examples, when to use 5-minute vs. 1-hour caches, and the conditions for a positive ROI, with concrete examples.

RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is a technique that has LLMs generate answers by referencing external documents. It allows AI to leverage internal knowledge and the latest information, and is effective for reducing hallucinations and protecting sensitive data.

RLAIF (Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback)

RLAIF — Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback

A reinforcement learning technique that uses the AI model itself as the evaluator. It goes beyond the limits of RLHF (human feedback) and forms the basis of Anthropic's Constitutional AI. It is highly scalable and can perform safety checks at large volume.

Solutions Engineering

Solutions Engineering — a role that addresses customer challenges with technical solutions

A technical role that understands customers' business challenges and designs and implements solutions by combining the company's products. Sales Engineers, Forward Deployed Engineers, and Customer Success Engineers are all derivatives of Solutions Engineering, and their role boundaries are shifting in the AI era.

Sub Agents (parallel execution)

Sub Agents — Claude's mechanism for calling and running sub-agents in parallel

Sub Agents are a mechanism in which the main Claude calls sub-agents with independent contexts and runs them in parallel. This article explains why complex tasks are decomposed, implementation examples with the Agent SDK/Claude Code, and common misunderstandings, with concrete examples.

Tool Use

Tool Use

Tool Use is the ability of an LLM to perform tasks using external tools, APIs, and databases. It is a Claude term that is nearly synonymous with OpenAI's Function Calling. This article explains the core mechanism of AI agents, representative tool implementations, and the relationship with MCP in an easy-to-understand way.

On-Premise AI

On-Premise AI

On-Premise AI is an approach in which AI models run on a company's own servers without sending data externally. This article explains in detail the differences from cloud AI in cost and regulatory compliance, the industries that should adopt it (healthcare, legal, finance), and its components such as GPU servers and LLMs.

Context Window

Context Window

The upper limit on the number of input and output tokens an LLM can reference in a single inference. Claude Opus 4.7 and Sonnet 4.6 support 1M (1 million) tokens, allowing documents equivalent to 100 pages of contracts to be processed in one go.

Hallucination

Hallucination

Hallucination is the phenomenon where an LLM confidently generates information that is not factual. It is one of the biggest risks in business AI use, and can be mitigated with RAG, Grounding, and fact-checking.

Fine-Tuning

Fine-Tuning

Fine-tuning is a technique that trains an existing LLM with additional data for specific tasks or business needs. It can achieve high accuracy but requires cost and time. For many use cases, RAG is the more rational choice.

Prompt Injection

Prompt Injection

Prompt injection is a type of cyberattack that embeds malicious instructions in user input or external data to induce unintended behavior in AI systems. It is essential risk vocabulary that companies deploying customer-facing AI or RAG in their operations must understand.

Prompt Engineering

Prompt Engineering

Prompt engineering is the technique of designing inputs to elicit desired outputs from an LLM. It systematically improves AI output quality through instruction wording, context provision, examples, and structuring. A core skill for business AI utilization.

Vector Search

Vector Search

Vector search is a technology that converts text and images into numerical vectors and retrieves results based on semantic similarity. It can find semantically similar information that keyword matching would miss, and is a core technology of RAG.

Multimodal AI

Multimodal AI

Multimodal AI is AI that can handle multiple media formats simultaneously, such as text, images, audio, and video. The latest LLMs including GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, and Gemini support it, expanding the scope of business applications.

Generative AI

Generative AI

Generative AI is a general term for AI capable of creating new content such as text, images, video, audio, and code. Representative technologies include LLMs and Diffusion models. Since the release of ChatGPT in November 2022, it has rapidly spread in business.

CategoryWeb Technologies

17 terms

App Router

Next.js App Router

App Router is the new routing system introduced in Next.js 13 and later. With the standardization of React Server Components, it enables building fast, SEO-friendly applications.

Cloudflare

Cloudflare is one of the world's largest CDN and security services. It provides free DDoS protection, a high-speed CDN, and edge processing via Workers.

CSR

Client-Side Rendering

CSR is a method where JavaScript generates HTML in the browser. It is mainstream for SPAs but disadvantaged in initial display and SEO. It is suited for authenticated content such as admin dashboards.

Hydration

Hydration

Hydration is the process of "applying" JavaScript on the client to server-generated static HTML to enable interactive functionality. It is an important stage executed after the initial display in SSR/SSG.

ISR

Incremental Static Regeneration

ISR is a hybrid of SSG and SSR. HTML is generated at build time and regenerated in the background at specified intervals. It is ideal for pages with moderate update frequency.

Next.js

Next.js is a React-based full-stack framework. It allows choosing SSG, SSR, and ISR on a per-page basis, and is the de facto standard for building fast websites. It is the primary technology in Tufe Company's production projects.

ONNX Runtime

ONNX Runtime is an AI inference engine led by Microsoft. It can run ONNX-format models cross-platform, from servers and mobile to browsers. It is a foundational technology for Local-First AI.

React Server Components

React Server Components (RSC)

React Server Components is a new official React concept. Components rendered on the server dramatically reduce JavaScript bundles while achieving an SEO-strong configuration.

Server Components

React Server Components

Server Components is a new specification that renders React components on the server. It reduces JavaScript bundle size and speeds up initial display. It is standard in the Next.js App Router.

Shopify

Shopify is the world's largest e-commerce platform. It integrates inventory, payments, and shipping, allowing EC sites to be launched in a short time. It is also the primary platform for EC projects at Tufe Company.

SSG

Static Site Generation

SSG is a method that pre-generates HTML at build time. It is fast, low-cost, secure, and SEO-strong, making it the optimal solution for static content sites. All of Tufe Company's blog, glossary, and industry pages use SSG.

SSR

Server-Side Rendering

SSR is a method that generates HTML on the server at request time. It can always display the latest data, but is slower and more costly than SSG. It is suited for user-specific dynamic pages.

Three.js

Three.js is a JavaScript library for displaying 3D graphics in web browsers based on WebGL. It is essential for building immersive websites and interactive landing pages.

Vercel

Vercel is a frontend-focused hosting service operated by the developers of Next.js. It integrates Git-based automatic deployment, CDN, and serverless functions. This article explains the key points of its pronunciation, usage, and pricing, including how it is actually used on this site.

WebAssembly

WebAssembly (Wasm)

WebAssembly is a low-level binary format that runs in the browser. It allows fast code written in C/C++/Rust to run in the browser, and is used in Local-First AI, in-browser image processing, and more.

WebGL

WebGL (Web Graphics Library)

WebGL is a Web API for rendering 2D and 3D graphics in the browser via the GPU. It is the foundational technology behind Three.js and R3F, enabling advanced graphical expression in the browser.

WordPress

WordPress is an open-source CMS used by 42.2% of all websites (W3Techs, May 2026). Thanks to its rich plugins and themes and ease of content management, it is widely adopted for corporate sites and blogs. Tufe Company also offers WordPress SEO auto-rewriting.

A/B Testing

A/B testing is an experimental method that delivers two variations simultaneously to compare their effectiveness. It is a fundamental technique for continuously improving CVR through optimization of LPs, CTAs, email subject lines, and more.

CAC

Customer Acquisition Cost

CAC is the cost required to acquire one customer. Measured as the total including advertising and labor costs, the LTV/CAC ratio is the most important indicator of business health.

CRM

Customer Relationship Management

CRM is a system for centrally managing customer information and maintaining and strengthening relationships. Salesforce and HubSpot are representative examples. It becomes powerful when integrated into business flows via AI agents or n8n.

CTA

Call To Action

A CTA is a button, link, or text that prompts users to take the next action. The wording, such as "Free Consultation," "Request Materials," or "Purchase," and its proper placement greatly influence CVR.

CVR

Conversion Rate

CVR is the percentage of website visitors who reach a conversion (purchase, application, inquiry). Industry average levels vary greatly by industry and channel. It has a more direct impact on sales than SEO traffic.

LP

Landing Page

An LP (landing page) is a long, vertically scrolling web page dedicated to a single purpose. Its design, which guides visitors arriving via ads or email newsletters toward conversion, is key.

LTV

Life Time Value

LTV is the cumulative revenue a single customer brings over their lifetime. The higher the LTV, the more [CAC] you can afford. It is a key metric for B2B SaaS and subscription businesses.

MA

Marketing Automation

MA is a mechanism for automating marketing tasks with systems. It automates email delivery, lead scoring, and segmented delivery, greatly improving B2B sales efficiency.

ROAS

Return On Ad Spend

ROAS is the ratio of revenue to advertising spend. It is a metric for measuring advertising efficiency, and healthy levels vary greatly by industry, gross margin, and media, so no universal standard exists.

ROI

Return On Investment

ROI is the ratio of profit to the amount invested. It is the most important metric for measuring the cost-effectiveness of initiatives and businesses, and serves as a criterion for decisions on marketing, AI adoption, web development, and more.

SFA

Sales Force Automation

SFA is a system that streamlines the sales process. It digitizes deals, activities, and results to strengthen sales management. It is often linked with CRM.

UGC

User Generated Content

UGC is content created voluntarily by users, such as reviews, posts, and photos. It is a powerful content source that works across SNS marketing, e-commerce, and SEO/LLMO alike.

Inbound Marketing

Inbound Marketing

Inbound marketing is a method of being found by customers. It combines SEO, content marketing, SNS, LLMO, and more, aiming for continuous lead acquisition without advertising.

Customer Journey

Customer Journey

The customer journey is the series of experiences a customer goes through from learning about a product/service to purchase and repeat purchase. Designing measures tailored to each stage maximizes results.

Cooling-Off (Specified Commercial Transactions Act)

Cooling-Off System (Specified Commercial Transactions Act, Article 9)

The cooling-off system allows consumers to cancel, without giving a reason, contracts concluded in specified transactions such as door-to-door sales and telephone solicitation, within a certain period from the date of receipt of the written document. For renovation door-to-door sales, the statutory period is 8 days.

Content Marketing

Content Marketing

Content marketing is a method of acquiring and nurturing customers by continuously delivering valuable information. It works closely with SEO/LLMO measures to build long-term traffic assets.

Lead Nurturing

Lead Nurturing

Lead nurturing is a measure that gradually develops prospects to guide them toward purchase. It deepens relationships through email, content, seminars, and more, raising CVR and closing rates.

AI Governance

AI governance is a general term for the policies, structures, and processes that enable companies to operate AI safely, fairly, and transparently. It refers to an organizational framework that balances risk management with business effectiveness while complying with government guidelines.

AI Pilot Project

An AI pilot project is a phase in which, before full-scale deployment, a company verifies the business impact of AI using real business data and frontline staff within a limited scope. It is distinguished from a PoC (proof of concept), which tests technical feasibility, and is the stage that asks whether the AI will actually work in the field without stalling.

AI Use Case

An AI use case is a specific application or scenario where AI is used within business operations. The success or failure of implementation is heavily influenced by which task is chosen first, making use case selection the most critical step in avoiding failure.

AI Readiness Assessment

AI Readiness Assessment

An AI readiness assessment is a framework for checking whether an organization is prepared to introduce and embed AI into its operations, examining five areas: data, talent, governance, business processes, and management commitment.

ROI of AI Implementation

Return on Investment

The ROI of AI implementation is a metric for measuring and evaluating the business benefits (cost reduction, revenue growth, and operational efficiency) gained from AI investments. Rather than stopping at the PoC stage, designing a system that tracks results numerically is the key to full-scale utilization.

IT Implementation Subsidy

(Digitalization and AI Implementation Subsidy)

A government program that subsidizes part of the costs for small and medium-sized enterprises and small businesses to introduce IT tools and AI. In fiscal year 2026, it will be operated as the "Digitalization and AI Implementation Subsidy," with a standard subsidy rate of up to 1/2 and amounts ranging from 50,000 to 4.5 million yen.

PoC (Proof of Concept)

Proof of Concept

A PoC is a process of verifying on a small scale whether a technology or idea actually works before full-scale development or investment. In AI implementation, it is an effective entry point for "trying small and fast," but if pursued without defining success criteria in advance, it risks ending as a mere experiment without leading to business value.

TCO (Total Cost of Ownership)

Total Cost of Ownership

TCO (Total Cost of Ownership) is the total lifetime cost of AI or IT systems, including not only initial costs but also operation, maintenance, personnel, training, and exit costs. It is a core metric for investment decisions alongside ROI in internal approvals.

Shadow AI

Shadow AI

The use of generative AI by employees for personal purposes without company permission or oversight. It leads to information leaks, governance breakdown, and inconsistent output quality, but establishing an environment where it can be used safely is more effective for adoption than outright prohibition.

Change Management

Change Management

Change management is a methodology for systematically driving the changes in organizations, people, and processes that accompany AI implementation and business reform, and achieving adoption on the ground. With a shortage of talent and know-how now the biggest challenge in AI utilization, designing organizational transformation in parallel with technology adoption determines success or failure.

Data Preparation and Preprocessing

Data Preparation

Data preparation and preprocessing is the process of collecting, cleansing, standardizing formats, and labeling input data to make AI systems work correctly. Poor data quality is one of the biggest reasons AI implementation projects are abandoned, and it is a foundational process that determines the success of RAG development and business automation.

Reskilling

Reskilling

Reskilling is a talent development initiative for acquiring the new skills needed to use AI. According to a survey by Teikoku Databank, "a shortage of personnel and know-how for AI operations" is cited as the biggest challenge with generative AI, making reskilling one of the most important initiatives for embedding AI implementation.
