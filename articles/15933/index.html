Job Profile: Cursor (Anysphere) FDE

Original title: 岗位档案：Cursor（Anysphere）FDE——工具公司也在招前置工程师

FDE中国 · 2026-09-04 · 1 min read

Summary

FDE China examines Anysphere's Forward Deployed Engineer posting for Cursor, arguing that even self-serve AI coding tools need FDEs once enterprises adopt them: workflow redesign, permission integration, and habit migration require human involvement. The interview process mirrors SWE hiring (a recruiter/manager screen plus four rounds), showing the company treats FDE as a pure engineering role without lowered technical bars. The piece notes Chinese parallels—Volcano Engine has referenced TRAE in solutions-role job descriptions—suggesting tool vendors building forward deployed capabilities is an industry trend, and recommends tracking tool-company FDE job postings as an indicator of how hard enterprise AI tool adoption really is.

Key points

  • Anysphere is hiring FDEs for Cursor, showing tool companies need forward deployed engineers too.
  • Enterprise adoption requires human work on workflows, permissions, and habit migration.
  • Interviews match SWE hiring: a screen plus four rounds, with no lowered technical bar.
  • Volcano Engine has referenced TRAE in solutions-role JDs, showing the trend reaching China.
  • Track tool-company FDE job postings as a barometer of enterprise AI adoption difficulty.

Full text

Editor's Note FDEChina Editorial Team

The signal in this job posting is that "tool companies also need FDEs": intuitively, tool products should be delivered self-serve, but Cursor still maintains a Forward Deployed Engineer role, showing that when AI coding tools enter enterprises, the steps of workflow transformation, permission integration, and migration of usage habits cannot be bypassed without people. The interview process is essentially the same as for SWE roles (initial screening plus 4 rounds), which also shows: these companies treat FDE as a pure engineering role and do not lower the technical bar just because it is "customer-facing."

The incremental insight for the Chinese context is that it gives AI coding tool teams a direct reference—major Chinese companies already show signs of writing products like TRAE into Solutions Engineer job descriptions (Volcano Engine), so tool vendors building forward deployed engineering capabilities is an industry trend, not an isolated case. Action recommendation: continuously track changes in tool companies' FDE job descriptions and read them as a barometer of "the difficulty of enterprise-level adoption of AI tools."
