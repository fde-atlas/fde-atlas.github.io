Forward Deployed Engineer

Lightcast · 2026 · 1 min read

Summary

This Lightcast page defines Forward Deployed Engineer as one entry in its Titles Taxonomy, built from over 1 billion job postings, resumes and profiles covering 20+ million real-world titles, standardized into 75,000+ entries. Title-to-occupation mappings stay flexible, unlike the fixed Occupation Taxonomy. The taxonomy helps organizations benchmark, analyze, and externalize internal job classifications, and is used by universities, public sector bodies, and Fortune 500 companies.

Full text

How does Lightcast define a title?

The Lightcast Titles Taxonomy distills over 20 million real-world jobs titles, collected from over 1 billion job postings, resumes and profiles, and standardizes them into a proprietary taxonomy of 75,000+ titles. While some titles, like "Air Traffic Controller," map directly to specific occupations, this relationship remains flexible, unlike the fixed structure of the Lightcast Occupation Taxonomy.

The Lightcast Titles enables organizations to standardize job data and integrate it into other labor market resources and taxonomies. This creates a universal framework for benchmarking, analysis, and transforming internal job classifications into actionable, externally relevant data.
