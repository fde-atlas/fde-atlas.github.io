Summary

Japanese company アンドドット (anddot) announced several updates centered on its resident FDE model. It launched "FDE for Security", extending on-site FDEs into the security domain to handle vulnerability discovery, fixes, and ongoing response end to end. The company is also active in the public sector: its CEO Ibaraki joined Nagoya City's DX advisory panel, held dialogues with MIC officials including Iimura and Koyama on regional DX/AX, and trained 460 Sapporo city employees on safe generative AI use. Training results include 85% of Mie Prefecture participants reporting reduced AI anxiety and 98% intent to use AI, over 90% satisfaction for Infomart programs, and 298 dispatched engineers passing a generative AI certification after anddot-led study sessions. Its sales staff also built the disaster-prevention app "Mamoruca" for Japan Junior Chamber using the company's own AI harness.

Key points

  • Launched "FDE for Security" with resident FDEs covering vulnerability discovery through continuous remediation.
  • CEO Ibaraki engaged with MIC officials and Nagoya City on regional DX and AX initiatives.
  • Trained 460 Sapporo city staff on safe generative AI use, covering 5 risks and 7 usage patterns.
  • Mie Prefecture program: 85% reported reduced AI anxiety, 98% intended to use AI.
  • Built disaster-prevention app "Mamoruca" for Japan Junior Chamber using its in-house AI harness.
