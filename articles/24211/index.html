Forward Deployed Engineer | Basic Capital | New York, NY

Zero G Talent · 2026-07-24 · 2 min read

Summary

Basic Capital, a New York fintech building a modern 401(k) and the first retirement mortgage for America's $1 trillion retirement industry, is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer. The role embeds with external partners such as third-party administrators, record-keepers, and HR/payroll processors, diagnosing their operational problems and building end-to-end full-stack products using software and AI. Requirements include 2+ years of forward deployed or solutions engineering experience, strong full-stack skills, React proficiency, and at least one language such as Kotlin, Rust, Go, Python, or TypeScript. Fintech or early-stage startup experience is preferred. The Tribeca-based team works in office.

Key points

  • Basic Capital is rebuilding retirement products, including a modern 401(k) and a retirement mortgage.
  • The FDE embeds with TPAs, record-keepers, and HR/payroll processors to build full-stack solutions.
  • Requires 2+ years of forward deployed or solutions engineering experience.
  • Tech requirements: React plus one of Kotlin, Rust, Go, Python, TypeScript, and others.
  • Role is based in Tribeca, NYC, with in-office work expected.

What you need

About Basic Capital

Basic Capital is a fintech company disrupting America’s $1T retirement industry by building the modern 401(k) built for today's workforce, and the first ever retirement mortgage. Our mission is to create products, platforms, and a credit marketplace that power a fundamentally new retirement system.

Our founding team includes leaders from Goldman Sachs, Uber, Block, Stripe, and Robinhood. We’re backed by top investors such as Lux Capital, Forerunner Ventures, BoxGroup, SVAngel, Inspired Capital, and Henry Kravis. Based in Tribeca, NYC, we’re assembling a lean, high-performance team committed to reducing wealth inequality.

For more information, visit Basic Capital’s website.

You will

  • Embed directly with external parties such as Third Party Administrators (TPA's), Record-Keepers, and HR/Payroll processors
  • Rapidly understand their toughest operational issues and use software and AI as a lever to quickly build end-to-end full stack products for them
  • Engage directly with external stakeholders, from technical teams to executives

Required Competencies:

  • 2+ years of forward deployed / solutions engineering experience
  • Strong full stack software engineering skills
  • Proficiency in any of the following: Kotlin, Rust, Java, Go, C/C++, Python, Ruby, or TypeScript
  • Proficiency with React

Preferred Competencies

  • Experience in fintech or early-stage technology companies
  • A highly analytical approach to solving business and operational problems using technology
  • Curiosity about the intersection of technology and business operations

Cultural Fit

  • Passionate about solving big social problems with pragmatic solutions
  • Aptitude for complexity and curiosity for financial markets
  • Enjoys learning new technologies and selecting the right tool for each job
  • Adaptable and open to contributing across the tech stack as the team evolves
  • Autonomous, high agency, accountable, and thrives in a fast-paced environment
  • Strong collaboration and communication skills, working effectively in cross-functional teams
  • Excited to be a part of an in-office team
