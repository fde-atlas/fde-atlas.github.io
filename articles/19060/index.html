Summary

This is a job-listing roundup from Japanese recruiting platform Findy covering Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) openings at over a dozen Japanese companies. It frames FDE as a new role that emerged as AI implementation matured, covering the full pipeline from requirements gathering and data analysis to customization, deployment, and operations. Featured employers include JDSC (capital alliance with SoftBank), Gen-AX, Sansan (¥11.52M–¥29.17M salary), ExaWizards, Loglass, Almondo, Stockmark, ACES (¥8.5M–¥14M), Upgrade, Algoage, DeNA (rolling out Cognition's Devin in Japan), and LayerX. Most positions are private listings visible only through Findy.

A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) is a new engineering position that has been gaining attention since last year, as AI implementation in society has become a serious reality. To bridge the last mile of AI implementation, FDEs stand on the front lines with customers, handling everything end-to-end from gathering requirements and analyzing data to customizing, implementing, and operating systems. As the number of FDE openings at companies in Japan has been increasing, we have compiled them into this featured jobs collection. If you are interested in FDE roles, please take a look.

JDSC Inc.

Findy Recommendation

JDSC Inc. is a company listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth Market that aims to improve the productivity of Japanese companies and transform industrial structures through the power of data science and engineering. In October 2025, it entered a capital and business alliance with SoftBank Corp. for AI agent development. As large-scale AI social implementation involving all of Japan and projects leveraging cutting-edge technologies such as OpenAI continue to expand, strengthening the development organization (Dev division) is an urgent priority. This role is recommended for those who want to lead, with a high degree of autonomy, the process from identifying issues to design, implementation, and operational improvement as an FDE deeply embedded in customer companies' business operations.

Findy Recommendation

Gen-AX Inc. is a company 100% funded by SoftBank Corp. that leads corporate "AI Transformation (AX)" on the front lines of generative AI. In addition to offering "X-Boost," a generative AI SaaS platform aimed at improving the efficiency of complex BtoBtoC inquiry response operations, the company provides "X-Ghost," a voice response solution powered by autonomous-thinking AI. As an FDE at the company, you will work alongside consultants and engineers developing products, driving technology selection, requirements definition, design, development, and operational design for AI adoption. Beyond technical discussions with customers and maximizing project value, you will also be expected to leverage your insights to further evolve the product. The role offers valuable opportunities such as direct collaboration with OpenAI, Microsoft, and the SoftBank group, as well as a highly technical environment where experts in speech and natural language processing gather.
