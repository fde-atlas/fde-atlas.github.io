Palantir Competitors and Alternatives, With Prices

FWD Deployment · 2026-10-11 · 5 min read

Summary

This roundup lists eleven Palantir competitors across data platforms, AI platforms and defense software, with list prices checked October 11, 2026. Palantir sells Foundry, AIP, Gotham and Apollo alongside forward deployed software engineers, reported $4.475B revenue for 2025 (up 56%), and publishes no contract prices beyond a free AIP Developer Tier. Databricks and Snowflake overlap Foundry most directly and publish usage pricing; C3 AI, Dataiku and IBM watsonx overlap AIP; Anduril's Lattice overlaps Gotham, while Scale AI and micro1 compete for government AI work. Databricks, Snowflake, Scale AI and C3 AI also staff forward deployed engineers, and Dplyz sells the engineering side alone without a platform.

Key points

  • Palantir's four platforms are paired with on-site forward deployed engineers; pricing is quote-based except a free AIP Developer Tier.
  • Databricks charges $0.15 per DBU upward; Snowflake credits cost $2–$4 depending on edition.
  • C3 AI prices per vCPU/vGPU-hour; Dataiku ($300M ARR) and Alteryx ($250/user/month Starter) vary in transparency.
  • Defense alternatives: Anduril's Lattice OS, Scale AI's government agent work, and micro1's agent builds and evaluation.
  • The article groups alternatives by need: one data platform, AI apps on your data, defense, or engineers only.

The reference point

What Palantir sells

Palantir sells four platforms: Foundry for data integration, AIP for AI agents on that data, Gotham for defense and intelligence, and Apollo for deploying software. It pairs them with forward deployed software engineers who work on site, a job Palantir says it pioneered.

Its five-day AIP Bootcamp aims to take a customer from zero to a working use case. Palantir reported $4.475 billion in revenue for 2025, up 56%. It doesn't publish contract prices beyond a free AIP Developer Tier.

That mix of software and on-site engineers is why Palantir competitors come from different directions. Some match the data platform, some match the AI tools, and some match the defense work.

Data and AI platforms

Best for: Data teams that want one platform for pipelines, analytics and AI agents

  • Founded 2013
  • $7B run-rate, Aug 2026
  • Billable FDEs

Databricks is a unified data and AI platform, which overlaps Foundry for data integration and AIP for building agents on company data. It prices by usage: the default view shows $0.15 per DBU for Lakeflow Jobs Classic plus cloud compute, and $0.22 to $0.70 per DBU for SQL warehouses. Its forward deployed engineers are billed on projects.

Best for: Enterprises that want governed data and AI in one cloud platform

  • $4.47B product revenue, FY26
  • $200M Anthropic deal

Snowflake overlaps Foundry for data integration and governance and offers AIP-style agents through Cortex AI. On-demand credits cost $2 on Standard, $3 on Enterprise and $4 on Business Critical in AWS US East, with storage at $23 per TB a month.

Best for: Industrial and government teams that want prebuilt AI applications

  • $250.3M revenue, FY26
  • Prebuilt AI apps

C3 AI pairs an enterprise AI platform with prebuilt operational apps, close to Foundry and AIP for industrial and government use. C3 Code is priced at $0.55 per vCPU-hour and $2.50 to $9.50 per vGPU-hour, with the first 20 developers included.

Best for: Enterprises that want models, a lakehouse and agents from IBM

  • Free trial tier
  • IBM calls prices indicative

watsonx.ai, watsonx.data and IBM's AI agents cover model building, a data lakehouse and agents, overlapping AIP and Foundry. watsonx.ai Essentials starts at $0 a month on pay as you go and Standard starts at $1,110 a month.

Best for: Mixed analyst and data science teams that need governance

  • Founded 2013
  • $300M ARR, Jan 2025

Dataiku is a governed platform for analytics, machine learning and AI agents with code and no-code tools, which overlaps AIP for building AI apps on company data. It passed $300 million in annual recurring revenue in January 2025. Prices aren't published.

Best for: Business analysts automating data prep and analytics

  • Starter $250/user/mo

Alteryx automates data workflows and analytics with governance for business users, which overlaps Foundry's data prep and analytics. Starter Edition is $250 per user a month billed yearly; Professional and Enterprise are quote-based.

Best for: Analytics teams that need modeling and governance together

  • Data, models and governance

SAS Viya combines data management, modeling and governance in one platform, which overlaps Foundry analytics and model deployment. SAS asks buyers to request pricing.

Government and defense

Best for: Agencies that want AI agents built, trained and evaluated

  • Agent builds for government
  • Cortex agent evaluation
  • $500M gross run rate (reported)

micro1 builds, deploys and evaluates AI agents for government work and recruits vetted experts to train them, which overlaps Palantir's and Scale AI's government AI work. It says it holds Awardable status on the CDAO Tradewinds Solutions Marketplace. Prices aren't published.

Best for: Government and enterprise teams that want AI agents built with them

  • Founded 2016
  • Meta investment, 2025
  • Public-sector unit

Scale builds AI agents and computer vision systems for government and enterprise with forward deployed engineers, which overlaps AIP and Gotham work. It doesn't publish prices.

Best for: US and allied defense customers

  • Lattice OS
  • $113K–$253K posted

Anduril builds defense systems powered by Lattice OS, its AI command-and-control software, for the US and allied militaries. Anduril FDEs deploy and support its products around the world, going forward with systems early in their lifecycle and working with engineering to improve them. Software-focused FDEs on the Strategic Defense team build mission software for integrated air and missile defense.

Questions

Common questions

Who are Palantir's main competitors?

For data integration and analytics, Databricks and Snowflake overlap Foundry most directly. For AI applications on company data, C3 AI, Dataiku and IBM watsonx overlap AIP. For defense software, Anduril's Lattice overlaps Gotham. Scale AI competes for government AI work.

Does Palantir publish its prices?

No. Palantir offers a free AIP Developer Tier, but its platform contracts are quoted. Databricks, Snowflake, C3 AI, IBM watsonx and Alteryx do publish usage or seat prices.

Is there a Palantir alternative that includes forward deployed engineers?

Databricks, Snowflake, Scale AI and C3 AI all staff forward deployed engineers. If you want the engineers without a new platform, firms like Dplyz embed engineers on the data and tools you already run.
