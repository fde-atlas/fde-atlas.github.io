Company Overview (Ebisu, Shibuya-ku, Tokyo)

Original title: 会社概要（東京都渋谷区恵比寿）

株式会社Sai · 2026-06-15 · 2 min read

Summary

This is the company profile page of SAI Inc., a hands-on consulting firm founded in June 2026 in Ebisu, Tokyo, led by representative director Ryo Shimizu, who previously served as a director at Sky Grid Inc. for 11 years and has worked with AI for 12 years. SAI offers new business development support (go-to-market assistance from planning to revenue), FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) co-development with engineers embedded on-site covering implementation, operations, and in-house capability building, and generative AI, data, and system development services. The company name encodes four values: respecting individuality and talent, pursuing top quality with first and fastest action, and continuous challenge and respect. The page also covers its philosophy, a message from the CEO, founding history, and office access details.

Key points

  • SAI Inc. was founded in June 2026 in Ebisu, Tokyo, led by Ryo Shimizu.
  • Services include GTM support, FDE on-site co-development, and generative AI consulting.
  • The name SAI stands for four values: Colorful, Talent, Best, and Restart.
  • Shimizu spent 11 years as a director at Sky Grid and has 12 years of AI experience.
  • Its stated mission is to be clients' first consultation partner and solve problems on-site.

Message

Message from the CEO

Thank you for your interest in us among the many technology companies out there.

Our company name "SAI" embodies four values: 彩 (respect for individuality), 才 (respect for talent), 最 (highest quality, first and fastest), and 再 (challenge and respect). Our mission is to think through what is "best" for our customers, serve as your first point of consultation, and together build an environment where we can challenge ourselves again and again.

We will work to solve essential problems on the ground, transcending the boundaries between strategy and operations, and between sales and development. We look forward to working with you.

Ryo Shimizu, Representative Director, SAI Inc.
