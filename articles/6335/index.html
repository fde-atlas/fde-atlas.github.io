Why Palantir?

Aced (formerly Exponent) · 2026-07-27 · 2 min read

Summary

This article compiles real interview experiences from candidates applying to Palantir for Forward Deployed Engineer, Deployment Strategist, and software engineering roles. The typical process includes a recruiter screen, technical coding interviews, and a final behavioral and problem-solving round. For Deployment Strategist roles, there is no single standard loop: early stages focus on motivation and fit, while the core is a technical plus decomposition round that tests whether candidates can break ambiguous problems into data-driven steps. Interviewers were described as collaborative, but time management was a common weakness among candidates.

Key points

  • Palantir interviews include recruiter screens, coding rounds, and behavioral problem-solving rounds.
  • Deployment Strategist loops have no universal format; decomposition is the most distinctive round.
  • Decomposition tests turning ambiguous problems into smaller data-driven steps.
  • Interviewers were collaborative; poor time management was a common candidate mistake.
  • One candidate faced a sliding window LeetCode question and a mini data project.

Interview experiences

5 shared

Palantir · 4 months ago"My interview process included a recruiter screen, technical coding interviews, and a final behavioral and problem-solving round. What went well was that the interviewers were clear, collaborative, and gave me opportunities to explain my thinking. I also felt strong when discussing tradeoffs and working through ambiguous problems. What did not go as well was my time management on one coding question—I spent too long refining my first approach instead of moving quickly to a working solution. Overall, the process was challenging but positive, and it helped me identify areas to improve."Software Engineer · Intern

Palantir · 5 months ago"The process was well organized and the interviewers were collaborative. I did well when thinking aloud and adapting to new information. I could have managed time better during the unfamiliar-codebase round."Forward Deployed Engineer · Mid Level / L4

Palantir · 9 months ago"Friendly recruiter, easy going but clearly wants to understand why you have applied for this position"Deployment Strategist · Entry Level / L3

Palantir · a year ago"The biggest thing I remember is that there really was not one clean, universal loop. The front of the process was mostly motivation and fit, then the core of it was technical plus decomposition, which was easily the most distinctive and intimidating part. I went in expecting the decomp to feel like a consulting case, and that was exactly the wrong mindset because they really want to see whether you can turn an ugly ambiguous problem into smaller data-driven steps. Later rounds felt more tailored to whatever concerns were still open, and even the 20-minute conversation after the "main part" still counted. The whole process felt like they were testing whether I could learn fast, stay calm under pressure, and operate with very little structure."Deployment Strategist

Palantir · a year ago"The process was much more difficult than I expected. Leetcode and a mini data project was presented. The leetcode project was a sliding window question."Software Engineer · Intern
