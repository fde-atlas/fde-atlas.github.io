Summary

This page is an index of practical guides from FDE Times, organized by theme: customer work, leadership, deployment, measurement, applied AI, foundations, and careers. The short pieces cover topics such as who may commit to feature dates, why stalled AI agents usually trace back to decision-less steering meetings, designing evals before building agents, idempotency and data-sync patterns, secrets and permission management, and interview insights for FDE roles at companies including Snowflake, Salesforce, Cohere, and Rogo. It serves as a case-based curriculum for the FDE craft rather than a single-argument article.

Key points

  • A categorized index of dozens of short FDE practice guides, not a single-argument piece.
  • Only product teams may commit to ship dates; FDEs should not promise them.
  • Stalled AI agents usually fail from meetings without decisions, not code.
  • Recurring themes: evals, idempotency, dead letter queues, secrets, and metering disputes.
