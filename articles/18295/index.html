Revenue Evolution FDE — Beside the Horizon Opened by Engineers, Another Horizon Called Experts | AlphaDrive

Summary

AlphaDrive proposes a new engagement model, the Forward Deployed Expert, positioned alongside the industry-standard Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) popularized by Palantir and adopted by Anthropic and OpenAI. Its argument: as AI collapses the cost of building finished software, the scarce capability is no longer building but deciding what to build, for whom, and why. The Expert works beside client business owners on business implementation, driving all four steps of the AX for Revenue Loop (AI Sprint, Plateau Detection, PI Injection, revenue structure redesign) as a single continuous actor. Because Experts can generate code and prototypes with AI and run Full-Product Launches, they operate without external engineers. AlphaDrive grounds the offering in its own businesses, experience from 260 clients and 23,800 projects, and a three-layer development network, targeting large companies' new-business units and mature businesses seeking adjacent growth, with the goal of cultivating AX Architects inside client organizations.

Key points

  • AlphaDrive introduces Forward Deployed Expert as a business-implementation counterpart to the FDE.
  • AI-era scarcity lies in deciding what to build, not in building capability.
  • One Expert drives all four AX for Revenue Loop steps continuously with client business owners.
  • Experts code with AI and run Full-Product Launches independently, without external engineers.
  • Backed by 260-client experience and a three-layer development network; aims to train client AX Architects.

01 / Background

The decisive capability: discerning what to build. The decisive capability of the AI era

In an era of Completion Cost Collapse, where the cost of producing finished software has collapsed, the methodology for new business is evolving from the "learn without building" MVP to a new paradigm: Full-Product Launch (FPL)—"launch a finished product into the market and learn from it". In a world where the manufacturing cost of finished products has collapsed, the ability to "build" itself is no longer a scarce capability.

Problem 01

Products can be launched in an instant

Through AI Orchestration, the process from design and coding to completed testing has shrunk from person-months to nearly zero. Products that were once "too costly to realize" are now economically rational to build.

Problem 02

But businesses are not born

Even in an era where anything can be built, unless "which customer challenge, what to build, and why" is defined, no business that reaches the market will be born. A fact AlphaDrive has witnessed repeatedly across 260 companies and 23,800 field engagements.

Problem 03

The practical knowledge of business development

The ability to construct hypotheses, break through internal decision-making, and continuously update business hypotheses from market response—that is, the practical knowledge of business development. This will become the decisively scarce capability of the AI era.

Beside the field of technical implementation that Engineers have pioneered, we establish a different horizon: the field of business implementation. — AX for Revenue, 2026

02 / Redefinition

The "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" is a core engagement model in the field of technical implementation in the AI era, established by Palantir Technologies in the 2000s and carried forward by the world's leading AI companies including Anthropic and OpenAI. It is precisely because there were engineers who wrote code on-site at customer companies and transformed challenges into products that business implementation in the AI era has moved forward.

A note of respect

AlphaDrive stands on a foundation of respect for the Forward Deployed Engineer and establishes a different horizon from the one they pioneered—an engagement model that exerts its power in the "field of business implementation": the Forward Deployed Expert. The two are not models that line up in the same field; they are distinct engagement models, each exerting its power in a different field. Which one to choose is determined by what the customer company wants to solve.

03 / What FDE Drives

Driving through to the finished-product launch. 5 actions FDE drives at the client field

The Forward Deployed Expert sits beside the customer company's business owner and drives every step of the AX for Revenue Loop together with them. This is neither consulting report delivery nor implementation on behalf of engineers. It is a person who drives revenue evolution as a party to the effort, as a comrade.

02 Realizing business development that learns by launching finished products into the market—without stopping at the MVP—through high-speed AI-driven product building. Putting the FPL paradigm into practice on the ground itself.

03 AI Sprint (thorough AI transformation of existing operations) / Plateau Detection (detecting limits) / PI Injection (injecting intelligence only humans can possess) / redesigning revenue structures—running these 4 steps as one with the customer team.

04 Unearthing the Primal Intelligence (PI) dormant within the customer company and connecting it with AI, thereby injecting into the business an intelligence that AI cannot, in principle, reach.

05 Drawing on the decision-making structures unique to large enterprises cultivated across 260 companies, accompanying the customer through internal approval, budgeting, and full-scale business launch. Not ending with proposals, but breaking through to decisions.

Yes, Experts write code too.

The Forward Deployed Expert naturally makes full use of AI-driven code generation and prototype implementation as standard tools. Precisely because AI has broadened the reach of engineering, it has become realistic for business development specialists to stand in the field while writing code—the Expert as a talent profile is founded on this very shift.

04 / AX for Revenue Loop

A single FDE drives it continuously. One Expert drives all 4 steps

A single FDE drives the 4 steps of the AX for Revenue Loop—AI Sprint / Plateau Detection / PI Injection / Revenue Structure Redesign—continuously, together with the customer's business owner. The person in charge does not change from step to step. One consistent personality climbs continuously, from the mountain of efficiency AI to the mountain of revenue-evolution AI.

  • Step 01 AI Sprint Thoroughly AI-automating existing operations with AI and seeing it through to completion. The executive's question: "How far can we go in AI-automating our business?"
  • Step 02 Plateau Detection Identifying the point of diminishing returns that inevitably arrives after you have done everything you can. Not data analysis, but a process where the executive and business owner face each other honestly and talk it through.
  • Step 03 PI Injection Searching for new veins of gold with Primal Intelligence in domains that AI cannot predict or calculate. Primary information dormant within the customer's company and AlphaDrive's cross-disciplinary thinking are injected into AI in an integrated way.
  • Step 04 Revenue Structure Redesign Converting the signs you have found into a model that can be reproduced repeatedly. At least one of "who to sell what to, and how" is rewritten discontinuously.

11 / Statement

Today, we put forward a single philosophy to the market. Around the world, "FDE = Forward Deployed Engineer" is spreading as a standard term. It is a core engagement format on the ground of technical implementation, originating with Palantir and carried forward by leading AI companies such as Anthropic and OpenAI. We pay respect to this movement.

That said, we ask: should the Forward Deployed engagement format, standing on the ground, be confined to the ground of technical implementation alone? In an era where AI writes code and AI launches finished products in an instant, what is scarce is not "the ability to build," but the practical business development know-how to determine "what to build, for whom, and why." That is why, alongside the horizon of the Engineer, we establish another horizon: the "Forward Deployed Expert," an engagement format that delivers impact on the ground of business implementation.

The substance of the Expert's capabilities is what AlphaDrive has already systematized as "AX Architect = BA capability × AI capability." Because Experts possess engineering capability in-house, on the ground of business implementation, the Expert functions as a complete, standalone persona. Without depending on external Engineers, the AX Architect drives and runs alongside the business leader of the customer company — that is the Forward Deployed Expert.

It is not the dispatch of AI engineers, nor the delivery of templates. As a stakeholder, as a comrade, we drive the revenue evolution of the customer company, and ultimately take responsibility for cultivating AX Architects within the customer company — that is our Forward Deployed Expert.

In all of these domains — AI × business development, AI × talent development, and AI enablement — we aim to remain the leading authority in Japan. We want to build, together, a society where AI opens up human possibilities, not one where AI takes away the reason for human existence.

12 / FAQ

Q.01 What is a Forward Deployed Expert (FDE)?

The "Forward Deployed Engineer" advocated by Palantir was an engagement format that delivers impact on the ground of technical implementation. The "Forward Deployed Expert," which AlphaDrive is establishing for the first time in Japan, is a different horizon standing beside it — an engagement format that delivers impact on the ground of business implementation. It is a service in which the AX Architect (BA capability × AI capability) systematized by AlphaDrive drives and runs alongside the business leader of the customer company.

Q.02 What is the difference between a Forward Deployed Engineer and a Forward Deployed Expert?

The two are not side-by-side within the same ground; they are different engagement formats, each delivering impact in a different ground. The Engineer delivers impact on the ground of technical implementation (i.e., the ground where products should be built to completion). The Expert delivers impact on the ground of business implementation (i.e., the ground where one determines what to build and redesigns the revenue structure). Which one to choose depends on what the customer company wants to solve.

Q.03 Does the Expert need to be used together with an Engineer?

Q.04 What is the substance of the Expert's capabilities?

It is the AX Architect = BA capability × AI capability that AlphaDrive systematized in White Paper 04, "The Implementation Theory of the AX Architect." BA capability covers corporate strategy, cross-organizational coordination, and the PI domain. AI capability consists of three components: AI SPRINT / AI Orchestration / Full-Product Launch.

Q.05 Can the Expert write code?

Yes. The Forward Deployed Expert uses AI-based code generation and prototype implementation as a matter-of-fact toolset. Precisely because we are in an era where AI has broadened the reach of engineering, it has become realistic for business development specialists to stand on the ground while writing code. The Expert as a talent profile is premised on this change.

Q.06 How does this differ from external dispatch?

The FDE is not a transient dispatch that leaves when the contract ends; it takes responsibility for cultivating AX Architects within the customer company. The very process in which the FDE drives and runs alongside the business leader is the process of transplanting AX Architect capability into the customer company. AlphaDrive's fundamental philosophy is "development first, accompaniment as complement" (WP-04 v4.0), and this service conforms to it.

Q.07 Is on-site work mandatory?

Q.08 What kind of companies are the intended customer companies?

We envision two main types. ① New business divisions: new business divisions of large enterprises that want to mass-produce multiple new business projects. ② Peripheral expansion of mature businesses: existing business divisions that want to capture peripheral opportunities around their current business and compound them. Both are companies that need a persona capable of fully running the AX for Revenue Loop in the field.

Q.09 Can we also request work in the efficiency AI domain?

Yes. As a stage preceding revenue evolution, the Revenue Evolution FDE can also start by accompanying you in the efficiency AI domain (AI Sprint and Plateau Detection). The moment you reach a plateau, the same FDE seamlessly continues into the revenue evolution phase (PI Injection and redesign of the revenue structure). One FDE continuously climbing both the efficiency AI mountain and the revenue evolution AI mountain — that is the value this service provides.
