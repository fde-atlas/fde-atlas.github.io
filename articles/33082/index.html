AWS forward deployed engineering expands to 15 countries

Kernavique · 2026-11-17 · 2 min read

Summary

AWS stated on November 17, 2026 that its forward deployed engineering organization now spans 15 countries, serving commercial and public sector customers. The claim appears as a note added to Amazon's June 30, 2026 announcement, which introduced the organization alongside a $1 billion investment in forward deployed AI engineers. Kernavique cautions that 15 is a count of countries, not roles, so it reveals nothing about headcount or locations, but it signals that a large vendor is stationing engineers near customers across many markets.

Key points

  • AWS says its forward deployed engineering organization covers 15 countries.
  • The organization serves commercial and public sector customers.
  • The figure was added as a note to Amazon's June 30, 2026 announcement.
  • That announcement included a $1 billion investment in forward deployed AI engineers.
  • The count measures countries, not the number of roles or where they sit.

AWS said on November 17, 2026, that its forward deployed engineering organization has expanded to 15 countries. The statement is a note added to Amazon’s own announcement of June 30, 2026, on About Amazon. The note says the organization now serves commercial and public sector customers. The page shows an update date of November 19, 2026.

What happened

The note sits on the About Amazon post that first announced the organization on June 30, 2026. We read the page on November 20, 2026. It names two groups of customers served: commercial and public sector. The count of 15 countries is AWS’s own figure.

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

This suggests forward deployed engineering work at AWS now spans many countries. The figure is a count of countries, not of roles, so it does not tell engineers how many positions exist or where they are.

If you are hiring them

AWS’s own figure of 15 countries suggests a large vendor is placing forward deployed engineers close to customers in many markets. If you are building a team, that is one sign of who else is hiring for this work.
