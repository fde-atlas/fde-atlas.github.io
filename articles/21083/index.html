Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu Fully Deploys FDE Talent to Strengthen Corporate Value-Based Consulting Services

Original title: デロイト トーマツ、企業価値起点のコンサルティングサービス強化に向け、FDE人材の活用を本格化

Deloitte · 2026-04-30 · 6 min read

Summary

Deloitte Tohmatsu (Japan) will formally launch its use of Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) from June 2026, establishing an FDE Management Office to support AI-driven enterprise transformation aimed at raising corporate value. FDEs will work alongside Deployment Strategists (DS)—consultants who analyze corporate value structures and identify priority areas for change—connecting strategy with on-the-ground AI implementation from conception through deployment and operational adoption. The firm will build internal certification and career models to develop FDEs both through external hiring and internally, and will team FDEs with different specialties (large-scale business solutions, multi-cloud/AI integration). Existing strategy and business consultants will also be developed as DS. The move reflects AI's shift from PoCs to full-scale implementation; a 2025 Deloitte global survey found 74% of organizations want AI to grow future revenue.

News

April 30, 2026

  • Through collaboration between DS (Deployment Strategists) and FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers), Deloitte Tohmatsu will support AI-driven, enterprise-wide transformation that enhances corporate value, through to implementation
  • An FDE Management Office will be established in June 2026 to build a firm-wide framework for FDE career development

News Release

Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu LLC, a member firm of the Deloitte Tohmatsu Group (Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; Representative Executive Officer: Kenichi Kimura; hereinafter "Deloitte Tohmatsu"), will strengthen its management consulting services to help client companies achieve management and business transformation through the use of AI. As part of this effort, the firm will establish an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) Management Office in June 2026 and begin the full-scale deployment of FDE talent.

With the spread of AI agents and agentic AI, the use of AI is moving beyond mere technology adoption and one-off PoCs (proofs of concept) into a full-scale implementation phase, in which AI is embedded in business processes and works in coordination with internal and external systems to continuously deliver results. In a global survey conducted by Deloitte in 2025, 74%* of organizations said they want to grow revenue in the future through AI initiatives, and expectations for full-scale implementation are rising even further. In management and business transformation, given the flexible and highly integrable nature of AI, it is essential to combine advanced engineering that iterates continuous improvement—identifying issues in actual operations and updating requirements—with a strategic perspective to create value for each company. The key lies in talent with broad technical and human skills who can drive "cross-functional business transformation," "implementation and operational adoption in the field," and "decision-making that engages senior management" to a greater extent than traditional IT solutions.

Against this backdrop, Deloitte Tohmatsu will begin the full-scale deployment of FDE talent who support transformation that goes beyond mere business efficiency and enhances corporate value, consistently from conception through implementation and adoption. FDEs, who handle AI implementation in the field, work in collaboration with DSs (Deployment Strategists), who analyze the structure of corporate value and identify priority areas for transformation, connecting strategy with execution to support AI-driven, enterprise-wide transformation. This aims to realize transformation that does not end at the conceptual stage but is geared toward delivering results.

Figure: Deloitte Tohmatsu's FDE framework enables an AI transformation execution model starting from corporate value

FDEs require, along with a mindset for creating customer value, a broad range of skills spanning front-end, back-end, and infrastructure to design and implement AI-driven system and data integration. Building on the DX and system development expertise cultivated through collaboration with numerous alliance partners, Deloitte Tohmatsu will chart a path for FDEs in this nascent stage to build their careers.

The firm will establish internal certification programs and career models, building a mechanism to develop FDEs internally in addition to external hiring. In addition, it will form teams of FDEs with specialized strengths—such as talent skilled in large-scale business solutions or in combining multiple cloud and AI services—creating a professional community that mutually blends knowledge and skills. Furthermore, existing strategy and business consultants with industry expertise will be developed and strengthened as DSs to support transformation aimed at enhancing clients' corporate value.

At Deloitte Tohmatsu, in preparation for the full-scale launch in June 2026, the firm will progressively advance FDE talent certification and development, as well as strategic assignment to projects, alongside institutional development. During the launch phase, the FDE Management Office will handle FDE recruiting, development, institutional design, knowledge sharing, and assignment coordination, aiming for firm-wide operations that balance quality and speed.
