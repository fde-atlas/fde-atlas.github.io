FDE Guides

fde.directory · 2026-11-13 · 11 min read

Summary

This is a guide index from fde.directory compiling role-by-role breakdowns of Forward Deployed Engineer positions at roughly 30 companies, including Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Stripe, and Salesforce. Each guide covers the actual job scope, posted salary bands, and interview process. Alongside company profiles, the collection includes cross-cutting analyses: a 2026 salary guide (median midpoint of $216,104 across 74 US ranges), an FDE vs Solutions Engineer comparison, market maps for the US, Europe (London-dominated), and India (585 live postings), remote-work trends, and reviews of FDE training programs such as IIT Delhi's and IIT Roorkee's certificates.

Key points

  • Company-by-company FDE guides covering role, pay bands, and interviews.
  • US median FDE salary midpoint: $216,104 across 74 posted ranges.
  • Explains how FDE differs from Solutions Engineer in scope and pay.
  • Market maps for the US, London-centric Europe, and India.
  • Reviews FDE credentials from IIT Delhi, IIT Roorkee, and others.
