Communication-Script-Library

Original title: Communication-Script-Library

FDE中国社区 · 2026-07-12 · 5 min read

Summary

This article documents Communication-Script-Library (v1.2), a skill package from the FDE China community addressing misalignment between FDEs' technical communication and business stakeholders, which causes scope drift and unmanaged expectations. It provides structured scripts for six audiences—executives, business owners, IT, compliance, frontline users, and career situations—each following a five-part structure: context, goal, script, taboos, and follow-up questions. It covers kickoff alignment, weekly demos, scope changes, delay notifications, and pre-launch expectation calibration, with stage gates, an AIBP collaboration interface, and a downloadable package importable into Claude, Cursor, and similar agent-skill tools.

Key points

  • Targets FDE-stakeholder communication misalignment causing scope drift.
  • Provides scripts for six audiences including executives, IT, and compliance.
  • Each script uses context, goal, script, taboos, follow-up questions structure.
  • Covers kickoff, weekly demos, scope changes, delays, launch calibration.
  • Includes stage gates, AIBP collaboration roles, and a downloadable skill package.

Problem Definition

Misalignment between FDE technical expression and business stakeholders' understanding, leading to scope drift, runaway expectations, and collaboration friction.

Methodology Framework

Six audience-specific script types: executives (value/ROI/risk), business (process/adoption), IT (integration/permissions), compliance (boundaries/human review), frontline (training/feedback), and career communication (referrals/interviews/promotions). Structure: Situation → Goal → Script → Taboos → Follow-up question list.

Career Script Module (New)

For FDE entry and promotion stages, avoiding "can do but can't say":

  • Referral requests: Present evidence first, then make the request; never mass-send template messages;
  • Informational interview invitations: Clarify the 20-minute boundary, purpose, and reciprocity;
  • Interview questions: Verify role authenticity, delivery boundaries, and support resources;
  • Salary and boundary communication: Discuss total compensation structure, responsibility boundaries, travel, and on-call;
  • Promotion conversations: Align around evidence gaps, observers, and the next review cycle.

FAQ

Q1: When executing [Communication-Script-Library], how do you avoid "explaining technical details to executives"?

A: Follow the README methodology step by step and present interim deliverables at the Weekly Demo; if PoC materials are incomplete, first produce a "to-be-validated list" before proceeding.

Q2: What additional constraints apply in private deployment/Xinchuang/MLPS environments?

A: Specify the deployment model in the prompt constraints section; when data cannot leave the domain, domestic components, or audit logs are involved, reference Private-Deployment-Gateway and RBAC-Audit accordingly.

Q3: What if the division of responsibilities with the AIBP is unclear?

A: Return to the AIBP-Collaboration-Playbook to update the RACI; the AIBP owns Ground Truth and business acceptance, while the FDE owns deliverable technical assets.

Q4: After the PoC passes, how do you transition to Beta/production?

A: Mark the next-stage Skill in the deliverables (e.g., RAG-Evaluation→Private-Deployment-Gateway→FDE-Adoption-Growth) and trigger the Launch Gate via the checklist.
