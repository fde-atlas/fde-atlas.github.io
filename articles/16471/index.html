Propel 26

Rocketlane Blog · 2026 · 13 min read

Summary

A recap of Propel 26, Rocketlane's conference for professional services leaders, gathering 500+ attendees to rethink how PS success is measured in the AI era. The page summarizes dozens of sessions: go-live as the wrong finish line, outcome-based pricing, NRR as the key implementation metric, AI for scoping and project risk scoring, agentic PSA product announcements, and a dedicated session on forward deployed engineering—when the FDE model (combining consultant, PM, and engineer) fits and when it doesn't, drawing on experience from Palantir and Rippling.

Key points

  • 500+ PS leaders met to rethink how success is measured.
  • Go-live is framed as the wrong finish line; outcomes and NRR matter more.
  • AI sessions cover scoping, risk scoring, and agentic PSA tools.
  • Clients requesting outcome-based pricing are driven by fear, not frugality.
  • A session explores when the FDE model works, citing Palantir and Rippling.
