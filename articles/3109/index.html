Palantir Glassdoor, Culture & Careers 2026

Palantir Technologies · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

This profile of Palantir Technologies covers its culture, Glassdoor ratings, and engineering structure. Founded in 2003 by Peter Thiel and Alex Karp and headquartered in Denver with about 4,000 employees, the company runs three platforms — Gotham, Foundry, and AIP — for government and enterprise clients. Its Glassdoor rating is 3.7 with 71% recommending the employer. The signature Forward Deployed Engineer role embeds engineers with clients on problems without standard solutions. Reviewers praise talent density, real-world impact, and equity upside, but cite poor work-life balance (2.8/5), burnout risk, heavy travel in deployed roles, and internal politics.

Key points

  • Glassdoor rating of 3.7; 71% would recommend; 299 open roles listed.
  • Pros: exceptional talent density, mission-critical impact, strong stock gains.
  • Cons: poor work-life balance (2.8/5), high-pressure culture, burnout.
  • Forward Deployed Engineers face significant travel and client pressure.
  • Small autonomous teams; engineers own features end-to-end with minimal oversight.

Culture Overview

What it's really like to work at Palantir

Palantir builds data platforms that governments and enterprises use to make critical decisions — from tracking supply chains to coordinating military operations. Co-founded by Peter Thiel and Alex Karp in 2003, the company has a unique culture: part Silicon Valley tech startup, part defense contractor, all intensity. Engineers work on product teams with minimal supervision and own end-to-end execution. The "Forward Deployed Engineer" role is Palantir's signature — embedding engineers directly with clients to solve problems that don't have textbook solutions.

Working at Palantir: Pros & Cons

✓ What employees love

  • ✓ The smartest colleagues you'll ever work with — talent density is extraordinary across engineering and business

Tech Stack

Products

Three core platforms: Gotham (intelligence analysis), Foundry (enterprise data integration), and AIP (AI-powered decision-making). Each handles petabytes of data with real-time analytical capabilities.

Forward Deployed

Palantir's signature role: engineers embedded with clients in the field, solving novel problems with Palantir's platform. High autonomy, high stakes, no runbook to follow. Explore roles →

Team Structure

Small, autonomous product teams with minimal management overhead. Engineers own features end-to-end. The culture rewards initiative and results over process and seniority.
