Summary

This page is a snapshot (dated 2026-11-02) of Sola's job description for Forward Deployed Engineers, presented as research excerpts rather than a hiring commitment. The role combines hands-on coding with customer-facing work: engineers build custom integrations, contribute production code to Sola's platform, and convert recurring customer needs into reusable improvements. Strong communication skills are required, including explaining technical findings to non-technical audiences. Sola says it is open to early-career engineers and new graduates with demonstrated programming ability, and aims to hire a small group to serve the world's largest enterprises and reshape how enterprise automation is built.

Key points

  • Sola is hiring a small group of Forward Deployed Engineers for large enterprise customers.
  • The role requires writing production code and building custom integrations.
  • Engineers should turn recurring customer needs into reusable platform improvements.
  • Candidates must explain complex technical findings to non-technical audiences.
  • Early-career engineers and new graduates with strong programming skills are welcome.
