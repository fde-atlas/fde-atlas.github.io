Acquires Anthropic Official Certification "CCAR-F" to Strengthen the Expertise of Its Companion Team

INSIGHT LAB Inc. (Headquarters: Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo; President and CEO: Isao Toyama; Securities Code: 227A; hereinafter "INSIGHT LAB") has launched its "AI Adoption Companion Partner" service, which uses AI to improve customers' actual operations. As part of this service, the responsible team includes members who have obtained Anthropic's official technical certification, "Claude Certified Architect – Foundations (CCAR-F)." This enables the company to provide a service with the expertise to support enterprises in a way that goes beyond mere technology implementation and accompanies their growth.

AI Adoption Companion Partner: About the Acquisition of Anthropic's Official Certification "CCAR-F"

INSIGHT LAB engineers have obtained Anthropic's official technical certification, "Claude Certified Architect – Foundations (CCAR-F)." With this, the company now has two CCAR-F certified engineers. In this service, the insights of these certified engineers are shared across the entire team of engineers and consultants, including FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) who work alongside customers on-site, and as an "AI Adoption Companion Partner," the company provides consistent support from planning and design through operational establishment, in line with each enterprise's growth phase.

CCAR-F is Anthropic's first official technical certification, which the company began offering in March 2026. The exam tests knowledge and implementation skills related to the Claude API, Agent SDK, Claude Code, and MCP (Model Context Protocol). At INSIGHT LAB, however, this expertise is viewed not merely as "proof of technical skill" but as "the ability to accurately identify on-site challenges and accompany enterprises at their own pace of growth," and it is applied to service delivery.

Rather than one-off technical support, INSIGHT LAB's vision of "companionship" means building business understanding and relationships over time and flexibly adjusting pace to fit each enterprise's situation.

Comment from the responsible engineer: Through obtaining the CCAR-F, I was able to systematically organize Claude's design principles. I hope to share what I learned with on-site team members, raise the quality of our companionship, and help drive our customers' growth.

Background of the Service

With the spread of generative AI, expectations for business automation and decision support are rising rapidly, while traditional cost-cutting measures are reaching their limits, and challenges such as person-dependent operations and difficulty embedding AI in the field due to labor shortages are becoming apparent.

Leveraging its track record of supporting data utilization at a cumulative total of 600 companies and more than 2,000 projects, and an organization in which roughly 70% of employees are data engineers, INSIGHT LAB launched this service to ensure that AI does not end as a one-time initiative but is delivered as a "system that continues to be used in the field."

Ahead of the official launch, the service has already led to contracts and the start of support with multiple companies, underscoring the strong demand in the field.

Features of the Service

■ AI Utilization Support Based on the Data Value Chain

The company applies the concept of the data value chain (strategy formulation → data preparation → data utilization and application → operational improvement) to this service, building on its accumulated data engineering and data analysis expertise to achieve reliable AI utilization.

■ A Hands-On Companion Style That Embeds Itself in the Field

At the core of this service are engineers in the role known as "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)." Going beyond requirements gathering, they visit the field to verify workflows and challenges, providing companionship that extends to designing operational rules and conducting regular reviews, thereby realizing AI utilization that takes root in the field. The CCAR-F acquisition supports the quality of this companionship, which is tailored to each company's pace of growth.

■ Contract Model Designed for Ongoing Support

This service adopts a monthly, companion-style contract rather than one-off development requests. By remaining continuously involved in the field, the company generalizes person-dependent business knowledge and builds a state in which AI utilization takes root in operations even amid labor shortages.

Companies for Which the Service Is Suitable

  • Want to adopt AI but don't know where to start
  • Ran a PoC, but it never took hold in actual operations
  • No in-house talent to drive AI utilization
  • Want to further accelerate business efficiency and DX
