Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (5/5, Feb 13, 2023): Great Company, Good Learning

Taro · 2023-02-13 · 1 min read

Summary

This Taro page compiles a Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer review and interview experiences. A former employee who worked there under a year rated the company 5/5 in February 2023, praising benefits and learning opportunities while noting the field is competitive. Three interview accounts follow: a New York process with a casual recruiter call, a 30-minute LeetCode medium/hard screen, and an onsite covering data manipulation, decomposition, and behavioral questions; a 2015 online coding challenge plus a shared-screen phone interview where the interviewer seemed distracted; and a Mexico HackerRank challenge with three progressively harder steps.

Key points

  • Former FDSE rated Palantir 5/5 after under a year
  • Pros: benefits and learning; con: competitive field
  • NY onsite covered data manipulation and behavioral questions
  • Technical screen was 30 minutes of LeetCode medium/hard
  • Mexico candidates faced a three-step HackerRank challenge
