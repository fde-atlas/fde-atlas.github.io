(Founding) Forward Deployed Engineer | horizontal.

Zero G Talent · 2026-11-21 · 3 min read

Summary

Horizontal, a Munich-based startup building AI agents for manufacturing (branded as a manufacturing aOS), is hiring a founding Forward Deployed Engineer. Founded by two Stanford robotics researchers with industrial experience at MAN Energy Solutions, BMW, and over 150 agentic AI rollouts, the company deploys agents that handle operational work inside factories' existing systems. The role involves on-site work with plant managers and quality teams, converting operational processes into agent workflows, integrating with ERP, MES, and quality tools, and running deployments from workshop to live production. Requirements include strong coding with daily AI tool use, a CS/engineering/physics background, fluent English (German preferred), and willingness to travel. The position is in-person in Munich, with a fast interview process and offer within 48 hours.

Key points

  • Horizontal is hiring a founding Forward Deployed Engineer, based in-person in Munich.
  • Founders met in Stanford robotics research; one rolled out agentic AI at 150+ industrial companies.
  • Role: turn factory operational processes into agent workflows on the company's platform.
  • Integrations cover ERP, MES, and quality tools, from first workshop to live production.
  • 90-day plan: on-site week one, first live workflow by day 30, independent deployments by day 90.

We're building the manufacturing aOS for enterprises. Our AI agents take over the operational work that keeps factories running, inside the systems those factories already use.

We met doing robotics research at Stanford. Jakob built ML systems for industrial machines at MAN Energy Solutions and BMW. Niklas sold and rolled out agentic AI at more than 150 industrial companies. We both saw the same thing: the work that keeps factories running is still done by hand across disconnected systems. Our team comes from ETH Zurich, Stanford and TUM, and we're based in Munich.

Forward Deployed Engineers bring Horizontal into our customers' factories. You'll work on site with large manufacturers, understand how their operations really run and build the agents that take over that work on top of our platform.

What you'll do

  • Work directly with plant managers, planners and quality teams at large manufacturers
  • Break down real operational processes and turn them into working agent workflows
  • Connect our platform to customer systems like ERP, MES and quality tools
  • Take deployments from the first workshop to live production
  • Bring what you learn in the field back into the core product

Your first 90 days

  • Week 1: on site with your first customer
  • Day 30: your first agent workflow live in a plant
  • Day 90: you run customer deployments on your own

Who you are

  • You code well and you're comfortable in front of customers
  • You learned to code the hard way and now build with AI coding tools every day
  • Background in computer science, engineering, industrial engineering or physics. Fresh grads are welcome
  • You've built things on your own initiative
  • You enjoy a shop floor as much as a code editor
  • Fluent in English and ideally German, since many of our customers are in Germany
  • Happy to travel to customer sites

This role is in person in Munich.

Interview Process

  • 20-minute intro call with a founder
  • On-site with both founders in Munich
  • Offer within 48 hours

About horizontal.

Every factory will be run by AI agents. Horizontal is building them. Today, the work that keeps production moving is scattered across people, teams, and disconnected software. When plans change, materials run short, or quality issues appear, factory teams piece together the context and coordinate the response by hand. Horizontal's AI agents take on that operational work inside the systems factories already use. They replan production around a disruption, investigate quality issues and coordinate order releases across teams and tools. Manufacturers start with one process and add agents over time, building toward a system that coordinates the factory end to end. Factories are demanding environments for AI: every plant has its own systems and procedures, and every action must work in the context of real production. Our team combines mechanical engineering and shop-floor experience with a track record of building and deploying agentic AI in global industrial companies. Horizontal connects the people, software and machines that run production.
