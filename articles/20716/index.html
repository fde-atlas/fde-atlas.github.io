What Does an FDE Do? An Introduction to Salaries and Entry Points for Beginners, Based on a Count of 63 Job Listings

Summary

AInformation counted Japanese AI job listings on November 2, 2026: 63 FDE postings from 47 companies out of 2,695 AI jobs. The median salary floor was ¥7 million, ¥1 million above the AI-job overall median, with a median ceiling of ¥12.5 million. 48% of FDE postings mention Claude Code, 2.7 times the overall rate. None welcomed inexperienced candidates; entry points were five Associate or internship roles. The article also compares OpenAI, Anthropic and Palantir Tokyo openings, notes search volume growth, and offers job-search tips plus a prompt for matching postings to one's resume.

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is an engineer who embeds themselves in the client's site and builds AI into their business operations until it actually works.

In English it is written as Forward Deployed Engineer. Even in Japanese job postings, companies have started putting "FDE" in the job title.

Counting the job titles of the 2,695 AI listings on AInformation's AI Job Navigator on November 2, 2026, 63 listings from 47 companies included FDE. 41 of them were posted on or after July 2026, accounting for nearly two-thirds.

Search interest is also growing: the monthly search volume for "fde 求人" measured with Google Keyword Planner grew from 0 searches in September 2025 to 1,300 searches in August 2026.

FDE has no fixed dates like effective dates or exam dates. No official statistics were found showing the number of FDEs in Japan or the total number of listings.

This article lines up the counts, salaries, and tools appearing in job postings that AInformation tallied with the official job listings of OpenAI, Anthropic, and Palantir. It answers with numbers whether you can apply even without English or extensive work experience.

How to search for FDE listings: searching job titles with 4 variations minimizes misses

How should you search for FDE listings? The answer is to search for listings whose job title includes one of "FDE", "Forward Deployed", "フォワードデプロイド", or "Field Deployed".

AInformation also searched its 2,695 AI listings using these 4 variations and found 63 listings.

Job titles for the same work vary from company to company

The job titles of the 63 listings are not standardized. Some companies write "Forward Deployed Engineer" in English, while others write it in katakana as "フォワードデプロイドエンジニア".

It was also common to append FDE after a different job title, such as "AI Solutions Engineer (FDE)", as seen at TOPPAN and CrowdWorks. Hakuhodo Technologies uses FDE as an abbreviation for Field Deployed Engineer.

Looking at related job types together reduces oversights. The same database has 10 listings with Deployment Strategist in the job title and 18 listings with Solutions Engineer.

Both are roles that often work alongside FDEs, but since their job titles do not include FDE, they are not counted in the 63.

Knowing where listings are posted also helps you find them faster. All 63 listings were on company career pages hosted on one of HRMOS Recruiting, HERP Careers, or Talentio.

The 3 foreign companies post elsewhere. Their official job boards are hosted on recruiting services: OpenAI on Ashby, Anthropic on Greenhouse, and Palantir on Lever.

FDE listings account for 63 of 2,695 AI job listings, posted by 47 companies

How many FDE listings are there in Japan? As of November 2, 2026, 63 of AInformation's 2,695 AI job listings had FDE in the job title.

That is 2.3 percent of the total, posted by 47 of 767 companies. When counted on September 28, there were 60 listings from 43 companies out of 2,643.

Here is how the count was made. The target was only the job titles (listing titles) of listings on the AI Job Navigator. Listings where FDE appears only in the body text were not included.

Note that the job DB is a snapshot of jobs posted on that day, so jobs that were posted and closed are not counted. Company names are counted with spelling variations normalized.

47 companies, from Genie with 4 postings to major firms

The company with the most postings was Genie (JAPAN AI) with 4. Finatext, Open Group, and LayerX followed with 3 each.

Those with 2 postings each were Frylu, COSOJI, Konica Minolta, Hackazouk, Degirize, overflow, and ExaWizards.

Well-known companies are included too. As of November 2, 2026, companies posting FDE jobs included Konica Minolta, TOPPAN, Hakuhodo Technologies, Preferred Networks, and BrainPad.

Raksul Group, CrowdWorks, and Kurashiru were also included. Job postings can close without notice, so when applying, please check each company's recruiting page to confirm the job is still open.

41 jobs have posting dates from July onward

When the 63 jobs were categorized by posting date on the job page, 41 were from July 2026 onward. By month, July had 9 and August had 13. September had 7, and October had 12.

The oldest was Genie's (JAPAN AI) Forward Deployed Engineer, posted on September 30, 2025. The LayerX job that was the oldest on September 28 (posted July 11, 2025) was no longer listed on November 2.

There are also figures compared against AI jobs overall. Of the 288 AI jobs posted in September 2026, 7 were FDE, and in October, 12 out of 408. However, since jobs that were posted and closed are not included, these figures do not represent how many jobs were added per month.

The median lower bound of FDE job salaries is 7 million yen, 1 million yen higher than the overall average

What are the salaries of FDE jobs? Looking at the 44 jobs that listed salaries, the median lower bound was 7 million yen and the median upper bound was 12.5 million yen.

Since the median for AI jobs overall is a lower bound of 6 million yen and an upper bound of 10.2 million yen, FDE is 1 million yen higher at the lower bound and 2.3 million yen higher at the upper bound.

We used the median rather than the average because jobs with very wide ranges are mixed in. The 44 jobs with salaries represent 70 percent of the 63. The remaining 19 had no salary listed, so their salaries are unknown.

Claude's answer for the lower bound of FDE salaries was 9 million yen, higher than the job listings

Can you learn the going rate for FDE by asking AI? On the night of September 28, 2026, I sent two questions to Claude to find out. The salaries it returned were higher than the figures AInformation counted from job listings, and the companies it named did not overlap much with the DB either.

Claude answered that the lower bound of salaries is 9-10 million yen

Regarding salaries of FDE jobs in Japan, I asked for figures as a guide to the lower and upper bounds written in job listings. Claude performed a web search and returned an answer in 27.4 seconds.

The answer was that the lower bound at domestic companies is roughly 9-10 million yen, with upper bounds exceeding 20-25 million yen. It also wrote that the median upper bound is 15 million yen.

Compared with the figures counted from job listings, Claude's answer is on the high side. Of the 39 jobs with salaries listed on September 28, the median lower bound was 7 million yen and the median upper bound was 13 million yen.

Even in the recount of 44 jobs on November 2, the lower bound was 7 million yen and the upper bound was 12.5 million yen. The sources Claude cited were three sites: Uravation, Venture-net, and Renue.

Of the 10 companies it named, jobs were found in the DB for only 3

When asked to list 10 Japanese companies posting FDE jobs, Claude returned 10 companies in 21.1 seconds.

Of those, 3 companies—LayerX, Kurashiru, and FUNDiT—were among the 43 companies posting FDE jobs in the September 28 database. All 3 are also included in the 47 companies as of November 2.

For the remaining 7 companies, no FDE jobs were found under the job titles in the database. Since they may be posted on recruiting pages that the database does not collect, the 7 companies cannot be called wrong.

Since only company names were requested, the answer did not cover whether the jobs are still open. When deciding where to apply, please open the recruiting pages yourself and check.

Claude added at the end: "OpenAI, Anthropic, Palantir, Sierra, and others are also hiring in Tokyo."

When I opened Anthropic's official job board the same day, there were no FDE positions among the 16 listings located in Tokyo. I also sent the same two questions to ChatGPT, but that night the input field did not appear, so I could not get answers.

Tools for writing code are the most frequently named

Among the most frequently named tools, both Claude Code and Cursor are tools for writing code. This suggests that FDE work involves not only listening to clients but also building and delivering working products.

Anthropic's FDE job posting also listed MCP servers, subagents, and agent skills as deliverables to clients.

FDE work sits between AI engineer (38 postings) and AI consultant (12 postings)

Is FDE an engineer or a consultant role? I classified the 63 postings using AInformation's job categories. AI engineer was the largest at 38 postings, followed by AI consultant at 12 and other AI roles at 10.

AI sales/business development had 2 postings, and AI product manager had 1. Spanning both engineering and consulting is a defining characteristic of the FDE role.

FDE work as described in job postings

Comparing how each company's postings are written reveals the substance of the work. Konica Minolta wrote that the role involves "embedding in customers' development sites" and driving the adoption of AI-driven development and solving on-site issues.

OpenAI describes it as a role deploying cutting-edge models to production together with its most important customers. Palantir writes that it is a role working directly with customers to grasp their biggest problems. The job includes designing and building systems that solve problems with data.

Work style: mostly partial remote in Tokyo

Tokyo was the work location for 59 postings. Kyoto had 2, and Kanagawa and Osaka had 1 each. For remote work, 38 postings were partial remote and 6 were fully remote.

The fully remote rate was 10 percent, unchanged from the 10 percent (262 postings) across all AI jobs. For employment type, 55 postings were full-time and 1 was a contract (gyomu itaku) position.

FDE vs. SES and consulting: 0 of 55 postings mentioned jun'inin contracts

Since FDE involves working at client sites, it can look the same as SES or consulting. The difference lies in what is being sold. Generally, SES is a contract that provides engineers' working hours to clients, often in the form of a jun'inin (quasi-mandate) contract.

Consultants focus mainly on analyzing issues and making proposals. FDE brings its own company's AI product into the client site and builds it until it works within the client's operations.

No openings welcome inexperienced candidates, but there are 5 entry points such as Associate and intern positions

Can you apply for FDE without experience? Among the 63 openings, not a single one was marked as welcoming inexperienced candidates. Across all AI job postings, 84 carry that mark, so FDE openings assume prior experience.

However, there are entry points. There were 5 openings whose job titles include Associate, Junior, or Intern.

  • GENIE (JAPAN AI) Associate FDE (welcomes recent graduates): 5 to 11.55 million yen
  • GENIE (JAPAN AI) two-day selection event on November 14 and 15 (FDE and Associate FDE): 5 to 20 million yen
  • Hackazouk Associate FDE: 5 to 7 million yen
  • LayerX Forward Deployed Engineer (Associate): no salary listed
  • LayerX FDE practical internship

The two GENIE openings list monthly salary in the job posting, converted to annual salary based on 14 months. The Rain and GenerativeX openings from September 28, and LayerX's 1Week internship, were no longer listed on November 2.

Even GENIE, which welcomes recent graduates, requires at least 1 year of practical experience

The most accessible-looking option is GENIE's Associate FDE, which welcomes recent graduates.

However, the required qualification is at least 1 year of practical experience as a software engineer, so it is not a track for complete beginners. The annual salary is 357,000 to 825,000 yen per month, which comes to 5 to 11.55 million yen over 14 months.

The job posting states that the FDE team will expand from 10 to 30 members by March 2027.

It is said to be a track for bringing in and developing engineers who will form the next core of the team early on, not just immediate contributors. The breakdown of duties is about 20% dialogue with customers and about 80% technical design and implementation.

Those without development experience should start with an internship

The option accessible to those without development experience is LayerX's practical internship. There was also a 1Week internship on September 28, but only the practical type was listed on November 2.

Please check the application requirements on the job page. The openings you can aim for by length of experience are as follows.

Years of experience are the required conditions stated in each company's job posting. Konica Minolta requires 3 or more years of team development experience using CI/CD. OpenAI's Tokyo opening requires 5 or more years of experience including customer-facing work.

OpenAI has 3 FDE openings in Tokyo, requiring both Japanese and English and 5 or more years of experience

Do foreign companies recruit FDEs in Japan? OpenAI does.

Looking at OpenAI's official job board on September 28, 2026, 25 of the 830 openings were in the Forward Deployed Engineering department. Of those, the following 3 are located in Tokyo.

  • Forward Deployed Engineer – Tokyo (posted September 16, 2025)
  • Deployment Lead (DL), FDE – Tokyo (posted April 7, 2026)
  • Manager, Forward Deployed Engineer – Tokyo (posted July 21, 2026)

One is a management position and one is a separate role called Deployment Lead. By location, FDE department openings are most numerous in New York with 7 and San Francisco with 5. Tokyo's 3 tied with Seattle for third place.

Requirements are both Japanese and English and 5 or more years of experience

The Tokyo Forward Deployed Engineer job posting requires the ability to speak and write both Japanese and English.

It also states that resumes should be submitted in English and interviews proceed in both languages. It requires 5 or more years of experience in engineering or technical implementation work, which must include customer-facing work.

The work style requires 3 days in the office per week, and the job mainly involves domestic travel. None of the 3 Tokyo openings list a salary. The San Francisco FDE opening lists an annual salary of $185,000 to $300,000, with equity granted separately.

At the September 28 rate of 157.52 yen per dollar, that is roughly 29.14 to 47.26 million yen. Since this is the US base salary range, it is not a guide for Tokyo salaries.

Anthropic has 0 FDE openings in Tokyo, while Palantir starts from 1 year of experience

Does Anthropic, the maker of Claude, recruit FDEs in Tokyo? As of September 28, 2026, the answer is "no."

The official job board has 624 openings, 16 of which are located in Tokyo. FDE is not among those 16.

There are 7 FDE openings, located in Munich, London, Paris, and the US (New York, San Francisco, Seattle).

The US openings have an annual salary of $280,000 to $320,000, which is roughly 44.11 to 50.41 million yen at 157.52 yen per dollar. The London opening is £225,000 to £255,000.

Anthropic's FDE builds production applications within customers' environments

According to the US job posting, Anthropic's FDE builds production applications using Claude within customers' systems.

The deliverables to customers listed include MCP servers, subagents, and agent skills. It requires 4 or more years of experience in customer-facing technical roles, and states that travel is about 25 percent.

There used to be an FDE job opening in Tokyo as well. However, on September 28 that page could not be opened, and it is unclear when it was closed. It has not been announced whether Tokyo will recruit again.

Palantir is recruiting an FDSE for the Japanese government

Palantir uses the job title Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE).

Of the 321 listings on the official job board, 57 are FDSE positions, and among the 3 in Tokyo there was 1 FDSE for the Japanese government. The remaining 2 are Deployment Strategist openings.

The requirements are proficiency in both English and Japanese and at least 1 year of practical experience after graduation. While the entry bar is lower than OpenAI's 5+ years, travel is higher at 25 to 75 percent. The requirements of the 3 foreign companies and the 63 domestic listings are compared in the table below.

Searches for "fde 求人" grew from 0 a year ago to 1,300 per month

How many people are looking for FDE jobs? Measured with Google Keyword Planner on September 28, 2026, monthly searches for "fde 求人" were 0 in September and October 2025.

In February 2026 it reached 260, and from May it has been moving at 1,000 to 1,300. The 3-month trend shown by Keyword Planner was also a 30 percent increase.

"fde エンジニア" has plateaued since May, while "fde 求人" is still growing

Terms that look up the job itself are searched far more often. "fde エンジニア" grew from 210 in September 2025 to 6,600 in May 2026.

From then until August it has stayed around 6,600. "フォワードデプロイドエンジニア" also grew from 110 in September 2025 and has been at 720 per month since May 2026.

People looking up the job title have plateaued since May, while people looking for jobs are still moving. This suggests people are shifting from the stage of learning what FDE is to the stage of searching for jobs.

Start preparing your application while search volume is still small

Employers are moving too. There are 41 listings posted in July or later, and Genie wrote that it is expanding its FDE team from 10 to 30 people.

The more people search, the more people see the same job postings. It is advantageous to start preparing your application while searches for "fde 求人" are still at 1,300 per month.

5 things to do now before applying for FDE jobs

Based on the numbers above, here are 5 things you can start doing today if you want to apply for FDE roles.

1. Search for jobs using 4 phrasings and save the career pages

Search for jobs using "FDE", "Forward Deployed", "フォワードデプロイド", and "Field Deployed", and save the pages of any listings that catch your interest.

Job postings close without notice. Also check closely related titles such as Deployment Strategist and Solutions Engineer.

2. Automate one task around you using Claude Code or Cursor

Claude Code appeared in 48 percent of FDE job postings and Cursor in 29 percent. Pick one task around you and build an automation with one of them until it actually runs.

If you can describe what you built and the time saved in numbers, you can put it on your resume. I summarized how to compare pricing in the article on Claude Code pricing.

3. Write about experience building things in front of customers on your resume

OpenAI requires 5+ years of experience including customer-facing work. Anthropic also requires 4+ years in customer-facing technical roles.

Whether with external customers or other internal departments, write down experiences where you listened to someone's workflow and built something for them. Using the prompt below, you can have AI help you rewrite your resume.

You are an editor supporting AI engineers in job changes. From 【My Career】 below, extract only experiences where you listened to the work of customers or other departments, built AI or systems, and ran them in real work. ・For each item, write 5 fields: "the counterpart", "the problem", "what you built", "the tools used", and "the change after it ran" ・For the change, use only numbers found in 【My Career】; if none, write "no numbers" ・Do not add experiences or results not in 【My Career】 ・Finally, write a 200-character summary of the resume for a Forward Deployed Engineer application 【My Career】 (paste your current resume here)

4. If you can use English, also look at foreign companies' openings

OpenAI's and Palantir's Tokyo openings require both Japanese and English. OpenAI also requires submitting your resume in English.

I did not count the language requirements of the 63 domestic listings this time. If you are not confident in English, check the language field of the job posting before applying.

5. Compare salary against the medians and confirm in interviews

For FDE job salaries, the median lower bound was ¥7 million and the median upper bound was ¥12.5 million. The market rates Claude gave came out higher than this, so it is safer not to take them at face value. 19 of the 63 listings had no salary listed, so ask about the range in interviews before comparing.

  • FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer, an engineer who embeds their company's AI into a client's operations until it actually runs there. Among AInformation's 2,695 AI job postings, there were 63 postings from 47 companies with FDE in the job title (as of November 2, 2026).
  • What is the difference between an FDE and SES?
  • SES is a contract that supplies a technician's working hours to a client, while FDE is a job that builds the company's own AI product until it runs at the client site. Among the 55 FDE job postings, "quasi-mandate (jun-i)" appeared in 0 postings and "SES" in 1. Words like "product" and "our own products" appeared in 46 postings.
  • How many FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) job openings are there in Japan?
  • Among AInformation's 2,695 AI job postings, there were 63 postings from 47 companies with FDE in the job title (as of November 2, 2026). This does not count the 10 Deployment Strategist postings or 18 Solutions Engineer postings that lack FDE in the title. I could not find any official statistics giving the total number of FDE job openings in Japan.
  • What is the salary of an FDE?
  • Among the 44 postings that listed annual salary, the median lower bound was 7 million yen and the median upper bound was 12.5 million yen. That is 1 million yen and 2.3 million yen higher, respectively, than the 6 million yen and 10.2 million yen for AI job postings overall. The highest lower bound was Golem's 15 to 25 million yen. Of the 63 postings, 19 did not list a salary.

  • Of the 63 FDE postings, 0 were marked as welcoming inexperienced candidates. Entry points are the 5 postings with Associate or intern in the job title. Even Geniee's Associate FDE, which welcomes recent graduates, required at least 1 year of professional experience. Those without development work experience yet will need to look for openings like LayerX's practical internship.
  • Is English required for FDE jobs?
  • It is required at foreign companies. Both OpenAI's Tokyo FDE and Palantir's Tokyo FDSE listed both Japanese and English as requirements. OpenAI also requires submitting your resume in English.
