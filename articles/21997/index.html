Japan AI Center Opens a Dedicated Desk for FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) Talent — Working On-Site, Leading AI Agents, and Driving Solutions Through to Implementation

Original title: 日本AIセンター、FDE（フォワード・デプロイド・エンジニア）人材の専門窓口を開設 ― 現場に入り、AIエージェントを率いて実装まで解き切る

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-07-31 · 4 min read

Summary

Japan AI Center Co., Ltd. (Shibuya, Tokyo, led by Haruki Ishida) has opened a dedicated corporate consultation service for FDE talent. The company argues that enterprise AI adoption often stalls at the PoC stage because organizations lack people who can embed AI into actual operations. Its FDEs work on-site, understand real business and data conditions, direct coding agents such as Claude Code, and stay engaged until systems run stably and teams can operate independently. Candidates are selected and trained from the company's own internal practice of running operations with human-AI collaboration. The service ties together its three businesses: AI training, AI talent staffing (SES), and AI agent implementation support.

Japan AI Center Co., Ltd. (Head office: Shibuya-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director: Haruki Ishida), which provides SES and AI consulting services, has launched "FDE Dedicated Desk," a consultation service for companies specializing in FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) talent. For companies whose AI adoption tends to stall at the concept or PoC (proof of concept) stage, the company provides talent who embed themselves on-site and lead coding agents to carry implementation through to adoption, end to end.

Desk page: https://ai-center.co.jp/fde.html

■ Background: AI Adoption Is Stalling on "People"

While the corporate adoption of generative AI is spreading, an increasing number of companies report that "we signed up for the tool, but no one in-house can integrate it into our operations" or "our PoC runs, but we can't move to production and adoption." AI utilization does not advance through tool contracts alone. It only moves forward when there are people who understand on-site operations and can put AI to work.

Amid this, attention is also growing in Japan for the role of "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)," which spread from the front lines of overseas AI companies. Rather than developing to a fixed specification, FDEs embed themselves in the customer's site and solve problems completely, from discovery through implementation and adoption.

■ What Is an FDE — Our Definition

1. Go to the front lines

Start from the field, not from documents, and understand the actual state of operations, systems, and data with your own eyes.

2. Lead AI agents

Delegate much of the implementation to coding agents such as Claude Code, letting people focus on design, verification, and decision-making. Even small teams can develop an order of magnitude faster.

3. Solve completely and make it stick

Don't stop at building; treat as part of the job the state where things keep running in the field and the team can operate on its own.

■ Overview of the "FDE Dedicated Desk"

- Target: Companies facing challenges in AI adoption, in-house development, or development acceleration

- Services: Assignment of FDE talent, engagement design starting from issue analysis, and hands-on support from implementation through adoption

- How to inquire: Contact via the form on the desk page (https://ai-center.co.jp/fde.html)

- Response: Handled directly by practitioners with hands-on experience working on-site at companies as FDEs. Our company operates itself with a system in which AI agents and people collaborate across management, development, hiring, and PR. We run in-house system development, daily sales communications, and corporate media article and diagram production under this system, and have publicly shared the process, including failures, on our website (https://ai-center.co.jp/insights.html).

The FDE Dedicated Desk offers companies talent selected and trained through this in-house practice, together with the same way of working.

■ Going Forward

Under the banner "From SIer to AIer," we operate three businesses: developing AI-capable talent (AI training), sending them out (AI talent), and introducing them into companies (AI agent adoption support). The FDE Dedicated Desk will operate as a touchpoint that unites these three businesses from the field side, demonstrating through real examples what AI-native SES and talent provision can look like.

■ Message from the Representative

The most common inquiries about AI adoption are "I don't know where to start" and "We can't get past the PoC." The FDE Dedicated Desk is an entry point for starting from the field, not from a proposal document. Consultations at the concept stage are welcome. Please tell us about the challenges on your front lines.
