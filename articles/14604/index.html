Adobe Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-07 · 12 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot counts 7 Adobe FDE-family postings (6 engineer, 1 management), tied for 18th among 114 employers. Roles span Marketing Intelligence, Workfront, Experience Platform, Firefly management in Bangalore, and a Senior seat in Noida, across the US, Bucharest, and India. None of the 7 are remote, versus 25.0% across the 704-posting sample. Three US seats list pay: Marketing Intelligence at $109,000–$200,700, and the Lehi Workfront and Senior San Jose seats at $159,200–$301,600. The article details per-seat requirements—5+ to 10+ years of experience, Python/SQL, Adobe Experience Cloud products, and GenAI skills—and confirms all 7 postings were still open on November 7, 2026.

Key points

  • Adobe has 7 FDE-family postings: 6 engineer seats and 1 management seat, tied 18th of 114 employers.
  • 0 of 7 postings are remote, against 25.0% across the full snapshot.
  • Marketing Intelligence asks 5+ years technical and 2+ years AI experience, paying $109,000–$200,700.
  • Lehi and Senior San Jose seats pay $159,200–$301,600 and require 8–12 or 8–15+ years.
  • The Bangalore Firefly manager seat requires 8+ years technical, 3+ years leadership, and up to 30% travel.

What Adobe calls the role

Adobe posts Marketing Intelligence, Workfront, Experience Platform, Firefly Manager, and Sr FDE titles on one Workday board. Six of the seven are engineer family; one is management. None are intern seats.

Marketing Intelligence is Forward Deployed AI Engineer, Marketing Intelligence & Consumer Insights (San Jose with Seattle and San Francisco). Workfront is Forward Deployed Engineer in Lehi. Experience Platform covers Senior Forward Deployed Engineer in San Jose, Senior Forward Deployed Engineer in Bucharest, and Senior Forward Deployed Engineer, Adobe Experience Platform in Bucharest. Firefly management is Manager, Firefly FDE in Bangalore. India also lists Sr Forward Deployed Engineer in Noida.

What the Marketing Intelligence seat asks for

Forward Deployed AI Engineer, Marketing Intelligence & Consumer Insights states 5+ years of technical experience and 2+ years building and deploying AI applications. Stack: Expertise in Python, SQL, and at least one other common language. Platform: Familiarity with Adobe's marketing technology ecosystem: Adobe Experience Platform, Adobe Analytics, GenStudio for Performance Marketing, Adobe Journey Optimizer, Workfront, etc.

US pay on that seat is $109,000 to $200,700. The full skill counts across employers are on Forward Deployed Engineer skills.

Experience Platform Senior seats

Senior Forward Deployed Engineer in San Jose and both Bucharest Senior seats state Typical background: 8–15+ years across solution architecture, software engineering, technical consulting, or customer-facing engineering roles. San Jose also states Masters or bachelor’s in computer science or any relevant field.

Platform language on the Senior seats includes AEP / RTCDP / AJO — Adobe’s core platform: data ingestion, segmentation, journey orchestration, audience activation. and Experience with Adobe Experience Cloud products (AEP, AJO, RTCDP, CJA, Target). The Bucharest texts embed the engineer with Adobe’s strategic EMEA customers: You will be embedded with Adobe’s most strategic EMEA customers.

Forward Deployed Engineer in Lehi is the Workfront customer-pod seat. It states Typical background: 8 to 12 years in software engineering, solution architecture, technical consulting, or customer-facing engineering. What matters is what that experience produced, not the title or the number of years. Stack: Strong skills in a modern language such as Java, JavaScript/TypeScript, or Python. and Experience building agentic or LLM-powered workflows in enterprise settings. Workfront framing: Adobe Workfront is the work management platform that enterprises rely on to plan, run, and measure work across thousands of people, projects, and approvals.

Manager, Firefly FDE (Bangalore)

Manager, Firefly FDE is the one management seat in the set. It states Technical Credibility – 8+ years of software engineering, AI/ML, or technical consulting experience, with recent hands-on development in full-stack, cloud, and GenAI applications. and People Leadership – More than 3 years in managing people or leading technical teams. Travel: Willingness to travel up to 30% to support customers and teams in person. That is the only Adobe seat with travel_pct_stated in the snapshot.

Firefly and GenAI lines include Adobe Platform Fluency – Strong working knowledge of Adobe Firefly APIs, Creative Cloud SDKs, GenStudio for Performance Marketing, and enterprise integration patterns. and GenAI Mastery – Direct experience with modern AI technologies, including LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta Llama, Mistral), diffusion models (Firefly, Stable Diffusion, DALL·E), RAG pipelines, vector databases, and agent frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex, AutoGen). Role framing: As a Manager, Forward Deployed AI Engineering, you will lead, mentor, and inspire a team of Forward Deployed AI Engineers (FDEs)—creative technologists who prototype, build, and launch GenAI-powered solutions and products directly with customers. No USD pay is posted. Broader management figures are on Forward Deployed Engineering manager.

Sr Forward Deployed Engineer (Noida)

Sr Forward Deployed Engineer in Noida states 10+ years of experience across solution architecture, software engineering, technical consulting, or customer-facing engineering. Degree: Bachelor’s or advanced degree in computer science or a relevant field, or equivalent practical experience. Platform: Design and validate enterprise solutions spanning AEP, AJO, Marketo, and adjacent Adobe systems. and Experience with Adobe Experience Cloud products such as AEP, AJO, Marketo, CJA, or Target is helpful. No USD pay is posted.

What this means if you are preparing for an Adobe FDE loop

  • Match the product vertical. Marketing Intelligence leans on Python, SQL, and Adobe's marketing stack. Workfront Lehi asks for modern languages plus agentic or LLM workflows. Senior AEP seats center AEP / RTCDP / AJO and Experience Cloud products.
  • Read the years line for the seat. Marketing Intelligence sits at 5+ technical / 2+ AI applications. Lehi states 8 to 12 as a typical background. Senior AEP seats state 8 to 15+. Noida asks for 10+. Manager Firefly asks for 8+ technical plus 3+ people leadership.
  • Treat remote as uncommon here. 0 of 7 are remote under the locations-index method used on this site.
  • Treat posted US bands as posted ranges only. Marketing Intelligence posts $109,000 to $200,700. Lehi and Senior San Jose post $159,200 to $301,600. Posted ranges are not offers.

Questions people ask

Does Adobe hire Forward Deployed Engineers?

Yes. Adobe had 7 FDE-family postings in FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot, tied for 18th of 114 employers and sole at 7. Six are engineer titles: Forward Deployed AI Engineer, Marketing Intelligence & Consumer Insights (San Jose, Seattle, San Francisco); Forward Deployed Engineer (Lehi Workfront); Senior Forward Deployed Engineer (San Jose); Senior Forward Deployed Engineer (Bucharest); Senior Forward Deployed Engineer, Adobe Experience Platform (Bucharest); and Sr Forward Deployed Engineer (Noida). One is management: Manager, Firefly FDE (Bangalore). On November 7, 2026, all 7 were still open on Workday CXS.

How much does an Adobe Forward Deployed Engineer make?

Three of Adobe's seven FDE-family postings list a US pay range on the November 3, 2026 snapshot. Forward Deployed AI Engineer, Marketing Intelligence lists US pay from $109,000 to $200,700 (California $138,600 to $200,700; Washington $136,600 to $197,800). Forward Deployed Engineer in Lehi and Senior Forward Deployed Engineer in San Jose each list US pay from $159,200 to $301,600 (Senior California $208,300 to $301,600). The Bucharest seats, Manager Firefly Bangalore, and Sr FDE Noida have no posted USD in the snapshot. These are annual ranges as posted. Do not treat them as OTE. Posted ranges are not offers.

What do Adobe Forward Deployed Engineer postings ask for?

Years and stack differ by seat. Marketing Intelligence asks for 5+ years of technical experience and 2+ years building and deploying AI applications, with expertise in Python, SQL, and at least one other common language, plus familiarity with Adobe's marketing technology ecosystem. Lehi Workfront states a typical background of 8 to 12 years and strong skills in Java, JavaScript/TypeScript, or Python, plus agentic or LLM-powered workflows. Senior AEP seats in San Jose and Bucharest state a typical background of 8 to 15+ years and Experience Cloud products such as AEP, AJO, RTCDP, CJA, and Target. Sr FDE Noida asks for 10+ years. Manager, Firefly FDE asks for 8+ years of software engineering, AI/ML, or technical consulting with recent GenAI hands-on work, plus more than 3 years managing people or leading technical teams, and Firefly API fluency.

Are Adobe Forward Deployed Engineer jobs remote?

Do Adobe Forward Deployed Engineer roles require a security clearance?

The seven open November 7 texts do not require a security clearance, Secret, TS/SCI, or ITAR, and they do not state a blanket U.S. Person rule. The page does not invent clearance language.
