FDE vs Solution Architect — Who Owns the Deployment Cycle

How to Become an FDE — Interview Kickstart · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

This article compares the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) with the Solution Architect across a six-phase deployment cycle. The roles overlap in discovery and solution design but diverge at the build phase: the Solution Architect produces designs and POCs, then hands off before production, while the FDE writes production code daily and owns the outcome end-to-end, from pilot through adoption and handover. The FDE's scope excludes model research and SDK/platform engineering — the job is everything in between, getting AI working inside the customer. The analysis draws on sources including Palantir, a16z, and Interview Kickstart's scan of 683 FDE job descriptions.

Key points

  • Both roles share discovery and solution design phases.
  • They diverge at Build: architect hands off, FDE ships code.
  • FDE owns pilot-to-production, adoption, and handover.
  • FDE excludes model research and SDK work — owns the middle.
  • Claims backed by a scan of 683 FDE job descriptions.

What the FDE owns — and what it doesn't

The FDE's territory is bounded on both sides. It doesn't own the model or the SDK — it owns everything in between: getting AI working inside the customer. That ownership, the production work an SA hands off, is exactly what gets an FDE hired.
