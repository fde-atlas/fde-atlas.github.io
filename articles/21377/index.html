Senior Forward Deployed Engineer | Prenuvo | Remote, Canada

Zero G Talent · 2026-09-24 · 5 min read

Summary

Prenuvo, a whole-body MRI screening company, is hiring a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer for remote work in Canada, with a base salary of CAD $140,000–$160,000 and no visa sponsorship. The role embeds with clinical, product, and operations teams to ship AI features end to end, deciding whether to build, wrap, or vet each initiative and setting validation standards for patient safety. Responsibilities include building an agentic layer (tool calling, RAG, MCP, human-in-the-loop), contributing to a shared AWS Bedrock platform, and defining evals before development. Candidates need 6+ years of product-grade software experience with production AI features, strong Python and cloud skills, and ideally healthcare or regulated-industry background.

Key points

  • Prenuvo seeks a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer, remote in Canada, CAD $140k–$160k.
  • The engineer embeds with teams and owns AI features from intake to production.
  • Key duties: build/wrap/vet decisions, agentic layer, AWS Bedrock platform, eval harness.
  • Requires 6+ years shipping production AI features, strong Python and cloud skills.
  • Healthcare or regulated-environment experience preferred; no visa sponsorship offered.

About Us

At Prenuvo, we are on a mission to flip the paradigm from reactive “sick-care” to proactive health care. Our award-winning whole body scan is fast (under 1 hour), safe (MRI has no ionizing radiation), and non-invasive (no contrast). Our unique integrated stack of optimized hardware, software, and increasingly AI, coupled with the patient-centric experience across our domestic and global clinics, have allowed us to lead the change against “we caught it too late again".

We are looking for a Senior Forward Deployed AI Engineer to embed with our clinical, product, and operations teams and ship AI features that are validated, governed, and monitored by default.

This role does not provide visa sponsorship. Applicants must be legally authorized to work in Canada at the time of hire and must not require employer sponsorship for a work visa (current or future).

Help reshape the world through proactive healthcare while working with cutting-edge technology and high performing teams with deep expertise - join us to make a difference in people’s lives!

What You’ll Do

  • Embed with one or two teams per cycle and own each AI solution end to end from intake brief to a shipped, monitored feature running in production.
  • Make the build / wrap / vet call on every initiative and set the validation bar it has to clear. This is the judgment that keeps un-vetted features away from patients.
  • Build the agentic layer: tool and function calling, RAG, MCP integrations, multi-step orchestration, and human-in-the-loop checkpoints.
  • Architect on the Prenuvo AI platform shared model serving, foundation-model contracts (AWS Bedrock), eval harness, monitoring, tool registry, prompt guardrails. No parallel stacks.
  • Define evaluation before you build: acceptance criteria, offline and online evals, and escalation triggers agreed up front rather than discovered later.
  • Run each initiative through our AI intake and risk categorization so governance is inherited rather than bolted on. You design to the standard; Regulatory, Privacy, and Legal hold formal sign-off.
  • Leave a reusable asset behind every engagement a validated template, a registered tool, or a reference architecture contributed back to the shared layer.
  • Raise the AI fluency of the teams around you through design reviews and office hours, so the next team can ship without you in the room.
  • Move us toward a world where more teams ship AI features than our AI department could ever build alone new teams starting from a template and a champion, not a blank page.

What You’ll Bring

  • Six or more years building and delivering product-grade software, including a track record of shipping AI-powered features to production LLM and/or imaging using tool calling, RAG, or agent orchestration. Notebooks and demos are not the bar. You're an experienced full-stack developer.
  • Probabilistic-software literacy: you evaluate models, reason explicitly about failure modes, and answer “what is the performance?” with a method rather than “we’ll find out in production.”
  • The judgment and standing to hold a validation bar with senior clinical, product, and engineering stakeholders and to explain the reasoning behind it in their language.
  • Product delivery instincts: you can scope a fuzzy request, say no to the wrong version of it, and translate a product need into a validated design.
  • Strong Python and practical cloud engineering and deployment (APIs, deployment, ideally AWS and Bedrock), and comfort working directly inside another team's codebase.
  • Hands-on with modern evaluation frameworks and human-in-the-loop design — you've built eval harnesses and acceptance criteria, not just consumed them.

Nice to Have:

  • Experience in regulated, clinical, or healthcare-data environments FDA, Health Canada, ISO 13485, or equivalent and comfort working with PHI.
  • AWS and Bedrock specifically, though any major cloud translates.
  • MCP, or experience building tool/function interfaces other teams consume.
  • Consulting, solutions architecture, or any form of forward-deployed experience —> you've been the outsider embedded in someone else's team before.

Our Values

First: we are Pioneers

  • Transforming healthcare requires divergent thinking, bias for action, disciplined experimentation, and consistent grit and determination to maintain momentum. This journey is as challenging as it is rewarding.

Second: we are Platform-Builders

  • We’re always building foundations that allow us to achieve tomorrow more than we did today. We never lose sight of what’s ahead – in a mindset of ownership and duty to our mission.

Above all: we are Patients

  • We could all be the next person who walks through our very doors, seeking clarity or peace of mind. We are proud of our impact on our patients’ lives, and we won’t stop till everyone can benefit from our work.
