Jiangsu Provincial Department of Industry and Information Technology — Policy Releases: Notice from the Provincial Department of Industry and Information Technology on Organizing the Solicitation of Artificial Intelligence Application Service Providers

Original title: 江苏省工业和信息化厅 政策发布 省工业和信息化厅关于组织开展人工智能应用服务商征集工作的通知

gxt.jiangsu.gov.cn · 2026-10-26 · 3 min read

Summary

Jiangsu Provincial Department of Industry and Information Technology has issued a notice to collect and register AI application service providers across the province, aiming to build a provincial resource pool. Covered services include consulting and planning, delivery and implementation, operations management, security governance, and supporting services such as training and testing. The provider profile includes a dedicated option for FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) teams, and providers with such teams must file an additional form. Applicants must be registered in Jiangsu with good credit records. Online filing runs from October 22 to November 10, 2026. The province will form a resource pool, service groups, and an FDE team database, recommend top providers to the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, explore tiered classification, and offer support such as project applications and scenario access. A summary report is due by November 15, 2027.

Key points

  • Jiangsu is collecting AI application service providers to build a provincial resource pool.
  • Services span consulting, delivery, operations, security governance, and supporting services.
  • The filing form includes a dedicated FDE team option with a supplementary form.
  • Online registration runs October 22 to November 10, 2026.
  • Top providers will be recommended to MIIT and receive tiered, ongoing support.

Full text

Industry and Information Technology Bureaus of all districts and cities, and relevant units:

To implement the decisions and deployments of the CPC Central Committee and the State Council on deeply advancing the "AI+" initiative, and to fulfill the requirements of the Notice of the General Office of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology on Launching the Special Action for Cultivating AI Application Service Providers (GongXinTingKeHan [2026] No. 414, Attachment 1), we are now organizing the province-wide collection of AI application service providers, comprehensively assessing the landscape of AI application service providers in our province, establishing a provincial-level AI application service provider resource pool, and empowering all industries in all respects. Relevant matters are hereby notified as follows:

I. Scope of Collection

AI application service providers (hereinafter referred to as "service providers") refer to enterprises or institutions that provide services such as AI solution consulting and planning, delivery and implementation, operations management, and security governance around the intelligentization needs of user organizations. Specifically, the following service types are included:

(1) Consulting and planning services. Providing services including but not limited to AI-related business strategy consulting, industry insight analysis, management system construction, and project implementation plan design.

(2) Delivery and implementation services. Providing services including but not limited to AI-related platform and tool usage, customized application development, and system integration.

(3) Operations management services. Providing services including but not limited to AI-related terminal equipment maintenance, system monitoring and inspection, software maintenance and updates, and product iteration and optimization.

(4) Security governance services. Providing services including but not limited to AI-related security protection, risk early warning, and security hardening.

(5) Supporting services. Providing services including but not limited to AI-related teaching and training, and testing and evaluation.

The Jiangsu Province AI Application Service Provider Archive (Attachment 2) includes a dedicated option for "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) teams". Service providers that have established FDE teams must also fill in the relevant supplementary form (Attachment 3).

II. Application Requirements

(1) Basic requirements. Applying units must be registered and operating normally within Jiangsu Province, have a good credit record, sound financial systems, and possess the corresponding service capabilities and professional service personnel. Requirements regarding basic qualifications, service credentials, service teams, service capabilities, and the number of service cases are specified in Attachment 5.

(2) Requirements for service consortiums. Service consortium types mainly include model-data resonance and computing-power coordination. In principle, each consortium shall be led by one service provider with no fewer than two upstream and downstream member units participating jointly. The lead unit shall fill in the Jiangsu Province AI Application Service Consortium Information Form (Attachment 6).

III. Work Arrangements

(1) Survey and mobilization. The industry and information technology bureaus of all districts and cities shall organize and mobilize eligible units in their regions to apply actively in accordance with the application requirements. In principle, units selected for national future industry innovation tasks in the AI field under the "open competition" mechanism, typical application cases, and large model registration, as well as provincial key AI enterprises, should all apply.

(2) Online submission. Applying units shall enter the dedicated collection column through the flagship store of the Jiangsu Provincial Department of Industry and Information Technology (website: https://www.jszwfw.gov.cn/col/col140127/index.html) under "Enterprise Service Items", log in using a legal entity account on the Jiangsu Government Services Network or by scanning the enterprise code (as legal representative or handler), fill in the relevant information online, and upload supporting materials. The online submission period is from October 22 to November 10, 2026.

(3) Merit-based recommendation. Our Department will establish a Jiangsu Province AI application service provider resource pool, service consortiums, and an FDE team database, and recommend high-quality service providers and service consortiums to the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology on a merit basis.

(4) Tracking and cultivation. Our Department will regularly organize the admission of AI application service providers into the pool and database, explore the classification and grading of service providers, and provide continuous support to high-quality service providers in areas such as project application, scenario opening, supply-demand matching, and publicity and promotion, while coordinating the resolution of difficulties encountered during implementation. All regions shall regularly guide relevant units to update information on the "Digital Industry and Information Technology" platform, dynamically track work progress and phased results, promptly compile and summarize work achievements such as benchmark service providers, solutions, outstanding service consortiums, and FDE teams, and submit a summary report (Attachment 8) before November 15, 2027.
