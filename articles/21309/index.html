Summary

This is a job listing on OpenWork for a Forward Deployed Engineer (Cloud domain) at Deloitte Tohmatsu in Japan. The role turns cloud strategy, migration, and Agentic AI advances into concrete results at client sites: working directly with business, IT, and frontline stakeholders, translating vague problems into implementable architectures, and iterating through short build-and-verify cycles. Core scope covers AWS, Azure, and GCP native services, spanning AI agent foundations, API/system integration, business applications, and data platforms. Engineers are also expected to distill project learnings into reusable templates, assets, and reference architectures. Requirements differ for manager-level and below, with generative AI-driven development experience mandatory. Salary ranges from 6.5 to 20 million yen including 40 hours of fixed overtime; positions are based in Tokyo or Osaka with client-site presence and remote work allowed.

Key points

  • FDE role at Deloitte Tohmatsu focused on cloud and Agentic AI delivery at client sites.
  • Covers AWS, Azure, GCP, AI agent platforms, system integration, and data infrastructure.
  • Engineers must convert vague client issues into architectures and iterate rapidly.
  • Project learnings should be returned as reusable templates and reference architectures.
  • Salary: 6.5–20 million yen; Tokyo/Osaka with client-site presence, remote allowed.

  • Job Title
  • FDE/Forward Deployed Engineer (Cloud Domain)
  • Job Description
  • This role is responsible for translating the evolution of Cloud Strategy and Migration centered on major Cloud Platforms, as well as Agentic AI technologies, into concrete results at client sites. Going beyond mere design, construction, and migration of Cloud infrastructure and systems, you will engage directly with clients' business departments, IT departments, and on-site managers, identify challenges in customer-facing operations, translate them into implementable architectures, and drive results through rapid build-and-verify cycles. With native services of AWS, Azure, GCP, and others as the core scope, you will develop a cross-functional understanding of areas such as AI Agent platforms, API/system integration, business applications, data platforms, data pipeline construction, and data utilization, and drive the creation of new value by proposing and implementing optimal Cloud utilization strategies and related solutions. You will be responsible for design, verification, and initial implementation launch toward creating client outcomes, while also being expected to organize the knowledge gained from projects into reusable templates, assets, reference architectures, and standard patterns, and give back to the organization. Specific responsibilities include: ① Deep engagement with clients' IT, services, and customer-facing operations ② Problem definition through direct dialogue with on-site managers and stakeholders capable of measuring impact ③ Solution design leveraging Cloud Platforms such as AWS, Azure, and GCP, and AI Platforms such as OpenAI and Anthropic ④ Concretizing implementation plans, including integration with core systems and data platforms ⑤ Rapid prototyping, verification, and improvement ⑥ Support for establishing solutions in projects without existing templates or assets ⑦ Development of reusable templates and standard architectures. The goal is not only to implement technology to solve problems, but to shift toward business models with future improvements in corporate value in view, providing feedback from an on-the-ground implementation perspective on the transformation scenarios envisioned by DS and on clients, and driving value creation in collaboration with FDEs in other domains as needed.
