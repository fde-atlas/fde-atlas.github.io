FDE & AI Engineer Salaries 2026: US & UK

Forward Deploying · 2026 · 1 min read

Summary

This November 2026 report compiles base salaries from 1,328 live job adverts read directly from employers' careers pages, covering five roles in the US and UK: Forward Deployed Engineer, Forward Deployed Architect, AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, and AI Engineering Leader. It is not a survey but a record of what companies currently publish. US medians range from $210k for FDEs to $280k for AI Engineering Leaders. Since only 48 of 274 UK postings disclose salaries, the UK figures are estimated bands by level. Role-specific guides break down pay by level and city, plus a market study based on 166 FDE adverts.

Key points

  • Based on 1,328 live job adverts from employer careers pages, not a survey.
  • US median base: FDE $210k, AI Engineering Leader $280k.
  • Forward Deployed Architect median is $248k; ML Engineer $244k.
  • Only 48 of 274 UK postings publish salaries, so UK figures are estimates.
  • Includes per-role guides by level and city, plus an FDE market study from 166 adverts.
