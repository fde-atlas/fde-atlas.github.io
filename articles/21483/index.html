Summary

This is a job posting from Japanese company Kurashiru for a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer. Kurashiru, known for its recipe video app with over 44 million downloads, is building Kurashiru AI OS, an AI agent platform for food industry manufacturers, wholesalers, and retailers. The role targets Japan's food distribution sector, where fax, paper, and Excel-based manual work persists amid labor shortages. The engineer would work on-site with customers to identify problems, design solutions using AI-OCR and unstructured data pipelines, build full-stack systems with coding agents, and run feedback loops post-deployment. Requirements include 5+ years of web development, LLM application experience, and AI-assisted development practice. Salary ranges from 12 to 18 million yen, based in Tokyo.

Key points

  • Job Description
  • At a new project of our company, which operates "Kurashiru," we are hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) to work on the ground in the food distribution industry (manufacturers, wholesalers, retailers), where enormous manual work still relies on fax, paper, and Excel, driving business automation using AI agents. With the evolution of Coding Agents, software implementation itself is no longer the bottleneck. The added value required of engineers has shifted to deeply understanding customers' fundamental challenges and translating them from requirements definition into concrete solution design and implementation. In this role, as a Builder with full-stack development skills, you will not only implement the product but also go into customers' sites yourself and deeply understand their operations and challenges. You will take end-to-end ownership of the entire process, from identifying challenges through prototyping, production implementation, and post-deployment adoption and improvement. Identifying on-site customer challenges Through customer interviews and on-site understanding, you will structurally organize complex business processes, identify the essential challenges, and propose concrete solutions. Solution design Based on validation results obtained from PoCs, you will design systems ready for real-world operation by combining technologies such as AI-OCR and unstructured data pipelines. Full-stack development Making maximum use of Coding Agents, you will implement all required features end-to-end, from frontend and backend to building AI agent harnesses. Operations, adoption, and continuous improvement Through guardrail design, fine-tuning, and more, you will run feedback loops from real-world operations to maximize the value delivered to customers.
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • Web application development experience (5+ years) Experience designing and implementing backends using REST APIs and databases (RDB) Experience developing applications using LLMs (OpenAI API, Claude API, etc.) Hands-on experience with AI-driven development using Claude Code/Codex, etc. (personal or professional) Smooth business communication skills in Japanese 【Preferred】 Experience in full-stack TypeScript web application development Experience handling unstructured data such as AI-OCR and image processing Experience designing and operating workflow engines Experience building and operating infrastructure on AWS (including IaC such as Terraform) Experience developing industry-specific business systems in distribution, logistics, manufacturing, etc.
