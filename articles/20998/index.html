FDE vs. Solutions Engineer: Responsibilities, Deliverables, and Role Comparison

Original title: FDE 和解决方案工程师的区别：职责、交付与岗位核对

FDECareer.work · 2026-09-21 · 2 min read

Summary

This guide compares Forward Deployed Engineers and Solutions Engineers, arguing the distinction should be judged by three factors: which project stage the role covers, what deliverables it produces, and who owns acceptance tasks like permissions, rollback, monitoring, and handover. It reviews public job postings from Dialpad, AWS, Wipro, Cohere, and Eve (verified 2026-10-13), showing the two roles overlap and no unified standard exists. The site's own classification requires explicit FDE titles or responsibilities plus signals of customer collaboration, real building, and production deployment. It also warns that AI agent keywords alone don't make a role an FDE, and that demo success isn't production delivery.

Key points

  • Compare roles by stage, deliverables, and acceptance responsibility, not pre-sales labels.
  • Employer-posted responsibilities are the basis for judging, not job titles.
  • Examples from Dialpad, AWS, Wipro, Cohere, and Eve show overlapping duties.
  • AI agent keywords alone don't qualify a role as an FDE.
  • Demo success doesn't equal production delivery; only real work belongs on resumes.

Both FDEs and Solutions Engineers may face customers; compare who writes code, which environment the work is delivered to, and who is responsible for launch acceptance. FDE roles often involve hands-on building and deployment within customer systems; Solutions Engineers may focus on evals, solution design, and pre-sales, but may also extend to launch. Employer job descriptions are the basis for judgment; don't draw the line simply by pre-sales or post-sales.

Compare responsibilities across three dimensions

Work stage: check whether the role covers requirements clarification, technical evals, integration, production launch, or operational handover. Deliverables: distinguish demos, eval conclusions, integration design, and production code. Acceptance responsibility: confirm who handles permissions, failure rollback, monitoring, releases, and handover, rather than just looking for the word AI.

Examples from employers' public job postings

Verified on 2026-10-13: Dialpad's FDE involves agent building, enterprise integration, evals, and launch stability; AWS's Senior FDE involves customer production systems, prototype-to-deployment, and operational standards; Wipro's deployment role emphasizes embedding with business teams, code building, and system integration. Cohere's Infrastructure FDE involves private cloud or on-premises deployment, collaboration with customer IT, and troubleshooting. Eve's Solutions Engineer covers sales collaboration, pilot evals, and integration design. These examples show overlapping responsibilities and do not represent a unified professional standard or hiring volume.

Keep site classification separate from career judgment

This site's strict FDE category requires that the public title or official responsibilities clearly indicate an FDE engineering role and match customer delivery; similar responsibilities, department names, or agent keywords do not automatically qualify as strict FDE. Automatic candidates under strong-relevance review still check for at least two types of signals: customer collaboration and hands-on building or production deployment. Manually confirmed public coverage also includes related AI agent, platform, research, architecture, and technical, product, and management roles; records with insufficient evidence are marked with their boundaries. Read the classification rationale for each entry rather than assuming every listed job is an FDE role.

How to present agent project experience

You can use a project clearly labeled as a teaching example to demonstrate: customer workflows and constraints, reproducible code, integration permissions, eval samples, failures and rollback, releases and handover. Only write the parts you actually did as personal experience; getting a demo running is not the same as production delivery. Frequently Asked Questions

Do Solutions Engineers write code?

Possibly. Eve's public responsibilities include integration design and pilot evals; you should still verify the actual code, environments, and delivery responsibilities, and not generalize based on the job title alone.

Does having agents in the responsibilities make it an FDE role?

Not necessarily. Agents are a technical scenario; you still need to assess customer collaboration, hands-on building, and deployment responsibility. This site's strict classification also relies on public titles and individual contributor roles.

Does clicking Apply on the original job page mean the application is complete?

No. This site only provides the original entry point; whether the application is complete is handled by the employer. We do not collect resumes or submit applications on your behalf.
