Forward Deployed Engineer jobs in Chicago (2026)

FDE Times · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Summary

This job-board roundup from FDE Times lists 16 open Forward Deployed Engineer roles in Chicago across 5 companies, sourced from official careers pages and updated daily as of 20 November 2026. Databricks leads with 7 openings spanning financial services, retail, public sector, healthcare, and media, while Palantir has 5, including Deployment Strategist and new-grad positions. Intercom, Amazon (AWS), and Stripe account for the rest. Median posted base pay ranges from $182k to $250k. For context, the article compares other markets: New York and the San Francisco Bay Area each list over 150 FDE roles, far exceeding Chicago's count.
