Reading Cognition's FDE Job Postings | 11 Renamed from Deployed Engineer and a Tokyo Opening

Original title: CognitionのFDE求人を読む|Deployed Engineerから改名した11件と東京の募集

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-10-30 · 23 min read

Summary

This article examines Cognition's job postings after the company renamed 11 positions from Deployed Engineer to Forward Deployed Engineer between October 20–22, 2026, changing titles only, not job descriptions. The editor classifies the 11 postings into three types: pre-sales-focused roles in Asia-Pacific including Tokyo, implementation-focused roles in the US, Europe, LATAM and Doha (25–50% travel), and a strategic-account Federal role. The Tokyo posting requires a STEM degree and enterprise deployment track record but lists no Japanese-language, years-of-experience, salary, or travel requirements. Only the Federal posting discloses pay: $180K–$280K plus equity and bonus. Compared with five other Tokyo FDE postings, Cognition's stands out for omitting language and experience requirements, resembling a sales engineer role.

Key points

  • Cognition renamed 11 postings to Forward Deployed Engineer in October 2026 without changing descriptions.
  • Postings split into three types: APAC pre-sales, global implementation, and Federal strategic accounts.
  • Tokyo role requires a STEM degree and deployment track record; no Japanese or experience requirements listed.
  • Only the Federal posting shows salary: $180K–$280K with equity and bonus.
  • Other Tokyo FDE postings all require Japanese; most ask for 5+ years of experience.

How do the 11 job postings differ in substance?

Even though the job titles are the same, the body text of the 11 postings falls into three types of templates. From the focus of the work, the editorial team calls the 4 Asia-Pacific postings including Tokyo "pre-sales type," the 6 postings for the US, Europe, Latin America, and Doha "implementation and adoption type," and the single Federal posting "strategic account type." Depending on the location, the work required under the same job title differs considerably.

What does this mean for job seekers and hiring companies in Japan?

The lesson for job seekers is that if you search for openings using the job title FDE, some postings will appear newly discoverable simply due to the renaming, and their substance cannot be determined from the title alone. For hiring companies, AI companies are recruiting for pre-sales and FDE combined into a single role in Japan as well, meaning people with technical sales experience will be competitors in hiring.

  • Job seekers: Even for the same job title at the same company, the body text differs by location. At Cognition, Tokyo is pre-sales type, the US is deployment-and-adoption type, and Federal is strategic-account type. Before applying, compare the body text for each location
  • Job seekers: For the Tokyo posting, confirm early in the selection process any Japanese-language requirements, years of experience, compensation, and travel that are not written in the posting. What is not written does not mean there is no requirement
  • Job seekers: Cognition's Tokyo posting requires a track record of enterprise deployments in a sales (GTM) or FDE role. People with pre-sales or sales engineering experience fit easily, and rather than years of development experience, what is tested is whether you can concretely describe a track record of successful deployments
  • Hiring companies: Simply changing the job title on a posting to FDE does not convey the substance of the work. Writing at the top whether the work is pre-sales or post-deployment makes it easier for applicants to judge whether the role suits them
  • Hiring companies: In Japan, Cognition is simultaneously recruiting for FDE, partner-facing, and sales roles. For companies wanting to hire people with SaaS pre-sales or customer engineering experience, foreign AI companies are competitors in hiring

Summary

Cognition's FDE postings had only the job title changed in October 2026, with the substance of the work divided into three types by location. The key points are as follows.

  • Between October 20 and 22, 2026, Cognition changed the job title of 11 postings from Deployed Engineer to Forward Deployed Engineer. It did not change the body text, and as of now 10 of the 11 still begin with "Deployed Engineers (DEs)"
  • The 11 postings fall into three types. The 4 Asia-Pacific postings including Tokyo are pre-sales type, the 6 postings for the US, Europe, Latin America, and Doha are deployment-and-adoption type (25-50% travel), and the 1 Federal posting is strategic-account type
  • The Tokyo posting requires a STEM degree and a track record of deployments in a sales or FDE role. There is no mention of Japanese language, years of experience, compensation, or travel

Frequently asked questions

Q: Are Cognition's Forward Deployed Engineer and Deployed Engineer different jobs?

They are the same job. Between October 20 and 22, 2026, Cognition changed the job title of 11 postings from Deployed Engineer to Forward Deployed Engineer, but did not change the posting IDs or the job description text. Even after the renaming, 10 of the 11 postings still begin with the term "Deployed Engineers (DEs)".

Q: Does Cognition's Tokyo FDE posting have a Japanese-language requirement?

As of October 30, 2026, the posting does not list a Japanese-language requirement. While postings for Latin America and Southern Europe at the same company explicitly require fluency in Spanish or Portuguese, the Japan and Korea postings contain no language requirements. What is not written does not mean there is no requirement, so it is safest to confirm early in the selection process.

Q: What is Cognition's FDE salary?

As of October 30, 2026, the only FDE posting listing compensation is "Forward Deployed Engineer - Federal" for the US government team, with the range shown in the applicant tracking system as $180,000-$280,000 plus equity and bonus. The Tokyo posting lists no compensation. The Applied AI Engineer in the same department is $180,000-$225,000.

Q: What kind of background suits Cognition's Tokyo FDE posting?

The job posting requires a STEM degree and a track record of successfully driving enterprise deployments in a sales or FDE role, while listing experience selling technical products or working as a software engineer as preferred qualifications. Starting from pre-sales demos and pilots, the role can be read as being close to SaaS/cloud presales or sales engineering experience.

What is a Partner Deployed Engineer?

A technical role at Cognition that serves major system integrators (GSIs) rather than end-customer companies. It is responsible for technical training of partners, joint pilots, and building reusable solutions and reference designs using Devin. As of October 30, 2026, there are four openings (US, India, Tokyo, and Europe, including one in Tokyo), with 25-50% travel required. The job title has not been renamed.
