Summary

This page is a case library from fdechina.ai collecting articles on forward deployed engineering practice. It covers AWS's $1 billion FDE organization embedding engineers in 45-day sprints with customers, Microsoft's follow-up Frontier Company with $2.5 billion and 6,000 engineers, and multiple pieces analyzing Palantir's FDE model, ontology engineering, and customer cases. The recurring argument: the bottleneck in enterprise AI adoption is no longer the models but organization, data architecture, and digital-twin-like ontologies, with practical guides such as starting ontology modeling on a single sheet of paper.

Key points

  • AWS committed $1 billion to an FDE organization using 45-day customer sprints.
  • Microsoft launched Frontier Company days later with $2.5 billion and 6,000 engineers.
  • Several articles dissect Palantir's FDE model and ontology engineering.
  • Authors argue enterprise AI moats lie in digital twins, not foundation models.
  • Practical guides include lightweight ontology modeling to start AI projects.
