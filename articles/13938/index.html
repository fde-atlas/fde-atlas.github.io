Sunway World Releases FDE Training Materials

Original title: 三维天地发布FDE培训教材

Summary

Chinese software company 三维天地 has released a training textbook on Forward Deployed Engineers (FDE), titled "FDE概念、技术与应用——解锁AI新职业". It argues the AI industry faces a gap between rapidly growing model capability and slow enterprise adoption, with many projects stalling at proof-of-concept. The book contends that success depends on engineering and organizational capability rather than parameter scale, defines the FDE as a hybrid role closing the loop between model capability and business value, and proposes a three-dimensional competency model covering technology, engineering, and business, with learning paths for universities and corporate training.

  • It argues engineering and organizational skills, not model size, determine AI success.

  • The book targets university teaching, corporate training, and upcoming FDE programs.

Full text

China Securities Journal · CSJ.com

CSJ.com News (Reporter Wang Jiafei) Recently, SunWay 3D released the training textbook "FDE Concepts, Technology and Applications — Unlocking the New AI Profession," which it organized and compiled. Centered on "model application engineering" and featuring the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) as the core role, the textbook systematically answers an industry-level question: how can AI move from "being able to demo" to "being able to create value."

The contrast in today's AI industry is increasingly stark: while model capabilities are growing exponentially, enterprises' ability to deploy them is climbing slowly. A large number of projects stall at the proof-of-concept stage, and only a few truly integrate into business processes and continuously create measurable value. The textbook summarizes this phenomenon as the AI industry's "scissors gap" — the chasm between excess capability and lagging deployment is becoming the biggest bottleneck for scaling AI applications.

According to the textbook, what determines the success or failure of AI applications is not parameter scale, but engineering and organizational capability. Whether the problem is precisely defined, whether the system can be reliably integrated, and whether quality can be sustainably verified — these dimensions are the key to whether AI model applications can truly be deployed.

What the textbook focuses on is whether the system can run long-term and create value, and whether it becomes stronger with use through iteration — not whether the model's answers look good or whether it can go live in one shot. Going live is only the starting point; continuously delivering value is the goal.

For the FDE, the textbook offers this definition: a hybrid engineering role that engineers the capabilities of foundation models into real business scenarios and closes the value-verification loop between "model capability and business value." Its core mission is to bridge the "last mile" between model capability and business value.

Unlike traditional delivery engineers, the FDE's key performance indicator is not "system launch" but "value realization." A complete FDE value loop includes: discovering scenarios — designing solutions — rapid validation — deployment — collecting feedback — continuous optimization — quantifying value — scaling and replication. Without quantified proof of value, AI projects can hardly secure sustained resource investment and organizational recognition.

To this end, the textbook builds a three-dimensional FDE capability model — technology, engineering, and business — and provides a reusable delivery methodology. It also includes learning paths and practical guides for different audiences, making it suitable both for undergraduate and graduate teaching and as a systematic textbook for corporate training and team capability upgrades. The value of the FDE lies precisely in turning "uncertainty" into verifiable, replicable, certain value.

The idea that "everyone can be an FDE" does not require everyone to write code; rather, it hopes that everyone in their own role can transform technical capability into business growth and user value. SunWay 3D will use the book as the core textbook for its upcoming FDE practical training programs, delivering more hybrid talent who understand technology, engineering, and business to the industry through training, and will strengthen cooperation with industry partners to jointly advance the deployment of AI technology in real business scenarios and the creation of value.
