Summary

A roundup of Analytics India Magazine's IT services coverage on how AI is reshaping Indian IT. Boutique AI-native consultancies are emerging as fast, small-scale rivals that move projects from concept to production in days. Meanwhile, earnings from Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech, and Tech Mahindra show AI revenue remains a small share and growth is muted, even as AI deflates pricing on existing contracts. Firms are responding through acquisitions, offshore hubs in Mexico, and new AI-specific disclosures.

Key points

  • Boutique AI consultancies promise production-ready AI projects in days, not months
  • Infosys cut FY27 guidance to 1%-2% despite $5.2 billion in large deals
  • Wipro's large-deal bookings fell 58.6% sequentially amid its AI push
  • HCLTech's raised guidance relies on AI and one acquisition for ~70% of growth
  • Mphasis argues domain context, not AI models, determines enterprise value
