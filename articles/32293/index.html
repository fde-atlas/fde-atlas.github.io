European FDE Firms vs US Firms With European Offices

Reveneau · 2026-10-21 · 12 min read

Summary

This editorial sorts seventeen firms offering forward deployed engineers into three groups: twelve based in Europe, three US firms naming European offices (Grid Dynamics, Distyl AI, Tribe AI), and two North American firms with none (Reveneau, Fluxon). National registers in Poland, France and the UK confirm addresses for seven European firms, while the US firms' European offices rest on their own websites alone. Notably, Tribe AI's entry in Anthropic's partner directory lists only the United States despite its site naming Lisbon. The piece closes with a buyer's checklist: contracting entity, registration, engineer locations, travel frequency, working languages, and data handling.

Key points

  • Twelve of seventeen firms are based in Europe; seven are register-verified.
  • Grid Dynamics lists sites in nine European countries; Distyl and Tribe name cities only.
  • Tribe AI's Anthropic directory regions list only the US, not Lisbon.
  • Reveneau and Fluxon name no European office; Fluxon hires remotely in Poland and Portugal.
  • Salesforce opened a Madrid office with FDEs and announced $1 billion for Spain.

The seventeen firms in this guide fall into three groups. Twelve are based in Europe. For seven of them a national company register confirms the address, and for Brdge it lists the company named in its footer. Three are US firms that name an office in Europe: Grid Dynamics, Distyl AI and Tribe AI. Two, Reveneau and Fluxon, are based in North America and name no European office. This page shows what separates the groups in facts a buyer can check: where the company is registered, where its engineers sit, which time zone and language they work in, and what each firm told Anthropic's directory about its offices.

Key takeaways

  • Twelve firms are based in Europe. Company registers in Poland, France and the UK confirm the addresses of seven of them and list the company behind Brdge.
  • Grid Dynamics lists sites in nine European countries. Distyl AI names London, and Tribe AI names Lisbon, both by city name only.
  • Reveneau lists offices in San Francisco and Vancouver. Fluxon lists open roles in Poland and Portugal. Neither names a European office.
  • The office regions in Anthropic's directory are written by each partner. Tribe AI's list only the United States, while its own site names Lisbon.

The seventeen firms in this guide use the words forward deployed, or describe engineers placed inside a client's team, on their own pages, in job ads or in directory text. They differ in where the company is based. This page sorts them into three groups and shows what a buyer can check about each one. Every page used here was read on 21 October 2026.

The group tells you where the company is based and registered, and which country's courts and rules apply to the company itself. Ask each firm where its engineers would work, and which company will sign your contract, because a firm can sign through a company in another country.

Firms based in North America

Reveneau

Reveneau's offices are at 435 Utah Street in San Francisco and on Floor 33, 909 Mainland Street, in Vancouver. It lists no office in Europe, so a European buyer should agree in writing on the hours its engineers will keep and how often they will travel. Reveneau offers forward deployed engineers. By its own account, AI writes 100 percent of its code, and every change has to pass a large eval suite before release. Reveneau says the suite is a set of automated tests written from the specification, and that Jev grades it and makes it run ten times faster than its previous grader did. It says it uses AI in place of hiring more engineers and puts that saving into its clients' prices, and that it takes responsibility for the whole project through production and after release. Anthropic's directory did not list Reveneau on 20 October 2026, when all 321 entries were searched [1].

Fluxon

Forbes gives Fluxon's headquarters as San Francisco and its staff as 200 employees, as of March 2026 [15]. Fluxon's own careers page says "300+ builders", says the company is 100 percent remote, and listed roles located in Poland and Portugal on 21 October 2026 [14]. No Fluxon page read for this guide names a European office. Its AI development page says its work is "Forward-deployed by default."

Firms based in Europe

A register entry is independent of the firm, so it is stronger evidence than the firm's own site. This guide opened registers in Poland, France and the UK.

Devoteam was taken off the Paris stock exchange in 2021 by its founding shareholders, with KKR, a US private equity firm, funding a major part of it, as Consultancy.eu reported in 2023. European FDE firms owned by larger companies explains both.

US firms that name a European office

Grid Dynamics

Grid Dynamics' contact page gives its global headquarters as San Ramon, California. It also labels an address at Oberallmendstrasse 18, Zug, Switzerland, as "Headquarter", and lists sites in the UK, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Poland, Germany, Romania, Serbia, Ukraine and Moldova [9]. Street addresses appear for London, Wormerveer in the Netherlands, Zug, Warsaw and Dresden, among others. Its shares trade on the Nasdaq exchange in New York.

Distyl AI

Distyl AI's about page lists New York, San Francisco and London as its locations, with no street address [10]. Crunchbase News called Distyl a San Francisco-based startup in September 2025 [11]. This guide found no other source for its London office.

Tribe AI

Tribe AI's site footer lists offices in New York, San Francisco and Lisbon, with no street address [12]. The office regions Tribe gave in Anthropic's directory list only the United States [1]. TechCrunch called it a Brooklyn-based startup in July 2024 and reported that it builds client projects with a network of over 500 contractors [13]. So the Lisbon office comes from Tribe's own footer alone.

Language

Most of the pages read for this guide are in English. Some offers and job ads are in other languages. SFEIR's forward deployed offer page is written in French [16]. Polynom's Forward Deployed AI Engineer job ad is in French. accilium advertises a "Forward Deployed Engineer with German" role in Bucharest. If your staff work in a language other than English, ask each firm which languages the engineers on your project speak at work.

Software companies are placing their own FDEs in Europe too

The firms in this guide sell engineers to any client. Companies that sell software also hire forward deployed engineers in Europe, to work with their own customers. Computing.es reported on 20 October 2026 that Salesforce had opened a five-floor office on the Paseo de la Castellana in Madrid, at the same time as it announced an investment of $1,000 million in Spain over the next five years. It reported that the office will house an AI implementation team of Forward Deployed Engineers who will work directly with clients on AI agent projects [17]. If you already use a software vendor with such a team, ask what it will do for you before you hire an outside firm. The guide to forward deployed engineering compares the ways to get this help.

Which group fits which buyer

If you want a firm whose code is written by AI and tested against the specification before each release, by its own account, and you can work with a team in North America, Reveneau describes that method. If you want a remote team that joins your daily meetings, Fluxon describes that offer and hires in Poland and Portugal. If your team must meet the firm's leaders in person in Europe, start with the twelve firms based in Europe, or ask the three US firms who works at their European sites. If you want a firm whose address a European register confirms, choose among Netguru, 10Clouds, STX Next, Devoteam, SFEIR, Polynom and Faculty.

Common questions

How does a European FDE firm differ from a US firm with a European office?

The difference is where the company is based and registered. Twelve firms in this guide are based in Europe, and for seven of them a national register confirms the address. The three US firms name their European offices on their own pages only. Ask any firm where the engineers on your project would live and work, and which company would sign.

Which US forward deployed engineering firms name an office in Europe?

Three firms in this guide: Grid Dynamics, which lists sites in nine European countries; Distyl AI, which names London; and Tribe AI, which names Lisbon. Distyl and Tribe give city names only. Grid Dynamics gives street addresses for several sites, including London, Warsaw, Dresden and Zug. All three facts come from the firms' own pages, read on 21 October 2026.

Is Grid Dynamics headquartered in the US or in Switzerland?

Its contact page gives its global headquarters as San Ramon, California, and also labels an address at Oberallmendstrasse 18, Zug, Switzerland, as Headquarter. Its homepage footer gives San Ramon. Grid Dynamics' shares trade on the Nasdaq exchange. If the legal entity matters for your contract, ask Grid Dynamics which company would sign it.

Which company registers confirm where European FDE firms are based?

This guide used three. The Polish National Court Register confirms Netguru, 10Clouds and STX Next. The French company register confirms Devoteam, SFEIR and Polynom. Companies House in the UK confirms Faculty and the company named in Brdge's footer, Iden Ventures Ltd. No register was opened for accilium, Enjins, Twistag or Netlight, so their addresses come from their own sites.

What do the office regions in Anthropic's partner directory show?

Each partner fills in its own regions under a filter called Office Locations (On-Site Services), so the regions are the firm's own statement. On 21 October 2026, 140 of the 321 entries ticked at least one European region. SFEIR ticked France and Benelux. Netlight ticked five regions, including the Nordics. Tribe AI ticked only the United States.

Does Tribe AI's directory entry mention its Lisbon office?

No. On 21 October 2026, the office regions Tribe AI gave in Anthropic's directory listed only the United States. Its own site footer lists offices in New York, San Francisco and Lisbon, with no street address. TechCrunch called Tribe a Brooklyn-based startup in 2024. If a Lisbon team matters to you, ask Tribe for the address and the names of the people there.

Which FDE firms publish their offer in French or German?

SFEIR's forward deployed offer page is written in French, and Polynom's Forward Deployed AI Engineer job ad is in French. accilium advertises a Forward Deployed Engineer with German role in Bucharest. The other firms publish their main pages in English. Ask each firm which language the engineers on your project would use with your staff.

Is Salesforce hiring forward deployed engineers in Spain?

Computing.es reported on 20 October 2026 that Salesforce opened an office in Madrid that will house an AI implementation team of Forward Deployed Engineers working directly with clients on AI agent projects. The report says Salesforce announced, at the same time, an investment of $1,000 million in Spain over the next five years. Salesforce sells software, so that team works for its own customers.

Which firms in this guide name no office in Europe at all?

Reveneau and Fluxon. Reveneau's offices are in San Francisco and Vancouver. Fluxon's careers page lists open roles located in Poland and Portugal and says the company is fully remote, but no Fluxon page read for this guide names a European office. Both firms sell engineers who work inside a client's team, so ask each about hours and travel.

Should a European company hire a US firm for forward deployed work?

It depends on what you need to check. A European register can confirm a European firm's address, while the US firms' European offices come from their own pages. A US firm may work across a time difference. Write down your needs for hours, travel, language and where data is kept, and ask every firm on your shortlist the same questions.
