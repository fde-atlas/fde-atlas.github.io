Modern Software & AI Engineering Course

Scaler · 2026-04-21 · 6 min read

Summary

This is a promotional page for Scaler Academy's Software & AI Engineering program. Its core pitch is an AI-led curriculum built on a Prompt → Review → Own workflow, where learners use AI tools from day one while validating outputs and taking ownership of what ships. The program targets roles like AI Backend, Front-end, Fullstack, and Forward Deployment Engineer. Projects are interview-ready and business-oriented, including an AI weather app, an AI-enhanced Kanban board, and a BookMyShow-style booking system. Mentors are practicing engineers, and the program includes an in-house coding platform, AI mock interviews, career support, and lifetime curriculum updates. Pricing starts at ₹3,99,000 with no-cost EMI. The page cites US Bureau of Labor Statistics data projecting ~25% software job growth from 2021 to 2031 and showcases alumni role transitions with salary figures.

Software development is the foundation of every technology we rely on from the banking app on your phone to the e-commerce platform processing millions of orders, to the AI systems reshaping how we work. Every one of these products is built by software engineers, and the demand for skilled engineers continues to grow faster than any other profession.

Scaler Academy: Software and AI Engineering is a structured, industry-led programme that takes you from programming fundamentals to production-grade software engineering, system design, and AI integration. Whether you are a working professional looking to break into tech, an engineer targeting senior roles, or a career-switcher building from scratch, this programme gives you the skills, mentorship, and portfolio to compete for the roles that matter.

Software engineering jobs are projected to grow by approximately 25 per cent from 2021 to 2031 roughly three times the average for all occupations (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics). Analyst reports show the global developer population is expected to grow from 18.9 million in 2019 to 45 million by 2030, yet supply remains far behind demand. Software engineers in India now regularly earn 20–40 lakhs per annum at top product companies, with senior engineers, architects, and AI specialists commanding significantly more. The opportunity has never been larger, and the window to enter at a premium is now.
