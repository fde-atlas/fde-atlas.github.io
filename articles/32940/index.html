FDE at Product Companies in India: Role & Pay (2026)

FDE India · 2026-10-27 · 9 min read

Summary

This guide explains the Forward Deployed Engineer role at product companies: deploying the company's own product in customer environments and feeding learnings back to engineering. It cites 2026 investments by OpenAI ($4B), Microsoft ($2.5B, ~6,000 engineers), Anthropic ($1.5B), AWS ($1B) and Google Cloud ($750M). Of 71 employers on Neurapath's October 2026 tracker, 48 were product or AI companies. Indian pay ranges from ₹9–12 LPA entry level to ₹50–80+ LPA senior. It contrasts product-company FDEs with IT services forward-deployed teams and offers practical entry advice.

At a product company, a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) has one job: make the company’s own product work inside a customer’s real systems, then carry what they learn back to the people who build the product. You are part engineer, part consultant and part product scout, and you are judged on whether the customer actually gets value from the product, not on how much code you shipped.

That is different from the forward-deployed teams now forming at Indian IT services firms, and the difference matters for your career. This guide explains how the role works at product companies, who hires for it in India, what it pays and how to get in. If you are new to the term, start with what a Forward Deployed Engineer does.

Why product companies need FDEs at all

Complex products rarely “just plug in”. Palantir created the role because its software was too complex for customers to set up alone, so it sent engineers to sit with customers and configure the platform for them, according to Gregor Ojstersek’s Engineering Leadership newsletter. Palantir’s FDEs focus on enabling many capabilities for a single customer, rather than building one capability for many customers.

AI has made that gap wider. Analytics India Magazine reports that the usual blockers to enterprise AI are poor data quality, legacy systems, governance and workflow redesign, not the model itself. A product company can have the best model and still lose the deal if nobody connects it to the customer’s data.

So in 2026, product companies are building FDE organisations on a scale nobody expected, as Analytics India Magazine reported in July:

Announced 2026 commitments. Microsoft’s unit is staffed by about 6,000 engineers, per Analytics India Magazine.

Salesforce is moving the same way. Its UK solutions engineering head said at Dreamforce 2026 that the company would double its UK FDE capacity to about 95 engineers, ITPro reported, with FDE openings across Agentforce and Data 360.

How the role works: the product-company FDE loop

Most product-company FDE work follows the same loop, repeated with each customer.

  • Scope the problem. Map the customer’s systems, data flows and goals, and agree on a use case tied to a business result. Salesforce describes the FDE’s purpose as helping customers “get value from technology faster”, per the Engineering Leadership newsletter, and its UK head said the aim is to build agents that “link directly to business value”.
  • Build on the platform. Write the integrations, data pipelines, custom apps and AI workflows that make the product fit. Databricks’s Bengaluru listing describes building and productionising customer data and AI solutions on its platform, including reference architectures and model integration.
  • Deploy and stabilise. Take it to production inside the customer’s environment, with their security and engineering teams. OpenAI’s FDE role includes deploying models on customer infrastructure, according to the same newsletter.
  • Feed the product. Send what broke, what was missing and what customers asked for back to engineering. Databricks says its FDEs give product feedback to engineering and support, and that the work can “influence the Databricks product roadmap”. Internal Salesforce FDEs test features in live customer environments to help improve the product.
  • Make it reusable. Turn one customer’s solution into accelerators, templates and best practices the next FDE can use. Databricks lists creating reusable accelerators and frameworks as part of the job.

Step 4 is what separates a product-company FDE from a consultant. PostHog describes how OpenAI’s FDEs worked with a call-centre automation customer, built evaluations, and fed the findings to the research team; the improvements later shipped in OpenAI’s Realtime API. Constellation Research’s Holger Mueller makes the same point in Analytics India Magazine: consultants can only customise and extend a product, while FDEs can finish the product during an engagement and feed learnings back into development.

Product company vs IT services: how the FDE job differs

Indian IT firms are building forward-deployed teams too. TCS plans to move at least 1% of its employees into a new operating model built on small, multi-skilled squads, and its COO said the approach differs from the one popularised by AI-native software firms, per Analytics India Magazine. Here is how the two compare:

FDE at a product company Forward-deployed team at an IT services firm
What you deploy Your own company’s platform (Databricks, Salesforce, OpenAI models…) Whatever mix of vendors’ tools the client uses
Why the role exists Drive adoption and usage of the product; prove it works in real settings Solve a specific client problem, often under a contract
Feedback loop Direct line to the product and engineering teams Mostly to the client and the delivery team
Typical scope Close to the model: solution design, strategic use cases Large-scale integration, governance, managed operations
Travel and customer time High; Databricks lists about 20% travel Varies by client and project

Gartner’s Arun Chandrasekaran expects AI vendors to keep the high-value work close to the model, while system integrators handle large transformation, integration and managed operations, according to Analytics India Magazine. Neither path is “better”. They build different muscles: product depth and roadmap influence on one side, multi-vendor delivery and domain breadth on the other.

What product companies expect you to own (KRAs)

Product-company listings rarely publish hard KPIs. Databricks’s senior FDE listing names none, but it stresses “measurable outcomes” and customers’ “successful understanding, evaluation and adoption” of the platform. Reading across the listings and company statements above, the recurring areas of ownership are:

  • Customer adoption: the customer actually uses the product in production, not just in a pilot.
  • Time to value: how fast the customer gets a working result (Salesforce’s “get value from technology faster”).
  • Delivery against scope: Databricks asks for experience managing scope, timelines and measurable outcomes.
  • Product feedback: clear, repeatable issues and feature requests passed to engineering.
  • Reusable assets: accelerators and patterns other teams can reuse.

The requirements are demanding. Databricks’s senior role in Bengaluru asks for 6+ years in data engineering, AI or analytics, Python, Scala or TypeScript, working knowledge of two or more clouds, deep Apache Spark experience, CI/CD and a Databricks certification. How to move into a product-company FDE role

  • Pick a platform and go deep. Product companies hire for their own stack. If you target Databricks, learn Spark and get certified; if you target Salesforce, learn Agentforce and Data Cloud.
  • Build one deployed project on that platform. Connect it to a messy, real data source, ship it, and write up what broke and how you fixed it. That write-up is your best interview story.
  • Practise the feedback habit. For every project, note three things the product should do better. Interviewers at product companies look for engineers who think about the product, not just the ticket.
  • Show customer-facing proof. Demos, client calls, on-call ownership or internal “customer” teams all count. Put them on your resume with outcomes.
  • Target the right level. Mid-level (2–5 years) listings are the most common on Neurapath’s tracker; senior platform roles like Databricks’s ask for 6+ years.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an FDE at a product company and at an IT services company?

At a product company, the FDE deploys the company's own platform and feeds customer learnings back into that product. At an IT services firm, forward-deployed teams solve specific client problems across many vendors' tools, usually under a client contract. Analysts quoted by Analytics India Magazine expect AI vendors to keep work close to the model, while system integrators handle large-scale integration and managed operations.

Do FDEs at product companies write production code?

Yes. Databricks's Bengaluru FDE listing asks for production data and AI architectures, coding in Python, Scala or TypeScript, and CI/CD experience, and descriptions of OpenAI's FDE role mention building full-stack solutions and production AI systems.

Which product companies hire FDEs in India?

Neurapath's October 2026 tracker lists FDE or related roles in India at companies including Databricks, Google, Cursor, OpenAI, Notion, MongoDB, DevRev, HighRadius, Adobe and Sarvam AI, plus many Indian startups. Check each company's careers page for current openings.

How much experience do product companies ask for in FDE roles?

It varies by level. Databricks's senior FDE role in Bengaluru asks for 6+ years, while HighRadius lists entry, mid and senior FDE roles in Hyderabad. Most product-company listings on Neurapath's tracker are tagged mid (2–5 years) or senior (5+ years).
