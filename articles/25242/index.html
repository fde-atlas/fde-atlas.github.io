Forward Deployed Engineer | Aravolta | Remote, US

Zero G Talent · 2026-04-10 · 1 min read

Summary

Aravolta, a YC-backed startup building software to monitor, control, and optimize data centers, is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer. The role involves on-site, embedded deployments at customer data centers, building custom integrations and automation workflows, debugging live systems, and feeding customer requirements back into the core product. The company stresses this is not a support role: candidates write production code and influence the roadmap. Requirements include 3+ years of software engineering experience, strong debugging skills, networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, DNS, VLANs, firewalls), client-facing experience, and willingness to travel 30–50% of the time.

Key points

  • Aravolta builds a unified platform for data center monitoring and control, backed by YC.
  • The FDE deploys the product on-site and writes production code, not just support work.
  • Requires 3+ years of software engineering experience and networking fundamentals.
  • The role includes building custom integrations and translating customer needs into feature requests.
  • Travel to customer sites can reach 30–50% of the time.

What you need

About Aravolta

Aravolta is the operating system for modern data centers. We build software that monitors, controls, and optimizes critical infrastructure - replacing fragmented legacy tools with a single platform that deploys in days, not months. Backed by YC and many more, we work with data center operators running some of the most demanding workloads in the world and complex critical environments.

About the Role

We're looking for a Forward Deployed Engineer to work directly with our customers - on-site and embedded - to deploy Aravolta in complex data center environments. You'll be the technical bridge between our product and our customers' operational reality, solving integration challenges, customizing workflows, and making sure the Aravolta experience is the best in the data center industry.

This isn't a support role. You'll write production code, debug live systems, and shape our product roadmap. If you thrive on solving hard problems in high-stakes environments and want to see your work powering critical infrastructure, this role is for you.

What You'll Do

  • Deploy and configure Aravolta at customer sites, working hands-on with their infrastructure and operations teams
  • Build custom integrations, data pipelines, and automation workflows tailored to each customer's environment
  • Troubleshoot complex issues across networking, device communication, and telemetry collection
  • Translate customer requirements into actionable feature requests and contribute directly to our core product
  • Develop internal tooling, scripts, and documentation to accelerate future deployments

What We're Looking For

Preferred:

  • 3+ years of software engineering experience
  • High agency, high ownership
  • Strong debugging and problem-solving skills - you're comfortable diving into unfamiliar systems
  • Experience working directly with customers or in client-facing technical roles
  • Solid understanding of networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, DNS, VLANs, firewalls)
  • Ability to work independently in fast-paced, ambiguous environments
  • Willingness to travel to customer sites (up to 30–50%)
