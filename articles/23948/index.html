    What is FDE? Explaining the Differences from Consulting and System Development [1st FDE Online Seminar]

    FDE（Forward Deployed Engineer）とは何か、コンサルティング・システム開発との違いを、役割・成果物・進め方の観点で解説したオンラインセミナーの録画です。
    • FDE is a role proposed by Palantir. FDEs embed themselves in the customer's field and take end-to-end responsibility for everything from problem discovery and requirements definition to prototyping and establishing adoption on-site.
    • The difference from software engineers and outsourcing lies in the starting point and the deliverables. FDEs go out to understand the customer's problems themselves, and their goal is not delivery but "a state where the solution is being used and producing results."
    • Consultants are responsible up to defining problems and proposing improvements. FDEs go further, working together through "building" and "getting it used."
    • Choose based on the situation: FDE when problems are ambiguous and results matter, SI when what you want to build is clear, and consulting when proposals alone are enough. After requirements stabilize, it's also possible to transition to SI-style maintenance and operations.
    • Common failures when choosing a business process to start with are three: starting from the tool, covering too broad a scope, and not talking with the field.
    • Narrow down the first business process to tackle using three criteria: the pain points are concrete, data is involved, and change can be measured in a short period.
    • As a first step for tomorrow, we recommend picking one recent issue and summarizing what happened in the field, who was involved, and how long it took to resolve.
