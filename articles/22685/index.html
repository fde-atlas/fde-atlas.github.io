Summary

This is a personal profile page of Hyde Huang, an enterprise software consultant and full-stack developer. He offers four services: custom software development for small and mid-sized businesses using Java/React/Node.js, AI integration for existing CRM and ERP systems (lead scoring, inventory forecasting, customer service automation), delivery in a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) model where he embeds with client teams from requirements diagnosis through operations, and managed operation of foreign-trade independent websites including SEO and Google Ads. The page also outlines his career path from junior programmer to senior engineer, product manager, project manager, and startup co-founder, plus a checklist of information prospective clients should prepare before engagement.

Key points

  • Author Hyde Huang is an enterprise software consultant and full-stack developer.
  • Services include custom development, AI upgrades, FDE-model delivery, and site operations.
  • FDE model means embedding in client teams across the full delivery lifecycle.
  • AI upgrade examples: lead scoring, inventory forecasting, service automation.
  • Career spans programmer, senior engineer, product manager, and co-founder roles.
