Author: Lu Hanzhi Editor: Li Na

Currently, more and more companies are recognizing the importance of technology implementation.

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) has become one of the hottest roles in the tech industry.

On July 2 local time, Microsoft announced a $2.5 billion investment to create the Microsoft Frontier Company, providing forward transformation services to global customers powered by artificial intelligence. Judson Althoff, CEO of Microsoft's commercial business, said Microsoft Frontier Company will go beyond the so-called FDE and "become the largest, most capable, and outcome-oriented engineering organization in the industry." Microsoft expects to deploy 6,000 industry and engineering experts at customer companies to co-design, co-innovate, deploy, and continuously improve large-scale AI systems based on measurable business outcomes.

Microsoft is racing to keep up with its rivals. Two days ago, Amazon announced a $1 billion investment to build an FDE team; two days later, Microsoft stepped up its investment to accelerate the implementation of AI in business scenarios.

This year, Microsoft has been mired in a slump in its stock price. On one hand, Microsoft has warned about AI hegemony; on the other, it is keeping pace with competitors by creating Microsoft Frontier Company to provide customer services.

Currently, more and more companies are recognizing the importance of technology implementation. An FDE is an engineer who embeds deeply within customer teams, delivers customized AI deployments, and is responsible for closing the loop between product and revenue. AI industry research firm Perplexity AI previously stated that FDE is the only organizational model capable of delivering enterprise-grade artificial intelligence. Customer teams handle customer retention, product engineers build the core platform, and in the gap between the two there are multiple problems: complex data schemas, lengthy security reviews, discrepancies between workflows and demos, etc. FDE teams are responsible for handling these issues.

Anthropic and OpenAI have previously established new companies to build FDE teams. On May 4, Anthropic announced a partnership with Blackstone, Goldman Sachs, and other companies to create an AI enterprise services company aimed at integrating Claude into customers' core business operations.

On May 11, OpenAI announced the creation of OpenAI Deployment Company and acquired AI consulting and engineering firm Tomoro, gaining 150 experienced Forward Deployed Engineers and deployment experts.

According to data from recruiting platform Paraform, FDE job postings in the first quarter of 2026 grew 350% year over year.

It's not just US tech giants—Chinese tech companies are also strengthening their FDE deployment. A Yicai search found that ByteDance, Tencent, Meituan, and other tech companies all posted multiple FDE-related job openings in June, and Alibaba also posted openings for enterprise customer Solutions Architects with responsibilities similar to FDEs.

FDE roles in China are entering a period of explosive growth. In the first half of 2026, postings for FDE-related positions on the Maimai platform surged 21-fold year over year. Among companies posting FDE positions, 49.3% have more than 1,000 employees, and 37.3% are AI-native companies.

High salaries are a hallmark of FDE roles. Maimai data shows that the average salary for FDE-related positions is between 45,000 and 78,000 yuan per month. Yicai noted that many companies are offering 15 months' pay per year, putting expected annual salaries for these positions between 670,000 and 1.17 million yuan. In addition, some headhunter postings for FDE leads show salaries as high as 200,000 yuan per month.

FDE salaries in the US are equally staggering. Data released by Perplexity AI in May shows that FDE has become the highest-paid generalist role in the AI field. Total compensation ranges from $215,000 (the median for a Forward Deployed Engineer at Palantir) to over $1.2 million (a chief-level Applied AI Engineer at Anthropic).
