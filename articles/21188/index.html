Summary

FDE board lists one Staff Forward Deployed Engineer opening at CADDi, Inc., a Tokyo-based manufacturing DX company founded in 2017 with roughly 900 employees. CADDi runs a platform for ordering custom parts and an AI data platform (AIDP) for manufacturers; its core technology turns unstructured drawing data into searchable assets, and over half of Nikkei 225 companies use its products. The FDE role sits in the Enterprise Solution department with a salary range of 14 to 30 million yen, among the highest in Japan. Hiring involves an online coding test, a work-sample interview, and a final interview with the CTO.

About CADDi, Inc.

The company uses AI to analyze manufacturing orders and drawing data, operating a custom parts procurement platform and an AI data platform for manufacturing called "AIDP." Its core technology converts drawings—masses of unstructured data—into searchable assets, and it has been adopted by more than half of Nikkei 225 companies. The company has around 900 employees. The technology that turns unstructured drawing data into searchable assets is central, aiming not only to streamline procurement but also to enable the reuse of manufacturing knowledge itself.

FDE characteristics at this company

The Enterprise Solution team employs Staff Forward Deployed Engineers, offering a starting salary range of ¥14 million to ¥30 million—one of the highest ranges in Japan. The selection process includes a technical challenge (online coding test) and a work sample interview, with the CTO conducting the final interview.
