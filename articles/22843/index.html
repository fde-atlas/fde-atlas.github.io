Summary

Crack ML Interview compiles 20 interview questions reported by candidates who interviewed at Scale AI: 14 coding, 2 behavioral, averaging 3.0/5 difficulty, with 18 reported within the past year (most recently October 2026). Seven questions map to forward deployed engineer (FDE) rounds, covering deployment system design, LLM API integration and output validation, debugging unfamiliar multi-file codebases, and customer-facing behavioral rounds. Recurring coding themes include task schedulers with dependencies and deadlines, interval aggregation, NumPy implementations of MLPs and attention, and prompt engineering. The site's editorial advice: prioritize practical coding, deployment system design, AI/LLM applications, and behavioral/customer prep.
