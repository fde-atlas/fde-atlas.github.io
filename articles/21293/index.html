Summary

Kyndryl Japan, an IT services company spun off from IBM in 2021 with roughly 90,000 employees worldwide, is hiring a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer for its new AI business in Fukuoka, with a salary range of ¥8–18 million. The role centers on working directly with customers to turn business problems into Generative AI and Agentic AI solutions: defining requirements, designing architecture (RAG, AI agents, data platforms, cloud), leading end-to-end delivery into production, managing teams, and converting project learnings into reusable assets. Candidates need production AI implementation experience, technical leadership background, and stakeholder management skills.

Key points

  • Kyndryl Japan is hiring a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer for a new AI business in Fukuoka.
  • Salary ranges from ¥8 million to ¥18 million per year.
  • The role spans customer discovery, Generative/Agentic AI architecture, and production deployment.
  • Required: production AI experience, technical lead background, and executive-level client communication.
  • Engineers also turn project knowledge into reusable assets to grow the AI business.

  • Job Description
  • [Position Overview] As a core member of the new AI business, you will engage directly with customers to concretize AI utilization concepts and requirements from management and operational challenges. You will define the technical direction for solutions leveraging generative AI and Agentic AI, proactively drive design, development, implementation, and improvement, and lead the team to contribute to delivering results and growing the business. [Responsibilities] Identifying customer challenges and setting outcome goals: Lead dialogues connecting executives and frontline users, organize future visions, constraints, and decision criteria, and drive consensus on priorities, scope, and outcome metrics. Building customer relationships and leading decision-making: Organize technical options, business value, and risks, and lead key decisions while engaging multiple stakeholders. Developing AI solutions and implementation roadmaps: Clarify the roles and responsibilities of AI and humans, and conceive and propose use cases, architectures, and implementation plans for Generative AI/Agentic AI. Architecture design and technical integration: Combine Generative AI, Agentic AI, RAG, APIs, data platforms, and cloud services to integrate into enterprise systems, and design, implement, and review performance, reliability, observability, security, and governance. End-to-end delivery leadership: Lead requirements definition, design, prototyping, implementation, testing, production deployment, and operational improvement; in critical phases, personally conduct verification and implementation, taking responsibility for quality and outcomes. Team leadership and talent development: Lead teams spanning multiple areas of expertise, maximizing team results through role assignments, technical reviews, problem-solving, and mentoring. Expanding the AI business and asset creation: Turn knowledge gained from projects into reusable assets, solution patterns, code libraries, delivery playbooks, and more, driving the creation of new projects and the expansion of services.
