Enterprise AI Services Hong Kong

Onyx Devs Lab · 2026 · 3 min read

Summary

This page is a service index for Onyx Devs Lab, a Hong Kong-based enterprise AI advisory and delivery firm. It lists roughly thirty practical guides covering the full enterprise AI lifecycle: choosing between RAG, fine-tuning, tools, and agents; build-versus-buy decisions; go-live and RFP checklists; vendor due diligence and exit planning; and stalled-project recovery. Sector-specific guides cover legal, accounting, manufacturing, and recruiting AI. The material emphasizes forward deployed engineering versus advisory and custom development, evidence gates for scale-or-stop decisions, agent authority tiers, and compliance with Hong Kong PCPD guidance.

Key points

  • Index of ~30 practical enterprise AI guides from a Hong Kong firm
  • Compares advisory, custom development, and FDE by uncertainty resolved
  • Frameworks for stop, redesign, hold, or scale decisions with evidence gates
  • Sector guides for legal, accounting, manufacturing, and recruiting AI
  • Covers agent security, authority tiers, RFPs, and vendor exit planning
