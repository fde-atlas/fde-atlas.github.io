FDE Forward Deployed Engineer

Original title: FDE前沿部署工程师

zhaopin.com · 2013-03-19 · 1 min read

Summary

A job posting from 360 for an FDE role supporting enterprise deployment of its nano Work product. The engineer works on-site with clients to understand business processes, build and tune agents, skills, workflows and knowledge bases, integrate with existing office and business systems, deliver training and ongoing support, and accumulate reusable industry know-how. Requirements include a CS-related background, familiarity with LLMs and agents, hands-on experience with at least one agent or automation platform, and basic Python and API skills.

Key points

  • 360 is hiring FDEs to deploy its nano Work enterprise product at client sites.
  • The role covers building agents, skills, workflows and knowledge bases.
  • Candidates need Python, API and scripting basics for troubleshooting.
  • Integration with clients' existing office and business systems is required.
  • The job emphasizes accumulating reusable industry best practices.

Full text

- yuan

  • Shanghai Putuo District
  • 1-3 years
  • Bachelor's degree
  • Part-time
  • Client company: Beijing Qiyi Technology Co., Ltd.

Job Description

FDE

1. Responsibilities: a. Responsible for accompanying the deployment of Nano Work Enterprise Edition at enterprise customers, understanding customer business processes and actual usage scenarios, identifying business pain points, and translating them into executable AI solutions; b. Building, debugging, and optimizing agents, Skills, workflows, knowledge bases, and other capabilities based on Nano Work; c. Assisting enterprise customers with product launch, usage training, troubleshooting, and ongoing operational support, driving AI tools to be truly applied in daily business; d. Completing the connection and adaptation with customers' existing office systems, business systems, and third-party tools according to customer needs; e. Continuously exploring customer needs, accumulating industry know-how, solutions, and best practices to form reusable industry cases; f. Collaborating with 360 product, technical, and ecosystem teams to continuously drive AI adoption in enterprises. 2. Requirements: a. Majors in computer science, software engineering, information technology, artificial intelligence, or related fields preferred, with relevant technical background or project experience; b. Familiar with basic concepts such as large language models, Agents, and agents, with strong interest in AI application deployment; c. Familiar with at least one AI agent, automation, or large model application platform, with hands-on usage or building experience; d. Capable of Python, API calls, scripting, or automation, able to perform basic development and troubleshooting; e. Familiar with common operating systems such as Windows, macOS, and Linux, and basic technical environments; f. Strong requirements understanding, communication, and problem-solving skills, able to independently communicate requirements with enterprise customers; g. Good learning ability and project execution skills, able to adapt to different enterprises and industry scenarios; h. Strong enterprise service and delivery mindset, able to drive projects with a focus on final business outcomes.
