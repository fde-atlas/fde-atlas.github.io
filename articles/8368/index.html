Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer Interview (No Offer): The interview process is very

Taro · 2025-06-01 · 3 min read

Summary

A candidate shares a Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer interview experience in New York (no offer). The process ran smoothly: a 20-30 minute recruiter call covering background and goals, a HackerRank online assessment, and a coding interview with an engineer. Aggregated data from 36 interview experiences shows a 14% pass rate, with 56% of candidates rating the experience positively. The post also includes employee reviews: smart coworkers, fast growth, and high pay, but no 401k matching, no bonuses, no raises with promotions, long hours, and technically unfulfilling work.

Key points

  • Palantir FDSE interviews are highly selective: 14% pass rate across 36 experiences.
  • Process: recruiter call, HackerRank OA, then engineer coding interview.
  • 56% of candidates reported a positive interview experience.
  • Employee reviews praise smart peers and growth but cite no bonuses or promotion raises.
  • Some employees find forward deployed work technically unfulfilling with long hours.

Positive ExperienceNo Offer

The interview process is very smooth. The first part is a call with a recruiter, usually 20-30 minutes, talking about past experience and projects, including school and future plans/any offers.

Next is a HackerRank OA, followed by another coding interview with an engineer.

Forward Deployed Software Engineer • Current Employee

New York, New York, United States

September 13, 2023

Pros: Fast-paced, very smart coworkers, and overall, the opportunity to grow is quite amazing.

Cons: No 401k matching, no bonuses, and no compensation increase with promotions or title promotions. Long work hours. Life as a Forward Deployed Engineer can be technically unfulfilling. It took 2 years for a company-wide compensation review to happen, and only a fraction of the company got a raise then.

5.0

Long hours and hard work, but worth the pay

February 26, 2023

Pros: Great salary. Working with some of the smartest people. Prestige.

Cons: Still a job. Could earn a lot more working a few years and then starting your own company. Long hours. Can't work on side projects or investments.

A great company to grow and learn

November 3, 2020

Pros: People are very smart. There is room to grow. It's not uncommon to see people who have been in the company for 1-2 years becoming Leads. Huge responsibilities from day 1. "Choose your own adventure" -> You can create your own growth path in the company if you see the need for it. For example, if you work on X product/customer but you see that there is a need to develop another product, you can start working on that without an issue (as long as you don't jeopardize your other role). D&I is big here. You can get as involved as you want with this, and that's awesome! Overall, this is an amazing company. I love it.

Cons: Too much autonomy at times. This is also a pro, but being independent and figuring out things on your own is not for everyone.
