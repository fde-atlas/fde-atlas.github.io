Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (5/5, Jan 11, 2025): What you make out of it

Taro · 2025-01-11 · 2 min read

Summary

An anonymous review from a Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer with under a year at the company, rated 5/5. The reviewer says the role offers unusual autonomy: you can choose how technical, intense, or fast-paced your work is, and with political savvy you can pick which skills to develop. The main downside is team assignment—landing on a bad team can cause technical skills to atrophy. The reviewer recommends the company and approves of the CEO despite a negative outlook. The page also includes brief interview notes: a smooth two-round phone process with fair questions, and a keyword-sensitive pre-screen tied closely to the job description.

Key points

  • Current FDE at Palantir rates the company 5/5
  • Role allows choosing your own technical depth and intensity
  • Biggest risk: bad team assignments cause skill atrophy
  • Reviewer recommends Palantir but has a negative outlook
  • Interviews are smooth; pre-screening depends on resume keywords
