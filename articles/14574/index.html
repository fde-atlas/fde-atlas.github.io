FDE Resource Hub: Courses, Bootcamps & Free Learning

Forward Deployed Course · 2026-10-01 · 3 min read

Summary

This article is an independent index of paid and free learning resources for the Forward Deployed Engineer role, covering courses, bootcamps, university diplomas, fellowships, YouTube channels, podcasts, reading and job boards. Paid options include ADaSci's 30-hour certification (US$164.99), Xebia Academy's foundation course, Educative and two Udemy courses, plus Sundeep Teki's coaching (US$69–5,499). Cohort programmes are mostly India-based: IIT Gandhinagar's 5.5-month residential diploma (₹647,500), IIT Delhi's 6-month certificate (₹165,000 plus GST), Scaler's 12-month track (₹399,000), and cheaper bootcamps from CodeKerdos and School of Core AI, alongside a selective a16z fellowship. Free resources include Hugging Face's AI Agents course, DeepLearning.AI short courses and a Coursera RAG/LangChain course. Prices were checked 19 Oct 2026 and are re-checked monthly; listing order is never paid.

Key points

  • Independent index of paid and free FDE learning resources across courses, bootcamps, fellowships and media.
  • ADaSci offers a 30-hour self-paced FDE certification for US$164.99.
  • Indian university and bootcamp programmes range from ₹45,000 to ₹647,500 in fees.
  • Free options include Hugging Face, DeepLearning.AI and Coursera courses on agents and RAG.
  • Prices checked 19 Oct 2026, re-checked monthly, with no paid listing placement.
