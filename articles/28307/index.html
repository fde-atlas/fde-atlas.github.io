Today's Take

If the main threads of the past few days were respectively the "embedded industry delivery package," "operations workstations + runtime governance," and the "foundation review layer," then today's new development worth capturing is: enterprise AI is further upgrading from "a few high-value scenarios" into an organization-level operating system.

Today's batch of signals shows four consistent shifts:

1. Not just building single-point assistants, but connecting customer conversations, sales follow-ups, development collaboration, financial approvals, and knowledge retrieval into continuous work chains.

2. Not just talking about model performance, but starting to fill in admin consoles, Admin APIs, analytics views, spend visibility, and permission boundaries.

3. Not just overseas giants pushing forward—domestic office entry points are also packaging Skills, knowledge bases, automated tasks, and team collaboration spaces into organization-level products.

4. No longer asking "should we try AI," but formally entering the stage of "whoever can turn AI into an organization's operating layer gets the long-term budget."

Taken together:

  • The value of the FDE in its next stage is upgrading from "helping enterprises land a few AI scenarios" to helping enterprises wire AI into an organization-level operating system.
  • The selling point of high-ticket projects will further upgrade from a single workflow or a single role's workstation to a full-package capability of "front-office touchpoints + internal processes + management control plane + local collaboration entry points."

Key Signals

Cars24 turns AI into a front-office conversion layer and an internal operating layer: enterprise AI is starting to grow from a "tool" into "part of the business system"

  • Source: OpenAI Customer Story | Original link
  • OpenAI published the Cars24 customer story on July 16, 2026. AI agents handle 1M+ conversation minutes per month, customer support resolution rates improved by 50%, turnaround time for key service processes dropped by 80%, and 12% of historically churned seller leads were reactivated through AI.
  • It's not just a customer service entry point—voice and chat agents extend across the full chain of buying cars, selling cars, financing, follow-ups, and after-sales.
  • Internally, ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex are deployed to about 600 employees with 85%-90% daily active usage. Codex has entered internal workflows such as Linear tickets, bug handling, GitHub summaries, financial analysis, and procurement approvals.
  • Why it matters: The logic behind enterprise purchases is no longer just "give employees a smarter assistant," but "let AI take over both customer touchpoints and internal execution chains." This pushes the FDE's focus from building individual feature points toward "organizational backbone transformation."

Intel and Google Cloud push Gemini Enterprise into engineering, supply chain, and corporate operations backbones

  • Source: Google Cloud Press Release | Original link
  • Google Cloud announced an expanded strategic partnership with Intel on July 16, 2026. Intel will roll out Gemini Enterprise across its global workforce, extending agentic workflows across three core business areas: engineering, supply chain, and corporate operations.
  • Gemini Enterprise will serve as a central hub to build and deploy agents. In the semiconductor development environment, Google Cloud infrastructure will enhance Intel's silicon development simulations.
  • Why it matters: Enterprise AI is no longer just "adding a copiloting layer to the office"—it is entering the main lines of engineering R&D, supply chain, and corporate operations. The FDE's role looks more like an "enterprise AI systems integration engineer."

OpenAI adds Admin keys, 120-day analytics history, and app sync under EKM: the management control plane is maturing

  • Source: OpenAI Help Center | Original link
  • OpenAI Enterprise Release Notes were updated on July 16, 2026. Admins can create workspace-scoped Admin keys in the Global Admin Console for group management, the Spend Controls API, cost reporting, and analytics.
  • Up to 120 days of credit and Codex analytics history has been added. Workspaces with EKM enabled can also use apps with sync.
  • Why it matters: The competitive focus of enterprise AI is shifting rightward—from "is the model strong enough" to "does the organization have a formal management control plane." Who issues keys, who reviews usage, who controls spend—these are already prerequisites for whether enterprise AI can be incorporated into formal IT systems.

WPS Comate officially launches: China's domestic office entry points are starting to build AI into an organization-level operating layer

  • Source: IT之家 | Original link
  • Kingsoft Office officially launched WPS Comate on July 15, 2026, defined as an "AI office client for employees."
  • It can connect organizational data, systems, and processes, letting AI integrate into organizational workflows and deliver business results. The six major modules include: AI role experts, a Skill ecosystem, automated tasks, team collaboration spaces, a Wiki knowledge base, and an app marketplace.
  • Why it matters: China's domestic office software is pushing enterprise AI from a "chat feature" to an "organization-level entry-point product." What is most likely to grow first in the domestic market may be the combination of "office entry point + knowledge base + Skills + automated tasks + team collaboration spaces."

WAIC Preview: The industry's core questions shift from "model hype" to "ROI, the last mile, and real scenarios"

  • Source: ITValue / TMTPost | Original link
  • ITValue published a WAIC 2026 preview on July 15, 2026, directly defining the industry's core proposition as "value realization."
  • McKinsey research: the median ROI of mid-to-large enterprises that have deployed AI is about 12%, with the top 30% exceeding 25% and the bottom 30% directly negative. IDC report: 68% of SME AI projects stall within a year of launch, 43% are terminated outright, and failed projects average an investment of RMB 1.89 million.
  • WAIC 2026 will be held July 17-20, covering 140+ forums, 1,100+ companies, 172 buyer delegations, and 63 core application scenarios.
  • Why it matters: China's domestic industry discourse is starting to ask the right questions—whether ROI can be calculated, who fills the last mile, and whether system and organizational governance can advance together. This is exactly where the FDE can step in.

Implications for FDE Practitioners

  • Don't just sell departmental tools: When designing solutions, ask whether the "front-office business chain" and the "back-office execution chain" can be restructured together. The Cars24 case proves that entering both layers simultaneously makes it much easier to deliver results.
  • The management control plane is not an add-on feature: Going forward, solutions should include control-plane design—"who governs, how it's monitored, how it's restricted, how it's approved"—much earlier.
  • Focus on local enterprise-level entry points: Products like WPS Comate show that the Chinese market can use a combination of "knowledge base + Skill + automated tasks + collaboration space" as the skeleton for FDE implementation.
  • Let the WAIC data speak: 68% stalled, 43% terminated, an average investment of 1.89 million—these numbers can be used directly in external communications to show that the "last mile" is where the truly valuable services lie.
