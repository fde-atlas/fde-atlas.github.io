Cognition forward deployed engineer interview

open-fde.ai · 2026-11-02 · 3 min read

Summary

This article compiles the interview process and reported questions for Cognition's Forward Deployed Engineer role. Stages include a recruiter screen, a take-home completed inside Devin with tight deadlines, a project presentation to a panel including a difficult-customer role-play, leadership one-on-ones, an executive pitch, and a simulated customer call; some candidates instead faced an onsite build with pair programming. All detail comes from a third-party guide and candidate submissions, not employer sources, and stage order varies. Reported questions cover project walkthroughs, customer scenarios, and motivation. Tips: lead with business impact, stay composed on unanswerable follow-ups, and treat the take-home as a customer would.

Key points

  • Roughly seven stages, from recruiter screen to executive pitch and a simulated customer call.
  • Take-homes run inside Devin: API-driven automation or an autonomous vulnerability-remediation system.
  • Some candidates did an onsite automation-engine build plus pair programming instead of pitch rounds.
  • Questions span project walkthroughs, roadmap-conflict scenarios, and why-FDE motivation probes.
  • Advice: lead with business impact; an offer recipient says the 'why' questions were hardest.

Full text

Cognition interview

Forward Deployed Engineer (US, Europe, APAC, ANZ, LATAM, Federal, etc.) · Partner Deployed Engineer · Deployed Engineer (earlier title)

Process

  • Recruiter screen Call with a non-technical recruiter. Assesses: Communication, motivation, customer orientation, fit for a fast-paced environment. [1]
  • Technical take-home Hands-on exercise inside Devin with little hand-written code; candidates report a substantial task and tight deadline. Assesses: Product understanding, hands-on skill, framing work around customer value. [1]
  • Project presentation About 45 min walkthrough of a past technical project to a panel; slides optional; some panelists play a less-technical or difficult customer. Assesses: Impact framing, clarity for non-experts, decision rationale, composure under follow-ups. [1]
  • Leadership one-on-ones Behavioral conversations with VPs and engineering leaders. Assesses: Presence with senior stakeholders, adaptability, judgment. [1]
  • Executive pitch Simulated pitch of the product to a panel playing executives. Assesses: Value articulation, persuasion, product command. [1]
  • Case-study customer call Fictional company with challenges, a fixed prep window, then a simulated live customer call. Assesses: Rapid structuring, real-time customer communication. [1]
  • Onsite build / pair programming (some candidates) One mid-level candidate describes a very hands-on onsite including building an automation engine and pair programming. Assesses: Hands-on building. [2]

AI tools in interviews: No employer statement found. Reported take-homes are done inside Devin, Cognition's own AI agent. [1]

Questions

Take-home 3
  • Set up the product (Devin) and run through a sample workflow.
  • Build an event-driven automation with the Devin API that solves an engineering-workflow problem. Candidate says the task is large and the deadline tight.
  • Using Devin's integrations, build an autonomous vulnerability-remediation system for a configured GitHub project.
AI build 1
  • Build an automation engine (onsite), followed by pair programming. The candidate names the task; the full spec is not in the report.
Customer scenario 1
  • Your largest customer pushes for a feature not on your roadmap. What do you do?
Presentation 3
  • Walk me through a technical feature you have led.
  • Walk us through a project you owned and why it mattered to the business; why that approach or tool? Guide attributes these to recent candidates and a Cognition interviewer.
  • Explain this project to someone with no technical background.
Domain 1
  • What experience do you have with agentic development?
Behavioral 2
  • Tell me about the most complex project you have led.
  • How do you handle a stakeholder who knows the subject as well as you do?
Motivation 3
  • Tell me about your background and what you want in your next role.
  • Why forward deployed engineering?
  • What do you know about Cognition?

Tips from the sources

  • Lead with impact: say why a project mattered to the business within the first few minutes, before build details. [1]
  • Expect follow-ups you cannot fully answer; acknowledge the gap, reason aloud, and offer to follow up rather than losing composure. [1]
  • Treat the take-home as a customer would: set up Devin, run a realistic workflow, and tie its function to customer value. [1]
  • An offer recipient says the hard part was not coding but the 'why' questions; they spent about a month learning what the role involves. [2]
  • In the case-study call, structure the challenges during prep but expect the customer to steer away from your plan. [1]
  • One senior candidate's process was stopped because the team decided it wanted more customer-facing experience; be ready to show it. [2]

Not found in public sources: No employer-published process. All stage detail comes from one third-party guide plus candidate submissions on the same site; the stage sequence varies across candidates (some report a hands-on onsite with pair programming rather than pitch/case rounds). A techinterview.org guide about Cognition SWE interviews was not used because it is not FDE-specific and cites no sources.
