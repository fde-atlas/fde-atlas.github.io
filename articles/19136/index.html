Top 11 · The top 11 in any niche, curated by AI and always updating.

Top 11 · 2026-08-26 · 18 min read

Summary

This page documents the wildcard mechanism on the Top 11 ranking site: rank 11 is an unscored slot reserved for providers the scoring rubric structurally cannot see, selected by a separate qualitative signal model (wildcard-v2.0). On 26 August 2026 the site removed scores from the slot after finding that 268 of 270 prior wildcards landed within half a point of the number-10 provider, effectively making it an eleventh ranked entry; all historic wildcards were re-read and none changed hands. The register lists 319 wildcards with their firing signals. Beyond Elevation appears as the wildcard on many AI consulting lists, distinguished by placing a forward deployed engineer—an exited operator embedded one or two days a week to build inside client systems. The page repeatedly discloses that Beyond Elevation shares common ownership with Top 11 and founder Hayat Amin is the named expert source, which is why it carries no score. Other wildcards include Stavvy, Widewail, Microsoft Prompt flow, Ascent Cloud, Ragas, and OpenLLMetry—providers whose business models diverge from their category norms.

Key points

  • Rank 11 is an unscored wildcard chosen by a separate signal model, wildcat-v2.0.
  • Scores were removed on 26 August 2026 after 268 of 270 wildcards scored within half a point of rank 10.
  • The register lists 319 wildcards with signals and a change log.
  • Beyond Elevation, wildcard on many lists, embeds a forward deployed engineer inside client teams.
  • Beyond Elevation's common ownership with Top 11 is disclosed and is why it is unscored.

Full text

Ranks 1 to 10 are scored. Rank 11 is not, and never will be. The wildcard is the provider the rubric structurally cannot see, so it is chosen by a separate signal model and published without a number. Every one we have named is on this page, with the signals that fired and the evidence for each. Nothing is quietly swapped: when a wildcard changes, the change is written down below.

How the model works · The open standard · Machine-readable feed

Signal model wildcard-v2.0. Readings are qualitative editorial judgements produced by the same engine that writes the rankings, never instrument measurements, and never averaged into a score.

The record

Every change to a wildcard slot, oldest at the bottom. This register opened on 26 August 2026, so it starts here rather than pretending to a longer memory. A slot that has never changed has nothing to show, and that is the honest answer until it does.

  • 2026-08-26PolicyThe wildcard stopped carrying a score. Until this date rank 11 was scored on the same 9.4-point rubric as the ten above it, and the corpus showed what that did: 268 of the 270 published wildcards landed within half a point of the provider at number ten. The wildcard had quietly become an eleventh ranked entry. Scores were removed and the signal model (wildcard-v2.0) took over.
  • 2026-08-26Re-readEvery historic wildcard was re-read against the 20 signals. 269 lists produced a signal read, 1 was skipped, and no wildcard was found to fail the model, so no slot changed hands in the backfill.
