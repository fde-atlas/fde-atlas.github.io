Palantir-FDE-Pattern

Original title: Palantir-FDE-Pattern

FDE中国社区 · 2026-07-12 · 4 min read

Summary

This is a best-practice skill package from the FDE China Community for adapting Palantir's forward deployed engineer model to Chinese contexts. Its methodology centers on embedding engineers at customer sites, Ontology, closed-loop delivery, and a platform-plus-services approach, with localization substituting Feishu, private deployment, and consulting-style DIVE for some practices. It defines a six-step process (study, compare, mark differences, select practices, pilot, review), stage gates from PoC to production, and role interfaces among FDE, AIBP, business owners, and IT/security. It warns against copying blindly, ignoring domestic compliance (xinchuang, data residency), and staying conceptual. A sample case resulted in adopting three practices, including a lightweight Ontology. The package includes checklist and evaluation files importable into Agent Skills tools.

Key points

  • Core Palantir pattern: on-site embedding, Ontology, closed-loop delivery, platform plus services.
  • Localization swaps in Feishu, private deployment, and consulting-style DIVE.
  • Six-step process: study, compare, mark differences, select, pilot, review.
  • Defines collaboration interfaces for FDE, AIBP, business owner, and IT/security.
  • Key pitfalls: blind copying, ignoring compliance, concept without implementation.

FAQ

Q1: How to avoid "copying blindly" when executing [Palantir-FDE-Pattern]?

A: Follow the README methodology step by step and present interim deliverables in weekly Demos; if PoC materials are incomplete, first produce a "to-be-validated list" before proceeding.

Q2: What additional constraints apply in private deployment/Xinchuang/MLPS environments?

A: Specify the deployment form explicitly in the prompt constraints section; when data cannot leave the domain, domestic components, or audit logs are involved, reference Private-Deployment-Gateway and RBAC-Audit accordingly.

Q3: What if the division of responsibilities with AIBP is unclear?

A: Return to the AIBP-Collaboration-Playbook to update the RACI; AIBP is responsible for Ground Truth and business acceptance, while FDE is responsible for deliverable technical assets.

Q4: After PoC passes, how to transition to Beta/production?

A: Mark the next-stage Skill in the outputs (e.g., RAG-Evaluation→Private-Deployment-Gateway→FDE-Adoption-Growth), and trigger the launch gate in the checklist.
