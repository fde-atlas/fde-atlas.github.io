Summary

This article archives a Google job posting for an AI Activation and AI Technical Community Development Lead, based in Singapore. It is a senior role on the Google Cloud team requiring five or more years of experience; pay is not disclosed. The posting describes helping customers of all sizes adopt cloud technology and understand their needs.
