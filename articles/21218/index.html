SCSK Adopts Salesforce's "Agentforce" to Build Sales AI Agents In-House — Developing 100 "FDE Talent" to Lead Field-Driven AI Implementation by 2027, Strongly Supporting Internal DX Promotion and Commercialization as an External Business

Original title: SCSK、Salesforceの「Agentforce」を活用し営業AIエージェントを内製化 2027年までに現場主導のAI実装を牽引する「FDE人材」100名を育成、社内DX推進と外販ビジネス化を強力に後押し

Salesforce · 2026-10-08 · 7 min read

Summary

SCSK has adopted Salesforce's Agentforce platform and its Signature Success Plan to build sales AI agents in-house. The company will train 100 FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) by 2027 to lead field-driven AI implementation in customer-facing and sales management operations. Following a 'Customer Zero' approach, SCSK will first validate AI use internally—building on a prior Sales Cloud reform that grew gross profit 200% year over year—then package the know-how into AI-native offerings for clients and explore multi-agent models leveraging its own intellectual property via Agent-to-Agent integration.

Key points

  • SCSK adopts Agentforce and Signature Success Plan for in-house sales AI agents.
  • 100 FDEs to be trained by 2027 to drive field-led AI deployment.
  • A prior Sales Cloud reform lifted gross profit 200% year over year.
  • Internal know-how will be productized into AI-native client offerings.
  • Agent-to-Agent integration will let agents use SCSK's intellectual property.

Full text

Salesforce Japan Co., Ltd. (Headquarters: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; Chairman and CEO: Shinichi Koide; hereinafter Salesforce) today announced that SCSK Corporation (Headquarters: Koto-ku, Tokyo; President and Representative Director: Takaaki Toma; hereinafter SCSK) has adopted Salesforce's AI agent platform "Agentforce" and its top-tier support plan, the "Signature Success Plan". In addition, SCSK is launching an initiative to develop 100 in-house "FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers)*" — field-accompanying engineers in the customer engagement and sales management domains who will lead the building and deployment of AI agents. Through this project, SCSK aims to promote company-wide AI adoption through the automation of sales operations and revamp its sales processes, while also extending the knowledge and expertise gained to customer-facing businesses, establishing a new revenue base as an Agentforce partner.

* FDE: A next-generation specialist profession who leverages cutting-edge technologies such as Salesforce's AI agent platform "Agentforce" and "Data 360" to work embedded at customer sites, providing end-to-end accompaniment and support from pilot implementation through production operation and adoption.

Background: SCSK has been working to enhance sales activities and improve operational efficiency across its business domains. In one of its specific business domains, the company previously implemented an SFA (sales force automation) system using Sales Cloud and reformed its sales processes, elevating activities from lead generation through order receipt and achieving significant results that improved profitability, including gross profit growing to 200% compared to the same period of the previous year. Building on these results, SCSK has been working to systematize the know-how it practiced and validated in-house into offerings and extend them to support customers' sales and business transformation. However, with the rapid advancement of generative AI and AI agent technologies, the standards required for enhancing sales activities have changed significantly, and SCSK's offerings also needed further enhancement premised on AI.

Accordingly, SCSK decided to undertake the adoption of Agentforce and the development of 100 FDE professionals based on the "Customer Zero (*)" philosophy of first practicing and validating the latest technologies in-house and then delivering the results back to customer companies. This will accelerate AI adoption and business innovation in SCSK's own sales operations, while at the same time, by combining the practical knowledge gained through in-house practice with the implementation and utilization expertise SCSK has cultivated over approximately 20 years as a major Salesforce partner, the company will deliver AI-native offering services to customer companies. * Customer Zero: An initiative in which Salesforce leads the way by being its own first user (Customer Zero) and extends the results it demonstrates to customer companies.

Reasons for Adoption: In accumulating AI utilization expertise through in-house practice and extending it to customer companies, SCSK focused on the fact that Agentforce goes beyond mere business automation, enabling autonomous AI agents to enhance and optimize sales activities themselves, and adopted it for the sales domain. The company also highly values its strong affinity with the existing Sales Cloud that has underpinned its past sales reforms, as well as its excellent extensibility that allows integration with company-wide core systems and information infrastructure.

Furthermore, to steadily advance in-house Agentforce utilization and accelerate the creation of AI-native offering services, SCSK adopted the Signature Success Plan. The Signature Success Plan features assignment of a dedicated Salesforce Customer Success Manager (CSM), the fastest technical support, 24/7/365 proactive monitoring to reduce the risk of failures and enable efficient operation, training courses on features such as Agentforce delivered by certified instructors, and tailored technical guidance and best practices from specialized architects. SCSK decided to adopt the plan, highly valuing that, by leveraging the advanced knowledge of the specialized team and close accompanying support, it can achieve in-house building and operation of Agentforce and the early accumulation and standardization of technical know-how within the company. It also expects that this support framework will accelerate the development of its internal talent.

Future Outlook: Toward the creation and expansion of AI-native offering services, SCSK will promote the following initiatives.

  • Development of 100 FDEs and Advancement of AI Agent Utilization: Through the development of 100 FDE professionals in the customer engagement and sales management domains, SCSK will accelerate AI adoption and business transformation within the company. In addition, through in-house practice using Agentforce, the company will continuously refine its AI agents, verifying their effectiveness itself while accumulating practical knowledge and generating results.
  • Realizing AX for Customer Companies Through AI-Native Offerings: SCSK will systematize the insights and know-how cultivated from the success experiences gained through in-house practice into offerings and deliver AI-native offering services. This will support the advancement of AI adoption and business transformation at customer companies, contributing to the realization of AI Transformation (AX).
  • Creating Next-Generation Offerings Leveraging SCSK's Intellectual Property: By having AI agents utilize SCSK's various intellectual property and information assets through Agent-to-Agent (AtoA) interactions, the company will realize advanced autonomous execution of business operations by multiple AI agents. Through this, SCSK aims to create a next-generation business transformation model that leverages its unique intellectual property.

Customer perspective: Mr. Masayuki Kikuchi, Executive Officer and Group Leader of the Intellectual Property Business Group at SCSK Corporation, stated: "We are convinced that proactively practicing and validating the latest AI technologies in our own operations and delivering solid results is what leads to providing true value to our customers. With the support of Salesforce's Signature Success Plan, we will rapidly develop 100 FDE professionals and, based on the knowledge and expertise gained from our own success experiences, expand these into AI-native offering services. Furthermore, by combining these with SCSK's original AX services, we will support the transformation of our customers' entire value chain through the realization of AX."

Salesforce perspective: Katsuyuki Watanabe, Managing Executive Officer at Salesforce, stated: "With SCSK's adoption of Agentforce and the Signature Success Plan, we are confident that the accumulation of insights gained from practicing AI agents within their own operations and the strengthening of their promotion framework through the development of 100 FDE professionals will accelerate even further. Salesforce will continue to work alongside and support SCSK in maximizing their AI-driven business transformation and the value delivered to their customers."
