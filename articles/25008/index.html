Symplectic Holds FDE Study Session

Original title: シンプレクスがFDE勉強会を開催

Summary

The Japan FDE Association's blog reports that Simplex will hold a study session for engineers titled around whether one should become an FDE and what skills and career paths to choose in the AI era. Speakers include an AI-driven development practitioner and the head of an AI Center of Excellence. The article frames FDEs as engineers who embed AI and software into client operations, arguing that engineers are shifting from merely using tools to delivering business outcomes on-site, and it promotes the association's FDE certification as a way to build relevant skills.

News Details

According to a Digital PR Platform release, Simplex will hold a study session for engineers. The theme is "Should You Become an FDE? ~What to Learn and What Kind of Work to Choose in the AI Era~," addressing what engineers should learn and what kind of work they should choose in the AI era. The speakers will be a practitioner of AI-driven development and the head of the AI CoE (Center of Excellence), who will discuss the role and career of engineers in the AI era.

Background

An FDE refers to an engineer who works on-site with customers, implementing and embedding AI and software into their business operations. As AI-driven development spreads, engineers are shifting from simply being tool users to roles that engage deeply with business operations and deliver results. The theme of this study session directly confronts the question of how engineers should choose their own careers amid these changes.

What This Means for FDE Practitioners

A session where a practitioner of AI-driven development speaks shows that the ability to leverage AI agents and LLMs for business improvement is being tested in real-world practice. To deliver results at customer sites, it is essential to have a perspective that goes beyond merely operating tools—one that organizes business processes and drives continuous improvement. Those who want to determine whether they are suited to be an FDE may also find Characteristics of People Suited to FDE | Assessing Your Aptitude helpful.
