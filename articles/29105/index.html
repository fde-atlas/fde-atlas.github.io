AIBP-Collaboration-Playbook

Original title: AIBP-Collaboration-Playbook

FDE中国社区 · 2026-07-12 · 4 min read

Summary

This is a skill-pack playbook from the FDE China community defining how Forward Deployed Engineers should collaborate with AI Business Partners (AIBP). It targets common failure modes: unclear role boundaries, missing ground truth, and demos disconnected from business outcomes. The division of labor assigns business outcomes and ground truth to the AIBP, stable delivery and technical assets to the FDE. It prescribes six steps—defining RACI, co-creating scenario cards, weekly demos, building a Golden Dataset, jointly signing launch gates, and operational handover—plus a four-stage gating table where production release requires evaluation metrics of at least 80%. It covers five trigger scenarios (PoC team formation, acceptance disputes, handover, etc.), common pitfalls, constraints for private deployment and Chinese compliance regimes (Xinchuang, classified protection), and ships as a ZIP importable into Claude, Cursor, or Codex.

Key points

  • Core split: AIBP owns business outcomes and ground truth; FDE owns delivery and technical assets.
  • Six-step process: RACI, scenario cards, weekly demos, Golden Dataset, launch gates, handover.
  • Production gate requires RBAC, evals, human review, and logging checks with metrics ≥80%.
  • Lists pitfalls like AIBP not supplying samples or FDE defining acceptance alone.
  • Ships as a skill pack importable into Claude, Cursor, and Codex.

Problem Definition

FDE and AIBP responsibilities are unclear, Ground Truth is missing, and Demos are disconnected from business outcomes.

Methodology Framework

Division of labor: AIBP = good business outcomes + Ground Truth; FDE = stable delivery + technical assets. Mechanisms: co-created scenario cards, weekly Demo with dual owners, joint review of failure samples, co-signed launch gate. Meetings: Kickoff/scenario review/weekly Demo/failure retrospective/launch gate.

FAQ

Q1: When executing the [AIBP-Collaboration-Playbook], how do you avoid "AIBP not participating in samples"?

A: Follow the README methodology step by step and present interim deliverables at weekly Demos; if PoC materials are incomplete, first produce a "pending verification list" before proceeding.

Q2: What additional constraints apply in private deployment, Xinchuang (domestic IT innovation), or classified protection environments?

A: Specify the deployment form in the prompt constraints section; when data cannot leave the domain, domestic components, or audit logs are involved, reference Private-Deployment-Gateway and RBAC-Audit accordingly.

Q3: What if the division of responsibilities with AIBP is unclear?

A: Return to the AIBP-Collaboration-Playbook to update the RACI; AIBP is responsible for Ground Truth and business acceptance, while the FDE is responsible for deliverable technical assets.

Q4: After the PoC passes, how do we transition to Beta/production?

A: Mark the next-stage Skill in the deliverables (e.g., RAG-Evaluation→Private-Deployment-Gateway→FDE-Adoption-Growth), and trigger the launch gate via the checklist.
