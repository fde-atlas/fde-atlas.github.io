Palantir AIP Consulting & Implementation

BD Emerson · 2026-11-17 · 5 min read

Summary

This BD Emerson service page describes how the firm implements Palantir AIP, the AI layer that connects LLMs to the Foundry ontology so agents can reason over live operations and take governed actions. Delivery relies on forward deployed engineers embedded with client teams. A seven-step approach starts with one workflow with a measurable baseline, builds ontology, permissions, and retrieval first, launches agents supervised, expands autonomy one action type at a time based on evals, logs every call, and maps controls to ISO 42001 or the NIST AI Risk Management Framework during the build. BD Emerson claims neutrality since it does not resell Palantir licenses, and it templates successful agents into repeatable delivery systems.

Key points

Full text

Palantir AIP put to work on your ontology: governed agents with a defined action set, human approval where the risk warrants it, and logging on every call.

Definition

Palantir AIP is the artificial intelligence layer of the Palantir platform: it connects large language models to the Foundry ontology so AI can read enterprise context, reason over live operations, and take governed actions. Deployed well, AIP turns AI from a chat window into an operator. BD Emerson deploys AIP through forward deployed engineering: our engineers embed with your teams, build agents against production workflows, and configure the evaluation, permissions, and audit that make enterprise AI defensible.

Services

Our approach

01 The first agent targets one operational workflow with a measurable baseline. Model choice follows the problem.

02 Ontology, permissions, and retrieval come first, so agent output is anchored in governed enterprise context rather than model memory.

03 Every agent starts supervised. Autonomy expands as evals and production history justify it, one action type at a time.

04 Once the first agents hold, we template the delivery: shared evals, reusable actions, governance checkpoints, and a growing internal team.

05 Prompts, retrieved context, actions taken, and approvals are logged per call. When someone asks why the agent did that, the record answers.

06 The first release reads and recommends. Write actions turn on one at a time, each behind an approval step, once evaluation results justify the change.

07 Agent controls are mapped to ISO 42001 or the NIST AI Risk Management Framework while the build happens, so the governance review becomes a read rather than a rebuild.
