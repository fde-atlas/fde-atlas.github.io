Pinterest AI & Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs (24)

Forward Deploying · 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Summary

Forward Deploying tracks 24 open AI engineering roles at Pinterest: 16 ML Engineer, 5 AI Engineer, and 3 AI leadership positions across 4 cities. The median published US base salary is $290k. AI Engineer roles pay 29% above the tracked market median ($225k), and AI engineering leadership pays 39% above ($280k). Nearly all listings (23 of 24) publish salary ranges, 22 mention equity, and 11 are remote-eligible. Four roles opened in the last 30 days, with a median posting age of 46 days. Data is as of November 14, 2026.
