Printable FDE salary snapshot

FDE Notes · 2026-09-18 · 1 min read

Summary

A printable one-page snapshot of published pay ranges for forward deployed and deployment-adjacent roles, based on the live job set as of 2026-11-21. Across 239 US roles with dollar-denominated annual pay, the median range is $185,000–$250,000, with a median midpoint of $225,000. Applied AI/ML engineering pays the most ($211,000–$282,500), followed by forward deployed roles ($185,000–$297,000). OpenAI discloses the most ranges (58, median midpoint $250,500), while Harvey has the highest median midpoint at $298,650. The methodology counts only employer-disclosed ranges from open postings, excludes equity and bonus, and withholds groups with fewer than five disclosed ranges.

Key points

  • Median midpoint across 239 US roles is $225,000.
  • Applied AI/ML engineering is the highest-paying role family.
  • OpenAI published 58 ranges, the most of any employer.
  • Harvey leads on median midpoint at $298,650.
  • Only employer-disclosed ranges from open postings are counted.

Median published range

Across the 239 US roles posted with annual pay in US dollars, the median lower bound is $185,000 and the median upper bound is $250,000. Half of those ranges sit between $160,000 and $300,000; the median of range midpoints is $225,000 — the figure directly comparable to single-number medians published elsewhere.

How these numbers are computed

  • Open roles only. Closed postings are excluded.
  • Employer-disclosed ranges only. Nothing is estimated or backfilled from comparable roles.
  • Currency and period are not mixed. Aggregates use US-dollar annual pay — 239 of the 260 disclosed ranges. Equity, bonus and benefits are never included.
  • Groups too small to quote are withheld. Fewer than 5 disclosed ranges means the whole group is held back; 5–9 is labelled a small sample.
