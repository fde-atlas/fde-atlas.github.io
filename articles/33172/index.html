ServiceNow FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer): Role, Skills & Prep

FDE India · 2026-11-10 · 8 min read

Summary

This guide covers ServiceNow's Forward Deployed Engineer role, based on its Applied AI FDE team listings. FDEs build production LLM applications end to end—backend, data orchestration and UI—often on-site at enterprise customers, while creating reusable libraries and feeding lessons back to product teams. A Forward Deployed Software Engineer (Generative AI) role was listed in Hyderabad in November 2026, requiring 8+ years of software engineering (10+ in the US/UK), 2+ years on customer-facing systems, and up to 30% travel. India handles over 45% of ServiceNow's product engineering, and Hyderabad is its largest engineering site with planned FDE hiring. India FDE pay is unpublished; the Santa Clara listing shows a $201,300–$352,300 base. The article also details required skills and preparation advice.

Key points

  • ServiceNow FDEs build full-stack LLM applications directly in customer environments, with up to 30% travel.
  • Hyderabad listing requires 8+ years of engineering experience; US and UK versions require 10+ years.
  • India hosts over 45% of ServiceNow's product engineering; Hyderabad is its largest engineering site.
  • India FDE salaries are unpublished; the Santa Clara range is $201,300–$352,300 base.
  • Key skills: Python/Node.js/Java, React, LangChain or Semantic Kernel, cloud platforms, and CI/CD.

ServiceNow hires Forward Deployed Engineers to build generative AI applications on top of its platform, working directly with large enterprise customers. The role sits in an Applied AI Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) team, and in November 2026 a Forward Deployed Software Engineer (Generative AI) role for that team was listed in Hyderabad, according to EchoJobs. It asks for 8+ years of software engineering. This is a senior, hands-on builder job, not a pre-sales or ServiceNow-admin role.

What the FDE does at ServiceNow

ServiceNow posts the same role under the title Forward Deployed Solution Engineer – Applied AI FDE in several locations, including Santa Clara and London. One version’s description calls it a “Senior Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE)”. Across the listings, including the Hyderabad one, the work is consistent:

  • Build LLM applications end to end. Backend services, data orchestration and front-end UI, taken from architecture to production in agile sprints.
  • Work inside customer environments. The listings describe on-site work in live customer settings and travel of up to 30%.
  • Make the work reusable. Turn each build into libraries, prompts, scaffolds, SDKs and documentation that the next team can pick up.
  • Close the loop with product. Share field lessons and developer-experience feedback with ServiceNow’s platform and product teams.

ServiceNow also lists a Principal AI Forward Deployed Engineer title on its careers site, so the team has a senior track above the main role.

Why Hyderabad matters

India is central to ServiceNow’s engineering. Storyboard18, citing a Moneycontrol report, says more than 45% of ServiceNow’s global product engineering is done from India. India has about one in five of its roughly 28,000 staff, and CTO Pat Casey described Hyderabad as the company’s largest engineering site worldwide. The same report says ServiceNow plans to grow its Hyderabad teams with AI engineers, ML specialists, research scientists and forward-deployed engineers.

In October 2026, NewsBytes reported that COO Amit Zavery wants India to become one of ServiceNow’s top three markets. It put the India workforce at about 6,000 people across Hyderabad and Bengaluru, and said the company is hiring more engineers, especially in AI. For the wider Hyderabad FDE market, see our Hyderabad FDE jobs page.

The London listing adds a machine learning list: MLflow, Weights & Biases, LoRA and PEFT fine-tuning, SageMaker, Vertex AI, AzureML, Kubeflow, Airflow, vLLM, Triton, and explainability tools such as SHAP and LIME.

What this means for you:

  • Full-stack depth matters. The role spans backend, data and UI. Engineers who have only built APIs or only built models will need to fill the gap.
  • Platform knowledge is a bonus, not a gate. ServiceNow experience is listed as good to have. Strong LLM application work elsewhere can carry you.
  • The bar is senior, with one exception. A second Hyderabad role, Senior Forward Deployed Engineer – Flow, dated 10 November 2026 on ServiceNow’s careers site, asks for 4+ years in a customer-facing technical role (forward deployed, solutions or implementation engineering) and centres on SSO (SAML, OAuth, Okta, Entra ID), REST APIs and webhooks, and automations across Jira, Workday, Slack and Microsoft 365. If you have under 8 years, that is the nearer target; our experience guide shows where other mid-level FDE roles sit.

How to prepare

  • Ship one full-stack LLM app. Build a Python or Node.js backend, a React front end and a retrieval layer with vector search, then deploy it on AWS, Azure or GCP with CI/CD. This matches the listing almost line for line.
  • Learn an orchestration framework properly. Go beyond tutorials in LangChain or Semantic Kernel: tool calling, multi-step agents, error handling and tracing. Be ready to explain why you chose one design over another.
  • Get familiar with ServiceNow’s platform. Use a free ServiceNow developer instance to understand workflows, tables and integrations, so you can talk about how an AI agent would plug into IT service or HR workflows.
  • Prepare customer-facing stories. Have two examples where you worked with a client or internal team in production, handled pushback and delivered under pressure. Our FDE interview questions guide has scenario examples.
  • Show production thinking. Add logging, latency measurement and evaluation to your demo, and be ready to discuss security reviews in regulated companies such as banks. Then set a job alert for “forward deployed” on ServiceNow’s careers site. If you want to compare with another enterprise platform’s FDE role, see our Salesforce FDE page.

ServiceNow FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Application: Apply through ServiceNow's careers site (careers.servicenow.com), where the Applied AI FDE roles are posted under 'Forward Deployed Solution Engineer – Applied AI FDE'. The Hyderabad role also appears on the EchoJobs aggregator.
  • Later rounds: ServiceNow has not published an FDE-specific interview format. Expect the listings' themes to be tested: building LLM applications end to end, cloud deployment and working on-site with customers.
