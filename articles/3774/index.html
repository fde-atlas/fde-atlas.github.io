Do Forward Deployed Engineers Really Work Remotely? The Honest Truth

Scaler Topics · 2026-09-13 · 6 min read

Summary

This article argues that forward deployed engineers (FDEs) are usually not remote, since the role exists precisely to embed engineers at customer sites to tackle messy data, legacy systems, and compliance issues that can't be solved over video calls. It breaks down how work models vary by company type, from Palantir-style on-site shops to OpenAI-style hybrid labs to SaaS firms using the title loosely, and offers tips for spotting misleading remote tags in job listings. It also notes remote FDE openings in India are rare.

Key points

  • FDEs are client-facing by design; most roles are on-site or hybrid with real travel.
  • Palantir-style firms demand heavy on-site embedding; OpenAI-style labs are hybrid and project-dependent.
  • Some SaaS companies use the FDE title for roles closer to technical account management.
  • Judge postings by listed responsibilities, not the remote tag or hidden travel clauses.
  • In India, FDE roles are limited, mostly on-site or hybrid, with few fully remote options.

Do Forward Deployed Engineers Work Remotely? The Short Answer

Usually not. A forward deployed engineer is client-facing by design, which makes it one of the least remote roles in software, closer to how a typical software engineer role is structured in the opposite direction. Most FDEs work on-site or hybrid, with real travel built into the job. Some fully remote or remote-first FDE roles genuinely exist, but they're the exception, not the norm. This page explains why, when remote is actually possible, and how to read a job listing honestly.

What "Forward Deployed" Actually Means, and Why It Decides the Answer

The whole answer is hiding in the name. "Deployed" means sent forward, to where the work actually is: the customer's organisation, their data, their systems, their people. The role exists precisely because the hard part of enterprise software, messy internal data, undocumented workflows, legacy systems, compliance rules, usually can't be solved from a vendor's HQ over a video call. That's why companies pay real money to put an engineer physically in the room. So "remote FDE" is a slight contradiction of what the role is actually for, not impossible, but swimming upstream against the whole reason the title exists. This is exactly the dynamic driving demand for embedded builders as companies rush to deploy AI systems on-site, a trend covered in more depth in why enterprises now hire engineers to deploy AI systems on site.

How to Read a "Remote" Forward Deployed Engineer Job Posting

A "remote" tag on an FDE listing can mean several different things, and it's worth learning to tell them apart before you get excited about one.

• Does it say "remote" but also list a travel expectation like "up to 50% travel"? That's not remote in any normal sense of the word.

• Is "remote-first" paired with "must be based near a major client hub"? That's really hybrid wearing a remote label.

• Does the actual responsibilities section describe embedded on-site work despite the remote tag up top? Trust the responsibilities, not the tag.

• Is the company a classic on-site FDE shop that's suddenly advertising a remote req? Worth a direct question in the first interview.

• Does it genuinely say fully remote with zero travel for a role literally called "forward deployed"? Read the fine print carefully before you get excited.

Is a Remote FDE Role Realistic in India?

Keep this honest and short, because India relevance here is genuinely low. FDE roles in India are limited and concentrated, mostly the local offices of global companies and a handful of startups, and they skew on-site or hybrid, not fully remote. The remote-FDE search itself is largely a global or US remote-jobs query. Most Indian FDE seats also sit at a similar career stage to how software engineering careers usually progress more broadly, meaning experienced engineers rather than freshers. Don't expect a large remote market here, and be skeptical of any listing claiming otherwise without a verified posting to back it up.

So, Should the "Remote" Question Change Whether You Want This Role?

If fully remote work is non-negotiable for your life right now, the FDE role is probably a poor fit, and that's genuinely worth knowing before you apply, not after you've accepted an offer and booked your third flight of the month. If you're energised by being on the ground with customers, the travel and on-site work are the actual point of the job, not an unfortunate price you pay for it. FAQs

Do forward deployed engineers work remotely?

Usually not. The role is client-facing by design, mostly on-site or hybrid with travel, though some remote or hybrid roles do exist at certain companies. Read the specific job description rather than assuming the title guarantees remote work.

Do forward deployed engineers travel a lot?

Often yes, especially at classic on-site FDE companies. Travel varies a lot by company and project phase, so treat any specific percentage you see quoted online with suspicion unless it's attributed to a named source.

What does "forward deployed" mean?

It means the engineer is sent to work at or inside the customer's organisation, rather than from the vendor's office. That's the entire reason the role is rarely fully remote, the name describes exactly where the work happens.

Is there such a thing as a remote forward deployed engineer job?

Yes, but they're the exception rather than the norm. Remote is most realistic in post-launch support phases, cloud-deployed products, or at companies using the FDE title loosely for what's really an account-management role.

Why isn't the forward deployed engineer role remote?

Because the hard part of the job, messy client data, legacy systems, undocumented workflows, usually can't be solved from a vendor's HQ. Companies deliberately put an engineer on site specifically to solve that problem in person.

Are there remote FDE jobs in India?

Limited. FDE roles in India are few and skew on-site or hybrid at global companies' local offices. Fully remote FDE roles are rare here, so don't expect a large remote market to exist yet.
