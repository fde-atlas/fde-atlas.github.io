FDE Enterprise AI Implementation Engineer Bootcamp | First Pilot Cohort

Original title: FDE 企业 AI 落地工程师实战营 | 首期样板班

FDE FAN · 2 min read

Summary

FDE FAN is launching a first cohort of its FDE Enterprise Bootcamp: a six-week, full-time, in-person program at Tianfu Software Park in Chengdu, priced at ¥19,800. Rather than simulated exercises, trainees work on two real projects—a college application advisory Agent and a cross-border e-commerce Agent—covering requirement interviews, knowledge base construction, Agent and workflow building, and delivery testing. Graduation uses a 100-point review; scoring 80 or above grants entry to the FDE Career Support program with one-on-one job assistance, while lower scores require retraining and another defense. The program targets applicants with existing foundations who can handle intensive training, and enrollment requires consultation and an interview.

Key points

  • Six-week offline bootcamp in Chengdu, tuition ¥19,800.
  • Trainees build two real projects: a college application Agent and a cross-border e-commerce Agent.
  • Full cycle from requirement interviews to Agent building and delivery testing.
  • 100-point graduation review; 80+ enters the FDE Career Support program.
  • Those below 80 must complete retraining modules and re-defend.

WHAT IS FDE

Enterprises don't just need AI users.

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) requires entering real enterprise business scenarios, understanding enterprise needs, breaking down business processes, designing AI solutions, building agents, knowledge bases, and automated workflows, and ultimately driving projects through testing and delivery. Enterprises need people who understand the business and can deliver. Forward Deployed Engineers enter real business scenarios, tear down processes, design AI solutions, build Agents and workflows, and drive projects to delivery.

6-WEEK TIMELINE

Six weeks of full-time practice: entirely focused on real project advancement. 6-week sprint focused on delivery.

Week 1 Requirement Research and Project Kickoff: Business process breakdown, user needs analysis, first version of job resume.

Week 2 Product Plan and Agent Prototype: AI solution design, Agent dialogue flow, first version of Agent prototype.

Week 3 Knowledge Base and Core Functionality Building: Knowledge base cleaning and classification, Agent core process building, core function testing.

Week 4 Real Project Development and Testing: Multi-scenario testing, error logging, user feedback collection, workflow adjustments.

Week 5 Enterprise Delivery and Personal Portfolio: Operation manual, project retrospective, personal portfolio upgrade.

Week 6 Project Roadshow and Employment Review: Agent live demonstration, simulated enterprise interviews, final resume, comprehensive scoring.

EVALUATION SYSTEM

Strict 100-point evaluation system and graduation review.

After six weeks, every participant must attend the graduation review. Those scoring 80 or above pass and enter the FDE Career Support program; those below 80 must continue retraining in key modules and defend again. Participants scoring 80+ enter the FDE Career Support program; others continue training until they meet the bar.
