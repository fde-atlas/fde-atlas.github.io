Hitachi and Google Cloud Expand Strategic Alliance in Physical AI Social Implementation via FDE and in the Security Domain

Original title: 日立とGoogle Cloud、FDEによるフィジカルAIの社会実装とセキュリティ領域での戦略的アライアンスを拡大

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-06-09 · 11 min read

Summary

Hitachi and Google Cloud are expanding their strategic alliance, first formed in May 2024, to accelerate social implementation of physical AI and strengthen cybersecurity. Hitachi will build an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) model combining its Lumada-based co-creation approach and GlobalLogic's AI-native engineers with Google Cloud's top engineers. The two companies will enhance HMAX by Hitachi using Gemini Enterprise and multimodal Gemini models to automate complex frontline operations in manufacturing and infrastructure, reusing FDE domain knowledge via a data platform for scalable delivery. On security, Hitachi's Cyber CoE will pair Google Security technologies (Wiz, Mandiant, Google SecOps, Google AI Threat Defense) with Hitachi's mission-critical SI expertise to counter AI-driven cyberattacks, citing Mandiant M-Trends 2026 findings that attack cycles have shortened.

Key points

  • Alliance expands on the May 2024 Hitachi–Google Cloud partnership.
  • FDE model pairs GlobalLogic engineers and Hitachi consultants with Google engineers.
  • HMAX gains Gemini Enterprise and multimodal Gemini models for autonomous operations.
  • FDE domain knowledge will be reused via a data platform for scale.
  • Security offering combines Wiz, Mandiant, Google SecOps with Hitachi's SI expertise.

Hitachi, Ltd. (hereinafter, "Hitachi") announced that it will expand its strategic alliance*1 with Google Cloud to accelerate the social implementation of physical AI and to deliver cybersecurity solutions against the threat of AI-driven cyberattacks, thereby supporting customers in solving their challenges. Physical AI, which is currently attracting significant attention, is a technology that connects AI analysis and judgments derived from field data to concrete actions such as the autonomous control and operation of various machines and equipment. By repeating this cycle, AI continuously learns, enabling optimal judgment and execution suited to the situation. Hitachi is working on the transformation of social infrastructure through the social implementation of physical AI.

To achieve this important mission, Hitachi will combine the co-creation approach of solving customer challenges by leveraging its strengths in IT, OT, and products cultivated through Lumada with Google Cloud's advanced AI, establish a Forward Deployed Engineers (hereinafter, "FDE") model, and deploy it globally.

FDEs are a group of specialists who work directly on-site in customers' businesses and handle everything end-to-end, from identifying management challenges to early value validation through PoCs and agile implementation into actual operations. Hitachi has a long history of "field-focused engineering," staying close to customers' sites and accompanying them from problem discovery through resolution, which has an extremely high affinity with the FDE model. By fusing this Hitachi DNA with Google Cloud's advanced capabilities, Hitachi's consultants and AI Transformation (AX) experts with deep expertise across a wide range of industries, together with AI-native software engineers from GlobalLogic, Hitachi's U.S. subsidiary, will collaborate with Google Cloud's top engineers to provide powerful support to customers.

The two companies will work closely with Hitachi's "Frontier AI Deployment Center" to enhance "HMAX by Hitachi" (hereinafter, "HMAX"), a suite of next-generation solutions that uses AI to innovate social infrastructure, through Gemini Enterprise, Google's agent platform that enables the use of Google's high-performance AI technologies. With this enhanced HMAX at the core, the two companies will drive the resolution of frontline workers' challenges through the autonomization of complex operations. Furthermore, through a data platform that makes the domain knowledge gained from FDE activities reusable as assets, they will achieve scalable deployment and deliver value to a broad range of customers.

In the field of physical AI, where AI connects directly with the real world, even more advanced security is essential. AI is also transforming the cybersecurity landscape: attackers are actively leveraging AI models to accelerate vulnerability discovery at an unprecedented scale and to automate the generation of exploits (attack methods). In addition, according to "Mandiant M-Trends 2026"*2 published by Google Cloud, it has become clear that the evolution of AI technology has significantly shortened the cyberattack process. To address these risks, Hitachi's dedicated security organization, "Cyber CoE," will collaborate with Google Cloud, combining Google Security technologies such as Google AI Threat Defense*3 with the mission-critical SI expertise Hitachi has cultivated through social infrastructure to support customers' security operations.

Through these efforts, the two companies will accelerate the creation of value through physical AI safely and globally.

■Background

Since their partnership in May 2024, the two companies have promoted advanced initiatives to create value through the use of AI. As part of this, based on a "Customer Zero" approach of first practicing thoroughly within Hitachi, Hitachi Power Solutions Co., Ltd., which handles maintenance businesses in the power and industrial sectors, conducted technical validation of maintenance and inspection work using Gemini Enterprise, confirming the feasibility of improving quality and efficiency*4. In this trial, it was demonstrated that Gemini Enterprise has a high affinity with the OT domain, such as manufacturing and infrastructure. It was also mutually recognized that field-focused, hands-on support that unravels field constraints and agilely creates and implements value is effective for safely connecting advanced AI with field operational equipment, business systems, and the tacit knowledge of experienced workers.

Currently, with a view to leveraging AI for company-wide business reform, Hitachi is strongly promoting the improvement of AI literacy by introducing Gemini Enterprise early in its Digital Systems & Services sector, which leads AX, and advancing numerous proof-of-concept projects in which field employees themselves use AI.

Building on these success stories across the Hitachi Group, the two companies are expanding their strategic alliance to safely scale physical AI to customers' sites.

■New Initiatives under the Strategic Alliance

(1) Accelerating the implementation of physical AI by strengthening FDE capabilities

GlobalLogic's AI-native software engineers, along with Hitachi's consultants

and AX experts, will collaborate with Google Cloud's top engineers to further develop and strengthen

the FDE model. To utilize continuously evolving AI technology in business and in the field,

an agile approach that rapidly validates practicality and adapts to change is essential.

This FDE model differs from traditional SI, which builds systems according to each customer's individual requirements, in that

distinguished by a process that validates effectiveness in real business operations.

This allows customers to visualize return on investment in advance and, while minimizing risk,

create business value through AI at overwhelming speed.

(2) Promoting the resolution of frontline challenges through HMAX enhancement

The two companies will embed and strengthen HMAX with Gemini Enterprise's autonomous AI and multimodal Gemini models.

Gemini Enterprise, which can simultaneously process and understand a wide variety of information such as

frontline camera footage and vast amounts of sensor data, has extremely high affinity with physical AI

that operates autonomously by capturing real-world situations. Leveraging these technical characteristics,

the companies will also incorporate use cases such as maintenance inspection checks through image comparison using Gemini Enterprise,

and promote the autonomization of complex operations in manufacturing and social infrastructure sites.

To realize these goals, Hitachi's proprietary domain knowledge and

Google Cloud's Agent Ready data utilization technologies will be incorporated into Hitachi's data platform.

This will create scalable business outcomes that go beyond simply providing AI services,

aiming to establish new business models in the infrastructure field.

(3) Next-generation cybersecurity against security threats in the AI era

To respond to changes in threats in cybersecurity,

the two companies will provide autonomous next-generation security solutions to the market.

This solution combines Google Cloud's security technologies, including Wiz, which delivers comprehensive visualization of and automatic risk reduction for cloud and AI risks,

and Mandiant, which possesses advanced expertise in cyber threats,

with Hitachi's mission-critical SI experience that has supported social infrastructure such as railways, power, and finance,

as well as its global OT knowledge.

Its effectiveness has already been demonstrated with customer zero, such as the adoption of Google SecOps at Hitachi group companies globally,

and the companies will work toward expanding proposals to customers going forward.

We will work on it.

In addition, Hitachi will consolidate the practical AI implementation knowledge and technologies cultivated on the front lines into the "Frontier AI Deployment Center." By broadly channeling these cutting-edge insights back to domestic systems engineers, the "Frontier AI Deployment Center" will help strengthen FDE across the entire group. Through these efforts, Hitachi will accelerate the rollout of the FDE model and further accelerate innovation through the use of AI globally.

■Comment from Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian

Combining Hitachi's deep domain knowledge, GlobalLogic's engineering capabilities, and Google Cloud's advanced AI and engineering resources is an innovative approach to accelerating AI transformation in the real world. With Gemini Enterprise's powerful multimodal AI, we will deliver leading examples of AI innovation globally. Furthermore, by integrating Google Security's advanced solutions with Hitachi's expertise in mission-critical domains, we will support customers in driving innovation in a safe and trusted environment in the AI era.

■Comment from Jun Abe, Vice President and Executive Officer, General Manager of the Digital Systems & Services Division, Hitachi, Ltd.

Hitachi has been actively implementing and practicing Gemini Enterprise as customer zero, experiencing significant benefits in operational efficiency and productivity improvement. The expansion of this strategic alliance is intended to enable both companies to take on solving more challenging problems, building on the strengthening of FDE development. By combining Google Cloud's world-leading AI implementation approaches cultivated on the front lines with Hitachi's customer co-creation approach and GlobalLogic's outstanding digital engineering capabilities, we aim to create the value of physical AI in customers' business operations. In addition, to deliver such value safely, we will link Hitachi's mission-critical system building expertise with Google Security's advanced solutions to protect social infrastructure from threats in the AI era and lead the realization of a sustainable society.
