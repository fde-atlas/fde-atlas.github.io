Summary

This page is a company profile of VACAN Technologies, the operator of FDE Soken, a Japanese media site focused on Forward Deployed Engineers. The company, a subsidiary of VACAN founded in January 2026 and led by Ryu Tamaki, supports AI-driven business transformation through three service models: AI onboarding, enterprise DX, and a startup studio. Its philosophy favors small, working deliverables and continued support until clients can operate independently. It leverages the parent company's deployments at over 20,000 locations and its vCore™ technology with 50+ patents. A sister media, AX Soken, covers enterprise AI transformation.

Relationship with FDE Research Institute

FDE Research Institute is an FDE-specialized media outlet operated by VACAN Technologies, Inc. It is run on an independent domain to provide neutral information, while its operating entity is openly disclosed.

We are a practitioner of the FDE model, embedding ourselves in customers' workplaces and delivering results through technology. The insights gained in the field are fed back into the articles of FDE Research Institute. For FDE basics, see What is an FDE? Roles, differences from engineers, required skills, and career paths; for corporate adoption, see How to introduce FDEs: Organization, project selection, and evaluation metrics explained in 4 stages.

Our Business

We work alongside our customers with three service models tailored to their situation.

  • AI Onboarding — Promoting the internal use and adoption of generative AI to make organizations truly "able to use" it.
  • Enterprise DX — Solving frontline managers' challenges through in-house system development.
  • Startup Studio — Accelerating new business launches through rapid prototyping.

Our Philosophy

Rather than "building one big dam," we "dig small wells for the challenges at hand and eventually connect them into a waterway." Instead of elaborate upfront planning, we stack small, functional deliverables and run a rapid feedback loop from idea to implementation. We don't just deliver a project and walk away; we stay alongside our customers until they can run on their own — this is our consistent stance.

Our Strengths

Our strength lies in leveraging the foundation of our parent company, Vacan, Inc. (VACAN). With deployment experience at more than 20,000 locations and proprietary technology vCore™ (over 50 patents) for congestion analysis and data integration, the technology and expertise honed in the field form the basis of our support.

Sister Media

VACAN Technologies, Inc. also operates AX Research Institute, a media outlet specializing in AI Transformation. While FDE Research Institute focuses on "the careers and practice of implementers who embed themselves in the field and deliver results — i.e., FDEs," AX Research Institute broadly covers the strategy, organization, and implementation of corporate AI transformation (AX). Though their themes differ, they share the perspective of "delivering results in the field." We encourage you to read both.
