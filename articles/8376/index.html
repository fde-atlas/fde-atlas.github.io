Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (5/5): Wide breath of experience, less depth

Taro · 2024-08-19 · 2 min read

Summary

A former Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer who spent four years at the company gives a 5.0 review, recommending it with a positive outlook. Pros include top-tier colleagues, early exposure to high-impact problems at major companies, and freedom to shape your own career path. Cons are that intensity varies with the customer you're paired with and your team, and the company offers little structured growth path—good or bad depending on preference. Ratings across work/life balance, culture, compensation, and management hover around 4.0. The page also includes three interview experiences for the New York role, covering a HackerRank screen, whiteboard onsite rounds, and a multi-stage process with unexpected final-round questions.

Key points

  • Former FDE gives 5.0 after four years, recommends Palantir
  • Praised for top talent and exposure to huge customer problems
  • Downsides: variable intensity and unstructured growth path
  • Most category ratings around 4.0, including compensation
  • Page includes three interview accounts: HackerRank, whiteboards, surprise final round
