Sarvam AI FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer): Role, Skills & Prep

FDE India · 2026-10-30 · 6 min read

Summary

This article profiles the Forward Deployed Engineer role at Sarvam AI, an Indian AI startup hiring on-site in Bengaluru. FDEs build and own production voice, WhatsApp and LLM agents on Sarvam's platform for enterprise clients like Tata Capital, SBI Life, CRED, IDFC and LIC, handling integration, evaluation pipelines and feedback to product teams. Two listings ask for 1–3 or 2–5 years of experience with Python, REST APIs, prompt engineering and vector search. Pay is unpublished; industry references suggest ₹25–70 LPA at Indian AI startups. Applications require a portfolio and a completed AI agent assignment, and the article offers preparation advice including building an Indian-language agent with measurable evals.

Key points

  • FDEs own production agents for enterprise clients across voice, WhatsApp and web channels.
  • Listings require 1–3 or 2–5 years of experience; roles are on-site in Bengaluru.
  • Hired engineers join teams like Samvaad, API Dashboard, Content Studio or ARYA.
  • Applications need a portfolio and a completed AI agent assignment.
  • Pay is undisclosed; Indian AI-startup FDE benchmarks run ₹25–70 LPA.

Sarvam AI hires Forward Deployed Engineers in Bengaluru to put its own Indian-language AI models and agents to work inside enterprise customers. It is one of the clearest “pure” FDE roles in India: you write production code on Sarvam’s platform, you own the customer’s deployment, and you carry what you learn back to the product team.

What the FDE does at Sarvam AI

Analytics India Magazine reports that Sarvam describes itself as building a sovereign AI platform for India, from research and foundation models to applications. According to the same report, its FDEs work with enterprise clients such as Tata Capital, SBI Life, CRED, IDFC and LIC, and the company is clear that the job is hands-on engineering, not technical project management.

Across Sarvam’s two listings, the FDE owns:

  • Agents in production. Building, deploying and monitoring conversational agents over voice, WhatsApp, web and in-app channels, and tuning them on real usage data.
  • Custom LLM applications built on Sarvam’s APIs, including prompt design for each use case.
  • Integration and reliability. Scoping a solution, writing integration scripts, fixing bugs and meeting the customer’s latency, accuracy and cost targets.
  • Evaluation. Building evaluation pipelines and quality metrics, then iterating on production data.
  • Feedback and demos. Giving technical demos to support sales, documenting deployments and passing customer feedback to product and research.

The 2–5 year listing says engineers are matched to a product team after hiring: Samvaad (conversational agents), API Dashboard (developer tooling and integrations), Content Studio (dubbing and live translation in client environments) or ARYA (MCP servers, multi-agent systems and event-driven pipelines).

Pay

Sarvam does not publish pay for its FDE roles; the 1–3 year listing only promises “competitive compensation”. For a reference point, Taggd puts FDE pay at AI startups in India at about ₹25–70 LPA. Treat that as the range for experienced hires: early-career FDE listings at Indian startups that do post pay have been much lower. How to prepare for a Sarvam AI FDE application

  • Build one Indian-language agent end to end. A WhatsApp or voice agent that handles a real task in Hindi or another Indian language, with retrieval over your own documents, is the closest thing to Sarvam’s day job.
  • Measure it. Add a small evaluation set and track accuracy, latency and cost per conversation. Sarvam’s listings mention evaluation pipelines and cost efficiency explicitly.
  • Write it up like a deployment. A short README covering the customer problem, architecture, trade-offs and what you would change becomes your portfolio piece, and the application asks for a portfolio.
  • Do the assignment properly. The application includes a completed AI agent assignment. Treat it as your first customer delivery: working code, clear setup steps and an honest note on limitations.
  • Prepare customer stories. Even internship examples count. Have two short stories ready about explaining something technical to a non-technical person and turning their feedback into a fix.

Sarvam AI FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Application: Sarvam's Forward Deployed Software Engineer listing asks for a resume, a portfolio and a completed AI agent assignment with the application.
  • Later rounds: The interview rounds after the assignment are not described publicly. Expect the agent you submit to be discussed, so be ready to explain your design choices, evaluation and cost.

Frequently asked questions

Does Sarvam AI hire freshers as Forward Deployed Engineers?

Not as pure freshers in the listings we found. One Forward Deployed Software Engineer role asks for 1–3 years (with 1–2 years of software development preferred) and accepts customer-facing internships as relevant experience; another asks for 2–5 years.

What does an FDE work on at Sarvam AI?

Building and owning generative AI agents on Sarvam's platform for enterprise customers, including voice and WhatsApp agents, custom LLM apps on Sarvam's APIs, translation and dubbing pipelines, and agentic workflows, plus demos for sales and feedback to product.

How much does Sarvam AI pay Forward Deployed Engineers?

Sarvam does not publish pay for these roles; the listing only says 'competitive compensation'. Published ranges for FDEs at AI startups in India run from about ₹25 to ₹70 LPA (Taggd), but fresher and early-career listings at startups often post much less.

What is the Sarvam AI FDE assignment?

The listing asks applicants to submit a completed AI agent assignment along with a resume and portfolio. The exact brief is not public, so build and document at least one working agent before you apply.
