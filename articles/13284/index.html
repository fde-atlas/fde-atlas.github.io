Recruits Lab · 2026-06-01 · 2 min read

Summary

Recruits Lab publishes hiring research and market intelligence written by the recruiters running searches in AI, life sciences, healthcare, cybersecurity, and technology. It has five published reports, including a 2026 Forward Deployed Engineer Talent Report covering FDE role definitions, talent sources, technical versus customer-facing requirements, and how FDE differs from software, AI, and sales engineering. Other reports cover AI engineer hiring, foundation model research staffing, and agentic AI hiring. Research is organized by recruiting market, and new reports are published when the underlying material supports them rather than on a schedule.

Key points
