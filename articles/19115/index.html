Anddot Launches Resident FDE in the Security Domain with New Service "FDE for Security"

Original title: アンドドット、常駐型FDEをセキュリティ領域へ展開し、新サービス「FDE for Security」の提供を開始

アンドドット株式会社 · 2026-11-09 · 8 min read

Summary

Japanese startup AndDot has launched "FDE for Security", extending its resident forward deployed engineer model into cybersecurity. The same FDE who diagnoses vulnerabilities at a client site also implements fixes, configures systems, and builds mechanisms for handling newly disclosed vulnerabilities, addressing the common gap where diagnostic reports go unremediated. Support is delivered by an FDU team (FDE, Success Partner, Expert) through a repeating four-stage process, with AI used to speed up impact checks for new vulnerabilities.

Key points

  • AndDot launches "FDE for Security", combining diagnosis and remediation in one resident FDE.
  • Targets companies whose vulnerability reports remain unaddressed due to staffing gaps.
  • Services include AI-based diagnostics, penetration testing, and implementing fixes.
  • Support is delivered by an FDU team: FDE, Success Partner, and Expert.
  • A four-stage cycle runs continuously; AI accelerates response to new vulnerabilities.

And Dot Inc., which provides end-to-end support for corporate DX through resident FDEs (head office: Shibuya-ku, Tokyo; CEO: Yuta Ibaraki; hereinafter "And Dot"), is launching a new service called "FDE for Security," in which the same engineer continuously handles both security "assessment" and "remediation" on-site at customer companies.

In this service, And Dot's resident FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) work inside the customer's infrastructure and applications to discover vulnerabilities and security holes. Rather than simply reporting the issues they find, the FDEs themselves implement fixes, configuration changes, and improvements to mechanisms, continuously building an on-site state of readiness that can also respond to new vulnerabilities.

What Is a Resident FDE?

A resident FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a service in which And Dot engineers join the customer's team on-site and provide end-to-end support, from identifying issues to designing solutions, development and verification, and operation and adoption. Rather than stopping at one-off tool deployments or proposals, under the philosophy of "DX-ing the entire company," they work alongside the customer until results take root in the field.

[Background] The Corporate Challenge: Remediation Stalls After Vulnerability Assessments

Business disruptions and data leaks caused by cyberattacks are issues that affect corporate management. Ransomware shutting down factories and core systems, intrusions via business partners and subsidiaries, and attacks exploiting publicly disclosed vulnerabilities continue to occur.

Meanwhile, many companies face the following challenges in advancing their security measures.

  • Even when vulnerability assessments uncover problems, there are not enough in-house staff to handle the fixes, so the findings in reports remain unresolved
  • With cloud, on-premises, SaaS, and in-house applications all mixed together, there is no one who can view the entire system landscape and prioritize responses
  • When new vulnerabilities are disclosed, it takes time to investigate which of the company's own systems are affected and begin remediation
  • Annual assessments cannot continuously track systems added or configurations changed afterward

And Dot believes that what corporate security measures require is not just assessment tools, but people who can execute fixes on-site where problems are discovered. Therefore, the company has restructured its existing resident FDE approach for the security domain and offers it as "FDE for Security."

[Service Features] Unifying "Find, Close, and Keep Protecting"

The greatest feature of FDE for Security is that it does not separate assessment and implementation roles. The same FDE understands the system configuration and business operations on-site at the customer company and provides the three functions of "finding," "closing," and "keeping protecting" as one continuous process.

  • Find | AI-Powered Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing We conduct AI-based vulnerability checks and penetration testing. Penetration testing is performed with the prior agreement of the customer company regarding scope and methodology, and under that permission. Covering infrastructure such as networks, servers, and cloud configurations, and applications such as web applications, APIs, and authentication functions, the FDE determines response priorities based on business impact and the realism of attack scenarios.
  • Close | The FDE Who Performed the Assessment Implements the Remediation For the issues discovered, the FDE who performed the assessment implements the remediation personally. This includes correcting configurations, modifying applications, reviewing permission design, and building or renewing authentication platforms, supporting the customer until the findings are actually resolved.
  • Keep Protecting | Systematizing Initial Response to New Vulnerabilities When new vulnerabilities are disclosed, AI cross-references them against the customer's system configuration to identify potential impacts, and the FDE reviews them, with a mechanism in place to prepare response procedures and fix proposals. This mechanism is integrated into the customer's operations, aiming for a state where in-house monitoring and initial response can continue even after FDE support ends.

[How It Differs from Other Security Support] The Same FDE Handles Everything from Assessment to Remediation Implementation and Operational Adoption

With typical external support, vulnerability assessment, system modification, and policy and regulation development may each be handled by different providers. In that case, fixing issues found during assessment and subsequent operations must be handed over to other providers or in-house staff. "FDE for Security" does not divide these roles among separate providers; the same FDE is resident on-site at the customer company. It handles everything end-to-end, from risk discovery to remediation planning, fix implementation, and operational adoption.

[Support Structure] The "FDU" Composed of FDE, Success Partner, and Expert

"FDE for Security" is not delivered by a single engineer alone, but by a team unit called an FDU (Forward Deployed Unit). The FDU is centered on the FDE who drives remediation on-site at the customer company, and is composed of a Success Partner and Experts.

  • FDE: As the core of the FDU, the FDE is resident on-site at the customer company, advancing assessment, remediation implementation, and mechanism building.
  • Success Partner: Working alongside from And Dot's head office, the Success Partner aligns the customer company with the goals of support and response priorities.
  • Expert: Joining support from head office as needed, Experts supplement specialized knowledge on advanced attack techniques and industry-specific issues.

Rather than leaving everything to the assigned FDE alone, the team leverages the design patterns, proven implementations, and security expertise that And Dot has accumulated.

[Service Process] Repeating Four Stages to Continuously Improve Assessment and Remediation

"FDE for Security" proceeds through the following four stages along the "FDE Journey," the support process of the resident FDE model. At each stage, agreements are confirmed with the customer company before moving to the next stage.

  • Risk Identification On-site at the customer company, the FDE grasps the system configuration and business operations, and through AI-based assessment and penetration testing, identifies the risks that should be prioritized.
  • Agreement on Remediation Policy Multiple remediation options are compared in terms of business impact, implementation difficulty, and operational burden, and the remediation to be implemented is agreed upon with the customer company.
  • Remediation Implementation In units of one to two weeks, the FDE advances configuration corrections, application modifications, authentication platform construction, and more. While involving the customer's on-site staff, remediation is completed to the point where it actually functions.
  • Embedding into Operations We build a mechanism for promptly responding to newly disclosed vulnerabilities into the customer's operations, establishing an operational framework that can be sustained in-house even after FDE support ends.

These four stages are not a one-time effort. Addressing one risk reveals new challenges, so while the FDE is on-site, diagnosis and countermeasure improvements are repeated in cycles.

Message from the Chief Technology Officer

Kosuke Takanezawa, Chief Technology Officer, AndDot Inc.

"A vulnerability assessment report is most valuable on the day it is received, and grows more outdated with each day it goes unremediated. What truly works in security measures is to fix configurations immediately when issues are pointed out, rewrite the code, and leave behind a mechanism that ensures the same hole never opens twice. With FDE for Security, engineers who understand the system architecture and business operations handle everything from assessment to remediation with their own hands. When a new vulnerability is disclosed, we use AI to shorten the initial impact investigation. We bring this approach, which we practice daily on our own development platform, directly to our customers' workplaces."

【Target】Companies facing challenges in post-assessment remediation and continuous operations

"FDE for Security" primarily targets companies facing the following challenges.

  • They undergo vulnerability assessments but have not made progress on addressing the identified issues
  • Systems differ across group companies and sites, making it difficult to standardize security levels
  • They want to review authentication infrastructure and access management but lack personnel who can handle everything from design to implementation
  • Each time a new vulnerability is disclosed, impact investigations consume significant man-hours
  • They want to advance security measures without waiting to hire or train security personnel

For implementation, we first review the customer's system environment and current security status, then design the support scope starting from the highest-priority areas.
