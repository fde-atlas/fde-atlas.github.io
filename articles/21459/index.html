What Is Xforce, Xero's FDE Team for Accounting Firms? | Understanding the Structure Through Announcements and Job Postings

Original title: Xeroの会計事務所向けFDE「Xforce」とは|発表と求人で読む体制

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-11-16 · 17 min read

Summary

This article examines Xforce by Xero, announced November 12, 2026 as a beta: teams of Forward Deployed Engineers and partner consultants help accounting firms design and build AI workflows, complementing XeroForce, the no-code agent builder launched in May. The author cross-checked all 101 listings on Xero's job board: the four mentioning Forward Deployed are all internal AI team roles embedded in internal departments, with none referencing Xforce. Vancouver salaries range from CAD 180,000–210,000 for Senior to CAD 240,000–260,000 for Lead. Client-facing Solutions Consultant roles require hands-on accounting or bookkeeping experience. The piece distills lessons for companies deploying FDEs: pick partner roles, define data-scope commitments, and start with a small beta cohort.

Key points

  • Xforce pairs FDEs with partner consultants to build AI workflows for accounting firms.
  • It extends XeroForce, Xero's natural-language agent builder from May 2026.
  • All four public FDE listings are internal AI team roles; none mention Xforce.
  • Vancouver FDE pay: CAD 180k–210k (Senior), CAD 240k–260k (Lead).
  • Lessons: choose partner roles, data-scope promises, and small beta cohorts.

Key Points of This Article

  • 01 On November 12, 2026, Xero announced "Xforce by Xero" in beta, a program that sends a team of FDEs and partner consultants to accounting firms to design and build AI-powered workflows together.
  • 02 Xforce is positioned as human support to make XeroForce—the natural-language AI agent builder announced in May—practical for accounting firms' real-world operations.
  • 03 As of November 16, 2026, among 101 postings on Xero's job board, the 4 job listings containing "Forward Deployed" in the title or body were all roles for internal AI teams embedded in internal business units, and not a single posting mentioned Xforce.
  • 04 Xero's internal FDE postings—4 at Senior and Lead levels—share identical body text except for the level title and salary details, with Vancouver base salaries of CAD 180,000–210,000 for Senior and CAD 240,000–260,000 for Lead.
  • 05 Xero's Solutions Consultant postings, which face accounting firms, list hands-on experience at accounting firms or bookkeeping operations as a requirement—useful material for considering the profile of the people who will be paired with FDEs.

こんにちは、編集部です。今週は、会計ソフトの大手Xero(ゼロ)が、会計事務所の顧客にFDE(Forward Deployed Engineer)を送る仕組みを発表したので、その中身を追いかけました。2026年11月12日に発表された「Xforce by Xero」は、FDEとパートナーコンサルタントのチームが会計事務所と一緒に、AIを使った業務フローを設計して作るものです[1]。

What companies that employ FDEs in-house and send them to customers want to know is: "Who will FDEs be paired with, what will be promised to customers, and where should hiring begin?" In this article, we compare Xforce's team structure with the FDEs Xero is actually hiring, by retrieving and cross-referencing Xero's announcement and blog post along with all 101 job listings on the company's job board. For an overview of the overall FDE hiring process, see How to Run FDE Hiring.

What Is Xforce by Xero?

Xforceは、XeroのFDEとパートナーコンサルタントが会計事務所と組み、事務所の業務の進め方に合わせたAIの業務フローを設計して作る支援です。チームはまず事務所のことを知り、そのうえでXeroForce・XeroのAPI・Xero OSを使って、繰り返し使えて規模を広げられる業務フローを一緒に作るとされています[2]。2026年11月12日時点ではベータ版で、オーストラリア・ニュージーランド・英国の数社の会計事務所と一緒に開発を始めた段階です[1]。

Xeroのブログは、会計事務所のAI活用が「あるチームの業務フローがここに、ある人のプロンプトがあそこに」とばらばらで、顧客のデータをどの道具に預けてよいか迷い、自分たちで作る余力もない、という課題を挙げ、「Xforceはそのためにある」と書いています[2]。業務フローがどの顧客とどのデータに触れるかは、会計事務所が決めるとも明記されています[1][2]。

Why Attach FDEs When There's Already a Tool for Building AI Agents?

Xeroは、会計事務所がAIを広く使うには、業務に組み込む実地の支援が要ると説明しています。XeroのCEOは、会計事務所は少ない人数でより多くの仕事を求められ、業界に入る人も減っているとしたうえで、AIは助けになるが、規模を広げるには業務に組み込む実地の支援が要ると述べています[1]。

Xforceの土台になるXeroForceは、2026年5月14日に発表された、自然言語でAIエージェントを作る道具です。発表では、コードを書いたり複雑なプロンプトを作ったりしなくてもエージェントを使えるように設計した、と強調していました(5月の発表時点では招待制のアルファ版、11月の発表ではベータ版)[3][1]。その半年後に、エンジニアを現場に送る仕組みを足したわけです…！ ノーコードの道具に、作り込みを支える人を組み合わせた形です。Xeroのブログは、事務所ごとに業務の進め方も使うソフトも違うこと、多くの事務所には自分でAIの業務フローを作る余力がないことを挙げています[2]。道具が簡単になっても「作る時間と人」が足りない、というのは日本の事務所でもよく聞く話ではないでしょうか！

"Our partner consultants provided that support when thousands of firms moved from desktop to cloud, and now Xforce is designed to do the same for AI."

Sukhinder Singh Cassidy (Xero CEO), Xero announcement (November 12, 2026)

How Do FDEs and Partner Consultants Work Together?

発表文とブログが明かしているのは、Xforceのチームが「パートナーコンサルタントとFDE」の2種類の人で組まれることと、パートナーコンサルタントがクラウド移行の時代から会計事務所の現場を支えてきた既存の職であることまでです[1][2]。1つの事務所に何人がどう付くのか、2つの職がどう分担するのかは書かれていません。

手がかりになるのが、Xeroが会計事務所に向けて募集している技術系の職です。オーストラリアのブリスベンとメルボルンで募集中のSolutions Consultantは、営業と組んで会計事務所のパートナー(Xeroを使う事務所)向けに解決策を設計し、デモと移行を導く職で、本文は2件とも同じです[9]。ただし、この職がXforceのいう「パートナーコンサルタント」と同じものかは、求人票にも発表にも書かれていません。

What does the FDE role in Xero's job postings actually do?

What to note in this listing: This is a typical write-up for an "internal FDE" who embeds into internal business departments rather than customer-facing teams. What is sought comes down to two things: production experience with LLM-based agents and the ability to translate non-technical people's pain points into design. Accounting knowledge is not among the requirements. For a full picture of the internal FDE way of working, see our article reading 7 internal FDE job postings.

What can companies sending FDEs to customers learn from Xero?

Xeroの例から読み取れるのは、FDEを顧客に送る仕組みを、既存の現場の職との組み合わせ、データの範囲を顧客が決める約束、少数のデザインパートナーとのベータの3点で設計していることです[1][2]。自社でFDEを顧客に出そうとしている企業が、決めておくべき点に置き換えると次のとおりです。

  • 誰と組ませるか: Xforceは、クラウド移行を支えてきたパートナーコンサルタントとFDEを同じチームにしている[1]。顧客の業界の言葉が分かる既存の職(導入コンサルタント・カスタマーサクセスなど)を、FDEの相方として先に決める
  • 顧客に何を約束するか: 業務フローがどの顧客とどのデータに触れるかは会計事務所が決める、と発表文に書いている[1][2]。顧客のデータを預かる業界では、作る範囲より先に、触れてよいデータの範囲を誰が決めるかを約束に入れる
  • どこから始めるか: オーストラリア・ニュージーランド・英国の数社をデザインパートナーにしたベータから始めている[1]。全顧客に広げる前に、少数の顧客と型を作る
  • 社内で経験を積ませるか: Xeroは社内の業務部門に入る社内FDEを5月から募集している[5]。社内で作った型を顧客向けに使えるかは発表からは分からないが、社内の業務で同じ道具を使い込む場は、顧客に出す前の練習台にもなりうる(編集部の推測)

In Japan as well, there are many cloud services aimed at accounting firms and professional practices, and the fact that each firm's workflows differ matches Xero's explanation. The editorial team believes Xero's judgment—that even a no-code tool goes unused if no one integrates it into the firm's operations—applies largely to SaaS for Japanese professional practices too. Hiring requirements and compensation structures for customer-facing FDEs are summarized in How to run FDE hiring.

What doesn't this article tell us?

This article is based on Xero's announcements, blog posts, and job listings on public job boards; we did not interview Xero. The following points cannot be determined from public information.

  • Who Xforce's FDEs are (whether they come from the internal AI team, are newly hired, or whether there are private job openings)
  • How many FDEs and partner consultants are assigned to a single accounting firm, and how they divide responsibilities
  • Xforce's pricing and which countries it will be offered in after the beta (not stated in the announcement)
  • Whether the Solutions Consultant is the same role as what Xforce calls a partner consultant
  • Whether Xero plans to offer the same structure in Japan

Job postings may end or change without notice. The posting details described here are as of November 16, 2026.

Summary

  • On November 12, 2026, Xero announced Xforce in beta, which sends teams of FDEs and partner consultants to accounting firms
  • Xforce is positioned as human support for integrating XeroForce, a no-code agent-building tool, into firms' operations
  • All four FDE-related job postings Xero has published are internal AI team roles embedded in internal business divisions; none relate to Xforce
  • The Solutions Consultant for accounting firms requires hands-on experience at accounting firms, supporting the model of pairing FDEs with people who understand the business language
  • Companies sending FDEs to customers should decide in advance who to pair them with, the commitment on data scope, and a beta with a small number of customers

Frequently asked questions

QWhat is Xforce by Xero?

Support for accounting firms announced by accounting software company Xero on November 12, 2026. A team of Xero FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) and partner consultants partners with accounting firms to design and build AI workflows tailored to the firm's operations using XeroForce and Xero's APIs. It is in beta as of the announcement.

QWhat is the difference between Xforce and XeroForce?

XeroForce is a product announced in May 2026 for building AI agents in natural language. Xforce is human support in which FDEs and partner consultants work on-site with accounting firms so they can actually use XeroForce in their operations.

QIs Xero hiring FDEs?

As of November 16, 2026, there are four job postings with "Forward Deployed" in the job title or description. The locations are Sydney and Vancouver, and none are roles sent to accounting firms; they are all internal AI team roles that embed within Xero's internal business divisions to build AI workflows.

QWhat is Xero's FDE salary?

In the Vancouver postings, the Senior Forward Deployed Engineer base salary is CAD 180,000–210,000 (plus bonus and equity), and the Lead Engineer - AI FDE base salary is CAD 240,000–260,000. The Sydney postings do not list compensation.

QCan Xforce be used in Japan?

As of the announcement, it is still at the stage of developing the beta with accounting firms in Australia, New Zealand, and the UK, and nothing is written about availability in Japan.
