Summary

This is a job posting on OpenWork by Alphakt, a Japanese consulting firm specializing in AI-driven business process reform (BPR) for major clients in manufacturing, telecom, energy, and finance. The role, Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) / AI×BPR Implementation Engineer, covers requirements definition through implementation of generative AI, LLM, RAG, and AI agent systems, plus internal tooling and in-house products like GISHO and PERKS. Generative AI experience is not required; candidates need roughly three years of web or business system development, Python or TypeScript, and cloud experience. Salary ranges from 6 to 10 million yen including 45 hours of fixed overtime, with performance-based incentives and promotion possible within one year. Work locations include Shibuya, Akasaka, Osaka, client sites, or home, with hybrid remote work and 120+ holidays per year. The company targets 30 billion yen revenue and 1,000 employees by 2030, with IPO ambitions.
