What is a Forward Deployed Banker? | Reading a Job Posting to Understand the Career Shift from Finance Roles to FDE-Type Positions

Original title: Forward Deployed Bankerとは|金融職からFDE型の職への転身を求人票で読む

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-11-04 · 24 min read

Summary

The article reads job postings from finance-focused AI companies to explain the Forward Deployed Banker role, where former bankers and investors embed at client institutions to integrate AI into daily work. Rogo had 18 such openings as of November 4, 2026, split by finance function; Harvey, 9fin, and Hebbia post similar roles requiring 1–6 years of front-office experience. None require production code, but all demand hands-on LLM use and an instinct for automating repetitive tasks—unlike engineer-track FDE roles at OpenAI, Databricks, and Coinbase, which require 5–6+ years of engineering. Rogo revised its Tokyo posting in late October 2026, lowering the experience bar to 3+ years and specifying Japanese-language demos and training. Per Business Insider, base pay (e.g., $115K–$180K at Rogo) often trails investment banking packages, with equity and schedule flexibility as offsets.

Key points

  • Forward Deployed Banker roles put ex-bankers at client firms to embed AI into workflows.
  • Rogo listed 18 Forward Deployed openings as of Nov 4, 2026, organized by finance function.
  • These roles don't require production code but do require real LLM use and automation instincts.
  • Engineer-track FDE roles at OpenAI, Databricks, Coinbase require 5–6+ years of engineering.
  • Rogo's Tokyo posting was revised to 3+ years experience with Japanese-language demos and training.

Key takeaways from this article

  • 01 Forward Deployed Banker is a role in which finance-focused AI companies send former bankers and former investors to customers' financial institutions to embed AI into their daily operations; as of November 4, 2026, Rogo has 18 job postings of this type.
  • 02 The job postings from Rogo, Harvey, 9fin, and Hebbia require 1 to 6 years of experience at financial institutions or professional services firms; they do not require writing production code, but do require using LLMs in one's own work and having a sense for automating repetitive tasks.
  • 03 For FDE postings for engineer roles covering finance, Databricks and Coinbase require 5 to 6 or more years of engineering experience, and OpenAI also lists 5 or more years among its criteria for suitable candidates; OpenAI and Coinbase additionally require knowledge of financial operations and systems.
  • 04 In late October 2026, Rogo rewrote its Forward Deployed Banker, Japan job posting, lowering the experience requirement from 4 or more years to 3 or more years and explicitly stating that demos and training would be conducted in Japanese.
  • 05 According to Business Insider, the base salary for Rogo's New York Forward Deployed Banker is $115,000 to $180,000, lower than the total compensation (up to $400,000) of a second-year associate at a major investment bank.

投資銀行やPEファンドで働く人のあいだで、「Forward Deployed」と名の付く職への転身が一つの選択肢になりつつあります。金融向けAIのRogoは「Forward Deployed Banker」、法律AIのHarveyは「Forward Deployed Finance Engineer」という職種名で、元バンカーや元投資家を採用しています[2][6]。Business Insiderは、AIが金融の若手に新しいキャリアの分かれ道を開きつつあると報じています[12]。FDE(Forward Deployed Engineer/フォワードデプロイドエンジニア)の仕組みを、エンジニアではなく金融の専門家に当てはめた職です。

In this article, the editorial team compares, in the original text, job postings seeking finance specialists from Rogo, Harvey, 9fin, and Hebbia, as well as FDE postings for engineer roles covering finance (OpenAI, Databricks, Coinbase), all retrieved on November 4, 2026. We also examine the realities of compensation and working style from a Business Insider article that interviewed a former banker who moved to Rogo. The content is aimed both at people considering a move from front-office finance roles and at engineers working on financial systems. For an overall picture of how to approach a career change to FDE, also read the FDE Career Change Guide; for those from consulting backgrounds, see From IT Consulting to FDE.

What kind of role is a Forward Deployed Banker?

Forward Deployed Bankerとは、金融機関向けのAI製品を売る会社が、元バンカーや元投資家を顧客の前に立たせ、AIを相手の業務に組み込ませる職です。Rogoの求人票は、Forward Deployedのチームを「元バンカーと元投資家で構成され、一流の金融機関に日々の業務と長期の戦略でAIをどう使うかを助言するチーム」と説明しています。ソリューションアーキテクトが技術面の合意(technical win)を取りつけるのに対し、この職は金融面の合意(financial win)を取りつける、という書き方です[1]。

この系統の職は、Rogoだけのものではありません。Harveyは2026年7月に資産運用会社向けのBenchmarkを買収し、投資チームが案件を回すための新しい製品「Harvey Deals」を作りました。Forward Deployed Finance Engineerは、そのHarvey Dealsを顧客に入れるチームで、Harveyは「法務側のLegal Engineeringチームに対応する、投資側のチーム」と位置づけています[5][14]。債券市場向けの9finと、投資家向けのHebbiaも、Forward Deployed BankerとForward Deployed Investorを募集しています[7][8]。

エンジニア職のFDEとの違いは、求人票が最初に求めるものにあります。エンジニアのFDEは、顧客の業務を学びながら本番のシステムを作ります。Forward Deployed Bankerは、顧客と同じ業務をしてきた経験を土台に、製品のデモ、試験導入、研修、使い方の設計を受け持ちます。HarveyのFinance Engineerの求人票は、この仕事を「デスクでやっていた仕事そのもの」と書いています[5]。

What do former bankers give up, and what do they gain?

報酬は、多くの場合で下がります。Business Insiderが2026年8月にRogoの社員を取材した記事は、ニューヨークのForward Deployed Bankerの基本給が11.5万〜18万ドル(求人票の記載)で、これに株式と賞与が加わる可能性があると書いています。一方で、同じ記事の中で人材紹介会社の経営者は、大手の投資銀行の2年目のアソシエイトは総報酬で最大40万ドルを得ると述べています[12]。

"It is challenging to try and pull the top investment banker who's being paid an incredibly high bonus, being well taken care of. It's going to be really difficult to pull that person out unless they have a real passion for AI"

Brianne Sterling (Head of Investment Banking at Selby Jennings) — Business Insider (republished on Yahoo Finance, August 10, 2026)

[12]

人材紹介会社の共同創業者は同じ記事で、現金だけでは人を引きつけにくく、株式の価値が鍵になると述べています。同じ記事で、Hebbiaの人事責任者は、Forward Deployedの金融職には賞与があるものの、株式を含むことも一因で伝統的な金融より「かなり小さい」と述べ、現金に慣れた元バンカーには報酬を「頭の中でまったく違う形で捉え直す」必要があると話しています[12]。Hebbiaの求人票も、賞与込みの報酬を$90,000–$160,000(加点要件を満たす人は$160,000–$225,000)とし、これに株式を加える形で示しています[8]。

What moved this past week?

2026年10月28日から11月4日までの1週間にも、この系統の求人は増えています。Harveyはプライベートクレジットの職をロンドンにも出し、Rogoはロンドンに投資家向けの職を足しました。CoinbaseとDatabricksの金融担当のFDE求人も、採用管理システム上の更新日が10月30日になっています[5][3][10][11]。

  • 2026.04 RogoがシリーズDで1億6,000万ドルを調達 調達した資金で、顧客企業の中に入るforward-deployed bankersとエンジニアを増やし、欧州・中東・アフリカとアジアに広げると書いた[13]
  • 2026.10 HarveyがForward Deployed Finance Engineerを3件掲載 10月6日、ニューヨークでバイアウト・プライベートクレジット・不動産の3職種(採用管理システム上の公開日。編集部の求人スナップショット)[6]

Harveyの求人票は、取引のある資産運用会社を150社超、毎月分析する文書を1億7,800万件超と書いています[5]。7月の買収の発表では125社超だったので[14]、資産運用会社向けの事業を広げながら、その導入を担う人を部門ごとに足していることになります。

"This capital lets us push further on every front: deeper integrations, more forward-deployed bankers and engineers embedded within the firms we serve, and continued expansion across EMEA and Asia."

Gabe Stengel (Rogo co-founder) — Rogo official announcement (April 29, 2026)

What does this mean for finance professionals and engineers in Japan?

For people working in Japan, the entry point depends on your background. The relevant openings differ for those in front-office finance roles, engineers who have built financial systems, and those who have worked in M&A or valuation at the Big Four and similar firms.

If you are preparing to move from a front-office finance role, working backward from the requirements in job postings, the order is as follows (editorial summary).

  • 01自分の部署の業務の流れ(案件の選別、モデル、資料作り、委員会)を、作業の単位まで書き出す。9finの求人票は、顧客の業務を分解して、AIを入れる場所を見つけることを最初の仕事にしている[7]
  • 02その中の繰り返しの作業を、いま使えるLLMで実際に置き換えてみて、できたこととできなかったことを言葉にする。Rogoは使ったうえでの限界についての自分の見方を、Hebbiaは何を作り試したかを、9finはAI製品を業務で使った経験を求めている[1][8][7]
  • 03社内のAIやDXの検討会、ベンダーの選定に手を挙げる。9finは、こうした社内の取り組みへの参加を、技術への関心の例に挙げている[7]

Summary

Forward Deployed Banker is a role at financial AI companies in which people who have done the same work as customers stand in front of those customers. Unlike the engineer FDE, the entry point is different: it tests business experience and the ability to make full use of AI.

  • As of November 4, 2026, Rogo has posted 18 "Forward Deployed" job listings, recruiting people with experience by department, including investment banking, investors, asset management, equity research, and wealth management (compiled by the editorial team)
  • Harvey・9fin・Hebbiaも同じ系統の職を募集しており、求める経験は1〜6年。本番のコードは必須ではないが、LLMを使いこなし、手作業を自動化する感覚を求めている[5][7][8]
  • OpenAI・Databricks・Coinbaseの金融担当のFDEは、5〜6年以上のエンジニアリング経験を求めており(OpenAIは向いている人の条件として)、金融系のシステムを作ってきたエンジニアの入口になる(編集部の見方)[9][10][11]
  • RogoのForward Deployed Banker, Japanは10月下旬に書き換えられ、3年以上のフロント職、日本語での研修、証券会社から資産運用会社までの担当を明記した[1]
  • 報酬は投資銀行の総報酬より下がることが多く、移った社員や採用担当者は、株式の価値と働く時間の自由を挙げている[12]

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat is a Forward Deployed Banker?

It is a role at companies building AI products for financial institutions, in which former bankers or former investors are sent into customers' financial institutions to embed AI into daily operations. Rogo, Hebbia, 9fin, and others are hiring. Rogo's job posting explains that while a solution architect secures technical agreement, this role secures financial agreement (financial win).

QDoes a Forward Deployed Banker need programming skills?

As of November 4, 2026, the job postings of Rogo, Harvey, 9fin, and Hebbia do not require writing production code. Instead, they seek things like using LLMs in your own work, experience using AI products in business operations, and a feel for automating repetitive manual tasks. Harvey treats Python and SQL experience as a plus.

QAre there Forward Deployed Banker openings in Japan?

As of November 4, 2026, Rogo is hiring a Forward Deployed Banker, Japan (Tokyo). It requires 3 or more years of experience in front-office roles at top-tier investment banks, PE firms, consultancies, or hedge funds, native or business-level Japanese, and English sufficient for professional work. No compensation is listed.

QWhat is the salary of a Forward Deployed Banker?

In New York postings, Rogo lists a base salary of $115K to $180K (with equity and bonus), 9fin lists $128K to $173K (with equity), and Hebbia lists $90K to $160K including bonus ($160K to $225K with equity for those meeting plus requirements). Harvey's Forward Deployed Finance Engineer (New York) is $254.6K to $350K (with equity). All are figures from job postings, not actual amounts paid.

QWhich openings are closest for a finance engineer wanting to become an FDE?

OpenAI's Forward Deployed Engineer, Financial Services (New York) and Databricks' Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer - Financial Services are close entry points. Databricks requires 6 or more years of data or software experience, and OpenAI lists, among the criteria for a good fit, 5 or more years of engineering or technical implementation experience and experience with financial workflows. Databricks' posting does not require financial knowledge, but the role serves customers in the financial industry.
