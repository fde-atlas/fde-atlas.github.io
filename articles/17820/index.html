Company Profile: Cursor (Anysphere)

Original title: 公司档案：Cursor（Anysphere）——AI 编码工具公司的 FDE 岗位

FDE中国 · 2026-09-04 · 1 min read

Summary

Anysphere, the company behind the AI coding tool Cursor, is hiring Forward Deployed Engineers, based on application pages recorded by the job-listing aggregator Simplify. Third-party interview tracking shows the process starts with recruiter screens, and the technical rounds are essentially the same as those for regular software engineers. The editors argue this shows the company treats FDEs as engineers who write code rather than demo-focused pre-sales staff, with coding ability as the first bar. They advise candidates to deeply understand Cursor's enterprise use cases, and note that AI coding tool companies hiring FDEs shows the role now extends beyond models and industry applications into delivering the development workflow itself.

Key points

  • Anysphere is hiring FDEs for Cursor; listing data comes from Simplify.
  • FDE technical interviews are nearly identical to SWE rounds.
  • The company treats FDEs as coding engineers, not pre-sales roles.
  • Candidates should master Cursor's enterprise use cases before interviewing.
  • AI coding tools hiring FDEs shows the role now covers development workflow delivery.

Editor's Note FDEChina Editorial Team

Notably, the interview design: the FDE technical rounds are essentially identical to those for SWE, meaning the company treats FDEs as "engineers who can write code" rather than "sales engineers who can demo" — coding ability remains the first gate.

For job seekers: FDE roles at developer tools companies typically require you to be a heavy user of the tools yourself; thoroughly understanding Cursor's enterprise use cases before interviewing will make you more convincing. For ecosystem observers: the fact that AI coding tools companies also set up FDE roles shows that the role's boundaries have extended beyond models and industry applications to the delivery of the development process itself.
