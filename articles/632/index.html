Understanding AI: recognizes and parses input content, such as OCR image recognition and speech transcription, belonging to discriminative models; Generative AI: creates entirely new content based on prompts and reference materials, which is AIGC. Text generation: LLM large language models, writing articles, scripts, and proposals.
