Summary

This post presents key excerpts from Picogrid's Forward Deployed Engineer job description, captured on November 2, 2026, and compiled by Shenzhen-based 真实智元 for research purposes. Picogrid builds infrastructure unifying sensors, autonomy, and operators, with deployments in active operations worldwide. The role sits at the intersection of product, engineering, and customers. Requirements include 3+ years combining software engineering with customer-facing work (solutions engineering, sales engineering, or developer relations), full-stack development skills, and knowledge of distributed systems, autonomy platforms, data pipelines, and edge software. Candidates should handle hybrid cloud, on-prem, and edge environments and communication APIs such as REST, gRPC, GraphQL, and MQTT. The listing notes the excerpts are informational only and not a hiring commitment.

Key points

  • Picogrid is hiring an FDE working across product, engineering, and customers.
  • Requires 3+ years mixing software engineering with customer-facing roles.
  • Expects full-stack skills plus distributed systems, data pipelines, and edge software knowledge.
  • Must handle hybrid cloud/on-prem/edge setups and REST, gRPC, GraphQL, MQTT APIs.
  • Snapshot dated 2026-11-02; excerpts are for research, not a hiring promise.
