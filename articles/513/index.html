Summary

This is a CSDN column by THS_Allen covering AI engineering across agents, model infrastructure, and deployment practice. Agent-related posts explain task loops, MCP protocol evolution, AgentScope 2.0 internals, sandboxing, and observability. Infrastructure posts cover vLLM's PagedAttention, QLoRA fine-tuning, DeepSeek-V3's MoE/MLA design, RoCE networking, and Kubernetes GPU scheduling. One post addresses a common hiring mistake around FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers): treating a team-based way of working as a single job posting for an all-in-one engineer who knows business, product, code, and AI—a role that is nearly impossible to fill and likely to burn out whoever takes it. The column suits readers focused on practical AI systems engineering.

Key points

  • Column spans agent engineering, inference, fine-tuning, and GPU cluster scheduling topics.
  • Agent posts cover task loops, MCP's three key changes, and AgentScope 2.0 source analysis.
  • Infra posts detail vLLM, QLoRA, DeepSeek-V3, RoCE networks, and K8s GPU virtualization.
  • One post warns against hiring an FDE as a single do-everything engineer instead of a team model.
  • Author argues enterprises should retain business context and data pipelines as models and frameworks change.
