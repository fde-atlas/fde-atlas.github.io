OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Experience (Rejected, Jul 2026)

Top Tech Transition · 2026-10-12 · 1 min read

Summary

A candidate shares their July 2026 OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer interview experience on Top Tech Transition, which ended in rejection. The process had two rounds: a recruiter screen (including whether they had used the OpenAI API) and a phone screen asking about the most complex project they led, plus customer-focused questions. They were rejected for not demonstrating enough backend experience. Their main tip: OpenAI now favors agentic architectures over deterministic approaches, with interviewers saying they prefer to let the model work on its own, so candidates should prepare projects reflecting that. They found both recruiter and interviewer friendly.

Key points

  • Two rounds only: recruiter screen and phone screen.
  • Rejected for insufficient backend experience shown.
  • Phone screen focused on a complex led project.
  • OpenAI favors agentic architecture over deterministic methods.
  • Candidate found the interviewers friendly.

Interview process

A recruiter reached out to me on LinkedIn for the forward deployed engineering role. Pretty easy recruiter screen with typical questions. The next stage was a phone screen, where I was asked to describe a project I drove e2e and several customer-oriented questions. Was rejected for not showing enough backend experience in the interview.

Interview rounds · 2

  • 1

    Recruiter screen

    • Q1. Have you used the OpenAI API before?
  • 2

    Phone screen

    • Q1. Tell me about the most complex project you have led.

Tips from the candidate

They are looking for agentic architecture and have since leaned away from using deterministic methods in the architecture. My interviewer said they now try to "step out of the way and let the model do it's thing", so prepare a project accordingly.

Company culture

The recruiter and the interviewer were both very nice! Seems like a good place to work!
