The Product OS Handbook: 65 Chapters, Five Seats

Falkster.AI · 2026-11-15 · 11 min read

Summary

Falkster.AI's Product OS Handbook is a free, 65-chapter guide for AI-era product organizations, organized into a shared core plus five seats: CPO, Product, Design, Product marketing, and forward deployed engineering. Its central argument: when building is cheap, outcomes hinge on the customer-to-adoption loop, which needs one owner—the CPO. Notable positions include replacing PRDs with evals, killing roadmaps in favor of a live bet portfolio, and treating engineering's job as building substrate (guardrails, evals, isolated deploys). The FDE section argues FDEs are product builders whose work starts after the contract is signed, must feed back into the roadmap, and only works economically when reporting lines, pricing, and the deployment-to-product loop are set up correctly.

Key points

  • Free 65-chapter handbook structured as a shared core plus five role-based seats.
  • Core claim: the customer-to-adoption loop needs one owner, the CPO.
  • Replaces PRDs with evals and roadmaps with a live bet portfolio.
  • FDE section: the job starts where the contract is signed, unlike sales engineering.
  • FDE economics work only when the loop closes and pricing ties revenue to outcomes.

About the handbook

  • What is The Product OS Handbook?
  • The handbook of The Product OS, one operating system for the AI-era product org. 65 chapters, grouped as the core every seat shares and then one section for each seat: CPO, Product, Design, Product marketing, and forward deployed engineering. Every chapter is free.
  • How is the handbook organized?
  • By the OS. The core comes first: the manifesto, the operating model, substrate-first engineering, the eval as the spec, killing the roadmap, and the impact loop. Then each seat's chapters, in reading order. A chapter belongs to exactly one section.
  • Where is Product Builder OS?
  • Product Builder OS, the six-skill standard for the Product Builder role, is the first chapter of the Product section. The Product seat page links it with the assessment, the course, and the career path.
  • Where are the Design chapters?
  • The 14 Design chapters are the Design section of this handbook. They keep their /design/<slug> addresses, and the Design seat page lists them in book order.
