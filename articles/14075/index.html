Our Network & Sourcing Tools

Forward Deploying · 2026 · 3 min read

Summary

This article explains how the recruiting firm Forward Deploying reaches Forward Deployed and AI engineers who rarely apply to jobs. It runs three channels in parallel: partner talent networks that make warm introductions with the engineer's consent, an AI sourcing platform with over 80 million profiles, and LinkedIn Recruiter. An internal market engine tracks open FDE and AI roles daily, and technical footprints on GitHub, Hugging Face and papers provide evidence of real work. Every tool only suggests; a person reviews each result. Referrals never skip screening and are validated with the same scorecard as other candidates. Clients meet three to five people, each with a dossier, and referrers receive a written reward if a placement succeeds. Data handling follows UK and EU GDPR.

Key points

  • Three parallel channels reach engineers who are not applying anywhere.
  • An AI sourcing platform covers 80M+ profiles, with human review of every result.
  • Referrals require the engineer's consent and never skip scorecard validation.
  • Clients interview three to five candidates, each with a dossier.
  • Data sharing follows UK and EU GDPR, and referrers get written rewards.

Forward Deployed and AI engineers who have shipped rarely apply to jobs. We reach them three ways at once: introductions through partner talent networks, an AI sourcing platform with 80M+ profiles, and LinkedIn Recruiter. Then an engineer decides who is worth your time.

What we search with

Named, so you know exactly how your search is run. Every tool suggests; none decides.

Partner talent networks

Technical communities, independent recruiters and talent networks in several countries who know Forward Deployed and AI engineers first-hand and introduce them to us, with their consent.

AI sourcing platform

Access to 80M+ professional profiles through a partnership with an AI sourcing platform, searched in plain language and ranked against your scorecard. A person reviews every result.

LinkedIn Recruiter

For mapping teams, titles and career paths, and for direct, personal outreach to people who are not looking.

Our market engine

Our own system reads every open Forward Deployed and AI engineering role at the companies we track, every day. It powers our salary data and tells us who is hiring your profile.

The technical footprint

GitHub, Hugging Face, papers and engineering communities: evidence of what someone has actually built.

Warm introductions

We do not cold-message the people our partners know.

When a partner network knows an engineer who fits, we ask the partner to make the introduction, with the engineer's agreement. A warm introduction from someone who has shipped with them gets a reply, and comes with context no CV has.

  • Reach. Engineers who are not applying anywhere.
  • Signal. First-hand context, which we check again in our field references.
  • Speed. Warm conversations move faster than cold outreach.

How a referral becomes a hire

  • You brief us on the deployment problem, not a list of frameworks.
  • We activate partners whose engineers have done that work, alongside our own search.
  • The engineer agrees to be put forward for your specific role. Nothing moves without that yes.
  • We validate every referral with the same scorecard as everyone else.
  • You meet three to five people, each with a dossier.

For engineers

Refer a Forward Deployed Engineer. Get rewarded.

If we place someone you introduced, we pay you a referral reward, confirmed in writing before we approach them. Free for the engineer, always.

Questions about our network

Which networks do you partner with?

Technical talent networks, engineering communities and independent recruiters in several countries who know Forward Deployed and AI engineers first-hand. We add partners only after working with them on real searches, and we name them only with their permission.

How is candidate data handled between partners?

A partner shares a profile with us only with the engineer's agreement, and we share nothing with an employer without the engineer's yes for that specific role. Data moves under written agreements that follow the UK and EU GDPR.

Which sourcing tools do you use?

An AI sourcing platform we access through a partnership, with 80M+ professional profiles, and LinkedIn Recruiter. Both suggest; a person reviews every result and decides who to approach.

Does a referral skip the screening?

No. A referral earns a faster first conversation; every candidate goes through the same engineer-led validation and scorecard.

Do you pay referral rewards?

Yes. If we place an engineer someone introduced to us, the referrer receives a reward, confirmed in writing before we approach the engineer.

Can my network become a partner?

If you run a technical community or talent network with real Forward Deployed or AI engineers in it, write to talent@forwarddeploying.com.
