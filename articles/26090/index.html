When Agents Build, the Scarce FDE Skill Is Leaving

Falkster.AI · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

Summary

Writing as an FDE at Sycamore, Milos Mandic argues that when agents do most of the building, the scarce skill shifts from staying to leaving. His State of FDE 2026 survey (24 responses) found seven respondents whose delivered systems stayed with them and twelve who spend over a tenth of each week keeping shipped systems running. What remains scarce are the two documents agents don't write: the day-60 page (promise, customer eval signal, watch list, named customer contact, recall rule) and the handback packet (delta, rows, reversals, count) with product's written decision due in two weeks. The metric to track: blank keys lines divided by systems shipped.

Key points

  • Agents make building fast, so the scarce FDE skill is leaving, not staying.
  • Survey of 24 FDEs: seven keep maintaining systems after handover.
  • Twelve respondents spend over 10% of their week on delivered systems.
  • The scarce artifacts are the day-60 page and the handback packet.
  • Track blank keys lines divided by systems shipped.

Full text

THE SHORT ANSWER

The leaving. Milos Mandic, who edits FDE Hub, joined Sycamore as a forward deployed engineer and wrote on November 10, 2026 that much of his day goes to directing agents, that the pace astonished him, and that 'speed like this changes which part of the job is scarce,' which he is still working out. His own State of FDE 2026 survey, 24 responses, already answered as he reported it: seven said the systems they build stay with them after handover, and twelve spend more than a tenth of their week keeping delivered systems running. When the build is fast, what stays scarce is the two pages agents do not write: the day-60 page (promise, signal with the customer's own eval rows and the go-live pass rate as the floor, watch list, keys, the named person at the customer who can change a prompt without calling you, plus a recall rule) and the handback packet (delta, rows, reversals, count), with product's written decision owed in two weeks. The number to track is blank keys lines divided by systems shipped.
