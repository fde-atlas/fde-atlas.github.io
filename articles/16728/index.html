Summary

Marina Wyss's blog offers practical career and learning guides for engineers moving into AI and machine learning. Notable posts include a primer on ML fundamentals for AI engineers (how models learn, overfitting, precision and recall, embeddings, transformers, RLHF), a 2026 roadmap for becoming a Forward Deployed Engineer based on analysis of 100 FDE job postings, a three-month plan for software engineers transitioning to AI engineering, and hands-on tutorials for building production AI agents on Google Cloud and getting started with AWS for machine learning. She also shares her end-to-end workflow for building AI projects from scratch, covering scoping, evaluation, monitoring and deployment.

Key points

  • ML fundamentals primer covering training, overfitting, metrics, transformers and RLHF.
  • FDE 2026 roadmap derived from 100 real job postings.
  • Three-month plan for software engineers to become AI engineers.
  • Tutorials on production AI agents on Google Cloud and AWS ML basics.
  • End-to-end workflow for building AI projects from scratch.
