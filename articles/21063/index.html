What Is an In-House FDE? Understanding Roles, Requirements, and How to Measure Results Through 7 Job Postings Including Stripe

Original title: 社内FDEとは|Stripeなど求人7件で読む職務・要件・成果の測り方

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-11-12 · 32 min read

Summary

The article analyzes seven job postings for internal FDEs—engineers embedded in a company's own departments to build and embed AI tools—published by Stripe, Coinbase, PayPay Card, STRACT, and YUIDEA as of November 2026. The key difference among postings is who builds the tools: Stripe, Coinbase, and STRACT require hands-on development experience, while YUIDEA requires no programming and instead directs internal and external AI engineers. Most postings measure success through time saved and adoption rather than tool counts. The article also documents how Stripe rewrote its posting in late October 2026, shifting from coaching marketers toward self-sufficiency to maintaining tools and proving impact via before/after comparisons, and lists five decisions companies should make before hiring an internal FDE.

Key points

  • The biggest difference among the seven postings is who builds the tools.
  • Success is mostly measured by time saved and adoption, not tool counts.
  • Stripe rewrote its posting in October 2026 for Sales Development with before/after metrics.
  • Coinbase's finance FDE pays the most ($218,025–$256,500) and requires SOX-ready auditability.
  • Companies should decide five things before hiring: builder, coverage, maintenance, metrics, and governance.

Key takeaways of this article

  • 01 An in-house FDE is a role that embeds into a company's own departments to build AI tools and make them stick; as of November 12, 2026, Stripe, Coinbase, PayPay Card, STRACT, and YUIDEA are hiring for it.
  • 02 The biggest difference separating the 7 postings is who builds the tools: at Stripe, Coinbase, and STRACT the person builds them themselves, while YUIDEA does not require programming experience and has internal or external AI engineers build them.
  • 03 How results are measured is written concretely in the job postings: for Stripe's marketing-focused role, the number of workflows permanently changed and the degree to which the cohort starts work from AI tools; for Coinbase's HR-focused internship, time saved, reduction in errors, and usage rate.
  • 04 In late October 2026, Stripe changed the job title under the same job ID from marketing-focused to sales development-focused, rewriting the role from guiding employees to autonomy to a role that maintains the built tools in a maintainable form and demonstrates impact through before-and-after comparison.
  • 05 Compensation varies widely across postings: Coinbase's finance-focused role pays an annual base salary of $218,025–$256,500, STRACT pays ¥10–15 million, and YUIDEA pays ¥4.76–6 million.

FDE(Forward Deployed Engineer/フォワードデプロイドエンジニア)は、顧客の現場に入り込んで自社の製品を導入するエンジニアを指す言葉として広まりました。その型を、顧客ではなく自社の社員に向ける求人が出ています。Stripeは自社のマーケティング部門と営業開発部門に張り付く「Forward Deployed AI Accelerator」を2職種[1][2]、Coinbaseは財務部門と人事部門に入り込むFDEを募集し[11][3]、日本でもPayPayカード・STRACT・YUIDEAが、自社の部門に入り込むFDEを募集しています[4][5][6]。

For companies considering hiring someone to drive internal AI adoption, the first question is likely: "If we place an in-house FDE, who should we assign what tasks to, and how should we measure results?" In this article, we read the full text of 7 in-house FDE job postings published as of November 12, 2026, and answer by comparing who builds, who they serve, how results are measured, requirements, and compensation. For how to build customer-facing FDE teams, see FDE Organizational Design; for a list of companies hiring FDEs in Japan, see FDE Jobs and Hiring Companies List.

What does an in-house FDE do?

社内FDEは、自社の事業部門やコーポレート部門に入り込み、業務を理解したうえで、AIを使った道具や自動化を作り、現場に定着させる職種です。米国の人材紹介会社Christian & Timbersは、社内FDE(internal FDE)を「企業が直接雇い、組織の中でAIシステムを作って改善するフルタイムのエンジニア」と説明しています[8]。顧客向けのFDEとの違いは、相手が社外の顧客か、同じ会社の同僚かという点です。

Why did Stripe place an FDE in the marketing department?

Stripeは、マーケティング組織の働き方そのものを変えるためにこのチームを作ったと書いています。求人票によれば、目標はAIを時々使う道具ではなく、すべての仕事の出発点にすることです[1]。マーケターの中にはすでに自分でダッシュボードを作ったり、何日もかかる作業を縮めるエージェントを作ったりしている人がいて、FDAチーム(Forward Deployed AI Acceleratorsの略)はその成功例を組織全体に広げる役割だとしています[1]。

"Your measure of success is the number of workflows you've permanently transformed and the extent to which those in your cohort start any task with an AI tool."

How is Coinbase embedding them in finance and HR?

Coinbaseは、財務部門には経験5年以上のエンジニアを、人事部門には範囲を区切ったインターンを置いています[11][3]。財務向けの「Forward Deployed Engineer, Finance」はCTO室に所属したまま財務部門に入り込む「創設メンバーのエンジニア」で、財務の関係者と、社内のAI基盤を保守するエンジニアリングチームをつなぐ役だと書かれています[11]。

Open Position

Coinbase

Forward Deployed Engineer, Finance [Office of the CTO]

  • Location
  • Remote within the US
  • Compensation (as stated in the job posting)
  • Annual base salary $218,025 - $256,500 USD (excluding equity and bonus)
  • Date the job posting was retrieved

Responsibilities

  • Embed with FP&A and finance leaders to understand workflows such as close, variance analysis, and liquidity stress testing on the ground before writing code
  • Prototype quickly on top of the company's existing internal AI infrastructure, and propose new builds afterward
  • Partner with FP&A and finance VPs to own a finance automation roadmap and translate complex finance workflows into engineering requirements
  • Decide whether finance tools should be built in-house, adopted from other teams, purchased externally, or discontinued
  • Ensure automated finance outputs are traceable, verifiable, and explainable to auditors. Design with SOX compliance, segregation of duties, and human review as baseline assumptions
  • Drive FP&A and finance teams to actually use the tools through workshops and prototype demos

Requirements

  • 5+ years of software development and a track record of shipping production systems in ambiguous, stakeholder-heavy environments
  • Experience deploying LLM-based applications (including prompt design, evaluation frameworks, safeguards against incorrect answers, and human review design for critical finance outputs)
  • Ability to embed with non-technical domain experts, understand complex workflows, and translate them into engineering requirements
  • Familiarity with ERP data flows (NetSuite, Workday, Anaplan), reconciliation concepts, trial balances, and SOX-controlled environments. CPA certification not required
  • Experience as an FDE, in solution engineering, or as an embedded technical advisor

Key takeaway from this posting: Because the tools built by finance-focused internal FDEs touch financial close and regulatory filings, auditable traceability and human review are built into the design assumptions. The role is also entrusted with authority to decide "build, buy, or kill" before building, a feature rarely seen in customer-facing FDE roles. This posting also lists the highest compensation among the seven.

How is the in-house FDE role designed at Japanese companies?

日本の3社は、同じ「社内FDE」でも設計がはっきり違います。PayPayカードは非エンジニア部門を相手に、作ることと全社の推進・研修を1人に任せます。STRACTは開発経験のあるエンジニアを業務部門に入れ、オペレーションを巻き取らせます。YUIDEAは作る仕事を社内外のAIエンジニアに任せ、本人には業務の理解と実装のディレクションを求めます[4][5][6]。

  • Location
  • Hybrid work between the Tokyo or Fukuoka office and home
  • Employment type

  • Compensation (as stated in the job posting)
  • Annual salary system (no amount specified)

Responsibilities

  • Embed yourself in the day-to-day operations of each internal department and drive everything from identifying and structuring issues to designing and building AI agents and automated workflows, deploying them to production, driving adoption, and measuring impact
  • Build AI agents and business automation workflows using tools such as Dify, Gemini Enterprise, and Amazon Quick, and design integrations with internal tools such as Slack and BigQuery
  • Drive and manage cross-company projects and report to management
  • Plan and run workshops and training on generative AI, and maintain internal guidelines
  • Support the development of rules for AI use and collaborate with the security and compliance departments

Requirements

  • Foundational knowledge of IT architecture such as API integrations, data structures, authentication, and web applications, plus an understanding of the development cycle
  • Experience applying generative AI to real work and turning it into reusable patterns using prompts and custom instructions
  • Experience structuring business workflows and developing improvement plans (BPR) that incorporate AI and automation
  • Experience working with multiple stakeholders to advance projects

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience building agents or implementing workflow automation with Dify, Gemini Enterprise, n8n, Make, and similar tools
  • Experience extracting and processing data with Python, JavaScript, SQL, or similar, or building simple systems through API integrations
  • Experience embedding technology into organizational culture through workshops and internal training

Key points of this job posting: The background section states that the company is moving generative AI adoption from "pilot deployment" to "embedding into business processes," and that requests for AI use from each department are increasing rapidly. The required qualifications center on foundational IT knowledge and business improvement experience, while programming is listed as a preferred qualification. By leaning on low-code platforms for the tools being built, the role is designed so that non-engineers can also be candidates.

What should companies decide before placing an internal FDE?

Working backward from the seven job postings, there are five things to decide before placing one. Items not written in the posting end up being shouldered alone by the internal FDE after joining.

  • 01誰が作るか。 本人が作るなら開発経験を必須にし(STRACT・Coinbaseの財務向け)[5][11]、ローコードで作るなら基盤を先に決め(PayPayカード)[4]、社内外のエンジニアに作らせるならディレクションと発注の経験を求める(YUIDEA)[6]
  • 02誰を何人受け持つか。 Stripeのマーケティング向けは25〜45人と人数を書いています[1]。部門単位にするか、業務フロー単位にするかも決めておく
  • 03作ったものを誰が持ち続けるか。 Stripeのセールスデベロップメント向けは本番の仕組みや機密データが絡むときにエンジニアリングなどのチームと組むとし[2]、マネーフォワードは本番化と運用保守を別のチームに渡すとしていました[10]。Coinbaseの財務向けは、作るか買うかやめるかの判断まで任せています[11]。決めずに置くと、社内FDEが作った道具の保守に追われる
  • 04成果を何で測るか。 削減時間、利用率、前後比較のどれを使い、誰に報告するか。Coinbaseの人事向けは指標を本人が決めるところから仕事に含めています[3]
  • 05ルールと統制をどこが持つか。 PayPayカードはAI利用のルール整備とセキュリティ・コンプライアンス部門との連携を職務に入れ[4]、Coinbaseの財務向けはSOX対応と職務の分離を設計の前提に置いています[11]。社内のデータを扱う以上、承認の窓口を先に決めておく

Summary

  • An internal FDE is a role that embeds into your own company's departments, understands the business, builds AI tools, and makes them stick. As of November 12, 2026, Stripe, Coinbase, PayPay Card, STRACT, and YUIDEA are hiring
  • The biggest difference separating the 7 listings is who builds. Listings where the person builds set development experience as a minimum requirement, while listings where they have it built weigh experience in grasping business issues and driving projects more heavily
  • Most listings measure results by time saved and usage/adoption. Adoption rate alone cannot distinguish genuine use from forced use, so combine it with before/after comparison metrics
  • When Stripe rewrote its role for sales development, it shifted the focus from a teaching role to one that maintains what is built in a sustainable form and demonstrates impact through before/after comparisons
  • Companies placing internal FDEs should state in the job listing five things: who builds, how many people they handle, who maintains what is built, how results are measured, and who owns the rules and governance for AI use

Frequently Asked Questions

Q What is an internal FDE?

A role that embeds not in client companies but in your own company's business or corporate departments, understands the business, and builds AI-powered tools and automation, making them stick in the field. As of November 2026, Stripe's Forward Deployed AI Accelerator, Coinbase's finance-focused FDE and HR-focused FDE internship, PayPay Card, STRACT, YUIDEA, and others are hiring.

Q Is programming experience required for an internal FDE?

It depends on the listing. Stripe, Coinbase's finance-focused role, and STRACT assume you build things yourself, setting proficiency with AI development tools or development experience as minimum requirements. YUIDEA's internal FDE does not require programming or AI development experience; the role involves directing internal and external AI engineers. PayPay Card requires basic IT knowledge, with programming as a preferred qualification.

Q What is the salary for an internal FDE?

As of job listings on November 12, 2026, STRACT offers 10-15 million yen, YUIDEA offers 4.76-6 million yen (assuming an average of 20 hours of overtime per month), and PayPay Card uses an annual salary system with no stated amount. In the US, Coinbase's finance-focused role offers an annual base salary of $218,025-$256,500, and Stripe's marketing-focused role offers $145,200-$217,800.

Q How are internal FDE results evaluated?

The most common criteria in job listings are time saved and whether the built tools are actually used (adoption rate and stickiness). Stripe's marketing-focused role counts the number of business workflows permanently changed and the degree to which work starts from AI tools, while Coinbase's HR-focused role cites time saved, reduced errors, and adoption rate. Since adoption rate alone cannot distinguish genuine use from forced use, combining it with before/after comparisons of work is realistic.

Q What should a company decide before placing an internal FDE?

Five things: who builds (the person themselves, low-code, or internal/external engineers), who and how many people they will handle, who maintains what is built, how results are measured, and who owns the rules and governance for AI use. If you place an internal FDE without deciding on maintenance of what is built, they will end up bogged down in tool maintenance and unable to keep up with new business needs.
