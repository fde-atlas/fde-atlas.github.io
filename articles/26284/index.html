Forward Deployed Engineer | Phonely | San Francisco, CA

Zero G Talent · 2026-11-10 · 4 min read

Summary

Phonely, whose product is an AI receptionist that answers business phone calls like a person, is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer in San Francisco. The role requires 2+ years of software experience plus familiarity with LLMs, APIs, and cloud. Responsibilities include building integrations, developing rapid proof-of-concepts, monitoring customer deployments, writing automated tests, and managing clients directly. The posting stresses it is a high-intensity, code-heavy technical customer support role, with an interview process including a technical round, a CEO meeting, and a paid working trial.

Key points

  • Phonely builds an AI receptionist that answers phones like a person.
  • Requires 2+ years of software experience plus LLM, API, and cloud knowledge.
  • Duties span integrations, POCs, deployment monitoring, and automated testing.
  • Role is client-facing: managing customers and iterating on solutions directly.
  • Interview includes a technical round, CEO meeting, and paid working trial.

At some point in the future, every business will answer their phone with voice AI. We are making sure that when they build that AI agent, the experience of doing so doesn’t suck.

We’re growing incredibly fast and need someone who thrives in a dynamic, high-pressure environment to work side-by-side with the our engineering and leadership team. This may be one of the most important roles in the company and is critical in helping us move faster, stay organized, and execute at the highest level.

We’re looking for someone who is highly organized, driven, and fun to work with. You’ll be working directly with our engineers, customers, and product team. You’ll be presenting to clients, building POCs and demoing our product. You’re core to the success of this company.

NOTE: This is an engineering and technical customer support position. You will be writing code, a lot of it and it will be a lot of work… if you’re looking for a cushy corporate job where nothing get done, I recommend checking out some of our competitors. If you’re looking to be a part of a winning team and to make a real difference read on.

Your day to day:

  • Build integrations across APIs, webhooks, and internal services to connect customer systems with the platform.
  • Develop rapid proof-of-concepts to validate workflows, features, and customer use cases before full-scale implementation.
  • Understand the customer’s technical environment, constraints, data flows, and operational needs in detail.
  • Review, analyze, and translate business and technical requirements into actionable engineering plans.
  • Iterate on workflows directly with customers, refining architecture and logic based on real-time feedback.
  • Closely monitor customer deployments, system health, and behavioral patterns to proactively identify issues.
  • Create automated tests and validation suites to run against the production agent and ensure reliability.
  • Partner closely with customer support and core engineering teams to resolve issues, escalate bugs, and share field insights.
  • Iterate directly with customers on workflows, refining agent logic, and prompts based on customer feedback.
  • Meet with and manage clients. You’ll problem solve with our clients and build out solutions.

Qualifications:

  • 2+ years writing software
  • Understanding of LLMs, APIs, Cloud
  • Comfortable with understanding technical specifications,
  • Understanding of good architecture, security practices
  • Strong technical and communication skills - Experience in a customer facing role in a technical manner a plus
