EnterpriseReady | Ep. #42, Elevating Code Search with Quinn Slack of Sourcegraph

Heavybit · 2022-06-16 · 51 min read

Summary

In episode 42 of the EnterpriseReady podcast, Grant Miller interviews Quinn Slack, CEO and co-founder of Sourcegraph. Slack describes teaching himself to code as a kid, then working as a forward deployed engineer at Palantir, embedded at JP Morgan and Bank of America, where his team gathered requirements from bankers by day and built tools overnight, solving problems like reducing foreclosures. The pain of navigating enormous bank codebases inspired him to co-found Blend (which later IPOed) and then Sourcegraph in 2013 with Beyang Liu, aiming to give every company the code search capability Google engineers enjoy internally. Sourcegraph raised only about $300,000 initially and took until late 2019 to find product-market fit, ultimately targeting companies with 50-plus engineers, spreading virally once a single developer adopted it self-hosted.

Key points

  • Quinn Slack was a forward deployed engineer at Palantir, embedded at JP Morgan and Bank of America.
  • Palantir's model compressed feedback loops: uncover business problems by day, build tools overnight.
  • Huge, hard-to-search bank codebases directly inspired Sourcegraph's founding in 2013.
  • Sourcegraph raised ~$300,000 initially and only found product-market fit by late 2019.
  • It targets 50-plus-engineer companies, growing organically via self-hosted adoption by individual developers.
