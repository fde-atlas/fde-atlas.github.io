Summary

A free online lecture announcement from UML software site, titled on the cognition and practice of Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs). Speaker Zu Tao, founder of Huolongguo Software Engineering, will present on December 19, 2026. The talk argues that FDEs are key to making AI applications land in practice: they proactively uncover AI application needs, design technical solutions, build the applications, and deliver them to users. This requires full-stack capability. The lecture will use real application examples to explain FDE work scenarios and the relevant technologies and skills.
