Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Course, Live Online

Datamarcos · 2026 · 15 min read

Summary

Datamarcos offers a 22-week live online Forward Deployed Engineer course starting 5 December 2026, aimed at engineers with roughly two years of experience in Java, Python, backend, data, or DevOps. The curriculum follows the full FDE engagement lifecycle—discovery, scoping, building RAG and agent systems, security and deployment, and handover—across 170+ live hours and four client-style engagements with panel reviews. Career support includes resume rewrites, mock FDE interview loops, and a CareerOS placement portal. The page cites demand figures: 700%+ year-over-year growth in FDE postings on Indeed, Salesforce's plan to hire 1,000 FDEs, and FDE teams at OpenAI, Anthropic, Palantir, and others. It is taught by Deepesh Mishra and awards an executive certificate, with no job or salary guarantee.

Ask a counsellor

What does a Forward Deployed AI Engineer actually do?

An FDE embeds with a customer, turns a vague business problem into working software inside the customer's real environment, and stays accountable until it creates value. The day mixes stakeholder discovery, production coding (integrations, RAG, agents, pipelines), debugging in locked-down environments, security reviews and handover.

Do I need AI experience?

No. You need solid Python and working engineering fundamentals. A bridge pre-work track covers Python, Linux, Git and SQL before Day 1, and the program builds LLM, RAG and agent skills from Week 5.

Who is this program for?

Engineers with about 2+ years of experience (or strong shipped projects): backend and full-stack engineers, IT-services engineers, data and ML engineers, solutions architects, QA/SDET and DevOps engineers, and tech leads. It is not for people who have never written code or who want a watch-only video course.

How much time per week do I need?

Plan for about 10 to 14 hours: two live weekend sessions, a weekday evening lab and project work. The program runs for 22 weeks (5 months).

Are the classes live? What if I miss one?

Yes, sessions are live online and mentor-led. Ask your counsellor how recordings and catch-up work for your cohort.

What projects will I build?

Four client-style engagements: Project Sentinel (BFSI knowledge copilot), Project Atlas (multi-agent operations copilot), Project Helix (production hardening for a regulated client) and your own capstone engagement, each with a client brief, constraints and a panel review.

Is security covered in depth?

Yes. There is a full week on AI security (prompt injection, OWASP LLM Top 10, PII masking, guardrails) and a full week on governance and compliance (DPDP, GDPR, HIPAA, EU AI Act essentials), plus red-teaming in the engagement studio.

Do you cover Databricks and Snowflake?

Yes. Week 14 is dedicated to platform FDE work on Databricks, Snowflake and cloud agent platforms such as AWS Bedrock and Azure AI Foundry.

Will I get a job or placement after this course?

No course can promise you an FDE job, and we do not guarantee placement, salary or hikes. What you get is a portfolio of four deployed, client-style engagements, a weekly career track, FDE-specific mock interview loops, resume and LinkedIn teardowns, and a referral and outreach playbook.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. The Datamarcos Executive Certificate in Forward Deployed AI Engineering is awarded on completing the program and defending your capstone.

What is the fee? Are EMI options available?

The fee, seat booking and EMI options are listed in the Fees & enrolment section on this page. A counsellor can help you choose.

When does the next cohort start?

All current batches start on 5 December 2026. Exact session days and times are confirmed on a counsellor call.

Can my company sponsor me?

Yes. Talk to admissions about corporate sponsorship and team cohorts.

Can a fresher join the Forward Deployed Engineer course?

Most FDE roles expect real engineering experience, so the program is built for engineers with about 2+ years, or strong shipped projects. If you are earlier in your career, a counsellor may suggest starting with the Generative AI & Agentic AI Engineer program and moving to FDE later.

What is the difference between a forward deployed engineer and a solutions engineer?

A solutions engineer mostly supports the sale with demos and proofs of concept. A forward deployed engineer builds, deploys and supports the real system in the customer's environment after the sale, and owns the result.

Is this an IIT, university or official FDE certification?

No. You earn the Datamarcos Executive Certificate in Forward Deployed AI Engineering after completing the program and defending your capstone. It is issued by Datamarcos, not by an IIT, a university or an industry body. We focus on the portfolio you can show: four deployed, client-style engagements.

Is the course available in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune or Delhi NCR?

Yes. It's live online, so you can join from anywhere, with live weekend sessions and a weekday evening lab.

Do you prepare me for FDE interviews?

Yes. A weekly career track includes FDE-style mock interview loops (system design with ambiguity, live debugging and customer scenarios), plus resume, LinkedIn and portfolio reviews. We don't guarantee interviews or offers.

How is the FDE program different from the GenAI & Agentic AI Engineer program?

The GenAI program builds LLM, RAG and agent engineering skills. The FDE program assumes engineering experience and adds the customer-facing half of the job: discovery, scoping, SOWs, security reviews, enterprise integration, deployment and handover, practised through four client-style engagements.

Is there a free forward deployed engineer course?

Free material is a good place to start. Our guides on what a forward deployed engineer does and how FDE courses compare are free, and so is the FDE interview prep page. This program is the paid, live, mentor-reviewed route, with four client-style engagements.

What is the difference between a forward deployed engineer and an AI engineer?

An AI engineer usually builds AI features for one product or platform. A forward deployed engineer takes AI into a specific customer's environment, with their data, security rules and stakeholders, and stays until it works in production and is handed over.

What skills do I need to become a forward deployed engineer?

Three groups. Engineering: Python, APIs, cloud, containers and CI/CD. Applied AI: RAG, agents, MCP, evaluation, security and LLMOps. Customer craft: discovery, scoping, SOWs, demos and handover. The program covers all three across 22 weeks.
