Shield AI AI & Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs (11)

Forward Deploying · 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Summary

Forward Deploying's tracker page for Shield AI lists 11 open AI engineering roles, including 2 Forward Deployed Engineer positions, across 5 cities with none remote-eligible. The median published US base salary is $250k, about 3% above the $244k market median across the 278 employers tracked. The mix is 5 ML Engineer and 4 AI Engineer roles, mostly senior (6 staff, 3 senior, 2 mid). Seven adverts explicitly require production code, five require a security clearance or eligibility, and ten mention equity. Only 3 of 11 publish salary ranges, and 2 mention travel. Shield AI operates in defence and public sector and ranks 46th among tracked employers. Eight roles opened in the last 30 days, with a median posting age of 15 days.

Key points

  • 11 open AI roles, including 2 FDE positions.
  • Median published US base salary is $250k.
  • 5 roles require security clearance or eligibility.
  • 8 of 11 roles posted within the last 30 days.
  • 10 of 11 adverts mention equity; 3 publish salary ranges.
