Turing vs Upwork (2026)

Ryz Labs · 2026-10-02 · 5 min read

Summary

Turing vs Upwork compared: an AI lab services company versus an open freelance marketplace, and why US teams pick Ryz Labs for senior engineers and AI pods.

Full text

Disclosure: we're Ryz Labs, and we compete with the companies on this page. Here's why we think we're the right call for most teams, with sources for every fact about the others.

Turing and Upwork sell very different things. Upwork is an open marketplace: you post a job, pick a freelancer, pay their rate plus a published 5% or 10% client fee, and screen and manage the work yourself. Turing says it helps "frontier labs improve models and enterprises deploy agentic systems," and sells quoted AI build work, pods and forward-deployed engineers from a global network.

For most US teams that want senior engineers on their own team on US hours, or an AI pod team that builds and ships in their own cloud, the better choice is Ryz Labs. You get top 1% senior engineers on US business hours, AI pods with systems in production, and a scoped plan, a price and the names of your people before you sign.

Here is every public price signal we found, checked November 2026. Most firms in this market don't publish a rate card, so we show the hourly band on their Clutch listing and convert it to a monthly figure for one full-time engineer.

Monthly figures are our math at 160 hours a month. Directory bands are entered by the firms themselves and are not quotes, so ask each vendor for a written monthly price for the same roles at the same seniority.

Turing was founded in 2018 and is based in San Francisco. Its core business, according to Sacra, supplies training data, evaluations and RL environments to frontier AI labs. For enterprises it sells Turing Intelligence in three parts: Advise (strategy and scoping), Augment (AI-native talent embedding) and Build (production systems and agentic workflows, starting with rapid prototyping). It also sells Turing pods and forward-deployed AI engineers. Turing raised a $111M Series E at a $2.2B valuation in March 2025. It doesn't publish pricing.

Upwork is a NASDAQ-listed online work marketplace headquartered in Palo Alto. Clients post jobs and hire independent freelancers and agencies on hourly or fixed-price contracts, then manage the work themselves. It says it has 18M+ freelancers across 180 countries, and its FY2025 10-K reports 785K active clients and $787.8M in revenue. Fees are published: 5% on Basic (3% for eligible US clients paying by checking account) or 10% on Business Plus, which adds an Expert-Vetted filter and AI shortlists. Enterprise programs run through Lifted, an Upwork Company.

Senior engineers on your team, vetted before you meet them. On Upwork, screening is your job. With Ryz, it's done before you see anyone. We've interviewed tens of thousands of engineers, and only the top 1% make it: recruiters source, our in-house ARC system ranks the pipeline, every candidate goes through a structured NTRVSTA AI interview, and recruiters review before and after. AI scores are advisory; people decide. Our engineers then join your standups, your repos and your roadmap from week one. See our vetting process.

New York hours for the whole team. Our Latin American engineers work on US business hours, so review, pairing and decisions happen the same day. On both Turing and Upwork, working hours depend on who you get.

AI pod teams that ship to production. A typical pod is about 7 senior engineers, including a tech lead, an ML engineer and backend engineers, working in your cloud (AWS, Azure, GitHub, Postgres, with Anthropic and OpenAI models), with weekly demos. One pod built fraud detection for a global fleet company that scored 244K+ invoices in under 30 seconds each and surfaced $5.94M in fraud confirmed by the client's fraud team. Others took compliance review of 8,000+ documents from days to hours for a global capital management firm and built an AI voice platform with 1M+ outbound calls. See our case studies.

Names before you sign. Our quote comes with a scoped plan, a price and the names of the people who would do the work. Fortune 500 engineering teams trust us, across every industry, including financial services.

  • You have a small, scoped task, want published fees, and will screen and manage a freelancer yourself (Upwork).
  • Your project is model training, evaluation or post-training research (Turing).
  • You need a contingent-workforce program across many freelancers (Lifted, an Upwork Company).
  • If you want top 1% senior engineers working on your team long term, pick Ryz Labs.
  • If you want a dedicated AI pod team building in your own cloud and shipping to production, pick Ryz Labs.
  • If your whole team needs to work New York hours, pick Ryz Labs.
  • If you want the names of your engineers and a scoped plan before you sign, pick Ryz Labs.
  • If you need one freelancer for a short, scoped task you'll manage yourself, Upwork is built for that.
  • If your work is frontier model training or evaluation data, Turing is built for that.
