Summary

Japanese consulting firm SORAMICHI posted a job listing on OpenWork for an AI full-cycle engineer, explicitly modeled on the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role at overseas AI companies. The hire would handle requirements gathering, architecture, full-stack and data development, deployment, and operations alone or in small teams, with AI-assisted development built in—core tools are Claude Code and OpenAI Codex. Clients are large enterprises including TSE Prime-listed firms in finance, real estate, retail, and manufacturing. Salary ranges from 9 million to 19.24 million yen. SORAMICHI announced a capital and business alliance with Itochu Corporation in October 2026.

Key points

  • SORAMICHI is hiring an AI full-cycle engineer equivalent to a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE).
  • One person or a small team covers requirements, design, implementation, deployment, and operations end to end.
  • Development assumes AI tooling, with Claude Code and OpenAI Codex as core tools.
  • Clients include TSE Prime-listed enterprises with enterprise constraints like security and legacy systems.
  • Salary is 9–19.24 million yen; Itochu alliance announced October 2026.

  • Job Description
  • ■Company Introduction 【Capital and Business Alliance with ITOCHU Corporation. Toward a Further Growth Phase】 SORAMICHI is a consulting firm that supports the marketing and DX domains, primarily for major companies in finance, real estate, distribution, manufacturing, and other industries, with the mission of "driving corporate transformation from strategy to execution through Strategy × Data × AI." Our strengths lie in a wide range of expertise, from marketing strategy, CRM, data analysis, and AI utilization to system development. In October 2026, we announced a capital and business alliance with ITOCHU Corporation. By combining the customer and business base of the ITOCHU Group with the expertise SORAMICHI has cultivated, we will expand our business domains and take on larger and more diverse corporate transformation projects. ■Background of the Recruitment As our business expands, we are looking for new team members across a wide range of areas, including consulting, marketing, data and AI, and system development. In addition to strengthening our existing business, we are also looking ahead to expansion into new customer and business domains. We welcome candidates who are eager to leverage their expertise and experience to tackle solving corporate challenges and realizing transformation together with us. ■Position Available AI Full-Cycle Engineer (FDE / Forward Deployed Engineer) This position corresponds to the role known as Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) at overseas AI companies. You will be an engineer who steps directly into the client's business operations, listens to their challenges firsthand, builds working prototypes to demonstrate solutions, puts them into production, and sees them through until results are achieved. Without dividing responsibilities by process or technical domain, you will complete everything from requirements gathering, requirements definition, architecture design, implementation (frontend/backend/data/infrastructure), release, and operational improvement, either alone or with a small team. The process begins with dialogue with the client's business and information systems departments. You will observe business operations to identify the true challenges, design solutions including AI utilization, and then present prototypes within days to weeks to validate hypotheses. Solutions proven effective will be developed into production systems that meet security, availability, and operability requirements, and after release, you will continue to improve them by monitoring usage and logs. Ultimately, you will be responsible for handing over the system in a state where the client can operate and maintain it themselves. Since one person covers the entire process, the use of AI agents is a given. Our company has shifted its entire development process—from upstream processes such as requirements definition and design, through design reviews, development, testing, and data migration—to an AI-first approach, with Claude (Claude Code) and OpenAI Codex as our core tools. The expected way of working in this position is to design yourself the scope delegated to AI and the scope where humans ensure quality, and to cover speed and scope far exceeding traditional approaches with a small team. The projects you will work on are for major companies, including those listed on the TSE Prime Market. These come with enterprise-specific constraints, such as integration with existing systems, compliance with internal regulations and security standards, and impact on systems in operation. This position demands the ability to deliver realistic solutions that work within those constraints without sacrificing speed. 【Differences from AI-Driven Development PG / AI Strategy Consultant】 The AI-Driven Development PG focuses on implementation and quality refinement under a designed development process. The AI Strategy Consultant leads the design and standardization of AI-first development processes and their adoption across the organization. This position combines both roles in one person, running short cycles from requirements gathering through operations. On large-scale projects, you will work alongside both roles, while on projects launching new domains or PoCs, you will handle everything from start to finish on your own. ■Job Description You can both deepen your own expertise and take on new domains. ・Discretion and opportunities for challenge unique to a period of growth Precisely because we are in a period of growth for both our business and organization, there are opportunities to proactively engage in new initiatives, business improvements, and organizational building, free from existing conventions. 【Major Clients】 Our clients are primarily major companies, including those listed on the TSE Prime Market, spanning a wide range of industries such as finance, real estate, distribution, and manufacturing.
