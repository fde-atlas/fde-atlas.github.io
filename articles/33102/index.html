TCS plans up to 8,900 forward deployed engineers: Reuters

Kernavique · 2026-07-12 · 2 min read

Summary

Reuters reported on July 12, 2026 that Tata Consultancy Services plans a forward deployed engineer team equal to 1% to 1.5% of its associates — roughly 5,900 to 8,900 people based on the end-June headcount of 593,798. CEO K Krithivasan gave the range, but TCS has not said whether these engineers will be hired externally or converted from existing staff. CFO Samir Seksaria said TCS is seeking AI acquisitions and spends about $1 billion annually on training and internal AI access. Analytics India Magazine cites a smaller figure of about 6,000, so the team's size and hiring route remain unsettled.

Key points

  • TCS plans 5,900 to 8,900 forward deployed engineers, per Reuters.
  • The team is sized as 1% to 1.5% of total headcount, not contract volume.
  • TCS has not clarified external hiring versus internal conversion.
  • CFO Samir Seksaria says TCS is seeking AI acquisitions.
  • Analytics India Magazine reports a smaller figure of about 6,000.

On July 12, 2026, Reuters reported, in a copy carried by Khaleej Times, that Tata Consultancy Services plans a team of forward deployed engineers equal to 1% to 1.5% of its associates, roughly 5,900 to 8,900 employees on end-June headcount. Chief executive K Krithivasan gave the range. Reuters also reported that TCS is seeking AI acquisitions.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: Khaleej Times (Reuters) (reported).

What happened

The Next Web reported on July 13, 2026, that the end-June headcount was 593,798. It reported that Aarthi Subramanian, chief operating officer, described these engineers as multi-skilled specialists who are deep in one area.

Reuters quoted Samir Seksaria, the chief financial officer, on the search for AI acquisitions. It reported that TCS spends about $1 billion annually on developing its people and on internal AI access. TCS’s own results statement was not opened.

The sources disagree on the size. Reuters gives a range up to 8,900, or 1% to 1.5% of associates. Analytics India Magazine, on July 24, 2026, gives about 6,000, or about 1%.

“What you need is a deep knowledge of the customer environment to make it work.”

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

The Next Web uses the word “convert” for existing associates, and also says TCS has not said whether the engineers will be hired externally or retrained. So the reports do not tell engineers outside TCS whether roles will open.

If you are hiring them

TCS sizes the team as a share of its total workforce, 1% to 1.5%, not as a number tied to contracts. If you compete for the same engineers, the size and the hiring route are both still unsettled.
