Frequently Asked Questions From Singapore Tech Leaders

Can ISHIR work alongside our in-house AI engineering team in Singapore?

Yes. Most Singapore clients already have internal engineering talent. ISHIR integrates seamlessly as an extension of your team, taking ownership of high-priority modules, accelerating delivery or leading the product roadmap while your in-house engineers remain focused on core operations.

Can you help us modernize legacy systems with Gen AI without shutting down operations?

Yes. We modernize in phases to minimize risk and eliminate downtime. Many Singapore AI-native enterprises choose ISHIR because we upgrade architecture, performance and security while keeping mission-critical systems running smoothly.

Does ISHIR force AI into every product or transformation initiative?

No. We only use AI where it drives real value. If automation, predictive intelligence or AI-native engineering accelerates outcomes, we recommend it. If not, we focus on strong fundamentals rather than chasing trends or buzzwords.

How does ISHIR overcome talent shortages in the Singapore market for specialized tech roles?

ISHIR solves the talent shortage by combining local expertise with global capability centers in India and other South East Asian markets. Instead of competing for the same limited pool of engineers in Singapore, we give companies immediate access to highly specialized talent across cloud, AI, data, cybersecurity, DevOps, and full-stack engineering.

How can you help Singapore companies accelerate innovation?

ISHIR drives innovation in parallel with core business activities through phased execution, dedicated PODs and risk-aware rollouts. Instead of pausing operations, we modernize and build new capabilities while existing systems continue running. Singapore teams get rapid innovation without downtime, bottlenecks or operational interruption, so transformation happens without breaking the business.

Does ISHIR work with companies in South Asian markets?

Yes. ISHIR works with companies across multiple South Asian markets and supports technology initiatives throughout the region.
