What Is an FDE's Salary? Market Rates from Job Postings in Japan and the US, and How Pay Is Determined

Original title: FDEの年収はいくら？日本と米国の求人票で見る相場と、決まり方

FDE Consulting · 2026-10-07 · 5 min read

Summary

This article compiles FDE salary data from job postings and public sources. In Japan, most postings cap pay at 10–20 million yen (34 of 48 postings listing a cap), while US AI companies pay far more: OpenAI offers $185k–$300k plus equity, Anthropic $280k–$320k, and Palantir's median total compensation is $205k per Levels.fyi. The author, writing from hands-on FDE experience, attributes high pay to surging demand (postings up over 800% from January to September 2025 per FT/Indeed data), the role combining consultant, engineer, and product manager work, and proximity to measurable business outcomes. It also outlines four factors driving pay differences and five cautions when reading salary ranges.

Key points

  • Japanese FDE postings mostly cap at 10–20 million yen; Sierra Japan offers 22–47 million yen under a different title.
  • US benchmarks: OpenAI $185k–$300k, Anthropic $280k–$320k, Palantir median $205k.
  • FDE postings grew over 800% between January and September 2025, driving up offers.
  • Pay varies by company type, implementation scope, equity, and outcome-linked evaluation.
  • Advice: quantify business impact, combine coding with domain knowledge, and build industry depth.

The annual salary for FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) in Japanese job postings most often has an upper limit of ¥10–19.99 million. In the November 2026 tally, 34 of the 48 postings that listed an upper limit fell in this range. In the US, OpenAI's FDE posting offers $185,000–$300,000 (equity compensation separate), and Anthropic's FDE posting offers $280,000–$320,000. For Palantir FDEs, salary information sites show a median total compensation of $205,000.

Here, rather than making up averages or market rates myself, I simply line up the figures from job postings and public data. On that basis, I write—from my position as someone working in the field as an FDE—about why the levels are what they are, what creates the differences, and how to read job postings.

An explanation of the FDE role itself is summarized in "What is an FDE? The Meaning and Work of a Forward Deployed Engineer". Here I focus on salary.

The wide range in Palantir's figures is because even among FDEs, compensation varies by assignment (government vs. commercial), years of experience, and how equity compensation is treated. Levels.fyi's aggregation is based on self-reported data, so it differs in nature from figures stated in job postings.

Why FDE Salaries Are High

There are three reasons.

1. Postings are surging and talent is scarce

According to a report by the UK's Financial Times analyzing data from the job site Indeed, FDE job postings grew more than 800% from January to September 2025 (FT; the figure was also reported by eWeek and others). The role is new, and experienced people are still few. Demand ballooned first, pushing stated salaries up.

2. One person does three jobs

An FDE understands the customer's business (business), writes code themselves (technology), and decides what to automate and what to leave to humans (design). Work traditionally divided among a consultant, an engineer, and a product manager is handled end-to-end by one person in the field. Compensation is paid for this breadth.

3. They sit close to outcomes

An FDE's work ends not with delivering a proposal or completing development to spec, but with moving the numbers in the field. Our support also combines a fixed monthly fee with success fees tied to KPI improvements. Work close to outcomes tends to have compensation linked to the size of those outcomes.

Four Factors That Determine Salary

Stated salaries in domestic job postings range from a lower limit of ¥4 million to an upper limit of ¥47 million. We see four factors creating the differences.

FactorHigher sideLower side
Company typeUS AI companies, foreign companies' Japanese subsidiariesJapanese SaaS and AI companies, consulting and SI firms
Breadth of roleImplementing yourself and carrying it to productionMainly proposals and implementation support, with implementation by a separate team
Compensation structureBase salary + equity compensationBase salary only, including fixed overtime pay
Link to outcomesEvaluation tied to KPIs or revenueEvaluation based on hours worked

On breadth of role, there is an individual study analyzing 35 Japanese FDE postings (26 Japanese companies, 9 foreign). In that analysis, based on job descriptions in the postings, the "Builder type" who implements themselves accounted for 81% of Japanese companies, all 9 foreign postings, and the pre-sales-leaning "Sales Engineer+ type" was 12% of Japanese companies (gaijineers, February 2026). Even with the same title, both the work and the compensation change with the proportion of implementation.

Five Cautions When Reading Salaries in Job Postings

  • You normally enter at the lower limit. The upper limit of a range is the figure for the most experienced person in that role
  • Check whether fixed overtime pay is included. JAPAN AI's posting explicitly states it includes 45 hours. If included, the hourly rate is lower than it appears
  • "Estimated theoretical annual salary" is a figure adding assumed bonuses and allowances. SB OAI Japan's posting uses this notation
  • Equity compensation is separate. OpenAI's and Sierra Japan's "+ equity" can be substantial if the company's value rises, but it is not cash
  • Foreign companies use different titles. Sierra Japan is hiring under "Software Engineer (AI Agents)". Some postings do not appear when you search for "FDE"

How to Increase Your Salary as an FDE

The material for raising your salary is neither certifications nor job titles; it is being able to state in your own words "which operations, which numbers, and by how much you moved them." This is the single point that hiring managers want to verify in interviews.

When the following three are in place, the offered amount tends toward the upper end of the range.

  • Track record you can speak of in numbers. By what percentage you reduced man-hours, how much revenue you increased, how far you raised retention rates
  • Both implementation skills and business understanding. Being able to code alone, or knowing the business alone, does not amount to the work of three people
  • Depth in a specific industry. Having one domain, such as manufacturing, finance, or healthcare, where you understand the language of the front lines

With few experienced professionals in the field today, transitioning from an adjacent role is a realistic entry point. If you have experience as a systems engineer, IT consultant, or product manager, you can make up for the missing piece on the job.
