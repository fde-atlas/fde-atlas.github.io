On-target earnings

FDE Interview Prep · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

This glossary-style article explains on-target earnings (OTE): base salary plus variable pay received only if targets are met, presented as one figure or range. It cites real postings—Okta's Principal FDE role (equity sits on top of OTE), Elastic's 70/30 pay mix, Notion's FDE GTM role, and Databricks and Anthropic postings where the range's meaning varies. It advises candidates to ask recruiters whether a range is base or OTE, what the split is, what the variable pay is measured on, and whether it is capped.

Key points

  • OTE combines base salary with variable pay tied to hitting targets.
  • Elastic's posting uses a 70/30 base-to-variable pay mix.
  • Notion's FDE GTM posting lists total on-target earnings as a range.
  • Databricks and Anthropic ranges differ for commissionable versus non-commissionable roles.
  • Ask recruiters about the split, metrics, caps, and whether equity is included.

Full text

On-target earnings (OTE) is base salary plus the variable pay you receive if you meet your targets, stated as a single figure or range. Okta’s US Principal posting, as of September 2026, defines its OTE as inclusive of base salary and incentive compensation, with equity where applicable in addition. Why it matters in interviews

A salary range and an OTE range are not the same number, because part of an OTE figure depends on targets someone else sets. Some postings leave the basis for you to confirm: Databricks’ US AI FDE posting describes its range as the expected salary for non-commissionable roles or on-target earnings for commissionable roles, and Anthropic’s FDE posting says that for sales roles its range is the OTE range. Source 4AI Engineer – Forward Deployed Engineering (AI FDE)PublisherDatabricks (careers site / Greenhouse)Source typecompany job postingSource 5Forward Deployed Engineer (New York City, NY; San Francisco, CA; Seattle, WA)PublisherAnthropic (Greenhouse)Source typecompany job posting On the recruiter call, ask: “Is this range base or OTE? If OTE, what is the split, and what is the variable part measured on: bookings, renewals, delivery milestones or company results?” Then state your own expectation in two parts: “I’m looking for a base of [your number]. I’ll weigh the variable part once I know what it’s measured on and whether it’s capped.” Ask separately whether equity is included in the figure; Okta’s posting puts equity on top of OTE.
