About: AI Consultant and Forward Deployed Engineer

Rohit Raj · 2 min read

Summary

This is a personal profile page for Rohit Raj, a Bengaluru-based forward deployed engineer and AI consultant, styled as a passport. He describes joining client teams directly in their Slack, repos and standups to build AI agents, MCP integrations and LLM features that reach production with evals, rather than stopping at demos. The page cites 10+ years of production experience, 35+ systems built with 35 still running, and prior work on Kafka pipelines and caching layers that cut database load by 80%. Projects include healthcare WhatsApp chatbots, chat-to-SQL assistants and privacy-first financial advisors, each shipped with CI/CD, observability and handover documentation.

Key points

  • Rohit Raj is a Bengaluru-based FDE and AI consultant with 10+ years in production software.
  • He embeds in client teams and ships production AI agents, MCP servers and LLM features with evals.
  • He has built 35+ systems, including healthcare WhatsApp bots and chat-to-SQL assistants.
  • He argues most agent failures come from surrounding plumbing, not the model itself.
  • His stack spans Kafka, Spring Boot, OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, RAG with pgvector and mobile frameworks.

I'm Rohit Raj, a forward deployed engineer and AI consultant in Bengaluru, India. I join your team (your Slack, your repo, your standups), find where AI earns its place in the product, and build it until it runs in production with evals proving it works. The work is agents, MCP integrations and LLM features on the stack you already run, and it rests on 10+ years of shipping production software, from Java and Kafka backends to React Native and Kotlin apps.

Observations · टिप्पणियाँ

  • What I build

    Agents, MCP servers and LLM features, plus the production systems they live inside: WhatsApp chatbots for healthcare, a chat-to-SQL assistant, AI travel planners, privacy-first financial advisors and multi-tenant SaaS platforms. Every project ships with the infrastructure to stay up (CI/CD, observability, load tests, deployment automation) and a handover your team can run without me. I don't build demos; I build systems that run in production.

  • How I got here

    Before going independent, I worked on distributed systems and event-driven architecture at scale: Kafka pipelines processing millions of events, caching layers that cut database load by 80%, microservices handling thousands of concurrent requests. That background is why the AI work holds up. Most of what breaks an agent in production is the plumbing around the model, and the plumbing is where I've spent my career.

  • Why this site exists

    I document what I build because engineering work is often invisible, and you should be able to check my work before you bring me into your team. This site is that record: the problems I've solved, the architecture decisions I've made, and the trade-offs I'm still working through. Most AI products stall between demo and production. If yours is stuck there, I'd like to hear about it.
