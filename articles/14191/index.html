Credentials | Re:Sourced — Specialist Tech Recruitment

Re:Sourced · 2026-02-01 · 3 min read

Summary

This is a credentials page for Re:Sourced, a Sydney-based tech recruitment firm founded by Matt Gold, who claims 13 years of experience and 700+ placements in forward deployed, AI, software, data, cloud and cyber hiring across APAC, EMEA and the US, drawing on a network of 17,500+ senior engineers. It showcases four client case studies, describes a four-phase search process, and highlights in-house AI tooling and quarterly salary benchmarking.

Key points

  • Founder Matt Gold: 13 years, 700+ placements, specialist in forward deployed and AI hiring.
  • Network of 17,500+ senior engineers across Australia, UK and US.
  • Case studies include 22 hires in 9 months (Homes.com.au) and 16 in 6 months (Medinet).
  • Four-phase search process; 90% of roles filled from the first three candidates.
  • In-house AI tools and quarterly salary benchmarks support delivery speed.

How every search runs

The same four phases run on every search, whether it is one principal engineer or a twenty-person build-out. Consistency is what makes the timeline predictable.

01 A working session to pin the brief, the must-haves, the comp band and what good looks like before any outreach begins.

02 Targeted headhunting against a mapped market, drawing on a network of 17,500+ senior engineers across three regions.

03 A calibrated shortlist, usually three CVs or fewer, with the interview loop managed to keep momentum.

04 Offers pre-closed and structured to hold against counter-offers, so acceptances land and start dates stick.

Tooling & TechOps

Much of the speed comes from the operation behind the search: proprietary data, AI-assisted tooling and delivery infrastructure we build and run in-house.

Proprietary market data

Priced against the live market

Shortlists that move in days
