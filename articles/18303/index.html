Helping small and medium-sized enterprises in Japan with AX. Green Waters Co., Ltd. launches "Deployed AX," an FDE service accountable for practical results

Original title: 中小企業から日本をAX。株式会社グリーンウォーターズ、実務成果に責任を持つFDEサービス「Deployed AX」を提供開始

Summary

Green Waters, the Tokyo-based company behind the education platform GSchool, will launch an FDE service called Deployed AX on August 20, 2026, targeting non-IT small and mid-sized businesses in Japan. A two-person team (implementation plus strategy) embeds at the client site, moving from problem identification to building an AI foundation and implementing solutions as AI agents within three months. Contracts run in three-month units starting at 300,000 yen per month excluding tax. The company cites government data showing only 23% AI adoption among SMBs, attributing the gap to limited resources and difficulty moving past pilot stages. The service emphasizes making clients self-sufficient rather than relying on permanent staffing, and bundles FDE talent training via GSchool plus zero-fee hiring through its GWork career platform.

Green Waters Co., Ltd. (Head office: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director: Hiroyuki Minakoshita; hereinafter "the Company"), which operates the education platform "GSchool," will launch a new service, Deployed AX, on Thursday, August 20, 2026. The service primarily targets non-IT small and medium-sized enterprises (the SMB market) and is committed to supporting everything from the practical application of AI to organizations becoming autonomously self-sustaining. Building on its track record of supporting AX (AI transformation) initiatives and AI talent development for many SMEs through the "GSchool" education platform, the Company will strongly support business transformation at customer companies through the new FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) approach offered by "Deployed AX."

Background of the Service Launch

The "resources and adoption" barrier that blocks AI adoption at SMEs

AI is evolving remarkably, and a growing number of companies are automating their operations with AI and achieving major time savings. On the other hand, the AI adoption rate among SMEs remains at just 23% (*), and the majority are companies that are interested but unable to take the first step due to resource shortages and other reasons. Through its corporate AX (AI transformation) training offered via the "GSchool" education platform, the Company has long engaged with the on-the-ground challenges of many companies. In the process, it has received numerous comments such as: "We can achieve minor efficiency gains, but frontline staff alone cannot push through to fundamental business reform," and "We remain stuck at the validation stage and face hurdles in rolling out to actual operations." In response to these challenges, the Company decided to launch this service because it wants to provide support that goes one step beyond training and contribute to solving problems from a position closer to the front lines.

*: SME Agency, "2026 White Paper on Small and Medium Enterprises"

An FDE service for SMEs that supports on-the-ground challenges end to end

"Deployed AX" is a new FDE service that takes responsibility for supporting everything from the practical application of AI to organizations becoming autonomously self-sustaining, aimed at companies struggling with business improvement in the AI era. An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is an engineer who embeds deeply in the customer's front lines and handles everything end to end, from hearing about challenges to system design, implementation, and operation. Although this role and service model was established in the United States, it has been rapidly gaining attention in Japan with the spread of generative AI. Having supported AX initiatives at many SMEs and gained deep knowledge of real frontline challenges, the Company is now uniquely able to offer an FDE service optimized for SMEs. Through this new service, the Company will support the resolution of frontline challenges in multiple ways, going beyond AX training.

"Deployed AX": Service Overview

  • Service name: FDE service "Deployed AX"
  • Offerings: Corporate AI solutions; AI infrastructure construction and self-sustaining support by the Company's FDE team
  • Launch date: August 20, 2026
  • Contract term: Contracts in 3-month units
  • Pricing: From 300,000 yen per month (excluding tax) *Please contact us for details

Features of "Deployed AX"

1. Fast commitment that delivers an AI solution in 3 months

A two-person team consisting of an implementation lead and a strategy lead embeds on-site and takes responsibility for everything from extracting challenges to proposing AI-driven systematization of operations. Through close collaboration between development and strategy, the team delivers a solution optimized for the front lines in as little as 3 months.

2. From infrastructure construction to building a system that can use AI autonomously

The team builds an "AI infrastructure" that streamlines actual operations and implements and delivers the proposed solution as AI agents. Furthermore, it provides hands-on support until the system can be operated solely by the customer company's own employees. Without assuming the Company's permanent on-site presence, it cultivates the organization's own autonomous problem-solving capability.

3. Consistent support from hands-on assistance to talent strategy for the AI era

As part of its self-sustaining support, the Company offers an FDE talent development program through the "GSchool" education platform. It is also possible to hire talent who have acquired AI skills through the career platform "GWork" (at zero hiring cost). The Company supports everything end to end, from on-site implementation to providing education and hiring talent, and strongly supports its customers' talent strategies.

Message from Representative Director Hiroyuki Minakoshita

In recent years, technological evolution, including generative AI, has been remarkable, and it has become an extremely important factor that determines corporate competitiveness. However, in conversations with many non-IT small and medium-sized enterprises, barriers to on-the-ground adoption have become apparent, such as "We don't know how to use it" and "We introduced it, but no one touches it." The philosophy we embody is "supporting the growth and success of people and continuing to create richness in life together." We work alongside our customers as stakeholders to solve business challenges, and ultimately we remain committed to them until they can run AI autonomously and their organization becomes stronger. We hope that "Deployed AX" will serve as a solid step toward the next stage of growth for small and medium-sized enterprises across Japan.

Hiroyuki Minakoshita

Representative Director and CEO, Green Waters Co., Ltd.
