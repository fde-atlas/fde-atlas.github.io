[Globalway] Joins the Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network to Expand Agentforce Success

Original title: 【Globalway】 Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Networkに参画、Agentforceの成功を拡大

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-06-08 · 6 min read

Summary

Japanese company Globalway has joined the Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) Partner Network as a certified partner for Agentforce. The network extends Salesforce's internal engineering knowledge and training to selected partners, aiming to close the gap between AI pilots and production results. Globalway will help enterprises integrate AI agents into real operations, covering data mapping, permission architectures, security guardrails, and system orchestration. It also offers an implementation support service for Agentforce for Service on Service Cloud, spanning education, use case selection, prototyping, testing, and operations. Executives from both companies emphasized moving customers from experimentation to scalable business outcomes.

Key points

  • Globalway joined Salesforce's FDE Partner Network for Agentforce.
  • The network targets the gap between AI pilots and production deployment.
  • Support includes data integration, security guardrails, and system orchestration.
  • Globalway offers Agentforce for Service implementation support for Service Cloud users.
  • Both companies' executives commented on scaling AI agents to real outcomes.

Full text

—With advanced engineering expertise provided by Salesforce, Globalway helps enterprises combine their own business IP with technical rigor and specialized training to bridge the gap between AI aspirations and real-world results— Globalway Inc. (Headquarters: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Chairman and CEO: Masato Kagami; hereinafter "the Company"), which supports corporate transformation and growth through human resources services and AI/digital enablement, announced that it has joined the newly announced Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) Partner Network. As a partner with a proven track record in delivering Agentforce, Globalway becomes part of an elite network of companies that leverage the deep knowledge, specialized skills, and professional training of Salesforce's internal teams to help enterprises bring their AI agent visions to reality.

As enterprises evolve into agentic enterprises, the experimentation phase of AI is over, and delivering concrete business outcomes is now required. However, a significant gap remains in execution. Many companies remain stuck at the "experimental point-solution stage" and have not scaled to a level where AI can be applied to actual business operations. The Salesforce FDE Partner Network closes this gap by providing the advanced engineering capabilities needed to address complex security environments and proprietary data challenges.

With this special designation, Globalway prioritizes technical rigor and production outcomes, helping customers achieve the following:

●Advanced technical integration: For AI agents to function properly, complex and fragmented data structures must be mapped, providing the necessary context and permission architectures for safe operation.

●Strengthening regulated environments: In industries where incorrect responses can lead to irreversible consequences, AI guardrails are built to meet strict security standards.

●System orchestration: Building the integrated foundations—integrations, APIs, and data pipelines—required for AI agents to function across the entire operational environment.

●Closing the gap in production: Combining expertise gained through Salesforce implementation support, AI enablement, and cloud solution deployment with Salesforce's internal engineering standards, Globalway supports the implementation and operation of AI agents for customers managing complex data and business processes. Globalway provides end-to-end support centered on Salesforce, from concept development, requirements definition, implementation, and maintenance to adoption and insourcing. In addition, it offers Agentforce implementation and enablement support, AI contact center implementation support, and adjacent solution support, helping enterprises build the structures needed to put AI agents to practical use.

Globalway also offers an "Agentforce for Service Implementation Support Service" for companies using Salesforce Service Cloud. This service supports education, use case selection, prototype creation, testing and tuning, and operational monitoring for the implementation and adoption of Agentforce for Service. It improves customer satisfaction and operational productivity in the customer support domain through AI enablement that works in the field.

With the expansion of this partnership, Salesforce customers no longer need to tackle the complex challenges of AI integration alone. Through the Salesforce FDE Partner Network, Globalway provides the ongoing expertise and technology governance needed to turn AI agent visions into scalable, long-term realities.

For more information about Globalway and its Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineering capabilities, please visit AgentExchange, a marketplace of prebuilt autonomous AI agents and skills offered by Salesforce. Comment from Salesforce

Mr. Atsushi Urano, Executive Officer and General Manager of the Alliance Business Headquarters at Salesforce Japan, said the following:

"Customers seeking success in the era of AI agents can concretely leverage Salesforce's advantages through our partner ecosystem. The Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network extends the deep expertise and specialized training cultivated within Salesforce to an elite partner network. This gives customers the engineering depth and confidence needed to move from the pilot stage to production and achieve business outcomes at scale."

Comment from Globalway

Masato Kagami, Chairman and CEO of Globalway Inc., said the following:

"While expectations for generative AI and AI agents continue to rise, many companies face practical challenges—data integration, permission design, security, and operational adoption—when moving from proof-of-concept to production use. Leveraging the expertise Globalway has built through Salesforce implementation support and AI enablement, we have supported customers in using Agentforce safely and continuously in their business operations. Through joining the Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network, we will further connect Salesforce's advanced engineering expertise with our delivery capabilities and strengthen our support for helping customers advance AI agents from the 'experimenting' stage to the 'delivering results' stage."
