How we count forward deployed engineer hiring

Kernavique · 2026-11-20 · 11 min read

Summary

Kernavique documents the methodology behind its monthly forward deployed engineer hiring tracker and pay table. It counts postings whose title contains “forward deployed” across public Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever job feeds, re-read monthly. On November 20, 2026, it read 177 feeds with 526 postings (507 excluding interns), 298 with a US location, and 63 US postings mentioning clearance. The authors stress this is a convenience sample, not a census: employers on Workday or custom careers sites are mostly missing, duplicates across cities are not merged, and the title rule also catches non-engineering roles. Pay figures come separately from 42 US employer ads under their own stated basis.

Key points

  • Counts postings with “forward deployed” in the title on Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever feeds.
  • November 20, 2026 read: 177 feeds, 526 postings, 298 US-based.
  • 63 of 298 US postings matched clearance keywords across 11 employers.
  • Workday and custom-site employers are missing, so it is a sample, not a census.
  • Pay table uses 42 US employer ads only, filed by stated pay basis.

Which feeds we read, and why

This page is the method behind two pieces: the monthly tracker, who is hiring forward deployed engineers, and the table of forward deployed engineer pay by employer.

For the tracker we read each employer’s own public job board feed on three applicant tracking systems. In each address, BOARD stands for the employer’s board name.

  • Greenhouse. 
    https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/BOARD/jobs?content=true
  • Ashby. 
    https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/BOARD?includeCompensation=true
  • Lever. 
    https://api.lever.co/v0/postings/BOARD?mode=json

The pages people see for the same boards are

job-boards.greenhouse.io/BOARD

,

jobs.ashbyhq.com/BOARD

and

jobs.lever.co/BOARD

. Every employer row in the tracker links to one of them.

We use these three because each returns an employer’s whole board in one public read: every title, its locations and the ad text. That lets us apply one rule to every posting and repeat it the same way each month. They are also the three feeds we used for our first check, on October 11, 2026, so the two reads can be compared.

On November 20, 2026, we read 177 feeds in one pass between 16:10 and 16:12 UTC. The feeds were read in memory and only the tallies were kept.

The 177 are a convenience sample: the 62 feeds from October 11, plus 115 more AI labs, data and AI platforms, AI application and agent companies and defense technology companies whose board name we could find. Board names were matched to employers by name.

Three employers were read another way, through their own public job search for “forward deployed”: Amazon (94 hits, all read), ServiceNow (the first 100 of 130 results) and Deloitte (68 results on its US careers site, titles only). A keyword search is not a full feed, so those counts are reported apart and never added to the feed totals.

What counts as a posting

One feed entry is one posting. The rules read the posting title only and ignore case.

Label Rule
Forward deployed The title contains “forward deployed” or “forward-deployed”
Deployment strategist The title contains “deployment strategist” and does not contain “forward deployed”
Applied AI engineer The title contains “applied AI” and “engineer” and does not contain “forward deployed”
Forward deployed AI engineer, a subset of the first row The title contains “forward deployed AI engineer”, “AI forward deployed engineer”, “AI engineer” followed later by “forward deployed”, or “AI FDE”
Intern The title contains “intern” or “internship”

Only the first row feeds the headline count. A title such as “Deployment Strategist - Germany Forward Deployed” counts once, as forward deployed. Deployment strategist and applied AI engineer titles are reported beside the count, never inside it.

Interns are counted, and we publish the figure with and without them. On November 20, 2026, the 177 feeds held 526 postings with the title in all locations, or 507 without interns.

For the table of title variants, each posting goes into the first group it matches, in a fixed order: AI engineer forms, software engineer, plain forward deployed engineer, specialist engineers, management, product manager, data scientist and researcher, architect, strategist, then professionals who are not engineers. The ten groups add up to the 526.

In practice, the rule is wide on purpose. It counts a “Forward Deployed Banker” and a “Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering” along with the engineers, and the variant table shows how many of each there are.

How a US location and a clearance mention are decided

US location. A posting counts as US if any location on it names the United States, a US state, “D.C.” or a major US city. On Ashby and Lever, a posting whose location text names no place, for example “Remote”, counts as US when the feed’s country field says United States.

Postings whose only location is “Hybrid”, “Distributed”, “Remote” or “North America”, with no country, are not counted as US. The tracker flags them. On November 20, 2026, that left out 11 postings at Cloudflare, Vannevar Labs and Tines.

A posting with several locations counts once, and counts as US if one of them is in the US.

Clearance. A US posting counts as mentioning clearance if its ad text contains any of “security clearance”, “secret clearance”, “TS/SCI”, “top secret”, “polygraph” or “clearance”.

This is a text match, not a reading of each ad. It catches “active clearance required”, “eligible to obtain” and “preferred” alike, and the tracker counts them together. On November 20, 2026, it matched 63 of the 298 US postings, at 11 employers. Our piece on cleared forward deployed engineer roles looks at that part of the market more closely.

Contract or permanent. Ashby and Lever feeds carry an employment-type field, and we report any posting typed contract, temporary or part-time. Greenhouse feeds have no such field, so the check does not cover Greenhouse employers.

How pay figures are taken

The pay table is a separate read and follows its own rules.

  • Employer ads only. Every figure comes from an employer’s own job ad, on its careers page or its applicant tracking system. Nothing comes from a job aggregator, a salary site or a search-engine summary.
  • Pay basis as labeled. Each range is filed under what the ad itself calls it: base salary, salary not stated as base, or on-target earnings. Ranges on different bases are kept in different tables and are not set against each other.
  • As written. Ranges are quoted as the ad gives them, with no rounding, and with the location the ad ties them to.
  • Read date. Every ad carries the date we read it. An ad we could not re-read on the day is left out, not carried forward.

The pay sample is smaller than the tracker and was found differently, by search and by re-checking ads from earlier articles. On November 20, 2026, it held 42 US ads from 31 employers. It is a convenience sample and not a market measure.

What the method misses

  • Workday and custom careers sites. Employers that do not use Greenhouse, Ashby or Lever are mostly missing. On November 20, 2026, the public Workday job search answered HTTP 429, the code for too many requests, for Booz Allen Hamilton, Accenture, NVIDIA, Leidos, CACI, CrowdStrike, Adobe, PwC, Workday, GDIT, Autodesk, HPE, Dell, Intel and Red Hat. The site addresses we tried were guesses and were not confirmed. Google’s careers search returned no job list as text. So large consulting firms, defense contractors and the biggest tech employers are thin in the count.
  • Roles without the phrase in the title. A job that is forward deployed work under another title is not counted. Deployment strategist and applied AI engineer titles are tallied separately for this reason, and the applied AI rule is itself broad: it also catches titles such as “Software Engineer, Applied AI”.
  • Roles with the phrase that are not engineering. The title rule counts them. The variant table separates them.
  • Duplicates across locations. Employers that post the same title once per city, as Databricks, OpenAI and Palantir do, have more postings than distinct jobs. We do not merge them.
  • Zeros. A zero means no posting matched the title rule on that feed at that time. It does not mean the employer has no forward deployed team.
  • Shortened location lists. Long lists of US locations were shortened when captured, so the tracker’s location cells for the largest employers are a sample.
  • Partial reads. ServiceNow’s search reported 130 results and we read the first 100. Deloitte was read as titles only, with locations and clearance not checked ad by ad. Salesforce allowed one page of 20 results out of 118 before the limit applied, so it has no count.
  • Board names. Five boards whose owner we could not confirm were left out: Ashby “magic” and “primer”, Greenhouse “eve”, and Lever “basis” and “cx2”. Three that looked partial were left out too: Greenhouse “dataiku” and “airtable”, and Ashby “hightouch”. All three showed no matching title.

We found no feed under the board names we tried for these employers, which may post elsewhere: Shield AI, Applied Intuition, Hebbia, Groq, dbt Labs, Retool, Rippling, Moveworks, EvenUp, Hippocratic AI, Norm AI, AI21, SSI, Adept, Contextual AI, Liquid AI, DataRobot, H2O.ai, Weights & Biases, Tecton, Replicate, Govini, Hadrian, Second Front, Castelion, Forterra, Overland AI, Clarifai, Regal, Bland, Retell, Unify, CrewAI, Lindy, 11x, Jasper, Sana, Wiz, Zip, ThoughtSpot, Augment, Windsurf/Codeium, Graphite, Spellbook, Luminance, Notable, Thoughtful, Qdrant, Browserbase, Luma, Skild, Flock Safety, Navan, Deel, Maven AGI, Ada and Forethought. Mistral had no public Lever feed under its name.

How often it is updated

  • Monthly. The tracker is re-read each month with the same board names and the same rules. Each edition states its read date and reports all-location and US counts side by side.
  • Quarterly. The forward deployed engineer hiring index sets our counts beside what data providers and employers have published, each with its source and date.

Only like-for-like comparisons are reported as change. When we add feeds, the total rises because the net is wider, so each edition compares the same set of feeds with the previous read. The first comparison covers the 62 feeds read on both October 11 and November 20, 2026: 312 postings then and 308 now, in all locations.

The October read did not record a US-only count. From November 20, 2026, we record one, so US figures can be compared from the next edition on.

How to repeat it, or report a correction

To repeat the count:

  • Take the board names from the employer links in the tracker.
  • Read the three feed addresses above for each board, in one pass, and note the time.
  • Apply the title rules to the posting title only, ignoring case.
  • Apply the US rule to the locations, and the clearance rule to the ad text of the US postings.
  • Report all-location and US counts side by side, with and without interns, and list the employers with none.

Your numbers will differ from ours if you read on another day. Boards change daily.

If you find an error, email jake@kernavique.com with the employer, the figure and what you believe is right. Tell us too if a board is matched to the wrong employer, or if an employer we could not read has a public feed. We check each report against the feed itself.

Two other pages give context for the numbers: the forward deployed engineer role page, and our comparison of the forward deployed engineer and the solutions engineer, which matters when a title blurs the two. Engineers who want to be considered for roles we work on can send us their details and résumé. Companies that need help with a hire can ask us about hiring forward deployed engineers.

What we left out

  • A list of all 177 feeds on this page. The tracker names every employer checked, with or without a matching title, and links each employer with a posting to its board.
  • A way to read Workday. We do not have one that works without a browser. Until we do, those employers stay out of the denominator.
  • Deduplication. We have not tested a rule for merging one job posted in several cities, so we publish postings and say so.
  • A required-or-preferred split for clearance. It needs a reading of each ad, which the keyword match is not.
  • Any weighting. Counts are raw tallies. Nothing is scaled to represent the market.

Questions

  • What counts as a forward deployed engineer posting?
  • Any posting whose title contains “forward deployed” or “forward-deployed”, in any case. The rule reads the title only, so it also counts managers, product managers and a few bankers and lawyers who carry the phrase. Deployment strategist and applied AI engineer titles without the phrase are counted separately and never added in.
  • Why do you read only Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever?
  • Because each publishes an employer’s whole job board as a public feed that can be read the same way every month. That gives us the title, the locations and the ad text for every posting. Workday and custom careers sites do not offer that, and on November 20, 2026, the Workday sites we tried refused our requests.
  • Is this a count of the whole US market?
  • No. It is a count of 177 public job feeds on one day, plus three employers read through their own job search. The employers are a convenience sample, mostly AI companies and other tech employers. A zero for an employer means no title matched on that feed at that time, nothing more.
  • How do you decide that a posting is in the US?
  • A posting counts as US if any location on it names the United States, a US state, “D.C.” or a major US city. On Ashby and Lever, a posting that names no place, such as “Remote”, counts when the feed’s country field says United States. Postings that say only “Hybrid”, “Distributed”, “Remote” or “North America” with no country are left out.
  • Can I repeat the count myself?
  • Yes. Read the same three feed addresses for the same board names, apply the same title, US and clearance rules, and report all-location and US counts side by side. Every employer row in the tracker links to the board we read. Counts move daily, so a later read will differ.
  • How do I report a mistake?
  • Email jake@kernavique.com with the employer, the figure and what you believe is right. If a board name is matched to the wrong employer, or a feed we missed exists, we want to know. We check each report against the feed itself.
