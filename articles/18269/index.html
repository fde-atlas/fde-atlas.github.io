Introduction: What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a role and service model in which AI experts embed themselves at customer companies and take end-to-end ownership, from understanding the business and its challenges to implementing AI agents and ensuring they take hold.

The meaning and origin of FDE

"Forward Deployed" is a term from the military referring to positioning troops on the front line. It describes a way of working in which engineers are placed not in their own company's office but at the customer's site (the front line), where they identify and solve problems. The model is said to have originated with the US data analytics giant Palantir Technologies, and is known as the mechanism that supported the company's growth.

Typical flow of support by an FDE (3 steps)

① Embed at the site and understand the business and its challenges

② Design and implement AI agents tailored to those challenges

③ Support the process through production deployment and company-wide adoption

*The scope of responsibility varies from company to company.

Why it is spreading worldwide now

Generative AI and AI agents are not "deploy and done" tools; business-specific customization and adoption support determine the results. While many companies stall at the PoC (prototype) stage without achieving real outcomes, the FDE model of "embedding at the site and seeing things through to production deployment" is gaining attention as a solution.

In May 2026, Anthropic and OpenAI established FDE organizations one after another, and on June 30, Amazon (AWS) announced a $1 billion investment in its FDE organization. Microsoft also announced the establishment of a new company to support AI adoption, and on June 8, Salesforce fully launched an FDE-style certified partner program in Japan. Following this trend, companies offering "FDE-style services" are rapidly increasing in Japan as well.

Differences from SES and on-site resident engineers

When you hear "engineers stationed at the client's site," many people in Japan think of SES (System Engineering Service). They may seem similar, but their underlying philosophies differ.

PerspectiveSES (on-site resident)FDE
Contract philosophyProviding engineers' working hours (person-months)Working alongside the client toward problem-solving and outcomes
Defining the problemThe client decides and gives instructionsThe FDE understands the business on-site and proactively proposes and defines problems
GoalCompletion of the requested work or developmentAI agents running in production and taking hold in the business

*This is a general summary. Actual contract structures and role divisions vary by company, so be sure to confirm "who defines the problem" and "what the goal is" before commissioning work.

The differences from consulting firms come down to three points: deliverables, who does the implementation, and how compensation is structured.

The differences from SIers (system integrators) come down to three points: whether requirements are fixed, where the built product ends up, and how completion is defined.

How to choose an FDE company | 5 criteria to avoid failure

Companies offering FDE-style services are increasing, but their substance is not uniform. Comparing them across the following 5 criteria will help you narrow down companies that fit your company's goals.

CriterionWhat to look at
① Service modelWhether it is a product-agnostic delegation model (implementation-resident type or consultant-accompaniment type) or a model premised on the provider's own products. Whether dependence on a specific AI product will arise
② Scope of supportHow much of the process—diagnosis, PoC, production development, company-wide adoption—can be delegated. Whether it will stall at the PoC stage
③ On-site and embedded engagement formatHow many days per week on-site, and whether remote work is combined (few companies publish this; most provide individual quotes)
④ Self-sufficiency after withdrawalWhether they support building a system (training and handover) so your company can continue operations on its own after the FDE leaves
⑤ Reliability of informationHow much can be verified through official announcements and third-party reporting (the "confidence" labels on this page)

① Service model (implementation-resident type, consultant-accompaniment type / own-product-premise type)

Even among companies calling themselves "FDE," there are those that handle AI adoption as a whole without being tied to specific products (implementation-resident types led by engineers, and consultant-accompaniment types led by consultants), and those that provide support premised on adopting their own AI products. The latter integrate the product and support, so be aware that continued use of that product after adoption is assumed.

② Scope of support (how far: from diagnosis to adoption)

"Building an AI PoC (prototype) and calling it done" and "running it as a production system and supporting it until it takes hold company-wide" yield very different results. Since the true value of an FDE is said to lie in the latter, confirm where the support ends before signing a contract.

③ Form of residency and accompaniment

Some companies let you choose the number of on-site days per week, from 1 to 5 (e.g., Digirise), but most do not disclose their arrangements. This is an area with little public information, including pricing structures, so we recommend contacting and comparing multiple companies.

④ Self-sufficiency after withdrawal

FDEs do not stay on-site forever. Whether your own team can continue operating and improving after they leave, and whether training and handover programs exist, will affect long-term costs.

⑤ Reliability of information

This market has just emerged, and there are large differences in how much companies disclose about their track records and structures. On this page, companies verified through official announcements plus third-party reporting are labeled with "high" confidence, while those with only self-published information are labeled "medium" or "low" (this does not indicate the quality of their services).

FDE companies by purpose

A quick-reference table for when you're stuck wondering "who should I actually consult?" For each use case, we clearly state the criteria used for selection and introduce representative companies (for a list of all companies, see the categorized list above).

Want to leave everything from deployment to adoption, without being tied to a specific product

Selection criteria: Implementation-resident type with no dependence on its own products, explicitly offering consistent support from diagnosis through company-wide adoption.

  • Dejirize: Dedicated AI engineers on-site 1 to 5 days a week. End-to-end support from AI diagnosis → PoC → full-scale development → company-wide adoption
  • SHIFT: Listed on the TSE Prime Market. Offers "Advanced FDE" from a technology-neutral position, explicitly not tied to specific products
  • Fortience Consulting: NTT Data Group (formerly QUNIE). Consistent support from AI-first business design through implementation and adoption

Want to start small as an SME or mid-size company

Selection criteria: Companies that officially state they serve SMEs and mid-size companies, and publicly disclose either case studies or pricing/contract terms.

  • Tecotec: "Results-linked FDE service" for SMEs and mid-size companies. Publishes case studies with client names anonymized
  • Minna Systems: Explicitly targets SMEs. Starts with an "on-site experience plan" where engineers visit the workplace to experience the work firsthand (service areas: Tokyo, Nagasaki, Gifu, Saga). Publishes case studies with client names
  • Green Waters: FDE service "Deployed AX" explicitly targeting SMEs in non-IT industries. Publishes cost guidelines: 3-month units starting from 300,000 yen per month (excluding tax)

Want to proceed with large corporate group structures and enterprise-grade standards

Selection criteria: Provision structures through large corporate groups or joint ventures/partnerships with overseas AI companies (enterprise type).

  • SB OAI Japan (SoftBank Group × OpenAI): Joint venture. Centered on Crystal Intelligence, responsible for taking projects beyond PoC to full production system building
  • Fujitsu (Uvance Wayfinders): A structure where consultants and engineers work together embedded in the field. Has announced partnerships with OpenAI and Anthropic, with manufacturing as a key focus area
  • Accenture: Established a dedicated FDE organization with Microsoft's cooperation. Introducing a shift to performance-based contracts

Want to supplement your own AI implementation capabilities as an SIer or development company

Selection criteria: Services that explicitly offer resident or contracted support to corporate IT departments and development companies.

  • Japan AI Center (FDE DESK): Provides FDE talent on an SES basis who go into the field, lead coding agents, and handle implementation through adoption
  • Shunkan AI (AI-adaptive development): Specializes in SIers and system development companies. FDEs enter development sites, build AI utilization infrastructure (harness engineering) and handle implementation on behalf of clients, while also supporting the establishment of in-house FDE teams

Want to proceed together with AI products you are using or planning to deploy

Selection criteria: Services where the provider of the product itself offers resident support and accompaniment, based on its own AI infrastructure or products (note that these are product-dependent).

  • Kurashiru (Kurashiru AI OS): Listed on the TSE Growth Market. Built on its own AI agent platform for manufacturers and wholesalers, with the same team handling AI consulting and accompanying development end-to-end
  • LayerX (Ai Workforce): Based on its own generative AI platform. A structure where an implementation-focused FDE and a coordination-focused DS enter as a pair
  • Taylor (Tailor Platform): Centered on the infrastructure for AI-first core systems (ERP), with consultants and engineers supporting everything from organizing business processes to design, implementation, and operation

Want to secure FDE talent for your own team

Selection criteria: Models with a matching/introduction service for FDE talent, or lab-type models where a dedicated team can be secured on a period basis.

  • DeFactory: Classified as consulting-accompaniment type. Also operates SES (development support) for high-skill talent and "SkillAssign", a project matching service for freelancers
  • Lightup: FDE-specialized recruitment agency (performance-based, listed on the TSE Growth Market). For companies wanting to hire FDEs as full-time employees
  • Commerce Robotics (Global AI Lab): Classified as implementation-resident type. A lab-type model that assigns its own FDE team exclusively, with publicly disclosed monthly pricing by role assuming on-site work

Want to search by industry, such as manufacturing

Selection criteria: Companies that explicitly state manufacturing as a target area on their official websites or press releases (added 2026-09-17).

  • Aising: Industry-specialized. Explicitly states "consistent support through implementation and operation, not limited to PoC" for manufacturing production equipment and processes
  • MapleSystems: Industry-specialized FDE. Officially publishes its composition ratios, stating "finance, IT, and manufacturing are the focus"
  • GenX: Own-product-dependent type. Provides accompanying support for manufacturers and BPO providers (note that this is product-dependent)
