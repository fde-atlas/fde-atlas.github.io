Palantir FDE Profile

Plank · 2026-07-18 · 1 min read

Summary

This profile examines Palantir, the company credited with inventing the FDE role. Its engineers embed with customers for months or years, customizing the Foundry and AIP platforms to each client's data models and workflows. Drawing on open job postings, the article notes that most FDE roles require or prefer security clearance—the highest rate among companies profiled—travel of up to 25% is standard, recent postings mention agentic decision-making engines signaling a shift toward autonomous AI agents, and a NYC new-grad fellowship feeds the FDE track. Hiring spans the US, UK/Europe, and Asia-Pacific, mostly mid-level and early-career roles.

Key points

  • Palantir invented the FDE role; engineers embed with customers for months or years.
  • Foundry and AIP are the core platforms FDEs customize per customer.
  • Most FDE postings require or prefer security clearance, the highest rate profiled.
  • Travel up to 25% is standard; recent JDs signal autonomous agent deployments.
  • A NYC new-grad fellowship serves as a pipeline into the FDE track.

The company that invented the FDE role. Engineers sit with customers for months, sometimes years, weaving software directly into operations. Palantir Foundry and AIP are the primary platforms; FDEs customize and extend them for each customer's data model and workflows.
