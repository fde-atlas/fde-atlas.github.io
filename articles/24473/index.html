Microsoft Establishes 6,000-Person FDE Organization in Search of AI Business Monetization

Original title: マイクロソフト、6000人規模のFDE組織を新設 AI事業の収益化模索

日本経済新聞社 · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

Summary

According to Nikkei, Microsoft has created a new organization of roughly 6,000 forward deployed engineers (FDEs) who will be stationed at cloud and other enterprise customers to help them adopt AI. US tech giants face heavy spending on AI model development and data center buildout, and are using FDEs as a core lever to deepen enterprise business and make their AI operations profitable. The article notes that competition to secure FDE talent is intensifying among major tech companies.

Key points

  • Microsoft launched a new FDE organization of about 6,000 engineers.
  • FDEs will be embedded at cloud and enterprise customers to support AI adoption.
  • Heavy investment in AI models and data centers is pressuring profitability.
  • Microsoft aims to monetize AI by deepening enterprise markets around FDEs.
  • Tech companies are racing to secure FDE talent.

Full text

Considering News from Diverse Perspectives

Major U.S. tech companies are rushing to secure "Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs)" who are stationed at client companies to support the use of artificial intelligence (AI). Microsoft has launched a new organization to deploy a 6,000-strong FDE force to customers of its cloud services and other offerings. As companies face mounting costs from AI model development and data center investments, they are seeking to monetize their AI businesses by using FDEs as a core to dig deeper into the corporate market.
