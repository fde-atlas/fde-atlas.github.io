FDE Salary in India 2026: ₹17.7 LPA Median + Free Estimator

FDE India · 2026-11-26 · 8 min read

Summary

This article compiles 2026 salary data for Forward Deployed Engineers in India, combining published benchmarks (Taggd, Scaler, Second Talent, Quess) with real employer-posted listings from JobFound, HireDoor and Glassdoor India, plus an estimator adjusted by experience, company type and city. Sources diverge widely: entry-level benchmarks suggest ₹18–26.5 LPA, but fresher-tagged listings tracked in October 2026 posted only ₹7–15 LPA. Mid-level (3–6 years) pay is typically ₹30–50 LPA. GCCs pay the most (₹35–90 LPA) and IT services the least (₹20–50 LPA). Bengaluru leads city ranges (₹28–80 LPA) while Chennai trails (₹20–55 LPA). Levels.fyi self-reported data shows a median of about ₹17.7 LPA from a small sample of 11 salaries.

Key points

  • Entry-level benchmarks say ₹18–26.5 LPA, but fresher listings posted ₹7–15 LPA.
  • Mid-level FDEs (3–6 years) typically earn ₹30–50 LPA.
  • GCCs pay highest (₹35–90 LPA); IT services lowest (₹20–50 LPA).
  • Bengaluru tops city ranges (₹28–80 LPA); Chennai is lowest (₹20–55 LPA).
  • Levels.fyi median is about ₹17.7 LPA from only 11 self-reported salaries.

How this is calculated

  • Your experience band gives a typical range: the median of the low ends and the median of the high ends of the published benchmarks below.
  • Company type and city each get a factor, from how that type or city compares with the average in the same source (Taggd). The two are averaged so they don't stack.
  • The result stays inside the lowest and highest figures any source gives for your band, rounded to ₹0.5 LPA. Real listings are shown unadjusted.

FDE salary FAQ

What is the FDE salary in Bengaluru?

Taggd's May 2026 benchmark puts Forward Deployed Engineer pay in Bengaluru at about ₹28–80 LPA, weighted towards experienced hires. Employer-posted pay for Bengaluru FDE listings we tracked in Nov 2026 ranged from ₹7–90 LPA. Junior roles pay less; use the estimator above for your experience.

What is the FDE salary in Mumbai?

Taggd's May 2026 benchmark puts Forward Deployed Engineer pay in Mumbai at about ₹24–65 LPA, weighted towards experienced hires. Employer-posted pay for Mumbai FDE listings we tracked in Nov 2026 ranged from ₹20–50 LPA. Junior roles pay less; use the estimator above for your experience.

What is the FDE salary in Hyderabad?

Taggd's May 2026 benchmark puts Forward Deployed Engineer pay in Hyderabad at about ₹25–70 LPA, weighted towards experienced hires. Employer-posted pay for Hyderabad FDE listings we tracked in Nov 2026 ranged from ₹14–20 LPA. Junior roles pay less; use the estimator above for your experience.

What is the average FDE salary in India?

Published benchmarks put a typical entry-level FDE at about ₹18–26.5 LPA and a mid-level FDE (3–6 years) at about ₹30–50 LPA. Self-reported data on Levels.fyi shows a median of about ₹17.7 LPA from a small sample of 11 salaries.

How much do fresher FDEs earn in India?

Benchmarks say ₹18–26.5 LPA, but fresher-tagged FDE listings we tracked in Oct 2026 actually posted ₹7–15 LPA. Treat the benchmark as what strong candidates at top companies can reach, and the listings as the wider market.

Which companies pay FDEs the most in India?

Published ranges put GCCs (₹35–90 LPA) and SaaS and product companies at the top, and IT services and consulting lower (₹20–50 LPA). Among listings with posted pay, a founding FDE role at Cartesia in Bengaluru showed ₹70–90 LPA.

Does city change FDE pay?

Yes. Published city ranges put Bengaluru highest (₹28–80 LPA) and Chennai lowest (₹20–55 LPA). Remote roles for global companies can pay above local bands.

How accurate is this estimator?

It is an indicative range, not an offer. It combines published benchmarks with a company-type and city adjustment, and shows real employer-posted listings next to it so you can see the spread. Check live listings before negotiating.
