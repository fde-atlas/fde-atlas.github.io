FDE Practice Problems From Real Deployments

Forward Deployed Course · 2026-10-01 · 1 min read

Summary

This piece from the Forward Deployed Course presents four practice problems modeled on real client deployments, graded easy, medium, and hard. Each problem includes a brief, tasks, and hints. The easy one covers mapping messy CRM exports into warehouse tables. Medium problems address making an at-least-once partner webhook idempotent to avoid duplicate orders, and building RAG-based Q&A over 400 overlapping HR policy PDFs with trustworthy citations. The hard problem asks readers to build an eval harness for a refund-approval agent, where wrong approvals cost money and wrong escalations cost time, to decide whether a new version can ship.

Key points

  • Four graded practice problems modeled on real client work.
  • Easy: design warehouse tables for messy CRM contact and deal exports.
  • Medium: make a flaky, at-least-once webhook idempotent.
  • Medium: RAG answers with citations over 400 overlapping HR PDFs.
  • Hard: build an eval harness gating a refund-approval agent's releases.
