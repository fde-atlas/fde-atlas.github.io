Summary

This is an article index page from Japanese company Gomumari, collecting its original research and practical guides on business data organization and automation. Notable items include a survey of 40 Japanese companies calling themselves FDEs (finding their websites don't clarify what services they actually offer), a 2026 map of Japan's FDE market, and criteria for choosing between in-house implementation and external FDEs. The page also lists hands-on tests of tools like Claude Code, Notion AI, and MCP, pricing surveys of outsourcing services (data analysis, AI development, business automation), and guides for using public data sources such as e-Stat and the corporate number registry. Articles emphasize measured figures, e.g., only 2 of 63 AI development firms publish prices on their websites.

Key points

  • Surveyed 40 Japanese companies calling themselves FDEs; names alone don't reveal services.
  • Offers a 2026 map of Japan's FDE market and in-house vs. external FDE criteria.
  • Documents hands-on tests of Claude Code, Notion AI, and MCP in real work.
  • Publishes pricing surveys for data analysis, AI development, and automation outsourcing.
  • Explains how to retrieve and use public data like e-Stat and corporate number registries.
