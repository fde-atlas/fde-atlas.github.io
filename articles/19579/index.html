RTS Labs vs Unió Digital (AI Integration Companies, 2026) — independently ranked

Top 11 · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

Summary

Top 11 compares RTS Labs and Unió Digital for US executives deciding between an AI integration firm, an automation vendor, or a forward deployed engineer. RTS Labs ranks #1 in the 2026 US AI integration list with a score of 8 out of 9.4; it targets mid-sized firms wanting AI wired into existing CRM and ERP systems, names systems and a 12-week plan, and prices a fixed fee before build (Clutch lists a $5,000+ minimum and $25–49 hourly rates). Unió Digital ranks #10 with 3.9; it is a Tucson, Arizona IT provider offering a free AI readiness assessment and managed IT at $100–250 per user monthly, with no printed AI project price. Rankings are independent and unpaid.
