What certifications are recommended for FDEs? Comparing acquisition order by career background and how they're valued in job listings

Original title: FDEにおすすめの資格は？経歴別の取得順と求人での評価を比較

FDE総研 · 2026-11-20 · 12 min read

Summary

FDE Soken, a Japanese FDE-focused media run by VACAN Inc., examines which certifications help FDE candidates and in what order to earn them by career background. It finds that Palantir and OpenAI FDE hiring centers on coding and case interviews rather than credentials, with certifications mainly reinforcing resume screening and customer trust. The recommended setup combines AWS and Google Cloud certifications with IPA's information processing exams. When priorities conflict, evidence of hands-on implementation comes first, then cloud certifications, then English scores. For new private credentials like the FDE certification, the site advises waiting until the organizer, evaluation scope, and hiring track record are verifiable.

Key points

  • Palantir and OpenAI FDE hiring relies on coding and case interviews, not certifications.
  • Certifications mainly help with resume screening and building customer trust.
  • Recommended: 2-3 certifications combining AWS, Google Cloud, and IPA exams.
  • Priority order when conditions conflict: implementation evidence, cloud certs, English scores.
  • New private FDE credentials should be avoided until organizer and hiring value are confirmed.

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is an engineering role that embeds with customers on-site, handling everything from problem definition to system implementation and adoption. It is known as a role popularized by Palantir. In this article, FDE Soken (a specialized FDE media outlet operated by Vacan, Inc.) organizes how to choose certifications and the order in which to obtain them by career background, using its own comparison framework of "where certifications help in FDE hiring and actual work."

Do FDEs need certifications? Which certifications are valued in Palantir and OpenAI job postings?

The FDE job postings on Palantir's careers page look at implementation and customer handling rather than certifications. FDE Soken positions certifications as one of several bonus factors.

FDE selection centers on coding interviews, case interviews, and dialogues simulating customer interactions. The FDE job listings on OpenAI's careers page also tend to place heavy emphasis on experience building production-ready systems together with customers. Requiring specific certifications is not the norm. It is best to avoid assuming that pass/fail is decided by certifications alone.

Certifications help in FDE job applications in two places: document screening and customer trust

Certifications for FDEs mainly help in two areas: "reinforcing document screening" and "earning customer trust." FDE Soken evaluates the effectiveness of certifications in the following three ways.

Table: FDE Soken's comparison framework for the "three ways certifications help" (as of November 2026, compiled by the FDE Soken editorial team)

How it helpsSpecific situationsExamples of certifications that helpSituations where it helps less
① Reinforcing document screeningCloud experience is hard to convey through work history aloneAWS certifications, Google Cloud certificationsCoding interviews, case interviews
② Earning customer trustConversations with the client's information systems or security teamsRegistered Information Security Specialist, cloud certificationsProposals to executives
③ Serving as a learning mapSystematically covering unfamiliar areas in a short periodApplied Information Technology Engineer, vendor product certificationsDifferentiating experienced candidates

In terms of Vacan's practical experience, what helps at customer sites is ②. However, when speaking with a customer's information systems department as an FDE, simply mentioning certification names rarely moves the conversation forward. It is more realistic to think of it as: whether you can use the common vocabulary learned through certifications (shared responsibility model, IAM, network isolation, etc.) changes how the first meeting progresses.

Palantir product certifications are highly valued in workplaces that use Foundry

Palantir offers certifications for its Foundry platform through Palantir Learning. When moving to companies or partners that use Foundry, they are a concrete bonus factor. On the other hand, since Foundry is not generally sold to individuals, setting up a learning environment is itself a hurdle. The certification tracks and how to obtain them are covered in detail in our Palantir certification article. For FDE job postings unrelated to Foundry, general cloud certifications can sometimes be easier to convey.

What are the recommended certifications for FDEs? Comparing exam fees, study time, and difficulty

FDE Soken recommends a combination of 2-3 certifications centered on AWS and Google Cloud certifications, combined with IPA's Information Technology Engineer Examination.

FDE work spans a wide range—"collect data → model it → deliver it as a business application → pass security review"—so no single certification covers everything. The difficulty of studying varies greatly depending on your experience. For example, for someone who has used AWS at work for over 2 years, it is not uncommon to reach the Associate level with only a short period of preparation.

Prioritize "relevance to your work" over certification difficulty for FDEs

When choosing certifications for an FDE career, prioritizing "relevance to your work" over difficulty is the approach least likely to fail. Rather than spending a year on an advanced-level exam, it tends to give you more to talk about in FDE selection processes to earn an Associate-level cloud certification and publish a small data integration app as a portfolio piece. Certifications supplement the explanation of "what you can build"; they are not a substitute for it.

In what order should you get FDE certifications based on your background? Does it change with years of experience, current role, English ability, and company size?

The FDE Research Institute's acquisition-order model starts with foundational certifications for those with less than 3 years of experience, and with cloud certifications for those with 3 or more years. Your English ability and the size of your target company raise or lower the priority of the TOEIC.

Prerequisites for FDE aspirants vary greatly from person to person. That is why the FDE Research Institute thinks in terms of the order in which to compensate for "what looks weak." For cases where conditions overlap, check the combination table below.

When conditions conflict, prioritize "proof of implementation" in FDE certification priorities

When conditions conflict for FDE aspirants, the FDE Research Institute prioritizes in the order "proof of implementation → cloud certification → English score."

Examples of conflicting conditions and decision priorities are as follows.

  • High English ability but short experience: A high TOEIC score helps with document screening at foreign companies, but FDE interviews test implementation skills. First gather proof of implementation with the AWS SAA and one app, and add your English score last.
  • Long experience but low English ability: For FDE roles at domestic companies or Palantir partner companies, there are positions that do not require English. It is realistic to first build up your credentials for "being trusted at customer sites" with cloud certifications and security-related certifications.
  • Working at a large company but targeting a small startup: At startups, the weight of certifications drops even further. It is better to redirect some of your certification study time toward building a prototype based on an imagined customer problem.

From VACAN's practical perspective, the closer someone is to being inexperienced, the more they tend to fill their anxiety with the number of certifications. However, what is required at customer sites is the expectation that "if we leave it to this person, will something working come out by next week?" We recommend deciding on a clear cutoff: once you have earned two certifications, switch your time to building things.

How does choosing FDE certifications differ from SWE, IT consulting, PdM, and SIer? Who are certifications not suited for?

According to the FDE Research Institute's comparison, choosing FDE certifications is more customer-oriented than for SWEs and more implementation-oriented than for IT consultants. It is not suited for people for whom obtaining certifications has become the goal itself.

The FDE is a role that "defines problems like a consultant and builds solutions like an engineer." Therefore, certifications that are standard in other roles are not necessarily advantageous as-is. Differences between the roles themselves are compared in FDE vs. SWE, FDE vs. IT consulting, FDE vs. PdM, and FDE vs. SIer.

People from SIer backgrounds, having had certifications valued internally, tend to put a list of certifications front and center in FDE selection processes too. In an FDE resume, it is easier for readers to follow if you summarize certifications at the end and write about customer problems and your own decisions, implementation, and results first.

People who are not suited to FDE certification are those for whom collecting certifications has become the goal itself

FDE certification is not suited to people for whom collecting certifications has become the goal itself, and to those who want to avoid work that involves talking with customers.

If any of the following conditions apply to you, we recommend reconsidering whether the FDE role suits you before adding more certifications.

  • Even after earning a third or later certification, you cannot explain what you built to others
  • Meetings with clients and business trips feel like a burden, and you want to focus on in-house development
  • You want to implement only after specifications are finalized (FDEs are involved from the stage of deciding specifications together with the customer)

We organize in detail who is and is not suited to FDE in People suited to FDE. Checking your fit before pursuing certifications helps you avoid detours.

Should you take new private certifications like the FDE exam now? What information is confirmed vs. unconfirmed?

Under the FDE Research Institute's criteria, there is no need to rush to take new private certifications calling themselves "FDE" until the organizer, scoring criteria, and track record of recognition in hiring are publicly disclosed.

As attention on FDE grows, private certifications and courses bearing the job title are appearing. The "FDE Certification" hosted by the IoT Certification Committee has been reported to have started its Level 1 main exam in November 2026 (online, 100 four-choice questions, 60 minutes). As of November 2026, FDE is not a profession with an industry-wide certification system, so it must be distinguished from certifications with clear organizers and evaluation targets, such as Palantir product certifications and AWS or Google Cloud certifications.

Evaluate FDE-related private certifications on three points: organizer, evaluation target, and hiring track record

Whether to take an FDE-related private certification should be decided after verifying three points through official information: the organizer, the evaluation target, and the hiring track record.

Table: Checklist for evaluating new FDE-related private certifications (as of November 2026, selected by the FDE Research Institute editorial team)

Item to verifyState that can be considered confirmedState to treat as unconfirmed
OrganizerThe corporate name, location, and responsible person are clearly stated on the official websiteOnly the company selling the course is known as the operator
Evaluation targetThe exam scope and passing criteria are publicly availableOnly abstract descriptions such as "skills needed for FDE"
Recognition in hiringThe certification name appears in the preferred requirements of job postingsOnly the organizer's promotional claims, with no evidence in job postings
CostExam fees and renewal fees are clearly statedTaking an expensive course is a condition for taking the exam

While any of the three items remains "unconfirmed," spending your time on AWS, Google Cloud, or IPA certifications, or on building a portfolio, will more reliably serve as material for job changes.

Read information about FDE certifications by separating official announcements from media coverage and promotion

When it comes to information about FDE certifications, separating the organizer's official announcements, media coverage, and social media promotion makes it easier to avoid misjudgments. Even if something is introduced as "valued in hiring by major companies," it cannot be considered confirmed information unless the certification name appears on that company's official recruiting pages or job postings. Treat certifications that have only been covered in the media as being at the "reported to be..." stage, and safely decide whether to take them only after confirming the official exam scope and how the certification is used in hiring.
