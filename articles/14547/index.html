Scale AI Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-01 · 10 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch's October 24, 2026 snapshot of Scale AI's public Greenhouse board counts 11 FDE-family postings, tied for ninth among 121 employers. Nine are engineer roles across three tracks (Frontier Agents, GPS full-stack, Public Sector FDSE) and two are Deployment Strategist seats for DHS/DOJ and Space Systems Command. Public-sector and government work dominates. All nine engineer postings mention Python, LLMs, cloud, and Kubernetes or Docker. Six US seats list pay, with a median midpoint of $240,640 across four engineer ranges; none of the 11 postings are remote, and three state travel percentages up to 50%.

Key points

  • 11 open FDE-family postings, tied with Cloudflare and Cohere for ninth of 121 employers.
  • Tracks: Frontier Agents (SF/NY), GPS full-stack (London, Riyadh, Doha), Public Sector FDSE across six US cities.
  • All 9 engineer postings require Python, LLMs, cloud, and Kubernetes or Docker.
  • Median midpoint of 4 US engineer pay ranges is $240,640, above the $216,104 all-employer figure.
  • No remote listings; 3 postings state travel, with the Public Sector seat at 50%.

Published

Scale AI posts Forward Deployed Engineer seats next to Deployment Strategist seats, with a public-sector and government theme. In FDE Dispatch's October 24, 2026 snapshot of public job boards it has 11 open, tied with Cloudflare and Cohere for ninth of 121 employers. 9 are engineer titles across three tracks: Frontier Agents Engineer (Forward Deployed Engineering) at three levels in San Francisco and New York, Full-Stack Software Engineer (Forward Deployed), GPS (Global Public Sector) with 5 postings in London, Riyadh and Doha, and Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Public Sector across 6 US cities at 50% travel. The other 2 are Deployment Strategist seats (DHS/DOJ in Washington DC; Space Systems Command in Los Angeles). 6 are US-located and 5 are outside the US; none list remote. 3 of 11 state travel. 6 of 11 list USD pay (all US seats); the 4 US engineer ranges have a median midpoint of $240,640. 6 of 11 mention equity. Eleven postings is a small sample. FDE Dispatch is not affiliated with Scale AI; the figures come from Scale AI's public job postings.

What Scale AI calls the role

Scale AI posts under four title tracks in this snapshot: Frontier Agents Engineer (Forward Deployed Engineering), Full-Stack Software Engineer (Forward Deployed), GPS, Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Public Sector, and Deployment Strategist. GPS expands to Global Public Sector in the posting texts. The 2 Deployment Strategist seats sit beside the engineer tracks; see deployment strategist vs forward deployed engineer for how that paired title compares across employers.

Public-sector and government work runs through the sample. The Public Sector engineer text describes embedding with U.S. government customers. The GPS full-stack texts describe Scale's Global Public Sector team building AI applications with public-sector counterparts. The strategist titles name DHS/DOJ and Space Systems Command.

Where the roles are, and how many are remote

6 of the 11 postings (54.5%) are US-located on the published locations index. 5 (45.5%) are outside the US, against 40.1% across all 725 postings: the GPS seats in London, Riyadh and Doha. US cities listed across the 6 US-located postings: Austin, TX, Colorado Springs, CO, Honolulu, HI, Los Angeles, CA, New York, NY, Omaha, NE, San Francisco, CA, St. Louis, MO, Washington, DC.

None of the 11 list remote, against 27.4% across the snapshot. All 11 leave the workplace field blank on the Greenhouse capture.

Metro and remote figures for every employer are on Forward Deployed Engineer jobs by location.

Scale AI's 11 FDE-family postings center on public-sector and government work: a Public Sector Forward Deployed Software Engineer seat across six US cities, five Global Public Sector full-stack seats in London, Riyadh and Doha, plus Frontier Agents roles that deploy production AI with enterprise customers. FDE Dispatch trains engineers for the customer-deployment rounds those hiring loops use.

How much Scale AI Forward Deployed Engineers travel

3 of the 11 postings mention travel, and all 3 state a percentage. Both Deployment Strategist seats say 20-30% (upper bound 30). Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Public Sector says 50%. The median stated upper bound among those 3 is 30%, matching the 30% all-employer median on how much forward deployed engineers travel. The Frontier Agents and GPS seats do not mention travel in this snapshot.

What Scale AI posts for pay

6 of the 11 postings list a USD pay range: every US-located seat. The 5 Global Public Sector (GPS) seats outside the US list no range. Across the 4 US engineer-title ranges the median midpoint is $240,640, against $216,104 across 255 US engineer-title postings on Forward Deployed Engineer salary. Posted ranges are not offers and exclude equity value. Eleven postings is a small sample.

What this means if you are preparing for a Scale AI loop

  • Public sector and government. GPS means Global Public Sector. The Public Sector engineer text describes embedding with U.S. government customers; the strategist titles name DHS/DOJ and Space Systems Command. Have a worked example of shipping software with a government or regulated customer.
  • Track. Frontier Agents seats focus on production AI agents with enterprise customers. GPS seats are full-stack builds for public-sector counterparts outside the US. Public Sector FDSE is the multi-city U.S. government engineer seat. Read the track in the title before you decide which seat fits.
  • Travel and location. Only 3 of 11 state travel. The Public Sector seat states 50% across six US cities. GPS seats sit in London, Riyadh or Doha with no travel figure in this snapshot.

Round formats for FDE loops are on the FDE interview loop, explained, and practice prompts on FDE interview practice questions.

Engineer median midpoint uses the 4 US engineer-title ranges. These 11 listings were live on the October 24, 2026 snapshot; counts are open postings on one day and do not measure headcount.
