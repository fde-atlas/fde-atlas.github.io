FDE Hiring Tracker: Which Companies Are Hiring Forward Deployed Engineers

Original title: FDE 招聘追踪：哪些公司在招前线部署工程师

FDE Times · 2026-11-15 · 2 min read

Summary

FDE Times has tracked official job boards of 181 companies daily since October 7, 2026, counting roles whose titles explicitly mention forward deployed engineering or deployment strategy. As of November 15, 2026, there are 863 open FDE positions, with Palantir (95), Databricks (94), and Amazon AWS (68) hiring the most. The page breaks down roles by type, region, and city, notes methodology and data caveats, and offers free JSON APIs and embeddable widgets. Spikes in the chart reflect expanded coverage, not hiring surges.

Methodology

We read the official job boards of 181 companies hiring FDEs multiple times a day (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workday, plus the official career sites of Amazon, Microsoft, ByteDance, and Tencent), keeping roles whose titles explicitly mention forward deployed engineering or deployment strategy. Once a company removes a role, it is dropped from the count. Seniority and role type are inferred from the job title.
