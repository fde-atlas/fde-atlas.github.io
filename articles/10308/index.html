Full text

FDE Pulse Glossary

Also: Forward Deployed AI Engineer, FDE (AI)

A Forward Deployed AI Engineer is the AI-era version of the FDE. The core job is the same, embed with a customer and ship working software, but the systems are AI systems: retrieval-augmented generation, evaluation harnesses, prompt and agent workflows, and integrations with models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and others.

Demand for this variant is climbing fastest because most enterprise AI pilots stall at deployment rather than at model quality. The Forward Deployed AI Engineer is the person who gets a model from impressive demo to production system inside a specific company.

On FDE Pulse, AI-focused roles carry a measurable pay premium over general FDE roles, which reflects the added LLM and retrieval skills on top of the standard forward deployed profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Forward Deployed AI Engineer do?

They embed with a customer and build AI systems in that customer's environment: RAG pipelines, agent workflows, evaluation frameworks, and model integrations. It is a Forward Deployed Engineer role specialized in shipping AI into production.
