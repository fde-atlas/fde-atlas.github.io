Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer Interview (No Offer): 7 rounds over 3 months, with

Taro · 2023-10-01 · 2 min read

Summary

This Taro post compiles interview and employee reviews for Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer role in New York. One candidate describes 7 rounds over 3 months, including a product design question using Foundry, only for the final round to be delayed a week and then canceled because the spot was already filled. Across 36 reported interview experiences, the pass rate is just 14%, though 56% of candidates viewed the process positively. Current employee reviews are mixed: smart coworkers and flexible growth paths, but no 401k matching, no bonuses, no raises with promotions, long hours, and uneven team quality.

Key points

  • One candidate went through 7 rounds over 3 months; the final round was canceled as the spot was filled
  • Only a 14% pass rate across 36 reported interview experiences
  • Interviews included a product design question using Foundry
  • 56% of candidates rated the interview process positively
  • Employees cite no bonuses, no 401k matching, no promotion raises, and long hours
