Summary

A community forum thread aggregating dozens of first-hand interview write-ups and compensation data for ML and software engineering roles at frontier labs and major tech companies, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Databricks, Scale AI, NVIDIA, and Stripe. Entries cover the full pipeline — recruiter screens, online assessments, technical phone screens, and onsite loops — with most processes ending in rejection or waitlist and a few resulting in offers. It also includes Forward Deployed Engineer experiences at C3 AI and Databricks. Sensitive compensation figures are restricted to members.

Key points

  • Dozens of first-hand interview reports covering OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Databricks and more.
  • Most processes end in rejection or waitlist; a few result in offers, with round-by-round question details.
  • Includes Forward Deployed Engineer interview experiences at C3 AI and Databricks.
  • Compensation package data is members-only.
  • Formats span recruiter calls, online assessments, phone screens and multi-round onsites.
