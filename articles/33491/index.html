Freshworks, the Chennai-founded maker of Freshdesk and Freshservice, has not advertised a role called Forward Deployed Engineer in India. Its closest match is the Lead AI Solution Engineer for Freshservice in Bengaluru, posted on 26 August 2026 on the Accel job board. The person in that role builds working AI agent proofs of concept for enterprise buyers, connects Freshservice to their data and tools, and helps get those agents live after the sale. Think of it as FDE work that starts in pre-sales.

What the FDE does at Freshworks

The demand comes from Freshworks’ push into AI agents. In June 2025, DT Next reported that Freshworks launched a new version of its Freddy AI platform, which takes actions across connected apps instead of only replying, along with Freddy AI Agent Studio, a no-code tool for building and deploying AI agents. Agents that act inside a customer’s systems need someone technical to wire them up, and that is this role.

From the Bengaluru listing, the Lead AI Solution Engineer:

  • Owns the AI story in big deals. The engineer is the technical lead on Freddy AI in strategic and competitive deals, including against ServiceNow’s Now Assist.
  • Builds proofs of concept. This means demos and agentic workflows in Agent Studio, built around the customer’s real use cases such as ticket deflection, agent assist and workflow automation.
  • Integrates. The engineer connects Freshservice to customer data and tools through MCP Gateway and APIs.
  • Handles reviews. This covers RFPs, executive briefings, and security and architecture reviews of the AI components.
  • Hands over to delivery. The role bridges pre-sales to Professional Services and Customer Success so the solution actually goes live.
  • Builds reusable assets. That means demo environments and ROI models, plus product feedback from the field and mentoring of other solution engineers.

A second, more post-sales role existed in Chennai. A Lead Customer Success Engineer listing on Built In Chennai asked for 3+ years in CX consulting, technical account management or solutions engineering. It covered workflow audits and advice on Freddy AI and automation. That listing was removed in July 2025 and did not mention coding or APIs, so treat it as an adjacent path, not an FDE role.

What this means for you:

  • The bar is senior, and pre-sales experience counts. Eight years of solutions or sales engineering is the stated floor. Backend engineers can still compete if they have done customer-facing work. Our solutions engineer vs FDE guide explains how the two roles overlap.
  • Agent knowledge is the differentiator. RAG, tool calling and MCP sit in the required list, not the nice-to-have list.
  • ITSM context helps. Knowing how IT service desks, incidents and asset management work will make your demos far more convincing.

How to prepare

  • Build an ITSM agent end to end. Make an agent that reads a support ticket, searches a knowledge base with RAG, calls a tool to reset a password or raise a request, and returns structured output. Use LangGraph or LlamaIndex, since the listing names them.
  • Learn MCP properly. Write a small MCP server that exposes an internal API, such as an asset inventory, and connect your agent to it. This mirrors Freshservice’s MCP Gateway work.
  • Try Freddy AI Agent Studio. Start a Freshservice trial, build one agentic workflow, and note where no-code stops and custom integration starts. That gap is where this job lives.
  • Rehearse a demo with objections. Prepare a 20-minute demo of your agent and practise answers on data privacy, hallucinations, cost and ROI. Dataford says the key round is a live demo with objection handling.
  • Prepare deal and deployment stories. Have two examples of a customer evaluation you led and a solution you took live, with numbers. Our FDE interview questions guide has scenario prompts. To compare with a similar platform role, see the ServiceNow FDE page.

Freshworks FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Recruiter screen: Dataford's Freshworks Solutions Engineer guide (updated Aug 2026) describes a first call on background and fit. Freshworks has not published an official process for the AI Solution Engineer role.
  • Hiring manager deep dive: A conversation on your skills and past deals or deployments, according to the same Dataford guide.
  • Demo round: Dataford describes a performance-based product demo in which you handle technical objections live, tailor it to an industry and frame ROI. It says coding is generally not asked, but API and integration concepts are.

Frequently asked questions

Does Freshworks hire Forward Deployed Engineers in India?

Not under that title as of November 2026. The closest role is the Lead AI Solution Engineer for Freshservice in Bengaluru, which builds AI agent proofs of concept, connects Freshservice to customer systems through APIs and MCP Gateway, and hands deals over to delivery teams.

What is the salary of a Solution Engineer at Freshworks in India?

Freshworks does not post pay on its India listings. AmbitionBox shows ₹6.7–20 lakh a year for Solution Engineers (12 salaries, 2–11 years) and ₹14.2–25 lakh for Senior Solution Engineers in Bengaluru (20 salaries, 2–8 years). These are self-reported and not specific to the newer AI role.

How much experience does the Freshworks AI Solution Engineer role need?

The Bengaluru listing asks for 8+ years in solutions engineering, sales engineering, solution consulting or technical pre-sales, ideally in IT service management or enterprise SaaS, plus hands-on experience deploying LLM-based solutions for customers.

Is there coding in the Freshworks Solution Engineer interview?

Dataford's guide says coding is generally not asked and the key round is a live product demo with objection handling. You should still understand APIs and integrations well, and for the AI role, be ready to explain how you would build an agent.
