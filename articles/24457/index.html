Summary

This blog roundup from the Japan FDE Association mixes practical guides with industry news. The guides cover designing AI audit trails, pitfalls in SSO and role integration with legacy systems, building internal controls for AI deployments, and documentation and handover practices to prevent knowledge silos. News items include reports that Anthropic is investing $100 million in AI talent development with a mention of five qualities needed in FDEs; a Unite.AI review of the book 'Forward Deployed' on Palantir's FDE model; Salesforce launching its Partner FDE program in Korea, aiming to train over 100 people by next year; Simplex hosting an FDE study session; and HCLTech hiring 6,136 new graduates in one quarter while planning over 10,000 for the fiscal year ending March 2027.

Key points

  • Anthropic reportedly invests $100 million in AI talent, citing five FDE qualities.
  • Salesforce launches Partner FDE training in Korea, targeting 100+ trainees.
  • HCLTech hires 6,136 graduates in a quarter; 10,000+ planned by March 2027.
  • Practical guides cover audit trails, permissions, internal controls, and handovers.
  • Unite.AI reviews 'Forward Deployed', a book on Palantir's FDE approach.

For Enterprises / DX Promotion

Designing AI Audit Trails: Recording Techniques You Can Explain Later

Explains audit trail design for keeping records of AI agent and LLM executions in a form that can be explained to third parties later. Organizes the log items to record, recording timing, and approaches to storage and reference so DX promotion staff can apply them to their own workflows.

Technical Skills / Tool Utilization

Common Sticking Points in FDE Authentication and Permission Integration

Summarizes, from practical experience, the areas where teams tend to get stuck when introducing SSO and role integration with existing business systems. Explains design principles based on permission mapping and audit trails.

FDE News Commentary

Anthropic Invests $100 Million in AI Talent

Overseas media reported that Anthropic will invest $100 million in AI talent development. The report also touches on five qualities required of FDEs who handle enterprise adoption. A concise summary from an FDE practitioner's perspective.

FDE Introduction / Careers

Introduction to FDE Internal Controls: How to Build Systems That Don't Break Down

Explains, from an FDE practitioner's perspective, how to build internal controls that keep operations running smoothly after AI adoption. Clearly organizes the overall picture of segregation of duties, approval flows, and audit trails.

FDE News Commentary

Book Explaining Palantir FDE Published

Unite.AI published a review of "Forward Deployed," a book explaining Palantir's FDE methodology. Reports fact-based highlights of the FDE model, which challenges traditional SaaS principles.

FDE News Commentary

Salesforce Launches FDE Training in South Korea

Salesforce has introduced its Partner FDE program for training AI agent specialists in South Korea. It held its first workshop in Seoul and aims to train more than 100 people by next year.

FDE News Commentary

Simplex Holds FDE Study Session

Simplex held an FDE study session for engineers. The session covers the role and career of engineers in the AI era, presented by practitioners of AI-driven development and an AI CoE lead.

FDE News Commentary

HCLTech Hires 6,136 New Graduates, Strengthening FDE Development

HCLTech hired 6,136 new graduates in a single quarter and is focusing on developing field-deployed engineers. It also plans to hire more than 10,000 new graduates in the fiscal year ending March 2027.

Field Practice / Project Case Studies

FDE Handover and Documentation: Preventing Knowledge Silos

Organizes, from a practitioner's perspective, the types and granularity of documentation FDEs should leave behind and the order of handover, so operations keep running even after the person in charge leaves after deployment. You'll learn where to start with a handover plan that avoids knowledge silos.
