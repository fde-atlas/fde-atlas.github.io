~ Strengthening its long-practiced FDE-style approach to accelerate manufacturing DX ~

Aising Corporation (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director and CEO: Junichi Izawa) has renewed its long-practiced, on-site companion-style AI implementation support service, AI Implementation Companion Service. The service is characterized by a support approach grounded in the essence of FDE (Forward Deployed Engineering), which is attracting growing worldwide attention. At the same time, the company has also released a free document, Concept Book — FDE Approach, which organizes the commonalities between the company's support approach and the essence of FDE.

■ Background of the Renewal

In recent years, the emergence of new technologies such as physical AI and world models has raised expectations for AI utilization in the manufacturing industry, making it an increasingly important management theme for enhancing corporate competitiveness. On the other hand, there are many cases where, even after conducting a PoC (proof of concept), companies fail to move on to full-scale implementation or continuous business improvement.

Behind this lies the characteristic that in industry, especially in areas like manufacturing, equipment configurations, production processes, quality standards, and available data differ greatly from company to company. For this reason, simply implementing AI is not enough; it is important to determine where AI should be applied most effectively and to implement it in a way that fits each site.

This FDE concept of "going deep into the field and taking charge of everything from organizing issues to designing, implementing, and deploying AI systems in production" has been spreading in recent years, centered on leading AI companies worldwide.

Even before FDE attracted worldwide attention, our company had practiced AI implementation support close to manufacturing sites for about 10 years, accumulating know-how for looking over the overall structure across processes, determining where AI would be most effective, rooting it into the field, and continuously generating value. The industry's AI implementation competition is also shifting from a stage of competing on model performance to a stage of competing on implementation capability that produces results in the field.

Based on these changes in the market environment and the expertise we have cultivated, we have renewed the AI Implementation Companion Service into a companion-style service that steadily advances AI implementation toward realizing the "future our customers want to achieve."

■ Overview of the AI Implementation Companion Service

In this renewal, we clarified the relationship between the four support phases we have practiced to date — "field surveys," "feasibility verification," "overall planning," and "requirements definition" — and the three functions organized in light of the essence of FDE — "issue translation," "results implementation," and "knowledge feedback" — and restructured the support content and features to be easier to understand. We consistently support everything from identifying where AI can be most effective in manufacturing to implementation and operation beyond PoC, creating continuous business results.

■ Release of the Concept Book — FDE Approach

This document organizes the essence of FDE, which is attracting worldwide attention, from the perspective of AI implementation in manufacturing, and explains the correspondence between the three functions we define — "issue translation," "results implementation," and "knowledge feedback" — and the AI Implementation Companion Service. Those who would like to learn more about how to apply an FDE-style approach to AI implementation in manufacturing can download it for free from our website.

■ About Aising Corporation

With the MISSION of "pioneering unexplored technological frontiers for humanity and implementing a future beyond imagination," the company aims to realize a society where humans and AI collaborate by making equipment intelligent through AI that is close to the real world. Since its founding in 2016, the company has expertise in both control technology and AI, and has achieved AI applications across a wide range of industrial fields, from individual control of products and FA in manufacturing to complex overall control in the energy and infrastructure industries, contributing to solving the challenges of many customers including Tokyo Gas, Hitachi Construction Machinery, and OMRON. Currently, the company supports the implementation of physical AI in manufacturing, centered on its FDE business (Forward Deployed Engineering business) and engineering infrastructure development business (Platform for Engineering business). The company has received numerous awards, including the Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry Award and selection as a J-Startup company.
