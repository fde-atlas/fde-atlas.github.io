Summary

Konica Minolta is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) for its AI SaaS business (ICW) in Tokyo. The role embeds in customer development teams to drive adoption and retention of AI-driven development, following rollout plans set by a separate AI-driven development lead. Duties include onboarding, training and coaching client engineers, monitoring usage and retention, documenting best practices, and feeding field issues back to the product team. Required: software development experience, 3+ years with CI/CD-based team development, and hands-on use of generative AI tools. FDE or solutions engineer backgrounds are preferred. Salary is 8–14 million yen including bonuses, with flextime and remote work (about one office day per week).

Key points

  • Konica Minolta's ICW unit seeks an FDE to embed AI-driven development in client engineering teams.
  • The role covers onboarding, coaching, usage monitoring, best-practice sharing, and product feedback.
  • Requirements include 3+ years of CI/CD team development and generative AI tool experience.
  • Preferred backgrounds: FDE, customer engineer, technical support, or solutions engineer roles.
  • Compensation: 8–14 million yen annually, Tokyo-based, flextime with remote work allowed.

  • Job Title
  • [Tokyo] FDE (AI SaaS Business)
  • Job Description
  • 【About the Department】 The ICW business is an "AI SaaS business" that solves challenges faced by companies and society through the power of AI. Aiming to achieve both social impact and business growth, you can take on the entire process end-to-end—from planning and development to commercialization and customer adoption—with the speed and autonomy of a startup. The greatest appeal is the ability to turn your own ideas into services, transform how customers work and how society operates, and grow the business itself in the process. 【Purpose of Hiring and Challenges to Solve】 In the ICW business, we are working to commercialize a service that transforms customers' development organizations into AI Native development processes, as part of realizing "AI-driven development" in which AI autonomously advances the development process while people focus on critical decisions and approvals. Launching this service requires two essential pillars: an AI-driven development lead who analyzes customers' development organizations and workflows and designs implementation policies while leading projects, and an FDE who embeds within customer sites to support post-implementation usage, adoption, and improvement. This hiring is being conducted to strengthen a system capable of consistently supporting everything from environment-specific implementation to on-site adoption for each customer, achieving dramatic improvements in development productivity and time-to-market, stabilizing quality, and transforming organizations so engineers can focus on more creative decisions. 【Job Description】 In this position, as a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), you will embed within customers' development sites and, using our AI products under development, take charge of promoting usage after AI-driven development is implemented, supporting adoption, solving on-site issues, and driving continuous improvement. Based on the implementation policies and plans formulated by the AI-driven development consultant, your role is to work alongside customer engineers on actual development tasks while establishing AI-driven workflows as part of their everyday development process. As the engineer closest to the field, the FDE understands customer engineers' usage status and challenges, provides necessary support and improvements, and is responsible for usage rates, adoption rates, results generated in target teams, and gathering feedback for the product. 【Responsibilities】 ・Onboarding and usage support for AI-driven workflows and AI products at customers' development sites ・Conducting training, coaching, study sessions, and consultation sessions for customer engineers ・Monitoring usage status, usage rates, continued usage, behavioral changes on-site, etc., for AI-driven development and AI products ・Creating success stories at customer sites and organizing and sharing best practices across the organization ・Collecting and structuring issues and improvement requests arising in the field, and providing feedback to the AI-driven development lead and AI product development team ・Verifying implementation results in collaboration with the AI-driven development lead and executing improvement measures, including product planning ※This includes regular visits to customer sites and on-site support for certain periods depending on project status.
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • 【Required Qualifications】 1. Professional experience in software development, with experience in multiple stages such as design, implementation, testing, and code review 2. Knowledge and experience with team development using CI/CD, code review, testing, etc.: 3+ years 3. Experience using generative AI or generative AI development support tools in software development work 4. Experience clearly explaining and supporting technical content or usage methods for engineers and users 5. Experience identifying and isolating issues arising in the field and resolving them in collaboration with other technical staff as needed ※Experience in formulating AI-driven development implementation policies, leading organizational transformation projects, or developing AI agent platforms is not required. 【Preferred Qualifications】 ・Experience as an FDE, customer engineer, technical support, solution engineer, etc. ・Experience in implementation, onboarding, and adoption support of customer-facing systems, development tools, SaaS, etc. ・Experience using generative AI development support tools such as Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, and Cursor in professional work ・Development experience in environments using agile development, Scrum, DevOps, CI/CD, etc. ・Experience in training, coaching, study sessions, onboarding, etc., for engineers ・Experience providing technical support to development organizations at enterprise companies
  • Appeal of the Position
  • ・You can deeply engage with customers' development sites and be directly involved in the moments when engineers' ways of working and development processes change. ・You can work on actual development tasks alongside customer engineers and contribute to generating the benefits of AI-driven development. ・Through customer reactions and usage data, you can see how your support contributed to usage rates and adoption rates. ・You can reflect issues and insights gained at customer sites into improvements to services and frameworks, contributing to the evolution of the entire service, not just individual customers. ・By dealing with different development environments, development cultures, and technical levels for each customer, you can deepen your expertise in coaching and adoption support in addition to engineering.
  • Ideal Candidate Profile
  • ・Someone who can enter the customer's field and face challenges from the same perspective as customer engineers ・Someone who finds fulfillment not in merely introducing technology, but in customers actually continuing to use it and achieving results ・Someone who can persistently encourage necessary improvements and behavioral change while building trusting relationships with customers
  • Expected Role
  • For the FDE, as the engineer closest to the customer's development site, we expect you to transform AI-driven development and AI products from "installed mechanisms" into "processes and services used daily." You will work hands-on alongside customer engineers, understand usage stumbling blocks and site-specific issues, and provide necessary support and improvements. Rather than independently designing and managing the overall framework application policy or implementation projects, we expect you to take responsibility for on-site usage rates, adoption rates, results generated in target teams, and feedback gathering, based on the policies formulated by the AI-driven development lead.
