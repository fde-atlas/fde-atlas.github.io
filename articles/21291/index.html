Summary

This is a job posting on OpenWork by Japanese consulting firm Alphakt for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) / AI×BPR implementation engineer at manager level, positioned as a development lead or future technical lead. Alphakt focuses on AI-driven business process reform for large enterprises in manufacturing, telecom, energy and finance, targeting 30 billion yen revenue and 1,000 employees by 2030 with an IPO plan. The role covers architecture design for generative AI, LLM, RAG and AI agent systems, technology selection, quality control, team management, and stakeholder alignment, plus work on in-house products GISHO and PERKS. Requirements include roughly five years of development experience, Python or TypeScript skills, and architecture and team leadership experience; hands-on generative AI experience is not mandatory. Salary is 10–14 million yen including 45 hours of fixed overtime, with hybrid remote work from Shibuya, Akasaka or Osaka offices.
