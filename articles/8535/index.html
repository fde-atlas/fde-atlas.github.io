Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (4/5, Jul 26, 2024): Smart People, Very Political

Taro · 2024-04-05 · 2 min read

Summary

A former Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer who spent four years there rates the company 4/5 and recommends it. Pros include very smart colleagues, good culture, and solid benefits like office meals. Cons: the environment is highly political, requiring both people and technical skills; work-life balance is poor (rated 2/5); many talented people leave to found startups; and the culture can feel cult-like. The reviewer argues the flat structure is illusory, with middle-management churn burying good people and ideas, and urges management to take care of employees.

Key points

  • Former FDE of 4 years gives 4/5 and recommends Palantir
  • Praise for smart colleagues, culture, and benefits
  • Highly political environment; people skills matter as much as tech
  • Work-life balance rated only 2/5; some staff leave to start companies
  • Reviewer says flat structure is a myth; middle management churn loses talent

A tad culty at times.

Advice to Management

The flat structure isn't really a thing as much as we pretended it was. A lot of great people and good ideas get lost in the mire of middle management churn. It's really essential to be engaged and take care of your people; without your people, there is no mission.
