FDE Consulting

Original title: ブログ | FDE Consulting

FDE Consulting · 2026-02-10 · 29 min read

Summary

This is an article index for the blog of FDE Consulting, a Japanese firm, written by Koin Mikata. Posts fall into two main threads: the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role and enterprise AI adoption. FDE articles cover the Palantir-originated FDE model, OpenAI and Anthropic FDE teams, a survey of 97 open FDE job postings at 65 Japanese companies (November 2026), comparisons with Deployment Strategists, Solutions Architects and Sales Engineers, plus certification and interview guidance. The AI adoption posts target buyers, covering tool selection (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot), PoC and contracting, security frameworks such as the EU AI Act, ISO 42001 and OWASP, and operations. Articles cite official Japanese government and vendor sources.

Key points

  • Blog index lists roughly 80 articles on FDE careers and enterprise AI adoption.
  • FDE salary post aggregates 97 postings from 65 Japanese firms, 53 with pay data.
  • Multiple posts compare FDE with Deployment Strategist, Solutions Architect, Sales Engineer roles.
  • AI adoption posts cover PoC, contracts, security compliance, evals and LLMOps.
  • Sources include Japanese ministries, IPA, AISI, NIST, OWASP and vendor documentation.
