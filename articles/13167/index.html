The Field Deployed Engineer's Handbook

Leanpub · 2026-05-06 · 7 min read

Summary

A Leanpub handbook for forward deployed engineers, structured in 27 chapters across six parts: the role, method, ground, orchestration, proof, and the human side. It deliberately avoids specific products and certification exams, focusing instead on transferable concepts, field judgement, and engineering discipline for AI deployments. The central claims: the model is not the product, non-determinism is an engineering problem rather than an excuse, and the FDE's real deliverable is customer autonomy plus reusable assets like harnesses and evals. Three fictional engagements run through worked examples, and each chapter includes takeaways, field exercises, and scenario questions, with appendices covering a skills self-assessment and a judgement index.

Key points

  • 27 chapters spanning the role, method, technical ground, orchestration, proof, and human side.
  • Avoids product specifics; emphasizes portable concepts and field judgement.
  • Core thesis: the model is not the product; non-determinism needs engineering.
  • Real deliverable is customer autonomy and reusable assets, not dependency.
  • Includes worked engagements, scenario questions, and a self-assessment appendix.
