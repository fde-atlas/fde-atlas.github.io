Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (4/5): Incredible work, people, and benefits

Taro · 2024-10-24 · 2 min read

Summary

A former Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer who spent six years at the company rates the role 4 out of 5 with a positive outlook. The review praises unmatched access to meaningful problems, world-class colleagues, and top-rated compensation and benefits, while noting the company expects no work-life balance and suits only highly committed performers. Diversity and inclusion scored just 1/5. The page also compiles three interview accounts from 2018, 2020, and 2024, all starting with a HackerRank online test, followed by behavioral phone screens and live coding or multi-round technical and non-technical interviews; one 2024 candidate noted an interviewer gave unclear instructions.

Key points

  • Six-year former employee rates the FDE role 4/5
  • Pros: great problems, world-class people, 5/5 compensation
  • Cons: work-life balance rated only 1/5
  • Diversity, equity, and inclusion also scored 1/5
  • Interviews start with HackerRank, then behavioral and technical rounds
