FDE Portfolio Projects: 12 Builds With Client Briefs

Forward Deployed Course · 2026-10-01 · 1 min read

Summary

This piece from the Forward Deployed Course lays out 12 portfolio projects for aspiring FDEs, each with a fictional client brief, deliverables, and a grading rubric. Projects are grouped into beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels, and the author suggests shipping one per level so interviewers can open a concrete portfolio. Beginner builds cover CSV-to-API syncing, a citation-backed FAQ bot, webhook handling, and KPI reporting. Intermediate ones include CRM lead enrichment, contract clause extraction with evals, ticket triage, and RAG across multiple tools. Advanced builds span multi-agent finance workflows, serving a model on client infrastructure, a prompt regression eval platform, and a full deployment with a handover pack.

Key points

  • 12 portfolio projects, each with a fictional client brief, deliverables, and rubric.
  • Projects are tiered beginner, intermediate, and advanced; ship one per level.
  • Beginner builds: CSV sync, FAQ bot with citations, webhooks, KPI reports.
  • Intermediate builds: lead enrichment, clause extraction with evals, triage, multi-source RAG.
  • Advanced builds: multi-agent finance ops, on-prem model serving, eval platform, full handover.
