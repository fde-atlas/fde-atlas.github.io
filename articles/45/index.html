Meet the a16z Forward Deployed Engineer Fellows

A16Z · 2026-07-22 · 2 min read

Summary

a16z announced the inaugural cohort of its Forward Deployed Engineer Fellowship, an 8-week program for FDEs and Applied AI leaders deploying AI in enterprises. From thousands of applications, 65 fellows were selected from companies including OpenAI, Mistral, Cognition, HappyRobot, Poetic, and Decagon. Many are Palantir alumni now founding members at companies like Hex, Anthropic, and ElevenLabs; others are FDE architects at high-growth startups such as Snowflake, Notion, Ramp, and Harvey, plus a next generation already deploying live.

Key points

  • a16z launched its first FDE Fellowship, an 8-week cohort.
  • 65 fellows chosen from thousands of applications.
  • Fellows come from OpenAI, Mistral, Cognition, Decagon, and others.
  • Many are Palantir alumni, now founders or early members at Hex, Anthropic, ElevenLabs.
  • Cohort includes FDE architects and builders already deploying AI live.

Full text

America | Tech | Opinion | Culture | Charts

We are excited to announce the fellows who will be part of the inaugural a16z Forward Deployed Engineer Fellowship, an 8-week cohort of FDEs and Applied AI leaders at the forefront of deploying AI into real-world enterprises.

We received thousands of applications from talented people around the world. Today, we’re thrilled to share more about the inaugural cohort of 65 fellows who are leading FDE at companies like OpenAI, Mistral, Cognition, HappyRobot, Poetic, and Decagon.

Many are Palantir alumni who are now founding members of companies pioneering the FDE playbook like Barry McCardel from Hex, Kevin Bai at Anthropic, Isa Gomez from OpenAI, Senta Knuth from ElevenLabs, and Vinoo Ganesh from Kepler.

Some are FDE architects behind top high-growth startups like Luv Kothari from Snowflake, Perry Ha from Decagon, Bo Sun at Notion, Leo Mehr at Ramp, Niko Grupen at Harvey, and Anitej Biradir from ElevenLabs.

Others are the next generation of FDE builders already deploying live like Priya Khandelwal from Nixo, Tom Kimbrell from Stuut, Lucas Kiefer from Strala, Kian Fattahy from Saronic, Chelsey Fang from Pylon, Amy Jin from Decagon, Rohan Ramani from Netic, Jakub Hojsan from Exa, and Mariano Pennini from Spade.

Congratulations and welcome, a16z Forward Deployed Engineer Fellows!
