Building an Enterprise FDE Practice: The Operating Guide

FDE Interviews · 2026-10-06 · 6 min read

Summary

This 108-page operating guide targets leaders building or scaling an enterprise forward deployed engineering (FDE) practice, organized around mandate, people, delivery, and economics. Its core argument: hiring one engineer is easy, but a durable practice needs a funded charter, explicit admission rules for which work to accept, visible authority, pilots that answer defined questions, and a handoff to an owner who can operate the result. It includes 26 chapters, 21 diagrams, four fictional worked cases, 24 editable forms, and a Python calculator. The author stresses the numbers are teaching examples, not benchmarks. Full access requires paid Premium; only three sample pages are public.

Key points

  • Guide for founders, vendor team leads, and internal enterprise teams building FDE practices.
  • Organized into 26 chapters across mandate, people, delivery, and funding decisions.
  • Stresses evidence gates: admission rules, question-driven pilots, and capable handoff owners.
  • Includes four fictional cases, 24 editable forms, and a Python-based economics calculator.
  • Explicitly warns staffing ratios and financial figures are teaching examples, not benchmarks.
