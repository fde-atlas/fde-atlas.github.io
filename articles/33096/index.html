Deloitte says it was first on Salesforce forward deployed work

Kernavique · 2026-08-12 · 1 min read

Summary

Kernavique reports that Deloitte claimed on August 12, 2026 to be the first global system integrator to deliver a Salesforce forward deployed engagement. The claim comes from Deloitte's own press release, which says forward deployed engineers are embedded directly within solution delivery teams but gives no headcount. Kernavique notes the "first" claim is self-reported and unread, and draws implications: consultancies can run forward deployed work on a vendor's platform alongside the vendor's engineers, though hiring managers get no data to judge capacity.

Key points

  • Deloitte claims to be the first global system integrator delivering a Salesforce forward deployed engagement.
  • The claim is from Deloitte's own August 12, 2026 press release, not independently verified.
  • Forward deployed engineers are embedded directly within solution delivery teams.
  • No headcount is disclosed, so team size and capacity are unknown.
  • Kernavique suggests consultancies can deliver such work alongside a vendor's own engineers.

Deloitte said on August 12, 2026, that it was the first global system integrator to deliver a Salesforce forward deployed engagement. The claim is in Deloitte’s own press release of that date. The release says Deloitte is embedding forward deployed engineers directly within solution delivery teams. It gives no headcount for those engineers.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: Deloitte.

What happened

The release is in Deloitte’s press room and was read on November 20, 2026. The claim to be first is Deloitte’s own statement about itself.

Our reading

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

Deloitte says it embeds forward deployed engineers directly within solution delivery teams. This suggests that at a consultancy the role may sit inside a larger delivery group. The release gives no headcount, so the size of the team is unknown.

If you are hiring them

This suggests a consultancy can deliver forward deployed work on a vendor’s platform, alongside the vendor’s own engineers. If you are weighing your own team against a consultancy, the release gives no headcount to judge capacity by.
