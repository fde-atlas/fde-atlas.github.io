Scale AI AI & Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs (29)

Forward Deploying · 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Summary

Forward Deploying tracks 29 open AI roles published on Scale AI's own careers page, including 6 Forward Deployed Engineer positions, ranking Scale AI 9th among the 278 employers it tracks. The median published US base salary is $243k; FDE roles have a median of $241k, about 15% above the market median of $210k. None of the roles are remote-eligible and only one is hybrid. Most adverts ask for production code experience, and 7 require or prefer a security clearance.

Key points

  • 29 open AI roles: 13 AI Engineer, 6 ML Engineer, 6 FDE.
  • Median published US base salary is $243k across all roles.
  • FDE median base is $241k, 15% above the $210k market median.
  • Zero remote roles; only one is hybrid, 4 mention travel.
  • 21 of 29 adverts explicitly require production code experience.
