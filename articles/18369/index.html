Summary

Japanese consulting firm R Street describes its corporate training programs for generative AI and FDE talent development. All courses are exercise-driven, using participants' own work as material rather than lecture-only formats. Four programs are offered: a comprehensive FDE development course covering problem discovery through implementation and governance; hands-on Claude Code and Codex training for all job types; a small-group executive program on investment decisions, risk, and governance; and low-code AI workflow training with Dify and n8n. Courses are customized to each company's environment (including Copilot and Gemini) and can connect to post-training implementation and in-house capability support.

Key points

  • R Street offers exercise-centered corporate training in generative AI and FDE development.
  • Four programs: FDE development, Claude Code/Codex, executive sessions, and Dify/n8n workflows.
  • The FDE course spans problem discovery, requirements, coding-agent implementation, and governance.
  • Executive training is a small discussion format covering ROI, risk, and organizational policy.
  • Training can extend into implementation support and in-house capability building.

We offer the methods used in our consulting practice as corporate training centered on hands-on exercises

This page introduces our corporate generative AI training and FDE talent development programs. For implementation support, see "FDE Consulting"; for an overview of our business including case studies, see "Our Services".

Approach Our Philosophy

Exercise-centered training that transforms your business

Our programs are designed for corporate promotion leads who want to turn generative AI into business results, and for executives considering how to develop such talent.

Knowledge gained from lectures alone does not change how work gets done. We design all of our programs around hands-on exercises, emphasizing that participants work through their own business tasks and learn by doing. Our materials are continuously updated to stay aligned with real FDE consulting engagements.

Programs

Four programs to choose from based on your goals

1. FDE Development Training

A comprehensive program that systematically develops professionals who can independently handle everything from problem discovery through implementation and adoption. Beyond technical skills, it covers business process decomposition, problem definition, and impact estimation across the board. Intended for core talent driving transformation, and for staff in IT and planning departments.

  • Problem discovery and business process decomposition
  • Requirements definition and impact estimation
  • Implementation exercises using coding agents
  • Operations design and governance
  • Capstone exercise based on real business tasks

Audience

Core talent driving transformation, and staff in IT, planning, DX, and AI promotion departments.

Format

Based on a multi-session course, designed to fit your organization and goals. Available in person or online, and conducted with an exercise-centered approach.

Key Topics

  • The current state of generative AI and an overview of business applications: key technical points and a map of application types that deliver business value
  • Business decomposition and problem definition: selecting target processes, understanding the current state, and methods for estimating impact
  • Requirements definition: designing what AI handles versus human involvement, and setting quality standards
  • Implementation exercises (basic): automating routine tasks with coding agents
  • Implementation exercises (advanced): integrating with internal data and existing systems
  • Operations design and governance: usage policies, review structures, and risk management
  • Capstone exercise: implementation based on participants' own work, with peer review

How It Has Been Delivered

We have delivered these programs in formats aimed at real-world implementation, such as intensive training for consulting firms and multi-session training for professional organizations. We can also connect graduates to post-training implementation and insourcing support.

2. Claude Code / Codex Training

Master coding agents as a core business skill. From environment setup and basic operations to automation exercises using real business data, and team operations through knowledge and skill design. Open not only to engineers but also to staff in planning and administrative departments.

  • Environment setup and basic operations
  • Automation exercises based on real business tasks
  • Knowledge and skill design
  • Team operations design
  • Business application through SaaS integration

Audience

All roles, not just engineers, including planning, administrative, and sales departments.

Format

From a one-day intensive to a multi-session course. Hands-on format with one environment per participant; we can prepare the exercise environment for you.

Key Topics

  • Environment setup and basic operations: safe usage configuration and fundamental patterns for business use {{44} Instruction design: how to provide context, how to get the AI to plan, and how to verify results
  • How to provide design context in instructions, how to have plans made, and verification of results
  • Automation exercises with real business data: building solutions for repetitive tasks such as aggregation, document creation, and research
  • Knowledge and skill design: organizing organizational procedures and know-how into forms AI can use
  • Team operations design: sharing and review mechanisms, and governance
  • SaaS integration: business application connected to everyday work tools

Example Exercises

Creating materials from meeting notes, aggregating monthly data into reports, automating the creation of standard documents, and more.

How It Has Been Delivered

We have delivered company-wide training with ongoing support after completion, including environment setup, regular refresher sessions, and Q&A support.

3. Generative AI Training for Executives

A small-group program for executives and board members. After covering the key technical points in plain terms, it addresses investment decision-making frameworks, risk and governance, and implementation strategies for organization and talent through discussions tailored to your company's situation.

  • The current state and outlook of generative AI
  • Thinking about return on investment
  • Risk management and governance
  • Implementation strategies for organization and talent

Audience

Executives, board members, and business unit leaders.

Format

Small-group discussion format. From 90 minutes to a half day, with topics designed around your company's situation.

Key Topics

  • The current state and outlook of generative AI: translating technology trends into the language of management {{65} Thinking about return on investment: identifying application areas and frameworks for investment decisions
  • Identifying the applicable areas for ROI thinking and frameworks for investment decisions
  • Risk management and governance: designing for both control and momentum
  • Implementation strategies for organization and talent: key issues in insourcing, development, and organizational structure

Approach

Lectures are kept to the essentials, with the focus on discussion tied to your company's agenda. Participants leave with issue analyses robust enough to support board and management meeting discussions.

4. Dify / n8n Workflow Training

Learn to build AI workflows with low-code tools through exercise-centered training. It systematically covers types of generative AI applications, business task decomposition, and the fundamentals and applications of RAG, and can be designed as a one-day intensive or a multi-session course depending on your goals.

  • Types of generative AI applications
  • Business task decomposition
  • RAG fundamentals and applications
  • Workflow building exercises
  • Operations and improvement design

Audience

Staff in business and IT departments responsible for building AI workflows and rolling them out internally.

Format

From a one-day intensive to a multi-session course. Hands-on focused, designed around your company's usage environment.

Key Topics

  • Types of generative AI applications: choosing between chat, workflow, and agents, and selection criteria
  • Business task decomposition: methods for breaking down work into units AI can handle
  • RAG fundamentals and applications: choosing retrieval methods, designing data chunking, and practical accuracy improvement
  • Workflow building exercises: building solutions for tasks such as internal inquiry handling and document processing
  • Operations and improvement: post-launch evaluation and designing improvement cycles

How It Has Been Delivered

We have delivered programs through IT service companies, and also offer individually designed programs tailored to specific industries and departments.

Overview of Our Track Record

We have provided training to companies across a wide range of industries, including advertising, consulting, construction, manufacturing, IT, PR, and services. We design the format to match your objectives and structure, from company-wide continuous training and department-focused practical workshops to small-group sessions for executives, delivery through partner companies, and creation of training materials. After the training, we emphasize ongoing support until the skills take root in daily work, including setting up the usage environment, regular sessions, and answering questions.

※ We support both in-person and online formats, and can prepare the exercise environment on our side. We can also design the program around your company's environment, such as Copilot or Gemini.

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions when considering our training

How much does it cost?

We provide individual quotes depending on the program structure, number of participants, and duration. After learning about your objectives and situation, we will propose the optimal structure along with pricing.

Can the training be taken online?

We support both in-person and online formats. We can prepare the exercise environment on our side.

Can it be tailored to our business operations and usage environment?

Exercises are designed using participants' actual work as material. We can also design the program around your company's environment, such as Copilot or Gemini.

Can we request training only?

Yes. Some engagements have started with training and expanded to implementation support or support for building in-house capabilities. We can also design training as a standalone offering depending on your objectives.
