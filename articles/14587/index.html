Shield AI Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-07 · 14 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot counts 8 Shield AI Forward Deployed engineer postings covering 6 distinct roles: Autonomy and Operations & Sustainment open-level seats in the US, plus a London ladder of FDE II, Senior, and Staff. All require modern C++; Autonomy and Ops add Python or Bash and demand roughly 50% travel, while London seats ask about 30%. Posted US pay runs $152,000–$228,000 for Autonomy Senior and $156,000–$234,000 for Ops Senior; London posts no ranges. A November 7 live check found only 4 seats still open, with both Staff seats closed or closing.

Key points

  • 8 postings equal 6 distinct roles; Autonomy and Ops were cross-posted on Lever and Ashby.
  • Modern C++ is required everywhere; Autonomy and Ops also want Python or Bash scripting.
  • Autonomy and Ops seats require about 50% travel, including multi-week deployments.
  • Posted US bands: Autonomy Senior $152,000–$228,000; Ops Senior $156,000–$234,000; no London pay.
  • Ops lists export-control compliance and security clearance as preferred qualifications; by November 7 only 4 seats remained open.

Published

Shield AI posts Forward Deployed engineer seats under Autonomy, Operations & Sustainment, and a London FDE II / Senior / Staff ladder. In FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot of public job boards, Shield AI has 8 in-page postings, all engineer family, tied for 16th of 114 employers with Thomson Reuters (15 employers have more). Those 8 rows are 6 distinct roles: the Autonomy and Operations seats were each posted twice, once on Lever and once on Ashby, and the snapshot counts each copy. 2 of the 8 rows are remote, against 176 of 704 (25.0%) across the snapshot. Autonomy's Senior Engineer pay line is $152,000 to $228,000 in the US. On the November 7, 2026 live check, 4 of the 8 rows were still open. FDE Dispatch is not affiliated with Shield AI; the figures come from Shield AI's public job postings.

What Shield AI calls the role

Shield AI posts three engineer tracks across Lever and Ashby: Autonomy (open level), Operations & Sustainment (open level), and a London ladder of FDE II, Senior, and Staff. All eight snapshot rows are engineer family. There are no intern seats and no management seats in this set.

London covers Forward Deployed Engineer II (5951) (Hybrid), Senior Forward Deployed Engineer (5961) (Hybrid), Staff Forward Deployed Engineer (5021) (Hybrid; on November 7 its page was still up but it had dropped off Shield AI's job feed, so this page treats it as closing and does not link it), and Staff Forward Deployed Engineer (R4962) (closed on the November 7 live check; not linked). Autonomy is Forward Deployed Engineer, Autonomy (open level) on Lever (remote; United States with San Diego, Dallas, and Washington, D.C. listed) plus an Ashby cross-post that returned 404 on November 7. Operations & Sustainment is Forward Deployed Engineer, Operations & Sustainment - open level (R5497) on Lever (Hybrid; San Diego with Washington, D.C. and Remote listed) plus an Ashby cross-post that returned 404 on November 7.

What the open-level and London seats ask for

Forward Deployed Engineer II in London states Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, and 2+ years of industry experience, OR Master’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, and 1+ years of industry experience. Stack: Strong proficiency in writing modern C++ code. Travel: Deploy with customers on site (~30% travel) to support software integration and development activities.

Senior Forward Deployed Engineer in London states Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, and 5+ years of industry experience or Master’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, and 3+ years of industry experience. The same modern C++ proficiency line and ~30% travel line appear on the Senior text.

Forward Deployed Engineer, Autonomy (open level) states 5+ years of experience of industry experience and 2+ years of experience in an integration/applications engineering role, plus Strong proficiency in writing modern C++ code. and Intermediate proficiency in writing Python code. Travel: Deploy with customers on site globally (~50% travel) to support software integration and development activities. The same text also states This role is travel-intensive, with frequent trips, often international, and sometimes lasting multiple weeks. Open-level pay bands on that seat cover Senior, Staff, and Senior Staff.

Forward Deployed Engineer, Operations & Sustainment states 5+ years of experience in field engineering, forward-deployed engineering, applications engineering, systems integration, flight test, robotics support, unmanned systems operations, technical operations, customer deployment, or a similar hands-on technical role. Stack: Proficiency writing, reading, and debugging modern C++ code. and Working proficiency with Python, Bash, or similar scripting for diagnostics, automation, data handling, and field support workflows. Travel: Deploy with customers on site globally, with an expectation of up to 50% travel, to support installation, configuration, validation, training, troubleshooting, and sustainment of Hivemind products. and Willingness and ability to travel at least 50% of the time, including frequent domestic and international travel and multi-week deployments.

London and Autonomy texts also describe Hivemind enterprise software, autonomy modules, and AI & Autonomy applications via the Shield AI SDK. The full skill counts across employers are on Forward Deployed Engineer skills.

Travel

Travel language appears on all eight snapshot rows. London FDE II, Senior, and both Staff seats state about 30% travel. Autonomy and Ops seats state about 50% travel, including multi-week deployments on Ops. Employer travel figures are on how much forward deployed engineers travel.

Export-control and clearance-preferred language on Ops

Operations & Sustainment required qualifications include Ability to comply with export-control, security, safety, sensitive-data, customer-site, and mission-specific operating requirements. Preferred qualifications on that seat include Active or prior security clearance, or eligibility to obtain one when required by program or customer need.

The open FDE II, Senior London, and Autonomy Lever texts do not state a security clearance, Secret, TS/SCI, ITAR, or U.S. Person block in required qualifications. What this means if you are preparing for a Shield AI Forward Deployed loop

  • Pick the track by the work. Autonomy and London FDE II / Senior seats emphasize modern C++ and Hivemind / Shield AI SDK customer deployment. Ops adds field troubleshooting across Linux, embedded, networking, and telemetry with Python or Bash for diagnostics.
  • Match the years line to the seat. London FDE II clusters at 2+ (or 1+ with a Master’s). Senior London and open-level Autonomy / Ops cluster at 5+. Staff asks for 7+ and sits outside the mid-page program framing.
  • Read Hybrid and remote as labeled. London seats are Hybrid. Autonomy Lever is remote. Ops Lever is Hybrid with Remote in locations. Two of eight rows are remote under the remote index used on this site.
  • Use the posted US bands as a check, not an offer. Autonomy Senior is posted from $152,000 to $228,000. Ops Senior is posted from $156,000 to $234,000. London seats post no pay range.

Questions people ask

Does Shield AI hire Forward Deployed Engineers?

Yes. Shield AI had 8 FDE-family postings in FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot, all engineer family, tied for 16th of 114 employers with Thomson Reuters: Forward Deployed Engineer II in London; Forward Deployed Engineer, Autonomy (open level) on Lever and Ashby; Forward Deployed Engineer, Operations & Sustainment (open level) on Lever and Ashby; Senior Forward Deployed Engineer in London; and two Staff Forward Deployed Engineer London seats. Autonomy and Ops were dual-listed on Lever and Ashby (each copy counted, so the 8 rows are 6 distinct roles). On November 7, 2026, 4 of the 8 rows were still open.

How much does a Shield AI Forward Deployed Engineer make?

Autonomy Lever posts leveled US bands: Senior Engineer $152,000 to $228,000, Staff Engineer $181,000 to $272,000, and Senior Staff Engineer $212,000 to $318,000 (ATS range $152,000 to $318,000). Ops Lever posts Senior Engineer $156,000 to $234,000, Staff Engineer $182,000 to $273,000, and Senior Staff Engineer $213,000 to $320,000 (ATS range $156,000 to $320,000). The Autonomy Ashby cross-post listed $152,000 to $228,000 on the snapshot and was closed on the November 7 live check. London FDE II, Senior, and Staff seats post no pay range. Posted ranges are not offers.

What do Shield AI Forward Deployed Engineer postings ask for?

Years differ by seat. London FDE II asks for a Bachelor's with 2+ years of industry experience or a Master's with 1+ years. Senior London asks for a Bachelor's with 5+ years or a Master's with 3+ years. Autonomy open level asks for 5+ years of industry experience and 2+ years in an integration or applications engineering role, plus strong modern C++ and intermediate Python. Ops open level asks for 5+ years in field, forward-deployed, applications, systems integration, or similar hands-on technical work, plus modern C++ and working Python or Bash. London seats emphasize strong modern C++ and Hivemind / Shield AI SDK work. Travel is about 30% on London seats and about 50% on Autonomy and Ops.

Are Shield AI Forward Deployed Engineer jobs remote?

Under the remote index (board marks posting remote with Greenhouse flags excluded, or a listed location contains Remote; Coinbase Remote Zone excluded), 2 of 8 are remote, against 176 of 704 (25.0%) across the snapshot: Autonomy Lever is labeled remote, and Ops Lever lists Remote among its locations while labeled Hybrid. London FDE II, Senior, and both Staff seats are Hybrid. The two Ashby cross-posts had no workplace label on the snapshot.

Do Shield AI Forward Deployed Engineer roles require a security clearance?

Operations & Sustainment required qualifications include ability to comply with export-control, security, safety, sensitive-data, customer-site, and mission-specific operating requirements. Preferred qualifications on that seat include active or prior security clearance, or eligibility to obtain one when required by program or customer need. The open FDE II, Senior London, and Autonomy Lever texts do not state a security clearance, Secret, TS/SCI, ITAR, or U.S. Person block in required qualifications. FDE Dispatch does not invent a blanket U.S. Person rule for Shield AI.
