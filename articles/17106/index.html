Summary

This is a collection of guides from the Rocketlane blog aimed at professional services and SaaS teams. The flagship piece is a complete guide to Forward Deployed Engineers, explaining how the role speeds up customer onboarding, improves product adoption, and delivers measurable outcomes in enterprise AI SaaS. The rest of the collection covers PSA software selection and implementation, PSA versus ERP differences, resource forecasting and planning, project planning and roadmaps, project-based accounting, risk management, and a large set of customer onboarding topics including playbooks, frameworks, communication, and escalation handling. The content is introductory and best-practice oriented, suited to services leaders, project managers, and customer success teams evaluating delivery processes and tooling.

Key points

  • Main guide explains the Forward Deployed Engineer role and its value.
  • Covers PSA software adoption, implementation, and PSA vs ERP comparison.
  • Multiple guides on resource forecasting, planning, and allocation.
  • Extensive customer onboarding content: playbooks, frameworks, escalations.
  • Introductory, best-practice material for professional services teams.
