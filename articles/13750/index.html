Forward deployed engineer vs consultant vs solutions engineer

Ortelian · 2026-10-11 · 4 min read

Summary

This Ortelian article distinguishes three roles: a forward deployed engineer builds and runs a system inside the customer's company until it works in daily use; a consultant produces recommendations while others build; a solutions engineer, employed by a vendor, helps evaluate and buy a product before the contract. The author argues many AI projects stall in the gap between plan and operation because no one owns it, and describes Ortelian's own model of a platform plus an on-site forward deployed team, hosted and operated in the EU. A decision table matches situations to the right role.

Key points

  • FDEs build on site and stay until the system runs in production.
  • Consultants recommend; the build and operation usually fall to others.
  • Solutions engineers support pre-sale evaluation, then move on.
  • Unlike product software engineers, FDEs serve one customer at a time.
  • Ortelian offers a platform plus a forward deployed team model.

A forward deployed engineer builds and runs the system inside your company. A consultant recommends. A solutions engineer helps you buy. How the three roles differ, and which you need.

A forward deployed engineer builds and runs the system inside your company until the work changes. A consultant studies the problem and recommends what to do, and someone else usually builds it. A solutions engineer helps you evaluate and buy a product, mostly before the contract is signed. They solve different problems, so the useful question is which one your situation needs.

Forward deployed engineer

A forward deployed engineer works inside the customer’s company. They map how the work runs, connect the systems it depends on, build and test the agents or software, and stay until it holds up in daily use. The role started at Palantir and spread across AI in 2026, when model labs, cloud providers and consultancies all launched forward deployed teams. Our map of forward deployed AI companies lists who offers it, and what is forward deployed engineering covers the buyer’s side in full.

Consultant

A consultant is good at deciding where AI should go: which processes, in what order, with what business case. The output is a plan. The build usually goes to an integrator or an internal team, and the running to whoever is left. That split is where many AI projects stall, because nobody owns the gap between the plan and the work in use. Some consultancies have started forward deployed practices of their own, so ask who builds and who stays. We compare the two models in Ortelian vs AI consultancies.

Forward deployed engineer vs solutions engineer

A solutions engineer works for a vendor and helps you decide whether its product fits: demos, technical questions, a proof of concept. They know the product well and usually move on once you buy. If you are still choosing a tool, talk to one. If you have chosen and nothing has changed in the work, a solutions engineer is not the role you are missing.

Forward deployed engineer vs software engineer

Both write production code. A software engineer at a product company builds features for many customers and rarely meets most of them. A forward deployed engineer builds for one customer at a time, inside that customer, and spends much of the week with people who are not engineers. What they learn on site often feeds back into the product.

A platform plus a forward deployed team

There is a fourth option, and it is how Ortelian works: a platform plus a forward deployed team. The team works on site to redesign the work, and the platform is what the agents run on afterwards. We work on site not because our product needs it, but because your company does. AI has the most impact when the work itself changes, and that takes time with the people who do it.

After launch, your team runs it or we keep running it for you. Either way, we host and operate the platform, in the EU. Inside an engagement, your own agents can use the platform through scoped tools we set up for you. More on the model on how we work and AI-native deployment partner.

If you have the engineers and the time, building in-house can work well. We compare that route in Ortelian vs building in-house.

Questions people ask

Is a forward deployed engineer a consultant?

Not in the usual sense. A consultant recommends and a forward deployed engineer builds and stays. The two overlap when a consultancy runs its own forward deployed practice, or when a forward deployed team starts with an audit, as we do.

Is a forward deployed engineer the same as a solutions engineer?

No. A solutions engineer works before the sale to show a product fits. A forward deployed engineer works after it, inside the customer, until the system runs in production.

Why not hire forward deployed engineers ourselves?

You can. The hard part is less the hiring than having a platform for the agents to run on and the time to redesign the work before automating it. See redesign the work before you automate it.
