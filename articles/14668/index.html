Saronic Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-06 · 12 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot of Saronic's public Ashby board found 9 Forward Deployed engineer postings, all engineer-family titles, tying for 12th place among 114 employers with Chalk, Cloudflare and Snowflake. Roles span software, senior software, build, electrical, hardware, mechanical and a Summer 2027 internship, all on-site in Austin, TX or San Diego, CA with none remote. Software seats emphasize C++, Rust and Python with up to 50% travel; the Build seat requires 50–70% travel; hardware-family seats ask for 3+ years on fielded systems. No posting lists a numeric salary range, eight of nine mention equity, and all include a U.S. Person export-control requirement.

Key points

  • Saronic has 9 FDE postings, tied 12th of 114 employers.
  • All roles are on-site in Austin or San Diego; none remote.
  • Software seats require C++, Rust or Python, up to 50% travel.
  • Build Engineer has the highest travel band at 50–70%.
  • No numeric pay posted; equity mentioned on 8 of 9 roles.

Published

Saronic posts Forward Deployed engineer seats under software, senior software, build, electrical, hardware, mechanical and intern titles. In FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot of public job boards, Saronic has 9 in-page postings, all engineer family, tied for 12th of 114 employers with Chalk, Cloudflare and Snowflake (11 employers have more). All nine are Ashby on-site in Austin, TX and/or San Diego, CA. Under the remote index, 0 of 9 are remote against 176 of 704 (25.0%). Four of nine mention travel, including Build at 50 to 70% and both Software Engineer seats at up to 50%. None post a numeric salary range. All nine were still listed on November 6, 2026. FDE Dispatch is not affiliated with Saronic; the figures come from Saronic's public job postings.

What Saronic calls the role

Saronic posts a software and hardware Forward Deployed mix on one Ashby board. Eight of the nine seats sit under the Forward Deployable Eng department; the Intern sits under Internships & Co-Ops. There are no strategist or management titles in this set.

Software covers Forward Deployed Software Engineer in Austin, Forward Deployed Software Engineer in San Diego, and Senior Forward Deployed Software Engineer in Austin. Build is Forward Deployed Build Engineer in Austin. Hardware-family seats are Electrical Engineer - Forward Deployed (Austin with secondary San Diego), Hardware Engineer - Forward Deployed in Austin, Hardware Engineer - Forward Deployed in San Diego, and Mechanical Engineer - Forward Deployed (Austin with secondary San Diego). The Forward Deployed Engineer Intern (Summer 2027) seat is Austin on-site.

What the software seats ask for

The Austin and San Diego Forward Deployed Software Engineer seats state no numeric years line. Both ask for Strong software engineering skills, with experience in C++, Rust, Python, or similar languages. Both state Willingness to travel (up to 50%), spending time in the field testing and improving ASV systems in real-world maritime settings (snapshot travel_pct_stated 50%). Both Software Engineer texts include a "This Role Is NOT" bullet kept only as a quote: A traditional SRE/DevOps role based entirely in an office — this role includes time in the field. Both are silent on a formal degree line.

Senior Forward Deployed Software Engineer in Austin asks for 5+ years of experience developing autonomy or robotics systems, with a strong foundation in software engineering and Proficient programming skills in C++, Rust, Python, or similar languages, with experience building and maintaining performance-critical systems. Snapshot travel_mentioned is false on the Senior seat, and the November 6 text states no travel percentage. The Senior text is also silent on a formal degree line and includes the same quoted field-time bullet as the Software Engineer seats: A traditional SRE/DevOps role based entirely in an office — this role includes time in the field.

The full skill counts across employers are on Forward Deployed Engineer skills.

Build Engineer seat and 50 to 70% travel

Forward Deployed Build Engineer in Austin states no numeric years line. The requirements ask for Hands-on experience building, testing, or maintaining integrated electromechanical systems, preferably in fielded or mission-critical environments and Associate degree or bachelor's in a technical field, military technical training, trade certification, or equivalent hands-on experience. The travel line is the highest percentage in this set: This role requires significant travel, typically 50–70%, to test sites and deployment locations, and long hours are common, especially during demos and deployments (snapshot travel_pct_stated 50 to 70%). The overview also states This is not a traditional technician role, kept only inside the quote.

Electrical, Hardware, and Mechanical seats

Electrical Engineer - Forward Deployed asks for 3+ years of experience working on integrated electrical/electromechanical systems, preferably in fielded or mission-critical environments and Bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering, Mechatronics, or a related field, with Master's as a plus. Preferred tooling includes Ability to write basic software tools (e.g., Python, shell scripts) for hardware bring-up or test automation preferred and Experience working with a Linux-based operating system.

Both Hardware Engineer - Forward Deployed seats (Austin and San Diego) ask for 3+ years of experience working on integrated electromechanical systems, preferably in fielded or mission-critical environments and Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or a related field, with preferred Python and shell scripts for bring-up or test automation and Linux-based OS experience.

Mechanical Engineer - Forward Deployed asks for 3+ years of experience working on integrated mechanical/electromechanical systems, preferably in fielded or mission-critical environments and Bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering, Mechatronics, or a related field, plus Linux-based OS experience. Snapshot travel_mentioned is false on Electrical, Hardware and Mechanical.

Intern seat

Forward Deployed Engineer Intern (Summer 2027) asks for Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical and Computer Engineering, or a related field and Programming skills in Python, C++, Typescript, Javascript, or Rust. Travel is mentioned without a percentage: Willingness to travel, spending time in the field testing and improving ASV systems in real-world maritime settings. The seat also states This role requires full-time in office presence (40 hours/week) based at our headquarters in Austin, TX, from May through August 2027. Snapshot equity_mentioned is false only on this Intern row.

Where the roles are

All nine are US-located. Primary locations are Austin, TX (seven seats) and San Diego, CA (two Software/Hardware primary seats). Electrical and Mechanical also list San Diego as a secondary location.

Ashby labels every seat on-site. Under the remote index (board marks posting remote with Greenhouse flags excluded, or a listed location contains Remote; Coinbase Remote Zone excluded), 0 of 9 are remote, against 176 of 704 (25.0%) across the snapshot. Snapshot snapshot remote flag is false on all nine.

Equity and the absence of posted pay

None of the nine snapshot rows list a numeric pay range. November 6 live Ashby compensationTiers are empty on all nine. Non-intern texts state Competitive Salary: Industry-standard salaries with opportunities for performance-based bonuses without a dollar figure. Do not invent dollar pay. Posted ranges are not offers, and here no numeric ranges are posted.

Equity language appears on 8 of 9 postings (absent only on the Intern). Those eight live texts state Equity options to give employees a stake in the company’s success. No grant sizes are stated. Employer travel figures that put Build's 50 to 70% and Software's up to 50% in context are on how much forward deployed engineers travel.

U.S. Person / export-control line

All nine November 6 texts include the same block: If this role is based in the United States, it requires access to export-controlled information or items that require “U.S. Person” status. As defined by U.S. law, individuals who are any one of the following are considered to be a “U.S. Person”: (1) U.S. citizens, (2) legal permanent residents (a.k.a. green card holders), and (3) certain protected classes of asylees and refugees, as defined in 8 U.S.C. 1324b(a)(3). That is a U.S. Person / export-controlled information line. The nine texts do not use the words security clearance, Secret, TS/SCI, or ITAR. The texts do not state a separate visa sponsorship stance beyond that line. How clearance and citizenship lines appear across employers is on do forward deployed engineer jobs require a security clearance and do forward deployed engineer jobs sponsor visas.

What this means if you are preparing for a Saronic Forward Deployed loop

  • Pick the track by the work. Software and Senior Software seats emphasize C++, Rust and Python on autonomy or robotics systems. Build is hands-on electromechanical deployment with the highest stated travel band. Electrical, Hardware and Mechanical seats ask for 3+ years on integrated systems plus degree lines in those disciplines.
  • Read the travel line before you assume office-only work. Software Engineer seats state travel up to 50%. Build states 50 to 70%. Senior Software, Electrical, Hardware and Mechanical state no travel percentage on the snapshot.
  • Treat on-site as the workplace label. All nine are Austin or San Diego on-site under Ashby, and 0 of 9 are remote under the remote index used on this site.
  • Do not expect a posted salary band. The board and texts post no numeric pay range. Equity options language appears on the eight non-intern seats.

What do Saronic Forward Deployed Engineer postings ask for?

Forward Deployed Software Engineer seats state no numeric years line and ask for strong software engineering skills with experience in C++, Rust, Python, or similar languages. Build Engineer states no numeric years line and asks for hands-on electromechanical experience. The Intern asks for enrollment in a Bachelor's or Master's program and programming skills in Python, C++, Typescript, Javascript, or Rust. Software Engineer seats state travel up to 50%. Build states travel of 50 to 70%.
