FDE Experience Required in India: 0–2, 3–6, 7+ Years (2026)

FDE India · 2026-10-26 · 10 min read

Summary

Based on a 12 October 2026 snapshot of 138 Indian FDE and related job listings, this article maps entry routes and pay by experience band. Only 2 of 43 postings with a stated minimum asked for a year or less, and the median was five years, so most roles demand production experience rather than a fresh degree. Requirements vary by employer: AI startups often accept 1–2 years, GCCs and global tech firms cluster in the middle, while services firms and principal roles ask for 3–5 or 5+ years. Customer-facing, integration, deployment and cloud work all count, even under titles like SDE or support engineer. Aggregator pay figures conflict widely—entry-level estimates range from ₹8 to ₹28 LPA—so the author advises verifying against live listings before negotiating.

Key points

  • Median stated experience in the October 2026 snapshot was five years; only 2 of 43 postings accepted a year or less.
  • AI startups ask for 1–2 years; Accenture-style services listings want 3–5 or more.
  • Customer-facing, integration, deployment and cloud work counts as FDE experience regardless of prior title.
  • Entry-level pay estimates range from ₹8 to ₹28 LPA depending on the aggregator.
  • FDE postings grew nearly 19x year-over-year even as India's top five IT firms cut a net 6,981 jobs in FY26.

Most Indian FDE listings ask for production experience, not a fresh degree. In a snapshot of 138 open FDE and related roles taken on 12 October 2026, only 2 of the 43 postings that state a minimum experience asked for a year or less, and the median is 5 years, according to Neurapath’s tracker. For most engineers the realistic entry point is 2–5 years of backend, cloud, data or integration work, with a lateral move into the role.

What FDE listings in India ask for, by employer type

Stated experience depends heavily on who is hiring. AI startups are the most open to shorter histories, GCCs and global tech firms cluster in the middle, and services firms and senior roles often ask for more.

Pay figures are aggregator estimates, not verified offers, so use them as a range rather than a promise.

AI startups

Startup listings are the most varied. Some are written for engineers with 1–2 years of work, while a Bengaluru listing for Cartesia’s India FDE role describes experience in forward-deployed, solutions, implementation, or customer-facing engineering roles and shows ₹70L–₹90L as employer-provided pay on Glassdoor India. That high figure comes from a single listing, so don’t treat it as a market rate.

GCCs

GCC demand is growing and skews towards experienced people. ANSR’s September 2026 report found approximately 62% of hires were professionals with three to eight years of experience, with a median of six years. Emids has also said it plans to hire 1,000 forward-deployed context engineers over 12 months, which it calls FDCEs (Analytics India Magazine, Oct 2026).

Consulting and IT services

Services listings tend to name a fixed year count. Beyond Accenture, Cisco’s FDE role in Karnataka asks for 4+ years of strong software engineering experience building production-grade applications, according to Indeed listings. Expect these roles to weigh delivery track record and client work more than novelty.

What counts as relevant experience

Recruiters are not only counting job titles. Several listings accept a wider mix:

  • Customer-facing work. One intern listing explicitly counts any customer-facing, consulting, support, freelance, or implementation experience.
  • Production shipping. A Bengaluru listing indexed on Glassdoor India says at least 1 year building or running software in production, and adds that it values ownership over tenure.
  • Integration and cloud deployment. Across Neurapath’s 138 listings, client-facing work is the most-mentioned skill (67 listings), then AI agents (55), AWS (38), Python (36) and APIs (35).
  • Applied AI delivery. A Glassdoor-indexed listing asks for experience deploying AI/GenAI solutions in production environments, which is where many 2–5 year engineers can show evidence.

Titles such as SDE, solutions engineer, support engineer or data engineer usually transfer if your work included deployments and client contact. Pre-sales-heavy experience counts less than hands-on building. One Indeed listing wants 5+ years of experience in a pre-sales engineering or a similar deep technical role, which signals that the employer still wants code.

Routes for 0–2 years

Entry is possible, but narrow. The most realistic routes are:

  • Intern or junior FDE listings. Some intern roles in Hyderabad list a stipend of ₹5,000 (Employer provided), and another intern FDE listing shows ₹10,000 on Glassdoor India. One junior role asks for 2+ years of experience in software engineering, solutions engineering, or a similar customer-facing technical role, so it is a stretch target for many freshers.
  • Services AI-deployment practices. IT services firms are building AI deployment teams, and their entry band is lower; OwnYourCareer puts IT services at ₹10–18 LPA for 0–2 years.
  • Applied AI titles with shipped work. Neurapath notes that freshers who get in usually come through a few entry postings, Applied AI Engineer titles or adjacent implementation roles, with shipped projects to show.

If you are in this band, a deployed project with authentication, error handling and a clear demo will matter more than a certificate. For skills in more depth, see our FDE skills guide.

Routes for 3–5 years

This is the most common hiring band in the listings reviewed. Two moves work well:

  • From backend, cloud or data engineering into FDE. Pick up customer calls, integration projects and deployment ownership inside your current company, then apply for FDE titles that list 2–5 years.
  • From support, solutions or implementation into a hybrid role. Add Python, APIs and cloud deployment to your portfolio. Listings with 3–5 years of professional experience in Python development reward that combination.

Pay in this band varies widely. The OwnYourCareer band puts 3–6 years at ₹28–45 LPA for AI-first startups and ₹35–55 LPA for MNC AI platforms with Indian offices. Services roles sit lower, so compare the employer type before you compare the number.

Routes for 6+ years

At this level, listings focus on ownership of production AI systems and team leadership. An Atlassian principal FDE listing in Bengaluru asks for 5+ years of experience delivering impactful AI/ML solutions in production environments, including experience as a founding or forward-deployed engineer. A global benchmark is OpenAI’s San Francisco role, which asks for 5+ years of engineering or technical deployment experience that includes customer-facing work.

For senior India roles, OwnYourCareer lists ₹55–90+ LPA for senior and global-remote roles, while Second Talent gives ₹28–65 LPA for senior FDEs and ₹55–100 LPA for lead or principal roles. These ranges are not consistent, so use your own offer data from Glassdoor India or LinkedIn India when you negotiate.

Why pay figures disagree

Sources do not agree on FDE pay, partly because they use different data. Some examples:

  • Glassdoor’s all-India average is lower than the banded estimates. One summary puts it around ₹13.2 LPA, while Glassdoor’s Bengaluru data shows an average at ₹16.99 LPA.
  • Conservative trackers put 0–2 years much lower. Naresh i Technologies gives ₹8–15 LPA for that band, while higher-end guides give ₹18–28 LPA.
  • Scaler’s table gives 3–5 years at ₹15-30 LPA, with a note that this is the sweet spot for most FDE hires in India.

Check the employer type, city and whether the figure includes ESOPs before comparing.

Layoffs and FDE hiring, side by side

FDE hiring and layoffs are happening in the same market. While Indian IT majors reduced staff, FDE listings grew quickly. India’s five largest IT companies recorded a combined net workforce reduction of 6,981 employees in FY26, compared with a net addition of 12,718 employees in FY25. Over the same period, Indeed data show that FDE job postings in India grew nearly 19x between June 2025 and June 2026, although growth moderated in July and August. Our weekly FDE jobs update tracks the latest movement.

Reported layoff numbers at individual companies also conflict. One tracker says Oracle’s India impact is reported at over 1,000 employees, with estimates of up to 2,000–3,000, while another reports Oracle reportedly cut around 12,000 roles in India. We are not linking these cuts to FDE hiring, because no source we reviewed makes that connection.

Step-by-step: check your fit and apply

  • Count your relevant years honestly. Add up production software, cloud deployment, integration and customer-facing time. Ignore pre-sales time that involved no building.
  • Match your band to an employer type. Use the table above. If you have 0–2 years, target intern, junior and services AI-deployment roles. If you have 3–5 years, target AI startups and GCC FDE roles that ask for 2–5 years. At 6+, target principal and lead roles.
  • Close the skill gap that listings repeat. Python, APIs, AWS, and AI agents appear most often, so build one deployed project that uses them.
  • Write a project that shows customer impact. Describe the problem, the user, what you deployed, what broke, and the result. Listings reward ownership.
  • Save listings and track the stated minimum. Compare the years requested against your profile before applying. Our FDE Fit Check can help you see where you stand.
  • Prepare for the role itself. Read our explainer on what an FDE does and earns in India before interviews, so you can talk about client work with specifics.

What this means for you

For most Indian engineers, the question is not whether FDE experience is required but whether your existing work already contains it. Customer contact, production deployment and integration work are the strongest signals. If you have fewer than two years, aim for the narrow entry routes and build a deployed project. If you have three to five years, your best move is usually a lateral step inside your current employer or a GCC or AI startup role that names your stack.

Frequently asked questions

How many years of experience do you need to become a forward deployed engineer in India?

Most Indian listings ask for roughly 2 to 5 years of production software or cloud experience, with a median of five years in one October 2026 snapshot. Some AI startups accept 1 year, and a few senior roles ask for 5 or more. Strong project work can help if your years are fewer.

Can a fresher get a forward deployed engineer job in India?

Rarely as a first role. Entry-level FDE postings are scarce, and freshers usually enter through intern listings, support or implementation roles, or Applied AI Engineer titles where they can show deployed projects.

Does customer-facing or support experience count toward FDE experience?

Yes, many listings explicitly welcome customer-facing, consulting, support, implementation and solutions engineering experience. The strongest profiles combine that with hands-on coding, APIs and cloud deployment.

What is the FDE salary by experience in India?

Figures differ by source. One widely cited band puts 0–2 years at ₹18–28 LPA, 3–6 years at ₹28–55 LPA, and 7+ years at ₹55–90 LPA or more, while other trackers report lower entry-level numbers. Treat these as estimates and check current listings.
