Palantir Foundry and AIP: forward deployed roles

Kernavique · 2026-11-20 · 16 min read

Summary

This Kernavique briefing surveys forward deployed engineering roles around Palantir Foundry and AIP. On November 20, 2026, Palantir's job feed had 72 postings titled forward deployed (53 in the US) plus 22 Deployment Strategist roles. Accenture's December 2025 Palantir Business Group claims over 2,000 Palantir-skilled professionals, Accenture Federal plans to train 1,000, and Deloitte claims the largest FDE cohort outside Palantir without giving numbers. Partner ads consistently require at least one year of hands-on Palantir work, with pay ranges that never specify base salary and travel requirements from 0 to 100%. Certification details are thin; treat credentials as a tiebreaker. Many federal roles require security clearances, with Palantir accounting for 36 of 63 clearance-mentioning US postings tracked.

Key points

  • Palantir's feed showed 72 forward deployed postings, 53 US-based, on November 20, 2026.
  • Accenture claims 2,000+ Palantir-skilled professionals; Deloitte gives no headcount.
  • All partner ads read require one year of hands-on Palantir experience.
  • Pay figures never state base salary; travel ranges from 0 to 100%.
  • 36 of Palantir's 53 US postings mention security clearances.

Palantir’s own forward deployed titles

On November 20, 2026, Palantir’s public job feed carried 306 postings. Of those, 72 had “forward deployed” in the title, 53 of them with a US location. On October 11, 2026, the count was 79. Among the job feeds we track, only Databricks carried more.

Palantir posts the same title once for each city, so there are more postings than distinct jobs. Fifteen of the 72 are internships, 11 of them in the US. The feed also carried 22 Deployment Strategist postings, 14 in the US.

The title comes in several forms. Besides Forward Deployed Software Engineer and Forward Deployed AI Engineer, the feed lists “Forward Deployed Infrastructure Engineer - US Government” and “Forward Deployed Reliability Engineer”. The ads we opened are below, all read on November 20, 2026.

Palantir title Location Pay as written What else the ad states
Forward Deployed Software Engineer - US Government Washington, D.C. $135,000 - $200,000/year On-site; travel “as needed”; Top Secret clearance or willingness to obtain one
Forward Deployed AI Engineer New York, NY $135,000 - $200,000/year Hybrid; travel up to 25%
Forward Deployed Software Engineer, New Grad - Commercial New York, NY $135,000 - $145,000/year Hybrid; 25–50% travel
Deployment Strategist New York, NY $110,000 - $170,000/year Hybrid; travel of 25 – 75% required

Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Internship - Warp Speed New York, NY $10,000/month On-site; “U.S. Person Required”; travel up to 25%
Year at Palantir - Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Internship - Commercial New York, NY $5,900 - $10,500/month On-site; a year-long internship

Palantir calls each figure an estimated salary range and never says base. Its ads say the estimate leaves out any sign-on bonus, benefits and long-term incentives. Palantir also leaves postings open for years, so a live ad is not a recent one.

The engineer ad describes “small, agile teams”. The Deployment Strategist ad says strategists work with forward deployed engineers on the customer’s data pipeline. For how other employers copy that pairing, see how companies build a forward deployed engineering team.

The partners building Foundry teams

Three consulting firms have said in public that they are building teams on Foundry and AIP. Here is what each published, as published.

Deloitte. In a press release of July 21, 2025, Deloitte said it has “the largest cohort of trained and certified Forward Deployed Engineers (FDE) outside of Palantir” and that it “expects to more than triple that number”. It gave no number. We read that release on October 11, 2026. A later Deloitte article, undated and marked © 2026, says the firm has set up its own deployment pool for these engineers and a center of excellence dedicated to Palantir, and that it trained employees in less than six months. It also gives no headcount.

Accenture. On December 16, 2025, Accenture and Palantir said they had formed the Accenture Palantir Business Group. The release says the group brings Palantir’s own forward deployed engineers together with “more than 2,000 Palantir-skilled Accenture professionals”. It names Accenture “a Palantir preferred global partner for enterprise transformation” and lists healthcare, telecommunications, manufacturing, consumer goods and financial services.

Accenture Federal Services. On June 30, 2025, Palantir and Accenture Federal Services announced a partnership “naming Accenture Federal as a Palantir preferred implementation partner for U.S. federal government customers.” The companies said they would train and certify Accenture Federal’s Data & AI team of 1,000 professionals on Foundry and AIP. The release does not use the words “forward deployed”.

So the stated numbers are two: more than 2,000 at Accenture and 1,000 to be trained at Accenture Federal. Deloitte gives none.

Palantir’s own view of the title is narrower. Channel Dive reported on August 5, 2026, that Palantir’s chief technology officer said on the second-quarter 2026 earnings call that only Palantir has such engineers. We did not open the call transcript.

Open roles the partners were advertising

On November 20, 2026:

  • Deloitte’s US careers search for “palantir” reported 49 jobs. We read the first page of results and opened three ads. Titles on that page include Palantir Engineer, Palantir AI and Data Science Engineer, and Project Dashboard Developer (Palantir / Foundry).
  • Deloitte’s search for “forward deployed” returned 68 results, 56 of them with the title. Those 56 cover many vendor platforms, Palantir among them, with Databricks, AWS, ServiceNow, Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, Snowflake, Salesforce, Microsoft and Oracle. We read titles only.
  • Accenture: one ad read. Its Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer ad lists “Multiple Locations”. We have no count of Accenture’s open Palantir roles, because we could not read its job search.

Our tracker of who is hiring forward deployed engineers has the counts for other employers.

Notes on the table:

  • Pay basis. Deloitte calls its figures “A reasonable estimate of the current range”. Accenture calls its figures a reasonable range of compensation. Neither says base. Deloitte’s senior ad adds a discretionary annual incentive program, and Accenture mentions bonus opportunities.
  • Accenture’s states. The ad gives separate ranges for 14 jurisdictions. Maine has the lowest figure. New York, New Jersey and California share the highest top figure, $235,100.
  • Locations. Deloitte’s two national ads say the same job is available in 43 and 46 locations. The associate role lists Atlanta, Austin, Chicago, Nashville, New York, Rosslyn, Sacramento and San Diego.
  • Closing dates. Both Deloitte national ads say hiring for the role ends on 12/31/2026. Accenture’s page said it expected the posting to stay up until 10/15/2026, but the Apply button was still there on November 20, 2026.
  • Sponsorship. Deloitte’s mid-level ad says limited immigration sponsorship may be available. Its associate ad requires work authorization without employer sponsorship.
  • “Not recorded” means our October read did not capture the item. It does not mean the ad is silent.

One year of hands-on Palantir work is the floor in all four ads read in November. That is the figure to plan around, whether you are applying or writing an ad.

Certifications: what is known, and how thin it is

The evidence here is thin. Palantir’s own certification site could not be read when we tried. What follows comes from the Palantir Community forum and from one old press release. The forum poster answers as “we” and announces policy, but the pages we read do not mark the account as Palantir staff.

What the ads say:

  • None of the three Deloitte ads names a Palantir certification.
  • Accenture lists “Palantir certifications (Foundry, Gotham, or AIP)” as preferred, not required.

What the forum says, in posts dated July 27 to August 7, 2026:

  • A free entry level. There is a new, free Foundry Aware Certification.
  • Associate level, live from August 3, 2026. It has refreshed Data Engineer and Application Developer exams and a new AI Engineer exam. The exams are timed, open-book and multiple-choice, and valid for two years.
  • Specialist level. Hands-on, live exams in a real Foundry and AIP environment, delivered by Ontologize, which the forum calls a training partner. We read this on October 11, 2026, and did not read it again.
  • Old Professional certifications. They stay valid for the time stated on the certificate, and sit between Foundry Aware and the new Associate level.
  • Vouchers. Codes issued before August 3 do not work for the new exams.
  • People outside partner firms. A user whose company is not a Palantir partner was told that the support team can issue a code to take the exam for free.

The program began on January 14, 2022, with four tracks: Data Engineer, Application Developer, Data Science and Data Analyst. It was open to existing Foundry customers and partners. We read that release as a copy on a training-industry site, because the original could not be opened.

What we cannot state: what an exam costs, any formal eligibility rule, the pass mark, or the rules on retakes. None of it is on a page we could open.

In practice, treat a certification as a tiebreaker. The ads ask for a year of real Foundry work first.

The partners we could not verify

Four firms often named as Palantir partners are missing from this page. That is because we could not read their pages, not because they have no roles.

  • Booz Allen Hamilton. Its careers search returned 20 unrelated jobs and no total, and we did not read later pages. A second job-search address we tried answered with a rate limit. We found no Booz Allen and Palantir partnership announcement. Booz Allen did advertise a Palantir Foundry Ontology Engineer role on Dice, which we read on October 11, 2026.
  • PwC. The US jobs site address we tried did not resolve. Two PwC documents on Palantir services appeared in search results as PDFs, which we do not open.
  • EY. We could not read its careers pages without a browser. We found no US Palantir ad and no recent announcement.
  • Capgemini. The same applies. Its consulting arm, Capgemini Invent, appears in Palantir’s 2022 certification release as one of the first partners able to use the certifications. We found nothing more recent.

If you work at one of these firms or are applying to one, read the firm’s own careers page. Nothing here tells you what it is or is not hiring for.

Two smaller sources of Foundry work also exist. Third-party firms advertise contract Foundry roles: one ad on Dice, from GSPANN Technologies, was for a 12-month contract in Fremont, California, covering Ontology, OSDK, Pipeline Builder and AIP agents (read November 20, 2026). It named no end client and gave no pay figure. And small Foundry specialist firms exist, but we could not read an ad on any of their own pages.

Clearance on federal Foundry work

Much Foundry work is for government, and the ads show it.

  • Palantir. Of its 53 US postings titled forward deployed on November 20, 2026, 36 mentioned a clearance in the ad text. The US Government ad asks for “Active US Security Clearance at or above the Top Secret level” or willingness to obtain or upgrade to it. Other Palantir ads ask for eligibility and willingness to obtain one.
  • Deloitte. The associate role serves federal, state and local government clients and asks for the “Ability to obtain and maintain a US government security clearance”. We did not check clearance wording across Deloitte’s other Palantir ads.
  • Accenture Federal Services. Its June 30, 2025, release covers US federal government customers. We have not read clearance wording in an Accenture Federal ad.
  • Booz Allen Hamilton. Its Foundry role in Arlington, Virginia, required the ability to obtain a Secret clearance (Dice, read October 11, 2026).

The Palantir count is a keyword match on ad text. It puts “active clearance required” and “eligible to obtain” in one number.

Across all the feeds we read that day, 63 of 298 US postings titled forward deployed, at 11 employers, mentioned a clearance. Palantir’s 36 are more than half of them. Our briefing on cleared forward deployed engineer roles sets out the levels and the wording employer by employer.

In practice

If you are an engineer with Foundry skills. The ads we read come from three kinds of employer: Palantir, its consulting partners, and third-party firms offering contract work. Partner ads ask for one year of hands-on Palantir work, so describe what you built: ontology, pipelines, Workshop applications, AIP agents. Ask what the pay figure counts, because none of these ads says base. Ask for the travel as a number: Deloitte states 50% on average and Accenture anything from 0 to 100%. If the work is federal, check the clearance wording before you apply. Our forward deployed engineer role page covers the role, and the comparison of forward deployed engineer and solutions engineer helps you tell a builder role from a pre-sales one. If you want Kernavique to know about you, send us your details and résumé.

If you are a company hiring Foundry engineers. You are competing with Palantir and with partners that count their Palantir-skilled people in the thousands. State what they often leave vague: the pay basis, the travel as a figure and the clearance level. Ask for a year of real Foundry work and test it hands-on, the way the Specialist exams do. The job description and scorecard are in how to hire a forward deployed engineer. If a deployment cannot wait, engineers on contract through their own companies can carry it while a permanent search runs. If you have not yet decided whether to build a team or use a partner, a Capability Blueprint sets out the roles and the hiring sequence. When you are ready to hire, ask Kernavique for help.

  • A headcount for Deloitte. Neither its press release nor its article gives one.
  • Deloitte’s other Palantir ads. The search reported 49 jobs. We opened three and read ten titles.
  • What Palantir certification costs, who may sit it, pass marks and retake rules. No page we could open states them. A third-party claim about a waiting period before a retake was not opened.
  • Hourly figures in contract Foundry ads. They are set by third-party firms, not by the employers who use the engineers.
  • A data company’s estimate of Palantir’s share of all forward deployed engineers. The company sells hiring data and does not state its method.
  • Partner announcements seen only in search results. Two involve firms outside the US. We did not open them.
  • Small Foundry specialist firms. One new-graduate ad was read only as a removed copy on a job site, and it did not mention Foundry.
  • Palantir’s interview method. Its careers pages could not be read as text.

Questions

  • Do you need a Palantir certification to get a Foundry forward deployed role?
  • Not in the ads we read. None of the three Deloitte ads read on November 20, 2026, names a Palantir certification, and Accenture lists Palantir certifications as preferred, not required. All four ads ask for at least a year of hands-on Palantir work instead.
  • What do Palantir partners pay forward deployed engineers?
  • Deloitte’s three ads give estimated ranges of $91,100 to $175,100, $134,500 to $265,100 and $155,600 to $306,800. Accenture publishes ranges by state, from $54,400 to $173,100 in Maine to $70,350 to $235,100 in California. None of these is stated as base salary. All were read on November 20, 2026.
  • How much Foundry experience do partner ads ask for?
  • One year. The three Deloitte ads ask for 1+ years with Palantir, naming Foundry, AIP or Maven, and Accenture asks for a minimum of 1 year with Palantir Foundry. General engineering experience runs from 2+ years for Deloitte’s associate role to 5+ years for its senior role.
  • Which consulting firms hire Palantir Foundry engineers?
  • We could verify Deloitte, Accenture and Accenture Federal Services from their own pages and press releases. Booz Allen Hamilton advertised a Foundry role on Dice that we read on October 11, 2026. We could not read the careers pages of Booz Allen, PwC, EY or Capgemini on November 20, 2026, so we cannot say what they are advertising.
  • Do federal Foundry roles need a security clearance?
  • Often. On November 20, 2026, 36 of Palantir’s 53 US postings titled forward deployed mentioned a clearance in the ad text. Deloitte’s associate role for government clients asks for the ability to obtain and maintain a US government security clearance. Some ads ask for an active clearance and others for eligibility, so read the exact wording.
  • How much travel do Foundry forward deployed roles involve?
  • It varies widely. Deloitte’s three ads state travel of 50% on average. Accenture’s ad says travel will vary from 0 to 100%. Palantir’s own ads range from up to 25% for the Forward Deployed AI Engineer to 25 to 50% for the New Grad role.

Terms on this page

  • AIP
  • Palantir’s Artificial Intelligence Platform, used alongside its Foundry data platform.
  • LLM
  • Large language model, the kind of model behind chat assistants and many AI products.
  • FDE
  • Forward deployed engineer: an engineer who works inside a customer’s organization to put a product into production.
