Ortelian vs Palantir for mid-market AI

Ortelian · 2026-10-12 · 4 min read

Summary

This vendor-written comparison from Ortelian positions the two companies' platform-plus-forward-deployed-team models for different buyers. Palantir, which its own materials say pioneered the FDE role, suits large, complex operational and government-grade programmes built on its software. Ortelian targets mid-market and enterprise knowledge work, hosts in the EU, agrees standards on real examples before building, ships in two-week cycles, and lets customers run the platform after launch or keeps running it. The article admits Ortelian is no substitute for Palantir in defence, intelligence, or programmes requiring Palantir as the system of record.

Key points

  • Both pair a platform with forward deployed engineers, but target different buyers.
  • Palantir fits large, complex programmes and government-grade procurement.
  • Ortelian targets mid-market and enterprise knowledge work, hosted in the EU.
  • Ortelian ships in two-week cycles after agreeing standards on real examples.
  • After launch, customers run it themselves or Ortelian keeps operating it.

Choose Palantir when you need its platform and forward deployed model for large, complex operational or government-grade programmes. Choose Ortelian when you want a platform plus a forward deployed team for mid-market and enterprise knowledge work, hosted in the EU, with a clear path for your team to run it or for us to keep running it.

Palantir pioneered forward deployed engineering and builds custom applications on its own software for each customer. What we say about Palantir here comes from its own public materials. Ortelian is a different product and a different scale of engagement. For the broader landscape, see what is forward deployed engineering and the map of forward deployed AI companies.

At a glance

Two platform-plus-team models, built for different buyers.

Both put engineers on site and leave you with a platform. The difference is who each is built for, how an engagement starts, and what “run it after launch” looks like. Palantir’s public materials describe forward deployed engineers who embed with customers and build on Palantir’s software. Ortelian pairs its own platform with a forward deployed team for knowledge work in mid-market and enterprise companies.

When to choose Palantir

When Palantir is the better choice.

Choose Palantir when its platform is the right foundation for the problem, and you want forward deployed engineers who build on that foundation. Palantir’s own job materials say it pioneered the forward deployed engineer role and that FDEs embed with the customer until the problem is theirs. That model fits large, complex programmes where Palantir’s software is already the planned system of record for the work.

It also fits when you need the breadth of what Palantir sells across defence, government and large commercial accounts, or when your procurement and security process is already built around that kind of vendor. Ortelian does not try to replace Palantir in those cases.

When to choose Ortelian

When Ortelian is the better fit.

Choose Ortelian when the job is to change how a specific piece of mid-market or enterprise knowledge work runs, such as planning, back-office checks or a sales process with many people and sources, and that work depends on context no single system holds. We work on site not because our product needs it, but because your company does. The detail is on how we work and on what an AI-native deployment partner does.

The platform is hosted in the EU. We agree a standard on real examples before we build, then ship in two-week cycles. After launch, your team runs it or we keep running it. Inside an engagement, your own agents can work with the platform through scoped tools. There is no self-serve product and no public API.

Honest limits

Where Ortelian is not a substitute.

Ortelian is not a defence or intelligence platform, and it is not trying to match Palantir’s product breadth or institutional footprint. If you need Palantir’s software as the operating layer for a large programme, shortlist Palantir. If you need a global systems integrator to redesign work across dozens of teams, shortlist those firms. If you only need company-wide search, a Work AI platform is a different category again.

Questions

Questions buyers ask when comparing.

What is the main difference between Ortelian and Palantir?

Both pair a platform with forward deployed engineers. Palantir pioneered that model and builds on its own software for large, complex programmes. Ortelian is a platform plus forward deployed team for mid-market and enterprise knowledge work, hosted in the EU, with a choice after launch for your team to run it or for Ortelian to keep running it.

Did Palantir invent forward deployed engineering?

Palantir’s public materials say it pioneered the forward deployed engineer role. The term has since spread across AI labs, cloud vendors and other platform companies.

Is Ortelian a Palantir alternative for every use case?

No. Ortelian is not a substitute for Palantir’s platform in defence, government or other programmes built around Palantir’s software. It is a fit when the job is specific knowledge work in mid-market and enterprise companies.

Where is Ortelian hosted?

Ortelian hosts and operates its platform in the EU. Agents use models with zero data retention, and your data is not used to train models. Check Palantir’s own materials for its hosting options.

Can we use both?

In theory, for different jobs. In practice, most mid-market buyers pick one platform-plus-team vendor for the work they want to change. Bring the work that is held up and we will say whether it is a job for us.
