Top AI Consulting Firms: Investments and Partners

FWD Deployment · 2026-10-11 · 7 min read

Summary

This roundup compares major AI consulting firms on three verifiable dimensions: committed AI investment, Anthropic and OpenAI partnerships, and whether they run forward deployed engineering teams. Accenture pledged $3B and reported $5.9B in generative AI bookings for FY25; Deloitte committed $3B through FY2030 and rolled out Claude to 470,000 staff. IBM, Capgemini, Slalom and BCG X all operate FDE-style teams, and OpenAI named four large firms Frontier Alliance partners in February 2026. The piece also profiles boutiques like Dplyz, Distyl, Tribe AI and Tenex, and offers guidance on choosing between large firms and boutiques.

Key points

  • Accenture: $3B pledge, $5.9B gen AI bookings FY25, Faculty acquired 2026.
  • Deloitte: $3B through FY2030, Claude for 470,000 staff, hires FDEs.
  • IBM's Forward Deployed Units claim a six-person pod equals a 30-person team.
  • OpenAI named Accenture, BCG, McKinsey and Capgemini Frontier Alliance partners in Feb 2026.
  • Boutiques like Distyl ($175M raise) and Tribe AI embed engineers in client teams.

Firm profiles

What the top AI consulting firms say about themselves, with dates, from their own newsrooms.

Best for: Large enterprises that want AI built, integrated and run

  • $5.9B gen AI bookings, FY25
  • Anthropic business group
  • Bought Faculty, 2026

Accenture launched forward deployed engineering practices with Microsoft and ServiceNow in 2026. It formed an Anthropic business group in December 2025 to train about 30,000 staff on Claude, and OpenAI named it a Frontier Alliance partner in February 2026. Its SEC filing reports $5.9 billion in generative AI bookings for fiscal 2025. It completed its purchase of London AI firm Faculty in March 2026.

Best for: Regulated enterprises that want AI with audit-grade governance

  • $3B gen AI through FY2030
  • Claude for 470,000 staff
  • Anthropic FDE roles

Deloitte's AI & Engineering practice hires forward deployed engineers tied to platforms like Anthropic, AWS, ServiceNow and Palantir. It has committed $3 billion to generative AI through fiscal 2030. In October 2025 it expanded its Anthropic alliance to bring Claude to more than 470,000 people and to certify 15,000 professionals.

Best for: Enterprises already on IBM software and infrastructure

  • Forward Deployed Units, May 2026
  • Anthropic partner
  • Riyadh Air, Nestlé

IBM Consulting launched Forward Deployed Units in May 2026: small pods of people working with AI agents that handle coding, testing and documentation. IBM names Riyadh Air, Nestlé, Heineken and Pearson as clients and says a six-person pod does the work of a 30-person team. It partnered with Anthropic in October 2025.

Best for: Companies that want strategy and AI build from one firm

  • ~3,000 technologists
  • OpenAI Frontier Alliance
  • Hires FDEs

BCG X is Boston Consulting Group's build and design unit, with nearly 3,000 technologists, scientists, engineers and designers across 80+ cities. It recruits forward deployed AI engineers, joined OpenAI's Frontier Alliance in February 2026, and has worked with Anthropic since 2023.

Best for: Leadership teams pairing AI builds with operating-model change

  • Joined McKinsey 2015
  • OpenAI Frontier Alliance

QuantumBlack is McKinsey's AI and engineering unit. It builds AI models and agents for clients, usually alongside McKinsey's strategy and operating-model work. It joined McKinsey in 2015 as a 45-person start-up, and OpenAI named McKinsey a Frontier Alliance partner in February 2026.

Best for: Firms that want AI tied to tax, audit and risk work

  • $1B AI investment, 2023
  • Anthropic and OpenAI alliances

PwC US committed $1 billion over three years in 2023 to expand its AI offerings and train its 65,000 people on AI tools. It lists Anthropic and OpenAI among its technology alliances.

Best for: Large enterprises on Google Cloud or OpenAI

  • €2B AI investment
  • Outcome Deployed Engineers
  • OpenAI Frontier Alliance

Capgemini launched Outcome Deployed Engineer pods in April 2026 that work inside client environments alongside Google's forward deployed engineers. It joined OpenAI's Frontier Alliance in February 2026 and committed €2 billion to AI over three years in 2023.

Best for: AWS-based companies that want embedded AI build teams

  • Forward Deployed Expertise
  • Anthropic partner, 2024
  • 54 offices

Slalom's Forward Deployed Expertise offer puts cross-functional teams inside client organizations to find, test and build AI on AWS. It partnered with Anthropic in November 2024 and lists 54 local offices in 12 countries.

Best for: Engineering-led teams that want custom AI software built well

  • Founded 1993
  • 10,000+ people
  • 47 offices

Thoughtworks is a software engineering consultancy that builds custom software, data platforms and AI systems. It was founded in Chicago in 1993 and lists 10,000+ employees in 47 offices across 18 countries.

Boutique AI consulting firms

All AI-native firms →

Smaller firms that focus on AI engineering and put senior engineers inside the client's team.

Best for: Mid-size companies putting AI agents into operations

  • Embedded team, project or advisory
  • Agents, integration and data
  • 20 industries listed

Dplyz puts senior engineers inside a business to connect its data, build products and run AI agents and workflows in production. It offers three ways to work: an embedded team, project delivery or technical leadership. Its services cover agentic AI and automation, AI integration, product development, platform and cloud work, and integration and data. It doesn't publish a price list and proposes pricing once the work is scoped.

Best for: Teams building and evaluating AI agents, including government

  • Agent builds for government
  • Cortex agent evaluation
  • $500M gross run rate (reported)

micro1 makes expert human data, RL environments and evaluations for frontier AI labs, and builds AI agents for clients. Its government page describes end-to-end agent development, from mapping workflows to production deployment, and says it holds Awardable status on the CDAO Tradewinds Solutions Marketplace. Its Cortex platform evaluates AI agents in production. TechCrunch reported in August 2026 that micro1 reached a $500 million gross run rate.

Best for: Large enterprises running company-wide AI programs

  • Forward deployed teams
  • $175M raised, Sept 2025
  • New York and San Francisco

Distyl sends teams of engineers and researchers into large companies to design and run AI programs, alongside its own enterprise AI software. It announced a $175 million raise at a $1.8 billion valuation in September 2025, and a Google Cloud partnership pairing its forward deployed engineer model with Gemini in April 2026.

Best for: Fortune 1000 companies that want embedded AI builders

  • Founded 2019
  • SOC 2 Type II
  • NYC, SF and Lisbon

Tribe AI places forward deployed engineers inside large enterprises to build, deploy and maintain AI systems, paired with industry specialists. It was founded in 2019, lists offices in New York, San Francisco and Lisbon, and says it partners with Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and AWS. In July 2026 it hired a new CTO to grow its forward deployed engineering team.

Best for: Companies that want AI engineering billed by output

  • Squads with FDEs
  • Two-week sprints
  • On site in New York

Tenex sells AI engineering squads, each with a technical product manager and forward deployed engineers, and bills by output measured in story points over two-week sprints. It also runs AI strategy work. Its managing partners are Alex Lieberman, a Morning Brew co-founder, and Arman Hezarkhani. It doesn't publish rates.

When a large firm fits

  • You need strategy, change management and delivery from one contract.
  • You're already on that firm's preferred platform, like IBM or Google Cloud.
  • Your compliance team wants an audit-grade vendor.

When a boutique fits

  • You know the problem and want engineers building it this quarter.
  • You want to talk to the people writing the code.
  • You'd rather keep the code than buy a platform. Compare forward deployed engineering companies.

Questions

Common questions

Which AI consulting firms partner with Anthropic?

Accenture (an Anthropic business group, December 2025), Deloitte (Claude for 470,000 people, October 2025), IBM (October 2025), BCG (since 2023) and Slalom (November 2024) all announced Anthropic partnerships. PwC lists Anthropic among its alliances.

Which AI consulting firms partner with OpenAI?

OpenAI named Accenture, BCG, McKinsey and Capgemini as Frontier Alliance partners in February 2026. PwC lists OpenAI among its alliances and built its 2023 AI investment on Microsoft's Azure OpenAI Service.

What is a boutique AI consulting firm?

A smaller firm that focuses on AI engineering instead of a full consulting menu. Dplyz, Distyl, Tribe AI, Tenex and Ode are examples on this site. Most put senior engineers inside the client's team.

Do AI consulting firms publish their rates?

None of the firms on this page publish rates. Expect a proposal after a scoping conversation.
