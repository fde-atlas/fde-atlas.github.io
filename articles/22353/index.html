Software Engineer Job in Seattle, WA — JeffreyM Consulting

Talentify · 2026-09-22 · 4 min read

Summary

JeffreyM Consulting is hiring a Software Engineer in Seattle for an early-stage startup building a supply chain transparency platform. The onsite role pays $150,000–$225,000 plus equity and involves building the platform from zero to one: prototyping, full-stack development, API and cloud integrations, and direct work with customers and non-technical stakeholders. Candidates need 5+ years of full-stack experience with TypeScript, React, Next.js, GraphQL, and Trigger.dev; startup experience and ERP or supply chain knowledge are preferred. Because the role touches ITAR-controlled data, applicants must qualify as U.S. persons, and visa sponsorship is not available.

Key points

  • Early-stage supply chain startup hiring a Software Engineer in Seattle, onsite five days a week.
  • Salary ranges from $150,000 to $225,000 per year, plus equity.
  • Role covers zero-to-one platform building, prototyping, integrations, and customer collaboration.
  • Requires 5+ years of full-stack experience with TypeScript, React, Next.js, GraphQL, Trigger.dev.
  • ITAR-controlled data means candidates must be U.S. persons; no visa sponsorship offered.
