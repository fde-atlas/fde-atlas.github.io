What's the difference between FDE and staff augmentation or outsourcing

Original title: FDE 與人力增補、外包有甚麼分別

Onyx Devs Lab · 2026-09-15 · 2 min read

Summary

Onyx Devs Lab distinguishes staffing augmentation, outsourcing, and FDE work by the unit of responsibility rather than job titles or on-site presence. Augmentation supplies capacity under client direction; outsourcing delivers an agreed scope; FDE owns a bounded operational problem, linking field learning, implementation, deployment, and evidence of outcomes. The article advises choosing the simplest model that places responsibility where necessary decisions are made, and covers contracting points: field access, decision rights, evidence reviews, change rules, and exit conditions. It also notes FDE work requires proximity to operations and fast decision loops, and should transition to stable delivery once uncertainty drops.

Key points

  • The three models differ by unit of responsibility: capacity, scope, or an operational problem.
  • Job titles and on-site presence alone do not determine the engagement model.
  • FDE requires proximity to operations and fast decision cycles; on-site ratio varies by context.
  • FDE engagements should define evidence gates, change rules, and stop conditions upfront.
  • Once uncertainty drops, FDE should shift to stable delivery or internal ownership.
