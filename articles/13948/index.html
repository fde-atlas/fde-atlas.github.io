Job Role Models Across Companies · OpenFDE

Original title: 各公司岗位形态 · OpenFDE

OpenFDE · 前沿部署工程师社区 · 2026-10-16 · 3 min read

Summary

This article surveys FDE-type roles across OpenAI, Google Cloud, Palantir, Mistral, Scale AI, and ByteDance's Volcano Engine, arguing that FDE is a family of roles rather than a single job. Each company's variant reflects its product and business model: OpenAI splits the role three ways, Palantir pairs FDSE with Deployment Strategist, Mistral leans toward ML/LLM application engineering, and Scale AI toward data infrastructure. The author also lists Chinese job titles that overlap with FDE and proposes a test: a role is FDE only if it combines on-site customer work, real coding, production deployment, and a product feedback loop; otherwise it degrades into pre-sales or consulting.

Key points

  • FDE is a role family, not one job title.
  • OpenAI splits it into FDE, FDSWE, and Technical Deployment Lead.
  • Palantir pairs FDSE with the PM-like Deployment Strategist.
  • Mistral and Scale AI emphasize ML apps and data infrastructure respectively.
  • FDE test: on-site work, coding, production delivery, feedback loop.

FDE is not a single role, but a family of roles. Different companies create variants based on their products, customers, and business models—some lean toward full-stack delivery, some toward ML/LLM applications, and some toward data infrastructure. Understanding these differences is more useful than obsessing over titles.

Company-by-company breakdown

FDE belongs to the Forward Deployed Engineering / Model Deployment for Business organization, covering discovery, technical scoping, system design, building through production rollout, and feeding frontline feedback back to Research and Product; FDSWE leans toward software engineering and abstraction, building custom software for strategic customers and abstracting it into reusable patterns; Technical Deployment Lead leans more toward scenario identification, system-level understanding, and executive communication. This shows OpenAI's deployment organization is no longer "a single FDE role." OpenAI FDE↗TDL↗

GenAI FDE requires AI system architecture on the cloud, structured and unstructured data pipelines, vector databases, and RAG. Preferred qualifications explicitly list LangGraph, CrewAI, Google ADK, ReAct, self-reflection, hierarchical delegation, as well as LLM metrics like tokens/sec, cost-per-request, and tracing. The job is to turn prototypes into production-grade agentic workflows, writing the connective tissue that links AI products to customers' legacy data, security boundaries, and business processes. It is not a "cloud solutions architect," but a production-grade agent / RAG / enterprise integration engineer. Google Careers↗

FDSE emphasizes engineering and customer deployment, while the Deployment Strategist (i.e., Echo) is more like a product manager, focused on business strategy and customer relationships; the boundary between the two can blur. Palantir calls FDSE its own "blueprint": embedding on-site with customers, handling large-scale data, building AI-driven mission-critical operational systems, building custom applications for customers, taking ideas from concept to deployment, and communicating directly with technical teams through executives. Palantir Lever↗Deployment Strategist↗

The Applied AI team covers everything from pre-sales to implementation, and the Forward Deployed ML Engineer is described as "owning end-to-end execution like a startup CTO," connecting AI research with real enterprise applications. Responsibilities include deploying production-grade GenAI use cases with business impact, complex fine-tuning, advanced RAG, agentic use cases, and improving products and models based on customer feedback. It leans more toward ML/LLM application engineering than OpenAI's generalist FDE. Mistral Lever↗

GenAI FDE targets top AI research labs and government agencies, emphasizing data infrastructure, customer collaboration, end-to-end full-stack development, rapid experimentation, influence on product and engineering strategy, and experience with cloud infrastructure, large-scale data, and distributed systems. It is closer to a hybrid of "AI data infrastructure + full-stack delivery + customer engineering." Scale AI↗

In China, roles explicitly titled FDE have already appeared, such as "Doubao Large Model FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) - Volcano Ark MaaS": serving top industry customers, deeply understanding the business, identifying core pain points, designing and implementing complex agent architectures and workflows, bridging demos and business value, and turning customer feedback into product capabilities. Requirements include Python/Java, full-stack, large model effectiveness and performance evaluation, post-training, RAG, agents, and multi-agent systems. Liepin Jobs↗

In China: the same role under different names

In China, more companies may not use the title FDE directly, but instead use these aliases—the actual work overlaps heavily:

  • Large model solutions engineer
  • AI application delivery engineer / large model delivery engineer
  • AI agent engineer / AI agent/RAG development engineer
  • Large model application architect
  • AI product solutions expert
  • Enterprise AI application development engineer

To judge whether a role is an FDE, don't look at the name—check whether it simultaneously satisfies: on-site with customers + real coding + production deployment + a product feedback loop. Missing any one of the four, it often degrades into pre-sales or consulting. For how to verify each item one by one, see Differences from adjacent roles.
