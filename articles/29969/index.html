How Many FDE Certifications Are There? A Clear Breakdown of CFDE, MIIT, and Tencent Cloud's Four Systems

Original title: FDE 认证到底有几种？CFDE、工信部、腾讯云四套体系一次分清

FDE 指南 · 2026-10-26 · 5 min read

Summary

This guide clarifies that the various "FDE certifications" on the market are distinct credentials from different organizations, not versions of one certificate. It covers five systems: TIIDA's three-level CFDE (introduced with DAMA Greater China), two separate certificates from two Ministry of Industry and Information Technology centers, Tencent Cloud's ADP FDE ecosystem certification launched in September 2026, and Lenovo's joint training certification. It also warns that the Ministry of Human Resources and Huawei's "AI-FDE pioneer talent program" is a training program, not an examinable certificate. The article offers three verification methods—checking the full certificate name, the issuing body, and the official lookup channel—and advises choosing based on intended use, noting the real risk is paying for multiple certificates when employers recognize only one.

Key points

  • FDE certifications are separate credentials from different bodies, not versions of one certificate.
  • CFDE (TIIDA with DAMA Greater China) has three levels—A, P, M—that must be taken sequentially with degree/experience prerequisites.
  • Two different MIIT centers issue two different certificates: a job competency evaluation and an advanced "FDE Solutions Engineer" certificate.
  • Tencent Cloud's ADP FDE certification (September 2026) is a 90-minute offline exam, 60 questions, 70 to pass.
  • Verify certificates by full name, issuing organization, and official query channel; the Huawei/MOHRSS program is not a certificate.

Bottom line up front

The currently publicly verifiable FDE-related certificates are not multiple versions of a single certificate, but different certificates launched by different organizations. You should at least distinguish the following five things, four of which are certificates or certifications, while the fifth is merely a talent development program.

Why there are several FDE certificates

FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer, commonly translated into Chinese as "前沿部署工程师" or "前线部署工程师". The role originated at Palantir and was later adopted by companies such as OpenAI and Anthropic, gradually becoming a generic job title.

Once a job title becomes popular, certificate issuers act on their own: international professional organizations introduce certification systems, units under ministries conduct job competency evaluations, and cloud vendors set up certifications for their ecosystem partners. So what appears on the market is not "multiple versions of one certification" but "different certificates launched by different organizations".

CFDE: the most complete system

CFDE was introduced through cooperation between DAMA Greater China and TIIDA (International Institute of Data and AI) in the US. The two parties signed a strategic cooperation agreement in August 2026 and simultaneously launched the Chinese translation and localization of the core textbooks.

The certificate is named TIIDA Certified Forward Deployed Engineer and has three progressive levels:

  • CFDE-A (Associate): entry level with no prerequisites
  • CFDE-P (Professional): has hard prerequisites
  • CFDE-M (Master)

The prerequisites for CFDE-P are: you must first pass CFDE-A and hold a valid certificate, then meet at least one of the following based on education and work experience: a doctoral degree; a master's degree plus at least 1 year of IT-related work after graduation; a bachelor's degree plus 3 or more years; an associate degree plus 5 or more years.

So the three levels are not "three exams taken at once" but a path that must be completed level by level. This point is often glossed over in marketing pitches.

The MIIT system: two different units, two different certificates

This is the easiest place to get confused. Under the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, two different units are each running FDE-related programs.

First, the Talent Exchange Center of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology. The "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Job Competency Evaluation Program" launched on September 2, 2026, implemented under the "General Rules for Job Competency Evaluation of MIIT Talent", with the certificate named "IITC MIIT Talent Job Competency Evaluation Certificate", which can be verified in the MIIT talent database.

It should be made clear: this is a job competency evaluation certificate, not a national vocational qualification, professional license, or professional title certificate. These three differ completely in legal effect and use; if an employer asks for a "vocational qualification", this certificate does not match.

Second, the Education and Examination Center of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology. Through training programs it runs, such as the FDE practical training camp, it issues the "FDE Solutions Engineer (Senior)" certificate. Note that the certificate name includes "Solutions Engineer" and "Senior"; it is not the same certificate as the one above.

One ministry, two centers, two certificates with different names—looking only at the five characters "MIIT FDE certification", you cannot tell which one you hold. For what each certificate covers and which direction suits you, see How Many MIIT FDE Certifications Are There? What Exactly Differs Between the Two.

Tencent Cloud: an ecosystem certification released in September 2026

On September 10, 2026, Tencent Cloud released the "Tencent Cloud ADP Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" certification system at its Xiamen summit, built on Tencent Cloud's agent development platform ADP and open to partner engineers and individual developers.

Its exam focuses on delivery capabilities on the ADP platform: agent design and workflow orchestration, knowledge bases and RAG, tool and MCP integration, production deployment and operations, and security compliance. It is positioned more as "a credential for delivering agent projects on Tencent Cloud", with coverage leaning toward the ecosystem.

The exam format is an offline theory exam: 40 single-choice plus 20 multiple-choice questions, 90 minutes, passing at 70 points. For detailed rules, see The Full Tencent Cloud ADP FDE Exam Process.

Two company-participated programs—don't confuse them with the certificates above

Lenovo FDE Training Certification: jointly released by the China Electronics Standardization Institute and Lenovo Group, it is a training certification recognized by both a company and an industry standardization body, with courses linking "business scenario identification—solution design—on-site delivery—continuous operations" into one line. It is a training certification, different in nature from the evaluation / professional certifications above; before registering, check the issuing body and the full certificate name.

One more thing that is easily mistaken for a certificate

The Social Security Capacity Building Center of the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and Huawei jointly launched the "AI-FDE Pioneer Talent Program". It is a talent development program, not a certificate you can register for. In searches it often appears alongside the certificates above; be careful to distinguish between a "program" and a "certificate", which are two different things.

Three ways to tell them apart

No need to memorize complex classifications; just remember these three.

First, look at the full certificate name. CFDE is TIIDA Certified Forward Deployed Engineer; the MIIT Talent Exchange Center's is the "IITC MIIT Talent Job Competency Evaluation Certificate"; the MIIT Education and Examination Center's is "FDE Solutions Engineer (Senior)"; Tencent Cloud's is the "Tencent Cloud ADP Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" certification. Different names always mean different certificates.

Second, look at the issuing organization. For international professional certification, look at TIIDA / DAMA; for job competency evaluation, the MIIT Talent Exchange Center; for training-type certificates, the MIIT Education and Examination Center; for cloud ecosystem certification, Tencent Cloud. This field cannot be faked and is the easiest to verify.

Third, look at the verification channel. Whichever official channel can verify it, that's the system it belongs to. If there is no verification channel and you can only see images or screenshots issued by the training provider itself, be extra cautious.

How to choose

Which one to choose depends on what you want to use it for. If you want to match scenarios one by one—"career change / cloud ecosystem delivery / government-affiliated settings / solution design"—first read Tencent Cloud FDE, CFDE, or MIIT FDE: Which One Should You Take, which includes a decision table; below is a quick overview.

  • Want to systematically build a complete capability for enterprise AI implementation and hope for a three-tier progressive learning path: the CFDE system is the most complete, and CFDE-A is the entry point with no prerequisites. See the CFDE dedicated article for the application requirements, exam formats, and course modules of the three tiers.
  • Need to prove job competency in government or state-owned enterprise settings: learn about the MIIT system programs, but first confirm whether they require a "job competency evaluation" or a "professional qualification".
  • Mainly do ecosystem delivery on a specific cloud platform: cloud vendor certifications are a better fit; Tencent Cloud ADP FDE is exactly this type.
  • Just want to know what an FDE does: don't rush to sign up for a course; read Is the FDE certification worth it first before deciding.

The three are not in conflict in positioning; they simply serve different purposes. The real pitfall is not choosing wrong, but assuming they are the same thing—paying three fees, getting back three certificates from different systems, and then discovering that recruiters only recognize one of them.
