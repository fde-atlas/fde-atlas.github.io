Dentsu Group in Japan Launches Skill Development Program for 3,000 FDE and FDM Professionals

- Strengthening the framework for driving AI transformation leveraging Gemini Enterprise -

  • Press Release

Dentsu Soken Inc.

Dentsu Soken Inc. (Headquarters: Minato-ku, Tokyo; President and CEO: Hirohisa Iwamoto), which realizes the evolution of companies and society through technology, has jointly launched, together with three Dentsu Group companies in Japan (Dentsu, Dentsu Digital, and Ignition Point), a group-wide FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer*1) and FDM (Forward Deployed Marketer*1) skill development program targeting approximately 3,000 people, leveraging "Gemini Enterprise," Google Cloud's enterprise AI platform. The program will strengthen the framework for embedding into clients' operations and driving AI-powered business transformation, promoting business transformation and the social implementation of generative AI.

In today's business environment, generative AI has evolved beyond its role as a tool for improving operational efficiency into a foundation for transforming companies' entire business processes and marketing activities. To translate this cutting-edge generative AI technology into business growth and improved customer experience (CX), companies need not only generative AI implementation and operational improvements, but also a comprehensive perspective aligned with corporate growth strategies and hands-on, side-by-side support that addresses frontline challenges. To address these challenges, the Dentsu Group in Japan will promote the use of "Gemini Enterprise," which serves as the foundation for AI utilization across all areas of business by enabling cross-functional use of internal data in a highly secure environment, while developing FDEs and FDMs who combine state-of-the-art AI technical skills with a keen sense of business realities.

Key Points of This Initiative

Development of 3,000 advanced AI professionals incorporating the use of "Gemini Enterprise" Targeting approximately 3,000 people across the Dentsu Group in Japan, the program will cover practical use of "Gemini Enterprise" through effective integration into business workflows. Going beyond simply acquiring generative AI skills, the group as a whole will develop advanced AI professionals with FDE and FDM skills who can identify challenges on the front lines of marketing and business and lead projects themselves. Consistent client support from strategic planning to frontline implementation FDEs will embed into clients' business operations and drive AI transformation projects in a side-by-side manner. From formulating business and marketing strategies to advanced insight discovery, generation and effectiveness testing of optimal creative, and integration into daily operations, the Dentsu Group in Japan will combine its expertise with "Gemini Enterprise" at every phase to deliver business results for clients.

Going forward, the Dentsu Group in Japan will continue to promote the use of Google Cloud AI solutions, including "Gemini Enterprise," contributing to the creation of business results for clients.

  • *1 Hands-on professionals who support business transformation at clients' sites, centered on the use of generative AI
  • *Google Cloud and Gemini Enterprise are trademarks of Google LLC.

The Dentsu Group in Japan is promoting its unique AI strategy, "AI For Growth," which contributes to the growth of clients and society by combining "human intelligence" and "AI intelligence."

For more information on AI For Growth, please visit the following webpage. https://www.dentsu.co.jp/labo/ai_for_growth/index.html
