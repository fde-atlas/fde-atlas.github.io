In 1935, Boeing’s Model 299 bomber crashed shortly after takeoff with an experienced test pilot at the controls. The crew had forgotten to release the locks holding its control surfaces in place while parked, leaving the aircraft uncontrollable. The crash helped spur the adoption of cockpit checklists to verify essential steps before flight.

The lesson was simple: as a machine becomes more complex, experience alone cannot guarantee that every critical detail gets checked. People need a system they can reliably follow.

Professional services teams face the same challenge as they grow. Staffing lives in one person’s head, margins stay invisible until month-end and every client update is assembled by hand. The strain is industry-wide: SPI Research’s 2026 benchmark found that billable utilization fell to 66.4% in 2025, its lowest in 19 years.

PSA software provides that shared system, a platform that connects the core business processes of delivery, time tracking, billing, financials and reporting, so leaders can spot problems while there’s still time to change the outcome.

This guide is for consulting firms, agencies and the professional services teams inside technology companies. If you run IT support for other businesses as a managed service provider or deliver IT services, an IT-focused PSA such as ConnectWise PSA, Autotask or HaloPSA will serve you better, and our routing section names them so you can skip straight there.

Certinia, formerly FinancialForce, is a PSA that runs natively on Salesforce, so sales and delivery teams work from the same customer record in Salesforce from the moment a deal closes. It brings deep resource management with skills, mature project financials, a separate revenue management product and Veda, its AI agent layer, which launched in April 2026.

Certinia fits firms where Salesforce is already the system of record and a Salesforce admin team is part of everyday operations, because the PSA inherits Salesforce's data model, permissions and reporting. That alignment can also improve client relationships by giving customer-facing teams shared context from sale through delivery.

It is a harder fit for teams without Salesforce admins, or for teams that want clients working inside their projects, since the client portal runs on Salesforce Experience Cloud and needs community licences.

A useful way to frame the decision is native versus connected. A Salesforce-native PSA lives inside your Salesforce org and shares its data, permissions and release cycle, while a Salesforce-connected PSA runs on its own platform and syncs with Salesforce, so native suits teams that want one org for everything and connected suits teams that want their PSA to move at its own pace.

Where it falls short: Reviewers’ most common complaint is a steep learning curve, and several say customizing it takes Salesforce admins or developers. Others report slow performance on complex reports and a high total cost once Salesforce licences are added.

  • PSA on Salesforce: Projects, time and resourcing live as Salesforce objects, so your CRM reports and workflows extend straight into delivery and give project delivery teams a shared operational view.
  • Resource management and skills: Resource requests, held and tentative assignments, and skill filters help staff large enterprise pools.
  • Project financials: Budgets, project financial tracking and revenue forecasting sit in the same org as your pipeline, giving teams timely access to financial data alongside pipeline context.
  • Revenue management: A separate Certinia product connects to the PSA to handle revenue recognition.

"PSA" covers three different jobs. Consulting firms, agencies and technology companies' professional services teams need a PSA that plans people against projects, supports service delivery, and turns time into revenue: Rocketlane, Kantata, Certinia, BigTime, Scoro, Productive and the rest of this list.

Firms that run IT support for other businesses need an IT-focused PSA built around tickets and devices, such as ConnectWise PSA, Autotask or HaloPSA. ERP-led firms often run project accounting inside NetSuite or Deltek. Jira and ServiceNow are work and service management tools, not PSAs, because they track tasks and tickets, not utilization, billing or margin.

Choose PSA software on the five dimensions below, not on the longest feature list, and test every vendor on your own month-end and delivery work instead of their demo script. The right tool is the one that removes the hours your team spends stitching people, time and money together today.

  • AI that does work: Ask the vendor to show an agent change something on a real project, not only summarize it.
  • Total cost: Ask for the price with seats, add-ons, AI and integrations included, plus the implementation fee and the seat minimum.
