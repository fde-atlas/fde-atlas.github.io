Summary

FDEInterviews.com's Field Guide is a 42-page paid Premium PDF for software engineers, project/program managers, and engineering managers moving into forward deployed engineering. A fictional warranty-review case runs through the book, covering problem discovery, choosing the smallest system that can prove value, evaluations that can stop a release, recoverable failure design, and measuring actual value. It also covers interview preparation and building an inspectable portfolio. The guide has 16 chapters, 8 technical diagrams, a 30-term glossary, and links to the site's courses and exercises. Where do you start?

The guide follows a fictional warranty-review workflow. You investigate why agents struggle to find the correct policy, then decide what the first release should prove. The same case continues through permissions, evaluation, recovery and value measurement.

It gives the engineering choices a context. A correct citation may still point to an obsolete policy. A useful answer may still cost more review time than it saves. The worked examples name those failures and ask what you would change.

All customer-case numbers are hypothetical teaching assumptions. No customer results or company interview rounds are invented.

Before you download

What is The Forward Deployed Engineer Field Guide?

An illustrated, 42-page guide to moving into forward deployed engineering and doing the work. It covers role selection, career transitions, discovery, AI system design, evaluation, production recovery, customer communication, interviews and portfolio evidence.

Who is the field guide for?

Software engineers, project and program managers, and engineering managers considering FDE work, plus practitioners who want a practical reference for customer engagements. The transition chapters distinguish each starting point instead of assuming the same skill gaps.

Do I need Premium to download the guide?

Yes. The complete PDF requires sign-in and active paid Premium or approved complimentary access. Three selected sample pages are public. The industry report, The State of Forward Deployed Engineering 2026, remains free with an account.

Is it included in my existing Premium access?

Yes. There is no separate purchase. You can download the guide while your paid Premium or approved complimentary access is active and keep the copy for personal study. Redistribution, resale and public uploads are not permitted except where allowed by law or written permission.

How does the guide connect to the courses and concepts?

Chapter links lead to the site's concept explanations, question bank, work samples and the Foundations, FDE Engagement and Hard Deployments courses. The PDF includes clickable links and a reading route for each starting point.

Were AI tools used to create the guide?

Yes. AI tools assisted research organization, drafting, editing and illustration. FDEInterviews.com confirms that the guide was manually curated, compiled and reviewed by its human editorial team. The customer scenarios and worked numbers are hypothetical teaching examples.

Is this the forthcoming FDE Interview Book?

No. The Field Guide is available now and spans the career transition and customer work. The FDE Interview Book is a separate forthcoming title, currently planned for November 2026.
