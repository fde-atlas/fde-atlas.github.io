~Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) provide end-to-end support from AI adoption planning to on-the-ground adoption. Alongside business expansion, the company is strengthening FDE hiring in Japan~

(July 30, 2026, QueryPie AI LLC)

QueryPie AI LLC (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Representative: Keizo Aribe; hereinafter "the Company") is fully launching its AI Transformation (hereinafter "AX") support business in the Japanese market, driven by Forward Deployed Engineers (hereinafter "FDEs"). As part of this initiative, the Company will establish a dedicated FDE organization in Japan and strengthen hiring.

An FDE is a role in which engineers go deep into customers' operations and drive the resolution of business challenges by leveraging data, AI, and software. The Company will support Japanese companies' AX initiatives from the front lines while further expanding its FDE service delivery framework.

For details on the FDE role and the skills it requires, please see What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)? An overview of the new engineering role gaining attention in the generative AI era.

Background

While the use of generative AI and AI agents continues to advance, Japanese companies are facing increasingly visible challenges, such as "tools were introduced but never took hold in day-to-day operations" and "we have a vision for AI adoption, but it never progresses to implementation and operation."

Behind this lies a shortage of talent who understand both technology and frontline operations, and who can design, implement, and improve AI adoption in the context of actual business challenges. FDEs combine engineering skills with business understanding and work alongside customers on-site until results are delivered.

The FDE is a field-implementation-oriented engineering model that developed in the data and AI space in the United States. This approach, known through Palantir Technologies and others, has in recent years drawn attention as a methodology for embedding data and AI adoption into frontline operations. The Company believes FDEs will play a key role in taking Japanese companies' AX initiatives to the next stage.

About QueryPie AI's FDE Business

Since the establishment of its Japanese entity, the Company has made deploying the FDE model for the Japanese market one of the key pillars of its business. Until now, founding members, including founder and CEO Hwang In-seo, have worked on domestic client projects as FDEs, providing comprehensive support from data platform construction to AI implementation and adoption support.

With the full launch of the AX support business and strengthened FDE hiring, the Company will expand its dedicated FDE organization as QueryPie AI, building on the knowledge gained from past projects. In providing support, the Company will offer optimal approaches tailored to each customer's challenges and existing environment, including integration with the enterprise AI platform "QueryPie AIP" and the suite of applications running on QueryPie AIP, "QueryPie APP." Through this, the Company will support the creation of environments where Japanese companies can put AI to work on the front lines and achieve continuous results.

About QueryPie AIP

QueryPie AIP is an enterprise AI platform that enables companies to build and manage the use of multiple large language models (multi-LLM), Model Context Protocol (MCP)-based business system integration, RAG-based knowledge utilization, and AI agent operations on a single platform.

FDEs face clients' AI adoption challenges on the ground, leveraging integration with QueryPie AIP and QueryPie APP as appropriate to customer challenges, and support everything from planning through implementation, business adoption, and production operation.

About FDE Hiring

The Company is currently hiring FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) in Japan as the AX support business expands. The FDE is a position that embeds within client companies' operations and solves real business challenges using data, AI, and software.

The main responsibilities are as follows.

  • Understanding client companies' business challenges and designing technical solutions
  • Building and supporting the deployment of AI adoption environments tailored to customer challenges
  • Designing and implementing AI use cases, including integration with QueryPie AIP and QueryPie APP applications such as Lingo and NotePie
  • Supporting business system integration and automation using RAG, MCP, AI agents, and more
  • Post-deployment improvement, adoption support, and hands-on accompaniment toward delivering results

We welcome candidates who:

  • Can take ownership of customer challenges
  • Have experience working deeply with at least one programming language
  • Have a strong interest in the latest technologies such as LLMs, MCP, RAG, and AI agents
  • Want to tackle problem-solving from both technical and business perspectives

We value not only your experience to date, but also curiosity, ownership, learning ability, and the drive to see things through.

Message from the Representative

"Until now, we have worked directly on challenges as FDEs on the ground at Japanese client companies, supporting the implementation and adoption of AI. Establishing an FDE organization in Japan and fully launching the AX support business is an important step in advancing these efforts as an organization. FDE is not merely a job title; it is an engineering philosophy of using technology in the field to create value together with customers. We will root this FDE model in the Japanese market so that Japanese companies' AI adoption does not end as a vision, but leads to real business results." — Brant Hwang, Founder and CEO, QueryPie, Inc.

About QueryPie AI LLC

Based in Toranomon, Minato-ku, Tokyo, QueryPie AI LLC provides QueryPie AIP, deployment support, and FDE services to Japanese enterprise companies. By combining hands-on FDE support with the enterprise AI platform QueryPie AIP, the Company supports Japanese companies' AI adoption from vision to on-the-ground adoption.
