Summary

This is a job posting on OpenWork for a Forward Deployed Engineer at SUPER STUDIO, the Japanese company behind the AI commerce platform ecforce. The role sits in a Consulting & FDE group and follows a 'SaaS × FDE' model: FDEs enter enterprise client operations and drive BPR using ecforce, generative AI tools (Claude Code, Cursor), and iPaaS like Make and Zapier. Requirements include 2-3 years of BPR or IT implementation experience and client-facing work; programming is not required but IT fundamentals are. Salary ranges from 8 to 20 million yen. The page also covers company values, a founder message, and an interview with a mid-career hire who moved from an SIer to a PdM role.

  • Maximizing the customer experience for everyone involved in things and experiences
  • SUPER STUDIO Inc. operates under the mission of "maximizing the customer experience for everyone involved in things and experiences," developing and providing the AI commerce platform "ecforce" as well as offering consulting services as part of its business support. Since its founding in 2014, the company has applied the expertise gained through its own D2C business to product development, supporting the promotion of commerce DX from both the system and business perspectives. ■About ecforce ecforce aims to optimize the entire business through commerce DX by quickly adopting cutting-edge technological innovations such as AI. In addition to marketing optimization and strengthening sales channels, it offers a wide range of products specialized in commerce business—from AI-driven data utilization to operational improvement and execution—creating an environment where teams can focus on highly specialized decision-making and creative initiatives. ■About Consulting Through "New Normal SI," which combines SaaS and SI, we solve the structural challenges of commerce businesses. From upstream strategy design to system implementation and on-site operations, we provide hands-on support with agile processes and reliable know-how based on our own brand management experience.

  • Employee Interview: Mid-Career Hire
  • Why did you choose a SaaS startup over a system integrator? ■About your previous job At a system integrator (SIer), I worked as a PG/SE and also served as a PM managing the progress of various projects. ■What prompted your career change My previous workplace had a good environment and was easy to work in, but I couldn't envision spending decades doing the same work. Also, since SIers fundamentally operate on client work, there was the dilemma of being unable to implement better ideas due to deadlines and budgets. ■What made you decide to join a SaaS company from an SIer I found the business model of "ecforce" attractive: although it is a to-B product, improving the service enhances the end user's customer experience, and increased sales benefit both the client and SUPER STUDIO. It is a business model with high social value, and I empathized with the "win-win-win" mindset. I was also impressed by how energized and motivated everyone seemed in their work. ■About your position when you joined Initially, I was offered a PM position handling data migration and similar work, but as we talked, the conversation turned to whether a PdM position—which had not yet been established at the time—might be a better fit. I was deeply impressed by how quickly they created a position tailored to the skills I wanted to develop and the things I wanted to do, and by their stance of not just seeking people who could contribute immediately but thinking about growing together with them, so I decided to take on the PdM role. ■What you feel after actually working here Employees and executives communicate on a flat basis with no barriers between them, showing genuine interest in one another—you can really tell that everyone loves people. Since it's a startup, there are still areas where we're finding our way, but there are endless opportunities to make things better, so you can approach your work with your own vision. For those who feel stuck at an SIer, I recommend this environment—it's full of stimulation.
  • Job Title
  • FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)
  • Job Description
  • 【◆Background of the Position】 To establish our position as "the first company that comes to mind for AI × Commerce," we are advancing DX support for large enterprises (enterprise clients) centered on our own AI commerce platform "ecforce" as a pillar of our company-wide strategy. Solving the complex challenges of large enterprises requires not only strategic proposals via slides, but also a "hands-on, field-oriented (FDE)" approach that dives deep into frontline operations and drives transformation by delivering mechanisms that actually work. We are pursuing a SaaS × FDE business model: advanced commerce capabilities and AI utilization are realized through ecforce, while complex internal business challenges that ecforce cannot fully cover are addressed through AI × BPR by FDE, strongly driving transformation of the entire business from the frontline. In this position, within the "Consulting & FDE Group" you will join, we are seeking core members who will serve as a bridge between business and technology and, on the front lines with clients, turn business transformation through "AI × ecforce" into reality with their own hands. 【◆Responsibilities】 You will embed yourself in the operations of large enterprise clients and drive business process reform (BPR) in the commerce domain (supply chain, customer support, marketing, etc.) by leveraging ecforce features, generative AI, and no-code tools. 1. Identifying frontline challenges and visualizing business processes: Deeply understand clients' frontline operations and identify bottlenecks—where inefficiencies exist and how dramatically they can be improved through ecforce utilization and generative AI adoption 2. Quick BPR validation using generative AI × ecforce: Combine ecforce's standard features and API settings, generative AI tools (ClaudeCode, Cursor), and iPaaS (Make, Zapier, etc.) to build and validate "automation workflows that work right now in the field" with your own hands 3. Embedding changes in the field (change management) and feeding back to the product: Provide total support for embedding the AI workflows you create and new ecforce operations, from training to hands-on accompaniment. Furthermore, feed back the full-scale system development needs and common issues revealed through field validation to our internal product development team and AI specialist team, driving product evolution 【◆What Makes This Position Attractive】 1. A hybrid career combining "consulting" and "FDE (technology utilization)": Neither a consultant who only draws slides nor an engineer who writes code to spec. Acquire the skill that will become most valuable in the coming AI era: "mastering generative AI and SaaS mechanisms to dramatically transform the business of the client in front of you" 2. The satisfaction of leading cutting-edge "AI × BPR" without coding: Even without full-scratch programming knowledge (Python, Java, etc.), you can leverage the solid foundation of our own product (ecforce) and AI agents. With API integration and prompt engineering as your weapons, experience the overwhelming speed at which you can turn your own ideas into immediate value for clients 3. Major business impact possible only through SaaS × FDE: Rather than FDE as an "RPA replacement," the existence of a SaaS foundation enables bold and rapid promotion of transformations that are truly valuable to client companies. Take on a variety of challenges while experiencing firsthand how your work benefits end users and clients
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • 【Required】 ・ Project experience in business reform (BPR) or IT implementation (approx. 2-3 years or more): Practical experience at a consulting firm, SIer, or in the DX promotion/business improvement division of a company ・ Client-facing work experience: Practical experience in requirements definition, issue extraction, and proposals/negotiations with clients ・ Business experience with or strong interest in generative AI: Experience using and validating LLMs such as ChatGPT in practical work (prompt construction, simple automation using iPaaS, etc.) ※ Programming experience is not required, but a lack of allergy to IT fundamentals such as API concepts, data structures, and SQL is essential. 【Preferred】 ・ Domain knowledge in EC/D2C, retail/distribution, or supply chain, or consulting/business improvement experience in these industries ・ Experience implementing and configuring various SaaS tools and improving business efficiency through API integration ・ Facilitation skills to engage multiple stakeholders, including clients' frontline staff and internal development teams 【Ideal Candidate Profile】 1. Someone who genuinely loves "trying the latest AI and IT tools to make work more efficient": Someone who enjoys proactively testing and realizing ideas, such as "If I combine this tool with generative AI, couldn't that tedious task be automated?" 2. Someone with excellent communication skills who can empathize with clients' frontline operations: Someone who can carefully listen to the concerns of frontline operators unfamiliar with IT and translate the value of technology in terms the other person can understand 3. Someone with a strong sense of ownership who can tenaciously bridge the gap between "strategy" and "execution": Someone with the passion to not let things end as armchair theory, but to personally configure ecforce and AI tools and see things through, no matter how gritty the work
