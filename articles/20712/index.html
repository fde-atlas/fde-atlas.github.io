"Forward Deployed Engineer" — What the Job Defined by PFN and SoftBank in 2026 Actually Involves | AI Time-Saving Lab

Original title: 「Forward Deployed Engineer」という職種──PFN・ソフトバンクが2026年に定義した仕事の中身

AI時短ラボ · 2026-09-04 · 9 min read

Summary

This article examines how the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role emerged in Japanese hiring during 2026, based on actual job postings. Preferred Networks posted an FDE role in June 2026, and SoftBank followed in May with a secondment to SB OAI Japan, its joint venture with OpenAI. Both define the job as embedding with clients 50-80% of the time to take generative AI from requirements through production and continuous improvement. The author links the timing to PoC projects maturing into production deployments, and notes Findy's March 2026 roundup already listed 12 other companies hiring FDEs.

Key points

  • PFN formalized a role its engineers had long done implicitly, with 50-80% on-site residency.
  • The role spans business analysis, agent implementation, production rollout, and feedback to model teams.
  • SoftBank's FDE is seconded to SB OAI Japan; salary listed at roughly ¥8.12M-¥20.35M theoretical annual.
  • Only SoftBank disclosed pay; PFN did not, and Sakana AI's posting details were inaccessible.
  • Findy's March 2026 feature listed 12 additional companies, including DeNA and LayerX, hiring FDEs.

Summary in 3 lines Preferred Networks posted a "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" job opening on June 18, 2026, and SoftBank (a secondment slot to SB OAI Japan G.K.) on May 18 of the same year. The FDE role as defined in PFN's job posting is to "embed oneself at customer sites 50-80% of the time and take end-to-end responsibility from understanding the business through implementation, production operation, and continuous improvement." A special feature compiled by Findy as of March 2026 already listed FDE job openings from 12 companies not including these two — this "new job title" is already being launched in parallel at multiple companies

Job postings with the title "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" have been increasing on Japanese recruitment pages since the start of 2026. Translated, it means "an engineer deployed to the front lines." The term was originally used early on by the US company Palantir Technologies, but 2026 became the year when Japanese generative AI companies and major corporations successively made this title explicit in their own job postings. This article reads the actual job postings and confirms what is "new" about them.

The "things PFN had been doing implicitly" that its job posting spelled out

The job posting published by Preferred Networks (PFN) on June 18, 2026 explains why it created this role now as follows (from the original posting).

We have decided to explicitly define as Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) the role of "implementing generative AI on the customer's front lines," which PFN engineers had previously taken on implicitly, and to strengthen it as an organization. PFN will take on this role from the unique position of being "the FDE of a product company that develops its own fully domestic LLM."

In other words, while the FDE title itself is new, the posting explicitly states that the working style of "embedding oneself at customer sites to implement solutions" is a style PFN has practiced since its founding. What changed is that it has been carved out as a standalone hiring position. As background, the posting cites the fact that "projects that embed AI agents into the core of business operations, rather than ending at PoC, are rapidly increasing," such as materials discovery simulations, insurance underwriting, manufacturing R&D, and public-facing counter services.

What the job actually involves

The posting lists five main responsibilities.

  • Deep dive into customer operations — Be stationed at or regularly visit customer sites, talking directly with everyone from frontline staff to management to structurally understand business processes, commercial practices, regulations, and tacit knowledge
  • Defining the problems to solve — Work with Business Development staff to identify operations where generative AI can truly deliver value, and drill down into requirements, success metrics, and operational design
  • Designing and implementing AI agents / generative AI applications — Handle technology selection for model choice (including the in-house LLM "PLaMo"), RAG, post-training, agent orchestration, and evaluation infrastructure, and implement them personally
  • Production deployment and continuous improvement — Deploy as systems that meet security and availability requirements, whether on-premises or in the cloud, and improve them while monitoring usage rates, accuracy, data drift, and more
  • Feeding assets back to product / model development teams — Abstract the insights, datasets, and evaluation sets gained in the field and feed them back into in-house model development and products

The stated required qualifications include at least 3 years of experience covering the full software development lifecycle from upstream to downstream, experience as a technical lead on system development using machine learning / LLMs / RAG / AI agents or on enterprise technology projects, and "experience being stationed at or frequently visiting customer sites (expected to spend roughly 50-80% of working time on-site at customers, depending on the project)." What is required is not just technical skill but an aptitude for a working style that involves spending long hours embedded at customer sites.

SoftBank is also recruiting around the same time — a secondment slot to its joint venture with OpenAI

In the same year of 2026, SoftBank also made a move. Its job posting published on May 18, "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) / Secondment to SB OAI Japan G.K.," is a slot for employees to remain employed by SoftBank while being seconded to "SB OAI Japan G.K.," its joint venture with OpenAI. The location section of the posting contains the following sentence.

Location: WeWork Takeshiba (Kaigan, Minato-ku, Tokyo) or other Tokyo offices Customer visits and business trips required (mainly domestic) Business trips to OpenAI headquarters in San Francisco, USA possible

The job summary reads: "Implement cutting-edge AI models into real businesses alongside Japan's leading companies and realize AI-native business transformation." The posting includes a mission, main duties, specific tasks, and application requirements (required / preferred), and what it shares with PFN's posting is the very structure of "embedding oneself inside a specific company to implement solutions."

Findy's feature doesn't include these two companies — it's spreading even wider

We have looked at PFN and SoftBank so far, but this is only the tip of the iceberg. The "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Job Opening Special Feature" published by the job site Findy on March 4, 2026 lists job openings from the following 12 companies, not including these two (in order of appearance).

JDSC, Gen-AX (a 100% SoftBank subsidiary), Sansan, ExaWizards, Loglass, Almondo, Stockmark, ACES, UPGRADE, Algoage, DeNA, LayerX

Findy's article explains that "a Forward Deployed Engineer (hereinafter FDE) is a new engineering position that has been a hot topic since last year, when the social implementation of AI began in earnest" and that "companies in Japan have also started posting more FDE openings." Furthermore, Sakana AI (a Japanese AI company) has also published an FDE recruitment page on its careers page (the job details redirect to an external ATS, so they were not verified for this article), showing that this title is not confined to a specific industry or company.

What is interesting is that JDSC, which appears alongside Findy's feature, is a company that entered into a capital and business alliance with SoftBank Corp. in October 2025 regarding AI agent development (separately from this, Gen-AX is a wholly owned subsidiary of SoftBank Corp. and is not a group company of JDSC). While SoftBank itself recruits FDEs, its capital partners and investees are also recruiting FDEs — a structure in which job postings for the same title are being launched in parallel within the same ecosystem.

Why the concentration in 2026 (including speculation)

What follows is the article's own analysis, not something written in the job postings. The timing of this title's emergence coincides with the point when the PoC (proof of concept) phase of generative AI had run its course and corporate interest shifted to "production deployment." PFN's job posting also explicitly states that "projects that embed AI agents into the core of business operations, rather than ending at PoC, are rapidly increasing," suggesting the background recognition is a shortage of "people who can carry implementation through to completion tailored to the customer's business" rather than model performance itself. However, this is speculation within the range that can be read from the companies' job postings, and is not backed by cross-industry statistics.

Only SoftBank discloses salary; PFN does not

  • Whether Pasona is posting jobs under the FDE title could not be found through searches or direct verification. It is possible they handle "FDE projects" as a staffing and placement business, but this could not be confirmed on Pasona's own recruitment pages
  • Sakana AI's job details page redirects to an external ATS (Workable), and only a Cloudflare verification screen was returned, so the body of the job description could not be verified. What was confirmed extends only to the fact (the existence of the URL) that "the company is also recruiting FDEs"
  • The causal claim that "the PoC phase ran its course and corporate interest shifted to production deployment" is the article's own interpretation based on the wording of PFN's job posting, and has not been verified with industry statistics
  • How salary ranges are handled varies by job posting. SoftBank's (secondment to SB OAI Japan) job posting explicitly states "Monthly salary: ¥523,750–¥999,000" and "Theoretical annual income: ¥8,123,000–¥20,346,200" (re-retrieved and verified on September 4, 2026; a note on the page states that monthly salary = base salary + overtime allowance, and theoretical annual income = monthly salary + bonus + special additional bonus). Meanwhile, PFN's job posting contained no salary information. Sakana AI's posting body itself could not be reached
