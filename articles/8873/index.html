Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer Interview (No Offer): A Palantir recruiter reached out

Taro · 2025-05-01 · 2 min read

Summary

A candidate recounts a rejected Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer application in London. A recruiter reached out on LinkedIn, followed by two technical rounds: an Analysis interview, described as a simpler version of decomposition, and a two-hour Learning & Decomp interview under NDA. There was no LeetCode-style coding stage. The candidate found decomposition thinking fairly difficult and the learning round forgiving of nothing. Aggregate data from three experiences shows a 33% pass rate, mostly neutral impressions, and employee reviews cite smart colleagues and impactful work alongside politics and occasional chaos.

Key points

  • Recruiter initiated contact via LinkedIn, no referral needed.
  • Analysis round was a simpler decomposition-style problem with feedback.
  • Learning & Decomp was a two-hour NDA technical interview.
  • No LeetCode-style coding stage in the process.
  • Reported 33% pass rate across three London interview experiences.

Full text

May 1, 2025

Neutral ExperienceNo Offer

A Palantir recruiter reached out to me on LinkedIn.

I progressed to the next two stages:

  • Analysis (technical - 2nd stage)
  • Learning & decomp (technical - 3rd stage, 2 hours, under NDA) interview.

Analysis was like a simpler version of the decomp interview, with a problem to break down and walk through the solution whilst incorporating the interviewer's feedback. Learning was not very difficult but doesn't allow much room for mistake. Decomposition, to me, was fairly difficult but just because of the type of thinking that it required for the given scenario.

There was no LeetCode-style interview stage.
