TechCamp Launches "TechCamp FDE" to Train the New AI-Era Profession of "FDE" — Forward Deployed Engineers Who Work Closely with Clients to Solve Business Challenges

Original title: テックキャンプ、現場に寄り添い業務課題を解決するAI時代の新職種『FDE』を育成する「テックキャンプ FDE」を提供開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-06-04 · 7 min read

Summary

Japanese education company div (operator of Tech Camp) will launch “Tech Camp FDE” on July 1, 2026, a four-month program training Forward Deployed Engineers. Aimed at business professionals with domain knowledge but weak implementation skills, it pairs each student with an active FDE for weekly one-on-one mentoring. The curriculum covers business process analysis, web development basics, building AI agents with Claude Code, and production deployment with security practices, ending with a one-month simulated AI consulting project for a fictional client. An early-bird cohort of 30 seats filled quickly, mostly with professionals in their 30s–50s. Corporate training options are also offered.

Become a professional who leads organizational business transformation with "front-line expertise × AI implementation skills." Active FDEs provide one-on-one support for 4 months

div Inc. (Headquarters: Minato-ku, Tokyo; CEO: Kei Ishihara; hereinafter "div"), a company operating AI and IT education businesses, is pleased to announce the release of "Tech Camp FDE," a 4-month one-on-one mentoring program that trains "FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers)," a highly anticipated new profession of the AI era who identify organizational business challenges and guide them through to AI implementation and resolution, on July 1, 2026.

In recent years, AI adoption at many companies has been shifting from the "using" stage to a phase of full-scale integration into their own business processes and systems to "drive business transformation." Along with this, demand for professionals who can deeply understand front-line challenges and lead AI implementation is rapidly expanding.

In light of these changes in market needs, div will leverage the AI and IT talent education expertise it has cultivated through operating the IT engineer training school "Tech Camp" and the unlimited generative AI learning school "Tech Camp AI College" to launch a program that enables business professionals with front-line expertise (domain knowledge) to acquire, in 4 months, the skills to directly solve organizational business challenges with AI and become FDEs.

For details about "Tech Camp FDE," please see below

Background of Tech Camp FDE's Launch

FDE: A New Profession of the AI Era Attracting Global Attention

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a new profession of the AI era that deeply understands a company's business challenges and uses AI to carry projects through to implementation and operation.

In May 2026, companies leading the AI era, such as OpenAI and Anthropic, successively established FDE organizations, positioning FDEs as core talent for integrating generative AI into business front lines. This trend has also spread to Japan, where job postings offering annual salaries exceeding 10 million yen have already appeared, mainly at IT companies and startups, and market demand and attention are rapidly rising.

FDEs Solve "AI Adoption" Challenges

Many companies are attempting to introduce AI across their organizations, but significant gaps have emerged on the front lines.

Business-side challenges: They know the front-line challenges well, but lack the "implementation skills" to deploy AI as systems into production environments.

Engineer-side challenges: They have the technology, but lack deep knowledge (domain knowledge) of the business operations where each industry's front lines are truly struggling.

div believes that one solution to this challenge is for professionals with extensive industry experience and specialized knowledge to lead business transformation with AI as FDEs, leveraging their years of accumulated experience. Therefore, we have developed an education program that draws on our past AI and IT education expertise and are offering it as "Tech Camp FDE."

The Advance Enrollment Announcement's Limited 30-Slot Capacity Filled Quickly

Ahead of this service launch, we conducted an advance announcement targeting past "Tech Camp" students and participants in div's various events. Applications came in at a pace far exceeding our expectations from the start of the announcement, and the limited 30 slots filled up quickly.

Most of the enrollees are business professionals in their 30s to 50s who have accumulated industry experience and specialized knowledge, and the program has attracted extremely high attention and expectations from those with challenges such as "wanting to solve chronic labor shortages through AI implementation" and "wanting to drive DX across the entire organization."

Three Features of "Tech Camp FDE"

1. One-on-One Mentoring by Active FDEs

Active FDEs who solve challenges through AI adoption support for major companies and sparring sessions with executives are exclusively assigned to each student, holding weekly 30-minute one-on-one meetings. Through feedback from a professional perspective, students rapidly develop practical-level thinking and skills that are difficult to reach through self-study alone.

2. End-to-End Curriculum from Fundamentals to Implementation and Operation

Going beyond mere tool usage, students systematically learn best practices covering business inventory analysis, IT fundamentals, web application development basics, AI agent implementation using Claude Code, and security-conscious production operation.

3. Experience "FDE Work" Through Simulated AI Consulting Practice

In the final month of the program, students conduct a simulated practicum with a fictional client. By experiencing the full process from business inventory analysis to challenge identification, prototype building, and improvement proposals, students are brought up to a level where they can "execute AI implementation for an organization."

Skills Acquired Through This Program

Students acquire five skills to go from no experience to becoming "a person who can solve organizational business challenges with AI."

  • Fundamental knowledge of computers and web application development: Systematically acquire the foundational knowledge needed to give AI accurate instructions and reflect them in production environments.
  • Business inventory analysis skills: A methodology for structurally breaking down any business process and identifying the points that should be rebuilt with AI.
  • Skills to implement automation tools using AI agents: The ability to implement custom tools optimized for business operations using Claude Code.
  • Skills to launch and operate systems in production: The ability to introduce AI as an organization's business infrastructure, including security and operations.
  • Ability to solve business challenges using AI: The ability to deliver value end-to-end, from challenge identification and solution design to prototype building and improvement proposals.

Learning Curriculum

The curriculum consists of a 3-month skill acquisition part in the first half and a 1-month simulated practicum part in the second half, allowing students to systematically acquire the skills required of an FDE.
