Google Cloud, AWS, and Anthropic—which one has the strongest FDE?

Original title: Google Cloud、AWS和Anthropic，哪家FDE强？

Google Cloud is betting big on Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), and the money is going to channel partners rather than its own hires. In Google's view, no other company in the market is playing this game—AWS, Anthropic, and Microsoft are all taking a different approach.

Samrah Khan, Google's Director of Partnerships for Global and Regional System Integrators, attributes the difference to three things: the $750 million FDE partner investment, the full-stack AI strategy, and the fast-growing cloud AI business. When asked why channel partners should choose Google Cloud's FDE strategy over those of AWS, Anthropic, and Microsoft, her answer was blunt: "I'm not sure Anthropic and those new frontier AI companies have Google's scale, capital, or commitment to do this."

As for the other hyperscalers, her answer was even more direct: "Just look at our Q3 sales growth (103% year over year). That's more than double the growth rate of any other hyperscaler."

In Q3 2026, Google Cloud sales grew 103% year over year to $30.7 billion, a record. Over the same period, AWS grew 39%, and Microsoft Azure grew 48% to $33.2 billion.

Khan also cited two figures: 80% of Google Cloud customers are also using Google's AI products; among Google's Fortune 100 customers, that figure is 90%. "The adoption speed we're seeing across all industries—and I'm talking even about banks, state and local governments, law firms, and healthcare, which are typically slow-moving, regulated industries—they're moving very fast on adopting Google AI," she said. "That's another reason our partner FDE opportunity is unique."

What the $750 Million Buys

This year, Google is putting $750 million into channel partners to help them train, build, and scale their own FDE businesses and teams. The money is broken into several pieces: channel incentives around sales engineering, AI proofs of concept (POCs), and solutions development; co-selling alongside Google's own FDE teams; free training to turn employees into qualified FDEs; and a partner program created specifically for FDEs, the "Gemini Enterprise Transformation" (GET) program.

Ben Kessler, CEO of Google Cloud AI partner 66degrees, believes that when it comes to an FDE strategy aimed at channel partners, Google sits at the top of the pyramid.

"Google is putting $750 million into partners' FDEs, while some other hyperscalers or model companies hire their own FDEs," Kessler said. "Specifically, Google is making a big bet on a partner FDE program, whereas Anthropic and OpenAI have chosen to invest in their own FDE teams and businesses. So Google is truly going all-in on the partner route—which means, with this $750 million Gemini Enterprise Transformation program, it will push it out to our FDEs."

Full Stack: The Combination No One Else Can Offer

In Khan's view, another key to Google's FDE strategy winning is that it can offer a genuinely complete AI technology stack.

"In the market, for system integrators and customers, we are the only partner with a unified full stack from chips all the way up to agents and applications," she said. "Whether you're talking about models, the runtime harness, the data stack, infrastructure, or chips. We're also the only vendor in the market that keeps research, building, development, and deployment all in-house. This is also the same stack that Google and Alphabet run themselves." She emphasized that Google's sole focus is helping every customer become an agentic enterprise.

Kessler put it more concretely: Google's approach is to build chips first, then data centers, then the agentic data stack, then AI models; not just models, but also an agent development kit for building custom agents, then monitoring, and finally Gemini Enterprise as the interface layer. "Nobody else has this."

In his view, OpenAI and Anthropic do a great job on models and interfaces, but lack the rest of the data and AI stack; AWS and Microsoft Azure hold the bottom of the AI stack, but don't necessarily have large models, nor an interface like Gemini Enterprise. "Google is the only hyperscaler that brings everything together."

The GET Program: Turning Partners' People into Certified FDEs

Google's new GET program focuses on training and empowering channel partners to expand their FDE headcount while bringing new customer opportunities. It aligns Google partners that are building FDE practices with major accounts, with the goal of moving enterprises from basic AI pilots to scaled, production-ready agentic AI operations.

"The GET program's partner funding mechanism has really grown our business," Kessler said. "It lets Google take Gemini Enterprise, Vertex, and other AI capabilities to market, and it lets us genuinely build AI into our customers' workflows."

By design, the program deploys Google-trained FDEs directly alongside enterprise customers and partners to jointly build, tune, and put custom AI agents into operation. Khan said Google will invest in partners through the sales engineering funding process, proofs of concept, and building minimum viable products (MVPs) for customers; will fund partners to develop Gemini-powered, industry-specific platforms; and will provide dedicated training, "making sure we get their people certified as Google FDEs and Google Agentic AI certified personnel."

The investment is already paying off at 66degrees. Kessler said roughly 20% of the company's employees are now FDEs. "Because we bet on Google's FDE investment, we can now genuinely turn Google's AI stack into business outcomes."
