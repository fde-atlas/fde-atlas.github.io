Hakuhodo Launches "DATA GEAR for AI-Transformation," an FDE-Type Consulting Service That Co-Creates AI Agents with Client Frontline Employees to Support AI-Driven Organizational Transformation

Original title: 博報堂、クライアント現場社員とAIエージェントを共作しAI-Drivenな組織変革を支援するFDE型コンサルティングサービス「DATA GEAR for AI-Transformation」の提供を開始｜ニュースリリース

博報堂 Hakuhodo Inc. · 2026-10-26 · 5 min read

Summary

Hakuhodo has launched DATA GEAR for AI-Transformation, an FDE-style consulting service in which specialist teams embed in client workplaces and co-build AI agents with frontline employees rather than delivering finished tools. The service spans three areas—people, processes, and organization—and uses a five-stage maturity model for AI adoption, from awareness to employees recommending AI to others. It complements DATA GEAR for AI-Ready, offered since June 2025, and aims to leave clients with staff who can continuously use, build, and improve AI themselves, plus guidelines defining what AI can do autonomously versus what requires human judgment.

Full text

Hakuhodo Inc. (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; President and CEO: Kenji Nagura; hereinafter Hakuhodo) has launched "DATA GEAR for AI-Transformation," an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)-style consulting service in which a specialized team embeds itself in clients' business operations and co-creates AI agents together with clients' frontline employees to transform people, work, and organizations around AI. Rather than developing and delivering AI agents itself, Hakuhodo works alongside the client employees who understand the work best, practicing everything from identifying tasks where AI can be used to creating, implementing, operating, and improving AI agents. Through this process, it transforms "people," "work," and "organization" together in an AI-Driven way, supporting organizational change that enables frontline employees to continuously transform their work by using, building, and fixing AI themselves.

■ Background of Development While the adoption of generative AI platforms for enterprises is advancing, actual usage often remains limited to individual-level applications such as summarizing meeting minutes or drafting documents. Challenges have also emerged, such as "not knowing where in our work to use it," "usage rules being unclear and hindering adoption," and "AI tools created by individuals not being retained as organizational assets." Meanwhile, advances in generative AI have dramatically lowered the barrier to creating AI agents. Even without specialized development knowledge, business owners themselves are increasingly able to set natural-language instructions and reference materials and build AI agents tailored to their own work. In light of these changes, Hakuhodo believes it is essential that frontline employees, who understand the work best, think about how to use AI together with AI experts and improve it while embedding it into actual operations. Going beyond simply introducing AI tools and training, Hakuhodo transforms work processes, people's roles, and organizational structures as a whole through on-site co-creation, creating a state in which employees themselves can continuously transform their work by using, building, and fixing AI. In addition, since June 2025, Hakuhodo has offered "DATA GEAR for AI-Ready," which organizes the processes, data, and organization of individual tasks into forms that can be delegated to AI. "DATA GEAR for AI-Ready" rebuilds individual tasks around AI, and this service spreads throughout the organization the practice of employees using, building, and fixing AI themselves, supporting "AI-Transformation" in which people, work, and organizations continuously change with AI as the premise.

■ The "AI-Driven" State This Service Aims For This service assesses an organization's AI usage across a five-level maturity scale, supporting the transition from a state where AI remains merely a convenient personal tool to one where the way work is done itself changes.

■ The Service's Three Support Areas Through FDE-style on-site accompaniment, it supports the three areas of "people, work, and organization" as a whole. Starting from co-creating AI agents with frontline employees, it consistently supports the development of talent who can use and build AI, the design of work processes incorporating AI, and the establishment of mechanisms to continuously leverage AI agents and know-how as organizational assets.

■ Features of This Service ① FDE-Style Support That Embeds On-Site and Co-Creates with Employees

Hakuhodo's specialized team embeds in companies' business operations and co-creates AI agents with frontline employees. Support is provided not only by engineers but also by consultants who understand the business and planners who move organizations, so in addition to systems that actually work, it is possible to leave behind people who can build systems and highly reproducible frameworks, enabling employees to continue using and improving AI themselves even after the engagement ends.

② Guidelines That Clarify What Can Be Delegated to AI, Not Just "Prohibitions" Rather than merely defining prohibited actions, the guidelines organize, for each task, "what AI can execute on its own," "what requires human confirmation before execution," and "what humans should decide," promoting AI usage while ensuring safety.

③ Designing Adoption as a Communication Challenge Viewing the adoption of AI usage as internal behavioral change, the service designs measures tailored to usage conditions, supporting continuous use and organizational penetration.

[The Five Stages of AI Usage and Examples of Concrete Measures] 1) Awareness (knows it exists and its scope of use) ⇒ Disseminate via management messages and internal communications 2) First use (has used it at work at least once) ⇒ Eliminate initial barriers through department-specific training, template distribution, etc. 3) Continued use (uses it at least once a week) ⇒ Indicate where to use it and embed it into work flows, etc. 4) Proactive use (builds AI agents themselves and uses them for decisions) ⇒ Support co-creation and in-house development through workshops that deepen inquiry and ambassador development 5) Advocacy (teaches others) ⇒ Build communities and provide opportunities for recognition and presentations

Going forward, as part of the activities of HCAI Professionals, the Hakuhodo DY Group's cross-organizational collective of AI experts, Hakuhodo will continue supporting client companies as an accompanying partner through its data/AI utilization solution suite "DATA GEAR," helping them connect generative AI to actual business results.
