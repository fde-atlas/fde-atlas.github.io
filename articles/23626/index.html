Founding Forward Deployed Engineer | Netter | Paris, France

Zero G Talent · 2026-11-18 · 2 min read

Summary

Netter, a Paris-based startup that centralizes mid-market data for analytics, workflows and ML pipelines, is hiring a founding Forward Deployed Engineer paying €60,000–100,000 plus equity. The role owns customer deployments end to end: defining problems with client leadership, preparing data on the Netter platform, deploying models to production, and converting learnings into reusable product. Candidates need strong Python and SQL skills, shipped data or ML projects, and comfort leading senior client conversations. Preferred backgrounds include Palantir, BCG X or consulting, plus experience with APIs, data pipelines, applied ML, or healthcare and retail data. The hire will also build the company's FDE playbook and help recruit and mentor future engineers.

Key points

  • Netter is hiring a founding FDE in Paris at €60–100k plus equity.
  • The role covers deployments end to end, from discovery to production rollout.
  • Requirements: Python, SQL, and shipped data or ML projects for real users.
  • The engineer will build the FDE playbook and help hire and mentor future FDEs.
  • Palantir, BCG X or consulting experience is preferred but not required.

Data & AI deployment | Paris
Full-time | Founding team | €60–100k + equity

Your role

  • Own customer deployments end to end, from discovery to rollout. Work directly with business teams and their leadership to define the problem and what success looks like.
  • Prepare and connect their data on Netter, build and deploy models in production, and help users become autonomous on the platform.
  • Turn customer learnings into reusable product and shape the roadmap with the founders.
  • Build the FDE playbook at Netter, then help hire and mentor the next FDEs.

Must have

  • You enjoy working at the intersection of technology and business. A background in engineering, analytics or a programme like X-HEC Data Science for Business is a good fit.
  • You’re fluent in Python and SQL and have shipped data or ML projects to real users.
  • You enjoy figuring out why something isn’t working and building a better solution.
  • You’re comfortable leading a conversation with senior clients, explaining trade-offs and agreeing on next steps.
  • You want early-stage ownership and are happy to take on whatever the company needs.

Nice to have

  • Experience at Palantir, BCG X or a consulting firm where you worked directly with clients on data or technology projects.
  • A tool you built for someone else, whether in a startup, a side project or on your own.
  • Experience with APIs, data pipelines, applied ML, or healthcare and retail data.

Not a fit

  • You’d prefer a purely internal role, or a clearly defined scope with established processes.

Compensation

  • €60,000–100,000 per year + founding equity, depending on profile.

About Netter

Netter centralizes and structures traditional mid-market data to deploy analytics, workflows and ML pipelines. - A nursing home group was chasing unpaid invoices manually across disconnected tools. Netter centralized the collection workflows, automated reminders, real-time visibility, task routing. - A logistics operator had trucks running empty. Netter connected their TMS, ERP and fleet data, and automated route and load optimization. Less fuel, better margins. - A retail chain had years of loyalty card data sitting unused. Netter cross-referenced it with public demographic data to build precise customer segments and trigger the right promotions to the right people.
