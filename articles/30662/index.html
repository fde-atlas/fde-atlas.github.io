What does an FDE do? IIInstitute for Information Industry whitepaper highlights AI deployment and communication needs

Summary

Taiwan's Institute for Information Industry (III) and 1111 Job Bank released the "2027 Future Talent White Paper", positioning the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) as a key talent development focus. The role combines requirements analysis, AI solution design, system deployment, and client communication. The article cites real job postings from Hon Hai and OpenAI's Tokyo office to illustrate duties and hiring requirements, and suggests structuring a portfolio around requirements, implementation, testing, and handoff. III plans to launch FDE training and competency assessments starting in 2027, though this remains a plan.

Key points

  • III's white paper names FDE a talent development focus.
  • Hon Hai's posting covers deployment, integration, troubleshooting, and training.
  • OpenAI's Tokyo role requires five years' experience and Japanese-English fluency.
  • Portfolios should cover requirements, implementation, testing, and handoff.
  • FDE training and assessments are planned for 2027, not yet launched.

Image / Media Corner Di Jia

(Media Corner Di Jia / Lin Cheng-chih comprehensive report) On November 23, the Institute for Information Industry (III) and 1111 Job Bank released the "2027 Future Talent White Paper," listing the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) as a talent development focus. This role requires going into enterprises to clarify requirements, integrate systems, and assist with launch, as well as explaining and delivering to users. For those preparing a career change, a skills comparison can start from real job postings: Hon Hai lists proposal-to-launch, troubleshooting, and training; overseas positions have additional requirements for engineering experience and language skills. III announcement.

Key information

  • What an FDE does: Forward Deployed Engineer, combining requirements clarification, technical integration, deployment, and client communication.
  • Taiwan job example: Hon Hai's official job listing includes FDE roles covering application deployment, solution delivery, technical support, and training.
  • Job search preparation: Use one hands-on project to present the requirements, your engineering work, test results, and handover approach, then compare against each job's requirements.
  • Training timeline: The III plans to launch FDE training and competency assessments starting in 2027.

From requirements to launch, the FDE brings systems into the workplace

The III explained that its FDE competency research collected international job postings, industry research, and practical data, then used expert surveys and interviews to map out the capabilities needed for requirements analysis, AI solution design, system deployment, and application adoption. This release also included AI product managers and AI governance in the role discussions, covering product requirements integration and application standards and risk management, respectively. III Institute for Digital Education announcement.

Deployment work involves very concrete questions: which part of operations to support, where the data comes from, how to connect to existing systems, and who can operate it after delivery. An FDE job description can be read along this work path.

In Hon Hai's official job listing, the FDE position located in Taiwan with code PP26081700010 requires deploying enterprise mobile app services to customer sites, completing setup, integration, and management; it also requires defining usage scenarios with clients, advancing from proposal, demonstration, and trial all the way to formal launch.

The same position also lists problem diagnosis, training, and standard operating procedure creation, and requires coordination between clients and platform R&D teams. This job includes the delivery that comes after writing features: who users turn to when they encounter problems, how they learn to operate the system, and how the R&D team receives feedback are all within the responsibilities.

Portfolio preparation starts with one workflow, keeping test and handover records

To present these capabilities with a portfolio, choose a project you have done that matches a job posting, and organize it into four parts: requirements, implementation, testing, and delivery. For requirements, clearly state who encountered what problem; for implementation, explain which code, data, or system integrations you were responsible for; for testing, keep both successful and failed examples; for delivery, attach operating instructions, known limitations, and follow-up handling.

For example, to practice with a "company document search assistant," you can use public or self-made data to show how the data was organized, how answers link back to sources, and how unanswered queries are handed to a human. Test records can also capture wrong answers, outdated data, or insufficient permissions, explaining how you found and fixed them. This is one way to prepare a portfolio; the actual topic can be replaced with a business process matching your target company.

Those with software development experience can add requirements discussions, deployment, and operations records; those familiar with business processes can first compare against job requirements to identify which engineering implementations they lack, then plan their learning. When organizing your resume, you can also refer to this site's portfolio and work record organization method to align your personal contributions, revision process, and results.

Overseas FDE roles have additional experience and language requirements; check each item before applying

OpenAI's Tokyo FDE full-time position lists the scope of work as requirements exploration, technical scoping, system design, development, and formal deployment, with results measured by actual adoption, workflow impact, and eval feedback.

This Tokyo position requires five or more years of engineering or technical deployment experience, including customer-facing work, and the ability to write and review production code in Python, JavaScript, or similar technologies. Language requirements include fluent Japanese and English, an English resume, and interviews conducted in both languages; the work arrangement includes a Tokyo office and travel mainly within Japan.

Therefore, when selecting positions, you can separately check "what to deliver, required engineering skills, work location and language, and on-site support arrangements." In interviews, you can further ask: which part of the first project you will be responsible for after joining, who decides acceptance criteria, who checks permissions and test results before launch, and how handover works after errors occur. These answers help you judge how much of the work your experience can handle.

The 2027 training is still in planning; what information is required to download the white paper?

The III's announced timeline is to launch FDE training and competency assessments starting in 2027. Those preparing to enroll can first organize the skills they lack based on target positions, then compare hands-on content, assessment methods, fees, and eligibility once courses are announced. III training plan.

The official download page for the "2027 Future Talent White Paper" indicates free download; the process requires reading a personal data consent form and filling in your name, organization, and job title; the disclosure includes sending industry or event information. Before obtaining the full text, you can first read the data usage terms and rights explanation listed on the page, then decide whether to provide your information.

If you are organizing your existing learning outcomes, this site also has a report on the III AI competency passport and resume skill recognition. For those preparing to apply for FDE roles, what can be done right now is to pick one job posting and match each required deliverable to your portfolio; for parts without implementation records, add them to your next practice or learning plan.
