Introducing Nitro: Rocketlane's Agentic AI PSA platform

Rocketlane · 2026-04-02 · 5 min read

Summary

Rocketlane introduces Nitro, an agentic AI PSA platform aimed at professional services delivery. The post argues delivery inefficiency is an execution-model problem, not a talent problem, and proposes giving every team member AI agents with full project and customer context. Nitro includes Documentation, Configuration, Migration, and Resourcing Agents, plus Project and Account Signals for risk detection and Nitro Analyst for decision support. Consultants shift from doing setup work to reviewing and approving agent output. Rocketlane reports teams using Migration Agents spend 50% less time per migration.

Key points

  • Nitro is Rocketlane's agentic AI platform for PSA delivery.
  • Core claim: execution model, not talent, causes delivery inefficiency.
  • Documentation Agents turn customer calls into traceable structured docs instantly.
  • Configuration and Migration Agents handle setup and data migration; humans verify.
  • Migration Agents reportedly cut migration time by 50%.

You've hired great people. You've built a team that knows how to deliver.

And yet, somewhere between the kickoff call and the go-live, the best people on your team are spending their nights on field mapping, their mornings rebuilding decks they've built before, and their weekends hoping a migration script doesn't break everything.

That's not a talent problem. That's an execution model problem.

And the answer isn't to hire more. The answer is to change what execution depends on.

Nitro gives every person on your team a virtual teammate, one with full context across every project, every customer, and every decision.

Agents that run the delivery, from planning to execution. So your team stops doing work that shouldn't need them, and starts doing the work only they can do.

The project plan is now where your team and agents work side by side. And for agents to work well, they need the right context. That's where it starts.

Think about how much time you spend after every customer call, cleaning up notes, making decisions, and making sure nothing slips through. Now imagine that work happens the moment the call ends.

Documentation Agents turn every conversation into a rich, structured document — instantly. It keeps evolving as new information comes in. Every section is reviewable.

Every answer is traceable to its source. No more copy-pasting transcripts. No more "I think we covered this on the call."

Every new customer means the same setup, over and over. Tenants created. Roles assigned. Environments configured. It's necessary work, but it shouldn't need your best people.

Configuration Agents handle the setup. You review, approve, and watch it go live. Your consultant's job shifts from doing to verifying, which is where their judgment actually belongs.

One bad transformation script can put a go-live at risk.

Migration Agents handle the mapping, the transformation, and flag anything that looks off. Resolving conflicts is a conversation, not a debugging session.

Your data is ready. Your specialist is rested. The go-live holds. Teams using Migration Agents report 50% less time spent per migration.

Steering committees, escalations, QBRs, your team is in these conversations every week.

Nitro Assistants travel with them through every customer interaction, making sure they have everything they need, right there.

No prep sessions. No hunting for the right slide. Just showing up ready.

Every project has signals. A milestone slipping. A blocker is sitting unresolved. A customer interaction that needs a follow-up.

Project Signals gathers all of it — surfacing risks before they escalate and opportunities as they come up, with the full story behind every signal.

Your team stays on top of every project without chasing status updates or waiting for someone to raise a hand.

Delivery problems rarely announce themselves early.

Account Signals continuously gathers account intelligence, surfacing risks before they become escalations and spotting expansion signals as they emerge.

Every signal comes with the full story behind it. Not just a flag. Context.

The problem was never a lack of data. It was data living in too many places, arriving too late. Nitro Analyst moves from what's happening → why it's happening → how to act on it. Instantly.

Built for the execs and analysts who make decisions — not for people who manage dashboards.

Time policies run automatically. Exceptions come to you — not the other way around.

Delivery guardrails flag conflicts before they become escalations. Your playbooks don't live in people's heads anymore. They run in the system.

The most expensive delivery problem often starts before delivery does. Resourcing Agents analyze upcoming deals and flag staffing gaps early.

When a deal is ready to kick off, you know exactly when to staff, so delivery starts on time, every time.

Operations are running without the admin tax. Leaders are always ahead. Teams, driving execution with virtual teammates by their side.

That's how you unlock radical efficiency. With Rocketlane Nitro.

Your next implementation doesn't have to start the same way as the last one.
