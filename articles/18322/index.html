Summary

Embedding in customers' workplaces to provide end-to-end support from AI-driven business transformation to business results

Brave Studio Inc. (Head office: Matsue City, Shimane Prefecture; Representative Director: Junki Nishimura; hereinafter "Brave Studio"), a group company of bravesoft Inc. (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director: Eiji Sugasawa; slogan: "Making Tomorrow Interesting"; hereinafter "bravesoft"), has newly launched its "AI FDE (Forward Deployed Engineering)" business, supporting companies' AI adoption from the ground up.

With the rapid evolution of AI and generative AI, the barriers to AI adoption in companies have dropped significantly. At the same time, challenges have become apparent, such as: "We introduced AI tools like ChatGPT or Gemini, but they haven't led to concrete business improvements," "AI usage is left up to individual employees," and "The costs of AI adoption don't show up in business results like revenue or profit."

The AI FDE service launched by Brave Studio is not simply about introducing and delivering AI tools or systems. It is a new form of development and support that embeds engineers in customers' workplaces and provides end-to-end assistance, from identifying business challenges to implementing AI-powered mechanisms, ensuring adoption in the field, and improving KPIs.

bravesoft supports this initiative as a group, combining the expertise it has built in app and web development, UI/UX design, and DX support with Brave Studio's development capabilities and on-site support strength, helping companies shift from "introducing AI" to "generating business results with AI."

What is AI FDE (Forward Deployed Engineering)?

Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) literally means "frontline-deployed engineering."

Unlike typical outsourced development, where systems are developed externally and simply delivered, this approach has engineers embed themselves in customers' workplaces, identify challenges from the customer's perspective, and provide support through solution design, implementation, and operation.

Brave Studio's AI FDE provides end-to-end support across the following process.

1. Identifying challenges

We gain a real understanding of on-site operations and workflows to identify bottlenecks that AI can improve.

2. Designing AI utilization

We organize "which tasks should be delegated to AI" and "where AI can be integrated for maximum effect," and design concrete solutions.

3. Implementation and development

We build systems and business processes incorporating AI and generative AI tailored to the challenges, implementing them to a state where they can actually be used in the field.

4. Ensuring adoption in the field

We improve operational methods together with the employees and field staff who will actually use them, preventing the "introduced but never used" situation.

5. Improving KPIs and business results

We don't stop at business efficiency as the goal; we verify whether improvements connect to the KPIs companies should truly pursue—revenue, profit, productivity—and carry out continuous improvement.

From "writing code and delivering" to "delivering results in the field"

What Brave Studio prioritizes in AI FDE is not "what was developed," but "how the developed solutions changed the customer's business."

By delegating tasks to AI, we create environments where people can focus on high-value work such as communication and decision-making.

By starting small in a specific business area and expanding step by step while verifying results, even companies with the following concerns can move forward while keeping risk low.

  • We introduced AI but don't know how to use it
  • It hasn't led to business efficiency or increased revenue and profit
  • We have no in-house talent to drive AI adoption

Against these corporate challenges, Brave Studio's AI FDE embeds in the workplace and provides support from challenge identification through implementation, adoption, and results creation.

Developing and providing its own AI products — expertise as a "creator of AI," not just a "user of AI"

In addition to supporting other companies' AI adoption through AI FDE, Brave Studio also develops and provides its own AI product, "HandoffAI".

Brave Studio's strength in offering AI FDE lies in being an engineering organization with experience not only "using" AI tools but also "developing and operating" products that incorporate AI. This allows the company to support customers with an understanding not only of on-site operations but also of AI model characteristics, accuracy, and the pitfalls of real-world operation.
