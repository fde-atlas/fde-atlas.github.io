Cloudflare Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-05 · 13 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot counts 9 Cloudflare FDE-family postings, all engineer titles, tied for 12th among 114 employers with Chalk, Saronic and Snowflake. Roles span four tracks: base FDE, Professional Services FDE (Munich and London), Senior FDE, and Senior Forward Deployed Infrastructure Engineer. Seven are labeled Hybrid and two Distributed; none are labeled Remote. The base FDE seat asks for 10+ years of technical customer-facing experience, Senior seats 7+ years of software engineering, with TypeScript, Rust and Go plus heavy Terraform emphasis. A November 5 live check found the London Senior seat closed and 8 still open. Posted pay includes $184,000–$253,000 for the New York base FDE, $194,000–$266,000 for Bay Area Senior seats, and €123,000–€169,000 in Munich.

Key points

  • Cloudflare has 9 FDE-family postings across four engineer tracks, tied 12th of 114 employers.
  • Seven seats are Hybrid, two Distributed (Munich, London); none labeled Remote.
  • Base FDE requires 10+ years customer-facing experience; Senior seats require 7+ years engineering.
  • Core skills are Terraform, TypeScript, Rust and Go; Infrastructure adds SRE and Kubernetes exposure.
  • Posted pay: $184k–$253k NYC base FDE, $194k–$266k Bay Area Senior, €123k–€169k Munich.

What Cloudflare calls the role

Cloudflare posts under four engineer title tracks in this snapshot: Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE); Forward Deployed Engineer, Professional Services; Senior Forward Deployed Engineer; and Senior Forward Deployed Infrastructure Engineer. Greenhouse departments split between Professional Services (base FDE and both Professional Services seats) and Developer Relations (the Senior and Infrastructure seats).

The base seat is Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) (Hybrid; Atlanta, Austin, Dallas, New York). Professional Services covers Forward Deployed Engineer, Professional Services in Munich (Distributed) and Forward Deployed Engineer, Professional Services in London (Distributed). Senior Forward Deployed Engineer covers Hybrid seats in Austin, Sydney, Sweden and San Francisco, plus a London Senior seat that closed on the November 5 live check. Senior Forward Deployed Infrastructure Engineer is Hybrid San Francisco; the listing also names Austin (Hybrid | Austin, San Francisco).

What the engineer postings ask for

Years differ by seat. Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) states 10+ Years of Technical Customer Facing Experience. Senior Sydney, Senior Sweden and Senior San Francisco each state 7+ years of professional software engineering experience. Senior Austin states 7+ years of professional software engineering experience, including time in a customer-facing engineering role — such as forward deployed, solutions or sales engineering, professional services, or developer relations. Both Professional Services seats state In Depth Experience in a technical customer-facing role such as Solutions Architect or Technical Account Manager with no numeric years line. The Infrastructure seat has no numeric years line.

Senior Sydney, Sweden and San Francisco name TypeScript, Rust, Golang. Austin Senior names TypeScript, Rust, and Go. Those Senior texts also include the phrase not just advising on them inside the production-systems line. Infrastructure asks for Go, Python, or Rust with Terraform, Ansible, Salt and Exposure to Kubernetes or similar platforms and workload scheduling on newly provisioned capacity.

Base FDE states Terraform Proficiency: Extensive experience implementing and supporting complex technical solutions using Terraform for infrastructure management. and asks you to Architect Application Security: Design and configure comprehensive, tailored solutions with a focus on Application Security, utilizing Cloudflare Workers to optimize and direct global traffic and Network Security. Both Professional Services seats call for Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) to ensure operational stability for customers.

The full skill counts across employers are on Forward Deployed Engineer skills.

Infrastructure track

Senior Forward Deployed Infrastructure Engineer sits beside the Senior FDE seats under Developer Relations. The text opens the requirements with Infrastructure & SRE Background: Solid experience in infrastructure engineering, SRE, or production engineering, with a track record of running and scaling large-scale production systems. Languages and tooling are Go, Python or Rust; Terraform, Ansible and Salt; and Kubernetes exposure. Available locations on the live text read Hybrid | Austin, San Francisco. Posted Bay Area pay matches the San Francisco Senior FDE band.

Professional Services Distributed seats

The two Professional Services seats are Distributed Munich (Greenhouse 8043305) and Distributed London (Greenhouse 8043339), next to the Hybrid Developer Relations Senior seats. The Munich live text states Available Location Germany and the London live text states Available Location UK; neither text defines Distributed. Neither states a numeric years line. Both use the Solutions Architect / Technical Account Manager experience line and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform). Munich posts a euro salary band that includes on-target incentive under the Sales Compensation Plan. London Professional Services posts no numeric salary line on the snapshot or the November 5 text.

Equity and export-control lines

Six of the eight live November 5 texts state equity eligibility, for example This role is eligible to participate in Cloudflare’s equity plan. (Munich uses a straight apostrophe in the same sentence). London Professional Services and Sydney Senior texts do not. No grant sizes are stated.

Every live text includes U.S. export control / Export Administration Regulations language: offer of employment may be conditioned on your authorization to receive software or technology controlled under these U.S. export laws. That is export-control authorization language. The eight live texts do not state a visa sponsorship stance. All eight are silent on a degree line. Four of nine snapshot rows have travel_mentioned true; travel_pct_stated is null on all nine. Travel percentage is not stated. Employer travel figures are on how much forward deployed engineers travel.

What this means if you are preparing for a Cloudflare FDE loop

  • Pick the track by the work. Base FDE and Professional Services lean on Terraform, Infrastructure as Code and customer-facing architecture. Senior FDE seats emphasize production software engineering with TypeScript, Rust and Go. Infrastructure asks for SRE and capacity work beside those languages.
  • Match the years line to the seat. Base FDE asks for 10+ years of technical customer-facing experience. Senior seats ask for 7+ years of professional software engineering. Professional Services and Infrastructure use experience language without a years number.
  • Read Hybrid and Distributed as Greenhouse workplace labels. None of the nine are labeled Remote under the locations-index method used on this site.
  • Check which currency the pay line uses. Snapshot bands appear in USD and EUR; Sweden posts SEK on the live board; several seats post no numeric range.
