FDE Policy Compilation (Continuously Updated)

Original title: FDE相关政策汇编（持续更新）

圣路可 Fde · 2026-05-08 · 2 min read

Summary

This page is a continuously updated compilation of Chinese government policies referencing Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs). Its centerpiece is an August 2026 notice from the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) encouraging AI application service providers to build FDE teams that work on-site with customers, with targets of over 2,000 providers in a national resource pool by end-2026 and at least 3,000 by end-2027. The article also lists the State Council's "AI+" action plan, a May 2026 inter-agency opinion on AI agents, and Shanghai's September 2026 FDE training program, and explains what each policy means for companies, service providers, and individuals.

Key points

  • MIIT's August 2026 notice explicitly encourages service providers to build on-site FDE teams.
  • National provider resource pool targets: 2,000+ by end-2026, 3,000+ by end-2027.
  • State Council targets 70% adoption of AI terminals and agents by 2027, 90% by 2030.
  • Shanghai's 2026 FDE training program targets state firms, transitioning engineers, and universities.
  • No unified subsidies or certifications for individuals yet; local rules will decide.

National ministry documents have directly named the Forward Deployed Engineer: an August 2026 notice from the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology encourages service providers to build Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) teams that are rooted at customer sites to ensure scenarios land successfully.

Policy Overview

DateDocumentKey points related to FDEs
August 2025State Council, "Opinions on Deeply Implementing the 'AI+' Action" (Guo Fa [2025] No. 11)By 2027, the adoption rate of new-generation smart terminals, AI agents, and similar applications is to exceed 70%, and exceed 90% by 2030
May 2026Cyberspace Administration of China, National Development and Reform Commission, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, "Implementation Opinions on the Standardized Application and Innovative Development of AI Agents"Lists 19 typical application scenarios; proposes cultivating interdisciplinary AI agent talent and developing specialized AI agent technology transfer service organizations
August 2026General Office of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, "Notice on Launching the Special Action to Cultivate AI Application Service Providers" (Gong Xin Ting Ke Han [2026] No. 414)By the end of 2026, the service provider resource pool is to exceed 2,000 providers, and no fewer than 3,000 by the end of 2027; encourages service providers to build Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) teams that are rooted at customer sites to ensure scenarios land successfully
September 2026Shanghai's "3+3+3" Forward Deployed Engineer Training ProgramTargets three groups: leaders of state-owned enterprises and government departments, existing engineers transitioning to FDEs, and universities

What This Means for Different Readers

  • Enterprises: When selecting an AI application service provider, you can use "whether they have an on-site FDE team" as an evaluation criterion.
  • AI application service providers: Local authorities will survey local service providers, establish records, and consolidate them into a national service provider resource pool; building FDE teams aligns with this cultivation direction. For specific submission procedures, refer to notices from your local industry and information technology department.
  • Individuals: Shanghai's training program covers groups such as existing engineers transitioning to FDEs, indicating that this capability is being incorporated into talent development systems.

This page is updated continuously as new documents are released; the original policy texts on the issuing authorities' official websites prevail.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enter the AI application service provider resource pool?

The MIIT notice requires each region to survey local service providers, establish records, and then consolidate them into a national resource pool. For specific submission procedures and criteria, refer to notices from the industry and information technology department of your province or city.

Are there subsidies or certifications for individual FDEs under these policies?

The MIIT notice targets service providers, focusing on the resource pool, service capabilities, and support guarantees. For individual-level training, certification, or subsidies, refer to local supporting documents, such as Shanghai's training program.
