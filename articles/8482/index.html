What's the difference between FDE and staff augmentation or outsourcing

Original title: FDE 与人力增补、外包有什么区别

Onyx Devs Lab · 2026-09-15 · 2 min read

Summary

Onyx Devs Lab distinguishes FDE work from staff augmentation and outsourcing. In staff augmentation, the client owns priorities, architecture, and outcomes while the vendor supplies capacity; outsourcing delivers against a fixed, agreed scope. FDE instead takes responsibility for a bounded operational problem, continuously linking on-site learning, implementation, deployment, and evidence of results. The author argues that job titles or on-site presence do not determine the model; teams should choose based on where responsibility sits, learning cadence, and exit conditions, starting from the business decision, baseline, constraints, and failure consequences. Fixed commercial scopes can work for limited phases, but evidence gates, change rules, and stop conditions must be explicit, and FDE should transition to stable delivery once uncertainty drops.

Key points

  • Staff augmentation supplies capacity; clients own priorities, architecture, and acceptance
  • Outsourcing delivers a predefined scope with milestones and change processes
  • FDE owns a bounded operational problem from diagnosis to result evidence
  • Titles and on-site presence alone do not determine the engagement model
  • Once uncertainty falls, FDE should shift to stable product delivery or internal ownership
