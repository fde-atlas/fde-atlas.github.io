Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer Interview (No Offer): Phone screen with recruiter, then

Taro · 2025-08-12 · 2 min read

Summary

A candidate shares a Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer interview experience on Taro that ended without an offer. The process involved a recruiter phone screen followed by a technical phone interview with an engineer, where the candidate was asked to walk through a resume project end-to-end and then solve a LeetCode easy/medium coding problem. Aggregated data from 139 interview experiences shows an 11% pass rate, indicating a highly selective process. Candidate sentiment was largely positive (48% positive, 30% neutral, 22% negative). Former employee reviews praise the caliber of colleagues, problem access, and compensation, but consistently cite poor work-life balance.

Key points

  • Process: recruiter phone screen, then technical phone interview
  • Asked to explain a resume project end-to-end
  • Coding question at LeetCode easy/medium difficulty
  • 11% pass rate across 139 reported interview experiences
  • Reviews praise pay and people but flag poor work-life balance
