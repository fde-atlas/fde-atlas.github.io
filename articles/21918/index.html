Fortience Launches "FDE-Type Consulting Service" Supporting Everything from AI-First Business Design to Implementation

Original title: フォーティエンス、AI前提の業務設計から実装までを支援する「FDE型コンサルティングサービス」を提供

Full text

June 17, 2026, 14:38

Fortience Consulting Co., Ltd. (formerly QUNIE Inc.; hereinafter "Fortience"), a company in the NTT Data Group, announced on the 16th that it will begin offering an "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineering)-type consulting service" in which the company's consultants work closely on-site with customers to provide end-to-end support, from business design premised on AI through application implementation, improvement, and adoption.

The "FDE-type consulting service" is a consulting service in which consultants with both business knowledge and implementation capabilities work directly within customer organizations to achieve business transformation that delivers results. After thoroughly understanding the challenges the business faces, its commercial practices, and the key points involved in changing operations, the service redesigns processes, roles, and decision-making methods, fully leveraging AI to rapidly realize the cycle from business design to implementation.

In addition, because the service consistently leads application design, implementation, and improvement tailored to actual on-site conditions rather than relying on templates, it can comprehensively support the introduction of AI that is actually used and delivers results.

Furthermore, a distinctive feature is that it provides support covering all the essential perspectives for achieving business transformation, from assisting with the establishment and operation of an AI-CoE (an AI promotion specialist organization), to supporting talent transformation including mindset change, and establishing AI governance. The company says this enables AI utilization directly linked to business outcomes while strengthening customers' own transformation capabilities.

Fortience believes it is essential that the process by which customer companies themselves implement and improve agentic AI evolves step by step within the organization until they reach a state where AI can be used autonomously, and the company will also support the improvement of in-house development capabilities and the resolution of organizational, operational, and cultural barriers that hinder transformation.

Pricing for the "FDE-type consulting service" is provided on a case-by-case quotation basis. Fortience plans to develop this service as a core business and aims for revenue on the scale of 10 billion yen in five years.
