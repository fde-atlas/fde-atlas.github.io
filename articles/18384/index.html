Walkers Co., Ltd. Launches AI Implementation Support Service Powered by FDEs

Original title: FDEによるAI実装支援サービスを株式会社Walkersが提供開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-06-24 · 4 min read

Summary

A press release from Tokyo-based Walkers & Co. (representative: Atsushi Watanabe) announces the launch of an AI implementation support service staffed by forward deployed engineers (FDEs). Targeting companies that lack AI talent or find generic SaaS tools insufficient for their business requirements, the service embeds engineers on-site to handle everything from identifying problems through implementation, deployment, and ongoing operations. The release highlights four features: dispatching AI-capable specialists who work alongside client staff; one-stop delivery from planning and development to operations, avoiding handover losses of conventional phased outsourcing; data-driven prioritization of automation decisions; and support for internalization, transferring know-how and training client staff so AI adoption continues independently. Walkers reports over 300 development projects and 200 client companies, and is currently offering free consultations.

  • Engineers embed on-site from problem identification through operations.
  • Four features: embedded staffing, one-stop delivery, data-driven decisions, internalization support.
  • Walkers cites 300+ projects and 200+ client companies; free consultations offered.

Walkers Co., Ltd. (Head office: Bunkyo-ku, Tokyo; Representative: Atsushi Watanabe) has begun offering its "FDE Service (https://walker-s.co.jp/fde/)".

What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?

Simply put, an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is "an engineer who works closely with the customer and drives everything forward end-to-end, from identifying issues to implementation and deployment."

Rather than simply receiving requirements and developing against them, the FDE's distinguishing feature is that they understand the customer's business processes, operational rules, and internal constraints, translate AI and systems into "forms that will actually be used," and stay alongside the customer until results are achieved.

  • No AI talent, or unable to hire it: There is no one in-house who can implement AI, and it is difficult to secure such talent in the hiring market
  • Existing tools don't fit the business: General-purpose SaaS cannot fully cover a company's unique business requirements
  • No partner to work alongside: There is no one who deeply understands the business and works hands-on together with you

The FDE Service breaks through these barriers not by selling tools, but by embedding itself in the field and building together with you.

» Service details here: https://walker-s.co.jp/fde/

Four Features of the FDE Service

1. Dispatch of AI-driven talent

Specialist talent capable of carrying the core of your business embeds itself in your workplace. Going beyond superficial interviews, they understand actual business workflows and decision-making processes from the inside, and drive AI-powered automation and business execution. Rather than leaving everything to external vendors, they function as a partner who works hands-on alongside your employees, enabling AI adoption with buy-in from the field.

2. One-stop service through to operations

Leveraging external expertise and existing AI assets, we provide end-to-end support from planning, design, and development through operations and continuous improvement. Under the conventional division-of-labor model where vendors change at each phase, misalignments and rework tend to occur at every handover. With this service, a single company handles everything from concept to results, achieving both speed and consistency while dramatically reducing wasted communication costs.

3. AI-powered decision-making

By combining field data with the insights of specialist talent, we support decision-making that does not rely solely on intuition and experience. We organize what should be automated and where humans should remain, with quantitative evidence, and clarify priorities. This enables steady AI adoption starting from areas with high return on investment, maximizing results even with limited resources while greatly improving operational efficiency.

4. Support through in-housing and adoption

We don't let results end as a one-off; we support building systems that remain within your organization. We transfer the operational know-how of the built AI in-house, while simultaneously developing and retaining talent who can operate independently. We build sustainable know-how and structures so that your company can continue to develop its AI use even after our support ends. Our goal is not continued dependence on outsourcing, but rooting AI as your company's own capability.
