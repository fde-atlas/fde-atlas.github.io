Summary

Based on Accenture's official announcements and job postings, this article maps out the company's Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) setup. Accenture built FDE programs with Palantir (2025), Microsoft (March 2026), Google Cloud (September), and Workday (October). Per Lightcast data, US FDE job postings from January to September 2026 totaled over 7,300, with Deloitte leading at 872 and Accenture second at 688, ahead of Google's 499. As of October 20, 2026, Accenture's careers site listed 151 FDE-titled openings across 8 countries, 96 in India—of which 66 were actually Application Support Engineer roles requiring ServiceNow skills—and zero in Japan. In Japan, Accenture instead recruits 2028 graduates for three roles under its own RDE (Reinvention Deployed Engineer) label, with base salaries of 4.8–5.509 million yen, supported by roughly 400 staff from acquired firm Yumemi.

Key points

  • Accenture built FDE programs with Palantir, Microsoft, Google Cloud, and Workday between 2025 and 2026.
  • US FDE postings (Jan–Sep 2026): Deloitte 872, Accenture 688, Google 499 per Lightcast.
  • Of 151 FDE-titled openings, 96 were in India; 66 were actually ServiceNow support roles; Japan had zero.
  • US postings demand 5+ years with enterprise systems like SAP and ServiceNow; California ranges reach $163,000–$434,000.
  • Japan recruits 2028 graduates as RDEs across three roles, with Yumemi's ~400 staff as the core.

アクセンチュアのFDE(Forward Deployed Engineer/フォワードデプロイドエンジニア)とは、AIを顧客の業務に本番導入するために、顧客の現場に入って実装まで担うエンジニアのことです。同社は2026年10月13日にWorkdayとの共同FDEプログラムを発表し[1]、その2日後には、米国のFDE求人の企業別件数で、アクセンチュアがDeloitteに次ぐ688件だったと報じられました[2]。また同社は、FDEに近いものの特定の製品に依存しない職種として「RDE(Reinvention Deployed Engineer)」を定義しており[9]、日本では2028年卒の新卒採用で3つの職種をRDEとして募集しています[11][12][13]。

This article, based on four official Accenture announcements, 151 FDE job postings the editorial team obtained from the company's official careers site on October 20, 2026 (including US salary ranges), the job descriptions for three new-graduate RDE roles in Japan, and two interview articles, organizes what Accenture's FDEs do and where and how many are being hired. For an overview of the FDE role, see What Is an FDE?, and for a career change from consulting, see From IT Consulting to FDE.

What Is Accenture's FDE?

アクセンチュアのFDEとは、AIの基盤を提供する会社(Microsoft、Google Cloud、Workday、Palantirなど)と組み、顧客の現場でAIを試作から本番まで持っていくエンジニアです。米国の求人票は、顧客の業務に深く入り込み、実験の段階を越えて現場で使われる状態にすることを役割に挙げています[5]。プロダクト企業のFDEと違い、1社の製品ではなく複数の提携先の製品を扱う点が特徴です。

アクセンチュアは、FDEと並ぶ職種として「RDE」も定義しています。ITmedia AI+の取材によれば、RDEは同社が社内で定義した職種で、特定製品の導入を重視するFDEに対し、特定の製品に依存せずに顧客を支援する点を特徴としています[9]。米国の公式サイトにも「Reinvention Deployed Engineering」というサービスのページがあり、「パイロットから成果へ」移ることを掲げていますが、FDEとの関係は書かれていません[14]。

How does Accenture develop FDEs?

アクセンチュアは、FDEを外から採るだけでは足りないとして、経験のあるソフトウェアエンジニアを育て直す方針を取っています。同社のFDE責任者はCNBCに、経歴によって30〜90日の研修とオンボーディングを行い、対象はすでにソフトウェア開発の経験が十分にある人だと話しています[2]。

"The reality is that experienced FDEs cannot simply be recruited at the scale clients need."

Ram Ramalingam (Accenture, head of FDE and Software & Platform Engineering) — CNBC

[2]

同じ記事によれば、Google Cloudと作る1,000人のFDE組織も、一部は経験のあるソフトウェアエンジニアを、顧客向けのAI導入の仕事に育て直して揃えます[2]。Lightcastの研究責任者は、FDEのスキルの約半分はソフトウェアエンジニアと共通しており、より広い人材から育てられると述べています[2]。AnthropicのClaude Frontier Academyの最初のコホートにも、アクセンチュアのエンジニアが参加しています[2]。各社の育成の仕組みはFDE育成はどう進めるのかで比べました。

What does this mean for job seekers and hiring companies?

For job seekers, Accenture is one of the major entry points for gaining FDE experience, but there is a wide range of work under the same job title. For hiring companies, it means that major consulting firms, not just AI companies, have joined the competition for FDE talent.

Summary

Accenture's FDE work involves deploying partner products into production in clients' businesses, and in the US it is one of the companies with the most FDE job postings, but the nature of the work varies greatly by country and division. In Japan, hiring has begun with new graduates for RDE positions, which are close to FDE.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Accenture's FDE?

I am an engineer who uses products from partners such as Microsoft, Google Cloud, Workday, and Palantir to deploy AI at customer sites, from prototyping to production. Many job postings in the US are written around implementations that connect foundation models such as OpenAI and Anthropic with business systems such as SAP and ServiceNow. Palantir-related postings are jobs that build data pipelines and applications on top of Palantir's products.

Q What is the difference between Accenture's RDE and FDE?

RDE (Reinvention Deployed Engineer) is a job title defined internally by Accenture, and it is also used in the Japanese subsidiary's new graduate hiring for the class of 2028. According to press coverage, unlike FDEs, which focus on deploying specific products, RDEs are characterized by not depending on a specific product and instead supporting customers with a small team of specialists in AI engineering, industry business knowledge, change management, and other areas. Chair and CEO of the global headquarters Julie Sweet has stated that RDEs are professionals who understand industries and business processes in addition to technology, and it has been reported that the company is investing heavily in developing RDEs.

Q Does Accenture hire FDEs in Japan?

As of October 20, 2026, neither the job titles nor the descriptions of mid-career job postings in Japan contained the terms FDE or RDE. For new graduates, for the class of 2028, the company is recruiting for three roles as RDEs: Strategy Consultant, Business Consultant, and Engineer.

Q What is the salary of an Accenture FDE?

US job postings state annual salary ranges in California of $70,350 to $235,100 for Team Lead/Consultant, $94,400 to $316,300 for Manager, and $163,000 to $434,000 for Associate Director (as of October 20, 2026). For new graduate RDE positions in Japan, the annual base salary is 4.8 to 5.509 million yen for Tokyo and Kansai hiring, with a theoretical standard annual income of 6.63 to 7.54 million yen.

Q Are all Accenture FDE job postings the same job?

No, they are not. As of October 20, 2026, 66 of 96 postings in India had Application Support Engineer as the role in the description, with ServiceNow operations management as a required skill. Palantir-related postings in the US and public sector postings in Singapore center on designing and implementing agents and data pipelines.
