Summary

This article presents key excerpts from Parloa's job description for a Forward Deployed Engineer (US), captured as of 2026-11-02. The role places engineers on the front lines of enterprise AI adoption: building capabilities that don't yet exist, adapting architecture to enterprise realities, and engineering integrations across systems and APIs. FDEs act as a feedback loop between field deployments and core engineering, design for enterprise-grade performance, reliability, and security, and work with Deployment Strategists to ensure scalable architectures and durable customer value. Skill tags span LLMs and agents, RAG, prompt engineering, Python and TypeScript, cloud platforms, Docker/Kubernetes, data pipelines, observability, security, and customer-facing skills like discovery, training, and ROI measurement. The piece closes by suggesting readers use such JDs to shape learning goals, without implying hiring guarantees.

Key points

  • Parloa is hiring a US-based FDE for enterprise AI deployments.
  • The role builds novel solutions and adapts architecture to enterprise constraints.
  • FDEs feed deployment learnings back into the core platform.
  • Skills span LLMs, agents, RAG, cloud, Kubernetes, and observability.
  • Customer discovery, enablement, and ROI measurement are emphasized.

Key original excerpts

Five key passages taken from the original JD.

The following are key excerpts from the job description, organized by skill category. For the full responsibilities and qualifications, please refer to the employer's original text.

Production delivery/end-to-end ownership As a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) at Parloa, you'll be on the front lines of enterprise AI adoption - delivering cutting-edge technology into some of the most complex environments in the world.
Production delivery/end-to-end ownership You will build what doesn't yet exist, adapt architecture to enterprise realities, and engineer integrations across systems and APIs to make deployments successful.
Production delivery/end-to-end ownership - Shape the product: Act as the feedback loop between the field and our core engineering teams - turning deployment learnings into platform evolution
Production delivery/end-to-end ownership - Design for scale and resilience: Architect deployment solutions that meet enterprise-grade requirements for performance, reliability, and security
Production delivery/end-to-end ownership - Deliver long-term impact: Work closely with Deployment Strategists to ensure scalable architectures and durable customer value
