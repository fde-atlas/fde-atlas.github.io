Anthropic FDE Interview Prep - AI-Powered Course

Educative · 2026-09-30 · 2 min read

Summary

Educative offers an interview prep course for the Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer role, created by co-founder and CEO Fahim ul Haq, a former Microsoft and Meta engineer. The course covers the FDE role and success metrics, the Claude stack including the Messages API and Claude Agent SDK, applied system design, and the Anthropic interview loop. It includes three case studies: loan document flagging at a regulated bank, a clinical scheduling assistant under HIPAA, and claims triage under insurance AI governance. It emphasizes building evals, debugging agent pipelines, and distinguishing FDEs from Solutions, ML, and Sales Engineers.

Key points

  • Prep course for the Anthropic FDE interview loop, designed to be completed in hours.
  • Covers the Claude stack, Messages API, and Claude Agent SDK for agent workflows.
  • Includes case studies in banking, healthcare (HIPAA), and insurance governance.
  • Teaches building evals to measure AI system performance against success criteria.
  • Clarifies how FDEs differ from Solutions, ML, and Sales Engineers.
