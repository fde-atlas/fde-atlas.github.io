Full text

FDE Leader Ex-@Google, Microsoft

Become the FDE customers ask for by name

I put 15 years of what I learned at Google and Microsoft into one 7-hour workshop, built to make you an exceptional Forward Deployed Engineer.

The gap

As a Software Engineer or AI Engineer, you can build agentic workflows and ship AI apps in days. Speed is the entry ticket for a good FDE.

What ends FDE careers

  • A CTO wants faster deployments, and you jump to CI/CD tooling.
  • The engineering team is uncomfortable you’re there, and you don’t notice.
  • Someone hands you a pre-scoped project, and you build without asking if it’s the right problem.

What wins the offer

FDE roles are everywhere. Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic are all building this function.

The engineers getting those offers ask questions and keep going past the first answer. They figure out who does the work, who feels threatened, and what’s politically doable. They read people and org charts as carefully as code, and they leave with scoped projects.

In this workshop you will

  • Run real discovery conversations with enterprise customers
  • Navigate stakeholders who don’t all agree, and some who don’t want you there
  • Turn a business goal into a scoped project you can deliver

Go from engineer to FDE with the SCOPE Method: uncover problems, manage politics, define outcomes, influence without authority.

  • Study the customer's business, industry, and public materials before you ever speak to them
  • Push for the executive-level conversation anything below gives you a pre-filtered problem
  • Connect to business pain (revenue, cost, time) not pre-scoped deliverables
  • Walk the customer's full process end to end (tools, teams, bottlenecks) through their stated goal
  • Turn a vague ask like "ship faster" into a specific, defensible project with measurable outcomes
  • Build the instinct: business outcome first, technical solution second
  • Read the room: spot the skeptical CTO, threatened engineer, VP with the impossible timeline
  • Hold different conversations with IT, finance, and leadership on the same project in one week
  • Earn trust through curiosity ask questions instead of performing expertise
  • Define measurable outcomes that map directly to the customer's business goals
  • Sequence your toolbox: show demos, prior work, and art-of-the-possible only after context is earned
  • Hand off cleanly with research, projects, and outcomes already locked ready for the next discipline

  • What nobody teaches engineers about becoming an FDE (30 mins))

    Why coding and AI skills alone won't get you there. The communication gap, the mindset shift, and why the SCOPE Method exists.

  • The SCOPE Method: 5-step FDE discovery framework (1 hour)

    Walk through Study, Connect, Observe, People, Execute how each step builds on the last, and where most engineers skip ahead and fail.

  • Deep-dive into each SCOPE step with real enterprise examples (2 hours)

    Live walkthroughs: desk research, pushing for the exec meeting, double-clicking a CTO's vague ask, reading stakeholder dynamics, scoping outcomes.

  • SCOPE Workout: run a full discovery on a real scenario (2-3 hours)

    You get a customer brief. You run the full SCOPE Method research, exec conversation, double-click, stakeholder map, scoped project. No slides. Just reps.

  • Optional bonus Q&A bring your real FDE challenges (1 hour)

    Open session to workshop your actual situations: difficult stakeholders, scoping problems, career transition questions. Top questions answered live
