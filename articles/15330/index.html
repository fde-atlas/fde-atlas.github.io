Eight FDE Segments | FDE Career Atlas

FDE Commons by The AI Runtime · 2026 · 1 min read

Summary

This article presents a framework of eight FDE segments, arguing that the deployed product boundary—not the job title—determines what the work actually involves. Each segment has a distinct technical stack, customer environment, success measure, and preparation path: frontier model deployment, agent/application integration, enterprise data platforms, inference and ML systems, evaluation and observability, vertical domain AI, public sector mission systems, and physical AI/robotics. Readers can use the framework to pick a segment and prepare accordingly.

Key points

  • The key question is what is being deployed, not the title.
  • Eight segments are defined by product boundary.
  • Each segment lists its own stack and success measure.
  • Segments span models, agents, data, infra, evals, verticals, mission, robotics.
  • The framework guides segment choice and preparation.
