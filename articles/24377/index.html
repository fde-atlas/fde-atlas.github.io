FDE Glossary | A List of Terms for Roles, Organizations, and Implementation

Summary

This article is a Japanese glossary of terms surrounding FDEs, organized into three categories: job titles, organizations/businesses, and implementation. It clarifies Palantir's internal names (FDSE called Delta, paired with Dev; Echo for the business-side Deployment Strategist), OpenAI roles (Deployment Lead, Applied AI Engineer, Applied AI Architect), and related titles like Solutions Architect. It also catalogs 2026 developments such as OpenAI Deployment Company, Ode with Anthropic, Fujitsu becoming a Palantir Global FDE Partner, and NTT Data's FDE-style consulting, plus implementation terms like PoC, evals, LLMOps, RAG, and MCP. The author warns that identical roles carry different names across companies, same names can mean different work, and service names like "FDE-type" lack fixed definitions—readers should verify who works on-site and what gets built to production.

Around FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer), English job titles, internal nicknames, and similarly named organizations are used interchangeably. Reading job postings and news, terms like FDSE, Delta, Echo, Deployment Lead, and FDE-style consulting appear without explanation.

This glossary briefly explains terms that frequently appear in FDE job postings, news, and AI implementation work, divided into three categories: job titles, organizations, and implementation. Facts published by companies are written only after verifying sources, and explanations of how terms are used are written as my own perspective. For a detailed explanation of the FDE role itself, see "What is FDE".

Job Title Terms

TermMeaning
FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)An engineer who works on-site with customers, listens to their business challenges, and builds production systems themselves. See What is FDE for details
FDSE (Forward Deployed Software Engineer)The official job title at Palantir. The role that FDE originated from; Palantir's official blog explains that this role is called Delta internally
DeltaThe internal name for FDSE at Palantir. Palantir's official blog (April 8, 2019) divides the main engineering roles into two, Dev and Delta, describing Delta as the role that delivers "many capabilities to one customer"
DevA product development engineer at Palantir. Paired with Delta, it is described as the role that delivers "one capability to many customers"
Echo (Deployment Strategist)The internal name at Palantir for the role that stands on the side of customers' business operations and challenges. The official blog (March 8, 2022) describes Echo as a PM-leaning role and Delta as a technology-leaning role
Deployment LeadA role within OpenAI's FDE team. The Tokyo job posting describes it as a role that translates business outcomes into technical plans and drives day-to-day execution across FDEs, researchers, and customer engineers
Applied AI EngineerThe job title at OpenAI and Anthropic for engineers who design, build, and deploy AI systems to production together with customers. Some Anthropic job postings describe joining the Applied AI team as an FDE
Applied AI ArchitectA role at OpenAI that serves as the technical lead for an assigned group of customers. Implementation is handed off to delivery teams. Compared in detail in "What is a Solution Architect"
Solution Architect (SA)A role at cloud and software companies that designs customer architectures using the company's products and supports implementation from a technical standpoint. AWS's recruiting page states that SAs do not do the hands-on implementation work on behalf of customers
GTM EngineerAn engineer who builds sales and marketing systems using data and AI. See What is a GTM Engineer for details

Job titles differ from company to company. Taking OpenAI's Tokyo job postings as an example, as of October 21, 2026, Forward Deployed Engineer, Deployment Lead, Applied AI Engineer, and Applied AI Architect are being recruited as separate roles. In my view, rather than the title, it is more reliable to judge whether a job is close to FDE by whether the posting says things like "build to production" or "write code". How OpenAI's postings describe outcomes is also instructive. The Applied AI Engineer – Tokyo posting states that success is measured by production systems, adoption, and customer impact, not by activity volume or demo success alone.

Organization and Business Terms

TermMeaning
FDE ModelA business approach that places a company's own engineers at customer sites and advances product implementation and business improvement together. Originated at Palantir. See What is the FDE Model? for details
OpenAI Deployment CompanyA company announced by OpenAI on May 11, 2026, responsible for AI deployment to enterprises. The acquisition of Tomoro added approximately 150 FDEs and Deployment Specialists
Ode with AnthropicAn enterprise AI services company created by Anthropic with an investment firm. Announced on May 4, 2026, and launched on July 15, 2026. The two companies' moves are summarized in "OpenAI and Anthropic's FDE"
Global FDE PartnerOne of the names for Palantir's partners. Fujitsu announced on September 10, 2026 that it had become a Palantir Global FDE Partner
FDE-style ConsultingThe name of a service that NTT Data Group's Fortience Consulting announced it would launch on June 16, 2026
Microsoft Forward Deployed Engineering PracticeAn FDE specialist organization partnering with Microsoft, announced by Accenture on March 18, 2026
FDE CertificationThe name of private certifications that started in 2026. There is the Japan FDE Association's "FDE Certification" (grades 5 through 1) and the IoT Certification Committee's "FDE Certification System" (3 levels). The Japan FDE Association explicitly states it is not a national qualification

A list of companies in Japan that have FDEs is in "List of Companies Adopting FDE". In my view, examples of using the job title as a service name, such as "FDE-style" or "FDE support", will continue to increase. Since the name alone does not reveal the substance, it is necessary to verify who goes on-site and what they build.

Field and Implementation Terms

A collection of AI implementation and operations terms that frequently come up in conversations about FDE work. Explanations are written briefly in my own words, with links to more detailed articles.

TermMeaning
PoC (Proof of Concept)Building something small before production to verify whether it works. A state where a project stays at PoC and never moves to production is called "PoC purgatory". How to escape it: Escaping AI PoC Purgatory
ProductionizationMaking what was built in a PoC into something used daily in business operations. Includes permissions, operations, monitoring, and even a support contact point
DiscoveryThe first phase of listening to the customer's business and challenges and deciding where to apply AI. Written as "discovery" in OpenAI's FDE job posting
Requirements definitionDeciding what to build and what will count as success. For AI, this also includes setting accuracy targets and preparing for errors. For details, see Requirements Definition for AI Development
Eval (evaluation)A mechanism for measuring how correct AI outputs are, using a predefined set of problems and criteria. How to build one: How to Build LLM Evaluations (Evals)
LLMOpsA system of monitoring, evaluation, and improvement for keeping generative AI running in production. For details, see What Is LLMOps?
RAGA mechanism that searches internal documents and has the AI generate answers based on the results. For details, see What Is RAG?
MCPA standard mechanism for connecting AI with business systems and data. For details, see What Is MCP?
AI agentA mechanism in which the AI decides its own steps toward a goal and uses tools to carry out tasks
HallucinationWhen an AI outputs content that contradicts facts in a plausible way. Countermeasures: Countermeasures for Hallucinations
Prompt injectionAn attack that embeds instructions into documents or inputs read by the AI, causing it to behave in unintended ways. For details, see What Is Prompt Injection?
In-house capabilityBringing operations and improvement to a state where they can be continued by the company's own people alone after external support ends

Notes on reading the glossary

In my view, there are three main points of confusion with terms around FDE.

  • The same job goes by different names. FDE, FDSE, Delta, and Applied AI Engineer can refer to similar work at different companies
  • The same name refers to different jobs. Even when labeled "FDE," the role may include proposal-focused work without implementation, or work close to being stationed at the client. How to tell the difference is covered in What's the Difference Between FDE and SES?
  • Service names and job titles get mixed up. "FDE-type" and "FDE support" are service names with no fixed definition

Whether you are reading a job posting or placing an order, checking "who builds what, to what extent, and when the work ends" right after the name will spare you from being thrown off by inconsistent terminology. How to verify as a client is covered in How to Choose an FDE Support Company, and the details of our support are described in Service Details.
