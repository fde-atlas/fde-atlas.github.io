Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (3/5, Sep 20, 2023): Not what it used to be

Taro · 2023-09-20 · 2 min read

Summary

An anonymous review from a former Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer in London who left after less than a year, rating the company 3/5 with a neutral outlook. The reviewer praises the quality of colleagues, impactful projects, and strong exit opportunities, but criticizes heavy internal politics, lack of pay transparency, limited growth, and long hours. Senior management received the lowest score (1/5), while career opportunities scored highest (5/5); work-life balance was rated 2/5. The page also lists several recent FDE interview experiences at Palantir's London office, describing multi-stage technical interviews including analysis and problem-decomposition rounds, some under NDA.

Key points

  • Former London FDE rates Palantir 3/5 after under a year
  • Pros: great colleagues, impactful projects, good exit options
  • Cons: politics, no pay transparency, limited growth, hard work
  • Senior management scored 1/5; career opportunities 5/5
  • Page includes recent London FDE interview experiences
