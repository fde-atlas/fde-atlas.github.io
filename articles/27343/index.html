Zero G Talent · 2026-09-30 · 3 min read

Summary

Databricks is hiring a Senior Engagement Manager in its Forward Deployed Engineering organization, focused on Public Sector customers. The role requires 10+ years of professional services leadership, expert knowledge of Databricks architecture and its ecosystem, active high-level security clearance, and 25-50% travel. Responsibilities include independently scoping multi-quarter initiatives without clear precedent, meeting regional booking and consumption targets, navigating multi-country regulatory complexity, mentoring junior engagement managers, leading sales of Professional Services & Training offerings, and maintaining senior stakeholder relationships. Compensation includes salary, bonus, and equity, with location-based ranges.

The Sr. Engagement Manager delivers measurable impact for our Public Sector customers as well as at the Business Unit (BU) or Regional team level in the Forward Deployed Engineering organization. You will own and independently scope multi-quarter, highly complex initiatives to solve problems with no clear precedent, operating with high autonomy in high-stakes environments.

The impact you will have:

  • Regional Impact: Drive excellence at the BU/Region level, contributing to team-level booking targets and consumption goals.
  • Complex Problem Solving: Navigate complex regional, regulatory, or market-specific complexities across multiple countries.
  • Mentorship: Coach and support junior Engagement Managers, acting as a go-to resource for peers on complex projects.
  • Sales Strategy: Shape account strategy to align with client goals and lead the sales of complex Professional Services & Training (PS&T) offerings.
  • Strategic Relationships: Establish and maintain strategic relationships with senior customer stakeholders across multiple engagements.

What we look for:

  • 10+ years of leadership experience in Professional Services.
  • Expert-level understanding of PS&T offerings and the ability to customize solutions for complex environments.
  • Proficient thought leadership, increasingly viewed as an external expert in the industry.
  • Participate in the hiring process to attract top-tier talent.
  • Expertise in Databricks architecture, ecosystem, and strategic account planning.
  • Ability to travel up to 25-50% as required.
  • Active high-level clearance
