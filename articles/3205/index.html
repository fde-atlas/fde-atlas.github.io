AI Forward Deployed Engineering

Varick Agents · 2 min read

Summary

This piece argues that as intelligence becomes purchasable by anyone, competitive advantage shifts from having AI to deploying it well — the job of the forward deployed engineer. The role combines business judgment with engineering: auditing real workflows, proving systems behave correctly through evals, and deploying inside existing systems via sandboxes, starting small and expanding authority as reliability is proven. It closes with a 30-day roadmap: build an agent completing a real loop, make it recoverable and predictable, measure failures and costs, then defend the system to both engineers and executives. Typical candidates come from consulting, product management, or software engineering.

Key points

  • Intelligence is commoditized; deployment is the moat
  • FDE work spans audit, evals, and deployment
  • Deploy over existing systems in a sandbox, expand gradually
  • 30-day roadmap: build, stabilize, measure, defend
  • Candidates typically come from consulting, PM, or engineering

Week 3: Make the system measurable and economically viable.
