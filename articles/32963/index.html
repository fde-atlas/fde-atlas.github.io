Infosys plans to hire 6,000 forward deployed engineers over the next few years, according to Deccan Herald. Together with TCS, that makes Indian IT services one of the biggest sources of FDE jobs in the country.

The plan has since grown into a wider reskilling push. In late September 2026, Analytics India Magazine, citing The Economic Times, reported that Infosys may move between a quarter and a third of its workforce into specialist roles, a plan management discussed at its Americas confluence in Washington, DC. The same report quotes CEO Salil Parekh from the July results: “Our plan is to have 6,000 frontier engineers over the next few years.” Infosys had not commented on the report.

What an Infosys FDE does

Infosys FDEs embed with client teams to move AI and digital projects from pilot to deployment. CEO Salil Parekh pointed to Topaz Fabric, the Infosys platform that lets clients use AI while keeping their data under their own control.

Infosys’s Bengaluru Senior FDE listings add the day-to-day detail. Both “Q1-27” postings on Foundit, including this one, were no longer accepting applications when checked on 24 November 2026; a “Q4-26” version of the same role on apna showed no closed notice and asks for at least 5 years of experience. The duties are the same across them:

  • Client discovery. Work with global client engineering teams on their goals; lead workshops, discovery sessions and technical discussions.
  • Delivery coordination. Manage scope, timelines and risks across distributed teams.
  • AI-assisted engineering. Use AI tools, LLMs and agentic coding systems to improve engineering workflows.
  • Integration. APIs, data pipelines, cloud services and toolchains.
  • Mentoring. Coach client and Infosys teams on modern, AI-enabled engineering.

Compared with a startup FDE, the Infosys role leans more towards leading client engagements and modernising how teams build, and less towards owning a single product’s deployments.

The experience tags are confusing: the Q1-27 Foundit postings say “Senior” in the title but are tagged 0–1 years and fresher, while the Q4-26 posting on apna asks for a minimum of 5 years. Go by the description. Leading client engagements and distributed delivery points to experienced engineers. A second Infosys listing, for agentic AI programmes in Bengaluru, did not show details publicly.

Pay

Infosys has not published FDE pay. For a reference point, Taggd puts FDE pay at IT services and consulting firms in India at about ₹20–50 LPA, lower than GCCs and product companies. Check your own band in the FDE Salary Estimator.

How to prepare

  • Show a modernisation story. Infosys wants FDEs who improve how client teams build. One example of bringing AI coding tools or automation into a team, with a measured result, is strong evidence.
  • Practise running discovery. Prepare to lead a 30-minute workshop: clarify the client’s goal, map their current systems and agree a first deliverable.
  • Ship an integration-heavy AI project. An LLM feature that pulls from an enterprise system through APIs, with access control and logging, maps directly to the listing.
  • Know Topaz at a high level. Read Infosys’s public material on Topaz and Topaz Fabric so you can speak its language in interviews.
  • Internal candidates: flag your interest in FDE roles with your delivery manager, and build a portfolio of client wins where you owned the technical outcome.
