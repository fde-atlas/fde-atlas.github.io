Deployment strategist

FDE Interview Prep · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

This article explains how the title "deployment strategist" varies across companies. At Palantir, it is a customer-facing role that works onsite with customer analysts to identify the questions to answer and partners with forward deployed engineers to build stable, extensible data pipelines; some postings require programming or statistics experience. At Databricks, the strategist acts as the product manager for the customer's problem, owning the "why" and "what" while FDEs own the "how". At Scale AI, the same title refers to quota-carrying business development, not engineering. For interviews, the author notes candidates may first meet a strategist in a behavioral call, that Palantir's decomp round is not engineer-only, and that candidates should own problem scoping themselves and clearly delineate the two roles in project deep dives.

Key points

  • Palantir deployment strategists find customer questions onsite and support FDEs building data pipelines.
  • Databricks frames the strategist as the customer problem's product manager, owning "why" and "what".
  • Scale AI's deployment strategist is a quota-carrying business development role, not engineering.
  • Palantir interview processes may start with a behavioral call with a deployment strategist.
  • In decomp rounds, candidates should own questions and scope rather than defer to a strategist.
