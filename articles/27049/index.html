"Forward Deployed Engineer" is just cargo-culting Palantir - Team Topologies: 2nd Edition

FDE Times · 1 min read

Summary

This piece argues that the "Forward Deployed Engineer" job title is largely cargo-culting Palantir: companies adopt the label without replicating the underlying organizational design. Drawing on the second edition of Team Topologies, the author contends that a title alone does not create the team structures, boundaries, and interaction modes that make Palantir's model work, and that firms should first clarify whether the role fits their team topology before adopting it. The page also includes FDE Times hiring data: 924 open roles (up 33 week-over-week), 80 new postings in seven days, 196 companies hiring, a $200k median US salary, and Databricks as the top hirer with 107 roles.

Key points

  • The FDE title is often imitation of Palantir without substance.
  • Team Topologies (2nd edition) provides the analytical framework.
  • Companies should match the role to their team structure first.
  • 924 open FDE roles tracked, up 33 in a week.
  • Databricks leads hiring with 107 roles; median pay $200k.
