Ode with Anthropic FDE interview loop: free dossier

A10X · 2026-08-03 · 6 min read

Summary

A10X published a free dossier on Ode's FDE interview loop. Ode launched July 15, 2026, backed by Anthropic, Blackstone and Hellman & Friedman and built on the acquired Fractional AI team, with roughly 100 engineers and about $1.5B in consortium capital commitments. CTO Eddie Siegel has published Fractional AI's process: two coding rounds where ChatGPT is allowed but Copilot is not, plus a whiteboard session structured like a client scoping call, and no take-home. Ode had 14 live requisitions as of 2026-10-28, with Staff Software Engineers at $275K-$400K and Principals up to $450K, mostly 4 days on-site. No first-hand candidate reports exist yet, and existing prep pages use generic SWE templates.

What we know about the loop, tagged by how we know it

  • Ode formally launched July 15, 2026, introduced by Anthropic, Blackstone and Hellman & Friedman, with Goldman Sachs, General Atlantic, Leonard Green, Apollo, GIC and Sequoia in the consortium. It is led by CEO Chris Taylor and CTO Eddie Siegel, two of the three co-founders of Fractional AI, acquired in May 2026.
  • Hiring runs on Ashby: 14 live requisitions as of 2026-10-28. Every one that states a work arrangement (13 of 14) says 4 days in person, Fridays remote. Current requisitions sit in San Francisco, New York and Raleigh-Durham, including a Staff Software Engineer in Raleigh-Durham (the SF Staff Software Engineer posting also lists the South Bay). No role on the board is in Dubai, though every posting says Ode has an office there, and no remote reqs exist.
  • Published comp on its own board as of 2026-10-28: Staff Software Engineer (SF and NY) $275K-$400K plus equity. Forward Deployed Product Manager $200K-$275K, posted twice (general and financial services). Principal, AI Transformation $400K-$450K, posted four times (general, plus healthcare and life sciences, financial services and cyber). Associate Principal $200K-$250K. Recruiting Coordinator $90K-$120K.
  • Ode delivers in small pods. The Bring Your Own Team posting calls them "tight-knit teams of engineers working on a single project together", and the Staff Software Engineer postings put the team at "2–5 engineers and a PM". The same Staff postings say "You'll spend roughly 75% of your time in code and 25% working directly with clients", often their CTOs.
  • Cadence, in its own words, two ways: its postings say "a typical engineer here ships two to three products per year", and its careers page says "At Ode, you will work on one product every quarter", with "bench time between engagements".
  • Fractional AI's interview loop, published May 2024 by Eddie Siegel, then its CTO and now Ode's: two hands-on coding sessions where ChatGPT is allowed, because they can watch you formulate a query and analyze the response, and Copilot is not, "because Copilot is prone to adding more noise". Plus a whiteboard session "structured similarly to a client call scoping a new project", on a prompt chosen to be less suited to AI. The post's summary calls the whiteboard "LLM-free". Its body is softer: they are "not against using ChatGPT necessarily". No take-home: they tried it and found it "too error-prone". The post sums all of this up as its "current thinking".
  • Ode hires intact engineering teams of 2 to 5 through a Bring Your Own Team track: the whole team interviews on the same day with the same panel, and Ode makes offers to everyone at the same time, which each person accepts or declines on their own.
  • A recruiting job on Ode's own board, Senior Recruiting Coordinator & Talent Ops Lead (published 2026-10-28), lists what it schedules: "tech screens, onsites, debriefs and founder and exec chats", aims for "onsite to founder chat in 48 hours or less", and says Ode runs "about 750 interviews a month" across engineering, product and commercial. A second recruiting posting shows the onsites are in person: the coordinator owns "preparing interview rooms" and "coordinating lunches".
  • Its Principal, AI Transformation postings, all four of them, say "We're more Palantir than McKinsey". "Forward Deployed" appears in only two of its 14 job titles, both product manager roles.
  • Named on Ode's own press page since launch: Citrin Cooperman Advisors engaged Ode for workflows that support its tax, accounting and advisory practices (2026-08-03); PointClickCare is its first senior care platform partner (2026-08-13); Baker Tilly launched a joint initiative (the release puts the Baker Tilly International network at 50,000 professionals); LogicGate, an AI GRC platform, works with Ode to "scale and accelerate the delivery of" its agentic roadmap; and on 2026-08-20 Ode acquired Casper Studios, an AI services firm that "helps companies put Claude to work inside the systems their teams already use".
  • Ode describes its work as partnering "closely with CEOs" on "the highest priority AI initiatives", positioning production systems over demos.
  • Its CEO's launch post says "over half of our team members are former founders" (team members, not only engineers).
  • Its engineering philosophy, from its own job descriptions: "applying standard software engineering discipline to the non-deterministic world of frontier AI... design systems around testable hypotheses, curate durable data sets, and ensure what we ship keeps working long after we've handed it over". One of its listed values is literally "Overuse AI".
  • Fabius (YC W23) joined as an intact team through the Bring Your Own Team track in 2026, per Ode's own posting.
  • An outcome number Ode published itself, in its 2026-08-20 Casper Studios release: for Sphera, a client it shared with Casper, Ode built a custom internal tool that "reduced bottlenecks from time-intensive operational tasks by 70%".
  • Fractional AI's pre-acquisition case studies are still live: Zapier (eval suite, hallucinations down 80%+), Airbyte (AI connector builder), Cando Rail (voice safety-assessment agent).
  • Press reports about $1.5B committed by the consortium: roughly $300M each from Anthropic, Blackstone and Hellman & Friedman, and about $150M from Goldman Sachs. Some outlets call the $1.5B a valuation, but the FT-sourced report says it is capital commitments and that no valuation was disclosed. Ode itself publishes no number.
  • Roughly 100 engineers at launch. Claude-first, but will use rival models when a customer needs them.
  • Some press coverage compares Ode to Palantir's forward-deployment model. Ode's own postings make a narrower comparison, quoted under VERIFIED above: "We're more Palantir than McKinsey".

What nobody outside can know yet

  • What happens inside each of Ode's interviews is unpublished: the recruiting job names what gets scheduled, not what each interview tests or which ones an FDE candidate sits. The two coding sessions with ChatGPT allowed and the client-call whiteboard (LLM-free per the post's summary) are Fractional AI's published 2024 process. Carryover is likely (same CTO, same delivery team, and a company value that says "Overuse AI") but it is an inference and we tag it as one.
  • No engagement length is published, and Ode's own two signals disagree: one product a quarter on its careers page, two to three a year in its postings. Comp is unpublished for four of the fourteen roles.
  • One web search per company on 2026-10-28, naming Blind, Reddit and Glassdoor, found no first-hand candidate report for Ode or Fractional AI. One prep site, Scoutify, has pages for both Ode and Fractional AI. Both show its "Standard software engineering loop" template, and both say the stages "describe the typical SWE loop", not one built for Ode or Fractional. You would be among the first loops through, which is exactly why the prep edge exists.
