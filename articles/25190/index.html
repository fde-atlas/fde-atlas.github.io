prepfully.com · 2026-10-08 · 14 min read

Summary

This Prepfully profile features an anonymous coach claiming 12 years of experience as a hiring manager at Google and Palantir, with 189 completed sessions, a 4.87 review score, and a 76.39% re-book rate. Coaching covers Palantir's decomposition interview, behavioral questions, coding, and system design, targeting candidates for Software Engineer and Forward Deployed Software Engineer roles. Reviews are mostly positive, praising concrete feedback and realistic simulations, though some criticize vague guidance, limited flexibility, or insufficient feedback time. Palantir coaches typically need 1-2 days' notice.
