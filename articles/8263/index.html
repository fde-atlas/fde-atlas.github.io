Taro · 2026-08-21 · 3 min read

Summary

A candidate shares a Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer interview experience in New York: contacted via LinkedIn by a recruiter, followed by an intro call covering compensation, then a 30-minute technical round on an easy LeetCode problem. They described the process as chill but fumbled and received no offer, still rating the experience positively. Aggregated data from 36 interview experiences shows a 14% pass rate, making the process extremely selective, though 56% of candidates reported positive feelings. Current employees rate the company 5.0, citing smart colleagues, fast growth into lead roles, day-one responsibility, and self-directed career paths; cons include heavy autonomy and occasional busyness.

Key points

  • 30-minute technical round on an easy LeetCode problem
