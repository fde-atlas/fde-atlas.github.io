FDE Team Launch Support | FDE Consulting — Developing FDE Talent

Original title: FDEチーム立ち上げ支援 | FDE Consulting — FDE人材の育成

FDE Consulting · 2026 · 3 min read

Summary

FDE Consulting offers a service to help companies build in-house FDE teams. Over roughly three months, it defines FDE roles and KPIs tied to customer outcomes (revenue, usage, retention), designs hiring criteria and evaluation methods based on its own standards, and has its own FDE accompany the client's first project to hand over a working playbook. Training is OJT-based within real projects; standalone training, staffing, and recruiting agency services are excluded. Pricing is quoted individually, starting from three months, and the engagement ends once the client can train subsequent FDEs independently, with optional monthly reviews.

Key points

  • Three-stage program: role/KPI design, hiring and training design, first-project accompaniment.
  • KPIs are tied to customer-side numbers like revenue, usage, and retention.
  • Training is OJT on a real project; no standalone courses are offered.
  • Excludes staffing, resident placement, and recruiting agency services.
  • Goal is client self-sufficiency in training future FDEs, with optional monthly follow-ups.

We develop Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) within your company—people who embed your products and AI into customers' operations. We support everything from designing roles and KPIs, to accompanying your team on their first project, to handing over the methodology.

Who This Is For

  • You have no one to see your product through to actual use at the customer's site after the sale
  • You created an AI department, but it hasn't penetrated business units or customer operations
  • You want to place FDE talent but don't know how to define the role, KPIs, or hiring criteria
  • You want to develop your own people rather than relying on external staff
  • You want to build an organization that can run FDE training and development in-house

Three Stages of Support (3 months and up)

  • 01 Role and KPI Design (Month 1) We define what the FDE is responsible for and how they are evaluated. We set KPIs tied to customer outcomes, such as revenue, usage rates, and adoption rates.
  • 02 Hiring Criteria and Training Design (Months 1–2) Based on our own hiring standards, we create required and preferred qualifications, interview evaluation methods, and assessment processes tailored to your company. FDE training is not classroom-based; it is conducted as OJT on the project in the next stage.
  • 03 Accompanying the First Project (Months 2–3) Our FDE joins your FDE on their first project. We work together on breaking down business processes, KPI design, implementation, and weekly improvements, and hand over the playbook.

Deliverables

  • FDE role definition and KPI design document
  • Hiring requirements, interview guide, and evaluation sheets
  • Documentation of how the first project was run (kept as your company's playbook)

Not Included

  • Staffing or on-site placement (available via FDE Implementation Support if needed)
  • Recruiting or hiring agency services

Duration and Pricing

  • Duration
  • 3 months and up
  • Pricing
  • Custom quote (determined by scope and duration)
  • Target
  • Business leaders, CTOs, and AI departments at companies with their own products or AI
  • Lead
  • Led primarily by Hiromichi Mikata (Representative)

After the Launch

Once the process is running on its own within your company, our involvement ends. The goal is for your FDE organization to be able to develop their second and third members independently. If needed, we can continue with monthly project reviews and hiring consultations only.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is an FDE?
  • An engineer who works on-site with customers, embeds products and AI into business operations, and takes responsibility for outcome metrics. For details, see our article "What is an FDE?"
  • How many people can we start with?
  • Just one. We start by selecting one initial project and developing the person responsible for it.
  • Can someone who isn't an engineer become an FDE?
  • Coding ability is required. However, the ability to talk with customers and break down their business processes is needed first. In the hiring criteria design, we decide together which to prioritize.
  • Can you work on-site at our company?
  • Launch support is about developing your own people, so on-site placement is not included. If you need additional implementation capacity, we offer FDE Implementation Support.
  • Can we request FDE training only?
  • We do not currently offer training as a standalone service. Development happens within the launch support, using real projects. Working through the first project together develops people faster than classroom learning.
  • What if we already have an AI department or in-house team?
  • We start by redefining roles and KPIs as an FDE organization. The most common consultation we receive is from teams that have the technical skills but haven't been able to engage with customer operations.
