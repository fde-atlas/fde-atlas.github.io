Do Customers Pay for Forward Deployed Engineers?

Falkster.AI · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

Summary

The article examines who actually pays for forward deployed engineers, citing October 28, 2026 comments from two CFOs. Key points

  • ServiceNow absorbs delivery expertise costs within subscription revenue, about 25 bps in Q3, 50 bps expected in Q4.

Full text

On October 28, 2026, two CFOs told investors how forward deployed engineers get paid for, and neither answer was the customer. ServiceNow's Gina Mastantuono said the company includes delivery expertise in select large AI deals, with the value carved out of subscription revenue, "about 25 bps in Q3 and expecting it to have approximately 50 bps in Q4," and added: "We do not expect to include this delivery expertise over the long term." Microsoft's Amy Hood said the Frontier Company investment is meant to get customers to "utilize both the Copilot license to its fullest, and understand when the consumption meter is delivering value to them."

Salesforce's own blog says its FDE program is "offered to select customers for free." AWS prices on fixed outcomes. The market price of a vendor's engineer is zero, and a services fee on top of a subscription is priced against free.

The three-column check is the part to take away: for your last five FDE engagements, what the customer paid for the engineers, what they consumed 90 days after go-live against the 90 before, and what came back into the product. Zeros in the first column are normal. Blanks in the other two are the problem.
