Cognition AI Deployed Engineer Interview Experience (2026)

Aced (formerly Exponent) · 2026-10-01 · 1 min read

Summary

This is a candidate-reported interview experience for Cognition AI's Deployed Engineer role, published on Aced (formerly Exponent). The process consists of two rounds with four questions total: a recruiter screen and a take-home project. The recruiter screen focuses on whether candidates fit the company's fast-paced environment. The take-home project is described as non-trivial with a tight submission timeline, which the author suggests is intentional to test how candidates handle deadlines. Candidate quotes note the process was smooth but slow between rounds, with a helpful recruiter, and emphasize that FDEs must be able to explain why their work matters to customers and the business.

Key points

  • Cognition AI's Deployed Engineer process has two rounds: recruiter screen and take-home project, four questions total.
  • The recruiter screen tests fit with a fast-paced environment.
  • The take-home is non-trivial with a deliberately tight deadline to test time management.
  • Candidates report smooth interviews but slow waits between rounds; the recruiter offered prep tips.
  • FDE candidates should articulate why their work helps customers and the business.

Interview process
