FDE Forward Deployed Engineer: Enterprise AI Transformation and Agent Engineering Delivery takes "value first" as its core theme. Starting from the difficulties enterprises face in implementing AI, it explains the responsibilities, capabilities, and collaboration models of the FDE; it then introduces the growth mechanism from individual to team, fully reconstructing the entire process from business research, requirement abstraction, PoC validation, and prototype building, to knowledge engineering, agent implementation, eval governance, and launch delivery. Combined with industry practices in manufacturing and cross-border e-commerce marketing, it helps readers complete the closed loop from AI demo prototypes to deployable, acceptable, and reusable intelligent business capabilities. FDE Forward Deployed Engineer: Enterprise AI Transformation and Agent Engineering Delivery is suitable for technical professionals who want to transition into FDE roles, AI application and agent development engineers, architects, as well as business leaders, engineering delivery personnel, and industry practitioners driving enterprise AI digital transformation.

Bing Yue is an enterprise AI transformation consultant, agent application expert, and FDE implementation practitioner with over ten years of experience in software development, technical management, and enterprise digital transformation. He has worked at leading internet companies including Alibaba, Meituan-Dianping, Kuaishou, and Sohu, participating in the construction of e-commerce platforms, commercialization systems, intelligent supply chains, and enterprise-level agent platforms, accumulating extensive practical experience in internet system architecture, technical team management, and AI-driven business efficiency. In recent years, he has focused on enterprise AI transformation, agent engineering delivery, and AI-driven business growth, conducting technical research and project practice in areas such as cross-border e-commerce, enterprise knowledge bases, intelligent manufacturing, and marketing customer acquisition, continuously promoting enterprise AI application practice and FDE talent development.

Preface With the rapid popularization of large models and agent technology, enterprise AI applications have moved from the technology trial stage into a new stage of business implementation and value delivery. Today, most enterprises do not lack AI models and technical solutions; what is truly scarce are professionals who can go deep into the business front line, break down the barriers between technology and business, and complete the delivery from prototype validation at scale to production applications. The FDE Forward Deployed Engineer has thus become an emerging core role in the AI industry. The industry currently faces widespread pain points of "difficulty in technical implementation, difficulty in closing the acceptance loop, and difficulty in scaling and reuse," and market demand for FDE talent combining business insight, engineering capability, and commercial thinking continues to grow, making the FDE role a critical necessity for enterprise digital transformation and AI project implementation. This book presents the complete methodology and practice system of the FDE. It opens by analyzing the underlying reasons why enterprise AI struggles to move beyond pilots, sorting out the various gaps in the implementation process and clarifying the FDE's scope of responsibility, six-dimensional capability model, collaboration methods, and authorization requirements. It then describes the path from individual FDE growth to team building, explaining how to conduct on-site business research and diagnosis, abstract vague business requirements into verifiable AI scenarios, and build the enterprise ontology. The book fully covers hands-on practice across the entire process of PoC validation, prototype engineering, RAG knowledge engineering, agent development, eval and observability, security governance, and gray-scale launch, while also dissecting real implementation paths in two typical industries: manufacturing and e-commerce marketing going global. The book's greatest feature is its practice-oriented approach, abandoning pure theory and tool stacking, with "value first" as its core theme, deeply integrating mindset theory, engineering practice, and commercial implementation, balancing hands-on delivery with risk management. This book is suitable for technical practitioners planning to transition into FDE roles, enterprise AI project leaders, architects, and delivery engineers, and can also serve as a reference for business personnel, entrepreneurs, and industry practitioners engaged in enterprise digital transformation and agent engineering implementation. We hope this book helps practitioners move beyond technical demos and truly root AI in enterprise business, creating sustained real value. The Author, September 2026

Today, with the rapid popularization of large model and agent technology, many enterprises can easily produce stunning AI demo demos, yet consistently fail to cross the gap from pilot validation to business-scale delivery. Technical solutions may be feasible, but difficult business acceptance, difficult value closure, and difficult asset reuse have become widespread pain points constraining enterprise AI transformation. The FDE Forward Deployed Engineer is precisely the emerging key role that bridges the gap between business needs and AI technical implementation. FDE Forward Deployed Engineer: Enterprise AI Transformation and Agent Engineering Delivery takes "value first" as its main thread and fully reconstructs the FDE's complete practical work system: from analysis of enterprise AI implementation difficulties, the FDE role capability model, on-site business research and diagnosis, business requirement abstraction and enterprise ontology construction, to PoC validation, knowledge engineering, agent implementation, eval governance, gray-scale launch, and project handover, covering the entire process of enterprise agent delivery and completing the full path from prototype to production-ready. The author has over ten years of experience in front-line internet development, technical management, and enterprise AI transformation. Without stacking empty theories, the methodology and work paradigms distilled from real projects are woven throughout the book; combined with two typical industries—manufacturing and e-commerce marketing going global—it dissects the implementation path of agents in real business scenarios, explaining in depth the key aspects often overlooked by technical books: business research, risk management, ROI evaluation, and organizational adoption. Whether you are a technical professional hoping to transition into an FDE role, an agent development engineer, an architect, or a delivery person or business leader responsible for enterprise AI digital transformation, FDE Forward Deployed Engineer: Enterprise AI Transformation and Agent Engineering Delivery can help you build a composite perspective of "business + engineering + commerce," learn to transform AI technology into real business value for the enterprise, escape the dilemma of "great demos but useless in production," and truly achieve agent projects that are acceptable, reusable, and scalable.
