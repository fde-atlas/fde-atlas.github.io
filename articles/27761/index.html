Full text

On August 26, the Shanghai session of the "FDE Enterprise AI Implementation Bootcamp," jointly hosted by the Hongkou District Science and Technology Commission and Datawhale, was held at the Hongkou North Science and Technology Innovation Reception Hall. As part of the Government Open Month "Building Ecosystems, Linking the Future" event series, the organizers selected 20 above-scale enterprises and 20 FDE experts from over a hundred registered companies for one-on-one matchmaking on site.

The event focused on implementing AI technology in real business scenarios, adopting a hands-on model of "enterprise problem diagnosis — FDE expert matching — on-site solution co-creation." Participating enterprises spanned retail, manufacturing, services, diversified operations, and other industries, bringing their real business problems to meet face-to-face with Datawhale-certified FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers).

FDE: Bridging the "Last Mile" of AI Implementation

FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer. This role was first systematically established around 2010 by the American big data analytics company Palantir, whose core model was to station engineers on-site at customers to deeply embed standardized products into enterprises' existing systems and workflows. Amid the wave of large AI model implementation, this model has been replicated and promoted by top global AI companies such as OpenAI and Anthropic. A report released by LinkedIn in 2026 showed that 1.3 million AI-related jobs were added globally between 2023 and 2025, with demand for FDEs growing 42-fold.

Image: AI-generated

Unlike traditional software implementation personnel, an FDE is not a delivery role that "sells and leaves," but a versatile engineer embedded in the customer's business site who truly connects AI capabilities to real production environments. The industry vividly describes FDEs as "bilinguals" between technology and business — able to understand model architectures as well as factory control protocols; able to write code as well as comprehend customers' jargon-filled requirements, and translate both into executable technical solutions. The core characteristics of an FDE can be summarized as "one customer, multiple skills, writing production code, and being accountable for results."

The FDE expert certification system launched by Datawhale is designed precisely to systematically cultivate such versatile talent with business understanding, model tuning, and engineering deployment capabilities, bridging the "last mile" from technical prototypes to production-grade AI implementation for enterprises.

Three Scenarios, Focused on Business Scenario Co-Creation

This bootcamp featured three core stages: enterprises submitting business problems on-site, precise matching of FDE experts by industry focus, and on-site co-creation of a first-version implementation plan between enterprises and experts. The AI implementation directions covered included store operations and inventory management, supply chain material organization, intelligent customer service pre-review, and automatic aggregation of business metrics.

The morning session focused on policy briefings and case sharing. The District Science and Technology Commission explained the tiered cultivation policies for "specialized, refined, distinctive, and innovative" enterprises, helping participants understand regional industry support priorities. The afternoon moved into the hands-on session, where FDE experts and enterprise representatives were paired in groups to break down requirements around specific business problems, determine technical paths, and produce verifiable preliminary solutions. After the on-site exchanges, some problems had already begun to take shape toward resolution: which scenarios to tackle first, what enterprises still need to prepare, what capabilities FDEs can provide, and where to go next. The discussions between enterprises and FDEs also advanced from "can AI do this" to "how can we do this business together." This event truly set the connection between enterprises and FDEs in motion.

This event was organized by the District Science and Technology Innovation and SME Development Promotion Center and the Hongkou Hidden Champion Enterprise Promotion Center, co-organized by the District State-owned Assets Management Co., Ltd. and Shanghai Zhilian Exhibition Services Co., Ltd., and supported by the Antai College of Economics and Management of Shanghai Jiao Tong University, SenseTime, and SenseTime Xiaohuanxiong (Raccoon).

As part of the "2026 Yangtze River Delta FDE Joint Action," this Shanghai session is the second enterprise AI implementation bootcamp Datawhale has held in the region, following the inaugural Hangzhou session in March 2026. Datawhale stated that the FDE expert roster has been expanded and updated, and it will continue to promote enterprise AI implementation talent development and scenario matchmaking nationwide.
