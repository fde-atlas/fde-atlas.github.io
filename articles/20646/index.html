Member of Technical Staff | Halluminate | San Francisco, CA

Zero G Talent · 2026-08-18 · 2 min read

Summary

Halluminate, a data research lab building benchmarks and RL environments for knowledge work, is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer in San Francisco (5 days in office). The role involves working directly with AI labs and enterprise customers, translating vague requirements into technical specs, prototyping customer-specific environments, and feeding patterns back to the platform team. Candidates need 2+ years in consulting or FDE roles, 0-to-1 shipping experience, and ideally familiarity with evals and AI agent environments. Compensation is $215–275k base plus 0.10–0.20% equity, with visa sponsorship including H-1B.

Key points

  • Work spans client-facing scoping, 0→1 prototyping, and last-mile delivery with project pods.
  • Requires 2+ years in consulting or forward deployed engineering for large enterprises.
  • Preferred: evals/benchmark experience, financial services exposure, founder background.
  • Pay: $215–275k base, 0.10–0.20% equity, H-1B sponsorship offered.

What you need

About Halluminate

Halluminate is a data research lab building benchmarks and RL environments to push the frontier of AI in knowledge work.

Our first area of focus is economically valuable workflows in financial services and consulting, but we are rapidly expanding into adjacent verticals.

The role

Base: $215–275k · Equity: 0.10–0.20% · Location: San Francisco, 5 days in office

Our FDEs will interface directly with our customers at AI Labs and Enterprises. You will sit in the customer research meetings, understand the intent behind the requests, and create software that drives forward $xMM projects. The work is a mix of client facing, 0 to 1, rapid prototyping, and product scaling with core platform teams. Your engineering will directly support revenue creation and frontier intelligence scaling.

Responsibilities

  • Work directly with frontier AI labs and enterprise customers to understand requirements and what they need from an RL environment
  • Translate customer requirements into concrete technical specs our platform and project pods can build against.
  • Build 0→1 — prototype, ship, and iterate on customer-specific environments and tooling before any of it becomes a platform feature.
  • Use and extend our internal platforms and dev tooling rather than rebuilding them.
  • Embed with SPL project pods to unblock delivery: integration, technical debugging, and the last-mile problems that decide whether a project lands.
  • Feed patterns back to the platform team so one-off solutions become reusable capability.
  • Own technical credibility in customer conversations, from scoping through delivery.

You should have

  • 2+ years of experience in consulting or forward-deployed engineering roles for large enterprises with custom software, systems, and requirements
  • Strong engineering ability paired with genuine comfort interacting directly in front of customers
  • A track record of 0→1 work: shipping something that did not exist, under ambiguity, without waiting for a spec.
  • The ability to translate fuzzy customer language into technical requirements, and technical constraints back into plain terms.
  • Startup speed: iterate quick, ask quick, respond quick, make mistakes quick.
  • Experience building evals, environments, and benchmarks for AI Agents

Nice to have

  • Financial services domain exposure (investment banking, private equity, hedge funds).
  • Former founders or experience at early-stage startups.

What we offer

  • Healthcare — Fully covered medical, dental, and vision for you. Subsidized for dependents.
  • Relocation — $10k+ relocation stipend to help you get to San Francisco.
  • 401(k) — Up to 4% employer match.
  • Food — Free breakfast, lunch, and dinner while working.
  • Health — Free gym membership.
  • Transportation — Free Ubers and Waymo rides to and from the office.
  • Visa sponsorship — We sponsor visas, including H-1B.
