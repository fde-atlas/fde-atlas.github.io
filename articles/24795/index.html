Overview of Companies Hiring FDEs

The Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role was established by US-based Palantir and is now hired as a core position by cutting-edge AI companies, enterprise SaaS companies, and AI consultancies/SIers. In Japan as well, Japan offices of foreign AI/SaaS companies and domestic AI implementation support firms are strengthening their hiring. This section organizes representative hiring companies by type.

Cutting-Edge AI Companies

CompanyPositioning of FDE
PalantirKnown as the company that popularized the job title FDE. A core role implementing the product into customer operations
OpenAIHiring for Forward Deployed Engineer/Solutions roles for enterprise (includes Tokyo-based positions)
AnthropicStrengthening customer implementation through Applied AI / Forward Deployed roles
Sierra, Cohere, etc.Actively hiring FDEs responsible for on-site implementation of AI agents/LLM deployments

At these companies, FDEs are positioned on the front line of revenue growth, with compensation at high levels (including equity).

Enterprise SaaS / Professional Services

Professional services and solutions engineering departments at major SaaS companies also effectively hire FDE-type talent. Their role is to integrate their own products with data platforms and business systems and support customers through to production operation. Job titles vary widely, such as "Solutions Engineer," "Implementation Engineer," and "Professional Services Engineer."

FDE Hiring in Japan

In Japan, the main sources of hiring are (1) Japan offices of foreign AI/SaaS companies, (2) domestic startups and AI consultancies supporting generative AI adoption, and (3) AI solution divisions of SIers. Since these are often posted under titles such as "AI Implementation Engineer," "Solutions Engineer," or "Customer Engineer," it is important to judge by the actual role (whether it involves implementing and embedding the company's or a specific product at customer sites) rather than by the job title.
