Palantir Interview Questions (11 Reported)

Crack ML Interview · 2026-11-11 · 6 min read

Summary

Crack ML Interview compiles 11 interview questions reported by candidates who interviewed at Palantir: 8 coding and 3 ML system design, averaging 2.9/5 difficulty. Six of them map to Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) rounds, emphasizing practical coding and decomposition — e.g., coupon optimization under loyalty-point budgets and designing products from taxi trip data. Common topics are string manipulation, greedy algorithms, system design, and decomposition. A forum post describes the FDSE process: about two weeks after applying, a roughly 30-minute phone screen focused on motivation and knowledge of Palantir. The editorial advice is to prioritize decomposition and practical coding in prep. Five questions were reported within the last year, the most recent in November 2026.
