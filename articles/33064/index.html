FDE Roles at GCCs in India: KRAs, Skills and Pay (2026)

FDE India · 2026-11-13 · 10 min read

Summary

Based on seven FDE listings in India opened in November 2026, this guide examines forward deployed roles at GCCs in two forms: enterprise GCCs serving internal business units (e.g., Warner Music Group in Bengaluru) and India teams of global platforms (Google Cloud, Databricks, UiPath, MongoDB, IFS) serving external clients. Required experience ranges from 2 to 8 years, and none of the listings posts a salary. Compared with startup FDE roles, GCC listings emphasize data platforms, security and governance, and measured outcomes like cost and price-performance. Taggd's May 2026 data puts GCC FDE pay at ₹35–90 LPA, the highest top end among employer types. Hiring concentrates in Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune.

Key points

  • GCC FDE roles split into internal-customer enterprise GCCs and external-customer platform India teams.
  • Minimum experience across seven listings ranges from 2 years (IFS, Google GenAI) to 8 years (MongoDB).
  • GCC listings stress data platforms, governance, and quantified results more than startup roles.
  • Taggd's May 2026 tables put GCC FDE pay at ₹35–90 LPA, the highest listed.
  • Bengaluru takes 30% of GCC hiring, followed by Hyderabad (15%) and Pune (12%).

FDE roles at GCCs in India ask you to take AI and data systems all the way to production inside a large, regulated organisation. At an enterprise GCC, the “customer” is usually the parent company’s own business units. At the India teams of global platforms such as Google Cloud and Databricks, it is the platform’s enterprise clients. Either way, these listings put more weight on data platforms, governance and measured results than startup FDE roles do.

This guide is built from seven forward deployed listings in India that we opened on 13 November 2026, plus recent GCC hiring data. It covers what you will own, how much experience you need, what pay looks like, and how to make the move.

Why GCCs are adding forward deployed roles

India’s GCCs are big and moving into AI work. Zinnov puts the number of GCCs in India at 2,117 in FY26, employing more than 2.36 million people, and says more than 1,200 of them have AI/ML capabilities, according to Business Today’s August 2026 report on the study. The same report says generative and agentic AI make up 52% of AI use cases in Indian GCCs.

Hiring follows. The foundit Insights Tracker, reported by Business Today in July 2026, says 64% of new GCC roles created in 2026 need AI, data science or intelligent automation skills. Analytics India Magazine, citing ANSR’s September 2026 GCC talent report, says FDE and AI-engineering postings rose roughly 1,000% over 12 months.

Most of that hiring happens in a handful of cities. Bengaluru takes 30% of GCC hiring, followed by Hyderabad at 15% and Pune at 12%, according to the foundit data.

Bengaluru takes nearly a third of GCC hiring; Hyderabad and Pune follow. foundit Insights Tracker via Business Today, July 2026.

Two kinds of GCC FDE role

The forward deployed jobs grouped under “GCC” fall into two quite different shapes.

1. Enterprise GCCs, where the customer is internal. These are captive centres of global companies outside the software industry. Warner Music Group’s Bengaluru role is a clear example: the Forward Deployed Engineer, Data & AI builds solutions for business stakeholders across WMG’s labels and global functions, not for paying clients. Simpliaxis makes the same point about GCC forward deployed work generally: the main difference is that “the customer is internal.” Many of these jobs carry other titles. Analytics India Magazine’s October 2026 piece cites a principal software and AI engineer at Fidelity International’s GCC whose team builds a context engine for AI agents. That is forward deployed work in substance, without the label.

2. India teams of global platforms, where the customer is external. Google Cloud, Databricks, UiPath, MongoDB and IFS all list forward deployed roles in India that embed with enterprise clients. Databricks’ Senior FDE, for example, builds and productionises data and AI solutions for customers and travels to them about 20% of the time. A Databricks senior FDE told Analytics India Magazine: “We are the trusted advisor for the client’s engineering teams.”

The two are not equally common under the FDE title. Platform companies use it widely. Enterprise GCCs mostly hire for the same skills under titles like AI engineer or applied AI engineer.

KRA 1: Ship production systems, not pilots

Every listing makes production the finish line. WMG asks its FDE to design, build and deploy production solutions using AI tools. Google’s Data Cloud FDE takes data and ML workloads from idea to launch, writing and tuning PySpark, SQL and Python on BigQuery, Spanner and AlloyDB. UiPath asks its senior FDE to own delivery from prototype to production and on to iteration. IFS wanted its FDE to launch a pilot for the first group of customers within three to six months.

At an enterprise GCC this usually means shipping fast for a business team that has no engineers of its own. WMG describes it as delivering quick solutions for many stakeholders, including dashboards in Streamlit and React.

KRA 2: Know the data platform deeply

This is where GCC and platform roles differ most from startup FDE work. Each listing names specific data platforms:

  • WMG: Python, ETL, and Snowflake or Databricks (either one is enough).
  • Google Cloud: BigQuery, Managed Spark, Spanner, AlloyDB, plus lakehouse design, metadata and access controls so agents work on trusted data.
  • Databricks: deep Apache Spark knowledge including runtime internals, and working knowledge of two or more clouds (AWS, Azure, GCP).
  • MongoDB: data modelling, indexing, sharding, and migrations from Oracle, PostgreSQL and SQL Server through dual-run and controlled cutover.

If you come from data engineering, this is your advantage. Our data engineer to FDE guide shows how to turn that background into an FDE profile.

KRA 3: Work within security, privacy and governance

Large enterprises run AI inside security perimeters, and the listings say so. Google asks its Data Cloud FDE to de-identify sensitive data such as payment and health records before building proofs on customer data. UiPath lists enterprise security, governance, compliance and operational readiness among its preferred skills. MongoDB’s architect role requires tenant isolation in multi-tenant designs. Analytics India Magazine describes GCC FDE work as operating inside security perimeters, compliance constraints and legacy stacks, including setting access controls for agent workflows.

In an interview, a story about getting an AI system approved by a security or risk team will carry real weight here.

KRA 4: Prove the result in numbers

GCC and platform listings ask for measured outcomes more often than vague “impact”. Google wants evaluation and monitoring in place for every ML and agent component, and migrations that show measured price-performance gains. IFS asked its FDE to tune AI agents for lower cost, efficiency and fewer errors. UiPath’s preferred skills include LLM evaluation and agent observability, and it asks FDEs to optimise performance, cost and reliability. Databricks’ listing does not mention evals by name.

KRA 5: Feed patterns back and make them reusable

Large organisations want each deployment to make the next one cheaper. Databricks asks FDEs to build accelerators and frameworks that feed the product roadmap. UiPath wants reusable patterns, playbooks and mentoring. Google and IFS both ask for feedback to the product team. WMG asks its FDE to contribute to engineering standards and align with its Data and Insights roadmap.

Pay: what is and isn’t public

None of the seven GCC and platform listings posts a salary. The best public benchmark is Taggd’s May 2026 FDE hiring guide. It puts GCC FDE pay at ₹35–90 LPA, the highest top end of any employer type in its table. It also gives ₹30–50 LPA for mid-level FDEs (3–6 years) and ₹50–80 LPA at senior level (7–12 years) across employer types.

Published bands, not posted offers. Taggd, May 2026.

How to move into an FDE role at a GCC

  • Choose your shape. Pick an internal enterprise GCC (stable, less travel, internal stakeholders) or a platform’s India team (external clients, 20–30% travel in some listings). Search both “forward deployed engineer” and “applied AI engineer”. Enterprise GCCs often use the second title.
  • Go deep on one data platform. Snowflake, Databricks or BigQuery came up repeatedly in these listings. Build one end-to-end pipeline with Spark or SQL and be ready to discuss query cost and performance tuning.
  • Ship one AI system on real-looking data with controls. Build a RAG or agent workflow over a dataset you have de-identified, with role-based access, an evaluation set and a basic monitoring dashboard. This covers KRAs 1, 3 and 4 in one project.
  • Write your results in numbers. Rewrite resume bullets as outcomes: cost per query cut, migration finished, accuracy on a fixed test set, time to production. The FDE Fit Check shows which KRAs your current experience already covers.
  • Collect stakeholder stories. Prepare two examples of shipping something for a non-technical team, and one of getting a system through a security or compliance review.
  • Target the hubs. Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune account for most GCC hiring in the foundit data. Our Bengaluru FDE jobs guide lists employers hiring there, and the Databricks and Google company pages cover those two employers’ India roles in detail.

Frequently asked questions

Do GCCs in India hire forward deployed engineers?

Yes, though the title varies. Warner Music Group lists a Forward Deployed Engineer, Data & AI role in Bengaluru that serves its labels and global functions, and India teams of global platforms such as Google Cloud and Databricks list FDE roles in Bengaluru. Many GCCs do similar work under titles like AI engineer or applied AI engineer.

How is a GCC FDE role different from a startup FDE role?

At an enterprise GCC the customer is usually internal: business units of the parent company, not paying clients. GCC and platform listings also lean harder on data platforms (Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery, Spark), security and compliance, and measurable cost or performance results, and some carry 20–30% travel to customers.

What is the salary of an FDE at a GCC in India?

None of the GCC or platform FDE listings reviewed for this guide posts pay. Taggd's May 2026 salary tables put FDE pay at GCCs at ₹35–90 LPA, against ₹25–70 LPA at AI startups and ₹20–50 LPA at IT services and consulting firms.

How much experience do GCC FDE roles ask for?

In seven India listings opened in November 2026, minimums ranged from 2 years (IFS in Pune, Google Cloud's FDE V GenAI role) through 3 years (Warner Music Group) and 5–6 years (Google Cloud Data, UiPath, Databricks) to 8 years for MongoDB's Forward Deployed Architect role.
