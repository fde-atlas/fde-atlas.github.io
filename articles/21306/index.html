Summary

This is a job listing from OpenWork for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) position in Helpfeel's newly established AI consulting division. The role involves understanding customer business challenges and driving AI-based solutions from concept and implementation through to adoption in the field, working alongside PM, sales, CS, and development teams. Duties include requirements gathering, PoC and prototype creation, architecture design and API integration with CRM and SaaS systems, organizing FAQs and internal documentation, and writing data extraction and cleaning scripts. The tech stack includes React, TypeScript, Python, Node.js, GCP, and MongoDB Atlas. Candidates need web application development experience and the ability to communicate technical topics to non-engineers; LLM, ETL, and knowledge-management experience is preferred. Salary ranges from 7 to 15 million yen, with remote work and flextime available.

Key points

Full text

  • Job Title
  • Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE / AI Consulting Division)
  • Job Description
  • In the AI Consulting Division, you will understand customers' business challenges and drive the conception, implementation, and adoption of solutions leveraging AI and technology. Working with PMs, business/operations consultants, sales, customer success, and development teams, you will connect the deployment and utilization of Helpfeel to improvements in customers' business performance. [Key Responsibilities] - Interviewing customers about their business workflows and AI utilization needs, and structuring their challenges - Building consensus with executives, business units, IT departments, and other stakeholders - Explaining technical matters, addressing security requirements, and creating PoCs and prototypes - Designing architectures that connect CRM, internal portals, and SaaS with Helpfeel, implementing API integrations, and defining technical requirements - Assessing and organizing FAQs, manuals, internal documents, etc., and documenting tacit knowledge - Creating scripts for data extraction, cleansing, and transformation - Designing and supporting the adoption of operational rules to keep information up to date - Feeding insights from the field back to the development team to drive general product improvements [Technical Environment] React, JavaScript/TypeScript, Node.js, Python, Heroku, Google Cloud Platform, MongoDB Atlas, GitHub Actions, and more. This position is ideal for those who want to dig deep into the background of customer requests and connect technology to business value. You can join the newly established AI Consulting Division at a stage where you can help shape the role and how the work is done.
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • [Required] - Experience developing or building web applications or business systems - Experience interacting with non-engineer customers or internal stakeholders to clarify requirements and challenges - Experience understanding business workflows and existing systems and translating them into technical solutions - Experience explaining technical content in a way tailored to the audience's level of understanding - Experience collaborating across multiple roles to move ambiguous challenges forward - Strong interest in AI/LLM and in perspectives that improve customers' business performance (P&L), along with a commitment to continuous self-learning [Preferred] ● Career and work experience - Experience supporting improvements in customers' ROI - Experience in business reform or system implementation at IT consulting firms, SIers, or consulting firms - Experience in implementation support, technical support, or professional services at SaaS companies - Experience in development, requirements definition, or project management involving customer negotiations - Practical experience as a pre-sales engineer, solutions engineer, or customer success engineer ● Technical and AI experience - Experience in business improvement or prototype development using AI/LLMs - Knowledge of LLMs, search, and knowledge systems - Experience with API integration and authentication/authorization design (OAuth/SAML, etc.) with multiple SaaS or on-premise systems - Practical experience in data extraction, transformation, and cleansing (ETL) ● AI-Ready and knowledge organization experience (especially welcome) - Experience designing, organizing, and operating FAQs, internal manuals, or knowledge management tools - Experience documenting and structuring organizational tacit knowledge - Experience facing and addressing "data quality" and "knowledge organization" challenges when introducing AI/LLMs - Experience in business improvement, knowledge organization, or FAQ operations in contact centers, customer support, or internal help desks
