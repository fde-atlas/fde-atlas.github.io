Databricks Forward Deployed Engineer: Role and Pay

FWD Deployment · 2026-10-11 · 2 min read

Summary

This article outlines the role and pay of forward deployed engineers at Databricks. FDEs sit in the professional services group, are billable on customer projects, and own architecture and implementation across data engineering, AI and apps, completing work to a written specification. A separate AI FDE team builds GenAI applications and targets experienced engineers rather than new grads. On October 11, 2026, Databricks's job board listed 101 postings with Forward Deployed in the title. Posted salary ranges include $182,000–$250,208 for a Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer in New York and $152,900–$210,155 for an AI FDE in the US. The piece also notes Databricks's $7B run-rate reported in August 2026.

Key points

  • Databricks FDEs are billable and deliver projects to specification.
  • They own architecture and implementation across data, AI and apps.
  • A separate AI FDE team targets experienced engineers, not new grads.
  • 101 Forward Deployed postings appeared on the job board in October 2026.
  • Posted pay ranges from about $153K to $250K depending on role and location.

What a Databricks forward deployed engineer does

Databricks FDEs own architecture and implementation across data engineering, AI and apps. The posting notes that FDEs are billable and finish projects to a written specification.

A separate AI FDE team delivers professional services engagements that build new kinds of GenAI applications, and Databricks says that role is for experienced engineers, not new grads.
