8 Skills to Become a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)

FDE Times · 1 min read

Summary

This FDE Times page centers on a codebasics video titled "8 Skills to Become a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" but does not list the eight skills in text. Its main substance is hiring-market data: 924 open FDE roles, up 33 in the past week on a like-for-like basis, with 80 new postings. A total of 196 companies are hiring, 25% of roles are remote-friendly, and the median US pay is $200k. Databricks is the top hirer with 107 open roles. The page also links related videos on explaining the FDE role, preparing for a Palantir FDE interview, and a solutions architect mock interview with a Salesforce SA, making it useful for readers exploring FDE careers and interview prep.

Key points

  • 924 open FDE roles, up 33 week over week.
  • 196 companies hiring; 25% of roles remote-friendly.
  • Median US FDE pay is $200k.
  • Databricks leads hiring with 107 open roles.
  • Main content is a video on 8 FDE skills.
