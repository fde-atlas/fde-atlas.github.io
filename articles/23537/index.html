Forward Deployed Engineer salaries in India vary widely in 2026. The role can range from an early-career customer-facing engineering position to a highly specialized AI deployment role requiring expertise in Generative AI, cloud architecture, data systems, and enterprise integrations.

For a complete introduction to the role, responsibilities and required skills, read what a Forward Deployed Engineer does.

Current salary data reflects that variation. Glassdoor reports a median total pay of around ₹13 lakh per year for Forward Deployment Engineers in India, with base pay commonly reported between ₹9 lakh and ₹16 lakh. However, recent FDE job postings show compensation reaching ₹25-₹50 lakh or more for experienced engineers and specialized AI roles.

So, how much can a Forward Deployed Engineer actually earn in India in 2026?

The answer depends heavily on experience, technical specialization, company type, location, and how much responsibility the engineer owns.

Forward Deployed Engineer Salary in India: 2026 Overview

Because FDE is still a relatively new job title in India, salary datasets remain smaller and more variable than for traditional Software Engineer or Data Engineer roles.

Glassdoor currently reports:

  • Median total pay: approximately ₹13 LPA
  • Typical base salary: approximately ₹9-₹16 LPA
  • Additional pay: approximately ₹50,000-₹4 lakh annually

Recent salary submissions also show 1-3 year FDE professionals reporting packages around ₹15-₹27 lakh in Hyderabad and Bengaluru.

At the same time, individual job postings show a much wider market.

For example, SurelyPlaced advertised a remote India FDE position at ₹10-₹15 LPA, while a 5+ year Bengaluru/Hyderabad position advertised through Weekday listed ₹25-₹40 LPA. FuturePath AI has also advertised an India-based FDE role at ₹35-₹50 LPA.

This means there is no single “standard FDE salary” in India.

FDE Salary by Experience Level

A useful way to understand the market is to look at experience together with role complexity.

ExperienceIndicative Salary RangeTypical Responsibilities
0-2 years₹6-₹15 LPACoding, debugging, integrations, client delivery support
2-5 years₹12-₹30 LPAEnd-to-end deployments, system design, cloud/data integrations
5+ years₹25-₹50+ LPAArchitecture, major customers, AI deployments, technical leadership

These should be treated as indicative market bands rather than guaranteed salary benchmarks. Company funding, equity, location, engineering depth, and AI specialization can push compensation significantly above or below them.

For example, Questt AI currently asks for 1-2 years of software engineering experience for its FDE 1 role, while its FDE II position asks for 5-6 years and includes architecture decisions, mentoring, and ownership across multiple client engagements.

The difference shows why years of experience alone do not determine compensation – the scope of ownership matters too.

Which Indian Cities Offer FDE Opportunities?

Current hiring suggests that Bengaluru is one of the strongest FDE markets in India, particularly for AI, SaaS, cloud, and enterprise technology companies.

Glassdoor reports an average Forward Deployed Engineer base salary of around ₹11 lakh in Bengaluru, although individual reported salaries vary considerably. Some Bengaluru employers show much higher company-level compensation.

Current FDE vacancies can also be found in:

  • Bengaluru
  • Hyderabad
  • Gurugram
  • Noida
  • Chennai
  • Coimbatore
  • Bhubaneswar
  • Remote India roles

Accenture alone has recently advertised FDE positions across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Gurugram, Chennai, and other Indian technology hubs.

For candidates interested specifically in compensation, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Gurugram, and remote roles at well-funded AI startups are worth watching closely.

Companies Hiring Forward Deployed Engineers in India

FDE hiring in India is expanding beyond small AI startups.

Companies with recent FDE or Forward Deployment openings include:

Accenture

Accenture is actively using the Forward Deployed Engineer title in India. Its recent roles cover Generative AI, Python, full-stack development, LLMs, Agentic AI, RAG, and enterprise workflow integration. Some positions require two years of experience, while senior roles require substantially more.

Wipro

Wipro advertised a Forward Deployed Engineer L1 position in Bengaluru requiring 5-8 years of experience and GenAI consulting skills for applications and infrastructure.

Questt AI

Questt AI has hired across multiple FDE levels in Bengaluru. Its early-career role emphasizes production-grade coding, debugging, client communication, and end-to-end delivery.

Ciroos

Ciroos currently lists an India FDE role for Bengaluru and Gurugram. The position combines customer implementation with production support, integrations, migrations, troubleshooting, and automation.

Ripik.AI

Ripik.AI has advertised a Forward Deployment Engineer role in Noida focused on deploying AI SaaS products for enterprise customers and coordinating across customers, product teams, engineering, and machine learning teams.

Other companies and startups are increasingly using related titles such as Forward Deployment Engineer, Forward Deployed AI Engineer, Customer Engineer, Deployment Engineer, and AI Solutions Engineer.

Which Skills Can Increase an FDE Salary in India?

Higher-paying FDE roles usually require more than general programming ability.

1. Generative AI and Agentic AI

Current Indian job postings increasingly mention:

  • LLMs
  • RAG
  • AI agents
  • Embeddings
  • Vector databases
  • Prompt engineering
  • Model evaluation
  • AI workflow orchestration

Accenture’s senior FDE listings explicitly call for experience with LLMs, Agentic AI, RAG, vector databases, semantic search, and AI governance.

2. Strong Programming Skills

Python is especially common, although Java, JavaScript, Go, and other languages appear depending on the company. FDEs are engineers, not simply consultants. Production-quality coding remains important.

3. Cloud and DevOps

Experience with AWS, Azure, or GCP can increase your value, especially when combined with:

  • Docker
  • Kubernetes
  • CI/CD
  • Monitoring
  • Cloud-native architecture

4. Data Engineering

SQL, data pipelines, ETL/ELT, databases, APIs, and enterprise data integration are highly relevant because many AI deployments depend on reliable data infrastructure.

Candidates who want deeper practical experience can also develop these skills through Prepzee’s Data Engineering course.

5. System Design

Senior FDEs may be responsible for architecture decisions involving scalability, reliability, security, APIs, and distributed systems.

6. Customer-Facing Engineering

This is one of the biggest differentiators.

Questt AI, Ripik.AI, and Ciroos all emphasize direct customer interaction and deployment ownership in their current roles.

Being able to convert an unclear business requirement into a production-ready technical system can significantly increase your value as an FDE.

Strong FDE capability comes from combining software engineering, AI, system design, deployment, and customer-facing problem solving rather than developing these skills in isolation. Prepzee’s Forward Deployed AI Engineer course brings these areas together through practical training focused on building and deploying production AI systems.

Does Generative AI Increase FDE Salary Potential?

Potentially, yes.

The higher end of India’s FDE market is increasingly connected with enterprise AI deployment rather than basic software implementation.

Companies need engineers who can take AI systems beyond prototypes and integrate them into actual business workflows. That requires knowledge of LLMs, RAG, AI agents, data pipelines, security, evaluation, APIs, cloud infrastructure, and production monitoring.

What Determines Your FDE Salary?

Two engineers with the same number of years of experience may receive very different offers.

Your compensation can depend on:

  • Software engineering depth
  • AI/GenAI expertise
  • Cloud experience
  • System design ability
  • Data engineering knowledge
  • Customer-facing experience
  • Production deployment experience
  • Company size and funding
  • Equity or stock options
  • Location
  • Leadership responsibility

An engineer who can independently discover a customer problem, design the architecture, build the solution, deploy it, and manage the customer relationship typically brings more value than someone who can perform only one part of that lifecycle.

Is FDE a High-Paying Career in India?

It can be.

Entry-level and conventional deployment roles may pay similarly to other engineering positions. However, experienced FDEs working in enterprise AI, well-funded startups, cloud platforms, or complex customer environments can command substantially higher compensation.

The current market already includes India FDE postings around ₹25-₹50 LPA, showing that the role has significant earning potential for engineers with the right combination of software, AI, cloud, and customer-facing skills.

The important point is that Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) salary growth comes from expanding ownership, not simply learning another programming language. A Forward Deployed AI Engineer course helps you develop skills in AI engineering, applied AI, machine learning, LLMs, and real-world AI deployment to accelerate your career growth.

If you can move from writing code to owning entire production deployments and business outcomes, your market value can increase considerably.

Final Takeaway

Forward Deployed Engineer salaries in India span a wide range in 2026 because the title covers everything from early-career deployment engineers to experienced AI architects working directly with enterprise customers.

General market data suggests compensation around the low-to-mid teens in lakhs for many roles, while experienced AI-focused FDE opportunities can reach ₹25-₹50 LPA or higher.

To compete for the better-paying roles, focus on the combination employers increasingly value: strong software engineering, data, cloud, Generative AI, system design, production deployment, and customer-facing problem solving.
