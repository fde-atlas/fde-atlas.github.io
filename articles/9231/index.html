Databricks FDE Salary 2026

FDE Pulse · 2026-11-01 · 2 min read

Summary

FDE Pulse compiles Databricks Forward Deployed Engineer pay from disclosed postings and the company's own posted salary bands. The median base is about $216K, with a disclosed range of $182K–$250K from one of 16 tracked postings. Official bands (Nov 2026) show FDE at $153K–$210K, Sr. FDE and AI FDE roles at $182K–$250K, and Senior Staff Applied AI Engineer up to $343K. Figures cover base salary only; equity and bonus would raise total compensation.

Key points

  • Median FDE base salary at Databricks is about $216K.
  • Disclosed range is $182K–$250K, based on 1 of 16 tracked postings.
  • Posted bands: FDE $153K–$210K; Sr. FDE and AI FDE $182K–$250K.
  • Senior Staff Applied AI Engineer tops out at $343K base.
  • Data comes from 63 US postings with pay bands, dated November 2026.
