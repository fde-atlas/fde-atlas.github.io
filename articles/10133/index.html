Logic, Inc. FDE Salary 2026

Summary

FDE Pulse compiles salary data for Forward Deployed Engineers at Logic, Inc. from 6 disclosed job postings. The median base salary is about $202K, with a disclosed range of $59K to $337K. Figures cover base pay only; equity and bonuses, common at AI labs and startups, would raise total compensation. Mid-level postings show a $140K median, while Senior+ and Manager/Lead roles both median around $202K. Open roles include FDE managers in Arlington and Chicago, healthcare-focused FDEs, and an AI-led FDE position in New York.
