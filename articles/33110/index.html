Google Cloud is hiring forward deployed engineers

On May 24, 2026, The Pragmatic Engineer reported that Google Cloud chief executive Thomas Kurian announced a new AI-focused organization inside the go-to-market team and is hiring forward deployed engineers for it. Author Gergely Orosz wrote that the interview process “reportedly” shrank from 4 to 6 interviews over several weeks to as few as two interviews in two days.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: The Pragmatic Engineer (reported).

What happened

The piece says Google hires these engineers as company employees and grants them stock. The author estimates that a Google role of this kind is about 25% coding, 50% integration and plumbing, and 25% meetings and customer hand-holding. That split is the author’s estimate.

The piece notes that engineers at the OpenAI and Anthropic ventures work for standalone companies, so any stock “likely” will not be in OpenAI or Anthropic. That is the author’s view. It quotes Aaron Levie, chief executive of Box.

Sources differ on one point. This piece says OpenAI “is not an investor” in the Deployment Company. Forrester, on July 30, 2026, and The Next Web, on July 8, 2026, say it is majority-owned and controlled by OpenAI.

“Hundreds (thousands?) technology companies will be hiring for these roles.”

Our reading

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

Three points bear on engineers: a reportedly shorter interview process, an estimated mix of 25% coding, 50% integration and 25% customer time, and equity in the employer, which may be a separate venture and not the model maker.

If you are hiring them

Google places the team inside go-to-market, not product engineering, and reportedly interviews in as few as two days. If you hire the same engineers, this suggests a slower process may cost you people.
