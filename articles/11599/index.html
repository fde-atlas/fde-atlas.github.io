PreSales blog for solution engineers — frameworks, demos, discovery

SE Rockstars · 2026-05-08 · 12 min read

Summary

SE Rockstars is an English-language blog for Solution Engineers, currently listing about 74 articles focused on PreSales work. Topics span discovery questions, demo technique, POC success criteria, earning technical wins, KPI systems, sales enablement and training, and career development, with an RSS feed. Recent posts increasingly address AI's impact on the role — including LLM-generated demo advice, skills for the AI era, and a comparison of Forward Deployed Engineers at OpenAI, Anthropic and Databricks versus traditional Solutions Engineers. Most pieces are practical 8–19 minute guides citing Gartner, Salesforce and Forrester data, and include templates and frameworks aimed at SEs and SE leaders.

Key points

  • Blog for Solution Engineers with ~74 PreSales articles
  • Covers discovery, demos, POCs, technical wins and KPIs
  • Recent posts examine AI's impact on the SE role
  • Includes an FDE vs. Solutions Engineer comparison
  • Offers templates, frameworks and industry statistics
