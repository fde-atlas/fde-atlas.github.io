Forward Deployed Software Engineer,… | Palantir Technologies

Zero G Talent · 2026-10-22 · 5 min read

Summary

Palantir is hiring Forward Deployed Software Engineers for its Warp Speed team, which focuses on revitalizing American manufacturing. FDSEs embed directly with customers, from factory floors to boardrooms, to identify problems and build custom solutions using large-scale data and AI. The role targets students graduating December 2026 or Spring 2027, requires US person status, and involves up to 25% travel. Estimated salary is $135,000–$145,000 per year, plus RSUs and a possible sign-on bonus. Palantir positions itself as the originator of the FDE model and emphasizes small autonomous teams, end-to-end project ownership, and mostly in-office or hybrid work.

Key points

  • Palantir claims to have pioneered the FDSE role, embedding engineers with customers.
  • The position sits in Warp Speed, focused on manufacturing and the US industrial base.
  • Applicants must graduate December 2026 or Spring 2027 and be US persons.
  • Salary is estimated at $135,000–$145,000, plus RSUs and possible sign-on bonus.
  • Travel up to 25%; most roles are office-based or hybrid.
