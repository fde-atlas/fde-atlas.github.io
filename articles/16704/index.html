Summary

This page is the full content directory of FDEChina, a Chinese-language site about forward deployed engineers, not a single article. It indexes 98 items organized into sections such as enterprise adoption, delivery practice, career development, and ecosystem resources, sorted by date. Recent entries include pieces on staffing a complete FDE team, FDE evolving from a role into an industry chain, Plank's FDE job market tracker covering 982 open positions, Perspective AI's compensation report based on 1,200 FDEs, case archives on Meituan's 310,000-line AI-driven refactor and Anthropic's multi-agent research system, plus job listings and programs from OpenAI, Accenture, and a16z. Useful as a navigation hub for browsing Chinese FDE resources.
