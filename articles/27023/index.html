Forward Deployed Engineer vs. Standard Software Developer: The Ultimate Guide

FDE Times · 1 min read

Summary

This guide compares forward deployed engineers with standard software developers. It explains that FDEs work on-site or close to customers, solving business-specific problems and shipping quickly, while traditional developers focus on building and maintaining internal products over longer cycles. The article covers responsibilities, required skills, career paths, and compensation, citing FDE Times hiring data: around 924 open FDE roles, a median US salary of about $200k, and Databricks as the top hirer with 107 openings. It concludes that FDE roles suit engineers who enjoy customer interaction and rapid delivery, while standard developer roles suit those who prefer deep product and systems work.

Key points

  • FDEs work close to customers and ship fast.
  • Standard developers focus on internal products and maintenance.
  • Median US FDE pay is about $200k.
  • Around 924 FDE roles are open; Databricks leads hiring.
  • FDE suits engineers who like client-facing, fast-paced work.
