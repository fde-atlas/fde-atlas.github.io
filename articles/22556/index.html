Forward Deployed Engineer Job in Houston, TX — Quanta Services

Talentify · 2026-09-25 · 4 min read

Summary

Quanta Services is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer in Houston, Texas. The role embeds a hands-on technical consultant in fast-moving projects, building AI applications, chatbots, internal tooling, and backend automation on the enterprise Databricks Lakehouse platform across domains like energy infrastructure, operations, finance, safety, and logistics. Requirements include 5-10 years in software development, systems integration, or technical consulting, 2+ years of hands-on enterprise Databricks experience, proficiency in a modern programming language, cloud familiarity (AWS, Azure, or GCP), and experience with AI tools such as GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, or Claude. Pay is not disclosed; flexible on-site, hybrid, or remote arrangements are offered.

About this Role

The Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) is a hands-on technical consultant embedded in some of Quanta's most innovative and fast-moving projects. You will partner with business leaders, domain experts, and engineers to design, prototype, and deploy real-world solutions focused on AI, applications, and automation build around our enterprise Databricks Lakehouse platform. From domain-specific chatbots to internal tooling and backend process automation, you'll work across a wide range of business and technical domains-energy infrastructure, operations, finance, safety, logistics, and more. This role emphasizes speed, adaptability, and using AI-powered development workflows to accelerate delivery and impact.

What You'll Do

Engage directly with internal stakeholders and operating units to understand technical needs and business prioritiesTranslate requirements into working software prototypes and full-featured solutionsUse AI-assisted tools for code generation, automation, data analysis, and research accelerationBuild integrations, applications, and scripts across cloud services, APIs, and enterprise systemsIterate rapidly on solutions with continuous feedback from subject matter expertsDeploy applications to production environments and provide post-deployment supportDocument learnings, design decisions, and reusable components to support scale and knowledge-sharingStay current with emerging AI tools and technologies-and help bring them into use across Quanta's ecosystem

What You'll Bring

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field- 5-10 years of experience in software development, systems integration, or technical consultingLicenses / Certifications:- None required- Cloud certifications or AI/ML specializations are a plusKnowledge and Skills:- 2+ Years hands on experience with Databricks in an enterprise setting- Proficiency in at least one modern programming language (e.g. Python, JavaScript, Java, Go)- Strong problem-solving mindset with a consultative approach- Familiarity with cloud environments (AWS, Azure, or GCP) and modern development practices (CI/CD, containers, APIs)- Experience using AI tools such as GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, or similar for development workflows- Ability to learn new technologies and domain contexts quickly- Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work across disciplines
