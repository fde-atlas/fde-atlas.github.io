What 100 Forward Deployed Engineer Job Descriptions Actually Ask For

FDE Handbook · 2026-09-04 · 9 min read

Summary

FDE Handbook classified 100 forward deployed engineer job descriptions by technical focus. Integration and deployment work was the most common dominant type, AI application roles came second, and platform/infrastructure-heavy roles were rare. Although 94% of postings mentioned AI or ML, only 34% were primarily AI-application roles, and over a third were strong hybrids. Atlassian alone contributed 23 postings, and the sample covers 89 unique description fingerprints across 50 company-role families. The authors caution this reflects postings, not employer market share, and advise candidates to prepare for interviews based on each JD's dominant technical center rather than a generic FDE interview.

Key points

  • Integration/deployment was the most common dominant role type.
  • 94% mentioned AI/ML, but only 34% were primarily AI roles.
  • Over one-third of postings were strong hybrids.
  • Atlassian contributed 23 of the 100 postings.
  • Interview prep should follow each JD's technical focus.

The short answer

The FDE title hides three different technical centers of gravity.

In this posting-level sample, integration and deployment work was the most common dominant type. AI application work formed a substantial second group, while roles dominated by lower-level platform or infrastructure concerns were much rarer. More than one-third of postings were strong hybrids.

The important distinction: 94% of these postings mentioned AI or ML, but only 34% were primarily AI-application roles. AI is increasingly part of the environment in which FDEs work; its presence alone does not tell you where the interview will go deepest.

AI application and agent-heavy

LLMs, agents, retrieval, evals, prompts, tool use, and model safeguards dominate the technical discussion.

Integration and deployment-heavy

APIs, authentication, workflows, prototypes, enterprise systems, and implementation details dominate.

Platform and infrastructure-heavy

Infrastructure, distributed systems, inference serving, latency, Kubernetes, GPUs, or reliability dominate.

How to interpret the numbers

This is a posting sample, not employer market share. The largest employer in the sample, Atlassian, contributed 23 postings. Location- and level-specific openings may share most of their JD.

The 100 postings represent 89 unique description fingerprints and 50 normalized company-role families. We report all three numbers so duplicated hiring campaigns are visible.

Classification describes where each JD appears to go deepest. It does not claim that a role performs only one kind of work.

What this means for interview preparation

Do not prepare for “an FDE interview” in the abstract. Read the JD for its dominant technical center, then weight coding, AI systems, integrations, or infrastructure accordingly.

Read the three-type interview guide →

Methodology

Inclusion: Active individual-contributor postings whose title explicitly contains Forward Deployed and Engineer.

Exclusions: Management titles, expired listings, recruiter-attributed listings, descriptions under 150 words, descriptions with no classifiable technical signal, and failed structured-data validation.

Sampling: The 100 most recent qualifying postings in the source feed. Multiple location- or level-specific postings from one employer may remain and are reported separately from unique description fingerprints and normalized role families.

Classification: A deterministic, disclosed keyword-evidence rubric assigns the dominant technical depth. Confidence reflects the gap between the two highest category scores; close scores are also reported as hybrid signals.

Copyright: Only derived signals, metadata, hashes, and source links are stored; full job-description text is not republished.
