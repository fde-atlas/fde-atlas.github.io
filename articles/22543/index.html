Forward Deployed Engineering Guides

FWD Deployment · 2026-10-11 · 2 min read

Summary

This page is a collection of guides from FWD Deployment for companies deciding how to engage forward deployed engineers. It covers hiring routes, scoping statements of work, pricing models, secure system access, code ownership, and handoff contracts. Industry-specific guides address healthcare (fixed pricing tied to FHIR and HIPAA acceptance tests), retail inventory, fintech data integration, insurance, telecom, government contracting under FAR, and defense staffing with a break-even method for cleared teams versus in-house hires. Several guides use Palantir as a benchmark, framing the core choice as buying a platform with its own engineers versus hiring an independent FDE firm on your existing data stack. Other pieces explain the Deployment Strategist role and the Applied AI Engineer role based on OpenAI job postings.

Key points

  • Step-by-step guides for hiring, scoping, pricing, and handing off FDE engagements.
  • Industry-specific advice for healthcare, fintech, retail, insurance, telecom, government, and defense.
  • Healthcare pricing: fixed prices where estimable, payments tied to FHIR and HIPAA acceptance tests.
  • Palantir used as a benchmark: platform with engineers versus independent FDE firm.
  • Access security protocol: agreement first, least privilege, time limits, monitoring.
