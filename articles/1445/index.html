Forward Deployed Engineer for AI Systems - AI-Powered Course

Educative · 2026-06-08 · 2 min read

Summary

An Educative course taught by Khayyam Hashmi, VP of Technical Content at Educative, covering the Forward Deployed Engineer role for AI systems end to end. It walks through customer discovery, scope documents, proof-of-concept sprints, pilot evaluation, and production handoff, plus AI architecture choices matched to enterprise constraints. The curriculum includes three case studies (financial knowledge assistant, shipment rerouting agent, hospital billing system) and an interview guide for top AI companies.

Key points

  • Covers the full FDE lifecycle from customer discovery to production handoff.
  • Teaches scoping, POC sprints, pilot evaluation, and stakeholder communication.
  • Includes AI systems architecture module with six lessons.
  • Features case studies: financial assistant, shipment rerouting, hospital billing.
  • Ends with FDE interview preparation for top AI companies.
