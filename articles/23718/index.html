Summary

This page presents "FDE前线部署工程师" by Bing Yue, published by Tsinghua University Press in November 2026 (ISBN 9787302729556), with sales reportedly over 2,000 copies. The 12-chapter book covers the FDE capability model, field research, enterprise ontology modeling, PoC validation, RAG knowledge engineering, agent engineering, evals and governance, and production delivery, plus case studies in manufacturing, e-commerce marketing, and cross-border expansion. The author also maintains an open-source companion guide for enterprise AI delivery.

Key points

  • 12 chapters spanning field research, ontology modeling, PoC, RAG, agents, evals, and production delivery.
  • Proposes a six-dimension FDE capability model and a five-level career growth path.
  • Stresses real authorization, responsibility boundaries, and organizational adoption over pure technical metrics.
  • Industry cases cover manufacturing, e-commerce marketing, and going global.
  • Sales exceeded 2,000 copies; an open-source delivery guide accompanies the book.

The following are publicly available enterprise AI cases from the website, free for extended reading.

According to the report case, to address the chlor-alkali industry's challenges of highly specialized production operations, major safety risks, and high production costs and energy consumption, Supcon Technology developed the TPT time-series large model application software for process industries, featuring intelligent Q&A, unit monitoring, predictive alerts, ion-exchange membrane lifespan prediction, dynamic current load allocation, and other functions, improving the intelligence level of production and equipment management. In its application at a chlor-alkali enterprise, it achieved an alarm accuracy rate above 95%, a 10% reduction in equipment costs, and energy savings of over 1%, providing a replicable case for digital transformation in the process industry.

According to the report case, RootCloud built a multimodal large model application for Shekou Youlian Shipyard based on its LinkRoot industrial large model foundation, aiming to optimize the quotation process for ship repair business through multimodal large model technology. The project uses a hybrid cloud architecture, integrating natural language processing, computer vision, and knowledge graph technologies. It can quickly parse complex all-English ship repair orders, technical drawings, and maintenance records, adaptively learn shipowner preferences, and accurately extract key information to generate structured quotation plans. The quotation cycle was shortened from 7 days to 1.5 days, paperwork workload was reduced by 35%, and the error rate dropped from 8% to 0.5%.

According to the report case, Baowu Equipment Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.'s remote equipment operation and maintenance platform for the steel industry (Baowu Zhiwei Cloud) focuses on industrial digital transformation and equipment management transformation and upgrading. With "four larges" at its core, it drives the intelligent evolution of equipment maintenance from "sensory judgment and experience-based decision-making" to "data-driven judgment and knowledge-based decision-making."
