Forward Deployed Engineer Leaders for Startups and Scale-ups

Beyond Elevation · 2026 · 5 min read

Summary

Beyond Elevation sells a fractional "Forward Deployed Executive" service for startups and scale-ups: operators with 15+ years of C-suite experience who have built companies past $1B in value and exited. Rather than advising, they embed in the client's leadership team and personally build and run AI systems for functions like finance, sales, operations, people, and compliance, one function at a time behind a named owner. Two engagement models: fractional at $5,800/month for 1–2 days a week, or 8–14 week projects from $30k. The first system goes live within 8 weeks, clients keep all code, agents, and documentation, and there is no notice period. The firm targets only startups and scale-ups, not enterprises.

Key points

  • FDEs are ex C-suite operators with exits, who build AI hands-on.
  • Fractional service starts at $5,800/month; projects from $30k over 8–14 weeks.
  • First system goes live in 8 weeks, one function at a time, with a named owner.
  • Clients own all code, agents, accounts, and docs; stop anytime.
  • Serves startups and scale-ups only, not 5,000-person enterprises.
