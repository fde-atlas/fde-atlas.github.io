Build Products Like a Forward Deployed Engineer

maven.com · 2026-03-23 · 1 min read

Summary

A free 30-minute lesson from Maven's AI-Native Product Manager course, taught by Vinoo Ganesh, CEO of Kepler and former FDE lead at Palantir (where he built Frontline) and Citadel. The session argues that companies like Palantir, OpenAI, and Anduril succeed by sending engineers into the field so products match real customer needs rather than going unused. It teaches the FDE mindset for product managers, including a week-by-week playbook of customer calls, support shadowing, and feedback loops, and covers avoiding two failure modes: overbuilding features nobody asked for and underbuilding products that collapse under real conditions.

Key points

  • Instructor Vinoo Ganesh built Frontline at Palantir and now leads Kepler.
  • Field-deployed engineers help products get adopted instead of shelved.
  • Includes a week-by-week playbook for customer calls and support shadowing.
  • Warns against overbuilding unwanted features and underbuilding fragile products.

Why this topic matters

Every AI company you admire, Palantir, OpenAI, Anduril, builds products differently. They send engineers into the field to watch software meet reality. The result: products that actually get adopted instead of sitting on a shelf. This session teaches you the FDE mindset so you can close the gap between what your team builds and what customers actually need.
