Forward Deployed Engineers In High Demand

Rediff.com · 2026-06-15 · 3 min read

Summary

Demand for Forward Deployed Engineers surged nearly 800% in the first three quarters of 2025, according to TeamLease Digital, though from a small base. The role, popularized by Palantir, places engineers inside customer environments to configure software, connect business goals to technical implementation, and speed up enterprise AI adoption. Roughly 500-800 roles are open globally, with 250-270 in India. Frontier AI firms like OpenAI and Anthropic lead hiring, alongside Palantir, Salesforce, Snowflake, and Google Cloud. Coforge plans to grow from 20 to 100 FDEs, and EPAM Systems from 50 to 100. Experienced FDEs earn about Rs 40 lakh annually, 23.7% more than customer support engineers.

  • India holds 250-270 of roughly 500-800 global openings
  • OpenAI, Anthropic, Palantir, and Google Cloud lead hiring

Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), dubbed the hottest job in tech today, are seeing surging demand in India as enterprises race to embed AI more seamlessly into client workflows.

Key Points

  • Demand for Forward Deployed Engineers has surged as enterprises seek faster and more effective AI adoption.
  • TeamLease Digital estimates FDE job demand jumped nearly 800 per cent during the first three quarters of 2025.
  • FDEs combine AI expertise, domain knowledge and business understanding to bridge technology and client needs.
  • Frontier AI firms, cloud providers and enterprise software companies are leading recruitment for these roles.

What Forward Deployed Engineers Do

A term made popular by Palantir, FDEs are software engineers embedded directly within the customer ecosystem to configure the technology firm's software to solve problems for the client.

They are not traditional enterprise architects but work in the client environment to facilitate AI platform adoption and bring huge domain expertise to command what can be built across the client's technology stack.

They also connect business objectives to technical implementation and design solutions using proven assets and patterns.

AI Adoption Drives Demand

Data from TeamLease Digital shows demand is growing at high double-digit rates, and in the first three quarters of 2025 alone, FDE job demand jumped by nearly 800 per cent.

This is on a small base but a clear indicator of how critical this role has become.

Globally, there are about 500 to 800 active roles at any given time with India having 250 to 270 open positions, making it an emerging hub.

As enterprises continue to witness slow adoption of AI due to a host of factors, many software and frontier model firms are looking to hire more such niche skilled engineers who would scale up adoption, ensure quicker turnaround, and better revenue.

IT Firms Scale FDE Hiring

Mid-tier information technology services firm Coforge, which has 20 such engineers now, wants to increase the number to 100 by the end of this financial year, its CEO and executive director, Sudhir Singh, said earlier this month.

"They are the cherry on the cake," Singh added. "That's the plan, going forward. And, a forward deployed engineer, given just the expectation from them, that's a skill set that is going to be gold.

"If we get to that number, we'll be very satisfied."

US software company EPAM Systems has about 50 such engineers in India and is also looking to scale that number to 100 during the same time period.

"The reason FDEs are prevailing today is they can have a good collaboration with business and they understand what business is talking.

"That reduces ideations.

"At the same time, the cost of tokens in an enterprise is increasing exponentially.

"With the help of FDEs, it will be lesser iterations, lesser costs, and more of the value which industry is expecting," said Srinivasa Kattuboina, vice-president of data and analytics practice at EPAM India.

Skills Behind The Role

When asked about the skills required, Kattuboina said the engineer "needs to have expertise in AI.

"You should have a good deal of data background or exposure to data, and then you should know the domain side."

Neeti Sharma, CEO of TeamLease Digital, says the two critical skills needed from such engineers are domain expertise and AI skills with experienced people making about Rs 40 lakh per annum.

"FDEs earn 23.7 per cent above customer support engineers and 16 per cent above technical account managers," she added.

Big Tech Bets On FDEs

Most of the hiring for this role is still being done by the frontier model firms such as OpenAI and Anthropic, followed by enterprise software firms which include Palantir, Salesforce, Snowflake, ServiceNow, and Oracle, besides professional services companies like PwC.

Google Cloud is hiring more FDEs to scale enterprise AI adoption.

'While having FDEs is not new for Google Cloud, the demand from customers and partners for Google enterprise AI products and Google engineers to help them embrace agent development is growing very rapidly,' CEO Thomas Kurian posted on LinkedIn.

  • Most of the hiring for such roles is being done by frontier model firms like OpenAI and Anthropic
