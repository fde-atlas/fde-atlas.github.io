Forward deployed engineers: role, skills and pay

Kernavique · 2026-10-11 · 8 min read

Summary

Kernavique explains the forward deployed engineer (FDE) role: engineers embedded in client programs who take platforms like Palantir Foundry and AIP from scoping to production. The article compiles hiring requirements and published salary ranges from OpenAI, Palantir, Databricks, Scale AI, Anthropic and others, drawing on Bloomberry's analysis of roughly 1,000 job postings. It also distinguishes FDEs from solutions engineers and describes Kernavique's contract and permanent staffing models.

Key points

  • FDEs own delivery end to end, from discovery to production rollout at the client.
  • Bloomberry found postings grew 1,165% year over year, with a median salary of $173,816.
  • Typical skills: Foundry pipelines and ontology, LLM workflows, Python or TypeScript.
  • 0% of analyzed postings were quota-carrying, separating FDEs from sales-adjacent roles.
  • Published ranges span Palantir's $135K–$200K to Anthropic's $320K–$405K for a manager role.

A forward deployed engineer works inside a client’s program to make Palantir Foundry, AIP or another AI platform solve a real problem, from scoping to production. Palantir and its implementation partners hire them, and so do AI companies such as OpenAI, Databricks and Scale AI. Kernavique fills the role two ways: Capacity places forward deployed engineers on contract through their own companies, and Search makes permanent hires.

Forward deployed engineer

What the role does

  • Takes an AI platform into a client’s operations, owning discovery, scoping, system design, build and production rollout with the customer.
  • Builds end-to-end LLM workflows and takes them into production; at Palantir the engineer also owns the customer’s generative AI strategy.
  • On Palantir programs, builds data pipelines and ontology in Foundry, working in tools such as Workshop and Ontology Manager.
  • Owns the architecture of the client’s data and AI solution and implements it across data engineering, AI and application code.
  • Feeds what the field teaches back to the product team, shaping the platform and the model roadmap.
  • Travels to the client: in ads read on October 11, 2026, OpenAI allows up to 50% travel, Palantir’s Forward Deployed AI Engineer up to 25% and Databricks’ Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer 20%.

Skills and credentials

  • Foundry pipelines and ontology

    Palantir partner ads ask for hands-on Foundry work. Accenture Federal Services wants Foundry data pipelines and ontology development, and Booz Allen’s Palantir Foundry Ontology Engineer ad lists Foundry Workshop, Ontology Manager and Maven Smart Systems.

  • AIP and LLM workflows

    Palantir’s Forward Deployed AI Engineer builds end-to-end LLM workflows and feeds what the field teaches back into the AIP product suite. The ad asks for past experience building with LLMs and ML basics: evaluation, training and problem decomposition.

  • Python and TypeScript

    Booz Allen asks for 3+ years of Python or TypeScript, and Palantir for strong coding in Python, Java, C++ or TypeScript/JavaScript. Bloomberry found Python in 66% of the forward deployed engineer postings it analyzed (updated January 25, 2026).

  • Customer-facing delivery

    OpenAI asks for 5+ years of engineering or technical deployment experience that includes customer-facing work, production code across frontend and backend, and experience building or deploying LLM-powered systems.

  • Platform certification

    Databricks’ Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer ad lists Databricks Certification. A Palantir Community forum post of July 27, 2026, said three Associate certifications would go live on August 3, 2026: Data Engineer, Application Developer and a new AI Engineer exam. The post describes each as timed, open-book and valid for two years, with hands-on Specialist exams in a live Foundry and AIP environment to follow.

  • Clearance for federal programs

    Federal Foundry work can need a clearance. Booz Allen’s ad in Arlington, Virginia, requires the ability to obtain a Secret clearance, and Accenture Federal Services’ Foundry role is on site in Washington, DC.

How we fill it

  • Forward deployed teams Capacity Two to five forward deployed engineers who join your Foundry, AIP or AI platform program together under a lead, on contract through their own companies.
  • Platform engineers Capacity A single forward deployed engineer on contract, matched to the written scope of a program you already hold.
  • Engineering search Search Permanent forward deployed engineers, hired against a specification we write back to you after the brief call.
  • Contract to permanent Capacity Start a forward deployed engineer on contract and convert them to a permanent employee at the end of the term, on terms agreed before the engagement starts.

Questions buyers ask

  • What does a forward deployed engineer do?
  • They work inside a client’s program, close to its users and its data, and take an AI platform from scoping to production. OpenAI’s San Francisco ad (published September 11, 2026) has its forward deployed engineers own discovery, scoping, system design, build and production rollout with strategic customers. Palantir likens its Forward Deployed AI Engineer to “a hands-on AI startup CTO”.
  • Forward deployed engineer or solutions engineer: what is the difference?
  • A forward deployed engineer is accountable for production systems, not for closing a sale. OpenAI’s ad measures its forward deployed engineers on production adoption, measurable workflow impact and feedback that changes product and model roadmaps. Bloomberry’s analysis of 1,000 postings (updated January 25, 2026) found that 0% were quota-carrying roles and only 8% mentioned on-target earnings. If the person you need mainly supports a sale before it closes, the role is closer to a solutions engineer, and the brief should say so.
  • What does a forward deployed engineer earn?
  • In the employer ads we read on October 11, 2026, Palantir gives $135,000 to $200,000 a year for its Forward Deployed AI Engineer in New York. OpenAI’s ad, read the same day, gives $185K to $300K with equity offered in San Francisco, and Scale AI’s gives a $252,000 to $315,000 base for its Staff Frontier Agents Engineer. Bloomberry (updated January 25, 2026) puts the median at $173,816 among postings that disclosed a range, and does not say whether that is base. Every ad is linked in the table above.
  • Should we hire forward deployed engineers on contract or permanently?
  • It depends on how long the work lasts and who owns it afterwards. A program with a defined scope and end date suits engineers on contract through their own companies, and contract Foundry roles are advertised on job boards such as Dice. Where the engineer will carry a product or a client relationship for years, hire permanently. Contract to permanent lets you start on contract and decide once you have seen the work.
  • How soon will we see forward deployed engineers?
  • We agree the shortlist timing with you at the brief call, based on the role and the market. Each engineer on the shortlist is named, with our notes on what they have built in Foundry, AIP or another AI platform.
