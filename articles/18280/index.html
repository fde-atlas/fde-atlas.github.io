ServiceNow and Accenture launch FDE program to accelerate enterprise-wide rollout of agentic AI

Full text

Over 300 pre-designed and pre-built AI agent capabilities and agentic workflows available on the ServiceNow AI Platform

【LAS VEGAS, USA】ServiceNow (NYSE: NOW), the "AI control tower" for business transformation, and Accenture (NYSE: ACN) today announced the launch of a Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) program to accelerate the deployment of agentic AI in enterprises from the pilot stage to full-scale, enterprise-wide adoption.

Through this program, ServiceNow's AI-native FDE team and Accenture's FDE team, equipped with industry expertise, will work together in customer environments to build agentic AI workflows on the ServiceNow AI Platform. This enables customers to quickly validate the value of AI in production environments before enterprise-wide rollout.

Comment from John Aisien, Senior Vice President and General Manager, Central Product Management and Security & Risk, ServiceNow This program is an initiative in which ServiceNow and Accenture collaborate to translate the business goals that our shared customers want to achieve with agentic AI into production operations that generate value. Rather than simply presenting implementation procedures, teams from both companies will enter customer environments and implement solutions by combining technologies from ServiceNow, the customer, and partner companies. The resulting value is visualized as concrete metrics on the ServiceNow AI Control Tower.

Comment from Ram Ramalingam, Head of Software & Platform Engineering, Accenture The challenge customers face is not whether to invest in AI, but how to leverage AI at enterprise scale. By combining Accenture's industry knowledge and implementation capabilities with the ServiceNow AI Platform, this program supports customers beyond vision and roadmap development, all the way to delivering concrete business outcomes. Through this collaboration, the two companies will elevate AI from the pilot stage to a core driver of accelerating business transformation.

From Pilots to Enterprise-Wide Outcomes According to Accenture's research report "Pulse of Change", while AI is expected to be a driver of revenue growth, only 32% of executives report experiencing sustained, enterprise-wide AI outcomes. This indicates that the challenge is not the technology itself, but the significant hurdle of moving from the vision and validation phases to full-scale deployment and outcome delivery.

Through this program, Accenture and ServiceNow teams will build agentic AI workflows directly into existing enterprise systems. They will drive the entire journey from initial build to enterprise-wide rollout, scaling to enterprise scale. This delivers business outcomes such as more advanced and faster operations, cost reduction, and improved customer experience.

ServiceNow and Accenture customers can implement agentic AI workflows by leveraging over 300 pre-designed and pre-built AI agent capabilities available on the ServiceNow AI Platform. This is supported by Accenture's deep industry knowledge and implementation capabilities, and ServiceNow's platform's broad deployment capabilities. By placing ServiceNow's AI Control Tower at the core as a common foundation for integrated management of AI agents, it becomes possible to centrally visualize agent performance and business outcomes while maintaining speed and appropriate control.

A key feature of this program is that the two companies work as one to rebuild each customer's unique value chain. The two companies will form dedicated "pods" (teams) specialized in the target value chain, combining platform- and AI-first design capabilities with industry-specific knowledge and expertise. This enables them to go beyond vision development and drive the entire journey from planning to production deployment as one.
