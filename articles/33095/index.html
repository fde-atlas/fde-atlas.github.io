Blend to grow forward deployed AI force from 500 to 750

Kernavique · 2026-08-20 · 2 min read

Summary

On August 20, 2026, Blend announced plans to grow its forward deployed AI engineering force from a stated 500 people to 750 by 2027. Kernavique notes that 750 is a target from Blend's own release, not a number already reached, and it does not indicate how many roles are currently open. CEO Oz Dogan is quoted in the announcement. Kernavique's reading: services firms of this kind now count forward deployed engineers in the hundreds, so companies building such teams may find themselves competing with Blend for the same candidates.

Key points

  • Blend plans to grow from 500 forward deployed engineers to 750 by 2027.
  • The 750 figure is a target, not an achieved headcount or current openings.
  • Both figures come from Blend's own release, quoting CEO Oz Dogan.
  • Kernavique says hiring firms may compete with Blend for the same engineers.

What happened

The release is on Blend’s own site and was read on November 20, 2026. Both figures are Blend’s own: 500 is its stated present count, and 750 is a target for 2027 and not a number already reached.

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

A target of 750 from a stated 500 suggests Blend plans to add forward deployed engineers by 2027. It is a target in a company release, so it does not tell you how many roles are open now.

If you are hiring them

Blend’s figures suggest a services firm of this kind already counts its forward deployed engineers in the hundreds. If you are building a team, firms like this may be seeking the same engineers you are.
