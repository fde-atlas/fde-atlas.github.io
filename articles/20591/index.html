Claude Code for FDE, the free Level 1 course

Traversaal · 2026-04-18 · 19 min read

Summary

Traversaal has open-sourced its highest-rated course, Claude Code for FDE, as the free Level 1 course in forward deployed engineering. Originally a four-week live cohort in June 2026, it comprises four sessions totaling 7 hours 49 minutes, split into 55 chapters and permanently free, with Moment Search answering questions from the recordings down to the exact second. Instructors are Hamza Farooq (Traversaal.ai founder and UCLA adjunct professor) and Aishwarya Ashok. Sessions cover Claude Code setup and CLAUDE.md, skills versus subagents, multi-agent systems and agent teams, evals with ground truth and rubrics, and deployment to Vercel or Fly.io. Reviews average 5.0, with learners praising its practical workflow patterns and accessibility for non-engineers; capstones include Sprint Zero, a kit that generates a product spec and working codebase from a company URL.

Key points

  • Free, open-sourced Level 1 FDE course: 4 sessions, 7h 49m, 55 chapters.
  • Topics span CLAUDE.md, skills vs subagents, multi-agent orchestration, evals, and deployment.
  • Taught by Hamza Farooq and Aishwarya Ashok; Moment Search indexes recordings to the second.
  • Rated 5.0 by learners, including many non-technical product managers.
  • Capstone includes Sprint Zero, generating a spec and codebase from one command.
