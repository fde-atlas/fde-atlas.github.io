Best Turing Alternatives (2026)

Ryz Labs · 2026-10-02 · 5 min read

Summary

The best Turing alternatives for 2026. Why US teams pick Ryz Labs for AI pod teams that ship on US hours, plus Tribe AI, Andela, Toptal and Globant.

Full text

Full disclosure: we're Ryz Labs, and we're one of the options on this page. We think we're the better choice for most teams, and we'll show you why. Everything we say about other companies comes from their own sites and public listings, linked at the end.

Turing is a San Francisco AI company. As of November 10, 2026, its homepage leads with "Accelerate superintelligence" and offers frontier AI labs datasets, RL environments and an expert network. For enterprises it sells "Turing Intelligence" in three parts (Advise, Augment and Build), delivered by "Turing pods" and forward-deployed engineers from a remote global network that it says has 4M+ engineers and experts. It doesn't publish prices. Buyers usually look at alternatives when they want something more specific: a dedicated senior team that builds an AI system in their own cloud and ships it to production, on New York hours, with named people they can meet before they sign. For that, our pick is Ryz Labs. Here's how the field compares.

Monthly figures are our math at 160 hours a month. Directory bands are entered by the firms themselves and are not quotes, so ask each vendor for a written monthly price for the same roles at the same seniority.

If you want AI built and running in production in your own systems, we're the best alternative to Turing. Our AI pod teams are dedicated groups of senior engineers (often around seven, including a tech lead, an ML engineer and backend engineers) that work in your repos, CI and standups, in your cloud. We build with AWS, Azure, GitHub and Postgres, using Anthropic and OpenAI models, and you see working software in weekly demos. Fortune 500 engineering teams trust us for exactly this.

Our pods' work is in production. Fraud detection for a global fleet company has scored 244K+ invoices in under 30 seconds each and surfaced $5.94M in fraud confirmed by the client's fraud team. A marketing compliance system for a global capital management firm took review of 8,000+ documents from days to hours. Others run an AI voice platform with 1M+ outbound calls, an AI driver-support agent (218K a year, in three languages) and an AI real-estate agent that works 24/7. See our case studies.

Our engineers work on US business hours, so review and decisions happen the same day. Only the top 1% make it, out of tens of thousands of engineers interviewed: recruiters source, our in-house ARC system ranks the pipeline, every candidate goes through a structured NTRVSTA AI interview, and recruiters review before and after. AI scores are advisory; people decide. If you came to Turing for engineers rather than a pod, we also provide top 1% senior engineers who work on your team, and you run the final interview.

Before you sign, you get a scoped plan, a price, and the names of the people who would do the work. For a head-to-head, see Ryz Labs vs Turing.

Tribe AI was founded in 2019 and builds production AI with forward-deployed engineers drawn from a network of senior AI practitioners (500+ as of July 2024, per TechCrunch). Its work runs in three phases: Map, Build and Activate. It joined Google Cloud's Gemini Enterprise for Legal and Financial Services program and says it is SOC 2 Type II certified.

Andela is a global technical talent marketplace, founded in 2014 and fully remote. It says it has 17,000 certified AI-native engineers and sells individual engineers, blended teams, AI system development and training. Its pricing page routes buyers to a discovery call, and time-zone overlap with US Eastern varies by region.

Toptal is a fully remote network of freelancers you can hire hourly, part-time or full-time, billed at a fixed weekly price plus a $79 monthly matching subscription. It says the average match takes under 24 hours and publishes a trial of up to two weeks. Clutch lists a $100-$149/hr band. You screen and manage the freelancers yourself.

Globant is NYSE-listed, grew out of Buenos Aires, and had 28,773 employees at the end of 2025. Its "AI Pods" are a monthly subscription with token-metered capacity, where AI agents do the work under Globant expert supervision, bought through its Glob.AI catalog. Clutch lists a $5,000+ minimum project and $25-$49/hr.

  • Your work is model training, evaluation or post-training research rather than a production system in your own stack.
  • You need AI specialists spread across many countries and time zones.
