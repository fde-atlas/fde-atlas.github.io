Wipro FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) India: Salary & Role

FDE India · 2026-11-06 · 8 min read

Summary

This article reviews Wipro's Forward Deployed Engineer hiring in India. A Chennai FDE role posted on 22 October 2026 and Bengaluru L1/L2 listings both ask for 5–8 years of experience combining agentic AI (LangChain/LangGraph, RAG) with hyperscaler cloud, Kubernetes, Terraform and DevOps work. The role covers building operational AI agents, deploying infrastructure, governance, and client-side delivery. Wipro's April 2026 AI-Native Business & Platforms unit and its June 2026 Claude Center of Excellence with Anthropic both rely on FDE teams, though Wipro has not disclosed headcount targets; Reuters reports TCS plans up to 8,900 FDEs and Infosys about 6,000. Wipro publishes no FDE salary data, so the article uses Levels.fyi's India median of about ₹25 LPA and AmbitionBox's Wipro full-stack range of ₹8.1–18.3 LPA as rough bounds, and closes with interview preparation advice.

Key points

  • Wipro is hiring FDEs in Chennai and Bengaluru, requiring 5–8 years of experience.
  • The role combines agentic AI with hyperscaler cloud, Kubernetes and Terraform.
  • Wipro's AI-Native unit and Claude Center of Excellence both depend on FDE teams.
  • Wipro has no headcount target; TCS plans ~8,900 FDEs, Infosys ~6,000.
  • No published Wipro FDE pay; India FDE median is roughly ₹25 LPA.

Wipro hires Forward Deployed Engineers in Chennai and Bengaluru to build and run agentic AI systems inside client environments. The India listings ask for 5–8 years of experience. They want someone who can write LangGraph agents and deploy them on Kubernetes with Terraform, not a pure data scientist.

What the FDE does at Wipro

The clearest picture comes from a Chennai role titled “Forward Deployed Engineer (AI, Hyperscaler Cloud & DevOps)”, posted on Wipro Careers on 22 October 2026. The FDE:

  • Builds AI agents for operations. Designs and deploys GenAI agents and agent workflows for incident response, root-cause analysis and reliability work.
  • Runs them on cloud infrastructure. Deploys on Azure, AWS or GCP and on Kubernetes, wired into CI/CD, observability and ITSM tools.
  • Builds reusable parts. Writes APIs and connectors that can be reused across clients.
  • Owns governance. Implements guardrails, audit logging and rollback for AI systems.
  • Owns the deployment. Handles rollout and troubleshooting, working with client DevOps, SRE and security teams.

Bengaluru has listed “Forward Deployed Engineer L1” and “L2” roles too. An L1 posting seen on Workopia and refresh.cv in November 2026 is on-site and full-time, with the same mandatory skill: “GenAI Consulting for Apps & Infra”.

Why Wipro is building FDE teams

Two announcements explain the push. In April 2026 Wipro set up an AI-Native Business & Platforms unit, led by Nagendra Bandaru. It owns platforms such as NetOxygen for lending and Wipro’s healthcare and telecom AI products, and it is “supported by dedicated forward-deployed engineering teams”.

In June 2026 Wipro opened an Applied AI Center of Excellence for Claude models at its Bengaluru hub, with Anthropic. Wipro says a global pool of FDEs trained on Claude will work within client environments. It also plans to certify 10,000 front-line delivery staff on Claude over 18 months.

In September 2026 CTO Sandhya Arun told Reuters that Wipro is expanding its FDE pool but declined to give a target. The same report says TCS plans up to 8,900 FDEs and Infosys about 6,000. See our TCS and Infosys pages to compare.

How to prepare

  • Build an ops agent end to end. Use LangGraph to build an agent that reads alerts, pulls logs and suggests a root cause. Add tool calling and a RAG step over runbooks. This mirrors the Chennai job description closely.
  • Deploy it the way Wipro would. Containerise it, run it on Kubernetes on one hyperscaler, and define the infrastructure in Terraform behind a CI/CD pipeline. Add dashboards in an observability tool.
  • Add governance. Put in guardrails, audit logs and a rollback path, and be ready to explain them. The listing names all three.
  • Get one hyperscaler certification. The Chennai role prefers certifications, and they help at IT services firms where clients ask for them.
  • Prepare client stories. Have two examples ready: a production incident you resolved with a client team, and a time you changed a design for security or compliance. Our FDE interview questions guide has worked answers.

Two of the three Wipro FDE listings we found were in Bengaluru — see FDE jobs in Bengaluru for other employers hiring there.

Wipro FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Application: FDE roles appear on Wipro Careers under titles such as 'Forward Deployed Engineer', 'Forward Deployed Engineer L1' and 'L2', and are mirrored on job boards. Some postings close within weeks, so search the exact title regularly.
  • Technical and client rounds: Wipro does not publish an FDE-specific interview format. Based on the Chennai listing, expect depth on agentic AI frameworks, RAG, one hyperscaler, Kubernetes, Terraform and CI/CD, and questions on working inside client environments.
  • Offer and grading: Listings are graded L1 or L2, but Wipro doesn't publish what those grades pay or how they map to its wider bands. Ask the recruiter which band the role sits in.

Frequently asked questions

Does Wipro hire Forward Deployed Engineers in India?

Yes. Wipro posted a Forward Deployed Engineer role in Chennai on 22 October 2026, and Forward Deployed Engineer L1 and L2 roles in Bengaluru have appeared on Wipro Careers and job boards. Some of the Bengaluru postings were no longer open when we checked in November 2026.

How much experience does a Wipro FDE role need?

The Chennai and Bengaluru L1 listings both ask for 5–8 years. The Chennai role wants hands-on agentic AI work in Python with LangChain or LangGraph, plus one of Azure, AWS or GCP, Kubernetes, Docker and Terraform.

What is the salary of a Wipro Forward Deployed Engineer?

Wipro doesn't post pay for its FDE roles and no Wipro-specific FDE salary data is public. As a rough guide, Levels.fyi puts the median FDE total pay in India at about ₹25 LPA from 11 salaries, while AmbitionBox shows Wipro Full Stack Engineers earning ₹8.1–18.3 LPA.

How many FDEs is Wipro hiring?

Wipro hasn't said. Its CTO told Reuters in September 2026 that Wipro is expanding its pool of forward-deployed engineers but declined to give a target, saying it would likely be in line with peers.
