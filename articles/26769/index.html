Tencent Cloud ADP Forward Deployed Engineer System, 25 Scenario Packages, and Joint Delivery Practices with KPMG

Original title: 腾讯云 ADP FDE 前沿部署工程师体系与 25 个场景包、毕马威联合交付实践

Tencent Cloud ADP · 2026 · 3 min read

Summary

Tencent Cloud describes how its ADP agent development platform distills real-world deployment experience into an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) system, arguing that most effort in enterprise AI projects goes to system integration, permissions, evaluation and iteration rather than prompt writing. It launched 25 service scenario packages across five industries, targeting 70% reuse and 30% customization, and partners with KPMG, which has privately deployed ADP for over a thousand internal users. The partnership covers pre-, mid- and post-deployment stages, with KPMG stressing that complete FDEs must combine engineering with industry and business expertise.

  • KPMG jointly delivers FDE services across pre-, mid- and post-launch stages, contributing business experts.

When an enterprise AI agent project goes live, beyond building the features, you also need to integrate systems, manage permissions, validate results, and keep iterating. Tencent Cloud has distilled ADP's experience solving these problems in real projects into an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) system, and is bringing it to more customers through 25 industry service scenario packages and partner collaboration. KPMG is both a user of ADP and a partner working with Tencent Cloud to jointly provide FDE services.

The Hard Parts of Deployment Go Beyond Features

Wu Yongjian, Vice President of Tencent Cloud and head of Tencent Cloud's intelligent product R&D, said that what makes Agent deployment hard is not just any single feature, but more importantly how to integrate systems, manage permissions, validate results, and keep iterating after launch. In real enterprise AI projects, most of the effort goes into these stages, with Prompt writing accounting for only a small part. The ADP team has solved these problems one by one in real projects, and distilled the hands-on experience into a deployment methodology.

Partners' Software Capabilities Are Becoming Agent Capabilities

Zhang Guo, Vice President of Tencent Cloud and head of ecosystem expansion, believes that for product-focused partners, the software features, industry knowledge, and professional tools they have accumulated will all become capabilities that agents can leverage in the future. Agents can invoke these capabilities far more efficiently than traditional tools and related software. A well-built industry software product with deep industry know-how, empowered by agents, can serve many more users.

ADP FDE System: Sharing Methods and Experience with Partners

ADP is Tencent Cloud's first product to offer FDE certification. Through ADP's FDE system, Tencent Cloud shares its deployment methods and experience with partners without reservation, helping them avoid detours.

25 Scenario Packages: 70% Reusable, 30% Customized

To help partners deliver better, Tencent Cloud has also launched a core partner enablement program, organizing the results accumulated from past projects into 25 service scenario packages covering five industries: retail, manufacturing, education, finance, and healthcare.

The goal for each scenario package is to become a standard service, or even a product. In the initial stage, the aim is 70% reusable and 30% customized, so delivery teams don't have to start from scratch on every project.

KPMG: A Dual Relationship as User and Partner

Liu Xiaoguang, Head Partner of KPMG China's AI Transformation Office and Head Partner of Digital and Intelligent Transformation Consulting, explained that KPMG's relationship with Tencent Cloud is twofold: as a user, KPMG uses Tencent Cloud's cloud and AI products; and as a partner, the two sides jointly serve the market and customers in the FDE space.

Internally, KPMG has completed a private deployment of ADP, which currently supports over a thousand users and hundreds of internal developers building agents on ADP.

KPMG: Joint FDE Coverage Across Early, Middle, and Late Stages

KPMG's FDE collaboration with Tencent Cloud is divided into three stages: early, middle, and late. In the early stage, high-value AI scenarios within the customer's organization are identified; in the middle stage, solid FDE work brings these scenarios to life; in the late stage, after launch, continuous promotion and operations ensure the solution is not just built well, but used well.

KPMG: A Complete FDE Requires Industry and Business Experts

In the division of labor between the two sides, Tencent provides advanced AI products, easy-to-use cloud services and models, and extensive ecosystem resources, while KPMG provides the people.

In Liu Xiaoguang's view, an FDE is not just an engineer. The first type of FDE is an industry and business expert who understands the customer's specific segment, can work with the customer to sort out AI requirements, and uses the language of business and measurable value to land AI requirements and scenarios through training and workshops. These capabilities combined, built on top of Tencent's ecosystem and AI product capabilities, make a truly complete FDE.
