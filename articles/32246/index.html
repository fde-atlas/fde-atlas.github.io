FDE hiring: like-for-like open roles fall by 60

FDE Times · 2026-11-16 · 4 min read

Summary

FDE Times' hiring tracker counted 865 open Forward Deployed Engineer roles for 9–15 November 2026, up from 646, but the rise came from adding 91 newly tracked companies that contributed 279 roles. On a like-for-like basis, openings fell by 60, the second consecutive weekly decline. Databricks saw the largest drop, from 124 to 94. Companies removed 113 listings while posting only 53 new ones. Palantir led with 95 open roles, and New York topped cities with 82. The median midpoint of US posted pay was $206,000, and Python remained the most common required skill at 64% of roles.

Key points

  • Like-for-like FDE openings fell 60, a second straight weekly drop.
  • Total count of 865 was inflated by 91 newly tracked boards.
  • Databricks posted the biggest decline, 124 to 94 roles.
  • 113 roles closed versus only 53 new postings.
  • Median US pay midpoint was $206,000; Python leads skills at 64%.

In brief

  • Open FDE roles rose from 646 to 865, but without newly tracked boards the count fell by 60, after a fall of 7 the week before.
  • Databricks went from 124 to 94 open roles, the largest drop of any company, with 31 closed and 1 new.
  • 113 roles closed against 70 the previous week, while new postings fell to 53 from 63.

FDE Times Hiring Tracker data shows the count of open Forward Deployed Engineer roles rising from 646 to 865 between 9 November and 15 November 2026. That rise comes from wider coverage, not more hiring. The tracker began reading the boards of 91 more companies during the week, which added 279 roles. With those set aside, open roles fell by 60.

The same like-for-like measure fell by 7 in the week from 2 November to 8 November.

Lighter: roles from newly tracked companies, not new hiring. Source: FDE Times Hiring Tracker, companies’ own careers boards.

Databricks posts the largest drop

Databricks went from 124 open roles to 94, closing 31 and posting 1 new one. No other company in the tracker lost as many. Salesforce fell from 28 to 22, Palantir from 100 to 95 and Scale AI from 16 to 12.

In total, 21 companies ended the week with fewer open roles and 15 with more. The biggest gains were small: OpenAI went from 37 to 39, Tennr from 2 to 4 and Thomson Reuters from 8 to 10. Each added 2.

More roles closed, fewer new ones posted

Companies took down 113 roles during the week, compared with 70 the week before. New postings on boards that were already tracked came to 53, down from 63. Every closed role carried a posting date, and the median closed role had been open for 35 days.

The number of companies with at least one open role rose from 97 at the start of the week to 183 at the end. Since tracking began on 7 October 2026, the tracker has seen FDE roles at 196 companies.

Palantir and Databricks still lead the table

Palantir ended the week with 95 open roles and Databricks with 94. Amazon (AWS), one of the newly tracked boards, came third with 68. OpenAI had 39, Salesforce 22 and ByteDance, also newly tracked, 20. Rogo had 19.

New York led the cities with 82 open roles, ahead of San Francisco with 60, London with 52 and Washington, D.C. with 38. Tokyo had 17.

Source: FDE Times Hiring Tracker, companies’ own careers boards.

North America accounted for 502 roles, or 58%. Europe and the UK had 152, or 18%, and Asia-Pacific 105, or 12%. Postings that are marked as remote or open to remote work numbered 209, which is 24% of open roles.

Mid-level FDE titles make up the bulk of the market

Roles titled Forward Deployed Engineer made up 584 openings, or 68%. Deployment strategists accounted for 108, or 12%, and AI and ML FDE roles for 76, or 9%.

Source: FDE Times Hiring Tracker, companies’ own careers boards.

Mid-level roles made up 60% of openings, with 518 roles. Senior, staff and lead roles came to 293, or 34%. Early-career openings numbered 54, or 6%.

Source: FDE Times Hiring Tracker, companies’ own careers boards.

Posted pay ranges in 228 postings, from 60 companies with US-located roles, put the median midpoint at $206,000. The median range ran from $163,000 to $250,000. The middle half of midpoints sat between $168,000 and $216,000.

Python still leads the skills list as JavaScript slips

Python appeared in 550 open roles, or 64%, down from 67% the week before. AI agents were named in 511, or 59%, and APIs in 370, or 43%. Machine learning came up in 37% and LLMs in 35%.

Source: FDE Times Hiring Tracker, companies’ own careers boards.

JavaScript fell to 25% of open roles from 34%, and GCP to 20% from 26%. TypeScript rose to 34% from 33% and AWS to 32% from 30%. ETL and data pipelines entered the top ten at 21%, while Azure dropped out of it.

Methodology

  • Source: the official careers boards (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever) of companies that hire FDEs, read several times a day. Only roles whose title names forward deployed engineering or deployment strategy count; 196 companies have had at least one such role since tracking began.
  • Period: 9 November 2026 to 15 November 2026, UTC.
  • New roles: roles first seen on the board of a company that was already tracked. Roles from a company whose board was just added are wider coverage, not new hiring.
  • Closed roles: roles the company took down. Days open are counted from the posting date the company gives.
  • Role type and seniority are read from the job title; region from the location in the posting.
  • Pay: only base-pay ranges published in US-located postings; the figure is the median of each range’s midpoint.
