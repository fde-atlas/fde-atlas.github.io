What exactly is an FDE

FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer. In Chinese it's called 前沿部署工程师, and some also call it 前端部署工程师. It fills the gap between "what the product can do" and "what the customer needs"—instead of sitting at headquarters writing feature modules, you take the product into the customer's factory, bank, or hospital and get things running on-site.

"Forward Deployed" is a military term, originally referring to special units deployed on the front lines. Borrowed for this role, it describes people who can both fight tough battles and go deep into the field. Palantir is the company that proved out this model—at one point, FDEs made up nearly half of its workforce.

What sets FDEs apart from on-site developers and implementation consultants is that FDEs distill field experience into product capabilities—they are strategic partners, not vendors billing by person-day. For specific differences, skill matrices, and real job postings, scroll down to each section.

Three main tracks, start here

Build the foundation

  • Python full-stack + frontend frameworks
  • LLM applications: RAG / Agent / Prompt
  • Cloud and containers: AWS, Docker, K8s
  • Linux and system deployment

Learn the business

  • Go deep in 1–2 industries (manufacturing/finance/healthcare)
  • Understand business processes and decision chains
  • Translate industry jargon into engineering language
  • Combine technical + business insight

Go to battle

  • Practice on real projects, don't just run demos
  • Get a relevant certification
  • Accumulate real-world case studies you can talk about
  • Use self-assessment questions to find and fill gaps
