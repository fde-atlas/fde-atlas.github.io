FDE Salary in India 2026: ₹13–18 Lakh Typical, Freshers & Senior

NeuraPath Academy · 2026-08-11 · 13 min read

Summary

This article compiles November 2026 salary data for Forward Deployed Engineers in India: Glassdoor shows an average base of ₹12.6 lakh (50 salaries) and Levels.fyi a median total pay of ₹17.6 lakh (12 salaries). The author, who sells an FDE course and discloses this, stresses that tiny samples make these figures unstable—Levels.fyi's median dropped from ₹31.6 lakh to ₹17.6 lakh in three weeks purely due to sample changes. The piece covers fresher entry paths (₹5–6 lakh via adjacent roles, ₹14 lakh+ at product companies), posted ranges from ₹5–7 lakh (Spacetil) to ₹70–90 lakh plus equity (Cartesia), city distribution of 104 open roles, and skill demand, where client-facing work tops AI agents and Python.

Key points

  • Typical Indian FDE pay is ₹13–18 lakh a year, but samples are very small.
  • Levels.fyi's median fell from ₹31.6 lakh to ₹17.6 lakh in three weeks due to sample churn alone.
  • Freshers enter via associate or solutions roles at ₹5–6 lakh, or ₹14 lakh+ at product companies.
  • Posted roles range from ₹5–7 lakh (Spacetil) to ₹70–90 lakh plus equity (Cartesia).
  • Across 104 postings, client-facing skill was most demanded, ahead of AI agents and Python.

Reported pay for a Forward Deployed Engineer in India sits around ₹13 lakh to ₹18 lakh a year. Glassdoor's average base pay is ₹12.6 lakh (50 salaries) and Levels.fyi's median total pay is ₹17.6 lakh (12 salaries), both read on 10 November 2026. The spread is wide: Levels.fyi's top quarter starts at about ₹42 lakh, and one posted India role paid ₹70–90 lakh plus equity. Freshers do get in: Indian AI startups such as Sarvam AI and HighRadius have posted FDE roles open to 0–3 years, and many freshers start as associate, solutions or AI engineers at about ₹5–6 lakh on typical reports, or ₹14 lakh and more at product companies, then grow into FDE pay as they ship.

Those numbers come with a warning label. FDE is a new title in India, so every salary site has a small sample, and small samples jump around. Three weeks before this update, Levels.fyi showed a median of ₹31.6 lakh from 20 salaries. Today it shows ₹17.6 lakh from 12. Nothing happened to FDE pay in those three weeks; the sample changed.

I teach an FDE course, so read this as one practitioner's summary. Every figure below has its source and the date it was read, so you can check it rather than take my word for it.

If you want one number, about ₹15 lakh a year is a fair middle for someone with a few years' experience. If you want a range, ₹9 lakh to ₹42 lakh covers most reported salaries. The top of the market is real but thin.

Why the salary sites disagree

Glassdoor and Levels.fyi are both honest about what they collect. They just collect different things from different people, and neither has many FDE salaries from India yet.

Site What its headline figure is
Glassdoor India ₹12.6 lakh: the average base pay across 50 salaries
Levels.fyi ₹17.6 lakh: the median total pay (base, bonus and stock) across 12 salaries

Both pages read 10 Nov 2026.

With so few salaries, three high reports are enough to move the median by lakhs. That is what happened in October, when Levels.fyi briefly showed ₹31.6 lakh. Its users also lean towards product companies and global firms, so its numbers run higher than most other sites for every title, this one included. And stock counts in its total, which at a startup can be a large part of the offer on paper.

Some pages are simply broken. Indeed India's FDE salary page reported "-1 salaries" when I opened it, so it is not quoted here. A "₹12 lakh average from Indeed" that was shared widely in October matches Glassdoor's January figures, not any Indeed dataset.

One survey you may come across, from FDE Academy (a training company that also sells an FDE course), puts the median for 0–3 years at ₹32 lakh. It gives no sample size or method, and every salary dataset above is far lower, so we would not plan your finances around it.

What does an FDE fresher earn in India?

Freshers do get into FDE work in India, and the way in is clearer than the title suggests. Indian AI startups hire most of them. Sarvam AI's Forward Deployed Software Engineer post asks for 1–3 years and counts customer-facing internships as experience; Gushwork asks for 1–2 years; HighRadius has posted entry-level FDE roles for 0–2 and 0–3 years. None of these publishes pay, so the figures below come from the closest titles.

Freshers also come in through titles that do the same work under another name: associate FDE, solutions engineer, implementation engineer, junior AI or GenAI engineer, and graduate or campus programmes at services firms and global capability centres. Here is what those first roles usually pay:

On PayScale, people with under a year of experience earn about ₹5.4 lakh as software engineers (414 reports), ₹5.5 lakh as solutions engineers (only 6) and ₹6 lakh as ML engineers (39). Levels.fyi's entry-level software engineer median is much higher, ₹14.5 lakh (2,209 reports), because its users skew to product companies and big tech. That gap is good news for a fresher: the kind of company you aim for moves your first offer more than the title does.

Pay also rises quickly once you are in. Recent Glassdoor reports from FDEs with 1–3 years' experience, submitted between 21 October and 3 November 2026, ranged from ₹6–8 lakh in Ernakulam and ₹7–9 lakh in Hyderabad to ₹14–17 lakh in Mumbai and ₹20–24 lakh in Bengaluru, with one Hyderabad report above ₹60 lakh.

One note on job boards. Our FDE jobs board listed 104 open FDE and related roles on 10 November 2026, most of them labelled mid or senior. Job boards show lateral hiring well and fresher hiring badly, because campus drives, trainee programmes and startup referrals are rarely posted there. So read it as a map of who is hiring, not as your odds.

What gets freshers hired into these roles, and at the top of the band, is something deployed that they can demo and explain to a non-technical person. A certificate on its own rarely moves the number; a working project with a short write-up usually does.

Mid-level and senior FDE salary in India

With three to six years of shipping software for customers, reported pay moves into the ₹15–42 lakh range that sits between Levels.fyi's 25th and 75th percentiles. Senior FDEs at product companies and well-funded AI startups report ₹42–55 lakh and above, and founding-team roles go higher with equity. For comparison, Levels.fyi's median for a solution architect in India, a common senior route for FDEs, is about ₹45.7 lakh (362 salaries).

Do FDEs earn more than software engineers in India?

The data cannot say yet. Levels.fyi's India software engineer median across all levels is about ₹30.5 lakh from more than 21,000 salaries; its FDE median is ₹17.6 lakh from 12. With a sample that small, the FDE figure tells you very little about how the two titles compare. What we can say from job posts is that FDE roles ask for the same engineering plus client-facing work, and that the ceiling depends on the company far more than on the title. Our full comparison is in Forward Deployed Engineer vs software engineer.

What that CTC means per month in hand

CTC is not what lands in your account. Here is a rough monthly take-home for four offers, so you can compare an offer letter with your rent.

Annual CTC Approximate in-hand per month
₹6 lakh ≈ ₹46,200
₹10 lakh ≈ ₹79,500
₹18 lakh ≈ ₹1,34,000
₹28 lakh ≈ ₹1,96,000

Our arithmetic, assuming the whole CTC is fixed pay, employee and employer PF at ₹1,800 a month each, ₹2,500 a year professional tax, the new tax regime slabs introduced for FY 2025-26 with the ₹75,000 standard deduction, and no other deductions. Variable pay, a higher PF contribution, insurance or a different regime all change it. Check the current slabs before relying on it.

If a startup offer includes stock, value it at zero when comparing monthly pay, and treat whatever it is worth later as a bonus.

Where the FDE jobs are in India

Of the 104 openings on our jobs board on 10 November 2026, Bengaluru had 36, Mumbai and Hyderabad 18 each, and NCR 19 (Gurugram 9, Delhi 7, Noida 3), with Pune at 6 and Chennai at 4. Only 8 were fully remote. By employer, 54 were at product companies, 23 at startups, 19 at services or consulting firms and 8 at AI labs. That is a fraction of all postings in India, not the whole market. Indeed India reported in October that FDE postings grew nearly 19-fold between June 2025 and June 2026, from a small base.

FDE salary in the US, for context

You will see headlines about seven-figure FDE pay. They are about the United States. Levels.fyi's US median for the title is $225,000 in total pay (158 salaries, November 2026), and a recruiter quoted in a Wall Street Journal report put the top tier at $586,000 to $1.45 million, much of it in stock. None of that converts to Indian pay. The useful comparison is Cartesia, which advertised the same FDE role at $180,000–$250,000 in the US and ₹70–90 lakh in India.

The skills that move the number

We counted the skills named in the 104 postings. Client-facing work came first, in 61 of them. Then AI agents (58), Python (36), APIs (35), AWS (34), RAG (32), Azure (32) and LLMs (32). Docker and TypeScript followed.

The most requested skill is not technical at all, and it is the one engineers practise least. The rest of the list is mostly ordinary engineering (cloud, APIs, Python) with the AI layer on top. If you want to be paid at the top of the band, be the person who can deploy the plain parts reliably and then explain the result to the customer's manager.

How to earn at the top of the range

  • 1Ship something to a live URL. A deployed service with tests and monitoring is worth more in an interview than three notebooks.
  • 2Build one AI system you can defend: a RAG assistant or an agent, with an evaluation that shows where it fails.
  • 3Practise the client conversation. Record yourself explaining a project to someone non-technical, then watch it.
  • 4Once you have that portfolio, apply to product companies and AI startups as well as services firms. That one choice changes the band more than another certificate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average FDE salary in India?

About ₹13 lakh to ₹18 lakh a year on reported data. Glassdoor's average base pay is ₹12.6 lakh (50 salaries) and Levels.fyi's median total pay is ₹17.6 lakh (12 salaries), both read on 10 November 2026. Both samples are small.

What is the salary of a fresher Forward Deployed Engineer in India?

Freshers usually start at about ₹5.4–6 lakh in software, solutions or ML engineer roles (PayScale) and at ₹14 lakh and more at product companies, where Levels.fyi's entry-level software engineer median is ₹14.5 lakh. Indian AI startups such as Sarvam AI, Gushwork and HighRadius have posted FDE roles open to 0–3 years, though they do not publish pay, and most recent Glassdoor reports from FDEs with 1–3 years fall between ₹6–8 lakh and ₹20–24 lakh.

What does a senior Forward Deployed Engineer earn in India?

Levels.fyi's 75th and 90th percentiles are about ₹42 lakh and ₹55 lakh in total pay, and posted senior roles have ranged from about ₹33–41 lakh base (5+ years) to ₹70–90 lakh plus equity for a founding FDE with 4+ years.

What do Emergent and Wonderful pay Forward Deployed Engineers?

Neither publishes FDE pay. Emergent's only posted engineering range was ₹10–80 lakh for an infrastructure engineer with 3+ years. Wonderful's careers page lists FDE roles without locations or pay, and we could not confirm an India office.

Does Palantir hire Forward Deployed Engineers in India?

Palantir listed no FDE roles in India when its careers page was checked at the end of October 2026. Neither did OpenAI or Anthropic. Google Cloud, Salesforce, Databricks, DevRev and MongoDB do hire FDEs in India, mostly with 3–8 years of experience.

Do Forward Deployed Engineers earn more than software engineers in India?

There is not enough data to say. Levels.fyi's India software engineer median is about ₹30.5 lakh from 21,000+ salaries, against an FDE median of ₹17.6 lakh from only 12. The ceiling depends far more on the company than on the title.

How much is an ₹18 lakh FDE salary per month in hand?

Roughly ₹1.34 lakh a month, assuming it is all fixed pay, PF at ₹1,800 a month and the new tax regime. Variable pay, higher PF or a different regime will change it.

Why do salary websites show such different FDE salaries?

The samples are tiny, some show base pay and others total pay, and different kinds of employees report to each site. Levels.fyi's India FDE median fell from ₹31.6 lakh to ₹17.6 lakh between mid-October and November 2026 as its sample changed from 20 to 12.
