Taking AI Customer Service Agents to Production for a Global Retail Brand

Entrans · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

Entrans's forward deployed engineers helped a global retail brand move AI customer service agents from pilot to production. Earlier chatbots only handled scripted FAQs and couldn't access live orders, returns, or inventory, with data spread across separate ecommerce, order management, and CRM platforms. An embedded pod studied real customer conversations and APIs, then built agentic assistants inside the client's own cloud, connecting all three systems via secure APIs and MCP tools without replacing them. Retail and Consumer

Forward Deployed Engineering

A global consumer retail brand selling through online and in-store channels wanted AI that could do more than answer scripted FAQs. Entrans Forward Deployed Engineers embedded with its teams and took AI customer service agents from pilot to production on live product, order, and customer data.

2 mins

Challenge

Solution

The Outcome

Earlier chatbot pilots could answer scripted questions but could not act on live orders or returns, and the data they needed sat across disconnected systems.

The existing chatbots handled a fixed set of FAQs with no access to live orders, returns, or product availability, so most real customer questions still reached the service team.

Product, order, and customer data lived in separate ecommerce, order management, and CRM platforms, and any AI assistant had to work across all three without replacing them.

An Entrans Forward Deployed Engineering pod embedded with the brand's ecommerce and service teams and built production AI agents inside the client's own cloud and repositories.

The pod studied real customer conversations, APIs, and data flows first, then scoped the workflows an AI agent could resolve end to end.

Engineers built an agentic assistant connected to the catalog, order management, and CRM through secure APIs and MCP tools, with no rip-and-replace.

The agent helps shoppers find products, checks live order status, and starts return requests, all in a single conversation.

Policy guardrails keep answers on track, and complex cases move to a service agent with the full conversation context attached.

One workflow went live first, and new workflows followed as usage and answer quality confirmed value.

The brand moved from contained chatbot pilots to AI agents running on its live commerce systems. Its service teams now have a production foundation they can extend to new customer workflows without rebuilding existing platforms.

Production Go-Live achieved on live catalog, order, and customer data.

Seamless Human Handoff delivered with full context for complex customer cases.

Reusable Agent Framework built for adding new service workflows over time.
