Summary

A Salesforce Japan job posting for a Deployment Strategist (DS), a role on the first-year team building out Agentforce deployment in Japan. The DS works alongside Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) to own the full project lifecycle, from deal qualification through deployment and adoption, covering AI strategy and architecture design, program management, executive stakeholder relationships, and product feedback loops. Requirements include leading AI/LLM solution design, 4+ years in enterprise cloud-facing roles, working knowledge of at least one programming language (e.g., Python, JavaScript, Java, Apex), fluent Japanese, business-level English for US headquarters training, and roughly 25% customer-site travel. Salary ranges from 8 to 30 million yen. The posting also cites Agentforce growth figures: production customers up about 50% quarter-over-quarter and over 2.4 billion tasks processed.

Key points

  • Deployment Strategist partners with FDEs to own full AI project lifecycles.
  • Requires AI/LLM design experience, 4+ years enterprise cloud experience, and coding skills.
  • Salary is 8-30 million yen; fluent Japanese and business English are mandatory.
  • Agentforce production customers grew ~50% QoQ; 2.4B+ tasks processed.
  • Onboarding includes 1-3 months training plus one week at US headquarters.
