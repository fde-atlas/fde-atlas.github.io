Full text

It's been almost 2 weeks since I joined the community. I became a fan after the offline sharing session by the class rep x Indigo in Vancouver. But the past four or five weeks happened to coincide with my job change, so I figured I'd wait until I was about to start the new job before writing this self-introduction. Thanks for sparing me.

I hope I'm still a middle-aged person without the greasy vibe: 22 years in software, graduated from Fudan University with a computer science degree in 2003, 4.5 years at IBM, 4.5 years at EMC, 7.5 years at Splunk, and the past 4 years as a startup cofounder. Tomorrow I'm joining an AI agent company as an FDE.

My work has mostly been enterprise software in the data infrastructure space, from full stack engineer, to team lead, to engineering manager. At startups I've leaned toward PM work, and I've also done some SA and DevRel.

I just posted on LinkedIn a slightly more detailed piece about what an FDE is, why AI companies are all hiring for this role, and who is a good fit. The image above is from that post. If you're interested, please check it out at https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/connecting-dots-my-next-chapter-ai-agent-fde-jove-zhong-ifyyc/

I'd love to discuss any cloud, data, or AI topics with everyone. I'm also helping some startups, offering some of my data points for reference only.

When I first came to Vancouver, I really loved all kinds of outdoor activities—skiing, camping, hiking. But the past four years of entrepreneurship, with 80 hours a week not being unusual, meant a lot of that fell by the wayside (or maybe that's just an excuse for laziness). I hope the new company will allow for a bit more work-life balance.

Looking forward to learning and growing together with like-minded folks, and enjoying growth, work, and life.
