Dentsu Group in Japan Launches FDE/FDM Skill Development Program for 3,000 Employees

Original title: 国内電通グループ、3,000人規模のFDE・FDMスキル開発を開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-10-14 · 4 min read

Summary

Dentsu's three domestic group companies (Dentsu, Dentsu Digital, Dentsu Soken) are partnering with Ignition Point to launch a group-wide skills development program for roughly 3,000 people, training Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) and Forward Deployed Marketers (FDMs) using Google Cloud's Gemini Enterprise AI platform. Beyond teaching generative AI skills, the program aims to develop practitioners who embed themselves in client operations and lead AI-driven business transformation, supporting clients from strategy formulation through insight discovery, creative generation, effectiveness testing, and integration into daily operations. The initiative is part of Dentsu's "AI For Growth" strategy.

Key points

  • Dentsu group companies and Ignition Point launch a 3,000-person FDE/FDM training program.
  • Training is built around Google Cloud's Gemini Enterprise AI platform.
  • FDEs will work on-site with clients to drive AI transformation projects end to end.
  • Support spans strategy, insight discovery, creative generation, testing, and daily operations.
  • The program supports Dentsu's broader "AI For Growth" AI strategy.

- Strengthening the framework for driving AI transformation leveraging Gemini Enterprise -

Ignition Point, Inc. (Head office: Shibuya-ku, Tokyo; President and CEO: Takafumi Suemune; hereinafter "Ignition Point") is launching a group-wide FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer *1) and FDM (Forward Deployed Marketer *1) skill development program for approximately 3,000 employees across the three Dentsu Group companies in Japan (Dentsu, Dentsu Digital, and Dentsu Soken), leveraging "Gemini Enterprise," an enterprise AI platform provided by Google Cloud. By strengthening a framework that embeds talent on-site with clients to drive AI-powered business transformation, the company will promote business transformation and the social implementation of generative AI.

In today's business environment, generative AI has evolved beyond its role as a tool for improving operational efficiency into a foundation for transforming companies' entire business processes and marketing activities. To translate this cutting-edge generative AI technology into business growth and improved customer experience (CX), companies need not only generative AI implementation and operational improvement, but also a comprehensive perspective aligned with corporate growth strategies and hands-on, side-by-side support that addresses challenges on the ground.

To address these challenges, the Dentsu Group in Japan will promote the use of "Gemini Enterprise," which serves as a foundation for AI utilization across all areas of business by enabling cross-functional use of internal data in a highly secure environment, while developing FDEs and FDMs who combine state-of-the-art AI technical skills with a keen sense of business realities through this initiative.

■Key Points of This Initiative

Large-scale development of 3,000 advanced AI professionals incorporating the use of "Gemini Enterprise"

A program targeting approximately 3,000 employees across the Dentsu Group companies in Japan will begin, covering everything from practical use of "Gemini Enterprise" to its effective integration into business workflows. Going beyond mere acquisition of generative AI skills, the group as a whole will develop advanced AI professionals with FDE/FDM skills who can identify challenges on the front lines of marketing and business and lead projects themselves.

Consistent client support from strategic planning to on-the-ground implementation

FDEs will embed themselves in clients' business operations and drive AI transformation projects in a hands-on, side-by-side manner. From developing business and marketing strategies to advanced insight discovery, generation and effectiveness testing of optimal creative work, and integration into daily operations, the Dentsu Group companies in Japan will combine their specialized expertise with "Gemini Enterprise" at every phase to deliver business results for clients.

Going forward, the Dentsu Group in Japan will continue to promote the use of Google Cloud AI solutions, including "Gemini Enterprise," to contribute to delivering business results for clients.

*1: Hands-on professionals who support business transformation on-site with clients, centered on the use of generative AI

*: Google Cloud and Gemini Enterprise are trademarks of Google LLC.

The Dentsu Group in Japan is advancing its unique AI strategy, "AI For Growth," which contributes to the growth of clients and society by combining "human intelligence" with "AI intelligence."

For more information on AI For Growth, please visit the following web page.
