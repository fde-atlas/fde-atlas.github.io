The Difference Between FDE and PM | Roles, Requirements, and Compensation, as Seen in 10 Forward Deployed PM Job Listings

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-10-29 · 32 min read

Summary

This Japanese-language article analyzes the Forward Deployed Product Manager (FDPM), a new role adjacent to the FDE. Drawing on 10 job postings from 6 companies (Scale AI, Cresta, Fin, Abridge, Exa, Snowflake) collected on October 29, 2026, it contrasts FDPM with traditional PMs and FDEs: FDPMs own outcomes for specific customer accounts rather than the product itself, deciding the "what and why" while FDEs handle the "how." Compared with FDE postings at the same companies, FDPM roles center on product management and deployment experience rather than production coding. Pay at Scale AI and Exa matched or exceeded comparable FDE ranges (e.g., Scale AI's New York FDPM at $240K–$300K base). The editor classifies FDPM postings into three types: builder, judgment/orchestration, and productization. None of the 10 roles were based in Japan; nearby Tokyo roles include OpenAI's Deployment Lead and Deployment Strategist positions at Palantir and Snowflake.

Key points

  • 10 FDPM postings from 6 companies, all under product or R&D departments.
  • FDPM owns customer-account outcomes; FDE owns technical implementation.
  • FDPM requirements emphasize PM/deployment experience over deep coding.
  • FDPM pay at Scale AI and Exa matched or exceeded FDE ranges.
  • No FDPM roles in Japan; closest Tokyo roles are Deployment Lead and Deployment Strategist.

How do FDEs and PMs differ, and what does an FDPM do? In this article, the editorial team read the full text of 10 postings (from 6 companies) whose job titles include Forward Deployed (or FDE) and Product, out of the postings collected on October 29 from 133 public job boards, and compared them with 4 FDE postings from the same companies. We will look in turn at the FDPM's work, the division of labor with FDEs, requirements, compensation, and similar postings in Japan. The work of FDEs themselves is covered in What is an FDE, and the career path from FDE to PM in FDE career paths.

What is a Forward Deployed PM (FDPM)?

Forward Deployed PM(FDPM)は、製品を作る会社に雇われながら顧客の組織に入り込み、その顧客のもとで製品が成果を出すことに責任を持つプロダクトマネージャーです[11]。プロダクトマネジメントの媒体Mind the Productは2026年10月19日の解説で、従来のPMが製品を持つのに対し、FDPMは顧客アカウントの成果を持つと整理しています[11]。

"A traditional PM owns a product, while an FDPM owns outcomes for a customer account."

Caroline Clark (former Product Director, leadership coach) — Mind the Product, "What is a Forward Deployed Product Manager, anyway?" (October 19, 2026)

[[1]](1)

同じ解説は、FDPMの仕事を2つに分けています。1つは、その顧客が成功するために何が要るかを見極めることで、プラットフォームを設定したり、実際の利用者と一緒に動く試作を作ったりすることが多いとしています。もう1つは、そこで学んだことをコア製品のチームに戻し、ほかの顧客にも効くようにすることです[11]。日々の体制は、FDPM・FDE・コア製品のPMの3人組が典型だと書いています[11]。

求人票の側でも、会社はFDPMを既存の職種と区別しようとしています。Scale AIのニューヨーク勤務の求人は、冒頭の職務概要で次のように線を引いています[1]。

"This is not a roadmap PM, a CSM, or a solutions engineer. The FDPM owns product outcomes inside a portfolio of enterprise accounts"

What is the difference between an FDE and a PM?

FDEとFDPMの違いは、何に責任を持つかにあります。Mind the Productの整理では、FDEは技術的な「どうやって」を持って解決策を書いて導入し、FDPMはその顧客にとっての「何を」と「なぜ」を持ちます[11]。従来のPMとの違いは、持つ対象が製品ではなく顧客アカウントの成果である点です[11]。

ソリューションアーキテクト 既存の製品を、その顧客に最適な形で導入する設計 導入で分かったことをもとに、製品そのものをどう変えるかの判断[11]
コンサルタント 提言をして次へ移る 製品が価値を出すまで顧客に留まり、結果に責任を持ち、学びを製品に戻す[11]
AIプロダクトマネージャー 市場の多くの顧客に向けたAI機能を作る 特定の顧客のもとで製品を機能させる[11]

Open Position

Scale AI

Forward Deployed Product Manager, Enterprise

  • Location
  • New York, NY
  • Compensation (as listed in the job posting)
  • Base salary $240,000—$300,000 USD (San Francisco, New York, Seattle). May be eligible for equity. Ranges may include multiple levels
  • Date job posting was retrieved

Responsibilities

  • Own product outcomes at high-impact enterprise customers. Be the PM accountable for product delivery and customer value
  • Build trust with VP- and C-level buyers and senior technical leaders, and be the reliable escalation point before problems surface
  • Drive deployments from contract to production. Know what "production-ready" actually means, and close the gap
  • Turn customers' real workflows, friction, and priorities into high-quality product input for the core platform teams. Not requirement documents, but perspectives backed by evidence and trade-offs
  • Distinguish whether the product is the constraint or whether execution, integrations, or change management is, and resolve each through the right channel
  • Work closely with FDEs, running accounts as one team rather than parallel bundles of work

Qualifications

  • 6+ years in product management, technical program management, or customer-facing product ownership
  • A track record of shipping product or platform capabilities to large organizations, not just trials or proofs of concept (able to speak to customers, scale, integrations, and what production looks like)
  • Extreme ownership, delivering results across teams without formal authority
  • Able to engage VP- and C-level customers and senior engineers at the same time
  • Technical understanding sufficient to genuinely discuss architecture trade-offs with platform engineers (not deep coding)

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with AI/ML platform products such as data labeling, RLHF, fine-tuning, and model evaluation
  • Startup or early-stage experience where the systems didn't exist and you had to build them
  • Experience in frontline, customer-embedded roles such as consulting, field PM, or owning solution engineering

What to note in this job posting: The posting's intent shows in the fact that it opens by distinguishing itself from three existing roles before describing the job. The verbs in the responsibilities center on own, drive, translate, and distinguish, and build is used only in the item about building trust with customers. The division of labor is that FDEs do the building, while the FDPM is responsible for the judgment of identifying what the bottleneck is and routing it to the right channel. A London posting for the same title lists some Python coding experience (

Some coding experience (Python)

) and describes leading a team including engineering and finance with end-to-end ownership of product development, written in a quite different style. On the ATS, the London posting was published in January and the New York one in March; in the editors' view, the later-written New York posting is the definition that makes the division of labor with FDEs clearer.

It is a hybrid role assuming at least three days a week in the office.

Is the FDPM just a renamed job?

名前だけでは判断できず、求人票の中身で見分ける必要があります。Mind the Productの解説も、FDPMの一部は呼び名の付け替えだと注意を促し、職種名ではなく職務記述を読んで判断するよう勧めています[11]。

Tom Hollands氏は2026年1月、ベンチャーキャピタルa16zのニュースレターに載せた論考で、Palantirが2011年に、ソリューションエンジニアや統合エンジニアに「forward-deployed engineer」という職種名を与えたことを、新しい職種名で組織の新しい力を示す「title arbitrage」の例として挙げています[12]。同氏は、新しい職種名が本物かどうかを見分ける問いを、次のように書いています[12]。

"The test is this: does the new title describe work that would have been unrecognizable five years ago?"

Tom Hollands — a16z, "Forward-deployed Job Titles" (January 27, 2026)

[12]

Mind the Productの解説は、FDPMの役割を引き受ける前に確かめることとして、次の5つを挙げています[11]。

  • 01コア製品に機能が必要になったとき、どうやってバックログに載せるのか[11]
  • 02自分が製品側に戻した取り組みのために、どれだけの開発の工数が確保されているのか[11]
  • 03自分の成果はどう測られるのか[11]
  • 04独自の機能や設定を作る裁量はどこまであるのか[11]
  • 05コア製品について約束する権限があり、機能を作るチームはその約束を守るのか[11]

同じ解説は不利な面も挙げています。1つの顧客に深く入るほど長期の製品戦略がおろそかになりうること、社内の別の誰かがした約束の後始末を負わされうること、1つの案件に近すぎると製品全体が見えにくくなることです[11]。

Conclusion

  • FDPM(Forward Deployed PM)は、顧客の組織に入り込み、その顧客のもとで製品が成果を出すことに責任を持つプロダクトマネージャー。従来のPMが製品を持つのに対し、FDPMは顧客アカウントの成果を持つ[11]
  • FDEとの分担は、FDEが「どうやって」(技術的な解決策の実装と導入)、FDPMが「何を・なぜ」と、作るもの・断るもの・コア製品に戻すものの判断[11][3]
  • As of October 29, 2026, there were 10 job postings at 6 companies whose job titles included Forward Deployed (or FDE) and Product, and all 10 listed their department as Product or R&D (compiled by the editorial team)
  • 要件の経験年数は「最低年数なし」(Fin)から10年以上(Scale AIの管理職)まで幅があり、手を動かす範囲で作り手型・判断と統括型・製品化型に分かれた(編集部の分類)[2][9]
  • ニューヨーク勤務のScale AIのFDPM(Enterprise)は基本給24万〜30万ドルで、同社とExaでは比べたFDE求人と同水準かそれ以上のレンジだった(等級の高いFDE求人はこれを上回る)[1][6][14][16][22]
  • 10件に日本勤務の求人は無く、東京で近い役割の例はOpenAIのDeployment Lead、PalantirとSnowflakeのDeployment Strategist[18][19][20]

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat is the difference between an FDE and a PM?

An FDE is the "how" person who writes and deploys technical solutions on-site at the customer, while an FDPM (Forward Deployed PM) is a product manager who decides what to build for that customer and why, and is responsible for the outcomes of the customer account. In the job postings the editorial team reviewed on October 29, 2026, FDE roles centered on the ability to write production code, while FDPM roles centered on years of experience in product management or deployment, plus enough technical understanding to hold discussions with engineers.

QDoes a Forward Deployed PM need to write code?

It depends on the posting. Scale AI's New York-based posting explicitly states that deep coding is not required, while Fin (formerly Intercom)'s posting says that if needed, you will set things up yourself, including building custom solutions. Cresta's posting lists designing, building, and testing AI agents in partnership with FDEs as a job duty, and asks for experience involved in building agents (personal projects are acceptable).

QHow much does a Forward Deployed PM earn?

According to US-based job postings as of October 29, 2026: Scale AI's FDPM pays a base salary of $240,000–$300,000 (San Francisco, New York, Seattle), the public sector FDPM pays $210,000–$345,000, Abridge pays $260,000–$290,000, Exa pays $155,000–$275,000, and Cresta pays an OTE (including incentives) of $170,000–$280,000. All of these are the ranges stated in the postings, not actual paid amounts.

QAre there Forward Deployed PM jobs in Japan?

Among the 133 public job boards the editorial team retrieved on October 29, 2026, none of the 10 postings whose titles included Forward Deployed and Product were based in Japan. Tokyo-based postings with similar roles include, for example, OpenAI's Deployment Lead and Deployment Strategist roles at Palantir and Snowflake, all of which involve working with FDEs and taking responsibility for customer outcomes and the substance of engagements.

QAre FDPM and Deployment Strategist the same role?

No. The FDPM postings we reviewed this time were placed in product departments (R&D at Fin) and included as part of the job bringing what is learned on-site at customers back into the core product. A Deployment Strategist defines customer problems and engagements and works with FDEs to drive deployment; Snowflake's Tokyo-based posting listed engaging with executives as part of engagements and writing statements of work (SOWs) as job duties.
