Summary

In this Tencent Cloud developer community post, author dongdonglog explains the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role and why companies are competing to hire for it in 2026. The article opens with a one-line definition, illustrates the work through e-commerce and finance scenarios, and contrasts FDEs with roles like solutions engineers. It traces the model's origin to Palantir's Delta approach, discusses why enterprises hire FDEs from big firms instead of recruiting internally, and offers a tech-stack cheat sheet plus FAQ for aspiring candidates. The author draws on personal experience using agent tools like Openclaw and Hermes to help small businesses.
