Job Profile: ByteDance (Volcano Engine) FDE

Original title: 岗位档案：字节跳动（火山引擎）FDE——国内大厂最直接的 FDE 岗位

FDE中国 · 2026-09-04 · 1 min read

Summary

ByteDance's Volcano Engine is openly hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer covering its full AI product line, described as the most direct FDE title among major Chinese tech companies. The job description explicitly names Agent frameworks such as LangGraph and CrewAI, marking the first time Agent engineering skills and industry solution experience appear in a Chinese big-tech FDE hiring standard. Citing a 36Kr report, the article lists monthly pay of 35,000-70,000 RMB with 15 months' salary, up to about 1.05 million RMB annually. The role targets enterprise-grade delivery in the Agent era rather than traditional on-site support. The author advises candidates to prepare a complete Agent delivery case structured as requirements, architecture, implementation, and results.

Key points

  • Volcano Engine's official FDE role covers its entire AI product line.
  • JD names LangGraph and CrewAI, formalizing Agent skills in FDE hiring.
  • Reported pay: 35k-70k RMB monthly, 15 months, up to ~1.05M RMB yearly.
  • Positioned as enterprise Agent delivery, not traditional on-site ops.
  • Candidates should prepare one full Agent delivery case with framework trade-offs.

Editor's Note FDEChina Editorial Team

The JD itself is a signal: ByteDance has brought the Silicon Valley FDE title directly into its domestic hiring system, covering Volcano Engine's full AI product line and explicitly naming Agent frameworks such as LangGraph and CrewAI — for the first time, Agent engineering skills and industry solution experience have been written into the FDE hiring standards of a major Chinese tech company. Combined with the salary figures reported by 36Kr (monthly salary 35,000–70,000 RMB, 15 months' pay, annual salary up to about 1.05 million RMB), the role is clearly positioned for enterprise-level delivery in the Agent era, not traditional on-site operations.

For job seekers, preparation should focus on the two hard currencies of "hands-on Agent framework experience + industry scenario case studies," aligning point by point with the JD's bonus criteria; for ecosystem observers, ByteDance has the most complete chain among major Chinese tech companies, with the Doubao large model as the foundation, Volcano Engine as the toB outlet, and FDE as the delivery lever. Action recommendation: before the interview, break down a complete Agent delivery case into a narratable story following the "requirements-architecture-implementation-results" structure, and be ready to explain the trade-offs behind your framework choices.
