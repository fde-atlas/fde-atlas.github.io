Annual salary of a million, hiring demand up 42x — what exactly does "the hottest job in the AI world" do?

Original title: 年薪百万，招聘需求涨42倍，“AI圈最火岗位”，到底是干嘛的？

36氪 · 2026-10-31 · 6 min read

Summary

This 36Kr-republished report from Zhongxin Jingwei examines the explosive rise of the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role. A LinkedIn report says FDE job postings grew 42-fold over two years, far outpacing AI engineer roles. OpenAI, Anthropic, ByteDance and Ant Digital all hire FDEs, with salaries reaching the equivalent of a million RMB, though practitioners note such pay applies mainly to top talent. Interviews with three FDEs describe the role as combining coding skills with deep customer engagement to embed AI products into client workflows. The article also covers entry requirements and career outlook.

Key points

  • LinkedIn data: FDE postings grew 42x in two years vs 13x for AI engineers.
  • OpenAI pays FDEs $162k–$280k; Anthropic $200k–$300k.
  • In China, million-RMB pay is limited to top-tier talent, not the average.
  • FDEs must code and work hands-on inside client business processes.
  • Practitioners compare its trajectory to the product manager boom a decade ago.

AI has created a new job that pays a million yuan a year. Data from some platforms shows that over the past two years, the number of FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) positions has surged 42-fold. On social media, many netizens call the FDE "the hottest job in the AI world."

On recruitment platforms, job postings from some large companies show annual salaries for this role reaching over one million yuan. What exactly does an FDE do? Can you really easily land a million-yuan salary?

01 Hiring demand surges, with salaries up to a million yuan

FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer. Yasha (a pseudonym), who works in Los Angeles, USA, has eight years of product manager experience, later transitioned into software development, and became an FDE two years ago.

She told Jiemian News (China Newsweek Economics) that two years ago, this role was "extremely niche." "At the time on LinkedIn, only the company I work for now and one other company were hiring."

She said the FDE role allows her to fully combine her product manager business experience with her programming skills, without having to give up the accumulated experience of either side.

Nowadays, Yasha frequently receives messages from headhunters. After two years in the field, she is already considered a "very senior" FDE in the eyes of headhunters. She revealed that headhunters are currently offering her an annual salary of $400,000 (about 2.71 million yuan) plus "fully remote" work and other perks to lure her away.

LinkedIn's global labor market trends insight report released in January 2026 shows that over the past two years, companies have added at least 1.3 million AI-related positions, covering data annotators, AI engineers, and Forward Deployed Engineers. These jobs didn't exist five years ago but have now become an indispensable part of the digital economy. Among them, the number of new Forward Deployed Engineer positions grew 42-fold from 2023 to 2025, compared with a 13-fold increase for AI engineers.

Jiemian News observed on platforms such as Liepin and BOSS Zhipin that companies including ByteDance and Ant Digital Technologies are recruiting for FDE positions.

ByteDance's job posting for "Doubao AI Large Model FDE" shows a salary range of 35,000 to 70,000 yuan per month, paid over 15 months a year, meaning a maximum of 1.05 million yuan. Ant Digital Technologies' B2B FDE position offers a monthly salary of 40,000 to 60,000 yuan, with 15 months' pay a year. Zhipu AI's FDE lead position has a monthly salary range of 60,000 to 80,000 yuan.

Screenshot source: recruitment platforms

However, interviewed practitioners believe that million-yuan salaries in China are concentrated among top-tier core talent, not the industry average.

Lai Juncheng (a pseudonym), a post-90s professional working at an AI company in Shanghai, told Jiemian News that lower-level FDEs at his company earn between 20,000 and 30,000 yuan per month, while higher-level FDEs are mainly paid annual packages, mostly above 400,000 yuan.

According to Yasha, FDE is not an entry-level position in the US. "A fresh graduate can't do this job; it generally requires several years of experience as a product manager or developer. So the starting salary isn't low either — around $200,000-plus in the first year. In Silicon Valley, there's no ceiling. I've heard of packages of $500,000 (about 3.38 million yuan) a year, or even higher."

OpenAI's official job listing shows that the FDE role is responsible for working with the most important strategic customers, leading the complex end-to-end deployment of frontier models in production environments, with an annual salary of $162,000 to $280,000 (about 1.1 million to 1.9 million yuan) plus equity incentives, based in New York, USA. In addition, Anthropic is also hiring FDEs, with an annual salary of $200,000 to $300,000 (about 1.36 million to 2.03 million yuan).

02 What does this job involve?

Lawted (a pseudonym), based in Shenzhen, was formerly a programmer at a big tech company. Not long ago, a logistics company approached him hoping he could help optimize their business processes with AI, so he transitioned into entrepreneurship and became an FDE.

"I found that their daily work was just repeating one thing: a customer sends over a PDF, and they manually extract the tracking numbers and shipping addresses, then enter them into Excel," Lawted said. The company has 40 to 50 employees, 20 to 30 of whom do this kind of repetitive work.

He used AI coding tools to build a demo. A PDF that originally took a person several minutes to process was parsed by AI in just a few seconds. He said that after seeing this efficiency improvement, the logistics company signed a letter of intent with him.

Nowadays, Lawted's day basically goes like this: in the morning, he visits various companies to research whether their business processes are suitable for AI transformation; in the afternoon, he stays on-site observing, seizing moments when staff take water breaks to ask questions; in the evening, he sorts out the day's requirements and does system development. "Before, I just sat in the office waiting for people to bring me requirements. Now I'm running all over Shenzhen, looking for companies and bosses," he said.

Lai Juncheng defines himself as the "all-around manager who handles everything except coding and administration" at a software development company. He has worked as an implementation engineer, pre-sales, product manager, and project manager, and has fully experienced the entire process of a product from template to final delivery. "This role happens to integrate the three stages of pre-sales, product, and project management," he said.

In Yasha's view, an FDE is a special kind of programmer. This role is not responsible for any specific functional module of the product; rather, it requires a comprehensive understanding of about 70% of the entire product, then using that understanding to engage with customers, deeply learn their workflows and data structures, and ultimately integrate the product with the customer so it truly lands and gets used.

03 What are the barriers to entry?

"In the past, enterprise software was often a standardized product — I build it, and you use it as is. But now we've entered the AI era, and most products must be deeply embedded in the customer's business processes," Yasha said. At this point, you need someone who understands technology and can write code to work on-site (or online) with the customer to analyze: which requirements can be implemented directly, which need to be worked around, and which can be turned into win-win solutions.

Yasha said, this means an FDE cannot be a product manager who only knows how to communicate and then relays things back to the programmers — that's too inefficient and prone to distortion. "You must have technical depth yourself, be able to make judgments, and even solve some on-site problems with your own hands."

In her view, the most important qualities for an FDE are strong analytical ability and patience. "Every day you face unclear requirements, like a tangled mess of threads. The customer is already anxious, and you need to calm down, untangle the threads one by one, guide them to articulate the real problem, and then push implementation forward step by step."

Lai Juncheng is currently also responsible for recruiting new FDEs for his company. When hiring, he values two things most: learning ability and the ability to see the essence of things. As for educational requirements, he said that employees on his team who studied design or less common foreign languages have all performed outstandingly.

On recruitment platforms, taking the aforementioned Doubao position as an example, the job requirements include a bachelor's degree or above, with preference for majors in computer science, communications, artificial intelligence, and related fields; a solid programming foundation (Python/Java, etc.), the ability to independently complete code development and debugging, and full-stack development capability; familiarity with the technical principles of large models, mastery of methods for evaluating large model performance and effectiveness, and familiarity with various Post-Training techniques; and familiarity with API calls for large model-related products.

As for the future of the FDE role, Lai Juncheng believes it can be compared to the trajectory of the "product manager" role when the mobile internet took off ten years ago. "It was hot for about five to ten years, then gradually cooled down. I think FDE will go through a similar process. But along the way, there will surely emerge top 'super individuals' like Zhang Xiaolong and Zhang Yiming, who profoundly influence our lives."

Yasha, on the other hand, values the role more as a stepping stone to entrepreneurship. "FDEs have seen too many success and failure cases and deeply understand user pain points and the details of project implementation. In the AI era, the barrier to entrepreneurship is getting lower and lower. If you have FDE experience, you'll know exactly what the market lacks and what product you should build."

Lawted, who had already received an offer of admission from Harvard University, decided to temporarily focus on his entrepreneurial practice, seizing the 1-2 year window for AI transformation of traditional enterprises. "Because AI is moving so fast. It used to take one or two years and several million yuan to build an enterprise management system. Now, with AI programming, we can deliver a custom system in just two months."

Yasha said she is currently very optimistic about the prospects of the FDE role. But she also knows that AI is evolving so fast that no one can accurately predict the future of any profession. "All we can do is see clearly the direction it's heading in, and jump onto the next wave at the right moment."
