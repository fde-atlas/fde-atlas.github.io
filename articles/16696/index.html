Cognition FDE interview questions

FDE Interview Prep · 2026 · 1 min read

Summary

This article compiles interview questions and background for Cognition's Deployed Engineer role, a customer-facing position in its Customer Engineering department focused on helping customers get value from Devin. A September 2026 census counted 15 matching public postings, and the core posting requires willingness to travel 25–50%. Cognition's careers page does not describe its hiring process. Jia Wu, a deployed engineering lead, said at AI Engineer World's Fair 2026 that Cognition hires from product management (for product sense) as well as founders and software engineers with deep technical skills. The listed questions, from a candidate write-up, cover describing your current role, working in fast-moving environments, and presenting a technical project to a mixed audience of engineers and executives.

Key points

  • Cognition's role is called Deployed Engineer, in Customer Engineering, centered on Devin.
  • 15 matching postings were counted in September 2026.
  • The core posting requires 25–50% travel.
  • Hiring draws on PMs, founders, and technically strong engineers, per Jia Wu.
  • Reported questions include a project presentation to engineers and executives.
