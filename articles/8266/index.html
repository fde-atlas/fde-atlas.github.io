Summary

This page is the discussion forum of Taro, a career platform for software engineers offering courses, job listings, and interview prep. Community members from companies like Microsoft, Palantir, Meta, Amazon, and Google post questions on topics such as system design, code review, promotions, and communication. Popular threads include how to understand an unfamiliar codebase, what separates high performers from low performers, navigating Amazon's SDE2-to-SDE3 promotion, and switching domains in tech. Threads are tagged by seniority level, and the most viewed have tens of thousands of views, though most date back several years.
