Support Engineer to Forward Deployed Engineer: A Realistic Path in India

FDE India · 2026-11-04 · 8 min read

Summary

This guide argues that support and implementation engineers in India already possess the customer-facing half of the FDE role—troubleshooting live systems, discovery, integrations, and onboarding. The real gap is production coding and LLM work, since listings from Darwinbox, DevRev, Cisco and others require Python or TypeScript, API integrations, RAG and evals. Entry bars range from 0–2 years (QuickReply.ai) to 8+ years (NTT DATA). Pay data shows support engineers earn ₹1.7–10.8 LPA while mid-level FDEs earn ₹14–28 LPA. The article closes with a 90-day plan: build production-grade Python, ship an LLM project with evals, then reposition your resume and target startup roles.

Key points

  • Support work already covers discovery, troubleshooting, integrations and onboarding.
  • The gap is production Python/TypeScript plus RAG and evals experience.
  • Experience requirements range from 0–2 years at startups to 8+ at NTT DATA.
  • Pay jump: support ₹1.7–10.8 LPA vs mid-level FDE ₹14–28 LPA.
  • A 90-day plan covers coding, an LLM project, and resume repositioning.

Yes, a support or implementation engineer can become a Forward Deployed Engineer in India, and you start with more of the job than most people think. Support teaches you to debug live customer systems under pressure, and implementation teaches you integrations and onboarding. What you must add is production coding and LLM work, because India FDE listings still hire engineers who build, not only engineers who fix.

This guide maps what transfers, what doesn’t, which India employers are realistic, and a 90-day plan to close the gap.

What a support or implementation background gives you

An FDE embeds with a customer, works out what they actually need, and builds and runs the software that delivers it. Read India listings closely and the customer half of that job looks a lot like senior support and implementation work:

  • Troubleshooting live systems. Cisco’s Bengaluru FDE listing, seen on Dreamwork in October 2026, asks the FDE to troubleshoot complex production issues across multiple layers of the stack. That is a support engineer’s daily work.
  • Customer discovery. Darwinbox’s Hyderabad FDE role (September 2026, Talentmate) runs discovery with CHROs, HR business partners and HRIS leads and turns their needs into technical designs. Customer-facing experience is a hard requirement.
  • Onboarding and data migration. Zenskar’s Senior FDE (Implementation, Data Migration) owns technical implementation for complex customer onboardings: configuring billing rules, contracts and pricing, and reconciling billing data.
  • Working with support and success teams. DevRev’s Bengaluru FDE listing says the role works with customer success, support and revenue teams, and is about 30% customer-facing.

Cisco’s listing goes further and names “Consulting, Solutions Engineering, or Technical Customer Success” backgrounds as preferred. HackerRank’s Bengaluru FDE asks for experience as a software, solutions or sales engineer. So non-developer customer roles are a recognised route in, provided the coding is there too.

The gap you need to close

Every listing above still asks for real engineering. This is where most support engineers fall short, and it is better to see it plainly.

Skill What support/implementation usually gives you What India FDE listings ask for
Coding Scripts, SQL queries, log analysis Production Python or TypeScript (Darwinbox, DevRev); Ruby or Python API scripts (HackerRank)
Integrations Configuring existing connectors, reading API docs Designing REST API and webhook integrations with payroll, ATS, SSO and ERP systems (Darwinbox)
LLM / AI Using AI tools for tickets Deploying RAG, agent orchestration and evaluation (Darwinbox); LLMs, RAG and evals (DevRev)
Ownership Resolve the ticket, escalate the bug Own the deployment end to end, from prototype to production (Darwinbox asks for a prototype in about a week)
Customer work Strong: calls, escalations, expectation-setting Strong: discovery with senior stakeholders, workshops, 25–40% travel (Darwinbox)
Production operations Strong: incidents, monitoring, root-cause analysis Observability and production readiness (DevRev, NTT DATA)

The bottom two rows are your advantage. The top four are your homework. Our guide to the skills Indian FDE employers want goes deeper on each.

Which FDE roles are realistic from support

Experience asked varies a lot, and the lower bands are where a support engineer with fresh coding proof can compete.

Minimum years asked in five India FDE listings from 2026 (Weekday, Talentmate, Foundit, Dreamwork, Jobera). The lower bands suit career switchers.

  • 0–2 years: QuickReply.ai, a Gurugram WhatsApp sales-automation startup, lists an in-office FDE role asking 0–2 years.
  • 1–4 years: Darwinbox asked for 1–4 years of professional software engineering including customer-facing work. That listing has closed, but it shows the profile HR-tech and SaaS firms want.
  • 2–4 years: SquadStack’s Noida Sr. FDE role asked 2–4 years to build voice AI agents and integrate them with CRMs and ERPs. It is closed now.
  • 4+ years: Cisco’s Bengaluru role wants 4+ years of production software engineering, with solutions and customer-success backgrounds preferred.
  • 5+ years: DevRev asks 5+ years in software development, systems integration or platform engineering.
  • 8+ years: NTT DATA’s Bengaluru FDE role asks 8+ years in solution architecture, consulting, AI engineering or customer engineering.

Notice the wording: most count software engineering years. Years in a pure support queue may not count in full, so the realistic first target is a startup or SaaS FDE role at the lower end, or an internal move. If you have under two years of any experience, read our guide for freshers aiming at FDE roles.

Pay: what the switch is worth

Support and implementation pay from AmbitionBox (March 2025 data); FDE bands from Second Talent (September 2026); SquadStack pay from its Foundit listing (October 2026).

AmbitionBox shows technical support engineers in India earning ₹1.7–10.8 LPA across 1–7 years, from about 64,900 reported salaries. Implementation engineers earn ₹2–13.8 LPA across up to 8 years, from about 3,200 salaries. Both are March 2025 figures.

Second Talent’s 2026 bands for FDEs are ₹6–14 LPA for 0–2 years and ₹14–28 LPA for 3–6 years. SquadStack’s 2–4 year Noida role listed ₹20–30 LPA plus ESOPs, and Dreamwork estimates Cisco’s Bengaluru FDE at ₹18–28 LPA (an estimate, not employer-posted).

The ranges overlap at the bottom, so the title alone won’t double your pay. The jump comes when you land a mid-level FDE role at a product company or startup. Compare offers with our FDE Salary Estimator.

A 90-day plan from support to FDE

This plan assumes you work full time and can give it 8–10 hours a week. Adjust the pace, not the order.

Days 1–30: get to production-grade Python

  • Pick one language and go deep. Python appears in almost every listing above. Move past scripts: functions, classes, tests with pytest, packaging and type hints.
  • Automate a real support pain. Build a small service that pulls tickets or logs through an API, classifies them, and posts a summary to Slack or email. Put it on GitHub with a README and tests.
  • Learn REST and webhooks properly. Build an endpoint with FastAPI that receives a webhook, validates it and writes to a database. This is the integration pattern Darwinbox and DevRev describe.

Days 31–60: add the LLM layer

  • Build a RAG assistant over documentation you know. Use your product’s public docs or knowledge base. Measure answer quality on 30–50 real questions you have answered before. That eval set is your edge: you know what the right answers are.
  • Add one agent action. Let the assistant call a tool, such as creating a ticket or looking up an order through an API, with guardrails for when it is unsure.
  • Deploy it. Containerise it, run it on a cloud free tier, add logging and a simple dashboard of latency, cost and accuracy. Production readiness is where your support instincts shine.

Days 61–90: reposition and apply

  • Rewrite your resume around outcomes. Turn “resolved 40 tickets a week” into the engineering behind it: “built a Python tool that cut triage time”, “led data migration for an enterprise onboarding”. Run it through the FDE Fit Check to see which gaps remain.
  • Ask for an internal stretch. Volunteer for integration, migration or AI pilot work at your current employer. One shipped customer deployment counts for more than any course.
  • Apply in the right band. Target 0–4 year FDE roles at startups and SaaS firms, plus adjacent titles such as solutions or implementation engineer at AI companies. Our comparison of roles similar to the FDE lists the closest ones.
  • Prepare your story. Interviewers will ask why you want to build, not support. Have one incident you diagnosed and fixed in code, and one customer whose real problem was different from the ticket.

Mistakes that stall the switch

  • Leading with tools, not code. Listing Zendesk, Jira and ServiceNow says support. Lead with what you built.
  • Only taking courses. No listing above asks for certificates. They ask for shipped integrations and production LLM work.
  • Applying only to big-tech FDE roles. Cisco, DevRev and NTT DATA ask for 4–8+ years of experience. Start where the bar matches your proof.
  • Underselling customer skills. Darwinbox made customer-facing experience a hard requirement and Cisco prefers customer-success backgrounds. Say plainly how you handled senior stakeholders and escalations.

If you are coming from a developer background instead, the backend engineer to FDE switch plan covers the opposite gap.

Frequently asked questions

Can a technical support engineer become a forward deployed engineer?

Yes, if they can also show production coding. India FDE listings value the customer and troubleshooting skills support builds, but they still ask for Python or TypeScript, API integrations and, increasingly, LLM work such as RAG and evals. A portfolio of shipped integrations closes most of that gap.

Do implementation engineers have an advantage for FDE roles?

Often. Some FDE roles are implementation work under a new title: Zenskar's Senior FDE (Implementation, Data Migration) owns enterprise customer onboarding, billing configuration and data reconciliation. Implementation engineers who already script migrations in Python are close to these roles.

How much more does an FDE earn than a support engineer in India?

AmbitionBox shows technical support engineers earning ₹1.7–10.8 LPA across 1–7 years (March 2025 data). Second Talent's 2026 bands put FDEs at ₹6–14 LPA for 0–2 years and ₹14–28 LPA for 3–6 years, and SquadStack posted ₹20–30 LPA plus ESOPs for a 2–4 year FDE role.

How long does the switch from support to FDE take?

There is no published average. A focused 90-day plan can build the proof most listings ask for: one deployed integration, one LLM feature with evals, and a resume rewritten around outcomes. Engineers with little coding may need longer.
