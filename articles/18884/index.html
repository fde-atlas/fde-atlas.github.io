FDE Job Posting Requirements: Dual-Sample Research (November 2026)

Original title: FDE岗位招聘要求双样本调研（2026-11）

深圳市真实智元科技有限公司 · 2026-11-04 · 4 min read

Summary

Shenzhen-based Zhenzhiyuan (真实智元) published a data room analyzing job descriptions for FDE-type roles across two separate samples: 29 Chinese companies and an initial 100 global companies, snapshotted in November 2026. Because Chinese firms rarely use the FDE title, the domestic sample is matched by keywords and is effectively an "AI delivery role" sample. All JDs come from official recruiting channels (Feishu, Moka, Ashby, Greenhouse, company sites), with sources, timestamps and SHA-256 hashes recorded, plus a downloadable package for recomputing the statistics. Counts use an explicit-mention criterion across the whole JD, with a narrow-scope sensitivity comparison, and the authors caution that mention frequency is not a market requirement. Headline findings: 22 of 29 domestic JDs mention production delivery or end-to-end ownership, 12 mention system/API integration, and only 9 mention client communication.

Key points

  • Two separate samples: 29 Chinese and 100 global FDE-type JDs.
  • 22/29 domestic JDs mention production delivery or end-to-end ownership.
  • Domestic sample is keyword-matched AI delivery roles, not strict FDE titles.
  • All data from official channels with SHA-256 hashes and a recheck package.
  • Wide vs narrow counting scopes shown side by side; results vary by scope.

What this is: A frequency count of capability requirements from two independent samples—FDE-type job JDs (naming conventions such as AI delivery / solutions / forward deployed, see the methodology section) from 29 domestic companies, and FDE job JDs from the initial 100 global companies (snapshots around 2026-11-04, same counting criteria, samples kept separate, not merged).

How it was collected: The domestic sample was taken entirely from companies' official recruiting channels (Feishu recruiting portals for 14 companies, Moka recruiting portals for 6 companies, official recruiting APIs and official site pages for the rest); 99/100 of the global initial sample was taken from Ashby / Greenhouse / company official sites as original text. Each entry includes its source, collection time, and SHA-256 checksum; directly collected records verify the body text, while screenshot-transcribed records verify the screenshot files.

How to verify: You can browse company job excerpts and sources, and download the statistics verification package to check the aggregated results of the published labels.

Domestic sample (2026-11-04 snapshot, n=29)

All 29 JDs in the domestic sample were taken as original text from companies' official recruiting channels: official Feishu recruiting portals (jobs.feishu.cn) for 14 companies, official recruiting site (careers.aliyun.com) · user-provided screenshot, manually transcribed for 1 company, official recruiting site (talent.antgroup.com) · user-provided screenshot, manually transcribed for 1 company, official recruiting API (jobs.bytedance.com) for 1 company, official Moka recruiting portals (mokahr.com) for 6 companies, official recruiting site pages for 3 companies, official recruiting site (NetEase Smart Enterprise recruiting page) · user-provided screenshot, manually transcribed for 1 company, official recruiting API (careers.tencent.com) for 1 company, official recruiting site (job.xiaohongshu.com) · user-provided screenshot, manually transcribed for 1 company. Collection respected robots.txt and rate limits (1 request / 2 seconds), and did not use platforms requiring login or with strong anti-scraping (Boss Zhipin / Lagou / Zhilian).

Job naming criteria for the domestic sample

Domestic companies rarely name roles directly as FDE. In this sample, official channel job titles were matched by keyword tiers: tier one covers strongly related names such as FDE / forward deployed / solutions (architect, engineer, specialist, consultant) / AI delivery / LLM application / Agent engineering; tier two covers weakly related names such as AI Engineer / LLM Engineer / AI Product Manager (added only when tier one is insufficient). 1-3 matching roles were collected per company, and the dataset keeps 1 best-matching role per company as the primary record. This sample is therefore a "domestic AI delivery-type role sample," not a strict "FDE role sample"—please note this criterion when citing.

Global initial sample (2026-11-02 snapshot, n=100)

The sample consists of 100 publicly posted JDs for global initial FDE-type roles: 99 taken directly as original text from companies' official recruiting channels (64 via the Ashby API, 21 via the Greenhouse API, 14 from company official sites), and 1 complete JD from a third-party aggregator site (marked separately; does not affect the main conclusions).

Counting criteria (identical for both samples)

Explicit mention across the full JD: responsibilities, required, preferred, company introduction, and unsectioned paragraphs all count (note: descriptions of products and business in company introduction paragraphs count toward frequencies); recruiting legal and equal-opportunity statements, benefits and compensation templates, and work authorization and recruiting process paragraphs are excluded from counting (excluded line by line). This statistic counts "explicit mentions" and does not represent hard market requirements. The sample covers AI delivery-type roles at domestic companies (role selection rules in the methodology notes), counted per company.

Skill frequencies were computed by line-by-line matching against the same dictionary; English skill terms were matched as whole words. The domestic sample uses statistics version v3 (2026-11-04), and the global initial sample uses v3 (2026-11-03).

Broad vs. narrow criteria sensitivity comparison (domestic sample)

Narrow criteria = counting only responsibilities + required + preferred sections; the choice of scope affects conclusions. Both criteria are shown side by side for readers to judge for themselves. (Narrow criteria rule: count only responsibilities + required + preferred sections, excluding company introductions and unsectioned text.) The choice of scope affects conclusions; please state which criterion you used when citing.

Data Verification and Source Limitations

You can download the statistics verification package (JSON and Python scripts) to recompute the aggregated results of the published skill labels. The verification package does not include mirrors of the full job postings; file verification of original text or screenshots requires obtaining files of the same version.

  • Each company's detail page lists source links, collection dates, and SHA-256 checksums.
  • The Alibaba Cloud, Ant Group, Xiaohongshu, and NetEase entries were manually transcribed from recruiting page screenshots; the checksums correspond to the screenshot files. The screenshots do not include specific job URLs, so verify through official recruiting channels before applying.
  • The domestic and global initial samples use the same counting rules and are aggregated separately; the two groups overlap and should not be added together directly.

Version Change Log

  • Domestic sample (2026-11-04): Expanded from 25 to 29 companies, adding recruiting screenshot records for Alibaba Cloud, Ant Group, Xiaohongshu, and NetEase, and updating skill statistics.
  • Global initial sample (2026-11-03): Unified skill counting based on job body text, excluding recruiting legal clauses, benefits, and recruiting process templates, and adding a comparison across different counting scopes.
