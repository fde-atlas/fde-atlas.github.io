Do FDEs Need English Skills? Required Proficiency and Strategies at Foreign AI Companies

Original title: FDEに英語力は必要か｜外資AI企業で求められるレベルと対策

FDE Jobs · 2026-06-21 · 3 min read

Summary

This Japanese-language article examines whether forward deployed engineers (FDEs) need English skills. The conclusion: English is not mandatory for domestic-focused roles, but is effectively required at foreign AI/SaaS companies with Japan offices, since firms like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Palantir conduct coordination with headquarters teams in English. The article ranks skill importance as reading first (technical docs and primary sources), then writing (async Slack and documentation), then speaking (needed for foreign and global roles). The practical goal is business-usable proficiency—grasping key points and confirming understanding—rather than perfection. Candidates anxious about English should build reading and writing first and start with domestic-leaning employers. English widens job options and salary ranges, but the core FDE value remains implementation ability combined with customer-facing skills.

Key points

  • English is optional for domestic roles but effectively required at foreign AI/SaaS firms.
  • OpenAI, Anthropic, and Palantir coordinate with HQ teams in English.
  • Skill priority: reading, then writing, then speaking.
  • Aim for practical proficiency, not perfection—read, write, and confirm key points.
  • Weak in English? Build reading/writing first and expand applications gradually.

Conclusion: Not essential, but it greatly affects your market value

For an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer), English is not essential if you focus on domestic companies, but is effectively required at foreign AI/SaaS companies. This is because at headquarters-driven companies like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Palantir, communication, documentation, and meetings with the product teams at headquarters are conducted in English. Being able to use English greatly expands the jobs you can apply for and your salary range.

Required English Level by Company Type

Company TypeRequired English Level
Japan offices of foreign AI companies (with HQ collaboration)Reading/writing plus meeting participation. Business-level English is preferred
Professional services at foreign SaaS companiesDocument reading plus some meetings. Intermediate level or above is advantageous
Domestic AI/SaaS companies and AI divisions of SIersNot required, but the ability to read technical documentation is a plus
Global remote positionsFluency is required if customers and teams use English

Which English Skills Matter

Here is a breakdown in order of importance for FDEs.

Reading (Top Priority)

Asynchronous communication with headquarters teams is primarily text-based. If you can write concisely and accurately, you can function well enough even without perfect speaking skills.

Writing Skills (Slack and Documents)

Asynchronous communication with the home-country team is mostly written. If you can write concisely and accurately, it works well even if your conversational skills aren't perfect.

Speaking (Required at Foreign/Global Companies)

Needed when there are regular meetings with headquarters or customer negotiations. Rather than perfection, the practical goal is a level where you can convey and confirm key points.

Strategies for Those Anxious About English

It is realistic to first solidify "reading and writing," then expand your application targets starting from domestic-oriented companies. Building practical, directly relevant habits is effective, such as reading technical documentation in English daily and writing Slack messages and documents in English. English is merely one weapon; the essential value of an FDE lies in implementation skills × customer-facing ability. It is best to think of it this way: the better your English becomes, the more foreign-company options and salary ranges open up to you.
