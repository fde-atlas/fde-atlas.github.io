Summary

This is an index of the Traversaal.ai engineering and product team blog, listing research posts, launch notes, and lessons from running production AI systems. It also covers forward deployed engineers, including a career guide, what Anthropic's S-1 filing reportedly says about FDEs, and Xero embedding FDEs in accounting firms. Useful for readers tracking agent productionization, costs, and security.
