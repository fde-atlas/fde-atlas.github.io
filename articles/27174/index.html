深圳市真实智元科技有限公司 · 2026-06-16 · 2 min read

Summary

This article presents a snapshot of Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE) job description, captured on 2026-11-02. It highlights that the role centers on small agile teams owning end-to-end execution of high-stakes projects, from ideation through deployment, with an emphasis on delivering real business value. Skill tags include client communication, requirements discovery, system/API integration, Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, cloud platforms, and RAG. The author notes the word Software in the title signals this is not a pre-sales role, and suggests practicing by writing delivery records covering the problem, actions, validation, and ongoing usage. The excerpts are for research only and not a hiring commitment.

Key points

  • Palantir describes itself as the original FDE role and calls FDSE its blueprint.
  • The role requires small teams to own end-to-end delivery of high-stakes projects.
  • Skills span client collaboration, integration, Python, cloud platforms, and RAG.
  • The Software in the title means it is not a pre-sales or demo-writing role.
  • Recommended practice: write delivery records documenting problem, actions, and validation.

Job reading and learning connections

Small teams owning projects from ideation to deployment

This FDSE snapshot emphasizes small teams, end-to-end execution, and real business value. The word Software in the job title reminds readers that it is not just about writing demo proposals, nor can all on-site work be classified as pre-sales.

How you can practice

Write a delivery record for a project: what the problem was, what was done, how it was verified, who continues to use it, and which changes are worth feeding back into the product.
