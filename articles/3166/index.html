Business — what's coming, and what it costs to be late

Keith Brown · 2011-10-15 · 5 min read

Summary

This is an index page for Keith Brown's Business blog section, collecting dated, falsifiable predictions (mostly two to five years out) and strategy teardowns. Recurring themes: software spending shifting from SaaS subscriptions to custom-built software, AI's impact on teams and jobs, and which software products survive. Notable posts cover Anthropic's S-1 showing over $6.6 million revenue per employee, forward-deployed engineers as the future of AI adoption, Kroger cutting 3,000 corporate jobs, AI ending entry-level work, and why ARR and recurring revenue models are breaking down.
