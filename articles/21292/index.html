Summary

This is a job listing from Japanese climate tech startup AAKEL (アークエル) for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE). The role embeds engineers within large energy-sector clients, covering core system rebuilds, PoC development, requirements definition, PMO support, and change adoption as part of the GX consulting team. AAKEL, founded in 2018 with about 60 employees, offers DX/GX consulting and EV adoption support, and was named in High-Growth Companies Asia-Pacific 2026. Salary is ¥5–8 million; locations are Fukuoka, Nagoya, and Tokyo, with remote work possible.

Key points

  • AAKEL is a climate tech firm using digital innovation for carbon neutrality.
  • The FDE works inside large energy clients from concept through system adoption.
  • Duties include core system rebuilds, PoC development, and PMO support.
  • Salary ranges from ¥5 million to ¥8 million including fixed overtime.
  • The 60-person startup is preparing for a potential IPO.

  • About Our Business
  • We provide optimal roadmaps and custom software to address the challenges companies face in decarbonized management and business process improvement. In particular, decarbonization in the energy and mobility sectors is extremely important and has a major impact on carbon neutrality across society as a whole. We specialize in these fields and provide various consulting services centered on our strengths in digital technology and green technology. ■DX/GX Realization Support Comprehensive support for "market research," "strategy formulation," "business process design," "partnerships, M&A, and due diligence," and "app development and operation" in DX promotion and new business development toward GX. ■Carbon Neutrality Promotion Support Comprehensive support from measuring greenhouse gas emissions through "strategy formulation" to "implementation and operation." ■EV (Electric Vehicle) Adoption Support Comprehensive support for converting gasoline vehicles to EVs, from "transition simulation" to "EV vehicle and EV charger installation" and "EV charging optimization."

  • Job Title
  • FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)
  • Job Description
  • ●What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)? This is a position that embeds deeply within a client's organization and drives problem-solving using technical expertise as its weapon. It goes beyond mere contracted development or proposals—as a member of the client's team, an FDE understands business processes, operates systems, and makes transformation take root. That is the role of an FDE. ●About This Position As a member of our GX consulting team, you will provide consistent support from concept development to implementation, primarily for clients in the major energy industry. You will be involved from the very start of projects, gaining a high-resolution understanding of the client's business. From PoC development, requirements definition, and system implementation support to embedding change on the ground, you will combine technical skills with customer understanding and take on social challenges together with Arcuel. [Specific Responsibilities] - Rebuilding client companies' core systems (planning/concept through requirements definition to development) - Planning improvement initiatives and designing To-Be processes - Leading PoC/prototype development and implementation phases - PMO and implementation support during execution phases - Communication that bridges technology, from management to on-site staff ●Three Reasons to Be an FDE at Arcuel ① Work directly with large enterprises and deepen your experience further Almost all of our projects are direct engagements with client companies. You discuss directly with clients' on-site staff and decision-makers, and your proposals are reflected as-is. This accumulation of tension and tangible results further deepens employees' technical skills, negotiation abilities, and business understanding. ② Enjoy significant discretion and demonstrate your own unique value Our flat organization where anyone can speak their mind frankly is Arcuel's culture. You will never find yourself in the situation of "having something I want to do but being unable to act due to my position." We offer an environment where you can act on your own judgment and make the results your own. ③ Your efforts directly contribute to the company's growth For us, a startup of about 60 people, the success of experienced professionals is the company's growth itself. Individual contributions that might go unnoticed at a large corporation shape the company's direction. The trust you build with clients and the solutions you bring to life will create Arcuel's next stage. This is an environment where each person can act as the face of the company.

  • About Arcuel
  • Founded in 2018, we operate under the mission of "achieving carbon neutrality through digital innovation," providing GX and DX consulting for major enterprises as well as developing our own SaaS products. Since our founding, we have built a track record of support centered on the energy and automotive industries, and were selected for the Asia-Pacific high-growth company ranking "High-Growth Companies Asia-Pacific 2026." We are now in our second founding phase with an eye toward an IPO, accelerating our business expansion.
