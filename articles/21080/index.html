Full text

September 3, 2026

Physical AI FDE Team turns tacit knowledge from the field into digital assets, providing end-to-end support from identifying management challenges to AI deployment and operation

Hitachi, Ltd. (hereinafter, Hitachi) today announced the launch of common solutions supporting "HMAX by Hitachi" (hereinafter, HMAX), a next-generation suite of solutions that uses AI to transform social infrastructure, with the aim of eliminating organizational and system silos in social infrastructure and industry and achieving autonomous optimization across entire field operations. Specifically, Hitachi will offer the "Physical AI FDE Service," in which a team of specialists called the "Physical AI FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) Team" that connects IT and OT (control and operational technology) through AI works alongside customers; "HMAX Data Fabric," a data platform that integrates tacit knowledge and data from the field; and "HMAX AI Operations," which supports safe autonomous operation. By seamlessly linking these solutions and accumulating tacit knowledge and data as digital assets for use and circulation across operations and organizations, Hitachi provides end-to-end support from identifying management challenges to AI deployment and operation.

As an approach in the field, the Physical AI FDE Team, composed of diverse specialists with advanced global AI technologies as well as domain knowledge cultivated through Hitachi's more than 110 years of manufacturing experience and infrastructure construction and operation, enters customers' sites and provides support starting from the identification of management challenges. The team performs high-quality cleansing and structuring of diverse, heterogeneous field data, and also extracts tacit knowledge that has never been verbalized, such as the experience and judgment criteria of skilled veterans. In addition, the data and tacit knowledge obtained in the field are systematically given meaning based on an ontology*1 and accumulated in HMAX Data Fabric as knowledge graphs*2 that AI can utilize. This platform also connects to environments for training AI models that control robots and other equipment using the accumulated data, and for developing and operating Agentic AI, providing AI-ready data. By linking these data and AI models with Hitachi's social infrastructure intelligence foundation model "IWIM (Integrated World Infrastructure Model)*3," AI becomes able to accurately interpret context such as physical operational limits and field control rules. As a result, AI balances across different systems while taking into account complex constraints such as operating rates and power, achieving advanced orchestration that optimally operates the entire field. Furthermore, HMAX AI Operations continuously monitors and reduces AI-specific risks and hallucinations, supporting safe autonomous operation even in mission-critical environments.

In this way, the integration of an advanced digital platform with expert accompaniment connects everything from the extraction of field knowledge to the utilization and operation of AI, enabling rapid AI deployment tailored to each customer's unique data environment and field rules, along with high adaptability to environmental changes. Through more sophisticated decision-making by management, accelerated company-wide AI utilization by DX departments, and reduced data integration costs for IT departments, this contributes to the continuous evolution of companies and industries as a whole.

  • 1 Ontology: A systematic definition of the meanings and concepts of data and the relationships between them. It is currently being redefined as a common language connecting corporate data platforms and AI agents.
  • 2 Knowledge graph: A database structured by linking diverse data and their relationships in a network (graph structure) based on an ontology. It serves as a foundation for AI to perform advanced contextual understanding and reasoning toward overall optimization by tracing connections between pieces of information.
  • 3 A social infrastructure intelligence foundation model announced by Hitachi in November 2025. Developed based on years of accumulated multi-layered OT knowledge and a deep understanding of physical phenomena, in order to implement physical AI in mission-critical social infrastructure safely and with high reliability.

In recent years, mission-critical sites in social infrastructure and industry, such as power, railways, and manufacturing, have faced serious labor shortages and the loss of tacit field knowledge due to the retirement of skilled engineers. While expectations are rising for the social implementation of physical AI as a means of solving these issues, in actual practice, simply deploying AI models is not sufficient. It is necessary to properly curate noisy field data, convert field-specific constraints and safety rules into a form that AI can handle, and continuously monitor data quality, AI model performance, security, and more after deployment to ensure safe ongoing operation.

Hitachi has, through its Lumada business, accumulated a track record of building and operating systems and utilizing data in mission-critical domains together with customers and partners. Its unique know-how in systematizing field insights as ontologies and making them reusable across industries, along with its secure system governance capabilities, are powerful strengths underpinning the HMAX Data Fabric announced today. Building on this long-standing track record and OT expertise, and to further advance the autonomy of entire field operations through HMAX, Hitachi is strengthening collaboration with the global AI ecosystem, including the announcement in July 2026 of the development of multi-agent orchestration technology leveraging NVIDIA's technology*4.

  • 4 July 16, 2026 press release: Hitachi and NVIDIA Collaborate on Integrated Control of Physical AI for Autonomy Across Field Operations and the Evolution of HMAX

To maximize the value of AI in mission-critical sites such as social infrastructure, it is essential to have mechanisms that convert field-specific tacit knowledge into a form AI can understand and safely utilize and operate on an ongoing basis. These new solutions are based on Hitachi's track record of supporting approximately 15,000 core systems together with customers and partners, and its expertise in foundational software, as well as years of know-how in integrating and giving meaning to distributed data to support advanced analysis and decision-making in areas such as railway operation management, power transmission and distribution, and manufacturing production and maintenance. Furthermore, by leveraging advanced AI technologies cultivated through collaboration with global partners, and with the Physical AI FDE Team working alongside customers, Hitachi will support the optimization of customers' entire field operations and their sustainable growth.

(1) Physical AI FDE Service: Turning field tacit knowledge into assets and providing accompaniment support for new value creation

Hitachi has established a global organization, the "Frontier AI Deployment Center," as a core hub for driving value delivery and customer zero through technology support, talent development, and the accumulation of use cases. With strong support from this organization, the Physical AI FDE Team—composed of Hitachi AI specialists, domain experts, and other IT, OT, and product specialists—provides consulting and delivery services that accompany customers through every stage, from identifying management challenges to AI development, implementation, and autonomous evolution. Specifically, by leveraging years of accumulated domain knowledge and AI interview techniques (in which AI conducts iterative, dialogue-based questioning with experts), the team accurately extracts tacit knowledge of experts that has not been verbalized, as well as their flexible approaches to troubleshooting and decision-making criteria. The team also performs high-quality cleansing and structuring of noisy field data, and converts and maps it into ontologies—knowledge systems that AI can utilize—thereby turning field knowledge into digital assets.

Furthermore, Hitachi offers a program that leverages its extensive library of proof-of-concept patterns to run short, workshop-style sessions, agilely executing everything from deep dives into each customer's specific field challenges to validation of AI model effectiveness. As a result, customers can quickly translate even challenges they had not clearly identified into concrete projects, and can select AI technologies suited to their field operations, visualize implementation effects, and achieve dramatic improvements in operational efficiency and the creation of new value.

(2) HMAX Data Fabric: Structuring complex field data to be AI Ready and achieving overall optimization

As part of its customer zero practice, Hitachi has accumulated expertise in advanced data structuring through initiatives such as improving manufacturing processes by fusing physical data from materials manufacturing and measurement at Hitachi High-Tech. To make effective use of distributed business, control, and equipment data in the field, it is necessary to go beyond simply linking information and to define the meaning and structure of data based on relationships such as physical connections between equipment and the sequence of manufacturing processes. Building on the technologies cultivated through this internal practice, Hitachi has developed "HMAX Data Fabric," which structures data so that AI can reference and reason over it and accumulate it as a knowledge graph.

In addition, by providing AI Ready data, this platform enables seamless connection and integration with environments for training AI models that control robots and equipment, and for developing and operating Agentic AI. Furthermore, by linking these developed AI models and structured knowledge graphs with IWIM, Hitachi's social infrastructure intelligence foundation model equipped with physical laws and expert domain knowledge, AI becomes able to accurately predict and interpret field context, such as "how a change in the operation of one piece of equipment affects power consumption in downstream processes or overall utilization rates." This enables AI to derive optimal operations across multiple systems while satisfying complex constraints, performing advanced orchestration of the entire field.

For example, in the railway sector, when signs of potential defects in rolling stock or railway infrastructure are detected, HMAX Data Fabric enables integrated correlation of equipment condition data from multiple sources with historical performance trends. This allows AI to rapidly analyze the root cause of anomalies and support appropriate corrective and preventive actions by operators and maintenance engineers, contributing to the prevention of service interruptions and equipment failures. Through such initiatives, Hitachi will support customers in evolving toward system foundations where AI can be used with confidence.

(3) HMAX AI Operations: Supporting continuous AI operations and high safety

As physical AI in the field repeatedly executes and learns in the real world, Hitachi performs safety control and monitoring, including performance monitoring of AI models and advanced security monitoring. Specifically, Hitachi leverages proven assets such as Hitachi Application Reliability Centers (HARC) for AI*5, which enable companies to use AI agents safely and effectively in production environments, to continuously observe and improve performance, governance, security, cost, and more.

Based on Hitachi's internal development and use of numerous AI agents and its operational expertise with its proprietary generative AI foundation, Hitachi continuously maintains data governance, access control, and auditing to address issues that continue to change after deployment, such as the impact of missing or noisy data and evolving threats. Hitachi reduces AI-specific risks and hallucinations, and safely and continuously evolves AI models and field operations under appropriate security policies. As a result, even in mission-critical environments, Hitachi strongly supports reliable, continuous operations while ensuring safety.
