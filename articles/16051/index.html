Forward Deployed Engineer jobs in Washington DC (2026)

FDE Times · 2026-11-20 · 4 min read

Summary

A job-board roundup of Forward Deployed Engineer openings in the Washington DC area, compiled from company careers pages and updated daily as of 20 Nov 2026. It lists 66 open roles across 14 companies, with a median posted base pay of $135k–$200k. Palantir dominates with 24 openings, followed by Amazon (AWS) with 12 and Databricks with 9. Most roles target US government, public sector, and national security customers, including many Palantir US Government positions and AWS government-focused FDE roles. Deployment Strategist positions are also common, notably at Palantir and Rivet, which hires for Army, Navy, and Air Force accounts.

Key points

  • 66 open FDE-related roles across 14 companies in the DC area.
  • Median posted base pay ranges from $135k to $200k.
  • Palantir leads with 24 openings; Amazon (AWS) has 12, Databricks 9.
  • Most roles serve US government, public sector, and national security clients.
  • Deployment Strategist roles are prominent, including Rivet's military-focused positions.
