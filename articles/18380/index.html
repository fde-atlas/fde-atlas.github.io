Manufacturing Industry In-House FDE Development: Implementation Guide Report

Original title: 製造業向け 社内FDE育成 進め方レポート

株式会社Aiworker · 2025-11-30 · 1 min read

Summary

A report by AIworker, a Japanese company, on developing internal FDEs in manufacturing. It explains why employees who know the business—specifically those in production control and quality assurance—should be trained to become builders of AI-driven systems for their own departments. The report covers how to select candidates, combining two types of internal FDEs, dividing tasks between AI and human judgment, a three-month training program followed by three months of accompaniment, and the organizational setup needed to sustain the practice in factories afterward.

Key points

  • Report targets manufacturers training internal FDEs from existing staff.
  • Candidates come from production control and quality assurance teams.
  • Internal FDEs are developed by combining two types.
  • Program: three months of training plus three months of accompaniment.
  • Also covers sustaining the setup in factories after training.

Full text

A summary of how to select and train production management and quality assurance employees over three months so they can use AI to transform their own department's operations

What this report covers

  • Why manufacturing companies should develop employees who know the operations into builders of systems
  • In-house FDEs in manufacturing: developing a combination of two types
  • What to delegate to in-house FDEs and what humans should decide
  • The process (3 months of training + 3 months of support)
  • The structure needed to sustain the program at the factory after training
