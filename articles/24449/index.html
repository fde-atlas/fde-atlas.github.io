[First in Japan] All In-House Engineers to Become "FDEs": anddot Announces Restructuring of Its Engineering Organization.

Original title: 【日本初】社内の全エンジニアを『FDE』へ。アンドドットがエンジニア組織の再構築を宣言。

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-03-23 · 7 min read

Summary

Japanese AX consulting firm anddot announced it will reorganize its entire engineering organization around the FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) model starting April 2026, claiming this is the first such move in Japan. With AI dramatically accelerating coding, the company argues engineer value should shift from implementation to delivering customer outcomes. It defined five core principles covering AI-first development, requirements definition, on-site problem discovery, autonomous proposals, and client partnership, and is recruiting engineers for this expanded role combining engineering, consulting, and product management.

Key points

  • anddot will convert all engineers to an FDE-based organization from April 2026.
  • The move responds to AI-driven coding automation, shifting value toward customer outcomes.
  • Five core principles span AI-first development, requirements work, and client partnership.
  • FDEs integrate engineering, consulting, and product manager roles end to end.
  • The company is actively recruiting engineers for the new role.

Full-Scale Launch of Next-Generation Engineer Hiring and Development for the AI Era, Redefining the Engineer's Role from Implementation to Business Outcomes

anddot Inc. (Headquarters: Shibuya-ku, Tokyo; CEO: Yuta Ibaraki; hereinafter "anddot"), which operates an AX production business (*1), announces that from April 2026 it will transform its in-house engineering organization into a structure premised on FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers).

Now that AI has dramatically improved development productivity, the essential value of engineers is being redefined from "implementation" to "creating customer outcomes." We will significantly strengthen the development and hiring of FDEs as "business-completion engineers" who drive business success on the front lines.

What Is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?

An FDE is an engineer who combines technical skill with business understanding. Rather than staying at a development site, FDEs are deployed to the front lines of the customer's business, handling everything end-to-end from identifying issues to AI implementation and delivering results. They are not merely developers: by integrating the three roles of "engineer," "consultant," and "product manager" and feeding frontline insights directly back into the product and strategy, they maximize business impact.

Background: Rising Engineer Productivity and the Purpose of Becoming an "FDE" Organization

Recently, the spread of autonomous multi-agent AI has dramatically boosted productivity in development. As coding work itself increasingly becomes the domain of AI, anddot is leveraging this technological shift to redefine its organization so that engineers can engage more directly with business impact.

Declaration of Organizational Transformation: All Engineers to Become "FDEs"

Accordingly, from April 2026 anddot will transition its entire engineering organization to an FDE-based structure. Guided by the following "five core principles," we aim to become a "business-completion" organization that handles everything end-to-end, from identifying customer issues to implementation and delivering results.

  • AI-First Development: Make full use of AI to accelerate design, implementation, and testing. Connect everything from infrastructure to front end single-handedly and implement in the shortest time possible.
  • Requirements Definition and Project Management Skills: Organize customers' vague requests technically and define what should be built. Do not leave progress or risk management to others; lead projects with accountability for deadlines and quality.
  • Deep Frontline Understanding and Issue Identification: Deeply understand frontline workflows and industry structures to uncover the "real issues" not captured in documents. Identify business bottlenecks from an FDE's perspective.
  • Autonomous Solution Proposals: Rather than waiting for instructions, independently devise and present solutions to identified issues. Engage in dialogue that considers technical feasibility and return on investment (ROI).
  • Building Partnerships: Engage directly with customers and earn trust as an expert technical partner. Become not just a worker but the "first expert partner consulted" who grows the business together with the customer.

Engineers' Scope of Work: Before / After

With this organizational transformation, engineers' scope of work will change as follows.

Before: The Traditional Development Style

In development until now, engineers typically specialized in the "development phase," performing design and implementation based on finalized specifications. They tended to take a passive stance in identifying customer issues and building consensus, and had limited opportunities to be directly involved in post-release operations or the business outcomes the system generates.

After: anddot's FDE (Business-Completion) Model

Engineers themselves join projects from the upstream stage, working alongside sales to directly handle "identifying customer issues, proposing solutions, and building consensus." In addition to implementation skills as engineers, they integrate strategic planning as consultants and product feedback as PdMs. By connecting technology and business, they evolve into a role that completes everything end-to-end, from implementation to "creating business outcomes."

Message from the CEO and CTO

Yuta Ibaraki, Representative Director and CEO, anddot Inc.

We are shifting engineers' market value from lines of code to "outcomes produced." anddot will turn all engineers into FDEs and move their primary work to "customer dialogue and problem solving." We will rebuild the organization on the premise that AI automates coding. If you want to build your career as an FDE who acts with a customer-first mindset beyond technical skill alone, please reach out for a casual chat.

Kosuke Takanezawa, Chief Technology Officer, anddot Inc.

Engineering organizations have traditionally focused on implementing value through coding against given requirements. Today, while AI-driven development lets coding proceed at more than 20X the previous speed, upstream decisions—"what to build and how to turn it into business impact"—matter more than ever. Through this FDE organization declaration, we aim to create an engineering organization with strong communication skills, execution ability, and speed—one that increases opportunities for customer engagement and handles everything from issue definition and consensus building to delivering working solutions in the shortest time.

Hiring Push: Recruiting "FDEs (Business-Completion Engineers)" for the AI Era

Along with the FDE transformation of our in-house engineering organization, we are recruiting engineers who want to raise their market value in a cutting-edge environment of "AI × fastest development × business innovation." We welcome ambitious professionals who want to go beyond development into consulting and PdM territory and move the business forward.

Future Outlook: Maximizing Product Evolution Speed to Set the Industry Standard

By having FDEs feed real frontline issues directly back into the product, anddot will dramatically improve market fit and evolution speed. We will build an environment where aspiring FDEs can grow, secure an advantage in the hiring market, and continue the challenge of redefining the industry-wide standard for what an engineer looks like in the AI era.

Going beyond mere support for introducing AI tools, it is a holistic service that rebuilds a company's business model itself on an AI-first basis to create sustainable competitive advantage. After deeply interviewing management challenges and frontline business processes, we provide consistent end-to-end support, from selecting the optimal AI technologies to implementation, and embedding them in organizational culture through education.
