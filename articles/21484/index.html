Summary

Japanese recipe platform Kurashiru is hiring a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) in Tokyo, with a salary of ¥12–18 million. The role supports Kurashiru AI OS, an AI agent platform for food manufacturers, wholesalers, and retailers. The posting argues that coding agents have made implementation itself no longer the bottleneck, shifting engineer value toward understanding customer operations and driving solutions from requirements to production. Duties span problem identification, PoC, system design (AI-OCR, unstructured data pipelines), full-stack development, and post-launch improvement via guardrails and fine-tuning. Requirements include 5+ years of web development, LLM application experience (OpenAI/Claude APIs), AI-driven development with tools like Claude Code, and Japanese communication skills.

  • Kurashiru, Inc. aims to create businesses that brighten people's lives and society, with flagship services "Kurashiru" and "Reshichalle." Guided by the vision "BE THE SUN" and the mission "Keep inventing to light up the world," the company has launched one business pillar after another, from the recipe video service "Kurashiru" to the money-saving app "Reshichalle" and now "Kurashiru AI OS." ■ "Kurashiru," one of Japan's largest recipe video services with over 44 million cumulative downloads ■ "Reshichalle," a money-saving app with over 15 million cumulative downloads ■ "Kurashiru AI OS," an AI agent platform for manufacturers, wholesalers, and retailers Through "Kurashiru" and "Reshichalle," we have supported consumers' daily dining tables with recipes and smart shopping. We aim to protect and expand the abundance of Japan's dining tables that we take for granted. Going forward, we will continue to create entirely new businesses discontinuously, not just extending our existing businesses.

  • Job Title
  • [AI Business/Development] Senior Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)
  • Job Description
  • With the evolution of Coding Agents, software implementation itself is no longer a bottleneck. The added value required of engineers has shifted to deeply understanding customers' fundamental challenges and translating them from requirements definition into concrete solution design and implementation. In this position, you will not only implement products as a Builder with full-stack development skills, but also proactively enter customers' workplaces and deeply understand their operations and challenges. You will take end-to-end ownership of the entire process, from identifying challenges through prototyping, production implementation, and post-deployment adoption and improvement. - Identifying customer workplace challenges: Through interviews with customers and on-site understanding, you will structurally organize complex business processes, identify the essential challenges, and propose concrete solutions. - Solution design: Based on validation results obtained from PoCs, you will design systems ready for real-world operation by combining technologies such as AI-OCR and unstructured data pipelines. - Full-stack development: Making maximum use of Coding Agents, you will implement all necessary features end-to-end, from frontend and backend to building AI agent harnesses. - Operations, adoption, and continuous improvement: Through guardrail design and fine-tuning, you will run feedback loops from real-world operations to maximize the value delivered to customers.
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • Web application development experience (5+ years) - Experience designing and implementing backends using REST APIs and databases (RDB) - Experience developing applications using LLMs (OpenAI API, Claude API, etc.) - Hands-on experience with AI-driven development using Claude Code/Codex, etc. (personal or professional) - Ability to communicate smoothly in Japanese for work purposes
