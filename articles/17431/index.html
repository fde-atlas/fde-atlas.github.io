Implementation Consultant: Implementation Consultant

Original title: 实施顾问：Implementation Consultant

Full text

The closest existing talent pool to the FDE in China is implementation consultants; this card explains where they are close and where they are far apart. Core breakdown: the key difference is the solution space—an implementation consultant's feasible solutions are limited to the configuration options the product allows, while a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) can use code to extend capabilities the product doesn't have. Three areas of implementation experience—process understanding, data governance, and change management—retain their full value; the gaps are in engineering implementation and AI evals. The most common misjudgment is that "on-site experience transfers directly"; in reality, the bulk of the transition work lies in building engineering skills. Incremental insight: AI projects are pushing implementation from "configuration-centric" toward "configuration plus extension development plus evals"—implementation consultants aren't being phased out; their solution space is being expanded, which is closer to reality than simply framing it as a role conflict. For managers, there's another layer: the transition from implementation consultant to FDE is generally shorter than training a fresh graduate engineer, because business communication and data understanding can't be learned quickly. Action recommendation: review your most recent project and distinguish which requirements were solved through configuration and which forced workarounds—the latter is evidence that you need to build coding skills. Turning those workaround requirements into a checklist is your learning roadmap for the next three months. — FDEChina Editorial Team · Practitioner's Perspective

Definition

An Implementation Consultant is an on-site role in the delivery systems of mature enterprise software (ERP, CRM, MES, OA, etc.), landing standard products in customer organizations through in-product configuration, parameterization, and process training. They have been the mainstay of toB delivery for the past two decades.

Deep Dive

The core difference from a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) lies in the solution space: an implementation consultant's feasible solutions are confined to the configuration options and parameters the product allows, and requirements the product doesn't cover can only be worked around or deferred to a future release; an FDE's solution space is the entire codebase, and they can extend product capabilities with code, integrations, and evals. As a result, the two have different project shapes—implementation projects center on blueprint design and key user training, while FDE projects center on prototype validation and eval baselines. Delivery in the AI era is shifting from "configuration-centric" to "configuration plus extension development plus evals"; implementation consultants won't disappear, but they will be expected to master a larger solution space.

For China's large population of implementation consultants, it's important to objectively separate what retains value from what's missing: experience in process mapping, data governance, and change management fully retains its value and is the entry ticket to an FDE team; the gaps are engineering implementation, APIs, and evals capabilities. The most common mistake is assuming that years of on-site experience transfer wholesale—what transfers is business understanding, not delivery methods.
