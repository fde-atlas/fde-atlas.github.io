Summary

This is a service page by Japanese company DeFactory promoting its FDE-style development support. Engagement is outcome-based: billing starts only after the client's problem is resolved, with a minimum 3-month contract. Teams consist of one PM plus one or two engineers (engineer rates roughly 850,000–1.1 million yen per month), unifying the client interface and hands-on work. Four service areas are covered: 0-to-1 MVP development, legacy system modernization without downtime (strangler pattern, cloud migration), building AI-driven development organizations, and implementing generative AI and AI agents. All areas follow a common method: articulate the problem, measure it, then leave behind internal capability. A case study at a company with roughly 1 billion yen annual revenue shows replacing SES contractors cut costs to about 50–70% of the previous ~6.3 million yen monthly outsourcing spend. The page also offers a 10-axis comparison of SES, SIer, and FDE procurement models.

Key points

  • Outcome-based billing: charges apply only after the problem is solved.
  • Minimum 3-month contract; teams of one PM plus 1–2 engineers.
  • Engineer rates around 850,000–1.1 million yen per month.
  • Four areas: MVP development, system modernization, AI-driven orgs, AI adoption.
  • Case study: replacing SES cut costs to roughly 50–70%.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long is the contract period?

The minimum is 3 months. After that, you can choose to continue or hand over the work in-house (internalize it).

What kind of team structure will be assigned?

A minimum of 2 people: 1 PM plus engineers. Typically 2-3 people, with additional staff for larger-scale projects.

When does billing start?

We use a results-based model where billing starts only after the issue has been resolved. The exact amount is finalized in an estimate after consultation.

Is it okay to just have a consultation?

Yes. It's a free 30-minute session with no pushy sales tactics. You can also choose options like "I just want a rough estimate" or "I'd like to discuss the contents of your materials" from the form.

Can I apply with a free email address?

We only accept company email addresses. Free email addresses are not accepted.
