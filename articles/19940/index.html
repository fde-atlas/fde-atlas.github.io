Services (AI Strategy, Development, Support, and Talent Development)

Original title: サービス（AI戦略・開発・伴走・人材育成）

株式会社Aiworker · 2025-11-30 · 2 min read

Summary

This is a service page from Japanese company AIworker, not an analytical article. It outlines three service lines: AI strategy consulting with hands-on accompaniment, generative AI contract development (e.g., quote-drafting AI, document-processing AI, AI agents), and corporate AI talent development. Its FDE offering places engineers on-site at clients to build systems integrated with internal infrastructure. Training programs, supervised by University of Tokyo AI experts, cover Copilot adoption and sales-focused AI use, and can qualify for Japan's human resources development subsidy under post-August 2026 rules.

Key points

  • AIworker offers AI strategy consulting, generative AI development, and talent training.
  • Flagship development cases: quote-drafting AI and document-processing automation.
  • AIworker FDE embeds engineers on-site to build client-integrated systems.
  • Training is supervised by University of Tokyo AI experts and practice-oriented.
  • Programs can qualify for Japan's human resources development subsidy.

Full text

AIworker supports companies' use of AI through three pillars: "AI strategy and accompaniment," "development," and "talent development." Specifically, we offer five services: contract development of quotation AI and AI agents, generative AI accompaniment support, in-house FDE (AI implementation talent) development, and corporate generative AI training. We are relentlessly focused on delivering results in real-world practice and are committed to growing our clients' businesses.

Service 1

AI Strategy & Accompaniment: AI Utilization Consulting

End-to-end support from issue analysis through implementation and adoption

We analyze your business challenges and provide accompaniment support from planning your AI adoption strategy through implementation and operational adoption. Rather than simply introducing tools, we foster an organization-wide culture of AI utilization and achieve sustainable results. Our offerings include "generative AI accompaniment support," which works on-site through to adoption, and "in-house FDE development," which trains employees who know the business to become AI builders.

See In-House FDE Development

Service 2

Development: Contract Generative AI Development

High-speed development powered by generative AI

We deliver application and system development centered on generative AI at overwhelming speed. We also handle additional development and non-AI development, and are committed to growing your business with optimal solutions leveraging cutting-edge AI such as natural language processing and image/audio generation. Representative examples include "Quotation AI," which creates draft quotations from request forms and drawings, and "AI-powered document processing," which reads purchase orders and invoices and connects them through to registration in core systems. Even at the stage where what to build hasn't been decided, our "AIworker FDE" service places engineers on-site at your company who develop systems through to integration with your internal systems.

Service 3

Talent Development: Building In-House AI Talent

AI Native X Training

For all industries and all job types. Practice-oriented training

Supervised by AI experts from the University of Tokyo, our programs directly tied to real work turn employees into "AI natives." With an intense focus on practical application, we build the hands-on skills needed to produce results immediately after training. Our programs can also be designed to qualify for the Human Resources Development Subsidy (aligned with the conditions revised in August 2026).
