Forward Deployed Engineers: the skills, overlap with Solutions Engineers, and the differences

SE Rockstars · 2026-10-06 · 1 min read

Summary

This podcast episode examines the forward deployed engineer (FDE) role, drawing on research, industry anecdotes, and the host's own experience. It separates hype from reality in the day-to-day work, weighing how much of the role is customer-facing versus technical. The host compares FDEs with traditional Solutions Engineers, outlining five real differences alongside a shared core. Key skills discussed include stakeholder management and the ability to say no—capabilities rarely taught in standard engineering career paths. The episode also covers why FDE engagements fail, lessons from the field, training gaps, success criteria, and how to build high-impact teams.
