Summary

FDE board lists one forward deployed engineer opening at Sansan, Inc., with a salary range of ¥11.52M to ¥29.17M. Sansan develops products including the Sansan business database, Eight contact app, Bill One invoice processing, and Contract One contract management, and is now shifting focus to Sansan AI, embedding AI agents into customer workflows. The FDE leads customer adoption of Sansan AI: designing business processes, defining KPIs, and personally building data models in BigQuery and dashboards in Looker. Hiring spans five Japanese locations: Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya, Fukuoka, and Kyoto.

About Sansan, Inc.

Sansan develops the business database "Sansan," the business card app "Eight," the invoice receiving cloud "Bill One," and the contract management tool "Contract One," supporting DX for corporate sales, accounting, and contract operations. In recent years, the company has shifted its focus to "Sansan AI," built on its accumulated contact and company data, and is moving toward embedding AI agents into customers' business workflows.

Characteristics of FDE roles at this company

FDEs lead the introduction of Sansan AI to customers; in addition to designing business workflows and defining KPIs, they personally handle data modeling in BigQuery and dashboard building in Looker. Salaries are offered across a wide range of ¥11.52 million to ¥29.17 million, with openings at five locations: Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya, Fukuoka, and Kyoto.
