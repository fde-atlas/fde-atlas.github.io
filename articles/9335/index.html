Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Guides

Rung · 2026 · 5 min read

Summary

Rung offers free, detailed interview guides for every round of the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) loop: coding, SQL, system design, case, and behavioral. The guides explain what the FDE role involves day to day, how it differs from software engineer, sales engineer, and solutions architect roles, and how to prepare for company-specific loops at Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, Scale AI, and others. They also cover resume positioning, take-homes, applied-AI topics like RAG and evals, salary drivers based on 292 job postings, and an eight-week preparation plan.

Key points

  • Free guides cover every FDE interview round: coding, SQL, system design, case, behavioral.
  • Case round uses the C.A.S.E. structure: Clarify, Architect, Solve, Evaluate.
  • Company-specific prep for Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, Scale AI, and more.
  • Compares FDE with software engineer, sales engineer, and solutions architect roles.
  • Salary and market data drawn from 292 open FDE postings across 11 companies.
