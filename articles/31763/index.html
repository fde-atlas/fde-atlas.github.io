System Design Masterclass, Build the systems behind the apps you use every day

systemdesign.academy · 2026-11-22 · 11 min read

Summary

This is a hands-on course for aspiring forward deployed engineers, structured around a single fictional customer, Bramfield Mutual, a Pennsylvania insurer with a claims backlog. Across 63 lessons in 8 parts, it walks the full engagement lifecycle: first meetings, discovery and scoping, integrating with legacy systems (SOAP APIs, SFTP file drops, SSO), cleaning and masking data, building an AI claims triage agent, running evals and shadow mode, deploying production inside the customer's AWS account, and finally handover and expansion. Of the 63 lessons, 18 are published, totaling about 15 hours with 10 labs (8 in the learner's own AWS account) and over 400 diagrams. Some lessons are free; the rest require purchase, with new lessons added as completed.

Key points

  • Entire course follows one fictional customer, Bramfield Mutual, end to end.
  • 63 lessons in 8 parts; 18 published, about 15 hours of material.
  • 10 hands-on labs, 8 run in the learner's own AWS account.
  • Covers discovery, integration, AI pilot, evals, production, and handover.
  • Emphasizes least-privilege access, data masking, shadow mode, and audit trails.

Locked
