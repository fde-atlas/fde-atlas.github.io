Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (4/5, Sep 10, 2025): Fast-paced and lots of exposure

Taro · 2025-04-02 · 2 min read

Summary

A current Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer in London, with four years at the company, rates the role 4/5 with a positive outlook. Pros: an agile, action-oriented culture where FDEs identify needs and build their own slices of projects, with staffing that follows demonstrated strengths; fast progress building on Foundry; and broad exposure to varied problem spaces. Cons: a sink-or-swim environment, limited technical depth since most work sits atop the mature Foundry platform, and frequent long hours depending on the project. Work/life balance scored 3/5 while compensation scored 5/5. The page also lists interview experiences: a 20-minute HR phone screen, an intense four-hour process including a two-hour round, and a two-hour NDA-covered technical stage covering analysis and decomposition.

Key points

  • London FDE with 4 years at Palantir rates it 4/5
  • Agile culture lets engineers self-direct project slices
  • Work builds on Foundry, so less technical than typical SWE roles
  • Sink-or-swim pace with frequent long hours on some projects
  • Interviews include HR screen, 4-hour rounds, and NDA technical stage

Pros

A very agile and action-oriented environment. FDEs are encouraged to find a need in the project and develop their own slice of it. Resourcing responds to this by moving you to other projects that can best make use of the spike you've demonstrated in the process.

You can quickly build on Foundry. Tackling technical problems at a high level means you get a huge amount of exposure to different problem spaces.

Cons

It can be a very sink or swim environment.

It's also not as technical as your usual software engineering roles, as you're generally building on top of Foundry, which is already a well-built-out platform.

Depending on your project, you can be frequently working long hours.
