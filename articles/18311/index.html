favy launches "FDE-style" custom development service for individual companies that have adopted its mobile ordering and subscription SaaS

Original title: favy、モバイルオーダー・サブスクSaaS導入企業向けに「FDE型」の個社カスタム開発サービスを提供開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-08-12 · 8 min read

Summary

Japanese restaurant SaaS company favy announced a per-client custom development service launching August 2026, which it describes as the first domestic application of the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) model to mobile ordering and subscription SaaS in food service and commercial facilities. favy engineers and project managers will embed at client stores and headquarters to observe operations and co-define requirements, building client-specific features on top of its existing SaaS in cycles as short as two weeks. Cited examples include customized analytics screens with generative AI improvement suggestions, and dynamic member pages linked to favy's member database with a dedicated CMS. Pricing is an initial development fee plus monthly operation cost. Widely useful custom features will be folded back into standard SaaS functionality.

Key points

  • favy launches per-client custom development service in August 2026.
  • Uses FDE model: engineers embed at client sites to co-define requirements.
  • Development cycles as short as two weeks on existing SaaS base.
  • Examples: AI-assisted analytics screens and dynamic member pages with CMS.
  • Reusable custom features will become standard SaaS functionality.

— Employees embed themselves in the "front lines" of physical stores and co-create feature development tailored to each client company's challenges —

favy Inc. (Head office: Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo; CEO: Takumi Takanashi; hereinafter "favy"), which provides store DX tools for physical stores and commercial facilities, will begin offering the "favy Individual Custom Development Service," a development service providing company-specific feature development, admin screens, and UI for companies using favy's SaaS tools, starting in August 2026.

This is the first initiative in Japan (※) to fully apply the "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" approach, which has been spreading mainly among leading overseas SaaS companies, to the food service and commercial facility domain.

※Based on favy research (web survey as of August 2026, regarding mobile ordering SaaS for food service)

■ Background: The "last mile" of generic SaaS is holding back results in the field

favy's mobile ordering and subscription services are currently deployed at more than 3,500 locations nationwide, and are used by a wide variety of operators with different management styles, from restaurant chains with multiple locations to food halls and large commercial facilities.

However, as client companies grow and diversify, we are seeing an increasing number of requests for "advanced customization tailored to unique business models and workflows," such as the following.

  • System integration: Want deeper, more seamless integration with their own member platforms, POS, and core systems
  • Brand expression: Want to meticulously craft the UI/UX of the ordering screen to match their brand's world view
  • Data utilization: Want dedicated dashboards optimized with the data and permissions needed for each role, such as headquarters, area managers, and store managers
  • Unified management: As operators of commercial facilities, want to manage sales and operating status across tenants on a single screen

In reality, these unique needs cannot be fully addressed by the standard features of generic SaaS alone. On the other hand, full-scratch development (building a system from zero) incurs costs on the scale of tens of millions of yen and lead times of a year or more.

This gap — "not enough with standard features, but too heavy for full-scratch development" — has been a hindrance to DX promotion for many operators. To solve this challenge and turn SaaS adoption into not merely "systematization" but "reliable business growth," favy has launched the "favy Individual Custom Development Service" as a new approach.

■ favy staff enter clients' front lines and execute feature development together

"FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" is a development model in which engineers are deployed to the front lines of client companies to adapt generic products to each customer's specific challenges. It has been attracting attention in recent years as an approach of "building together until results are achieved" rather than "selling the system and walking away."

With the "favy Individual Custom Development Service," favy's engineers and project managers go directly into client companies' front lines (stores and headquarters) to observe and interview staff about actual operations. Based on this, they add and provide dedicated features tailored to each company, built on favy's existing mobile ordering and subscription SaaS.

The biggest feature is "defining challenges together in the field, rather than waiting for a requirements document." Through on-site dialogue, development, implementation, and verification of features are executed quickly in cycles as short as two weeks.

■ Development examples through the "favy Individual Custom Development Service"

Implementation example 1: Customization of analytics screens (improving field work efficiency and AI utilization)

Challenge

The admin screen displayed only basic transaction history and sales aggregates, so staff had to manually perform secondary analysis of the data and create improvement proposals in order to use it in the field.

Changes after customization

We built dedicated screens enabling intuitive analysis by the dimensions each company values most, such as by product, time of day, and location. In addition, we implemented a prompt output feature that allows generative AI to propose store operation improvement ideas based on field data. As a result, secondary data processing by staff is no longer needed, and field staff can directly review data and immediately take concrete improvement actions.

Implementation example 2: Customization of customer-facing screens

Challenge

The members-only page was created and operated as a static web page, posing a security risk of dedicated URLs leaking externally. In addition, every time stores opened or closed, page update work and production costs were incurred.

Changes after customization

We developed a feature that integrates directly with favy's member database and automatically generates dedicated pages according to each customer's membership status. We also provide a dedicated CMS (content management system) for each company, establishing an operational framework that allows easy in-house updates, resolving security risks and significantly reducing production costs.

■ Approach from implementation to operation

We stay close to the field and quickly and reliably deliver results through the following cycle.

  • Understanding the current state: Through store visits and interviews with headquarters, we visualize actual workflows and bottlenecks.
  • Prototype creation: We present a working prototype as quickly as possible and verify usability and effectiveness in the field.
  • Production implementation: Verified features are implemented on the existing SaaS platform and seamlessly integrated with standard features.
  • Continuous improvement: Even after implementation, a dedicated team works alongside clients, reviewing actual operational data and making continuous updates.

Among the company-specific features developed through the "favy Individual Custom Development Service," those with high versatility that are useful to other companies will be gradually incorporated as standard features of favy's SaaS. This will achieve continuous value improvement across the entire platform.

■ Comment from Representative Director and President Takanashi

In the restaurant industry, the way operations are run and relationships with customers are built has its own unique character for each store and company. Because we ourselves operate restaurant businesses, we have come to realize through daily experience that there are detailed needs that cannot be fully covered by standard system features alone.

Through the "favy Individual Company Custom Development Service," favy will evolve beyond being merely a "SaaS provider" into a company that creates true value through a "co-creation process" with its customers.
