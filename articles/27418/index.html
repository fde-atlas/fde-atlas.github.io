FDE Practical Workshop · On the Front Lines of Delivery | 2026.09.21 Hangzhou Yungu Center

Original title: FDE 实战松 · 前线交付战｜2026.09.21 杭州云谷中心

GOAI 开源周 · 2026-09-20 · 4 min read

Summary

GOAI Open Source Week will hold an FDE-focused hackathon on September 21, 2026 at Yungu Center in Hangzhou, where 20 teams deliver complete client-facing solutions in one day. Two challenges come from real business needs: turning robot data collection into monetizable data assets, and proposing AI upgrades for a business park. Participants interact with a client-simulating AI agent to uncover requirements, then submit a solution document, commercial quote, and runnable Demo on ModelScope's Creator Space. Judging emphasizes requirement insight, business value, and pricing rather than code, with a 19,000 RMB prize pool across 7 winning teams and on-site clients and investors offering project-matching opportunities.

Key points

  • One-day FDE hackathon on 2026.09.21 at Hangzhou Yungu Center, 20 teams.
  • Two real-world challenges: robot data asset monetization and park AI upgrades.
  • Participants query a client AI agent; inquiry records count toward scoring.
  • Deliverables include a solution doc, commercial quote, and runnable ModelScope Demo.
  • 19,000 RMB prize pool, 7 winners, with client and investor project matching on site.

Full text

2026 / FDE HACKATHON · GOAI Open Source Week

Real-world practice · Closing deals Test your solution skills with real requirements

The two challenges are not two industries, but two kinds of clients. Choose one when registering; team formation and competition access open after your application is approved.

01 A data collection team of over a thousand people, a dozen remote sites, thousands of devices—turn real operations in real scenarios into data assets that can both train robots and sell for real money. From collection, annotation, and governance to thousand-person crowdsourced operations, pick the segment you think is most valuable and build it.

Collection Annotation Governance Crowdsourced Operations Data Asset Monetization

02 As an AI + industry ecosystem community, we face different pain points in daily operations across various segments—business attraction and recruitment, enterprise services, industry operations, and property services. Which ones are worth doing with AI, and what can be achieved? That's for FDEs to uncover. Ultimately, deliver an AI empowerment upgrade plan for the park that management can approve on the spot.

Business Attraction Enterprise Services Industry Operations Property Services Which Task to Pick

This is not a coding competition—it's a full-cycle solution delivery exercise.

The challenges come from real business pain points and real projects under negotiation, not assigned essay topics. The clients themselves will be on site on 9/21.

Interact with Agents to simulate real client communication and uncover key information such as requirements, budgets, and available documents.

Deliver a solution, a runnable Demo uploaded to ModelScope Studio, and a commercial quote. The pitch isn't about code—it's a presentation to the boss.

Clients and investment institutions will observe on site; outstanding solutions get project matching and showcase opportunities.

September 21, 2026—one day, start to finish.

03 7 winning teams in total.

19,000 RMB

Special awards and the Open Source Contribution Award cannot be combined with the First Prize

  • All Demos must be uploaded to ModelScope Studio; teams competing for the Open Source Contribution Award must also make their code public on ModelScope Studio.

We're judging "can you close this deal," not lines of code.

  • Depth of requirement insight—what did you uncover from the client Agent that others didn't? Your inquiry records serve as scoring evidence.
  • Business value—does it solve what the client truly cares about? Would the boss want to move forward immediately after hearing it?
  • Demo and deliverables—all Demos must be uploaded to ModelScope Studio and must be accessible and runnable on site. Screen recordings pretending to be live runs are prohibited.
  • Commercial plan and quote—quotes count toward scoring. A high quote backed by a solid plan beats undercutting yourself. This is the biggest difference between this event and other hackathons.
  • Pitch presentation—no code talk; present to the boss. Can your PPT + Demo hold the room in 7 minutes?

Dimension weights and detailed scoring criteria will be released with the official rules; the final version published by the organizing committee prevails.

Please confirm the following before registering.

  • D1 Solution document—PDF or document, ≤15 pages; must include pain point insights, target users, product form, core workflows, implementation plan (data/cost/deployment path), risk statement, and commercial quote.
  • D2 Commercial quote sheet—one separate page; must include one-time implementation fee, subscription/maintenance fees, staffing estimates, delivery timeline, and acceptance criteria.
  • D3 ModelScope Studio Demo—required from all teams; must be a runnable Studio Demo. Before the awards, visibility may be set to "public experience only"; teams competing for the Open Source Contribution Award must set it to "public" after winning. Using Qwen Office is not mandatory; choose your own tools.
  • D4 Pitch PPT—PPT files only (.ppt/.pptx); the on-site pitch = presentation materials + Demo demo, 7 minutes total, delivered to clients and judges as a presentation to the boss.
  • D5 Pre-existing assets list (optional)—honestly list any pre-existing code, components, corpora, models, and third-party services; write "none" if you truly have none.
  • Screen recordings pretending to be live runs are prohibited.
  • Each team has 1–3 members; solo registration is allowed. The organizing committee and the team formation plaza can help you find teammates.
  • Photography and videography will take place on site; by registering, you consent to the use of related footage for event promotion.
  • All matters are subject to the interpretation of the organizing committee.
  • Registration closes 9/17. Once your application is approved by the organizing committee, form teams freely in the team formation plaza. Team formation closes at 9/20 14:00; unconfirmed teams will be automatically locked for review at that time.
  • After the team leader "confirms the team," the organizing committee reviews each team. Admission results will be emailed by 9/20 18:00 at the latest; team information cannot be modified after admission.
  • Submissions close at 9/21 16:15; late submissions will not be counted.
  • Please confirm you can attend the full day on 9/21 (check-in at 09:00 through the end of the awards ceremony at 21:00).
  • Personal information—ID numbers are used solely for purchasing competition insurance. For usage, retention period, and withdrawal methods, see the "Personal Information Collection Notice" in the registration form, which requires separate confirmation before submission.

Judges' hard requirement: real hands-on technical delivery experience.

Real clients will observe the pitches on site, and outstanding solutions get project matching and showcase opportunities—closing deals on the day is this event's biggest selling point.

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is not ops support—it's someone who brings technology to the customer's doorstep and turns vague business pain points into actionable solutions. This competition puts the FDE's most authentic scenario—**presenting a solution to a client—on stage, deliberately stripping away some of the hacker flair: the pitch isn't about code, it's a presentation to the boss.

This event is one of the six parallel activities of the GOAI Open Source Week (2026.09.20–09.24 · Yungu Center, Hangzhou, China), held on September 21. Other concurrent events include the World AI Open Source Competition, the Open Source Market, Free Meetups, industry/VC matchmaking sessions, a tech talent job fair, and a Finalists visit to Yunqi.
