FDE Hiring Tracker: who is hiring Forward Deployed Engineers

FDE Times · 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Summary

FDE Times has tracked Forward Deployed Engineer openings daily since 7 Oct 2026 by reading official careers boards (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workday and major company sites). As of 14 Nov 2026, it lists 838 open FDE roles across 169 companies, with 256 posted in the last 30 days and 24% open to remote work. Palantir leads with 95 openings, followed by Databricks (94) and Amazon (AWS) (68). Most roles are in North America (487), concentrated in New York, San Francisco and London, and are mid-level (496) pure FDE positions (555). The article explains its counting methodology and offers public JSON APIs for the data.

Key points

  • 838 open FDE roles at 169 companies as of 14 Nov 2026.
  • Palantir (95), Databricks (94) and Amazon (AWS) (68) post the most roles.
  • 256 roles were posted in the last 30 days; 24% allow remote work.
  • North America dominates (487 roles); top cities are New York, San Francisco and London.
  • Most roles are mid-level (496) and pure FDE positions (555).

How we count

We read the official careers boards (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workday, and the career sites of Amazon, Microsoft, ByteDance and Tencent) of 169 companies that hire FDEs, several times a day, and keep roles whose title names forward deployed engineering or deployment strategy. A role leaves the count when the company takes it down. Seniority and role type are read from the job title.

Use the data

Free to cite with a link. The full list of roles is available as JSON: /api/jobs.json

The daily history (open roles, hiring companies, new and closed roles per UTC day) is at /api/hiring-history.json.
