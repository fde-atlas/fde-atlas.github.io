Introducing the a16z FDE Fellowship

a16z.news · 2026-05-28 · 1 min read

Summary

a16z has launched an FDE Fellowship aimed at building a community for forward deployed engineers. The article argues that in AI-native companies, forward deployment is a form of R&D, not just delivery: strong FDEs extract patterns from real customer workflows and convert them into reusable product capabilities, making future deployments faster and more reliable. Without that feedback loop, the work degrades into bespoke consulting. The author also references their own practical guide covering discovery, evals, and enterprise deployment, and has signed up for the fellowship to share insights on deploying AI safely and accountably.

Key points

  • a16z launched an FDE Fellowship community
  • Forward deployment is framed as R&D, not just delivery
  • FDEs should turn customer lessons into reusable product features
  • Without feedback loops, work becomes bespoke consulting
  • Author joined the fellowship to share AI safety deployment ideas

Full text

This announcement captured something important early: in AI-native companies, forward deployment is not just delivery. It is also R&D.

The best FDEs do more than close the gap for one customer. They uncover patterns in real workflows, turn those lessons into reusable product capabilities, and make every future deployment faster and more reliable. Without that feedback loop, the work risks becoming bespoke consulting, however fashionable the title may be.

Creating a space where practitioners can compare what is working, pressure-test ideas, and develop the playbooks together is exactly what this still-emerging field needs.

I recently published a practical guide exploring this same operating model, from discovery and evals to enterprise deployment and turning customer-specific “gravel roads” into scalable product highways:

Although I am not a traditional FDE, I have had the privilege of developing a means by which any AI can be deployed and used safely in every field and held accountable. I have signed up for the fellowship and I look forward to an opportunity to share my thoughts, experiences and insights before a less reliable, more heavily financed competitor closes this window. I'm available for communication with anyone who agrees or disagrees with the gravity of the work I am proposing.
