Japan's first new job category and certification system defining the "results-driven professional" FDE for the AI era

Original title: AI時代の“成果創出人材”FDEを定義する国内初の新職種・新資格制度

IoT検定制度委員会 · 2026-06-02 · 8 min read

Summary

Japan's IoT Certification Committee has announced a plan for the country's first FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) certification framework, defining FDE as a new profession that designs, implements, embeds, and scales AI-agent-driven business transformation while owning continuous results. Unlike conventional credentials that test knowledge, the framework uses a three-tier model—exam (knowledge), accreditation (practical skill), and certification (measured outcomes)—across three levels, with embedding and scaling as mandatory responsibilities. Alpha and beta Level 1 exams are planned from July 2026, Level 2 accreditation within the year, and Level 3 within roughly a year, alongside partnerships with AI vendors, SaaS firms, SIers, and educational institutions.

Key points

  • The initiative was driven by practitioners Toru Yamaguchi, Mitsuhiro Oishi, and Mitsuru Chikamori after field DX work.
  • FDE covers four phases: DESIGN, BUILD, EMBED, and SCALE, with the last two mandatory.
  • The three-tier model evaluates knowledge, practical implementation, and actual business results.
  • Level 3 certifies 'FDE Consultants' who can reproduce results across organizations.
  • Level 1 exams start July 2026; Level 2 operation is targeted within the year.

Full text

The IoT Certification Committee announces Japan's first "FDE Certification Framework (Forward Deployed Engineer Certification Framework)" concept

The IoT Certification Committee's FDE Certification Project has announced the "FDE Certification Framework (Forward Deployed Engineer Certification Framework)" concept as Japan's first※1 certification system, independently defining "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" with consideration for Japan's circumstances as a new professional profile required in the DX and AX era, with the aim of developing and evaluating such professionals.

This system aims to evaluate not "people who can use AI" but "people who can continuously generate business results using AI agents." Through a three-tier evaluation model that progressively certifies knowledge and application skills, practical abilities, and results, it makes the results-generating professionals of the AI era visible.

While DX promotion and the use of generative AI are advancing rapidly in companies, many organizations face challenges in moving from the PoC (proof of concept) stage to production operation, embedding solutions in the field, and generating results.

The FDE Certification System is not a system that certifies people who can use AI.

It is a system that evaluates professionals who can achieve business transformation using AI agents and continuously generate results from it.

With the spread of generative AI, many companies have begun adopting AI. However, in reality, only a limited number of organizations reach the point of embedding it after implementation and generating results.

The FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a new profession defined to solve this challenge.

Designing business transformation with AI agents, implementing it, embedding it in the field, and expanding its results across the entire organization.

The FDE Certification System is Japan's first talent evaluation system that assesses such practical abilities and results-generating capabilities.

※1 Based on our own research, conducted in May 2026. The survey targeted certification systems in Japan whose names include "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" and whose evaluation targets are business transformation and results generation using AI agents.

Challenges in DX and AI Implementation

In recent years, many companies have been investing in AI and DX.

However, in the field,

・Projects end at the PoC stage ・Systems are implemented but not used ・Business transformation is not achieved ・Results are not measured ・Adoption after implementation does not progress

and many other challenges arise.

The IoT Certification Committee believes the root cause is not a shortage of "people who can implement" but a shortage of "professionals who take responsibility for producing results in the field."

The FDE Certification System is also a proposal for a new definition of a profession in the AI era.

Rather than the conventional job categories of "engineer," "consultant," and "project manager," we define the FDE as a new profession responsible for field transformation and results generation while leveraging AI.

What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?

An FDE is a professional who designs business transformation using AI agents, implements it, embeds it in the field, and takes responsibility all the way through to continuous results generation.

An FDE is not merely a professional who uses AI.

Their mission is to root AI agents in the field, build new business processes where humans and AI collaborate, and deploy the results across the entire organization.

The FDE Certification System is designed as a new evaluation standard to make such professionals socially visible.

StagePhaseRole
Stage 1DESIGNAnalyze business challenges and redesign operations with AI as the premise
Stage 2BUILDBuild systems that leverage AI and digital technologies
Stage 3EMBEDEmbed solutions in the field and create a state of continuous use
Stage 4SCALEDeploy results across the entire organization in a reproducible form and connect them to continuous value creation

The greatest distinguishing feature of the FDE is that "EMBED (embedding)" and "SCALE (expanding results and organizational deployment)" are defined as mandatory areas of responsibility.

Many DX and AI projects end at "implementation" or "deployment." However, the FDE's mission is to take responsibility through to embedding in the field and horizontal deployment of results, achieving continuous value creation.

The World's First Three-Tier Evaluation Model: Certification, Accreditation, and Verification

The FDE system introduces a new mechanism that evaluates not only professionals' knowledge but also "the embedding of business transformation using AI agents and the generation of results."

FDE Talent Evaluation Framework

CategoryEvaluation TargetWhat Is Certified
Certification (Kentei)Knowledge and understandingMeasures and certifies the skills that underpin implementation capability
Accreditation (Nintei)Practical abilityMeasures and certifies implementation and deployment capability in the field
Verification (Ninsho)Track record of resultsEvaluates and certifies results generated in the field

Conventional certification systems were mechanisms for proving "possession of knowledge."

The FDE system goes further, evaluating the entire growth process of:

Having knowledge ↓ Being able to implement ↓ Being able to embed ↓ Being able to produce results

This design is based on the belief that what companies truly seek is not "certificate holders" but "results generators."

Level Design

The FDE system currently consists of three levels.

Level | Goal

Level 1Shift to AI-first thinking
Level 2Implementation and deployment
Level 3Results generation, organizational transformation, and reproducibility
レベル3 成果創出・組織変革・再現性

A System Designed for the AI Agent Era

In the FDE system, AI agents are positioned not merely as tools but as

"a structure for redesigning business operations."

They are treated as a concept to understand at Level 1, a core of implementation at Level 2, and a means of results generation at Level 3, aiming to develop practical professionals suited to the AI agent era.

Background of the System's Creation

This system was born from the sense of crisis felt by practitioners who support DX and AI implementation in the field.

Background of the System's Creation

This system was born out of the sense of crisis felt by practitioners who support DX and AI implementation in the field.

Toru Yamaguchi, Mitsuhiro Oishi, and Mitsuru Chikamori, three members of the DX project on the IoT Certification Board, had long felt a common challenge through their years of hands-on involvement in corporate transformation.

That challenge was:

"Technology keeps evolving, yet results are not being achieved."

This is the reality they faced.

The emergence of generative AI and AI agents has dramatically expanded the possibilities for companies. However, in many organizations, the introduction of these technologies has become an end in itself, and they have not reached the results they should originally be achieving.

On May 22, 2026, during a conversation after the event "Practical Seminar on Generative AI and AI Agent Utilization for Executives" where the three spoke, these three, who continue to support the front lines, arrived at a shared realization.

"Now is the time when we must change the very definition of talent and roles."

From there, the project members and generative AI engaged in relentless discussion and design, rapidly advancing the formulation of the concept for this certification system and preparations for its announcement.

Precisely because we ourselves have struggled in the field, suffered in the field, and challenged ourselves to create results in the field, this certification system embodies the passion of practitioners.

The origin of the FDE concept lies in the idea that "talent capable of designing and implementing optimal AI utilization for the unique challenges of each company is essential."

An FDE is not a role that simply introduces a specific AI service.

They analyze a company's current state, extract challenges, select the AI models and AI agents best suited to solving them, and take responsibility for everything from implementation in the field to the creation of results.

Furthermore, the "Engineer" in FDE does not necessarily mean a technical specialist who writes programs.

The essence of an FDE is a practitioner who understands frontline operations and the characteristics of AI while driving operational improvement and transformation.

Comments from the Project Members

"At this moment, as generative AI and AI agents are set to transform society dramatically, what is truly needed is not people who can use tools, but talent who can create results in the field.

The FDE Certification System is a challenge aimed at making transformation-driving talent socially visible.

We hope this system will accelerate DX promotion and AI utilization at Japanese companies, leading to new value creation and the resolution of social issues in the Society 5.0 era."

FDE Certification Project: Toru Yamaguchi, Mitsuhiro Oishi, Mitsuru Chikamori

Future Developments

The IoT Certification Board will promote the following initiatives going forward.

・Formulation of the FDE certification syllabus ・Design of the accreditation system ・Design of the certification system ・Implementation of corporate demonstration projects ・Collaboration with educational institutions ・Recruitment of partner companies ・Preparation for launching the Level 1 exam

After conducting alpha and beta exams for the Level 1 exam starting in July 2026, the official exam will begin. We also aim to launch Level 2 accreditation operations within the year. Furthermore, we are considering launching Level 3 certification operations within one year.

In addition, we will advance collaboration with AI vendors, SaaS companies, SIers, and educational institutions, aiming to build a cross-industry talent development ecosystem.

What the FDE Certification System Aims to Achieve

The FDE Certification System is not merely a qualification program.

It is a new definition of professions in the age of AI agents, and a challenge toward new standards of talent evaluation.

We aim for a society where

"what you know"

is not what matters, but rather

"what you have achieved"

is what is evaluated.

The FDE Certification System will promote the creation of a new foundation to make visible to society not "people who can use AI," but "people who can deliver results with AI agents."
