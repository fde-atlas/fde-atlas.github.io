The Forward Deployed Engineer Guide

FDE Interviews · 2026-08-09 · 1 min read

Summary

This is a free, open reference guide from FDEInterviews covering the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role, last updated August 2026. It explains the role's Palantir origins, day-to-day work and the deployment lifecycle, 2026 salary bands in the US and LPA ranges in India, and the skills tested in hiring. It also maps the 2026 hiring landscape from Palantir to OpenAI's deployment company, distinguishes FDEs from Solutions Engineers, Sales Engineers and Applied AI Engineers, defines the Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE) variant, and walks through the Palantir decomposition interview with a worked example. A practical starting point for anyone considering the role.
