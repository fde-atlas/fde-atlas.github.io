Best platforms for hiring Forward Deployed Engineers

Uplers Solutions Private Limited · 2026-08-11 · 5 min read

  • If you want India’s top 1% engineers, screened and matched for you, start with Uplers. You get 3 to 5 vetted profiles in about 48 hours and you never sift through the pile yourself.
  • If you want fast freelance or contract-to-hire and don’t mind doing the vetting, Upwork gives you global reach and a paid pilot before you commit.
  • If you are hiring senior GTM-flavored FDEs in the US, Betts knows that exact talent pool.
  • Below that, I’ll walk you through LinkedIn referrals, FDE Pulse, and Hacker News, and where each one fits.

Hiring a forward deployed engineer means you need someone who can sit inside a client’s actual system and make sure your product works there. So the platform you pick needs to understand the actual depth of this requirement.

We recommend 6 platforms, each for different situations. Here is what each one actually does, how it works, and which stage of company it suits.

1. Uplers

Uplers is a hiring platform that matches startups with India’s top 1% of engineering talent. Every kind of engineer and technical role that a startup needs is available here including Forward Deployed Engineers. Talent goes through a basic AI screening plus a human recruiter actually speaks to them, so you see the top few profiles. Each one is scored on startup readiness and technical skill.

You get 3 to 5 vetted profiles in about 48 hours. You don’t source. Uplers does that, you make the final call.

3 engagement models, pick what fits: sourcing-only, full-time, and contract with payroll and benefits managed for you. Full-time hires come with a 90-day replacement. Contract hires come with a lifetime replacement.

If you are an early-stage startup looking for engineers (including Forward Deployed Engineers) in India, then Uplers is your go to.

2. Upwork

Upwork is a freelance marketplace. You post the role, freelancers apply, you pick directly. No agency in the middle. You write the post yourself or use their draft tool, then filter by skill, rating, job history, and badges like “Top Rated Plus” or “Expert-Vetted.”

You can find talent across India, Pakistan, Turkey, Colombia, and dozens more countries, often well below US rates.

Structure it hourly, fixed-price by milestone, or contract-to-hire. Payment sits in escrow, so you release money only once you approve the work. Best for project-based or specialized short-to-mid-term work, and when you want a paid pilot before committing.

3. Betts Recruiting

Founded in 2009, it places go-to-market talent for scaling tech companies, and forward-deployed engineers sit inside that world. Their sourcing tool, Betts Connect, holds profiles of tens of thousands of GTM candidates, including many from Palantir and prominent AI companies.

Their recruiters know how an FDE differs from a sales engineer or a solutions architect, and they work across SaaS, AI, GovTech, and cybersecurity. They also advise on market trends and pay benchmarks. Best for US companies hiring senior, GTM-heavy FDEs.

4. Hire via Referrals

Post the role on LinkedIn and let referrals do the work. For an early-stage startup this is often the strongest path. People in your network can vouch for a candidate, so that person is more likely to fit your culture and stay.

However, you won’t get many applications this way. Later, once you’re bigger, you get the opposite problem: so many applications that finding the right ones gets hard. Great for your first few hires through trusted connections.

5. FDE Pulse

FDE Pulse is a niche job board and market-intelligence site built only for forward-deployed engineers. It pulls FDE postings from major boards and company career pages into one searchable place, with salary benchmarks and company profiles. You can browse openings across 50+ companies and filter by location, from San Francisco and New York to London and India.

There is no “post a job” button yet. For now, if you want your role listed, email hello@fdepulse.com and ask because they do pick up open roles from other job boards and post it here. The good part about this is that only serious FDEs would be exploring this site and applying so you only get the choicest and most serious applications. Helps in cutting down the volume.

6. Hacker News “Who Is Hiring”

Every month, Hacker News runs a “Who Is Hiring” thread, and startups post open roles in the comments. It is free, and it reaches an engineering-heavy crowd that reads and trusts the site.

There’s no vetting and no filtering. You post your role, interested engineers reply, and you take it from there. It works well for technically strong candidates who follow the startup world closely. Just know the sourcing and screening are fully on you so if you don’t have time to manually screen these applications then best to go with a hiring platform.

Where we would ask you to go

Hiring FDEs is not like hiring any other regular role, you need to be very careful. Referrals and Hacker News are great when you’re tiny and hands-on. Betts is strong for senior US GTM hires. FDE Pulse is the place to check pay before you write an offer. Upwork is the fastest way to test someone on a real paid task. But if you want the top 1% of engineers, screened and matched, without spending your week reading resumes, Uplers should be your go to.

Pick the one that matches your stage and how much of the work you want to own.
