Notion AI & Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs (8)

Forward Deploying · 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Summary

Forward Deploying tracks 8 open AI engineering roles published on Notion's own careers page: 3 FD Architect, 3 AI Engineer, and 2 AI leadership positions. The median published US base salary is $272k; AI Engineer roles have a median midpoint of $288k, about 28% above the $225k market median the site tracks. Six of eight listings publish salary ranges, six explicitly require production code experience, five are remote-eligible, and the median experience requirement is 6 years. Two roles opened in the last 30 days; data is as of November 14, 2026. The site notes it is not recruiting on Notion's behalf.
