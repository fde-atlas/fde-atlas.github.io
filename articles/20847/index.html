What is FDE in Defense Tech? | Differences from AI Companies Seen Through 12 Anduril Job Listings and Japan's Entry Point

Original title: 防衛テックのFDEとは|Andurilの求人12件で見るAI企業との違いと日本の入口

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-11-08 · 18 min read

Summary

Based on job postings captured on November 8, 2026, this article analyzes 12 FDE openings at US defense tech company Anduril and compares them with FDE roles at OpenAI and Shield AI. The author notes Anduril's FDE postings grew from 2 in late September to 12 by November 7, and that the title now covers product managers and manufacturing leads, not just engineers. Defense-sector FDEs differ sharply from AI-company FDEs: 9 of 12 roles require or expect US security clearances, travel ranges from 10% to 80% with some postings requiring stays up to two months, and salary caps ($113,000–$254,000 base) sit below OpenAI's New York FDE range. In Japan, Anduril has no FDE openings; the only Tokyo-based related roles are Sakana AI's defense-focused FDE and Palantir's FDSE for the Japanese government.

Key points

  • Anduril's FDE postings grew from 2 to 12 between late September and November 7, 2026.
  • The 12 roles include 6 engineers, 4 product managers, and 2 production leads.
  • 9 of 12 postings require US security clearances; travel spans 10–80%, some up to two months per trip.
  • Base salaries of $113,000–$254,000 are below OpenAI's $185,000–$300,000 range on all 12 postings.
  • Japan entry points are limited to Sakana AI's FDE and Palantir's FDSE in Tokyo.

Anduril

Forward Deployed Engineer, Air Defense (FDE)

  • Location
  • Costa Mesa, California, United States
  • Compensation (as stated in the job posting)
  • USD 113,000 - 155,000 (US Salary Range)
  • Date the job posting was retrieved

Job Description

  • Handle on-site technical operations and maintenance, as well as customer support and training, for a military air defense project
  • Report the status of problems that occur on site to the company and the customer
  • Perform root cause analysis on failures of on-site defense systems

Requirements

  • 3+ years of experience solving problems spanning hardware, software, and networks
  • Ability to travel to remote locations around the world for up to 2 months at a time, up to 80% of the year
  • Ability to obtain and maintain a US Top Secret or Secret clearance
  • Ability to meet pre-deployment health and medical requirements
  • Ability to climb towers to install and maintain cameras and radars, and to repair generator, solar power, and hydraulic systems
  • Work on site in Costa Mesa (no remote work; relocation if necessary)

Key points of this job posting: The posting itself says the internal title is Technical Operations Engineer. The company's job board lists 21 openings whose job titles include Technical Operations Engineer (as of November 8, 2026), suggesting that the FDE name applies only to some of the same work. With requirements extending to climbing towers to maintain equipment, the core of the job is on-site maintenance and troubleshooting rather than writing code.

Is there an entry point to work as an FDE in the defense sector in Japan?

There is currently no Anduril FDE position based in Japan. The related publicly posted Tokyo-based positions are Sakana AI's FDE serving the Ministry of Defense and Self-Defense Forces, and Palantir's FDSE for the Japanese government. Palantir's job posting does not state that it is limited to defense.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is an FDE at a defense tech company?

An engineer or related role at a defense tech company who goes into the field, such as units, bases, and test ranges, to deploy and keep the company's software and equipment running. U.S. companies Anduril and Shield AI are hiring for these roles. Of Anduril's 12 openings, 9 require holding or obtaining a U.S. security clearance, and travel ranges from 10-80% depending on the position.

Q: What is the salary for Anduril FDEs?

The compensation sections of Anduril's 12 FDE postings as of November 8, 2026 ranged from $113,000 to $254,000. They vary by role and location; the 6 engineer roles range from $113,000 to $254,000 (3 of them at $166,000-$253,000). The compensation sections show base salary only, and while the postings state that most full-time offers include equity compensation, the amounts are not specified.

Q: Can you apply for Anduril FDE roles from Japan?

As of November 8, 2026, Anduril has no FDE positions based in Japan. Many of its U.S. positions require holding or obtaining a U.S. security clearance, and our editorial team considers them unrealistic to apply for from Japan. Anduril's establishment of a Japanese subsidiary was reported in December 2025, and the company said it planned to expand hiring in Japan. A business development position to be based in Tokyo or elsewhere has been posted.

Q: Is there a way to become a defense-sector FDE in Japan?

Among publicly posted positions as of November 8, 2026, Sakana AI's FDE (Defense), serving the Ministry of Defense and Self-Defense Forces, is being hired for a Tokyo-based role. It requires hands-on experience in data science and machine learning, LLM application development, Python, and more, plus native-level Japanese, while work experience at the Ministry of Defense or Self-Defense Forces, or project experience in SI for defense-related manufacturers, trading companies, or government agencies, is a preferred qualification. Palantir is also hiring an FDSE for the Japanese government in Tokyo, but it is not stated whether the role is limited to defense.

Q: Is the experience required for defense FDEs different from that for regular FDEs?

It depends on the role. Some roles, such as Anduril's Air Defense, require experience in problem-solving spanning hardware, software, and networks, as well as on-site maintenance and troubleshooting. On the other hand, the company's FDSE is a software role requiring development experience in languages such as Go and Python. FDEs at generative AI companies focus on experience building production applications within customers' systems.
