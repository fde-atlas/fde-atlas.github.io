Summary

Written by Shohei Tokumitsu, CEO of DeFactory, this article outlines the company's Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) offering in Japan. It positions the FDE as a hybrid of software engineer and consultant who works on-site at clients, committing to problem resolution rather than just delivering hours like SES vendors or advice-only consultants. The piece compares FDEs with SES and traditional consulting across engagement style, accountability, and contracts, lists seven support areas (0-to-1 development, legacy modernization, generative AI adoption, data platforms, tech organization building, DX, security), and describes a phased engagement process under quasi-delegation contracts, including a flexible lab-style model that sometimes recommends not building.

Key points

  • FDE is framed as a hybrid of engineer and consultant who embeds at the client site.
  • Unlike SES, FDEs commit to solving problems, not just supplying man-hours.
  • Seven support areas span 0-to-1 builds, legacy modernization, generative AI, and data platforms.
  • Claimed results: development lead time cut to one-third, on-site efficiency up 2.5x.
  • Engagements use flexible lab-style, quasi-delegation contracts, sometimes advising against building.

Many companies stall their DX efforts at this wall.

Requirements are defined. The system is running. But it never takes hold in the field. The vendor delivered and left. No one in-house understands the technology. Before you know it, the same challenges remain.

The essence of this problem is that there is no "person" between technology and the field who can commit to the business.

DeFactory's FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) serves as that "person."

Neither a consultant making recommendations from outside nor a developer (SES) building to spec, the FDE deploys to the client's front lines, working directly with data, operations, and technology to lead and accompany the resolution of challenges.

The FDE is a hybrid role: "software engineer × consultant."

Understanding the business and management context, the FDE works with clients at the front lines (Forward) to identify challenges and guide them to solutions. Writing code, redesigning business processes, making technology selection decisions—all are part of the role.

From 0→1 development and architecture design to improving and extending existing products.

  • You can't define an MVP (minimum viable product), and development has stalled
  • You have no one who can handle upstream design for new features or modifications to existing ones

Resolving legacy system debt, plus design and execution support for modernization and cloud migration.

  • Your legacy system is aging, but you can't make migration decisions
  • Technical debt has accumulated, and development speed is slowing
  • You can't design the migration from on-premises to the cloud (AWS / GCP)

From embedding generative AI into operations, designing and implementing AI agents, and designing PoCs for AI use to production deployment.

  • You tried ChatGPT and similar tools, but they never took hold in your operations
  • You can't design the requirements for integrating AI agents into business workflows
  • You can't calculate ROI from AI use or design PoCs
  • You can't design countermeasures for AI risks (hallucinations, data leaks)

From building data pipelines, setting up DWHs, introducing BI, and managing data quality. We transform decision-making into a data-driven process.

  • Data across multiple systems isn't connected, and you have no analytics platform
  • You're stuck in a "we have data but can't use it" state
  • Executive dashboards aren't in place
  • Data is siloed by department, preventing cross-functional use

Building technology organizations, complementing the CTO function, and designing and executing in-housing roadmaps. We help you break free from vendor dependency.

  • You have no CTO or can't hire one, and technical decision-making has stalled
  • You can't map out a roadmap for transitioning from outsourcing to in-house development
  • Development processes and CI/CD aren't automated
  • You have no evaluation system or management structure for your engineering team

From formulating DX strategy to visualizing, standardizing, and embedding tools in business workflows. We resolve the "implemented but unused" problem.

  • You want to drive DX but don't know "where to start"
  • You introduced SaaS tools, but they never took hold in the field
  • Operations depend on specific individuals, and work stops when they leave
  • The coexistence of digital and analog processes has made operations complicated

Secure architecture design, compliance with personal information protection laws and regulations, and building testing frameworks.

  • You want to leverage the cloud and generative AI, but can't get past your company's security policies
  • You can't organize the requirements for compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act and GDPR
  • Production failures keep recurring, and root cause analysis isn't being done

CTO function support for seed to early-stage companies, technology stack selection, PoC advancement, and technical due diligence for fundraising.

  • You have to start product development without a CTO
  • No one has been decided to handle technology stack selection and infrastructure design
  • You can't select and manage development contractors
  • PoCs for new businesses aren't moving forward

DeFactory's FDE support resolves phase-by-phase bottlenecks end-to-end, from planning and conception through to self-sufficiency.

Challenge: Requirements in a vacuum. Ideals and needs diverge, and it's unclear what to build.

Value delivered: Prototype-driven development creates mockups in just a few days. By discussing while looking at working products, we accelerate requirements definition. We also provide quick technical verification (PoK) using real data, so you won't be swayed by buzzwords.

Challenge: Deployed but not used in the field, becoming a shell. Field resistance and distrust of data become obstacles.

Value delivered: We embed technology directly into field workflows to improve adoption rates. We identify key people and drive transformation based on purpose and goals, while rebuilding a foundation of trust through data cleansing and monitoring.

Challenge: Security walls and data silos. Hidden shackles during deployment and scaling.

Value delivered: We support coordination with IT and legal departments through masking processes and compliant environment design. We lead ETL automation and integration into a DWH, building a data platform ready for use at any time.

Challenge: Stuck at PoC. The first project succeeded, but there's no repeatability for subsequent ones. Stalled by legacy system integration.

Value delivered: We shorten the path from MVP design to production to 3-4 months with an agile approach. By templating the deployment process, we build a scalable structure. We also handle integration architecture design, including API Gateways.

Challenge: Vendor lock-in. Know-how doesn't stay in-house, and the system remains a black box.

Value delivered: Through pair programming-based hands-on technology transfer, we provide skill transfer until your team members can modify code and operate the system on their own. We build automated detection and retraining pipelines to maximize the system's sustained value.

Our core business is marketing support. We have expertise in new business development and business operations for our own services, and support clients with a deep understanding of their business models.
