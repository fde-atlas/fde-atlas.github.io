NTT Data FDE Job Openings: Roles and Salaries from 3 Positions in Japan and the US

Original title: NTTデータのFDE求人|日本3件と米国の求人で読む役割と年収

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-11-20 · 18 min read

Summary

Based on NTTデータ's official job postings and announcements, this article examines what its FDE roles involve, whom they target, and how they pay. On November 18, 2026, NTTデータ announced a plan with Microsoft to deploy over 500 Microsoft Frontier engineers worldwide. As of November 20, its Japanese careers site listed three FDE positions with salaries of 7–14 million yen (technology) and 7–11 million yen (finance, public sector), none requiring a minimum years of experience—instead emphasizing customer-facing work and generative AI prototyping. The technology posting defines FDE as close to Palantir's model, recruiting both business-side Echo and engineering-side Delta. US postings at NTT DATA, Inc. require 5+ years of experience, and a Managing Director role to build a global FDE function leads a team of up to 20 under a "70% standard, 30% custom" model, signaling a shift from bespoke projects toward productized AI services.

Key points

  • NTTデータ plans 500+ Microsoft Frontier engineers with Microsoft partnership.
  • Three Japanese FDE postings pay 7–14 million yen, no experience-year requirements.
  • Technology posting mirrors Palantir's FDE, hiring both Echo and Delta profiles.
  • US FDE roles demand 5+ years; the Managing Director role requires 15+.
  • US FDE head role: 20-person team, 70% standard / 30% custom model.

NTT DATA

【テクノロジー】生成AI×AI-BPRコンサルタント/FDE(AIエージェントによる業務変革の構想策定から実装・成果創出まで)<1350>

  • Expected annual salary: 7 to 14 million yen

Job Description

  • Embed deeply at customer sites and rapidly iterate on the implementation and improvement of generative AI and AI agent systems to directly drive business outcomes
  • Echo (business side): Define transformation themes based on an understanding of the customer's business and challenges, and lead implementation, adoption, and results at the site
  • Delta (engineering side): Design and prototyping of AI agents and RAG, implementation using the company's own AI agents, and integration with business systems and data
  • Continuously refine prompts, workflows, and evaluation mechanisms based on feedback from the field
  • Lead projects from PoC through production and rollout, feeding field insights back into general-purpose solutions and products

Required Qualifications

  • Experience advancing projects while engaging customers and related departments
  • Experience leading a team of three or more (for the assistant manager position)
  • Experience in customer-facing roles (consulting, SE, pre-sales, implementation and adoption support, etc.)
  • Experience in application design and development, or in developing systems and prototypes using generative AI (LLMs, RAG, AI agents)

Preferred Qualifications

  • LLM app development, development in Python or similar, building cloud-based infrastructure
  • MLOps/LLMOps and building AI evaluation frameworks
  • English proficiency (TOEIC 700 or above)

Key points of this job posting: Echo covers the business side and Delta the engineering side, and candidates may take on either one or both. Either way, facing customers is a given, and the posting emphasizes experience in customer-facing work.

What can we learn from the posting for the head of the FDE division?

責任者の求人から分かるのは、NTTデータがFDEを、案件ごとの手作りから、繰り返せる製品型のAIサービスに移るための仕組みとして作ろうとしていることです[7]。求人は、FDEの機能を新しく作り、それを世界で率いる人を探すと書いています[7]。

最大20人です。発表の「500人超」とは桁が1つ違います[1][7]。ただし、500人はMicrosoftと組む業界向けの解決策のための計画で[1]、責任者の求人はAIを製品のように提供するためのFDEの部門です[7]。編集部は、NTTデータの中で「FDE」の言葉が、Microsoftと組む大人数の現場の部隊と、製品化を進める少数の部門の、少なくとも2つの意味で使われていると読んでいます。米国の採用サイトだけでも、製品の導入、営業のための試作、Salesforceの事業部の席と、FDEの中身は3通りに分かれていました[8][10][11]。

How should people at SIers view NTT Data's FDE?

日本の求人は、SIerでSEやプリセールスとして顧客の前に立ってきた人を、正面から対象にしています[2][4]。テクノロジー分野の求人は、FDEの条件にSE・プリセールス・導入支援を例に挙げ、公共の求人はアプリ開発からアーキテクチャや事業の領域に役割を広げた経験を歓迎しています[2][4]。

  • 生成AIで1つ作っておく: テクノロジー分野と金融の求人は、生成AIを使った試作や技術検証の経験を条件の候補に挙げている[2][3]。業務で無ければ、自分の業務を題材に小さく作って説明できるようにする
  • 顧客と課題を決めた経験を話す: 顧客の業務課題を聞き取り、要件や解決策に落とした経験を、金融と公共の求人は条件の候補に挙げている[3][4]。テクノロジー分野のFDEも、顧客と直接向き合う仕事の経験を必須にしている[2]
  • 年収の幅を確かめる: テクノロジー分野は700〜1,400万円、金融と公共は700〜1,100万円で、想定役職は課長代理・主任など[2][3][4]。相場はFDEの年収・給料の相場と比べる
  • 働き方を確かめる: テクノロジー分野の求人は、残業が月平均20時間、出社は多い人で週3〜4回、リモート中心の人で月2〜3回と書いている[2]

The overall approach to moving from an SIer to an FDE role is summarized in Changing jobs from SIer to FDE. An example of a Singapore SIer building an FDE organization was introduced in NCS's iFDE.

What remains unclear from this article?

Some things remain unclear from the job postings and announcements alone.

  • 500人の内訳: 発表は計画の人数で、何人を新しく採り、何人を社内から移すのか、日本に何人置くのかは書かれていない[1]
  • 1,000億円の範囲: テクノロジー分野の求人は「生成AIビジネスは2027年までに1,000億円の売上を目指す」と書いているが[2]、もとのOpenAIとの提携の発表は「OpenAI関連ビジネスで2027年度末までに累計1,000億円規模」としている[13]
  • Group company postings: This article examined only NTT Data's experienced-hire site in Japan and the NTT DATA, Inc. careers site. Postings at other group companies, such as in Europe, were not counted
  • 米国の給与の中身: 米国の求人の給与は全米の勤務地を通した開始時の目標報酬の幅で、日本の想定年収とは定義が違う[7][8]

Summary

  • On November 18, 2026, NTT Data announced a plan to deploy more than 500 engineers worldwide to customer sites in partnership with Microsoft
  • There were three FDE postings on the official Japanese careers site, with expected annual incomes of 7-14 million yen and 7-11 million yen
  • None of the three Japanese postings required years of experience, while the US staff-level FDE postings required 5 or more, or 6 or more, years
  • The technology-sector posting defines the FDE as a role close to Palantir's, and recruited for both Echo and Delta
  • The US posting for the head of the FDE unit described a team of up to 20 people, moving from case-by-case handcrafted work to product-type AI services

Frequently asked questions

QWhat does an FDE at NTT Data do?

It is a role that embeds at customer sites, builds generative AI and AI agent systems to deliver business results, and feeds the insights gained back into the company's own solutions and products. The Japanese technology-sector posting defines it as a role close to Palantir's FDE.

QHow much does an FDE at NTT Data earn?

As of the Japanese postings on November 20, 2026, the technology sector had an expected annual income of 7-14 million yen, and the finance and public sectors 7-11 million yen. Titles include section manager, deputy section manager, and senior staff.

QAre there years-of-experience requirements for NTT Data's FDE?

None of the three Japanese postings require years of experience. They require experience facing customers, system development, prototyping with generative AI, and so on. The US NTT DATA, Inc. staff-level FDE postings require 5 or more, or 6 or more, years of experience (the leader posting requires 15 or more).

QWhat are Echo and Delta in NTT Data's FDE?

They are the two aspects of the FDE role shown in NTT Data's Japanese postings. Echo is the business side, understanding customers' operations, setting transformation themes, and leading implementation and adoption; Delta is the engineering side, handling AI agent design, prototyping, and implementation. You can cover both.

QWhat is NTT Data's Microsoft Frontier engineers?

It is the engineer workforce NTT Data plans to mobilize worldwide through its partnership with Microsoft, announced on November 18, 2026. They are embedded at customer sites and build industry-specific AI solutions using NTT Data's industry knowledge and Microsoft's technology. The plan is for more than 500 people.
