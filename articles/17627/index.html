Locations · Sydney NSW

Re:Sourced is headquartered in Sydney. We recruit senior engineers across AI, software, data, cyber, cloud and forward-deployed disciplines for scale-ups, public companies and global teams hiring in NSW. 21-day median, brief to signed, 90-day replacement guarantee.

The Sydney market

Sydney is Australia's largest tech hiring market. The deepest pools sit in fintech (Atlassian, Canva, Block, Airwallex, Up), AI scale-ups (Harrison.AI, Cortical Labs, Eucalyptus), enterprise cloud (CBA, Westpac, Macquarie, IAG), and a long tail of Series B-D scale-ups across health, govtech and infrastructure.

Our active Sydney network spans roughly 700 senior engineers. Hybrid is the modal pattern (2-3 days in office) but full-remote is increasingly negotiated as part of total compensation.
