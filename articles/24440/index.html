Microsoft to Support AI Customers with 6,000 Resident Engineers, Establishing New "FDE" Division

Original title: Microsoft、AI顧客を常駐技術者6000人が支援 「FDE」の新部門設立

日本経済新聞社 · 2026-07-02 · 1 min read

Summary

Nikkei reports that Microsoft announced on February 2 the creation of a new unit, Microsoft Frontier Company, to help enterprise customers adopt AI. The organization draws software engineers and industry-specialized consultants from within the company while also hiring AI talent externally, assembling roughly 6,000 FDEs who will be embedded on-site at customer organizations. The move reflects how AI adoption results have become a competitive differentiator, pushing AI vendors to work closely in the field rather than just selling software.

Key points

  • Microsoft launched a new unit called Microsoft Frontier Company to support enterprise AI adoption.
  • About 6,000 FDEs will be stationed at customer sites to provide hands-on support.
  • Staff are drawn from internal engineers and industry consultants, plus external AI hires.
  • The move signals vendors are embedding deeply with customers as AI results face scrutiny.
