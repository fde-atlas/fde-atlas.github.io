Forward Deployed Engineer at Cloudflare: Role, Salary, and Interview (2026)

Cloudflare is the third-largest employer of Forward Deployed Engineers on the market. FDE Pulse counts 31 open FDE roles at the company, behind only Google (74) and Deloitte (54). That is more than OpenAI, Anthropic, or Palantir post at any given moment. And yet Cloudflare almost never comes up when people talk about FDE careers.

The reason is that Cloudflare’s FDE seat does not look like the lab version. Our job board lists it as “Forward Deployed Engineer, Workers AI,” and the AI-at-the-edge story is real. But when you read the actual postings, the center of gravity is enterprise application security, Terraform, and customer migrations. The AI work is the garnish.

What the postings actually say

There is no single Cloudflare FDE job. We read four live or recent postings and they describe at least three different jobs under one title.

The Professional Services variant (Germany, distributed) is a senior technical-advisory seat. Responsibilities are architectural design sessions, solution reviews, and managing configurations through Infrastructure as Code (Terraform). The must-haves are a Solutions Architect or Technical Account Manager background and deep knowledge of Cloudflare’s Application and Security portfolio. Workers and Workers AI coding experience appears only under “nice to have.”

The US FDE posting (Austin, Dallas, Atlanta, or NYC, posted February 2026) asks for 10+ years of customer-facing technical experience. It describes architecting “complex multi-stage migrations,” designing application-security solutions on Workers and network security tools, and advanced Terraform. AI shows up as “drive AI innovation initiatives aligned with customer goals” and a “solid understanding of AI/LLM concepts.”

The engineering-flavored variants (Sweden, the Netherlands, and a Senior FDE seat in London on the Developer Relations team) are closer to the Palantir model. They want 5 to 7+ years of software engineering, TypeScript, Rust, or Go, and ownership of production services. The Swedish posting asks for people “spending 51-100% of time shipping code,” and the work is to write production code inside the customer’s infrastructure and sit in their standups and incident response. Several postings mention on-site presence at customer locations.

The contrarian read: if you want to be the person tuning inference for a frontier model, this is not that job. If you want to be the engineer a bank’s security team trusts to move a decade of legacy edge configuration onto Cloudflare, with some agent work on top, it is. Cloudflare is also pushing hard on the AI side of the platform. In April 2026 it announced that OpenAI’s models, including GPT-5.4, were available inside its Agent Cloud, built on Workers AI. That is the demand FDEs will eventually be pulled into, but today’s postings are still hiring for the platform business.

Compensation: posted bands

Cloudflare publishes pay ranges in jurisdictions that require them, which gives us a rare multi-country comparison. All figures below are from the postings themselves.

Role and location Posted range
FDE, US (NYC/NJ-based band, posted Feb 2026) $184,000–$253,000
Senior FDE, London (hybrid) £95,000–£135,000
FDE, Professional Services, Germany €123,000–€169,000 (including on-target incentive)
Sr. FDE, Hebrew speaker, Netherlands (April 2026, now closed) €101,000–€130,000 plus equity eligibility
FDE, Sweden (hybrid) SEK 760,000–1,045,000

Two things to note. First, the Germany figure explicitly includes on-target incentive pay, so it is not directly comparable to the others, which the postings do not label as base or total. Second, the US band tops out at $253K, well below the $200K–$350K total-comp range on our job board. That board figure is an estimate that likely assumes equity; the posted band is the sourced number. Compared with frontier-lab bands, which routinely clear $300K, Cloudflare pays like a large public infrastructure company, not an AI lab. Our salary guide has the cross-company picture.

The interview

No FDE-specific loop has been published. The closest evidence is Glassdoor’s Cloudflare software-engineer interview data, which is thin: 8 submitted interviews, mostly one-on-ones (42%), with phone screens and skills tests at 17% each, and single reports of a panel, a presentation, and a personality test. Difficulty ranges from average to difficult. Reviewers describe an average 52-day hiring process (about 25 days for software engineers). Treat this as a proxy, not a loop description.

One reported question is useful for FDE prep: end-to-end troubleshooting, such as “how do you troubleshoot a user not able to reach the website.” That is a networking question dressed as a customer problem, and it fits a company whose product is the network. Expect to explain DNS, TLS, caching, and routing out loud, then connect it to a customer’s business impact. For the generic FDE loop structure, see our interview guide.

Process reviews are not uniformly positive. One 2023 candidate called the hiring process the worst they had encountered. Do not over-read a single review, but do not assume a quick reply either.

Where Cloudflare fits

Cloudflare is one of the few FDE employers with real geographic spread: postings across the US, UK, Germany, Sweden, and the Netherlands, and the company advertises distributed roles. If you are outside the US, it is among the better doors; see our Europe guide and remote FDE roundup. If your background is solutions architecture, TAM, or professional services and you want to move toward writing code in customer environments, the Professional Services and US seats are a natural step. If you are a strong full-stack engineer with Rust, Go, or TypeScript, the Sweden, Netherlands, and London seats are the better match.
