Full text

Leveraging Microsoft's AI technologies to accelerate advanced, AI-centered transformation tailored to industry characteristics

[NEW YORK] Accenture (NYSE: ACN) today announced the establishment of a "Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE)" organization, in collaboration with Microsoft, to help enterprises design and build AI more rapidly and deploy and operate it at enterprise scale in production environments.

For many companies, the barrier to AI adoption is not a lack of technology, but the fact that engineering expertise is not being fully applied in the field. To address this challenge, Accenture is partnering with Microsoft and, through the newly established FDE organization, will bring together thousands of engineers with AI skills to work directly with clients. By combining Microsoft's advanced AI technologies with Accenture's deep knowledge of industries and business processes, the organization will help client companies overcome the challenges they face and achieve and scale results across the enterprise.

Comment from Manish Sharma, Chief Strategy and Services Officer, Accenture What matters for creating value with AI is not simply having access to technology, but the ability to turn it into sustained business outcomes. AI adoption succeeds when strategy and engineering come together as one. This collaboration with Microsoft puts engineers at the center of AI-driven transformation and establishes a new model that goes beyond the ideation stage to deliver measurable results across the enterprise.

Comment from Nick Parker, Executive Vice President and Chief Business Officer, Microsoft What customers are looking for is a clear path to business outcomes powered by AI. By combining Microsoft's AI platform, which delivers comprehensive services built on both intelligence and trust, with Accenture's deep industry expertise, the FDE organization will rapidly deliver the highest-quality outcomes for customers.

Working closely with clients, the two companies will move AI initiatives from the ideation stage to production environments in a matter of days, a process that previously took months. Microsoft will provide the AI platform and advanced technologies, while Accenture will lead organizational change management, business process redesign, building industry-specific workflows, and global rollout. In addition, the two companies will leverage Microsoft's Frontier Suite products and proven accelerators to create measurable business value with AI through co-creation with clients and support enterprise-wide scaling of those results.

This initiative builds on the strategic partnership that Accenture and Microsoft have cultivated over many years. The newly established FDE organization will play a central role in driving AI-driven transformation, helping thousands of clients put AI into practical use and achieve coherent enterprise transformation.
