【Dev】FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)【Full-Time】

Original title: 【Dev】FDE（Forward Deployed Engineer）【正社員】

株式会社Jdsc · 2026-03-27 · 5 min read

Summary

Japanese AI company JDSC (listed on Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth, founded 2018, about 274 employees) is hiring a full-time Forward Deployed Engineer in Tokyo. The role embeds in customer worksites to identify problems, shape AI/LLM solutions from vague requirements, lead PoC through production, and continue improving deployments. Requirements: 2+ years of development experience; no prior FDE or AI project experience needed. Salary ranges from 5 to 25 million yen, with flextime, remote work, and benefits including LLM usage subsidies and certification support.

Key points

  • JDSC is hiring an FDE to implement AI transformation (AX) in real operations.
  • The role spans problem discovery, PoC, production rollout, and ongoing improvement.
  • Requires 2+ years of engineering experience; FDE or AI background is not required.
  • Salary is 5–25 million yen with flextime and remote options in Tokyo.
  • Benefits include LLM tool subsidies, book reimbursement, and AWS/GCP certification support.

JDSC Inc.

Description

■ Role We are looking for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) to help implement AX (AI Transformation) not as a "concept" but as a "mechanism that works in the field." The FDE is a position that deeply embeds into the business operations of client companies and leads everything from problem discovery to design, implementation, and operational improvement with a high degree of autonomy. Your mission will be to determine "what to build" and "how far to build it" through dialogue with clients, starting from a state where requirements are not fully defined, and to establish AI/LLM-based solutions in actual business operations. Specifically, you will be involved in the following tasks: ・Embedding into client companies' business operations to identify issues based on workflows, decision-making processes, and constraints ・Collaborating with business consultants and technology consultants to organize AI/LLM utilization policies and priorities from a field perspective ・Leading the conception and PoC development of AI solutions optimized for client businesses ・Quickly bringing prototypes to the field and driving them through to production implementation while incorporating feedback ・Continuing to engage with clients after deployment and leading improvements and enhancements in response to business changes and usage patterns ■ Career Development Highlights ・By taking on technical decision-making on the front lines with clients, you can gain engineering experience that goes beyond just "building things." ・There is a culture that respects on-the-ground judgment in technology selection and architecture design, allowing you to work with a high degree of autonomy. ・We have programs that support self-development investment, such as certification acquisition and book purchases. ・We provide an environment and support for actively using LLMs such as ChatGPT and Claude, as well as AI-driven development tools, in your work. ・We support flexible work styles, including flextime and parental leave systems. ■ Work Style At JDSC, each member can realize diverse work styles suited to their lifestyle and values. Currently, many people work in diverse ways that accommodate their individual circumstances, such as primarily working from the office or remotely, taking parental leave, or using the flextime system. Other examples of benefits related to this position are listed below. Since our founding, many people have valued the "try it first" approach, and as a result, the following have been realized: ・Book purchase reimbursement ・O'Reilly Online Learning ( https://note.com/jdsc/n/n8aced0fb1765 ) ・Subsidies for business use of LLMs such as Claude and ChatGPT ・Subsidies for coding agents (Indy agents) ・Support for obtaining Google Cloud and AWS certifications ・Support programs for working professionals pursuing master's and doctoral degrees

Requirements

・Development experience as an engineer (2+ years) ・Experience organizing issues through dialogue with stakeholders and carrying them through to implementation, even when requirements are unclear ※No prior FDE or AI project experience required.

Preferred Experiences

・Development experience using Python ・Experience developing and operating a variety of software (e.g., mobile, native, PWA, hardware, data platforms, etc.) ・Development experience using LLMs and building RAG systems ・Development experience using modern development environments ・Knowledge of AI agents ・Experience in development and improvement related to business systems and business processes ・Experience driving projects while interacting directly with clients ・Experience in design and operation in cloud environments (AWS/GCP, etc.) ・Knowledge of cloud-based data warehouses (DWH) such as Databricks and Snowflake ※No specific educational background or certifications required.

We are looking for

・Someone who resonates with our company mission of "Upgrade Japan" ・Someone interested in AX (AI Transformation) at large enterprises ・Someone attracted to exploring, proposing, and realizing the ideal form of next-generation business operations using AI ・Someone who takes an interest in clients' business domains and can approach problem-solving from the client's perspective ・Someone who enjoys taking on 0-to-1 challenges
