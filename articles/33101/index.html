On July 14, 2026, Insight Partners published “Demystifying the forward deployed engineer”, the first of a three-part series by Santosh Iyer and Samma Hafeez. It summarizes a panel with speakers from Postman, Databricks, Alteryx and ServiceNow. One example is a Databricks engagement that began as a $30,000 proof of concept and grew into a multi-million-dollar engagement. There is no survey or sample.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: Insight Partners.

What happened

Insight Partners said the first panel was moderated by George Mathew, Managing Director at Insight Partners, with Ankit Sobti of Postman, Jason Martin of Databricks, Rajkumar Irudayaraj of Alteryx and John Aisien of ServiceNow. Insight says it invested in Postman, Databricks and Alteryx.

Part one also describes reconciliation work of 300 to 400 hours a year automated for a U.S. quick-service restaurant chain. It says that once an outcome is proven, ownership transfers to the teams who productize it and run it at scale.

Part three, dated July 27, 2026, gives time-boxed production windows of 3 to 4 weeks at Rocketlane, 6 to 8 weeks at nCino and 12 to 14 weeks at ServiceNow. Its panel had speakers from Rocketlane, ServiceNow, nCino and Wonderful. Part two was not found.

“It’s a very high ownership role. You’re responsible for making it work.”

Our reading

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

The series gives engagement lengths by employer, from 3 to 4 weeks at Rocketlane to 12 to 14 weeks at ServiceNow. If you are comparing roles, this suggests the pace of the work differs widely by company.

If you are hiring them

Two patterns are described: a small paid proof of concept as the entry point, and handover to product teams once the outcome is proven. Both are panel accounts from a handful of companies, not measured data.
