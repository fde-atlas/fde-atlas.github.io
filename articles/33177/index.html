Summary

The roles sit in a dedicated FDE organization and involve building AI solutions with customers from prototype to production. Microsoft hires Forward Deployed Engineers in Bengaluru to build AI solutions hands-on with customers and take them from prototype to production. The India roles sit in a dedicated Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) organization. They ask for less experience than most big-tech FDE roles, but they do expect real production ML or LLM work.

What the FDE does at Microsoft

The Bengaluru postings seen on Foundit and Jobboardly in October 2026 describe the same job. One version is titled “Forward Deployed Engineer/Operator”. The FDE:

  • Builds with the customer. Designs and delivers AI solutions with customer teams, on-site, virtually or hybrid.
  • Takes work to production. Moves projects from concept to prototype to production, iterating with stakeholders as requirements change.
  • Writes the plumbing. Data pipelines, integrations, custom applications and scalable AI workflows.
  • Stays after launch. Monitors and refines what was delivered and drives adoption.
  • Feeds product. Passes what it learns in the field back to Microsoft engineering to shape the AI platform.
  • Raises the bar. Mentors peers and helps set delivery standards.

A senior FDE role in Microsoft’s CoreAI group in Redmond, posted in October 2025, adds ownership across several deployments at once. It also asks the FDE to turn working patterns into reusable tools and playbooks, and to report to Product on where agents, models and tools fall short. That is a good preview of what a senior India FDE would own.

The bigger picture: Frontier Company

In early July 2026 Microsoft set up Frontier Company, a separate unit with $2.5 billion and 6,000 engineers and consultants. They embed in client organizations to deploy AI, according to MarketScale. It draws on Microsoft’s existing forward-deployed engineers and consultants. Accenture and EY plan to align their own FDE programs with it, and you can read about Accenture’s version on our Accenture FDE page. The coverage we found does not say how many Frontier roles will be in India.

A few days after the launch, Microsoft announced cuts of about 4,800 roles. Forkast reports that the company did not link the cuts to Frontier. An analyst it quotes ties them to the cost of AI investment instead. Treat the two as separate news.

Two things stand out in the Bengaluru listing:

  • The bar is skills, not years. One year of coding is the minimum, but the listing also wants ML/LLM solutions in production, DevOps skills, and a solid grasp of security, scalability and reliability. A strong engineer with 1–3 years and one shipped LLM feature fits this. Our guide on freshers becoming FDEs covers how to build that proof.
  • Background checks are mandatory. Every listing requires the Microsoft Cloud background check at hire and every two years after, plus any customer or government screening.

Apna lists the same role under Microsoft Corporation India Pvt Ltd as a full-time, work-from-office job in Bengaluru.

Microsoft doesn’t say which level its FDE roles map to. A role asking for 1–3 years would normally sit near the entry levels, but that is our reading, not a published fact. For comparison, InterviewBit cites Glassdoor’s figure of about ₹14.6 LPA average total pay for FDEs across the Indian market, from 50 self-reported salaries. How to prepare

  • Ship one LLM feature to real users. The listing asks for production ML/LLM work. Build a RAG or agent feature on Azure OpenAI or Azure AI Foundry, deploy it and measure accuracy, latency and cost. Be ready to explain what broke.
  • Show DevOps, not just notebooks. Put that project behind a CI/CD pipeline, containerise it and define the infrastructure as code (Bicep or Terraform). These skills are named in every Bengaluru listing.
  • Practise integration-heavy design. Rehearse adding an AI workflow to an existing enterprise system: identity, data access, security boundaries and monitoring. Explain the trade-offs out loud.
  • Prepare two customer stories. A time requirements changed mid-project and a time you drove adoption after launch. Both map directly to the listing’s responsibilities. Our FDE interview questions guide has worked examples.
  • Brush up coding fundamentals in C#, Python or Java. Microsoft’s process still includes coding. Practise clean, testable code on data-parsing and API problems, not only puzzles.

Microsoft FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Application: India FDE roles appear on Microsoft Careers and are mirrored on job boards such as Foundit and Apna under the title 'Forward Deployed Engineer'. Postings open and close quickly, so set an alert for the exact title.
  • Technical and customer rounds: Microsoft does not publish an FDE-specific interview format and says the process varies by team. InterviewBit's guide suggests preparing coding, system design, project deep-dives, applied AI and cloud scenarios, and behavioural questions.
  • Background screening: Every FDE listing we found requires passing the Microsoft Cloud background check at hire or transfer and every two years after, plus any customer or government screening.

Frequently asked questions

Does Microsoft hire Forward Deployed Engineers in India?

Yes. Microsoft has listed Forward Deployed Engineer roles in Bengaluru in its Forward Deployed Engineering organization, seen on Foundit, Apna and Jobboardly in October 2026. Some of those postings have already closed, so check Microsoft Careers for live openings.

How much experience does a Microsoft FDE role in India need?

The Bengaluru listings ask for a computer science degree and 1+ years of coding in C#, Java, JavaScript or Python, or equivalent experience. They also want production experience building ML or LLM solutions and DevOps skills. Foundit lists the role as 1–3 years.

What is the salary of a Microsoft Forward Deployed Engineer in India?

Microsoft does not post pay on its India FDE roles. As an estimate, Levels.fyi shows Microsoft software engineer total pay in India of about ₹33 LPA at level 60, ₹56 LPA at level 62 and ₹94 LPA at level 64. Microsoft doesn't say which level its FDE roles map to.

What is Microsoft Frontier Company?

It is a separate Microsoft unit launched in early July 2026 with $2.5 billion and 6,000 engineers and consultants who embed in customer organizations to deploy AI. It draws on Microsoft's existing forward-deployed engineers, according to MarketScale.
