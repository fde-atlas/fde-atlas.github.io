Full text

The core of FDE Soken's credibility. Every article is supervised by real FDE practitioners with hands-on field experience. Even when AI drafts the content, the supervisors are responsible for fact-checking, adding first-hand experience, and final accountability (Editorial and AI Use Policy).

Ryu Tamaki is from Yamanashi Prefecture. After graduating from the Department of Physics, Faculty of Science, Chiba University, he joined a major IT company as a new graduate and worked on web application development. Selected for the company's overseas dispatch program, he traveled between San Francisco and Japan while driving the development and operation of native games, gaining experience in creating value from both technical and business perspectives through product development in Japan and abroad. He then led the launch of "vCore," a platform that visualizes congestion conditions at any location, at Vacan, Inc. From architecture design to project management, with an approach of "driving the business without being confined to the engineer's role," he grew it into a congestion analysis and data integration platform backed by deployments at more than 20,000 locations and over 50 patents. In January 2026, he founded VACAN Technologies, Inc., which provides end-to-end support for customers' business transformation using generative AI and data utilization, from planning, development, and operation to in-housing, and became its Representative Director. Rather than "building one big dam," his philosophy is to "dig small wells and eventually connect them into a waterway"—an iterative, practical approach that values small, functional deliverables over elaborate upfront planning, running fast feedback loops from idea to implementation. He works closely on-site with customers, accompanying them from problem discovery through implementation and in-housing. As a practitioner who implements FDE himself, he verifies the factual accuracy of published articles at FDE Soken based on his first-hand field knowledge.
