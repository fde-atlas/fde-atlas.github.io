FDE hiring report: 932 open roles, 85 new postings

FDE Times · 2026-11-23 · 4 min read

Summary

FDE Times' hiring tracker recorded 932 open Forward Deployed Engineer roles for 16–22 November 2026, up from 891, but the headline overstates demand: 37 roles came from 23 newly tracked careers boards, so real net growth was just 4. New postings rose to 85 from 53 while closures fell to 81 from 94. Amazon (AWS) added 9 roles to reach 77, while Salesforce cut 22 to 22 and Microsoft fell to 5. Databricks leads with 106 openings, followed by Palantir at 94. Most roles are mid-level (61%) under the classic FDE title (68%); Python appears in 63% of postings. North America holds 58% of roles, with a median US pay midpoint of $210,000.

  • Amazon (AWS) grew to 77 roles; Salesforce and Microsoft shrank.
  • Databricks leads with 106 openings, ahead of Palantir's 94.

In brief

  • Open FDE roles went from 891 to 932, with 37 coming from 23 newly tracked boards; the hiring change excluding coverage was 4.
  • New postings rose to 85 from 53 the previous week, while closed roles fell to 81 from 94.
  • Amazon (AWS) added 9 open roles to reach 77; Salesforce fell by 8 to 22 and Microsoft by 5 to 5.

The number of open Forward Deployed Engineer roles rose from 891 to 932 in the week from 16 November to 22 November 2026, FDE Times Hiring Tracker data shows. That headline figure flatters the market: 37 of those roles entered the count because 23 companies’ careers boards started being tracked. Excluding that wider coverage, the hiring change was 4 roles, after a fall of 41 in the week from 9 November to 15 November.

More roles were posted and fewer taken down

Companies posted 85 new roles on boards that were already tracked, up from 53 the week before. Closures eased at the same time: 81 roles were taken down, against 94 in the previous week.

The 81 closed roles had all carried a posting date, and the median one had been open for 25 days. The number of companies with at least one open FDE role went from 183 at the start of the week to 199 at the end.

Lighter: roles from newly tracked companies, not new hiring. Source: FDE Times Hiring Tracker, companies’ own careers boards.

The newly tracked boards include Gecko Robotics, Qventus, Firecrawl, Candid Health and Recidiviz. Their 37 roles widen the picture of the market; they are not evidence of new hiring.

Amazon added roles while Salesforce and Microsoft cut back

Amazon (AWS) posted the biggest rise, going from 68 to 77 open roles after posting 15 and closing 6. OpenAI went from 39 to 42, and Thomson Reuters, Intercom, Twenty and CrossCountry Consulting each added 3.

Salesforce moved the other way. It closed 22 roles and posted 14, taking it from 30 to 22 open. Microsoft halved nothing in its posting but closed 7 against 2 new, falling from 10 to 5, while Scale AI went from 12 to 9 after closing 3 and posting none.

Databricks still lists the most open roles at 106, down from 108, followed by Palantir at 94 and Amazon (AWS) at 77. Across the week 22 companies grew their open count and 23 shrank it; Brainbase Labs and Tines both went from 2 open roles to 0.

Mid-level engineers and Python remain the core of the job

The classic Forward Deployed Engineer title accounts for 637 open roles, or 68%. Deployment strategists make up 111 roles (12%), product, architect and lead roles 102 (11%), and AI / ML FDE roles 82 (9%).

Source: FDE Times Hiring Tracker, companies’ own careers boards.

By seniority, 566 roles (61%) are mid level and 314 (34%) are senior, staff or lead. Early-career openings number 52, or 6%.

Source: FDE Times Hiring Tracker, companies’ own careers boards.

Python appears in 63% of open role descriptions (583 postings), against 64% the week before, and AI agents in 60% (559). Machine learning slipped from 38% to 36%, while TypeScript rose from 308 postings to 329 and moved ahead of LLMs, which sit at 313.

Source: FDE Times Hiring Tracker, companies’ own careers boards.

North America holds 58% of roles, with pay centred on $210,000

North America accounts for 539 open roles, or 58%. Europe and the UK follow with 174 (19%), Asia-Pacific with 107 (11%), other places with 98 (11%), and 14 roles (2%) name no place beyond remote.

Source: FDE Times Hiring Tracker, companies’ own careers boards.

New York leads the cities with 90 open roles, ahead of San Francisco at 72, London at 54 and Washington, D.C. at 39; Tokyo has 17. In all, 227 roles (24%) are marked as remote or open to remote work.

Posted pay ranges in 243 postings from 65 companies in the US put the median midpoint at $210,000. The median range runs from $180,000 at the low end to $250,000 at the top.

Methodology

  • Source: the official careers boards (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever) of companies that hire FDEs, read several times a day. Only roles whose title names forward deployed engineering or deployment strategy count; 219 companies have had at least one such role since tracking began.
  • Period: 16 November 2026 to 22 November 2026, UTC.
  • New roles: roles first seen on the board of a company that was already tracked. Roles from a company whose board was just added are wider coverage, not new hiring.
  • Closed roles: roles the company took down. Days open are counted from the posting date the company gives.
  • Role type and seniority are read from the job title; region from the location in the posting.
  • Pay: only base-pay ranges published in US-located postings; the figure is the median of each range’s midpoint.
