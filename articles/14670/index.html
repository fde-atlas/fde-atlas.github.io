Chalk Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-06 · 10 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot counts 9 FDE-family postings at Chalk, tied for 12th among 114 employers: six Forward Deployed Engineer seats (New York, London, Dublin, Singapore, Japan, Tel Aviv), two Forward Deployed Strategist seats (San Francisco, New York), and one Director role in San Francisco. The article details experience requirements, skills, degree lines, workplace types, and posted US salary ranges, re-verifying on November 6 that all nine were still live.

Key points

  • Engineer seats require Python and SQL; NY asks 2+ years, international seats 4+.
  • Director asks 10+ years including 3+ leading customer-facing teams, $300k–$350k.
  • Four of nine postings are remote; only four US seats list salary ranges.
  • Strategist seats sit in Sales, require strong Python and SQL, no years line.
  • None of the nine postings state a visa sponsorship stance.

Published

Chalk posts Forward Deployed Engineer seats in New York, London, Dublin, Singapore, Japan and Tel Aviv, Forward Deployed Strategist seats in San Francisco and New York, and a Director, Forward Deployed Engineering seat in San Francisco. In FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot of public job boards, Chalk has 9 in-page postings, tied for 12th of 114 employers with Cloudflare, Saronic and Snowflake (11 employers have more). Four of the nine list a US salary range, topped by Director, Forward Deployed Engineering at $300,000 to $350,000. Four of nine are remote under the locations-index method (London, Singapore, Japan, Tel Aviv). All 9 were still listed on November 6, 2026. FDE Dispatch is not affiliated with Chalk; the figures come from Chalk's public job postings.

What Chalk calls the role

Chalk posts under three title tracks in this snapshot: Forward Deployed Engineer, Forward Deployed Strategist, and Director, Forward Deployed Engineering. Ashby departments split between Engineering (all six engineer seats and the Director) and Sales (both Strategists).

Engineer seats cover Forward Deployed Engineer in New York (on-site, secondary San Francisco), London (Remote), Dublin, Ireland (on-site), Singapore (Remote), Japan (Remote), and Tel Aviv (Remote). Strategist seats cover Forward Deployed Strategist in San Francisco and New York (both on-site). The Director, Forward Deployed Engineering seat is on-site in San Francisco with secondary New York. What the engineer postings ask for

Years differ by seat. Forward Deployed Engineer NY states 2+ years experience of professional backend software engineering experience. London, Dublin, Singapore, Japan and Tel Aviv each state 4+ years experience of professional backend software engineering experience.

All six engineer seats ask for Proficient in Python, SQL. All six also state Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent.

The New York on-site seat includes office attendance language: We're in the office 5 days a week. When unavoidable conflicts come up, we’re flexible. This is not a hybrid role. Traveling to customer sites is rare since you support teams from multiple customers while working in our office.. The same text is the only engineer row with travel_mentioned true on the snapshot.

The full skill counts across employers are on Forward Deployed Engineer skills.

Strategist seats

Both Forward Deployed Strategist seats (San Francisco and New York) sit in Sales and are on-site. Neither states a numeric years line in WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR. Both ask for Real depth in production infrastructure, and a feel for how systems carrying live traffic actually fail and Strong Python and SQL. Bonus points list Experience deploying software into a customer's own cloud and optional Rust or C++. Both texts are silent on a degree line.

Director seat

The Director, Forward Deployed Engineering seat in San Francisco (secondary New York) asks for 10+ years of engineering experience with 3+ years leading customer-facing engineering teams (forward deployed, solutions architecture, field engineering, or similar). It asks for hands-on skills that are proficient in Python and SQL, and states Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent. The office line matches the New York engineer seat: We're in the office 5 days a week. When unavoidable conflicts come up, we’re flexible. This is not a hybrid role..

Where the roles are and how many are remote

Four of the nine are US-located: Director San Francisco, Forward Deployed Engineer New York, and both Strategists. Five are outside the US: London, Dublin, Singapore, Japan and Tel Aviv.

Ashby labels five seats on-site and four remote. Under the locations-index method, four of nine are remote (London, Singapore, Japan, Tel Aviv), against 176 of 704 (25.0%) across the snapshot. The on-site seats are Director San Francisco, Forward Deployed Engineer New York, Forward Deployed Engineer Dublin, and both Strategists.

What this means if you are preparing for a Chalk FDE loop

  • Pick the track by the work. Engineer seats ship customer backend and platform work in Python and SQL. Strategist seats run technical discovery and scope in Sales. The Director leads customer-facing engineering teams.
  • Match the years line to the seat. New York engineer states 2+ years of professional backend software engineering. The international engineer seats state 4+. The Director states 10+ with 3+ leading customer-facing teams. Strategists give no years number.
  • Read workplace and location together. Treat US NY, SF Director and Strategist seats as on-site. London, Singapore, Japan and Tel Aviv are Remote under the locations-index method.
  • Check the pay line before you assume a range. Only four of nine list US salary bands; the five non-US engineer seats post no compensation tier.

Method and data

Population: the 9 Chalk rows counted in page statistics among the 704 FDE-family postings from 114 employers in the November 3, 2026 postings snapshot. All 9 come from Chalk's public Ashby board. Workplace type, travel and pay fields come from the snapshot rows; years, languages, degree lines, office lines and Ashby departments are hand-verified from the posting texts re-fetched November 6, 2026. Counts are open postings on one day and do not measure headcount.
