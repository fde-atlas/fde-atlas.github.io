Course Curriculum & Free Lessons (2026)

FDE Interviews · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

FDEInterviews outlines its 2026 course curriculum for Forward Deployed Engineer interviews, structured as reading paths through real FDE work rather than isolated question banks. The main path has two courses: Foundations of Forward Deployed Engineering (11 modules covering the role, discovery, reading unfamiliar systems, and a capstone) and The FDE Engagement (11 modules spanning technical discovery, enterprise integration, security reviews, production AI features, and proving value). Three deeper electives cover production AI agents (25 lessons, ten projects), harness engineering (44 lessons, ten projects), and Hard Deployments, which tackles air-gapped environments, multi-tenant isolation, and rescue engagements. Every course includes free lessons with no card required, and the material is grounded in real FDE interview loops.

Key points

  • Two main courses, each with 11 modules, form the core learning path.
  • Three electives cover AI agents, harness engineering, and hard deployments.
  • Hard Deployments addresses air gaps, multi-tenancy, and rescue engineering.
  • All courses offer free lessons; no payment card needed.
  • Curriculum is based on real FDE interview loops and senior-engineer review.

Full text
