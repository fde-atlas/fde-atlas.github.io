Insights on Security, Compliance, M&A & AI

BD Emerson · 2025-12-10 · 17 min read

Summary

This BD Emerson article is a practical guide to the handoff process when Palantir Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) leave a project. It outlines what a client team should have in place before the FDEs depart so the organization can run and maintain its Foundry or AIP deployment independently. The piece is structured as a checklist covering documentation, knowledge transfer, and operational readiness, aimed at buyers and platform teams working with Palantir rather than at Palantir itself.

Key points

  • Topic: preparing for Palantir FDE handoff before engineers leave.
  • Aimed at client teams running Foundry or AIP deployments.
  • Structured as a practical checklist of handoff materials.
  • Covers documentation, knowledge transfer, and operational readiness.
  • Goal: client independence after the FDE engagement ends.
