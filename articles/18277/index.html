FDE Provider Market Map

Original title: FDE提供企業 カオスマップ

aiagent-navi.com · 2025-06-01 · 19 min read

Summary

This Japanese AI-agent directory article presents a landscape map of 53 companies offering Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)-style services in Japan, as of November 2026. Companies are grouped into eight categories: on-site implementation (20), consulting-led accompaniment (9), own-product-based (12), enterprise-focused (4), industry-specialized (5), certified partner programs (1), and talent matching (2). Each entry records confidence level (high/medium/low based on evidence strength), service launch date, public customer cases, disclosed pricing, and how engineers enter client sites. Examples include EExKey's Forgent service from 150,000 yen per month, LayerX pairing FDEs with Deployment Strategists in a 'Japanese Palantir model' with published cases like Fukuoka Bank, Accenture's April 2026 FDE organization of several thousand engineers, and Salesforce's FDE Partner Network with nine initial Japanese partners. The author stresses all data comes solely from official company statements, and confidence reflects evidence quality, not service quality.

Key points

  • Maps 53 Japanese FDE-style service providers across eight categories, from on-site implementation to talent matching.
  • Each company is tagged with evidence confidence, launch date, public cases, pricing, and on-site engagement style.
  • LayerX pairs FDEs with Deployment Strategists in a 'Japanese Palantir model', with cases including Fukuoka Bank.
  • Accenture launched an FDE organization of several thousand engineers in April 2026, claiming days-long AI deployments.
  • Salesforce's FDE Partner Network named nine initial Japanese partners in June 2026.

How to read this map

  • Confidence
  • Strength of evidence for the information (High = official information + third-party coverage / Medium = company's own communications only / Low = unverified). This is not a measure of service quality
  • Start date
  • Month the FDE-style service was announced or launched. — means no official launch announcement could be confirmed
  • Case studies
  • "Case study available" if the company officially publishes customer implementation case studies (the link goes to the case study page). — means none published (only a launch announcement, or only internal use)
  • Pricing and contract terms
  • Displays exactly what the company officially publishes about pricing and contract terms. — means not stated
  • How they engage on-site
  • Official description of whether they work on-site full-time or combine visits with remote work. — means frequency and format are not officially published

Start dates, case studies, and pricing/contract terms are based solely on each company's official statements and do not include our own assessment. You can check quoted text and sources via "View details" on each card. For commentary and selection guidance, see the FDE company list.
