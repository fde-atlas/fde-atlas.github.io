Reading Lovable's FDE Division | 4 Job Openings, Compensation, and Hiring Process for the Team Launched in October

Original title: LovableのFDE部門を読む|10月に立ち上げたチームの求人4件と報酬・選考

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-10-31 · 23 min read

Summary

This article analyzes Lovable's newly created FDE function through its October 2026 job postings. Lovable is hiring four roles—Staff FDE and FDE Lead in London and the US (New York, San Francisco, Boston)—as founding members of the team. The FDE roles sit in the technical organization (EPD / Enterprise Engineering), separate from the sales-side Solutions Architect. US Staff FDE postings require 8+ years of experience, up to 50% travel, and pay $199K–$325K with equity; FDE Lead requires 12+ years and pays $241K–$390.5K. Compared with Replit, Vercel, and Figma, Lovable has the heaviest experience requirement. The editor links the move to Lovable's push to get AI-prototyped apps into enterprise production environments.

Key points

  • Lovable opened 4 FDE roles in London and the US in October 2026 as founding team members.
  • FDEs sit under EPD / Enterprise Engineering, distinct from the GTM-side Solutions Architect.
  • US Staff FDE: 8+ years experience, up to 50% travel, $199K–$325K plus equity.
  • FDE Lead: 12+ years and team leadership, $241K–$390.5K plus equity.
  • Among Replit, Vercel, and Figma, Lovable's 8-year requirement is the strictest.

AIとの対話でアプリを作るサービス「Lovable」(本社ストックホルム)は、2026年10月、顧客企業に入り込んでアプリを本番まで届けるForward Deployed Engineer(FDE)の部門を立ち上げる求人を、ロンドンと米国で出し始めました。求人票は、採用する人を「FDE部門の創設メンバー」と位置づけています[1][3]。同じ10月には、顧客企業が自社のAWSやAzureの中でアプリを動かせる機能などをまとめて発表し[7]、29日にはロンドン拠点の開設を発表し、拠点のチームの一つにforward-deployed engineeringを挙げました[8]。

In this article, using snapshots of Lovable's public job board that the editorial team has been collecting since September 29, we track how FDE job postings moved in and out during October, read the full text of the job postings as of October 30, 2026 (four total: Staff FDE and FDE Lead for the US and London, plus one Solutions Architect posting for comparison), and organize their organizational placement, requirements, compensation, hiring process, and how they differ from the FDE job postings at Replit, Vercel, and Figma, which likewise sell "tools for building apps." For a general overview of the FDE role, see What Is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?.

Why Is Lovable Creating an FDE Division?

LovableはFDE部門を作る目的を発表では説明していませんが、同じ10月の発表は、試作のアプリを本番に載せる工程に条件があることを強調しています。同社は10月20日の発表で、大企業のチームは毎日AIで試作を作っているが、残す価値のあるものは本番に進む必要があり、それには会社の実データへのアクセスと、アプリをどこで動かし誰が使えるかについてのセキュリティ部門の承認が要る、と書いています[7]。

Lovableは2024年11月に公開されたサービスで、求人票には、これまでに6,000万件を超えるプロジェクトが作られ、AdidasやZendeskのチームも使っていると書かれています[1]。10月29日の発表では、作られたプロジェクトは7,000万件を超え、毎週120万件以上が新しく作られているとしています[8]。8月12日には、評価額133億ドルで4億ドルのシリーズCの調達を発表し、Fortune 500の企業の3分の2近くで社員が使っていること、今年中に約450人の体制にすることを書いていました[9]。

What does the Staff FDE job posting say?

Staff FDEは、顧客と一緒に本番のコードを書く実務の職で、同時にFDEという機能の作法を最初から決める役割を負います。米国とロンドンの2件は、冒頭の段落の切れ目と、ロンドンにだけある一文(多くの技術職は製品か顧客のどちらかに近いが、この職は両方に近い)を除けば、本文は同じです[1][3]。

Key takeaway from this job posting: Of the six required qualifications, only one—"the ability to work directly with users"—touches on customer interaction; the rest are about development, design, prototyping, and self-direction. In our editors' view, Lovable prioritizes the ability to see production code through to completion on your own over customer-facing experience. The fact that "experience building with LLMs" is listed as preferred rather than required also suggests they are looking for engineers who can build entire applications rather than AI specialists.

What does the FDE Lead job posting ask for?

FDE Leadは、勤務地ごとにFDEのチームを作って率いながら、自分も手を動かし続ける職です。求人票は、少人数の「FDEリーダー」の一人として、この機能を世界でどう運営し広げるかを決めると書いています。米国とロンドンの2件の本文は、ロンドンの冒頭に「in your location(勤務地で)」の3語がある点を除いて同じです[2][4]。

Key takeaway from this job posting: The posting repeatedly emphasizes "hands-on work" in three places—the role description, requirements, and responsibilities—despite it being a management position. In our editors' view, Lovable in its founding stage is looking first for someone who can serve as a role model for FDEs, rather than someone who merely manages them. The inclusion of "collaborating with senior customer stakeholders" in the requirements is a difference not found in the Staff FDE posting.

What does Lovable's FDE organization mean for readers in Japan?

2026年10月30日時点で、LovableのFDE求人に日本の勤務地はありません[10]。勤務地はロンドンと米国だけで、FDEの求人票には在宅勤務やビザについての記載がありません[1][3]。10月30日に取得した米国の隣接職種(Solutions Architect)の求人票は、ビザの支援をしないと明記していました[6]。日本の読者にとっての意味は、求人そのものより、FDEの役割の変わり方を示す例として大きいと編集部は見ています。

  • 求職者にとって:Lovableは、誰でもアプリを作れるサービスでも、本番に通す仕事にはフルスタックで8年以上の経験者を求めています[1]。AIで作る人が増えるほど、作られたものを社内のデータ・認証・審査に通せる技術者の価値が上がる、という方向を示す例です
  • 求職者にとって:選考に「自分が作ったものを見せる」段階があり、英語の書類で応募します[1]。SIerやコンサルで顧客の本番環境に載せた経験は、作ったものとして語れる形にしておくと生きます
  • 採用企業にとって:AIでアプリを作る道具を社内に広げても、本番に進めるには会社の実データへのアクセスとセキュリティ部門の承認が要る、と提供する側が書いています[7]。道具を入れるだけでなく、中央でガードレールを決める担当と、試作を本番に通す担当を決めておく必要があります

What can't be determined from the sources in this article?

The sources for this article are Lovable's job postings and official blog, plus coverage of them. None of the sources state how many people are currently on the FDE team, who leads it, or which customers they serve.

Summary

Lovableは、誰でもアプリを作れるサービスを大企業の本番に通すために、経験の長い技術者でFDE部門を立ち上げようとしています[1][7]。2026年10月30日時点の要点は次のとおりです。

  • Lovableは2026年10月、FDE部門の創設メンバーとしてStaff FDEとFDE Leadを、ロンドンと米国で募集し始めた。10月下旬に米国の2件が公開され、ロンドンのFDEは職種名に「Staff」が付き、ストックホルムのリーダーの求人は掲載が終わった[1][2][3][5]
  • 同じ10月に、顧客のクラウド内でアプリを動かす機能などを発表し、ロンドン拠点の開設を発表して拠点のチームにforward-deployed engineeringを挙げた。FDEの役割は、試作のアプリを顧客の社内データとセキュリティ審査の中で本番に通すことと読める[7][8]
  • FDEは営業部門ではなく技術部門(EPD / Enterprise Engineering)に置かれ、営業部門のSolutions Architectとは分けて募集されている[1][6]
  • 米国のStaff FDEは8年以上・最大50%の出張・19.9万〜32.5万ドル、FDE Leadは12年以上・24.1万〜39.05万ドル(いずれも求人票のレンジ、株式あり)[1][2]

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat is Lovable's FDE salary?

As of the US job postings on October 30, 2026, Staff Forward Deployed Engineer pays $199K-$325K and Forward Deployed Engineering Lead pays $241K-$390.5K, both with equity grants. The London posting does not list compensation. These are the ranges stated in the job postings, not actual payout amounts.

QHow much experience does Lovable require for its FDE roles?

As of the job postings on October 30, 2026, Staff Forward Deployed Engineer requires 8+ years of experience building production software, and Forward Deployed Engineering Lead requires 12+ years of experience across software development, technical architecture, and customer-facing technical roles, plus team leadership experience.

QIs Lovable recruiting FDEs in Japan?

As of October 30, 2026, Lovable's FDE openings are located only in London and the US (New York, San Francisco, Boston); there are no locations in Japan. The job postings require applications in English and state that English is the company's common language.

QWhat is the difference between Lovable's FDE and Solutions Architect roles?

As of the job postings on October 30, 2026, the FDE is placed in the engineering division (EPD / Enterprise Engineering) and builds and operates production systems together with customers, turning insights into product improvements. The Solutions Architect is placed in the sales division (GTM / Revenue) and works alongside sales on technical requirements gathering, proposal design, demos, and proof-of-value.

QWhat is Lovable's FDE hiring process like?

As of the job postings on October 30, 2026, Staff Forward Deployed Engineer has four stages: a form and short intro call, a short live coding session, multiple technical interviews, and a stage showing your own work. Forward Deployed Engineering Lead replaces live coding with a technical leadership interview and adds a final conversation with the leaders you would work with.
