Summary

This is a job posting from Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu LLC in Japan for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) focused on ServiceNow. The role goes beyond standard implementation: the FDE works directly with client business units, translates vague problems into implementable requirements, and centers on Agentic AI to redesign processes under an 'Agentic BPR' approach, delivering results through short build-and-verify cycles. Candidates need generative-AI-driven development experience and must turn project learnings into reusable templates and assets. Two levels are hired (Manager+ and Consultant/Senior Consultant), with salaries from 6.2M to 20M JPY, based in Tokyo or Osaka with hybrid remote work.

Key points

  • Deloitte Japan is hiring an FDE for its ServiceNow practice.
  • Core scope is Agentic AI-driven business process redesign (Agentic BPR).
  • The role requires translating ambiguous client issues into business, data, and system requirements.
  • Salary ranges from 6.2M to 20M JPY; Tokyo or Osaka, hybrid remote.
  • Manager-level candidates need ServiceNow-plus-AI transformation experience.

  • Job Title
  • FDE/Forward Deployed Engineer (ServiceNow domain) [ETP-TS&T]
  • Job Description
  • You will play a role in translating the evolution of technologies centered on ServiceNow—business workflows, AI, and data-related technologies—into concrete results at client sites. Going beyond mere ServiceNow implementation, configuration, and deployment, you will engage directly with clients' business departments and on-site managers, identify business challenges spanning the front office, middle office, and back office, translate them into implementable requirements, and drive results through short-cycle build-and-verify iterations. In particular, your core scope will be business execution and automation leveraging Agentic AI, promoting new value creation through business transformation × Agentic AI. Rather than simply automating existing operations, under the concept of Agentic BPR, you will redesign business processes with AI as a given, and be responsible for implementing and embedding them in ways that deliver results in the field. In addition, you will be expected to organize the knowledge gained from projects into reusable templates, assets, and standard patterns, and give back to the organization. Specifically, you will take on the following roles: ① Problem definition through dialogue with on-site managers, business supervisors, and stakeholders involved in effect measurement, and business redesign based on the Agentic BPR perspective ② Design of solutions leveraging ServiceNow modules, Now Assist, and Agentic AI ③ Concretization of implementation policies, including integration with peripheral systems, master data, and business data ④ Promotion of prototyping, verification, and improvement combining AI agents, workflows, and automation logic ⑤ Development of reusable templates and standard architectures The goal is not merely to implement technology and solve problems, but to shift toward business models with future improvements in corporate value in view. You will provide feedback from an on-the-ground implementation perspective on the transformation scenarios envisioned by DS and on clients, and drive value creation in collaboration with FDEs in other domains as needed.
