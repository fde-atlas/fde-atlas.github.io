Anduril Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Experience (2024)

Aced (formerly Exponent) · 2026-08-17 · 1 min read

Summary

A candidate recounts their 2024 interview process for Anduril's Forward Deployed Engineer role, which consisted of three rounds—a recruiter screen, a technical interview, and a final round—with six questions total. They credit thorough preparation (notes, podcasts, mock interviews) for their success. Quoted experiences from other candidates describe a deliberately confusing HackerRank question about arranging football teams for a photo, and a system design round asking candidates to model a radar tower for a moving ship; despite claims that defense experience wasn't required, unfamiliarity with air defense specifics appeared to frustrate the interviewer.

Key points

  • Three rounds: recruiter screen, technical interview, final round; six questions total.
  • The author credits preparation: notes, podcasts, and mock interviews.
  • One candidate faced a deliberately confusing HackerRank question but still advanced to finals.
  • A system design round required modeling a radar tower for a moving ship.
  • Lack of air defense knowledge frustrated an interviewer despite stated no-defense-background requirement.
