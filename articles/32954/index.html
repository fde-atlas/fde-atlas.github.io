Google FDE Salary India 2026: ₹35–90+ LPA, Role & Interview

FDE India · 2026-10-30 · 8 min read

Summary

This article reviews Google Cloud's Forward Deployed Engineer hiring in India. As of 19 November 2026, three roles were open: FDE V GenAI and Data Cloud in Bengaluru, and Staff FDE Developer AI across Bengaluru, Gurugram and Mumbai, requiring 2, 5 and 8 years of experience respectively. Google does not publish India FDE pay; Scaler estimates ₹35–55 LPA at L4 and ₹55–90+ LPA at L5 based on Glassdoor and Levels.fyi data. The reported interview loop covers practical coding, integration-focused system design, and a customer-facing round that candidates tend to under-prepare for. Notably, the India GenAI role asks only 2 years while the same title in Singapore asks for 8.

Google Cloud hires Forward Deployed Engineers in Bengaluru, with some roles also open to Gurugram and Mumbai, to take AI agents and data workloads from prototype to production inside large customers. On 19 November 2026, Google Careers listed three open FDE roles for India. It is also a demanding one: the listings ask for real production experience and the interview tests customer skills as seriously as code.

What a Google FDE does

Google’s India FDE postings sit in Google Cloud’s AI and Data Cloud teams. Across the open FDE V, GenAI, Data Cloud and Staff Developer AI roles, and the earlier Cloud Applied AI and GECX roles, the FDE:

  • Co-builds with strategic customers. Designs, develops, debugs and deploys AI agents and solutions with the customer’s engineers, writing bespoke code and custom tooling.
  • Connects Google’s AI to messy reality. Integrates conversational and agentic products with customer APIs, legacy data and security boundaries.
  • Builds evaluation and observability. Pipelines to improve agent reasoning, tool selection and latency.
  • Builds data foundations and migrations. The Data Cloud and GenAI roles ship production workloads on BigQuery, Spark, Spanner and AlloyDB, prove results on customer data (including de-identifying sensitive data) and migrate costly analytics workloads with measured price-performance gains.
  • Rebuilds how developers work (Staff role). The Developer AI role designs agentic workflows for large refactors, language migrations and spec-to-pull-request pipelines, connected to tools like ServiceNow, GitLab and Slack.
  • Turns field work into product. Converts repeated customer problems into reusable modules, documentation or feature requests for engineering.
  • Advises on production readiness. Agent improvement, performance tuning and CI/CD pipelines.

Google’s FDE II posting for another region describes the same pattern, adding MCP servers, tool calling and OAuth for secure agentic workflows. Expect these to come up for India roles too.

Pay in India

Google does not publish pay on its India FDE postings. Scaler estimates ₹35–55 LPA total compensation at L4 and ₹55–90+ LPA at L5, mapping FDE roles to Google’s software engineering bands on Glassdoor and Levels.fyi, and says FDE-specific India data is limited. Those figures fit the GCC and global platform bands in our FDE Salary Estimator.

How to prepare

  • Practise integration coding, not puzzles only. Write clean code that ingests messy data: parse logs, normalise events, load a legacy table into a warehouse. Candidates report these tasks.
  • Design into an existing system. Rehearse “how would you add Vertex AI to this bank’s on-prem data without moving regulated records?” Draw the current system first, then the change.
  • Build and evaluate one agent on Google Cloud. Vertex AI or Dialogflow plus RAG over real documents, with an eval set for accuracy and latency. It gives you concrete stories for every round.
  • Prepare customer stories with numbers. A stakeholder who disagreed, a deadline that slipped, a launch you owned. The customer round is the one people under-prepare for.
  • Check level fit. With 2–4 years and Python plus SQL, the FDE V, GenAI role is the realistic entry point; with 5+ years of data work, target Data Cloud; the Staff Developer AI role needs 8+ years. With under 2 years, aim first for Google Cloud customer engineering or adjacent roles.

Google (Google Cloud) FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Recruiter screen: Checks role fit and customer-facing motivation early, according to candidate reports summarised by Scaler. This is a reported pattern, not an official process, and it varies by team and level.
  • Coding: Practical data and integration problems, such as normalising event streams or building a pipeline from a legacy database into BigQuery. Clean, maintainable code matters because customers inherit it.
  • System design and integration: Fitting Google products into an existing customer system rather than greenfield design, for example connecting on-prem data to Vertex AI or migrating an ETL pipeline from another cloud.
  • Customer-facing and behavioural: Explaining technical work to non-technical people, handling ambiguity and difficult customer conversations, and owning outcomes. Scaler notes most candidates under-prepare for this round.

Frequently asked questions

Does Google hire Forward Deployed Engineers in India?

Yes. On 19 November 2026 Google Careers listed three open Google Cloud FDE roles for India: FDE V, GenAI and FDE, Data Cloud in Bengaluru, and Staff FDE, Developer AI across Bengaluru, Gurugram and Mumbai. Individual postings open and close, so check Google Careers for live roles.

How much does a Google FDE earn in India?

Google does not list pay on its India FDE postings. Scaler estimates ₹35–55 LPA total compensation at L4 and ₹55–90+ LPA at L5, based on Google's software engineering bands on Glassdoor and Levels.fyi, and notes that FDE-specific India data is limited.

What experience do Google FDE roles in India need?

It depends on level. The Bengaluru FDE V, GenAI role asks for 2 years in software engineering, data engineering, solution architecture, ML or technical consulting, with Python and SQL; the Data Cloud role asks for 5 years of the same. The Staff FDE, Developer AI role asks for 8 years in cloud computing or a technical customer-facing role.

What is the Google FDE interview like?

Candidates report a recruiter screen, practical coding on data and integration problems, an integration-focused system design round and a customer-facing behavioural round. It varies by team and level.
