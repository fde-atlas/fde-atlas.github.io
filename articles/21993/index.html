AlphaDrive Establishes Japan's First Unique Concept "FDE=Expert," Evolving the Global Norm of "FDE=Engineer," and Launches AI-Driven Business Development Support Service

Original title: AlphaDrive、世界の常識「FDE=Engineer」を進化させた独自概念「FDE=Expert」を、日本で初めて確立。AI駆動型・事業開発伴走サービスを提供開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-06-01 · 12 min read

Summary

AlphaDrive launched an AI-driven business development service in Japan, "AX for Revenue FDE Service," and proposes extending the FDE concept—originated by Palantir and carried on by Anthropic and OpenAI—beyond engineers to a new role it calls the Forward Deployed Expert. Arguing that collapsing completion costs make building itself no longer scarce, the company says the rare skill is defining what to build and breaking through internal decision-making. The Expert is grounded in AlphaDrive's "AX Architect" profile (business architecture ability multiplied by AI ability). These experts embed at client sites, drive the AX for Revenue Loop, and ultimately transfer capabilities to client organizations. The service starts June 1, 2026, backed by experience from 260 companies and a three-tier development network.

Key points

  • AlphaDrive proposes "Forward Deployed Expert" as a second FDE definition alongside Engineer.
  • Rationale: AI makes building cheap, so business development judgment is the scarce skill.
  • The Expert role is based on AlphaDrive's AX Architect (BA ability × AI ability).
  • Experts embed with client business owners through full-product launches and decision breakthroughs.
  • Service launches June 1, 2026, drawing on 260 client companies and a three-layer dev network.

Forward Deployed Expert is an evolution of the FDE inherited by the world's leading AI companies. Experts who amplify practical business development knowledge with AI work alongside customer companies' business owners, driving and accompanying them through to the launch of a finished product.

AlphaDrive, Inc. (Head office: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director, President & CEO / CAXO Yoichi Aso; hereinafter "AlphaDrive") announces the launch of an AI-driven business development accompaniment service, "Revenue Evolution FDE / AX for Revenue FDE Service", as a new form of engagement for business development in the AI era.

This service is an engagement model in which AlphaDrive evolves the concept of "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)," which originated at Palantir Technologies and spread worldwide, into "Forward Deployed Expert," establishing it for the first time in Japan. Around the world, "FDE=Engineer" is becoming established as the standard term. While paying respect to that trend, AlphaDrive today presents to the market another original evolved concept: "FDE=Expert."

FDE is not only "Engineer." There is also "Expert."

We first put forward one idea to the market.

FDE = Engineer is the core talent model for business implementation in the AI era, established by Palantir in the 2000s and inherited by the world's leading AI companies, including Anthropic and OpenAI. Engineers who are embedded at customer sites, converting on-the-ground challenges into products while writing code, have moved AI-era business forward. But is it really enough for those deployed forward to the front lines to be Engineers alone?

AlphaDrive's answer is no.

In the era of Completion Cost Collapse, where the manufacturing cost of finished products plummets, the methodology for new business is evolving from the "learn without building" MVP approach to a new paradigm: "launch a finished product to the market right away and learn" — Full-Product Launch (FPL) (see the May 11, 2026 release "AX for Revenue Launches" and the book "AI Revenue Evolution Theory" by Yoichi Aso, published May 22, 2026, for details).

In a world where the cost of building finished products has collapsed, the ability to "build" is no longer scarce. What is scarce is the power to define "what to build, for which customer challenges, and why," to break through internal decision-making, and to continuously update business hypotheses based on market response — in other words, practical business development knowledge.

That is why those who stand forward deployed in the field should not be Engineers alone. Experts who amplify practical business development knowledge with AI should also stand in the field. This is the idea AlphaDrive puts to the market today.

Expert alongside Engineer. Another definition of FDE

"FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" has been the figure of the engineer who continually stands on the front lines of AI implementation. Since Palantir, it has been inherited by the world's leading AI companies, including Anthropic and OpenAI, and precisely because they stood in the field while writing code, business implementation in the AI era has moved forward.

AlphaDrive stands on respect for this Forward Deployed Engineer, and places beside it another core talent model: the "Forward Deployed Expert." The two are not opposing concepts, but two core talent models standing side by side on the front lines of AI-era business implementation.

Forward Deployed Engineer Forward Deployed Expert
Primary assetEngineering skills + AI implementation capabilityPractical business development knowledge + AI implementation capability
Primary dialogue partnersTechnology leaders and field staffBusiness owners and executives
Primary deliverableProductRedesign of revenue structures
Business development phase coveredCompleting the implementation phaseFrom hypothesis building through finished-product launch and breaking through internal decision-making
Stance toward AIUses AI as a weapon to write code and see the product through to completionUses AI as a weapon to determine what to build and drive the business forward

Expert and Engineer are two core talent models with different roles, standing side by side on the front lines of AI-era business implementation. The Engineer handles the field of building things to completion, while the Expert handles the field of determining what to build and breaking through internal decision-making. AI-era business implementation is completed when both exist in the same field, each with their own expertise.

Note that the Forward Deployed Expert naturally makes full use of AI-based code generation and prototype implementation as standard tools. Precisely because we are in an era where AI has broadened the base of engineering, it has become realistic for business development experts to stand in the field while writing code — the Expert talent model is founded on the premise of that change.

The substance of the Expert is the "AX Architect" that AlphaDrive has defined

The name "Expert" did not suddenly appear today. The substance of its capabilities is the "AX Architect" talent model that AlphaDrive has already systematized.

In 2026, AlphaDrive defined "AX Architect = BA capability × AI capability" as the core talent model for driving business transformation in the AI era, from both policy research and business implementation perspectives (see White Paper 04 "AX Architect: The Implementation Theory." for details).

  • BA (Business Architect) capability: the ability to translate corporate strategy into business structures, to move stakeholders, and to build consensus across organizations. This is a capability in an area that only humans can handle, and whose importance is rising both relatively and absolutely in the AI era.
  • AI Capabilities: AI SPRINT (the ability to instill massive data and ideas into AI to bring it up to performance standards), AI Orchestration (the ability to integrate multiple AIs), and FPL (the ability to leverage AI to bring finished products fully to market).

The talent profile formed by multiplying "BA capabilities × AI capabilities" is AlphaDrive's AX Architect. And the engagement model in which that AX Architect is embedded on-site at client companies is called "Forward Deployed Expert".

In other words, the structure of this service is as follows:

Capability ProfileAX Architect = BA capabilities × AI capabilities ↓ The engagement model that deploys this capability on-site at clients is:
Engagement ModelForward Deployed Expert (FDE) ↓ The service that provides this FDE is:
ServiceRevenue Evolution FDE / AX for Revenue FDE Service

While the world has established "FDE=Engineer" as the standard term, AlphaDrive is the first in Japan to present the market with another solution: "embedding AX Architects on-site in a Forward Deployed model."

What the Revenue Evolution FDE does on-site at client companies

The FDE (= an on-site AX Architect) sits beside the client company's business owner and drives every step of the AX for Revenue Loop together with them.

  • Co-building business hypotheses: Working side by side with the client's business owner to identify customer challenges and market opportunities and design business models
  • Executing AI-driven Full-Product Launch: Achieving business development that goes beyond MVPs, launching finished products to market and learning from them, through AI-powered rapid product building
  • On-site driving of the AX for Revenue Loop: Running the four steps—AI Sprint (thorough AI transformation of existing operations), Plateau Detection (detecting limits), PI Injection (injecting intelligence only humans possess), and redesigning revenue structures—as one with the client team
  • Accompanying PI Injection: Unearthing the Primal Intelligence (PI) dormant within the client company and connecting it with AI, injecting into the business an intelligence that AI can never reach in principle
  • Breaking through commercialization decisions and internal decision-making: Drawing on decision-making structures unique to large enterprises, honed through work with 260 companies, and accompanying clients all the way through internal approvals, budgeting, and full-scale commercialization
  • Accompanying the development of in-house AX Architects: Through the process of the FDE continually standing beside the client, transferring knowledge so that AX Architects grow within the client company

The FDE is not the end of external dispatch, but the beginning of AX Architect development

There is an important structural feature here.

AlphaDrive's Revenue Evolution FDE is not a one-off dispatch that enters the client site as external contracting and leaves when the contract ends. The very process of the FDE continually sitting beside the client's business owner is the process of transferring "business development know-how for the AI era = AX Architect capabilities" into the client company.

AlphaDrive positions "development" as the primary goal for AX Architects, with external dispatch serving as "accompanying support during development" (see the white paper "AXアーキテクトの、実装論。" (The Implementation Theory of the AX Architect)). This service is grounded in the same philosophy. While the FDE works alongside the client company, it equips the client's in-house talent with AX Architect capabilities — this is the ultimate value this service leaves behind.

AI × business development, AI × talent development, AI enablement — we aim to simultaneously demonstrate capabilities across all of these areas.

Note that the Revenue Evolution FDE can also begin, as a precursor to revenue evolution, with accompanying support in the efficiency AI domain (AI Sprint and Plateau Detection). The moment a Plateau is reached, the same FDE seamlessly continues into the revenue evolution phase (PI Injection and revenue structure redesign). A single FDE continuously climbing both the efficiency AI mountain and the revenue evolution AI mountain — this is the value this service provides.

The three foundations that allow AlphaDrive to call itself FDE=Expert

The structural basis for AlphaDrive's ability to offer the "Forward Deployed Expert" rests on the following three foundations.

  • ① First-hand ownership: Not a consulting-only firm, but real-world experience as a business operator facing, as a principal, the question of generating revenue with AI
  • ② Cross-industry breadth: Ideas that deviate from industry conventions and connect two unrelated things, gained from 260 companies and 23,800 business development engagements (= the ability to supply Crazy Intelligence)
  • ③ AX Architect development system: The five-stage AX Architect development model and four-element cyclical structure systematized by AlphaDrive in WP-04 "AXアーキテクトの、実装論。" (The Implementation Theory of the AX Architect). This enables the FDE to be provided sustainably not as "dispatching a specific star performer" but as "on-site staffing from a developed talent pool"

The organizational capability to see finished products through to launch is guaranteed by the development and implementation network that the AlphaDrive Group has built in three tiers since 2025.

Became a subsidiary in March 2025. A dedicated development subsidiary of the AlphaDrive Group with a global development team of about 50 people.

Established in April 2025, a development JV with Sun*, which has strengths in product development for new business development.

Launching in October 2025, a development partner alliance that achieves optimal partner assignment by industry and technology domain.

While the FDE itself handles AI-driven development and implementation, it makes use of this three-tier network according to the scale, complexity, and security requirements of each project, guaranteeing delivery through to finished product launch.

Positioning of the Revenue Evolution FDE: The implementation solution layer of AX for Revenue

Among the philosophy, methodology, and implementation solution layers of AX for Revenue, this service is positioned as the core of the implementation solution layer.

The execution means for putting into action, at customer sites, both the philosophy presented in the book "AI Revenue Evolution Theory" and the methodology known as AX for Revenue Loop — that is the Revenue Evolution FDE. Alongside equipping executives with AX capabilities, implementing the methodology within organizations, and "AX Dejima," the core execution solution that makes AI-driven business development possible even under the security constraints of large enterprises, we position this service as a key engagement model of AX for Revenue.

Around the world, "FDE = Forward Deployed Engineer" is spreading as a standard term. Originating with Palantir and carried forward by Anthropic, OpenAI, and other leading AI companies worldwide, business in the AI era has moved forward precisely because these engineers stood on the front lines while writing code. We stand on a foundation of respect for the Forward Deployed Engineer.

That said, we ask ourselves: is it really enough for only Engineers to be the ones deployed forward to the front lines?

In an era where AI writes code and AI can launch finished products in an instant, what is scarce is not the ability to "build," but rather the practical business development expertise to determine "what to build, for whom, and why." That is why there is another core talent profile that should stand on the front lines: the profile we are establishing today as the Forward Deployed Expert.

The substance of the Expert's capabilities is what AlphaDrive has already systematized as the "AX Architect = BA capabilities × AI capabilities." The engagement model that drives and accompanies this AX Architect right beside the business owner at the customer company is the Forward Deployed Expert.

This is not the dispatch of AI engineers, nor the delivery of templates. As stakeholders and comrades-in-arms, we drive the customer's revenue evolution, and ultimately take responsibility for cultivating AX Architects within the customer company itself — that is our "Forward Deployed Expert."

Through simultaneously delivering capabilities across all of these domains — AI × business development, AI × talent development, and AI enablement — we want to build together a society where AI opens up human potential, not one where AI takes away human purpose. Through the Revenue Evolution FDE, we will unearth the Primal Intelligence dormant in companies across Japan and increase AX Architects across regions and industries.

About AX for Revenue

AX for Revenue is a new domain launched by AlphaDrive in May 2026 that proposes and implements a "Revenue Evolution AI System" that uses AI to drive discontinuous evolution of corporate revenue, advancing the application of AI to business development and the systematization of talent development methods for the AI era. Based on the recognition of the era of Completion Cost Collapse, it supports revenue evolution that goes beyond mere efficiency gains, centered on original concepts such as Full-Product Launch, AI Orchestration, and Field Intelligence. It redefines the talent profile responsible for business development in the AI era as the "AX Architect," and the development and organizational implementation of such talent is also included in its scope of support.
