Summary

Japanese startup Gomumari surveyed the official websites of 40 domestic companies that market themselves as offering FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) services. The finding: from names and site copy alone, prospective clients cannot tell what work these firms can actually be hired to do. Definitions of the FDE role and the scope of services vary widely across companies, with vague service descriptions and no shared standard. The piece highlights how the FDE label has spread in Japan faster than a common understanding of what it means, creating confusion for buyers evaluating vendors.

Key points

  • Gomumari reviewed websites of 40 Japanese companies branding themselves as FDE providers.
  • Company names alone do not reveal what services can actually be commissioned.
  • Definitions of the FDE role and service scope differ significantly across firms.
  • The FDE label lacks a shared standard in Japan, confusing procurement decisions.
