Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (4/5): Interesting Problems, Stressful Times

Taro · 2025-04-09 · 2 min read

Summary

This article compiles Glassdoor-style reviews and interview accounts from Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineers. A current Washington, DC employee with four years at the company rated it 4.0/5, citing interesting problems as the main pro and stress as the main con; work/life balance, DEI, and senior management scored low (2.0), while career opportunities scored 4.0. Interview experiences from 2016 to 2024 describe a recruiter call, a technical phone screen, and an on-site round of roughly 4-5 hours covering pure technical, design, and mixed behavioral-technical interviews.

Key points

  • Current FDSE employee rates Palantir 4.0/5 after four years
  • Interesting problems cited as pros, stress as cons
  • Work/life balance, DEI, and senior management scored 2.0
  • Career opportunities scored 4.0, compensation 3.0
  • Interviews: recruiter call, technical screen, 4-5 hour on-site
