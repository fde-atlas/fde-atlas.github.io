What to expect for Legora's Legal Counsel interview and how Nora AI helps.

22 July 2026

Legora is an AI-native legal technology company that has scaled to $100M+ in ARR, serving 1,000+ customers across 50+ countries, including firms like Cleary Gottlieb, Linklaters, White & Case, and Barclays. The Legal Counsel role (posted as Commercial Counsel) sits in the San Francisco office within Legora's forward-deployed, AI-first legal function, reporting to the General Counsel in Stockholm. Your primary focus is drafting and negotiating commercial contracts: leading customer and supplier agreements, advising on risk allocation, pricing, and liability, and building templates and playbooks that increase deal velocity.

Legora wants a pragmatic, business-minded lawyer who can operate independently across time zones and partner closely with Growth, Operations, and Procurement to enable deals while managing risk. The posting emphasizes strong B2B SaaS negotiation experience, sound legal judgment, and genuine enthusiasm for AI and legal tech. This is a high-ownership, fast-paced role where the culture values outcomes over titles and direct, ego-free feedback.

Quick Stats

* Typical process: 4 to 5 rounds, roughly 3 to 5 weeks

* Format: Recruiter phone screen, then video interviews with the GC and cross-functional stakeholders, possibly a final onsite or extended virtual round

* Core focus: Commercial contract drafting and negotiation, B2B SaaS agreements, risk allocation, data privacy, legal tech enthusiasm, independent judgment

* Difficulty: Moderate to hard, because the role expects senior-level negotiation skill, autonomy across time zones, and pragmatic risk decisions under deal pressure

What Legora Looks For

* J.D. (or equivalent) with admission in at least one U.S. state or common law jurisdiction, plus 5+ years at a top-tier firm and/or in-house

* Strong, proven experience negotiating B2B SaaS agreements and supporting commercial teams as an excellent negotiator

* Confident, independent legal judgment focused on meaningful business outcomes and clear, actionable advice

* A proactive, pragmatic mindset with genuine curiosity about AI and legal tech in real-world settings

What to Expect

A recruiter or talent partner will walk through your background, bar admission, years of experience, and motivation for joining a fast-growing legal tech company. Expect questions about your commercial contracting experience, comfort working independently across time zones and why Legora specifically. They will also confirm location, compensation expectations ($207.4K to $244K plus equity), and availability. This is your chance to deliver a crisp pitch that ties your SaaS contracting background to Legora's mission.

Example Questions

* "Walk me through your experience negotiating B2B SaaS agreements."

* "Why Legora, and why the legal tech space right now?"

* "How comfortable are you working independently with a manager in a different time zone?"

* "What are your compensation expectations for this role?"

Tips

* Have a 60-second story ready that connects your commercial contracting track record to Legora's need for deal velocity and pragmatic risk management.

* Show genuine curiosity about AI and legal tech; the posting explicitly values this, so reference how you have used or want to use these tools.

* Rehearse this conversational mix with Nora AI's Standard Mode so your pitch, motivation, and pay expectations come out clean and confident.

This is the core round with the General Counsel. Expect a deep discussion of your commercial contracting experience, how you approach risk allocation, and how you balance speed against protection when closing deals. The GC will probe your legal judgment: how you make decisions independently, when you escalate, and how you translate legal risk into clear business advice. Because this legal team is forward-deployed and AI-first, expect questions about how you would partner with Growth, Operations, and Procurement and how you think about scalable, repeatable contracting.

* "Walk me through how you approach negotiating a customer SaaS agreement from first draft to signature."

* "How do you decide when to hold firm on a liability cap versus concede to close a deal?"

* "Tell me about a time you made an independent legal decision under time pressure and how it turned out."

* "How would you build a playbook to standardize negotiations and improve deal velocity here?"

* Frame answers around business outcomes, not just legal correctness; the posting stresses pragmatic solutions and meaningful results.

* Prepare a concrete example of a contract or negotiation you owned end to end, including the risk trade-offs you made.

* Practice these judgment and negotiation stories in Nora AI's Behavioral Mode to keep your STAR structure tight and outcome-focused.

This role-specific round tests your substantive contracting skill. You may be asked to talk through how you would redline or negotiate specific clauses (indemnification, limitation of liability, data protection, pricing structures, termination) or given a hypothetical customer or supplier agreement scenario to reason through live. Expect data privacy and IT security questions in the context of commercial contracts, plus corporate matters given the meritorious qualifications listed. The goal is to see how you allocate risk pragmatically without slowing execution.

* "A prospect wants unlimited liability and rejects our data processing terms. How do you handle it to still close the deal?"

* "How do you approach liability caps and indemnification in a B2B SaaS agreement?"

* "What data protection and IT security terms do you consider non-negotiable in a customer contract?"

* "Where would you standardize a template versus leave room for negotiation to protect deal velocity?"

* Reason out loud and show your trade-offs; interviewers want to see judgment, not memorized clauses.

* Tie every position back to protecting the business while enabling the deal, which mirrors the posting's emphasis on managing risk without slowing execution.

* Drill these clause-level and scenario questions in Nora AI's Technical Mode so you can articulate risk allocation and privacy reasoning crisply under pressure.

You will meet stakeholders from Growth, Operations, or Procurement, the teams you will partner with daily. This round assesses whether you can be a trusted, accessible legal partner rather than a bottleneck, and whether you fit Legora's high-ownership, direct-feedback culture ("mission before ego, no one coasts"). Expect situational and behavioral questions about collaboration, communication, and operating in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment across time zones.

* "How do you keep sales teams moving fast while still protecting the company legally?"

* "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a business stakeholder and how you resolved it."

* "How do you give and receive direct, ego-free feedback?"

* "Describe how you prioritize when multiple deals need legal review at once."

* Emphasize enablement: show you see legal as a partner to revenue, not a gate, which is central to this posting.

* Bring examples of building trust with commercial teams and turning legal complexity into clear, actionable guidance.

* Rehearse these collaboration and values-fit scenarios in Nora AI's Behavioral Mode to sound partnership-oriented and confident.

1) How many rounds are there?

Typically 4 to 5 rounds: a recruiter screen, a hiring manager interview with the General Counsel, a role-specific contract and commercial deep-dive, and a cross-functional and culture-fit round. Some processes add a brief final conversation with a founder or senior leader. Legora does not publish its exact process, so treat this as a realistic structure for a role of this type and seniority rather than a guaranteed sequence.

2) What topics are most common?

* Drafting and negotiating B2B SaaS customer and supplier contracts, including liability, indemnification, and pricing

* Risk allocation, data privacy and IT security in contracts, independent judgment, and partnering with commercial teams to drive deal velocity

3) How long does the process take?

Expect roughly 3 to 5 weeks from recruiter screen to offer, depending on scheduling across time zones (your future manager is based in Stockholm). Fast-moving startups like Legora can compress this when there is strong mutual interest.

4) How should I prepare?

* Review your own strongest SaaS and commercial contract negotiations, and be ready to explain the risk trade-offs you made

* Refresh clause-level knowledge (limitation of liability, indemnification, data protection, termination) and how to standardize via playbooks

* Study Legora's product, customers, and AI-native approach so you can speak credibly about enthusiasm for legal tech

* Practice with Nora AI: use Standard Mode for the recruiter screen, Technical Mode for the contract and clause deep-dive, Behavioral Mode for judgment and culture-fit stories, and Salary Negotiation Mode to handle the $207.4K to $244K plus equity offer conversation with confidence
