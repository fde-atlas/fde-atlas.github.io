Summary

This is a job listing from OpenWork for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) position at Japanese IT firm System Eye (システムアイ). Founded in Yokohama in 1998, the company built its reputation on banking system development as a Fujitsu core partner, joined the Tokyo Prime-listed SHIFT group in 2019, acquired SRE specialist RealGrove Automated in 2022, and plans a North America research center in 2023. The FDE role involves embedding at client sites to define what should be built, rapidly prototyping, building data pipelines, and communicating with stakeholders from field staff to executives. Requirements include 5+ years of web development and client work plus upstream process experience. Salary ranges from ¥4–7 million with full flextime, remote work, and 120 holidays per year. Company figures: FY2023 revenue ¥4.44 billion, 295 employees, 12.6% turnover, ~20.7 hours monthly overtime.

Key points

  • FDE role: define problems on-site, prototype fast, deploy working solutions.

Because we have a stable sales base as part of a Prime-listed group and earn our customers' technical trust, winning numerous projects, we are able to support the career you envision.

  • ■ Highlights of This Position ■ - Full two-day weekends (complete five-day work week) - 120 annual holidays - Retirement benefit plan available - Remote work / working from home allowed - Support for obtaining certifications ● Specific Job Responsibilities In short, we would like you to "get deeply involved at the client's front lines, leverage technical expertise and business insight to redefine complex problems into a 'solvable form,' and implement and resolve them on the spot." While typical engineers act after "what to build (What)" has been decided, the mission of an FDE is to derive "what should be built (Why/How)" from the customer's actual site and assemble prototypes at blazing speed. - Extracting and modeling problems on-site: Absorb the client's domain (industry) knowledge, understand scattered data structures, and translate them into technically solvable problems. - Rapid prototyping: Based on feedback obtained on-site, rewrite code within the same day, and build and deploy solutions that can withstand real-world operation. -
