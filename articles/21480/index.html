Summary

Japanese proptech startup Canary is hiring an AI-domain Forward Deployed Engineer in Tokyo, with an annual salary of 10–15 million yen. The role sits in the AI Solutions division and involves embedding with major enterprise clients to redesign business processes and lead end-to-end AI agent requirements, implementation, and operations, paired with AI consultants from strategy consulting backgrounds. The stack includes Go, TypeScript, Python, agent frameworks like LangGraph and ADK, and GCP infrastructure. Insights from client work feed back into Canary's SaaS product and its master property database and vertical AI agent ambitions. The company raised roughly 4 billion yen in a Series C round in December 2025 and has capital alliances with Yamada Holdings and Credit Saison.

Key points

  • Canary is hiring an AI FDE in Tokyo at 10–15 million yen annually.
  • The role covers business process redesign through AI agent implementation and operations.
  • Stack: Go, TypeScript, Python, LangGraph, ADK, and GCP.
  • Domain learnings feed into Canary Cloud and a master property database initiative.
  • Canary raised ~4 billion yen Series C in December 2025.

  • Job Description
  • In the AI Solutions Division, you will embed yourself on-site with major industry clients and lead the entire process end-to-end, from restructuring business processes to requirements definition, implementation, and operation of AI agents. Working in pairs with AI consultants from strategy consulting firms, you will distill management and on-site challenges into AI-implementable specifications. Beyond solving individual clients' problems, you will feed the domain knowledge gained back into our own SaaS, "Canary Cloud," and drive architecture design with an eye toward building the foundation for the "Master Property DB Vision" and "Vertical AI Agents." [Specifically] - Decomposing and analyzing clients' business processes and identifying opportunities for replacement with AI agents - Requirements definition, solution design, PoC development, and production implementation in collaboration with AI consultants - Development of multi-agent systems (orchestration, Tool Use, Human-in-the-Loop, etc.) - Building RAG pipelines (ingesting business data, designing search infrastructure, context engineering) - Building AI agent evaluation platforms (Evals), guardrail design, and operational monitoring - Designing integration with enterprise customer environments and existing systems (APIs, data pipelines) - Feeding domain knowledge gained in the field back into "Canary Cloud" and the "Master Property DB Vision" [Development Environment] Languages: Go, TypeScript, Python AI agent frameworks: ADK (Agent Development Kit), LangGraph, Mastra Infrastructure/DB: GCP (Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, GKE, Cloud Spanner, etc.)
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • Practical experience in backend development and hands-on system design - Basic knowledge of cloud environments such as GCP and AWS, and various databases - Empathy with the FDE way of working, handling everything from hearing about clients' on-site challenges to proposals and implementation - The ownership to independently structure ambiguous, complex problems and drive them to resolution [Preferred] - Experience developing AI agents using frameworks such as LangGraph / ADK - Experience designing and developing multi-agent systems (orchestration, etc.) - Experience designing and building RAG pipelines and search infrastructure (vector search, etc.) - Experience building AI agent evaluation platforms (Evals) and designing guardrails - Experience leading technical decision-making as a tech lead or architect - Experience developing and proposing solutions for enterprise customers
