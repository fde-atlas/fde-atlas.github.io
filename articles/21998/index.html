Integration with Existing Systems and Per-Company Customization to Bring AI-Driven Daily Communication to Procurement Operations

EGGHEAD Inc. (Headquarters: Ota-ku, Tokyo; CEO: Shun Sakai) will begin developing and implementing AI procurement agents tailored to each company's operations, built on its "Smart AI Procurement" platform.

Engineers work with procurement staff to review operations, then proceed with integration into existing systems and customization of features. This is "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) model" support, working on-site to handle everything from design and development to operational improvement.

AI Handles Daily Communication, Humans Make Key Decisions

The goal is procurement operations where AI advances daily communication. AI takes on organizing supplier responses, following up with non-responders, and recording confirmed information, creating an environment where staff can focus on approvals and exception handling.

In development, the tasks delegated to AI and the conditions requiring human review are determined according to each company's rules. Responses that deviate from standard conditions or require approval are designed to be handed over to the responsible staff member.

While leveraging the existing features of Smart AI Procurement, the scope of tasks that AI can autonomously handle will be expanded step by step. Extension to order confirmation and delivery management is also under consideration. Autonomous execution is a development goal; target operations and timing will be specified in the future.

Practical Use of AI Requires Adapting to Each Company's Rules and Systems

Through proposals and implementations of "Smart AI Procurement" to date, the need for adjustments tailored to each company's operations and for system integration has become clear. This support initiative is based on insights gained from interactions with implementing companies.

For example, the timing of reminders for quotation responses and the conditions requiring confirmation with staff differ by company. Registering responses in internal systems also requires aligning item numbers and management fields.

Support from System Integration to Post-Implementation Improvement

We review each company's challenges and existing systems, then propose a combination of the necessary support.

1. Determine Which Tasks to Delegate to AI

We review daily tasks and decision criteria, and organize which tasks AI will handle and which require human review and approval.

2. Connect to Existing Systems

We determine the data to be integrated, such as item and supplier information and quotation results, and connect using methods suited to the existing systems.

3. Develop to Fit Each Company's Operations

We design and develop the necessary features to match management fields, forms, and approval workflows.

4. Improve Through Use

We work with staff to review usability and challenges, and make repeated improvements. Insights gained in the field are also applied to improving Smart AI Procurement.

We also accept consultations on building new procurement systems or replacing specific functions.

Individual development and on-site visits are conducted according to each customer's needs and challenges; they are not mandatory for all companies. The scope, duration, and cost of support are discussed individually.

Accepting Inquiries from Current Users and Companies Considering Implementation

Please tell us about your current challenges, such as "which tasks in our procurement operations can be delegated to AI" or "can it integrate with our existing systems." We start with a meeting with your procurement/purchasing and information systems staff to review what you want to achieve and what is required.
