Summary

This is a listing page for the "FDE 观察" (FDE Observations) column on FDE中国, a Chinese knowledge site about forward deployed engineers. The column holds 70 articles, updated through September 4, 2026, and aims to help readers understand what the FDE role is, who does it, and where the market is heading—offering definitions, role comparisons, hiring and funding trends, and industry reports rather than actionable playbooks. Key themes include definitions (12 articles), role comparisons (28), market dynamics (36), and paradigm choices (19). Recent pieces compare FDEs with AI Engineers and Solutions Engineers, alongside term cards on multi-agent systems, fine-tuning, vector databases, implementation consultants, and delivery roles.
