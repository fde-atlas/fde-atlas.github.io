What is an FDE? Definition and job responsibilities of a Forward Deployed Engineer

Original title: FDE是什么？前线部署工程师的定义与工作内容

Summary

This article defines the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) as someone embedded at the customer's business site who turns AI capabilities into runnable, auditable, controllable workflows and is accountable for outcomes. It traces the role's origins to Palantir's Delta (engineering) and Echo (Deployment Strategist) roles, lists what FDEs actually do, and explains why the role is suddenly prominent: an MIT study found roughly 95% of enterprise generative AI pilots show no measurable profit impact, Anthropic and OpenAI have both launched enterprise delivery organizations (OpenAI's acquisition of Tomoro brought about 150 deployment specialists), FDE job postings on Paraform grew 350% year over year in Q1 2026, and China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology has explicitly encouraged building FDE teams. It also distinguishes FDEs from ordinary on-site staff by ownership of results.

A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) is someone embedded at the enterprise's business front line who turns AI capabilities into business processes that can run, be inspected, and be controlled, and who is accountable for the results of implementation.

Three key points

  • On-site: Instead of sitting at headquarters waiting for requirements, they go to where the business actually happens and walk through the process alongside front-line staff.
  • Accountable for results: An FDE is measured not by how many features were configured, but by whether the business actually uses the solution and whether the impact can be quantified.
  • Built to last: What they deliver is not just a working system, but also clearly written rules, verification records, and a permissions scheme, so the enterprise can continue to iterate after taking over.

Where the FDE comes from

This way of working first took shape at Palantir. According to Palantir's official blog, internally it calls forward deployed software engineers Delta, responsible for deploying software platforms to customer sites and creating value with technology; and it calls Deployment Strategists Echo, whose role is closer to that of a product manager. The two typically work together on the same project. See Two types of FDE: engineering-focused and business-focused.

Why it is being talked about so much now

  • Many pilots, few make it into actual workflows. Preliminary research published by MIT NANDA in 2025 states that about 95% of enterprise generative AI pilots produced no measurable profit impact. The sample is small, so it should be treated as a directional judgment.
  • Model companies are doing delivery themselves. On May 4, 2026, Anthropic announced a joint venture with Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, and Goldman Sachs to build an enterprise AI services company; on May 11, OpenAI announced the creation of the OpenAI Deployment Company, in which it holds a controlling stake, and agreed to acquire Tomoro, bringing in about 150 Forward Deployed Engineers and deployment experts.
  • Rapid growth in job demand. Data from the recruiting platform Paraform shows that FDE job postings on its platform grew 350% year over year in Q1 2026.
  • Named in domestic policy. A notice issued by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology in August 2026 encourages service providers to build Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) teams. See Compilation of FDE-related policies.

What an FDE actually does

  • Identify real problems within the enterprise worth solving.
  • Understand how information, materials, approvals, and responsibilities flow.
  • Convert business staff's tacit knowledge into explicit rules.
  • Judge what an AI platform can and cannot do.
  • Use desensitized samples to verify whether digital workers are reliable.
  • Discover missing business conditions from errors and anomalies.
  • Design permissions, human confirmation, and phased rollout plans.
  • Distill project experience into reusable Skills and industry templates.

The core deliverable of an FDE is a digital worker workflow that can run, be inspected, be controlled, and that business staff are willing to use.

Frequently asked questions

Is FDE a job title or a way of working?

Both. At model companies and service providers, FDE is a job title; at most enterprises, it is more like a role that can be held concurrently by key staff familiar with the business. What matters is not the title, but whether the person goes on-site and is accountable for business outcomes.

How does an FDE differ from "on-site staffing"?

On-site staffing describes where you work; FDE describes what you are accountable for. On-site staff may simply complete configurations from a checklist; an FDE must first judge whether the requirement itself is right, then write the rules clearly, verify with samples, design the rollout approach, and be accountable for the results.
