Today's Judgment

Tencent ADP's official article and a Suzhou government meeting report each provide clues about service productization and software industry transformation. Reusable scenario packages and ongoing services are worth tracking, but target ratios and future plans must not be written up as verified results.

Key Signals

1. Tencent ADP launches twenty-five service scenario packages, offering FDE services jointly with KPMG

On November 2, the ADP team introduced that it has organized project experience into twenty-five scenario packages covering five industries, and is conducting FDE services with partners. KPMG is both a deployment user and a joint service partner.

Evidence boundary: The goal of achieving seventy percent replication and thirty percent customization at the starting stage is a target, not a measured reuse rate; the article does not disclose scenario package pricing, partner revenue sharing, or independent customer acceptance.

2. Suzhou approves in principle the "AI + Software" action plan

According to a November 3 report on the Suzhou government website, the executive meeting on November 2 reviewed and approved in principle the 2026–2028 action plan. It covers software intelligence, industry scenarios, and service-oriented transformation, and sets goals for 2028, including one hundred fifty products and solutions, five co-creation centers, and one hundred application cases.

Evidence boundary: The above are future construction goals and cannot be regarded as completed; the meeting report also does not equate to subsidy details or application channels.

Implications for FDE Practitioners

  • Break service packages clearly into standardized parts, customer-customized parts, and ongoing operations responsibilities, and define acceptance criteria for each.
  • Use project records to calculate reuse levels, maintenance costs, and customer adoption, rather than substituting target ratios for measured data.
  • Follow up on official policy documents and project outcomes, distinguishing planning from supply-demand organization and customer benefits.
