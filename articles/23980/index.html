Staff Forward Deployed Engineer, GenAI, Google Cloud, Data at Google — FDE Job Description

How to Become an FDE — Interview Kickstart · 2026-07-27 · 1 min read

Summary

This page reproduces a Google job posting for a Staff Forward Deployed Engineer (Data and AI) based in Singapore, an individual-contributor role requiring 8+ years of experience. The FDE would embed with large enterprise customers' engineering and data architecture teams, working on production data lakes, warehouses, and transactional systems under real latency, throughput, and governance constraints, and take Google's stack — BigQuery, Dataproc, Dataflow, Dataform/dbt, Gemini for Data — from design to production-grade workflows. Pay is not disclosed. The page also lists other Google FDE-adjacent openings in India, the US, and the Nordics, and promotes Interview Kickstart's advisory service.

Key points

  • Google is hiring a Staff FDE (Data and AI) in Singapore, senior IC level.
  • The role requires 8+ years of experience; salary is not disclosed.
  • FDEs embed with enterprise customers to deploy Google's data and AI stack in production.
  • Work spans BigQuery, Dataproc, Dataflow, Dataform/dbt, and Gemini for Data.
  • The page also lists related Google FDE roles in India, the US, and the Nordics.
