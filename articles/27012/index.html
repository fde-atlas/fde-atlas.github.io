Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Explained | Skills, Role, AI Engineering & Interview Preparation

FDE Times · 1 min read

Summary

This FDE Times page centers on a video by Mayank Aggarwal that explains the Forward Deployed Engineer role: what FDEs actually do, the skills required, how the role connects to AI engineering, and common misconceptions about the job. It is bundled with related videos, including Palantir FDE interview preparation, a solutions architect mock interview with a Salesforce SA, and a talk on deploying an LLM-based conversational agent in a hospital. The page also shows current hiring data: 924 open FDE roles (up 33 in a week), 80 new postings, 196 companies hiring, Databricks leading with 107 openings, a median US pay of $200k, and 25% of roles remote-friendly. Useful as a quick orientation for readers exploring the FDE career path or preparing for interviews.
