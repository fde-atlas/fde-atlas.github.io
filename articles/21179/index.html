Summary

FDE board lists one forward deployed engineer opening at JDSC Inc., a Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth-listed company founded in 2018 that develops and licenses machine learning algorithm modules, builds and operates IT systems, and provides data science consulting. With about 234 employees, headquarters in Bunkyo, Tokyo and a branch in Osaka, JDSC defines its FDE role as turning AX concepts into mechanisms that actually work in the field. The hire engages customers before requirements are fixed to decide what to build. Salary ranges widely from 5 million to 25 million yen, and prior FDE or AI project experience is not required.

About JDSC Inc.

A company listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth Market that develops and licenses algorithm modules using machine learning, develops and operates IT systems, and provides data science advisory and consulting services. Its mission is to boost the productivity of Japanese companies through data science and engineering, and to transform industrial structures themselves. It has 234 employees and also has a branch office in Osaka.

Characteristics of FDE Roles at This Company

The FDE role is defined as implementing AX not just as a concept but as systems that actually function in the field. You will be entrusted with deciding "what to build" by engaging in dialogue with clients even before requirements are finalized, with a wide salary range of ¥5 million to ¥25 million. Prior experience with FDE or AI projects is not required.
