Summary

This is a job-board style listing of 15 open Forward Deployed Engineer roles in Boston, aggregated from the careers pages of four companies and updated daily. Amazon (AWS) accounts for 7 openings, spanning individual contributor, senior, and manager levels up to Director of Product Management for FDE. Databricks has 5 senior FDE and Deployment Strategist roles organized by industry verticals such as financial services, manufacturing, and healthcare. n8n has 2 roles including a remote US East Coast Deployment Strategist, and Owkin seeks a Forward Deployed Computational Biologist. Median posted base pay ranges from $182k to $250k. The page also compares Boston's market with larger hubs like New York (158 roles) and the San Francisco Bay Area (154).
