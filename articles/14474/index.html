H-1B labor condition application

FDE Interview Prep · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

This article explains the Labor Condition Application (LCA, Form ETA-9035), which US employers must have certified by the Department of Labor before petitioning for H-1B, H-1B1 or E-3 workers. An LCA states the job title, worksite, prevailing wage and offered wage, covering base pay only—no bonus, equity or commission. Using DOL's public LCA disclosure files, the author analyzed 387 LCAs certified from October 2024 to June 2026 with 'forward deployed' in the title: the median lower-bound base wage was $161,637, with 25th and 75th percentiles of $145,000 and $190,000. The piece also shows how candidates can filter the disclosure data by employer, title, wage range, PW_WAGE_LEVEL (a rough seniority signal) and worksite, while cautioning that LCA figures cover only sponsored workers' base pay and can understate pay when filed ranges are wide.

Key points

  • An LCA must be DOL-certified before an H-1B, H-1B1 or E-3 petition.
  • LCA wages cover base pay only, excluding bonus, equity and commission.
  • 387 forward-deployed LCAs showed a median lower-bound base wage of $161,637.
  • Candidates can filter DOL disclosure files by employer, title, wage range and PW_WAGE_LEVEL.
  • LCA figures are not total compensation and wide filed ranges may understate actual pay.

Full text

A Labor Condition Application (LCA, Form ETA-9035) is the filing a US employer must have certified by the Department of Labor before it petitions for an H-1B, H-1B1 or E-3 worker (DOL, H-1B program). It states the job title, the worksite, the prevailing wage and the wage the employer will pay, as a single rate or a range, and it has no field for bonus, equity or commission. The record layout for DOL’s public LCA disclosure files defines that wage as the wage paid to nonimmigrant workers at the first worksite location.

In an offer

The public files show what an employer offered as base pay for a named title, which helps when a recruiter asks for your expectations. In H-1B filings certified from October 2024 to June 2026, 387 LCAs had “forward deployed” or “forward-deployed” in the job title, and their offered base wage, taken at the lower bound of each filed range, had a median of $161,637, a 25th percentile of $145,000 and a 75th percentile of $190,000.
