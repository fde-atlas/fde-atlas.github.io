WTF is Forward Deployed Engineer? Full Roadmap + Real Indian Salaries [2026]

FDE Times · 2026 · 1 min read

Summary

This FDE Times guide explains what a Forward Deployed Engineer does and offers a step-by-step roadmap for entering the role, covering skills, preparation, and interviews. It focuses on the Indian job market, publishing real 2026 salary figures across experience levels so readers can judge the financial case for the career. The article is framed by FDE Times hiring data: 924 open FDE roles across 196 companies, a median US pay of $200k, and Databricks as the top hirer with 107 openings. It targets readers deciding whether and how to pursue an FDE career in 2026.
