Summary

A job posting from Japanese company Bring Out (ブリングアウト) for a Forward Deployed Engineer to integrate its conversational analytics AI into client systems such as CRM, SFA, and ERP. The role covers API design, LLM/RAG architecture, PoC-to-MVP prototyping using OpenAI, Anthropic, and Vertex AI, and standardizing reusable modules with BizDev, AI, and backend teams. Requirements include 5+ years of software development or 2+ years of AI/ML experience, production-level Python or TypeScript, cloud experience, and LLM/RAG knowledge. Salary ranges from 7 to 20 million yen, with remote work, flexible hours, and a three-month probation.

Key points

  • Bring Out is hiring an FDE to integrate its conversational analytics AI into client platforms.
  • Work spans API design, LLM/RAG architecture, and fast PoC-to-MVP prototyping.
  • Requires 5+ years development or 2+ years AI/ML experience, with Python or TypeScript.
  • Salary is 7-20 million yen depending on experience, with a 3-month probation.
  • Fully remote with flexible hours; most staff work roughly 9:30-17:00.
