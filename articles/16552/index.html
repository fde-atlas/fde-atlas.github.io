Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)

Original title: 前线部署工程师（FDE）— 职责、技能与市场

FDE Role Definition Project · 2026-04-16 · 2 min read

Summary

This article outlines the role of forward deployed engineers (FDEs), covering their core responsibilities, required skills, and market landscape. Originating at Palantir, the role spans software development, systems integration, and customer delivery, from technical research through production deployment and handover. It documents 2026 corporate announcements: Microsoft's $2.5B investment with 6,000 experts planned for customer teams, AWS's $1B dedicated engineering organization, OpenAI's deployment company of roughly 150 engineers including an agreed acquisition of Tomoro, and Anthropic's partner program for mid-market Claude implementations. It also compares published US salary ranges from individual job postings, verified September 9, 2026.

Key points

  • FDE work spans software development, systems integration, and customer delivery.
  • The role originated at Palantir and spread to model developers and cloud providers.
  • Microsoft announced $2.5B and plans to deploy 6,000 experts to customer teams.
  • AWS committed $1B to a dedicated forward deployed engineering organization.
  • OpenAI plans a ~150-person deployment company and agreed to acquire Tomoro.

01 / Role

Core responsibilities

An FDE handles implementation work from technical research to production deployment and operational handover. Specific duties vary by employer.

01 Software development

Developing and maintaining applications, services, and production infrastructure.

02 System integration

Connecting data, applications, and infrastructure in the customer's environment.

03 Customer delivery

Clarifying requirements, agreeing on scope, and supporting adoption and handover.

The following are common areas of responsibility. For full duties, refer to each employer's job description.

  • Core software engineer
  • Reusable products
  • Solutions Architect
  • Technical solutions
  • Sales Engineer
  • Technical purchase decisions
  • Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)
  • Deployed systems actually running for customers

The role originated at Palantir and has since expanded to model developers, cloud providers, and software companies. Source · Open in new tab
