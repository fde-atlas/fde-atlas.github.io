Snowflake Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-05 · 14 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot counts 9 FDE-family postings on Snowflake's public Ashby board, tied for 12th among 114 employers. Roles span five tracks: Frontier Engineering, Applied AI, Migration, an AI Deployment Strategist seat in Tokyo, and a Software Engineering Manager for AI Migrations in Menlo Park. Four postings list US salary ranges, topping out at $304,000–$437,000 for Senior Principal Frontier Engineering. Five of seven engineer postings require eval or evaluation-framework experience for LLM or agent systems, while Migration Leads emphasize Python, Go, Java or C++ and customer delivery. Six of nine state 20% or 25% travel; only two postings are marked remote.

Key points

  • Snowflake has 9 FDE-family postings across five tracks, spanning the US, Europe and Asia-Pacific.
  • Four postings list US pay; the top range is $304,000–$437,000 (Senior Principal Frontier).
  • Five of seven engineer postings require evals and quality metrics for LLM or agent systems.
  • Migration Leads ask for Python, Go, Java or C++ with no stated years requirement.
  • Six of nine postings state 20% or 25% travel; only two are listed as remote.

Snowflake posts Forward Deployed Engineer seats under Frontier Engineering, Migration and Applied AI titles, plus an AI Deployment Strategist seat in Tokyo and a Software Engineering Manager, AI Migrations (Forward Deployed) seat in Menlo Park. In FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot of public job boards, Snowflake has 9 in-page postings, tied for 12th of 114 employers with Chalk, Cloudflare and Saronic (11 employers have more). What Snowflake calls the role

Snowflake posts under five title tracks in this snapshot: Frontier Engineering; Migration; Applied AI (including Staff Applied AI Engineer (FDE)); AI Deployment Strategist; and Software Engineering Manager, AI Migrations (Forward Deployed). Ashby departments split between Engineering (Staff Applied AI, Warsaw Applied AI, Principal Frontier, Senior Principal Frontier, Manager) and Professional Services (Strategist Tokyo, Frontier New Zealand, both Migration Leads).

Frontier Engineering covers three seats: Forward Deployed Engineer, Frontier Engineering (New Zealand Remote), Principal Forward Deployed Engineer I, Frontier Engineering (Menlo Park Hybrid), and Senior Principal Forward Deployed Engineer, Frontier Engineering (Menlo Park Hybrid). Applied AI covers Forward Deployed Engineer, Applied AI in Warsaw (OnSite; closed on the November 8, 2026 live check; not linked) and Staff Applied AI Engineer (FDE) in Menlo Park and Bellevue (Hybrid). Migration covers Lead Forward Deployed Engineer - Migration (Netherlands and Germany Remote) and Lead Forward Deployed Engineer - Migration (Australia) in Melbourne and Sydney (Hybrid); the Australia Ashby title uses an en dash (Lead Forward Deployed Engineer – Migration). What the engineer postings ask for

Years differ by seat. Staff Applied AI Engineer (FDE) states 7+ years of professional software engineering experience. Applied AI Warsaw (closed on the November 8, 2026 live check; not linked) states 3+ years of professional software engineering experience, and Frontier New Zealand states 3+ years of professional software engineering experience. Principal Frontier asks for 15+ years of software engineering experience, including at least 3 years building and shipping production AI/ML or agentic systems at enterprise scale. Senior Principal Frontier asks for 15+ years of software engineering experience, including a sustained track record of building and shipping production AI/ML or agentic systems at enterprise scale. Neither Migration Lead states a years number in its REQUIREMENTS block.

Frontier New Zealand and Applied AI Warsaw both ask for solutions using Python and SQL. Staff Applied AI and Frontier New Zealand list Familiarity with core data science libraries and tools (e.g., pandas, numpy, Snowpark). Both Migration Leads ask for Python, Go, Java or C++ (Netherlands, Australia). Principal and Senior Principal list Familiarity with Cortex AI, Snowpark, or modern enterprise data infrastructure platforms.

Five of the seven engineer texts mention eval or an evaluation framework. Staff Applied AI asks for Hands-on experience defining quality metrics and evaluation frameworks for LLM or agent systems, and using evals to systematically improve quality over time. Frontier New Zealand asks for Hands-on experience defining quality metrics and running evaluations for LLM or agent systems, and using evals to systematically improve quality. Both also prefer eval sets from production traces, A/B tests, ablations, offline evals and failure-mode analysis on agent or RAG systems. Applied AI Warsaw asks you to contribute to evaluation frameworks and observability infrastructure. Principal says Own the AI quality bar: Lead how the team measures and improves AI quality, turning ambiguous customer goals into clear, testable evaluation standards. and asks for Deep expertise in production AI architectures—including RAG pipelines, multi-step agentic systems, evaluation frameworks, and guardrails. Senior Principal says Set the AI quality and reliability bar: Define organization-level standards for evaluation, observability, reliability, security, guardrails, and responsible deployment. The two Migration Leads do not put eval or evaluation frameworks in their REQUIREMENTS lines.

Migration track

Both Migration Lead texts focus on customer project delivery, programming languages and a degree line, without a years number. Netherlands / Germany states Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field. and the role may involve travel up to 20% of the time to collaborate with teams and stakeholders. Lead Forward Deployed Engineer - Migration (Australia) states Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems or a related field, or equivalent practical experience and Travel: This role may involve up to 20% travel to collaborate with customers, teams and stakeholders. Both ask for proficiency in Python, Go, Java or C++.

Strategist and manager seats

The AI Deployment Strategist seat in Tokyo (Professional Services) has no years number in WHAT YOU WILL DO or WHAT YOU BRING. The text asks you to Serve as the filter for opportunities originated by Snowflake's field AE organization. Evaluate each for FDE fit across technical complexity, deployment readiness, and commercial viability. It states no travel percentage and no degree line.

The Software Engineering Manager, AI Migrations (Forward Deployed) seat in Menlo Park asks for 7+ years of experience in technical roles involving customer-facing delivery, engineering, 5+ years of people management experience — mentoring, coaching, and developing technical teams, and 3+ years of experience building and delivering enterprise software products, particularly involving AI/ML or developer tools. It lists Extensive hands-on expertise with SQL, SQL analytics, and scripting (Python, Perl, Bash) and hands-on experience with large-scale systems (Oracle, Teradata, Netezza, SQL Server, Greenplum, Hadoop), plus University degree in computer science, engineering, mathematics, or related fields (or equivalent experience). Travel is not mentioned on the snapshot row or in the November 5 text.

Where the roles are and how many are remote

Four of the nine are US-located: Principal Frontier, Senior Principal Frontier, Staff Applied AI and Manager, all in Menlo Park (Staff also lists Bellevue). Four are outside the US: Strategist Tokyo, Applied AI Warsaw, Frontier New Zealand and Migration Australia. The Netherlands Migration Lead is mixed (Netherlands and Germany Remote).

Two of the nine list remote: Frontier Engineering in New Zealand and Migration Lead in the Netherlands/Germany. That is against 176 of 704 (25.0%) across the snapshot on the same locations-index method. The other seven do not list remote: Hybrid Menlo Park Principal, Senior Principal and Staff Applied AI; Hybrid Australia Migration; OnSite Warsaw; Strategist Tokyo; and Manager Menlo Park. Do not read the Menlo Park Hybrid seats as fully remote.

Degree and travel lines

Warsaw Applied AI, Frontier New Zealand and Migration Australia state a bachelor's or equivalent practical experience. Migration Netherlands states Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field. with no "or equivalent" in that REQUIREMENTS line. The Manager states a university degree or equivalent experience. Staff Applied AI, Principal Frontier, Senior Principal Frontier and Strategist Tokyo are silent on a degree line in the November 5 texts.

Six of nine snapshot rows have travel_mentioned true with travel_pct_stated 20% or 25%, against 424 of 704 snapshot postings that mention travel. Staff Applied AI, Principal Frontier, Senior Principal Frontier and Frontier New Zealand state at least 25% onsite (Spend at least 25% of your time onsite, working closely with Snowflake's most strategic customers. on Staff and New Zealand; Spend at least 25% of your time onsite working with customers. on Principal and Senior Principal). Migration Leads state up to 20%. Strategist and Manager have travel_mentioned false. Employer travel figures are on how much forward deployed engineers travel.

None of the nine state a visa sponsorship stance or an equity grant. equity_mentioned is false on every Snowflake in-page snapshot row. Employer education rules are on do you need a degree to be a forward deployed engineer.

What this means if you are preparing for a Snowflake FDE loop

  • Pick the track by the work. Frontier and Applied AI seats ship customer AI systems with RAG, agents and evals. Migration Leads focus on customer data-platform migrations with Python, Go, Java or C++. The Strategist qualifies FDE-fit engagements. The Manager leads AI migration delivery teams.
  • Bring production AI evidence that matches the seat. Staff Applied AI and Frontier New Zealand ask for quality metrics and evaluation frameworks for LLM or agent systems. Principal and Senior Principal ask you to own or set the AI quality bar, including RAG pipelines and multi-step agentic systems. Migration Leads ask for customer project delivery and the language list above.
  • Read travel and location lines together. Expect 25% onsite language on Staff Applied AI and Frontier seats, and up to 20% on Migration Leads. Treat the Menlo Park seats as Hybrid.
  • Check the pay line before you assume a range. Only four of nine list US salary bands; the other five post no compensation tier.

Does Snowflake hire Forward Deployed Engineers?

Yes. Snowflake had 9 FDE-family postings in FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot, tied for 12th of 114 employers with Chalk, Cloudflare and Saronic: 3 Frontier Engineering seats (New Zealand Remote, plus Principal and Senior Principal in Menlo Park), 2 Migration Lead seats (Netherlands/Germany Remote and Melbourne/Sydney), 2 Applied AI seats (Warsaw and Staff Applied AI Engineer (FDE) in Menlo Park/Bellevue), 1 AI Deployment Strategist seat in Tokyo, and 1 Software Engineering Manager, AI Migrations (Forward Deployed) seat in Menlo Park. On November 8, 2026, 8 of 9 were still listed; the Applied AI Warsaw seat had closed. A Staff Product Manager - AI FDE seat is in the file as adjacent non-engineering and is not counted.

How much does a Snowflake Forward Deployed Engineer make?

Four of Snowflake's nine FDE-family postings list a US salary range on the November 3, 2026 snapshot. Staff Applied AI Engineer (FDE) lists $200,000 to $270,000. Principal Forward Deployed Engineer I, Frontier Engineering lists $264,000 to $379,500. Senior Principal Forward Deployed Engineer, Frontier Engineering lists $304,000 to $437,000. Software Engineering Manager, AI Migrations (Forward Deployed) lists $236,000 to $339,200. The other five postings have no compensation tier. The Senior Principal midpoint is $370,500, above the US engineer-title median midpoint of $216,104 across 255 US engineer-title postings in the October 24, 2026 salary index. These are Ashby Salary annual USD ranges as posted. Do not treat them as OTE. Posted ranges are not offers.

What do Snowflake Forward Deployed Engineer postings ask for?

Years differ by seat: Staff Applied AI asks for 7+ years of professional software engineering experience; Applied AI Warsaw and Frontier New Zealand ask for 3+ years; Principal and Senior Principal Frontier ask for 15+ years with production AI/ML or agentic systems at enterprise scale; Migration Leads state no years number; the Manager asks for 7+ years in technical customer-facing delivery, 5+ years of people management, and 3+ years building enterprise software. Frontier New Zealand and Applied AI Warsaw call out Python and SQL; Migration Leads ask for Python, Go, Java or C++; Staff Applied AI and Frontier New Zealand name pandas, numpy and Snowpark. Five of the seven engineer texts mention eval or evaluation frameworks, including RAG and agentic workflows on Applied AI and Frontier seats.

Are Snowflake Forward Deployed Engineer jobs remote?

Two of the nine list remote: Frontier Engineering in New Zealand and Migration Lead in the Netherlands/Germany. That is against 176 of 704 (25.0%) across the snapshot. The other seven do not list remote, including the Hybrid Menlo Park Principal, Senior Principal and Staff Applied AI seats, the Hybrid Australia Migration Lead, OnSite Warsaw, Strategist Tokyo and Manager Menlo Park. Do not read the Menlo Park Hybrid seats as fully remote.

Does Snowflake sponsor visas for Forward Deployed Engineer roles?

The nine in-page postings do not say. None of the November 5 texts mention visa sponsorship or an equity grant, and equity_mentioned is false on every Snowflake in-page snapshot row.
