FDE Interview Process: Rounds, Timelines & a 4-Week Prep Plan

FDE India · 2026-11-08 · 9 min read

Summary

A forward deployed engineer (FDE) interview usually runs three to six rounds: a recruiter screen, practical coding, a decomposition or case round, a system design or technical deep-dive, and a behavioural or customer round. The whole process can take one week at a fast-moving startup or six to eight weeks at a large platform company. The rounds that decide most outcomes are the ones normal software interviews don’t have: breaking down a vague customer problem, and showing you can be put in front of a client.

This guide is about the process: what each round is for, how long it lasts, how long companies take, and how to plan four weeks of preparation. For a bank of actual questions with model answers, see our FDE interview questions guide.

How many rounds an FDE interview has

There is no standard loop, but the published data points the same way:

  • Dataford, which analysed 1,083 FDE candidate reports (updated 6 November 2026), puts the median loop at 3 rounds.
  • Educative’s FDE course says loops “typically run 4 to 6 rounds over 3 to 5 weeks”, built around four core round types: decomposition, customer simulation, technical and behavioural.
  • DataInterview’s Databricks guide (updated 28 October 2026) lists 5 to 8 rounds from recruiter call to executive conversation.

The difference is mostly about how you count. Dataford counts rounds as candidates report them, so a half-day onsite with three interviews may be logged as one round. Big companies split the same content into more, shorter sessions.

Dataford also shows which stages appear most often in the reports:

Six in ten reports mention a take-home or assessment. Source: Dataford, 1,083 FDE candidate reports (Nov 2026).

The number to notice is the take-home: 61% of reports mention an assessment or take-home, almost as many as mention a technical interview. Plan your calendar around that.

The FDE interview rounds, one by one

1. Recruiter or hiring-manager screen (30–45 minutes)

A non-technical call on your background, why you want a customer-facing role, notice period, location and travel. Exponent’s Google FDE guide gives 30–45 minutes; DataInterview gives 30 minutes for Databricks, with a separate 45-minute hiring-manager conversation later. Have a crisp two-minute story of one project where you worked directly with a user or client.

2. Online assessment or take-home (1–2 hours, or longer)

Formats vary more here than in any other stage. One candidate who reviewed Statisfy’s FDE process on Glassdoor in January 2026 described a 1–2 hour Coderbyte test on problem-solving, coding and data reasoning. In India, Sarvam AI’s Bengaluru Forward Deployed Software Engineer listing (1–3 years’ experience) asks applicants to submit a completed AI agent assignment along with their resume and portfolio, before any interview.

3. Practical coding (45–60 minutes)

Expect data handling, APIs and clean code rather than hard algorithm puzzles, though some companies still ask them. DataInterview describes Databricks’ FDE coding round as 60 minutes in a notebook. A July 2025 Glassdoor review of DevRev’s FDE loop mentions merge intervals and a rate limiter in its two technical rounds. The Statisfy reviewer’s technical round covered Python, SQL, JSON handling and data validation.

4. Decomposition or case round (45–60 minutes)

You get an open business problem and must turn it into a plan: clarify the goal, find the data, pick the smallest useful version and define success. techinterview.org (updated 5 August 2026) says Palantir’s version lasts 45 minutes with little or no code and that only about four in ten candidates clear it. Educative and DataInterview both list it at 60 minutes. This is the round to practise most.

5. System design or technical deep-dive (45–60 minutes)

Integration-first design: fitting an AI system into a customer’s existing data, identity and security setup. Exponent describes an agentic and ML system design round in Google’s FDE loop, plus a “vibe coding” session where you build with AI tools. It notes these are likely components, not confirmed rounds. The Statisfy reviewer’s second technical round covered LLM prompting, dashboard reporting and workflow design.

6. Customer simulation and behavioural (30–60 minutes)

A role-play with an “upset customer” or a values conversation, often folded into other rounds. techinterview.org says Palantir adds 15–20 minutes of behavioural questions inside nearly every round. The Statisfy loop had a separate 30-minute customer communication round and a 30-minute culture-fit round with the CEO.

How hard each round is

Perspective AI’s prep guide (June 2026) gives reported pass rates per stage: about 80% for the screen, 60% for coding, 55% for the technical deep-dive, 40% for the ambiguous case study and 60% for behavioural. The guide doesn’t say how these were measured, so treat them as rough. They agree with techinterview.org on one point: the case round is where most people drop out.

Two points for Indian candidates:

  • Indian startups often run shorter loops. The New Delhi DevRev report is the clearest example: one week, one round. Other India listings don’t publish their steps. HackerRank’s Bengaluru FDE role, first seen on 3 April 2026 and still listed on Jobera on 8 November, asks for experience as a software, solutions or sales engineer but says nothing about its loop. Ask the recruiter for the full sequence on the first call.
  • Global platforms keep the full loop in India. DataInterview notes that a Databricks India posting levels Senior FDE at L5, the same ladder as elsewhere. Expect the full set of rounds, and budget the long timeline against your notice period. Our Databricks and Google company pages cover their India roles.

A slow process isn’t always bad news. The PracHub candidate who went through Palantir’s loop in July 2026 waited about five weeks after the hiring-manager round before hearing back.

A 4-week FDE interview prep plan

This plan assumes you have a few hours each weekday and longer weekends. It follows the rounds above.

Week 1 — Read the listing and build your stories

  • Copy the job description into a document and turn every requirement into a likely question (“integrate with a customer’s CRM” becomes “design a sync from Salesforce to our system”).
  • Write four or five customer stories using situation, problem, what you did and a number: a missed deadline, an angry stakeholder, a changing requirement, a production incident.
  • Ask the recruiter for the exact rounds, their order, and whether there is a take-home.

Week 2 — Coding and the take-home

  • Do one 60-minute practical problem a day: parse messy JSON, merge and dedupe records from two APIs, add retries and rate limiting. Cover intervals and rate limiters too, since they come up in DevRev reports.
  • Build a small AI agent with tools and a 20-case evaluation set, ideally the kind of assignment Sarvam AI asks for. Push it to GitHub with a short README on trade-offs.

Week 3 — Decomposition and design

  • Practise one decomposition prompt a day, out loud, against a 45-minute timer: “Why are deliveries late?”, “Help a bank reduce loan-processing time”. Spend the first ten minutes only on questions.
  • Do two integration-first designs: an LLM assistant over an on-prem database, and a document workflow with role-based access. Cover data access, security review, monitoring and cost.

Week 4 — Mock loop and logistics

  • Run one full mock loop with a friend: coding, decomposition, design and a customer role-play, back to back.
  • Prepare questions for the interviewers about engagement length, travel, how success is measured and who you would report to.
  • Rehearse your two-minute story and your salary expectation in LPA. Our salary hub has current bands by employer type.

If you aren’t sure which parts of your background match the role, run your resume through the FDE Fit Check before Week 1. The FDE skills guide explains each skill area employers test.

Frequently asked questions

How many rounds are there in a forward deployed engineer interview?

Usually three to six. Dataford's analysis of 1,083 FDE candidate reports gives a median of 3 rounds, Educative describes typical loops of 4–6 rounds, and DataInterview's Databricks guide lists 5–8 steps from recruiter call to executive round.

How long does the FDE hiring process take?

Anywhere from one week to two months or more. A DevRev candidate in New Delhi reported one week on Glassdoor, techinterview.org puts OpenAI at three to five weeks, and Exponent says Google typically takes 6–8 weeks from first recruiter call to decision.

What is the decomposition round in an FDE interview?

You get a vague business problem and have to break it into a plan you could build: clarify the goal, find the data, choose the smallest useful version and say how you would measure success. At Palantir it lasts about 45 minutes with little or no code, according to techinterview.org.

Do FDE interviews in India include a take-home assignment?

Often. Sarvam AI's Bengaluru FDE listing asks for a completed AI agent assignment with the application, and 61% of the candidate reports Dataford analysed mention an assessment or take-home. Ask the recruiter early so you can plan time for it.
