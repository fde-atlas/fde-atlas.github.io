Original title: Software Deployment Engineer（SDE）／SB OAI Japan合同会社 出向 | キャリア採用

Summary

SoftBank is hiring a Software Deployment Engineer (SDE) who will be seconded to SB OAI Japan, its joint venture with OpenAI launched in November 2025. The role implements cutting-edge LLM systems for major Japanese enterprises, going beyond PoCs to production systems, with a path to grow into a Forward Deployed Engineer position. Requirements include 3+ years of software engineering, cloud and LLM application experience, and business-level Japanese. Monthly salary ranges from 455,000 to 688,000 yen, implying roughly 7.1 to 14.1 million yen annually.

Key points

  • SDE role at SoftBank, seconded to SB OAI Japan, the OpenAI joint venture.
  • Focus on production AI systems for large Japanese enterprises, not just PoCs.
  • Career path explicitly extends toward becoming a Forward Deployed Engineer.
  • Requires 3+ years engineering experience, cloud/LLM skills, business-level Japanese.
  • Salary: 455,000–688,000 yen monthly, about 7.1–14.1 million yen per year.

JOB Software Deployment Engineer (SDE) / Secondment to SB OAI Japan G.K.

Advanced Technology Engineer (AI, Data, R&D)

Implement cutting-edge AI models in real-world businesses alongside Japan's leading companies to drive AI-native business transformation

See all open positions in the SB OAI Business Division here
    • About the Hiring Department
    • The SB OAI Business Division is the organization driving SB OAI Japan G.K., which was established in November 2025. SB OAI Japan is a joint venture with OpenAI, with the goal of realizing Crystal intelligence—bringing "AI-native management transformation" to enterprises—and is a company that provides the services and products to achieve this. (Employment is on the premise of secondment to SB OAI Japan G.K. after joining.) ・About Crystal intelligence https://www.softbank.jp/sbnews/entry/20250204_02
    • Job Description
    • 【Mission】 As a Software Deployment Engineer (SDE) at SB OAI Japan, you will implement cutting-edge AI models in real-world businesses alongside Japan's leading companies to drive AI-native business transformation. Rather than mere PoCs, you will build production systems that become embedded in operations and continuously deliver results, creating market advantages for customers. In the future, you will expand your role as a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), leading customer transformation. 【Main Responsibilities】 ・System implementation of LLM/generative AI technologies for strategic customers ・Design and implementation based on the technical scope/architecture defined by FDEs ・End-to-end implementation from prototype development to production deployment ・Close collaboration with customer engineers and business departments ・Improvement implementation based on model evaluation (Eval) and operation of feedback loops ・Collaboration with OpenAI and SB OAI Japan's Product/Research/Security teams 【Specific Duties】 ・Design and implementation of generative AI systems in customer environments (cloud/on-premises) ・Building RAG, agent design, and workflow automation ・Prompt design, evaluation design, and establishing quality improvement cycles ・Full-stack development including API/backend/frontend ・Implementation design that meets security/governance requirements ・Technical explanations and deployment support for customer executives and department heads ・Templatizing success patterns and creating reusable assets
    • What Makes This Job Attractive
    • ・Direct involvement in the management transformation of Japan's leading major corporations ・Fastest implementation of the world's most advanced models in the Japanese market ・Reflecting real, on-the-ground customer feedback into product evolution ・Taking responsibility through to "production adoption," not just PoCs ・Working closely with FDEs and building a foundation to drive transformation as an FDE in the future ・Gaining development experience at a global standard through collaboration with OpenAI
    • Qualifications (Required)
    • ・3+ years of software engineering experience ・Consistent experience in design, implementation, and operation in product or platform development ・Experience designing and implementing production-quality code using Python, JavaScript, etc. ・Experience building and operating systems in cloud environments (AWS/GCP/Azure, etc.) ・Experience designing and implementing application features using LLMs/generative AI ・Practical experience with development operations (CI/CD or automation) using GitHub/DevOps, etc. ・Japanese: business level or above
    • Qualifications (Preferred)
    • ・Experience driving projects including customer engagement ・Experience designing and building REST APIs ・Experience implementing AI agents using RAG ・Practical experience in AI agent prototyping ・Experience in CI/CD pipeline maintenance and operation automation ・Experience deploying with Docker ・Experience designing and operating MLOps/LLMOps ・Experience in large-scale enterprise system deployments ・Experience in data security/governance design ・Experience working in a startup or new business environment ・Experience collaborating with overseas teams
    • Ideal Candidate Profile
    • ・Someone who wants to solve customers' fundamental challenges with technology as their weapon ・Someone who enjoys both writing code and discussing with customers ・Someone who can move forward while balancing speed and quality ・Someone who is not afraid of ambiguity and can define, decide, and act on their own ・Someone who can make calm judgments even in high-pressure situations ・Someone who is seriously committed to the social implementation of AI
