Transcript: Palantir's chief architect Akshay Krishnaswamy on forward-deployed engineering and AI in enterprise workflows

TBPN Digest · 2025-04-09 · 38 min read

Summary

In a TBPN interview, Palantir chief architect Akshay Krishnaswamy, who has been there about 12.5 years, traces the company's evolution from defense and intelligence decision-support into commercial work at BP, Airbus, hospitals, and AIG. He argues forward deployed engineering is not rebranded sales engineering: it requires an organization where the field has primacy and FDEs share a hiring profile and engineering core with product teams, feeding back what to build. He describes ontology as a shared model of decision-making, a digital twin of business processes, and cites nurse scheduling at Cleveland Clinic, HCA, and Tampa General plus AIG underwriting as examples of AI agents being introduced incrementally with guardrails, comparing enterprise AI adoption to gradually training an employee rather than a binary flip.

Key points

  • Palantir began by helping defense and intelligence operators make better decisions, requiring data integration first.
  • Forward deployed engineering means field primacy in the org, not relabeled sales engineering.
  • FDEs and core engineers share the same hiring profile; product leaders were once FDEs.
  • Ontology models objects, actions, compliance, and business rules as a process digital twin.
  • AI agents should roll out incrementally, like training an employee, not as a bit flip.
