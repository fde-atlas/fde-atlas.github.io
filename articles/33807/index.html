Kinetic IT launches forward deployed AI engineering practice

Kernavique · 2026-07-08 · 2 min read

Summary

Consultancy.com.au reported on July 8, 2026 that Australian IT services firm Kinetic IT launched an AI engineering practice built on a forward deployed engineer model, embedding AI technicians inside client organizations. Marcio Sete, previously at Neu21 and a former principal consultant at Elabor8, leads the practice. Kishore Jayaram has taken a newly created chief transformation officer role, and Dean Langenbach has been CEO since March. Target sectors are government and critical infrastructure, including aviation, utilities and resources, with security, governance and reliability cited as key requirements in regulated settings. No headcount was disclosed.

Key points

  • Kishore Jayaram fills a newly created chief transformation officer role.
  • Targets are government and critical infrastructure: aviation, utilities, resources.
  • No team size or hiring numbers were reported.
