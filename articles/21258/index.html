Compare FDE PRO, APEX & NEXUS

Cloud Soft Solutions · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

Cloud Soft Solutions compares its three flagship training programs. FDE PRO is a 12-week, ₹30,000 course for developers who want to build production AI systems, covering RAG, AI agents, LangGraph, AWS and Kubernetes, with five enterprise projects. APEX is a 16-week, ₹30,000 program for beginners and career switchers, requiring no coding experience and spanning Python, machine learning, cloud, DevOps and cybersecurity. NEXUS is a 6-month, ₹60,000 program (plus ₹60,000 after placement) targeting DevOps, SRE and platform engineers, with 18 real-time projects across AWS, Azure, GCP, Terraform and CI/CD. All three offer classroom training in Ameerpet or live online, plus placement support. Two additional 2026 programs, ASCEND and HORIZON, are also mentioned.

Key points

  • FDE PRO: 12 weeks, ₹30,000, for engineers building production AI systems.
  • APEX: 16 weeks, ₹30,000, no coding experience required.
  • NEXUS: 6 months, ₹60,000 plus ₹60,000 after placement, for DevOps/SRE roles.
  • All programs offer Ameerpet classroom or live online formats with placement support.
  • ASCEND and HORIZON programs are also offered for 2026.
