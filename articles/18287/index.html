From AI planning to implementation, working closely with the field and moving forward together.

Original title: AIの企画から実装まで、 現場に寄り添い、共に進める

cotofure.com · 2026-10-30 · 4 min read

Summary

This is a service page from Cotofure promoting its Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) offering for companies that want to use AI but lack in-house expertise or resources. Experienced PMs and engineers work as part of the client's team, covering problem definition, AI technology selection, prototyping and validation, system integration, deployment, and ongoing improvement. Case studies include an AI double-eyelid simulator for TCB Tokyo Central Cosmetic Surgery, app and PHR development for H.U. Group Holdings / Fujirebio, and a video-recognition AI (person detection, mosaic processing, movement analysis) taken from PoC to commercial use for a major telecom carrier. Engagement follows a four-step process from consultation to operation, with scope, staffing, and pricing agreed per project.

Key points

  • Cotofure's FDE service targets firms lacking AI talent or unable to move from PoC to production.
  • Support spans problem scoping, technology selection, prototyping, implementation, and post-launch improvement.
  • Cases include TCB's AI simulator, Fujirebio's app and PHR, and a carrier's video-analysis AI.
  • Work proceeds in four stages: consultation, scoping/planning, validation/implementation, and operation/improvement.
  • Onsite, visit, or online support models and costs are negotiated based on scope and data.

Cotofure FDE Service

Forward Deployed Engineering

PMs and engineers with AI development experience work together with members involved in planning and developing our own products. We provide end-to-end support, from organizing business challenges to selecting AI technologies, implementing systems, and improving them after deployment.

You want to leverage AI, but your company lacks the know-how and resources, and there's no clear path to making it happen. We solve these challenges through hands-on support from planning through implementation and improvement.

Hard to integrate

Unclear how to implement AI that fits your existing systems and workflows.

Can't sustain it

Even after deployment, there's no bandwidth for evaluating results or continuous improvement.

We make AI usable in real business operations.

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineering) is a development approach in which we deeply understand our customers' operations and challenges, and leverage technical expertise to drive everything from organizing challenges to system implementation and improvement as one integrated effort.

In Cotofure FDE Service, PMs and engineers with AI development experience collaborate with members involved in planning and developing our own products. As members of your team, we consistently support AI adoption from planning through implementation and improvement.

From concretizing plans to technology selection, development, and post-deployment improvement. We support from whichever stage is needed, depending on your current challenges.

We organize business challenges and candidate use cases, and concretize requirements and approach based on feasibility.

We select appropriate AI technologies and verify business usability through prototyping and effectiveness evaluation.

We develop AI models and API integrations, implementing them in a form that integrates with your existing systems and operations.

We establish how the solution is used and operated in the field, supporting adoption and establishment in your business.

Based on effectiveness evaluation, we improve features and support additional development and expansion into new AI use cases.

Leveraging extensive expertise gained through diverse AI development, including image recognition AI and generative AI, we support everything from technical validation to system development and implementation, tailored to your business challenges.

PMs, engineers, and planning members work together, covering business understanding, planning, and project management. As members of your team, we support everything from concretizing AI use cases to implementation.

We plan, develop, and operate our own products, including the facial recognition "ProoFace" and people counting and attribute analysis "EMOTICS". Leveraging this expertise, we support AI adoption that continues to be used in the field.

At each stage, we review results and approach, agree on the scope of support, and proceed.

  • 01 Consultation first: We hear about your challenges, requirements, and current initiatives.
  • 02 Challenge organization and planning: We review the target operations and data, and propose the scope of support, evaluation methods, and costs.
  • 03 Technical validation and implementation: Through repeated prototyping and evaluation, we develop the required AI features and system integrations.
  • 04 Deployment, operations, and improvement: We support introduction into your operations and ongoing operation, continuously improving while verifying effectiveness.
