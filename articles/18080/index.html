Summary

This Top 11 comparison ranks Andela (#8) above Cognition, maker of Devin (#11), in its 2026 list of US firms for hiring either a forward deployed engineer or a full stack developer. Andela, founded in 2014 and based in New York, offers 17,000 vetted engineers who work under a client's technical leadership, with example monthly rates of $6,500–$8,500. Cognition, founded in 2023 in San Francisco, suits teams testing whether an AI agent can absorb well-specified backlog work, priced via a $20/month Core plan plus usage-based agent compute units. The article targets founders and CTOs with one senior hire's budget and an unclear hiring need, and states no vendor pays for placement.
