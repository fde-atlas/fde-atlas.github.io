Palantir Levels and Salary Bands (2026)

ResumeAdapter · 2026-07-04 · 1 min read

Summary

Palantir publishes no numbered engineering ladder, unlike Google's L3-L8 or Meta's E3-E9, and levels.fyi offers only role-title aggregates rather than level bands. Compensation must therefore be reconstructed by role: community-sourced July 2026 medians are roughly $251K for Software Engineer, $211K for Forward Deployed Software Engineer, and $300K for Software Engineering Manager. Full text

The spine of this page is that Palantir has no numbered ladder. Where Google runs L3 to L8 and Meta runs E3 to E9, Palantir exposes no public engineering level codes at all, and levels.fyi has no L-band or PT-band map for it, only role-title aggregates. There is nothing to target by number.

So compensation is reconstructed by role. levels.fyi aggregates a Software Engineer median around $251K, a Forward Deployed Software Engineer around $211K, and a Software Engineering Manager around $300K, all July 2026 and all community-sourced. These are not official Palantir figures, and they vary by location and stock.

The distinction Palantir does draw is Dev versus Delta: a Dev builds one capability for many customers, and a Delta brings many capabilities to one customer. A Deployment Strategist (Echo) blends product, engineering, and strategy, and levels.fyi does not separate a salary for it, so none is asserted here.

Because there is no level label to lean on, the resume has to carry the scope on its own. For a Dev pitch that means platform depth quantified (throughput, users, reliability); for a Delta pitch it means a customer outcome quantified. Tools-and-duties bullets with no numeral leave a reviewer no way to place you.
