How to Use the Templates

Templates are meant to drive decisions and preserve evidence, not to fill in blanks. Each document has a version, owner, review status, date, and evidence links; items not yet verified should be marked "pending verification," and it is forbidden to fill in "passed" in advance. The templates below can be downloaded directly; fill in synthetic examples or replace them within an authorized environment.

Evidence Organization

It is recommended to organize the project directory by problem, design, implementation, evaluation, release, handover, and retrospective. File names should include the object and version, evidence should use relatively stable addresses, and beyond screenshots, keep inputs, outputs, configurations, and test methods.

Public repositories should only contain synthetic or approved, de-identified materials. Real acceptance signatures, customer logs, and account information stay in authorized systems; public templates retain only structure and examples.

Review and Maintenance

Review whether the templates help make decisions: a missing owner means no one will take responsibility; no failure threshold means nothing can be blocked; no evidence means nothing can be re-verified. After a project ends, delete unused fields and add judgments for things actually missed, but keep historical versions to explain decisions made at the time.
