Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs: Who Hires and Where

MarsLab · 2026-11-01 · 9 min read

Summary

Based on job boards from Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks and Ramp checked on 15 November 2026, this MarsLab page maps who hires forward deployed engineers and how. About 200 openings carried "forward deployed" in the title, led by Databricks (83) and Palantir (72). Postings typically ask for 4-6+ years of customer-facing engineering, Python skills, and 20-50% travel; fully remote roles are rare. The same work often appears under titles like Applied AI Engineer or Deployment Strategist. Palantir is the main source of internships and new-grad roles. The article closes with a checklist for distinguishing genuine FDE roles from renamed sales jobs.

Key points

  • Five employers posted roughly 200 FDE openings in November 2026.
  • Databricks (83) and Palantir (72) list the most FDE roles.
  • Typical requirements: 4-6+ years experience, Python, 20-50% travel.
  • Few roles are fully remote; most are hybrid or on site.
  • A checklist separates real FDE jobs from quota-carrying sales roles.

Forward deployed engineer jobs are posted mainly by AI labs, enterprise data and software vendors, and software companies selling to governments. In the postings we read in November 2026, those that state a number ask for 4 to 6 or more years of customer-facing engineering, with Python and travel of 20 to 50 percent. Few are fully remote.

This page is about finding the jobs. For what the role is, read our forward deployed engineer guide. Everything below about employers comes from the public job boards of Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks and Ramp, which we opened on 15 November 2026. Postings change weekly, so treat each detail as a snapshot and check the live page before you rely on it.

Who hires forward deployed engineers?

Three kinds of company post the most: AI labs, data and enterprise software vendors, and software companies that sell to governments. Fintech and other product companies are starting to add the role. The common thread is a product that needs engineering work inside each customer before it pays off.

  • AI labs: OpenAI and Anthropic both describe FDEs who embed with strategic customers to put models into production.
  • Data and enterprise software vendors: Palantir, which uses the title Forward Deployed Software Engineer, and Databricks, which posts FDE roles by industry such as retail, manufacturing, healthcare and life sciences, and public sector.
  • Product companies: Ramp, a finance software company, posts a Software Engineer, Forward Deployed, AI Solutions role in New York.

We did not read other employers' pages, so this is a sample and not a census.

How many forward deployed engineer jobs do the main employers post?

On 15 November 2026 the boards we read listed about 200 openings with "forward deployed" in the title, spread across five employers. Databricks and Palantir post the most. Treat the counts as a rough guide, not a market size. We keep a live count of forward deployed engineer jobs by employer.

  • Databricks: 83 of 896 openings had "forward deployed" in the title.
  • Palantir: 72 of 302.
  • OpenAI: 36 of 839.
  • Anthropic: 8 of 670.
  • Ramp: 2 of 180.

The counts include managers, strategists and the same role repeated across cities, so they overstate the number of distinct jobs. They also understate demand at OpenAI and Anthropic, which post much of the same work as Applied AI Engineer or Architect.

What other titles is the same job posted under?

Expect Applied AI Engineer, Applied AI Architect, Deployment Strategist, Forward Deployed Software Engineer and Solutions Engineer. Some are the same work and some are not, so read the responsibilities.

  • Palantir: Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Forward Deployed Infrastructure Engineer, Forward Deployed Reliability Engineer and Deployment Strategist.
  • Databricks: Forward Deployed Engineer, AI Engineer (Forward Deployed Engineering), Associate FDE and Deployment Strategist.
  • OpenAI: Forward Deployed Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, Applied AI Architect and Applied AI Delivery Lead.
  • Anthropic: Forward Deployed Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, Applied AI Architect and Technical Deployment Lead.
  • Ramp: Software Engineer, Forward Deployed, AI Solutions, next to an AI Solutions Engineer role that is a different job (see below).

Palantir's Deployment Strategist posting that we read describes customer-facing data and workflow work, with programming or scripting experience listed and travel of 25 to 75 percent. OpenAI's Applied AI Delivery Lead posting describes running delivery programs, with 7 or more years leading enterprise deployments, and is not mainly a coding role.

Where should you search for forward deployed engineer jobs?

Start with the employers' own job boards, because every listing elsewhere is a copy of one of them. Then use LinkedIn or a similar aggregator to find employers you had not thought of, and follow each listing back to the employer's page before you apply.

  • Company boards: Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks and Ramp all show the full posting text, location and travel terms.
  • Aggregators such as LinkedIn: useful for discovering employers, but confirm the role is still open on the employer's own page.

We could not read LinkedIn's pages, so we say nothing about how its search behaves.

What search phrases find the most forward deployed engineer roles?

Search the exact title first, then the neighboring titles, then add a filter for location or industry. A short phrase such as "forward deployed" also catches titles that carry a suffix, such as a city or an industry.

  • Exact titles: "forward deployed engineer" and "forward deployed software engineer".
  • Neighboring titles: "applied AI engineer", "applied AI architect", "deployment strategist" and "technical deployment".
  • Short forms: "FDE", which some employers put in brackets after the title.
  • Modifiers for your case: "remote", "new grad", "intern", or an industry such as "healthcare" or "public sector".

A title with "solutions engineer" in it needs extra care, because it is sometimes pre-sales or support work. The section on renamed sales jobs below covers how to check.

What do forward deployed engineer postings ask for?

Most ask for several years of production software work with customers, strong Python, and comfort with ambiguity. At the AI employers they also ask for hands-on LLM or agent experience. The years of experience listed run from 2 to 6 or more in the postings we read.

  • Anthropic, Forward Deployed Engineer (London): 4 or more years in a technical, customer-facing role, production experience with LLMs, and proficiency in Python.
  • OpenAI, Forward Deployed Engineer (San Francisco): 5 or more years of engineering or technical deployment experience that includes customer-facing work, production code in Python or JavaScript, and LLM systems experience.
  • Databricks, Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer: 6 or more years in data engineering, data platforms or software engineering, plus working knowledge of two cloud platforms and Apache Spark.
  • Ramp, Software Engineer, Forward Deployed: hands-on LLM systems experience including agents, retrieval and evals, and experience working directly with customers.
  • Palantir, Forward Deployed Software Engineer: an engineering degree is preferred, with proficiency in one or more languages such as Python, Java, C++ or TypeScript.

Soft requirements repeat across employers: working with executives and engineers, deciding without full information, and handing over systems the customer can run. Anthropic's Staff FDE posting says candidates do not need to have shipped agents to production, but must have deep experience building and operating production systems.

Are forward deployed engineer jobs remote?

A few are, but most are hybrid or on site, and every one we read also involved travel to customers. Palantir's board tags 37 of its forward deployed postings as on site and 35 as hybrid on 15 November 2026, and none as remote.

Databricks is the clearest source of remote roles among those we read. Several of its Sr. FDE postings list locations such as Remote - California, Remote - New York and Remote - Washington D.C., and its AI FDE posting says it is open to remote locations. Its Associate FDE posting is hybrid, with New York and Mountain View as hub cities.

OpenAI's FDE posting in San Francisco uses a hybrid model of three days a week in the office and offers relocation assistance. Anthropic's postings say it expects all staff to be in an office at least 25 percent of the time. If you search for "forward deployed engineer remote" or "forward deployed engineer jobs remote", read each posting for office days as well as the remote tag.

How much do forward deployed engineers travel?

Postings state between about 20 and 50 percent, and Palantir's strategist roles go higher. The figure varies by employer, team and customer, so ask for the real number for the team you would join.

  • Databricks: travel to customers 20 percent of the time on the Sr. FDE postings, and up to 20 percent on the Associate FDE posting.
  • Anthropic: 25 to 50 percent on the London FDE posting and 25 percent on the Staff FDE posting.
  • Palantir: 25 to 50 percent on the Forward Deployed Software Engineer postings, and 25 to 75 percent required on the Deployment Strategist posting we read.
  • OpenAI: up to 50 percent is required on the San Francisco FDE posting.
  • Ramp: the role description says occasional travel, while the requirements list willingness to travel up to about 50 percent. Postings can disagree with themselves, so ask.

Are there forward deployed engineer internships?

Yes, at Palantir. Its board listed forward deployed software engineer internships in the US and several other countries on 15 November 2026. We found none at the other four employers we read.

Palantir's commercial internship postings ask for programming proficiency and hands-on experience applying AI, which can come from coursework or personal projects. One posting says the opportunity is open to freshmen and sophomores as well as juniors and seniors. Internships tied to US Government work require an active security clearance or eligibility and willingness to obtain one.

Databricks' Associate FDE posting tells recent graduates to look at the internship openings on its careers page, which suggests the company routes students there. We did not open those internship postings.

Are there entry-level or new grad forward deployed engineer jobs?

A few, mainly at Palantir. Most other FDE postings ask for years of experience, and even Databricks' "Associate" title asks for 2 or more years.

Palantir's board listed new-graduate postings for Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Forward Deployed Infrastructure Engineer and Deployment Strategist in several locations. The New York commercial posting we read asks for an engineering degree, one or more programming languages, and graduation in December 2026 or spring 2027. It says new graduates get responsibility from day one and 25 to 50 percent travel is expected.

Databricks' AI FDE posting says it is not intended for internship, new graduate or entry-level applicants. If you are early in your career, the usual alternative is a software engineering or customer-facing technical role first. Our guide to becoming a forward deployed engineer covers that path.

How can you tell a real FDE role from a renamed sales or support job?

Check what the posting says you will build, who measures you and how. A real FDE role ships and runs software for customers, while a renamed sales role carries a quota. Ramp's board shows both kinds side by side.

Ramp's AI Solutions Engineer posting describes itself as a product-focused, quota-carrying technical sales role. Its Software Engineer, Forward Deployed posting describes owning the technical plan, implementation, evals and monitoring. The titles look alike and the jobs are different. Use this checklist on any posting:

  • Code: does it say you will write and ship production code? OpenAI's FDE posting lists contributing directly in the code when progress depends on it.
  • Quota: a revenue quota, deal stages or "closing" language marks a sales job.
  • Success measure: production adoption and workflow impact point to engineering. Revenue or tickets closed point elsewhere.
  • Where you work: an FDE embeds with the customer and the posting states the travel. Pre-sales and support roles usually stay in one place.
  • Technical bar: named languages, system design and cloud experience should appear in the requirements.
  • Billing: Databricks' Sr. FDE posting says FDEs are billable, so ask how utilization is tracked and how much time goes to reusable work.

Before you accept, ask who owns the code, how handover works, and how much of the week is building and how much is meetings. To see what a full posting contains, read our forward deployed engineer job description, and for a short definition see the glossary.
