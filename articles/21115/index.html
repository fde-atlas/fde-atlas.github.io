FDE Demand Grows 19x in India | Comparing Indeed Data and 17 FDE Job Openings in India with Japan

Original title: FDEの需要はインドで19倍に|Indeedのデータとインド勤務のFDE求人17件を日本と比べる

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-10-22 · 23 min read

Summary

Indeed data shows FDE job postings in India grew roughly 19-fold year-on-year, though growth slowed to about 16x in July and just over 6x in August. The FDE Talent editorial team's own scan of 133 public job boards on October 22, 2026 found only 17 India-based FDE postings from 7 companies, equal to Japan-related postings. The authors suggest the surge may be driven by consulting firms rather than AI companies—Accenture alone listed 96 FDE postings in India. Analyzing 17 postings, the article identifies three work patterns: embedding with customer teams, executing Asia-Pacific projects behind the scenes (Notion), and serving customers on US hours (Outreach, Level AI). Japan postings at Databricks and Cursor require Japanese, while India postings do not. Glassdoor data puts average base pay at about 1.2 million rupees (~2.03 million yen), though Indeed says the sample is too small for a reliable median.

Key points

  • Indeed reports India FDE postings grew ~19x in a year, with growth slowing afterward.
  • The blog's job board scan found 17 India postings, matching Japan's 17.
  • Accenture alone had 96 India FDE postings, suggesting consultancies drive the surge.
  • India FDE roles split into three types: customer-facing, back-office APAC execution, and US-hours support.
  • Databricks and Cursor require Japanese only for their Tokyo FDE roles, not India ones.

How much have FDE job postings in India grown?

Indeedのデータでは、インドのFDE求人は2025年6月から2026年6月の1年で約19倍になりました[1]。6月の前年同月比は1,807%増で、伸びは2026年3月から加速し、7月は前年の約16倍、8月は6倍強と、ピークを過ぎて落ち着きつつあります[1][2]。求人の勤務地はベンガルール、ハイデラバード、チェンナイ、ニューデリー、ムンバイと、リモートでした[2]。

Sashi Kumar (Managing Director, Indeed India) — statement reported by Analytics India Magazine

How does this compare with Japan and other Asia-Pacific countries?

On the public job boards this blog monitors, FDE postings for India-based roles are roughly the same scale as Japan's. Of the 510 FDE postings (77 companies) collected from 133 boards (131 of which responded) on October 22, 2026, 17 postings (7 companies) were located in India, and 17 postings (9 companies) were Japan-related (editorial team's tally). Singapore, South Korea, and Australia also had 13-16 postings each, with the major Asia-Pacific countries at similar levels.

How are the numbers for India moving on the boards we track?

On the boards this blog tracks, FDE job postings for India-based roles have remained essentially flat for three weeks. In data collected from September 30 to October 22, 2026, the count fluctuated between 14 and 18 postings (compiled by the editorial team). Since this blog's data only goes back to the end of September 2026, we cannot compare with a year ago, so we can neither confirm nor deny Indeed's 19x figure. However, the boards we track are mainly job boards of US AI and SaaS companies, and do not include the consulting firms or Indian IT companies that Indeed cites.

What does this mean for job seekers and hiring companies in Japan?

インドの数字から日本の読者が受け取れるのは、FDEがアジア太平洋で「どこで、誰が、どの部分を受け持つか」の分業が進み始めているという事実です。外資系のプロダクト企業は、日本とインドの両方でFDEを募集し、DatabricksとCursorは日本の求人に日本語の要件を付けています[7][10]。ここから先は、求人票の記載をもとにした編集部の読みです。

  • 求職者にとって:外資系企業の日本のFDEは、顧客の前に立つ役割として募集され[5][10]、日本語を要件にする会社もあります[7][10]。実装の一部をインドなど他の拠点と分け合う体制では、英語で仕事を切り分けて渡す力も問われると考えられます。
  • 求職者にとって:「Forward Deployed Engineer」という職種名だけで仕事を判断できません。インドの求人には、米国時間で働く導入後の支援職[12]や、本文の多くが障害対応や監視・保守に充てられた職[16]も含まれます。日本で同じ名前の求人を見るときも、本文で顧客との距離と仕事の中身を確かめる必要があります。
  • For hiring companies: Companies hiring FDEs in Japan are competing for the same talent not only with Japanese candidates but also with foreign companies that combine Asia-Pacific locations. People who can consolidate customer requirements in Japanese are indispensable even for companies with implementation bases in India.
  • 採用企業にとって:自社のFDEを置くとき、顧客の前に立つ役割と、裏側で実装を進める役割を分けて設計するのは1つの選択肢です。Notionの求人票は、どちらが何を決め、何を引き継ぐかを細かく書いています[4]。

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much have FDE job postings in India grown?

According to data from the job site Indeed, FDE job postings in India grew roughly 19-fold in the one year from June 2025 to June 2026 (a 1,807% increase compared with the same month the previous year in June). Since then, growth has settled down to about 16-fold in July and just over 6-fold in August. These figures reflect the growth in the number of postings, not the number of people hired.

Q Which has more FDE job openings, India or Japan?

Among the 133 public job boards this blog monitors, as of October 22, 2026, there were 17 FDE job postings for positions in India (7 companies) and 17 Japan-related postings (9 companies), roughly the same scale. However, the monitoring focuses mainly on US AI and SaaS companies, and does not include many Indian IT companies or consulting firms. Accenture alone had 96 FDE job postings in India (as of October 20, 2026).

Q Is the work of an FDE in India different from that of an FDE in Tokyo?

It varies by company. Notion's job posting states that FDEs in India execute migration work for Asia-Pacific customers behind the scenes, while requirements definition and design with customers are handled by regional FDEs. Notion's FDE in Tokyo handles everything from technical discovery, requirements definition, and design through implementation. Databricks' and Cursor's Japan postings require both Japanese and English (as of October 22, 2026).

Q What is the salary of an FDE in India?

According to posts on Glassdoor, the average base salary for an FDE in India is about 1.2 million rupees per year (about 2.03 million yen), with a range of 900,000 to 1.6 million rupees (28 posts). There are also individual posts reporting total compensation of around 2.2 to 2.7 million rupees for 1-3 years of experience. Indeed itself states that the sample size is too small to produce a reliable median, so these figures should be treated as a rough guide. Yen conversions are based on the midpoint of Mitsubishi UFJ Bank's published rates on October 21, 2026 (1 rupee = 1.69 yen).

Q If a job posting in India says "Forward Deployed Engineer," is it a job developing at customer sites?

Not necessarily. FDE job postings in India also include roles such as a managed services position working remotely on US hours to expand AI agents for existing customers (Outreach), and positions that list ServiceNow IT service management as a required skill and state in the body that the role handles incident response and maintenance (some Accenture postings). Rather than relying on the job title, you need to check the body text for the degree of customer proximity and the nature of the work.
