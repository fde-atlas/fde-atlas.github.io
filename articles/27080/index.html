Outsource account growth without the headcount

quivly.ai · 2026 · 4 min read

Summary

Quivly sells "Account Growth as a Service": it embeds a forward-deployed engineer into a customer's team, who learns the product and growth motions, then builds custom AI agents on a visual canvas connected to CRM, usage, and communications data. Agents handle onboarding nudges, expansion signals, renewal prep, advocacy, and health monitoring across customer segments. Quivly claims first agents go live within two weeks, coverage of roughly 3x more accounts than a lean in-house team, and full logging of agent actions. It positions this against hiring senior CS staff, which takes 6–12 months to ramp and scales linearly with headcount, while FDEs cross-pollinate effective agent patterns across customers without sharing customer data.

Key points

  • Quivly embeds an FDE who builds custom post-sale AI agents for each customer.
  • Agents cover onboarding, expansion signals, renewal prep, advocacy, and health monitoring.
  • Claims: first agent live in 2 weeks, 3x account coverage vs. lean in-house teams.
  • Pitched as an alternative to 6–12 month hiring cycles for senior CS and AM talent.
  • FDEs share agent patterns across customers without exposing customer data.

Common questions about Account Growth as a Service

They learn your product, customers, and growth goals — then build and deploy custom AI agents that handle expansion outreach, renewal prep, adoption nudges, and health monitoring. Your team stays focused on strategy and relationships; Quivly runs the account growth engine.

CSMs are great for high-touch relationships, but hiring enough to cover your full book is expensive and slow. Account Growth as a Service gives you an embedded expert plus AI agents that scale across every account — without adding dozens of headcount or building internal automation from scratch.

Your FDE learns your business deeply — product, ICP, sales motion, and customer segments. They compose agents on Quivly's visual builder, connect your data sources, set guardrails, and monitor performance. Quivly FDEs work across multiple high-growth companies.
