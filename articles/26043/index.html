What does "on-site five stages" mean? — Enterprise AI deployment terminology | FDE Encyclopedia

Original title: 现场五关是什么意思？— 企业AI落地术语

FDE百科 · 1 min read

Summary

This glossary article explains the "Five Checkpoints on Site" framework, proposed by Jiao Bo (Shuijing), which turns the FDE five-step working loop (on-site immersion, diagnosis, building, delivery, enablement) into five verifiable gates: pain point, entry point, prototype, accounting, and self-sustaining operation. Each gate has one gating question; if you cannot answer it, you should not move to the next stage. The framework serves as a practical acceptance test for enterprise AI implementation work.

Key points

  • Jiao Bo (Shuijing) proposed five checkpoints mapping to the FDE five-step loop.
  • Pain point gate: the pain must be witnessed on site, not heard in meetings.
  • Entry point gate: pick the easiest win, small and precise like a surgical cut.
  • Prototype gate: something usable within two weeks; slides do not count.
  • Final gates: results must reconcile financially, and the client must run a second use case independently after you leave.

Full text

The FDE five-step closed loop has an acceptance gate at each step: the pain point gate, the entry point gate, the prototype gate, the numbers gate, and the self-sustaining gate—one gatekeeping question per gate. If you can't answer it, don't move on.

The five on-site gates were proposed by Jiao Bo (Shuijing), turning the FDE five-step work closed loop (on-site residency → diagnosis → building → delivery → enablement) into five verifiable checkpoints:

Pain point gate (acceptance of residency)—Did you see this pain point with your own eyes, or hear about it in a meeting room? Secondhand requirements will deceive you; pain points on the ground will not.

Entry point gate (acceptance of diagnosis)—Is the entry point you chose "the easiest to win" or "the one the boss most wants to win"? The first scenario should be like a surgical incision: small and precise.

Prototype gate (acceptance of building)—Within two weeks, where is the thing the business team can click open and use when they come to work tomorrow? A PPT doesn't count; only something they can click open and use counts.

Numbers gate (acceptance of delivery)—Can finance reconcile the saved person-hours and increased output? Deliveries that can't be reconciled are all self-indulgence.

Self-sustaining gate (acceptance of enablement)—One month after you leave, can they run a second scenario on their own? Your work is only done when the enterprise can do without you.

Read together, the five gates are the entirety of FDE work: find the pain point, choose the right entry point, build the prototype, do the math, and make it self-sustaining.
