Google Forward Deployed Engineer vs. Customer Engineer: What Is the Difference?

Design Gurus · 2026-11-20 · 4 min read

Summary

This article compares two Google Cloud roles. The Customer Engineer is a pre-sales position that builds demos and proofs of concept to secure the technical win, with a US base of $152,000–$221,000 and a bonus target up to 42.86%. The Forward Deployed Engineer is an engineering role that ships production systems, such as Gemini-based agents and data pipelines, inside customer environments, with a base of $174,000–$252,000 and a 15% bonus target. Interviews differ accordingly: CE loops test product knowledge and presentation, while FDE loops include DSA coding, vibe coding, and agentic system design. Google also hires an AI Outcome Customer Engineer bridging pre-sales and delivery. The author advises choosing based on whether you prefer shaping many deals or owning one customer's production system.

Key points

  • CE is pre-sales; FDE is a delivery engineering role.
  • CE bonus target reaches 42.86%; FDE is 15–20%.
  • FDE base pay is higher: $174,000–$252,000.
  • FDE interviews include DSA, vibe coding, and agentic design.
  • AI Outcome Customer Engineer sits between the two roles.

A Google Cloud customer engineer helps win the sale, and a forward deployed engineer builds the system after the customer commits. A customer engineer (CE) is a technical pre-sales role. The CE learns what a prospective customer needs, builds demos and proofs of concept, and works toward the technical win, which is Google's term for the customer accepting the proposed solution. A forward deployed engineer (FDE) is an engineering role. The FDE works inside the customer's environment, writes and debugs production code, and ships an agent, data pipeline, or AI application that the customer runs every day. Both roles sit in Google Cloud's Go-To-Market organization, but they are measured differently, interviewed differently, and paid differently.

What a Customer Engineer Does

A Google Cloud customer engineer is the technical partner of a sales team. A current Customer Engineer III posting for Cloud AI says the CE will "drive the technical win" for AI workloads. The same posting says the CE writes code to build prototypes, proofs of concept, and demos. The CE presents to technical leaders and executives, answers detailed product questions, and designs the first architecture. Once the customer signs, other teams usually build the production system.

The pay structure reflects this sales focus. That same Customer Engineer III posting lists a US base of 152,000 to 221,000 with a 42.86 percent bonus target. A large variable share is typical for roles that are measured on revenue.

What a Forward Deployed Engineer Does

A Google Cloud FDE takes a working prototype into production. The GenAI FDE postings call the role an "embedded builder" and say the FDE will code, debug, and ship custom agent systems together with the customer. Daily work includes connecting Gemini agents to the customer's APIs and older data systems, building evaluation pipelines, tracing failures across services, and keeping cost and latency inside the customer's budget. FDEs also report repeated problems in Google's products back to the engineering teams.

An FDE III posting lists a US base of 174,000 to 252,000 with a 15 percent bonus target. The base is higher and the bonus share is lower, because the role is measured on delivery rather than on revenue. Google FDE levels and pay lists every level.

The Role Between Them

Google also hires an "AI Outcome Customer Engineer" inside the FDE organization. That posting describes a person who links "pre-sales agreement shaping and post-sales execution." This engineer checks whether a deal is technically feasible, validates requests for FDE help, and leads the first technical design. If you enjoy the early customer conversations but also want to stay close to delivery, this role sits between the two.

How the Interviews Differ

The CE loop focuses on role-related knowledge, which means your knowledge of cloud and AI products, and on how you explain a design to a customer. Candidates often describe a presentation or a customer conversation acted out by the interviewer. Live coding is lighter or absent.

The FDE loop uses the Google software engineer coding bar. It adds a vibe coding round on a loose specification and an agentic system design round. The full sequence is in What is the Google forward deployed engineer interview process like?

How to Choose

Choose the customer engineer role if you enjoy presenting, shaping deals, and designing many first versions across many accounts. Choose the forward deployed engineer role if you want to own one customer's system until it runs in production, and if you still want to write most of the code yourself. Recruiters sometimes describe both roles in similar words, so confirm the exact job title and the coding expectations before the first interview.

How to Prepare

  • For the FDE path, prepare round by round. Grokking the Google Forward Deployed Engineer Interview covers the DSA and vibe coding rounds, agentic system design, and the Google AI stack.
  • Compare FDE with other engineering roles. Forward deployed engineer vs. solutions architect vs. software engineer covers the same choice outside Google.
  • For the CE path, practice explaining designs. Present one architecture to a non-technical listener each week and answer their questions.
  • For the FDE path, prepare for real coding. Grokking the Coding Interview covers the patterns used in the DSA round.
  • For either path, learn customer discovery. Grokking the Forward Deployed Engineer Interview teaches how to turn a vague customer problem into a buildable plan.
