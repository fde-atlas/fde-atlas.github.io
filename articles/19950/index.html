AI-Powered Document Processing | From Purchase Order and Invoice Reading to Core System Integration

Original title: 書類業務のAI化｜注文書・請求書の読み取りから基幹システム連携まで

株式会社Aiworker · 2025-11-30 · 13 min read

Summary

This is a service page by Japanese company AIworker describing its document-processing AI offering. The AI reads orders, invoices, quotes and delivery slips arriving on paper, fax or PDF, cross-checks them against customer and part-number master data, price lists and order records, flags only mismatches for human review, and registers verified results directly into core systems. Reported results include a staffing firm cutting contract-application rejection rates from 62% to 18%, and 100% reading accuracy in a 60-file structural-calculation test. The company claims 100+ corporate clients since July 2024, with pricing from 3 million yen for validation, 10 million yen for full development, and 150,000 yen monthly for maintenance. It stresses that humans keep final approval on payments and that clients should retain staff able to update the checking criteria.

Key points

  • AI reads documents and cross-checks against master data and price lists, flagging only mismatches.
  • Case study: contract application rejections fell from 62% to 18% at a staffing firm.
  • 100% reading accuracy in a 60-file structural calculation validation.
  • Pricing: validation from 3M yen, development from 10M yen, maintenance 150K yen/month.
  • Humans retain payment approval; clients are trained to maintain the criteria tables themselves.

The work that remains after reading

What's happening on the document front lines

In consultations about document processing, we often hear the following. The work that remains is not in reading the text, but in cross-checking the read content against internal data and registering it.

Different formats for each business partner

Since partners decide the format of purchase orders and work notifications, we can't standardize them into a single form.

Retyping PDFs into the core system

Entering product numbers, quantities, and delivery dates one by one; input can't keep up at month-end or during busy periods.

A few errors in prices or product numbers every month

Mistakes are discovered at the invoicing or shipping stage, which then requires back-and-forth with the business partner.

Only one person in accounting can do the checks

The knowledge of where to look for errors lives in one person's head, and processing stops when they're away.

Introduced AI-OCR, but the checking workload hasn't decreased

Even though the text is read, people still handle matching against masters and registration, so work time has barely changed.

More than reading documents, it's the effort of verifying the read content and entering it into the core system that consumes staff time.

We'll sort out together, in 30 minutes, which documents to start with.

Overview of the document AI

We connect everything from receiving documents to registering them in the core system as one flow. People only look at the documents and items that failed the matching check.

Receiving Email, FAX, PDF, paper

Reading Extracting business partners, product numbers, quantities, and amounts

Matching Comparing against masters, order data, and price lists

Staff verification Checking only the mismatched items

Registering to the core system Importing via CSV, API, etc.

Documents with partner-defined formatsFor work notifications and partner-specified forms, it transcribes core system information to create drafts.

Division of labor between AI and staff

For each document type, we design what the AI and programs handle and what judgments remain with staff. Finalizing amounts and approving payments are never left to the AI.

Actual screens

Staff open a single screen with marks on the mismatched items. The original document and the read content are shown side by side, shortening verification time.

  • Load documents Documents arriving by email or FAX are imported, and the business partner and document type are identified.
  • Read items are displayed Boxes appear on the relevant parts of the original document, so you can verify where each item was read from.
  • Mismatched items are marked Prices that differ from the price list or product numbers not in the master get a "needs confirmation" mark with the reason.
  • Verify and register Only the content verified by staff is registered in the core system.

The screens are samples. The items to read, what to match against, and where to register are designed to fit your company's documents and systems.

Development case studies

Company names are withheld; industry, size, and target documents are shown. For projects in development, we note that fact and that results have not yet been measured.

Automatically generating work notifications in each customer's format

ChallengeWork notifications submitted to building management companies before on-site work come in about 100 different formats across about 100 customers, and staff were transcribing 7,000–8,000 of them per year.
Support providedWe are developing an AI system that references core system project data and past work notifications, transcribes the content into the customer's specified format, and creates drafts for staff to verify.
StatusIn development toward an initial release in December 2026; results not yet measured.

Reducing contract application rejection rate from 62% to 18%

ChallengeAbout 60% of contract applications were rejected by accounting, and the effort of checking and rework kept piling up every month.
Support providedWe interviewed accounting about the items they always check and common mistakes, had the AI learn them, and rolled out a process across the company for applicants to verify their own applications before submitting.
ResultsTwo months later, the rejection rate dropped from 62% to 18% (100 out of 160 applications → 28 out of 148).

Reads structural calculation reports and shows the cause of NG results and candidate fixes

ChallengeEvery time a structural calculation returned an NG result, designers had to trace back through the report to find the cause. The less experienced the designer, the longer each case took.
What we didWe are developing an AI that reads structural calculation reports and request sheets, then identifies the NG items, likely causes, and candidate fixes. The designer makes the final decision on the fix.
ResultsIn validation using 10 real cases and 60 files, the reading accuracy for both structural calculation reports and request sheets was 100%. Full operation is scheduled for December 2026.

A case where drawings and specifications were read to create estimate drafts is introduced on the Estimate AI page (time per estimate reduced from 150 minutes to 30 minutes).

Our strengths

Document reading, matching, and verification records are held as a common framework. On top of that, we build the fields to read, the matching criteria, and the registration destinations to fit your company.

AI for reading, programs for verification

Calculations with a single correct answer are not left to the AI

Quantities and amounts read by the AI are verified using master data and price lists according to fixed rules. We also record which document and which part each value was read from, so it can be checked later.

Judgments based on your verification items

The points your accounting and order staff always check become the judgment criteria

Our approach is to interview you about the items that cause rework or inquiries at your company, compile them into a verification criteria table, and then build the system.

Development through registration to core systems

The same person handles everything from matching after reading through registration

From CSV import, APIs, and screen operation automation, we choose and connect a method that complies with your system and information security rules. Post-launch improvements and training staff who can update the criteria table in-house are also within our scope.

What remains with your company after implementation

Our goal is a state where documents can be verified against the same criteria even when staff change. To achieve this, we leave behind not only the system but also the criteria table and trained people.

Document processing system

  • A system connecting reading, matching, and registration
  • A verification screen that flags mismatched items

Judgment criteria table and records

  • A criteria table compiled from the verification items staff used to check
  • Accuracy validation results and measurements of processing time before and after implementation

An internal staff member who can update the criteria table

  • A staff member who can adjust the settings when business partners or document formats increase
  • Operational rules for recording decisions on exceptions

Of the three, the most important is having an internal staff member who can update the criteria table. With such a person in place, the system can continue to be used even when business partners or unit prices change.
