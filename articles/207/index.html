AI Consultancy & Product Development

&Above · 2026 · 5 min read

Summary

&Above, a London-based AI consultancy and product development agency, describes its two service pillars: AI workflows and agents for internal operations, and AI product development for customer-facing products. Engagements start with workflow design, then embedded forward-deployed engineers or cross-functional squads ship systems into production, with IP and capability transferring to the client. Cited results include a Google Cloud agent system saving 5,000 sellers 64% of their time, Tesco's ad approval cycle cut from four weeks to days, and Upstix cutting operating costs 78% with an MVP live in three months and 7x qualified leads.

Key points

  • &Above runs two pillars: internal AI workflows/agents and customer-facing AI product development.
  • Google Cloud's agent system, built by &Above, saves 5,000 sellers 64% of their time.
  • Tesco's retail-ad approval cycle went from four weeks to days; Upstix cut operating costs 78%.
  • Engagements are scoped around a workflow, with accountability for production deployment.
  • Clients own the IP; capability transfers to their teams as work ships.

AI in most businesses is individual. People sat next to each other are building the same prompts, workflows and agents in different tools. Company knowledge lives in people's heads, disparate hubs and disconnected systems, so AI starts from a blank slate every time. And everything built inside one model provider is stuck there. Individuals are getting smarter. The organisation isn't. We turn that into a system your company owns: the processes your business runs every week, built into agents that work from what your business already knows. We don't just place engineers, we deliver working AI.

Every engagement is scoped around a workflow and the result it has to produce, and we're accountable for that workflow running in production. Building in-house is the right instinct, and your workflows and company knowledge are IP you should own, not rent from a third-party platform. The burden is what stops it: hiring is slow, managing pulls your people off the day job, and internal builds are quick to start and slow to finish. We carry both, and capability transfers to your team as the work ships. See our forward-deployed engineers, AI agency and AI agent development services.

We close it with one embedded squad: strategy, design and engineering working together from concept to production and beyond. For Tesco, that meant proving which model caught compliance issues with the highest confidence before building the product around it. Straight answers for teams evaluating an AI consultancy or product partner.

&above is an AI consultancy and product development agency based in London. We work across two pillars: AI Workflows & Agents, automating the processes your team runs every week, and AI Product Development, building the products your customers use. The same embedded model builds both.

Our work splits by what you are buying. AI Workflows & Agents automates the work inside your business: it starts with Workflow Design, then embedded forward-deployed engineers building on Agent Mesh. AI Product Development builds what your customers use: a Proof of Concept, an AI Product Squad to ship it, and a Design Squad to take it to market.

Yes, building is the core of what we do. Our AI Product Squads are embedded cross-functional teams of AI engineers, product managers, designers and strategists who ship AI-native products to your customers, and our AI Workflows & Agents pillar covers the systems your own team uses. See our digital product agency services for how we take products from idea to production.

We ship working products, not slide decks. Clients work directly with senior practitioners in small embedded squads that move at startup speed. Tesco went from four-week ad approval cycles to days, and Google Cloud's agent system is used by 5,000 sellers. You get enterprise-grade delivery without enterprise-consultancy overhead. If you're comparing firms before you shortlist, see our review of the best AI consulting firms in London and the UK, and the questions worth asking in how to choose an AI consultancy.

Building in-house is the right instinct, and it's what we set clients up to do. The difference is the burden: an embedded &above team feels in-house but we hire, manage and take accountability, and capability transfers to your people as we go. You get the owned system without the hiring risk, and without pulling your staff off the day job while an internal build stalls.

Recent work includes Google and Google Cloud, Tesco, Sage, Revolut, Oxford Quantum Circuits, Upstix and YourAvatar, spanning internal AI workflows and customer-facing product builds.

Fast, because the first steps are deliberately small. Workflow Design maps where AI pays back before anything is built, and a Proof of Concept validates a product idea before major investment. Build engagements are iterative, shipping working software from the first sprints.

Start with Workflow Design. We discover and define the core workflows your business runs on, and help you decide what to build, what to buy, and how to scale it in-house. If it shows something worth building, we scope the build around it with success measures agreed upfront.
