What Is OpenAI's Forward Deployed Engineer Salary? Comparing 272 Job Listings Across 59 Companies Including Anthropic and Palantir (September 2026)

Original title: OpenAIのFDE年収は?Anthropic・Palantirなど59社の求人票272件で比べる(2026年9月)

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-09-30 · 25 min read

Summary

Drawing on 522 FDE job postings scraped from 132 companies' public job boards on September 30, 2026, this article analyzes the 272 postings (59 companies) that disclose compensation. The median of company-level salary ranges is $177.5K–$249K. OpenAI's San Francisco FDE role lists $185K–$300K base pay plus equity, Anthropic's US FDE lists $280K–$320K, and Palantir's New York FDSE lists $135K–$200K. The article breaks down ranges by seniority, role type, and location, and cautions that ranges may mean base salary only or OTE including incentives. Notably, among 16 Japan-based FDE postings, only Palantir's two US-government-related roles disclosed pay.

Key points

  • Median company-level FDE salary range across 59 companies: $177.5K–$249K.
  • OpenAI SF FDE: $185K–$300K base; Anthropic: $280K–$320K; Palantir NY: $135K–$200K.
  • Seniority matters: new-grad median $127.5K–$147.5K vs. manager median $225.9K–$275K.
  • Ranges vary in meaning: base only (Coinbase, OpenAI) vs. OTE with incentives (Notion).
  • Only 2 of 16 Japan-based FDE postings (Palantir, US government work) disclosed pay.

Key Takeaways

  • 01 As of September 30, 2026, OpenAI's FDE job posting for San Francisco lists an annual salary of $185,000–$300,000 (approximately ¥29.13M–¥47.24M) plus equity grants.
  • 02 Among FDE job postings with compensation information, the 253 postings (56 companies) listing annual amounts in US dollars show a median salary range of $177,500 (lower bound) to $249,000 (upper bound) when aggregated by company.
  • 03 The median salary ranges aggregated by company differ greatly by role: $127,500–$147,500 for new-graduate postings and $225,900–$275,000 for managerial postings.
  • 04 The salary ranges in FDE postings vary in what they include: Coinbase lists base salary excluding equity and bonus, while Notion's US postings list OTE including incentives.
  • 05 Of the 16 FDE postings for Japan-based or Japan-focused roles found on the public job boards of the 132 companies observed, only 2 from Palantir, for US government work, included compensation information.

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) salaries are often reported in media articles and aggregation sites as "tens of millions of yen per year." However, in many cases it is not stated whether those figures are base salary, whether they include equity or incentives, or which company and role they refer to.

In this article, of the 522 FDE job postings the editorial team collected from the public job boards of 132 companies on September 30, 2026, we aggregate the 272 postings (59 companies) that include compensation information and present the salary ranges written in each company's job posting—including OpenAI, Anthropic, Palantir, and Databricks—broken down by role, location, and type of compensation. For an overview of FDE salaries, see How Much Do FDEs Earn? (Overview).

Summary

From the 272 FDE postings (59 companies) with compensation information collected on September 30, 2026, we found the following.

  • The median of salary ranges aggregated by company is $177,500 at the lower end and $249,000 at the upper end. The median per posting is skewed by Databricks' single value, so we look at it on a per-company basis
  • OpenAIのFDE(サンフランシスコ)は18.5万〜30万ドル、Anthropicは28万〜32万ドル、Palantirは13.5万〜20万ドル[1][2][3]
  • 職位で差が大きく、会社ごとの中央値は新卒で12.75万〜14.75万ドル、管理職で22.59万〜27.5万ドル。例としてPalantirの新卒は13.5万〜14.5万ドル、Anthropicの管理職は32万〜40.5万ドル[5][7]
  • Even at the same company, the range changes by job type (Strategist vs. FDE) and location (Zone, city)
  • The ranges mix base salary, OTE, and unspecified types. Verify the breakdown before comparing
  • 外資系企業の東京のFDE求人は、米国政府向けのPalantirの2件を除いて金額が書かれていない[14][27]。日本企業の求人は円のレンジを示していることが多い[32]

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat is OpenAI's FDE annual salary?

As of September 30, 2026, OpenAI's job posting for a Forward Deployed Engineer in San Francisco lists an annual salary of $185,000–$300,000 (approximately ¥29.13M–¥47.24M at $1 = ¥157.46) plus equity grants. The job page states that this range is the base salary, with equity provided separately and, for eligible roles, a performance-based bonus added. The job posting for a Forward Deployed Engineer in Tokyo does not list any amounts.

QWhat is Anthropic's FDE annual salary?

As of September 30, 2026, Anthropic's job postings list Forward Deployed Engineer salaries of $280K–$320K in the US (New York, San Francisco, Seattle) and £225,000–£255,000 in London. The managerial role, Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering (New York), pays $320K–$405K.

QWhat is Palantir's FDE annual salary?

As of September 30, 2026, Palantir's job postings list Forward Deployed Software Engineer salaries of $135K–$200K in New York, and $135K–$145K for new graduates. Both are described as expected salary, with total compensation potentially including equity (RSUs) and signing bonuses.

QAre the FDE salary ranges in job postings base salary?

It depends on the company. Coinbase, OpenAI, and Deepgram list base salary; Notion's US postings list OTE combining base salary and target incentive; Palantir lists expected salary excluding equity and other items. Some postings, such as Exa's and Cartesia's, do not specify the type of range, so you need to check the breakdown before comparing.

QCan job postings tell you the annual salary of FDEs working in Japan?

As of September 30, 2026, none of the foreign companies' Tokyo FDE postings—OpenAI, Databricks, Notion, or Cohere—list amounts. Among Japanese companies, some postings show annual salaries in yen, such as SoftBank (estimated theoretical annual salary of approximately ¥8.12M–¥20.35M) and Money Forward (approximately ¥7.91M–¥15M per year).
