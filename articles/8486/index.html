Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (1/5, Apr 6, 2018): Be ready to be on your own

Taro · 2018-04-06 · 2 min read

Summary

A former Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (1 year in New York, reviewed April 2018) rates the company 1.0 and does not recommend it. The review praises high pay and generous benefits, but says the tradeoff is an expectation of around-the-clock availability, almost no onboarding direction, and a culture where asking for help is seen as weakness. The reviewer claims 70+ hour weeks and describes staff as treated like code monkeys rather than people. The page also collects interview experiences: a 2016 phone screen plus NYC onsite, a 2017 coding challenge where finishing a working solution mattered more than optimal complexity, and a 2022 process (HR call, online assessment, Karat) where a candidate passed but was told the role was filled.

Key points

  • Former FDSE rates Palantir 1.0, doesn't recommend
  • Great pay and benefits, but 24/7 work expectations
  • Little direction; asking for help seen as weakness
  • Reviewer claims 70+ hour workweeks
  • Page includes interview experiences from 2016-2022

Pros

Salary is great, benefits are amazing, and they pretty much pay for everything.

Cons

The counterpart to having everything paid for you is that Palantir acts as if they own you and expect you to be working 24/7.

Also, very little direction is given, and you're expected to figure things out by yourself from the get-go. And god forbid you ask for help, as you will be seen as weak and inefficient.

It's a soul-crushing place that does not see workers as humans but merely as code monkeys.

Advice to Management

Stop acting as if you're the best. If you were, you would not require devs to work 70+ hours and shame them for wanting to have a life and not just a job.
