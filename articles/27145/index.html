Summary

This page presents key excerpts from Wonderful's Forward Deployed Engineer job description, published by Shenzhen-based 真实智元 and captured on 2026-11-02. The role centers on converting complex enterprise workflows into AI agents that work in production. Engineers lead projects end to end, from discovery through deployment, and are expected to have strong foundations in software architecture, system design, and production engineering, including API integration, evals, observability, and turning deployment lessons into reusable tools and best practices. The excerpts are for research comparison only and do not constitute a hiring commitment.

Key points

  • Wonderful's FDE role turns enterprise workflows into production AI agents.
  • Engineers own projects from initial discovery through production deployment.
  • The JD stresses software architecture, system design, and production engineering.
