Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer Interview (No Offer): The process began with a 45-minute

Taro · 2016-04-01 · 3 min read

Summary

A candidate shares a Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer interview experience in New York (no offer): a 45-minute phone interview on HackerRank, followed by an on-site in the Meatpacking District with whiteboard coding and an open-ended system design round. Aggregated data from 36 interview experiences shows a 14% pass rate, with 56% positive sentiment. Employee reviews praise the salary, talent, and exposure to high-impact problems, but note long hours, limited depth, and an unstructured growth path.

Key points

  • 45-minute HackerRank phone screen, then NYC on-site
  • On-site rounds were whiteboard-based plus open-ended system design
  • Only 14% pass rate across 36 reported interview experiences
  • 56% of candidates rated the process positively
  • Reviews: great pay and talent, but long hours and unclear growth paths

Full text

April 1, 2016

Positive ExperienceNo Offer

The process began with a 45-minute phone interview, followed by an on-site interview in NYC.

The company's offices in NYC are located in the Meatpacking District and seemed nice.

The phone interview was conducted on HackerRank, which allowed my code to compile and display output – a very positive experience.

The on-site interviews were conducted entirely on a whiteboard. I also participated in a system design interview, which was open-ended and quite enjoyable.

Q: How can you tell if a Linked List is a palindrome?

Q: Given multiple sets, how can I dedupe unique entries?
