Forward Deployed Engineer · AI agents in production

Dinacode · 2026 · 4 min read

Summary

Dinacode, a Spain-based firm, describes its Forward Deployed Engineer offering: engineers embed within client teams, map real workflows, and build AI agents that handle entire processes in production. Deliverables include system integrations, eval suites on real cases, guardrails, human approval points, and per-action audit trails. The page cites an example freeing 312 hours monthly with 94% case coverage, outlines a three-phase method (workflow audit, agent build, measured iteration), states timelines of a 2-week audit and 4-8 weeks to first production agent at fixed per-sprint cost, and notes clients own all code, prompts, and integrations. It also lists its stack: frontier and open-weight models, LangGraph, MCP, RAG, and Kubernetes.

Key points

  • FDEs embed in client teams and build agents for full workflows in production.
  • Work includes integrations, evals, guardrails, approvals, and audit trails.
  • Example result: 312 hours freed monthly, 94% of cases covered.
  • Audit in 2 weeks; first agent in production in 4-8 weeks, fixed per-sprint cost.
  • Clients keep all IP: code, prompts, evals, and integrations.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What people ask before we start

Engineer profile, working model, intellectual property and delivery timelines.

  • What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

    An engineer who works inside your team: learns the real process, decides the architecture and builds the agent that solves the workflow in production. They speak both languages, business and engineering, and answer for the result on your systems.

  • Do you work on site or remotely?

    Both models. On-site sessions for the mapping and the milestones, and the day-to-day inside your channels and rituals. As if we were in the office next door.

  • How long until the first agent is in production?

    The audit closes in 2 weeks with the chosen case and its metric. The first agent goes into production in 4 to 8 weeks, depending on integrations. Fixed cost per sprint.

  • Who owns the code, the prompts and the integrations?

    The client. NDA before the first technical session, and all the IP —code, prompts, evals and integrations— stays in your repository.

  • What happens when the agent hits a case it does not cover?

    It escalates to the person in charge with full context and the case enters the eval suite. Every exception that shows up widens the coverage of the next iteration.
