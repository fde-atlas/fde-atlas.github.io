RTS Labs vs DreamzTech vs Intuz: 11 Best AI Integration Companies in the USA (2026)

Top 11 · 2026-11-10 · 18 min read

Summary

This ranking of ten US AI integration firms judges transparency: whether a vendor names the specific CRM and ERP systems it connects, publishes a timeline, and shows pricing before the first call. RTS Labs ranks first for mid-sized firms (roughly 50–1,000 employees), naming Salesforce, SAP, NetSuite and Snowflake with a 12-week plan and fixed pre-build pricing. DreamzTech prints tiered prices from $15,000 to $300,000+, and Intuz names the most industry systems (Epic, Procore, Clio, Yardi). Only three of ten firms publish prices. Beyond Elevation appears as an unscored eleventh wildcard due to common ownership with the publisher; its founder, Hayat Amin, works as a forward deployed engineer.

Buyer's guide6 questions

What is the best AI integration company in the USA?

On this page, RTS Labs for a company of roughly 50 to 1,000 people: its integration page names Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics 365, SAP, Oracle and NetSuite, sets out a 12 week plan with a go or no go call at week 2, and fixes the price before any code. DreamzTech if you want a printed price first. Intuz if you run an industry system such as Epic, Procore, Clio or Yardi.

How much does AI integration cost?

From the firms' own pages, read 10 November 2026: DreamzTech prints $15,000 to $30,000 for a single system MVP, $50,000 to $150,000 for a multi system production integration and $150,000 to $300,000+ for an enterprise platform. SoluLab starts at about $10,000 to $15,000 for one system. ScienceSoft prices an AI workflow or agent at $50,000 to $150,000+. Seven of the ten print nothing.

AI integration company or forward deployed engineer?

An integration company is the right buy when the job is scoped: these five systems, this connector, this date. A forward deployed engineer is the right buy when nobody yet knows which systems disagree or why, and you want one senior person inside the business who finds the break and builds the fix.

Why do AI pilots fail on real data?

The demo runs on a clean extract. Production meets the CRM, the ERP and the spreadsheet nobody owns, with different customer IDs in each. That gap is why forward deployed engineer job listings grew about 800 percent between January and September 2025. Ask every firm here who maps the IDs across your systems, and in which week.

Where do the engineers sit?

Ask. RTS Labs says its employees are 100 percent US based. Azumo builds mainly from Argentina in US time zones. DreamzTech, Intuz, Simform, SoluLab and Krazimo list offices in India on Clutch alongside their US ones. None of that is a problem if you know it before you sign.

What should I fix before I hire anyone?

Write down the systems by name, the field that should match across them, such as the customer ID, and who retypes data between them today. A firm that cannot price that list on the first call is selling discovery, not delivery.

How to choose

  • 1This week, list every system the process touches by name: the CRM, the ERP or accounting system, the data warehouse, the spreadsheets. Next to each, write who types data into it and who reads it. That one page is the brief every firm here will price against.
  • 2Pick one record that should be the same everywhere, usually the customer or the order, and check it across three systems. If the IDs or the totals disagree, the first job is the join, not the AI, and no model will fix it.
  • 3Ask each firm three questions: which of my systems have you connected before, who writes the code and where do they sit, and is the price fixed before build? RTS Labs answers the third in writing. DreamzTech, ScienceSoft and SoluLab print a range.
  • 4Get the price in writing before discovery. Printed bands on this page run from about $10,000 for one system at SoluLab to $300,000+ for an enterprise platform at DreamzTech. Seven of the ten print nothing, so make them name a figure.
  • 5Check proof the firm does not control: Clutch review counts, named clients tied to named work, partner designations in the partner's own words. Treat any outcome percentage without a named client as a vendor claim.
  • 6Make sure your company owns the connectors, the code, the accounts and the model keys from day one, in the contract.
  • 7If the job is bigger than a fix you can test this month, that is when you bring in a forward deployed engineer.
