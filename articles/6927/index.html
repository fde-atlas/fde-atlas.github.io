AI Engineer, Forward Deployed

Careers at Secure Engage · 2026-06-11 · 4 min read

Summary

Secure Engage, an AI platform for cybersecurity and compliance, is hiring two AI Engineers on its forward-deployed team: one senior and one junior, remote or hybrid across Canada/US. Engineers work directly with customers to build AI workflows for compliance assessments, evidence mapping, risk flagging, and reporting against frameworks like OSFI B-10, OCC, and SOC2. The role involves integrating LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral), RAG, prompt engineering, fine-tuning, and human-in-the-loop review. The senior role requires 5+ years in software with 2+ years in applied AI/ML; the junior role accepts 1-2 years including internships. The page also lists other openings, including a Sales Engineer, COO, CMO, and GTM lead.

Key points

  • Focus: AI workflows for compliance assessments, evidence mapping, and risk flagging

Careers

AI Engineer – Forward Deployed (Senior & Junior Roles Available)

Location

Hybrid or Remote (Secure Engage – Canada/US)

Type

Full Time

About the Role

  • Secure Engage is building a category-defining AI platform for cybersecurity and compliance.
  • We’re looking for two AI Engineers (1 Senior, 1 Junior) to join our forward-deployed engineering team—working directly with customers, product, and internal stakeholders to bring powerful AI workflows to life.
  • These roles are ideal for engineers who want to work on meaningful, real-world applications of AI in a high-trust domain, where performance, transparency, and reliability matter.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and maintain AI-powered tools and workflows for compliance assessments, evidence mapping, risk flagging, and reporting
  • Work with customers and internal teams to deploy and adapt AI solutions to specific use cases (e.g., OSFI B-10, OCC, SOC2)
  • Integrate large language model APIs (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral) with structured data and proprietary workflows
  • Collaborate with QA and Product to test model outputs, build redaction pipelines, and enable human-in-the-loop review
  • Assist in the development of fine-tuning strategies, prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and performance monitoring
  • Communicate technical insights to non-technical stakeholders and provide training where needed

Senior Role – Required Skills & Experience

  • 5+ years of experience in software engineering with 2+ years in applied AI/ML (preferably LLMs)
  • Strong proficiency in Python, APIs, and ML libraries (e.g., LangChain, OpenAI SDK, Hugging Face)
  • Experience deploying LLMs into production or compliance-sensitive environments
  • Solid understanding of prompt design, vector databases, document parsing, and privacy constraints
  • Experience working in fast-paced startups, especially in regulated or enterprise domains
  • Strong problem-solving, architectural thinking, and documentation skills

Junior Role – Required Skills & Experience

  • 1–2 years of experience in software development or AI/ML research (internships and academic projects acceptable)
  • Exposure to LLMs, prompt engineering, or ML tooling (e.g., OpenAI, Pinecone, Haystack, LangChain)
  • Strong curiosity and willingness to learn about cybersecurity, compliance, and enterprise workflows
  • Comfortable writing Python scripts and working with APIs and structured data
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills; coachable and hungry to grow

What We Offer

  • Remote-first flexibility with collaborative support from senior team members
  • Competitive compensation with equity participation
  • Hands-on exposure to cutting-edge AI in a mission-critical domain
  • Opportunities for growth, mentorship, and advancement into product or platform roles
  • A purpose-driven, transparent, and agile work environment
