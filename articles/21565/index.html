Summary

DeFactory analyzed 7,408 freelance engineer listings received between June 9 and October 19, 2026, of which 4,926 included rates, ranking 22 skills by average monthly rate. The overall average was ¥789,000 (median ¥750,000). SAP topped the list at ¥1.063 million, followed by RAG, data platforms, Databricks, LLM, and AI agent roles; COBOL, .NET/C#, and Java ranked lowest. Listings with upstream/lead keywords in the title averaged ¥916,000 versus ¥667,000 for operations/maintenance/testing roles. Listings mentioning multiple AI keywords (RAG, LLM, generative AI, AI agents) paid progressively more, reaching ¥1.001 million with three keywords. The article recommends building on Python or TypeScript plus AWS, then adding AI or advanced cloud skills.

Key points

  • Overall average was ¥789,000/month across 4,926 rated listings from 7,408 total.
  • SAP ranked highest at ¥1.063 million; RAG, LLM, and AI agent skills followed.
  • Upstream/lead roles averaged ¥916,000 vs ¥667,000 for ops/maintenance/testing.
  • Listings with three overlapping AI keywords averaged ¥1.001 million.
  • Recommended path: Python or TypeScript plus AWS, then AI or cloud skills.
