Tencent Cloud FDE: Full Process from Registration to Certification: 6 Steps, 7 Bottlenecks, 3 Deadlines

Original title: 腾讯云 FDE 报名到拿证全流程：6 个步骤、7 个卡点、3 个死线

FDE 指南 · 2026-11-01 · 6 min read

Summary

This guide walks through the full registration-to-certificate process for Tencent Cloud's ADP Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) certification, based on official pages as of November 2026. Key facts: the exam costs ¥1200 and one purchase includes only one attempt—no-shows count; orders expire after one year; refunds are only available within five days of purchase and before booking; rescheduling or cancellation is free only until 24 hours before the exam; certificates last two years from the score release date. The author debunks a circulating claim of three attempts (that applies to practitioner-level certifications) and recommends checking exam seat availability before paying.

Key points

  • One purchase covers one exam attempt only; missing the exam forfeits it.
  • Refunds only within 5 days of purchase and before booking a seat.
  • Orders expire after 1 year; changes or cancellations blocked within 24 hours of the exam.
  • Offline exam: 90 minutes, 60 questions, 70 to pass; multiple-choice has no partial credit.
  • Certificate is valid 2 years from score release; renewal means repurchasing the exam.

Let's get three things straight first

This article is about registering for the Tencent Cloud ADP FDE certification. If you haven't decided which one to take yet, spend two minutes on the comparison of the four FDE certifications — different certifying bodies have completely different registration portals, fees, and rules, so don't go through the wrong door.

The official registration process for the Tencent Cloud ADP Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) is broken into three steps: purchase, scheduling, and exam. It sounds simple, but three facts determine how you must plan:

ItemOfficial policy
Fee¥1200 (certification exam fee; the accompanying online course is free for a limited time)
Exam attempts included per registration1 attempt; if you fail or miss the exam, you must purchase again, and no free retake is supported
Order validity1 year; expired orders become invalid and cannot be scheduled
Refund windowWithin 5 days of purchase, and only if you have not yet scheduled
Rescheduling / canceling a bookingFree if done more than 24 hours before the theory exam; not supported within that window
Certificate validity2 years from the date results are released; after expiry, you must purchase the exam again

If you overlook any one of these six points, the cost is another ¥1200. Below, everything is laid out in the order you'll actually go through it.

Step 1: Purchase

The prerequisite is a Tencent Cloud account with personal real-name verification completed. The official registration guide notes that this process applies to individual primary accounts (except for practitioner-type certifications).

The entry point is the Tencent Cloud Training and Certification Center: find the FDE certification card and click into it — that's this detail page.

The button on the detail page changes based on your account status: if you haven't purchased, it shows the purchase entry; if you've purchased but haven't scheduled, it shows "Schedule Exam". So if you open the page and see "Schedule Exam", it means this exam is already in your order and you don't need to buy it again.

The purchase steps are: fill in your personal information → check the box agreeing to the "Tencent Cloud Certification Exam Service Terms" → place the order → pay. Once payment is done, registration is complete.

If you have an exam code applicable to this exam, the order page has an "Exam Code Redemption" entry, so you can redeem it directly without paying cash.

One registration includes only one exam attempt; free retakes are not supported.

This is the official wording verbatim. It means two things: if you fail, you must buy again, and if you register but don't show up (miss the exam), you must also buy again. Factor the cost of taking time off into your budget, not just the registration fee.

By the way, let's clear up a rumor circulating online: FDE is not "3 attempts"

If you search "Tencent Cloud FDE exam attempts", you'll find articles saying "one purchase includes 3 exam attempts". That claim isn't made up, but it refers to a different category of certification.

Tencent Cloud training and certification registration rules are split into two sets of documents by product line:

DocumentWhich certifications it coversExam attempts
"Individual Registration and Exam Guide"Specialized technical certifications; FDE falls into this category1 attempt, no free retake
"Practitioner Certification Registration and Exam Guide"Tencent Cloud Practitioner, Tencent Cloud AI Practitioner, WorkBuddy OPC Practitioner3 attempts

The first document states its scope at the beginning as "(excluding practitioner-type certifications)" — and the excluded category is exactly what the second document covers. The two documents are complementary, not contradictory.

So: FDE is 1 attempt; practitioner certifications are 3. Budgeting for FDE based on "3 attempts" means miscalculating by ¥2400.

Step 2: Scheduling

Buying doesn't mean you can take the exam — there's still scheduling in between. The entry point is Personal Center - Order Management.

The process is: select the subject → click "Schedule Exam" → read the scheduling rules and check the personal data privacy disclosure → click "Schedule at Test Center" → select the exam city → select the test center, date, and time → fill in your ID number, name, phone number, and email → complete the booking.

After booking, you'll receive SMS and email notifications. The exam day time, test center address, and ID requirements can all be reviewed again via Personal Center - Certification Exams by clicking "Go to Exam".

Don't put off this step. Official wording:

Certification exam product orders are valid for 1 year; after expiry, the order becomes invalid and exam scheduling is no longer possible.

The system will remind you via in-site messages, email, and SMS 90, 60, 30, and 15 days before the order expires. But there's a prerequisite — your contact email and phone must be bound and verified. Many people fill these in casually at registration, and the reminders never reach them. After purchasing, go confirm both contact methods work.

Step 3: Rescheduling, cancellation, and refunds — three things with completely different deadlines

This is the most confusing part; the official policy gives three different deadlines that don't overlap.

What you want to doDeadlineWhere to do itCost
Reschedule (postpone the exam)More than 24 hours before the theory examPersonal Center - Certification ExamsFree
Cancel a bookingMore than 24 hours before the theory examPersonal Center - Certification ExamsFree; the order remains
RefundWithin 5 days of purchase and not yet scheduledPersonal Center - Order ManagementNo refund after the deadline

Two key points:

Canceling a booking is not the same as a refund. Canceling only releases the exam slot you booked; the ¥1200 stays in your order and can be rescheduled. The only window for an actual refund is "within 5 days of purchase and never scheduled".

The 24-hour mark is a hard deadline, not an approximation. Within 24 hours of the theory exam, neither rescheduling nor cancellation is supported. If you're already in the test center city and something comes up, that money is gone.

(The official policy for "hands-on exams" is 72 hours. FDE currently only has the theory exam, so 24 hours is all you need.)

Step 4: Exam day

The exam itself: in-person computer-based test, 90 minutes, 60 questions — 40 single-choice + 20 multiple-choice, passing at 70 points (out of 100). Single-choice questions are worth 1.5 points each, multiple-choice 2 points each.

For multiple-choice questions, both wrong selections and missing selections score zero — no partial credit. The 20 multiple-choice questions are worth 40 points, making them the biggest point-loss risk in this exam. For details on how to allocate your time, see the module-by-module breakdown of the exam syllabus.

Step 5: Checking results and getting your certificate

The two official pages don't say exactly the same thing, so here's the honest picture:

  • The "Individual Registration and Exam Guide" states: 30 minutes after the theory exam ends, you can check your results and certificate in Personal Center - Certification Exams
  • The "Results Query" section of the certification detail page states: theory exam results are released within 3 days

The two statements don't conflict — you can read it as "as fast as half an hour, and guaranteed within 3 days at the latest". In practice, go by what's shown in the Personal Center — don't wait at the test center door after the exam; just check the Personal Center when you get home. If you pass, the certificate is on the same page.

Step 6: When does the 2-year validity start counting?

Official wording:

The certificate is valid for 2 years from the date the most recent passing exam result is released; if you need to hold a valid certificate, please re-register for the exam before or shortly after the certificate is about to expire.

Note that the starting point is the result release date, not the registration date or the exam date. The three can differ by several months — especially if you buy and then wait over half a year to take the exam.

Summary of the seven pitfalls

PitfallConsequence
1. One registration includes only one exam attempt, and missing the exam counts as usedAnother ¥1200
2. Refunds are only available within 5 days of purchase, and only if you haven't booked an exam yetOnce you book, the money is locked in
3. Orders are valid for only 1 yearAfter expiration they become void — no refund, and you can't take the exam
4. Exam seats are offline and must be checked in real time; they're not always availableIf you can't get a slot, you'll have to coordinate or wait for the next session
5. Within 24 hours before the exam, you can neither reschedule nor cancelYou lose both the time and the money
6. There is no "renewal"; once it expires, you buy and take the exam againOnce every 2 years, ¥1200
7. The two official pages state different result release times (30 minutes / 3 days)Don't schedule anything afterward based on the shorter one

An order that won't cost you money

Working backward from the deadlines above, the safer approach is:

  • Check exam seats before paying. The details page has an "offline exam seat real-time query" entry — first confirm that your target city has availability in your desired time window. Because after payment you only have 5 days to get a refund, and once you book, no refund is possible. If you can't find a suitable seat, on weekdays you can call 400-613-7050 or email chinareg@prometric.com to coordinate.
  • Book your exam the same day you pay. The 1-year order validity in practice means "the longer you wait, the easier it is to forget." Once you have a date, your prep has a countdown.
  • Immediately after booking, confirm that your email and phone can receive notifications. Reschedule reminders, exam venue info, and order expiration reminders all depend on these two channels.
  • Put the 24-hours-before-exam point on your calendar. That's your last chance to reschedule for free.
  • Check your results in the personal center 30 minutes after the exam. If you pass, save a copy of the certificate — you'll need to retake the exam in 2 years, and you don't want to be unable to find when you last passed.
