What is FDX? From AI implementation to AX completion.

Original title: AI導入から、AX完了へ。

FDX株式会社 · 2026-05-15 · 19 min read

Summary

This is a corporate overview page for FDX Inc. (Forward Deployed Transformation), a Japanese company founded in 2013 that argues AI adoption alone is not transformation. It defines five conditions for "AX completion": changed business processes, AI doing ongoing work, employees who can improve workflows themselves, measurable outcomes, and the ability to continue transformation without external vendors. FDX offers integrated training, expert staffing, business process redesign, and operations, citing over 30,000 AI reskilling trainees and more than 150 client companies. Its philosophy extends the Forward Deployed Engineer model from Palantir and OpenAI to all job functions. A featured case, Shin Tairiku, achieved AX in six non-engineer departments and reported operating profit tripling from FY2023 to FY2025.

Key points

  • FDX defines five conditions for completing AX (AI Transformation).
  • Over 30,000 trainees and 150+ client companies across industries.
  • Philosophy extends Palantir/OpenAI's FDE model to all job roles.
  • Client Shin Tairiku saw operating profit triple from FY2023 to FY2025.
  • FDX tests AI on its own operations, like AI-run phone reception.

AX Completion Check

Operations, AI, people, results, and the ability to sustain transformation. Using the 5 conditions of AX completion as a guide, you can start by checking where your company stands today.

Our Worldview

It is not about which AI you adopted.

AI's evolution is rapidly shrinking the gap in technology available to companies. What will differentiate corporate competitiveness going forward is how much AI you deploy into operations, how you redesign the way people and AI work together, and whether you can continuously produce results. Yet in many companies, training, PoCs, and system implementations proceed in silos, and AI never reaches the point of actually working in the field.

There is no value in simply introducing AI. The value lies in how AI transforms work, people, and companies.

The remaining middle-layer operations.

In the AI era, the smile curve structure of corporate activities will change dramatically. Manual work in the middle layer (production, development, operations, tasks) will be compressed by AI, and value will concentrate in the upstream (strategy, design) and downstream (customer touchpoints, improvement). The middle layer will not disappear entirely. Routine tasks will be absorbed into the upstream and downstream and become thinner, while high-value-added areas remain, such as quality assurance, complex exception handling, governance, production operations responsibility, and on-site adoption.

What We Reject

What FDX confronts is not other AI companies. It is the mindset of considering an initiative complete the moment it is introduced, even if the work and results have not changed. FDX calls this "AI implementation completion-ism."

  • AI tools have been introduced — this does not mean AI implementation is complete
  • AI training has been conducted — this does not mean talent development is complete
  • A PoC has been conducted — this does not mean AI transformation is complete
  • A system has been delivered — this does not mean the project is complete
  • An AI specialist department has been established — this does not mean AI promotion is complete

If the work and results have not changed, it is merely implemented, not transformed.

  • Ending with training

From "implementing" AI to "realizing" transformation. FDX's goal is the realization of sustainable transformation.

7 Beliefs

FDX's services and communications are based on the following seven beliefs.

  • BELIEF 01 AI is not the goal. It is a means to produce results. We do not evaluate by the number of models or tools implemented. We evaluate by time savings, quality improvement, revenue growth, cost reduction, speed of decision-making, and customer experience.
  • BELIEF 02 AI evolves from "something you use" to "something that works." Beyond simply asking questions in chat, AI continuously handles research, analysis, document creation, CRM input, inquiry response, and knowledge updates. From a company that uses AI to a company where AI works.
  • BELIEF 03 Training and implementation must not be separated. Learn, build, run in the field, improve. We design these four as one continuous process. Training is not only a place to gain knowledge; it is also the entry point for business transformation. Learn → Build → Deploy → Improve
  • BELIEF 04 AI is not merely a technology for reducing headcount; it is a technology for evolving people. We redesign the roles of AI and people so that people can move to higher-value work. Human Upgrade
  • BELIEF 05 Transformation capability should remain inside the company. What ultimately remains within our clients is talent, AI workflows, data, knowledge, operational methods, and the ability to keep improving. Not a company that performs transformation on your behalf, but a company that builds companies capable of transforming themselves.
  • BELIEF 06 AI implementation should start from business operations, not from technology. Before asking what the latest AI can do, we look at where time is being spent, where work depends on specific individuals, and where the bottlenecks are.
  • BELIEF 07 Start small, and expand from results. Select a business process, build small, test with real data, review KPIs, and improve. Standardize what produces results and roll it out across the organization.

AX Completion

The five conditions for AX completion.

AI actually handles the work, the way people and AI work changes, business results are produced, and the company can continue the transformation on its own. FDX defines the state where all five conditions are met as the completion of AX (AI Transformation).

  • 01 WORK Business processes have changed The actual business processes have changed. Mechanism that realizes it: Business OS
  • 02 AI AI is working AI continuously handles part of the business operations. Mechanism that realizes it: Business OS
  • 03 PEOPLE People have evolved Employees can use AI and improve business processes themselves. Mechanism that realizes it: FDX Training
  • 04 OUTCOME Results are being produced Results such as time, quality, revenue, and cost improvements are being generated. Mechanism that realizes it: AXceed
  • 05 CAPABILITY Transformation can be sustained The company can continue improving internally, even without external vendors. Mechanism that realizes it: CompanyBrain

Not implementing AI, but implementing within the company "the capability to keep transforming with AI." This is what FDX means by AX.

AX Factory | Operating Model

What defines FDX is "a company that implements within enterprises the capability to keep transforming with AI." The method for realizing this is the AX Factory, and the goal is AX completion. In the AX Factory, AI education, AI workflows, AI agents, business templates, and operational design are embedded for each role and business process in a form that can be used continuously in-house, creating a state where AX can be mass-produced internally.

Defining formula

FDX = Education × Solution × AX Factory

  • Education: Develop employees within client companies who can transform business operations with AI
  • Solution: Embed the company's intelligence foundation CompanyBrain and the Business OS (MarketingOS / SalesOS / ProjectOS) that drives business operations, in a form that can be used continuously in-house
  • AX Factory: Building a framework into the company so that customers can continuously design, implement, and improve transformation in-house

We proceed in the order of Assessment → Build → Enable → Optimize. Training and implementation are not separated; we start small and expand from what delivers results. When starting with training, we begin with "Enable" and proceed in parallel with building. The entry point can be anywhere, but the goal state is the same.

  • Phase 1 Assessment Purpose: Determine the application areas and priorities for AX, and quantify the current state. Deliverables: Business task list, workload map, AI adoption priorities, ROI hypotheses
  • Phase 2 Build Purpose: Redesign business processes with AI as the premise, and implement the intelligence foundation and business OS. Deliverables: Business design, CompanyBrain, Business OS, AI workflows and agents, integration design
  • Phase 3 Enable Purpose: Bring the organization to a state where frontline teams master the framework and can operate it themselves. Deliverables: Role-based curricula, operating rules, FAQs, usage reports
  • Phase 4 Optimize Purpose: Measure results, continuously improve the framework and talent, and roll out across the company. Deliverables: KPI reports, improvement plans, rollout models, internal CoE design

Human Upgrade

What AI compresses is mid-tier routine work. FDX redesigns the roles of AI and people, supporting the shift of people toward higher-value work.

  • Evolution of jobs: We extract routine tasks that AI can handle, shifting human work toward judgment, exception handling, and quality assurance.
  • Building organizational capability: We develop employees who can change business processes using AI, for each role, creating an organization where improvement is not concentrated in a few specialists.
  • Shifting to high-value work: Freed-up time is redirected toward governance, accountability for production operations, support for frontline adoption, and customer touchpoints.

Roots | From FDE to FDX

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) has evolved as a role that enters the customer's frontline and turns technology into business results. What resonated with FDX was the philosophy of not stopping at building technology, but deploying it until results appear in the field. The FDX philosophy came first, and it aligned deeply with the FDE way of thinking.

FDE is a role and organizational model that Palantir systematized in the 2010s and that gained sudden attention when OpenAI organized it as the "OpenAI Deployment Company" in May 2026. It refers to "engineers embedded at customer sites who turn AI and software into business results."

Palantir's FDE

Converts natural language requests into operations on Foundry, performing data transformation, code repository management, Ontology construction, and more. Recently, the implementation work itself that human FDEs do is increasingly being turned into AI agents.

Breaking down the definition of FDE yields the following five actions.

  • Enter the field: See challenges on the front lines where work happens, not just in management and IT departments
  • Discover challenges: Identify business bottlenecks, not abstract AI usage
  • Implement with AI: Implement workflows, applications, agents, and data integrations
  • Make it stick: Bring it to a state where the frontline can use it in daily work
  • See results: Measure impact through adoption rates, time savings, quality improvements, and revenue contribution

To every role.

Engineers alone are not enough for corporate transformation. Sales, HR, accounting, CS, planning, marketing, management. Every role will need people who can deploy AI into their own work. That is why FDX extended the Forward Deployed philosophy to company-wide Transformation.

FAQ

FDX (Forward Deployed Transformation) is a company that installs "the power to keep transforming with AI" inside enterprises. We provide education, business process redesign, AI workflows and AI agents, data integration, and operations as one integrated offering, creating a state where work changes, AI works, results appear, and customers can sustain that transformation themselves. The name comes from taking the philosophy of the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) beyond the engineering role and extending it to all job types at customer companies.

There are four reasons. First, we provide all four in-house: training, specialist staffing, business redesign, and outsourced operations, allowing training and implementation to be designed as one continuous process. Second, our AI reskilling training has surpassed 30,000 cumulative participants, and we have been adopted by more than 150 companies across finance, manufacturing, logistics, retail, and local government. Third, since our founding in 2013, and from 2015 in a business using natural language processing, we have continued hands-on implementation in the field. Fourth, we deliver to customers only what we have used and validated ourselves, such as operating our main phone line's first-point reception with AI. As a representative case, at Shin Tairiku Co., Ltd., AX was achieved across all six non-engineering departments, and the company reported that its operating profit for fiscal 2025 tripled compared to fiscal 2023.

FDX defines AX completion as the state where five conditions are met: business processes have changed (WORK), AI continuously handles part of the work (AI), employees can improve operations using AI (PEOPLE), results such as time, quality, revenue, and cost savings are being achieved (OUTCOME), and improvement can continue in-house without external vendors (CAPABILITY). Merely introducing AI is "adopted," which we distinguish from "transformed."

FDE is a role and organizational model referring to "engineers embedded at customer sites who turn AI and software into business outcomes." It was systematized by Palantir in the 2010s and drew sudden attention when OpenAI organized itself as the "OpenAI Deployment Company" in May 2026. The scope of the role extends from entering the field, discovering issues, implementing with AI, and embedding it, through to measuring results.

The FDE at OpenAI Deployment Company collaborates with business leaders, operators, and frontline teams to identify areas where AI can have impact, redesign critical workflows, and turn them into sustainable systems. Its distinguishing feature is end-to-end ownership of discovery, technical scoping, system design, building, and production rollout.

Palantir's FDE is described as an agent that converts natural language requests into operations on Foundry, performing tasks such as data transformation, code repository management, and Ontology building. Palantir has indicated a direction in which "the very implementation work performed by human FDEs is itself becoming AI-driven."

FDE refers to a role within the "engineer" job family, meaning people who implement AI and software at customer sites. FDX is a concept that extends this philosophy to "all job types and departments at customer companies." In Japanese companies, not every employee becomes an engineer, but it is necessary to develop employees who can change their work using AI in their respective roles. Sales has sales AI deployment, HR has HR AI deployment, and administrative departments have administrative AI deployment.

AX Factory is an Operating Model proposed by FDX Inc. for completing a company's AX. It embeds AI education, AI workflows, AI agents, business templates, and operational design into each job type and business process in a form that can be used continuously in-house, creating a state where AX can be mass-produced internally. It is distinct from companies or services with the same name. What defines FDX is the statement "a company that installs the power to keep transforming with AI inside enterprises"; AX Factory is the method for realizing it, and AX completion is the goal.

FDX BPO/BPR is not mere task outsourcing; its purpose is to redesign operations with AI and ultimately hand over a transformation model to the front line. While visualizing results through outsourcing in the short term, we rebuild the workflows themselves to be AI-native, leaving behind operational rules, templates, and even KPIs.

FDX does not aim solely at replacing people. With the compression of mid-tier work as a premise, our design philosophy is "Human Upgrade": evolving people's roles, redeploying them, and shifting them toward high-value-added areas. This is the very embodiment of FDX's guiding principle of "Human Forward" - expanding human potential. Our support covers job evolution, organizational capability development, and transition into new high-value-added work, not "restructuring support."
