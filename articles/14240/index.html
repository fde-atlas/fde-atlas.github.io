What to focus on in your first three months as a Forward Deployed Engineer, from shadowing to owning a deployment.

Updated 2026-10-20By Forward Deployed Course editorial1 min read

The first three months set how customers and your team see you. Treat them in three phases.

Days 1–30: learn the product and shadow

  • Get the product running yourself, end to end, as a customer would.
  • Read past handover notes and post-mortems; they show where deployments break.
  • Shadow a senior FDE on customer calls and write up what you notice.
  • Ship one small fix, however minor, to learn the release process.

Days 31–60: own a slice

  • Take ownership of one bounded piece of a live deployment, such as an integration or an eval set.
  • Run part of a discovery session with a senior colleague in the room. Use the discovery checklist.
  • Send the customer a weekly update yourself.

Days 61–90: own an outcome

  • Lead a small engagement or a pilot phase from scope to demo.
  • Agree the success measure with the customer up front and report against it.
  • Write a handover note that a colleague can run without you. Use the handover checklist.
  • Feed at least one product improvement back to the core team.

What good looks like at day 90

A customer who asks for you by name, a deployment that runs without you, and one change in the product that came from what you saw in the field.
