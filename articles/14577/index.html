FDE Course Buyer's Checklist: 249 Questions to Ask

Forward Deployed Course · 2026-10-01 · 1 min read

Summary

This article offers a pre-purchase checklist for people considering FDE (forward deployed engineer) training courses: 249 questions organized into 7 groups, from placement outcomes to refunds. The author advises asking every provider the same questions and getting answers in writing before paying. Placement and outcomes is the largest group with 58 questions, followed by fees, EMI and refunds with 41. Smaller groups cover schedule and format, eligibility, curriculum, mentor support, and comparisons with degrees or free resources. Another 49 questions about the FDE role itself are covered elsewhere.

249 questions learners ask before they pay, in 7 groups. Ask every provider the same questions and get the answers in writing.

1. Placement and outcomes

58 questions

  • ☐ Is there an audited placement report I can read?
  • ☐ Which companies hired graduates of this programme?
  • ☐ What happens if I am not placed?

2. Fees, EMI and refunds

41 questions

  • ☐ Are there costs beyond the quoted fee?
  • ☐ What happens to my EMI if I stop midway?
  • ☐ Is a scholarship or discount available?

3. Schedule and format

27 questions

  • ☐ Weekday, weekend or mixed classes?
  • ☐ Can I watch recordings later, and for how long?
  • ☐ When does the next batch start?

4. Eligibility and admission

27 questions

  • ☐ Is there an entrance test, and what does it cover?
  • ☐ Is there a minimum score to qualify?
  • ☐ Can experienced engineers skip the basics test?

5. Curriculum and projects

17 questions

  • ☐ What does each module cover?
  • ☐ How many real deployment projects are included?
  • ☐ Is there a self-paced component?

6. Mentors and support

16 questions

  • ☐ Can I change my mentor?
  • ☐ How many mock interviews are included?
  • ☐ Is the placement team separate from instructors?

7. Comparisons and reviews

14 questions

  • ☐ How is this different from a master's degree?
  • ☐ What do independent reviews say?
  • ☐ Why pay when free resources exist?

The other 49 questions are about the role itself: see What is FDE and Careers.
