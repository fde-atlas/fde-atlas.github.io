Snowflake AI & Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs (25)

Forward Deploying · 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Summary

Forward Deploying tracks 25 open AI roles at Snowflake, including 5 Forward Deployed Engineer positions, ranking Snowflake 11th among 278 tracked employers. The median published US base salary across roles is $267k; FDE roles have a median midpoint of $283k, about 35% above the tracked market median of $210k. Roles span 7 cities, 10 are remote-eligible, and 24 of 25 publish salary ranges. Only 10 adverts explicitly require production code experience, and 5 mention travel with a median of 25%. Median posting age is 56 days, with 5 roles opened in the last 30 days.
