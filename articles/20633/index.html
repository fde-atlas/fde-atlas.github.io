Forward Deployed Engineering Series

Maven · 2026-08-02 · 2 min read

Summary

Maven is running a four-part live series on forward deployed engineering for technical PMs and full-stack engineers building production AI applications. Sessions cover the FDE role, Knowledge Graphs and GraphRAG versus traditional RAG, context engineering for realistic prototypes, and AI system design. Instructors include Hamza Farooq, Priyanka Vergadia (Microsoft), Benjamin Squire (Neo4j), Ravi Mehta (ex-Tinder CPO), and Aishwarya Ashok, with sessions running August 2–11, 2025.

Key points

  • Four live sessions run August 2–11, 2025.
  • Opening masterclass covers thriving as an FDE.
  • Neo4j's Benjamin Squire covers when GraphRAG beats traditional RAG.
  • Context engineering session focuses on prototypes that match real products.
  • System design session warns AI-native teams ship 3–5x faster but accrue debt.

A four-part lightning series for technical product managers and full-stack engineers building production AI applications. Learn how to design AI systems, communicate technical ideas through visual storytelling, leverage Knowledge Graphs and GraphRAG, engineer reliable context pipelines, and apply proven architectural patterns used in production. Every session is practical, code-aware, and grounded in real-world deployments.

Sun Aug 2·4:00 PM UTC

Forward Deployed Engineering Masterclass

Forward Deployed Engineers don't just build softwares, they become trusted technical partners to customers. They translate complex AI capabilities into real-world impact by combining engineering excellence with deep customer empathy. This session explores what it takes to thrive in one of the fastest-growing roles in AI.

Knowledge Graphs & Graph RAG for Enterprise AI

Context Engineering for Forward Deployed Engineers

Getting from idea to a clickable prototype now takes minutes. The real challenge is building something that actually looks and behaves like your product. That comes down to context: what the model knows about your features, your design language, your data. Context engineering is the core skill behind production AI systems, with prototyping as the fastest way to learn it.

You'll learn from

Ravi Mehta

Ex-EIR, Reforge | Former Chief Product Officer, Tinder

Aishwarya Ashok

AI Product Builder | Podcaster, The Founder’s Foyer | Startup Advisor

Tue Aug 11·4:00 PM UTC

AI System Design for Full Stack Developers

AI-native teams now ship 3–5x faster — but velocity without architectural literacy is how technical debt paralyzes a roadmap six months later. Claude Code has collapsed the time from idea to running code, not the complexity underneath it. This session gives you the full-stack mental model to catch the problem before you own it in Q3.
