Summary

This is a services page for SAI, a Japanese consulting firm that positions itself between traditional consultancies and development vendors. Instead of stopping at strategy decks or one-off deliveries, SAI embeds engineers at client sites, combining go-to-market support for new businesses with Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) style development, continuing until systems are running and clients can operate them in-house. Its JISEKI offering takes on new business ventures end-to-end with no upfront development fees, paid only via revenue share, subject to screening due to limited capacity.

Key points

  • SAI is a hands-on consultancy bridging strategy proposals and actual implementation.
  • GTM support covers ideation, demos, sales support, and first customer onboarding.
  • FDE engagement embeds engineers on-site through implementation and internal enablement.
  • JISEKI charges no upfront fees, using revenue-share contracts with unlimited pivots.
  • Screening checks real problems, decision-maker involvement, and SAI's technical edge.

SAI is a hands-on consulting firm where engineers embed themselves in the field and fully "build" both the sales mechanism (GTM) and the working systems (FDE).

"We have a great idea. But who will actually turn it into something that works?"

Most consultants leave behind a polished proposal and walk away. Most development firms build what they're told, deliver it, and call it done. SAI fills that "gap in between." A team that understands both business and technology embeds itself in your field and runs alongside you until the product is selling, being used, and can run on its own within your company.

New Business Development (GTM Support)

A new revenue pillar, from planning to sales.

"We want a new revenue pillar for our company." We join you from the planning stage of your business idea. A tech-savvy team runs alongside you through market validation, sales meetings, building working demos, and onboarding your first customers, staying with you until revenue materializes. We can also support expanding existing services, but our strongest focus is launching new businesses. Revenue-share contracts tied to results are also available upon consultation.

Glossary: GTM (Go-To-Market) = The series of activities involved in bringing a product or service to market and growing it. Revenue share = A performance-based contract model where compensation is based on sales.

Key Services

  • Involvement from business idea planning and brainstorming
  • Technical explanations in sales meetings and rapid building of working demos
  • Support through initial onboarding
  • Revenue-share, performance-based contracts available

JISEKI (Taking Responsibility)

If it doesn't generate sales, that's my responsibility.

SAI takes on new business development end to end, from planning through development and growth. We charge no initial development fees. If something isn't working, we pivot as many times as needed. SAI takes responsibility if sales don't materialize, and we won't let go until the numbers move. Compensation is performance-based revenue share, and participation is subject to a screening process to identify projects we can truly commit to.

Glossary: Revenue-share contract = A model where no upfront fees are charged and a portion of generated sales is shared as compensation. Pivot = Changing the direction of a business based on validation results.

See more about JISEKI

Key Services

  • Getting started with no initial development fees
  • End-to-end from planning, development, and attending sales meetings to growth
  • Unlimited pivots (no cap on the number of times)
  • Performance-based revenue-share contracts, subject to screening

FDE Embedded Development

Embedding in the field and building things that actually work.

We don't stop at handing over a proposal. Our engineers embed themselves in your workplace and actually implement systems and data structures tailored to your operations. We stay alongside you until the solution is used in the field and can run on its own within your company (in-house capability).

Glossary: FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) = A way of working where engineers go to the customer's "front line" and implement solutions themselves through to problem resolution (originated at US-based Palantir).

Key Services

  • On-site presence and high-frequency embedded support
  • Implementation of systems and automation tailored to your operations
  • Building data platforms and dashboards
  • Support for operational adoption and in-house capability (self-sufficiency)

Turning cutting-edge technology into practical, usable form.

This is the technical foundation behind our GTM and FDE work. From applying generative AI to business operations, building data utilization platforms, and developing web and business systems, we translate cutting-edge technology into forms that "truly work in the field."

Glossary: PoC (Proof of Concept) = A small-scale prototype built before full implementation to verify effectiveness.

Frequently Asked Questions

We answer even technical questions as plainly as possible.

Q. What kind of company is SAI Inc.? +

We are a hands-on consulting firm that doesn't stop at strategic proposals; our engineers embed themselves in the field and actually "build things through to completion." Our core services are GTM support for bringing new businesses and products to market, and FDE, where we work on-site alongside you through development and operations.

Q. What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)? +

It's a way of working where engineers embed themselves in the customer's field (front line), implement systems and data platforms tailored to the business, and stay alongside until operations are established. The hallmark is that we don't stop at proposing; we build working solutions together with you. This model is known to have been established by US-based Palantir.

Q. What is GTM (Go-To-Market) support? +

Simply put, it's "embedded support for new business development." For companies that want "a new revenue pillar," we join from the planning stage of the business idea and support, from both technical and business perspectives, technical explanations in sales meetings, building demos and PoCs (value validation through prototypes), and initial onboarding. We also support expanding existing services.

Q. Can we contract on a revenue-share (performance-based) basis? +

Yes. For new business development support, in addition to fixed monthly contracts, we can discuss revenue-share contracts tied to your business's sales. This contract model suits situations where you want to keep initial costs down and have us share the risk with you. Detailed terms are proposed individually depending on the nature of the business.

Q. How does "JISEKI" differ from new business development (GTM support)? +

GTM support runs alongside your team, with contracts combining monthly fees and revenue share, subject to consultation. With JISEKI, SAI Inc. takes on everything from planning through development, attending sales meetings, and growth, with no initial development fees. Compensation is received solely through performance-based revenue share. Because SAI bears the risk upfront, the number of projects we can run simultaneously is limited, so participation is subject to screening.

Q. Why doesn't "JISEKI" charge initial development fees? +

This is because when the client pays upfront, the feeling of "we've already paid this much, so we can't turn back" clouds judgment. We have structurally removed the state of becoming attached to money already spent (sunk cost). Since SAI Inc. puts forward both people and time in advance, decisions on whether to continue or change direction can be made based on numbers alone. This is not a promise of monetary refund. The substance of our responsibility lies in bearing the costs upfront and not withdrawing until the numbers move.

Q. What do you look for in the review for "JISEKI"? +

Three things: whether the problem being addressed actually exists, whether the decision-maker is involved as a stakeholder, and whether SAI Inc. can create a winning path with its technology. Because of our structure of taking on risk upfront without receiving initial development costs, the number of projects we can handle simultaneously is limited. We may decline, and in that case we will explain the reason as well.

Q. How are you different from typical consulting firms or SIers? +

Rather than stopping at presenting documents and strategies, our engineers themselves get hands-on and go all the way to implementation and operation. We value applying what we learn from one client to the next engagement, and ultimately aim for a state where the client can run things on their own (in-house capability).

Q. What kinds of companies are eligible? +

Companies facing challenges such as "we have a vision, but haven't fully put it into action" when it comes to launching new businesses or products, leveraging DX and generative AI, or building data platforms. We serve everyone from startups to large enterprises.

Q. Can we request your services remotely? +

Yes. We can accommodate on-site presence, frequent visits, or fully remote arrangements. Please feel free to contact us first as your "initial consultation partner."
