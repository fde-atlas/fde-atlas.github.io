Xforce by Xero: AI Workflows Built With Your Firm

Xero · 2026 · 6 min read

Summary

Xero has launched Xforce, a team of partner consultants and forward deployed engineers who work alongside accounting and bookkeeping firms to redesign and build workflows using XeroForce (Xero's builder for custom AI agents and workflows) and Xero APIs. The service is in beta with a small group of design partners in Australia, New Zealand and the UK, including Liston Newton Advisory, Nexia, Shepherd Hensman, Azets and Sumer. Xero argues that a platform alone cannot change how a firm operates: firms must supply a sponsoring leader and workflow expertise, while Xforce handles design and implementation. Goals include reducing repeatable work and delivering consistent service across clients. Pricing is not yet available, and registering interest does not guarantee beta access.

Key points

  • Xforce pairs consultants with forward deployed engineers on-site.
  • Built on XeroForce, Xero's AI agent and workflow builder.
  • Beta design partners span Australia, New Zealand and the UK.
  • Firms must sponsor the work and contribute workflow knowledge.
  • Aims to cut repeatable work and standardize client service.

Register your interest in Xforce by Xero

Tell us about your firm and where your team spends time on repeatable work. We’re starting with a small number of firms in the beta, so registering interest doesn’t confirm a place. If it looks like a good fit, our team will be in touch. We'd like to hear from firms:

  • Who use Xero at the core of their firm.
  • Have repeatable work across multiple clients that they’d like to improve, whether or not they’ve picked a workflow yet.
  • Can nominate a firm leader to sponsor the work and involve the people who know the workflow.
  • Are willing to co-design and test a workflow with us and share feedback.

Our first design partners

Xforce by Xero is in beta and is initially being developed with a handful of design partners in Australia, New Zealand and the UK, including Liston Newton Advisory in Australia, Nexia and Shepherd Hensman in New Zealand, and Azets and Sumer in the UK.

From AI experiments to workflows designed for your firm.

Firms are experimenting with AI, but the challenge is often bigger than choosing another tool. It's deciding which work to change, joining up the steps across Xero and the rest of the firm's tech stack, and making a new workflow fit the way the firm serves its clients.

XeroForce is Xero's builder for custom AI agents and workflows. But a platform alone won't redesign how a firm runs.

That's where Xforce comes in: We’re planning for partner consultants and forward-deployed engineers to work alongside your team to redesign and build the workflows that matter, starting with a small group of design partners in our beta.

A team that builds with you, on Xero.

Xforce by Xero is a team of partner consultants and forward-deployed engineers. They will help accounting and bookkeeping firms redesign and build workflows using XeroForce and Xero APIs, connected to the other software your firm uses where it makes sense.

The team brings knowledge of the workflows that matter to accountants and bookkeepers, and how to build on Xero. Your firm will bring the strategy, sponsorship and workflow expertise. The work will be designed and built together, starting with our beta design partners.

A team to redesign workflows with you.

Xforce by Xero will bring partner consultants and forward-deployed engineers to work with your team on the workflows that matter. Your firm brings a leader to sponsor the work and the people who know those workflows best. Together, you redesign and build ways of working around your strategy, using XeroForce, Xero APIs and your existing tools where they fit.

Start with your firm's process, then build across your tools.

Xforce by Xero combines an understanding of accounting and bookkeeping workflows with the technical experience to build on Xero. The team will works with your people to identify where a workflow can improve, then help design and build it to fit the way your firm works, using XeroForce, Xero APIs and other tools where they make sense.
