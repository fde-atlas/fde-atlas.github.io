ElevenLabs Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-02 · 9 min read

Summary

Using an October 24, 2026 snapshot of ElevenLabs's public Ashby job board, FDE Dispatch counts 12 open FDE-family postings: 4 Forward Deployed Engineer - Software Engineer seats and 8 Deployment Strategist seats (6 country roles plus 2 Lead roles). The article compares required skills against a 563-posting index, maps locations and remote status, and notes gaps in pay and travel disclosure, while cautioning that 12 postings is a small sample.

Key points

  • Deployment Strategist seats outnumber engineer seats 8 to 4.
  • 10 of 12 roles are outside the US; 9 of 12 list remote, versus 27.4% across the snapshot.
  • All 4 engineer postings require Python and APIs or integrations, above index averages.
  • All 7 retrieved strategist texts ask for basic Python and API familiarity; country seats require 3+ years with customers.
  • No posting lists pay or equity, and none states a travel percentage.

Published

ElevenLabs posts Forward Deployed Engineer seats next to Deployment Strategist seats, and strategists outnumber engineers 8 to 4. In FDE Dispatch's October 24, 2026 snapshot of public job boards it has 12 open, eighth of 121 employers. 4 are Forward Deployed Engineer - Software Engineer titles (Argentina, Mexico, USA, United Kingdom). The other 8 are Deployment Strategist titles: 6 country seats (Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Mexico, Italy, South Korea) and 2 Deployment Strategist Lead seats (India, USA). 2 are US-located and 10 are outside the US; 9 of 12 list remote. None state a travel percentage. None list pay; equity_mentioned is false on all 12. Twelve postings is a small sample. FDE Dispatch is not affiliated with ElevenLabs; the figures come from ElevenLabs's public job postings.

What ElevenLabs calls the role

ElevenLabs posts under three title tracks in this snapshot: Deployment Strategist, Deployment Strategist Lead, and Forward Deployed Engineer - Software Engineer. The 8 Deployment Strategist seats sit beside the 4 engineer seats and outnumber them 2 to 1; see deployment strategist vs forward deployed engineer for how that paired title compares across employers. ElevenLabs is fourth among strategist employers by posting count in that index (8 of 86 strategist postings).

Country Deployment Strategist texts describe deploying ElevenAgents with enterprise customers: problem definition, workflows, demos and getting agents to production beside Forward Deployed Engineers. Lead texts describe opening a new market, hiring the team and winning first customers around voice and audio AI. Engineer texts describe owning end-to-end projects for strategic customers, working with customer engineers and building integrations.

What the postings ask for

Two requirements-index flags hold up against the ElevenLabs engineer posting texts. All 4 engineer-title rows mention Python and APIs or integrations, against 76.2% and 69.8% of the 563 engineer-title postings in the FDE requirements index (posting text re-fetched October 25, 2026). The 8 Deployment Strategist postings are outside that engineer-title cut; strategist figures below use the 7 of 8 strategist texts re-fetched October 28, 2026 (South Korea text not retrieved).

The customer-facing keyword flag is 0 of 4 in the index even though every engineer role text describes embedding with strategic customers and asks for experience working with customers, so this page does not publish that flag as an ElevenLabs comparison. A mention is not a hard requirement. ElevenLabs has 4 rows in the index, under the cut used to rank employers with 10 or more postings.

Among the 7 retrieved strategist texts, all 7 ask for basic Python and API familiarity. 5 of 7 (the country Deployment Strategist seats) also match the agents, LLM and customer-facing flags and state a 3+ years minimum working with customers in a technical capacity. Both Lead texts state no years figure. The automated travel flag overcounts on Deployment Strategist Lead - USA (benefits Social travel stipend only), so travel is not published as 7 of 7 for strategists.

Where the roles are, and how many are remote

2 of the 12 postings (16.7%) are US-located on the published locations index: Deployment Strategist Lead - USA (New York) and Forward Deployed Engineer - Software Engineer - USA (San Francisco). 10 (83.3%) are outside the US, against 40.1% across all 725 postings. Titles name Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Mexico, Italy, South Korea, India, the USA and the United Kingdom.

9 of the 12 list remote (75.0%), against 199 of 725 (27.4%) across the snapshot. Workplace is Remote on those 9. The Argentina Deployment Strategist, Chile Deployment Strategist and Argentina Forward Deployed Engineer rows leave remote and workplace blank on the Ashby capture. Strategist remote on the all-employer cut is 19 of 86 (22.1%) on deployment strategist vs forward deployed engineer.

Metro and remote figures for every employer are on Forward Deployed Engineer jobs by location.

ElevenLabs posts eight Deployment Strategist seats next to four Forward Deployed Engineer seats in this snapshot, with country-named roles from Argentina and Chile through India, South Korea, the USA and the UK. If you're going for the engineer seats, FDE Dispatch trains you for the customer-deployment rounds those loops use.

How much ElevenLabs Forward Deployed Engineers travel

All 12 postings have travel_mentioned = true in the postings snapshot. None state a travel percentage, so there is no employer median upper bound to compare with the 30% all-employer median on how much forward deployed engineers travel. Hand-read posting texts: all 4 engineer texts and 6 of 7 retrieved strategist texts say regular travel for customer onsites. Deployment Strategist Lead - USA has no customer-onsite travel sentence (only the benefits Social travel stipend). The South Korea strategist text was not retrieved.

What ElevenLabs posts for pay

None of the 12 postings list a pay range in the Ashby pay field. Equity is not mentioned on any of the 12 (equity_mentioned false), against 53.5% across all employers with FDE-family postings. Pay is not listed, so this page does not compare ElevenLabs pay to the USD engineer median on Forward Deployed Engineer salary. Twelve postings is a small sample.

What this means if you are preparing for an ElevenLabs loop

  • Seat family. Strategist seats outnumber engineer seats 8 to 4. Country Deployment Strategist texts center on ElevenAgents deployments with customers; Lead texts center on opening a market and hiring. Engineer texts center on owning technical delivery and integrations for strategic customers. Read the title before you decide which seat fits.
  • Geography and language. Titles name Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Mexico, Italy, South Korea, India, the USA and the United Kingdom. Country seat texts that were read ask for English plus a local language and regional familiarity.
  • Remote and travel. 9 of 12 list remote. None state a travel percentage; expect regular customer-onsite travel language on the engineer seats and on most strategist seats that were read.

Round formats for FDE loops are on the FDE interview loop, explained, and practice prompts on FDE interview practice questions.

Method and data

Population: the 12 ElevenLabs rows with in_page_stats = true among the 725 FDE-family postings from 121 employers counted on the salary, job titles, locations and travel pages. Snapshot pulled October 24, 2026 from ElevenLabs's public Ashby board. Engineer requirements come from the October 25, 2026 requirements index, which re-fetched the posting text; the 4 engineer-title ElevenLabs postings are in it. Strategist requirement figures are hand-verified from posting texts re-fetched October 28, 2026 for 7 of 8 strategist rows (Deployment Strategist - South Korea text was not retrieved).

Track: title text (Deployment Strategist, Deployment Strategist Lead, Forward Deployed Engineer - Software Engineer). US-located and remote-listed use the published locations-index definitions: a posting is US-located when every listed location is in the US, and remote-listed when the board marks it remote or a listed location contains Remote. Travel upper bound = largest number in the stated percentage string, the rule on the travel page; ElevenLabs states no percentage. Pay: none listed. These 12 listings were live on the October 24, 2026 snapshot; counts are open postings on one day and do not measure headcount. Twelve postings is a small sample.

Downloads: ElevenLabs JSON, field notes, and the per-posting postings JSON. All published datasets are listed on FDE Dispatch job-posting data. Built by
