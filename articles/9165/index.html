Summary

This FDEInterviews page compiles system design and production engineering questions for AWS's Forward Deployed Engineer interview loop, with senior-level sample answers. Topics include designing token-based billing for a multi-tenant LLM API without double-billing retries, delivery semantics (at-least-once vs exactly-once), rate limiting algorithms, observability, and VPC/air-gapped deployments. It notes AWS formally announced a Forward Deployed Engineering organization in September 2026, working with Hoxton Wealth on production agentic AI. AWS has not published an FDE-specific interview sequence, so candidates should prepare leadership-principles behavioral questions, agentic system design on AWS services, and customer delivery discussions, and confirm the applicable track with their recruiter.

Concepts behind AWS's System Design & Production Engineering round

The vocabulary and mental models these questions assume. Start with the foundations free; the deeper, interview-defining ideas are part of premium.

FoundationalFrom Proof-of-Concept to ProductionA notebook that answered one question correctly during a demo is not a deliverable. Production is the unglamorous work that turns a one-time success into a system the customer can run, trust, and operate without you in the room. Closing that gap is most of the FDE job.

FoundationalRetries, Exponential Backoff and JitterWhen a call fails on a transient error, retrying immediately is the worst thing you can do: it piles load onto an already-struggling service and synchronizes every client into a stampede. Exponential backoff spaces retries out, and jitter de-synchronizes the clients so they stop arriving in lockstep.

AWS's Forward Deployed Engineer loop draws system design & production engineering questions such as "Design metering and billing for a multi-tenant LLM API charged by tokens. No double-billing on retries, and quotas that actually hold.", "What's the difference between at-least-once and exactly-once delivery, and why should an FDE care?", "Explain how rate limiting works, fixed window, sliding window, token bucket.
