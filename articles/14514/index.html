7 Best Certinia Alternatives for PS Teams in 2026

Rocketlane · 2026-09-01 · 35 min read

Summary

This Rocketlane-published buyer's guide reviews seven alternatives to Certinia (formerly FinancialForce), a Salesforce-native PSA platform. It argues that while Certinia made sense when sales ran on Salesforce, its architecture becomes costly and slow to change as services organizations scale, with implementations often taking 6 to 12 months. The top pick is Rocketlane for B2B SaaS and technology PS teams of 25 to 150 consultants, priced from $49/user/month with a 4 to 12 week go-live and a 4.7/5 G2 rating. Kantata suits larger consulting firms needing deep resource planning (and offers a Salesforce-native SX edition); BigTime fits mid-market firms focused on billing; Scoro and Accelo serve agencies and smaller retainer-based businesses. The article also routes buyers whose real need is financial management (general ledger, revenue recognition) toward ERP tools like Sage Intacct or NetSuite. Note the vendor bias: Rocketlane wrote the piece and ranks itself first.

Key points

  • Certinia's Salesforce-native architecture drives cost and admin overhead as PS teams scale.
  • Rocketlane is the top pick: $49/user/month, 4 to 12 week go-live, 4.7/5 on G2.
  • Kantata leads on resource planning and is the only Salesforce-native alternative via Kantata SX.
  • BigTime, Scoro, and Accelo fit billing-heavy, agency, and small retainer use cases respectively.
  • Buyers needing general ledger or revenue recognition should evaluate ERPs, not PSAs.

Certinia, formerly FinancialForce, is a Salesforce-native platform for professional services automation and financial management. Its strengths include resource management, project financials, forecasting, billing, and sophisticated revenue recognition connected to the Salesforce environment.

Its Salesforce-native architecture is central to understanding both Certinia's appeal and the alternatives market. PS data can sit alongside customer and opportunity information in the Salesforce environment, giving organizations that have standardized on Salesforce a closely connected commercial and services data model.

For some teams, the trigger is total cost and admin overhead rather than Certinia’s subscription price alone. Licensing depends on how PSA users interact with Salesforce, while configuration, reporting, permissions, and newer AI capabilities can add Salesforce licensing and admin requirements.

Certinia implementations can also run 6–12 months and involve significant services effort. That model can work well for organizations with established Salesforce operations, but becomes harder to justify when PS teams want faster changes and greater operational independence.
