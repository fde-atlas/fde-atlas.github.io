FDE Jobs in India: Remote ₹20–28 LPA Role, ServiceNow Cuts

FDE India · 2026-11-10 · 8 min read

Summary

FDE hiring in India stayed flat for a second week: Glassdoor India shows 564 broad and 125 exact-title listings, with 52 in Bengaluru. The notable new posting is Smart Bricks' fully remote AI Engineer (Forward Deployed) role at ₹20–28 LPA for 2–4 years of experience, filling a pay gap between early-career and 5+ year bands. Cisco lists a hybrid Bengaluru FDE role for 3–8 years, and Kore.ai an FDE Technical Lead in Hyderabad for 7+ years, both without pay. ServiceNow confirmed layoffs affecting India, the UK and the US without numbers, while saying it still hires for AI-focused skills. Accenture's Hyderabad FDE role is no longer accepting applications.

FDE hiring in India is holding steady, not surging. Listing counts are flat for a second week, but a new remote role fills a gap in the pay picture: Smart Bricks wants a forward deployed AI engineer anywhere in India at ₹20–28 LPA for 2–4 years. Cisco and Kore.ai are also advertising FDE roles in Bengaluru and Hyderabad, while ServiceNow has confirmed fresh cuts that reach India.

This Pulse follows Sunday’s Sycamore and healthcare FDE update. Below: the counts, the new roles, what experience employers now ask for, and the week’s layoff news.

FDE job listings in India: flat for a second week

Glassdoor India’s numbers have not moved since 5 November:

  • The broad forward deployed engineer search shows 564 listings.
  • The exact Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) title search shows 125.
  • Glassdoor’s Bengaluru FDE-title search shows 52 roles, so the city accounts for a large share of exact-title listings.

Naukri and LinkedIn India totals are still not publicly reported in pages we could access. Two flat readings in a row suggest the late-October rise has paused. It is not a decline, and new roles are still being posted, as the next section shows.

One listing has also dropped off. Accenture’s Forward Deployed Engineer role in Hyderabad, which asked for 5–7 years with Generative AI and Python, now shows “no longer accepting applications” on Foundit. Accenture’s other India FDE roles are covered on our Accenture company page.

New FDE roles in India this week

Smart Bricks: remote, ₹20–28 LPA for 2–4 years

Smart Bricks posted an AI Engineer (Forward Deployed) role on 8 November 2026, according to JobFound. It calls itself an applied AI lab for real assets, building agents for real estate funds, institutional investors and family offices. The role is fully remote within India.

Detail What the listing says
Location Remote, India
Experience Tagged 2–4 years; the profile section says 3+ years shipping production software
Posted pay ₹20–28 LPA
Work Agents for sourcing, underwriting, due diligence, investment memos and portfolio monitoring
Stack Python, TypeScript, LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, RAG, MCP, AWS

The job is a full FDE loop. You build multi-step agents and document pipelines, set up evals for accuracy, cost and latency, join discovery calls with the sales team, and own the client relationship through rollout. You are also expected to turn one-off client builds into reusable platform features.

Why it matters: most well-paid India FDE roles we have tracked are tied to an office in Bengaluru, Mumbai or Pune. A ₹20–28 LPA remote band for under five years of experience is useful for engineers in Kochi, Jaipur, Indore or other cities without many FDE employers. It sits between Zorba AI’s ₹10–25 LPA and the ₹28–36 LPA that DevRev and Sycamore post for 5+ years.

Smart Bricks fills the gap between early-career and 5+ year bands. Employer-posted pay via JobFound, 1–8 Nov 2026.

Cisco: Forward Deployed Engineer, Bengaluru, 3–8 years

Cisco has a Forward Deployed Engineer role in Bengaluru, hybrid and full-time, listed on OwnYourCareer for 3–8 years of experience. The summary describes working with customers to deploy and customise Cisco solutions in live environments, with travel. Pay is not disclosed. Glassdoor India first showed a Cisco FDE role in Bengaluru in late October; this listing confirms the experience range.

Kore.ai: FDE Technical Lead, Hyderabad, 7+ years

Kore.ai is advertising an FDE – Technical Lead in Hyderabad on Weekday, asking for 7+ years. Skills named include LangChain, microservices, AWS, Azure or GCP, WebRTC, and enterprise work in banking and customer service. No pay is shown. This adds a senior hands-on level to the FDE Level 1, Developer, Lead and Manager roles on our Kore.ai company page. See the Hyderabad FDE jobs guide for other employers in the city.

Still listed: Commure’s healthcare FDE in Bengaluru

Following Sunday’s healthcare story, Commure’s Forward Deployed Engineer (India) role in Bengaluru is still listed on SportsTechJobs. It asks for 1+ years of software engineering, US-aligned working hours, and Python, JavaScript, React and SQL, with work on healthcare clients’ compliance and integration needs. It was first posted in late September and shows no employer-posted pay, so confirm it is open before applying.

What experience Indian FDE roles ask for now

Put the current listings side by side and the experience bar is wide:

Kore.ai’s Technical Lead (7+ years) and Commure (1+ years) state only a minimum. JobFound and OwnYourCareer listings, Nov 2026.

The pattern: early-career roles (Commure, Smart Bricks) want proof that you have shipped production code and can talk to clients. Senior roles (Sycamore, Kore.ai) want system design and enterprise integration experience. Cisco’s 3–8 year band is the widest entry point among large employers this week.

Layoffs and FDE hiring this week, side by side

These are reported next to FDE hiring for context. No source links these cuts to forward deployed engineer roles.

  • ServiceNow: People Matters reported on 6 November that a new round of layoffs affects employees in India, the UK and the US. No headcount or India split was given. The company said it is driving efficiencies while “actively investing in and hiring for AI-focused skills”. People Matters also notes Q3 2026 severance costs of $87 million, against $24 million a year earlier. This follows an earlier ServiceNow round in late July that ThePeoplesBoard’s India tracker says hit India teams early.
  • 2026 tally: SkillSyncer’s tracker, updated 9 November, counts 232,946 workers affected in 567 tech layoff events this year, against 205,773 across 338 events in 2025. It tags 37% of 2026 events as AI-related. That is AI adoption broadly, not FDE hiring.
  • India context: ThePeoplesBoard’s India tracker lists PayPal’s cut of about 600 India staff and Zomato’s closure of a Hyderabad support centre (about 240 people), both from late August. It has no November entries yet.

ServiceNow’s statement is the clearest example this week of a company cutting overall headcount while hiring for AI skills. It does not say which roles it is hiring for in India.

What this means for you this week

  • Outside the big metros? Look at remote FDE roles. Smart Bricks’ ₹20–28 LPA remote listing shows mid-band FDE pay no longer needs a Bengaluru address. Filter job boards for “remote India” plus “forward deployed”.
  • Build one agent with evals. Smart Bricks asks for evals on accuracy, cost and latency. A small project that measures all three is stronger proof than a demo. Our interview questions guide shows how to talk through it.
  • Mid-career at a services firm? Try Cisco’s band. At 3–8 years, Cisco’s Bengaluru role is open to engineers who have done customer deployments but have not led FDE teams.
  • At ServiceNow or a similar firm? Map your platform and integration work to FDE skills with the FDE Fit Check, and benchmark offers on our salary page.

Glassdoor India shows 564 listings for a broad forward deployed engineer search and 125 with the exact Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) title. Its Bengaluru FDE-title search shows 52 roles.

Did ServiceNow lay off employees in India in November 2026?

It varies widely. Current listings range from 2–4 years at Smart Bricks and 3–8 years at Cisco to 5–12 years at Sycamore and 7+ years for Kore.ai's FDE Technical Lead.
