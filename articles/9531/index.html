FDE Knowledge Framework and Learning Path

Original title: FDE 知识框架与学习路线

FDE 从入门到精通 · 2026-10-14 · 4 min read

Summary

This article presents a knowledge framework and learning path for Forward Deployed Engineers, organized around six delivery stages, seven capability domains, and 58 work items, mapped to 46 learning topics. Stages can be revisited iteratively, and capabilities are reused across stages; readers pick relevant work per project, with AI-related activities enabled as needed. Topics span customer interviews and problem definition, prototyping, data pipelines, AI evals, acceptance and handover, observability and troubleshooting, security and permissions, customer adoption, and productization, ending with an end-to-end capstone project and delivery templates as evidence of competence.

Key points

  • Framework: 6 stages, 7 domains, 58 work items
  • 46 learning topics; stages allow iterative back-and-forth
  • Covers full delivery lifecycle from problem definition to handover
  • AI evals, RAG, and troubleshooting content enabled on demand
  • Capstone project, evals, and templates serve as delivery evidence
