One day a week on-site plus online support on weekdays to establish AI as your organization's business infrastructure

DAIJOBU Inc. (Head office: Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director: Yuki Yamanaka; hereinafter "DAIJOBU"), a company specializing in software testing and quality assurance that also runs Claude Code corporate training programs, has launched "Weekly On-Site Consultant," an implementation support service to establish Anthropic's AI agent "Claude Code" as an organization's business infrastructure, starting August 10, 2026.

This service targets companies that have completed DAIJOBU's "Claude Code Corporate Training" or are already advancing organizational use of Claude Code. A DAIJOBU AI consultant works on-site at the client company's office one day per week to drive implementation, and provides online sharing of the latest information and Q&A support on other weekdays. It is an implementation support service on a scale of 0.3 to 1 person-month per month.

Background of Service Development

The corporate adoption of coding agent AIs such as Claude Code is advancing rapidly, and the number of companies reaching the state where "individuals can use it" through training and onboarding support is steadily growing. However, the following hurdles lie ahead.

"We got individual adoption working, but the design and implementation to integrate it into organizational workflows is not progressing"

"We have no in-house staff who can dedicate themselves to driving AI adoption"

"Quality and security operations are put on the back burner, and adoption hits a plateau"

Between "understanding" through training and actually getting daily work to "run" with AI, there is a substantial amount of implementation work required to build skills, rules, and knowledge foundations.

Through providing corporate training, DAIJOBU has confirmed demand from organizations saying, "Even after training, we want deeper support through to implementation." This service was newly established to fill this "implementation gap," which weekly lightweight Q&A sessions and regular meetings cannot cover.

Service Details

"One day per week on-site" is a delivery model in which a consultant works on-site at the client company's office one day per week to drive implementation in the field. On other weekdays, support continues online. Rather than ending with a report, we work hands-on alongside the client company's members to build working systems together. This type of on-site support is known in the US AI industry as an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer).

【What We Provide】

    • On-site implementation, reviews, and hands-on sessions
  • 常駐日以外の平日はオンラインで伴走
    • Sharing of the latest AI and Claude Code information, Q&A support, and asynchronous implementation support
  • 稼働量：
    • A total of 0.3 to 1 person-month per month (designed according to the scope and structure of support)
  • 支援範囲の例：
    • AI automation of business workflows (design and implementation of department-specific skills and custom agents)
    • Advancement and operational establishment of an organization-specific, organization-wide CLAUDE.md and knowledge foundation (context design)
    • Regular reviews of usage status, improvement implementation, and support for developing internal champions
    • Sharing of DAIJOBU case studies

Note that what this service delegates to AI is "work." Decisions on what to automate and acceptance of deliverables remain with the client company. The goal is to build an AI business infrastructure that the client company can keep running on its own, without depending on specific individuals or vendors.

Since we accept clients only within the capacity that allows us to guarantee support quality, companies considering the service are encouraged to consult with us early.
