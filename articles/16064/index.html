FDE Times · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Summary

This is a regularly updated job board listing 34 open Forward Deployed Engineer roles in Germany, concentrated in Berlin and Munich, aggregated from the careers pages of 18 companies. Databricks has the most openings with five, followed by n8n, Dataleap, and Taktile with three each. Other notable employers include OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon (AWS), Celonis, GitLab, Vercel, and Applied Intuition. Roles span individual contributor, staff, lead, and manager levels, plus Deployment Strategist positions, with some remote or hybrid options across Europe.
