How we work — engagement models

FetchSky Technologies · 2026 · 4 min read

Summary

FetchSky Technologies describes four engagement models clients can mix and adjust over time: staff augmentation with experienced developers, QA, DevOps and designers; forward deployed engineers embedded in operations, aimed at AI work; fixed-scope project delivery; and managed services where the original engineers stay on call. Each model lists who it suits, what is delivered, and how it starts via a free consultation. Engagements follow a standard path: an engineer-led call, scoping of systems and constraints, a written proposal with scope, team, timeline and cost, and a start usually within weeks.

Key points

  • Four engagement models: augmentation, forward deployed engineers, project delivery, managed services.
  • Clients can mix models and shift along the scale as work changes.
  • Forward deployed engineers embed in operations to find, build and ship what matters, suited to AI.
  • Project delivery offers fixed scope and dates; managed services keep original engineers on call.
  • Engagements start with a call, scoping, a written proposal, then a start within weeks.

How does an engagement start?

  • Conversation

    A call with an engineer about what you need and what is in the way. No sales script.

  • Scoping

    We look at the systems, data and constraints involved, and agree what success looks like.

  • Proposal

    Scope, team, timeline and cost, with the assumptions written down.

  • Start

    Usually within a few weeks of agreement.
