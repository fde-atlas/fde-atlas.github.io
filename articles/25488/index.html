Summary

This is a job posting on OpenWork for a Deployment Strategist (DS) / AI-IT consultant (manager level) at Alphakt, a Japanese consulting firm specializing in AI-driven business process reform (BPR) for large companies in manufacturing, telecom, energy, and finance. The DS role involves embedding in client organizations to lead end-to-end business transformation: discovering issues, designing To-Be processes and KPIs, securing executive buy-in, and managing project profitability, while partnering with Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) teams on technical decisions. Requirements include 5+ years leading system-development reform projects and managing teams. Salary ranges from 10 to 30 million yen with performance-linked incentives and promotion possible within one year. Alphakt targets 30 billion yen revenue and 1,000 employees by 2030, with IPO plans.

Key points

  • Alphakt is a Japanese consulting firm focused on AI×BPR for core industries.
  • The DS role leads business transformation end-to-end, from issue discovery to executive consensus and profitability.

We accompany major companies that underpin Japan's core industries—including manufacturing, telecommunications, energy, and finance—providing end-to-end support from strategy formulation through implementation. ◼︎Business Areas Starting from AI×BPR (business process reform), we cover a wide range from upstream strategy development to midstream business and infrastructure transformation, including AI agent development, data platform construction (DWH, ETL, BI), and cloud infrastructure design support. Rather than being mere advisors, our distinguishing feature is a thorough commitment to client outcomes while personally mastering AI. ◼︎Business Outlook We have set a goal of 30 billion yen in revenue and an organization of 1,000 people by 2030, and we position an IPO as a lever to accelerate growth. We will apply the expertise gained through consulting to our own products and new businesses, and in the future we envision evolving into a corporate entity that operates as a "digital manufacturer" generating multiple global businesses.

  • ■MISSION Implement cutting-edge technology and contribute to the evolution of the global economy. Alphakt will remain an organization that never stops growing and can execute continuous investment under any circumstances. We translate technology into "systems that are actually used" end-to-end—from strategy through requirements definition, design and development, and operations—and solve clients' management challenges through implementation that bridges decision-making and the front lines, creating tangible results. We always think from the client's perspective and remain thoroughly committed until success is achieved. The foundation supporting this execution lies in three core values: self-transformation, altruism as self-interest, and volume of action. By embodying these three values, client success generates our own sustainable growth, which in turn cascades into the career fulfillment of each of our members. Through this virtuous cycle, we create a better future. ■VISION To become an organization that continuously creates businesses contributing to the evolution of the global economy as a "digital manufacturer." We drive transformation centered on AI utilization and PM support across core industries such as manufacturing, telecommunications, energy, finance, and transportation. What we aim for beyond that is evolution into a corporate entity that operates multiple global businesses as a "digital manufacturer." As an organization that continuously creates world-class products and business models, we will establish our presence in global markets. We will support the transformation of industrial structures through the power of technology and contribute to global development through sustainable value creation. ■VALUES We drive our business by the Law of 8x Goals. We strive to be considerate of all people. We act on the principle of altruism as self-interest. We act with immediate response and immediate execution. We commit to building systems that are actually used. We plan backward from our goals and act to achieve them.

  • We productize the common challenges discovered through client support, and our employees use them as their own tools. At Alphakt, we develop the common challenges we discover while working with clients in consulting engagements into our own products and services. These are not only offered to clients but also serve as practical tools that our employees use in their daily work. ◼︎Skillva This is a platform that matches corporate challenges in the digital domain with talent possessing advanced professional skills on a project basis. Alphakt builds its own professional talent network to achieve optimal assignments tailored to each company's management challenges. We also provide human resource development services such as AI talent training, cybersecurity training, infrastructure talent training, and assessments. ◼︎PERKS AI This is a system that leverages AI across all business processes to achieve automation and efficiency. Internally, consultants use PERKS AI, enabling an environment where multiple advanced projects can be advanced simultaneously. Going forward, we will continue investing in developing and expanding the insights gained through solving client challenges into products and services.
