Consistent support from designing AI adoption strategy to building AI agents and developing in-house talent and operational frameworks

AuthenticAI, Inc. (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director: Toru Ueda; hereinafter "Authentic AI"), a developer and provider of a generative AI platform for enterprises, has launched "Maison AI FDE Partner," a professional service in which Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) support each company's AI adoption and utilization on the ground within "Maison AI."

"Maison AI FDE Partner" is a service that provides consistent support, from designing AI adoption strategy to designing and building AI agents tailored to each business and brand, developing in-house talent, and building operational frameworks. As a time-based consulting service, support offerings are combined within contracted hours and carried out as a project.

Authentic AI has developed and provided "Maison AI" with the aim of realizing a society where everyone uses generative AI as a matter of course. Through this service, by combining generative AI technology with an understanding of industries and business operations in a hands-on, accompanying style of support, the company maximizes the creativity and strategic capability of organizations and individuals, and promotes the social implementation of AI utilization in enterprises.

※ This service is available to companies that have adopted "Maison AI."

■ "Maison AI FDE Partner": Supporting post-adoption operations and adoption consolidation

While the use of generative AI is expanding in enterprises, challenges have emerged, such as which business processes to automate with AI first and how to build a framework for continued in-house use. It is also not uncommon to see cases where usage remains limited to a few people in charge and does not spread across the company, where companies cannot design AI agents suited to their own business and brand, or where there is a shortage of personnel and promotion structures to handle operations.

In "Maison AI FDE Partner," Authentic AI's Forward Deployed Engineers embed themselves in the client's workplace and support the design and implementation of AI utilization based on each adopting company's business processes and organizational structure. Rather than simply explaining how to use AI tools, the distinguishing feature of this service is continuous accompaniment through post-adoption operations and in-house consolidation, tailored to each company's challenges.

■ Service offerings and plans

In this service, within the contracted monthly working hours, support offerings are combined according to the company's challenges and priorities and carried out as a project. Rather than offering a fixed package uniformly, working hours are allocated to the support needed at the time, so the service can proceed while reviewing the support content in line with progress and internal circumstances. The service offerings are three types: "AI business consulting," "AI training sessions and workshops," and "AI agent and chatbot design."

Pricing is 200,000 yen per month for the 10-hour plan (up to 10 hours/month), 380,000 yen per month for the 20-hour plan (up to 20 hours/month), and 540,000 yen per month for the 30-hour plan (up to 30 hours/month) (all excluding tax).

※ Hours include meetings, research, design, reviews, document creation, and more.

※ For projects with fixed deliverables and deadlines, individual quotations are also available.

Note that the support offerings listed are examples. Specific support content is designed for each company, so please contact us via the inquiry form below for details.

■ Example support offering | Talent development and operational frameworks to spread AI utilization across the company

To advance AI utilization continuously, it is important not only to introduce tools but also to put in place personnel who promote AI use and operational frameworks within the company. In "Maison AI FDE Partner," we conduct training sessions and workshops tailored to the audience—executives, department heads, and frontline staff—as well as hands-on sessions by department.

Furthermore, in collaboration with the personnel promoting AI utilization, we support organizing target business processes and departments, establishing AI usage guidelines and methods for managing data and permissions, and monthly issue organization and action plan formulation. The goal is not just for a few staff with AI knowledge, but for each department to be able to use AI on its own.

■ Example support offering | Designing and building AI agents tailored to each company's business and brand

In "Maison AI FDE Partner," we design and build AI agents suited to the business based on the company's business workflows, brand worldview, and the knowledge and rules accumulated in-house. Target business areas span a wide range of departments, including product planning and merchandising, content production, EC and store operations, customer insight analysis, and corporate planning and business development.

We organize business decision criteria and brand expression policies, and design the AI agent's role, the information it references, and how it is operated. This makes it possible not only for individuals to use generic AI, but also to build an environment where AI agents leveraging the company's knowledge and business know-how can be shared and operated across the organization.

■ Comment from Representative Director Toru Ueda

Adopting generative AI does not end with signing up for a tool. It is important to consider which business processes to apply it to, who will use it, and how it will be shared in-house, and to create a state where it is actually used in the field on an ongoing basis.

As the number of companies using Maison AI grows, we are increasingly consulted about post-adoption operations and consolidation. Even when there is a desire to use AI, usage sometimes remains limited to a few people in charge because the company lacks specialized personnel.

In "Maison AI FDE Partner," Forward Deployed Engineers enter the client's workplace and provide support, from organizing business challenges to designing AI agents, developing personnel who use AI in-house, and building operational frameworks.

Rather than AI unilaterally replacing human work, our role is to design the division of roles between people and AI for each company so that people can focus on the judgment and creative work they should concentrate on. Going forward, through Maison AI, we will continue to think together with our customers about how AI should be used, and promote the social implementation of AI rooted in companies' business operations and organizations.

■ Overview of the Generative AI Platform "Maison AI"

"Maison AI", provided by AuthenticAI Inc., is a generative AI platform for companies specializing in lifestyle and creative fields such as fashion, interior, and general goods.

In addition to text generation AI such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Claude, AI search, and image generation AI available on a single platform, it also features AI agents by job type and functionality to create and share company-specific AI agents.

Through features such as AI chat, AI search, AI agents, image and video generation, and AI orchestration, it supports improving creativity and productivity across a wide range of business operations, including product planning, marketing, e-commerce, store operations, corporate planning, and internal operations.

It also includes features designed for use within companies, such as workspace functionality, permission management, and usage history management, and provides hands-on support from implementation through operation.

"Maison AI" official website: https://maisonai.io/
