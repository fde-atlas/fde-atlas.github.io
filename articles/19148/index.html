Summary

This ranking reviews ten US AI consulting firms a construction contractor can hire, scored on construction focus, whether they build or only advise, fluency with Procore, Sage and Viewpoint, printed prices, and verifiable proof. Lumeor Studio ranks first as the only firm that names the construction stack and publishes prices (assessments $2,500–$7,500; custom AI agents $15,000–$60,000). Buyer's guide5 questions

What does AI consulting for a construction company actually include?

On the pages read, four shapes: building agents and document search on Procore and your files (Lumeor Studio, Master of Code, MetaCTO, POLR AI), joining the systems before the AI (Quandary), vendor neutral assessment and roadmap (Burger Consulting, Dan Cumberland Labs, HatchWorks), and AI advice from the firm that does your books or ERP (Wipfli, Forvis Mazars). Pick the shape before the firm.

How much does AI consulting cost for a construction company?

Only one firm here prints it. Lumeor Studio: readiness assessment $2,500 to $7,500, workshops $3,000 to $15,000 a session, a custom agent $15,000 to $60,000, and $50 to $500 a month to run a typical agent. MetaCTO's enquiry form lets you pick a budget from under $10k to $150k+. The rest quote after a call.

Should a contractor hire an AI consultant or a construction technology firm?

If you haven't chosen the ERP or project system yet, a vendor neutral firm such as Burger Consulting first. If the systems are chosen and the pain is retyping between them, a builder. If the AI exists and stalls on real job data, a forward deployed engineer inside the company fixes what a roadmap won't.

Does Procore's own AI make a consultant unnecessary?

For tasks inside Procore, often yes: start there. A consultant earns the fee when the answer needs data Procore doesn't hold, such as job cost in Sage 300 CRE or Viewpoint Vista, the estimate in a spreadsheet, or drawings in SharePoint.

Which firms here actually build, rather than advise?

Lumeor Studio, Master of Code, MetaCTO and POLR AI build agents and workflows on Procore and your documents. Quandary builds integrations and automation. Dan Cumberland Labs builds one workflow per engagement. Burger Consulting, Wipfli, HatchWorks (on its published case) and Forvis Mazars lead with assessment, strategy or ERP.

How to choose

  • 1This week, take one live job and follow one RFI from the field to the answer, and one pay application from the sub's invoice to the owner's payment. Count every time someone types the same number into a different place: Procore, the accounting system, the spreadsheet, the email. That count, and the hours behind it, is what every firm on this page should be judged against.
  • 2Write down the one number you can't see until month end. For most contractors it's job cost to date against budget, or over and under billing by job. If the answer lives in two systems that don't talk, the first job is joining them, not buying AI.
  • 3Check what you already own. Procore's own AI agents, Microsoft Copilot and the AI inside Trimble or your ERP may cover the first use case without a new contract. Forvis Mazars' own advice is to pilot meeting summaries, cost analysis and RFI management in tools you already have.
  • 4Decide the shape. Not sure the software is right: Burger Consulting. Documents and RFIs: Master of Code, Lumeor Studio or MetaCTO. Systems that don't share data: Quandary. Staff who need training by role: POLR AI.
  • 5Ask every firm for the first price in writing before a second meeting, and for who approves anything an agent writes back into Procore or the accounting system.
  • 6If the systems still don't share one job record after the roadmap, and nobody in house can join them up, that's when you bring in a forward deployed engineer rather than another consultant. Does a small construction company need an AI consultant?

Not to switch on AI inside Procore, Buildertrend or Microsoft 365. It needs one when job data has to move between two or more systems, when project managers spend hours a week on RFIs, submittals and daily logs, or when the owner can't see job cost until month end.

Is AI consulting for construction worth it?

It is when the first step is priced, someone builds as well as advises, and the work moves one number you chose before you signed, such as hours on pay applications or days to answer an RFI. A roadmap with nothing built behind it rarely is.
