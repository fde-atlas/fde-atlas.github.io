Full text

On September 10, 2026, Tencent Cloud officially launched the Tencent Cloud ADP Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) certification program, along with the simultaneous launch of FDE partner recruitment. This is the industry's first engineer certification established for AI agent delivery roles.

This certification is aimed at professional engineers at Tencent Cloud partners and individual developers. Built on Tencent Cloud's AI agent development platform ADP, it focuses on capabilities such as requirements analysis, solution design, application building, delivery implementation, and security compliance in enterprise-level AI agent projects.

To prepare for the FDE, you can start with hands-on practice on ADP. During the event period, eligible new users who purchase designated packages will receive a complimentary 100 RMB PU credit. View FDE certification event details


I. Why FDE Is Needed

Once AI agents enter enterprises, the complexity of projects mainly comes from real business environments.

What customers need is not just an application that can hold conversations, but a set of applications that can adapt to business processes, connect to enterprise data and systems, meet performance requirements, and run continuously. Engineers need to participate in requirements analysis, solution design, application development, project implementation, performance evals, and launch operations, and the project process also involves requirements around permissions, security, and compliance.

This places new capability requirements on talent.

Traditional AI application development skills can solve problems like model invocation, Prompt, RAG, or Agent building, but enterprise projects also require further solving questions such as "Is the solution correct?", "Can the project be delivered?", and "How to maintain it after launch?"

FDE is precisely an engineering role built around this layer of capability. Unlike traditional delivery, which leans toward document relay and passive scheduling, enterprise-level AI agent projects require engineers to go deep into the business field to complete system integration and continuous tuning:


II. What Capabilities Does the FDE Certification Focus On

The FDE certification covers several key capability areas in enterprise-level AI agent projects.

Scenario understanding and solution design, focusing on understanding customer business needs, judging AI agent applicability, and designing sound technical solutions.

AI and ADP application capabilities, requiring mastery of essential generative AI fundamentals and understanding of ADP's product positioning, core capabilities, and application building methods.

Industry solutions and project delivery, covering industry scenarios, data preparation, application implementation, performance evals, troubleshooting, version iteration, and launch operations.

Security compliance and responsible AI, covering data permissions, privacy protection, content safety, unauthorized access, model hallucinations, and generative AI compliance boundaries.

These capabilities all point to one goal: enabling engineers not only to build AI agent applications but also to advance them to the stage of actual enterprise use. III. What Value Does the FDE Certification Offer Individuals

The FDE certification first addresses the question of how enterprise-level AI agent delivery capabilities can be standardized and proven.

The official Tencent Cloud certification page also provides corresponding online learning content, exam guides, mock questions, and other materials.

After passing the certification, the relevant certification information can also enter Tencent Cloud's official technical talent system.

For individuals, the value of this certification is not just obtaining a certificate. Enterprise-level AI agent roles ultimately value actual project capabilities; certification can provide a unified proof of capability, while project experience, industry understanding, and delivery practice determine whether engineers can take on real business projects.


IV. FDE Certification and the Tencent Cloud ADP Partner Ecosystem

From the very beginning, the FDE certification has not only been aimed at individual engineers but is also connected to the building of Tencent Cloud ADP's partner ecosystem.

Tencent Cloud ADP service partners currently have different tiers: Certified, Preferred, and FDE. As partner tiers rise, the requirements for certified technical talent, project experience, and service capabilities also increase accordingly.

For enterprises, the significance of the FDE certification is therefore not just "employees gaining another certificate," but that it can be further used to build enterprise-level AI agent delivery teams.

Among these, AI applications, delivery operations, and FDE capabilities together constitute important technical capability components of partner teams. Enterprises can plan certifications based on their existing personnel structure, and then participate in the Tencent Cloud ADP partner system in combination with actual projects and service capabilities.

Tencent Cloud has opened the corresponding official application portals. For specific personnel requirements, enterprise conditions, project requirements, and review materials, please refer to the current rules on the application pages: Certified Partner Application, Preferred Partner Application, FDE Partner Application.

Along with launching the FDE certification, Tencent Cloud also announced professional enablement training for partners, aimed at improving the delivery capabilities of enterprise-level AI agent projects through real customer scenarios and project practice, along with support such as joint solution promotion and ecosystem events.

V. How to Take the FDE Certification

The FDE certification currently uses Prometric offline closed-book computer-based exams, with test centers located nationwide.

The exam consists of 60 objective questions, including 40 single-choice and 20 multiple-choice questions; the exam duration is 90 minutes, with a full score of 100 and a passing score of 70 or above. The current exam fee is 1,200 RMB per person, the certificate is valid for 2 years, and there are no other certificates required as prerequisites for registration.

After the exam is completed, theoretical exam scores are published according to the official process. The Tencent Cloud certification page indicates that theoretical exam scores are generally published within 3 days.

When registering, it is recommended to follow the latest rules shown on the Tencent Cloud certification details page and to check specific test seat availability through the Prometric page.

FAQ

Who is the FDE certification for?

It is mainly aimed at professional engineers at Tencent Cloud partners and individual developers, suitable for technical personnel in AI application development, solutions design, project delivery, and implementation.

Does registration require other Tencent Cloud certifications as prerequisites?

No, there are currently no other certificate prerequisites.

Is the exam online or offline?

It uses Prometric offline closed-book computer-based exams, with test centers in cities nationwide.

What is the exam fee?

The certification exam fee is 1,200 RMB per person, and if you do not pass, there is no retake opportunity.

How long is the certificate valid?

2 years. Industry knowledge changes rapidly and the platform is constantly updated, so continuous learning is required.

Where can I find official learning materials?

The Tencent Cloud official learning center provides corresponding courses and exam preparation materials, and you can also refer to the ADP official product documentation.
