FDX株式会社 · 2026-11-13 · 20 min read

Summary

This is a knowledge hub by Japan's FDX株式会社 (FDX Inc.) collecting 67 articles on Forward Deployed Engineers (FDE) and AI transformation (AX), organized into strategy, implementation, case studies, tech notes, and market trends. The FDE-focused pieces cover the role's Palantir origins, comparisons with SES providers, SIers, and strategy consultants, pricing, hiring, and organizational integration. Other articles address AI agents, evals, MCP, LLM routing, knowledge graphs, moving beyond PoC pilots, in-house capability building, and industry-specific AI adoption, aimed mainly at Japanese executives and transformation leads.

Key points

  • FDX Inc. publishes a 67-article knowledge base on FDE and AX topics.
  • FDE articles cover definitions, 8-axis comparisons with SES/SI/consulting, costs, and career paths.
  • One piece compares FDE organizations at Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Scale AI.
  • Technical notes span AI agent evals, MCP, LLM routing, knowledge graphs, and semantic layers.
  • Case studies cite results like ~90% CRM input time reduction and 83% AI-answered inquiries.
