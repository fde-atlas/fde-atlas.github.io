FDE Jobs in India: Infosys and TCS Build FDE Teams (27 Oct 2026)

FDE India · 2026-10-27 · 9 min read

Summary

India's FDE job market plateaued this week, with roughly 503 Glassdoor listings versus 504 the week before. The headline shift is domestic: Infosys CEO Salil Parekh said on the Q2 FY27 call that the firm is building FDE capability, while TCS plans to convert 1–1.5% of staff (about 5,900–8,900 people) into forward-deployed roles. Employer-posted pay mostly ranged from ₹8–25 LPA, topping out at ₹30–40 LPA at Antino Labs. Microsoft offered voluntary buyouts to about 60,000 employees including India, and Wipro shed 5,828 staff in H1 FY27, though neither is linked to FDE hiring. Gartner warns vendor FDE reliance risks lock-in and IP leakage.

Key points

  • Infosys is building FDE capability; TCS plans to move 5,900–8,900 staff into forward-deployed roles.
  • Glassdoor showed about 503 FDE listings in India, essentially flat week-on-week.
  • Employer-posted pay ranged from ₹7–10 LPA to ₹30–40 LPA, mostly ₹8–25 LPA.
  • Microsoft's 60,000-person buyout offer and Wipro's 5,828-person decline are unrelated to FDE hiring.
  • Gartner cautions that heavy vendor FDE reliance can cause lock-in and customer IP leakage.

The biggest FDE story for India this week comes from Indian IT itself. Infosys’s CEO told analysts the company is “building out the FDE capability”, and TCS is already moving staff into a forward-deployed model. Meanwhile FDE listings held steady at around 500 on Glassdoor, so the market is flat, not shrinking.

This Pulse builds on last Sunday’s baseline in FDE Jobs in India: Postings Cool After 19x Surge. We cover what changed in the counts, who posted new roles, what they pay, and what the IT services shift means for your next move.

Neurapath also flagged just 7 new roles this week, against 11 a week earlier. Glassdoor India’s Noida search showed 42 FDE listings in October 2026, which makes Delhi NCR a clear second hub after Bengaluru. Naukri and LinkedIn India totals are still not publicly reported in pages we could access.

Read this as a plateau. Career-page trackers drop roles as they fill or close, so a dip of eight roles in one week is not a trend on its own. We will watch whether it continues next Sunday.

Which companies posted FDE roles in India this week?

The mix keeps tilting towards global product firms running India teams, plus IT services and smaller Indian firms. Names on Glassdoor India’s FDE-title search this month include Cisco (Bengaluru, posted about 10 days ago), Atlassian (Principal FDE, Bengaluru, asking 5+ years of AI/ML solutions work), Apple (Bengaluru), Lenovo (Bengaluru), Databricks (remote and Bengaluru), Accenture (Gurgaon, 3–5 years asked), Kyndryl (Principal FDE, Mumbai), NTT DATA (Bengaluru) and Fractal (Senior/Lead FDE, Bengaluru). Wipro had a Chennai FDE listing posted within the last 24 hours.

Two newer listings are worth a closer look:

  • JLL, Bengaluru — a Forward Deployed AI Engineer role posted on 22 October, with pay listed at ₹10–15 LPA on JobFound. It is tagged “Fresher”, but its must-haves ask for shipped production agents, Claude Agent SDK or MCP experience, and several data stores. Treat it as an early-career role for people with real projects.
  • Kore.ai, Hyderabad — an FDE Technical Lead role asking 7+ years in enterprise software, platform or solutions engineering. Skills include RAG, multi-agent frameworks such as LangGraph or CrewAI, LLM evaluation, MCP servers and voice AI telephony. Pay is not listed.

For a breakdown of what these skill lists mean in practice, see Forward Deployed Engineer Skills: 6 Areas Indian Employers Want.

FDE pay in India: what employers posted this week

Most listings show only Glassdoor estimates, and many of those (₹4–5 lakh for a Principal FDE) are not credible. Employer-provided figures tell a clearer story. On Glassdoor India, Antino Labs posted ₹30–40 LPA, VIVANET in Noida ₹15–25 LPA, Route Mobile in Noida ₹8–20 LPA for a conversational-AI FDE, and PinnacleU HR in Bengaluru ₹7–10 LPA for 6 months to 1 year of experience. JLL’s Bengaluru role, as noted, lists ₹10–15 LPA.

Most employer-posted FDE pay this week sits between ₹8 and ₹25 LPA; higher bands are rarer. Source: Glassdoor India and JobFound, October 2026.

For context, The Register reported on 22 October that Levels.fyi puts median US FDE total pay at $216,250. Indian pay is not close to that, but the gap explains why global firms keep opening India FDE seats.

Indian IT moves into forward-deployed engineering

This is the shift to watch. On Infosys’s Q2 FY27 earnings call, published on 26 October, CEO Salil Parekh said the company is building out FDE capability, though he gave no headcount target. Infosys ended the quarter with 328,062 employees and hired about 13,000 freshers in the first half, against a full-year target of roughly 20,000. It also cut its FY27 revenue growth guidance to 1–2% in constant currency, CRN Asia reported.

TCS went further earlier. According to The Next Web in July, TCS plans to convert 1–1.5% of its associates into forward-deployed AI engineers — roughly 5,900 to 8,900 people. COO Aarthi Subramanian described the profile as “multi-skilled but deep in one particular area.” Analytics India Magazine reported that TCS calls these “rapid-build engineer” squads. One analyst told AIM that hiring true FDE-level talent laterally is hard for services firms.

What this means for you: much of India’s FDE demand may be met by internal moves at large IT firms, not only by outside hires. If you are at TCS or Infosys, internal FDE squads are a realistic route. If you are outside, the lateral bar is high, and firms will look for proven deployment work.

Hiring at the big five is uneven. Angel One reported on 24 October that TCS added a net 13,537 people in April–September 2026, Infosys 3,948, HCLTech 3,158 and Tech Mahindra 1,240, while Wipro’s headcount fell by 5,828.

TCS added the most staff in H1 FY27; Wipro shrank. Source: Angel One, 24 October 2026.

Layoffs and FDE hiring: this week, side by side

Microsoft: On 26 October, Microsoft offered voluntary buyouts to about 60,000 employees, roughly a quarter of its workforce, CBS News reported. The offer covers staff at vice-president level or below in Canada, India, the UK and the US. Data-centre, sales and marketing staff are excluded. It follows 4,800 job cuts in July.

Wipro: As above, its headcount fell by 5,828 in H1 FY27, per Angel One.

On the hiring side, FDE listings held near 500 on Glassdoor, and Infosys and TCS are both building FDE teams. No source we found links Microsoft’s buyouts or Wipro’s decline to FDE hiring, and this post makes no such claim. They are separate trends running in parallel.

Where global FDE demand is heading

The Register’s 22 October report sums up the vendor push. AWS has put $1 billion into a Forward Deployed Engineering unit. Anthropic has committed $100 million to training FDEs. SAP, Salesforce and Workday are also investing in the model alongside Accenture. AIM noted earlier that Microsoft set up a $2.5 billion “Frontier Company” unit with 6,000 engineers.

There is also a warning. Gartner told The Register that heavy reliance on vendor FDEs can create lock-in and risk leaking customer IP. It advises clear rules on governance, IP ownership and knowledge transfer. For Indian engineers, that means clients will value FDEs who document well and hand over cleanly — not only those who build fast.

What this means for you this week

  • Ask about internal FDE squads. If you work at TCS, Infosys or another large IT firm, ask your manager or internal job portal about forward-deployed or rapid-build teams. Both firms have said publicly that they are building them.
  • Filter for Noida and Gurgaon, not just Bengaluru. Glassdoor’s Noida search alone showed 42 FDE listings, with employer-posted pay up to ₹15–25 LPA at VIVANET.
  • Build one agent project with MCP. JLL and Kore.ai both name MCP, and Kore.ai adds evaluation and guardrails. A small agent that calls a real API, with an evaluation script, matches both listings. The FDE Fit Check shows which gaps to close first.
  • Check your experience band before applying. Roles range from 6 months (PinnacleU HR) to 7+ years (Kore.ai). Our guide on experience required for FDE roles in India explains how to position yourself for each band.

Frequently asked questions

Are Indian IT companies hiring forward deployed engineers?

Yes, increasingly. TCS has said it will move 1–1.5% of its associates into a forward-deployed model, and Infosys's CEO said in October 2026 that it is building out FDE capability. FDE-titled listings also appear from Wipro, Accenture, NTT DATA and Kyndryl on Glassdoor India.

How many FDE jobs are open in India this week?

Glassdoor showed about 503 forward deployed engineer listings in India in late October 2026, and about 100 with the exact 'Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)' title. Neurapath's tracker of employer career pages counted 131 roles from 74 companies.

What salary do FDE listings in India offer?

Employer-provided pay in listings seen this week ran from ₹7–10 LPA for an early-career Bengaluru role to ₹30–40 LPA at Antino Labs. Many listings show only Glassdoor estimates, which are often far lower than real offers, so rely on employer-provided figures.

Is Microsoft's buyout offer open to employees in India?

Yes. CBS News reported on 26 October 2026 that the voluntary exit offer covers employees at vice-president level or below in Canada, India, the UK and the US, with about 60,000 people eligible worldwide.
