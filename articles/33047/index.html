FDE Jobs in India: New Hyderabad Roles, Kyndryl Mainframe FDEs

FDE India · 2026-11-15 · 6 min read

Summary

This week's Indian FDE hiring news centers on Hyderabad. Saras Analytics posted a Forward Deployed Engineer role at ₹14–20 LPA for 2–4 years' experience, focused on dbt, BigQuery, SQL and Python. ServiceNow listed a Senior FDE – Flow role requiring 4+ years, half the experience of its other Hyderabad FDE posting. NeuraPath counted 75 open FDE roles across 24 companies on 15 November, 47 with the FDE title. Kyndryl added four FDE roles for mainframe modernization, applying agent frameworks to legacy systems. Qualcomm's India layoffs, reportedly 5–6% of staff, are spreading beyond Bengaluru, though no source links them to FDE hiring.

Hyderabad had the most useful FDE news this week: a mid-band role with posted pay at Saras Analytics and a ServiceNow FDE job that asks for half the experience of its earlier one. Kyndryl put the FDE title on mainframe modernisation work, and Qualcomm’s India cuts are reported to be moving beyond Bengaluru.

The week in four numbers

The NeuraPath job aggregator showed 75 open Forward Deployed Engineer roles in India on 15 November, from 24 companies. Of those, 47 carry an FDE job title; the rest are aligned roles such as solutions or deployment engineering. Eight were new this week.

That is one board’s view. Our last Pulse used Glassdoor and Indeed counts, which measure differently, so compare like with like week to week rather than across boards.

Roughly six in ten roles on the board use the FDE title itself. Source: NeuraPath, 15 Nov 2026.

Hyderabad adds two FDE roles, one with posted pay

Saras Analytics, a data and AI analytics firm for ecommerce and omnichannel brands, posted a Forward Deployed Engineer role in Hyderabad on 12 November, according to JobFound. The header shows ₹14–20 LPA and 2–4 years, though the body asks for 3+ years (typically 3–6) of hands-on data and software work. Hours run noon to 9 PM IST.

The stack is data-first: dbt, BigQuery and Git for the data build, SQL and Python, and a BI tool such as Tableau or Power BI. The FDE also owns discovery, requirements and client demos, and builds on the company’s own Saras IQ product. If you are a data engineer, this is the clearest example yet of the path in our data engineer to FDE guide.

ServiceNow lists a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer – Flow role in Hyderabad on its careers site, dated 10 November, with a flexible or remote work setting. It asks for 4+ years in a customer-facing technical role. The ServiceNow FDE role we profiled earlier asks for 8+ years. The Flow role is integration-heavy: SSO with SAML, OAuth, Okta and Entra ID, REST APIs and webhooks, and automations across Jira, Workday, Zoom and Microsoft 365. No pay is shown.

Here is how Saras compares with other posted pay in new India FDE listings this month, all from JobFound: Ascendion in Chennai at ₹6–8 LPA (tagged fresher, posted 7 November), Zorba AI in Bengaluru at ₹10–25 LPA (2+ years, 3 November) and Sycamore in Bengaluru at ₹28–36 LPA (5–12 years, 5 November).

Saras sits in the middle of this month’s posted bands. Source: JobFound listings, Nov 2026.

More Hyderabad employers are on our Hyderabad FDE jobs page.

Mainframe modernisation gets the FDE title

NeuraPath listed four new Kyndryl roles this week titled Forward Deployed Engineer – Mainframe Modernization & Cloud Transformation. An earlier Kyndryl Modernization Engineer (FDE) listing on The Muse names Bengaluru or flexible work and asks for Python (or C#, Node.js or TypeScript), agent frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph or Semantic Kernel, RAG with vector databases, and Azure, AWS or GCP with Docker and Kubernetes.

This is a new kind of FDE job in India: applying agents to legacy estates rather than greenfield AI products. It fits the wider services push covered in our IT services FDE guide. Engineers with COBOL, mainframe or migration experience now have a direct route to the title.

Qualcomm’s cuts spread past Bengaluru

People Matters reported on 12 November that Qualcomm’s India cuts, which began in Bengaluru on 9 November, are expected to extend to Hyderabad, Delhi NCR and Chennai. Citing Moneycontrol, it puts the affected share at about 5–6% of an estimated 20,000 India staff; the company has not confirmed a number.

Reported severance is three months’ salary plus tenure-based pay, with an extra month for signing exit papers within three days. Qualcomm has not confirmed these terms. Nation Press says senior engineers in RF, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and GNSS teams are most affected. Neither report links the cuts to FDE or AI deployment hiring.

Before next Sunday: three moves

  • Data engineers: apply to Saras with a dbt or BigQuery project you can demo end to end, discovery to dashboard.
  • Integration and SSO specialists with 4–7 years: the ServiceNow Flow role rewards Okta, SAML and webhook work. Lead your resume with integrations you shipped for customers.
  • Engineers leaving Qualcomm’s affected teams: if you have customer-facing or field experience, check whether you fit; the FDE Fit Check takes a few minutes. Take the severance timeline seriously and read your papers before signing.
