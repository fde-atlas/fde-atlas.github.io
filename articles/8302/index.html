Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer Interview (No Offer): The recruiter reached out to me

Taro · 2017-02-14 · 2 min read

Summary

A candidate recounts a negative Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer interview in New York. A recruiter reached out via LinkedIn, but the process was disorganized: calls were missed three or four times and the on-site was rescheduled twice. Technical questions were easier than at other companies, and interviewers—mostly young white men—seemed disengaged and uninterested in the candidate's non-traditional background. A form rejection arrived two days after the on-site with no feedback. Aggregated data from 36 interview experiences shows a 14% pass rate, with 56% positive sentiment. Current employee reviews praise smart coworkers and interesting challenges but cite long hours, no bonuses, no 401k matching, no raises with promotions, and technically unfulfilling FDE work.

Key points

  • Recruiter outreach, but calls flaked on 3-4 times and on-site rescheduled twice
  • Technical questions were relatively easy; interviewers seemed checked out
  • Form rejection two days later with no feedback
  • Only 14% pass rate across 36 reported interview experiences
  • Employee reviews cite long hours and no raises with promotions

Full text

February 14, 2017

Negative ExperienceNo Offer

The recruiter reached out to me via LinkedIn and was very pleasant. However, the rest of the process was pretty frustrating. I got flaked on for various phone calls 3 or 4 times (after making arrangements to work from home to take a call) and the on-site was rescheduled twice.

I found the technical questions on the easier side, compared to other companies I interviewed with. Interviewers were exclusively 20-something white dudes, and (with one exception) they seemed pretty checked out. I have something of a non-traditional background for a SWE and the recruiter thought it would be relevant to the FDSE role, but I didn't get a lot of interest on that front from my interviewers. At the end of the on-site, another recruiter (kind of awkwardly) assured me that there would be some further steps. I got a form letter rejection 2 days later and no further feedback or communication.
