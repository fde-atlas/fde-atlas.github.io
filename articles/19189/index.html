Buyer's guide5 questions

What is comparative rating software?

Comparative rating software lets an agent type an applicant's details once and get quotes from several carriers. It saves the work of filling in the same form on each carrier's site. Most tools also prefill data, compare quotes side by side and produce a proposal. Some can bind the policy from inside the tool. Personal lines raters such as EZLynx, PL Rating, Epic Quotes and TurboRater describe home and auto quoting. Commercial tools such as Tarmika, Semsee, Bold Penguin and CoverForce describe lines like BOP, general liability and workers' comp.

How is a comparative rater different from an agency management system?

A management system holds your clients, policies, documents and commissions. A rater gets you the price. The two are often sold together. EZLynx sells its rater and management system on one platform, and Applied describes Epic Quotes inside Applied Epic. Others plug into a system you already have. Vertafore lists AMS360, Sagitta and QQCatalyst for PL Rating, and HawkSoft's rater page lists ten third-party raters it can send client data to. Check that your management system is on the list before you pay.

Why are personal and commercial raters different tools?

On the pages we opened, the two groups are described differently. Personal lines tools talk about real-time quotes for home and auto from hundreds of carriers. Commercial tools talk about submissions, appetite, class codes and ACORD forms. Semsee offers a NAICS tool and class code finder, Bold Penguin turns emailed submissions into structured data, and EZLynx checks carrier appetite with a NAICS lookup. If your book is mixed, ask each vendor how it handles the line you write most.

How should I read a carrier count?

Carefully. The counts on these pages are the vendors' own and they are not counted the same way. EZLynx says over 330, PL Rating says 300+ and also 320+ and Epic Quotes says more than 525 insurers. QuoteSweep's pricing page lists 15 carriers on Solo and 25 on Pro. CoverForce shows 20+ on one plan and 60+ on its home page. Ask for the list of carriers in your state and check that your top five are on it.

What does comparative rating software cost?

Most vendors here do not publish a price. EZLynx, PL Rating, Epic Quotes, TurboRater, Bold Penguin, Tarmika, Zywave and Commercial Submissions showed no dollar figure on the pages we opened. The ones that do are Semsee at $0 and $50 per user per month, CoverForce at $99 per month after a 60-day credit, and QuoteSweep at $49 to $299 per month. Bold Penguin describes a percentage of direct written premium, a platform fee or a mix.

How to choose

  • 1First, decide which lines you quote most. A book that is mostly home and auto points to a personal lines rater. A book of restaurants, contractors and offices points to a commercial tool.
  • 2Second, check the carrier list for your state. Ask each vendor for a written list and check that your five most-used carriers are on it, quoting live and not only linked.
  • 3Third, check your management system. Vertafore lists AMS360, Sagitta and QQCatalyst, Applied describes Epic, and HawkSoft lists ten raters. Several commercial tools name no system on their pages.
  • 4Fourth, ask for the price in writing, with the unit. Per user, per month, per submission, or a share of premium are all in use here.
  • 5Finally, run ten real quotes from last month. Compare the time taken and the carriers that answered with what your team did by hand.

Frequently asked6 answers

Which comparative rater has the most carriers?

On the pages we opened, Zywave Enterprise Quoting claims 1,200+ carrier partnerships and Applied Epic Quotes says more than 525 insurers. EZLynx says over 330 and PL Rating 300+. These are vendor claims counted in different ways, and Zywave's is for a panel setup that takes 6 months or more.

Is there a free comparative rater?

Semsee lists an Essential plan at $0 with appetite checking, ACORD forms and submission to multiple carriers. QuoteSweep's home page lists a Free tier with 3 submissions in total, though its quoting is in private beta. EZLynx offers a free trial, and the length is not stated.

What is the difference between EZLynx and PL Rating?

Both describe personal lines raters with 300+ carriers. EZLynx runs on the same platform as its own management system and adds commercial submissions. PL Rating links to AMS360, Sagitta, QQCatalyst and over 20 other systems and lists instant bind in the rater. Neither page shows a price.

Can I bind a policy from the rater?

Some say so. PL Rating and Commercial Submissions list instant bind in the rater. CoverForce says quote and bind across 20+ carriers on your own appointments. Bold Penguin says you can quote and bind through North Peak Access, and Semsee lists bind in platform.

Do I need new carrier appointments?

It depends on the tool. Bold Penguin says no carrier appointment or production minimum is needed through its market access. CoverForce's Independent Agency plan is for quoting on your own appointments. Semsee's Essential plan lists access to Semsee appointed carriers, and Professional adds quoting on your direct appointed carriers.

How often is this list updated?

We re-score every 90 days, and the next review is 2027-01-25. Vendors change prices and carrier lists, so confirm every figure on the vendor's own page before you buy.

  • Carrier counts are the vendors' own and are not counted the same way. Several pages give more than one number for the same product, and we say so in the entry.
  • Most vendors here publish no price. Entries without a price score lower on Pricing Transparency by design. That is a statement about the page, not the product. Semsee, CoverForce and QuoteSweep rank partly because their pages print numbers.
  • Scores are an AI editorial judgement against the published rubric, using only the pages we read. Savings and speed claims, such as Zywave's 75% and Semsee's 48 minutes per client, are the vendors' own and were not tested. Scores are not a measure of customer satisfaction.
