FDE Certification

Original title: FDE検定 | 日本FDE協会

日本Fde協会 · 2026-05-01 · 1 min read

Summary

The Japan FDE Association has announced the FDE Certification, a private (non-governmental) exam that validates the skills of Forward Deployed Engineers who use AI to solve business problems in the field. It defines five grades, from Level 5 (assistant, beginners) up to Level 1 (architect, those who design FDE organizations and strategy), each with defined competency levels. The certification is positioned as complementary to existing IT credentials such as IT Passport, G Test, and PMP, not a replacement. Details of the system are not yet finalized; updates will be shared with registered members first.

Key points

  • Japan FDE Association runs a private FDE skill certification, not a national qualification.
  • It certifies the ability to integrate AI into field operations and deliver results.
  • Five grades from Level 5 (assistant) to Level 1 (architect) map to career stages.
  • Explicitly complements, rather than replaces, existing IT certifications like IT Passport and G Test.
  • Program details are pending; registered members get updates first.

Full text

The FDE Certification is a private certification administered by the Japan FDE Association that certifies the skills of engineers (Forward Deployed Engineers) who solve business challenges using AI. It is not a national qualification.

What is required of an FDE is not just technical skill, nor just business understanding. It is the ability to integrate AI into real-world operations and deliver results. We have designed a certification system that recognizes this capability across grades from Level 5 to Level 1.

GRADES

Grade System

GradeTitleTarget AudienceCertified Capability Level
Level 5AssistantBeginners interested in becoming an FDEUnderstands FDE fundamentals and basic concepts of AI utilization
Level 4OperatorPractitioners who have started using AI and IT tools in their workCan apply AI tools to business operations and achieve routine process improvements
Level 3PractitionerFDEs who solve problems on-site with customersCan identify customers' business challenges and design and implement solutions using AI and technology
Level 2SeniorFDEs who lead multiple projectsCan lead complex projects and drive organization-wide business transformation
Level 1ArchitectTop-tier professionals who design FDE organizations and systemsCan formulate FDE strategy, build talent development frameworks, and design overall business architecture

RELATION

Relationship with Existing Certifications

The FDE Certification does not negate or replace existing IT certifications (IT Passport, G Test, Registered Information Security Specialist, PMP, etc.); it certifies the skills unique to FDEs: "solving problems using AI in real customer environments."

We will announce further details of the system here as soon as they are decided. The latest information will be shared with registered members on a priority basis.
