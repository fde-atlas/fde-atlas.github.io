Senior Forward Deployed Engineer | Icarus | Los Angeles, CA

Zero G Talent · 2026-03-24 · 3 min read

Summary

Zero G Talent lists a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer opening in Los Angeles for Icarus, an aerospace startup building solar-powered aircraft that fly at 60,000 ft for weeks, marketed as a $100K-per-unit 'cell tower in the sky' for defense. The role deploys with aircraft and ground systems to test ranges and customer sites, owning integration, field debugging across avionics, RF, power, software, and mechanical systems, flight tests, and customer training. Candidates need 2+ years of field experience with real hardware and comfort operating independently in remote, high-pressure settings. Hiring involves a technical interview, a one-on-one with the CEO, and an in-person team meeting.

Key points

  • Icarus builds solar-powered drones flying at 60,000 ft for weeks, priced around $100K per unit.
  • The FDE role covers on-site integration, cross-domain debugging, flight tests, and customer support.
  • Applicants need 2+ years of hands-on field experience in aerospace, robotics, defense, or hardware startups.
  • The company emphasizes a small, engineer-heavy team with minimal management overhead and full subsystem ownership.
  • Interview process: technical interview, CEO one-on-one, in-person team meeting.

What you need

Icarus was founded on the belief that the Stratosphere is the Forgotten Frontier and that reclaiming it will define the next era of aerospace and what is possible from the sky. We build solar-powered aircraft that fly at 60,000 ft for weeks at a time, delivering persistent, affordable, and scalable presence from this layer of the atmosphere.

Mission

Re-establish and maintain dominance in the Stratosphere.

What you’ll do

  • Deploy forward with aircraft, ground systems, and engineers to real-world test sites and customer environments
  • Own on-site integration, troubleshooting, and rapid iteration of flight hardware and software
  • Act as the connective tissue between flight operations, engineering, and customers
  • Debug avionics, RF, power, software, and mechanical issues in the field under time pressure
  • Execute flight tests, demos, and exercises with minimal support and high autonomy
  • Collect ground truth, flight data, and operator feedback and drive it back into the product
  • Support customer training, mission planning, and operational workflows

What we’re looking for

  • 2+ years working on real systems in the field (aerospace, robotics, defense, autonomy, or hardware startups)
  • Strong hands-on debugging skills across hardware and software
  • Comfort working across disciplines: avionics, RF, embedded software, mechanical systems, and ops
  • Ability to operate independently in ambiguous, high-pressure environments
  • Strong communication skills with engineers, operators, and customers
  • Willingness to travel frequently and operate out of test ranges, airfields, and remote locations

Why Icarus

  • Meaningful equity
  • Top market salary and bonuses
  • Health, dental, and vision insurance
  • Factory in El Segundo
  • Real ownership, real hardware, real flight
  • Small team, massive responsibility
  • High signal (engineering) to noise (management) culture
  • Dream desk setup
  • Daily lunch
  • Unlimited celsius

This is not a normal engineering job

  • We are pioneers, not corporate managers. Icarus deliberately optimizes for a high signal (engineers) to noise (management) ratio, where engineering, flight, and execution matter more than process, politics, or management overhead
  • You will work side by side with the CEO and a small team of world-class builders. You will own your subsystem end to end. Design it. Build it. Break it. Fix it. Validate it in the desert. Fly it. Then do it again, faster
  • We expect full extreme ownership. This is a high-trust, no-BS team, and everyone pulls their weight
  • We are hiring across the stack: mechanical, aerodynamics, RF, GNC, electrical, avionics, power electronics, energy storage, software, and systems
  • We want builders. No hand-holding. No fluff. No fake projects. No slide decks. No sitting in meetings all day. Just raw engineering, real hardware, and real flight
  • You must thrive under pressure, learn fast, troubleshoot in the field, and push hardware to its limits. We move fast. We go to Mojave at 4 am in the morning. We expect results and an obsession with quality and speed
  • You will be a core executor of flight hardware, taking systems from concept to reality and driving rapid iteration loops. You will work across avionics, power electronics, energy storage, integration, and production bring-up. If you want real ownership, real hardware, and real flight milestones, this is it

No tourists. No bystanders. Only builders.
