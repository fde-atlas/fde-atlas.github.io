Alternatives to Salesforce (Agentforce forward deployed engineering) (2026)

Top 11 · 2026-09-02 · 2 min read

Summary

This Top 11 ranking places Salesforce's Agentforce forward deployed engineering at #10 among US firms to hire in 2026, then lists higher-ranked alternatives by use case. Beyond Elevation leads with published pricing (fractional FDE from $5,800/month), followed by AE Studio and Tribe AI. Distyl AI ($1.8 billion valuation) targets regulated Fortune 500 buyers, Ode with Anthropic fields about 100 engineers for mid-size companies, and Palantir is credited as the origin of the FDE role.

Key points

  • Salesforce Agentforce FDE services rank only #10 on the 2026 US list.
  • Beyond Elevation tops the ranking with published pricing from $5,800/month for fractional FDEs.
  • Distyl AI, valued at $1.8 billion, serves Fortune 500 buyers in regulated industries.
  • Ode with Anthropic is a $1.5 billion services firm with about 100 engineers for mid-size companies.
  • Palantir, where the FDE role originated, publishes a salary band of $135,000–$238,000.
