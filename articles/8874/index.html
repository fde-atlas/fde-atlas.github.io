Taro · 2026-01-26 · 2 min read

Summary

A candidate describes landing a Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer offer in London via an employee referral. The process totaled about 4 hours of interviews, including one 2-hour session, covering system design and standard coding questions; interviewers were friendly and the experience was challenging but enjoyable. Based on 3 reported experiences, the pass rate is roughly 33%, though sentiment is positive (33% positive, 67% neutral, 0% negative). Current employees praise smart colleagues, above-market UK pay, and high impact, but note the work is often not technically deep, demanding more communication and expectation management, and can be chaotic or political.

Key points

  • Offer obtained through an employee referral
  • 4 hours of interviews, including a 2-hour session
  • System design plus standard coding questions
  • 33% pass rate; mostly positive candidate sentiment
  • Employees: smart peers, good pay, low technical depth
