Michael Couch: writing, essays, and case studies (couch.cx)

Michael Couch | couch.cx · 2025 · 18 min read

Summary

This is Michael Couch's personal site collecting his essays on forward-deployed engineering and enterprise AI adoption. His central thesis is that durable advantage lies in the harness around models—interfaces, memory, permissions, evals, workflows, and operations—rather than in model choice. He proposes a minimal four-person frontier team (Frontier Lead, Creative Technical Lead, Applied Builder, Domain Champion), argues FDE teams should make enterprises independent of them rather than indispensable, and extends the same operating system to hardware, factories, and air-gapped environments, covering the Agent Skills open standard, evals, kill switches, and open-weight portability.

Key points

  • The harness around models matters more than the model itself.
  • A minimal four-role frontier pod beats bloated AI teams.
  • FDE teams should transfer capability, not create dependency.
  • Agent Skills folders are now open infrastructure; evals and kill switches are essential.
  • The same FDE playbook applies to hardware, plants, and air-gapped sites.
