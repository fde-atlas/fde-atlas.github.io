FDE Jobs in India: Gurugram Roles Open, Qualcomm Cuts Begin

FDE India · 2026-11-12 · 8 min read

Summary

Gurugram dominated this week's India FDE news. Shipsy lists a Forward Deployed Engineer role for 1–4 years' experience with 30–50% client-site travel, while a UnifyApps FDE listing on HireDoor shows ₹20–45 LPA for 1–5 years. Cursor is hiring a Regional Director, Forward Deployed Engineering for India, based in Bengaluru, Mumbai or New Delhi, to lead 6–8 FDEs and train partner engineers at GSIs. Glassdoor India's FDE counts (564 broad, 125 exact-title) are flat for a third reading; Indeed returns 1,097 results but includes unrelated jobs. Separately, Qualcomm began India layoffs in Bengaluru on 9 November, with roughly 1,000 roles reportedly at risk, though no source links the cuts to FDE hiring.

Key points

  • Shipsy and UnifyApps both opened early-career FDE roles in Gurugram.
  • UnifyApps' listing shows ₹20–45 LPA for 1–5 years' experience.
  • Cursor's new India director will lead 6–8 FDEs across three cities.
  • Glassdoor India FDE counts stayed flat for a third consecutive reading.
  • Qualcomm's India layoffs (~1,000 roles) are unrelated to FDE hiring.

Gurugram had the most useful FDE news this week. Shipsy and UnifyApps both advertise forward deployed engineer roles there for engineers with roughly one to five years of experience, and Cursor is hiring a director to build an India FDE team across Bengaluru, Mumbai and New Delhi. On the other side of the ledger, Qualcomm started India layoffs in Bengaluru on 9 November.

This Pulse follows Tuesday’s remote ₹20–28 LPA role and ServiceNow cuts. Below: the counts, the Gurugram roles, Cursor’s India team, and the week’s layoff news.

FDE job listings in India: flat for a third reading

Glassdoor India’s search pages still show the same totals they have shown since 5 November:

  • 564 listings for the broad forward deployed engineer search.
  • 125 listings for the exact Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) title.

We also checked Indeed India this week. Its search for “forward deployment engineer” returns 1,097 jobs. Treat that as an upper bound, not a like-for-like count: the results mix genuine FDE roles (Databricks, Cartesia, Pattem Digital, Infosys, Kyndryl, Persistent) with unrelated jobs such as a mechanical “field deployment” engineer in Vijayawada.

Indeed’s broader keyword match catches non-FDE jobs, so the exact-title count is the better trend line. Indeed India and Glassdoor India, Nov 2026.

Naukri and LinkedIn India FDE totals are still not publicly reported in pages we could access. Three flat Glassdoor readings in a row confirm the October rise has levelled off, but individual employers keep posting, especially in Delhi NCR.

Gurugram’s early-career FDE roles

Shipsy: Forward Deployed Engineer, 1–4 years, heavy travel

Shipsy, a logistics software company with offices in Gurugram, Bengaluru and Dubai, lists a Forward Deployed Engineer role on its careers page under its Growth Strategy team. Shipsy says it serves more than 150 enterprise customers.

Detail What the listing says
Location Gurugram, with customer-site travel about 30–50% of the time
Experience 1–4 years in the header; the profile section asks for 3+ years of production engineering, or a consulting/GCC background
Education Tier-1 institute (IIT, NIT, BITS or equivalent)
Posted pay Not shown
Work Configure Shipsy to each customer’s operations; integrate with SAP, Oracle, TMS and WMS
AI work Build and deploy agents such as dispatch copilots and invoice reconciliation agents

The role owns the path from pilot to go-live, so it is closer to a delivery engineer than a pre-sales one. Note the two routes in: an engineering profile, or a consulting profile from firms like MBB or a banking GCC. That second track is unusual and worth a look if you are a technically strong consultant.

UnifyApps: ₹20–45 LPA for 1–5 years

A Forward Deployed Engineer listing for UnifyApps in Gurugram on HireDoor shows posted pay of ₹20–45 LPA, for 1–5 years of experience, on-site and full-time. HireDoor showed it as posted about three weeks ago. Skills named are GenAI, LLMs and API integration, with work on turning customer problems into product solutions.

Two cautions. First, HireDoor is an aggregator that routes applications through a referral form, so confirm the role on UnifyApps’ own careers page. Second, an earlier UnifyApps Forward Deployed Software Engineer listing on Foundit (1–3 years, Tier-1 degree required) is marked closed. If the new listing is genuine, it is one of the highest posted bands we have seen for engineers with under five years. Our Delhi NCR FDE jobs guide has been updated with both employers.

Entry points start below a year at PinnacleU. Indeed India, Shipsy careers page and HireDoor, Nov 2026.

For comparison, PinnacleU’s Bengaluru FDE role on Indeed India still asks for 6 months to 1 year at ₹7–10 LPA. Kyndryl also appears in Indeed’s results with a Forward Deployment Engineer role in Gurugram, without pay. If you are a fresher or have one to two years, our fresher FDE guide explains how to show the client-facing skills these listings want.

Cursor is building an India FDE team

Cursor is advertising a Regional Director, Forward Deployed Engineering – India, listed on Vibehackers and on Cursor’s own careers site. The role is based in Bengaluru, Mumbai or New Delhi and asks for 8+ years leading technical teams that ship to production.

What the listing tells us about Cursor’s India plans:

  • Team size: the director will lead and grow a team of 6–8 FDEs working on strategic customer projects.
  • Partner track: the director will build a programme to train FDEs at GSIs and consulting partners. That points to Indian IT services firms learning to deploy Cursor for their own clients.
  • Commercial role: the job covers technical discovery with sales, delivery quality and C-suite relationships.
  • Hands-on bar: Python and JavaScript/TypeScript, plus experience debugging production AI workflows.

No pay is shown. Cursor already lists an India FDE role across the same three cities, which we covered on 1 November. A director with a defined team size suggests that hiring is now a planned team, not a one-off. If you are a senior engineer in Bengaluru or NCR, the 6–8 FDE seats under this role are worth tracking.

Trend: services firms talk up their own FDEs

Altimetrik, an AI engineering services firm, said on 11 November that its “forward-deployed engineers” help clients move agentic AI from pilot to production. The line came in a sponsored press release about a Google Cloud Gemini healthcare launch. It gives no headcount, so read it as positioning, not a hiring number.

Large platforms keep posting senior India FDE roles. A Cloud Soft Solutions roundup lists Databricks roles in Bengaluru and remote India from 5+ to 15+ years, and two MongoDB roles in Gurugram (Forward Deployed Engineer, 5+ years; Forward Deployed Architect, 8+ years), all without disclosed pay. See our Databricks company guide for that employer’s India FDE ladder.

Layoffs and FDE hiring this week, side by side

These are reported next to FDE hiring for context. No source links any of these cuts to forward deployed engineer roles.

  • Qualcomm: Business Today reports that the first India exits began at the Bengaluru facility on 9 November, affecting senior engineers among others, and are expected to extend to Hyderabad, Delhi NCR and Chennai. Both Business Today and The Week put potentially affected India roles at about 1,000, citing earlier reports. Qualcomm has not confirmed a number, and Business Today says the final figure “could be considerably lower”.
  • Why: People Matters reported in October that Qualcomm’s CEO memo described “performance-focused” global cuts, citing performance, business priorities and skills. The Week links the restructuring to weaker handset revenue. Business Today notes Qualcomm wants to grow in automotive and AI, but neither article says the cuts are caused by AI.
  • Severance: The Week cites unverified employee posts describing three months’ salary plus one month per year of service. Qualcomm has not commented.
  • ServiceNow: no new figures since Tuesday’s update.

Business Today says Qualcomm has an estimated 20,000 employees in India, so even 1,000 exits would be a small share. For senior engineers affected, FDE roles that value deep systems debugging and customer work are one direction to weigh.

What this means for you this week

  • In NCR with 1–5 years? Apply to Gurugram first. Shipsy and UnifyApps both want early-career engineers who can work on site with clients. Expect a Tier-1 degree filter at both, so lead with shipped integrations if you do not have one.
  • Prepare for travel and integration questions. Shipsy expects 30–50% travel and SAP/TMS integrations. Practise explaining one integration you built end to end; our interview questions guide has a framework.
  • Senior and at a services firm? Watch Cursor’s partner track. If your employer becomes a Cursor partner, partner-FDE training could be your route into AI-native delivery work. Check where you stand with the FDE Fit Check.
  • Affected by Qualcomm’s cuts? Map your systems and debugging experience to FDE job descriptions, and compare pay on our salary page before you negotiate.
