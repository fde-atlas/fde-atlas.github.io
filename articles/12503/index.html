Questions about access and payment

A free account gives you 20 answers per topic, free and sign-in course lessons, foundational and core concepts, starter labs and easy practice tests. Premium opens every answer, every course lesson and concept, and all lab missions and practice-test levels. Paid Premium also includes the complete Premium PDF guides and companion files. Your progress and bookmarks work on both plans.

If you came from a locked question, concept, lesson or lab, use the continuation link after payment to return to it. Otherwise, open Courses and choose a course for your current goal. Foundations of Forward Deployed Engineering is a starting point for the role. Mark lessons as read so you can continue from Courses or your account next time. Your account also contains the guide downloads.

Every FDE Lab mission is included, from debugging API integrations and constraining agents to recovering a failing system during a timed incident. You work in a simulated customer environment in your browser, with a terminal, logs, traces and metrics. Nothing to install. Missions include checked objectives, progressive hints and a debrief to help you explain your decisions.

Paid Premium includes all Premium PDF guides and their companion downloads: worked take-home solutions, case-round practice kits and executable system-design examples. Download them while your access is active and keep your copies for personal study. On this website, videos follow their written page's access requirements: free pages are open, sign-in lessons need a free account, and Premium lessons require Premium. The videos are also public on our YouTube channel. Not every written lesson has a video yet. Referral Premium covers online study, including lesson videos, but excludes guide PDFs and their companion files.

No. One payment unlocks six months of full access. Nothing recurring, no card stored, nothing to remember to cancel.

Your account reverts to free. You keep your progress, bookmarks, and the free answers, and you can re-up for another six months whenever you want, never automatically.

Available payment options: $25 by card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) through Razorpay; $25 with PayPal; ₹2,000 by UPI, card, or netbanking through Razorpay. Each unlocks the same Premium access. We never see or store your card or UPI details. Access is tied to your Google account and unlocks once payment is confirmed.

Yes. Free answers in every topic are fully unlocked, including complete code, diagrams, and tables, so you can judge the depth before spending anything.

Write to us and we will extend it. If your loop lands a few days past your six months, email support at fdeinterviews.com and we will make sure you are covered through the interview, at no charge. Same for anything else that goes wrong: billing trouble, access that did not unlock, an answer you think is mistaken. A person reads every message.

All sales are final, so there are no refunds. Payment grants access to the Premium curriculum, answers, guides and practice materials immediately. Try the free answers, video lessons and guide samples before paying so you can judge whether the depth is right for you.
