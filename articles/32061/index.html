Lemei Packaging case study: Nancal Technology partners with Amazon Web Services to bring FDEs truly into customer business processes

Original title: 乐美包装案例：能科科技携手亚马逊云科技，让FDE真正进入客户业务流程

同花顺财经 · 2026-10-23 · 7 min read

Summary

Lemei Packaging ran a generative AI agent POC with Amazon Web Services and Nengke Technology. Using an FDE model, Nengke's team embedded on-site, narrowed 13 candidate needs from 6 departments down to 4 scenarios, and delivered four assistants—financial profit analysis, IT ticket creation, resume screening, and HR policy Q&A—on the company intranet in about 40 days. Financial analysis Q&A exceeded 90% accuracy, monthly reporting dropped from 3–5 days to hours, and ticket creation fell to under 30 seconds. The project also produced reusable assets like MCP services and business rules for future scenarios such as CRM and warehousing.

Key points

  • 13 candidate needs from 6 departments were narrowed to 4 POC scenarios.
  • Four AI assistants were built and deployed on the intranet in roughly 40 days.
  • Financial analysis Q&A hit over 90% accuracy; monthly reports cut from 3–5 days to hours.
  • IT ticket creation time dropped from 5–10 minutes to under 30 seconds.
  • Reusable assets (MCP services, rules) enable expansion to CRM and warehousing.

After generative AI enters the enterprise, the truly difficult part is often not proving what the model "can do," but finding business problems worth solving, connecting data, rules, systems, and people, and ultimately getting AI into real workflows.

As its business scale continues to expand, Lemei Packaging has accumulated growing amounts of data, rules, and knowledge in internal operations such as finance, IT, and human resources, and the company began thinking about how to further improve the efficiency of business analysis, process handling, and knowledge services. For this project, Lemei Packaging did not treat it as a simple AI tool trial.

"The current stage belongs to Lemei's AI transformation journey. This is not merely a technology application, but a systematic transformation oriented toward continuously creating value for customers, covering business models, organizational models, and ways of thinking."

— Lemei Packaging

Lemei hopes that AI can ultimately deliver measurable results in efficiency, cost, quality, customer value, or business returns. Around this goal, Lemei Packaging joined hands with Amazon (AMZN) Web Services and Nengke Technology (603859) to launch a generative AI agent (886099) POC.

1

Subtracting from 13 requirements to find the problems truly worth solving with AI

Enterprises never lack ideas for AI applications; what is truly hard is judging what deserves priority investment.

After the project kicked off, the Nengke Technology (603859) FDE team went deep into Lemei's business sites and conducted requirements research across multiple departments. From 13 candidate requirements gathered across 6 departments, and after internal customer review, 5 scenarios entered the POC scope, and ultimately 4 scenarios—financial profit analysis, intelligent ticket creation, intelligent resume screening, and HR policy knowledge Q&A—were identified as deep delivery targets. The project did not pursue the number of scenarios, but adhered to the principle of "genuinely usable and able to verify business value."

All four problems came from real friction points in daily work. The finance department relied on tools like Power BI for profit analysis, but when management asked follow-up questions, they often needed to re-pull tables for verification; IT ticket submission required filling in many fields, creating a high barrier for employees; the recruiting team had to screen resumes from different channels one by one; and when employees looked up policies on leave, reimbursement, benefits, and so on, they had to go through multiple documents, with many questions ultimately ending up back at HR. This project adopted the FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) model. Nengke Technology (603859) extended the FDE delivery boundary from "completing a system" to business outcomes, spanning scenario identification, prototype validation, engineering integration, deployment, user adoption, and results acceptance around business goals, letting the technical team truly enter the business site rather than developing item by item against a fixed requirements list.

2

Amazon Web Services provides the foundation; Nengke Technology makes technology truly enter the business

When generative AI enters core business scenarios such as finance, human resources, and IT, the security, data governance, and scalability of cloud services and generative AI services become foundational conditions. Lemei Packaging stated that in choosing Amazon (AMZN) Web Services, it mainly valued their capabilities in security and scalability, as well as their support for Lemei's own requirements in information security, data governance, and technical standards.

Based on this need, the project formed an overall architecture that balances enterprise data boundaries with generative AI capabilities. Amazon Quick serves as the natural language interaction entry point for business users; MCP services are deployed on Lemei's intranet, responsible for data parsing, business rule execution, and result structuring; raw business files remain inside the enterprise; while Amazon Bedrock and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore handle model inference and analysis generation, complemented by mechanisms such as identity authentication, multi-factor login, and permission control to strengthen access security.

The Amazon (AMZN) Web Services team provided technical architecture guidance, answered security questions, maintained timely project communication, and participated in the overall architecture review. Nengke Technology (603859)'s role was to further transform these cloud and generative AI service capabilities into applications that could enter Lemei's business processes.

From requirements research, scenario selection, and technical architecture design, to development, intranet deployment, data import, integration testing, and training, the FDE team spanned the entire project. Within about 40 days, all 4 intelligent assistants were completed and ran real business processes in Lemei's intranet environment.

3

Four real scenarios, turning AI from "can do" into "can use"

Financial profit analysis was one of the most representative scenarios in this project.

The Nengke Technology (603859) FDE team first confirmed with finance staff, item by item, more than 10 business definitions such as region, product, factory, revenue, freight, cost, unit conversion, SKU, package type, and exchange rate, then distilled these definitions into knowledge and analysis rules. Finance staff could then directly ask questions in natural language about revenue, cost, and profit changes, and continue to drill down along different dimensions. Business users could also add metric definitions and proprietary terms on their own, without relying on developers to modify the program each time.

POC results showed that the accuracy of data retrieval and analysis Q&A for common financial metrics reached over 90%; monthly business analysis reports that originally took 3–5 days were shortened to hours, and ad hoc business data retrieval reached the minute level.

In the IT scenario, Nengke Technology (603859) connected natural language understanding capabilities with real OA processes. Employees only need to describe the problem, and the system can identify the module and urgency, proactively ask follow-up questions when information is missing, and complete ticket generation and approval routing. The project process has been confirmed and validated by Lemei's IT team; the accuracy of element extraction and automatic classification reached over 90%, and the time to create a single ticket was reduced from 5–10 minutes to under 30 seconds.

In the human resources scenario, the intelligent resume screening assistant breaks down a JD into a job profile, hard requirements, and scoring rules, then parses resumes, performs matching scores and tiered recommendations, and provides recommendation reasons, making results explainable and reviewable. The project has completed connectivity verification with the recruiting system interface and covered 7 positions and 106 resumes using real business samples.

The HR policy knowledge Q&A assistant transforms scattered policy documents into an instantly queryable knowledge service. After an employee asks a question, the system generates an answer and cites the original policy text; for sensitive content, permission filtering by role and department is applied at the retrieval stage; questions that cannot be answered are escalated to humans with a record kept, so unresolved questions can continue to accumulate and feed back into the knowledge base.

4

What FDE delivered is not just four assistants, but a set of capabilities that can be replicated

In this project, what Nengke Technology (603859) truly accumulated was not just four front-end applications.

Around the four scenarios, the FDE team gradually formed reusable assets including financial analysis rules, metric semantics, job profiles, scoring standards, a policy knowledge base, OA interface adapters, and MCP services. In the technical architecture, the MCP Server, service layer, unified model invocation layer, vector database, document parsing module, database storage, asynchronous processing, and authentication and failure retry mechanisms have also been built and verified.

This means that when new business scenarios are added later, there is no need to build the entire system from scratch. New applications can continue to be mounted onto the existing MCP services, business rules can be further distilled into Skills, and Amazon Quick can continue to serve as the unified interaction entry point. Directions such as CRM operations and warehousing have already entered the scope of subsequent exploration.

This is precisely the core value of Nengke Technology (603859)'s FDE model in this project: not simply adding a few AI features for the customer, but entering the business site and transforming tacit business rules, data logic, and process experience into sustainably reusable enterprise intelligence assets.

5

From one POC to continuous AI transformation

For the next phase, Lemei Packaging did not define its goal as "launching more agents."

The customer stated that they will comprehensively consider business value and the fit with Amazon Web Services' (AMZN) technical capabilities, and continue to select higher-value scenarios to advance the collaboration.

From 6 departments and 13 candidate requirements to 4 real business intelligent assistants, Lemei Packaging's POC validated a more pragmatic path for enterprise AI adoption: Amazon Web Services provides a secure, scalable cloud and generative AI technology foundation, along with support at the architecture and security levels; NCC, through the FDE model, works deep in the business field, connecting Amazon Web Services' cloud and generative AI service capabilities with Lemei's data, rules, and processes, completing the full journey from scenario identification to development, deployment, adoption, and results verification.

For Lemei Packaging, this POC was therefore not just a technical validation, but also an accumulation of methods and capabilities in the AI transformation process—first finding problems truly worth solving, going deep and solid on the scenarios, then consolidating the successful experience and continuously replicating it across more business processes.

About Lemei Packaging

Lemei Packaging (Kunshan) Co., Ltd. is a subsidiary of the APP Group and an international supplier of high-quality paper-based aseptic packaging. Its main products include aseptic brick packs, aseptic pillow packs, Lemei cans, coated paper, and composite paper, with an annual capacity of 5 billion packs; approximately 90% of its products are exported to nearly 80 countries and regions.

About NCC

NCC (603859) was founded in December 2006 and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange Main Board in October 2016 (stock code: 603859.SH). The company serves the digital and intelligent transformation of industrial enterprises. Based on digital twin and artificial intelligence technologies, it provides customers with industrial software and AI agent products and services, supporting customers in achieving "digital-intelligent" transformation based on their business goals, helping industrial enterprises realize new industrialization and build new quality productive forces.
