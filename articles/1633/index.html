FDE Dispatch job-posting data: Forward Deployed Engineer datasets and methodology

FDE Dispatch · 2026-10-28 · 5 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch publishes the full list and methodology behind its Forward Deployed Engineer job-posting datasets, scraped from public Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday boards. The October 24, 2026 snapshot covers 725 postings from 121 employers, with companion indexes for skills, experience, travel, deployment strategists (86 postings), Palantir (105) and OpenAI (32). The page documents title-matching rules, cleaning steps, known limitations, and reuse terms; most datasets are free to reuse with credit, and there is no fixed update schedule.

Key points

  • October 24, 2026 snapshot: 725 postings from 121 employers.
  • Data pulled via public job-board APIs plus 16 Workday keyword searches.
  • Separate indexes cover skills, experience, travel, Palantir and OpenAI.
  • Matching is title-only; LinkedIn-only employers and closed postings are gaps.
  • Most datasets are reusable with credit; no promised update schedule.

Methodology

Collection: public job-board APIs for Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby (260 board names probed), plus keyword search on 16 Workday career sites. FDE Dispatch checked 276 employers and found 206 with a live public board and 121 with at least one matching posting.

Title rule: a posting counts when its title contains forward deployed (any spelling), FDE, deployment strategist, deployed engineer, deployed software/AI/ML engineer, or AI deployment engineer. Role families in the postings file are engineer, deployment_strategist, management, or adjacent_non_engineering. In-page stats use engineer, deployment strategist and management rows (725 of 778). Cleaning removed 9 recruiting-agency postings for an unnamed client and 43 duplicate listings of the same employer, title and location set.

Title families on the guides: of the 725 postings, 568 use an engineer title, 86 use a deployment strategist title and 71 use an FDE management or program title. The skills and experience indexes start from engineer-title postings in the October 24 snapshot with internships excluded (565), re-fetch posting text on October 25, 2026, and keep 563 postings from 115 employers after 2 postings had closed. The travel index uses the same 725 postings as salary, titles and locations. The deployment strategist index is the 86 postings with role_family = deployment_strategist among those 725. The Palantir index is the 105 Palantir rows with in_page_stats among those 725. The OpenAI index is the 32 OpenAI rows with in_page_stats among those 725. The Databricks index is the 122 Databricks rows with in_page_stats among those 725. The Salesforce index is the 50 Salesforce rows with in_page_stats among those 725. The Accenture index is the 40 Accenture rows with in_page_stats among those 725. The FDE management index is the 71 rows with role_family = management among those 725. The LangChain index is the 17 LangChain rows with in_page_stats among those 725, joined to the requirements index on posting URL.

Known limits, as stated on the READMEs and guide method sections: matching is by title only; employers that post only on LinkedIn, Indeed or other applicant systems are missing; posted ranges are not offers and exclude equity value; a mention is not a hard requirement; keyword matching is approximate; counts are open postings on one pull date, not headcount; postings close, so check the URL before relying on a row. One snapshot per file, no history yet.

Reuse: the postings, skills, experience, travel, Palantir, OpenAI, Databricks, Salesforce, Accenture, FDE management and LangChain READMEs state that the files are free to reuse with credit to FDE Dispatch and a link to the matching guide page. The locations and deployment strategist READMEs do not state a license.

How to cite

Questions people ask

Where does the FDE Dispatch data come from?

FDE Dispatch pulls live Forward Deployed Engineer postings from public Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday job boards. The October 24, 2026 snapshot checked 276 employers, found 206 with a live public board and 121 with at least one matching posting, and counts 725 postings with in-page stats after removing recruiting-agency and duplicate listings.

How often is the FDE data updated?

Each dataset is a dated snapshot. The published files cover October 24, 2026 and October 25, 2026. New editions will be added to this page as FDE Dispatch publishes them. There is no promised update schedule.

What counts as an FDE posting in these datasets?

A posting counts when its title contains forward deployed (any spelling), FDE, deployment strategist, deployed engineer, deployed software/AI/ML engineer, or AI deployment engineer. Page totals use engineer, deployment strategist and FDE management titles. Adjacent non-engineering titles stay in the postings file with a flag and are left out of those totals. The skills and experience indexes use engineer-family postings only, with internships excluded.

Can I reuse or cite the FDE datasets?

Yes. Where a dataset README states reuse terms, the files are free to reuse with credit to FDE Dispatch and a link to the matching guide page. Cite FDE Dispatch, the dataset name, the snapshot date and this page URL. Locations and deployment strategist READMEs do not state a separate license; do not invent one. Postings close, so check each source URL before relying on a row.
