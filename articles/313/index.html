Summary

AY Automate is an AI automation agency that builds and maintains n8n workflows and AI agents inside clients' existing stacks, targeting B2B, SaaS, and ecommerce teams. A single workflow starts in the low four figures and goes live in one to two weeks; full systems with AI steps take four to eight weeks and are scoped after a free audit call. What We Actually Ship

The worry with automation is that it fails quietly and nobody notices for a week. Every build has the same five parts, with error handling, retries and monitoring running under all of them.

01 Trigger

Something happens

  • Webhook
  • Schedule (cron)
  • New email or form
  • Row added

02 Workflow logic

n8n runs the rules

  • Branching and conditions
  • API calls
  • Data mapping
  • Claude Code for custom logic

03 AI step

Only where judgment is needed

  • Classify a message
  • Extract fields from a messy document
  • Draft a reply
  • RAG answers from your data

04 Human review

A person approves the uncertain cases

  • Low-confidence results
  • Drafts before they send
  • Exceptions

05 Your systems

Results land where you work

  • CRM
  • Database
  • Spreadsheets
  • Email and Slack
  • Private APIs

FAQ

AI Automation Agency Questions

What is the difference between an automation agency and an AI automation agency?+

An automation agency connects tools with rules: when this happens, do that. An AI automation agency adds model steps where a rule cannot decide, like reading a support message to classify it or pulling fields out of a messy invoice. In practice most of a good build is still plain workflow logic. AI is one or two steps inside it, not the whole system.

When does a workflow need an AI agent instead of plain automation?+

When the next step depends on judgment over messy input and cannot be written as a rule. Routing a form by a dropdown value is plain automation. Reading a free-text request, deciding what it is and drafting a reply is agent work. We build the agent only for that part, keep the rest deterministic, and send low-confidence cases to a person for review.

What is an AI automation agency, and how is AY Automate different?+

An AI automation agency builds the workflows and agents that remove manual work from your operations. At AY Automate one senior engineer directs a fleet of AI agents, so you get one accountable builder for strategy, build and post-launch support instead of three separate vendors.

Do you build custom workflow solutions or use templates?+

Custom. We scope your actual systems in an audit, then build on n8n and code where templates fall short. Pricing is scoped after the audit, not sold as a fixed package.

How do I hire an AI automation agency?+

Start with the audit call: a real agency scopes your actual systems before quoting a number, rather than selling a fixed package sight unseen. Check that they can show working builds, not just slides, and that they stay accountable after launch through a maintenance retainer or handover documentation.

What does an AI automation agency do?+

An AI automation agency, sometimes called an AI automation company, replaces repetitive manual work with systems that run on their own: lead routing, reporting, document handling, support triage, data syncs between the tools you already use. AI matters where judgment is involved, like classifying a message or extracting data from a messy document. We are tool-honest: n8n where it is the fastest reliable path, custom code where the workflow needs more control, and no AI at all where a plain rules-based flow does the job cheaper.

Is an AI automation agency the same as a workflow automation agency?+

Yes, same service, different vocabulary. Both describe an agency that designs and builds systems to remove manual work from your operations. We use n8n for the workflow logic and add an AI agent only where judgment is actually needed, like classifying a message or drafting a reply for review, not everywhere by default.

What does an AI automation agency cost?+

Focused single-workflow builds start in the low four figures and ship in one to two weeks. Full automation systems with AI steps, monitoring, and integrations typically run four to eight weeks and are scoped after the audit call, because the honest answer depends on how many systems the workflow touches. If you need a dedicated engineer embedded full time instead of a scoped project, placement pricing starts from $3K/month. A US in-house hire typically costs $150,000+ in salary plus roughly $30,000 in benefits.

How is an AI automation agency different from hiring in-house?+

An in-house hire costs $150,000+ in salary plus benefits and takes weeks to recruit before they start producing. An agency engagement starts with a scoped audit, builds against your existing stack, and you are not carrying a full-time salary, payroll taxes, or a hiring pipeline for a role you may only need part of the time.

How is this different from hiring a freelancer?+

A freelancer builds the happy path. We build the happy path plus error handling, retries, monitoring, and documentation, and stay accountable for the system after launch. That difference is invisible in the demo and decisive in month three.

Do you use AI in every automation?+

No. AI adds cost and variance, so it only goes where judgment is involved: classification, extraction from messy input, or drafting for human review. Deterministic steps stay deterministic.

Can I just use a no-code tool myself instead of hiring an agency?+

For a single simple trigger, yes, a no-code tool alone can work. Most teams come to an agency once the workflow needs conditional logic across more than two systems, error handling, or an AI agent making decisions, which is where DIY no-code setups tend to break down.

How long does an AI automation agency engagement take?+

A single focused workflow is typically live in one to two weeks. A full automation system, with an AI layer, monitoring, and integrations across multiple tools, typically takes four to eight weeks and is timed during the audit call.

Can you work with our existing tech stack?+

Yes. We connect the CRM, database, spreadsheets, email, Slack, and private APIs you already run rather than forcing a new stack. Most builds are integration-heavy by design.

What happens after the automation ships? Do you maintain it?+

Either a clean handover with documentation and a working session for your team, or a maintenance retainer where we watch the monitors, handle API changes, and extend the system as your process evolves.

Is an AI automation agency the same as an AI agent development company?+

They overlap. Agent development is one of the services an automation agency delivers, building autonomous agents for complex logic, alongside workflow automation, API integration, and RAG pipelines. If you already know you need an agent specifically, our AI agent development page covers that scope in detail.

What's the difference between an AI automation agency and an automation consultant?+

A consultant typically advises and hands you a recommendation. An agency scopes the audit, then builds and ships the system itself. We do both under one team: the audit call is the consulting step, and the build plus maintenance is the agency work, instead of a hand-off between two separate vendors.

Do you work with companies in the USA, or only internationally?+

Both. Every engagement is remote-first: the audit call, the build, and ongoing maintenance happen over video and your existing tools. We work with teams across the USA on US business hours and with international teams the same way, so location does not change the engagement.

Are you a B2B AI automation agency, or do you also build consumer apps?+

B2B. Our engagements automate internal operations and customer-facing workflows for companies, not consumer social apps. Most clients are SaaS platforms, ecommerce operators, agencies, and internal operations teams removing manual work from processes their own staff or customers run into daily.

Do you build automations for SaaS and ecommerce companies specifically?+

Yes, alongside other B2B teams. SaaS companies typically automate onboarding, usage checks, and support triage. Ecommerce operators automate order handling, inventory syncs across channels, and customer service. Both run through the same audit, build, maintain model as every other engagement.

Is this different from IT automation or CRM automation specifically?+

No, it's the same underlying work described with different vocabulary. IT systems and CRM automation are two of the systems we connect into a workflow, alongside your database, spreadsheets, email, and internal APIs, scoped to your engagement.

How do I find the best AI automation agency for my business?+

Pick the one that scopes your systems before it quotes, shows working builds instead of slides, and stays accountable after launch. Ask how they handle errors, retries and monitoring, and who fixes the workflow when an API changes. If the answer is a fixed package sold before anyone has seen your stack, keep looking. Our comparison of AI automation agencies lists what to check.

What is the smallest project I can start with?+

One focused workflow. It starts in the low four figures and is typically live in one to two weeks, with error handling, retries and monitoring built in. Most teams start there, see it run on their own data, then expand into a full system with AI steps once the first workflow has proved itself.

What happens if the automation does not work?+

We do not call a build done until it runs on your real data inside your systems. Every build is tested in a sandbox first, then shipped with error handling, retries and monitoring. After launch you choose a documented handover or a maintenance retainer, where we watch the monitors and fix breakage when an API or edge case changes.

What does an AI workflow automation agency build first?+

The workflow the audit shows pays for itself fastest. That is usually lead routing, reporting, document handling, support triage or a data sync between two tools your team copies between by hand. AI is added only at the steps that need judgment, like classifying a message or pulling fields out of a messy document.

Is an AI implementation agency different from an AI automation agency?+

Mostly the name. Both should take AI from idea to a system your team uses every day. The hard part is production: IDC data reported by bex.co in September 2026 puts about 88% of enterprise AI proofs of concept as never reaching production. We scope, build, deploy into your systems and maintain, so the work does not stop at a demo.

Who is an AI automation agency not a good fit for?+

Teams with one simple trigger a no-code tool already handles, teams that need a full-time engineer embedded rather than a scoped project, and teams not ready to give access to the systems the automation must touch. In those cases we will say so on the audit call and point you to Forward Deployed Engineers or a DIY setup instead.

Which tools do you build automations with?+

n8n for triggers, webhooks and workflow logic, Claude Code and custom code for logic a trigger cannot handle, and RAG pipelines when answers must come from your own data. We connect into the CRM, database, spreadsheets, email, Slack and private APIs you already run instead of moving you onto a new stack.

Do you work in languages other than English?+

Yes. We deliver engagements in English, French and Arabic, remote-first over video and your existing tools. The audit, build and maintenance steps are the same in every language, and a French version of this page is linked above for teams who prefer to read the scope in French first.
