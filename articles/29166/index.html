Feishu-Integration

Original title: Feishu-Integration

FDE中国社区 · 2026-07-12 · 4 min read

Summary

This is a skill-pack document (v1.1, maturity: usable) from the FDE China community addressing Feishu integration for AI applications. It argues that domestic FDE delivery depends heavily on Feishu, and poor integration causes low adoption and data silos. The methodology covers integration modes—bots, mini-programs, Base (multidimensional tables), approvals, Webhooks, and SSO—with the principle that AI output must embed into existing collaboration flows rather than standalone pages. It specifies inputs/outputs, a six-step process, stage gates (production requires evaluation.md metrics at 80% or above), role divisions among FDE, AIBP, business owners, and IT/security, plus common pitfalls like excessive permissions, missing SSO, and unaudited bots. Includes five scenario deep-dives, an FAQ, a sample case (5-person pilot with 100% weekly active usage), and a downloadable package for Claude, Cursor, or Codex.

Key points

  • Targets low adoption and data silos from poor Feishu integration.
  • Core principle: embed AI output into existing collaboration flows.
  • Integration modes include bots, Base tables, approvals, Webhooks, SSO.
  • Production gate requires evaluation.md metrics of at least 80%.
  • Pitfalls: excessive permissions, no SSO, unaudited bots.

FAQ

Q1: When executing [Feishu-Integration], how do you avoid "excessive permissions"?

A: Follow the README methodology step by step and showcase interim deliverables in weekly Demos; if PoC materials are incomplete, first produce a "to-be-validated list" before proceeding.

Q2: What additional constraints apply in private deployment/Xinchuang/MLPS environments?

A: Specify the deployment form explicitly in the prompt constraints section; when data must stay on-premises, domestic components, or audit logs are involved, reference Private-Deployment-Gateway and RBAC-Audit accordingly.

Q3: What if the division of responsibilities with AIBP is unclear?

A: Go back to the AIBP-Collaboration-Playbook and update the RACI; AIBP is responsible for Ground Truth and business acceptance, while the FDE is responsible for deliverable technical assets.

Q4: After a PoC passes, how do we transition to Beta/production?

A: Mark the next-stage Skill in the deliverables (e.g., RAG-Evaluation→Private-Deployment-Gateway→FDE-Adoption-Growth), and trigger the launch gate via the checklist.
