Summary

This article presents key excerpts from Speakeasy's Forward Deployed Engineer job description, captured on 2026-11-02. The role spans pre-sales, deployment, product feedback, and engineering, serving as the technical bridge between customers and Speakeasy's platform. It requires end-to-end ownership of complex opportunities from evaluation through production rollout, working with engineering leaders, platform and security teams at companies deploying AI. Skill tags include API integration, AI agents, evals, observability, security and governance, MCP, and building reusable assets. The article notes the excerpts are for research comparison only and do not constitute a hiring commitment.
