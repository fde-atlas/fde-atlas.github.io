Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) / Seconded to SB OAI Japan LLC | Career Hiring

Original title: Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)／SB OAI Japan合同会社 出向 | キャリア採用

ソフトバンク採用 · 2026-05-18 · 5 min read

Summary

SoftBank is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer who will be seconded to SB OAI Japan, its joint venture with OpenAI launched in November 2025. The role leads technical implementation of cutting-edge AI models for major Japanese enterprises, going beyond PoCs to production systems: RAG, agent design, evals, workflow automation, and full-stack development, while coordinating with OpenAI and SB OAI Japan product, research, and security teams. The position is based in Tokyo (WeWork Takeshiba) with domestic client travel and possible trips to OpenAI's San Francisco headquarters. Requirements include 5+ years of software engineering experience, production-quality coding in Python or JavaScript, cloud experience, and business-level Japanese. Monthly salary ranges from 523,750 to 999,000 yen, with theoretical annual compensation of roughly 8.12 to 20.35 million yen.

Key points

  • SoftBank FDE role seconded to SB OAI Japan, the OpenAI joint venture.
  • Focus on production deployment, not PoCs, for large Japanese enterprises.
  • Work spans RAG, agents, evals, and full-stack development.
  • Requires 5+ years engineering experience and business-level Japanese.
  • Theoretical annual pay ranges from about 8.12 to 20.35 million yen.
