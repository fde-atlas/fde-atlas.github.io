Full text

A study puts the qualified pool at about 2,000 engineers

An executive-search study by Christian & Timbers, based on 250 C-suite interviews across 180 companies and 300 FDE interviews between January and June, estimates around 2,000 US engineers have the mix of domain knowledge and applied AI experience to reliably deliver enterprise AI returns. The firm was explicit that this is 2,000 total, not 2,000 available. It counted roughly 17,000 FDEs in the US market overall, a large share already at Palantir, and found the share of companies planning FDE hires rose from 5 to 10% in early 2026 to 70% by the second quarter. TechCrunch→

The read

Microsoft's 6,000 are largely existing employees moved into a new organization; AWS and the Pentagon are recruiting; Ode has about 100 people. The study caps the experienced end at roughly 2,000, most already employed. Expect continued pressure on compensation, and some engagements staffed below the experience pitched. TechCrunch quoted Ode CEO Chris Taylor to the same effect: very few engineers can build a company's flagship AI product.
