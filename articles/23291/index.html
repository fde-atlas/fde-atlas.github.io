Summary

This is a column index from FDE Talent, a Japanese recruiting service specializing in Forward Deployed Engineers. It lists roughly twenty articles covering FDE fundamentals, required skills, career-change and hiring guides, interview preparation, salary data, and case studies, all based on primary sources such as job postings from Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Databricks.
