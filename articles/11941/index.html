VPC and Air-Gapped Deployment

FDE Interviews · 2026-06-14 · 1 min read

Summary

This piece covers a core part of forward deployed engineering: deploying software inside enterprise customers' security perimeters. Because large enterprises won't let data leave their boundary, FDEs deploy into the customer's private VPC, reach data over PrivateLink rather than the public internet, authenticate via the customer's SSO, and encrypt data at rest and in transit. For air-gapped environments, self-hosted models must be shipped as well. The page also lists related course lessons on mapping pilot stakeholders and decisions, designing boundaries of ownership, trust, network and meaning, and identifying constraints that could invalidate a pilot before building it.

Key points

  • Enterprises won't let data leave their security boundary
  • Software is deployed inside the customer's private VPC
  • Data access uses PrivateLink, not the public internet
  • Customer SSO plus encryption at rest and in transit
  • Air-gapped environments require shipping self-hosted models

Large enterprises will not let their data leave their security boundary, so you deploy your software inside the customer's private VPC, reach their data over PrivateLink instead of the public internet, authenticate through their SSO, and encrypt everything at rest and in transit. For air-gapped environments you ship self-hosted models too. This is core Forward Deployed Engineer work.
