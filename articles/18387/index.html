Cotofure FDE Service, which provides end-to-end support from AI utilization planning through implementation and improvement, launches on November 2

Original title: AI活用の企画から実装・改善まで伴走する「Cotofure FDE Service」を11月2日より提供開始

Summary

Cotofure, a Tokyo-based AI product company, will launch its Cotofure FDE Service on November 2, 2026. Based on the forward deployed engineering approach, the service supports corporate AI adoption end to end: identifying business challenges, selecting AI technologies, running PoCs, building systems, and improving them after deployment. Teams combine AI development PMs and engineers with members experienced in planning Cotofure's own AI products. The company cites its track record in image recognition AI development and products like ProoFace (face authentication) and EMOTICS (crowd analytics) as the basis for helping firms that lack AI talent or struggle to move beyond PoC stage.

  • PMs, engineers, and product planners collaborate per project.
  • Built on image recognition AI and generative AI API development experience.

~ Supporting Corporate AI Adoption and Utilization by Leveraging Technical Expertise and Planning/Development Experience Built Through AI Development ~

Cotofure Inc. (Headquarters: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; CEO: Toyoshi Takahashi; hereinafter "Cotofure"), a company that develops AI-powered products, will begin offering "Cotofure FDE Service (FDE: Forward Deployed Engineering)" on November 2, 2026, providing consistent support for corporate AI adoption and utilization, from planning through system implementation and post-deployment improvement.

In this service, PMs and engineers with AI development experience collaborate with members who have experience in planning and developing Cotofure's own AI products, depending on the project. We support everything from organizing customers' business challenges to AI technology selection, system development, and deployment into operations, driving the practical application of AI.

Service website: https://www.cotofure.com/services/fde

■ Background of the Service Launch

While advances in AI technologies, including generative AI, have expanded the possibilities for AI utilization in companies, challenges remain, such as "not knowing where to start," "lacking in-house AI specialists or development resources," and "being unable to move from PoC (proof of concept) to practical application."

Cotofure has been engaged in contract development of various AI systems centered on image recognition AI, as well as planning, development, and operation of its own AI products. Leveraging this experience, we are launching this new service to provide more practical support for corporate AI utilization.

■ About Cotofure FDE Service

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineering) is a development approach that involves deeply understanding customers' operations and challenges, and leveraging technical expertise to drive everything from problem-solving to system implementation and improvement in an integrated manner.

With "Cotofure FDE Service," we work with customers to organize challenges from stages where development requirements are not yet defined, accompanying them from AI utilization planning through implementation and improvement.

Main Support Areas

  • AI utilization planning and challenge organization: Understanding business challenges, examining AI utilization areas, and proposing implementation methods
  • Technology selection and PoC: Selecting AI technologies and models, verifying feasibility, and developing prototypes
  • System development and implementation: Developing AI applications and APIs, and integrating with existing systems
  • Deployment, operation, and continuous improvement: Supporting deployment into operations, and improving features and adding development after operations begin

■ Expected AI Utilization Examples

● Automation and workforce reduction of operations using image recognition AI

For operations that use images and video, we handle everything from AI model selection and technical verification to system implementation, supporting reduced workload and workforce reduction.

● Embedding AI features into existing business systems

We embed features such as generative AI and image recognition AI into business systems and web services, supporting improved operational efficiency and enhanced services.

● Streamlining internal information search and inquiry handling

We support building systems for searching and referencing internal documents and handling inquiries using generative AI.

*The above are expected use cases and do not represent individual deployment track records.

■ Three Key Features of Cotofure FDE Service

1. Technical expertise cultivated through AI system development and implementation

We have experience in AI model selection and training, API and application development, and implementation into commercial systems, centered on image recognition AI. We also leverage our experience in customer-facing development using generative AI APIs to support the practical application of AI.

2. Integrated support by PMs, engineers, and planning members

PMs and engineers from AI development projects collaborate with members experienced in product planning and development, depending on the project. We drive problem-solving not only from a technical perspective but also from the perspectives of business operations and users.

3. Experience in planning, developing, and operating our own AI products

We apply the knowledge gained from planning, developing, and operating our own products, such as the facial recognition AI "ProoFace" and the people-counting and attribute analysis AI "EMOTICS," to support AI adoption and utilization.

Under its vision of "Bringing 'AI (love)' to everyone," Cotofure will connect AI technologies to companies' actual operations through this service, aiming to realize a society where AI is used more widely and familiarly.
