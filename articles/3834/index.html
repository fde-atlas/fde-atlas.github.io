Why Every AI Company Needs Forward Deployed Engineers | Why You Cannot Build Vertical AI Without Living at the Customer | The 10X Deployment Unlock Nobody Talks About | Pablo Palafox on Building Happy Robot from $0 to Millions in 2 Years

TransistorFM · 2025-11-03 · 2 min read

Summary

In this focal podcast episode, host Pascal Unger interviews Pablo Palafox, co-founder and CEO of Happy Robot, an AI-native operating system for logistics that grew from zero to millions in revenue in under two years. Palafox explains how startups can adapt Palantir's forward deployed engineer motion: founders are often the first FDE, engineers must embed onsite and become industry insiders rather than rely on sales pitches, and hiring should target "nerds who can sell." He details FDE compensation (1-2% equity versus 0.1-0.5% for regular engineers), the 80/20 split between building and onsite work, expanding accounts from $30K to $3M, organizing FDE pods by vertical, and his biggest mistake: delaying verticalization. He also covers when the model doesn't fit and predicts FDEs will eventually build vertical products autonomously. Happy Robot has raised over $60M from a16z, YC, and Base10, serving customers like DHL.

Key points

  • Pablo Palafox explains how Happy Robot reached millions in revenue in two years using FDEs
  • FDEs must embed onsite and become industry insiders, not just tech experts
  • Hire "nerds who can sell"; give FDEs 1-2% equity vs 0.1-0.5% for regular engineers
  • FDEs spend 80% building, 20% onsite; accounts expanded from $30K to $3M
  • His biggest mistake was waiting too long to verticalize FDE teams

The forward deployed engineer model took Happy Robot from zero to millions in revenue in under 2 years.

In this episode, Pablo Palafox reveals exactly how to implement Palantir's FDE motion at a startup - including when to use it, who to hire, and the costly mistakes to avoid. Learn why embedding engineers directly with customers beats traditional sales approaches for complex AI products.

Pablo Palafox is the co-founder and CEO of Happy Robot, the AI-native operating system for supply chain and logistics. Prior to Happy Robot, Pablo completed his PhD in computer science and deep learning. Happy Robot has raised over $60M from investors including a16z, YC, and Base10, and serves enterprise customers like DHL.
