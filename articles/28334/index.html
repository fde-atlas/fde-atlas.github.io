Forward Deployed Engineer at Replit: Role, Salary, and Interview (2026)

fde.directory · 2026-11-20 · 6 min read

Summary

This fde.directory piece analyzes Replit's newly created Forward Deployed Engineer function. Despite launching a self-serve Enterprise plan (contracts up to $200K without sales) and a Solution Partner Program with Accenture, Slalom, and Hexaware, Replit is hiring FDEs at $180K–$300K (head of function $300K–$400K) for accounts too large or regulated for self-serve or partners. The role demands production LLM experience—retrieval, tool calls, evals—and centers on wiring apps into warehouses and identity systems. Context: revenue grew from $2.8M to $150M annualized, a $400M Series D at $9B valuation, with a $1B ARR target for 2026, though third-party estimates suggest ~$500M run-rate and weak gross margins. Interview prep and equity risks are also covered.

Key points

  • Replit's self-serve Enterprise plan handles deals up to $200K without sales involvement.
  • FDEs target accounts too big, regulated, or strategic for self-serve or SI partners.
  • IC pay is $180K–$300K; the head-of-function role was $300K–$400K, Foster City, three days onsite.
  • Candidates need production LLM skills: retrieval, tool calls, and evals.
  • Valuation assumes ~$1B ARR by end of 2026; Sacra estimates margins from 36% to negative 14%.

Most companies hire Forward Deployed Engineers because their product can’t get into an enterprise without help. Replit has spent 2026 doing the opposite: making it easier to buy without talking to anyone.

In May the company launched a self-serve Enterprise plan that lets a customer sign a contract worth up to $200,000 with no sales rep involved, according to Pulse 2.0. SSO, SCIM, audit logs, and role-based access come included. The same week it announced a Solution Partner Program with Accenture, Slalom, and Hexaware as founding partners, so systems integrators can sell and deliver Replit projects themselves.

And yet Replit is building an FDE team, with a $300K–$400K head-of-function seat and individual contributor roles at $180K–$300K. Self-serve covers the small deals and partners cover the mid-market. The FDEs get the accounts that are too big, too regulated, or too strategic to leave to either.

The company you’d be joining

Replit’s rise is one of the fastest in software. TechCrunch reported in September 2025 that annualized revenue went from $2.8 million to $150 million in under a year, alongside a $250 million round at a $3 billion valuation led by Prysm Capital. Six months later, on March 11, 2026, Replit closed a $400 million Series D at $9 billion, led by Georgian with a16z, Coatue, Accenture Ventures, Okta Ventures, and Databricks Ventures participating. Georgian’s announcement said the company expects to reach $1 billion of ARR by the end of 2026.

Treat the revenue trajectory with care. Third-party trackers put Replit somewhere around $500 million in run-rate this autumn, but that’s estimated, not disclosed. Sacra also estimated gross margins ranging from 36% to negative 14% during 2025. Agent-driven coding burns a lot of inference, and margin is the number to ask about if you’re weighing equity.

The enterprise side is the part that matters for this job. Replit says it has more than 50 million users, with users at 85% of the Fortune 500. Named customers include Zillow, PayPal, Adobe, Atlassian, Databricks, Okta, and Talkdesk. Zillow has grown to roughly 600 seats, per Georgian.

What the role actually is

Replit’s FDE function is new and built top-down. The Head of Forward Deployed Engineering posting appeared first, about five months ago, and has since come down. It reported to the President and Head of AI, asked for 12+ years of experience, and promised to “build and lead the team responsible for helping our most strategic enterprise customers operationalize Replit.” The individual contributor FDE posting went live on Replit’s Ashby board around August 24, 2026.

The IC posting groups the work under five headings: Build with Customers, Architect Enterprise Solutions, Own Technical Deployments, Bridge Customers and Product, and turning “field patterns into templates, playbooks, and product requirements.” The integration list is concrete: SSO, data warehouses, internal APIs, and SaaS systems.

The requirements are where Replit differs from its closest comparison, Lovable:

Replit FDE Lovable FDE
Experience 5+ years shipping production software 5+ years full-stack (Staff: 8+)
AI requirement LLM apps in production: “retrieval, tool calls, and evals” Not a core requirement
Stack named None; identity, warehouses, APIs React and TypeScript
Published IC band $180K–$300K Not published
Office Foster City HQ, 3 days a week NYC / Boston / SF / London, on-site or hybrid

That AI requirement is the tell. Lovable’s FDE mostly hardens web apps that a customer’s employees already built. Replit’s posting asks for engineers who have shipped retrieval and evaluation pipelines, because the enterprise use case is increasingly agents and internal tools that touch company data. Replit has added native Snowflake, Databricks, and BigQuery connectors, per Sacra. Expect most deployments to be about wiring apps safely into a warehouse and an identity provider, not the front end.

Here’s the contrarian read. With self-serve and an SI channel both live, Replit’s FDE team doesn’t need to be large, and it probably won’t be. A small team covering only the biggest accounts means high visibility and direct influence on the product roadmap, which the posting explicitly offers. It also means each FDE carries a few very large, very demanding customers. If you like breadth across dozens of deployments, this isn’t that job.

Where the seats are

Board listings show the IC role in Foster City, California, with some mirrors also listing SoHo, New York. The Ashby application asks directly whether you can work from the Foster City HQ three days a week, and asks about relocation. Plan on the Peninsula, not remote. Compensation

Replit is one of the few AI-coding companies that publishes its FDE pay:

Role Location Base range Source
Head of Forward Deployed Engineering Foster City, CA $300,000–$400,000 Posting via Teal / Welcome to the Jungle (now closed)
Forward Deployed Engineer Foster City, CA $180,000–$300,000 Posting via SV Angel job board, Aug 2026

The IC band is wide. The top reaches frontier-lab territory in our salary guide, and the bottom sits below what Cursor and Cognition typically quote for comparable seats. The posting promises equity but gives no figure. Benefits include a 401(k) with a 4% match. On equity: at $9 billion against an estimated half-billion of run-rate, the price assumes Replit hits something near its $1 billion target and fixes margins along the way. Ask for the share count, the latest preferred price, and any tender history.

The interview loop

We found no first-hand reports of Replit’s FDE loop, and we won’t invent one. Engineering reports on Glassdoor give a reasonable picture of the house style, though, and they’re consistent on one point: Replit tests by building.

Product Engineer candidates describe a recruiter screen, then a one-hour live programming session, then a virtual onsite of about six hours spent building a scoped project and sharing progress, with a founder conversation at the end. An older report describes presenting the onsite project directly to CEO Amjad Masad. A Design Engineer candidate in February 2026 described a pair-coding screen that turned a stream of keystrokes into editor operations, plus TypeScript questions.

Several 2025–2026 reviews also describe rescheduled calls and slow follow-up, so keep your own timeline.

For the FDE seat, prepare for what the posting stresses. Expect to build a working LLM feature live, with retrieval, a tool call, and some way to evaluate it. Expect a design round that starts from an enterprise constraint like SSO, data residency, or a warehouse you can’t copy out of. And have a story about owning a deployment from architecture through production. Build something real with Replit Agent before your first call, and note where it breaks. Our interview guide covers the general format.

Who this role is for

Replit’s FDE seat suits a senior engineer who has shipped LLM features to production, wants a short list of very large customers, and is happy to be in Foster City most of the week. The upside is a founding-era team at a company with real enterprise pull and a published, competitive band. The risks are a valuation that bets on hitting $1 billion, inference-heavy margins, and a hiring process candidates describe as uneven.
