Forrester: forward deployed engineers alone are not enough

On July 9, 2026, Forrester analyst Ted Schadler published a post on the firm’s evaluation of 10 AI consulting providers. He wrote that hyperscalers are investing heavily in forward deployed engineers, and that AI transformations cannot be done with those engineers alone. The post advises buyers to require blended provider teams and names no forward deployed headcounts.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: Forrester.

What happened

Forrester evaluated Accenture, Bain & Company, Boston Consulting Group, Capgemini, Deloitte, EY, IBM, KPMG, McKinsey & Company and PwC. The results are in “The Forrester Wave: AI Consulting Services, Q2 2026”, which is for Forrester clients only and was not opened.

The post says all 10 providers show a willingness to tie what they are paid to results, and that a handful do so most of the time. The fifth of its six lessons is to require blended provider teams.

“Even hyperscalers are recognizing the need for forward-deployed engineers and investing heavily in them.”

Our reading

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

The post gives no roles, pay or places for forward deployed engineers. It suggests that buyers are being advised to place these engineers inside blended teams, alongside other provider staff.

If you are hiring them

Forrester advises a provider team that mirrors the buyer’s own AI operations team. If you are building a forward deployed team, this suggests buyers may ask who works alongside your engineers, not only how many you have.
