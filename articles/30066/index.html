FDE Exam Booking: What to Do When You Can't Find Available Test Slots—Don't Wait Until After Paying to Discover There's No Spot

Original title: FDE 约考：考位查不到怎么办，别等付完钱才发现没位置

FDE 指南 · 2026-11-05 · 4 min read

Summary

This guide covers booking the offline theory exam for Tencent Cloud's ADP FDE certification. Its core advice: check seat availability before paying, since refunds are only allowed within 5 days of purchase and only if no booking was made. Orders stay valid for one year; canceling a booking forfeits money but allows rebooking. If no seats appear, try other dates or cities, or contact Prometric via the official phone number or email. The article also distinguishes rescheduling, cancellation, and refunds, lists key deadlines (24 hours before the exam, 30-minute check-in), and stresses bringing the exact valid original ID entered at booking, or entry is denied without refund.

Key points

  • Check seat availability before paying; refunds only within 5 days, before any booking.
  • Orders expire after 1 year; canceling a booking keeps money in the order for rebooking.
  • No seats? Change date or city, or call 400-613-7050 / email chinareg@prometric.com.
  • No rescheduling or cancellation within 24 hours of the exam; missing it means repurchasing.
  • Bring the valid original ID used at booking, or you are denied entry without refund.

Discovering there are no seats only after paying is the worst outcome

Booking is part of the process for the Tencent Cloud ADP FDE certificate; for which certificate to take and how it differs from other FDE certifications, see Comparison of the Four Systems.

The FDE is an in-person theory exam, so you have to take it at a test center on a computer, with seats released by city and by date. On the certification details page, the "Exam Booking" section shows "Real-time In-person Seat Query" and "Theory Exam Seat Query", with a line of small print next to them:

For seat coordination, call 400-613-7050 on weekdays or email chinareg@prometric.com

This phone number and email are official, and they are the only officially sanctioned option when you truly cannot find a seat. As for how often seats are released or how many days in advance, the official page doesn't say. Don't take seriously the claims online about "seats released every X day"—when you check, either seats are there or they aren't.

By the way, a note on the name. The official "List of Acceptable Forms of Identification" calls the test center "Prometric Test Center" (普尔文考试中心), and the coordination email is also prometric.com. What people mean by "booking with Prometric" is exactly the thing described below.

To see the whole path from buying the course to getting the certificate, check Full Process from Registration to Certification; if you already failed the exam, see Failing and Retaking.

Check seat availability before paying

According to the official guide, refunds are only granted "within 5 days of purchase and before any booking has been made". Once you click book, that window closes. Canceling a booking doesn't refund money either; the money stays in the order, and you can rebook, but you can't get it back. The order itself is valid for 1 year, after which you can't book.

So the order is rigid: check seats first, confirm that your target city actually has seats on your target days, then pay, and book as soon as possible after paying.

There's another pitfall when checking. The query page showing seats doesn't mean they're held for you. The query is a real-time display, and booking is a separate operation; in between, someone else may take them. Once you find seats, don't delay.

When clicking book

The entry point is Personal Center - Order Management. The official guide gives this process:

  • Select the subject
  • Click "Book Exam"
  • Read the booking rules and check the personal data privacy disclosure
  • Click "Book at Test Center"
  • Select the exam city
  • Select the test center, date, and time
  • Fill in your ID number, name, phone number, and email
  • Complete the booking
  • Receive SMS and email notifications

In step 7, the ID number field must be filled with the exact ID you'll present on exam day. The official requirement is the original document and within its validity period; if you don't bring it or it's expired, you won't be admitted and won't be refunded. Which IDs are accepted depends on the current version of the official list—don't copy from third-party articles, as the list changes.

After booking, an SMS and email will be sent. The time, test center address, and ID requirements can be reviewed again by clicking "Go to Exam" in Personal Center - Certification Exams. It's best to test on the spot that your phone and email actually receive messages; reschedule reminders, test center info, and order expiration reminders all go through these two channels.

When you truly can't find a seat

There are really only a few options: change the date, change the time slot, or change the city. The booking process requires selecting a city anyway, so factor in travel and lodging costs for another city yourself. If none of that works, call 400-613-7050 on weekdays or email chinareg@prometric.com for official coordination, as written on the details page.

The worst deal is paying first and gambling that "seats will show up in a few days". When there really are no seats, all you're left with is an order you don't know when you can book, and a 1-year countdown. Once 5 days pass, you can't even get a refund.

When an order approaches the 1-year mark, the system sends in-site messages, emails, and SMS at 90, 60, 30, and 15 days before. This assumes your email and phone are bound and verified. Confirm this right after purchase, so reminders don't go somewhere you can't receive.

After booking, keep these times firmly in mind

Point in timeWhat to doSource
Within 5 days of purchase, before bookingLast chance for a refund"Individual Registration and Exam Guide"
More than 24 hours before the theory examLast chance to reschedule or cancel the booking for free"Individual Registration and Exam Guide"
30 minutes before the theory examArrive and check in"Certification Exam Discipline Rules"
Before the order reaches 1 yearOrder expires, no more booking possible"Individual Registration and Exam Guide"

The 24-hour rule is the most critical. Less than 24 hours before the exam, no rescheduling or cancellation is allowed. If you're already in the test center city but suddenly can't make it, that attempt is used up—and one purchase gives only one attempt, so even a no-show requires repurchasing. The "72 hours before the computer-based exam" in the guide applies to computer-based exams; FDE has only the theory exam, so it doesn't apply.

Rescheduling, cancellation, and refund are three different things. Rescheduling changes the time, cancellation releases the seat, and a refund gets your money back. After canceling, the order still exists and you can rebook; the only case where money can be refunded is "within 5 days of purchase and never booked".

On exam day, don't let your ID be a problem

The exact words from the official "List of Acceptable Forms of Identification":

You must bring at least one of the above documents (the original) and it must be within its validity period to take the exam; other documents will not be accepted. If you cannot prove your identity with any of the above documents, you will not be admitted to the exam and no refund will be given. Candidates holding a Chinese Resident ID card must cooperate with test center staff to complete online identity verification.

The list specifies which IDs are accepted; check the current version on the official page yourself—we won't copy it here, since copying it risks it going stale. Before leaving, verify: the ID you filled in when booking, the one you're carrying, and one on the list are the same document, and it hasn't expired.

The number of questions, duration, and passing score are in Exam Syllabus and Format.
