Summary

Crack ML Interview tracks 81 interview questions reported by candidates who sat Snowflake loops: 68 coding questions, 10 ML system design questions, plus a few behavioral and ML fundamentals items. Four questions map to forward deployed engineer (FDE) rounds, covering deployment system design, behavioral/customer scenarios, and practical coding. The article also links forum posts from candidates, including a new-grad backend onsite and a same-day coding plus system design phone loop, and advises weighting FDE prep toward practical coding, deployment system design, and behavioral questions.
