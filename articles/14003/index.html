OpenAI AI & Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs (93)

Forward Deploying · 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Summary

Forward Deploying tracks 93 open AI engineering roles published on OpenAI's own careers page, including 24 Forward Deployed Engineer and 16 Forward Deployed Architect positions. The median published US base salary across all roles is $284k. FDE roles pay a median of $253k, about 20% above the tracked market median, while Machine Learning Engineer roles reach $398k. Most adverts ask for production code experience, 66 roles are remote-eligible, and only one FDE advert uses pre-sales-style language, suggesting OpenAI's FDE roles are delivery-focused rather than re-titled sales positions.

Key points

  • OpenAI has 93 tracked open AI roles, 24 of them FDE positions.
  • Median published US base salary is $284k across all roles.
  • FDE median base is $253k, roughly 20% above market.
  • 72 of 93 adverts explicitly require production code experience.
  • Only 1 of 40 FDE adverts uses pre-sales language over delivery language.

What the adverts say
