About FDE at LayerX

Original title: LayerXにおけるFDEについて

Speaker Deck · 2025-08-08 · 8 min read

Summary

A slide deck by Masanori Onda, manager of the FDE team in LayerX's AI/LLM division, explaining how the company applies forward deployed engineering to its Ai Workforce product, which automates document processing in finance, manufacturing, healthcare and real estate. Drawing on Palantir and OpenAI job descriptions, he defines FDEs as engineers embedded at the customer front line who understand domain workflows, regulations and tacit knowledge, restructure business processes into LLM-native AI workflows, and build missing product features themselves. He reports workflow build times dropping from about two weeks to a two-business-day record, describes using tools like Claude Code and Devin, and closes with a recruiting pitch for FDE candidates in Japan, where the title is still little known.

Key points

  • LayerX's Ai Workforce automates document workflows across regulated industries.
  • FDEs deeply learn customer domains: documents, stakeholders, laws and tacit knowledge.
  • Workflow build lead time shrank from ~2 weeks to a 2-business-day record.
  • Missing features are built in-house, aided by Claude Code and Devin.
  • The FDE title is unfamiliar in Japan, complicating hiring efforts.
