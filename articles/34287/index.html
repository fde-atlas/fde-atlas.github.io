How much do forward deployed engineers travel?

Top FDE Jobs · 2026-11-10 · 3 min read

Summary

Top FDE Jobs analyzed 1,114 live FDE postings as of November 10, 2026; 355 stated a travel figure. The median is 50% of working time, and 11% of postings ask for more than half. Palantir requires the most travel (typically 25-75%) while Addepar requires the least (15%). Remote roles still median 30% travel. Senior roles ask for less (40%) than mid-level (50%), and Deployment Strategist roles travel the most at a 75% median. Figures reflect what employers write in postings, not employee reports.

Key points

  • Median stated travel is 50% of working time across 355 postings.
  • Palantir asks for the most travel (25-75%); Addepar the least (15%).
  • Remote FDE roles still require a median of 30% travel.
  • Deployment Strategist roles travel most, with a 75% median.
  • Senior roles ask for less travel (40%) than mid-level roles (50%).

What the postings say

  • Half of FDE postings that give a number ask for up to 50% travel. 31% stay at 25% or less, and 11% go above half the time.
  • Palantir asks for the most of the companies we can measure (typically 25–75%), Addepar the least (15%).
  • Remote does not mean no travel: remote FDE roles still ask for a median of 30%, against 50% for on-site roles.
  • Senior roles ask for less travel than mid-level roles: a median of 40% against 50%.
  • Deployment Strategist roles travel the most of any title, with a median of 75%.

How we counted

  • We read every live, editor-reviewed FDE posting on Top FDE Jobs (1,114 on November 10, 2026).
  • 51% mention travel. 32% give a number, and only those are counted here. Lines like "travel as needed" are left out.
  • We take the highest figure a posting states: "up to 50%" and "25–50%" both count as 50%. Time on site at customers counts as travel.
  • Boilerplate that says travel "varies from 0 to 100%" is left out, because it says nothing about the role.
  • This is what employers write in the posting, not what FDEs report after they join.

Questions

  • How much do forward deployed engineers travel?
  • Most FDE jobs that state a figure ask for 25% to 50% travel, often written as "up to 50%". The median is 50%, from 355 live postings as of 2026-11-10. 11% ask for more than half the time.
  • Do remote FDE jobs require travel?
  • Yes, usually. Remote FDE postings that state a figure ask for a median of 30% travel, mostly to customer sites. 42% of them stay at 25% or less.
  • Which companies ask FDEs to travel the most?
  • Among companies with 4 or more postings that state a figure, Palantir asks for the most (typically 25–75%). Addepar asks for the least (15%).
