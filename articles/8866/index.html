Forward Deployed Engineer Salary in India

Salary.com · 2025-04-22 · 2 min read

Summary

According to Salary.com Global Salary IQ data as of April 22, 2025, the average annual salary for a Forward Deployed Engineer in India is ₹14,07,380, or about ₹677 per hour. Salaries range from ₹11,29,889 (25th percentile) to ₹16,53,596 (75th percentile), depending on location, education, and experience. Entry-level engineers average around ₹12,83,131, while those with over eight years of experience reach about ₹15,47,025.
