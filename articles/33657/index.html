HCLTech FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) India: Salary & Role

FDE India · 2026-11-06 · 8 min read

Summary

This article reviews HCLTech's Forward Deployed Engineer hiring in India, which runs mainly through its software unit HCLSoftware. A Forward Deployed Software Engineer role was posted in Bengaluru on 13 June 2026 to deploy the company's agentic platform for enterprise clients, including API integration, field debugging, and work in secure or air-gapped environments. The listing is inconsistent on experience (2–5 years in the header, 7+ in requirements). India pay is not disclosed; comparable US roles list US$132,000–263,000. HCLTech reported FY26 revenue of US$14.7 billion and an advanced-AI run rate above US$620 million.

HCLTech hires Forward Deployed Engineers in India mainly through HCLSoftware, its software products business. In June 2026 HCLSoftware posted a Forward Deployed Software Engineer role in Bengaluru to deploy its agentic platform inside enterprise clients. Most of HCLTech’s other FDE-described roles we found are in the US, so India openings are fewer and you need to watch for them.

What the FDE does at HCLTech

The India role, posted on 13 June 2026 and seen on GetMeReferred, is a product FDE rather than a consulting one. The engineer:

  • Customises the platform. Turns HCLSoftware’s agentic platform into production-ready solutions for each enterprise client.
  • Integrates. Builds APIs, automation scripts and custom data pipelines into both legacy and modern client systems.
  • Debugs in the field. Diagnoses issues on-site or remotely, including edge cases in production.
  • Works in locked-down sites. The listing mentions highly secure and air-gapped environments.
  • Drives adoption. Leads technical discovery workshops and trains client teams.
  • Builds apps on top. Creates custom workflows and operational applications.

HCLTech’s services side uses the FDE model too, mostly in the US. Two Senior Group Technical Architect roles on HCLTech Careers (Dallas and Jersey City) describe a hands-on FDE who builds LLM workflows and copilots for healthcare payer and provider clients. A Chicago Senior Solution Architect role also hires FDEs who own the deployment lifecycle. A Platform Forward Deployed Engineer role deploys AI platforms into cloud, hybrid or on-premises client environments. An Agentic AI FDE role in New Jersey, now closed, led a team of three agent engineers.

Where FDEs fit in HCLTech’s AI push

In its April 2026 annual results, HCLTech reported FY26 revenue of US$14.7 billion and an annualised advanced-AI revenue run rate above US$620 million. It reported more than 227,000 employees. The release doesn’t mention FDEs or give an FDE headcount.

Press coverage in May 2026 from Whalesbook and the Financial Express (via TelecomLive) names HCLTech alongside Infosys, Wipro and Coforge as firms adopting the FDE role. Whalesbook says “HCLTech and Coforge are also scaling these specialized roles”, but gives no numbers. Compare the published plans on our Wipro and Infosys pages.

How to prepare

  • Build an integration project, not just a demo. Connect an LLM agent to a legacy-style system (a SQL database plus a SOAP or REST service) using Python or Java. Secure it with OAuth/JWT. This matches the Bengaluru role’s core work.
  • Ship it the enterprise way. Containerise it, run it on Kubernetes in one cloud, and deploy through CI/CD. Add a vector database for retrieval.
  • Practise offline deployment. Install and run your project with no internet access. Air-gapped work is named in the listing, and few candidates can talk about it.
  • Learn the compliance basics. Know what ISO 27001, SOC 2 and GDPR require of a deployment, such as access control, audit logs and data residency. These are listed nice-to-haves.
  • Prepare a workshop story. Be ready to describe a discovery session or client training you ran and what changed as a result. Our FDE interview questions guide has worked answers.

HCLTech FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Application: HCLTech's FDE roles don't always carry the FDE title. In the US they sit under titles such as 'Senior Group Technical Architect' and 'Senior Solution Architect', with the FDE role described in the body. In India, search for 'Forward Deployed' on HCLTech and HCLSoftware listings as well as job boards.
  • Technical and client rounds: HCLTech doesn't publish an FDE-specific interview format. Based on the Bengaluru listing, expect questions on APIs (REST, GraphQL, OAuth/JWT), Python or Java, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, one cloud, and on debugging and running workshops with client teams.
  • Offer: Pay is not posted on the India listing and no HCLTech-specific FDE salary data for India is public. Ask the recruiter for the band and whether the role is graded as engineer or architect.

Frequently asked questions

Does HCLTech hire Forward Deployed Engineers in India?

Yes, through HCLSoftware. A Forward Deployed Software Engineer role in Bengaluru was posted on 13 June 2026. Most of HCLTech's other FDE-described roles we found were in the US, so India openings are fewer and worth tracking closely.

How much experience does an HCLTech FDE role need?

The Bengaluru HCLSoftware listing is inconsistent: the header says 2–5 years, but the must-have list asks for 7+ years in software solutions, architecture or IT consulting. Plan for the higher figure. HCLTech's US FDE roles range from 2–6 years to 15+ years of client-facing work.

What is the salary of a Forward Deployed Engineer at HCLTech?

HCLTech doesn't post pay for the India FDE role and no India salary data specific to it is public. Its US listings that describe the FDE role post US$132,000–234,000 or US$155,000–263,000 a year, which shouldn't be read as an India benchmark.

The Bengaluru role turns HCLSoftware's agentic platform into production solutions for enterprise clients. That means building APIs, scripts and data pipelines into client systems, debugging production issues on-site or remotely, and running discovery workshops and training.
