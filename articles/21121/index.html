FDE Practical Knowledge: From Field Evidence to Acceptance-Ready Deliverables

Original title: FDE 实战知识：从现场证据到可验收交付

FDECareer.work · 2026-10-26 · 6 min read

Summary

This FDECareer hands-on guide breaks forward deployed engineering work into six knowledge units: reconstructing field evidence, selecting and validating scenarios, modeling business objects and data contracts, handling identity/authorization/approval/execution, evaluating unknown outcomes and recovery, and cost, adoption and handover. Each unit requires leaving verifiable evidence for the next stage. A synthetic access-request exercise runs throughout, referencing public sources like OWASP, AWS idempotency docs, and Google SRE monitoring, while explicitly noting the drills only prove behavior within the fixtures, not real customer outcomes.

Key points

  • Six units span field evidence to acceptance-ready delivery.
  • Separate direct observation, interviewee judgment, and analyst assumptions.
  • Authentication, authorization, approval, and idempotency are designed and tested separately.
  • Classify outcomes as definitely happened, not happened, or unknown before retrying.
  • Handover is complete only when the successor can independently operate and handle failures.

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) needs to advance a business problem to a verifiable result. This topic breaks the work into six knowledge units, each leaving behind evidence that the next stage can use. You can first complete an access request drill, then revisit the methods with the run records in hand.

These methods are FDECareer's engineering recommendations; the sample forms, organizations, people, and business data are independently designed synthetic materials and do not represent real customer experiences. Public engineering documents are used to verify technical principles, and drill results only prove behavior within the specified fixtures.

Field Evidence and Process Reconstruction

Applicability

The requirement comes from a single sentence, "approvals are too slow," but the team does not yet know where the waiting happens. First choose a task that allows replay, and observe the request, verification, approval, execution, confirmation, and expiration handling. When you are not permitted to use real data, practice the method with synthetic records and do not call the exercise field research.

Procedure

Ask the operator to walk through the most recent task step by step: what triggered it, which systems were opened, what the judgment was based on, and when they waited on someone else. For each observation, record the evidence location, time of occurrence, role, and questions to be confirmed. Keep direct observation, interviewee judgment, and analyst assumptions separate; when the described process conflicts with system records, preserve the discrepancy and schedule a follow-up check.

Sample Entry

Synthetic record E-01: the request was approved, but no provisioning receipt was issued. What can be confirmed is that "the outcome remains unknown after approval completes"; "execution service failure" is only a hypothesis and can only be established by querying execution records. The next step should be to find the receipt query entry point and the interface owner.

Acceptance and Boundaries

Have another participant reconstruct the task's start and end points, key decisions, and where exceptions went, using only the records. Every decision must be linked to at least one piece of evidence or explicitly marked as unknown. The number of interviews and pages of documentation cannot substitute for accuracy, nor can they be used to infer that the entire enterprise follows the same process.

Scenario Selection and Hypothesis Validation

Applicability

Multiple problems have been identified, and you need to decide which to validate first. First check ownership, data authorization, failure fallbacks, and observable outcomes; candidates lacking these conditions go into the evidence-gathering queue, and a single overall score should not mask blockers.

Procedure

Write the requirement as "who, under what trigger, turns what input into what outcome." Compare three implementation approaches: process adjustment, deterministic rules, and model assistance. List the evidence most likely to overturn the value hypothesis, then choose the smallest experiment: validate one dependency, one error, or one user action, and avoid building a complete platform from the start.

Sample Entry

In the access request, whether an employee is active should be determined by the authoritative personnel record, and whether permissions are allowed should be determined by policy; the model can assist in organizing the request rationale but is not responsible for approval. The first round of validation—"when the same approved request is called repeatedly, is there still only one provisioning effect"—is more concrete than validating whether a paragraph of text reads smoothly.

Acceptance and Boundaries

The scenario decision table must be able to explain why the chosen option was selected, why others were deferred, what evidence is still missing, and when to stop. Priority is a choice under current conditions; the template has no industry-universal weights and does not promise that any particular type of AI agent will deliver benefits.

Business Objects and Data Contracts

Applicability

Multiple parties use similar terms that actually point to different objects or time states. For example, "available account" might mean the employee is still employed, the license has remaining balance, or a particular access has not yet expired. These meanings should be modeled separately.

Procedure

List the request, employee, software, policy, approval, provisioning receipt, and revocation receipt. For each object, record the authoritative source, primary key, owning organization, status, version, validity period, and maintainer. For actions, specify preconditions, allowed fields, return values, errors, and query methods. First form a sufficient object layer within one task, then decide whether to extend it.

Sample Entry

Request REQ-01 belongs to the north organization, requests access to canvas-room, with viewer permission and an expiration date of simulated day 10. What manager-01 approves is this exact payload and policy version. The revocation receipt is a separate object; the expiration date having passed does not prove that external access has been revoked.

Acceptance and Boundaries

Walk through "employee deactivation," "permission change after approval," and "policy version change" one by one, confirming who provides the new fact and which old decision becomes invalid. The drill uses JSON status lines to simplify storage; it is not a recommendation to build all real identity systems with the same data structure.

Identity, Permissions, Approval, and Execution

Applicability

The system will produce business actions, or may read data from other organizations. Authentication confirms identity, authorization determines what can be done, human approval confirms a specific action, and idempotency controls the repeated effect of the same request. All four must be designed and verified separately.

Procedure

Obtain organization and role from a trusted server-side identity, and check permissions by resource and action. Have the approver confirm the specific object, permission scope, expiration date, and policy version; at execution time, re-verify eligibility, approval validity, and the current policy. When a request is denied, preserve an explainable state; never silently widen the permissions instead.

Sample Entry

After viewer is approved, the requester changes the permission to editor. Even though editor is a permission supported by the software, the old approval did not authorize this change, and the result should be approval_invalid. Likewise, a request whose approver has revoked approval must not continue to be provisioned.

Acceptance and Boundaries

Test for privilege escalation with different roles and the same resource ID, checking events and business effects rather than only whether the interface hides buttons. OWASP's authorization guidance supports least privilege, deny by default, and per-request checks; this topic applies those principles to synthetic tasks. The web role selector is only a teaching simulation and does not provide real authentication.

Evals, Unknown Outcomes, and Recovery

Applicability

An operation may time out, repeat, or exit midway. First distinguish "certainly did not happen," "certainly happened," and "outcome unknown," then decide whether to retry, query, or take over.

Procedure

Save expected results in the same format for normal, boundary, and rejection samples. Each time the process changes, use the same set of samples to compare status, provisioning count, revocation count, and receipts. For operations that were submitted but whose response was lost, query the business outcome first; recovering the original receipt should not produce a second provisioning. Save failure samples together with the remediation decisions.

Sample Entry

The offline drill first writes one provisioning effect, then simulates a lost response, closes the connection and reopens SQLite, and then queries the original receipt. The final state should be receipt_recovered, with a provisioning effect of 1. The web page only simulates in-memory snapshots; only the downloadable package performs real file persistence and connection reopening.

Acceptance and Boundaries

Passing means the assertions for the current scenario hold; it does not mean model accuracy or production performance targets are met. AWS's idempotency documentation explains how repeated requests avoid duplicate actions; this example only validates local state constraints, and real SaaS connections still require idempotency, querying, and reconciliation to be implemented or verified downstream.

Cost, Adoption, and Handover

Applicability

You are preparing to expand the pilot, or someone else needs to take over. First confirm whether the work has actually decreased, whether the risks are manageable, and whether maintenance responsibility will persist.

Procedure

Separately record the original manual operations, waiting time, added reviews, rework, and maintenance effort. Record time savings first as a capacity change; financial benefits are confirmed separately by the business side. At handover, have the successor perform normal tasks, query unknown outcomes, and handle revocation failures without developer guidance, and state when they are authorized to disable the automation.

Sample Entry

After the revocation interface fails, the status is human_owned, with provisioning effect 1 and revocation effect 0. The handover checklist should specify who checks the actual permissions, how manual revocation evidence is recorded, and how the event is closed. A red alert on the interface by itself cannot substitute for completed handling.

Acceptance and Boundaries

The handover is complete only when the person taking over can find the strategy owner, incident records, result queries, and manual handling paths. Google SRE's monitoring methodology distinguishes user-visible symptoms from internal causes; accordingly, this exercise keeps "pending recovery" rather than guessing the root cause of the interface failure. The sample contains no customer adoption rate or cash benefit data.

Applying the method to an independent exercise

First fill in the interview evidence and scenario decision table, then run the access request project. Pick a failure branch, write down the expected result, actual result, evidence, and fix, then have another person reproduce it. All four tables include blank rows and synthetic examples; when filling in unknown items, keep "pending verification" and do not fabricate research findings.

Primary engineering sources and update boundaries

OWASP Authorization Guide is used to verify the distinction between authentication and authorization, least privilege, and per-request checks.

Python 3.10 sqlite3 documentation is used to verify local transactions, commits, and the behavior of reopening connections after closing; the runtime environment is Python 3.10 or later.

AWS EC2 idempotent request documentation is used to explain the relationship between repeated requests and business effects; it does not mean this exercise connects to AWS.

Google SRE monitoring methodology is used to distinguish symptoms, causes, and actionable signals.
