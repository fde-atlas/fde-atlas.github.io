BeyondSoft Becomes an Alibaba Cloud FDE Certified Partner, with Its FDE Practices Gaining Further Ecosystem Recognition

On September 22, the 2026 Yunqi Conference opened in Hangzhou. At the "AI-Native: Reshaping Enterprise Productivity" sub-forum, a joint signing ceremony was held for the Alibaba Cloud Public Cloud Ecosystem Partner FDE program. Beyondsoft (002649) attended the ceremony as a representative of certified partners, completing the signing together with Alibaba Cloud and other ecosystem partners to further deepen ecosystem cooperation in the FDE field.

Becoming a certified partner in the Alibaba Cloud Public Cloud Ecosystem Partner FDE program is an important ecosystem recognition of Beyondsoft (002649)'s FDE practice, and also marks a further deepening of cooperation between the two parties in the areas of the FDE model, AI applications, and enterprise digital transformation.

FDE Certification

Capability Built Through Long-Term Practice

As AI accelerates its entry into core enterprise business, the value of technology services is extending from single-project delivery to business scenario understanding, technical solution implementation, and continuous operations. The FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) model is an effective practice that connects technology and business—industry experts and technical teams go deep into customers' business sites, solve problems in real scenarios, and continuously accumulate practical experience into reusable capabilities.

For Beyondsoft (002649), FDE is not a new model that only began to be explored in the AI era.

Over more than thirty years of digital technology services practice, Beyondsoft (002649) has continuously served customers in finance, energy (850101), manufacturing, internet, and other industries, steadily accumulating industry know-how, software engineering, and technical delivery capabilities in complex business scenarios. Entering the AI era, the company has further integrated these capabilities with AI, big data, cloud computing (885362), and other technologies, continuously exploring service approaches that are closer to business needs.

A project for an overseas branch of a state-owned bank is a representative FDE practice. Beyondsoft (002649) assembled an FDE team to go deep into the customer's business system, guided by "technology restructuring plus deep business engagement," participating in the construction and optimization of multiple systems, continuously embedding technical capabilities into overseas banking business scenarios, with the service boundary extending from technical support to business system construction.

In a manufacturing R&D governance project, Beyondsoft (002649) likewise used the FDE approach to integrate business understanding, R&D governance, and AI engineering capabilities into the customer's R&D system, driving technical capabilities deep into R&D and production scenarios.

From going deep into business sites, to solving real problems, to accumulating reusable capabilities, FDE is becoming an important practice for Beyondsoft (002649) to connect technology and business.

Long-Term Ecosystem Cooperation

Driving Further Deepening of FDE Practice

This FDE certified partner signing is also built on the foundation of long-term cooperation between Beyondsoft (002649) and Alibaba Cloud.

The cooperation between the two parties covers multiple areas including cloud deployment, operations and maintenance services, solutions, overseas business, and large model applications, and continues to expand cooperation around enterprise digitalization and AI applications. Previously, the two parties jointly launched solutions based on the Cloud Well-Architected Framework for enterprises going global, and carried out ecosystem collaboration in areas such as financial AI agents. Joining the Alibaba Cloud Public Cloud Ecosystem Partner FDE program further extends the cooperation to the FDE service model and enterprise AI implementation practices.

Going Deep into Business with FDE

Making AI Truly Create Value

In the AI era, the value of technology ultimately needs to be validated through real business scenarios. Being granted Alibaba Cloud FDE certified partner status is both a recognition of Beyondsoft (002649)'s existing FDE practice and technical service capabilities, and opens up new space for the next stage of cooperation between the two parties.

Going forward, Beyondsoft (002649) will continue to use the FDE model as a key lever, deepening collaboration with Alibaba Cloud and other ecosystem partners, leveraging industry experience, technical capabilities, and a global service system to move AI from technical capability into real enterprise business scenarios, and explore new paths from technical delivery toward co-creation of value.
