Structural Map of FDE Hiring at Leading Overseas AI Companies (2026-11): Job Families, Levels, and Regional Comparison

Original title: 海外头部 AI 公司 FDE 招聘结构图谱（2026-11）：岗位家族、职级与区域对比

FDECareer.work · 2026-11-15 · 6 min read

Summary

Based on a frozen snapshot of 676 verified open roles (491 overseas, 177 in China) as of 2026-11-15, this FDECareer report maps how top AI companies structure their forward deployed engineering hiring. Searching only for Forward Deployed Engineer misses over half of client-facing delivery roles, since OpenAI, Anthropic and Mistral AI use titles like Applied AI Engineer, Applied AI Architect and AI Deployment Strategist. The top five overseas employers (Databricks 130, OpenAI 119, Palantir 64, Anthropic 49, Mistral AI 48) account for 83.5% of the overseas sample. Entry-level roles are rare—only Palantir lists 14 graduate positions—while senior and lead roles dominate. Salary disclosure varies by state transparency laws and ATS platform. China's 177 roles skew toward mid-level and graduate positions, concentrated in Beijing, Hangzhou, Shenzhen and Shanghai.

Key points

  • Top five overseas employers hold 410 of 491 overseas roles (83.5%).
  • OpenAI, Anthropic and Mistral hide FDE work under Applied AI titles.
  • Only Palantir lists graduate-level roles (14); senior/lead dominates overseas.
  • Salary disclosure: Anthropic 75.5%, Databricks 63.1%, OpenAI 50.4%.
  • China's 177 roles center on Beijing, Hangzhou, Shenzhen, Shanghai.

I. Role Family Composition: Don't Search Only for the Phrase "Forward Deployed"

If a job seeker searches only for

Forward Deployed Engineer

on overseas job pages, they will miss more than half of the roles that actually handle on-site AI delivery for customers. As shown in the chart below, the five companies have a clear taxonomy of role families for their frontline delivery teams:

  • Palantir (64 roles): A highly pure FDSE/FDIE system, with 62 roles directly belonging to the Forward Deployed Software / Infrastructure Engineer family and no separate proliferation of frontline management titles.
  • Databricks (130 roles): FDE engineering roles (72) run in parallel with frontline delivery management roles (Manager / Sr. Manager / Director, 40 in total), reflecting an organizational form of building out teams by region and industry line.
  • OpenAI (119 roles): Uses "dual-track naming"—besides 30 roles directly named Forward Deployed Engineer, another 46 Applied AI Engineers, 20 Applied AI Architects, and 23 management/lead roles jointly handle enterprise integration and production deployment.
  • Anthropic (49 roles): Applied AI Architect (22) and Applied AI Engineer (11) are the dominant titles, with only 3 roles directly named Forward Deployed Engineer.
  • Mistral AI (48 roles): In its expansion across Europe and Asia-Pacific, it heavily uses a combination of AI Deployment Strategist (24) and Applied AI Engineer (15).

II. Seniority Structure and Salary Disclosure: Entry-Level Pipeline Narrowing, Senior and Lead Roles Dominant

Based on public job titles and structured seniority fields, overseas frontline delivery roles place a markedly high demand on candidates' ability to operate independently:

  • Graduate / Entry channel: Among the overseas top five, only Palantir publicly lists 14 FDSE roles aimed at new graduates or early-career candidates; Databricks, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Mistral do not list any Graduate-level FDE roles in the current sample (mostly using generic individual-contributor titles without level suffixes, or directly requiring Senior/Lead).
  • Senior and Lead/Manager: Databricks explicitly marks Senior (64) and Lead/Manager (49), together accounting for 86.9% of its total; OpenAI marks 30 Lead/Manager roles, with the remaining 89 using unified titles without a seniority prefix (e.g., Applied AI Engineer / Forward Deployed Engineer).
  • Public salary disclosure rate (counted only via public ATS endpoints and on-page text): due to pay transparency laws in California, New York, Washington and other US states, roles located in these regions generally disclose base salary ranges—Anthropic's disclosure rate is 75.5% (37/49), Databricks' is 63.1% (82/130), and OpenAI's is 50.4% (60/119); meanwhile, Palantir and Mistral roles on the Lever platform's public endpoints do not return salary ranges in structured fields.

IV. Independent Slice of the 177 Domestic Open Roles and Job-Seeking Action Recommendations

Since domestic recruitment platforms (Liepin 78 listings, BOSS Zhipin 51, company websites 30, Zhaopin 15, 51job 11) use job titles and leveling conventions different from overseas ATS systems, this report lists the 177 domestic open roles separately for comparison:

  • Top domestic employers: Meituan (10), Tencent (10), Alibaba (9), Alibaba Cloud (7), ByteDance (7), Ant Group (3), SenseTime (3), Digital China (3), and DiDi (2) lead the list, with roles concentrated in four cities: Beijing (51), Hangzhou (27), Shenzhen (23), and Shanghai (14).
  • Domestic seniority levels and role types: unlike the overseas emphasis on Senior/Lead roles, the domestic sample has a relatively high share of roles explicitly at the Mid level (54) and Graduate/entry level (12), with a large number centered on two main tracks: "Agent platform / Agent harness engineering" and "private LLM delivery architecture for government, enterprise, and financial clients".
  • Three direct recommendations for job seekers:
  • Expand your search terms: when searching for overseas opportunities, besides Forward Deployed Engineer, be sure to also search for Applied AI Engineer, Applied AI Architect, and Deployment Strategist;
  • Prepare compliance and language skills by region: OpenAI and Anthropic have many regional on-site roles in Tokyo, Singapore, Munich, London, and Paris, which typically require local business language proficiency and the ability to work on-site across time zones;

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Palantir and Mistral show a salary disclosure of 0?

Both companies use the Lever recruiting system, whose public job listing endpoint and pages, unlike some Greenhouse/Ashby postings, do not uniformly publish salary ranges in structured salary fields. We therefore record them as "salary not disclosed" as-is, without filling in external estimates.

Do OpenAI's and Anthropic's Applied AI Architect roles count as FDEs?

Under this site's inclusion criteria, a role is manually verified and tagged as strongly relevant (Strong) only when its public description explicitly includes responsibilities such as architecture design for enterprise customers, prototype building, systems integration, or production deployment. Although the title does not contain the words "Forward Deployed", the core work content overlaps heavily.

Why do the role counts in the report differ by a few from the real-time numbers on the homepage?

The homepage shows real-time open role counts that change daily with collection and closing tasks, whereas the industry report is locked to a frozen snapshot taken on 2026-11-15 (676 open roles) to keep the charts, body text, and CSV strictly consistent.
