The Elite FDE Scarcity Study

Christian & Timbers · 2026-07-30 · 1 min read

Summary

Christian & Timbers' Elite FDE Scarcity Study analyzes six months of demand signals showing elite Forward Deployed Engineer hiring outpacing every other enterprise AI role. It maps the elite FDE talent pool using two independent methodologies that converge on the same count, finding the pool concentrated in a single lineage. Drawing on 250+ executive interviews and a survey of 80 Fortune 500 executives, cross-referenced with Indeed, Live Data Technologies, LinkedIn Economic Graph, and Lightcast data, the report argues only companies with elite FDEs see ROI on AI investment. It also reports proprietary figures on internal elite FDE team building and Palantir clients hiring primarily for its FDE talent rather than the software.

Key points

  • Six-month analysis shows elite FDE hiring outpacing all other enterprise AI roles
  • Two independent methods converge on the same elite FDE talent pool count
  • Pool is concentrated in a single lineage, per the study's supply-side mapping
  • Based on 250+ executive interviews and a survey of 80 Fortune 500 executives
  • Some Palantir clients engage mainly for its FDE talent, not the software
