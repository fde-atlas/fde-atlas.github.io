FDE Career Guide | Paths to Becoming an FDE for New Graduates, Career Changers, and Those Without Experience, Plus Internships

Original title: FDE就職ガイド|新卒・第二新卒・未経験からFDEになるルートとインターン

Summary

This Japanese career guide, based on official job postings as of October 2026, maps routes into Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) roles for new graduates, early-career engineers, and people without development experience. Of 517 job listings containing FDE-related titles across 133 job boards, only 36 targeted new graduates or interns, 33 of them from Palantir, which hires new-grad Forward Deployed Software Engineers in the US at $135,000–145,000. No new-grad FDE full-time roles exist in Japan; LayerX offers student FDE internships. Low-experience-entry roles include JAPAN AI's Associate FDE (1+ year), Palantir Tokyo, and JDSC (2+ years). Non-engineers can enter via customer-facing FDE roles at companies like Andpad and RevComm, while those without coding experience should first work as developers for 2–3 years.

Key points

  • Only 36 of 517 FDE job listings targeted new grads or interns; 33 were Palantir's, none based in Japan.
  • Palantir hires new-grad FDSEs in the US at $135,000–145,000; C3 AI and Sierra have similar programs.
  • Japan's student entry point is LayerX's FDE internships; Fujitsu's month-long internship was not accepting applications.
  • Low-barrier roles: JAPAN AI Associate FDE (1+ year), Palantir Tokyo (1+ year), JDSC (2+ years), LayerX Associate.
  • Non-engineers can apply via customer-facing FDE roles; those without coding experience should start as developers.

Written and reviewed by: FDE Talent Editorial Team / Primary sources such as official job postings and official announcements from each company are cited

An increasing number of students and young engineers want to work as an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer). However, most FDE job openings in Japan target candidates with practical work experience, and there is almost no consolidated information on questions like "Can new grads or early-career professionals become FDEs?" or "Which companies can they apply to?"

Based on each company's official job pages, this article organizes examples of new-grad FDE openings and internships, positions that less-experienced candidates can apply for, career paths into FDE roles from non-engineering jobs, and what students should prepare while still in school.

Key Takeaways of This Article

  • New-grad FDE positions exist overseas, mainly in the US; as of October 2026, Palantir is recruiting Forward Deployed Software Engineers targeting those graduating in December 2026 or spring 2027, with an annual salary of $135,000–$145,000.
  • When the editorial team tallied 133 public job boards used mainly by overseas companies on October 16, 2026, of 517 job postings whose titles included Forward Deployed, FDE, or FDSE, 36 explicitly mentioned new grad or internship in the job title (33 of which were Palantir), and none were based in Japan.
  • In Japan, as of October 2026, no full-time FDE positions for new grads were found, but LayerX is recruiting an FDE internship for students (Fujitsu's winter FDE internship could not be accessed via the application page as of October 16).
  • FDE openings that people with limited work experience can apply for include JAPAN AI's Associate FDE, which welcomes early-career professionals with 1+ years of experience; Palantir (Tokyo), which requires 1+ years of work experience after graduating from university; and JDSC, which requires 2+ years of development experience (as of October 2026).
  • For those without practical software development experience, a realistic path is to first get a job as a developer and then aim for an FDE role in their second or third year.

New-Grad FDE Openings: Examples from the US and Elsewhere

Palantir, the company where the FDE role originated, publishes Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE) openings for new grads and student interns, mainly in the US.

Palantirの新卒・インターン向けFDSE求人(米国、2026年10月時点)
Job Title on PostingEligibilityLocationCompensation Listed

Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Internship - CommercialThose graduating in December 2027 or spring 2028New York$10,000 per month

The same new-grad FDSE roles are also open in Chicago, London, and Seoul in addition to New York. The Seoul posting requires business-level Korean and English reading, writing, and conversation. New-grad and internship FDSE positions in Tokyo were not posted as of October 16, 2026.

Besides Palantir, other companies offer new-grad tracks. C3 AI is recruiting "Forward Deployed Engineer, New Grad 2027" (California, base salary $120,000–$150,000) for those graduating in December 2026 or summer/fall 2027. Sierra, which calls FDE-type work "Agent Engineer," is recruiting for its new-grad role "Software Engineer, Agent (New Grad 2027)" in San Francisco and New York ($150,000–$180,000 annually) and Singapore (S$200,000–$240,000 annually) (all as of October 16, 2026).

However, new-grad tracks are still limited to a small number of companies. When the editorial team tallied 133 public job boards used mainly by overseas companies on October 16, 2026, of 517 job postings whose titles included Forward Deployed, FDE, or FDSE, 36 explicitly mentioned new grad or internship in the job title, 33 of which were Palantir postings. There were no new-grad or internship positions based in Japan.

Related article: What Is Sierra's FDE "Agent Engineer"? Job Postings Including a New-Grad Track and Why They Don't Call It FDE

According to The Pragmatic Engineer, Palantir also hires people with about a year of professional experience after graduating from university, and while the US fintech company Ramp requires five or more years of experience for senior FDEs, it also hires outstanding new graduates as FDEs. First Round Review lists "not bringing in past ways of doing things" as one of the qualities required of FDEs, noting that new graduates bring fresh eyes. FDE is not a role determined solely by years of experience.

Experience handling customer problems in an internship can be presented as an achievement both in job hunting and when transferring to an FDE role a few years later. FDE job openings that less-experienced people can apply for

Most FDE job openings in Japan target experienced candidates, but there are openings requiring fewer years of experience, as well as roles that lead to FDE positions.

  • JAPAN AI "Associate FDE / Welcome to Second New Graduates": Required conditions are one or more years of professional experience as a software engineer (or equivalent continuous development track record) and experience prototyping and developing LLM-based applications yourself (whether at work, in an internship, or as personal development). This is a track for developing people to handle end-to-end implementation within a year while receiving reviews from senior FDEs, with a monthly salary of ¥357,143 to ¥825,000 (including a fixed overtime allowance for 45 hours)
  • ExaWizards "Junior Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" (launching the new Fukuoka company Exa Forward Kyushu): Required qualifications are any one of: one or more years of professional development experience, professional experience in SES, contract development, or in-house SE roles, or being equivalent to a second new graduate from an information science or engineering faculty with development experience whether personal or professional (AI experience not required). Annual salary is ¥5.04 million to ¥6.6 million (including deemed overtime pay for 45 hours)
  • JDSC "[Dev] FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) [Full-Time]": Required qualification is two or more years of development experience as an engineer; FDE or AI project experience is not required
  • Palantir (Tokyo) "Forward Deployed Software Engineer - Japan Government": One or more years of professional experience after graduating from university. Both Japanese and English are required, and holding or being able to obtain a security clearance is also a condition
  • SoftBank "Software Deployment Engineer (SDE)": Three or more years of software engineering experience. The job posting states that the role will expand to FDE in the future
  • LayerX "Forward Deployed Engineer (Associate)": A role handling implementation projects alongside senior members, with no specified years of experience among the requirements, and an estimated annual salary (guideline) starting from ¥8 million. It is set up separately from Forward Deployed Engineer, which requires one or more years of design and development experience (estimated annual salary guideline starting from ¥12 million)

Moving into FDE from non-engineer roles

Some FDE job openings do not specify years of development experience and instead require experience working directly with customers. For people who have worked in presales, implementation consulting, or customer success, such openings serve as an entry point.

  • ANDPAD: Professional experience in any of presales, implementation consulting, or systems engineering, plus the technical literacy to read and write code
  • RevComm: Experience in customer engagement in the BtoB SaaS and IT solutions domain, plus experience with APIs and data integration
  • Channel Talk: Experience serving enterprise customers (presales, solution engineer, technical consultant, CSM, etc.)

However, all of these openings have technical requirements. Since it is difficult to apply without being able to write code at all, you need hands-on experience with API integration, SQL, and implementing simple applications. Another option is to pursue the Deployment Strategist role, which focuses mainly on problem discovery, recruited by Palantir and KINTO Technologies.

What to prepare while still a student or early in your career

  • 01 Build one application incorporating an LLM from start to finish: Include authentication, error handling, and logging, and get it to a state where people can actually use it
  • 02 Implement evaluation (Evals): Prepare test cases and make it possible to measure how accuracy changes when prompts or models are changed
  • 03 Talk to real users: Listen to the pain points in familiar operations, such as in your lab, part-time job, or club, and build experience rephrasing requests into problems
  • 04 Explain results in terms of changes for users: Learn to speak not in terms of "what you built" but in terms of "whose what changed and how"
  • 05 Reading primary sources in English: OpenAI, Databricks, and Palantir's FDE positions in Tokyo require English in addition to Japanese (as of October 2026)

Should your first job be an FDE or a development role?

In Japan, there are limited openings for new graduates to work as FDEs. A realistic path is to start in a development role, gain experience building and operating production systems, and then aim for an FDE position. The technical hiring bar for FDEs is the same as for regular software engineers, and Palantir describes the scope of the FDSE role in its job posting as "similar to a startup CTO." Delivering projects with a small team and minimal supervision requires the ability to build things end-to-end on your own.

If you start in a development role, choosing a company or department where you can speak directly with customers and users will make the transition to FDE faster. Experience with contracted development or working on-site at client companies, joining pre-sales meetings, and internal business improvement projects are all experiences that are directly valued in FDE hiring processes.

Summary

FDE positions for new graduates exist overseas at companies like Palantir and C3 AI, and in Japan, LayerX recruits student-oriented FDE internships. For second-time new graduates and those with limited professional experience, entry points include JAPAN AI's Associate FDE, Palantir (Tokyo), JDSC, SoftBank's SDE, and LayerX's Associate roles. If you have no professional development experience, start in a development role and aim for an FDE position while gaining experience working with customers. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q: Can you become an FDE as a new graduate?

In the US and elsewhere, there are FDE positions for new graduates. Palantir is recruiting Forward Deployed Software Engineers at an annual salary of $135,000–$145,000 for those graduating in December 2026 or spring 2027. C3 AI also recruits new-graduate FDEs for those graduating in December 2026 or in the summer or fall of 2027. In Japan, as of October 2026, no full-time FDE positions for new graduates were found, though LayerX recruits student-oriented FDE internships.

Q: Are there FDE internships?

Yes. In Japan, LayerX recruits two types: a 1-week internship and a practical internship. Fujitsu also has a page for a one-month FDE internship (December 1–25, 2026), but as of October 16, 2026, the application screen was not accessible. In the US, Palantir recruits interns at $10,000 per month for those graduating in December 2027 or spring 2028.

Q: Can second-time new graduates apply for FDE positions?

There are openings you can apply to. JAPAN AI offers an Associate FDE track welcoming second-time new graduates, requiring at least one year of professional experience as a software engineer (or equivalent continuous development track record). ExaWizards' Junior Forward Deployed Engineer (Fukuoka) is open to those equivalent to second-time new graduates from information science or STEM faculties with development experience. Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer in Tokyo requires at least one year of professional experience after graduating from university, and JDSC requires at least two years of development experience. SoftBank's Software Deployment Engineer requires three or more years of experience and is a role that will expand into FDE over time (all as of October 2026).

Q: Can humanities graduates or non-engineers become FDEs?

There are FDE openings that do not specify years of development experience and instead require experience in pre-sales, implementation consulting, or client-facing work (Andpad, RevComm, Channel Talk). However, they do set technical requirements such as the technical literacy to read and write code, and experience with APIs and data integration. Your academic background doesn't matter, but implementation experience is necessary.

Q: Is English required to become an FDE?

It depends on the employer. OpenAI's Tokyo FDE position requires proficiency in both Japanese and English, with resumes submitted in English. Databricks' Tokyo FDE position requires fluent Japanese and business-level English. Some positions, like Notion's Forward Deployed Architect, list English as a preferred qualification, while others, like JAPAN AI, conduct customer work mainly in Japanese with English as a preferred condition (as of October 2026).
