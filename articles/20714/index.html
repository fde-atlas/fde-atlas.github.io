Summary

This page is "FDE board," a Japanese job aggregator for Forward Deployed Engineer roles run by Westeria Co., Ltd. It crawls company career pages and ATS systems daily and updates weekly, listing 75 openings from 47 companies as of November 10, 2026. Employers include foreign AI firms (Palantir, OpenAI, Databricks, 16 postings) and domestic companies and consultancies (59 postings). Among the 31 postings with public salary ranges, the median upper bound is ¥15 million, with a maximum of ¥30 million. Most roles require software development and client-facing experience, while 5 postings explicitly accept candidates without prior FDE experience. The site also lists 11 adjacent roles such as Solutions Engineer and Applied AI Engineer, and links directly to original postings without handling applications.

Frequently asked questions about FDE job postings

Q. What kinds of companies are hiring FDEs in Japan?

As of 2026-11-10, 47 companies are hiring. They fall broadly into foreign AI companies such as Palantir, OpenAI, and Databricks (16 postings) and Japanese companies with AI products or consulting firms (59 postings). Postings by company can be viewed in the list of "Companies hiring FDEs."

Q. What is the typical salary range for FDE job postings?

31 of the 75 postings disclose salary; among those, the median of the upper bounds is ¥15M and the highest is ¥30M (aggregation of postings as of 2026-11-10). Note that foreign companies' Japan offices often keep salaries undisclosed. The distribution by range is summarized on the salary page.

Q. Can I apply for FDE jobs without prior experience?

Most job postings require experience in software development and client-facing work, but 5 postings explicitly state "no experience required" (as of 2026-11-10). You can filter by the "No experience required" tag. Since FDE itself is a new role, "FDE experience" is not required, and most postings expect candidates to transition from related roles (development, SE, consulting).

Q. How is the job information collected, and when is it updated?

We crawl each company's careers page and applicant tracking system (ATS) daily to check for expired postings and add new ones (last updated 2026-11-10). Applications link directly to each company's careers page (primary source). This site never acts as an intermediary for applications.
