About Plank

Plank · 2026-06-14 · 2 min read

Summary

Plank builds AI-native forward deployed engineering teams for enterprise clients. Rather than recruiting scarce talent, it trains engineers in its own AI Accelerator—covering agent pipelines, evals, and production deployment—then embeds them as co-located pods working directly in client codebases. The company positions itself as vendor-neutral across OpenAI, Anthropic, and open models, and measures success by production deployments rather than pilots, with evals on every pull request. It was founded by Privahini Bradoo (previously CEO of BlueOak) and Anusheel Bhushan (previously CTO of Plato.ai), and operates in-person centers in San Francisco, Brazil, Spain, and Poland.

Key points

  • Plank trains its own AI-native FDEs instead of brokering talent.
  • Engineers embed as co-located pods inside client teams and codebases.
  • Vendor-neutral across OpenAI, Anthropic, and open models.
  • Production-first: evals run on every pull request.
  • Founded by Bradoo and Bhushan; centers in SF, Brazil, Spain, Poland.

Plank builds co-located, AI-native engineering teams that embed with the companies building the future, and take their hardest technical challenges to production.

AI is rewriting how software gets built. We’re rewriting how teams do.

The hardest part of enterprise AI was never the model. It’s the deployment, the integration with real systems, the judgment, and the engineers who can take a pilot all the way to production. That talent is scarce, and hiring it takes months.

So we build it. Plank trains AI-native forward-deployed engineers through an intensive accelerator, then embeds them as co-located pods alongside your team, on your codebase, with your customers, the same day. Vendor-neutral, production-first, and backed by QA on every pull request.

What we believe

How we operate

Trained, not sourced

Every engineer is forged in the Plank AI Accelerator before they embed, on agent pipelines, evals, and production deployment. We build the talent, we don’t broker it.

Vendor-neutral

We work across OpenAI, Anthropic, and open models. Your architecture stays yours, and you are never locked into a single lab or platform, or to us.

In person, in your day

Pods work together in person from Plank centres, with deep overlap into your working day. Real teams with shared chemistry, not scattered freelancers.

Production-first

We measure ourselves by what ships and sticks in production, not pilots and demos. A harness-driven SDLC with evals on every pull request makes that the default.
