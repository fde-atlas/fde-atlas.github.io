FDE Role Decoder

FDE Commons by The AI Runtime · 2026 · 1 min read

Summary

This article decodes the FDE role by comparing it with adjacent positions—Solutions Engineer, Solutions Architect, Applied AI Engineer, Forward Deployed SWE, and Deployment Lead/Strategist—along three dimensions: direct code ownership, customer outcome ownership, and center of gravity. It argues job titles are used inconsistently, so candidates should evaluate actual ownership boundaries rather than titles. It offers seven concrete interview questions, such as what production artifact you will personally own and who owns the outcome after a proof of concept. It also outlines a seniority ladder: from scoped delivery, to converting field patterns into platform primitives, to sequencing teams and managing risk. The author cautions these are market patterns, not universal rules, and job descriptions should be read individually.

Key points

  • Six roles compared by code ownership, customer ownership, and focus.
  • FDEs combine high code ownership with very high customer outcome ownership.
  • Forward Deployed SWEs emphasize production code and reusable abstractions.
  • Seven interview questions help reveal real ownership boundaries.
  • Seniority grows from scoped delivery to platform-building to team sequencing.

Full text

01 System

From implementing one integration to owning a multi-system production architecture.

ROLE DECODER

Titles are reused inconsistently. Compare technical ownership, customer outcome ownership, specialization, and the artifact you are expected to ship.

These are market patterns, not universal rules. Read each company's job description.

Role Direct code ownership Customer ownership Center of gravity
Solutions Engineer Low-medium High Technical evaluation, demos, integrations, pre/post-sales enablement
Solutions Architect Low-medium High Architecture, constraints, technical strategy, stakeholder alignment
Applied AI Engineer Medium-high Medium-high Model behavior, prototypes, evals, and AI application implementation
Forward Deployed Engineer High Very high Production outcome across discovery, build, rollout, and learning
Forward Deployed SWE Very high High Production code, custom systems, and reusable software abstractions
Deployment Lead / Strategist Low-medium Very high Workflow shaping, sequencing, adoption, KPIs, and multi-team execution

EVALUATE THE ROLE

A good interview works both ways. Replace vague claims about “impact” with concrete ownership boundaries.

  • 01What production artifact will I personally own?
  • 02How often do I write and review production code?
  • 03Who owns the outcome after the proof of concept?
  • 04How much time is discovery, building, rollout, and adoption?
  • 05Does field learning change the core product? How?
  • 06What travel, on-site, clearance, or support load exists?
  • 07How are success and seniority measured?

SENIORITY

From scoped delivery to shaping ambiguous, consequential workflows with senior stakeholders.

From solving once to turning field patterns into platform primitives and product improvements.

From individual delivery to sequencing teams, managing risk, and raising engineering quality.
