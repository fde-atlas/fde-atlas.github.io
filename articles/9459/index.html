Best Firm for FDEs

Best Firm by AIM Research · 2026-07-15 · 1 min read

Summary

AIM Research and Best Firm offer an employee-driven workplace certification aimed at organizations employing FDEs and adjacent AI deployment professionals, roles the article describes as bridging AI innovation and business outcomes. Eligibility covers any India-based organization with technology, data, or AI talent willing to run an anonymous culture survey. Scoring relies on employee approval ratings across five pillars: Alignment; Execution & Accountability; Growth & Learning; Inclusion & Belonging; and Psychological Safety. Organizations meeting thresholds on each pillar earn certification for the calendar year. The process takes roughly 4–6 weeks, from registration through survey administration, analysis, and announcement. Pricing varies by organization size and number of surveyed locations and is quoted on request.

Key points

  • Certification targets Indian employers of FDEs and AI deployment talent.
  • Scores come from anonymous employee surveys, not self-assessment.
  • Five pillars include alignment, execution, growth, inclusion, and psychological safety.
  • Meeting thresholds on all pillars earns a calendar-year certification.
  • End-to-end process takes 4–6 weeks; pricing is size-based.

Full text

Forward Deployed Engineers and adjacent AI deployment professionals are rapidly emerging as the critical bridge between cutting-edge AI innovation and real business outcomes. From architecting enterprise AI solutions to deploying, governing, and scaling them in production, these roles are reshaping how organizations realize value from AI.

FAQ

Best Firm for FDEs is an independent, employee-driven workplace certification by Best Firm and AIM Media House. It evaluates organisations across five pillars: Alignment, Execution & Accountability, Growth & Learning, Inclusion & Belonging, and Psychological Safety.

Any organisation in India that employs technology, data, or AI talent and is willing to invite its employees to participate in an anonymous, research-backed culture survey is eligible to apply.

Scoring is based on the approval rating from employee survey responses across the five pillars. Organisations meeting the threshold on each pillar are awarded certified status for the calendar year.

Typical end-to-end timeline is 4–6 weeks: registration, survey administration to employees, data analysis, and certification announcement.

Pricing depends on organisation size and number of locations surveyed. Contact the Best Firm team at [email protected] for a tailored quote.
