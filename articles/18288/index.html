Resident FDE | Transforming an entire company through DX, thoroughly

Original title: 常駐型FDE｜会社まるごと、DXしつくす

アンドドット株式会社 · 2026 · 10 min read

Summary

Japanese company and. (AndDot) offers a resident FDE service built around the "Forward Deployed Unit" (FDU), a team embedded in client sites covering the full cycle from problem discovery through planning, development, and operational adoption. The article argues enterprise DX stalls because four types of talent are missing at once, and a single FDE combines consulting, engineering, and project management. A standard unit pairs an FDE on-site four days a week with a Success Partner joining weekly and Experts on demand; if an FDE leaves, a Success Partner steps in within 72 hours. Quality is systematized via a four-stage "FDE Journey" process, the CEPO talent framework, and the AndDot Knowledge internal repository. Seven example scenarios list estimated annual hours saved, which the article notes are illustrative, not actual results.

Key points

  • Argues DX stalls because four talent gaps coexist: structuring problems, deciding priorities, implementing, and sustaining operations.
  • FDU unit combines an on-site FDE, a weekly Success Partner, and on-demand Experts; gaps are filled within 72 hours.
  • Four-stage FDE Journey process requires client agreement at each phase to reduce rework.
  • CEPO framework scores talent on four axes; members averaging 4+ are designated Senior FDEs.
  • Seven case scenarios cite estimated hours saved, explicitly labeled as illustrative assumptions, not actual results.

FORWARD DEPLOYED ENGINEER

DX, done completely.

Anddot's resident FDE continuously drives DX/AX across all of your business operations.

No special instructions or complicated preparation required. Simply having Anddot's resident FDE at your company is enough to move DX/AX forward. The FDE understands your business on the front line, and repeatedly handles issue discovery, solution proposals, system development, and operational adoption in-house.

The Shortest Path

The shortest path to DX/AX is a resident FDE.

What DX/AX requires is talent that can drive initiatives across departments. With a resident FDE, you can place that driver inside your company immediately. No hiring or training needed.

1 Challenges companies face

DX/AX stalls at large enterprises because they lack in-house talent to drive it. Specifically, shortages in the following four types of talent are occurring simultaneously. That is why unresolved issues keep accumulating inside enterprises.

01 No one who can structurally organize frontline pain points and reframe them as lines, not isolated points.

02 No one who can compare multiple options by business impact and implementation difficulty and decide on a direction.

03 No one who can translate the decided direction into working systems and operations.

04 No one who can embed the built mechanisms into the organization and keep them running in operations.

※ In recent years, these shortages have been compounded by a lack of talent able to use generative AI effectively in business.

2 Value we provide

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a talent who can handle consulting, engineering, and project management alone. They fill all four of these talent shortages at once, and continuously realize AI-driven DX on your frontline.

Forward Deployed Unit

A dedicated business improvement team, placed inside your company.

FDU (Forward Deployed Unit) means "a unit deployed to the frontline." At its core is the FDE. Rather than leaving the resident FDE alone, one unit is formed from three roles: a Success Partner and an Expert supporting from behind.

Systemized

A systemized FDE framework.

To prevent results from depending on individuals, Anddot has systemized three aspects of the FDE: "process," "talent," and "back-end support." That is why improvements keep running at consistent quality on any frontline.

1 Systemizing the FDE process: "FDE Journey©"

FDE projects proceed through four stages, modeled on the Fit Journey method for new businesses. Each stage confirms "what has been agreed upon," creating a structure with minimal rework in later stages.

◯ As long as the FDE is there, this cycle keeps turning

CPFSTEP 01

Discovering the essential problem

Customer Problem Fit

Rather than taking requests at face value, the FDE embeds into the frontline and structures "what truly needs to be solved."

ゴール：

The true problem can be explained in the customer's own words

PSFSTEP 02

Discovering the optimal solution

Problem Solution Fit

Multiple options are evaluated by business impact, difficulty, and operational load, and one option to pursue is agreed upon.

ゴール：

The direction of the solution is agreed upon with the customer

SPFSTEP 03

Implementing the solution

Solution Product Fit

Development proceeds in 1–2 week sprints, engaging the frontline to shape a working product.

ゴール：

A working product is being used in actual operations

POFSTEP 04

In-house adoption and operations

Product Operation Fit

A state that runs even without the FDE is deliberately built, embedding operations and improvement loops into the organization.

ゴール：

Operations are running and results are being delivered

"View problems as lines, not points." When one business process improves, the next problem becomes visible. That is why the journey does not end after one pass; it keeps turning inside your company.

2 Systemizing FDE talent: "CEPO©"

An on-site FDE is not just someone with development skills. They possess four axes, "CEPO," covering issue analysis, project management, and how they engage with people as a human being. That's why solutions don't lean solely toward "development," leading to genuine business improvement.

C

Consulting

Objectively analyzes issues and organizes what should be solved. Structures the real problems.

E

Engineering

Full-stack development capabilities leveraging AI. Builds across domain boundaries.

P

Project Management

Manages plans, budgets, progress, and quality. Personally serves as PM to drive projects.

O

Open & Friendly

A way of engaging with people that creates an atmosphere where others feel comfortable being candid.

Each item is rated on a 5-point scale internally, and members averaging 4 or above are defined as "Senior FDEs." This prevents capabilities from depending on individuals, ensuring a consistent standard as a company.

3 Strong Backing from AndDot Headquarters: "AndDot Knowledge"

On-site FDEs are never working alone. AndDot headquarters supports them from behind through "AndDot Knowledge," constantly bringing in knowledge accumulated within the company.

The on-site FDE brings AndDot Knowledge accumulated by AndDot (sample code, architectures, design patterns, and expert security and industry insights) into your projects and uses it freely. Rather than relying on individual ability, the company's full strength raises the quality and reproducibility of results.

  • Sample code and proven implementations accelerate ramp-up
  • Reusing design patterns and architectures ensures stable quality
  • Expert security and industry insights ensure sound decision-making
  • By not writing from scratch, more time can be spent on essential improvements
