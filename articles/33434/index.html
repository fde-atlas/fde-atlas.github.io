Accenture, Workday Launch Forward Deployed Engineering Programme for AI Agents

Analytics India Magazine · 2026-10-14 · 1 min read

Summary

Accenture and Workday have launched a joint forward deployed engineering programme to deploy AI agents in HR and finance. Accenture engineers will work inside clients' existing processes, data, policies and controls to bring Workday's agentic capabilities into production faster, aiming to accelerate returns on investment. They will also collaborate with Workday's product teams to build new agentic capabilities based on client deployments. The move mirrors HCLTech's partnership with Anthropic and fits a wider trend: TCS targets 1%–1.5% of its workforce (up to 8,900 FDEs) and Infosys around 6,000. Financial terms, customer names and deployment timelines were not disclosed.

Key takeaways

  • Accenture and Workday have introduced a forward deployed engineering programme for deploying AI agents in HR and finance.
  • The initiative aims to integrate AI capabilities into existing client workflows, enhancing implementation speed and investment returns.
  • Accenture's engineers will collaborate with Workday's teams to develop new AI capabilities based on real-world client experiences.
  • The programme reflects a broader trend of companies employing forward deployed engineers to support enterprise AI integration.

Accenture and Workday have launched a joint forward deployed engineering (FDE) programme to help businesses deploy AI agents in human resources (HR) and finance.

The teams will work within clients’ existing processes, data, policies and controls to bring Workday’s agentic capabilities into production. The companies said the programme was intended to speed up implementation and help clients realise returns on their investments sooner.

The arrangement resembles HCLTech’s collaboration with Anthropic, which also uses FDEs to support enterprise AI deployments.

The focus on FDEs extends beyond individual partnerships. TCS has targeted 1% to 1.5% of its workforce, or up to 8,900 employees, for these roles, while Infosys is targeting about 6,000 FDEs. Accenture has also been recruiting for the roles.

The programme will draw on Accenture’s work deploying and extending Workday’s agentic capabilities at enterprise scale. The announcement did not disclose financial terms, name participating customers or specify when the first deployments would begin.

Accenture engineers will also work with Workday’s product and technology teams to develop new agentic capabilities, drawing on experience from client deployments.

“When AI is helping run payroll and close the books, almost right isn't good enough,” Workday Co-Founder, CEO and Chair Aneel Bhusri said.
