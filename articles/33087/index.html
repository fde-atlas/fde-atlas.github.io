AWS launches forward deployed credentials for partners

Kernavique · 2026-10-08 · 2 min read

Summary

Dutch IT Channel reported on October 8, 2026 that AWS has launched forward deployed credentials for its partners at two levels, Validated and Advanced. Three learning paths, named Ground, Orchestrate and Prove, are offered free through AWS Skill Builder. Assessment is practical, conducted in AWS-provisioned environments rather than by multiple choice. Applicants must hold an active AWS certification, though the report does not specify which one. Kernavique links the move to AWS's partner-led approach to forward deployed engineering and its $1 billion investment, noting it may shape partner job requirements and give buyers a verifiable signal when hiring partner teams.

Key points

  • AWS launched two credential levels: Validated and Advanced.
  • Three learning paths are free via AWS Skill Builder.
  • Assessment is hands-on in AWS-provisioned environments.
  • Applicants need an active AWS certification first.
  • The credentials tie into AWS's $1 billion partner investment.

On October 8, 2026, Dutch IT Channel reported that AWS launched forward deployed credentials for its partners, at two levels. The Dutch-language report says three learning paths, named Ground, Orchestrate and Prove, are delivered free through AWS Skill Builder. Assessment is practical, in environments set up by AWS, and is not multiple-choice. This item rests on that report alone.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: Dutch IT Channel (reported).

What happened

Dutch IT Channel names two credential levels, Validated and Advanced, each carrying the forward deployed label. It says people who apply must hold an active AWS certification. The report does not name which certification.

The report ties the credentials to AWS’s partner-led approach to forward deployed engineering and to its $1 billion investment. The report is in Dutch and returned no direct quotes, so none is given here.

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

If you work at an AWS partner, this suggests a named credential, tested by practical assessment, may start to appear in role requirements. The report says an active AWS certification is needed first and does not say which.

If you are hiring them

The report suggests AWS partners can now show people certified by AWS at two levels, instead of describing them as forward deployed on their own word. That gives buyers of partner teams one more thing to ask for.
