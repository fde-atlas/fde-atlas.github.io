Why this hire is hard

Forward Deployed Engineers are scarce, and the title is unreliable.

The title is new and used loosely. A large share of roles advertised as Forward Deployed are really pre-sales or support, so most CVs carrying the title do not have the production engineering you need. The engineers who do are rarely looking, and the best ones are passed between people who have worked with them.

What good looks like

  • Shipped code that real users depend on, not demos
  • Has sat in a room with a customer and turned chaos into requirements
  • Can explain a trade-off to an executive and to a staff engineer in the same day
  • Writes evals before claiming something works

The role, in one paragraph

Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) sit with a customer and ship production software on that customer's own data and systems. Palantir made the role famous; AI labs, AI-native startups and enterprise software companies now run FDE teams because a pilot that never reaches production earns nothing.

Hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer: questions

What does a Forward Deployed Engineer do?

An FDE builds and ships software inside a customer's environment, using the customer's data and constraints. The job mixes production engineering, customer discovery and enough domain knowledge to model the business. Success is measured by what goes live, not by slides.

Is an FDE a sales role?

No. FDEs rarely carry a quota. They work alongside account teams, but they are hired and promoted as engineers, and most interview loops are coding and system-design heavy.

What background do FDEs come from?

Mostly software, data and solutions engineers who enjoy customer contact. Ex-founders and engineers from consulting firms are common too. What matters is evidence of shipping in someone else's environment.

How do you tell a real FDE from a re-titled solutions engineer?

We ask for evidence of production code in a customer environment and run a deployment case: an ambiguous brief, messy data and a deadline. People who have only demoed show it within minutes.

How does your fee work?

We are paid on success. The fee is a share of the hire's first-year base salary, agreed in writing before we present anyone, and invoiced only when your candidate signs. There is no retainer on contingent searches.

Do you run retained or exclusive searches?

Yes, for leadership roles and multi-hire Forward Deployed team builds. Those run in stages with a dedicated researcher, and the stage payments are credited against the final fee.

Who screens the candidates?

An engineer who has built and deployed AI systems. Every profile we send comes with our scorecard notes: production engineering, LLM systems, evals, integration, customer judgment and communication.
