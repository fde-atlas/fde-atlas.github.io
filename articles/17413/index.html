Companies Hiring AI Sales Roles

Zarif Automates · 2026-09-26 · 7 min read

Summary

This article compiles 23 open AI sales postings (SDR, BDR, AE, sales engineer, account manager, CSM), each verified live on employer job boards on September 26, 2026. Its main point is that pay labels vary widely: Clay's $90K–$110K is a fully guaranteed base, while Writer's identical range is on-target earnings including commission. Runway and Cohere postings even contradict themselves between header and body, so candidates should confirm figures with recruiters. The author also notes that entry-level openings are almost exclusively SDR/BDR roles, that industry domain knowledge matters for matching candidates to employers, and that most roles are on-site or hybrid in San Francisco or New York.

Key points

  • 23 verified AI sales postings across six role types, checked September 26, 2026.
  • Same $90K–$110K figure means guaranteed base at Clay but OTE at Writer.
  • Runway and Cohere postings contain internally conflicting pay figures.
  • Only three SDR/BDR roles explicitly welcome candidates without sales experience.
  • Most roles are San Francisco or New York based, on-site or hybrid.

Clay's SDR posting and Writer's both print $90K to $110K. At Clay, that's a guaranteed base salary. At Writer, it's on-target earnings (OTE): base plus the commission you'd make if you hit your number. Same figure, different job.

That's why this list quotes pay exactly as printed. It covers 23 open sales postings at AI companies, grouped by role. Every row was opened live on the employer's own job board on September 26, 2026, and was accepting applications that day.

This is a hand-picked sample, not a full list, and not an endorsement. Postings close and ranges change, so open the link before you apply. For what the pay terms mean, read how OTE and sales pay plans work, then run a range through the AI Sales Pay Calculator.

How to read this list

Check the label before the number. Anthropic's "Annual Salary" is OTE for sales roles. OpenAI's "Estimated Base Salary" is base, with commission on top. Baseten, Sierra and Modal print a range without saying which. Runway and Cohere contradict themselves between header and body. Ask the recruiter which figure is correct before comparing offers.

Entry-level doors are few, and they are SDR doors. Of the 23 rows, only Rogo's SDR (0 to 2 years), Legora's BDR (internships welcome) and Clay's ClayDR (open to exceptional entry-level candidates) explicitly invite people without sales experience. Everything else asks for prior quota or customer-facing work. Breaking into AI sales covers how people get from outside into one of those seats.

Domain knowledge counts. Harvey's CSM welcomes people from legal firms, Rogo sells to banks and private equity, and Cohere's SLED role wants public-sector sales history. If you come from an industry, look for the AI company that sells into it.

Location is mostly in-office. Most rows are on-site or hybrid in San Francisco or New York. Runway, Cohere, Sierra and Modal list remote options.
