Forward deployed AI companies in 2026: who embeds engineers

Ortelian · 2026-10-11 · 6 min read

Summary

Ortelian maps the companies offering forward deployed AI engineers as of October 2026, grouping them by what their engineers actually deploy: model labs (OpenAI Deployment Company, Ode with Anthropic, Anthropic Applied AI), cloud and software vendors (AWS, Microsoft Frontier Company, Salesforce), platform companies with deployed teams (Palantir, Distyl AI, Ortelian), consultancies (Accenture, Deloitte), and acquired applied AI boutiques. It notes 2026's wave of launches — OpenAI's $4bn+ deployment company buying Tomoro, Microsoft's $2.5bn Frontier Company with 6,000 experts, AWS's $1bn organization — and lists buyer questions: whose stack, who operates after launch, hosting location, definition of done, and team size. Ortelian discloses it is itself on the list.

Key points

  • Forward deployed engineering spread industry-wide in 2026, with OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS and Microsoft all launching units.
  • OpenAI Deployment Company launched with over $4bn and acquired Tomoro, adding about 150 engineers.
  • Microsoft Frontier Company launched with $2.5bn and 6,000 embedded experts; AWS committed $1bn with five-to-six-person pods.
  • Palantir claims to have pioneered the role; Distyl AI and Ortelian pair platforms with deployed teams.
  • Buyers should ask whose models are used, who runs systems after launch, and where they are hosted.

Which companies embed AI engineers on site in 2026: model labs, cloud and software vendors, platform companies and consultancies. Sourced, checked 11 Oct 2026.

The companies that offer forward deployed AI engineers fall into four groups: model labs (the OpenAI Deployment Company, Ode with Anthropic, Anthropic Applied AI), cloud and software vendors (AWS, Microsoft Frontier Company, Salesforce), platform companies with a forward deployed team (Palantir, Distyl AI, Ortelian) and consultancies such as Accenture and Deloitte.

Forward deployed engineering went from one company’s way of working to the way much of the AI industry sells deployment. In 2026 alone, OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS and Microsoft each launched a forward deployed business, and large consultancies built practices around the term. This map groups the companies that offer forward deployed AI engineers by what their engineers actually deploy, because that decides what you are left with when they leave.

Every claim about another company links to its own announcement or to press coverage. Last checked on 11 October 2026. Ortelian wrote this page and is one of the companies on it.

Model labs and their deployment companies

OpenAI Deployment Company

OpenAI launched the OpenAI Deployment Company on 11 May 2026. It is majority-owned and controlled by OpenAI, launched with more than $4 billion of initial investment, and agreed to acquire the applied AI firm Tomoro, which brings about 150 forward deployed engineers and deployment specialists. OpenAI describes an engagement as a focused diagnostic, then a small number of priority workflows, built inside the organisation and connected to the customer’s data, tools and controls.

Ode with Anthropic

Anthropic, Blackstone and Hellman & Friedman introduced Ode with Anthropic on 15 July 2026 as a standalone AI services company that pairs Anthropic’s models with a team of AI engineers. TechCrunch reports that Ode is built on Fractional AI, an applied AI boutique the venture acquired, that it works “Claude-first” but can use other providers, and that Anthropic’s own applied AI team keeps doing strategic deployments.

Anthropic Applied AI

Anthropic also hires its own forward deployed engineers in its Applied AI team. Its job posting describes engineers who embed with strategic customers to ship applications on Claude models.

Cloud and software vendors

AWS Forward Deployed Engineering

AWS announced a Forward Deployed Engineering organization backed by $1 billion on 30 June 2026. AWS says it will embed thousands of engineers with customers and design each deployment so the customer is self-sufficient when it ends, with the systems running in the customer’s own AWS account. CNBC reports that engineers go in as pods of roughly five or six.

Microsoft Frontier Company

Microsoft launched Microsoft Frontier Company on 2 July 2026 with a $2.5 billion investment and 6,000 industry and engineering experts embedded at customers. Microsoft says customers can choose models from several providers on its platform, and names forward deployed partnerships with Accenture, Capgemini, EY, KPMG and PwC.

Salesforce

Salesforce is hiring forward deployed engineers for Agentforce who build agentic solutions inside customer environments, working in pods alongside a deployment strategist.

Platform plus forward deployed team

Palantir

Palantir’s job description says it pioneered the position, and its forward deployed engineers build custom applications and LLM workflows on Palantir’s software for each customer.

For a mid-market comparison, see Ortelian vs Palantir. For how to evaluate any forward deployed partner, see how to choose a forward deployed engineering partner.

Distyl AI

Distyl AI pairs a platform for enterprise AI delivery with a forward deployed engineer model that puts engineers on site to own the outcome with the customer. It announced a partnership with Google Cloud on 22 April 2026.

Ortelian

Ortelian is a platform plus a forward deployed team for complex companies in Europe and the US. The core idea is to get the most impact from AI in the business, so the team works on site to redesign the work before agents take it over. We work on site not because our product needs it, but because your company does. After launch, the client’s team runs it or we keep running it. Either way, Ortelian hosts and operates the platform, in the EU, and inside an engagement the client’s own agents can use it through scoped tools. Ortelian works across model providers. The company is Ortelian, Inc. More on how we work and on what an AI deployment partner does.

Consultancies and systems integrators

Accenture launched a forward deployed engineering practice with Microsoft on 18 March 2026, bringing together thousands of engineers, with Microsoft providing the platform and Accenture leading process redesign and change management. TechCrunch notes that Deloitte has built its own forward deployed team as well. If you are weighing a consultancy against a smaller team, see Ortelian vs AI consultancies.

Applied AI boutiques are being bought

Two applied AI boutiques now sit inside lab ventures: OpenAI agreed to acquire Tomoro for the OpenAI Deployment Company, and Fractional AI became the base of Ode. For buyers who want a small, senior team, that makes it more important to ask whose models the team will build on.

How to choose

Question to ask Why it matters
Whose models and platform will they build on? Lab and vendor teams build on their own stack. That is fine if you have chosen it already.
What runs after they leave? Some aim to leave you self-sufficient, some keep operating it. Know which before you start.
Who hosts it, and where? Hosting region and who operates the platform matter for data protection rules such as the GDPR.
How is “done” defined? A standard agreed on real examples before the build makes the result measurable.
How big is the team you’ll get? Large firms send pods into large programmes. Mid-size companies often need a few senior people for longer.

For more on what to agree before you start, see what is forward deployed engineering and how to deploy AI agents in an enterprise.

Questions people ask

Which companies offer forward deployed AI engineers?

As of October 2026: model labs (the OpenAI Deployment Company, Ode with Anthropic and Anthropic’s Applied AI team), cloud and software vendors (AWS, Microsoft Frontier Company, Salesforce), platform companies with forward deployed teams (Palantir, Distyl AI, Ortelian) and large consultancies such as Accenture.

Who invented forward deployed engineering?

Palantir says it pioneered the role, embedding engineers with customers to build on its software. The term spread across AI companies, most visibly in 2026.

Is there a forward deployed option hosted in the EU?

Ortelian’s platform is hosted in the EU and its team works on site with companies in Europe and the US. For the others, ask each provider where the systems they build will run. For what to ask any provider, see what to check in an EU-hosted AI agent platform.

Is a forward deployed team the same as a consultancy?

Not usually. A consultancy recommends and a forward deployed team builds and stays. The line blurs when consultancies run forward deployed practices. See forward deployed engineer vs consultant.

Which partners embed engineers on site to deploy AI?

Model lab ventures such as the OpenAI Deployment Company and Ode with Anthropic, cloud vendors such as AWS and Microsoft, platform companies such as Palantir, Distyl AI and Ortelian, and consultancies such as Accenture. They differ in whose models and platform they build on and in who runs the system after launch.
