Company Profile: Cognition

Original title: 公司档案：Cognition——Devin 与 Windsurf 背后的 FDE 编制

Summary

This company profile from FDE China examines Cognition, the maker of Devin and Windsurf, and its hiring of Forward Deployed Engineers and Applied AI Engineers. The interview process spans six rounds, with salary figures drawn from third-party job sites. The editor highlights that pairing FDE and Applied AI Engineer roles shows delivery positions at agent product companies combine engineering implementation with customer deployment—heavier than traditional pre-sales and closer to the business than pure R&D. Engineers familiar with enterprise development practices (CI/CD, coding standards, legacy systems) have an edge, while AI coding companies building FDE teams signals that agent deployment competition is shifting from model capability to organizational fit.

Full text

Cognition (the company behind Devin and Windsurf) is hiring for FDE and Applied AI Engineer roles, with a 6-round interview process and compensation tiers documented by third parties (per third-party job sites). Cognition's products directly impact enterprise customers' software development workflows...

Editor's Note FDEChina Editorial Team

Notably, the combination of job titles—FDE alongside Applied AI Engineer—shows that delivery roles at agent product companies handle both engineering implementation and customer deployment, carrying heavier responsibilities than traditional pre-sales and being closer to the business than pure R&D.

For job seekers: engineers familiar with enterprise development processes (CI/CD, code standards, legacy systems) have a unique advantage at these companies; merely showcasing AI tool usage skills is not enough. For ecosystem observers: AI coding companies building FDE organizations is a signal of "tool products moving toward process delivery"—the competition for agent deployment is shifting from model capability to organizational adaptability.
