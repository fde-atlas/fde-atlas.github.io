Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering | HackerRank

Zero G Talent · 2026-03-24 · 2 min read

Summary

HackerRank is hiring a Manager of Forward Deployed Engineering (Global), based in Santa Clara, CA, to lead its expanding FDE function. The hands-on role involves directly managing US-based FDEs while partnering with regional GMs in EMEA and APAC. Responsibilities include leading pre- and post-sales customer engagements, feeding customer-specific solutions into the product roadmap, improving demos and proofs of concept, and building repeatable delivery playbooks. Candidates need 8+ years in engineering or solutions engineering, 2+ years managing technical teams, and strong communication across technical and business audiences. FDE, developer tools, or API-first SaaS experience is a plus.

What you need

HackerRank is scaling its Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) function globally. We are hiring a Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering (Global) to set the strategy, standards, and execution bar for this team. This is a hands-on leadership role. You will lead the global FDE function, directly manage FDEs in the U.S., and partner closely with regional GMs in EMEA and APAC.

What you’ll do

  • Lead, coach, and grow a team of FDEs that lead customer engagements (pre- and post-sales) to define and deliver solutions.
  • Partner with Sales, Product, Engineering, and Customer Success to shape solutions that solve real customer problems.
  • Work with Product and Engineering to incorporate customer-specific solutions into the product roadmap.
  • Provide hands-on technical leadership across APIs, integrations, and platform capabilities.
  • Elevate how we run demos, proofs of concept, and onsite engagements.
  • Build repeatable playbooks, delivery standards, and best practices to move fast at scale.

Who you are

  • A seasoned technical leader with 8+ years in engineering, solutions engineering, or technical delivery, and 2+ years leading high-performing technical teams.
  • Obsessed with operating alongside the team, reviewing designs, challenging assumptions, and stepping in when problems get ambiguous or high-stakes.
  • Highly experienced in leading customer-facing technical teams where success depends on credibility, clarity, and execution, not scripts or handoffs.
  • Fluent across engineering and business contexts, you can debate architecture with engineers and align outcomes with sales, product, and customer leaders.
  • Excellent presenter and communicator who can modulate the message across both technical and non-technical audiences.

Even better if you have

  • Experience as a Forward Deployed Engineer, Solutions Engineer, or Technical Consultant.
  • Background in developer tools, HR tech, or API-first SaaS platforms.
  • Experience working in globally distributed or matrixed teams.

You will thrive in this role if

  • You enjoy building teams, systems, and standards from the ground up.
  • Ambiguity energizes you, especially in fast-moving environments.
  • You hold high standards for ownership and quality, with a bias toward action and urgency.
