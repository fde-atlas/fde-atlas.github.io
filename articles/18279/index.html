FDE-Based AI Implementation Support Services | Taking AI All the Way to Real Results in the Field.

Original title: FDE型AI導入支援サービス｜AIを、現場で成果が出るところまで。

株式会社ナレッジコミュニケーション · 5 min read

Summary

Knowledge Communication, a Japanese company, describes its FDE-style AI implementation support service. Rather than picking a product or model first, it examines business flows, decision rules, available data, and existing systems, then implements AI where impact is expected and accompanies clients through production operation. Services include business assessment and KPI setting, data and RAG infrastructure, AI agent development with system integration, and post-launch improvement, including productionizing existing PoCs. Supported platforms include Microsoft Foundry, Amazon Bedrock, Databricks, and the company's own platform, usable in combination. Full delivery from planning to production takes roughly two to four months, with example use cases in sales, support, approvals, finance, IT helpdesk, and reporting.

Key points

  • FDE-style support means solving problems close to the field, not requiring on-site residency.
  • Support spans planning, data infrastructure, development, productionization, and operations, and can start partially.
  • Works with Microsoft Foundry, Amazon Bedrock, Databricks, and its own platform, including combined setups.
  • End-to-end delivery from planning to production takes about two to four months.
  • Example use cases cover sales, customer support, approvals, finance, IT helpdesk, and reporting.

AI implementation support services

We support everything from organizing business processes, selecting an AI platform, and preparing data, to AI agent development, production deployment, and operational improvement. We review your existing cloud and data environments and implement solutions ready for real-world use.

What is FDE-style support?

Applications

developed alongside your team.

Rather than deciding on products or models first, we review business flows, decision rules, available data, and existing systems. We then implement starting with tasks where impact is expected, and proceed together through production operation. Our FDE-style support means taking ownership of problem-solving from a position close to the front lines, regardless of whether we are stationed on-site.

We conduct interviews with your teams and organize business processes to determine priority tasks and KPIs.

We design and implement everything including data, authentication, existing system integration, and security.

We review usage and business outcomes, and improve accuracy, data, and business flows.

SERVICE

In addition to consulting from the planning stage, we combine support tailored to your current situation, such as building data and knowledge platforms, productionizing existing PoCs, and post-implementation operational improvement.

We organize internal documents, data, and business know-how, and make them safely accessible and usable by AI.

  • Inventory of documents, data, and APIs
  • Building RAG and search platforms
  • Designing access permissions

  • AI agent development
  • Verification of accuracy, usability, and security

Examples of supported tasks

For example, the following tasks can be targeted. Beyond internal information search, we integrate AI into content verification, document creation, and registration into various systems, in line with your current workflows.

SALES

Proposal and quote creation

We review deal information, past proposals, price lists, and contract terms to create proposal drafts and quote conditions. After review by the person in charge, this connects through to CRM registration.

What the AI does

  • Retrieves deal information and past documents
  • Creates proposal and quote drafts
  • After review, registers deal information in the CRM

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Inquiry handling

We organize inquiries from email and forms, and create response drafts by referencing FAQs, contract details, and customer information. When necessary, we hand off to the person in charge and keep a record of responses.

  • Classifies inquiry content
  • Creates response drafts by referencing necessary information
  • Records history and hands off to the person in charge

WORKFLOW

Internal applications and approvals

We check expense, purchasing, and various application contents against internal policies, and return missing items or points requiring confirmation to the applicant. Applications meeting the conditions are routed to approvers, and results are registered in the system.

  • Checks application content against internal policies
  • Identifies errors and missing information
  • Sends approval requests and registers results in the system

FINANCE

Invoice and payment processing

We read invoices and match them against purchase orders, contracts, and acceptance data. If there are discrepancies in amounts or terms, we confirm with the person in charge; if there are no issues, we create registration data for accounting and payment systems.

  • Reads invoice contents
  • Matches against purchase order, contract, and acceptance data
  • Verifies discrepancies and creates registration data

IT SUPPORT

Internal IT help desk

We handle inquiries about PCs, accounts, SaaS, and more, answering based on manuals and past response histories. For unresolved issues, we perform initial triage, create tickets, and hand off to the person in charge.

  • Reviews and classifies inquiry content
  • Answers by referencing manuals
  • Creates tickets and hands off to the person in charge

DATA & REPORTING

Data aggregation and regular reports

We retrieve multiple data sources such as sales, deals, and usage, and create regular reports. We extract differences from the previous month and significant changes, and summarize points requiring review by the person in charge.

  • Retrieves data from multiple systems
  • Aggregates, compares, and creates reports
  • Presents changes and items to review to the person in charge
