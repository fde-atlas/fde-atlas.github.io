Amid the Tech Industry Layoffs, This Type of Engineer Is Booming, with Job Postings Surging 729% in a Year

Not all jobs in the tech industry are disappearing — what companies really lack are people who can put AI tools into practice. According to Indeed data, Forward Deployed Engineer job postings surged from 643 in April 2025 to 5,330 in April 2026, an increase of roughly 729% year over year. Anthropic, OpenAI, Palantir, and Stripe are all hiring for this kind of talent, because after companies buy AI products, they still need someone to step into their workflows and connect the tools to actual business operations.

The Forward Deployed Engineer may become one of the hottest new roles in the tech industry. | Photo Credit: Andriy Onufriyenko / Getty Images

Even though the tech industry has been hit hard by layoffs, one role actually seems to be growing rather than shrinking: the Forward Deployed Engineer.

Box CEO Aaron Levie said on LinkedIn this week: "Forward Deployed Engineers, or roles doing similar work, are about to become one of the most in-demand jobs in tech, and one of the most important functions in AI adoption."

Data provided to Business Insider by Indeed shows that in April 2025, there were 643 Forward Deployed Engineer-related job postings on Indeed; by April 2026, that number had grown to 5,330, an increase of roughly 729% year over year.

Forward Deployed Engineer job postings up 729% in one year

As demand for enterprise AI tools grows, tech companies such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Palantir, and Stripe are all recruiting Forward Deployed Engineer talent.

Earlier this week, Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian also said on LinkedIn that the company is expanding hiring for this role, driven by rising demand from customers and partners for Google's AI products.

So what exactly does this kind of tech talent do?

Forward Deployed Engineers, often called FDEs, work with companies to integrate AI into their workflows and customize AI tools to meet business needs, making work simpler or more efficient.

The role was popularized by Palantir, which sends engineers directly to customer sites to build software tailored to their needs.

Salaries can reach $200,000 — companies want people who can work on-site

According to Indeed data, salaries for this role range from about $170,000 to over $200,000.

The consulting industry is also starting to rely on Forward Deployed Engineers, as consulting firms are becoming the latest adoption channel for Silicon Valley's AI innovations, helping companies understand AI and integrate it into actual work.

For consulting firms like McKinsey and Boston Consulting Group, the ideal consultant today must also have technical skills.

A "Senior Forward Deployed Engineer" job posting at QuantumBlack, McKinsey's AI division, states that candidates should have more than 8 years of hands-on experience in software, platform, or infrastructure engineering, as well as a bachelor's or master's degree in a technical field such as computer science, machine learning, applied statistics, mathematics, engineering, or artificial intelligence.

Alex Singla, a McKinsey senior partner and co-leader of QuantumBlack, previously told Business Insider: "What we want to do is find people who have the potential to be great McKinsey consultants or great technologists, and then develop them into both."
