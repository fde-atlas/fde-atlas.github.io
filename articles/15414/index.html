Forward Deployed Engineer in 2026: Who Is Hiring, Pay and Skills

Prepare.sh · 2026-11-04 · 5 min read

Summary

Prepare.sh counted 297 open postings with "Forward Deployed" or "FDE" in the title across 19 job boards on November 4, 2026, led by Databricks (111) and Palantir (72). The article compiles who is hiring, posted US base salaries (roughly $135k–$376k), required skills, and experience levels, and argues the title actually covers three distinct jobs—builder, sales-engineer style, and internal tools—so candidates should read postings carefully rather than trust the label. It also cautions that postings overstate openings and that 2025 growth figures come from small bases.

Key points

  • 297 FDE postings across 19 boards; Databricks and Palantir dominate.
  • Palantir's roles skew government; OpenAI spun out a unit with ~150 FDEs and Deployment Strategists.
  • Posted US base pay ranges from $135k to $376k, with Anthropic's Staff FDE at $280k–$320k.
  • Bloomberry data: 60% of roles are builder-type with 30–50% travel; 0% pure coding-free.
  • Top skills: Python (66%), TypeScript and AI agents (35% each); most roles want 3–5 years' experience.

On November 4, 2026, we counted 297 open postings with "Forward Deployed" or "FDE" in the title across 19 company job boards. Databricks had 111 and Palantir 72, well ahead of anyone else. About 241 are individual contributor engineering roles. The rest are managers, product managers and strategists.

That's a sample and not the whole market. We only counted public board feeds we could read, so companies on other systems, including Google and Salesforce, aren't in the total.

The engineer and manager buckets are our reading of each title. How to read the numbers:

Postings aren't openings. Databricks repeats roles per location: 62 "Sr. Forward Deployed" postings map to only 15 requisition IDs. Other boards also list some titles more than once.

Palantir's list is mostly government. Of its 65 titles beginning "Forward Deployed", 35 say "Government" and 7 more require a security clearance in the posting text. Our Palantir FDE guide covers the role, and the Palantir question hub has the company's practice questions.

Google and Salesforce are hiring too, but we couldn't count them cleanly. Google's careers pages list levels from FDE III to Partner FDE V, and variants in Google Public Sector, Google Labs and Kaggle. Salesforce has said it committed to building a team of 1,000 FDEs, and in March 2026 said it had tripled the team in six months. Their site searches are fuzzy, so their result counts aren't FDE headcounts.

Boards with no matching title. Sierra, Decagon, Perplexity, LangChain and Linear had none on the day we looked, though some may use other titles.

OpenAI also set up a separate company for this work. Its announcement from May 2026 mentions "approximately 150 experienced Forward Deployed Engineers and Deployment Specialists".

Demand over time

The clearest numbers are from 2025, and the sources differ. Bloomberry's analysis of 1,000 postings, using Revealera data, found titles up 1,165% year over year for January to October 2025. Growth from a small base looks dramatic, and this is one vendor's dataset. For India, a report on Indeed data from October 2026 says postings surged "1,807 per cent year-on-year in June", then "remained nearly 16 times higher in July and over six times higher in August", so the pace there was already slowing, though from a much higher level.

We don't have a clean US figure for 2026. Reports circulate citing Indeed, but we couldn't read a primary source, so we're leaving them out.

Bloomberry found a median of $173,816 across its 2025 sample, with 70% of postings mentioning equity. Its sample skews to small companies, so it's lower than most of the table above. Levels.fyi blocks our server, so we don't have verified total compensation.

The title covers three different jobs

This is the most useful finding in Bloomberry's data, and it's why reading the posting matters more than the title:

Type Share of postings Coding Travel Typical pay
Builder: embeds with customers and ships production systems 60% 70 to 90% 30 to 50% $140,000 to $250,000, high equity
Sales engineer style: demos, proofs of concept, hands off to others 30% 30 to 40% Under 20% $120,000 to $200,000 plus commission
Internal tools: automation for the company's own sales teams 10% Internal-facing Under 5% $100,000 to $180,000

Bloomberry says "exactly 0%" are quota-carrying roles while also labelling the second type quota-carrying, so treat that detail with care. Quick checks when reading a posting: who are the customers, internal or external? Does it report into engineering or sales? Does it mention commission? Salesforce's postings, for example, are tagged "Sales" as a function even though its careers site lists FDEs under tech and product.

Experience. 60% ask for 3 to 5 years, 20% for 6 to 8, 8% for 9 or more, and 12% for 0 to 2. Current postings at large AI companies sit higher: OpenAI's run 6+ to 7+ years, and Google's start at 5. There are entry routes, mainly at Palantir, whose new grad FDSE roles include commercial and government tracks.

Where people come from. From 100 LinkedIn profiles Bloomberry checked, 45% were software engineers, 22% solutions engineers or architects, 15% in data roles, 10% technical consultants and 8% founders or early startup staff.

Company size. Bloomberry found 58% of roles at companies with 11 to 200 employees in 2025. Our count looks different, with Databricks and Palantir far ahead, but ours is a hand-picked list of boards, so the two aren't comparable.

What this means if you're looking

If you're an engineer with a few years' experience and you want this path, the market is real. Just read each posting for travel, and for how much of the job is engineering. Travel ranges from not stated up to "Travel up to 50%" at OpenAI and "25-75%" at some Palantir roles.

If you're preparing for interviews, our Palantir interview guide and Databricks interview guide cover two of the biggest hirers, and the AI engineer questions cover the skills most of these roles now ask about.

We can only say how this looks today. A title that grew this fast could consolidate, or companies may rename it, so it's worth keeping an eye on the underlying skills and not on the label.
