FDE Salary Benchmarks: What Forward Deployed

FDE Pulse · 2026-04-30 · 3 min read

Summary

FDE Pulse analyzes base salaries from 206 of 1,205 active Forward Deployed Engineer postings (17% disclosed ranges) as of April 30, 2026. San Francisco leads with a $212K median, followed by New York at $190K. On-site roles pay a median of $186K versus $175K for fully remote, a 6% gap. The ten highest-paying roles are all senior or director-level, topping out at NVIDIA's $320K–$489K range. Only 9% of postings mention equity and 3 mention OTE. With 83% of postings not disclosing pay, the article suggests using the $181K market median as an anchor in negotiations. Figures exclude equity, bonuses, and benefits.

Key points

  • Only 17% of 1,205 tracked FDE postings disclose salary ranges
  • San Francisco median is $212K; New York follows at $190K
  • On-site roles pay ~6% more at the median than remote roles
  • Top-paying roles are all senior or director-level, maxing at $489K (NVIDIA)
  • Use the $181K market median as an anchor when employers don't disclose pay

FDE Pulse tracks compensation data from active Forward Deployed Engineer job postings. As of April 30, 2026, 206 of 1205 tracked postings (17%) disclose salary ranges. The data below covers base compensation only. Equity, sign-on bonuses, OTE, and benefits are excluded from all calculations.

Remote vs. On-Site Pay

Based on 1205 active postings, on-site FDE roles pay a median of $186K versus $175K for fully remote roles. That is a 6% gap at the median. Remote roles carry lower base salaries but eliminate cost-of-living expenses in major markets, narrowing the effective pay gap for engineers outside New York and San Francisco.

Understanding FDE Compensation Structure

Base salary is one component of FDE total compensation. Based on 1205 active postings, 114 (9%) include equity. At pre-IPO companies, equity grants can represent 20 to 50 percent of annual total compensation if the company eventually goes public or is acquired. At public companies, RSUs vest over four years and carry a predictable cash value.

3 postings mention OTE, indicating pre-sales FDE roles that include a variable commission component tied to deal closure. 3 postings offer uncapped commission, the highest ceiling for variable FDE pay.

The 83% non-disclosure rate (999 postings) introduces uncertainty into market comparisons. Companies that do not disclose salary ranges are not necessarily lower-paying, but candidates negotiating without publicly available data are at a disadvantage. If a company does not disclose salary, use the $181K market median as your anchor point for initial discussions.

Methodology

Salary figures drawn from 206 of 1205 active FDE postings that disclosed compensation ranges as of April 30, 2026. All values represent annual base compensation. Equity, sign-on bonuses, commissions, OTE, and benefits are excluded. Metro grouping uses the primary city in the posted location. Remote postings are classified separately regardless of listed location. Median is the statistical median of all disclosed values in each category.
