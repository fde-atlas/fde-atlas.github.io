Why this role exists

The model came from Palantir, and frontier AI labs adopted it for the same reason.

Palantir embedded roughly 120 engineers directly with JPMorgan in 2009, because the data was too sensitive and the workflow too specific for a product handed off remotely (source). The pattern stuck: a model or a product alone does not produce a working result inside someone else's stack. Somebody has to sit with the mess and build the thing that closes the gap.

Frontier AI labs hit the same wall. OpenAI's own forward deployed engineering team grew from 2 to 39 people in a single year, targeting problems worth tens of millions to low billions, because roughly 95% of enterprise AI deployments never make it to production (source). In 2026, OpenAI went further and stood up a dedicated deployment company through the Tomoro acquisition, embedding engineers with adopters including HP, Intuit, Oracle, and State Farm (source).

We are not affiliated with and do not partner with Palantir or OpenAI. They are cited here as the origin and market context for the FDE model, not as our clients.

How the engagement works

A repeating loop: audit, evals, deployment. Each phase has to earn the right to the next.

Audit

Week 1

We learn how the work really happens by sitting with the people who do it, not by reading the documented process. Output: a today-vs-with-AI operating map.

Evals

Week 1-2

We build a golden dataset of real queries with hand-labeled correct answers, and track pass rates and failure categories before anything touches production.

Deployment

Week 2-3

We build on what you already have, sandbox first, increase autonomy gradually, and monitor everything. No forced migrations.

Iterate and defend

Ongoing

We document what v1 got wrong, how it improved, and report the outcome in terms a technical lead and a VP both trust.

When an FDE fits

  • You have AI ambition but no in-house AI-native engineering depth.
  • You need production code shipped in weeks, not after a long hiring cycle.
  • You want one accountable engineer directing a fleet of agents, not a rotating cast.

When it does not fit

  • You want a strategy deck and a roadmap, not shipped code -- that is AI strategy consulting, not an FDE.
  • Your scope is small, fixed, and well-defined with no ambiguity -- a scoped project with an agency is cheaper.
  • You need a large team of specialists across many domains at once -- an FDE is one deep generalist, not a department.
  • You are not ready to give an engineer real access to your systems and data -- embedding does not work at arm's length.

Forward Deployed Engineer Questions

What is a forward deployed engineer (FDE)?+

A forward deployed engineer is an engineer who embeds inside a customer's team and writes production code on top of a product, rather than advising from the outside. The model started at Palantir, where engineers embedded on-site with government and defense customers whose data was too sensitive to hand off remotely.

Can I hire forward deployed engineers as a service?+

Yes. AY Automate places a forward deployed engineer inside your team as a service: we match the engineer, they join your repo and your sprint within 2-4 weeks, and a 90-day replacement guarantee covers the fit. You get an embedded FDE without running the search and hiring process yourself.

How is an FDE different from a consultant?+

A consultant is accountable for a recommendation. An FDE is accountable for the shipped result. FDEs write production code themselves, embed for weeks at a time, and use evaluation-driven development instead of a slide deck to earn trust.

How is an FDE different from staff augmentation?+

Staff augmentation places a person against your hours and org chart. An FDE is placed against an outcome: a workflow that needs to run, an agent that needs to hit a quality bar. Same embedded model, different accountability.

Do I need a forward deployed engineer or an AI strategy consultant?+

If you already know what you want built and need someone to build and ship it inside your stack, that is an FDE. If you need help deciding what to build first, start with AI strategy consulting, then bring in an FDE to execute.

How fast can AY Automate place a forward deployed engineer?+

Our standard placement window is 2-4 weeks from requirements to a working engineer inside your sprint, backed by a 90-day replacement guarantee if the fit is wrong.
