Sierra AI Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Experience (Waiting, Jun 2026)

Top Tech Transition · 2026-07-01 · 1 min read

Summary

A mid-level candidate shares their June 2026 interview experience for a Forward Deployed Engineer role at Sierra AI, posted on Top Tech Transition. The process had three rounds: a recruiter screen covering behavioral and technical questions, a take-home assignment on diagnosing a drop in an AI agent metric, and a final onsite round including a system design question about north star metrics for a healthcare financial AI agent. The candidate found communication smooth and interviewers kind and skilled, and advises preparing deep technical projects and strong communication, since the role is customer-facing.

Key points

  • Three rounds: recruiter screen, take-home assignment, final onsite.
  • Take-home focused on diagnosing a declining AI agent metric.
  • Final round asked about north star metrics for a healthcare financial AI agent.
  • Candidate advises deep technical project preparation and strong communication.
  • Interviewers described as warm, personable, and highly capable.

Interview process

The process was smooth in terms of communication and scheduling. The people I met were incredibly kind and high caliber. They were highly personable and seem they are trying to find others who can continue its culture of excellence.

Interview rounds · 3

  • 1

    Recruiter screen

    BehavioralTechnical
    • Q1. How do you use AI on a regular basis?
    • Q2. Tell me about a technical project
    • Q3. What are you looking for in your next opportunity?
  • 2

    Take-home assignment

    TechnicalSystem Design
    • Q1. Metric X for our AI agent is down. How would you diagnose the issue? What data sources will you look at? What are the possible fixes you’d build for this, and how would you prioritize between them?
  • 3

    Final / onsite round

    BehavioralSystem Design
    • Q1. What are the north star success metrics for an AI agent for a healthcare financial platform?

Tips from the candidate

Prepare projects that have been technical and prep to dive deep into those experiences. This is also a front-facing role so ability to communicate effectively and represent the company well is very important.

Company culture

Very warm people. They really do abide by their values so important to understand what those are.
