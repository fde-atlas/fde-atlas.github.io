Stripe Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Experience (Rejected, Jan 2025)

Top Tech Transition · 2026-08-31 · 1 min read

Summary

A candidate shares a rejected January 2025 application for a Forward Deployed Engineer role at Stripe, posted on Top Tech Transition. The process was smooth with friendly HR, but only one recruiter behavioral screen is described, with no technical rounds mentioned. Questions covered launching Uber in a new country, approaching an unfamiliar Stripe integration, handling difficult clients, and making decisions on a client's behalf, with follow-ups probing the reasoning. The candidate advises preparing Integration Engineer-style situational questions and being ready to explain decision-making factors.

Key points

  • Rejected after applying for Stripe FDE in January 2025.
  • Smooth process, friendly HR, but only one recruiter behavioral screen described.
  • Questions covered new-market launches, novel integrations, and difficult clients.
  • Advice: prepare Integration Engineer-style questions and decision-making rationale.
  • Candidate's impression of company culture: cool, with good engineers.

Interview process

The process was smooth, and the HR team was friendly. During the behavioral interview, the interviewer asked me two main questions and then followed up with additional questions based on my answers.

Interview rounds · 1

  • 1

    Recruiter screen

    Behavioral
    • Q1. You are going to implement Uber in a new country, what will you do?
    • Q2. How should we approach a new integration that the Stripe team hasn’t done before?
    • Q3. Tell me about a time you handled difficult clients.
    • Q4. How would you make decisions on behalf of a client?Follow-up questions
      • What factors will you consider when making those decisions?
    • Q5. How do you manage challenging situations?

Tips from the candidate

He should prepare for Integration Engineer–related questions, such as how to handle difficult clients, how to manage challenging situations, and how to make decisions on behalf of a client. He should also be ready to explain the factors he would consider when making those decisions.

Company culture

Seems cool, good level of engineers, nothing to add more
