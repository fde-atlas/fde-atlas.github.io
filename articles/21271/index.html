FDE Recruitment Support | Forward Deployed Engineer Staffing & Hiring Requirements Design

Original title: FDE採用支援|フォワードデプロイドエンジニアの人材紹介・採用要件設計

FDE Talent · 2025 · 6 min read

Summary

This is a service page for FDE Talent, a Japanese recruiting agency specializing in Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) hiring. It argues FDE hiring is hard because the role only emerged in Japan around 2025, experienced candidates are nearly nonexistent, requirements tend to balloon into unrealistic wish lists, and low awareness of the job title causes mismatches and early attrition. The agency offers requirements design, candidate referrals, interview design (including mock customer-hearing scoping exercises and timed practical tasks), and offer negotiation, charging success fees only upon placement. Candidates are sourced from adjacent roles—SIer engineers, web engineers, hands-on IT consultants, and pre-sales—selected for end-to-end delivery track records rather than FDE titles. Its guide, based on 523 public job postings, reports Japanese FDE salaries of roughly ¥10–20 million.

Key points

  • FDE became a common job title in Japan only after 2025, so few experienced candidates exist.
  • Overly broad requirements and unclear role definitions cause mismatches and early attrition.
  • Services span requirements design, referrals, interview design, and offer negotiation.
  • Candidates come from adjacent roles like SIer, web engineering, consulting, and pre-sales.
  • Success-fee pricing; Japanese FDE salaries run roughly ¥10–20 million per its 523-posting analysis.

There are almost no experienced FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) in the Japanese job market. FDE Talent provides end-to-end support, from designing your hiring requirements to introducing candidates with FDE aptitude and designing your selection process.

Challenges

Three reasons FDE hiring is difficult

  • Very few experienced candidates FDE is a new role in Japan, with job postings increasing only after 2025. Searching for "experienced FDEs" yields almost no candidate pool, so hiring focuses on potential candidates from adjacent roles.
  • Requirements tend to become overly complex Production-quality implementation skills, plus the ability to identify customer problems and build consensus. Making both mandatory turns requirements into "someone who can do everything," and applications stop coming in.
  • The job title alone doesn't communicate Most candidates don't yet know the FDE role. Recruiting with vague work allocation or travel ratios leads to post-hire mismatches and early turnover.

Services

FDE Talent's recruitment support

  • Designing hiring requirements We document work allocation, such as the ratio of implementation, customer conversations, and travel, and narrow down mandatory requirements. We also consult on job title and how to write your job posting.
  • Candidate introductions We explain the FDE role and your company's work to candidates, and verify not only their background but also their motivations before introducing them.
  • Selection process design support In addition to technical interviews, we help design selection processes that assess FDE aptitude, such as scoping exercises through mock discovery sessions and timed hands-on assignments.
  • Negotiation through onboarding We mediate between candidates and your company, from aligning conditions such as salary and on-site ratio to coordinating start dates.

Talent

Backgrounds of the candidates we introduce

Candidates with FDE aptitude are found in adjacent roles that have stood between technology and customers. Rather than years of experience, we select candidates based on a track record of delivering projects end-to-end and a motivation to work on-site with customers.

  • SIer and outsourced development engineers Accustomed to working on-site at clients and developing under constraints, they adapt quickly to the FDE way of working.
  • Web software engineers Fully capable of production-quality implementation. We verify their motivation and aptitude for working on-site with customers during selection.
  • IT consultants (hands-on type) Immediately effective at identifying problems and building consensus. We verify whether they can actually write code during selection.
  • Pre-sales and customer engineers Translators between technology and customers. We verify whether they are motivated to carry things beyond PoC into production.

Process

From consultation to onboarding, in four steps.

  • Consultation We ask about your hiring background, the work you want to assign to FDEs, and your current structure. Your job posting doesn't need to be complete yet.
  • Requirements refinement We organize work allocation and mandatory requirements, and articulate the backgrounds of your target candidates and the appeal to communicate to them.
  • Introduction and selection We introduce candidates who match your requirements. We can also consult on designing your selection process and assignments.
  • Offer and onboarding We align conditions and coordinate start dates. Placement fees are charged only when a hire is confirmed.

Pricing

Success-based pricing

Placement fees are charged only when a candidate we introduce is hired. There is no cost for consultations on hiring requirements or information gathering. Fee rates and other conditions will be explained during your consultation.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about FDE hiring

Can you introduce candidates with hands-on FDE experience?

Since there are almost no candidates with hands-on FDE experience in the Japanese job market, our introductions center on candidates from adjacent roles with FDE aptitude, such as SIer engineers, web engineers, IT consultants, and pre-sales. Rather than whether they hold the FDE title, we select candidates based on a track record of delivering projects end-to-end and a motivation to work on-site with customers.

When do FDE hiring costs apply?

Our pricing is success-based. A placement fee is charged only when a candidate we introduce is hired, and there is no cost at the consultation stage. Fee rates and other conditions will be explained during your consultation.

Can I consult even if my hiring requirements or job posting aren't finalized?

Yes. We can start from identifying the work you want to assign to FDEs, organizing the ratio of implementation, customer conversations, and travel, and consulting on the job title and how to write your job posting.

What types of companies do you serve?

We serve companies looking to hire FDEs or equivalent positions, such as AI startups, SaaS companies expanding into enterprise markets, and businesses implementing AI in-house.

Can I consult on whether to post the role as FDE, solution engineer, or customer success?

Yes. Posting a role as "FDE" when writing production code at customer sites is a small part of the job leads to post-hire mismatches. We work together from choosing a job title that fits the actual work and how to write the job posting.
