OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer: Role and Pay

FWD Deployment · 2026-10-11 · 2 min read

Summary

This article outlines OpenAI's forward deployed engineer role and posted pay. FDEs own end-to-end deployments for strategic customers in sectors like healthcare, finance and legal, covering discovery, scoping, system design, build and production rollout. Success is measured by production adoption, workflow impact, and eval feedback that feeds OpenAI's product and model roadmaps. The San Francisco role is hybrid with three office days weekly and up to 50% travel. Posted ranges (checked October 11, 2026): $185K–$300K plus equity for FDE in San Francisco, and $153K–$325K plus equity for Forward Deployed Software Engineer in New York. Separate sales, solutions engineering and customer success teams handle the same accounts, letting FDEs focus on building, and the team also delivers through Frontier Alliance partners including Accenture, BCG, McKinsey and Capgemini.

Key points

  • FDEs own deployments end to end, from scoping to production rollout.
  • Success metrics: production adoption, workflow impact, and eval feedback.
  • San Francisco FDE pay: $185K–$300K plus equity.
  • New York Forward Deployed Software Engineer: $153K–$325K plus equity.
  • Delivery also runs through Frontier Alliance partners like Accenture and BCG.

The role

What a OpenAI forward deployed engineer does

OpenAI's FDEs own discovery, technical scoping, system design, the build and the production rollout, working directly with customer engineering and domain teams. OpenAI measures success by production adoption, workflow impact and eval feedback that shapes its product and model roadmaps.

The San Francisco role is hybrid, three office days a week, with travel up to 50%. Specialized postings cover sectors like healthcare.

OpenAI lists sales, solutions engineering, solutions architects and customer success as separate teams that work the same accounts, so FDEs focus on building.

Team: Forward Deployed Engineering, alongside sales and solutions teams. In OpenAI's words: "lead complex end-to-end deployments of frontier models in production alongside our most strategic customers" (posting)

Best for: Enterprises shipping production systems on OpenAI models

  • FDE team
  • Frontier Alliance partners

OpenAI's forward deployed engineering team runs end-to-end deployments with strategic customers in fields like healthcare, finance and legal, owning scoping, design, build and rollout. It also works through Frontier Alliance partners such as Accenture, BCG, McKinsey and Capgemini.
