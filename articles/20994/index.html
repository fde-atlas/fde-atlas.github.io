FDE vs AI Engineer vs GenAI Engineer (2026)

Cloud Soft Solutions · 2026-11-12 · 9 min read

Summary

This career guide for freshers compares three overlapping roles: AI Engineer, Generative AI Engineer, and AI Forward Deployed Engineer. AI Engineers build AI-powered systems with machine learning; GenAI Engineers specialize in LLM applications using RAG, prompting, and agents; FDEs own end-to-end delivery of AI solutions inside customer environments, combining cloud, DevOps, enterprise integration, and customer-facing work. The author recommends freshers first build Python, software engineering, cloud, and GenAI foundations, start in junior AI or GenAI roles, and move toward FDE responsibilities after gaining production experience. The article includes a seven-phase learning roadmap, three portfolio projects, job-search tips, and notes that salary comparisons are unreliable because companies define the titles differently.

Key points

  • The three roles differ in focus: AI systems, LLM applications, and enterprise AI delivery.
  • FDEs are distinguished by ownership of outcomes in the customer's environment.
  • Recommended path: junior AI/GenAI roles first, then grow into FDE work.
  • A shared seven-phase roadmap covers Python, ML, GenAI, RAG, cloud, DevOps, and enterprise skills.
  • Salary rankings are unreliable since companies define these titles inconsistently.

AI is opening new careers for the next generation of software engineers, from intelligent applications and AI agents to enterprise AI solutions. Students and graduates keep asking the same question:

Should I become an AI Engineer, a Generative AI Engineer or a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)? Which gives freshers better opportunities in 2026?

The three paths overlap, but their responsibilities, required skills and daily work are different:

  • An AI Engineer builds intelligent applications.
  • A GenAI Engineer specialises in applications powered by large language models (LLMs).
  • An AI Forward Deployed Engineer takes AI solutions into real business environments and integrates, deploys and runs them there.

This guide compares all three for freshers: skills, a typical project for each, entry-level job titles, and a shared roadmap. If you already work in the industry and only want the two-way comparison, read FDE vs AI Engineer.

1. What is an AI Engineer?

An AI Engineer is a software engineer who builds applications and systems that use AI to solve business problems. It is a broad category. Depending on the company, the work can involve machine learning, deep learning, predictive analytics, computer vision, NLP or generative AI.

Responsibilities

  • Build AI-powered applications and integrate ML models into software.
  • Process and analyse data, and evaluate model performance.
  • Expose models through APIs.
  • Deploy on the cloud and monitor reliability, scale and cost.

Technologies:

  • Programming and data: Python, NumPy, Pandas, scikit-learn, SQL.
  • Models: TensorFlow or PyTorch, ML and deep learning fundamentals.
  • Apps and deployment: FastAPI and REST APIs, AWS or Azure, Docker and Kubernetes, model deployment and monitoring.

Typical project: fraud detection for a bank. Collect and prepare transaction data. Train and evaluate a fraud model. Serve predictions through a REST API. Deploy on AWS or Azure, and monitor prediction quality over time.

2. What is a Generative AI Engineer?

A GenAI Engineer builds applications on LLMs and other generative models. These apps generate and summarise text, answer questions over documents, produce structured output and call external tools.

Responsibilities

  • Integrate LLMs and design prompts.
  • Build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with embeddings and vector databases.
  • Build agents and multi-step workflows with tool calling.
  • Add guardrails, evaluate responses, and tune latency and cost.

Technologies

Area What to learn
Back end Python, FastAPI, REST APIs, PostgreSQL
Generative AI LLMs, transformers, tokenisation, embeddings, context windows, prompting
RAG Ingestion, chunking, vector databases, semantic and hybrid search, reranking, retrieval evaluation
Agentic AI LangChain, LangGraph, tool and function calling, MCP, multi-agent workflows, human-in-the-loop
Cloud AI Amazon Bedrock, Bedrock Knowledge Bases, Bedrock Guardrails, Microsoft Foundry (Azure OpenAI models), Azure AI Search

Typical project: an enterprise banking RAG assistant.

  • Ingest loan policies, support procedures and compliance documents from Amazon S3.
  • Extract the text, create embeddings and store them in a vector database.
  • Retrieve the passages relevant to each question.
  • Have the LLM answer with source references.
  • Add guardrails and access control.
  • Evaluate answer quality.
  • Deploy on AWS.

3. What is an AI Forward Deployed Engineer?

An FDE works closely with customers and business teams to build and implement software for their actual requirements. An AI FDE combines AI engineering, cloud architecture, software development, DevOps, enterprise integration and customer-facing problem-solving.

The key difference is ownership. An AI or GenAI engineer usually owns building an application or a platform component. An FDE owns making the solution work *in the customer's environment* and deliver measurable business value. See What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

Responsibilities

  • Understand customer requirements and find problems AI can solve.
  • Design end-to-end architectures, and build APIs, RAG and agents.
  • Integrate with enterprise systems, identity and security.
  • Deploy on AWS or Azure with Terraform and CI/CD.
  • Set up monitoring, and troubleshoot in production.
  • Explain the solution to customers and improve business outcomes.

Technologies: everything the GenAI engineer uses, plus:

  • DevOps: Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, Jenkins or GitHub Actions, CloudWatch or Azure Monitor.
  • Enterprise engineering: API integration, identity, networking, observability, system design, requirement gathering and technical documentation.

Typical project: AI-powered IT operations automation. An enterprise handles hundreds of incidents a day, and engineers search logs and runbooks by hand. The FDE builds a platform that:

  • Collects alerts.
  • Retrieves troubleshooting documents with RAG.
  • Analyses logs and uses agents to investigate likely causes.
  • Opens tickets through APIs.
  • Recommends fixes and asks a human to approve risky actions.
  • Runs on AWS or Azure with full monitoring.
  • Is evaluated and improved over time.

5. Which career is best for freshers in 2026?

No single path is best for everyone. Choose based on your programming foundation, your interests and how each role is hired at entry level.

Choose AI Engineering if you enjoy Python, maths and statistics, ML algorithms, data processing and model building.

  • Entry-level titles: Junior AI Engineer, Junior ML Engineer, Python AI Developer, Associate AI Developer, AI Application Developer.

Choose GenAI Engineering if you enjoy LLM apps, prompting, RAG, vector databases, agents and knowledge assistants.

  • Entry-level titles: Junior GenAI Engineer, Generative AI Developer, LLM Application Developer, Junior RAG Engineer, Agentic AI Developer.

Choose the FDE path if you enjoy end-to-end engineering across Python, cloud, generative AI, DevOps and integrations, plus working with customers and troubleshooting.

  • Titles to target: Associate FDE, Junior AI Implementation Engineer, AI Integration Engineer, AI Solutions Engineer, Associate Cloud AI Engineer, Applied AI Engineer.

Our recommendation for freshers: build Python, software engineering, cloud and GenAI foundations first. Target junior AI or GenAI roles, then grow into FDE responsibilities as you gain production and integration experience. Direct FDE hiring does happen, but many employers prefer people who have already worked on production, customer-facing systems. We cover this route in detail in Can freshers become AI Forward Deployed Engineers?

6. What about salary?

There is no reliable universal salary ranking for these three titles, because companies define them differently. Pay depends on the employer, city, education, skills, experience, interview performance and portfolio. What tends to raise it:

  • depth in Python and back-end engineering;
  • experience with production AI;
  • AWS or Azure;
  • RAG and agentic AI that you have actually delivered;
  • Kubernetes and Terraform;
  • enterprise architecture;
  • customer-facing delivery with measurable results.

Choose on skills and interest, not advertised salary figures, and compare current vacancies in India before deciding. Our jobs board lists live AI, GenAI and FDE roles from employers' own career pages.

7. A shared roadmap for all three paths

All three careers start from the same foundations. Then you go deeper in the area that matches your target role.

Phase Learn Project
1. Python and software engineering Python, OOP, data structures, SQL, Git, Linux, REST APIs, FastAPI Python app with a database and REST APIs
2. AI and ML fundamentals Supervised and unsupervised learning, regression and classification, evaluation, neural networks, NLP basics Customer churn prediction app
3. Generative AI LLM architecture, tokenisation, embeddings, prompting, function calling, model APIs AI assistant using an LLM API
4. RAG and agentic AI Chunking, vector databases, RAG, LangChain, LangGraph, MCP, guardrails, evaluation Enterprise RAG assistant with an agent that uses approved tools
5. AWS and Azure EC2, S3, IAM, Lambda, Bedrock, OpenSearch, or Azure Functions, Foundry, AI Search, Entra ID, AKS Deploy your GenAI app on one cloud
6. DevOps and production Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, monitoring, logging, security Containerised app shipped through a pipeline onto Terraform-managed infrastructure
7. FDE and enterprise engineering Requirement gathering, architecture, integrations, identity, documentation, troubleshooting, cost, business impact Full solution with architecture docs, deployment, monitoring and a customer-style demo

Where each path goes deeper:

  • AI Engineers spend longer on phase 2.
  • GenAI Engineers spend longer on phases 3–4.
  • FDEs need phases 5–7 most.

9. How freshers get their first AI job

  • Build the foundation: Python, SQL, APIs, Linux and Git.
  • Pick a direction: AI Engineering, GenAI Engineering, or the longer FDE path.
  • Complete enterprise-style projects with real data, integrations, testing, security and deployment.
  • Publish on GitHub with a README, architecture diagram, setup steps and a demo video.
  • Write a technical résumé that shows the problem, the stack, your own contribution and measurable results. Use the FDE résumé and portfolio guide.
  • Prepare for interviews in Python, SQL, AI fundamentals, RAG, agents, cloud, APIs and troubleshooting.
  • Apply under several titles, such as junior GenAI developer, AI application developer, Python AI developer and AI implementation engineer, not only "AI Engineer". Start with the fresher jobs board.
  • Get production experience through internships, entry-level roles and project teams.

Final verdict

  • AI Engineer: for those who love programming, AI systems and machine learning.
  • GenAI Engineer: for those who want to build LLM apps, RAG systems, assistants and agents.
  • AI Forward Deployed Engineer: for those who want to combine AI, Python, cloud, DevOps, integration and customer-facing engineering.

For most freshers: learn Python and software engineering, build strong GenAI skills, complete enterprise projects and get cloud deployment experience. Then specialise in GenAI or grow toward FDE as you gain production experience. The future belongs to engineers who can turn AI into real business solutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better: AI Engineer or GenAI Engineer?
Both are valuable. AI Engineering is broad and can include classical machine learning; GenAI Engineering specialises in LLMs, RAG, prompting and agents. Choose by interest and the roles your target companies hire for.

Can freshers become Generative AI Engineers?
Yes, by building Python, API, LLM, RAG and cloud skills and proving them with practical projects.

Can freshers become Forward Deployed Engineers?
Yes, but direct FDE roles are competitive because many employers want production and customer-facing experience. A junior AI, back-end, cloud or implementation role is a practical first step.

Is FDE better than GenAI Engineering?
Neither is universally better. GenAI engineering specialises in LLM applications; FDE combines AI development with enterprise integration, deployment and customer problem-solving.

Should freshers learn AWS or Azure first?
Start with one and learn it properly. AWS suits Bedrock-based GenAI apps; Azure is equally relevant where companies use Microsoft services and Microsoft Foundry.

Is Kubernetes required for a GenAI Engineer?
Not for every role, but containers and deployment knowledge help, and Kubernetes matters more for production and FDE roles.

Does Cloud Soft Solutions guarantee an AI job?
No course can guarantee selection. Training and placement preparation improve readiness; hiring depends on skills, communication, interviews, eligibility and vacancies.
