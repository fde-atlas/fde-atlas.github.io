skool.com · 2026-08-29 · 3 min read

Summary

This is a promotional page for a paid Skool community targeting people who want to move into FDE or Applied AI engineering roles. It was created by Paden Gayle, an FDE at Notion and former SWE at Google and Bloomberg, who authored a book on forward deployed engineering for O'Reilly. The offering includes seven modules covering RAG, MCP, agents, and evals, Python code samples, GitHub repos, weekly 1:1 Q&A sessions, and claimed recruiter introductions to companies such as Cursor, Palantir, and Decagon. The price is listed at $79 with claims of limited spots. The page features multiple positive member testimonials, but it is fundamentally marketing material, and the employment and salary claims are not independently verified.
