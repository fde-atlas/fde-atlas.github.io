Summary

Japanese AI startup ExaWizards is hiring a junior-class Forward Deployed Engineer in Fukuoka. The role involves implementing and deploying AI solutions, applications, and AI agents built on the company's enterprise AI platform exaBase Studio, working alongside consultants from technical validation through production launch. No AI experience is required; new hires complete a training program and then join real projects under senior FDEs. Requirements include at least one year of professional programming experience in any language, with Python or TypeScript preferred. Annual salary ranges from 5.04 million to 6.6 million yen, with 10:00-19:00 hours and full two-day weekends.

Key points

  • ExaWizards is hiring a junior Forward Deployed Engineer in Fukuoka.
  • Work centers on deploying AI solutions via the exaBase Studio platform.
  • No AI experience needed; training plus mentorship from senior FDEs.
  • Requires 1+ years of programming experience; Python/TypeScript preferred.
  • Salary: 5.04-6.6 million yen; hours 10:00-19:00, weekends off.
