How much do forward deployed engineers make?

Across the 27 FDE roles that disclosed a US pay band in November 2026, total compensation ran a median of $185K to $300K, with a floor near $132K and a top around $445K plus equity. That is senior-software-engineer compensation for a role many engineers have never heard of. Most of Palantir's roles and international postings did not publish bands, so the real market is likely even broader.

How this estimate works

The base band is the real disclosed median for a mid-level US FDE. The calculator then applies a level multiplier (a junior role trends lower, staff and above higher) and a location factor (US roles pay the most; the UK, Europe, and other international roles trend lower). Those adjustments are modeled from how engineering compensation typically scales, not from company-specific FDE data we do not have. Treat the output as a realistic range to anchor a negotiation, not a promise.
