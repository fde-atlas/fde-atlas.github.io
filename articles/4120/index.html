The agentic workspace for forward-deployed engineers

Tandem · 2026-07-14 · 2 min read

Summary

Tandem is an agentic workspace aimed at forward-deployed engineers. It builds a self-updating 'brain' per customer deployment by ingesting calls, Slack threads, tickets, CRM data, commits, Claude Code sessions, and customer files. From this shared context it drafts daily briefs, status reports, SOW updates, and handoff docs, and flags risks such as scope drift against the SOW, unexplained repo changes, or silent customers. The brain can be read and written over MCP from Claude or other AI interfaces, and it runs in the vendor's environment rather than the customer's. All agent edits carry visible markers and require engineer approval before shipping.

Key points

  • One self-updating shared brain per deployment, fed from calls, Slack, tickets, and code.
  • Daily sourced briefs cover progress, blockers, and go-live risks without prompting.
  • Risk detection compares shipped work against the SOW, flagging drift or expansion.
  • MCP lets Claude or Claude Code pull deployment context in and sync it back.
  • Agent edits are marked and require engineer approval before being written back.

Full text

Connect everything

Calls, Slack, email, tickets, CRM, and files. Plus commits, product events, Claude Code sessions, and the customer's own spreadsheet. Or log a fact in one line from Claude.

Tandem for forward-deployed engineers

One brain per deployment, built from your calls, Slack, code sessions, and the customer's tools. Nobody updates it. The recap, the risk flag, and the next move, drafted for you.

Backed by

Founders from

Every call, thread, ticket, commit, and Claude Code session, in one brain per deployment. What was said, decided, promised, and shipped. It stays current on its own.

“Can Acme still hit the September go-live?” Same answer every time, every fact with its source. The whole pod's context, not just yours.

Tasks, recaps, status reports, SOW updates, handoff docs. Drafted from the brain, written back to your tools. You approve, one by one or in bulk.

Brief

What moved, what's blocked, what threatens the go-live. Per deployment, sourced, every morning, in the app or in Slack. No prompt tuning, no re-reading transcripts.

Risk

Every deployment checked against what was scoped, said, and shipped. Scope drift vs. the SOW, a repo change nobody mentioned, a customer gone quiet. Flagged with receipts, as risk or as expansion. Your call.

Headless / MCP

Read and write the brain over MCP, from Claude, Claude Code, or any AI interface. Pull the customer's context into a coding session, sync it back when you're done. One brain for the whole pod.

Delivery, both sides

The customer tracks in their Excel or Jira. You track in Tandem. Both stay current, no copy-paste. Every agent edit carries a visible marker. Nothing ships without your approval.

Build on it

MCP, API, webhooks. Pipe deployment context into your own dashboards and workflows. You keep building, the brain stays true underneath. Runs in your environment, not the customer's.
