Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineer: Role and Pay

FWD Deployment · 2026-10-11 · 2 min read

Summary

This article outlines Salesforce's forward deployed engineer role and pay. FDEs build and deploy Agentforce AI agents inside enterprise customer environments, working from configuration to full-stack builds alongside a Deployment Strategist who advises on AI adoption. Postings require production experience and daily use of tools like Cursor, Claude, and Salesforce's Vibes. As of October 11, 2026, a US public sector posting lists $88,970–$287,910, rising to $316,750 in California, New York, and select metros. Salesforce reported $41.5 billion in fiscal 2026 revenue; Agentforce is priced from $2 per conversation or $500 per 100,000 Flex Credits.

Key points

  • Salesforce FDEs deploy Agentforce agents in customer environments.
  • They pair with Deployment Strategists advising on AI adoption.
  • Posted US pay ranges from $88,970 to $287,910.
  • CA, NY, and select metros reach up to $316,750.
  • Agentforce costs $2 per conversation or $500 per 100,000 Flex Credits.

The role

What a Salesforce forward deployed engineer does

Salesforce FDEs work from configuration to full-stack builds on customer delivery teams, paired with a Deployment Strategist who advises the customer on AI strategy and adoption.

The US public sector posting says its builders use AI tools such as Cursor, Claude and Salesforce's own Vibes every day, and it asks for production experience.

Its careers site also listed FDE roles in Rome, London, Singapore, Espoo, Paris, Madrid and Dubai on 2026-10-11, alongside Deployment Strategist and Forward Deployed Consultant roles.

Team: Agentforce delivery teams, paired with Deployment Strategists. In Salesforce's words: "Production experience required. Builder mindset non-negotiable." (posting)

Best for: Salesforce customers deploying AI agents on CRM data

  • Agentforce
  • $41.5B revenue, FY26

Salesforce hires forward deployed engineers, paired with deployment strategists, to build and ship Agentforce agents inside enterprise customers. Agentforce is priced from $2 per conversation or $500 per 100,000 Flex Credits. Salesforce reported $41.5 billion in revenue for fiscal 2026.
