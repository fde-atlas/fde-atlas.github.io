OpenWork · 2026-11-13 · 12 min read

Summary

Ark With Tech, a subsidiary of consulting firm Ark With Consulting founded in 2024, is hiring Forward Deployed Engineers in Japan with salaries of 5-15 million yen. The company bills itself as a high-payout SES firm, returning 65-115% of client billing rates to employees, who also get full control over project selection. The FDE role covers the full cycle from identifying client problems through AI/system design, development (LLM, RAG, AI agents, cloud on AWS/Azure/GCP), to production deployment. Requirements emphasize general development experience; prior FDE or generative AI experience is not mandatory.

Key points

  • Ark With Tech is a 2024-founded SES subsidiary of Ark With Consulting, claiming 1.5 billion yen revenue in three years.
  • FDE salaries range from 5 to 15 million yen with a 65-115% payout rate of client billing.
  • The role spans client problem discovery, tech selection, AI/system development, and deployment.
  • Development work includes LLM, RAG, AI agents, Python/TypeScript, and cloud platforms.
  • Employees have 100% project choice, remote work, and flexible hours; no prior FDE experience required.

  • ▍Why We're Hiring ￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣ "We want to build a company where excellent consultants and engineers are properly rewarded for their work." With that vision, we are a high-payout SES firm founded in 2024, originating from a consulting firm. In recent years, the spread of generative AI has rapidly accelerated corporate AI adoption, but challenges are also increasing, such as: "We introduced AI but haven't been able to apply it to actual business operations" "Integration with existing systems is difficult, and PoCs never progress beyond the proof-of-concept stage" "Even though it's technically feasible, it doesn't lead to solving on-the-ground problems" "We want to use AI, but we don't know what to develop or how" That's why we are now hiring FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) who provide end-to-end support, from identifying client challenges to technology selection, AI/system design and development, and implementation and adoption. ▍About the Position ￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣ From identifying client challenges to AI/system design, development, and implementation. We are hiring FDEs who solve business problems with technology. An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is an engineer who works deeply on-site with clients and takes end-to-end responsibility, from understanding business challenges to exploring technical solutions and implementing and deploying systems. With the spread of generative AI, what companies need is not simply introducing AI, but embedding AI into existing operations and systems to achieve real business improvements and business results. In this FDE role, you will leverage your past development and client-facing experience, combining AI, cloud, and data technologies to lead client problem-solving from a technical perspective. ▍Key Responsibilities ￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣ - Interviewing clients about their business and system challenges and organizing requirements - Considering AI/software utilization strategies and technology selection based on the challenges - Application design and development using Python, TypeScript, etc. - Developing systems using LLMs, RAG, AI agents, etc. - Designing and implementing integration with existing systems, SaaS, APIs, and internal data - Building cloud environments using AWS, Azure, GCP, etc. - Technical support from PoC through production deployment and operational improvement - Technical discussions with clients, improvement proposals, and project advancement ▍Example Projects ￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣ - Business transformation and system development using generative AI at major companies - Designing and building RAG systems that leverage internal knowledge - Developing business automation solutions using AI agents - Embedding AI features into existing business systems - Developing applications that connect enterprise data with AI - PoC, production deployment, and continuous feature improvement of AI products *The above are examples of expected duties and projects. Actual assignments will be decided based on your experience, preferences, and project availability. ▍Career Paths as an FDE ￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣￣ The appeal of being an FDE is that you can leverage your technical skills while also being directly involved in defining client challenges and exploring solutions. For example: - From SE to FDE, taking responsibility through to solving client challenges - From AI engineer to a position involved in client relations and business design - From full-stack engineer to solution development using AI and cloud - From tech lead to a role leading technology selection and architecture design - Eventually, to AI architect, tech lead, or IT consultant These are the kinds of career paths you can pursue. Our company has adopted a "performance-based compensation system" and a "project selection system," so you can aim to increase your income, deepen your expertise, or prioritize your preferred way of working. We welcome those who want to not just build technology, but use it to transform clients' businesses.
