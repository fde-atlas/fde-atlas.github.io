What's the Chinese term for FDE: 前线, 前沿, or 前导?

Original title: FDE的中文叫法：前线、前沿还是前导？

圣路可 Fde · 2026-10-17 · 1 min read

Summary

This article surveys Chinese translations of Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE). An August 2026 notice from China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and a September 2026 Shanghai training program both use “前线部署工程师” (frontline deployed engineer), while Alibaba Cloud and some vendor courses prefer “前沿部署工程师” (frontier deployed engineer), which has higher search volume. The author argues “frontline” is more accurate because Forward refers to being closest to the business, on-site and accountable for outcomes, not to cutting-edge technology. For formal documents, the recommendation is “前线部署工程师（FDE）” with a note that it is also translated as the frontier variant for searchability. The article also distinguishes FDE from “AI application engineer”: the former emphasizes working at customer sites and owning deployment results, so job duties matter more than titles.

The full English name of FDE is Forward Deployed Engineer. The August 2026 notice from the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology uses the name "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)", Shanghai's training program also uses this term, and this site consistently uses "Forward Deployed Engineer".

According to media reports, Tencent's related position is called "AI Forward Deployed Engineer", while ByteDance's similar position is called "AI Application Engineer".

Why "frontline"

Forward here refers to being closest to the business on the front line, not to the technological cutting edge. "Frontline" emphasizes being on-site and being accountable for outcomes; "cutting edge" is easily understood as "someone who knows the latest technology", which deviates from the original intent of this role.

How to use it in writing and recruiting

For formal documents, it is recommended to write "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)". So that people searching for "前沿部署工程师" can also find it, you can note in the body text "also translated as 前沿部署工程师".

Frequently asked questions

Which term should be used when recruiting or writing a resume?

It is recommended to write "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)", consistent with the ministry documents; at the same time, note in the body text "also translated as 前沿部署工程师" to make it easier to find.

Are FDE and "AI Application Engineer" the same thing?

The work overlaps, but the focus differs. AI Application Engineer more often refers to someone who develops AI applications; FDE emphasizes being embedded at the customer's or business's site and being accountable for implementation outcomes. Look at the job responsibilities, not the title.
