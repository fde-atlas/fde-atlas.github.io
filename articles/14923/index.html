AI Engineering Recruitment, Australia

Re:Sourced · 2026 · 3 min read

Summary

Re:Sourced, a Sydney-based recruitment firm, describes its specialist AI engineering search service covering ML platform, applied science, MLOps and leadership roles. It sources talent from foundation-model labs, big-tech AI orgs, Australian scale-ups like Leonardo.ai and Harrison.ai, and academia. Its process involves a 90-minute intake, outreach to 200-400 named engineers per mandate, and shortlists of 3-5 candidates with technical memos. Median time to signed offer is 21 days. In 2026, senior AI engineer base salaries run AUD 180-220k in Sydney and AUD 175-210k in Melbourne, with principal levels at AUD 220-260k; frontier scale-ups often add 30-50% in equity. About half of placements are platform-side. The firm also recruits forward deployed engineers under a dedicated specialism.

Key points

  • Places ML platform, MLOps, applied science and Head of AI roles.
  • Talent pool includes alumni of Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind and Meta FAIR.
  • 200-400 engineers contacted per mandate; median 21 days to signed offer.
  • 2026 Sydney senior AI base salaries: AUD 180-220k; principal AUD 220-260k.
