Senior Forward Deployed Engineer Job in San Francisco, CA

Talentify · 2026-11-05 · 3 min read

Summary

JeffreyM Consulting is hiring a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer in San Francisco for an early-stage, venture-backed AI startup automating semiconductor design and verification workflows. The role pays $180,000–250,000 plus equity, requires 2–6 years of experience, and involves owning 1–2 enterprise chip customer engagements end to end, debugging an AI agent platform live in air-gapped on-prem environments. Candidates need strong Linux, container, networking, and Python skills, must work onsite five days a week, and no chip design background is required. Only H-1B transfers are sponsored, with occasional travel to South Bay, Texas, and possibly Asia.

Key points

  • JeffreyM Consulting is hiring for a VC-backed AI startup automating chip design and verification workflows.
  • Salary is $180,000–250,000 plus equity, with mandatory five-day onsite work in San Francisco.
  • The engineer owns 1–2 large chip customer deployments end to end, each lasting 3–6+ months.
  • Requirements include 2–6 years of experience and deep Linux, container, networking, and Python expertise.
  • No semiconductor background needed; only H-1B transfers sponsored; occasional travel required.
