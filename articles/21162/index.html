A list of articles to learn about FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) | FDE board

Summary

This is an index page from Japan's FDE board (run by Westeria Inc.) compiling ten articles about the Forward Deployed Engineer role. It covers the role's definition and Palantir origins, why AI companies like OpenAI hire FDEs, salary benchmarks in Japan and the US using levels.fyi and job postings, where to find FDE jobs in Japan, career-change routes, required technical and non-technical skills, distinctions from solutions engineers and related roles, interview preparation, career paths, freelance contracting, and advice for companies hiring FDEs. Content is based on job listings as of August 2026.

Key points

  • A ten-article collection covering the FDE role end to end.
  • Explains FDE's Palantir origins and adoption by AI companies.
  • Salary data drawn from levels.fyi and disclosed job ranges.
  • Interviews assess coding, problem decomposition, and client handling.
  • Includes guidance for freelancers and for companies hiring FDEs.

Full text

About the "Forward Deployed Engineer" role, which embeds with customers to implement AI products. This section covers what the job involves, salary, how to find openings, required skills, and the hiring process and career paths. The articles are located under FDE board (/fde/). 10 articles in total.

  • What Is a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)? | Why the Role Born at Palantir Is Growing in Japan This article organizes the definition of Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), how the role originated at Palantir, the background behind its adoption by AI companies like OpenAI, and how it differs from solution engineers and contracted development, based on job postings verifiable as of August 2026.
  • FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) Salary | Market Rates in the US and Japan and What Makes the Difference This article organizes Forward Deployed Engineer salaries using US public data (levels.fyi and disclosed ranges in company job postings) and Japanese job postings. It explains why salaries differ between the US and Japan, the four factors that determine salary, and how to read salary ranges in job postings.
  • Where to Find FDE Jobs in Japan | Types of Hiring Companies and How to Read Job Postings This article categorizes companies hiring Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) in Japan into three types and explains, based on actual job postings as of August 2026, how to search without missing openings due to variations in job title wording, and the items you should always check in a job posting.
  • How to Become an FDE | Transition Paths by Background and the Gaps to Fill This article organizes transitions into Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) roles by original profession, including backend engineers, SIer/consulting, data scientists, and PMs. It explains the actual years of experience demanded in job postings, a realistic path for those without direct experience, and what to prepare.
  • Skills Required for FDEs | Technical and Non-Technical Breakdown Extracted from Job Posting Requirements This article organizes the requirements actually written in Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) job postings, dividing them into technical skills (languages, LLMs, cloud, data) and non-technical skills (problem definition, negotiation, English), and shows the priority order for filling the gaps.
  • FDE vs. Solution Engineer | A Side-by-Side Comparison with Sales Engineers, SAs, and Contracted PMs This article compares Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) with solution engineers, sales engineers, solution architects, customer engineers, and PMs on contracted development projects in terms of scope of responsibility and evaluation criteria. It also summarizes how to distinguish them from job postings.
  • FDE Interviews and Hiring Process | Typical Flow, Common Questions, and How to Prepare How does the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) hiring process differ from a standard software engineer process? This article organizes the evaluation perspectives across three areas—coding, problem decomposition, and customer interaction—and the preparation needed, from taking inventory of your work history to preparing reverse questions.
  • FDE Career Paths | Where to Go After a Few Years and the Experience That Pays Off Later What do you gain after a few years as a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), and where can you go from there? This article organizes five paths—internal promotion, product manager, engineering manager, entrepreneurship, and going independent—along with the risks that can arise.
  • FDE as Contract Work | The Reality of Freelance Projects, How to Think About Rates, and Contract Considerations This article organizes the reality of taking on Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)-equivalent work as a contractor or freelancer. It covers project patterns, how to think about rates, choosing between quasi-delegation (jun-i) and contract (ukeoi) agreements, practical aspects of the Freelance Act, and how to proceed while avoiding disguised contracting.
  • Why Hiring FDEs Is Hard and How to Write Job Postings That Attract Them | For Companies For companies looking to hire Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), this article covers the causes of hiring difficulty, how to judge whether your company truly needs one, how to write job postings, how to set salary ranges, hiring process design, and assignment and evaluation design. Includes an introduction to FDE board, which offers free listings.
