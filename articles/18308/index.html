A New Consulting Model for the Generative AI Era, Integrating Strategy Development Through AI Implementation

LTS Inc. (Headquarters: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director, President and Executive Officer: Hiroaki Kaburajima; hereinafter "LTS") will fully launch a new consulting service model for the generative AI era, "Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE)," starting in July. Targeting the contact center / BPO domain, where the benefits of generative AI are high and demand for business transformation is rapidly growing, strategy consultants and AI/software engineers will work as a single team to support everything from concept development to production implementation and operation.

FDE is a support model spreading mainly among leading AI companies in Europe and the US, in which "consultants with deep understanding of industry challenges" and "AI/software engineers" enter customer companies as a single team and jointly handle everything from strategy development to implementation and operation of production systems. This service goes beyond generative AI adoption support and PoCs (proofs of concept): a strategy consulting team well-versed in the contact center / BPO industry and specialist teams handling business consulting, AI and data utilization, engineering, and production system development work as one, accompanying clients from concept development through implementation and establishment.

■ Background: Transformation into a "Value Creation Hub"

LTS believes that transforming contact centers / BPO from "cost centers" into "corporate value creation hubs" is key to competitiveness in the generative AI era. Contact centers / BPO are hubs of value creation where customer voices gather, feeding into sales, product development, and CX improvement. They are also one of the areas where generative AI implementation delivers the greatest impact. The potential to dramatically transform customer-facing operations is expanding, including operator support, call and chat summarization, VOC analysis, knowledge utilization, quality evaluation, training, and downstream administrative processing.

At the same time, in addition to talent shortages and rising labor costs, companies are required to address diverse management challenges, such as maintaining and improving service quality, enhancing customer touchpoints, and shifting to performance-based BPO and BPaaS (Business Process as a Service). Moreover, operations are complex and quality requirements are high, so simply introducing AI tools does not produce results. The front lines contain complex elements such as customer-specific operational differences, exception handling, quality management, escalations, and industry-specific business requirements. Without fully understanding them, AI adoption often stalls at the PoC stage, fails to take root in operations, or fails to deliver expected results.

FDE services solve these challenges by driving strategy through implementation without fragmentation. Furthermore, we aim to achieve transformation into a "value creation hub" by leveraging data obtained from customer touchpoints to drive sales, product development, and CX improvement.

・Industry Expertise

LTS has provided extensive support to contact center and BPO providers as well as user companies, covering business strategy, business reform, AI utilization, BPO model transformation, M&A and alliances, shared services center (SSC) establishment, and back-office reform. This service leverages that industry expertise. First, the strategy consulting team organizes management and business challenges and defines the operations to be transformed, the relevant KPIs, and the areas to be turned into platforms.

・Business Transformation Design

Next, business consultants visualize front-line operations and redesign which tasks are handled by people, which by AI, and which should be enhanced through data utilization.

・AI Implementation

Then, the AI, data, and engineering team develops and implements AI agents, business applications, data integration platforms, and more as systems that run in production environments, supporting adoption and operational establishment. By driving strategy through implementation as a single team, we aim to realize transformation that delivers business results.

■ Services Offered

This service provides support in the following areas.

✓ Strategy: Development of business and AI utilization strategies

✓ Business reform: AI-assisted customer response support; advanced quality management; transition to BPaaS

✓ Implementation: AI application development, data platforms, joint platform development

■ Comment from Yu Kamemoto (Director, LTS; Head of the Strategy Consulting Division)

Generative AI is a technology that rewrites business operations themselves. However, without strategy, AI does not connect to business results, and without an understanding of operations, it never becomes a mechanism that is actually used. Moreover, without deep knowledge of industry structure and operations, solving essential problems is difficult. Without the ability to write production code, concepts never turn into results. Consulting support that only draws up strategy, or merely introduces AI, is not enough.

For AI implementation, it is essential to understand both management challenges and front-line operations. The distinctive feature of LTS's FDE services is delivering "strategy," "industry expertise," and "implementation capability" as an integrated whole. Strategy consultants who understand the industry and AI, data, and engineering talent work as one, taking responsibility all the way to delivering systems that run in production, realizing transformation that does not end at the PoC stage.

Furthermore, going forward, through FDE services we will promote co-creation with contact center providers, BPO providers, user companies, and technology partners, supporting the creation of new customer touchpoint models for the AI era.

LTS has supported corporate transformation through the twin pillars of strategy consulting and DX implementation. Building on that expertise, we will roll out FDE as a new transformation model for the generative AI era. And as a transformation partner that has engaged deeply with the contact center / BPO domain, we will root FDE in Japan and increase the number of companies that "transform their business with AI," rather than companies that merely "adopt AI."
