The front lines of AI agents arrive every morning.

Original title: AIエージェントの 最前線が毎朝届く

aiagent-navi.com · 2026-09-06 · 4 min read

Summary

This is a promotional page for AIエージェントナビ, a Japanese-language newsletter focused exclusively on AI agents. It is sent every weekday morning and presents point-form summaries readable in about one minute, covering both domestic and overseas news, with a stated policy of relying on official documents and announcements. New subscribers receive three free resources: a Claude Code beginner's guide, a first-time guide to hiring FDEs (forward deployed engineers) that includes market pricing based on public fees and a 20-item pre-contract checklist, and a 2026 MCP server landscape map covering 188 servers across 47 categories with an Excel file. An optional outage alert email covers major incidents officially acknowledged by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot, roughly zero to four emails per month. The target audience includes executives, department heads, DX/IT staff, consultants, and startups moving from generative AI experimentation to agent-driven execution.

Key points

  • Japanese newsletter dedicated solely to AI agents, sent weekday mornings.
  • One-minute point-form summaries based on official primary sources.
  • Free sign-up gifts include an FDE hiring guide with pricing and a 20-item checklist.
  • MCP server map covers 188 servers in 47 categories, with Excel data.
  • Optional outage alerts for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot.
