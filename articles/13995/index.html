Forward Deployed Engineer Salary 2026

Forward Deploying · 2026 · 1 min read

Summary

Forward Deploying published a November 2026 salary guide for Forward Deployed Engineers based on 116 live US and Canadian job postings, using the midpoint of each employer-published salary range. The US median base salary is $216k, excluding equity and bonus. San Francisco leads at $243k median, while Washington, DC sits lowest at $168k. By seniority, Mid roles median $185k, Senior $216k, Staff $256k, and Lead $262k. Company-level bands and actual closed offers are gated behind free registration. Data comes from postings in states with salary transparency laws and updates as listings refresh.

Methodology

We read the salary range each employer publishes in its own job posting (required by law in California, New York, Washington, Colorado and other states), take the midpoint, and group by city and seniority. Postings without a salary are excluded. Rows with fewer than three postings are hidden. Updated every time the job data refreshes.
