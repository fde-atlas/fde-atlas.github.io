AI Implementation FDE | Transforming the very shape of business with AI

Original title: AI実装FDE｜AIで、事業の形そのものを変える

ゴムマリ株式会社 · 2026-11-15 · 8 min read

Summary

Japanese company Gomu Mari offers an "AI Implementation FDE" service aimed at AX—transforming the business itself with AI rather than merely adding AI tools. Forward Deployed Engineers embed in the client's workplace, identify what to change, rebuild workflows and decision-making around AI, and build AI agent systems on the client's existing infrastructure such as Notion or Copilot. They leave behind operation manuals and decision-rule documents so the change persists as the client's own mechanism. The page cites MIT NANDA research reporting that 95% of corporate generative AI pilots show no measurable P&L impact. The service starts with a business data diagnostic (from 500,000 yen, one week) and differs from consulting, SIer staffing, and off-the-shelf AI tools by handling everything from problem discovery through production rollout.

Key points

  • AX means redesigning workflows and decisions around AI, not just deploying tools.
  • Cites MIT NANDA: 95% of enterprise GenAI pilots lack measurable P&L impact.
  • FDEs embed on-site and build AI agents on existing infrastructure like Notion and Copilot.
  • Deliverables include manuals and decision-rule documents; the team exits once a mechanism is established.
  • Entry point is a data diagnostic from 500,000 yen over one week, with two large-enterprise cases done.

AI Implementation FDE is a service that realizes AX (business transformation through AI) from the front lines. A Forward Deployed Engineer embeds in your workplace, finds what needs to change, builds it with AI, and leaves the transformed operations in place as part of your company's systems.

Why AX

Introducing AI tools does not change the shape of your business.

Companies roll out generative AI tools company-wide and teach people how to use them. But that alone leaves workflows and decision-making exactly as they are. AI remains limited to assisting with parts of existing work, and the shape of the business does not change.

The shape of the business changes only when frontline workflows and decision-making are redesigned with AI as the premise. What to redesign, and how, cannot be decided from the outside. It only becomes visible once someone understands the front lines and the challenges of the operations.

AI Implementation FDE is a service that advances that redesign together with you, from the front lines.

A research survey from MIT reported that 95% of corporate generative AI pilots have not produced measurable profit-and-loss impact.

Source: MIT NANDA, "The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025"

If what you want to build is already decided, you can start right away with custom AI development; if it is a single operation that runs every month, AI agent implementation, products, or specialized operations AI-BPO are available from the start.

Approach

Embed, redesign, and leave behind as a system.

Enter the front lines

Enter the front lines and find what needs to change.

By observing operations and data on site, we identify where the challenges lie and where change will benefit the business. You may have set company-wide goals but not decided where to start. Operations may span multiple departments, making it unclear where fixing things will have an effect. Data may be all there, yet it is unclear what will change. We start from exactly these situations.

Redesign

Redesign the shape of operations with AI as the premise, then build and run it.

We decide together with you what to build, and create AI agents and the systems that run them on the platforms you already use (such as Notion or Copilot). What we build is used on the front lines and refined until it fits the operations.

Leave it behind

Leave the transformed operations in place as part of your company's systems.

We create operation manuals and decision rule definition documents so the process runs within your company. In the bilingual data preparation case, we left behind a dedicated tool that let the front lines run things on their own even as device models increased, complete with operating procedures. We embed only until it becomes a system.

Build as many capabilities as needed, for a single customer.

Ordinary software engineers build one feature for many customers. FDEs do the opposite. Palantir, which pioneered this way of working, describes its own FDEs as follows.

"FDSEs focus on enabling many capabilities for a single customer." Palantir's own description of its FDEs

OpenAI's FDE job posting likewise describes the scope of responsibility as spanning from discovery through release (own discovery, technical scoping, system design, build, and production rollout).

Difference

Not a consultancy, not an SIer, not an AI tool.

The person who proposes (sales), the person who advises (consultants), and the person who builds (engineers) are normally separate. An FDE crosses those boundaries and carries the work through to the end in a single role.

Difference from consultants. Their work centers on advice and documents, and building is left to a separate development company. AI Implementation FDE carries the work through to building, with the same understanding formed during the advisory phase.

Difference from SIers and on-site dispatch engineers. They enter after what to build is decided, and build what was decided. AI Implementation FDE enters from the stage of deciding what to change and what to build.

Difference from AI tools. Tools are delivered, and mastery is left to the front lines. AI Implementation FDE redesigns the shape of operations and builds through to the point where it runs on the front lines.

"FDEs excel at upstream processes: clarifying the challenges customers face, redesigning business processes, and defining system requirements." Nikkei Cross Tech, "'FDE': Similar to but Different from On-Site Dispatch, Strong in Upstream Work, Small Elite Teams for Knowledge-Intensive Work" (June 5, 2026)

First step

The first step: operations data diagnosis

Before deciding what to change and what to build, this diagnosis examines your operations and data to determine where to start. AI Implementation FDE begins here. You can request the diagnosis on its own.

Starting with on-site workshops and interviews, we analyze all raw data, identify the essence of the challenges and the approaches to address them, and compile an execution plan.

Scope

Front lines that have both operations and data, but no clear sense of where to start cutting

Deliverables

On-site workshops, full analysis of raw data, identification of the essence of the challenges, recommendations, and an execution plan (full set of reports)

Pricing and duration

From 500,000 yen / from 1 week

Track record

Two engagements at major enterprises (full analysis of a satisfaction survey / full re-evaluation of tens of thousands of exception-handling cases)

Price and duration depend on the types and volume of logs to read, variations in log notation, and the number of departments and sites included in workshops and interviews (counting unit = one operation diagnosed).

If you only need fully remote data analysis, see operations data analysis reports. This diagnosis is the full version, including on-site support, identification of the essence of the challenges, and an execution plan.

Process

From diagnosis to leaving behind a working system.

What we need from your team are the people on the ground who can teach us the work and your system administrators. We do not work on-site full-time; we combine weekly site visits with remote work. During the engagement, Gomumari will support you with any questions.

01 Business Data Diagnosis (from 1 week)

We inventory your business processes and data, and identify the areas to apply AI and the order to start.

02 Decide what to change and what to build

Based on the diagnosis results, we decide with you which processes to change, what to build, and in what order.

03 Build it and run it in the field

We build on top of your systems and refine them while they are used in the field.

04 Leave behind a system and exit

We leave behind an operations manual and a rule definition document for decision-making, and exit once it has become a system.

Fees and timelines after the Business Data Diagnosis will be discussed and decided once what we build has been determined.

FAQ

Data handling and frequently asked questions

Data handling

  • We take custody of your data after signing a non-disclosure agreement (NDA)
  • Data is processed in environments that are not used for AI training
  • Environments are isolated per project, and access rights are kept to the minimum necessary
  • We maintain and operate an information security management system in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022

※ We have self-declared compliance with IPA "SECURITY ACTION Two Stars" (you can search for "Gomumari" in the list of IPA declaring businesses). For details, see our Information Security Basic Policy and DX Strategy.

How does a project start?

It starts with the Business Data Diagnosis (from 1 week). We inventory your business processes and data, identify the areas to apply AI and the order to start, and then decide with you what to build.

Do we need in-house engineers or an IT department?

Not necessarily. Gomumari does the building. What we need from your team are the people on the ground who can teach us the work and your system administrators.

Is on-site presence required?

No. We adapt to your setup, for example by combining weekly site visits with remote work. We embed ourselves only until the system is in place.

What is your team structure?

The same person handles everything from consultation to delivery. Since the actual work is run by an organized team of AI agents, there is no fragmented relay of information. This team is something we run daily on our own business. All deliverables and operating procedures are documented to prevent dependence on individuals.

I am concerned about data handling.

After signing a non-disclosure agreement (NDA), any data you entrust to us is processed in environments that are not used for AI training. We also provide, as a set, the arrangements needed for internal approvals and audits, such as connection allowlists and operation logs. For details, see our Information Security Basic Policy.

Can we consult with you even if we have not yet decided to engage you?

Yes. Please contact us first. We will ask about your situation and propose processes where AI is likely to help and how to proceed. You can decide afterwards whether to move on to the Business Data Diagnosis (paid).
