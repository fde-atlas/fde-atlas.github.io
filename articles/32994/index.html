Forward deployed engineer vs customer success engineer

Kernavique · 2026-11-21 · 7 min read

Summary

Kernavique compared four same-employer pairs of US job ads for forward deployed engineers versus customer success (or customer) engineers, read on November 21, 2026, from 76 employer feeds. In all four pairs the forward deployed role paid more at both ends of the range, and all four forward deployed ads required production code, versus one of the four others. The customer success engineer title is rare (3 US postings found) and has no official US definition; GitLab's handbook is the only employer definition cited. The author stresses the sample is too small for firm conclusions.

Key points

  • Four hand-picked same-employer ad pairs, read on one day.
  • Forward deployed pay was higher at both ends in all 4 pairs.
  • All 4 FDE ads require production code; only 1 of the others does.
  • No official US definition exists for customer success engineer.
  • None of the 8 ads mentions a quota; equity is never valued.

How much evidence is there?

Not much, and this piece is short for that reason.

On November 21, 2026, we read the public job feeds of 76 employers, mostly AI and tech companies. The US postings by title were:

  • Customer Success Engineer: 3 postings at 3 employers (Deepgram, Together AI and Onebrief).
  • Customer Engineer: 2 postings at 1 employer (Anyscale).
  • Customer Success Manager: 54 postings at 15 employers. These were counted, not read.
  • Forward deployed, for scale: 298 postings at 65 employers.

All 3 customer success engineer employers also advertise a US forward deployed role, which gives 3 pairs. We added the Anyscale customer engineer pair and labeled it.

There is no official US definition of the role. Neither the Bureau of Labor Statistics page nor the O*NET page we read defines a customer success engineer.

How does an employer define the role?

We read one employer definition, GitLab’s handbook, last modified September 24, 2026.

It says the team “focuses on the themes of align, enable, and expand.” It files the role in its sales job library and sets levels from Associate to Staff, then Manager. It describes advising, onboarding and expansion. It does not describe writing production code for the customer.

GitLab is one employer and is not one of the four in the pairs below.

Do forward deployed engineers get paid more?

In these 4 pairs, yes, on the first range each ad gives.

  • Higher at both ends: 4 pairs. Onebrief is compared on its Senior tier.
  • Lower, the same, mixed or not comparable: none.

Kernavique reading of employers’ ads, November 21, 2026. A full bar is all 4 pairs.

Two results need care. Deepgram’s customer success ad carries a tier name with on-target earnings above its range for three cities, and the feed does not explain how the two relate. Together AI’s sentence calls the same figure base and on-target earnings, so the label is unclear. No ad values equity.

What do the requirement lines show?

Counts are from the 4 pairs, read November 21, 2026.

Coding. All 4 forward deployed ads ask for production code. Onebrief’s says “You will write production-grade code”. One of the 4 other ads asks for it, also Onebrief. Deepgram’s customer success ad says “You don’t need to be a software engineer”. Together AI’s lists Python or Bash as preferred.

Customers. Anyscale’s customer engineer ad asks for “Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple customer needs simultaneously”.

Travel. Two forward deployed ads state travel (Onebrief at 25–50%, Anyscale as needed), and Deepgram’s states on-site time. One of the other ads states travel (Onebrief, about 20%).

Quota. The word is in none of the 8 ads. On-target earnings or variable pay shows in 2 customer success ads (Deepgram and Together AI) and 1 forward deployed ad (Together AI).

Years. Both ads give a number in 3 pairs: equal in 2, and lower for the forward deployed ad at Anyscale.

Which should you choose?

This is our reading, and 4 pairs is too few to lean on. If you want to write production code, the forward deployed ads ask for it in 4 of 4. If you want customer work with less code, 3 of the 4 other ads do not ask for production code.

Which should you hire?

Our reading again. Open the forward deployed role when the customer needs code written. Open the customer success engineer role when the work is onboarding and expansion, as GitLab’s handbook frames it.

What are the limits of this comparison?

  • Four pairs, picked by hand, on one day. One is a customer engineer, not a customer success engineer.
  • Headings, bullet lines and keyword lines were read, not whole ads. “Not stated” means none of those lines said it.
  • The 54 Customer Success Manager ads were not read.

See also vs sales engineer, vs deployment strategist, vs solutions engineer and vs software engineer.

In practice

If you are an engineer. Ask each employer how much of the week is code. Browse open forward deployed engineer jobs, or send us your details and résumé.

If you are hiring. Say in the ad whether the holder writes production code. If you want help defining the role, you can ask us about hiring forward deployed engineers.

Questions

  • What is the difference between a forward deployed engineer and a customer success engineer?
  • In the 4 pairs of ads read on November 21, 2026, the forward deployed ads ask for production code and the other ads mostly do not. All 4 forward deployed ads ask for it, against 1 of the 4 customer success or customer engineer ads. Four pairs is a small base, so treat this as a pattern in these ads only.
  • Do forward deployed engineers get paid more than customer success engineers?
  • In these 4 pairs, yes, on the first range each ad gives, read November 21, 2026. The gap at the bottom of the range runs from $8K at Deepgram to $80K at Anyscale. Deepgram’s customer success ad also names higher on-target earnings for three cities, and no ad puts a value on equity.
  • Is there an official definition of a customer success engineer?
  • We found none. Neither the Bureau of Labor Statistics page nor the O*NET page read on November 21, 2026 defines the role. The one employer definition read is GitLab’s handbook, last modified September 24, 2026, which files the role in its sales job library.
  • How common is the customer success engineer title?
  • Rare on the boards we read. On November 21, 2026, the 76 employer feeds held 3 US postings titled Customer Success Engineer, 2 titled Customer Engineer and 54 titled Customer Success Manager. The same feeds held 298 US postings titled forward deployed.
  • Do customer success engineers carry a quota?
  • The word “quota” is in none of the 8 ads in these pairs, read November 21, 2026. Two of the 4 customer success ads show on-target earnings or variable pay in their pay figure (Deepgram and Together AI), as does 1 forward deployed ad (Together AI).
