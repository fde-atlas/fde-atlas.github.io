What is a Salesforce FDE? | Salary Range and 95 Official Job Postings: A Look at Tokyo Job Requirements

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-10-23 · 23 min read

Summary

Based on Salesforce's official blog, newsroom, and 95 FDE-related job postings scraped from its careers site on October 23, 2026, this article details the company's Forward Deployed Engineer team: its size, division of labor with Deployment Strategists, salaries, and Tokyo requirements. The team launched in April 2025 and tripled within six months. FDEs pair with Deployment Strategists on engagements typically capped at 12 weeks. US base pay for FDEs ranges from $88,970 to $287,910, and Tokyo roles require 5+ years of experience and Japanese-English fluency, with Salesforce experience not mandatory.

Key points

  • FDE team started April 2025 and tripled in six months, staffed from three internal departments.
  • As of October 2026: 95 FDE-related openings across 29 countries, 52 in Europe, only 2 in Tokyo.
  • US FDE base salary: $88,970–$287,910; up to $316,750 in higher-paying regions.
  • Deployment Strategist roles mostly require Salesforce product knowledge; FDE roles usually list it as preferred.
  • Tokyo FDE posting asks for 5+ years and bilingual fluency; OpenWork listing shows ¥8M–¥30M salary.

SalesforceのFDE(Forward Deployed Engineer/フォワードデプロイドエンジニア)とは、同社のAIエージェント基盤「Agentforce」を、顧客の現場に入って本番稼働まで持っていくエンジニアのことです。同社は2025年4月にFDEのチームを立ち上げ[1]、2026年3月には「半年でFDEチームを3倍にした」と公表しました[2]。10月19日には、スペインに置くAI導入チームにFDEを集めると発表しています[6]。一方で、10月22日のThe Registerは、ベンダー各社がFDEにAI投資の回収を託していると報じ、その例にSalesforceを挙げました[8]。

Based on six official Salesforce blog posts, newsroom articles, and press releases, plus 95 FDE-related job postings (including US salary ranges and 2 Tokyo postings) that the editorial team retrieved from the company's official careers site on October 23, 2026, this article organizes who Salesforce FDEs work with, what they do, where they are recruited, at what pay, and what is required of them. For a general overview of the FDE role, see What Is an FDE?; for a comparison of how companies build FDE organizations, see How to Build an FDE Organization.

What Is a Salesforce FDE?

SalesforceのFDEは、顧客の業務課題をAgentforceのエージェントに落とし込み、設計・構築・導入までを自分の手で行うエンジニアです。公式ブログによれば、チームは2025年4月に発足し、多くのFDEは1人で顧客を担当する一方、Deployment Strategist(導入戦略の担当)1人とFDE2人のポッドで1社に約3か月専念する形も始まっています(2025年11月時点)[1]。同じ記事は、Salesforceが1,000人のFDEチームを作ると約束していると書いています[1]。

The "33%" from the earnings call and what came after

2025年12月3日の決算説明会で、Salesforceの経営陣は、FDEを活用する顧客は導入が33%速いと述べました。第三者の書き起こしでは、この発言はRobin Washington氏(President & Chief Operating and Financial Officer)の冒頭の説明にあります[9]。The Registerは同じ数字をMarc Benioff CEOの発言として報じていますが[8]、編集部が書き起こしを確認した範囲では、Benioff氏の発言にFDEや33%への言及は見当たりませんでした。Salesforceの投資家向けサイトの公式の書き起こしは、編集部の環境からは開けませんでした。

一方、2026年8月26日の決算説明会で、Benioff氏は、新しいAIの機能を見せた顧客について「大勢のFDEを雇わなくても動かせていた」と語っています[10]。編集部の見方では、FDEを増やす一方で、製品がFDEの手間を減らすことも売りにしており、FDEは「製品が追いつくまでの橋渡し」という位置づけにも読めます。

How do FDEs and Deployment Strategists work together?

求人票では、FDEは「Technical Builder」、Deployment Strategistは「Strategic Builder」と呼ばれ、2つの職種が1つのデリバリーチームを組むと書かれています[13][14]。Deployment Strategistの求人は、案件を「通常12週間以内」の期間を区切ったものとし、1社の戦略顧客を担当する場合も複数の顧客を同時に担当する場合もあるとしています[14]。編集部が数えた範囲では、Deployment Strategistの27件すべてに「12週間以内」の記載がありました。

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat is a Salesforce FDE?

An engineer who works on-site with customers to design, build, and deploy Salesforce's agent platform, Agentforce, through to production. The team was formed in April 2025, and in some cases works alongside a Deployment Strategist (responsible for deployment strategy), dedicating about three months to a single company. The role also includes feeding insights gained in the field back to the product teams.

QHow much does a Salesforce FDE earn?

According to US job postings as of October 23, 2026, the base salary for an FDE (Mid/Senior Level) was $88,970–$287,910 per year, and $97,860–$316,750 in states such as California and New York. Bonuses and stock are not included. The official Japanese recruiting site does not list salaries, but the Tokyo FDE posting the company placed on OpenWork listed an annual salary of 8 million to 30 million yen.

QDo you need Salesforce experience to apply for a Salesforce FDE role?

For most FDE postings, it is not required. The Tokyo FDE posting explicitly states that "Salesforce experience is preferred but not required," and standard postings in the US and Europe also list Salesforce experience and certifications as preferred qualifications. However, some postings, such as FDEs for Data 360, list Salesforce certifications as a requirement. Meanwhile, most Deployment Strategist postings require an understanding of Salesforce products.

QWhat is the difference between a Salesforce FDE and a Deployment Strategist?

An FDE is the technical lead responsible for agent design, implementation, and data integration, while a Deployment Strategist is the customer-side driver responsible for shaping AI strategy, managing project progress, and tracking usage metrics. Job postings state that the two roles form a single team, and that Deployment Strategist engagements typically last within 12 weeks.

QIs Salesforce hiring FDEs in Japan?

As of October 23, 2026, two positions were listed on the official recruiting site: Forward Deployed Engineer (Multiple Levels) and Deployment Strategist (Multiple Levels), both based in Tokyo. Both were posted starting August 5, 2026.
