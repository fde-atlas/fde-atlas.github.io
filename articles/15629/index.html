Industrial AI & Forward-Deployed Engineering

Fidelis Associates · 2026 · 5 min read

Summary

Fidelis Associates describes its forward-deployed engineering model for industrial AI: engineers embed on-site with plant teams, build prototypes from real operational data in days or weeks, and stay accountable until operators actually use the tools daily. The article contrasts this with consultants who only deliver reports and vendors who install generic platforms. Example outcomes include cutting a 10-week P&ID review cycle to 3 days, AI-assisted failure analysis, RAG-based knowledge systems, and predictive maintenance models trained on 2+ years of sensor data that flag failures 2-4 weeks ahead. Fidelis targets oil & gas, chemicals, power, and general industrial facilities, emphasizing human-in-the-loop design and knowledge transfer to client teams.

Key points

  • FDEs embed on-site and build tools from real, messy operational data.
  • Success is defined by daily operator adoption, not model completion.
  • One deployment cut P&ID review from 10 weeks to 3 days.
  • Predictive maintenance models warn of failures 2-4 weeks in advance.
  • Solutions use human-in-the-loop design and include knowledge transfer.

Forward-Deployed Engineering (FDE) is an approach where AI engineers embed directly into client operations — working alongside plant operators, maintenance teams, and engineers — to build and deploy AI tools using real operational data. Unlike traditional consulting or vendor implementations, FDE produces custom tools adapted to your specific equipment, workflows, and safety requirements.

We cut through the noise and extract value from AI systems that integrate with (not replace) your processes, delivering practical and measurable results.

Kelli Whiteneck

Vice President

Traditional consultants hand over reports, and vendors install tools then disappear. Our FDE model embeds engineers directly into daily operations. We work beside your teams, use your systems, and build solutions that are adopted, trusted, and used — not shelved.

What Forward-Deployed Engineers do

We live in your world

FDEs operate on-site, inside your live systems and processes. We learn equipment behavior, workflows, and real operational nuance — including tribal knowledge, undocumented rules, and failure patterns that only appear in real use.

We speak both languages

We translate between advanced AI capability and practical operations. Model architectures and computer-vision pipelines become outcomes like extracting thousands of P&IDs or predicting failures early, while operator insights become structured, analyzable data.

We build fast

Instead of presentations or theoretical documentation, we rapidly develop working prototypes using your real environment and noisy data. Your team sees tangible value early, and we refine tools on-site until they align with workflows and operational realities.

We own the outcome

An FDE engagement is successful when operators, engineers, and supervisors rely on the tool daily — not when a model is built. We stay engaged until the solution works in the real world: with messy data, aging assets, and inconsistent documentation.

Why this model works

The gap between AI technology and plant reality is massive. Traditional approaches fail because IT teams don’t understand operations, consultants don’t build, and vendors can’t adapt their platforms to your unique environment. The forward-deployed approach closes that gap by combining:

  • Deep technical capability in machine learning, NLP, and computer vision
  • Operational understanding of process safety, reliability, and industrial workflows
  • Rapid prototyping that delivers working tools in weeks, not months
  • Hands-on implementation embedded in your team and your systems
  • A mission-first mindset focused on measurable operational outcomes

This is why companies like Palantir pioneered the model, and why it’s the most effective approach for industrial AI today.

How Fidelis deploys this capability

Embedded Problem Discovery

We work directly with operators, SMEs, and engineering teams to identify meaningful use cases and locate hidden data sources. P&IDs, logs, inspection reports, maintenance histories, and tribal knowledge are analyzed to define practical, high-value starting points.

Rapid Prototyping & Testing

Using real data, we build and validate solutions quickly. Whether document intelligence, computer vision, predictive tools, or reasoning engines, prototypes evolve in days — not months — showing value early and grounding development in operational reality.

Human-in-the-Loop Deployment

Every solution is designed to support, not replace, experts. Confidence scoring, review capability, override options, and transparent reasoning ensure trust, safety, and alignment with regulatory and engineering expectations in critical environments.

Iterate, Refine, & Scale

Once the first deployment proves measurable value, we expand capabilities across equipment, systems, or facilities. Each rollout is adapted to standards, constraints, and context — ensuring reliability and seamless integration across the organization.

Example outcomes from the Forward-Deployed model

P&ID Analysis Acceleration

Manual review of engineering drawings can take weeks or months. Our AI-driven P&ID processing tools automate extraction and validation. One deployment reduced a 10-week manual review cycle to 3 days while improving accuracy, consistency, and documentation quality.

AI-Assisted Failure Analysis

Forward-deployed tools structure investigation workflows and surface hidden patterns in equipment failure data. Engineers spend less time gathering data and more time solving problems — transforming root-cause analysis from a multi-week effort into a faster, more consistent process.

Domain-Specific Knowledge Systems

RAG-based knowledge engines reason across standards, manuals, inspection reports, and technical publications. Unstructured content becomes accessible expertise, enabling teams to find answers in seconds instead of searching through disorganized or siloed information.

Predictive Maintenance Models

ML models trained on 2+ years of sensor data predict equipment failures 2-4 weeks before occurrence. Maintenance teams shift from reactive to proactive intervention, reducing emergency repair costs and extending asset service life.
