AI Consulting, Custom Development, or FDE: How to Choose

Original title: AI 咨询、定制开发与 FDE 怎么选

Onyx Devs Lab · 2026-09-07 · 2 min read

Summary

Onyx Devs Lab compares AI consulting, custom development, and FDEs as three service models suited to different problem states. Consulting resolves decision uncertainty about what to build and whether to invest; custom development fits problems whose users, processes, interfaces, and acceptance criteria can be defined upfront; FDEs diagnose and deliver on-site when the right solution must be learned from real operations. A comparison table maps each model's core uncertainty, deliverables, acceptance evidence, and transition conditions. The article advises choosing based on which uncertainty you need to eliminate and what evidence the project must produce, not on service labels, and notes the models can hand off to each other.

Key points

  • Consulting resolves uncertainty about what to do and whether to invest
  • Custom development suits problems with definable specs and acceptance criteria
  • FDEs fit high-value problems whose solution boundaries must be learned on-site
  • If field evidence overturns specs, development should shift to an FDE loop
  • Choose services by the uncertainty to eliminate, not by appealing names

Full text

Choosing AI consulting

Leadership needs to determine the opportunity, feasibility, investment, and governance boundaries.

Each is suited to a different problem state.

Users, processes, interfaces, and acceptance criteria can be defined before implementation.

The problem is valuable, but the right solution must be learned from real operations.

When success criteria and delivery methods don't match, problems appear quickly.

If no one is responsible for choosing and moving to the next stage, the consulting engagement has no closed loop.

When operators see the software, core requirements keep changing, so fixed specifications fail.

Without business participation, data access, and an owner for results, embedded delivery cannot run.

Choose based on the uncertainty you need to eliminate and the evidence the project must produce, not on the most attractive service name.

Clarify what is to be decided or changed, and who is responsible.

Assess value, process, data, integration, and adoption risks separately.

Buy only enough scope to resolve the key uncertainty.

Define the evidence needed to expand, adjust, or stop.
