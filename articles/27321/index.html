What forward deployed engineers earn in India in 2026, from AmbitionBox, Levels.fyi, 6figr and a posted job, with sample sizes and why the numbers differ.

Quick answer: Published figures for forward deployed engineer (FDE) salaries in India disagree because the title is new and most samples are small. On 17 October 2026, AmbitionBox showed a typical range of ₹19.9 to ₹23.6 lakh a year for 0 to 7 years of experience (149 reported salaries), Levels.fyi showed a median total compensation of about ₹17.5 lakh, and 6figr showed an average of ₹16 lakh from just 3 profiles. A Y Combinator-listed Indian posting offered ₹10 to ₹13 lakh for 1+ years of experience. Higher claims such as ₹35 to ₹55 lakh circulate online, but we could not trace them to a method.

Key takeaways

  • There is no single FDE salary in India yet. The sources we checked give central figures between about ₹10 lakh and ₹24 lakh a year, with a long tail of reports above ₹35 lakh.
  • Sample size matters more than the headline number. The largest source we found has 149 reports. Two others have a handful of profiles or do not show their sample at all.
  • Estimates are not reports. AmbitionBox labels some company figures as its own estimates, and those are not salaries employees reported.
  • Employer type changes the answer more than the job title. IT services firms, product companies and AI-first startups appear in different parts of the range.
  • Treat claims of "crores" or "₹35 to ₹55 LPA for freshers" as unverified until you can see the sample, the date and whether the figure is base pay or total compensation.

Why the numbers disagree

The title is new and used loosely. The same work appears as Forward Deployed Engineer, Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, Solutions Engineer or Implementation Engineer. A salary page for one title may contain mostly roles that are closer to another. Our comparison of FDE, solutions engineer and AI engineer roles explains where the lines fall.

Samples are small. 6figr's average comes from 3 profiles. AmbitionBox's company-level figures, such as ₹36.7 to ₹40.6 lakh at Cornerstone OnDemand, rest on 4 reported salaries. One extra high or low report moves these numbers a lot.

Estimates are mixed in with reports. AmbitionBox marks some company figures "AmbitionBox Est." For example, it shows an estimated ₹42.8 to ₹47.3 lakh at SarvM.AI. That is a model's guess, not what employees told AmbitionBox they earn.

Pay is defined differently. Some sites show base pay, others total compensation including bonus and stock. Levels.fyi reports total compensation. A "₹35 LPA" claim might be base or total, and might be a remote role paid by a foreign employer.

Employer type matters. In the AmbitionBox company list, reported ranges differ widely between employers, from ₹11.4 to ₹16.7 lakh at Bluecore Software (6 salaries) to ₹26.9 to ₹29.8 lakh at Splunk (3 salaries).

Claims you will see, and how to test them

Search results for this keyword include a LinkedIn post headlined "Forward Deployed Engineer salary in India ₹35-₹55 LPA" and an Instagram reel saying FDEs are "being paid in crores in India". We could not trace either to a published method or sample, so we do not repeat the figures as fact. Ask five questions of any salary claim:

  • How many people is this based on? Fewer than 20 is a story, not a statistic.
  • Is it reported or estimated? Employees reporting their own pay is different from a model's guess.
  • Is it base pay or total compensation? Bonus and stock can add or remove lakhs.
  • Is the employer Indian or foreign? Remote roles paid in dollars sit in a different market.
  • Is the job really an FDE role? Check the job description for customer-facing delivery, not just the title.

What moves FDE pay

This section is our editorial view, not measured data. It draws on what the sources above and the job descriptions we read suggest.

  • Years of shipped production work. AmbitionBox's headline range covers 0 to 7 years of experience, and the two individual reports it showed with pay disclosed (1 and 3 years) were ₹12.5 and ₹14.2 lakh. Showing real deployments matters more than the title.
  • Employer type. The same title pays differently at an IT services firm, a product company and a funded AI startup, as the table above shows.
  • Technical range. The Peakflo posting asks for Python, SQL, machine learning, LLM work, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and agent workflows. AmbitionBox's skills list for the title includes Python, SQL, DevOps, Docker, cloud and generative AI. Our guide to the skills an FDE needs covers the full list, and our MCP server tutorial shows one deployable skill in practice.
  • Customer-facing ability. The role exists because someone has to make AI work inside a customer's environment. Clear communication and judgement are part of what employers are paying for.
  • Domain knowledge. Finance, healthcare, logistics or manufacturing experience makes you more useful to a customer who works in that domain.

How to estimate your own number

  • Pick your experience band: 0 to 2, 3 to 5 or 6+ years.
  • Pick your target employer type: services, product or AI-first startup.
  • Open three sources and write down the sample size next to each number. Discard any with fewer than about 10 reports unless you only need a rough sense.
  • Check adjacent titles, such as Applied AI Engineer and Solutions Engineer, at the same company, then compare with software engineer pay there.
  • Ask the recruiter for the pay band before you interview. Many postings leave it out, and asking early saves everyone time.

What about US and global figures?

US numbers are higher and are not comparable with Indian rupee figures. In September 2026 we noted that Anthropic's live Forward Deployed Engineer posting listed $280,000 to $320,000 for its US locations, an employer-published range (see our FDE career guide). Postings change, so check the live listing. A US range does not predict what an Indian-based role pays.

Do forward deployed engineers earn more than software engineers in India?
