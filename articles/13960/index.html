FDE variants across companies ​

Original title: 各公司的 FDE 变体 ​

ai-alchemy-lab.github.io · 2026-09-06 · 11 min read

Summary

This wiki page compiles a comparison of forward deployed engineering roles across a dozen companies, based on public job board APIs (Lever, Greenhouse, Ashby) read on 2026-09-06. It borrows Palantir's internal codenames to classify roles: Delta positions (code-delivering, e.g. FDSE) and Echo positions (SOW, stakeholder and value-measurement owners, e.g. Deployment Strategist). It documents naming variants at OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, Scale AI, Cohere, Sierra, Decagon, Cognition and Harvey, with salary bands where available, and notes Databricks explicitly bills FDEs unlike Palantir's R&D model. A section on China covers five competing Chinese translations, companies avoiding the FDE label, and traps like unrelated test-engineering roles and outsourced staffing.

Key points

  • Palantir is the prototype: FDSE base $135K–$200K, Deployment Strategist $110K–$170K, with code-reflow and talent-rotation pipelines.
  • OpenAI names a team Forward Deployed Engineering; FDE base $185K–$300K, and it launched a separate Deployment Company in May 2026.
  • Databricks merged Professional Services into its FDE organization in June 2026 and explicitly marks FDEs as billable.
  • Harvey uses Legal Engineer instead of FDE, showing deep verticals replace the generic title.
  • Chinese employers avoid the FDE label; beware same-name test roles and disguised outsourcing positions.

II. Palantir: The Prototype ​

Organizational structure ​

Two pipelines are essential to this model, and neither can be missing: the code feedback pipeline (Delta can write code for the core product, but must coordinate with the product team, align major features with the roadmap first, and undergo continuous review during development) and the talent rotation pipeline (frequent movement between Dev and Delta, with the company encouraging perspective shifts). (Dev versus Delta, 2019-04-08)

Job families (official careers page, 2026-09-06) ​

Palantir's Forward Deployed family is no longer just the FDSE role; publicly posted variants include: Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Forward Deployed AI Engineer, Forward Deployed Infrastructure Engineer, Forward Deployed Security Engineer, Forward Deployed Site Reliability Engineer, Forward Deployed Reliability Engineer, Forward Deployed Enablement Engineer (Customer Success), and Forward Deployed Engineer - Mixed Reality. The Deployment Strategist family is split by customer line (US Government, UK Government, Intel, Warp Speed, Japan/Korea Forward Deployed, etc.), and includes internship and new grad positions (Deployment Strategist, Internship / New Grad).

This means two things: first, Palantir treats the "front line" as a department where job types can be subdivided, rather than a single role; second, DS has a new grad entry point, while FDSE requires passing a full software engineer interview loop (see hiring-bar.md).

Compensation (per official JD wording) ​

  • FDSE (US Government line): "The estimated salary range for this position is estimated to be $135,000 - $200,000/year. Total compensation for this position may also include Restricted Stock units, sign-on bonus and other potential future incentives."
  • Deployment Strategist (US Government line): $110,000 - $170,000/year + RSU + sign-on bonus.

III. OpenAI: Turning the "Front Line" into a Team Name ​

In OpenAI's recruiting API, the team field literally reads

Forward Deployed Engineering

—this is the company's hardest organizational evidence on the matter. The team's self-description (original JD text):

"OpenAI's Forward Deployed Engineering team partners with customers to turn research breakthroughs into production systems. We operate at the intersection of customer delivery and core platform development."

Three job tiers (all part of the Forward Deployed Engineering team):

RoleKey JD quoteYears of experiencebase + equity
Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)"own discovery, technical scoping, system design, build, and production rollout"; "measure success through production adoption, measurable workflow impact, and eval-driven feedback that changes product and model roadmaps"5+ years$185K–$300K
Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSWE)"design abstractions to solve customer problems, and then use them to scale our speed and quality of delivery across all Forward Deployed engagements"7+ years$185K–$325K
Technical Deployment LeadSet by region (Tokyo, Singapore, Semiconductors lines)—Not found in this API pull

Two notable details, both written in the JDs: hybrid work with 3 days per week in office, and travel up to 50%. There are also industry-specific lines: Healthcare (SF/NYC/Seattle), Legal (NYC/SF), Semiconductors, and OpenAI for Gov.

At the organizational level, OpenAI separately established the OpenAI Deployment Company in May 2026: led by TPG, with Advent, Bain Capital, and Brookfield as co-lead founding partners, and a total of 19 investment firms, consultancies, and system integrators participating; it also acquired Tomoro in London (about 150 deployment engineers). (OpenAI official announcement)

⚠️ The claim circulating in Chinese-language circles that "OpenAI's FDE team grew from 2 to 52 people" lacks a primary source; this library has not been able to verify it, so please flag it as secondhand when citing.

IV. Anthropic: Applied AI is the parent body, and FDE is one branch within it ​

Anthropic has long used the name Applied AI Engineer for the same thing, and starting in 2026 it added the Forward Deployed Engineer title under the same Applied AI team. The original FDE job description reads:

"As a member of the Applied AI team at Anthropic, you will be a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) who embeds directly with our most strategic customers to drive transformational AI adoption… You will sit at the frontier of enterprise AI deployments and serve as one of our founding FDEs who helps to shape our forward-deployed motion."

Among the responsibilities is a very specific list of deliverables that no other company's JD contains:

"Deliver technical artifacts for customers like MCP servers, sub-agents, and agent skills that will be used in production workflows."

Anthropic's Echo is called Technical Deployment Lead, part of the Claude Agentic Solutions team, and its JD spells out the division of labor more clearly than any other company:

"You'll own engagements end-to-end, from SOW through production deployment. You'll work alongside Forward Deployed Engineers who build the technical solution, while you own product scoping, stakeholder management, value measurement, and the organizational complexity that comes with deploying AI agents in enterprise environments."

Compensation (from the official JDs): FDE $280,000–$320,000; Technical Deployment Lead $275,000–$380,000.

In addition, Anthropic has a larger family of Applied AI Architect roles (segmented by industry and customer group: Commercial, Cyber, Digital Natives, Enterprise Tech, Industries, Partnerships, Public Sector, Startups), as well as Manager-level roles (Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering / Manager, Applied AI Architecture / Manager, Technical Deployment).

V. Databricks: Transforming Professional Services wholesale into an FDE organization ​

Databricks' official blog post on June 11, 2026 announced that Professional Services would be merged wholesale into the Forward Deployed Engineering organization, disclosing that it had served 1,900+ customers within 12 months, with named cases including JPMorgan Chase (migrating 5+ PB and 500+ notebooks in 4 months and training 600+ users). (Databricks blog, 2026-06-11)

Its organizational language is worth noting separately: Field Engineering is the large department (including Solutions Architect, Specialist SA, and industry Managers), and FDE is one product line within it. The job leveling is the most complete in this sample:

, with a horizontal split by industry (Financial Services, Healthcare & Life Sciences, Manufacturing, Retail, CMEG, Public Sector, National Security) and function (Full Stack, Architect); the management track is

Manager / Sr. Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering

, plus

Engagement Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering

as the Echo position.

The most important sentence in a JD, because it is the exact opposite of Palantir's economic model:

"FDEs are billable and know how to complete projects according to specification with exceptional customer empathy."

The Palantir-style FDE treats deployment as an R&D investment, while Databricks explicitly states that FDEs are billable. This directly determines that your day-to-day pressure at these two companies comes from different sources—at one you watch customer metrics, at the other you also have to watch utilization.

Compensation: Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer, New York base $182,000–$250,208 (all four salary zones for this role list the same range).

VI. Sierra, Decagon, Cognition, Harvey: four ways of renaming ​

These four companies best illustrate "same function, different naming."

Sierra (Bret Taylor) — calls it Agent Engineering and fully replicates the Echo/Delta structure. Within its engineering division there is a sub-division called Agent Engineering (29 open roles), with job titles such as

Software Engineer, Agent -「行业名」

(Financial Services, Healthcare, Insurance, Public Sector, Retail, TMT, Travel & Hospitality) —

按行业切，不按技术栈切

which is itself an organizational hallmark of the FDE. Under the product lines there is also a separate department

Agent Strategist

(22 open positions), functionally corresponding to Echo. In addition there are

Deployed Infrastructure Engineer

(the London role even has a Spanish-language dedicated position).

Natalie Meurer at Sierra, who leads agent engineering for the financial and TMT industries (ex-Palantir, 2015–2021), explains that what the industry now calls agent engineering "is just forward deployed engineering with a new name," and that "the boundaries are blurring in both directions — product engineering is also becoming more customer-facing." (AI Engineer World's Fair 2026 talk)

Decagon — calls it Agent Deployment Engineering, already an independent unit with a Director. Positions include Agent Deployment Engineer (San Francisco, New York, Toronto, London, with dedicated German, French, and Spanish roles), Director of Agent Deployment Engineering, plus a large batch of Solutions Engineer / Solutions Architect (Infrastructure, Salesforce directions) and Field Engineer roles.

Cognition (Devin / Windsurf) — calls it Deployed Engineer, and invented a channel role. Besides regionally distributed Deployed Engineers (Europe / APAC / ANZ / LATAM / Federal / South Korea), it also has a Partner Deployed Engineer — a technical expert facing global systems integrators (GSIs), whose JD reads verbatim: "enable services partners such as Global Systems Integrators to successfully deploy, scale, and deliver value with Devin and Windsurf across their client base." This is the clearest example of running a delivery organization as a channel. The Echo seat is called Applied AI Transformation Manager.

Harvey (legal AI) — doesn't use the term FDE at all; it's called Legal Engineer. As of 2026-09-06 there are 33 Legal Engineer family positions, segmented by customer type: Law Firm Corporate, Law Firm Litigation/Regulatory, In-House, Product Specialist, plus a Custom Solutions direction and a full management track (Manager / Director / Head of Legal Engineering). Harvey's approach is the most important counterexample on this page: when a vertical domain is deep enough, companies name the role with the domain word rather than FDE — it hires engineers who understand law, not generalists who understand engineering.

VII. What Chinese companies call it ​

The Chinese hiring market's naming for this role is even messier than the English-speaking world's, and clients are deliberately avoiding the three letters "FDE."

7.1 A record of translation chaos ​

For the same Forward Deployed Engineer, at least five Chinese translations are in parallel use:

Chinese translationExamples of users
前线部署工程师Zhidx, most tech self-media, Fan Bing's handbook
前沿部署工程师Fuzhou Gulou Guotou, Gaodun Education, and a batch of client-side JDs such as Sibote/Noteland
前置部署工程师Some Chinese-language LinkedIn content
前端部署工程师Cailian Press report of 2026-06-05 (this is a mistranslation, easily confused with front-end development)
驻场 / 交付工程师Carried over by traditional IT service providers

This repository uniformly uses 前线部署工程师, but preserves the original party's wording when quoting.

7.2 The six most common job titles for in-house roles at clients ​

The names Chinese clients give to in-house FDE roles mostly do not carry the three letters FDE:

  • AI 应用工程师 / 智能应用工程师 (Shanghai Lianjia's "Intelligent Application Engineer (FDE)", China Europe Fund, Taikang Online, Guilin Pharmaceutical)
  • AI 赋能工程师 / 数字化 AI 赋能工程师 (bodor Bond Laser, Wanchen Group)
  • AI 解决方案工程师 (East China Architectural Design Group, IRIS OHYAMA, Aohai Technology)
  • AI 落地专家 / AI 应用专员 (Yinxing Intelligence, Xige Industrial, Weikang Medical)
  • AI 转型 / 流程变革工程师 (Midea's "Process Transformation Senior Engineer - AI", XPeng Motors, Canaan Technology)
  • AI Captain / AI Business Partner / Internal FDE (a position released via headhunters by a Shanghai cloud computing company of roughly ten thousand employees, whose JD carries a full set of organizational language: Innovation Office / AI Enablement Team / AI Champion / Agent Demo Day / 30-60-90 day goals)

"FDE development track" has become a standard job-title suffix: Minmetals Securities' "Product Manager (FDE Engineer Development Track)", Zhongda Consulting's "AI Delivery Engineer (FDE Development Track)", Shanghai Shendu Zhilian's "Real Estate AI Data Knowledge Expert (FDE Track Development)". Both clients and vendors are cultivating their own.

7.3 Chinese companies that have institutionalized and directly copied Echo/Delta ​

  • AsiaInfo Technologies (01675.HK): recruiting FDE Echo roles in parallel across four industry lines — manufacturing, supermarket retail, insurance & finance, and transportation & logistics — with the JD explicitly stating "collaborating with the internal Delta engineer team". This is currently the most complete public evidence in the Chinese market of replicating Palantir's dual-role structure.
  • Shanghai Shendu Zhilian (CRIC / E-House group): claims to have formed "China's first real estate FDE team" in July 2026.
  • Shanghai Kupasi Technology: the job title directly reads "Marketing Business Role (FDE Team - Culture, Education & Health direction)", showing FDE has become the name of an organizational unit.

7.4 FDE roles for external delivery at big tech companies ​

  • Meituan "CatPaw FDE Engineer": The responsibilities include "building eval sets and scoring criteria for each delivered capability," "tracking core metrics such as adoption rate, task success rate, and human intervention rate," and "distilling common requirements and failure modes discovered at customer sites into reusable components, templates, and best practices, feeding them back into CatPaw" — the feedback loop is written into the JD.
  • DiDi "Forward Deployed (FDE) Expert": "Consolidate on-site customer solutions into reusable assets and delivery methodologies, feed frontline insights back to the product R&D team, and influence the product and technology roadmap."
  • ByteDance "FDE Expert", Tencent "AI Forward Deployed Engineer · FDE" (including a 2027 campus recruiting position, Shenzhen), Shopee "Class of 27 Campus Recruiting Forward Deployed Engineer" — major companies have already put this role into their campus recruiting pipelines.
  • Lumi United Aqara "FDE Expert": The deliverables are defined most sharply — "abstract single-project experience into standardized, replicable solution packages (including SOPs, reference architectures, configuration templates, and best practices)... the deliverable is not the 'project' itself, but industry scenario solutions that 'the product can satisfy and the ecosystem can deliver'."

7.5 Two unique features of the China variant ​

  • The tech stack is dual-track. Top companies use Claude Code / Cursor / MCP / LangGraph (Shanghai Lianjia's skill tags literally say "Claude Code/Cursor," and TCL says "Spec Coding, Vibe Coding"), while mid-tier companies use Feishu + Dify + Coze. Joyoung Soy Milk's JD even lists "Feishu Efficiency Pioneer certification" as a plus.
  • "Internal coaching" is part of the job. See section 5 of what-is-an-fde.md for details.

7.6 ⚠️ Two pitfalls in Chinese job titles ​

  • Same-name pitfall: In the smartphone ODM / consumer electronics / semiconductor industries, "FDE" refers to a long-existing testing role (Goertek, iSoftStone, ThunderSoft, Longcheer, Xiaomi, etc.), unrelated to AI, with salaries of 6–22K/month.
  • Outsourcing pitfall: A batch of positions carry the FDE title but are actually staff augmentation; the tell is that the hiring entity is a human resources services company.
