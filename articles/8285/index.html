Summary

Onyx Devs Lab explains forward deployed engineering (FDE): engineers work inside a client's real operations, linking diagnosis, implementation and validation in one evidence loop. The article argues that when requirements are vague and data, legacy systems and human decisions interact, success should be measured by operational metrics rather than a frozen feature list. It outlines a workflow—establish a baseline, deploy a minimal intervention, measure and iterate—and describes when FDE fits and how long a first cycle typically takes.

Key points

  • FDEs work on-site with domain operators, owning end-to-end delivery.
  • Success is defined by operational outcomes, not feature checklists.
  • Workflow: baseline, bounded intervention, measure, iterate or stop.
  • FDE suits valuable problems that resist upfront specification.
  • First cycle spans diagnosis, intervention and operational evaluation time.

Forward deployed engineering (FDE) puts requirements discovery, software engineering, deployment, and outcome validation in the same loop, making it a good fit for complex operational problems that cannot be fully defined before a project begins.

On the ground working alongside domain operators

End-to-end accountable from diagnosis to actual deployment

Evidence-driven baselines, interventions, and outcome validation

When FDE is a good fit

When the team needs to learn from actual operations, and the problem itself changes as evidence emerges.

Requirements are still vague

Users can describe pain points, but don't yet know the right system intervention.

The environment is complex

Data, policies, legacy systems, and human decision-making interact in ways that requirements documents alone cannot capture.

Outcome accountability is needed

Success must show up in operational metrics, not just in completed software features.

What makes FDE different

FDE connects consulting, specification, engineering, and adoption through a single evidence loop.

Compared to consulting

The team that diagnoses the problem also implements and tests the intervention.

Compared to outsourcing

The unit of success is the operational outcome, not the completion of a frozen feature list.

Compared to staff augmentation

The team owns a specific problem and its validation loop, rather than providing generic headcount.

Delivery approach

Outcome validation

Measure results, review failure modes, and iterate or stop based on evidence.

FAQ

What is FDE?

Here, FDE refers to Forward Deployed Engineering, where engineers work deep inside a customer's real operating environment, connecting requirements discovery, implementation, and validation.

Is FDE only for large enterprises?

No. It can be a good fit whenever the problem is valuable enough but hard to define in advance, and decision-makers and operators can participate in the evidence loop.

How long does an FDE project usually take?

It depends on the validation cycles of the process and outcomes. The first loop typically includes diagnosis, a bounded intervention, and enough operational time to evaluate the agreed metrics.
