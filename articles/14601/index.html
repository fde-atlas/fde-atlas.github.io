Thomson Reuters Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-06 · 16 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot catalogs 8 Thomson Reuters FDE-family postings on Workday: 5 engineer seats across CoCounsel, Legal Products, and Tax and Audit, plus 3 management seats. All are Hybrid, none remote, and every seat posts a USD base range; engineer seats span $109,000 to $340,000. A November 8 live check found 6 of 8 still open. The article details per-seat requirements: Python, RAG, and orchestration frameworks for software seats; JD/LLB plus legal practice for the Legal Engineer; CPA/EA/CIA credentials for the tax seat; and 12+ years for the Principal CoCounsel role.

Key points

  • 8 postings total: 5 engineer and 3 management, ranked 16th among 114 employers.
  • Engineer base pay spans $109,000 to $340,000; all seats list USD ranges.
  • All 8 seats are Hybrid; 0 of 8 are remote under the site's method.
  • Software seats require 5+ years plus 2+ years of production AI/ML or LLM work.
  • Domain seats demand credentials: JD/LLB for legal, CPA/EA/CIA for tax and audit.

What Thomson Reuters calls the role

Thomson Reuters posts a legal AI CoCounsel mix, a Legal Products Legal Engineer seat, and Tax and Audit engineer seats on one Workday board, with three management seats beside them. Five of the eight are engineer family; three are management. None are intern seats.

CoCounsel covers Principal Software Engineer / Architect AI, CoCounsel FDE (Hybrid; New York primary with Frisco, Toronto, Ann Arbor and Eagan) and Senior Software Engineer - AI, CoCounsel FDE (Hybrid; Frisco TX, US-located; closed on the November 8, 2026 live check; not linked as a current listing). Tax and Audit covers Senior Software Engineer AI, Tax and Audit, FDE and Senior Tax and Audit AI Engineer, FDE (both Hybrid; Brooklyn NY primary with multi-site US and Toronto). Management covers Senior Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering Tax and Audit, Senior Solutions Manager, Tax and Audit AI FDE, and Senior Solutions Manager, Legal AI FDE (closed on the November 6 live check; not linked as a current listing).

What the engineer seats ask for

Principal Software Engineer / Architect AI, CoCounsel FDE states 12+ years of software engineering experience, including significant technical leadership at the Principal, Architect, or senior individual-contributor level. The same text keeps the hands-on bar inside a quote: This is not an architecture-only or advisory role. We are looking for a Principal-level technical leader who still actively writes, reviews, and debugs production code. It also asks for Significant experience with AI/ML, Generative AI, or LLM-powered systems in production environments.

Senior Software Engineer - AI, CoCounsel FDE (closed on the November 8, 2026 live check; not linked) states 5+ years of software engineering experience with a strong foundation in system design, distributed systems, and clean architecture. and At least 2 years of hands-on experience building and deploying AI/ML or LLM-powered applications in production. Stack lines include Strong proficiency in Python and backend engineering, with experience building RESTful APIs, async services, and event-driven systems. and Experience with orchestration frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, PydanticAI, or AutoGen, along with solid understanding of RAG architectures and vector databases. The workflow line names CoCounsel tooling: Develop and configure multi-step agentic workflows using CoCounsel, Westlaw, Practical Law, third-party AI APIs, and enterprise systems.

Senior Software Engineer AI, Tax and Audit, FDE states 5+ years of software engineering experience, with a strong foundation in system design, distributed systems, and maintainable software architecture. and the same AI/ML line: At least 2 years of hands-on experience building and deploying AI/ML or LLM-powered applications in production. Stack and orchestration match the CoCounsel Senior SWE pattern: Strong proficiency in Python and backend engineering, including REST APIs, asynchronous services, and event-driven systems. and Hands-on experience with RAG architectures, vector databases, and AI orchestration frameworks, such as LangChain, LangGraph, PydanticAI, or AutoGen.

Senior Legal Engineer, Legal Products Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) states 5+ years of total professional experience, including at least 2 years in legal practice at a law firm or in-house legal department, or in a legal technology product role. Credentials: JD or LLB (required or strongly preferred). Stack: Build and iterate on AI workflow prototypes and automations using Python and AI-assisted development tools, applying sound practices for security and reliability.

Senior Tax and Audit AI Engineer, FDE states Five or more years of hands-on experience in public accounting, corporate tax or audit, with a relevant professional credential—such as Certified Public Accountant (CPA), Enrolled Agent (EA) or Certified Internal Auditor (CIA)—or equivalent depth of professional experience. Tooling: Advanced Excel skills and working knowledge of SQL or Python. and Demonstrated experience applying AI to real tax or audit work, including prompt engineering, custom agents or assistants, and AI coding tools such as Claude Code, Cursor or GitHub Copilot.

The Senior Software Engineer and Principal seats are silent on a formal degree line in the November 6 texts. Employer education rules are on do you need a degree to be a forward deployed engineer.

What this means if you are preparing for a Thomson Reuters FDE loop

  • Pick the vertical by the work. CoCounsel and Tax and Audit Senior Software Engineer seats emphasize Python, backend systems, RAG and orchestration frameworks. Legal Engineer asks for legal practice or legal technology experience plus a JD or LLB. Tax and Audit AI Engineer asks for accounting or tax depth with CPA, EA or CIA credentials or equivalent.
  • Match the years line to the seat. Senior engineer seats cluster at 5+ years (with a 2+ AI/ML line on the SWE seats). Principal CoCounsel asks for 12+ with technical leadership. Management seats ask for 7+ or 10+.
  • Read Hybrid as the workplace label. All eight are Hybrid under Workday, and 0 of 8 are remote under the locations-index method used on this site.
  • Use the posted USD bands as a check, not an offer. Every seat posts a primary eligible-office USD base range; engineer seats span $109,000 to $340,000.

Questions people ask

Does Thomson Reuters hire Forward Deployed Engineers?

Yes. Thomson Reuters had 8 FDE-family postings in FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot, tied for 16th of 114 employers with Shield AI. Five are engineer titles spanning CoCounsel, Legal Products, and Tax and Audit: Principal Software Engineer / Architect AI, CoCounsel FDE; Senior Legal Engineer, Legal Products Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE); Senior Software Engineer - AI, CoCounsel FDE; Senior Software Engineer AI, Tax and Audit, FDE; and Senior Tax and Audit AI Engineer, FDE. Three are management titles: Senior Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering Tax and Audit; Senior Solutions Manager, Legal AI FDE; and Senior Solutions Manager, Tax and Audit AI FDE. On November 8, 2026, 6 of the 8 were still open; the Legal AI Solutions Manager seat (JREQ203442) and the Senior Software Engineer - AI, CoCounsel FDE seat (JREQ200170) had closed.

How much does a Thomson Reuters Forward Deployed Engineer make?

All eight Thomson Reuters FDE-family postings list a primary eligible-office USD base compensation range on the November 3, 2026 snapshot. Across the five engineer seats the posted US ranges span $109,000 to $340,000: Senior Legal Engineer $109,000 to $203,000; Senior Tax and Audit AI Engineer $117,700 to $218,600; Senior Software Engineer AI, CoCounsel FDE $127,000 to $235,000; Senior Software Engineer AI, Tax and Audit, FDE $136,000 to $253,000; and Principal Software Engineer / Architect AI, CoCounsel FDE $183,100 to $340,000. Management seats also post USD ranges on the snapshot; those figures are listed on the guide under management seats and are not part of the engineer span. Posted ranges are not offers.

What do Thomson Reuters Forward Deployed Engineer postings ask for?

Years and credentials differ by seat. Principal CoCounsel asks for 12+ years of software engineering experience with significant technical leadership, plus AI/ML or LLM-powered systems in production and hands-on production code. Senior Software Engineer CoCounsel and Senior Software Engineer Tax and Audit each ask for 5+ years of software engineering plus at least 2 years building and deploying AI/ML or LLM-powered applications in production, with Python/backend and orchestration frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, PydanticAI, or AutoGen alongside RAG architectures. Senior Legal Engineer asks for 5+ years of total professional experience including at least 2 years in legal practice or legal technology, with JD or LLB required or strongly preferred, and Python prototypes with AI-assisted development tools. Senior Tax and Audit AI Engineer asks for five or more years in public accounting, corporate tax or audit with a CPA, EA or CIA credential or equivalent depth, plus SQL or Python, Excel, and AI coding tools. Senior Manager Tax and Audit asks for 10+ years including 5+ managing engineering teams. Senior Solutions Manager Tax and Audit asks for 7+ years of product management, solution architecture, or related product-delivery experience. The closed Legal AI Solutions Manager seat has no live text on the November 6 check.

Are Thomson Reuters Forward Deployed Engineer jobs remote?

Workday labels all eight snapshot seats Hybrid. Under the locations-index method (board marks posting remote with Greenhouse flags excluded, or a listed location contains Remote; Coinbase Remote Zone excluded), 0 of 8 are remote, against 176 of 704 (25.0%) across the snapshot. None of the eight seats are marked remote on the board. Locations are multi-site US (New York / Brooklyn, Frisco TX, Ann Arbor, Eagan MN, Chicago, Richmond VA on some rows) plus Toronto, Canada. Seven of eight rows are marked mixed US location; one Senior Software Engineer CoCounsel row is US-located.

Do Thomson Reuters Forward Deployed Engineer roles require a security clearance?

The six open November 8 texts do not require a security clearance, Secret, TS/SCI, or ITAR. Standard EEO citizen-status boilerplate is not a clearance. Senior Legal Engineer states JD or LLB required or strongly preferred. Senior Tax and Audit AI Engineer states a CPA, EA or CIA credential or equivalent depth. The Senior Software Engineer and Principal seats are silent on a formal degree line. The texts do not state a separate visa sponsorship stance beyond that EEO boilerplate.
