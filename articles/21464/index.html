Summary

This is a job posting on OpenWork for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) at ALGO ARTIS, a Japanese company whose optimization-algorithm software handles complex industrial planning tasks such as power plant fuel procurement, chemical plant production scheduling, bus shift planning, and vessel allocation. The company has redefined roles previously called algorithm or full-cycle engineers as FDEs, responsible for embedding with customers, framing problems as optimization problems, prototyping and validating on site, building production-grade implementations, handing over to operations, measuring impact, and feeding learnings back to the organization. Fields include energy and shipping, transport, manufacturing, and new industries. Required experience covers software implementation, requirements definition with stakeholders, and architecture design; heuristic and combinatorial optimization, competitive programming (AtCoder), and industry backgrounds are pluses. Salary ranges from 8 to 18 million yen annually, based in Chiyoda, Tokyo, with flextime and remote work.

Key points

  • ALGO ARTIS builds optimization software for industrial planning in energy, shipping, transport, and manufacturing.
  • The FDE role was redefined from earlier algorithm/full-cycle engineer titles and is now being hired.
  • FDEs own the full cycle: on-site discovery, optimization modeling, prototyping, production rollout, and handover.
  • Annual salary is 8-18 million yen, with the position based in Chiyoda, Tokyo.
  • Requirements include software engineering, requirements definition, and architecture design; optimization skills are preferred.

  • Job Description
  • ■ Business Overview ALGO ARTIS provides software that streamlines planning operations in industrial settings using proprietary optimization algorithms. For complex planning tasks involving astronomical numbers of combinations—such as fuel procurement for thermal power plants, production planning for chemical plants, bus operation scheduling, and vessel allocation operations—we create high-quality plans in practical timeframes. ■ Background of the Hiring As our business expands, there is a growing need for engineers who can take end-to-end ownership: identifying challenges at customer sites, building technical solutions, and integrating them into operations. We are redefining the roles previously called "Algorithm Engineer" and "Full-Cycle Engineer" as "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)," a title that better reflects the actual work, and are now recruiting for this position. ■ Responsibilities The FDE deeply engages with customer sites, identifies challenges, constructs solutions using optimization algorithms, integrates them into operations, and takes responsibility for delivering value. ・Understand business operations by working on-site with customers ・Formulate challenges as optimization problems ・Build prototypes and validate them in the field ・Implement in a form that can withstand production use, integrate into operations, and hand over ・Measure impact and connect it to the next challenge ・Contribute knowledge and patterns gained from projects back to the organization ■ Fields by Industry ・Energy and vessel allocation ・Transportation ・Manufacturing ・New industries ■ Appeal of the Position ・Experience the satisfaction of seeing the code you write and the algorithms you design actually running at power plants, plants, and operations sites. ・Engage as an equal with people making decisions on the front lines of industry, from industry experts to executives. ・Drive both technology and business, and take responsibility all the way to operational adoption and value delivery. ・Enjoy the technical challenge of designing original heuristics in areas with no textbooks.
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • ■ Required Qualifications ・Experience implementing software as a software engineer ・Experience defining requirements through dialogue with stakeholders such as customers and business owners ・Experience designing architectures based on defined requirements ■ Preferred Qualifications ・Experience working as a tech lead ・Professional or research experience in energy, manufacturing, chemicals, logistics, infrastructure, or railways ・Experience implementing heuristic optimization or combinatorial optimization ・Activity in competitive programming (e.g., AtCoder) ・Experience launching products or new businesses from zero to one ・Actuary qualification or an academic background in mathematical sciences ・Professional experience applying mathematical optimization, machine learning, or simulation
