Palantir and Fujitsu Deepen Strategic Partnership to Expand Enterprise AI Adoption, with Fujitsu Strengthening Its Capabilities as a Palantir Global FDE Partner

Original title: Palantirと富士通、企業のAI活用拡大に向けて戦略的パートナーシップを深化し、富士通はPalantirのGlobal FDE Partnerとして体制を強化

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-09-10 · 5 min read

Summary

Palantir and Fujitsu announced a deepened strategic partnership under which Fujitsu becomes a Palantir Global FDE Partner, strengthening its forward deployed engineering capabilities. Fujitsu signed new agreements covering Palantir AIP and Palantir Foundry, and will handle sales, customer problem identification, use case design, and operational implementation. The partnership emphasizes data sovereignty, using ontologies, access controls, audit functions, and customer-managed environments so enterprises can use AI in governed settings. Fujitsu will combine its Takane LLM, Uvance business model, and industry expertise. The companies have cooperated since 2020, and Fujitsu says its FDE talent is recognized by overseas clients.

Key points

  • Fujitsu becomes a Palantir Global FDE Partner, expanding forward deployed engineering capacity.
  • New agreements cover Palantir AIP and Foundry sales and implementation support.
  • The partnership focuses on sovereign AI: governed data, ontologies, and customer-managed environments.
  • Fujitsu will leverage its Takane LLM and Uvance business model alongside Palantir platforms.
  • The companies have partnered since 2020; Fujitsu's FDE skills are validated globally.

Palantir Technologies Inc. (Note 1) (hereinafter "Palantir") and Fujitsu Limited (Note 2) (hereinafter "Fujitsu") have agreed to expand their strategic partnership and establish a collaborative framework to accelerate AI transformation for enterprises in Japan and abroad. Alongside this, Fujitsu has concluded new agreements with Palantir Technologies Japan G.K. (Note 3) covering "Palantir AIP" and "Palantir Foundry." As a Palantir Global FDE Partner, Fujitsu will sell and provide both products and drive the identification of customer challenges, use case design, and business implementation.

As interest grows in the sovereignty of corporate assets such as data, enterprises are increasingly required to exercise management and control over data, AI models, infrastructure such as execution platforms, and operations. Leveraging Palantir's platform, Palantir and Fujitsu will help customers use AI in a secure and controlled environment by linking AI models to properly governed data, ontology-based (Note 4) business processes, access controls, audit functions, and execution environments managed by the customers themselves.

Fujitsu is strengthening its Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) delivery framework for customers in Japan and worldwide in collaboration with Palantir. Leveraging advanced AI technologies including the large language model "Takane," extensive industry knowledge, offerings from its business model "Uvance," and personnel with advanced expertise in the use of Palantir, Fujitsu will support customers through the introduction and utilization of "Palantir Foundry" and "Palantir AIP," and help them rapidly develop and deploy their own AI applications in production environments where sovereignty is ensured.

Comments from the Representatives of Both Companies

Comment from Kevin Kawasaki, Global Head of Business Development, Palantir Technologies Inc.: The aim of this partnership is to bring Palantir's sovereign AI architecture (Note 5) to Fujitsu's customers across Japan. Fujitsu is investing in the FDE capabilities needed to help enterprises build their own AI applications and AI models on "Palantir AIP" and "Palantir Foundry," based on their own data and business processes, with proper governance ensured under their own operating environments.

Comment from Minami Takahashi, Director, Executive Vice President and COO, Fujitsu Limited: Since 2020, through our strategic partnership with Palantir, Fujitsu has honed its ability to turn data and AI into real business outcomes. FDE has attracted attention in recent years, but I understand it is a concept that Palantir pioneered and established. Through our collaboration with Palantir, which has led the field in FDE, Fujitsu has learned about FDE practices, methods of value creation, and engineering and other capabilities, and has built an FDE framework that goes beyond mere technology adoption to deliver rapid results in the field at customer sites. Moreover, Fujitsu's FDE talent has been highly regarded not only in Japan but also by customers overseas, and I am confident that this expertise and execution capability are competitive on a global scale. Through this expanded collaboration, we will combine the knowledge and execution capabilities of both companies to turn AI Transformation for more customers into concrete and sustainable results.

The two companies will continue to collaborate to support customers in Japan and around the world. Fujitsu will support customers introducing and using "Palantir Foundry" and "Palantir AIP" by leveraging its extensive industry knowledge, service delivery capabilities, and practical engineering expertise. Furthermore, under its business model "Uvance," which addresses societal issues, Fujitsu will realize business transformation that connects data, AI, and frontline operations, advancing customers' sustainable growth and enhanced competitiveness.
