Summary

This is a job posting from Japanese AI startup Jitera for Forward Deployed Engineers acting as AI consultants. Jitera's AI context platform parses legacy system code and auto-generates design documents; since launching in September 2024 it has been adopted by major enterprises including Nippon Steel Solutions, SCSK, Sumitomo Electric, Panasonic, and Hitachi. The role covers technical explanations in sales meetings, designing and running PoCs with evals and A/B testing, tuning configurations, onboarding client teams, and feeding insights back into the product, sometimes via direct implementation in Go or React. Requirements include hands-on software engineering experience, client-facing experience, daily LLM use, and business-level Japanese. Pay is ¥8–12 million with 45 hours of included overtime, stock options, and 16 extra flex holidays. The position is fully remote but requires living within commuting distance of Tokyo for client meetings.

Key points

  • Jitera is hiring Forward Deployed Engineers as AI consultants for enterprise clients.
  • Its platform has been adopted by Nippon Steel Solutions, SCSK, Panasonic, Hitachi, and others.
  • The role spans technical sales support, eval-based PoCs, tuning, and product feedback.
  • Salary is ¥8–12 million including 45 hours of fixed overtime, plus stock options.
  • Fully remote and flex-time, but candidates must live in the Tokyo area.

Full text

  • Jitera's Mission
  • Creating the era that comes after "creating." Advances in AI are changing the way we "create" in daily life and business. "Creating" is the act of bringing something new into the world, but the process requires a great deal of "making" work. Preparing application development plans, coding websites, and writing meeting minutes are all examples of "making." We believe that by having AI take over "making," we can create an environment where people can focus on the essential act of "creating." Expanding new possibilities and realizing the era that comes after "creating" is our mission.
  • Jitera is a global AI startup born in Japan.
  • To meet talented people from around the world, we have built a global, fully remote work environment. Our official company languages are Japanese and English. Depending on the position, you may need to converse in English with engineers overseas. However, it's fine even if your English isn't very strong when you join. Through six months of practical study, some members have developed English skills they can use on the job.
  • What Is the AI Context Platform "Jitera"?
  • AI provides one-stop support from design to documentation, code generation, and testing. Jitera is an "AI context platform" that understands context and automates development. We are a global AI startup with the mission "Build the Next Era of 'Build.'" Our AI context platform, "Jitera," reads the code of existing systems, analyzes their structure, and automatically generates highly accurate design documents. It is a context-based, next-generation development environment that automates system development and modification, delivering efficiency and cost reduction. Since its launch in September 2024, it has been adopted by major SIers such as Nippon Steel Technology and SCSK, as well as dozens of enterprises, and continues to grow at an overwhelming pace. Its applications go beyond "development AI agents." By continuously learning all of a company's internal context, it is expanding into "business AI agents" that can even replace operations such as sales and administration. We are creating a future that solves deep-rooted corporate challenges, including over-reliance on individuals, and allows people to focus on the essential act of "creating."
  • The Appeal of Building Your Career at Jitera
  • Make an impact on your career. In an era where AI is the norm, this is an environment where you can always stay at the forefront of technology and increase your market value. ▶ A grand mission to create the future: Transform the future of all kinds of work, starting with software development, through AI. You can be involved in work that makes a major impact on society at a global level. ▶ An outstanding growth environment: Work with cutting-edge technology at a rapidly growing startup and achieve great autonomy and personal growth that you can't experience elsewhere. ▶ A truly global experience: Work with diverse members in an organization where about half are foreign nationals. While not required for your work, it's an environment where you can naturally polish your English skills. ▶ Top-tier flexible work arrangements in Japan: With full remote and full flex-time systems, plus 16 flex holidays per year granted separately from paid leave, you can enrich your work-life balance without being bound by time or place. ▶ The excitement of aiming for an IPO: With our stock option system, the whole company can take on goals with high motivation as one team.
  • Teaching AI tacit knowledge to change the common sense of modernization.
  • ◤When AI learns "tacit knowledge," the landscape of development changes◢ —Please tell us about the mission of Jitera's modernization team! Our mission is to migrate customers' existing systems to modern technology stacks using an innovative approach leveraging AI agents, maximizing business value. Our greatest distinguishing feature is that we strategically combine various AI tools, including our own product "Jitera," to achieve overwhelming development speed. For example, AI dramatically streamlines human work across code analysis of existing systems, automation of requirements definition, generation of design documents, and creation of test cases, making it possible to shorten project timelines to less than half of the conventional duration. We aim to achieve both "resolution of technical debt" and "creation of new value" through AI. —Jitera is evolving as an "AI context platform." How will that change your work? We believe modernization work will change qualitatively in a big way. The key lies in how we handle "tacit knowledge," which has been difficult to systematize until now. Every organization has undocumented rules and know-how that exists only in the minds of certain veteran employees, right? For example, customs and historical circumstances like "for this process, with Company A's system, you must always follow this procedure." Such "tacit knowledge" has been a factor causing unintended bugs and slowing down the ramp-up of new members. Jitera will be able to learn such tacit knowledge as textual information. —So AI will understand each company's unique "ways of doing things"? Exactly. By deeply understanding a project's background and context, AI will be able to support even the areas that previously relied on veterans' "intuition," such as "for this customer, when adding this feature, updating this DB table as well is the 'proper way.'" That means we can prevent mistakes caused by "tacit knowledge" while focusing on more strategic and creative work. We customize the technical optimal solutions proposed by AI to fit the context of the customer's business, providing comprehensive support that extends to organizational transformation. The role of us humans will shift toward a more advanced "bridging of technology and business." I am confident that a future where we can provide even greater value to our customers is coming.
  • Job Title
  • Forward Deployed Engineer
  • Job Description
  • ▶︎ Background Large enterprises are rapidly adopting AI coding agents in their development environments. At the same time, new challenges have emerged: "the impact cannot be measured quantitatively," "adoption doesn't take hold on the ground," and "there is no basis for deciding on company-wide rollout." We are strengthening our technical consulting services that analyze customers' actual AI usage, demonstrate its impact with data, and support them through to expanded adoption. We are hiring multiple AI consultants to take charge of this key battleground. ▶︎ Track Record and Current Position - Proven deployments at major companies, starting with a technology partnership with Nippon Steel Solutions, and including SCSK, Sumitomo Electric, Panasonic, Hitachi, Asahi Life, and Capcom - Providing AI-powered system development support and digital consulting services - A global organization with members of diverse nationalities - As enterprise adoption of generative AI accelerates, we are preparing to launch a new business alongside our existing operations (details will be disclosed after signing an NDA during the selection process). ▶︎ Mission You will be responsible for everything from proposals to customers through production deployment and operational adoption. Insights gained from deployments will also feed back into product improvements. Your responsibilities will include technical explanations, designing and running PoCs (Evals), tuning configurations, and providing feedback to the product team. - Explain architectures and mechanisms in your own words in proposals and technical discussions - Build Evals from customers' systems and challenges, and demonstrate impact through A/B testing - Tune context, routing, and safeguards based on actual usage - Turn issues found in the field into requests for the product team or your own implementations ▶︎ Key Roles and Responsibilities ① Customer Engagement You will provide technical explanations and proposals in sales meetings, PoCs, and security reviews. You will answer questions like the following accurately on the spot: - Cost reduction mechanism: "How exactly does it reduce costs?" - Server and infrastructure configuration: "What is placed where, and how is data protected?" - Connectivity: "Can our existing systems built with Function calling be connected as-is?" - Technical strengths: "We've received proposals from other vendors as well—what makes Jitera stand out?" ② Designing and Running PoCs (Evals) - Work with sales to make proposals to customers, and when a PoC is needed, formulate hypotheses, goals, and execution plans - Create Eval datasets and evaluate the effectiveness of the AI control platform in terms of quality, cost, and speed - Produce quantitative reports and production rollout plans, and present them to customers ③ Deployment, Tuning, and Adoption - Onboard customers' development teams and support their development as needed - Continuously tune the AI control platform's configuration based on customers' usage - Guide customers on how to leverage AI agents, not limited to the AI control platform - Support the creation of operational rules for AI agents ④ Feeding Back into the Product - Share insights gained in the field with the product development team - Rather than turning customer requests directly into feature requirements, dig into the underlying issues and translate them into design - Implement improvements yourself as needed, seeing them through to a Pull Request
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • [Required Skills and Experience] - Practical software engineering experience (experience making technical decisions yourself in design, implementation, or operations) - Experience engaging directly with customers (in any form: technical discussions, PoCs, deployment support, technical support, etc.) - Daily use of LLMs and coding tools, with the ability to explain token and cost behavior - Ability to troubleshoot and resolve connectivity, configuration, and operational issues in customers' environments by examining logs and requests - Business communication in Japanese (since most engagements are with large enterprises) [Preferred Skills and Experience] - Experience deploying products in industries with strict security reviews and internal regulations, such as finance or trading companies - Experience with Evals and evaluation design - Implementation experience with Go, PostgreSQL, or React - Experience deploying or operating self-hosted or on-premises products - Experience in pre-sales, solution architecture, or customer success - English (the core team is multinational, and internal communication includes English)
  • Work Style
  • Jitera is fully remote with full flextime across the company. However, this position involves meetings at customer sites, so it is open to candidates living within commuting distance of the Tokyo metropolitan area who can attend in-person meetings as needed. We place great importance on reducing reliance on synchronous communication (meetings, verbal exchanges) and conveying information asynchronously through documents, code, and PRs. We value creating a state where "you can understand just by reading."
  • Selection Process
  • The selection process proceeds through the following steps. 1. Casual Interview (30 minutes): To build mutual understanding, we will ask about your future career vision and have a casual conversation about Jitera. 2. HR Interview (30 minutes): A relaxed conversation to see whether you are a good fit for Jitera's culture and work environment. 3. Aptitude Test: A test to understand your personality and strengths. We do not make hiring decisions based on the aptitude test alone. 4. Team Interview (30-60 minutes): A conversation with members of your prospective team about the specific work and the team. 5. Executive Interview (30-60 minutes): The CEO will ask about your thoughts and vision.
  • What Makes This Position Attractive
  • A rare environment in Japan where you can work with real data on generative AI adoption at large enterprises - Since you can handle everything from technical discussions to validation, deployment, and improvement proposals on your own, you gain autonomy and hands-on experience that siloed roles cannot offer - You don't stop at identifying issues—you can become a "builder" who fixes the product itself using Go / React - You can drive the evolution of the product on the front lines together with a multinational core team
  • Examples of expected career backgrounds
  • Software engineers at enterprises or startups (with a strong passion for AI adoption and a desire to move into a customer-facing role) - Professionals from IT or tech consulting backgrounds who can also write code themselves - Tech leads or architects at SIers with experience making proposals to customers
