Forward Deployed Engineer jobs by location: cities and remote share

FDE Dispatch · 2026-10-26 · 6 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch analyzed 725 live Forward Deployed Engineer postings from 121 employers, pulled from public job boards on October 24, 2026. New York (152 postings) and the San Francisco Bay Area (148) lead, followed by London (77) and the Washington, DC area (75). About 27.4% of postings list a remote option, though Databricks alone accounts for 95 of those; excluding it, the share drops to 17.2%. Most postings outside the US concentrate in London, Singapore, Munich, Sydney and Toronto. Nearly 60% of postings mention travel, so listed cities are home bases rather than where work happens. Figures are a single-date snapshot of open postings, not headcount.

Key points

  • New York (152) and the Bay Area (148) have the most FDE postings; the Bay Area has the most employers (64).
  • 199 postings (27.4%) list remote, but Databricks accounts for 95 of them.
  • Excluding Databricks, the remote share falls to 17.2% (104 of 603).
  • 24 employers list remote on every FDE posting, including GitLab, Reddit and Tailscale.
  • 59.3% of postings mention travel; listed cities are home bases, not work locations.

A posting counts once per metro it lists, so the column adds up to more than 725. 317 postings (43.7%) list more than one location, and some Accenture postings list 42 to 45 US offices each, which lifts mid-size US metros (Accenture appears with 9 postings in many US rows). Employer counts are the steadier measure: the Bay Area has the most employers (64) even though New York has slightly more postings. Palantir alone has 25 of the 75 Washington, DC area postings and 24 of New York's 152.

US vs outside the US

386 postings list only US locations, 291 list only non-US locations, 31 mix both, and 17 are unclear. Outside the US the most-listed metros are London (77 postings, 27 employers), Singapore (22, 13), Munich (15, 9), Sydney (15, 8) and Toronto (15, 6). Posted pay is far more common in US postings (83.9% vs 6.9% outside the US), as reported on the Forward Deployed Engineer salary page.

Are FDE jobs remote?

About one in four postings lists a remote option, but most of that comes from one employer. 199 of 725 (27.4%) list remote; Databricks has 95 of those; excluding Databricks the share is 17.2% (104 of 603).

  • By employer. 48 of 121 employers (39.7%) have at least one remote-listed FDE posting, 24 list remote on every FDE posting they had open, and 73 list none.
  • By role family. Engineer 161 of 568 (28.3%), deployment strategist 20 of 86 (23.3%), FDE management 18 of 71 (25.4%).
  • US vs non-US. US-only postings 129 of 386 (33.4%) vs non-US-only 56 of 291 (19.2%).
  • Most remote-listed postings by employer. Databricks 95, ElevenLabs 9, Cursor 7, LangChain 7, Cohere 6, Salesforce 6.
  • Workplace type, where the board states one (389 of 725 postings): hybrid 141 (36.2%), on-site 134 (34.4%), remote 64 (16.5%), flexible office arrangements 50 (12.9%).

Wherever the role is based, the interview rounds are similar. FDE Dispatch trains you for them.

Which companies list every FDE posting as remote

24 employers list remote on every FDE posting they had open in this snapshot: ATLAS, Arize AI, AssemblyAI, Broadcom, Commure, Defense Unicorns, Eudia, GitLab, Glean, Level AI, LiveKit, Neon, OpenHands, Origin, Oscilar, Reddit, Render, Rerun, Runpod, Stability AI, Tailscale, Tonic.ai, Workday, n8n. Most have 1 to 4 open FDE postings, and several also list offices (for example Glean lists Mountain View, San Francisco and New York alongside Remote - US), so "remote listed" means a remote option appears in the posting. Several of these roles also name an office.

Office location is not where the work happens

Treat the listed city as a home base, with time at customer sites on top. 59.3% of the 725 postings mention travel (full breakdown on Forward Deployed Engineer travel; also published on the salary page) and the skills page counts travel in 58.8% of 563 engineer postings (requirements index).

Method and data

The population is the same 725 postings as the salary and job titles pages: public Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday boards, pulled October 24, 2026, with agencies and duplicates removed. Metro matching uses patterns (Bay Area includes Palo Alto, Mountain View, San Jose and related cities; DC area includes Arlington, McLean, Reston and Herndon); 161 postings name no matched metro. Remote listed means the board marks remote or a listed location contains "Remote"; the Coinbase pay-zone label "US - Remote Zone 1" is not counted.

The salary page's 26.3% remote figure (191 postings) counts only the remote field in the posting file, which did not read Ashby, Lever or Workday location text; this index reads both, hence 27.4%. The salary page's top-locations line counts the first listed location only (New York 93, San Francisco Bay Area 83); this index counts every listed location.

Limits: these are open postings, not headcount, and the figures are a snapshot from one pull date.
