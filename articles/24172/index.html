Accompanying Your AX Journey, Achieved at the Fastest Speed

Original title: AXの伴走、最速の実現｜VACAN（バカン）

株式会社Vacan Technologies · 2026-09-07 · 5 min read

Summary

VACAN Technologies, a new company (founded January 2026, capital 25 million yen) spun out from Japan's Vacan Inc. under CEO Ryu Tamaki, offers AI transformation (AX) services built on a forward deployed engineering model. Its core claim: AI initiatives stall because strategists and implementers are different people, so its FDEs join client sites from requirements through prototyping, full development, and internal handover. The company dogfoods five internal tools—WorkflowOS (39 screens, 125 APIs on AWS), Proposal Studio, OverAll, Marketing Automation, and Internal Portal—designed as systems AI primarily uses. It offers three service models: AI education, AI transformation, and AI product development, emphasizing small working outputs and fast feedback loops over grand plans.

Key points

  • FDEs handle both proposal and implementation, staying on-site until clients can self-operate.
  • Five in-house AI-first tools are used daily in the company's own operations.
  • WorkflowOS runs in production on AWS with 39 screens, 125 APIs, and 62 data models.
  • Three service models: AI training, AI transformation, and AI product/new-business development.
  • Parent company Vacan's products won the iF Design Award 2023 and Good Design Award.

WorkflowOS

Making the work itself AI-driven

Keeping AI embedded in a single line of work, from deals to monthly reporting.

We designed it not as a "business system that can also use AI," but as a business system that AI primarily uses. Deals → contracts → projects → monthly reporting are connected in one line, so the deals people see on screen and the deals AI reads are the same. We believe this is the shape of business systems after AX, and we are testing it daily in our own sales operations.

02 Proposal Studio

AI-driven proposal work

AI drafts the proposal; people review it and send it out.

An example of shifting proposal writing to AI. AI prepares the draft, and people spend their time on judgment and finishing touches. Because it is white-label, it can be directly applied to a client's proposal workflow.

Zero dependencies ── easy to bring into client environments Python standard library only

03 OverAll

AI-driven management decisions

Gathering the materials for management decisions in one place, in a form AI can read.

It consolidates clients, deals, budgets vs. actuals, and KPIs, creating a state where everything is ready before you search. Not a tool for collecting and organizing numbers, but a foundation for asking AI questions from an already-consolidated state.

Next.js + Prisma + PostgreSQL

04 Marketing Automation

Customer acquisition via AI

A tool for customer acquisition in an era when customers ask AI instead of searching.

The core metric is "the rate at which our company appears in the answer when someone asks AI." We track everything in one line, from generating articles that raise that rate to inquiries and conversion into sales meetings.

Actual screens Python + SQLite

05 Internal Portal

AI-driven knowledge

Internal decisions and procedures in one place, readable by both people and AI.

From writing to publishing takes about 90 seconds. The context given to AI is drawn from here, so the basis of its answers stays aligned with primary internal information.

Static HTML + S3 + CloudFront + GitHub Actions
