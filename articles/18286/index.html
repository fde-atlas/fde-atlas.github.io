FDE | Modeling the Future of Business Fastest with AI

Original title: FDE | AIで、事業の未来を最速モデリング

株式会社Elith - Ai研究・開発・教育 · 2022-12-01 · 3 min read

Summary

This is a service page from Japanese AI company Elith describing its field-embedded AI development offering. Forward Deployed Engineers join client sites to implement UI, legacy system integration, workflows, and AI models end to end, staying responsible for post-launch improvement. Aimed at manufacturing, finance, and other core operations, the design emphasizes safe connections to existing data platforms and authentication, plus human approval, rollback, and logging as standard operations. The page outlines a flow from requirements gathering and ROI estimation through desk and field PoCs to deployment and operational support, and lists research-backed capabilities such as RAG, OCR, image AI, and AI agents.

Key points

  • Elith's FDEs work on-site, handling UI, integration, workflows, and AI models as one effort.
  • Safety design covers secure system connections, human approval, rollback, and audit logging.
  • Delivery spans requirements, ROI estimates, desk and field PoCs, deployment, and ongoing support.
  • Targets core operations in manufacturing and finance; capabilities include RAG, OCR, and image AI.
  • Company cites academic publications and joint research as the basis for custom AI work.
