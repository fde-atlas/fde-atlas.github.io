Engineer Salary Checker 2026: FDE, AI & Software Pay Bands

resourced.com.au · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

Australian recruiter Re:Sourced publishes 2026 base-salary bands for forward deployed engineers, drawn from accepted offers on its live searches over the 12 months to Q1 2026, calibrated with public data from Glassdoor, Levels.fyi and others. Senior FDEs earn AUD 180-240k in Sydney, 175-230k in Melbourne and 160-215k in Brisbane; Sydney tech leads reach 230-270k. Bands cover the 25th-75th percentile of accepted offers, excluding equity, bonus and super. AI-native scale-ups typically pay the top of band plus structured equity, with the premium tied to proven customer-embedded delivery rather than title. Outliers like frontier-lab pay are excluded. The firm reports a median 21 days from brief to signed offer.

Key points

  • Senior FDE base pay: AUD 180-240k in Sydney.
  • Sydney FDE tech leads earn AUD 230-270k base.
  • Bands reflect accepted offers, not advertised ranges.
  • AI-native scale-ups pay top of band plus equity.
  • Premium follows customer-embedded delivery experience.

For employers

Forward deployed engineers (FDEs, sometimes called forward deployment engineers) are among the highest-priced engineering hires in Australia: the pool of engineers with proven customer-embedded delivery is small, and demand from AI-native and data-platform companies keeps climbing. Methodology

Bands above are calibrated against Re:Sourced active and recent search briefs in the 12 months ending Q1 2026, supplemented with public salary data from Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, Robert Half AU, Hays AU and Talent International.

Bands represent the 25th to 75th percentile of accepted offers for like-for-like roles. Outliers (top 10% AI engineering at frontier labs, deep-startup early stage) are excluded from the band but cited in the full Salary Guide 2026.
