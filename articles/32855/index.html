Who is hiring forward deployed engineers in the US: November 2026

Kernavique · 2026-11-20 · 18 min read

Summary

On November 20, 2026, Kernavique read the public job feeds of 180 AI and tech employers. 79 listed at least one posting with "forward deployed" in the title; 65 of the feed-based employers had 298 US postings. Databricks (60) and Palantir (53) dominate, together over a third of US postings, followed by OpenAI (21) and Anduril (13). New York and San Francisco are the most common locations. The authors stress this is a one-day snapshot, not a census, and the 298 includes managers, product managers, and non-engineering roles like bankers and lawyers. Related titles such as deployment strategist and applied AI engineer are counted separately.

Key points

  • One-day count of 180 employers; 298 US postings with "forward deployed" titles.
  • Databricks (60) and Palantir (53) together account for 113 US postings.
  • 42 employers name New York and 37 name San Francisco in their locations.
  • Palantir, Anduril and others frequently mention security clearance in postings.
  • The count includes managers, PMs, and non-engineers like Rogo's forward deployed bankers.

The count this month

On November 20, 2026, between 16:10 and 16:12 UTC, we read the public job feeds of 177 AI and tech employers. We counted every posting whose title contains “forward deployed”. We also read three more employers, Amazon, ServiceNow and Deloitte, through their own public job search. That makes 180 employers checked.

  • Employers with the title. 79 of the 180 had at least one open posting with “forward deployed” in the title: 76 of the 177 feeds, plus Amazon, ServiceNow and Deloitte.
  • Postings on the 177 feeds. 526 in all locations. 298 of them list at least one US location, at 65 employers.
  • Without interns. 507 in all locations and 284 in the US.
  • The three other sites. Amazon 82 roles (57 US). ServiceNow 13 (6 US), from a partial read. Deloitte 56 on its US careers site, with locations not checked ad by ad.
  • Employers with none. 101 of the 177 feeds had no posting with the title.

This is a count of public job feeds on one day. It is not a census of the market. The employers are a convenience sample, mostly AI companies and other tech employers, and one feed entry is one posting, so an employer that posts the same job once per city has more postings than jobs.

On the Ashby and Lever feeds, no posting with the title carried a contract, temporary or part-time employment type. Palantir’s internships are typed “Internship”. Greenhouse feeds have no employment-type field, so we could not check Greenhouse employers.

How we count forward deployed engineer hiring sets out the rules. For what data providers say about growth in these postings, see the quarterly forward deployed engineer hiring index.

Where the roles are

New York and San Francisco lead. Of the 65 employers with a US posting on the feeds, 42 name New York in the US locations we captured and 37 name San Francisco or the San Francisco Bay Area. 28 name both. 14 name neither.

The feeds give locations per posting, and we kept only each employer’s list, so we cannot say how many of the 298 postings sit in each city. Because long lists were shortened, read the 42 and the 37 as minimums.

Other patterns in the lists:

  • Washington, D.C. Seven employers name it: Databricks, Palantir, OpenAI, Cursor, Scale AI, Cognition and Deepgram. C3 AI lists Tysons, Virginia, and Amazon lists Arlington and Herndon, Virginia, and Jessup, Maryland.
  • Beyond the big cities. Palantir’s list includes Honolulu, Norfolk, San Diego, Kitsap, Laurel and Fayetteville. Anduril lists Costa Mesa, Honolulu, Colorado Springs and Tacoma. Picogrid lists El Segundo and Fort Bragg. All three have postings with clearance wording.
  • Remote. Several employers list the United States or a remote US location with no city, including Cresta, ClickHouse, Onebrief, Reddit and Monte Carlo.
  • Outside the US. 228 of the 526 postings on the feeds list no US location we could count. Wonderful’s 35 postings are all outside the US.

So the 298 is not 298 engineering jobs. In the US, 21 postings are for managers of these teams, 11 are for product managers and 11 are for bankers, investment professionals, lawyers and other people who are not engineers. One Rogo title, “Forward Deployed, Equity Research”, is a finance role that fell into the research group because of the word “research”.

Two related titles do not use the phrase and are counted separately:

  • Deployment strategist. 83 postings in all locations and 36 in the US, at 13 employers. Palantir has 22, Wonderful 24 (none in the US) and Databricks 12.
  • Applied AI engineer. 121 postings in all locations and 75 in the US, at 32 employers. OpenAI has 48, Anthropic 13, Mercor 9 and Scale AI 7. The rule is broad and also catches titles such as “Software Engineer, Applied AI”.

The titles themselves show how unsettled the naming is. Snowflake posts a “Staff Applied AI Engineer (FDE)” and a “Staff Forward Deployed Engineer, Applied AI”, which reads as one job under two names. Scale AI, OpenAI and Anthropic keep the two title families apart. Monte Carlo splits the role by sales stage, with one title marked “Pre Sales” and another “(Post-Sales)”, and Tennr labels its roles “(Post-Sales)”. We compared titles only, not the ad text.

Our piece on the forward deployed engineer and the solutions engineer covers where the two roles differ. The forward deployed engineer role page describes the job itself.

This is a keyword match, not a reading of each ad. “Active clearance required”, “eligible to obtain” and “preferred” are counted together. Palantir’s ads include both kinds: some ask for an active clearance at or above Top Secret, and others accept eligibility and willingness to obtain one. Databricks’ public-sector ads ask for US citizenship and eligibility for a Secret clearance. Auterion says eligibility for a Secret clearance is preferred. Cursor’s one match is a public-sector regional director role.

Three things sit outside the 63. Vannevar Labs puts the requirement in the title, “Forward Deployed Engineer (Secret Clearance Required)” and “Forward Deployed Engineer (TS/SCI Clearance Required)”, but its feed names no country, so neither posting is in the US count. Amazon’s search returned 15 US ads that mention a clearance. And we did not check ServiceNow or Deloitte for clearance wording.

What changed since October 11

Only one comparison is like for like. On October 11, 2026, we checked 62 feeds for our article on how to hire a forward deployed engineer. We read the same 62 feeds again on November 20, 2026, with the same title rule, in all locations.

Without interns, the 62 feeds showed 291 postings on October 11 and 292 on November 20. That is flat. OpenAI moved most, from 26 to 38. Databricks went from 101 to 94 and Palantir from 79 to 72.

The October check did not record a US-only count, so the 183 has nothing to compare with yet. Next month it will.

Do not read the 526 on this page against October’s 312. This month we read 115 more feeds. The larger number is a wider net, not growth.

One change inside a feed: Anthropic now lists a US role for an individual engineer, “Staff Forward Deployed Engineer”, in New York City, San Francisco and Seattle. On October 11 its only US posting with the title was a manager role.

How to use this

If you are an engineer. Use the tables as a list of employers to check, not as a list of open jobs. Each name links to the board we read, and the boards change daily. Look at the variant before the title: a forward deployed product manager or strategist is a different job from a forward deployed software engineer. If a role is in government work, expect clearance wording. For published ranges, see forward deployed engineer pay by employer. If you want to be considered for roles we work on, send us your details and résumé.

If you are a company. The tables show who you are competing with for these engineers and where. 42 of the 65 employers with a US posting name New York and 37 name San Francisco, so in either city you are hiring alongside most of them. They also show how others word the title. If your role is post-sales delivery, say so in the title, as Monte Carlo and Tennr do. Our guide on how to hire a forward deployed engineer covers screening and interviews. If you need help with the hire, ask us about hiring forward deployed engineers.

What we left out

  • Employers on Workday. The public Workday job search answered HTTP 429, the code for too many requests, for Booz Allen Hamilton, Accenture, NVIDIA, Leidos, CACI, CrowdStrike, Adobe, PwC, Workday, GDIT, Autodesk, HPE, Dell, Intel and Red Hat. The site addresses we tried were guesses. This is why consulting and defense contracting firms are thin in the tables.
  • Salesforce. We read one page of 20 results from a search that reported 118 before the same limit applied. Those 20 included forward deployed engineer titles in eight European cities and no US title. With 98 results unread, we give no count.
  • Google, Microsoft, IBM, Oracle and the large consulting firms. Google’s careers search returned no job list as text. Microsoft, IBM, Oracle, McKinsey, BCG, Capgemini, KPMG, EY and Slalom were not attempted or could not be read without a browser. Booz Allen Hamilton’s own careers search returned unrelated jobs and no total.
  • Employers with no feed under the names we tried. Among them Shield AI, Applied Intuition, Hebbia, Groq, Rippling, Retool, dbt Labs, DataRobot and Mistral. They may post elsewhere. The full list is on the method page.
  • Eight boards we found and did not use. Five whose owner we could not confirm, and three that looked partial.
  • A split by employer type. The research groups the 101 employers with no such title by type, but not the 65 with one, so we did not sort the tables that way.
  • Postings per city. We kept each employer’s list of US locations, not a count of postings in each place.
  • A reading of each ad. Clearance is a keyword match. We did not separate required from preferred, and we did not compare ad text across title variants.
  • Contract roles on Greenhouse. Greenhouse feeds carry no employment-type field, so a contract or temporary role there would not be seen.
  • Pay. It is in a separate piece, forward deployed engineer pay by employer.
  • Market growth figures. They are in the quarterly hiring index, with each provider’s method and limits.

Questions

  • Which companies are hiring the most forward deployed engineers in the US?
  • On the 177 public job feeds we read on November 20, 2026, Databricks listed 60 US postings with “forward deployed” in the title and Palantir 53. OpenAI listed 21 and Anduril 13. Amazon’s own job search, read separately, returned 57 US roles with the title. One feed entry is one posting, so an employer that posts the same job once per city has more postings than jobs.
  • How many forward deployed engineer jobs are open in the US?
  • Nobody has a full count, and this page is not one. On 177 public job feeds read on November 20, 2026, 298 postings titled “forward deployed” listed a US location, or 284 without interns. Amazon showed 57 more US roles and ServiceNow 6 in a partial read. Employers on Workday and on their own careers systems are mostly missing.
  • Where are forward deployed engineer jobs located?
  • Mostly New York and San Francisco. Of the 65 employers with a US posting on the feeds we read on November 20, 2026, 42 name New York in their US locations and 37 name San Francisco or the San Francisco Bay Area. 28 name both and 14 name neither. Seven employers name Washington, D.C.
  • Do forward deployed engineer roles need a security clearance?
  • Most do not mention one. 63 of the 298 US postings, at 11 employers, had clearance wording in the ad text on November 20, 2026. Palantir accounts for 36 of the 63 and Anduril for 10. The count is a keyword match, so required, preferred and eligible-to-obtain wording are counted together.
  • Is forward deployed engineer hiring up since October?
  • On a like-for-like basis it is flat. The same 62 feeds we checked on October 11, 2026, showed 312 postings on 27 feeds then and 308 on 26 feeds on November 20, 2026, in all locations. Without interns it was 291 then and 292 now. The larger totals on this page come from reading 115 more feeds, not from growth.
  • Why is an employer I know is hiring not in the table?
  • Most likely because we could not read its jobs the same way. The count covers Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever feeds, plus Amazon, ServiceNow and Deloitte read through their own job search. Employers on Workday, including Accenture, Booz Allen Hamilton and NVIDIA, refused our requests on the day. A role without “forward deployed” in its title is also not counted.
