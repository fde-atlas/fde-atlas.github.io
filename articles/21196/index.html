Comparison of Celonis FDE job openings with the internal job title Value Engineer | Tokyo

Original title: CelonisのFDE求人は社内名Value Engineer|東京の募集職種と比較

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-11-14 · 21 min read

Summary

Celonis posted two Forward Deployed Engineer openings (Munich, Nov 9; Paris, Nov 10, 2026), but both listings state the internal title is Senior Value Engineer in the Value Engineering department. The role centers on value estimation, PoVs, and business cases from deal closing through renewal, with technical requirements limited to Python or SQL knowledge. The editors compared all 264 listings on Celonis's job board and found the identical Munich posting published under six different job titles. Actual on-site implementation through production is handled by Deployment Engineers in the Services department, with Tokyo openings (6+ and 12+ years experience) requiring fluent Japanese. The lesson: judge roles by body text, department, and verbs, not job titles.

Key points

  • Celonis's FDE listings carry the internal title Senior Value Engineer.
  • One Munich posting body appeared under six different job titles.
  • The FDE role is value selling; tech requirements are just Python or SQL.
  • Tokyo Deployment Engineer roles require Japanese and 6+ or 12+ years experience.
  • Job titles alone are unreliable; read body text, department, and verbs.

What is required of a Value Engineer with the FDE name?

求められるのは、データ分析と業務改善で顧客の価値を示してきた経験と、経営層と話す力です。技術の要件は「PythonかSQLの知識」と、RPAやBIツールの導入、ダッシュボードやアプリを作った経験にとどまり、開発経験の年数は書かれていません[1]。理想的な経歴としては、ソフトウェア会社のソリューションコンサルティング、セールスエンジニアリング、ITや経営のコンサルティングが挙がっています[1]。

Its focus differs from what one generally imagines when hearing "FDE"—a job embedded at customer sites writing code.

Which role handles implementation at customer sites?

顧客先で実装を担うのは、Services部門のDeployment Engineerです。Celonisは東京の求人で、Servicesを自社の顧客対応部門(Customer Organization)の「納品のエンジン」と呼び、Deployment Engineerを「Celonisの納品の背骨」と位置づけています[8]。担う範囲は、要件を決めるワークショップから、データの接続、設計、構築、価値の測定、本番稼働と顧客への引き継ぎまでです[8]。

What can people looking for FDE jobs learn from these examples?

学べるのは、FDEという職種名は会社によって指す仕事が大きく違い、同じ会社の中でも職務の名前ではなく呼び名の1つとして使われることがあるということです。Celonisでは、FDEの名前が付いた求人は価値提案の職務で、実装の職務には別の名前が付いていました[1][8]。

  • 本文の冒頭と社内名を読む。 Celonisのように、本文に社内名を書いている会社がある。社内名が分かれば、同じ会社のほかの求人と比べられる[1]
  • 部門を見る。 CelonisのFDE求人はValue Engineering部門、東京の実装職はServices部門に属している[1][8]。ただし、マドリードのValue Engineer系の求人はServices部門に置かれており、部門だけでは決められない[15]
  • Count the verbs. Whether the focus is on "communicate, show, drive" or on "build, validate, put into operation" reveals where the weight of the work lies
  • FDE以外の職種名でも探す。 東京のDeployment Engineerのように、職務がFDEに近くても職種名にFDEを含まない求人がある[8]

The relabeling of job titles is not unique to Celonis. The example of Cognition aligning job titles to FDE without changing the body text is covered in Cognition's FDE posting, and how the proliferation of job titles inflates the number of postings is covered in Is it true that FDE postings grew 4.6x in a year. The general differences between roles close to FDE are summarized in The difference between FDE and Solution Architect.

  • The reason for varying job titles. Celonis does not explain the reason in the postings. It is unclear whether they are comparing how applicants gather by job title, or matching local naming conventions by region

Frequently Asked Questions

Are QCelonis's FDE and Value Engineer the same role?

As of November 14, 2026, it is the same on Celonis job postings. The FDE postings for Munich and Paris explicitly state the internal title "Senior Value Engineer" in the body text, and the Munich posting for Retail & Consumer Goods had a body identical to a posting titled "Value Engineer." Both belong to the Value Engineering department.

What does a Value Engineer at Celonis do?

It is a role that demonstrates the value of Celonis to customer companies and drives adoption and expansion. You create value estimates, PoVs, business cases, and demos, run value discovery workshops, and handle everything from winning deals to expanding usage and contract renewals. Technical requirements include knowledge of Python or SQL and experience implementing RPA or BI tools.

What does a Deployment Engineer at Celonis do?

It is a role in the Services department responsible for implementing Celonis at customer sites. You handle everything from requirements gathering to data connection, design, build, impact measurement, production go-live, and handover. The Tokyo posting includes having the company's AI agents create data models and dashboards and verifying them as part of the role, and requires proficiency in PQL, SQL, and Python, 6+ years of experience (12+ years for Principal), and Japanese.

Is Japanese required to apply for Celonis jobs in Tokyo?

As of November 14, 2026, the Tokyo Deployment Engineer (Senior and Principal) positions require fluency in Japanese and business-level English. The Orbit Program for new graduates requires both Japanese and English. The Value Engineer posting is written in both Japanese and English, with English listed as a preferred qualification.

Do Celonis FDE postings mention compensation?

No. Neither the Munich and Paris FDE postings nor the Tokyo postings list compensation amounts; they only mention that equity compensation (RSUs) is granted to all full-time employees.
