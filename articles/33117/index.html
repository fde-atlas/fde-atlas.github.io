Rackspace holds about 400 Palantir certifications

Kernavique · 2026-07-10 · 2 min read

Summary

On July 10, 2026, Rackspace Technology said, in a release read as a copy on iTWire, that it had launched an operating framework with Palantir for regulated enterprises. Rackspace said it “has scaled to approximately 400 Palantir certifications”, including a large global cohort of Palantir-certified forward deployed engineers. The release gives no count of the engineers themselves.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: iTWire (reported).

What happened

Rackspace said the two companies’ initial announcement was in February 2026. The release claims a 94% reduction in quote cycle time and a first joint deployment closed in under 2 months. These are the company’s own figures.

The framework is aimed at regulated enterprises, and healthcare is named. The release quotes Alex Karp, Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Palantir Technologies Inc., and Gajen Kandiah, Chief Executive Officer of Rackspace Technology. Rackspace’s own newsroom page was not opened.

“Sovereign AI requires more than access to a model.”

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

Palantir certification is the credential named. If you work on Palantir’s platform, this suggests the certification carries weight with services firms. The release does not say how many certified forward deployed engineers Rackspace has.

If you are hiring them

Rackspace shows a services firm building a certified cohort on one vendor’s platform. It counts certifications, approximately 400, not people, so the figure does not show how large the forward deployed team is.
