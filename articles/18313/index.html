Announcement of the Launch of a PoC for Next-Generation Inquiry Process Automation Using the Interactive AI Agent "Terry2"

Original title: 対話型AIエージェント「Terry2」による次世代問い合わせ業務自動化PoC開始のお知らせ

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-06-19 · 3 min read

Summary

Hmcomm, a Tokyo-based AI voice technology company, announced a proof-of-concept with Okayama-based Zip to automate inquiry-handling operations using its conversational AI agent Terry2. Rather than conventional contracted development, the project applies a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) approach: redesigning inquiry workflows themselves as AI-native on top of the Terry2 platform. The design avoids complex RAG operations and large-scale data preparation, relying instead on lightweight, fast prompt-based control so business departments can run their own AI improvement cycles. Partnering with Zip, which runs contact center and BPO operations, the PoC aims to build a working model for AI agents in real operations, targeting automation, cost reduction, lighter knowledge management, and faster business rollout. The PoC starts June 19, 2026.

Key points

  • Hmcomm and Zip launch a Terry2 inquiry-automation PoC.
  • FDE approach redesigns workflows as AI-native.
  • Prompt-centric design avoids complex RAG operations.
  • Goals: automation, cost cuts, faster rollout.
  • PoC begins June 19, 2026.

Full text

~ Promoting Social Implementation Leveraging "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" in Partnership with ZIP Co., Ltd. ~

Hmcomm Co., Ltd. (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director, President and CEO: Koji Sanamoto; hereinafter "Hmcomm") hereby announces that it has formally decided internally today to launch a PoC (proof of concept) for automating inquiry handling operations using its conversational AI agent "Terry2" in partnership with ZIP Co., Ltd. (Head office: Setouchi City, Okayama Prefecture; Representative Director and President: Hiroshi Kanzaki; hereinafter "ZIP"), as detailed below.

1. Overview of This Initiative

This PoC aims to verify the potential of transitioning inquiry handling operations to next-generation operations leveraging generative AI and voice AI.

In partnership with ZIP, we will apply a voice- and text-capable AI agent using Terry2 to the inquiry handling domain and proceed with verification in view of actual operations.

2. About the "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" Approach

Unlike conventional contract development models, this initiative adopts an "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" approach that redesigns the inquiry handling workflow itself to be AI-native, based on our proprietary conversational AI agent platform "Terry2."

In addition, this PoC does not assume complex RAG operations or large-scale data preparation, but instead adopts a lightweight, high-speed AI operation model centered on prompt control, featuring a structure that allows business departments to drive AI improvement cycles on their own.

Furthermore, by partnering with ZIP, which possesses actual know-how in operating inquiry handling services, we aim not merely at technical validation but at building a model for utilizing AI agents in real operational environments.

3. Expected Benefits

Through this, we aim to build the following new business infrastructure models:

  • Automation of inquiry handling
  • Reduction of operational costs
  • Streamlining of knowledge management
  • Faster business launch

With these, we aim to build a new business infrastructure model that differs from conventional contact center/BPO operations.

4. Future Outlook

Going forward, we will continue to leverage our "Voice x AI" technology foundation to promote the social implementation of next-generation business infrastructure centered on AI agents, and, focusing primarily on the BPO and contact center domains, aim to enhance corporate value over the medium to long term while strengthening collaboration with operational partners.
