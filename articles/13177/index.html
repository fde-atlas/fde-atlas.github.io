State of FDE 2026 survey

FDE Council · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

Summary

The FDE Council is preparing State of FDE 2026, an anonymous community survey on how the FDE role is defined, paid, staffed and trained. It targets current or recent FDEs and similar embedded-engineering roles, team leads, and hiring managers, and takes roughly 10–12 minutes. Topics include background, optional compensation, skills and AI tooling, customers and industries, career paths, hiring and training, and views on credentials. Results will be published free in aggregate form and serve as early input to the framework's job task analysis, which they do not replace. Privacy commitments include anonymity, no name/email/employer collection, country-level location only, a North America focus, aggregation with groups under 10 respondents combined or dropped, and no selling of data.

Key points

  • Anonymous FDE Council survey, planned but not yet open.
  • Targets FDEs, related embedded roles, leads, and hiring managers.
  • Covers compensation, skills, AI stack, careers, hiring, credentials.
  • Takes about 10–12 minutes; aggregate results published free.
  • Groups under 10 respondents combined or excluded; North America focus.

An anonymous community survey on how the FDE role is defined, paid, staffed and trained. Results will be published free, in aggregate, for everyone.

What it will cover

  • Role and background
  • Compensation (optional, encouraged)
  • Skills, tools and AI stack
  • Customers and industry
  • Career path
  • Hiring and training
  • Views on credentials

Who should take it

People working as FDEs or in closely related embedded-engineering roles now or in the last 24 months, people who lead FDE teams, and FDE hiring managers.

Time

About 10–12 minutes (estimate; to be confirmed in pilot).

How it feeds the framework

Results give early input to the framework’s job task analysis. They don’t replace it.

Our privacy commitments

  • Anonymous. We won’t ask for your name, email or employer in the survey.
  • Location is limited to country, state/province and (US only) metro area. We’re focusing on North America (US, Canada, Mexico) first; responses from elsewhere are reported separately or left out if too few.
  • We publish aggregates only. Any group with fewer than 10 respondents is combined or left out.
  • We never sell responses or use them for marketing.
  • If you want a copy of the report, you’ll use a separate form that can’t be linked to your answers.

Tell me when the survey opens.

The survey is open.

Leave your email and we’ll send one note when State of FDE 2026 opens.

Thank you for taking part. It takes about 10–12 minutes.
