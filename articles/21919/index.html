Hakuhodo Launches "DATA GEAR for AI-Transformation," an FDE-Type Consulting Service That Co-Creates AI Agents with Client Frontline Employees to Support AI-Driven Organizational Transformation

Original title: 博報堂、クライアント現場社員とAIエージェントを共作しAI-Drivenな組織変革を支援するFDE型コンサルティングサービス「DATA GEAR for AI-Transformation」の提供開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-10-26 · 5 min read

Summary

Hakuhodo has launched DATA GEAR for AI-Transformation, an FDE-style consulting service in which specialist teams embed at client worksites and co-build AI agents with frontline employees rather than delivering finished tools. Full text

Hakuhodo Inc. (Headquarters: Minato-ku, Tokyo; President and CEO: Kenji Nagura; hereinafter "Hakuhodo") has launched "DATA GEAR for AI-Transformation," an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)-type consulting service in which specialized teams embed themselves in clients' business operations and co-create AI agents together with on-site employees, transforming people, work, and organizations around AI.

Rather than developing and delivering AI agents itself, Hakuhodo works alongside the client's on-site employees—who understand the work best—to practice everything from identifying tasks where AI can be used, to creating and implementing AI agents, to operating and improving them. Through this process, "people," "work," and "organization" are transformed together in an AI-Driven way, supporting organizational change that enables on-site employees to continuously transform their work by using, building, and fixing AI themselves.

■ Background of Development

While the adoption of generative AI platforms for enterprises is advancing, actual usage often remains limited to individual-level applications such as summarizing meeting minutes or drafting documents. Challenges have also emerged, such as "not knowing where to use it in our work," "usage rules are unclear, so adoption doesn't progress," and "AI tools created by individuals don't remain as organizational assets."

Meanwhile, advances in generative AI have dramatically lowered the barrier to creating AI agents. Even without specialized development knowledge, business owners themselves are increasingly able to set natural-language instructions and reference materials to build AI agents tailored to their own work.

In light of these changes, Hakuhodo believes it is essential that on-site employees, who understand the work best, think about how to use AI together with AI experts and improve it while embedding it into actual operations. Going beyond simply introducing AI tools or providing training, Hakuhodo transforms work processes, people's roles, and organizational structures as a whole through on-site co-creation, in order to create a state in which employees themselves can continuously transform their work by using, building, and fixing AI.

In addition, since June 2025, Hakuhodo has offered "DATA GEAR for AI-Ready," which organizes the processes, data, and organization of individual tasks into forms that can be delegated to AI. "DATA GEAR for AI-Ready" rebuilds individual tasks around AI, while this service spreads throughout the organization the practice of employees using, building, and fixing AI themselves, supporting "AI-Transformation" in which people, work, and organizations continuously change with AI as the premise.

■ The "AI-Driven" State This Service Aims For

This service assesses an organization's AI adoption across five levels of maturity, supporting the transition from a state where AI remains merely a convenient personal tool to one where the way work is done itself changes.

■ Three Support Areas of This Service

Through FDE-type on-site accompaniment, Hakuhodo provides integrated support across three areas: "people, work, and organization." Starting from co-creating AI agents with on-site employees, it consistently supports the development of talent who can use and build AI, the design of work processes incorporating AI, and the establishment of mechanisms to continuously leverage AI agents and know-how as organizational assets.

■ Key Features of This Service

① FDE-Type Support That Embeds On-Site and Co-Creates with Employees

Hakuhodo's specialized teams embed themselves in clients' business operations and co-create AI agents with on-site employees. Support is provided not only by engineers but also by consultants who understand the business and planners who move organizations, so in addition to systems that actually work, it is possible to leave behind people who can build systems and highly reproducible frameworks, enabling employees to continue using and improving AI even after the support ends.

② Guidelines That Clarify What Can Be Delegated to AI, Not Just What Is "Prohibited"

Rather than merely defining prohibited items, the guidelines organize, for each task, "what AI can execute on its own," "what should be executed after human review," and "what humans should decide," promoting AI adoption while ensuring safety.

③ Designing Adoption as a Communication Challenge

Viewing the adoption of AI as internal behavioral change, Hakuhodo designs initiatives based on usage status, supporting continuous use and organizational penetration.

[Five Levels of AI Adoption and Examples of Concrete Measures]

1) Awareness (knows it exists and its scope of use) ⇒ Communicate through management messages and internal PR

2) First use (has used it at work at least once) ⇒ Eliminate initial barriers through department-specific training, template distribution, etc.

3) Continued use (uses it at least once a week) ⇒ Indicate where to use it and embed it into work flows, etc.

4) Proactive use (builds AI agents themselves and uses them for decision-making) ⇒ Support co-creation and in-house development through workshops that deepen inquiry and ambassador development

5) Advocacy (teaches others) ⇒ Build communities and create opportunities for recognition and presentations

Going forward, as part of the activities of HCAI Professionals, a cross-group collective of AI specialists within the Hakuhodo DY Group, Hakuhodo will continue to support client companies as an accompanying partner through its data/AI utilization solution suite "DATA GEAR," helping them connect generative AI to actual business results.
