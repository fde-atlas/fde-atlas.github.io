Deployed Lab · 2026-11-15 · 9 min read

Summary

Deployed Lab's November 15, 2026 snapshot lists 367 open forward deployed engineer, deployment strategist, and applied AI roles across 10 companies, with base pay taken from public job boards. Among the 144 openings that publish US-dollar ranges, the median midpoint is about $243K, with posted base salaries spanning $146K to $445K; equity and bonuses are excluded. OpenAI has the most openings (95), followed by Palantir (91, no pay published) and Databricks (86). Anthropic's posted ranges reach $400K, and Harvey's midpoints are the highest at $302K. The article also distinguishes FDEs from solutions engineers: FDEs build and own production systems at customers rather than supporting pre-sale evaluation.

What does a forward deployed engineer do?

A forward deployed engineer (FDE) is a software engineer who works directly with customers to make a product work in their real environment: scoping the problem, building the integration, shipping it to production and handing it over. Companies from Palantir to the large AI labs hire for it under titles such as Forward Deployed Engineer, Deployment Strategist and Applied AI Engineer or Architect.

New to the role? Start with what an FDE actually does, then try the free LLM VRAM calculator and the RAG lab to see the kind of technical depth these roles expect.

Frequently asked questions

How much do forward deployed engineers make?

Across the 144 openings in this snapshot that publish a US-dollar base salary range, the midpoint of the posted ranges has a median of about $243K, and posted bases run from $146K to $445K. These are base salaries only (no equity or bonus), and they vary by company, level and location.

Which companies hire forward deployed engineers?

In this snapshot: OpenAI, Palantir, Databricks, Anthropic, Scale AI, Cohere, Decagon, Ramp, Harvey, Stripe. Job boards change daily, so check the posting for the current status.

Is a forward deployed engineer the same as a solutions engineer?

No. Solutions engineers usually help prospects evaluate a product before a deal closes, while forward deployed engineers build and own what runs in production at the customer. The free first chapter compares the roles side by side.

Where does this data come from?

From the public job-board interfaces that companies publish for their own careers pages (Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever). We keep titles that match forward deployed engineer, deployment strategist and applied AI engineer or architect roles, and exclude management and recruiting roles. This is an independent snapshot, not an endorsement by or affiliation with any employer, and company names and logos belong to their owners.
