Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-04 · 12 min read

Summary

This FDE Dispatch snapshot (November 3, 2026) catalogs Anthropic's public Forward Deployed Engineer postings: six in-page seats across London, Munich, Paris, New York, San Francisco and Seattle, spanning engineer, Staff engineer, and manager tracks, with two later manager openings in Munich and Paris excluded from totals. It details per-seat experience requirements, Python and production LLM expectations, travel and office policies, posted salary ranges, visa sponsorship, and education lines, plus a November 8 live check confirming six seats still open.

Key points

  • Six in-page postings: 3 engineers, 1 Staff, 2 managers; none remote.
  • London asks 4+ years; Munich and Paris 8+ years plus native German or French.
  • Non-Staff engineer seats require production LLM work including evaluation frameworks and MCP artifacts.
  • Travel is 25-50% on most seats (25% for Staff); office attendance at least 25%.
  • Staff pay is $280k-$320k; NYC Manager tops out at $405k.

Anthropic posts Forward Deployed Engineer seats in London, Munich and Paris, a Staff Forward Deployed Engineer seat across New York City, San Francisco and Seattle, and Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering seats in New York City and London. In FDE Dispatch's November 3, 2026 snapshot of public job boards it has 6 open in-page postings, tied for 19th of 114 employers with Applied Intuition, C3 AI, Clay, DevRev and Invisible Technologies (18 employers have more). What Anthropic calls the role

Anthropic posts under three title tracks in this snapshot: Forward Deployed Engineer; Staff Forward Deployed Engineer; and Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering. The London, Munich and Paris Forward Deployed Engineer texts place the role on the Applied AI team and say the hire will serve as one of our founding FDEs (London, Munich, Paris). The Staff text places the role on the GTM team (Staff). Manager NYC and London texts say As the first FDE Manager (NYC, London). See Forward Deployed Engineering manager for how management titles compare across employers.

Staff scope language includes Customers bring you in to lead, not only to implement, Own technical strategy for a key account or vertical, and work with VPs and C-level stakeholders (Staff posting).

What the postings ask for

Years differ by seat. London Forward Deployed Engineer states 4+ years of experience in a technical, customer facing role (London). Munich and Paris state 8+ years of experience in a technical, customer facing role (Munich, Paris). Staff gives no number: Experience in a senior technical, customer facing role (Staff). Managers state 10+ years plus 2+ years of people management (Manager NYC, Manager London). Every text also says Years of experience required will correlate with the internal job level requirements for the position.

All four engineer texts ask for proficiency in Python (and ideally in one or more additional languages like TypeScript, Java, etc). Staff adds everyday use of AI coding tools with the judgment to review and steer.

The three non-Staff Forward Deployed Engineer texts ask for Production experience with LLMs including advanced prompt engineering, agent development, evaluation frameworks, and deployment at scale. and to Deliver technical artifacts for customers like MCP servers, sub-agents, and agent skills that will be used in production workflows. Staff has no evaluation-frameworks line. It says You don't need to have shipped agents to production. and Hands-on experience with LLMs and agents is expected. Side projects and prototypes count.

Munich asks for Native German speaker with fluent English proficiency. Paris asks for Native French speaker with fluent English proficiency. All four engineer texts say Former technical founders are also encouraged to apply. Financial services and healthcare/life sciences appear as a plus or nice to have. The excluded GTM Strategy & Operations, FDE preferred line asks for Enough technical fluency to talk with engineers about how LLM applications are built (agents, evals, MCP) (that seat closed on the November 8, 2026 live check and is not linked).

Where the roles are and the office policy

Of the 6 in-page postings, 2 are US-located (Staff across NYC/SF/Seattle; Manager NYC) and 4 are outside the US (London and Munich/Paris engineer seats, plus Manager London). None of the Anthropic rows list the role as remote. Every posting text says we expect all staff to be in one of our offices at least 25% of the time.

Metro figures for every employer are on Forward Deployed Engineer jobs by location.

Three of Anthropic's four engineer postings ask for production LLM experience that includes evaluation frameworks. FDE Dispatch participants build that record one-to-one: each ships a live AI deployment for a real business with an evaluation suite behind it, then rehearses each round in recorded simulations with written feedback.

How much Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineers travel

All 6 in-page postings state a travel percentage. 5 state 25-50% (the three Forward Deployed Engineer seats and both Managers). Staff states 25%, matching the Anthropic row on how much forward deployed engineers travel. Engineer wording is Travel frequently (25-50%) to customer sites to build in person with customers. on the non-Staff seats and Travel frequently (25%) to customer sites to build in person with customers. on Staff. Manager wording is Travel to customer sites as needed (25-50%).

Visa and education lines

Every Anthropic posting text in this cut says We do sponsor visas! However, we aren't able to successfully sponsor visas for every role and every candidate. The visa sponsorship guide counts Anthropic as a sponsor employer (4 postings in its 651-text index).

Every text also states Bachelor’s degree or an equivalent combination of education, training, and/or experience, consistent with the degree guide, which lists Anthropic under required_or_equivalent.

What this means if you are preparing for an Anthropic loop

  • Seat and level. London states 4+ years; Munich and Paris state 8+ years and a native-language line; Staff asks for senior customer-facing scope without a years number; Managers ask for 10+ years plus people-management time. Read the title and location before you decide which seat fits.
  • Production LLM evidence. The three non-Staff Forward Deployed Engineer texts ask for production LLM work that includes evaluation frameworks, plus MCP servers, sub-agents and agent skills as customer artifacts. Staff softens the agents bar and counts side projects. Bring a shipped example that matches the seat you are applying to.
  • Travel and office. Expect 25-50% customer travel on most seats (25% on Staff), and an office expectation of at least 25% of the time. None of these rows are marked remote.

Round formats for FDE loops are on the FDE interview loop, explained, and practice prompts on FDE interview practice questions.

Questions people ask

Does Anthropic hire Forward Deployed Engineers?

Yes. Anthropic had 6 open FDE-family postings on its public Greenhouse board in the November 3, 2026 snapshot, tied for 19th among 114 employers with Applied Intuition, C3 AI, Clay, DevRev and Invisible Technologies. 4 are engineer titles (Forward Deployed Engineer in London, Munich and Paris, plus Staff Forward Deployed Engineer in New York City, San Francisco and Seattle) and 2 are Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering titles (New York City and London). A GTM Strategy and Operations, FDE seat is in the file as adjacent non-engineering and is excluded from those totals. On November 8, 2026, 6 of the 7 snapshot rows were still open; the excluded GTM Strategy and Operations, FDE seat had closed. Six postings is a small sample.

How much does an Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer make?

Pay figures are the Annual Salary lines as posted. Forward Deployed Engineer London lists £225,000 to £255,000 GBP. Munich and Paris engineer seats list no range in the text. Staff Forward Deployed Engineer (NYC/SF/Seattle) lists $280,000 to $320,000 USD. Manager New York City lists $320,000 to $405,000 USD; Manager London lists £255,000 to £325,000 GBP. The excluded GTM Strategy and Operations, FDE seat lists $270,000 to $310,000 USD. Every text also carries sales-role OTE boilerplate; the FDE and manager ranges are labeled Annual Salary, so read them as annual salary ranges. The Staff USD midpoint of $300,000 sits above the US engineer-title median midpoint of $216,104 across 255 US engineer-title postings in the October 24, 2026 salary index. GBP and EUR ranges are left out of that comparison. Posted ranges are not offers.

What do Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer postings ask for?

Years differ by seat: London Forward Deployed Engineer states 4+ years; Munich and Paris state 8+ years; Staff gives no number and asks for experience in a senior technical, customer facing role; Managers state 10+ years plus 2+ years of people management. All four engineer texts ask for proficiency in Python and ideally TypeScript, Java or similar. The three non-Staff Forward Deployed Engineer texts ask for production LLM experience that includes evaluation frameworks, and for MCP servers, sub-agents and agent skills as customer artifacts. Staff has no evaluation-frameworks line; it says you do not need to have shipped agents to production, and that hands-on LLM and agent experience is expected, including side projects. Munich asks for a native German speaker with fluent English; Paris asks for a native French speaker with fluent English. Former technical founders are encouraged on the engineer texts.

Are Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer jobs remote?

None of the Anthropic postings in this snapshot list the role as remote. Every posting text says Anthropic expects staff to be in one of its offices at least 25% of the time, and some roles may require more.

Does Anthropic sponsor visas for Forward Deployed Engineer roles?

Every Anthropic posting text in this cut says Anthropic does sponsor visas, with the caveat that it is not able to successfully sponsor visas for every role and every candidate. The visa sponsorship guide counts Anthropic as a sponsor employer (4 postings in its 651-text index).
