Fujitsu Launches "Global Fujitsu Distinguished Engineer" Program

Original title: 「Global Fujitsu Distinguished Engineer」制度を開始

info.archives.global.fujitsu · 2021-12-06 · 4 min read

Summary

Fujitsu announced in December 2021 the launch of its Global Fujitsu Distinguished Engineer (Global FDE) program, which certifies top engineers worldwide against a common standard based on technical expertise and business contribution in key technology areas. The title is linked to Fujitsu's job-based HR system, clarifying career paths and providing role models for engineers. Seven certification areas were set for fiscal 2021: hybrid IT, networks, cybersecurity, AI, data, computing, and project management, to be revised as business needs change. Thirty-two engineers were certified globally in 2021, with annual certifications targeting roughly 100 by fiscal 2023. The program supports the Fujitsu Uvance brand and its technology strategy announced in October 2021.

Key points

  • Fujitsu launched the Global FDE certification for its highest-tier engineers.
  • Certification uses global common criteria on expertise and business contribution.
  • Seven areas defined for fiscal 2021, revisable over time.
  • 32 engineers certified in 2021; target of about 100 by fiscal 2023.
  • The program ties into job-based HR and the Fujitsu Uvance strategy.

Engineers with outstanding technical expertise to accelerate global business, technology, and talent strategies

Fujitsu today announced the launch of the "Global Fujitsu Distinguished Engineer" (hereinafter, "Global FDE") program, which certifies, under a globally unified standard, engineers who possess outstanding technical expertise in technology areas critical to the company's future business growth strategy and who contribute to business strategy and the creation of customer value. Under this program, the highest-caliber engineers across the Fujitsu Group will be recognized and given titles, and engineers will be treated in conjunction with the job-based personnel system, clarifying their career paths.

Global FDEs will contribute to Fujitsu's growth by continuously advancing technology to create impact in society. As a technology company, Fujitsu will accelerate the creation of innovation by leveraging technologies aligned with its key focus areas, contributing to the development of people, society, and the economy.

Background of the Program

In October 2021, Fujitsu established its new business brand "Fujitsu Uvance," aiming to realize its purpose of "making the world more sustainable by building trust in society through innovation." Under Fujitsu Uvance, the company will accelerate the creation of innovation by leveraging technologies in line with its technology strategy (Note 2) supporting the businesses on which it will focus to realize its purpose (hereinafter, Key Focus Areas (Note 1)).

As the face of Fujitsu's technology, Global FDEs will play a leading role in solving customers' business challenges through the application of their outstanding technical expertise. As the highest-caliber engineers in the Fujitsu Group, they will also participate in formulating the company's business and technology strategies. In addition, they will be positioned as role models for engineers under the job-based personnel system, playing an important role in further accelerating the company's human resources strategy.

With their high level of technical expertise and experience, Global FDEs will serve as technology experts in critical technology areas for the future, solving highly difficult technical challenges and contributing to the realization of business strategy and the creation of customer value.

Figure: Global FDEs accelerate global business, technology, and talent strategies

Certification Process

To realize Fujitsu's business strategy, this program evaluates and certifies engineers under globally unified criteria, regardless of nationality, age, or gender, based on their expertise and technical proficiency in current and future critical technology areas, as well as their business contributions. For fiscal 2021, seven certification areas were established: "Hybrid IT," "Network," "Cybersecurity," "AI," "Data," "Computing," and "Project Management." The certification areas will be reviewed as needed in line with business conditions.

Future Plans

In fiscal 2021, 32 individuals worldwide (Note 3) were certified as Global FDEs. Going forward, certifications will be conducted annually, with a goal of approximately 100 Global FDEs by fiscal 2023.

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), adopted by the United Nations in 2015, are common goals that the world as a whole should achieve by 2030. Fujitsu's purpose—"making the world more sustainable by building trust in society through innovation"—represents a commitment to contributing to the SDGs.
