FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) Salary Range | Up to ¥30 Million, 75 Listings | FDE board

Original title: FDE(Forward Deployed Engineer)の年収相場｜最高3,000万円・掲載75件｜FDE board

株式会社ウェステリア · 2026-11-10 · 2 min read

Summary

This article from FDE board (operated by 株式会社ウェステリア) compiles salary data from 75 active Forward Deployed Engineer job listings in Japan, updated November 2026. Among listings that disclose salary (41% of the total), the median upper bound is ¥15 million. The largest share of listings (45%) falls in the ¥12–16 million range, while 35% exceed ¥16 million. The highest listed ceilings, ¥30 million, appear at キャディ株式会社 and 株式会社VARIETAS. The article cautions that figures cover only its own listings, foreign-currency salaries are converted at 1 USD = ¥150, and listed ceilings are not guaranteed offers.

Key points

  • Based on 75 active FDE listings on FDE board, updated 2026-11-10.
  • Median salary ceiling among disclosed listings is ¥15 million.
  • 45% of listings cap between ¥12–16 million; 35% exceed ¥16 million.
  • Top ceiling of ¥30 million at キャディ and VARIETAS.
  • Figures are listing ceilings only; actual offers depend on experience and interviews.

How to read these figures

  • The tally covers only job postings currently listed on FDE board. It does not necessarily cover all FDE job openings in Japan.
  • Postings denominated in foreign currency are converted to yen at 1 USD = 150 JPY, with the original figures shown on the job detail pages.
  • Salaries in job postings are the "upper limit of the range"; actual offers depend on experience and the selection process.
