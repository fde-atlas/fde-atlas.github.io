Wipro says it has 1,500+ certified forward deployed engineers

Kernavique · 2026-08-27 · 2 min read

Summary

Kernavique reports that on August 27, 2026, Wipro announced an expanded Google Cloud partnership, saying it will empower over 10,000 AI-certified specialists, of whom more than 1,500 are certified forward deployed engineers, and that Wipro is scaling its internal Gemini Enterprise deployment to these specialists. The release does not state who issues the certification or whether the engineering team is new or existing; CRN Asia describes a dedicated team helping clients move AI from pilots to company-wide rollout. Kernavique's reading: a large services firm now treats forward deployed engineers as a named subset of a wider certified pool, adding a major employer to competition for these engineers.

Key points

  • Wipro announced an expanded Google Cloud partnership on August 27, 2026.
  • The release cites over 10,000 AI-certified specialists in total.
  • More than 1,500 are certified forward deployed engineers, within that pool.
  • The certifying body and whether the team is new are unspecified.
  • Kernavique sees this as a large employer entering competition for FDEs.

On August 27, 2026, Wipro announced an expanded partnership with Google Cloud, in a release read as a copy on CXOToday. The release says the partnership will empower over 10,000 AI-certified specialists, including 1,500 forward deployed engineers. The same text also says more than 1,500 certified forward deployed engineers. As written, the 1,500 are within the 10,000, not additional.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: CXOToday.

What happened

The release says Wipro is scaling its internal Gemini Enterprise deployment to these specialists. Kevin Ichhpurani, President, Global Partner Ecosystem at Google Cloud, and Kanwar Singh, Managing Partner and Global Head of Technology Services at Wipro Limited, are quoted in it.

CRN Asia, in an undated report, describes the creation of a dedicated team of over 1,500 such engineers who help clients move AI from early trials to company-wide rollout. The release text we read does not make clear whether the team already exists or is newly created.

The word certified implies a certification, but the certifying body is not stated in what we read. Intelligent CIO reported on the expanded partnership on August 28, 2026.

Our reading

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

The release counts forward deployed engineers as a named group of about 1,500 inside a pool of over 10,000 AI-certified specialists. What we read does not say who certifies them, so engineers cannot yet judge what the credential involves.

If you are hiring them

This suggests a large services firm can treat forward deployed engineers as a named subset of a wider certified pool, about 1,500 of over 10,000 here. It adds another large employer to the competition for the same engineers.
