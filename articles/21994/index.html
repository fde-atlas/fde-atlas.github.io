Anddot Launches "Resident FDE" Service, Dispatching Staff to Client Companies to Drive DX/AX End-to-End

Original title: アンドドット、顧客企業に常駐しDX／AXを一気通貫で推進する「常駐型FDE」サービスの提供を開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-07-23 · 8 min read

Summary

Japanese company AndDot has launched a resident FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) service in which its engineers embed at client enterprises to handle the full DX/AX cycle: identifying problems, designing solutions, building systems, and ensuring operational adoption, all AI-driven. Projects follow a proprietary four-phase framework called FDE Journey (CPF, PSF, SPF, POF), with explicit sign-offs between phases to reduce rework. Engineer quality is standardized via the CEPO rubric (consulting, engineering, project management, openness); those averaging 4+ on a 5-point scale are designated senior FDEs. Onsite engineers draw on AndDot Knowledge, a shared library of code, architectures, and patterns. The service targets enterprise business DX, starting with one or two engineers. AndDot says it has supported over 350 companies and 300 municipalities, and moved all its engineers to an FDE-based structure in April 2026.

Key points

  • AndDot launched a resident FDE service covering problem discovery through operational adoption.
  • Projects follow a four-phase FDE Journey framework with stage-gate agreements.
  • Engineers are rated on the CEPO rubric; 4+ average earns senior FDE status.
  • Onsite FDEs leverage AndDot Knowledge, a shared code and design library.
  • AndDot has supported 350+ companies and 300+ municipalities; all engineers became FDEs in April 2026.

And Dot Inc. (Headquarters: Shibuya-ku, Tokyo; CEO: Yuta Ibaraki; hereinafter "And Dot"), which supports corporate DX end-to-end through resident FDEs, is launching a service called "Resident FDE," in which an engineer—an "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)"—who deeply understands the business and sees problem-solving through to completion is stationed at the client company, providing AI-driven, end-to-end support from problem discovery through solution planning, system development, and operational adoption. Along with this, a special page introducing the service details (https://www.and-dot.co.jp/lp/fde) was published on July 16, 2026. Since its founding in 2023, And Dot has fostered a culture in which engineers directly solve customer problems, and this new service refines that approach and offers it in a resident format.

What Is Resident FDE?

Resident FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a service in which And Dot engineers embed themselves at the client's workplace as members of the team, accompanying them end-to-end from problem discovery through solution design, development and verification, and operation and adoption. Rather than stopping at one-off tool deployments or concept proposals, under the philosophy of "DX-ing the entire company, thoroughly," we work alongside clients until results take root in the field.

In March 2026, And Dot announced the restructuring of its engineering organization, and from April of the same year transitioned all in-house engineers to an FDE-based structure (announced March 23, 2026: "All In-House Engineers Become 'FDEs.' And Dot Announces Restructuring of Its Engineering Organization"). The "Resident FDE" service now makes the expertise cultivated under this structure directly available to client companies.

Why Fundamental DX (Business Process Automation) Stalls

The main reason DX/AX fails to advance at many large enterprises is that the functions required to drive it are fragmented across the organization. (1) Identifying business problems, (2) planning solutions, (3) implementation and promotion, and (4) establishing operations—these may exist in-house, may be left unassigned and floating, or may be outsourced, and the situation varies unevenly from company to company. Even when each function exists, the absence of cross-functional talent who can connect them end-to-end across departmental and internal/external boundaries means that even identified problems never reach full adoption. In recent years, this has been compounded by a shortage of people who can implement generative AI into business operations.

Right now, "problems that never get solved" continue to pile up inside companies.

An FDE Who Single-Handedly Handles Problem Discovery, Solution Design, Development, and Promotion Is Stationed On-Site

An FDE is an "end-to-end delivery engineer" capable of handling consulting, engineering, and project promotion alone. In the Resident FDE service, this FDE is stationed at the client company, understanding frontline operations firsthand while continuing in-house cycles of problem discovery, solution proposal, system development, and operational adoption until problems are eradicated. Under the philosophy of "viewing problems as lines, not points," improvements keep accumulating as long as the FDE remains on-site.

＜Value Delivered＞

• Startable with just 1–2 people: Areas traditionally handled by teams are flexibly covered by a small number of staff.

• Fast ramp-up with stable quality: Leveraging in-house knowledge, we quickly deliver high-quality results.

• End-to-end from discovery to adoption: Problem discovery, solutions, implementation, and operational adoption are all completed in-house.

• Improvements continue as long as the FDE is there: We continuously address problem-solving that raises the company's value.

Structuring the DX Process: "FDE Journey©"

PhaseActivities
① CPF (Customer Problem Fit)Discover essential in-house problems and prioritize them. Once problem identification is complete, move on to "② PSF."
② PSF (Problem Solution Fit)Evaluate multiple proposals based on business impact, difficulty, and operational load, and agree on one proposal to pursue. After agreement, move on to "③ SPF."
③ SPF (Solution Product Fit)Develop in one-week sprints, involving the field to shape a working product. Once ready for actual operation, move on to "④ POF."
④ POF (Product Operation Fit)Incorporating field feedback, establish a state in which the system is actively used in-house, along with an environment enabling system operation and improvement loops.

All projects proceed through four stages following our proprietary framework, the "FDE Journey©." Because each stage confirms "what has been agreed upon" before moving forward, the structure minimizes rework in later stages.

Structuring Talent Quality: "CEPO©"

The resident FDEs are not people with development skills alone.

They possess four proprietary evaluation criteria, "CEPO©," covering problem analysis, progress management, and even interpersonal engagement.

• C (Consulting): Objectively analyze problems and clarify what should be solved. Structure the real underlying issues.

• E (Engineering): Full-stack development capability leveraging AI. Build across domain boundaries.

• P (Project Management): Manage plans, budgets, progress, and quality. Drive projects themselves as PMs.

• O (Open & Friendly): An interpersonal approach that creates an atmosphere where others feel comfortable sharing their honest views.

Each criterion is rated on a five-point scale in-house, and members averaging 4 or above are defined as "Senior FDEs." This prevents capability from depending on individuals, ensuring a consistent standard as a company.

Structuring Back-End Support: "AndDot Knowledge"

The on-site FDE is not alone. Resident FDEs bring And Dot's accumulated sample code, architectures, design patterns, and expert knowledge on security and industry domains (= AndDot Knowledge) into client companies and use it freely. By not writing everything from scratch, they accelerate ramp-up, stabilize quality based on proven implementations, and spend time on essential improvements.

Target: Enterprise business DX

Primarily, we cover all business operations of large enterprises. If the organization has a DX promotion department or information systems department, we work together with them to pursue business reform and in-house development. Expected areas of application include AI automation of approval and review processes, automation of document creation, renewal of inquiry handling, and automation of data integration and transcription between systems. Based on a deep understanding of each company's operations, we comprehensively deliver high-impact DX.

DX/AX support for 350 companies and 300 municipalities

AndDot has provided AX support to more than 350 companies and more than 300 municipalities to date. In July 2026, it will hold a seminar themed on FDE development, sharing its knowledge of FDE development and support outside the company as well.

Future outlook: Delivering AX execution to the front lines

AndDot will grow its resident FDE service into one of its core offerings, serving as the driving force that takes AI utilization beyond the concept stage and implements and embeds it in the field.

Comment from Representative Director Yuta Ibaraki

Having accompanied many companies on their DX journeys, I am convinced of one thing: "Packaged solutions can only solve problems that are common to multiple companies." Even if operations can be made more efficient, they cannot automate from the root the operations unique to that company. What is truly needed is to build a system optimized for that company, exclusively for that company.

That is what resident FDEs deliver. Thanks to coding automation, it is now possible to turn optimal systems into reality at overwhelming speed. FDEs can, in turn, spend less time writing code and more time digging into customer challenges and defining the questions to be solved. We insist on the resident model precisely because that is where the greatest value lies.

In addition, we have DX promotion frameworks and FDE development knowledge cultivated through past projects. We are proud that this depth is among the best in Japan.

Resident FDEs deliver results on a completely different order of magnitude from initiatives that merely introduce tools.

True to our concept of "thoroughly transforming entire companies with DX," we will contribute to the DX of all kinds of companies.
