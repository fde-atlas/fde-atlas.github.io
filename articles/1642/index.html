Forward deployed engineer salary: pay ranges as employers post them

FDE Dispatch · 2026-10-22 · 11 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch compiled employer-posted pay from 725 live FDE job postings pulled October 24, 2026. Across 255 US engineer-title postings with pay listed, the median posted range is $180,000–$250,208 with a median midpoint of $216,104 ($208,000 counting each employer once). Twenty-four hand-verified postings span floors from $88,970 (Salesforce) to a $390K ceiling (Sierra). The article also covers travel requirements, equity mentions, remote share, top locations, and four factors driving pay differences: location bands, seniority, public-sector/clearance roles, and equity or OTE wording.

Key points

  • Median posted US range: $180,000–$250,208; midpoint $216,104.
  • Per-employer median midpoint is $208,000 across 73 employers.
  • 59.3% of postings mention travel; 20% is the most common stated share.
  • 53.5% of postings mention equity, RSUs, or stock options.
  • Pay varies by location bands, seniority, clearance roles, and OTE wording.

Method and limits

A posting counts when its title contains forward deployed, FDE, deployment strategist, deployed engineer or AI deployment engineer. Engineer, deployment strategist and FDE management titles count in the totals. Titles that borrow the phrase for other jobs, such as forward deployed banker, lawyer, product manager, architect or researcher, stay in the file with a flag and are left out of every figure here. US pay uses engineer titles only, excludes internships, requires every listed location to be in the US and records the first range on each posting. The snapshot removes 9 recruiting-agency postings for an unnamed client and 43 duplicate listings of the same employer, title and location set.

Limits: matching is by title, so FDE work posted under another title is missing. Employers that post only on LinkedIn, Indeed or applicant systems outside these four boards are missing. Posted ranges are not offers and do not include equity value. This is one snapshot with no history yet. None of these figures predicts the pay of any candidate, including anyone in an FDE Dispatch program.

What these posted ranges do and do not tell you

Posted ranges are the pay-transparency figures on a single req: base salary, OTE, or a wide band that covers several levels. They show the floor and ceiling the employer published for that title and location set, and whether the posting mentions equity, bonus, commission, or location bands. They are not a promise of total compensation, and they are not an average across the market.

They do not tell you the offer you will get, the equity grant size, refreshers, signing bonus value, or how often hires land near the top of the band. Location bands, seniority in the title, and public-sector or clearance roles move the numbers inside this table.

What drives FDE pay differences in these postings

Four patterns drive the pay differences in these rows: location bands, seniority in the title, public-sector and clearance roles, and equity or OTE wording.

  • Location bands. Scale Public Sector posts three US base bands. Salesforce posts a typical US base range and a higher band for California, New York, and select metros.
  • Seniority in the title. Scale lists Frontier Agents Engineer and Senior Frontier Agents Engineer as separate postings with different base floors. Palantir lists an experienced FDSE band and a narrower new-grad US Government band; every Palantir US posted range by role family is on Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer.
  • Public-sector and clearance roles. OpenAI Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Gov, Palantir New Grad US Government (clearance or clearance eligibility), and Scale Public Sector sit in this table with their own posted ranges.
  • Equity and OTE wording. Most rows mention equity or RSUs. Harvey posts OTE with a 75/25 split. Salesforce posts base salary only.

How titles differ

Employers post the same family of work under Forward Deployed Engineer, Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Software Engineer, Forward Deployed, Frontier Agents Engineer (Forward Deployed Engineering), Agent Deployment Engineer, Deployed Infrastructure Engineer, and related strings. The title alone does not fix pay. See Forward Deployed Engineer job titles for how companies name the role and which postings list pay, experience, and travel.

What employers ask for in return

The employers in this table ask for production software skills, applied AI and agent work, evaluation, systems integration, customer discovery, and often travel or on-site time. Questions people ask

How much do Forward Deployed Engineers make?

Across 255 US engineer-title FDE postings with posted pay, pulled from public job boards on October 24, 2026, the median posted range runs from $180,000 to $250,208 and the median midpoint is $216,104. Counting each of the 73 employers once, the median midpoint is $208,000. Each figure is a range the employer published. In the twenty-four hand-verified postings checked October 22, 2026, the lowest posted USD floor is $88,970 (Salesforce typical base range) and the highest posted USD ceiling is $390K (Sierra Deployed Infrastructure Engineer).

What is the OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer salary?

As posted on October 22, 2026: OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) - SF lists $185K – $300K + equity. OpenAI Forward Deployed Software Engineer - SF lists $185K – $325K + equity. OpenAI Forward Deployed Software Engineer - NYC lists $153K – $325K + equity. OpenAI Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Gov lists $145.8K – $280K + equity.

What do Anthropic and Palantir post for FDE pay?

Anthropic Staff Forward Deployed Engineer lists an annual salary of $280,000–$320,000 USD. Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer in London lists £225,000–£255,000 GBP. Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer in New York lists $135,000–$200,000/year and may also include RSUs and a sign-on bonus. Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer, New Grad - US Government in Washington, D.C. lists $135,000–$145,000/year with the same RSU and sign-on note, and asks for an active US security clearance or eligibility to obtain one.

Do these Forward Deployed Engineer salary ranges include equity?

It depends on the posting. Twenty-one of the twenty-four rows mention equity, RSUs, or stock options as part of compensation or eligibility. The two Anthropic postings and the 84.51° posting do not mention equity in their pay text. Salesforce says certain roles may be eligible for equity, and states its posted range is base salary only and excludes company bonus, equity, and benefits. Harvey posts OTE with a 75/25 split plus equity. Across the 725 postings in the October 24, 2026 snapshot, 388 (53.5%) mention equity, RSUs or stock options. Posted ranges do not state equity value.

Why do Forward Deployed Engineer pay ranges differ so much across postings?

Differences visible in this table include location bands (Scale Public Sector posts three US bands; Salesforce posts a typical US band and a higher CA/NY metro band), seniority in the title (Scale Frontier Agents vs Senior Frontier Agents; Palantir new grad vs experienced FDSE), public-sector or clearance roles (OpenAI Gov; Palantir New Grad US Government; Scale Public Sector), and whether the figure is base, OTE, or a wide mid/senior band. Title variants are covered on the FDE job titles page.

What do employers ask for in return for these FDE salary ranges?

The same employers ask for production engineering, applied AI or agent work, evaluation, systems integration, customer discovery, and often travel or on-site work. Those requirements are quoted from live postings on the Forward Deployed Engineer skills page.
