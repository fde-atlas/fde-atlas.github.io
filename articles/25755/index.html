Forward Deployed Engineer Program

Forward Deployed Engineer Program · iHUB DivyaSampark, IIT Roorkee · 2025 · 11 min read

Summary

iHUB DivyaSampark, IIT Roorkee's innovation hub, offers a seven-month online certificate program training Forward Deployed Engineers, with a January 2027 batch aimed at graduates and postgraduates in the Middle East. The six-module curriculum plus capstone covers Python, SQL, data pipelines, RAG with citations, bounded AI agents using MCP, and production deployment, requiring 8–10 hours weekly. Instructors come from Microsoft, Google, Thomson Reuters, and Qatar's Ministry of Defense. Total fee is US$1,505 after a US$5 qualifier test. Certification requires 65% attendance and a 30% cumulative score. The page cites rapid FDE job growth and a US$220K median US salary.

Key points

  • Seven-month online FDE certificate program starting January 2027, run with Masai.
  • Six modules plus a real-world capstone; 8–10 hours per week commitment.
  • Covers data pipelines, RAG, controlled agents with MCP, and monitored production deployment.
  • Faculty from Microsoft, Google, Thomson Reuters, and Qatar's Ministry of Defense.
  • US$1,505 total fee; certification needs 65% attendance and 30% cumulative score.

Number and letter series, analogies, odd one out, syllogisms, seating and arrangement puzzles, blood relations, directions, coding-decoding, statements and conclusions, and data sufficiency.

The mode of delivery of the program is online through the Zoom platform.

Yes, international students can apply for the program.

The Registration Fee secures your seat in the program. The Course Fee is the total program cost. Your enrollment is confirmed only after the full Course Fee is paid.

The receipt will be emailed to your registered email address within 24–48 business hours of payment.

The fee payment deadline is available on your dashboard. Kindly ensure you complete the fee payment within the specified timelines to complete your enrolment in the program.

Classes will be conducted on weekdays, in the evening from 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM (local time zone).

You'll need to dedicate approximately 8–10 hours per week, which includes attending live sessions, completing tutorials, and engaging in self-paced study to stay on track with the program.

The class schedule will be communicated primarily through the Learning Management System (LMS).

We recommend that you attend live sessions. In any unforeseen circumstances, if you miss attending live classes, recordings will be made accessible to you on the Learning Management System (LMS). Kindly ensure you watch the entire class recording to ensure you don't miss important concepts covered.

Yes, you'll receive the class schedule and updates every Saturday for the upcoming week, allowing you to plan your time effectively and stay organised.

If you miss a live session, you can still maintain attendance by watching the recorded session within the allowed window. The recording must be watched in full or at least 80% to count toward your attendance.

To successfully complete the program, students must maintain 65% attendance, combining both live sessions and recorded sessions. They must also achieve at least 30% overall score in the evaluations, which include assignments, quizzes, and offline exams. Students must meet both criteria to receive the Certificate of Completion.

Yes, to be eligible for course completion, you must maintain a minimum of 65% attendance, which can be achieved through either live sessions or recorded lectures.

Attendance is calculated automatically through the Learning Management System (LMS). You will be marked present if you complete either of the following in full:

  • Live Attendance: Counted when you join the live session in full using your registered email ID and attend the class.
  • Recording Attendance: Counted when you watch the session recording in full within 30 days of the class.

If a student does not meet the required attendance criteria, they will not be eligible to receive the course completion certificate.

In such cases, you can raise a support ticket with your respective Program Coordinator, along with the necessary supporting documents. Exceptions may be granted after reviewing your request.

Once your enrolment is complete, you will be onboarded on the Learning Management System (LMS). You can raise your queries through ticketing support and your Program Coordinator for all non-conceptual queries during the tenure of the course.

You will have opportunities to engage throughout the program. You can connect with industry mentors and TAs during dedicated doubt-clearing sessions. Additionally, you can interact and collaborate with fellow learners through our learning platforms and private Discord community.

Yes, a dedicated live helpline session is available to address your queries. Details of the session will be shared as part of your weekly schedule to help you stay informed and supported throughout the program.

You will have access to a variety of learning resources, such as notes, pre-reads, class recordings, and assignments to support your learning on the Learning Management System (LMS).

Assessments will be conducted periodically throughout the program, with evaluations at the end of each module and ongoing project work to track your progress.
