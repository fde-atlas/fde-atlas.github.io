Forward Deployed Consultant | Varos | Remote, US

Zero G Talent · 2026-04-16 · 1 min read

Summary

Varos, an agentic AI startup that has raised $10m from investors including YC and Mosaic, is hiring a Forward Deployed Consultant with 3–5 years of experience for a remote US role. The hire will own a portfolio of enterprise accounts, running POCs, guiding onboarding, managing projects, and driving adoption of Varos's AI Business Analyst platform. The role is heavily product-management oriented: product requirements are largely driven by the FDC team's learnings from customers. Varos describes it as a hands-on, early-team position focused on ensuring clients get measurable value.

Varos is a rapidly growing agentic AI startup and we need someone to help support new clients in using the product. We've raised $10m from the best VCs in the world, including YC and Mosaic

We’re looking for a hands-on Forward Deployed Consultant with 3–5 years of experience to join Varos as an early member of our FDC team. You’ll own a focused portfolio of enterprise accounts — conducting POCs, guiding onboarding, managing projects, driving deep adoption of our AI Business Analyst platform, and ensuring clients are consistently extracting measurable value from the product.

The ideal candidate is naturally curious, very detail oriented, likes to use AI tools and is motivated to work hard.

The role also involves a lot of product management related work as product requirements are largely driven by the FDC team and their learning of the customer's needs.

This is a roll up your sleeves, get in the weeds and do an amazing job type of role.
