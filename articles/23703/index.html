Forward Deployed Engineer Path

Forward Deployed · 3 min read

Summary

This is a structured study path for Forward Deployed Engineer candidates interviewing within two to eight weeks, authored by Sourav Banerjee. It contains 95 required, 25 optional, and 22 not-yet-written modules across 27 tracks, spanning AI engineering foundations, RAG, agents, MCP protocols, AI system design, customer-site delivery, and behavioral interviews. Completion criteria include finishing all required modules with confidence ratings of 3+, two timed technical worksheets (45 minutes each), four scored mock interviews with no dimension below 3, and a story bank of 8-12 stories each tellable in under three minutes. Modules already mastered can be skipped. A variant covers loops with coding rounds and 3-5 hour take-home builds.

Key points

  • Targets FDE candidates interviewing in two to eight weeks.
  • 95 required, 25 optional, 22 pending modules across 27 tracks.
  • Covers RAG, agents, MCP, system design, and customer delivery.
  • Requires four scored mock interviews and timed worksheets.
  • Story bank of 8-12 stories, each under three minutes.

Ready When

  • Every required module completed: lessons read and confidence rated 3 or more.
  • Two full technical worksheets answered inside 45 minutes each.
  • Four timed mock interviews scored on the rubric, with no dimension below 3.
  • Eight to twelve stories in your story bank, each told aloud in under three minutes.

When to Skip

Any module you can explain without notes and rate 4 or more for confidence.
