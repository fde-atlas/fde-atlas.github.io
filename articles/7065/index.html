Understanding Anthropic's Applied AI Organization and FDE Role

Educative · 2026-10-06 · 1 min read

Summary

This piece explains how Anthropic's Applied AI department is organized and what its Forward Deployed Engineers do. Applied AI is a standalone unit, separate from Sales, Product, and Research/Engineering, that helps customers take Claude from initial technical evaluation to production systems. The article stresses that FDEs never work alone: customer qualification, deliverables, and value metrics are settled before an FDE writes code, while scheduling, stakeholders, and executive relationships are handled by others. FDEs focus on prototyping, architecture, and gathering field signal, and their insights flow back to improve Claude and the platform after engagements end.

Key points

  • Applied AI is a standalone department housing the FDEs
  • Customer qualification and deliverables are set before FDEs code
  • FDEs focus on prototyping, architecture, and field signal
  • Scheduling and executive relationships are managed by others
  • Field feedback from engagements improves Claude and the platform

Full text

Understand how Anthropic's Applied AI department operates, focusing on the Forward Deployed Engineer role. Learn how FDEs collaborate with various teams, contribute technical solutions inside customer systems, and provide valuable feedback to improve Claude and the platform. Gain insight into engagement stages, teamwork, and the balance of FDE responsibilities across prototyping, architecture, and field signal work.

An FDE rarely carries an engagement alone. Before an FDE writes the first line of code inside a customer’s systems, other people at Anthropic have qualified the customer, agreed on what the engagement should deliver, and planned how its value will be measured. While the FDE builds, others manage the schedule, the stakeholders, and the relationship with the customer’s executives.

This structure explains much of how the FDE role works. It shows which decisions an FDE makes alone, which ones the FDE shapes with others, and where the FDE’s field knowledge goes once an engagement ends.

Where Applied AI sits at Anthropic

Anthropic runs Applied AI as its own department, separate from Sales, Product, and Research and Engineering. FDEs are members of this team.
