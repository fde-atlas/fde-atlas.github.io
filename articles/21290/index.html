Summary

A job listing from OpenWork for Trust, a Japanese financial DX startup, recruiting a Forward Deployed Engineer with a salary of ¥8–15 million. The role targets large financial institutions such as megabanks, using generative AI and the company's own product Trust TLanP to modernize legacy systems and improve business processes. It is a hybrid consulting-engineering position covering requirements gathering, architecture design, implementation, and production deployment, including code analysis, cross-language conversion engines, and LLM-based code understanding. Founded three years ago with over 300 employees, Trust is preparing for an IPO and expansion to New York and Singapore. Candidates need web/enterprise system architecture experience and generative AI or LLM development experience.

Key points

  • Trust is hiring an FDE at ¥8–15 million annual salary.
  • The role modernizes financial legacy systems using generative AI and Trust TLanP.
  • It is a hybrid consulting-engineering role spanning design, implementation, and deployment.
  • Trust has 300+ employees, is preparing for IPO, and expanding overseas.
  • Required: architecture experience plus generative AI/LLM development experience.

Wanting to engage with the essence of issues at a higher level and deliver greater value, I resolved to become an IT consultant. Q. What motivated you to join Trust? A. From my experience in banking system development, I aspired to create value in "finance × IT," and I also empathized with the company's commitment to solving industry-wide issues. The reassurance of the support system and alignment with the vision led me to decide to join. Q. What kinds of projects have you been involved in since joining Trust? A. I am attracted to the environment where the more proactively you act, the more growth opportunities you gain, and I approach my work positively every day.

  • Job Description
  • <Details of Trust's AI & Data Division> Our AI & Data business, one of our core businesses, is committed to leveraging AI and data to create solutions that are actually used in the field and generate business value. We provide broad support to clients, including financial institutions such as major banks, covering everything from conception to execution and establishment, including data analysis and AI utilization, data platform construction, launching and developing AI/data organizations, and building governance. Leveraging the knowledge and know-how we have cultivated through this support in the financial industry, we are now expanding our support to other industries. Furthermore, beyond solving problems for individual clients, we are feeding our knowledge back into our own products, such as "Trust TLanP," and working to deliver value to more companies and industries. In addition, Trust has surpassed 300 employees in its third year since founding, and is preparing for an IPO while also expanding its presence to Osaka and Fukuoka, and preparing for global expansion in New York and Singapore. As the company grows, the industries and themes involved in our AI & Data business continue to broaden. Our team includes members with diverse experience, such as launching AI/data organizations, developing AI and machine learning models, and building large-scale applications. Precisely because we are in a phase of expanding the possibilities of our business from the financial expertise we have built to other industries, global markets, and our own products, we are looking for teammates to join us in driving the creation of new value through AI and data. [Job Summary] In this position, you will play an active role as a "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" who leads problem-solving from a technical perspective, using cutting-edge technologies including generative AI and our own product Trust TLanP to address business and system challenges faced by financial institutions. Trust TLanP is a product that supports a wide range of business and system transformation at financial institutions, such as modernization of legacy systems and improving and streamlining business processes using generative AI. FDEs go beyond mere consulting and technical proposals; after deeply understanding the client's business and current systems, they take consistent ownership of everything from organizing issues and technical verification to architecture design, implementation, validation, and production deployment. This is a position where you flexibly deliver technical solutions according to customer needs, such as utilizing Trust TLanP, running PoCs for new AI solutions, and developing client-specific features. Advancing static and structural analysis - Design and implementation of call relationship/dependency analysis - Building a platform for structural abstraction and visualization of legacy code - Design of logic for verifying consistency before and after conversion Building a modernization conversion platform - Design of cross-language code conversion engines - Implementation of automated refactoring/conversion logic Developing the AI integration platform - Design of syntax infrastructure enabling code understanding and generation using LLMs and other tools - Architecture design premised on AI integration [Examples of In-House Product Development] - Trust TLanP (a tool that supports legacy system renewal using AI) [Specific Responsibilities] - Technical implementation design and implementation promotion of Trust TLanP based on customers' business and system challenges - Planning, technical verification, and PoC promotion for legacy system modernization and business efficiency and sophistication using generative AI, in collaboration with customers and internal stakeholders - Architecture design, technical consideration, and implementation promotion toward full-scale development and production deployment based on PoC and technical verification results - Design and development of AI solutions and features tailored to customer-specific challenges - Feeding knowledge gained from projects and customer deployments back into product feature improvements and product strategy <Attractive Points> ◤Project Appeal◢ Consistent involvement from conception to implementation and production deployment for the business and system challenges of major financial institutions Work on a wide range of themes, such as legacy renewal and business transformation through generative AI, using our own product Trust TLanP Engage with a wide range of technologies and architectures, from legacy to cloud-native and generative AI utilization Be on the front line of creating customer value by embedding generative AI into business and systems Shape solutions together with customers through repeated cycles of verification and improvement ◤Position Characteristics◢ A hybrid role combining consulting and engineering The discretion to take consistent ownership from requirements organization through design, implementation, and production deployment Free to choose and implement optimal technologies based on problems, without being bound by specific solutions Feed insights gained in the field back into Trust TLanP and directly contribute to product evolution
