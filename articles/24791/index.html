The Skills an FDE Needs: "Implementation Ability × Customer-Facing Ability"

The essence of the skills required of a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) lies in the dual pillars of technical implementation ability and customer-facing ability. Neither alone is sufficient. What is tested is the ability to translate a customer's words into a working system and deliver real value in the field.

Technical Skills

An FDE is expected to "build it themselves." The following technical skills are core.

Full-Stack Implementation Ability (Python, etc.)

The ability to work independently, centered on the backend but extending to the frontend and data processing as needed. Fast prototyping adapted to the customer's environment is required.

API and System Integration

The ability to connect the customer's existing systems and data sources with your company's product. Knowledge of authentication, data pipelines, and infrastructure building proves valuable.

LLM / RAG Implementation Expertise

For FDEs in the generative AI space, LLM API usage, RAG development, vector databases, prompt design, and evaluation (Eval) design become powerful weapons.

Business and Interpersonal Skills

Just as important as technical skill is customer-facing ability.

Requirements Definition and Problem Decomposition

The ability to structure a customer's vague requests and define "what to build to drive business outcomes." It is important to identify the essential problem rather than retreat into technical solutions.

Client Negotiation and Expectation Management

The ability to translate and communicate technology to people in different positions, from decision-makers to on-the-ground staff. You control expectations and build trust.

How to Develop These Skills

The most effective approach is to gain "experience deploying an AI product to production, even a small one, and having customers use it." Achievements that don't stop at the PoC stage—such as internal deployments, contracted work, or productionizing PoCs—directly translate into market value. LLM/RAG skills can be fully caught up with through OSS and personal projects, and customer-facing ability can be transferred from presales, SIer, or consulting experience.
