Buyer's guide2 questions

What is review management software?

Review management software is a platform that helps businesses collect new reviews, monitor existing reviews across Google, Yelp, Facebook, and industry directories, and respond to feedback from a central dashboard. The best platforms also analyze sentiment trends and benchmark your rating against local competitors.

How do you evaluate review management platforms?

Evaluate on five factors: (1) how effectively it generates new reviews via SMS and email automation; (2) how many review sites it monitors in real time; (3) whether it helps you respond quickly with templates or AI drafts; (4) the quality of analytics for tracking rating trends by location; and (5) total cost per location at your scale.

How to choose

  • 1For multi-location retail or healthcare groups, prioritize platforms with per-location dashboards, bulk response templates, and competitive benchmarking by ZIP code.
  • 2If your primary goal is getting more Google reviews, SMS-based request campaigns (Podium, Birdeye) consistently outperform email-only approaches by 3-5x in response rate.
  • 3Agencies managing multiple client accounts need white-label reporting and client-level access controls — check whether these features require an expensive enterprise tier.
  • 4Confirm the platform does not engage in review gating (filtering negative respondents before directing them to Google), as this violates Google's policies and can result in review removal.

Frequently asked4 answers

How do I get more Google reviews for my business?

The most effective method is automated review requests sent via SMS within 24 hours of a transaction or service completion. SMS review request rates average 8-12%, versus 1-3% for email. Tools like Podium and Birdeye automate this workflow and can trigger requests from your CRM or POS system.

Can review management software remove negative reviews?

No legitimate software can remove reviews — only Google or the original reviewer can do that. What software can do is alert you to negative reviews within minutes, provide response templates, and help you follow up with unhappy customers to resolve issues, which sometimes leads the reviewer to update their rating voluntarily.

What is the difference between review management and reputation management?

Review management is a subset of reputation management focused specifically on customer-generated review platforms (Google, Yelp, etc.). Broader reputation management also covers news monitoring, social media mentions, and search result profiles. Platforms like Reputation.com and Birdeye blur the line by offering both in one product.

How much does review management software cost?

Pricing ranges from $30-$50/month for single-location SMB tools (NiceJob, GradeUs Starter) to $250-$800/month for multi-location platforms (Birdeye, Podium). Enterprise pricing for 50+ locations typically requires a custom quote and can reach $1,500-$5,000/month.

How this was scored

Every entry is scored on a 9.4-point scale across 5 weighted criteria, reviewed quarterly. Top 11 takes no payment from any provider on this list. Scores are computed from a public weighted rubric; methodology weights were locked before entry research began. Re-scored every 90 days.

  • Google accounts for over 80% of all review searches, so the quality of a platform's Google Business Profile integration matters more than its coverage of 50+ niche directories.
  • Review gating (preventing unhappy customers from reaching a review site) is offered subtly by some vendors and violates Google's guidelines. We disqualified any platform that enables gating as a default workflow.
  • Most candidates in this category are US-focused; international businesses managing reviews on Trustpilot or country-specific platforms may find coverage thinner.
