Perplexity AI & Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs (14)

Forward Deploying · 2026-11-14 · 3 min read

Summary

Forward Deploying tracks 14 open AI engineering roles at Perplexity, drawn from the company's own careers page: 8 AI Engineer, 4 ML Engineer, and 1 AI leadership position. The median published US base salary is $328k, about 46% above the $225k market median across tracked employers. Roles are based in San Francisco, New York, and London; 3 are remote-eligible and 9 of 14 publish salary ranges. Most roles ask for 4+ years of experience (median 7), and 7 adverts explicitly require production code experience. Data is current as of November 14, 2026, with 3 roles opened in the last 30 days.
