Forward Deployed Engineer Salary: 10 Posted Ranges

FWD Deployment · 2026-10-11 · 3 min read

Summary

This article compiles ten posted salary ranges for forward deployed engineers from official job listings at OpenAI, Anthropic, Palantir, Databricks, Scale AI and Ramp, checked October 11, 2026. Across eight US individual-contributor postings, ranges start between $135,000 (Palantir) and $189,000 (Ramp) and top out between $200,000 and $330,000. Anthropic's manager role in New York posts $320,000–$405,000, and its London FDE role lists £225,000–£255,000. The author advises comparing offers carefully: some ranges are base pay only while others note equity, levels differ, and travel requirements vary (Palantir up to 25%, OpenAI up to 50%).

Key points

  • Eight US IC postings range from $135K–$200K (Palantir) to $189K–$330K (Ramp).
  • Anthropic's New York manager posting lists $320,000–$405,000.
  • OpenAI and Ramp note equity; Scale AI's range is labeled base salary.
  • Palantir's Deployment Strategist posts $110,000–$170,000 in New York.
  • Travel requirements differ: Palantir up to 25%, OpenAI up to 50%.

What the numbers say

Forward deployed engineer salary across the postings

Across the eight US individual-contributor postings, the forward deployed engineer salary range starts between $135,000 (Palantir) and $189,000 (Ramp). The top of the range runs from $200,000 (Palantir) to $330,000 (Ramp).

The AI labs and fintech post the widest ranges. OpenAI's New York posting spans $153,000 to $325,000, which suggests one posting covers several levels. Palantir posts the same $135,000 to $200,000 range in New York and Palo Alto.

Pay for related roles

Management and strategy roles sit on either side of the engineering band. Anthropic's Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering posting in New York lists $320,000 to $405,000. Palantir's Deployment Strategist, the less technical partner to its FDEs, lists $110,000 to $170,000 in New York.

What to compare before you take an offer

  • Base or total. Scale AI labels its range as base. OpenAI and Ramp note equity on top.
  • Travel. Palantir's posting lists up to 25% travel and OpenAI's up to 50%. That changes the job as much as the pay.
  • Level. "Senior" and "Manager" titles carry their own ranges. Compare the same level.

Questions

Common questions

How much does a forward deployed engineer make?

In the eight US individual-contributor postings we checked on October 11, 2026, posted ranges start between $135,000 and $189,000 and top out between $200,000 and $330,000. A manager role at Anthropic in New York posts $320,000 to $405,000.

Do forward deployed engineers get equity?

Some postings say so. OpenAI's and Ramp's FDE postings both add "Offers Equity" to the range. Scale AI's range is labeled base salary. Ask each employer how equity is valued before comparing offers.

How much does a Palantir forward deployed engineer make?

Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer postings in New York and Palo Alto list $135,000 to $200,000 a year. Its Deployment Strategist posting in New York lists $110,000 to $170,000.

Why do ranges differ so much between companies?

Postings differ by level, location and what the range includes. Some ranges are base pay only, some include equity notes, and Databricks shows one range across four pay zones. Compare the same level in the same city.
