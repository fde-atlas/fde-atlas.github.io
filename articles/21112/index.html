Summary

This page from Japanese company Westeria describes FDE board, a free job board aiming to list every Forward Deployed Engineer opening in Japan. Employers submit a job URL; the operators review it and within two business days add a Japanese summary, salary range, and tags before publishing. Applications go directly to the company's own recruiting page. Listings qualify if the title or text explicitly mentions FDE, or if the main work involves embedding AI or data products at customer sites, provided the role is based in Japan or open to remote applicants living there. Closed listings stay online marked as ended. Because visitors come specifically for FDE roles, the site claims smaller but more motivated applicant pools. Westeria also offers contract development, delivering working prototypes in one to two weeks while hiring is underway.

FDE board is a job board aiming to collect every Forward Deployed Engineer job opening in Japan. There is no listing fee. Simply send us the job URL, and we will review the content and publish it. Since only people interested in FDE visit the site, even a small applicant pool leads to highly motivated applications.

Listing Criteria

We list jobs that meet at least one of the following. If you are unsure, feel free to send it anyway.

  • The job title or description explicitly mentions "Forward Deployed Engineer", "FDE", or "フォワードデプロイド"
  • Even without explicit mention, the primary role is embedding with customers to implement and deploy AI/data products
  • The position is located in Japan, or is a remote job open to applicants living in Japan

How Listing Works

  • 01 Send us the job URL via the form below.
  • 02 We read the job posting and create a Japanese summary, salary range, and tags (we reply within 2 business days).
  • 03 After you confirm the listing content, we publish it. Applications are sent directly to your company's recruiting page.
  • 04 After recruitment ends, we keep the page and switch it to "Closed" (so visitors arriving via search can check the status).

From the company behind FDE board

For companies that want to move their AI product forward without waiting to hire an FDE.

Hiring an FDE takes time. Westeria Inc., the company that operates FDE board, provides contract development services in which a small team works on-site with clients, handling everything from organizing requirements to prototyping, production implementation, and operations. We can serve as a stopgap or alternative to hiring, even before your recruitment is finalized. We will show you a working prototype within 1-2 weeks.
