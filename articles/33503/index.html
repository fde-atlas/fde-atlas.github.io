Atlassian FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) India: Salary & Role

FDE India · 2026-11-05 · 8 min read

Summary

Atlassian launched its Forward Deployed Engineering program on 7 October 2026, embedding senior applied AI engineers with enterprise Cloud customers on roughly 12-week engagements to build production Rovo agents. The team had 46 engineers in October 2026, growing toward 100 across the US, EMEA and India. India postings so far are senior Principal and Senior Principal roles in Bengaluru or remote, requiring 10–15+ years of backend experience plus 5+ years of production AI/ML. Atlassian does not publish pay; Dreamwork estimates ₹70 lakh–₹1.2 crore. Both listings appeared closed by late August 2026.

What the FDE does at Atlassian

Atlassian announced its Forward Deployed Engineering program on 7 October 2026. According to the press release, it embeds senior applied AI engineers in customer environments, usually for 12-week engagements. Customers put their own engineers on the project alongside Atlassian’s. The program page describes four phases: Discover, Build, Adopt and Value. It claims 80+ production AI agents shipped across 100+ enterprise customers.

In practice, the work covers:

  • Custom Rovo agents and automations for high-friction workflows such as incident triage, support and knowledge management.
  • Context and connectors. FDEs feed customer data into Atlassian’s Teamwork Graph. SiliconANGLE reported in May 2026 that the graph holds more than 150 billion connections.
  • Integrations using Python or JavaScript, APIs and microservices at enterprise scale. This comes from the India listings.
  • Rollout and adoption. The FDE stays until people actually use the agent. Atlassian says engaged accounts show 50% higher AI adoption.
  • Product feedback. In Atlassian’s own blog, the team describes carrying what it learns from customers back into product teams.

Built In reports that Atlassian FDEs sit in R&D and hold engineering titles, not consulting or pre-sales ones. Its profile of the team sums up the role as “an AI practitioner, not an AI engineer”. That matches the wider pattern we describe in what a forward deployed engineer is.

Team size and India

The Atlassian blog says the team had 46 engineers in October 2026 and was “moving quickly towards 100”. It names the US, EMEA and India as deployment regions. Atlassian has not said how many of those engineers are in India. The program is open to a select group of enterprise Cloud customers using Rovo.

Three things stand out:

  • Founding or 0-to-1 experience counts. Both roles ask for experience as a founding or forward-deployed engineer in 0-to-1 product development.
  • Compliance is part of the job. AI risk assessment, data privacy and GDPR are listed as requirements, not just nice-to-haves.
  • Rovo knowledge helps but isn’t required. Experience with Atlassian Intelligence or Rovo is listed under “great, but not required”.

Foundit showed both listings as closed when they were last updated on 29 August 2026. Treat them as a guide to what Atlassian wants, not as live openings. If you have fewer years, look at mid-level FDE roles at other employers. Our experience guide maps typical bands.

How to prepare

  • Build one real Rovo or Atlassian-integrated agent. Use Rovo Studio, or the Rovo MCP server, which Atlassian made generally available in early 2026, with your own agent. Automate something concrete, such as triaging Jira issues or archiving stale Confluence pages. Then measure the time it saves. Atlassian’s case studies are all framed as hours saved and adoption gained.
  • Show production AI, not demos. The listings ask for 5+ years of AI/ML in production. Prepare two stories that cover evaluation, monitoring and what you changed after launch.
  • Prepare a compliance answer. Be ready to explain how you would deploy an agent for a bank or hospital. Cover permissions, data residency, GDPR and AI risk review, since both listings name these.
  • Practise the 12-week arc. Rehearse how you would run discovery, pick one or two high-value use cases, ship them and drive adoption. Our FDE interview questions guide has customer-scenario examples.
  • Lead with leadership. These are principal-level roles that involve mentoring other FDEs and setting direction. Prepare examples of raising engineering standards across a team. Compare how other big platforms frame the role on our Microsoft FDE page.

Foundit showed both as closed when last updated on 29 Aug 2026, so join Atlassian's talent community and watch for new postings.
  • Recruiter screen, technical deep-dives, final round: Atlassian does not publish an FDE-specific interview format. Dataford's prep guide (updated Sep 2026, not confirmed by Atlassian) describes three stages over roughly 3–5 weeks: recruiter screen, technical deep-dive sessions, and a final round on leadership and values fit.

Frequently asked questions

Does Atlassian hire Forward Deployed Engineers in India?

Yes. Atlassian has posted Principal and Senior Principal Forward Deployed Engineer roles for Bengaluru with remote working, and its October 2026 blog says the FDE team is deployed across the US, EMEA and India. The two Bengaluru postings showed as closed on Foundit by late August 2026, so check Atlassian's careers site for new ones.

How much experience does an Atlassian FDE role in India need?

The India listings are senior. The Principal role asks for 10+ years of backend development and 5+ years delivering AI/ML in production. The Senior Principal role asks for 15+ years, including 5+ years at principal or staff level.

What is the salary of an Atlassian Forward Deployed Engineer in India?

Atlassian does not post pay for these roles; the listings only say they may include bonuses and equity. Job site Dreamwork estimates ₹70 lakh–₹1.1 crore for the Bengaluru Principal FDE and ₹85 lakh–₹1.2 crore for the Senior Principal FDE, but these are its own estimates, not employer figures.

What does an Atlassian FDE work on?

Atlassian FDEs embed with enterprise Cloud customers to build custom Rovo agents and automations, connect data into Atlassian's Teamwork Graph and get AI workflows into production. Engagements usually run about 12 weeks, and the engineers feed what they learn back to Atlassian's product teams.
