What Is the FDE Certification Exam? | Inside the Two Exams and How to Prepare Now

Original title: FDE検定とは｜2つの検定の中身と、今できる準備

FDE Consulting · 2026-10-22 · 9 min read

Summary

Japan now has two unrelated certifications both called "FDE检定". One, run by the IoT检定制度委员会, has a Level 1 exam (FDE-A Associate) planned for October 2026: 60 minutes, 100 multiple-choice questions, 19,800 yen fee, no prerequisites, with blockchain-based certificates. Its three-level structure (exam, accreditation, certification) has Levels 2-3 still undefined. The other, from the 日本FDE协会, spans grades 5 to 1 but has no fixed exam format or fees yet. The author argues these certifications are useful as a learning map for FDE work—especially for business-side staff—but won't yet help with hiring, since employers value real deployment experience and measurable results. The article closes with preparation suggestions mapped to the four exam domains.

Key points

  • Two same-named FDE certifications exist in Japan, run by different organizations.
  • IoT检定制度委员会's Level 1 starts October 2026: 100 questions, 19,800 yen, no prerequisites.
  • The three-level system covers knowledge, practical implementation, and business results; Levels 2-3 are undefined.
  • 日本FDE协会's five-grade certification has no fixed format or fees yet.
  • Certifications help learning but hiring decisions favor real deployment experience and results.

Searching for "FDE Certification" brings up two systems with the same name. One is the "FDE Certification System" by the IoT Certification Committee, whose Level 1 is called the "FDE Certification." The other is the "FDE Certification" by the Japan FDE Association, which has grades from 5 to 1. Both are private systems that started in 2026, but their operations and content differ.

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a role that goes into customers' sites, identifies business challenges, builds AI-based systems, and takes responsibility for running them in production. An explanation of the role itself is in What is an FDE? The meaning and work of a Forward Deployed Engineer, and the path to becoming an FDE is in How to become an FDE?. Here, I check what is currently written on the official pages of the two certifications, and organize the value of taking them and what preparation is possible now. The figures and dates were confirmed by opening the official pages on October 22, 2026.

The two systems called "FDE Certification"

First, an overview. The names are similar, but they are different systems run by different organizations.

For now, only the IoT Certification Committee's Level 1 has a defined exam content and fee. For the Japan FDE Association, the concept of the grades is published, but the exam format and fee have not been decided.

Content of the IoT Certification Committee's FDE Certification (Level 1)

The IoT Certification Committee announced the concept of the FDE Certification System in June 2026. In the announcement, it explained that the system evaluates not "people who can use AI" but "people who can continuously produce business results using AI agents" (June 2026 announcement). On September 3, 2026, it began accepting applications for the beta exam and published the skill map (beta version) for the Level 1 exam. Applications for the beta exam have already closed.

Three levels

The system consists of three stages: "certification test" to verify knowledge, "accreditation" to verify practical ability, and "authentication" to verify results.

LevelNameWhat is evaluatedStatus (official page)
Level 1FDE-A Associate (FDE Certification)Basic knowledge required for an FDE and the ability to apply it to business challengesMain exam planned to start in October 2026
Level 2FDE-P Professional (FDE Accreditation)Ability to organize business challenges and implement AI and digital technologies as systems that work in the fieldAccreditation content to be finalized in Q4 2026
Level 3FDE-C Consultant (FDE Authentication)Results produced through field implementation and the ability to embed and spread those results across the organizationDetails to be announced in Q1-Q2 2027

Level 2 is described as considering a format that verifies everything from problem setting to design, implementation, verification, and explanation through practical cases and hands-on assignments. Level 3 is considering an assessment combining documents such as data showing before-and-after improvement and evidence of operation, with interviews and presentations. For both, the evaluation methods and costs have not yet been decided.

There are four question areas: FDE fundamentals and role (definition, value, ethics, collaboration with stakeholders), business understanding (business processes, problem setting, value hypotheses, effectiveness metrics), AI, data, and technology (generative AI, AI agents, data utilization, basics of system integration), and implementation and operational judgment (applicability, security, legal and ethical issues, risk, continuous improvement). As study materials, the official page lists the "Japanese-Style FDE Official Textbook" and the "Japanese-Style FDE Question Collection [Level 1: Fundamentals]" with 167 questions in total. Free practice questions are also available.

The definition of "FDE" in this system

One thing to note when reading is the definition of FDE. The IoT Certification Committee states that it "independently defined FDE taking Japanese circumstances into account," and explains that the "Engineer" in FDE is not limited to technical professionals who do programming.

On the other hand, job postings from overseas AI companies that popularized the FDE job title require writing code. For example, OpenAI's FDE job posting in Tokyo requires handling technical deliverables from prototyping to production deployment and, when necessary, directly modifying code. In my view, what this certification measures is knowledge as "a person who connects business and AI," which partially overlaps and partially does not overlap with the "ability to build things that work in production yourself" that overseas job postings require.

Content of the Japan FDE Association's FDE Certification

The Japan FDE Association offers the FDE Certification as a private certification of FDE skills. Its official page presents the concept of the five grades.

GradeNameTarget and level (summary of official page)
Grade 5AssistantBeginners interested in FDE. Understand basic FDE knowledge and fundamental concepts of AI utilization
Grade 4OperatorPractitioners who have started using AI and IT tools in their work. Can apply AI tools to their work and carry out routine business improvements
Grade 3PractitionerAn FDE who solves problems hands-on at customer sites. Can identify business challenges and design and implement solutions using AI and technology
Grade 2SeniorAn FDE who leads multiple projects. Can drive complex projects and advance organization-wide business transformation
Grade 1ArchitectSomeone who designs FDE organizations and systems. Can formulate FDE strategy, build talent development frameworks, and design the architecture of the business as a whole

On the official page, the exam format is listed as "under design" and the exam fee as "in preparation." Eligibility requirements are planned to be set for each grade, and skipping grades and taking multiple grades simultaneously are also under consideration. The page explicitly states that this is not a national qualification and that it neither negates existing IT certifications nor serves as a replacement for them. Sample questions for Grade 3 and a skill self-assessment that takes about two minutes are available.

Is the FDE certification worth taking?

In my view, the FDE certification is useful for "getting a map of the FDE job," but it does not yet serve as a credential for "getting hired as an FDE." There are three reasons.

  • It has just started, so there are few or no certified holders yet. Recruiters are not yet at a stage where they can judge what passing means
  • Looking at the FDE job postings from various companies that I have examined on this blog, none of them list these certifications as application requirements
  • What FDE selection processes evaluate is experience finding problems in the field, building things, and moving the numbers. What the certification currently measures is the knowledge that comes before that

Still, there are people for whom taking it makes sense. In my view, they are the following three types.

Who it applies toWhy it is worth taking
People in sales, planning, or business improvement who have been put in charge of AI adoptionYou can cover the key points around business, AI, and risk. You gain a common vocabulary for internal discussions
HR and managers who want to develop people to drive AI internallyThe four exam areas can be used as a draft for training items
Engineers who lack confidence in business-side issuesYou can identify gaps in non-technical areas such as problem framing and effectiveness metrics

Conversely, for engineers already preparing for FDE selection processes, I believe that creating experience delivering one real project in your current job and moving the numbers is a stronger application asset than studying for the certification.

What you can prepare now

Registration for the actual exam has not yet opened. Below is the preparation you can do while waiting, organized by the Level 1 exam areas. All of it is directly useful for FDE work, regardless of whether you take the certification.

Exam areaWhat you can prepare nowWhat you will have left over
FDE fundamentals and rolesCompare the definition of FDE in both the IoT Certification Committee's explanation and overseas job postingsA one-sentence description of the FDE you aspire to be
Business and operations understandingPick one process in your own department and write out its steps, time required, and pain pointsA process flow diagram and a draft of the numbers you want to move (effectiveness metrics)
AI, data, and technologyActually try out part of that process using generative AI and AI agentsNotes on the results and why things did not work
Implementation and operational judgmentWrite out how information is handled, permissions, and safeguards against wrong answers when putting the tested system into productionA list of risks and countermeasures

The fourth area, "implementation and operational judgment," is hard to develop through knowledge alone. I have written about where things stall when putting AI into production in Breaking out of AI PoC stagnation, and about how to think about the permissions to give AI agents in AI agent security. What is asked in interviews is summarized in FDE interview preparation.

Once registration for the exam opens, it is best to check the passing criteria and retake conditions before applying. The official page currently states that for Level 1, cancellation and rescheduling after registration are not possible.

The FDE certification from the client's perspective

For companies that outsource AI adoption, whether the vendor's staff holds the FDE certification is, for now, of little use as a decision factor. In my view, what you should verify is whether the person proposing the solution will get into the field and build it, whether they can show something that actually runs in production, and which numbers they take responsibility for. I have summarized seven criteria for choosing a vendor in How to choose an FDE support company.

If you want to develop people for FDE roles internally, use the certification's exam areas as a draft for training items and make the scope of training extend to actually delivering one project in production. That way, both knowledge and experience take root. I have written about how far we go and what we build in Service details.
