Forward Deployed Engineer at Databricks

Forward Deployed Engineer · 2026 · 1 min read

Summary

This is a job listing roundup for Forward Deployed Engineer roles at Databricks. The company runs one of the industry's largest FDE organizations, helping enterprises operationalize data and AI on its Lakehouse and Mosaic AI platforms. FDEs partner with customer data and ML teams to design architectures, build pipelines, and take GenAI applications to production. Teams are organized by vertical such as financial services and manufacturing, with both IC and management tracks and numerous remote US positions. It suits engineers who enjoy data infrastructure and want direct customer impact.

Key points

  • Databricks has one of the largest FDE organizations in the industry.
  • FDEs help customers operationalize data and AI on Lakehouse and Mosaic AI.
  • Work spans architecture design, pipeline building, and shipping GenAI apps.
  • Teams are vertical-focused, e.g., financial services and manufacturing.
  • Roles include IC and management tracks plus many remote US positions.

Databricks employs one of the largest Forward Deployed Engineering organizations in the industry, helping enterprises operationalize data and AI on the Lakehouse and Mosaic AI platforms. FDEs work alongside customers' data and ML teams to design architectures, build pipelines, and ship GenAI applications into production. Databricks runs vertical-focused FDE teams (financial services, manufacturing, and more) and hires across individual-contributor and management tracks, including fully remote US roles. It's a strong fit for engineers who like data infrastructure and want high customer impact.
