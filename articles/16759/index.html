Accenture, in collaboration with Microsoft, establishes a "Forward Deployed Engineering" unit to support rapid company-wide AI deployment

Full text

Leveraging Microsoft's AI technology to accelerate advanced, industry-specific AI-centered transformation

[NEW YORK] Accenture (NYSE: ACN) today announced the establishment of a "Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE)" organization, in collaboration with Microsoft, to help enterprises design and build AI more quickly and deploy and operate it at enterprise scale in production environments.

For many companies, the barrier to AI adoption is not a lack of technology, but the fact that engineering expertise is not being fully applied in the field. To address this challenge, Accenture is partnering with Microsoft and, through the newly established FDE organization, will bring together thousands of engineers with AI skills to work directly with clients. By combining Microsoft's advanced AI technology with Accenture's deep knowledge of industries and business processes, the organization will help clients overcome the challenges they face and achieve and scale results across the enterprise.

Comment from Manish Sharma, Accenture's Chief Strategy and Services Officer The key to creating value with AI is not simply having access to technology, but the ability to turn it into sustained business outcomes. AI adoption succeeds in enterprises only when strategy and engineering come together as one. This collaboration with Microsoft places engineers at the center of AI-driven transformation and establishes a new model that goes beyond the ideation stage to deliver measurable results at enterprise scale.

Comment from Nick Parker, Executive Vice President and Chief Business Officer at Microsoft What customers are looking for is a clear path to business outcomes with AI. By combining Microsoft's AI platform—which delivers comprehensive services with both intelligence and trust—with Accenture's deep industry expertise, the FDE organization will rapidly deliver the highest-quality outcomes for clients.

Working closely with clients, the two companies will take AI initiatives from the ideation stage to production in a matter of days, a process that traditionally took months. Microsoft will provide the AI platform and advanced technologies, while Accenture will lead organizational change management, business process redesign, building industry-specific workflows, and global rollout. In addition, the two companies will leverage Microsoft's Frontier Suite products and proven accelerators to co-create with clients, generating measurable business value with AI and supporting its deployment across the enterprise.

This initiative builds on the long-standing strategic partnership between Accenture and Microsoft. The newly established FDE organization will play a central role in driving AI-driven transformation, helping thousands of clients put AI into practice and achieve coherent enterprise-wide transformation.

About Accenture Accenture is a leading global solutions and services company that helps the world's leading enterprises and organizations transform. It embeds digital technology at the core of businesses and organizations, harnessing the power of AI to create enterprise-wide value at unmatched speed. With approximately 786,000 people, proprietary assets and platforms, and strong ecosystem partnerships, Accenture supports clients as their transformation partner of choice. It also prioritizes being the world's most focused on client value creation, leveraging AI, and remaining a great place to work. Through its business unit "Reinvention Services," which combines capabilities across Strategy, Consulting, Technology, Operations, Song, and Industry X with deep industry expertise, Accenture delivers innovative solutions and services to clients. Guided by its purpose "to deliver on the promise of technology and human ingenuity," Accenture measures its success by the 360° value it creates for all its stakeholders.
