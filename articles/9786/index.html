Forward Deployed Engineers: Closing the gap between AI pilots and value

KPMG US · 2026-09-18 · 3 min read

Summary

A KPMG report argues that as AI agents spread through enterprises, execution speed becomes the key factor in realizing value, and Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) fill that gap. Working alongside business teams, FDEs identify high-value use cases, accelerate solution development, validate outcomes, and drive adoption. The report outlines a connected capability ecosystem for AI at scale: AI factories, forward deployed engineering, change adoption, and workforce transformation, with governance, security, and responsible AI embedded throughout. Leaders who deploy FDE talent against the right problems are best positioned to turn AI's promise into durable advantage.

Key points

  • AI agents make execution speed the defining factor in value realization
  • FDEs identify use cases, accelerate deployment, and validate outcomes
  • Scaling AI requires AI factories, change adoption, and workforce transformation
  • Governance, security, and responsible AI should be embedded in implementation
  • FDE-built capabilities should persist and scale beyond single projects

The rise of a new AI delivery model: Why forward deployed engineers matter now

AI agents are changing how organizations operate, creating demand for teams that can bring together technical expertise, business context, and execution.

Forward Deployed Engineers work at the intersection of strategy, technology, and operations. Their role is to identify opportunities, accelerate implementation, validate outcomes, and help organizations build capabilities that can scale across the enterprise.

As organizations expand their use of AI agents, execution is becoming a defining factor in how quickly value can be realized.

Bringing AI closer to where value is created

Connecting innovation to operational impact

As AI initiatives mature, organizations are increasingly focused on translating technical innovation into business outcomes.

Working alongside business teams, FDEs help organizations:

  • Identify high-value use cases
  • Accelerate solution development
  • Measure and validate outcomes
  • Increase organizational adoption
  • Build capabilities that scale beyond a single project

These capabilities help ensure AI initiatives remain closely aligned with business priorities while supporting long-term value realization.

The leaders who learn to deploy FDE talent against the right problems and alongside the right complementary capabilities will be best positioned to translate AI's promise into durable business advantage.

The architecture of AI value: Capabilities that support AI at scale

Organizations generating meaningful outcomes from AI are investing in a connected ecosystem of capabilities that support execution, adoption, and sustained transformation.

  • AI factories
    Establish repeatable processes for building, deploying, and scaling AI solutions.
  • Forward deployed engineering
    Bridge technical innovation and business outcomes through hands-on execution.
  • Change adoption
    Accelerate organizational readiness and support new ways of working.
  • Learning and workforce transformation
    Prepare employees with the skills required to thrive in an AI-enabled future.

Together, these capabilities create a foundation for enterprise-wide AI transformation while supporting trust, governance, and business alignment.
