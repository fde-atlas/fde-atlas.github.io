Summary

This is a PR TIMES listing page aggregating press releases from companies in Sasebo, Nagasaki Prefecture, covering roughly July–October 2026. Most items concern local tourism and business news—Huis Ten Bosch events, hotel offerings, and JR Kyushu trains. The FDE-relevant content comes from Minna Systems (株式会社みんなシステムズ), which launched an FDE platform called "FDE Japan" in August 2026 under the slogan "AI for all Japan," aiming to bring AI to small and mid-sized businesses across all 47 prefectures by having engineers work on-site; its first client was the Murase Group. In October, the company announced support for Win Carrier (ウィンキャリア株式会社) as its first FDE project in Gifu Prefecture. The rest of the page is unrelated to forward deployed engineering.

Key points

  • Minna Systems launched the FDE platform "FDE Japan" in August 2026.
  • Its "AI for all Japan" goal targets SMEs in all 47 prefectures via on-site engineers.
  • The first FDE client was the Murase Group in Sasebo.
  • Win Carrier became the company's first FDE project in Gifu Prefecture.
  • Remaining items are local Sasebo tourism and business news, not FDE-related.
