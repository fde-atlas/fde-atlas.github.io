Forward Deployed Engineer Salary in the US (2026)

MarsLab · 2026-11-01 · 7 min read

Summary

MarsLab compiles 2026 US salary data for forward deployed engineers. Levels.fyi reports a median total compensation of $225,000, while Bloomberry's analysis of 1,000 job postings with disclosed ranges shows a median of $173,816; the roughly $51,000 gap reflects different methods, dates, and whether stock and bonus are counted. The article also cites Palantir's median of $233,000, Scale AI's location-based base salary bands, and factors like security clearance and travel, while noting limits of self-reported and posting-based data.

Key points

  • Levels.fyi median total compensation is $225,000; the 75th percentile reaches $300,000.
  • Bloomberry's median from 1,000 postings with disclosed ranges is $173,816.
  • Palantir FDEs earn a median $233,000 based on 232 submissions.
  • Scale AI's same role pays different base ranges by city, e.g. San Francisco vs St. Louis.
  • Postings suggest clearance, on-site travel, and equity matter, but quantify none of them.

A forward deployed engineer in the United States has a median total compensation of $225,000 a year, according to Levels.fyi in November 2026. Job postings that disclose pay give a lower median of $173,816, according to Bloomberry in January 2026. The first counts stock and bonus. The second comes from posted pay ranges.

This page goes deeper on pay than our forward deployed engineer guide, which covers what the role is and when to hire one. Every figure below names its source and its date, and where sources disagree we show both. We have not averaged, adjusted or converted any number.

What is the average forward deployed engineer salary in the US?

The most recent public figure we found is a median of $225,000 in total compensation, from Levels.fyi's forward deployed engineer page, which it marked as updated on 14 November 2026. The same page gives the spread, because a median hides a wide range.

  • 25th percentile: $170,000 a year in total compensation.
  • Median: $225,000.
  • 75th percentile: $300,000.
  • 90th percentile: $361,000.

Levels.fyi says its figures come from salaries and offers that employees report to it, with outliers removed. The summary line of that page does not name a country, so check the location filter on the page before quoting it as a US figure.

Why do Levels.fyi and Bloomberry give different salaries?

They count different things at different times. Levels.fyi reports total compensation from employee submissions. Bloomberry reports a median from job postings, and only from the postings that disclosed a salary range.

Bloomberry's analysis covers 1,000 forward deployed engineer postings supplied by the jobs data provider Revealera, and the page is marked as updated on 25 January 2026. It puts the median at $173,816 for postings that disclosed a range. It adds that 70% of the postings mention equity, 8% mention on-target earnings and none are quota-carrying roles.

Three differences could explain the gap of about $51,000 between $225,000 and $173,816. Stock grants and bonuses sit inside the Levels.fyi figure. Postings state what an employer plans to pay, while submissions record what people were offered. The two also cover different months. We did not find a description of how Bloomberry reduced each posted range to a single figure, so we do not claim to know which of these differences matters most.

What is the difference between base salary and total compensation?

Base salary is the fixed yearly pay. Total compensation adds the yearly value of stock grants and any bonus. Levels.fyi defines its figure this way: base salary plus the yearly value of stock grants plus bonus.

The split matters because stock can be a large share. Levels.fyi's Palantir data shows it: for the Software Engineer level, the average total is $288,000, made up of $175,000 base, $106,000 of stock a year and $6,600 of bonus. Check the split in any offer before you compare it with a number from a posting, because a posted range is usually base pay and may exclude equity.

How much does Palantir pay forward deployed engineers?

At Palantir, Levels.fyi reports a median yearly compensation package of $233,000 in the United States, within a range of $225,000 to $320,000. That figure is based on 232 submissions and the page was last updated on 23 October 2026.

The figures are on Levels.fyi's Palantir forward deployed engineer page. Palantir is the company most tied to the title, but it is one employer, and its pay says little about a startup or a services firm. Our guide explains where the role came from.

How does forward deployed engineer pay vary by company, level and location?

Widely. Posted ranges for the same title differ by employer and also by city. The clearest public example we found is a single posting that prints three location bands.

Scale AI's Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Public Sector posting, which we read in November 2026, lists a base salary range for each group of locations:

  • San Francisco, New York and Seattle: $184,000 to $292,560.
  • Hawaii, Washington DC, Texas and Colorado: $165,600 to $263,304.
  • St. Louis: $138,000 to $219,420.

The same job pays a different base depending on where it is filled, and each band is wide. Level also moves pay, but we found no source that breaks forward deployed engineer pay down by seniority, and Bloomberry's analysis gives none either, so we offer no figure for it.

Do forward deployed engineers earn more than software engineers?

At Palantir the answer depends on the measure. Levels.fyi shows a higher median for software engineers, but a higher average total for forward deployed engineers at the entry level. We found no source that supports a general claim across all employers.

  • Median total compensation in the US at Palantir, Levels.fyi, 23 October 2026: forward deployed engineer $233,000 (232 submissions), software engineer $255,000 (733 submissions, on Levels.fyi's Palantir software engineer page).
  • Average at the Software Engineer level, same date: forward deployed engineer $288,000 total ($175,000 base, $106,000 stock a year, $6,600 bonus); software engineer $273,000 total ($184,000 base, $85,100 stock a year, $4,100 bonus).

The two measures use different groups of people, so they can point in different directions. In the second line the forward deployed engineer package has the lower base and the higher stock. We would not generalize from one employer, and our page on forward deployed engineer versus software engineer covers the differences in the work itself.

What raises forward deployed engineer pay?

Postings point to three things: a security clearance, time at customer sites, and equity. None of the postings we read says how much any of them adds, so we give no percentages.

  • Security clearance: the Scale posting above requires an active TS/SCI clearance or eligibility to obtain one. It does not say what that adds to the range.
  • Travel and time on site: the same posting says the role requires significant time on site with customers, and expects 25% travel with up to 50% as necessary.
  • Equity: Bloomberry found that 70% of postings mention it, and Levels.fyi counts stock inside total compensation.
  • Domain expertise: we found no posting or study that puts a price on it.

The posting's own wording is the best guide. A role that needs a clearance, or that sends you to customers for weeks, is asking for something that fewer candidates can offer. It is a reason to ask about pay. It does not show that pay is higher. Our piece on what a forward deployed engineer does week by week shows where the travel comes from.

How do you read a salary range in a job posting?

Treat the range as the employer's plan for a hire, not a promise. The bottom is usually for someone who just meets the requirements, and the top for someone who exceeds them, in the most expensive location.

  • Find out what the range covers. Scale's posting says it is a base salary range. Other postings may include or exclude bonus and equity, so look for the sentence that says which.
  • Match the band to your location. In the Scale posting the top of the St. Louis band, $219,420, is only a little above the bottom of the San Francisco band, $184,000.
  • Read the width. The San Francisco band runs from $184,000 to $292,560, so your level and experience decide where you land.
  • Ask the recruiter where candidates with your experience usually land. That is a fair question, and the answer is better evidence than the range.

Postings change or close without notice. Everything we describe here is what we saw in November 2026, and the postings may differ by the time you read this.

Do these figures apply outside the United States?

No. Every figure here is US data in US dollars. Pay for the same title differs by country, and we report no figures for the UK, Canada, the UAE or any other country.

If you are outside the US, use a local source and check its currency and whether it counts stock. The job titles are the same, so our forward deployed engineer jobs page and the glossary definition apply anywhere.

Which sources did we use, and what is missing?

We used Levels.fyi, Bloomberry and one Scale AI posting, all opened in November 2026. We could not read Glassdoor, which refused an automated request, so none of its figures appear here.

Three limits apply. Salary submissions are self-reported. Postings that disclose a range are a subset of all postings. And a median on its own says nothing about a person's package. For a decision about a specific offer, use the range for that employer, that city and that level.

What do these figures mean if you are hiring a forward deployed engineer?

If you are on the hiring side, the figures above are a reference point for the salary of an in-house forward deployed engineer, before overhead and a long hiring cycle.
