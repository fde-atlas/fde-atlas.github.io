The OpenAI Deployment Company FDE interview loop: free dossier

A10X · 2026 · 5 min read

Summary

A10X's free dossier compiles what is known about the FDE interview loop at The OpenAI Deployment Company (DeployCo), OpenAI's deployment subsidiary launched in May 2026 and seeded by acquiring Tomoro, an applied-AI consultancy of roughly 150 engineers. DeployCo raised over $4B from 19 investors led by TPG, with Advent, Bain Capital and Brookfield co-leading. As of 2026-10-28 it lists 30 live requisitions across FDE, Technical Deployment Lead and other roles in cities including New York, London, Paris, Singapore, Sydney and Abu Dhabi. US FDE pay is $170K-$400K plus equity, requiring 4+ years of engineering experience. No candidate has published an interview report for DeployCo itself, and the company publishes no process for individual candidates; only the team-application track discloses interviews with individual conversations, technical assessment and a collaborative working session. Existing prep pages reuse generic templates, which the author frames as an information gap.

What we know about the loop, tagged by how we know it

  • The official name is The OpenAI Deployment Company, self-shortened to DeployCo. It describes itself as "the deployment arm of OpenAI" and "a subsidiary of OpenAI and funded with a partnership of outside investors". Both lines come from the job descriptions on its hiring board: as of 2026-10-28, 28 of its 30 postings carry the first word for word and 27 the second; 2 more reword the second.
  • Hiring runs on Ashby: 30 live requisitions as of 2026-10-28. Forward Deployed Engineer roles in New York, London, Paris, Singapore, Sydney and Abu Dhabi, plus a cybersecurity FDE and a cleared FDE (active U.S. government security clearance required, also listing Washington, D.C.) in New York; Technical Deployment Lead roles in New York, including a cybersecurity one and a cleared one, and in Abu Dhabi; Principal, Strategic Accounts roles in New York (general, cybersecurity, life sciences) and Abu Dhabi; an "Apply with Your Team" track; a product lead for internal products; plus IT, security, revenue operations, legal, finance, corporate development, workplace and real estate, travel and expense, executive support and recruiting roles. Most US roles list San Francisco as a second location.
  • The US Forward Deployed Engineer role: New York or San Francisco, hybrid 3 days a week in office, up to 50% travel, $170,000 to $400,000 plus equity. Under "You may thrive in this role if you" it lists 4+ years of engineering experience, production-grade code across frontend and backend in JavaScript or Python, and 0-to-1 reusable-capability building. The posting has no separate list of requirements. The posting describes "player-coach" expectations with a fast ramp to management.
  • Non-US FDE comp, published on the board: London £90K to £150K, Paris €115K to €200K, Singapore SGD 145K to 350K, Sydney A$135K to 360K, Abu Dhabi AED 580K to 1.1M, all plus equity. The Singapore posting's board publish date is 2026-08-21, and it is live as of 2026-10-28.
  • The companion delivery role is Technical Deployment Lead ($170K to $400K plus equity in New York). It leads day-to-day technical delivery, coordinates FDEs, researchers and customer engineers, owns value cases, KPIs, adoption tracking and change management. Under "You may thrive in this role if you" it lists 5+ years in any of customer-facing technical delivery, technical program management, deployment, consulting, solutions engineering or implementation leadership.
  • Northslope is NOT another name for this org. It is a separate applied-AI firm that DeployCo agreed to acquire on 2026-07-08, its second acquisition after Tomoro. Announced on DeployCo's own site.
  • Tomoro's old site, tomoro.ai, now redirects permanently to deploy.co.
  • On deploy.co, under "Forward deployed engineering at OpenAI", it describes the delivery motion: FDEs "work directly with customers to solve a specific problem, validate impact, and then identify patterns that can scale", "build from first principles", and "deliver early value, then iterate toward scale".
  • It showcases two OpenAI customer stories, which link to openai.com: BBVA scaling ChatGPT Enterprise to 120,000 employees across 25 countries, and John Deere.
  • Launch coverage reports more than $4B of initial capital from 19 investors led by TPG, with Advent, Bain Capital and Brookfield co-leading, plus consultancies Bain & Company, Capgemini and McKinsey among the partners. OpenAI holds majority ownership and control.
  • Tomoro brought roughly 150 engineers and deployment specialists. Its clients included Tesco, Virgin Atlantic, Supercell, Fidelity International, Red Bull and Mattel.

What nobody outside can know yet

  • No candidate has published an interview report for DeployCo itself that we could find (one web search naming Blind, Reddit and Glassdoor, 2026-10-28; what it turned up covers OpenAI's parent-company loop or FDE interviews in general). OpenAI's parent-company FDE loop is documented publicly by third parties, but whether DeployCo inherits any of it is unknown: separate subsidiary, separate board, and no published process for individual candidates. One prep site, Scoutify, has a DeployCo page ("Sources reviewed 2026-10-19"), but its interview section is the "Standard software engineering loop" template it uses for other companies. The one published exception is DeployCo's "Apply with Your Team" track: that posting says a team's interviews include "individual conversations and technical assessment, alongside a working session designed to understand how you solve problems together", with every person "held to the same bar as other DeployCo candidates for the relevant role". The simulation therefore trains the judgment its postings describe, not a memorized stage list.
  • No pod size is published, and no engagement length for DeployCo's FDE work. The nearest number is a deploy.co post from 2025-12-19, before DeployCo launched, giving an "idea to live" cadence of 8-12 weeks for one offering, ChatGPT Apps builds. The "Apply with Your Team" posting says DeployCo "works in small, multidisciplinary teams", without a headcount. It takes applicant teams of two to five, but that is who may apply together, not a pod size. The equity component of comp is unstated.
