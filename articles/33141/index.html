FDE hiring in India was steady this week rather than fast. The listing counts did not move, but two new roles widen the pay picture: Sycamore wants a Forward Deployed & Product Engineer in Bengaluru at ₹28–36 LPA, while Ascendion lists a Chennai FDE role at ₹6–8 LPA. The more interesting signal is outside job boards: a Mumbai company has started placing forward deployed engineers inside hospitals.

This Pulse follows Thursday’s FDE Jobs in India: Keyrus Posts ₹45–70 LPA, Bengaluru Holds 34%. Below: the new roles, the healthcare move, and the week’s layoff numbers.

FDE job listings in India: flat since Thursday

The Glassdoor India counts we track have not changed since 5 November:

  • The broad forward deployed engineer search still shows 564 listings.
  • The exact Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) title search still shows 125.

Globally, FDE Jobs List shows 386 live FDE postings as of 7 November, up from 383 on Thursday. Its first page is still led by Anthropic and Scale AI roles in the US, Europe and the Gulf, with no India role among them. Naukri and LinkedIn India totals remain not publicly reported in pages we could access.

A flat week after three straight rises is not a slowdown signal on its own. It does mean the best use of your time this week is the specific roles below, not waiting for a wave of new posts.

New FDE roles in India this week, with posted pay

Sycamore: ₹28–36 LPA in Bengaluru for 5–12 years

Sycamore posted a Forward Deployed & Product Engineer role in Bengaluru on 5 November 2026, according to JobFound. The company describes itself as building an agent operating system for enterprises and says it has raised $65 million from Coatue and Lightspeed.

Detail What the listing says
Location Bengaluru, with travel within India when useful; team spans India and Palo Alto
Experience 5–12 years of software engineering, with exceptions either way
Posted pay ₹28–36 LPA, plus equity (no figure given)
Stack TypeScript, React, Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Kubernetes, MCP
Interview 30-minute intro, two 60-minute technical rounds (system design, coding), take-home

This is the builder end of the FDE spectrum. The listing says plainly that it is not a pre-sales, solutions or ML research role. You map a customer’s workflow, then build the agentic app end to end: UI, APIs, data model, agent behaviour, integrations and permissions. You also turn one-off customer work into reusable platform patterns with the core engineering team.

Its band is identical to DevRev’s Mumbai role from 1 November. Two unrelated employers landing on ₹28–36 LPA for 5+ years is a useful anchor for experienced engineers moving into product-company FDE work.

Ascendion: ₹6–8 LPA in Chennai

Ascendion listed a Forward Deployed Engineer role in Chennai on 7 November, hybrid, with a posted ₹6–8 LPA. The work leans towards data: SQL transformations, data models and cloud pipelines, plus explaining model or retrieval quality and how outputs are checked. Skills named are agentic AI, APIs, SQL, data modelling and responsible AI practices.

Like Keyrus’s listing on Thursday, it carries a “Fresher” tag yet asks for relevant AI engineering experience and hands-on depth in at least one area. Read that as “early career with real projects”, not a pure campus role. Our fresher FDE guide covers how to show that depth.

The top of Keyrus’s band is more than eight times Ascendion’s. Employer-posted pay via JobFound, 1–7 Nov 2026.

Across the five listings in the chart, the range now runs from ₹6 LPA to ₹70 LPA. The title alone tells you little; the employer type, client geography and how much you build decide the band. Compare offers on our salary page.

Trend: FDEs move into Indian hospitals

Dimension NXG, the Mumbai deep-tech company behind AjnaLens, launched OJAS Forward Deployed Engineering on 7 November, eHealth Magazine reports. Its engineers will sit inside hospitals, health systems and government health programmes and connect AI modules to the hospital’s existing information system. The first modules cover clinical documentation, dermatology and metabolic health.

Three details matter for engineers:

  • Location: the team starts with multiple engineers based in Mumbai, serving India, the US and the Gulf.
  • Constraints: the AI runs on the hospital’s own servers behind its firewall, and a clinician reviews every output before it reaches the patient record.
  • Timing: the launch comes about six months before the 13 May 2027 deadline for full compliance with India’s DPDP Rules.

No headcount or pay was disclosed, so treat this as a direction, not a job count. But it fits a pattern Analytics India Magazine described in October: FDE value is highest where data exists and workflows are well defined. Hospitals, with strict SOPs and privacy rules, are exactly that. If you have worked on HL7/FHIR integrations, on-premise deployments or data-privacy compliance, healthcare is a niche where FDE demand may grow ahead of supply.

Layoffs and FDE hiring this week, side by side

These are listed next to FDE hiring for context. No source links any of these cuts to forward deployed engineer roles.

  • 2026 tally: Onmanorama, citing layoffs.fyi data up to 29 October, counts 1,35,446 tech layoffs across 334 companies this year, against 1,22,606 across 278 companies last year. It says about 69% of this year’s cuts were attributed to AI. That attribution is to AI adoption generally, not to FDE hiring.
  • Largest cuts: the same report lists Oracle at 21,000, Amazon at 16,000, Dell at 11,000 and Meta at 10,400, with Block and Cisco at 4,000 each.
  • Microsoft: a follow-up. Onmanorama confirms eligible India staff have until 1 December to opt into the voluntary exit scheme, with severance of one month’s basic pay per year of service. See our Microsoft company guide for its India FDE context.

None of these reports gives an India-specific count for the companies listed, and we found no new India layoff announcement dated this week.

What this means for you this week

  • If you have 5+ years, anchor on ₹28–36 LPA. Sycamore and DevRev both post that band for product-company FDE work. Push higher only with enterprise client delivery or GMT-shift premiums like Keyrus.
  • Prepare for a build-heavy loop. Sycamore’s process has system design, coding and a take-home. Practise designing an agent app with permissions and integrations end to end; our interview questions guide has a framework.
  • Early career? Lead with data work. Ascendion’s role is SQL, pipelines and retrieval quality. One project showing how you checked a RAG system’s answers is worth more than another certificate.
  • Look at regulated domains. Healthcare FDE work rewards on-premise and privacy experience. Check where your gaps are with the FDE Fit Check, and see our Chennai and Bengaluru guides for employers.
