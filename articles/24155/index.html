Forward Deployed Engineer | Lio | Munich, Germany

Zero G Talent · 2026-11-12 · 3 min read

Summary

Lio (formerly askLio), an a16z-backed, Y Combinator-built startup with offices in Munich and New York, is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer to build AI agents for procurement at Fortune 500 companies. The role involves integrating agents with enterprise systems like SAP, Oracle, Coupa, Databricks, and Snowflake, owning customer outcomes from workshop to go-live, and turning single-customer solutions into shared product features. Requirements include fluency with AI coding tools, APIs, and integrations; English is mandatory, German and ERP experience are pluses. The job is office-based five days a week with high intensity and weekly or daily production releases.

Key points

  • Lio hires an FDE to build AI procurement agents for Fortune 500 clients.
  • Work spans SAP, Oracle, Coupa, Databricks, and Snowflake integrations.
  • Engineers own customer outcomes from workshop through go-live.
  • Single-customer solutions become product features for all clients.
  • Five days in office, high intensity, English required, German a plus.

What you need

  • Solves technical problems across APIs, integrations, and distributed systems
  • Builds reliable, scalable solutions with strong ownership
  • Works closely with customers to translate challenges into product improvements
  • Communicates clearly across Engineering, Product, and customer teams
  • Thrives in fast-moving environments and learns quickly

What you'll do

  • Build enterprise integrations across APIs, SAP, and business systems
  • Design scalable technical solutions with enterprise customers
  • Debug and resolve complex issues in production environments
  • Own customer-driven features from MVP to production
  • Automate deployment and implementation workflows with Product and Engineering

Forward Deployed Engineer (all genders)

Procurement touches every company, every product, every euro spent, and it’s still one of the most traditional spaces out there. We’re rebuilding it with AI.

At Lio, we’re building the AI workforce for procurement. We lead the largest AI deployments in procurement, at Fortune 500 companies around the globe, and we’re growing like few companies ever do. We need exceptional engineers to build what comes next.

What this job really is

  • AI-native, from the ground up. No pre-AI SaaS legacy to work around. You build AI products and agents from scratch, from agents across the whole procurement process to voice avatars in strategic negotiations.
  • Wired into real enterprise systems. You connect AI agents to the systems that run Fortune 500 companies: SAP, Oracle, Coupa, Databricks, Snowflake. Millions of orders, contracts and suppliers, live.
  • You own the customer outcome. From the first workshop through integration to go-live, and the value after. Core engineering owns the platform. You own whether the agent actually works at the customer.
  • Built for one customer, live at many. You don’t just work through tickets. You dig into a customer’s problem, design the solution, then make it part of the product everyone runs on. Spot a pain point across customers? Push for a new agent and own it.
  • Agentic speed. We don’t spend months defining things upfront. We ship to production every week, often every day, and fix fast. High intensity, and the steepest learning curve you’ll find.
  • No boundaries. You work with the latest frontier models, without limits. While many companies are still cautiously rolling out AI tools, you use any means to achieve your goals as fast as possible.
  • More than an FDE. The role spans engineering, product, sales and project management: worldwide go-lives, workshops, demos to Fortune 500 companies, presenting your agent on stage, driving upsells. Where you sit on that spectrum is up to you.

What you should know before you apply

  • We’re in the office five days a week.
  • This is not a 9 to 5 job. We grow faster than our processes can keep up, and there are weeks when a project needs everything you have. In return, you’ll learn more in a year than in five years anywhere else.

What you bring

  • You build with AI. You’re fluent with AI coding tools and agents, you’re comfortable with APIs, data and integrations, and you’ve shipped real projects where you can show your part.
  • Ownership and curiosity. You grab opportunities instead of waiting for them, teach yourself whatever the problem needs, and step up when something breaks.
  • You want to understand a problem before you solve it, you enjoy working with customers, and you hold your work to a high bar.
  • Fluent English is a must, German is a plus.

Procurement knowledge or experience with ERP systems such as SAP or Oracle is a plus, but not a requirement.

Why Lio

Lio is backed by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) and built at Y Combinator, with offices in Munich and New York. We’re winning, and every employee is a shareholder, so you win with us.

You’ll work with some of the smartest, most driven and unconventional people in Europe, and no two weeks look the same: one week a16z drops by for a lecture, the next you’re on a flight to a client.

On top of that, you get an unlimited AI coding budget, the newest tools within a week of release, free breakfast and dinner, an office gym, a subsidized Wellpass or Deutschlandticket, events from hackathons and game nights to Wiesn, and 30 days of vacation.

Sounds like you? Let’s talk.
