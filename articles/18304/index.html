AndDot Launches "Resident FDE" Service, Dispatching Engineers to Client Companies to Drive DX/AX End-to-End

Original title: アンドドット、顧客企業に常駐しDX／AXを一気通貫で推進する「常駐型FDE」サービスの提供を開始

アンドドット株式会社 · 2026-07-23 · 8 min read

Summary

Japanese startup AndDot Inc. has launched a resident FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) service in which its engineers embed at client companies to handle DX/AX end to end: identifying problems, designing solutions, building systems, and ensuring operational adoption, all AI-driven. The offering builds on AndDot's March 2026 restructuring that shifted all engineers to an FDE model. It targets enterprise business DX and includes a four-phase framework (FDE Journey), a four-axis engineer quality standard (CEPO), and a shared knowledge base called AndDot Knowledge. Deployments start with one or two engineers and use weekly sprints. AndDot says it has supported over 350 companies and 300 municipalities.

Key points

  • AndDot launches a resident FDE service embedding engineers at client firms.
  • Covers problem discovery through development and operational adoption, AI-driven.
  • Uses a four-phase FDE Journey framework with weekly sprints.
  • CEPO standard rates engineers on consulting, engineering, PM, and openness.
  • Backed by AndDot Knowledge; company has served 350+ firms and 300+ municipalities.

AndDot Inc. (Head office: Shibuya-ku, Tokyo; CEO: Yuta Ibaraki; hereinafter "AndDot"), which supports corporate DX end-to-end through resident FDEs, has launched a service called "Resident FDE," in which engineers known as "FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers)" who deeply understand the business and see problems through to resolution are embedded at client companies, providing AI-driven, end-to-end support from problem discovery to solution planning, system development, and operational adoption. Along with this, a dedicated page introducing the service (https://www.and-dot.co.jp/lp/fde) was published on July 16, 2026. Since its founding in 2023, AndDot has had a culture of engineers directly solving customer problems, and this new service refines that model and offers it in a resident format.

What is Resident FDE?

Resident FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a service in which AndDot engineers embed themselves at the customer's site as members of the team, accompanying them end-to-end from problem discovery to solution design, development and validation, and operation and adoption. Rather than stopping at one-off tool deployments or concept proposals, under the philosophy of "DX-ing the entire company, thoroughly," they work alongside the client until results take root in the field.

In March 2026, AndDot announced the restructuring of its engineering organization, and from April of that year transitioned all in-house engineers to an FDE-first structure (announced March 23, 2026: "All in-house engineers become 'FDEs.' AndDot announces restructuring of its engineering organization"). This "Resident FDE" service makes the expertise built under that structure directly available to client companies.

Why fundamental DX (business process automation) stalls

The main reason DX/AX fails to advance at many large enterprises is that the functions required to drive it are fragmented across the organization. (1) Identifying business problems, (2) planning solutions, (3) implementation and execution, and (4) establishing operations—these may exist in-house, may be unassigned and left floating, or may be outsourced, and the situation is patchy from company to company. Even when each function exists, there is a gap in cross-functional talent who can connect them end-to-end across departmental and internal/external boundaries, so even identified problems never reach full adoption. In recent years, this has been compounded by a shortage of people who can implement generative AI into business operations.

Right now, "problems that never get solved" continue to pile up inside companies.

An FDE who handles problem discovery, solution design, development, and execution—all by one person—embedded on site

An FDE is a "business-completion engineer" who alone can handle consulting, engineering, and project execution. In the Resident FDE service, the FDE is embedded at the client company, understands frontline operations firsthand, and continues in-house problem discovery, solution proposals, system development, and operational adoption on a repeating cycle until problems are eradicated. Under the philosophy of "viewing problems as lines, not points," improvements keep accumulating as long as the FDE remains on site.

<Value delivered>

- Startable with just 1–2 people: Flexibly covers areas that traditionally required a whole team with a small number of staff.

- Fast to start, consistently high quality: Leverages in-house knowledge to quickly deliver high-quality results.

- End-to-end from discovery to adoption: Problem discovery, solutions, implementation, and operational adoption are all completed in-house.

- Improvements continue as long as the FDE is there: Sustainably addresses problem-solving that raises the company's value.

Structuring the DX process: "FDE Journey©"

Projects proceed in four stages following "FDE Journey©," a proprietary framework inspired by the Fit Journey method for new businesses. Each stage confirms "what has been agreed upon" before moving forward, a structure that minimizes rework in later stages.

Implementation proceeds in one-to-two-week sprints, building working products while engaging the field. In the final POF stage, the FDE deliberately creates a state where operations continue even after the FDE leaves, rooting the improvement loop inside the client company.

PhaseActivities
① CPF (Customer Problem Fit)Discover essential in-house problems and prioritize them. Once problem identification is complete, move to "② PSF."
② PSF (Problem Solution Fit)Evaluate multiple options by business impact, difficulty, and operational load, and agree on the one option to pursue. After agreement, move to "③ SPF."
③ SPF (Solution Product Fit)Develop in one-week sprints, building a working product while engaging the field. Once ready for actual operation, move to "④ POF."
④ POF (Product Operation Fit)While incorporating field feedback, create a state where the system is actually used in-house, along with an environment that enables system operation and an improvement loop.

Structuring talent quality: "CEPO©"

The resident FDEs are not people with development skills alone.

They possess four proprietary evaluation criteria, "CEPO©," covering problem analysis, project management, and even how they engage with people.

AxisOverview
C: ConsultingObjectively analyzes problems and organizes what should be solved. Structures the real problems.
E: EngineeringFull-stack development capability leveraging AI. Builds across domain boundaries.
P: Project managementManages plans, budgets, progress, and quality. Personally acts as PM to run projects.
O: Open and approachableA way of engaging with people that creates an atmosphere where others feel comfortable speaking candidly.

Each item is rated on a five-point scale in-house, and members averaging 4 or above are defined as "Senior FDEs." This prevents capabilities from depending on individuals and ensures a consistent standard as a company.

Structuring back-end support: "AndDot Knowledge"

The FDE in the field is not alone. The resident FDE brings AndDot's accumulated sample code, architectures, design patterns, and expert knowledge on security and industries (= AndDot Knowledge) into the client company and uses it freely. By not writing everything from scratch, they accelerate ramp-up, stabilize quality based on proven implementations, and spend time on essential improvements.

Target: Enterprise business DX

Primarily, we cover all business operations of large enterprises. If the organization has a DX promotion department or information systems department, we work together with them to pursue business reform and in-house development. Expected areas of application include AI automation of approval and review processes, automation of document creation, renewal of inquiry handling, and automation of data integration and transcription between systems. With a deep understanding of each company's operations, we comprehensively deliver high-impact DX.

DX/AX support at the scale of 350 companies and 300 municipalities

AndDot has provided AX support to more than 350 companies and more than 300 municipalities to date. In July 2026, it will hold a seminar themed on FDE development, sharing its knowledge of FDE training and support outside the company as well.

Future outlook: Delivering AX execution to the front lines

AndDot will grow its on-site FDE service into one of its core offerings, serving as the driving force that takes AI adoption beyond the concept stage and implements and embeds it in actual operations.

Comment from Representative Director Yuta Ibaraki:

Having accompanied many companies through their DX journeys, I am convinced of one thing: packaged solutions can only solve problems common to multiple companies. Even if operations become more efficient, they cannot automate from the root the operations unique to a given company. What is truly needed is to build a system optimized for that company, for that company alone. That is what on-site FDEs deliver. Thanks to the automation of coding, we can now turn optimal systems into reality at overwhelming speed. This means FDEs can spend their time not on writing code, but on digging into customer challenges and defining the questions to be solved. We insist on the on-site model precisely because that is where the greatest value lies. Furthermore, we have the DX promotion frameworks and FDE development knowledge cultivated through past projects. We are proud that this depth is among the best in Japan. On-site FDEs produce results on a different order of magnitude from initiatives that merely introduce tools. True to our concept of "thoroughly transforming entire companies with DX," we will contribute to the DX of all kinds of companies.
