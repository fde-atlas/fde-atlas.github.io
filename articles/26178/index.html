Who Owns the Deployment Agent? Product, Not the Field

Falkster.AI · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Full text

Product owns it. The agent that does the on-site build runs in every deployment, so it's a product with a backlog, and the field team is its first user and its best source of bug reports.

On November 10, 2026, IBM and Microsoft announced Imagine Enterprise with "a fleet of more than 6,000 AI agents and assets" and forward-deployed engineers attached for qualifying engagements. Two weeks earlier ServiceNow's chief product officer, Amit Zavery, told investors the company is building "AI implementation agents," and Microsoft's Kevin Shatzkamer described FDE teams "augmented with coding assistants and deployment agents."

What stays with the person is the deciding: the choice, the judgment, the bar, and the handback. Count the human hours per go-live, mark each one as a decision or a gap, and send the gaps to a named product manager.
