How to Build an FDE Organization | Comparing Placement, Team Structure, and Evaluation Metrics at Microsoft, OpenAI, Databricks, and More

Original title: FDE組織の作り方|置き場所・チーム構成・評価指標をMicrosoft・OpenAI・Databricksなどで比較

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-10-08 · 32 min read

Summary

Based on official announcements and 515 FDE job postings collected on October 8, 2026, this article compares how Microsoft, OpenAI, Databricks and others structure forward deployed engineer organizations: where to place them, how to compose teams, and what metrics to use. In 2026, OpenAI launched a separate deployment company (over $4 billion), Microsoft started a $2.5 billion business with 6,000 experts, AWS invested $1 billion in a dedicated FDE organization, while Google Cloud and Salesforce built partner-based models. The piece identifies four placement patterns, pod structures pairing Deployment Strategists with FDEs, evaluation axes, common failure modes, and a minimal starting configuration for Japanese companies.

Key points

  • In 2026 OpenAI, Microsoft, Anthropic and AWS each spun FDE work into separate companies or business units.
  • FDE organizations sit in product teams, go-to-market teams, independent units, or partners like Accenture.
  • Of 515 FDE postings, 74 were management, strategist, engagement, or PM roles, signaling a business-unit shape.
  • Evaluation axes span customer outcomes, delivery reliability, feedback into product, and customer self-sufficiency.
  • A minimal starting pod is one problem-definer plus two FDEs, with the exit condition defined upfront.

Key Takeaways

  • 01 In 2026, OpenAI spun up a separate company, Microsoft launched a new business unit, Anthropic formed a new company with investment firms, AWS established a dedicated FDE organization, and Google Cloud and Salesforce created mechanisms to place FDEs in partnership with SIs and consultancies.
  • 02 At Salesforce, many FDEs handle customers alone, but a new model has also begun in which a pod of one Strategist and two FDEs dedicates about three months to a single company; CTERA also began offering FDE and Strategist pairs on October 6, 2026.
  • 03 Of the 515 FDE job postings counted by the editorial team on October 8, 2026, 407 had "engineer" in the job title, while a combined 74 were management, strategist, engagement management, and PM roles—positions that lead organizations or drive projects.
  • 04 OpenAI's FDE-related job postings in Tokyo list as evaluation criteria measurable value against customer goals, stability of team shipping, signals fed back to research and product teams, and reuse of patterns across deployments.
  • 05 According to the editorial team's analysis, when Japanese companies start an FDE organization, the minimal configuration derived from these companies' examples is to begin with a pair of one person who defines problems and two FDEs, and to decide upfront how requests will be submitted to the product team and the exit criteria—completion when the customer can independently add the next use case.

FDE(Forward Deployed Engineer/フォワードデプロイドエンジニア)を自社に置こうとすると、最初に「どの部門に置くか」「何人・どの職種で組むか」「何で評価するか」が問題になります。2026年は、この問いに大手各社が別々の答えを出した年でした。OpenAIはFDEを集めた会社を新設し[2]、Microsoftは6,000人の専門家を顧客先に置く新しい事業を立ち上げ[1]、Google CloudとSalesforceはSIer・コンサルと組んでFDEを置く仕組みを作りました[6][5][9]。

This article compares where FDE organizations are placed, team units, and evaluation metrics by reading each company's official announcements, statements from FDE organization leaders, and the 515 FDE job postings (including management positions based in Tokyo) that the editorial team collected from 133 public job boards on October 8, 2026. We also outline the minimal configuration for companies starting an FDE organization. For an overview of FDE hiring requirements and selection processes, see the FDE Hiring Guide.

What Are the Options for Where to Place an FDE Organization?

2026年10月時点で各社が選んだFDE組織の置き場所は、大きく4通りに分けられます。製品・エンジニアリング部門の中、営業(Go-to-Market)部門の中、独立した事業や別会社、そしてSIer・コンサルなどパートナーの中です。1社が複数を併用する例も多く、OpenAIは社内のFDEチームを持ちながら、FDEを集めた別会社も設立しました[16][2]。

How many people and which roles make up an FDE team?

How do you connect the FDE organization with the product organization?

FDE組織を作った会社の多くは、現場の要望を製品に戻す道筋を明文化しています。SVPGのMarty Cagan氏は、Palantirを例に、FDEだけでは特注のシステムが数千もでき、保守し続けることになると指摘し、プラットフォーム製品の組織がFDEの見つけた機能を一般化して取り込む形を説明しています[21]。

"if all they had were FDE's, then they would quickly end up with thousands of large, bespoke solutions that would need to be maintained and improved indefinitely."

Marty Cagan (SVPG), "Forward Deployed Engineers" (September 17, 2025)

[21]

Lining up how each company is building that path, we get the following.

  • Ramp: 顧客のために急いで作るか、何百・何千の顧客が使う機能に時間をかけるかをよく問い、一般化した機能を好む。場当たりのカスタマイズはコードベースを汚し、保守性を下げるとしている[13]
  • LayerX: 顧客ごとに車輪の再発明をせず、製品の機能のオン・オフや設定で対応する。顧客価値に必要なら、足りない機能を製品に実装することにもコミットする[15]
  • LayerX: 製品に足りない機能は、FDE自身がAIのコーディングツールや社内のエンジニアの支援を得て開発することを理想とする。プロダクト企画チーム(PdM)に要望を上げる場合は、他の案件の要望との整合性と優先順位、コードが負債になりすぎない抽象化、製品全体で一貫した設計と体験になっているかを確かめる[15]
  • Salesforce: FDE部門のディレクターBen Kracker氏は、現場で得た知見を製品チームとエンジニアに戻していると述べている[8]。パートナー向けのFDE Partner Networkでも、社内の製品チームへの直接の窓口を提供している[9]
  • OpenAI: FDEマネージャーの職務に、うまくいったことをツール・プレイブック・ロードマップへの入力にまとめることが入っている[16]

What patterns of failing FDE organizations exist?

公開されている論考と調査からは、うまくいかない型が少なくとも4つ挙がります。契約額の小さい顧客にFDEを張り付ける型、未熟な製品の穴埋めにFDEを使う型、要望が営業から製品開発へ伝言で流れる型、そしてベンダーのFDEが作って去る型です[22][13][23]。

If a Japanese company starts an FDE organization, what is the minimal configuration?

各社の例を重ねると、最小構成は「顧客の課題を定義する人1人とFDE2人の組を、製品部門への要望の出し方と出口を決めたうえで、1社の1〜2用途に張る」形になります。Salesforceで始まっているポッド[8]、CursorとCTERAの組[24][12]、Rampが2人から始めた経緯[13]が目安です。以下は、各社の例をもとにした編集部の整理です。

  • 01最初の組: Deployment Strategist(または案件の責任者)1人とFDE2人。1社の1〜2用途に約3か月を目安に専念する(Salesforceで一部始まっている形。Cursorも1人+1〜2人のポッドを通例としている)[8][24]
  • 02置き場所: 製品がまだ変わる段階なら製品・エンジニアリング部門に置き、営業とは連携の線でつなぐ。足りない機能はFDEが自ら実装するか、PdMに上げて他の案件との整合性・抽象化・製品全体の一貫性を確かめる(LayerXの例)[15]
  • 03評価: 受注額だけでなく、顧客の目標に対する測定値、マイルストーンの達成、製品に戻した変更を測る(OpenAIの例)[17]
  • 04出口: 顧客が自力で次の用途を追加できたら完了、と案件の最初に決める(CTERAの例)[12]
  • 05管理職: 組が増えたらマネージャーを置く。Databricksの東京の求人では、マネージャー1人が約10人のFDEを率いる[18]
  • 06言語: 東京のFDEマネージャーの求人は、OpenAI・Databricksとも日本語を必須にしている。日本の顧客を担う管理職には日本語が前提になっている[16][18]

自社で人数をそろえられない場合は、パートナーのFDEを使う道もあります。SalesforceのFDE Partner Networkには、日本ではアクセンチュア、NTTデータ、グローバルウェイ、テラスカイ、デロイト トーマツ、電通総研・電通デジタル、PwCコンサルティング、富士通、フレクトの9社が初期パートナーとして参加しています[10]。同社の浦野敦資氏は、多くの日本企業がAIエージェントの試験導入から先に進めないという課題を挙げています[10]。案件をどの順序で進めるかは、AtlassianのFDEプレイブックが12週間の工程を具体的に示しています。

Summary

The design of an FDE organization is determined by five points: placement, team unit, evaluation criteria, how work flows back into the product, and exit conditions. The key points that can be read from public information as of October 2026 are as follows.

  • FDE組織の置き場所は、製品・エンジニアリング部門、営業部門、独立した事業・別会社、パートナーの4通り。2026年はOpenAI・Anthropic・Microsoft・Amazonが別の事業として切り出した[5]
  • チームの単位として、課題を定義するDeployment Strategist(Lead)とFDEの組が広がっている。Salesforceでは1人+2人のポッドで1社に約3か月専念する形が一部で始まっている[8]
  • Of the 515 FDE job postings counted on October 8, 2026, 74 were for roles that lead organizations or define and drive projects, suggesting that FDE organizations take ownership of project definition, execution, and customer relationships
  • 評価軸は顧客の成果・提供の確実さ・製品への還流・顧客の自立の4つに、受注・利用拡大が加わる。OpenAIの東京の求人は顧客への価値と製品へのシグナルを同時に測る[16][17]
  • うまくいかない型は、小さな契約への張り付け、未熟な製品の穴埋め、要望の伝言、作って去る形の4つ[22][13][23]
  • For Japanese companies, the minimal configuration is a 1+2 pod, a defined way to submit requests to the product department, and an exit condition based on customer self-sufficiency, decided upfront

Frequently Asked Questions

QShould an FDE organization be placed in the sales department or the product department?

There is no single correct answer. Based on public information as of October 2026, Ramp and LayerX place FDE organizations on the product/engineering side, while Google Cloud places them within its sales (Go-to-Market) department. If the product is still evolving and you want field learnings fed back into the product, the product side fits; if you want to operate in tandem with winning large customers, the sales side fits. Either way, you need a separate mechanism to connect with the other side.

QHow many people can an FDE team start with?

In organizational launches, Ramp started with 2 engineers and grew to 16 in a year and a half. As a unit handling a single project, Salesforce has begun in some cases using a pod of 1 Deployment Strategist and 2 FDEs dedicating about 3 months to one customer (many FDEs handle customers alone), and Cursor also typically uses a pod of 1 Strategist and 1-2 FDEs. Once headcount grows, managers are added; Databricks' Tokyo posting indicates one manager leads about 10 FDEs.

QWhat does a Deployment Strategist do?

It is a role that works alongside FDEs and takes responsibility for the "why" and "what" of the customer's problem. Databricks' job posting explains that, acting as the product manager for the customer's problem, they define the scope of experimentation and success criteria, write requirements documents, while FDEs handle the "how." OpenAI has a similarly named role called Deployment Lead, and Cursor has Forward Deployed Strategist.

QHow should the results of an FDE organization be measured?

Public information shows four criteria in use: measurable value against customer goals, delivery reliability such as milestone achievement, the degree to which field learnings are fed back into the product, and customer self-sufficiency; Databricks' engagement managers are also expected to contribute to deal size and usage targets. OpenAI's Tokyo posting states evaluation is based on the team's shipping consistency and signals to research and product departments, and CTERA treats a project as complete when the customer can add the next use case on their own. Note that measuring only by deal size pushes FDEs closer to technical sales.

QWhy did OpenAI and Microsoft turn FDEs into separate companies or businesses?

OpenAI explains that it established the OpenAI Deployment Company as an independent business unit to build its own way of operating, speed, and customer focus required for deployment support, while keeping customers closely connected to research and product departments. Microsoft invested $2.5 billion to launch the Frontier Company as a new business placing 6,000 experts at customer sites. TechCrunch reported that each company sees implementation support itself as a major business.
