Salesforce to Fully Launch the "Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) Partner Network" in Japan, Accelerating Agentforce Implementation Together with Partners

Full text

Together with nine initial partners in Japan, Salesforce will support customers in moving AI agents from pilot to production, accelerating the transformation to the agentic enterprise

Salesforce Japan (Headquarters: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; Chairman and CEO: Shinichi Koide; hereinafter "Salesforce") today announced the full-scale launch of the "Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network" (hereinafter "FDE Partner Network") in Japan.

The FDE Partner Network was announced at TDX 2026, held in the United States in April this year. To accelerate the production deployment of Salesforce's AI agent platform "Agentforce," it provides carefully selected partner companies that have passed a rigorous screening process with deep access to Salesforce's engineering expertise and product roadmap. Nine initial partners have joined in Japan, and the network is now being fully launched in the Japanese market. Through this, Salesforce will further strengthen its framework for supporting customers' transformation into agentic enterprises together with its partners.

Background and Challenges

While momentum for AI adoption is growing among Japanese companies, many remain stuck at the pilot stage and struggle to move to full production deployment. The main cause is not the performance of AI models, but a lack of enterprise-grade implementation know-how, such as data integration with complex legacy systems, establishing governance frameworks, and embedding AI into business processes. To close this "gap between pilot and production," Salesforce will further strengthen its investment in the partner ecosystem.

About the FDE Partner Network

The FDE Partner Network is a select group of partner companies evaluated not on company size or length of business relationship, but on their track record of Agentforce implementations and depth of technical expertise. Salesforce shares the engineering methodologies and product knowledge it has cultivated internally with certified partners, adopting a collaborative model in which partners' specialist engineers engage deeply in customer projects.

Globally, FDE Partner Network partners already account for one-third of all successful Agentforce implementations, a track record that underpins the credibility of this partner network.

The benefits that Salesforce's FDE Partner Network provides to partners and customers are as follows.

  • パートナー向け：
    • Priority access to Salesforce's engineering expertise and product roadmap
    • Acquisition of advanced technical skills in Agentforce implementation and differentiation
    • Eligibility to join an ecosystem that can successfully lead customers to production deployment
  • 顧客向け：
    • Reliable and rapid transition from AI pilots to production deployment that actually generates business value
    • Practical, hands-on support from partners who have mastered Salesforce's engineering methodologies
    • Beyond mere configuration and deployment, business transformation through AI agents that fundamentally integrate business processes with AI agents while maintaining governance

The nine initial partners of Salesforce's FDE Partner Network in Japan are as follows. (Listed in Japanese syllabary order, including group companies)

  • Accenture Corporation
  • NTT Data Corporation
  • Globalway Corporation
  • Terrasky Corporation
  • Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu LLC
  • Dentsu Research Institute Corporation / Dentsu Digital Inc.
  • PwC Consulting LLC
  • Fujitsu Limited
  • Flect Corporation
A Perspective from Salesforce

Atsushi Urano, Executive Officer and General Manager of the Alliance Business Headquarters at Salesforce Japan, said: "For Japanese companies to achieve real results with AI, close collaboration with partners who combine both technical capability and business understanding is essential. While many Japanese companies recognize the potential of AI agents, they face the challenge of being unable to move beyond the pilot stage. The FDE Partner Network was designed to break down this barrier by sharing Salesforce's engineering expertise with partners. Globally, partners in this network account for one-third of successful Agentforce implementations, and I am confident we will replicate that success in Japan. For our customers, we offer not just deployment support, but a partnership that achieves production deployment that reliably generates business value. Together with the nine partners joining this initiative, we will fully accelerate the AI transformation of Japanese companies and their transformation into agentic enterprises."

Through the FDE Partner Network, Salesforce will further expand its partner ecosystem in Japan, increasing Agentforce production deployments in the Japanese market and further strengthening customers' transformation into agentic enterprises.
