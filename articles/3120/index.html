The FDE job market, mapped: 1262 live roles

Plank · 2026-06-12 · 14 min read

Summary

Plank compiled a verified census of 1262 live forward deployed engineer postings across 555 companies, drawn from ATS boards and job aggregators. Strictly FDE-titled roles number roughly 1,000-1,400 globally, but counting solutions, implementation, and field engineers doing the same work exceeds 20,000. Median posted base is $200K, with frontier labs paying far more once equity (44-70% of total comp) is included. The article argues a company's first FDE req signals a shift to enterprise revenue, and that vertical matters more than seniority for pay.

Key points

  • 1262 live FDE postings verified across 555 companies; median posted base is $200K.
  • Strict FDE-titled roles: ~1,000-1,400 globally; broadest definition exceeds 20,000.
  • Vertical shifts pay more than seniority: $250K at foundation-model labs vs $148K in industrial AI.
  • Frontier-lab equity is 44-70% of total comp, pushing packages past $700K.
  • 95% of enterprise GenAI pilots return nothing; embedded deployment engineers double production odds.

Six things the data makes clear

  • 02The role is more global than its reputation: two-thirds of the openings sit in the United States and a third run international, yet US demand still clusters on the coasts at a median 22% above the heartland, pricing a travel-defined job as if nearness to capital, not to the customer, set the wage.

  • 05An employer’s vertical shifts a forward-deployed engineer’s pay more than seniority does: $250K at a foundation-model lab versus $148K in industrial AI is a $102K swing, wider than the $71K gap from mid-level to head of the function.
  • 06The frontier labs now pitch operating companies forward-deployed engineers who deploy their own stack, but since deployment is only about 20% of an AI system’s lifetime cost, the lock-in lands in the 80% paid for later.

How many FDE jobs are there, really?

It depends entirely on where you draw the line, so here is the whole ladder, from an exhaustive June 2026 sweep of every major job source. Roles literally titled “forward deployed engineer” numbered ~1,000-1,400 worldwide, de-duplicated. But the job itself, a customer-embedded engineer who ships in production, is also done by solutions, implementation, and field engineers; count all of them and you clear 20,000 globally. What isn’t real is the “56,000+” LinkedIn shows: that’s OR-matched word-soup, and Indeed’s own count drops ~70× the moment you restrict it to the title.

  • All customer-embedded delivery rolesbroadest18,000-25,000Solutions, implementation, field, and professional-services engineers worldwide. Many do the same job an FDE does, embedding with a customer and shipping in production under a different title. The solutions-engineer family alone is roughly 18,000 to 24,000 worldwide across the major ATS aggregators. This is the definition under which “20,000+ FDE jobs” is true.
  • FDE + AI-era equivalentsAI-inclusive3,000-6,000The FDE title family plus the AI-native rebrands doing identical work: applied AI engineer, customer engineer, agent engineer, forward-deployed AI engineer. The boundary here is a judgment call, not a clean measurement.
  • All FDE-titled variants, de-duplicatedtitle family1,500-2,000Every genuine FDE title (“forward deployed,” “deployed,” and “field deployed” engineer), de-duplicated, global, with roughly 900 to 1,300 in the US. Aggregator data shows most “deployed engineer” titles already are forward-deployed, so this barely exceeds the strict count.
  • Strict “forward deployed engineer,” de-duplicatedstrict, global~1,000-1,400Roles literally titled forward deployed engineer, de-duplicated across hundreds of company ATS systems. The leading ATS aggregator's exact-phrase count is 1,031 live worldwide (1,391 all-time), and 650 to 885 in the US. Growing fast: Indeed indexed +729% year over year.
  • Plank census, verified & individually linkedour census1,276The subset we confirmed live and linked one by one, across 547 companies, each row pointing to the company's own posting. Built by re-scanning every employer's ATS and a site-scoped sweep of Ashby, Lever, Greenhouse, Rippling, and Workable, counting distinct role-and-location postings (so a role open in several cities counts once per city). That it nearly reaches the strict-titled estimate above, built from a single aggregator's de-duplication, suggests that estimate is conservative.

Both answers are true. They ask different questions. Roles literally titled “forward deployed engineer” number roughly 1,000 to 1,400 worldwide and are climbing fast. But the job itself, a customer-embedded engineer who ships in production, is also done by solutions, implementation, and field engineers, and counting all of them clears 20,000 globally. What you won't find is tens of thousands of roles actually titled FDE. The “56,000+” on LinkedIn is OR-matched word-soup, not real demand.

Cross-reference the market

Two patterns jump out of the base-pay data: base climbs with company maturity, and a coastal premium persists even for a role defined by travel. Read both with the equity caveat below, since base is the minority of a frontier-lab package.

Base is not the offer

What posted base leaves out: equity

Every salary figure above is posted base, the only pay companies disclose at scale (just 31% of postings disclose even that). But at the labs and platforms base is the minority of the package. Posted base pay tells less than half the story: at frontier AI labs equity, not salary, is now the majority of total comp, lifting senior-IC packages from a ~$300K base to $700K-$900K+ all-in.

OpenAI Profit Participation Units (PPUs)

A PPU (Profit Participation Unit) was OpenAI's primary equity-style compensation instrument through 2024-2025: unlike RSUs or options, it conferred no ownership, no voting rights, and no strike price, just a contractual claim on a slice of OpenAI's future profits, granted at no cost to fit the company's then-"capped-profit" structure. PPUs vested 25% per year over four years with no cliff, plus a roughly 2-year lock-up, and had no public market, value was set by the most recent price investors paid in company-run secondary tenders (most prominently the ~$6.6B October 2025 secondary at a ~$500B valuation), with legacy upside capped at 10x the original grant value. A representative mid/senior engineer offer was ~$300K base plus a ~$2M PPU grant vesting over four years (~$500K/yr), so equity ran roughly 1.5-3x base annually, making it ~60-75% of total comp. Note the timeline: after OpenAI's October 28, 2025 conversion to a Public Benefit Corporation, outstanding PPUs convert to conventional shares (removing the 10x cap) and reporting indicates new grants moved to standard RSUs from around January 2026, so "PPU" describes the 2024-2025 era while 2026 hires increasingly receive RSUs.

Anthropic pays in RSUs on private stock (4-year vest, 25% in year 1 then ~2.08%/month, effectively a 1-year cliff) with no cash bonus for engineering, comp is base plus equity only, where equity runs ~44% of total for Senior SWE ($563K) and rises to ~58% at Lead SWE ($785K). Because those RSUs are on illiquid private stock, realized value depends on tender/IPO timing against a fast-climbing 2026 valuation (reported into the hundreds of billions, with a confidential IPO filing), pairing significant upside with a real illiquidity window.

So read the frontier-lab and foundation-model pay above as a floor on base, not the offer: the real gap from an early-stage startup to a lab is measured in equity, not the $93K of base between them.

Methodology & caveats

Collection. Postings were observed on 2026-10-30. Most postings come straight from employers’ own job boards: 821 boards across Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Rippling, Workable, SmartRecruiters, and Breezy, read through each provider’s public API and matched against forward deployed / FDEtitles. Getro-powered VC portfolio boards do two jobs: they surface employers whose own boards are then registered and read directly, and they fill in roles the census has no other way to reach. Manual sweeps of Y Combinator’s Work at a Startup, Wellfound, LinkedIn, Indeed, and Google/Salesforce/IBM/AWS careers add the rest. Everything was de-duplicated by normalized URL and by company-plus-title-plus-location, so an identical role reposted in the same city counts once while genuinely distinct cities stay distinct; rows from an employer’s own board take precedence over portfolio-board and sweep rows.

Tagging.For companies we researched directly, vertical, stage, and segment come from public funding data; for the long tail, vertical is assigned by a keyword classifier over company and title (so expect some sitting in the catch-all “Enterprise software” bucket) and segment defaults to “Startup / scale-up.” Region is normalized from the posting location; multi-location postings count once. Seniority is inferred from the title only. Salary is captured only where the posting discloses a base range, 397 of 1,276 postings (31%), and is base only. Equity and bonus are almost never posted, so every pay figure here understates total compensation, severely so at the frontier labs, where equity is the majority of the package (see the note on equity above).

Caveats. This is a census of postings, not budgeted headcount. One posting can represent many seats (Palantir) or an evergreen pipeline (labs). Treat every count as a floor: broad-match searches return far more (in June 2026, Indeed showed ~2,150 US results and LinkedIn 1,000+ in the past month for the quoted phrase), but we include only title-matched, individually linked postings. Engineering-track titles only, so forward-deployed marketer/PM/sales/operator roles are excluded, as are FDE-equivalent roles under other names (Cognition’s and LangChain’s “Deployed Engineer,” Sierra’s “Agent Engineer”). Postings mirrored on LinkedIn or Indeed for companies already covered through their ATS were dropped as duplicates. Several VC boards (Sequoia, a16z, Lightspeed, Greylock) are client-rendered and could not be crawled; Workday-hosted sites and Google’s careers system are only partially covered. Postings expire, and links were live on the observation date. Reuse with attribution: hello@joinplank.com.
