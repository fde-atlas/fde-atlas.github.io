Principal Forward Deployed Cloud Security Architect at Google — FDE Job Description

How to Become an FDE — Interview Kickstart · 2026-07-01 · 2 min read

Summary

This article archives a Google job posting for a Principal Forward Deployed Cloud Security Architect within Mandiant, Google Cloud's cybersecurity unit, based in Ottawa, Canada. The senior role required 8+ years of experience, paid roughly $198K–$203K, and involved embedding with clients in Canada's national security and defence sector to build cloud security capabilities. The posting appeared in early July 2026 and was closed by late July; the archive keeps it as evidence that Google created and funded this FDE-style role, even though the requisition is no longer open.
