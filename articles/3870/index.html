Decagon's CTO on Forward-Deployed Engineering

StartupHub.ai · 2026-07-28 · 1 min read

Summary

In a StartupHub.ai interview, Decagon CTO Sunny Rekhi explains how the AI agent company uses forward-deployed engineering. He traces how the model evolved as Decagon grew, describes FDE teams' dual role building solutions and working closely with customers, and stresses designing for scalability rather than one-off custom work, with the goal of empowering customers to run the product themselves.

Key points

  • Decagon CTO Sunny Rekhi explains the company's forward-deployed engineering model.
  • FDE teams play a dual role: building solutions and partnering with customers.
  • The model evolved as Decagon scaled, not fixed from day one.
  • Scalability matters: avoid becoming a pure custom-services shop.
  • Success means empowering customers to operate the product independently.

Full text

Decagon's CTO, Sunny Rekhi, details the company's forward-deployed engineering model, highlighting its evolution, the dual role of its teams, and the importance of scalability and customer empowerment.
