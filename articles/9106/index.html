Original title: 2026 Agentic AI 系统学习路线图 | FDE 学习中心

luoboask.github.io · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

This article compiles several 2026 learning roadmaps (from Lamhot Siagian, roadmap.sh, Machine Learning Mastery, Scaler) into a practical path for Chinese developers building agentic AI systems. Its guiding principle is “foundations first, protocols as king, closed-loop practice.” The roadmap spans seven layers over roughly 16-22 weeks: Python and LLM basics, prompt/context engineering, Harness engineering, frameworks and MCP, RAG, agent memory (Mem0), multi-agent orchestration with A2A, guardrails, and production deployment with FastAPI and Docker. It closes with interview prep, a six-agent demo project, and platform-building guidance. Key 2026 trends include Harness Engineering (Agent = Model + Harness), MCP standardization, A2A adoption by 50+ companies, and a Gartner prediction that 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by 2027 due to weak engineering practices. An FDE job-knowledge map covering 14 real job descriptions is appended.

Key points

  • Seven-layer roadmap, roughly 16-22 weeks, from basics to production.
  • Core idea: learn collaboration mechanisms, not frameworks; protocols matter most.
  • Harness Engineering emerges as a paradigm: Agent = Model + Harness.
  • Gartner predicts 40% of agentic AI projects cancelled before 2027.
  • Includes an FDE job map: 14 real JDs with skill-frequency analysis.

Core Philosophy​

Foundations first, protocols are king, close the loop with practice

The essence of Agentic AI engineering is not "learning frameworks," but understanding how agents collaborate safely and controllably with tools and other agents.
