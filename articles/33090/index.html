Nagarro among about 80 Salesforce forward deployed partners

Kernavique · 2026-09-17 · 2 min read

Summary

A September 17, 2026 diginomica report, summarized by Kernavique, describes Nagarro as one of roughly 80 Salesforce forward deployed partner firms. Nagarro's Salesforce practice has about 800 consultants and over 2,500 certifications, and Salesforce invests in some Nagarro teams to run workshops, requiring Data Cloud certification. Nagarro incentivizes AI and Agentforce certifications. Partner counts vary across sources, from over 30 to 84 firms.

Key points

  • Nagarro is one of about 80 Salesforce forward deployed partners.
  • Its Salesforce practice has ~800 consultants and 2,500+ certifications.
  • Salesforce part-funds some Nagarro teams; Data Cloud certification is a workshop prerequisite.
  • Nagarro rewards staff for AI and Agentforce certifications.
  • Partner counts differ across sources: 30+, 80, or 84 firms.

On September 17, 2026, diginomica reported on Nagarro’s work as a Salesforce forward deployed partner. The report gives around 80 such partner firms. It says Nagarro’s Salesforce practice has around 800 consultants and holds 2,500+ Salesforce certifications, and that Salesforce invests in some Nagarro teams to help run workshops, with Data Cloud certification as a prerequisite for those workshops.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: diginomica (reported).

What happened

The diginomica report says Nagarro gives its staff incentives to earn AI and Agentforce certifications as part of its partner certification program. Viyom Jain, MD and Global Business Head, Enterprise Products at Nagarro, described a train the trainer approach.

The partner count differs between sources. This report gives around 80 firms. A later diginomica report by the same author, dated September 25, 2026, gives 84 partners with 800+ verified practitioners. Salesforce’s own launch post of April 15, 2026, said over 30 firms.

The report also mentions a proposed takeover bid for Nagarro by Persistent Systems of roughly $1.45 billion. It states no contract terms for the forward deployed partner work.

“The FDE partners basically do a lot of handholding.”

Our reading

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

The report says Data Cloud certification is a prerequisite for the workshops, and that Nagarro rewards AI and Agentforce certifications. If you work in a Salesforce partner practice, this suggests vendor certifications decide who gets this work.

If you are hiring them

The report says Salesforce invests in some Nagarro teams to help run workshops. This suggests a vendor may part-fund a partner’s forward deployed team, which would change how such a team is sized and paid for.
