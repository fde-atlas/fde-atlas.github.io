Companies hiring FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) in Japan have increased rapidly since 2025. The trend extends beyond foreign AI and data companies to major telecom carriers, consulting firms, domestic AI startups and SaaS companies, and even companies that place "internal FDEs" embedded in their own business units.

In this article, based on each company's official job pages and official announcements as of October 16, 2026, we have compiled a list of companies hiring FDEs in Japan. Job titles from postings, salary information, and key application requirements are organized by company type.

Key Takeaways of This Article

  • As of October 2026, OpenAI, Palantir, Databricks, Cursor, SoftBank (SB OAI Japan), LayerX, Sansan, Sakana AI, and others are hiring FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) in Japan.
  • Among Japanese FDE job postings that publish salary ranges, upper limits range from 6.6 million yen for ExaWizards' Junior FDE (Fukuoka) to 30 million yen for Caddy's Staff FDE; in a tally of 35 domestic postings (February 2026), the median upper limit for Japanese companies was 15 million yen.
  • Application requirements for FDE jobs vary widely by company, with required years of experience ranging from 1+ years (ExaWizards' Junior FDE, Palantir) to 10+ years (Databricks' Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer).
  • Many foreign companies' FDE postings require English in addition to Japanese, but the level differs by posting: OpenAI, Cursor, and Salesforce Japan require fluency in both Japanese and English, while Databricks requires fluent Japanese and business-level English.
  • Some companies, such as Anthropic's Tokyo office and Cognition, hire customer-facing technical roles under titles other than FDE, such as Applied AI Engineer or Deployed Engineer.

Overview of FDE Jobs in Japan

FDE jobs in Japan increased rapidly from 2025 to 2026. An analysis of domestic job postings published on note (February 2026) counted a total of 35 FDE postings: 26 at Japanese companies and 9 at foreign companies. According to the same analysis, the median upper limit of salaries for FDE postings at Japanese companies was 15 million yen.

When the editorial team tallied 133 public job boards centered on overseas companies (131 of which responded) on October 16, 2026, there were 16 Japan-related postings from 8 companies whose job titles included "Forward Deployed," "FDE," or "FDSE." These 16 include 2 Palantir postings for the US government, not aimed at Japanese customers, and Rogo's Forward Deployed Banker, which is not an engineering role. Domestic companies' recruiting pages (HRMOS, HERP, and their own websites) were outside the scope of this tally.

Job postings continue to increase overseas as well. According to labor market data from Lightcast, postings containing "forward-deployed" grew more than 1,000% year-over-year from January to August 2026, and the median advertised salary exceeded $188,000 (Fortune, September 2026).

The job information in this article reflects content posted on each company's official job pages as of October 16, 2026. Postings may be changed or closed without notice. The Money Forward and Cohere (Tokyo) FDE postings that were listed as of September 29, 2026 had been removed by October 16, so they were excluded. When applying, always check each company's latest job page. Listed companies are introduced based on public information and this does not indicate that they are clients of this service or postings we can introduce.

Trends in application requirements

  • Years of experience: Ranges widely, from 1+ years for ExaWizards' Junior FDE (Fukuoka) and Palantir's Tokyo FDSE, to 2+ years of development experience at JDSC, 5+ years at OpenAI, Cursor, and Salesforce Japan, and 10+ years for Databricks' Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer
  • Language (foreign companies): Required proficiency varies by posting. OpenAI, Palantir, Cursor, and Salesforce Japan require fluency in both Japanese and English; Databricks requires fluent Japanese and business-level English. Notion's Forward Deployed Architect requires Japanese with English as a plus, while Notion's Forward Deployed Engineer lists no language requirements
  • Language (Japanese companies): In a survey of 35 domestic postings (February 2026), only 3 of 26 Japanese company postings included English ability as a requirement, all at the "preferred skill" level. JAPAN AI's posting states that English is not required but is a plus. Sakana AI requires native-level Japanese and lists business-level English as a plus
  • Technical skills: Many postings require experience building or deploying LLM/generative AI applications (SoftBank, KINTO Technologies, JAPAN AI, Sakana AI, Cursor)
  • Customer engagement: experience driving projects including client negotiations (SoftBank), experience working with enterprise customers (Channel Talk), experience stationed at or frequently visiting customer sites (Preferred Networks), etc.

Job postings that don't use the title "FDE"

Roles that involve the same way of working as an FDE are sometimes recruited under different titles. If you search only for "FDE," you will miss these postings.

  • Deployment Strategist (Palantir, KINTO Technologies, JAPAN AI): identifies customer issues and handles implementation and adoption. Works alongside FDEs who handle the implementation work
  • Applied AI Engineer / Applied AI Architect (Anthropic Tokyo, OpenAI Tokyo): customer-facing technical roles. Anthropic's Applied AI Architect targets people with experience as solution architects, sales engineers, and similar roles
  • Deployed Engineer (Cognition Tokyo): a customer-facing technical role responsible for the technical aspects of deploying the AI software engineer "Devin" to customers and getting it into actual use
  • Software Engineer, Agent (Sierra): Sierra calls its FDE-style role "Agent Engineer" and recruits for it on the Agent Engineering team. As of October 16, 2026, Sierra's Tokyo-based job postings consist of 4 roles in recruiting and PR, with no openings for agent development roles
  • Software Deployment Engineer (SoftBank): recruited as a role that will later expand into an FDE position

Salesforce's "FDE Partner Network"

On June 8, 2026, Salesforce announced the full-scale launch in Japan of the "Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) Partner Network" to accelerate the implementation of its AI agent "Agentforce." The initial partners in Japan are nine companies: Accenture, NTT Data, GlobalWay, Terrasky, Deloitte Tohmatsu, Dentsu Research Institute & Dentsu Digital, PwC Consulting, Fujitsu, and Fractea. The FDE way of working is spreading not only to product companies but also to consulting firms and SIers. Initial partner Deloitte Tohmatsu, as of October 16, 2026, lists FDE job openings in 8 areas including SAP, Salesforce, ServiceNow, and cloud on the careers page of its consulting division (Deloitte Tohmatsu Consulting LLC).

FDE internships for students

There are also FDE internships aimed at students. LayerX is recruiting for two types: "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) 1-Week Internship" and "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Practical Internship." Fujitsu also has a page for a one-month FDE internship (December 1-25, 2026), but as of October 16, 2026, it was not possible to proceed to the application screen.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q: Which companies are recruiting FDEs in Japan?

As of October 2026, OpenAI, Palantir, Databricks, Notion, Cursor, Salesforce Japan, SoftBank, SB OAI Japan, KINTO Technologies, PayPay Card, LayerX, Sansan, Sakana AI, CADDi, ExaWizards, JDSC, JAPAN AI, Loglass, andpad, RevComm, Channel Talk, and others are recruiting FDEs. The consulting firm Deloitte Tohmatsu also posts FDE openings by domain. Job openings change frequently, so check each company's official careers page for the latest postings.

Q: What are the salaries for FDE positions in Japan?

Among job postings that disclose salary, Caddy's Staff Forward Deployed Engineer offers an annual salary of ¥14 million to ¥30 million at hire, Sansan offers ¥11.52 million to ¥29.17 million, SoftBank (secondment to SB OAI Japan) has a theoretical annual income of ¥8.12 million to ¥20.34 million, JDSC offers ¥5 million to ¥25 million, and ExaWizards' Junior FDE (Fukuoka) offers ¥5.04 million to ¥6.6 million. In an aggregation of 35 domestic postings, the median upper limit of annual salary for FDE positions at Japanese companies was ¥15 million. The job postings from OpenAI, Databricks, Notion, Cursor, and Salesforce Japan, as well as 3 listings from Palantir's Tokyo job list, do not include salary information (as of October 16, 2026).

QIs English required for FDE positions at foreign companies?

It depends on the job posting. OpenAI's Tokyo FDE position requires proficiency in both Japanese and English, and resumes must be submitted in English. Cursor's and Salesforce Japan's FDE postings also require fluency in both Japanese and English. Databricks requires fluent Japanese and business-level English. Notion's Forward Deployed Architect requires Japanese, with English as a preferred qualification, while Notion's Forward Deployed Engineer does not list language requirements in its posting (all as of October 16, 2026).

QAre OpenAI and Anthropic recruiting FDEs in Japan?

OpenAI is recruiting not only "Forward Deployed Engineer - Tokyo" but also the manager role "Manager, Forward Deployed Engineer - Tokyo" and "Deployment Lead (DL), FDE - Tokyo," which oversees deployments. Forward Deployed Engineer - Tokyo requires proficiency in both Japanese and English. As of October 16, 2026, Anthropic's Tokyo office job listings do not include any position titled FDE; it is recruiting Applied AI Engineer and Applied AI Architect as customer-facing technical roles.

QAre there FDE positions that can be applied for even with limited development experience?

Yes. ExaWizards' Junior Forward Deployed Engineer (Fukuoka) requires at least 1 year of professional development experience, or professional experience in SES, contracted development, or in-house systems engineering. Palantir's Tokyo Forward Deployed Software Engineer requires at least 1 year of professional experience after graduating from university, and JDSC requires at least 2 years of development experience. SoftBank's Software Deployment Engineer requires 3 or more years of experience and is a role that will expand into FDE work over time. LayerX has an Associate position (expected annual salary from ¥8 million), and JAPAN AI's "Associate FDE / welcomes recent graduates" requires at least 1 year of professional experience as a software engineer.

QWhat is an Internal FDE?

An Internal FDE is an FDE who embeds not in customer companies but in their own company's business divisions to drive AI implementation in operations. As of October 2026, PayPay Card is recruiting for an "AI Transformation Lead (AI Forward Deployed Engineer)" and STRACT for an "Internal FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)." Money Forward was also recruiting for a "Forward Deployed Engineer (Internal)" as of September 2026 (the posting had ended as of October 16).
