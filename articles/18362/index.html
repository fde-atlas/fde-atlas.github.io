About This Document

Roughly 95% of generative AI investments fail to deliver measurable business outcomes—not because of the technology, but because of fragmentation across business understanding, implementation, and operations. This document brings together the complete picture of leveraging FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) in a single volume: the definition of an FDE that handles everything from diagnosis through implementation to operational handover, an 8-axis comparison with SES, SI, and strategy consulting, cost benchmarks by role and 24-month TCO, and a hybrid 18-month model that combines external and in-house capabilities.

Contents

  • The definition of an FDE and its 4 steps (diagnosis → design → implementation → handover), with real examples from 4 overseas AI companies
  • An 8-axis comparison of FDE × SES × SI × strategy consulting, plus a 3-question decision flow
  • Cost benchmarks by role, a 24-month TCO comparison, and the design of an 18-month hybrid rollout

Who this document is for

  • Companies whose AI investments are stuck at PoC and have not progressed to production operation
  • Executives and business owners choosing an external implementation partner
  • Procurement staff who want to compare the appropriate use and costs of FDEs, SES, SI, and strategy consulting

Table of Contents

  • 01 The Challenge: Most generative AI investments fail to deliver results. While more than 70% of companies have run PoCs, fewer than 20% have reached production operation. This section organizes the typical reasons PoCs never make it to production.
  • 02 What Is an FDE: Definition, 4 Steps, and Examples from Overseas AI Companies. Covers the definition of an FDE and the 4 steps of diagnosis → design → implementation → handover, including a comparison table with on-site SES and a comparison of 4 overseas AI companies.
  • 03 Comparing 4 Models: FDE, SES, SI, and Strategy Consulting. An 8-axis comparison table covering unit price, contract type, definition of outcomes, and more, plus a 3-question decision flow for choosing a model. Also covers the division of roles with Deployment Strategists and the standard ratio of 1 DS : 2–3 FDEs.
  • 04 Adoption Decisions: External or In-House. Five decision criteria including urgency, talent market, and TCO, plus the KPIs and prohibitions of the hybrid 18-month model that shifts from external-led to in-house-led.
  • 05 Costs and TCO: Unit Prices by Role and a 24-Month Comparison. Monthly rate benchmarks from junior to team-based arrangements, a comparison of 12/24-month TCO for external-led, hybrid, and in-house-led approaches, and 4 hidden cost items.
  • 06 FDX's Approach. The 4-service structure of AX Factory, an approach that starts with 1 DS + 2 FDEs and evaluates whether to continue or withdraw each quarter, and 2 case studies.
