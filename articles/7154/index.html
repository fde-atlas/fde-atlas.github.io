The FDE Brief: September 2026

Plank · 2026-06-20 · 1 min read

Summary

September 2026 saw system integrators convert earlier hyperscaler commitments into capacity. Accenture and Google Cloud launched a business group targeting 1,000 forward-deployed engineers, Palantir renewed its partnership with Fujitsu as a Global FDE Partner, and Devoteam certified 20 engineers, launched Europe's first FDE certification, and plans over 300 FDEs in 2026 across AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft accreditations. The author argues integrators, who already employ engineers and own enterprise relationships, are solving the staffing question raised in July, extending forward deployment to the midmarket and regions the way enterprise software has always spread, with Fujitsu's dual Salesforce and Palantir roles as the clearest signal.

Full text

The integrators commit, and the capacity arrives

On September 8, Accenture and Google Cloud formed the Accenture Gemini Enterprise Business Group with a planned 1,000-person forward-deployed engineering workforce, drawing on Accenture's roughly 50,000 Google Cloud-skilled staff. On September 10, Palantir and Fujitsu renewed their 2020 partnership with Fujitsu as a Global FDE Partner, pairing Fujitsu's Takane model and Uvance offerings with Foundry and AIP for customers in Japan and worldwide. On September 28, Devoteam certified 20 engineers through Google Cloud's FDE enablement program in Madrid, and on September 30 it said it is on track to field over 300 FDEs in 2026 under accreditations with AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft, including a five-year AWS agreement to scale FDE work across EMEA, and launched FDE Foundation by Devoteam, the first FDE certification from a European consulting firm, with a first cohort of 20 starting October 12. Microsoft added a Frontier Transformation Engineer certification for partners in July. Devoteam→

The read

Two months ago the open question was whether July's commitments from AWS and Microsoft could be staffed. September answered part of it: the integrators, who already employ the engineers and own the enterprise relationships, are standing up FDE practices under the hyperscalers' accreditations. That is how enterprise software has always reached the midmarket and the regions, and it is now happening for forward deployment, with certification programs attached. Fujitsu is the clearest signal. It joined Salesforce's FDE Partner Network in June and became Palantir's global FDE partner in September, so a Japanese manufacturer can now buy embedded engineering for two platforms from one integrator it already trusts. Devoteam's certification is the first in Europe and starts with 20 engineers, which is small, but it is the training pipeline the market has been missing.
