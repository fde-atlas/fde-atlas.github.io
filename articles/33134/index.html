Entry-level forward deployed engineer jobs

Kernavique · 2026-11-20 · 11 min read

Summary

A Kernavique one-day count on November 20, 2026 of 76 employer job feeds found 297 US postings titled “forward deployed,” of which only 27 carried an entry-level label: 14 internships, 12 new-grad roles and 1 junior posting. Palantir accounted for 21, with new-grad pay listed at an estimated $135,000–$145,000/year and internships at $10,000/month. Among the 10 largest hirers, no ordinary posting stated zero years of experience; the lowest floor was 1+ year. The authors caution this is a single-day convenience sample, not a market trend measure.

Key points

  • 27 of 297 US forward deployed postings were entry-level on Nov 20, 2026.
  • Palantir posted 21 of the 27; C3 AI posted 2.
  • Palantir new-grad pay: estimated $135,000–$145,000/year; internships $10,000/month.
  • 10 of 27 entry-level ads required a security clearance; none listed deadlines.
  • Largest hirers' ordinary roles required at least 1+ year; none allowed zero experience.

How many entry-level forward deployed engineer jobs are there?

Few, and most sit at one employer.

On November 20, 2026, we read the public job feeds of 76 employers, mostly AI and tech companies. They held 297 US postings with “forward deployed” in the title. Our tracker’s read earlier the same day found 298; the count fell by one between the two reads. Of the 297, 27 carried an entry-level word: 14 said intern or internship, 12 said new grad and 1 said junior.

Six employers account for all 27 in that reading. Palantir posted 21. C3 AI posted 2. Domino Data Lab, Coinbase, Saronic and Legora posted 1 each. Without Palantir, there were 6 entry-titled postings at 5 employers.

This is a one-day count from a convenience sample. It is not a market measure, and it cannot show whether the number is rising or falling.

What do the entry-level ads ask for?

These points come from a keyword read of each ad on November 20, 2026, not a full read. An ad may say more in words the check did not look for.

  • A graduation window. Twelve of the 27 ads state one. Palantir’s commercial new-grad ad says: “Must be graduating in December 2026 or Spring 2027.” C3 AI’s ads name December 2026 or Summer/Fall 2027. Domino Data Lab’s internship names Spring 2028.
  • A general-purpose language. Palantir asks for proficiency in languages “such as Python, Java, C++, TypeScript/JavaScript, or similar”. Domino Data Lab says Python is required.
  • Clearance, in government versions. Ten of the 27 ads require a clearance or eligibility to obtain one. Two call it a plus but not required.
  • Travel. Palantir’s commercial new-grad ad says: “Expect 25–50% travel, depending on team and location.”
  • No stated deadline. None of the 27 ads gave an application deadline in that check.

Our briefing on how to become a forward deployed engineer covers these programs in more depth. The interview guide covers what employers publish about hiring.

Do ordinary forward deployed roles take people with little experience?

Some do. None says zero.

We looked at the 10 employers with the most US forward deployed postings on the feeds on November 20, 2026. For each, we took the lowest number of years in any ad without an entry-level title.

  • 1+ years: Palantir (“1+ years of relevant, post-college work experience.”) and Invisible Technologies.
  • 2+ years: Rogo, for finance experience, not engineering.
  • 3+ years: Anduril and Saronic.
  • 4+ years: Scale AI.
  • 5+ years: Databricks, OpenAI and Cursor.
  • No number in any ad: Intercom.

None of the 10 had an ordinary forward deployed ad that states zero years.

Five of the 10 had at least one engineering ad with no number of years. No number does not mean open to beginners. One Databricks ad gives no number and says: “It is not intended for internship, new graduate, or entry-level applicants.”

Across all the feeds, 19 postings at 12 employers had no entry-level title but asked for two years or fewer. Not all are engineering roles. A title tells you little here; forward deployed engineer levels explains why.

Can I become a forward deployed engineer with no experience?

It depends on what “no experience” means.

You are a student or about to graduate. Yes. The campus programs above ask for a degree in progress and a graduation window, not years of work. Expect most of the openings to be at Palantir.

You have internships or projects but no full-time job. A few ads count them. Invisible Technologies’ Data Scientist (Forward Deployed) ad asks for 1+ years “through professional roles, internships, or coursework/research projects”, read November 20, 2026. Its Software Engineer (Forward Deployed) ad gives no number and uses similar wording.

You are not a student and have no engineering work behind you. The ads we read do not show a way in. The lowest stated floor at the 10 largest hirers was 1+ years.

What can this count not show?

  • Whether an ad is still open. The feeds list postings. They carry no flag for accepting applications.
  • Employers outside the 76 feeds. Employers that do not publish through Greenhouse, Ashby or Lever are not in this count.
  • Any trend. This is one day’s reading.

Questions

  • Are there entry-level forward deployed engineer jobs?
  • Yes, a small number. On the 76 employer job feeds we read on November 20, 2026, 27 of 297 US postings titled “forward deployed” carried an entry-level word in the title. Palantir posted 21 of the 27. C3 AI posted 2, and Domino Data Lab, Coinbase, Saronic and Legora posted 1 each.
  • Can I become a forward deployed engineer with no experience?
  • As a student or new graduate, yes, through a campus program with a graduation window. Outside those programs, the ads we read on November 20, 2026 do not show an opening that states zero years. Among the 10 largest hirers on the feeds, the lowest stated floor was 1+ years, at Palantir and Invisible Technologies.
  • What do forward deployed engineer new grad roles pay?
  • Two employers published new-grad figures in the feeds we read on November 20, 2026. Palantir’s new-grad ads list an estimated salary range of $135,000 - $145,000/year, not labeled base. C3 AI’s two new-grad ads list a base pay range of $120,000—$150,000 USD. The labels differ, so the two cannot be compared directly.
  • What does a forward deployed engineer internship pay?
  • It depends on the ad, and the labels differ. Read November 20, 2026, Palantir’s internship ads list a salary of $10,000/month, and its Year at Palantir ads list $5,900 - $10,500/month. Domino Data Lab lists $50—$55 USD hourly and Coinbase lists $50—$50 USD hourly. Saronic’s feed entry showed no pay figure.
  • Do I need a security clearance for an entry-level role?
  • Only for some. Of the 27 entry-titled postings read on November 20, 2026, 10 required a clearance or eligibility to obtain one, and 2 called it a plus but not required. The government versions of Palantir’s programs and C3 AI’s federal new-grad ad carry those lines. That count rests on a keyword read of each ad.
  • When do applications close?
  • The ads do not say. A keyword check of all 27 entry-titled ads on November 20, 2026 found no stated application deadline, and the feeds have no closing-date field. Twelve of the 27 state a graduation window instead. Check the ad itself before you rely on it being open.
