GenerativeX Raises a Total of 650 Million Yen in Series A Funding

Original title: GenerativeX、シリーズAで総額6.5億円の資金調達を実施

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-06-01 · 8 min read

Summary

GenerativeX, a Tokyo-based FDE consulting firm founded in June 2023, raised approximately 650 million yen in a Series A round led by Nissei Capital, with Salesforce Ventures, Angel Bridge, Deepcore, and SMBC Venture Capital participating. In three years the firm has served over 80 large enterprises across finance, pharma, manufacturing, and other sectors, with clients' combined market capitalization exceeding 950 trillion yen (about $6 trillion). Funds will go toward expanding its San Francisco and New York operations, hiring consultants and FDEs, and M&A focused on finance-domain AI agents. The company also emphasizes helping clients build in-house FDE teams to reduce vendor dependence and speed up delivery.

Key points

  • GenerativeX raised ~650 million yen in Series A led by Nissei Capital.
  • Clients include 80+ enterprises with combined market cap of ~950 trillion yen.
  • Funds target US expansion in San Francisco and New York, hiring, and M&A.
  • The firm promotes in-house FDE teams to cut vendor dependence.
  • Focus areas include AI implementation, internalization, and AI security.

GenerativeX Inc. (Headquarters: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director and CEO: Rei Araki; hereinafter "the Company") is pleased to announce that it has raised a total of approximately ¥650 million in its Series A round.

This round was carried out through a third-party allotment of new shares, with Nissei Capital Inc. as lead investor, and Salesforce Ventures, Angel Bridge Inc., Deepcore Inc., and SMBC Venture Capital Inc. as subscribers.

[Company Overview]

Since its founding in June 2023, the Company has operated as an FDE consulting firm, providing end-to-end support to major Japanese and international companies in finance, pharmaceuticals, manufacturing, and other sectors, spanning everything from AI agent design and implementation to strategy and organizational transformation.

Within three years of its founding, the combined market capitalization of the Company's client companies exceeded approximately ¥950 trillion (about $6 trillion), surpassing 80 companies. Covering a wide range of industries including finance, pharmaceuticals, manufacturing, IT and telecommunications, and trading companies, the Company is driving the forefront of generative AI adoption in the enterprise space.

While the rapid evolution of generative AI has dramatically improved software development productivity, the transformation of entire business processes is still a work in progress. The Company is promoting "end-to-end business transformation," in which AI agents go beyond mere development assistance and transform business processes themselves through human-AI collaboration.

[Business Outlook]

The Company's key focus areas going forward are as follows.

1. AI implementation as a management agenda

The rapid evolution of frontier models has dramatically reduced the cost of AI development and implementation. However, in reality, on-the-ground operational transformation has not kept pace with technological evolution when it comes to generating ROI directly tied to management outcomes and transforming into AI-native organizational structures. The Company's strength lies in its ability to consistently support everything from top-down strategy formulation to frontline operations with high agility, and professionals in each domain will drive transformation at home and abroad at the speed of technological evolution.

2. Promoting the internalization of FDE capabilities at client companies

With the evolution of coding agents, the advantage of having in-house AI implementation capabilities has never been greater. By embedding FDE talent who can bridge business requirements with IT/AI expertise inside client companies, the Company helps them break free from vendor dependence and fundamentally improve speed and cost structures from decision-making to release. The Company will work together with clients to build self-sustaining internal organizations that can operate without relying on outsourcing.

3. AI risk and security

With the evolution of the latest generation of frontier models such as Claude Mythos, the sophistication and increasing frequency of zero-day attacks and supply chain attacks have become an irreversible trend. As large-scale deployment of AI agents accelerates, many companies face the dilemma of an expanding attack surface. To help companies balance proactive AI adoption with resilience, the Company will strengthen its consulting support, leveraging expertise in governance design, risk assessment, and AI-powered vulnerability response.

[Use of Funds]

The funds raised will be invested in the following areas.

1. Global business expansion centered on the United States

The Company will strengthen its presence in San Francisco and New York and accelerate the expansion of its customer base in the North American market.

2. Strengthening organizational infrastructure

The Company will accelerate the hiring of executives, consultants, and FDEs to continuously improve the quality of client support.

3. Execution of M&A

The Company will execute strategic investments and capital and business alliances to promote the use of AI agents in the finance and M&A domain, one of its key focus areas.

■ About GenerativeX Inc.

GenerativeX Inc. is an FDE consulting firm founded in 2023. Specializing in enterprise business transformation support using AI agents, the company provides services to major clients in Japan and overseas, including in finance, pharmaceuticals, and manufacturing. Headquartered in Marunouchi, Tokyo, with offices in San Francisco and New York in the United States, the company is expanding its business globally.
