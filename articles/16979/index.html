Summary

This is a promotional page for QuickAI, a custom AI development service by Japan's Aiセキュリティソリューションズ, built around the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) model. Engineers embed in the client's workplace, observe operations directly, and rapidly build and iterate on bespoke AI tools and AI agents, in contrast to conventional development that gathers requirements remotely and follows lengthy specifications. The service follows three steps: analyzing workflows to identify AI opportunities, developing and deploying tools or agents, and providing ongoing post-launch improvement and operational support. Target use cases include inquiry handling, internal knowledge search, research summarization, data entry, and email drafting.

What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is an engineer who, rather than staying confined to a development site, goes directly into the client company's operations and takes end-to-end responsibility for AI design, development, and operations. While traditional IT consultants handle strategy planning and requirements definition, and SEs or outsourced development vendors build to specification, an FDE iterates on hypotheses in the field even before specifications are finalized, rapidly building prototypes and integrating them into operations. The most common failure in AI adoption is investing tens of millions of yen in AI that never gets used in the field. An FDE adjusts data quality, tacit knowledge, UI/UX, and operational workflows on the spot, ensuring AI doesn't end as a mere experiment but evolves into a system that generates ROI. With this FDE-style development model at its core, QuickAI is a development partner that designs, builds, and embeds dedicated AI tools and AI agents for your company in a short timeframe. We also develop systems that let AI agents handle tasks involving judgment and procedures—such as inquiry handling, internal knowledge search, research summarization, data entry, and email drafting—tailored to your business workflows. In addition, if you want AI to take over routine tasks, please also consider the AI Business Outsourcing Center, or if you want to validate ideas or build new businesses from zero to one, the AI Prototype Consultation Room.
