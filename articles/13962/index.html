JD Breakdown

Original title: JD 拆解 – FDE中国

FDE中国 · 2026-05-01 · 2 min read

Summary

This page is an index of FDEChina's job-description breakdown series, collecting nine position profiles of FDE roles across major companies: Anthropic, OpenAI, Palantir, Google Cloud, Cursor (Anysphere), Mistral, Tencent, and ByteDance (Volcano Engine). Each profile analyzes the role's stated responsibilities and organizational signals, such as Anthropic's 40/30/30 split across building, architecture, and customer discovery; Google Cloud's CEO-announced plan to hire hundreds of FDEs; and Palantir placing FDSEs under business development rather than engineering. The series aims to identify China-based equivalents by responsibilities rather than job titles.

Key points

  • Index of nine JD breakdowns covering FDE roles at major US, European, and Chinese companies.
  • Anthropic's FDE role splits work 40/30/30 across building, architecture, and customer discovery.
  • Google Cloud's CEO personally announced hiring, with 59 openings in week one and plans for hundreds.
  • Palantir, originator of the title, organizes FDSEs under business development, not engineering.
  • Domestic equivalents include Tencent and ByteDance (Volcano Engine), which use the FDE title directly.
