What is an FDE? The Meaning and Role of a Forward Deployed Engineer, and How It Differs from an SIer

Original title: FDEとは？Forward Deployed Engineerの意味・役割・SIerとの違い

株式会社本質 · 2026-07-10 · 3 min read

Summary

This explainer from Japanese firm Honshitsu defines the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE): an engineer embedded at the customer's site who implements and iterates on AI and software within real business contexts. It contrasts FDEs with traditional SIers and IT consultants, who gather requirements and deliver months later, while FDEs build small, improve quickly, and transfer skills so clients can operate independently. The article outlines four FDE roles—problem discovery, implementation, operational adoption, and knowledge transfer—and argues the model suits SMBs lacking IT departments, solving both the requirements-definition barrier and unused-system problems. It closes by describing Honshitsu's own FDE-based AX/DX support from Tokyo and Akita.

Key points

  • FDEs embed at customer sites and build AI/software within business context.
  • Unlike SIers, they build small and iterate rather than deliver after months.
  • Four roles: problem discovery, implementation, adoption, and tech transfer.
  • Model fits SMBs without IT staff, avoiding unused-system failures.
  • Honshitsu offers FDE-based AX/DX support from Tokyo and Akita.

An FDE is an engineer who embeds themselves at the customer's site and implements AI and software within the context of actual business operations. The role originated at US tech companies and is gaining attention as a core profession of the generative AI era. This page explains what an FDE is, their role, how they differ from traditional outsourcing, and how small and medium-sized businesses can make use of them.

DEFINITION

Definition of FDE

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) literally means "an engineer deployed on the front lines." Rather than waiting for specifications in a development office, an FDE embeds themselves at the customer's site (the front lines), understands the business, and implements and improves software and AI on the spot.

This role was established by a US data analytics company, and in recent years generative AI companies and major SaaS providers have rapidly expanded hiring, making it a defining profession of the generative AI era and drawing worldwide attention. In Japan as well, companies are beginning to adopt FDE-style support models as a way to fill the "last mile" of AI adoption.

COMPARISON

Differences from traditional SIers and IT consultants

AspectTraditional (SIer / IT consultant)FDE
ApproachTakes requirements back to the office and delivers months laterEmbeds on-site, builds small, and improves immediately
RequirementsThe client must have the ability to define requirementsDiscovers requirements together through observation of and dialogue about the business
GoalDelivery of the systemA state where operations keep running and the site can operate on its own
After deliverySupport limited to the maintenance contractTransfers knowledge through documentation and workshops to support self-sufficiency

ROLE

The role and work of an FDE

01 Identifying issues

Through observation of and interviews about the business, identifies root causes rather than surface-level requests.

02 Implementation

Combines generative AI, existing SaaS, and code to build a "usable form" by the shortest route.

03 Operational adoption

Establishes operations the team can comfortably continue using, and iterates on improvements.

04 Knowledge transfer

Uses documentation and workshops to hand over to the customer's team so they can operate on their own.

FOR SMB

Why FDE works especially well for small and medium-sized businesses

For SMBs without an IT department, an FDE is a practical option that solves both the "requirements definition barrier" and the "adopted but unused" problem at the same time.

  • No need to define requirements yourself — The FDE observes your operations and translates challenges into requirements.
  • No dedicated IT staff needed — The FDE accompanies you from implementation through operational adoption.
  • No "build it and done" — Knowledge transfer to a self-sufficient state is included in the goal.
  • Small initial investment — Suited to a small start from a single business process rather than large-scale development.

OUR MODEL

The FDE model of Honshitsu Inc.

Based in Ginza, Tokyo and Odate, Akita, Honshitsu Inc. supports SMBs' AX (AI Transformation) and DX through its FDE model along two pillars: talent development and implementation (self-sufficiency). An FDE embeds on-site to implement solutions, AI training develops people who can use them effectively, and the end state is one where your company runs everything on its own.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about FDE

How do you pronounce FDE?+

It is pronounced "eff-dee-ee." It is an abbreviation of Forward Deployed Engineer.

What is the difference between an FDE and a regular engineer (SE)?+

They differ in where they work and the scope of their responsibility. While an SE develops in a development office based on specifications, an FDE embeds at the customer's site and handles everything end-to-end, from identifying issues through implementation, operational adoption, and knowledge transfer.

Can we outsource FDE services instead of hiring one in-house?+

Yes. At Honshitsu Inc., we offer "implementation and self-sufficiency support," in which an FDE embeds at your site to implement AI systems and accompanies you until you can operate them on your own, on a contract-based (outsourcing) arrangement.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on the scope and duration of the support. The initial consultation is free, so please tell us about your current business challenges. We will propose the optimal form of support and an approximate estimate.
