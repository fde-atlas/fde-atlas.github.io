RoomClip Inc. FDE Job Opening: 1 Position, Salary up to 9 Million Yen

Original title: FDE board

ルームクリップ株式会社 · 2026-06-30 · 1 min read

Summary

FDE board lists one Forward Deployed Engineer opening at RoomClip, Inc., a Japanese company running the home-living photo platform RoomClip and the furniture brand KANADEMONO. The company holds over 6 million user-uploaded photos and more than 30 million annual search queries, which it monetizes through a B2B business serving furniture, appliance, housing-equipment, and daily-goods makers. The FDE would use RoomClip's API and data to solve DX and marketing problems for housing-related companies, covering system design, integration of campaign systems and corporate admin screens, and operational adoption support. Salary reaches up to 9 million yen. The role is hybrid; fully remote work is not allowed. The company, founded in 2011 and based in Shibuya, Tokyo, has around 140 employees.

About RoomClip, Inc.

The company operates "RoomClip," a housing lifestyle platform for posting and browsing photos of homes, as well as the furniture brand "KANADEMONO." It holds over 6 million user-generated photos and more than 30 million search behavior data points annually, and is growing its B2B business that leverages this data for marketing by manufacturers of furniture, home appliances, housing equipment, and daily goods. The company has around 140 employees.

Characteristics of FDE Roles at This Company

As an FDE, you will use the company's photo data and behavioral logs as your weapons to solve DX and marketing challenges for housing-related companies through technology. You will be responsible for everything from system design combining RoomClip's APIs and data, to integration into campaign systems and corporate admin dashboards, and support for operational adoption. Hybrid work is required; fully remote work is not available.
