Can You Switch to an FDE Role with No Experience? A Realistic Path and the Preparation You Need

Original title: 未経験からFDEに転職できる？現実的な道筋と必要な準備

FDE総研 · 2026-07-23 · 6 min read

Summary

This article from FDE Soken examines whether people without direct FDE experience can transition into the role. It argues that starting from zero is hard, but candidates with software engineering or client-facing backgrounds can bridge the gap, since implementation ability is the core of the job. It cites a Financial Times report that FDE-related job postings grew over 800% year-on-year in 2025, and lists Japanese companies hiring, including Palantir, Salesforce, LayerX, and ANDPAD. It notes Anthropic's job posting requiring four-plus years in technical, client-facing roles, and proposes a five-step preparation path from building coding fundamentals to applying for FDE positions.

Key points

  • Implementation skill is the foundation; pure beginners face a steep start.
  • SWE, SES/contracting, generative AI, and domain expertise all transfer well.
  • FT reported FDE-related job postings grew over 800% year-on-year in 2025.
  • Anthropic's posting cites 4+ years of technical, client-facing experience.
  • The author recommends five sequential steps, from coding basics to applying.

Can you switch to FDE with no experience?

It is not easy from a completely inexperienced starting point, but if you have SWE or client-facing experience, it is well within reach. Since FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a role where you implement things yourself, you first need a technical foundation. On top of that, accumulating customer-facing experience opens the path. With demand expanding rapidly right now, the opportunity to enter the field itself is growing.

What is the difference between complete beginners and those with experience?

The dividing line is "whether you can build and run things yourself," and having implementation skills is the first hurdle. Because FDEs solve problems hands-on at customer sites, starting from a state where you cannot write code puts a heavy load on you. On the other hand, people with software engineering (SWE) or client-facing experience can bridge into FDE using one of those as a foundation. For example, someone who can implement but has little customer interaction should focus on customer touchpoints, while someone strong in negotiation but shallow in implementation should focus on building implementation skills (this is just one example).

Why are implementation skills essential for FDE?

Because FDE is a role where you implement things yourself at customer sites, and implementation ability is the very foundation of the job. FDEs do not stop at making proposals; they take requirements from customers and are responsible for building something that works and delivering it on the spot. That is why creating a state where "you can build and run things yourself" is the starting point for those without experience. It is also why jumping straight to FDE from zero programming experience is difficult — this foundation is a prerequisite. Flip it around, though, and once you have that implementation foundation, you can layer customer-facing experience on top and move closer to FDE. Starting by building a programming foundation is the realistic first step.

How much are FDE hires growing right now?

FDE-related job openings are expanding both in Japan and abroad, and the market is at a prime opportunity for entry. Following a surge in overseas job postings, multiple companies in Japan have also begun hiring for FDE and similar roles. With the market expanding now, it is a great time to enter.

Which companies in Japan are hiring for FDE?

Companies include Palantir, Salesforce, SB OAI Japan, ExaWizards, LayerX, AI Shift, and ANDPAD. These companies are reported to have started hiring for FDE-type positions (Findy). The fact that these are companies centered on generative AI and data utilization suggests that FDE demand is tied to the spread of LLM production deployment. Not only domestic AI startups but also global companies like Palantir are on the list, showing that FDE and similar roles are spreading beyond a single niche — meaning those without experience are not limited to just one option.

How fast are job openings growing?

The UK's Financial Times reported that FDE-related job postings grew more than 800% year-over-year in 2025. This statistic is referenced by SB Bit as related information. Growth of over 800% year-over-year is one example showing that the FDE role itself is in a stage of rapid emergence. With the market expanding, now is a time when the door to entry is easier to open, even for those without experience.

What backgrounds give you an advantage in an FDE career change?

Backgrounds with implementation skills, customer-facing experience, or both work in your favor. Since FDEs bridge technology and customers, there are many cases where the strengths you have built in your career so far can be used as they are.

What backgrounds are valuable for an FDE career change?

Software engineering, SES/contract development, generative AI and data utilization, and industry domain experience form the foundation. Here is a summary of the main backgrounds and how they translate to FDE work.

BackgroundWhy it helps
Software engineerImplementation skills are the very foundation of FDE
SES / contract developmentExperience balancing client interaction and implementation
Generative AI / data utilizationDirectly connected to demand for LLM production deployment
Industry domain experienceAbility to deeply understand customer problems

For example, people with SES or contract development experience have already worked in a style where they talk with clients while implementing, so they are considered a strong fit for the FDE way of working (one example).

Can you bridge over from a completely different industry?

Even from a completely different industry, you can bridge over if you have either technical skills or customer-facing experience. Industry domain experience becomes a strength in "deeply understanding customer problems," while generative AI and data utilization experience connects directly to "demand for LLM production deployment." The realistic approach is to identify which part of your background forms the foundation for FDE and fill in the missing side.

What years of experience give you an advantage in an FDE career change?

Anthropic's job posting lists 4+ years of experience in technical roles with customer interaction as an example requirement. This is one example shown in Anthropic's official job posting. A career that has touched both technology and customer interaction over a span of several years serves as a rough benchmark for being favorably evaluated as an FDE candidate. It is worth noting that what is emphasized here is the combination of "technical roles with customer interaction." It can be read that what matters is not years of implementation alone or customer interaction alone, but how much experience you have where the two intersect. Even when preparing without experience, deliberately accumulating experience where these two overlap is likely to be more effective than sheer years.

What are realistic steps to aim for FDE without experience?

Prepare step by step in five stages, from building an implementation foundation to building a customer-facing track record. Rather than jumping straight into job applications, the shortcut is to build up, one by one, the material you can talk about in your resume.

What are the five steps from implementation to customer touchpoints?

Build up in the order: "build → deliver → work with AI in production → customer touchpoints → apply." Specifically, the flow is as follows.

  • Build an implementation foundation — Using Python and the like, aim for a state where you can build and run things yourself
  • Build small and deliver — Create deliverables that someone actually uses, such as business tools or automation
  • Work with generative AI in production — Practice prompt design and agent development
  • Build a track record of customer touchpoints — Experience the full cycle of hearing issues, proposing solutions, and implementing them
  • Apply for FDE positions — Apply once you can speak to the above in your work history

Each step builds the foundation for the next. Only once you can implement things can you "build small and deliver," and only once you have experience delivering can you speak to a track record of customer touchpoints—it's a cumulative structure. The more patiently you proceed in order, the richer the material you can present in your work history. In particular, experience "working with generative AI in production" is a strength that directly connects to demand for LLM production operations, making it easy to highlight in your work history and a stage that tends to become compelling material for those without prior experience. Ultimately, the realistic order is to first get to the point where you can speak to this series of efforts in your work history, and then apply for FDE positions.
