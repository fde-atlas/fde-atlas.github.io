Report File: Plank FDE Job Market Tracker

Original title: 报告档案：Plank FDE 岗位市场追踪器——982 个在招职位的供需与定价

FDE中国 · 2026-09-04 · 1 min read

Summary

Plank's continuously updated FDE job market tracker (updated 2026-09-04) collected 982 open FDE positions across 462 companies, estimating roughly 1,000-1,400 exact-title roles globally (650-885 in the US). Median base pay varies by vertical and funding stage: frontier labs around $250K versus $148K for industrial AI, and $150K at seed-stage companies versus $243K at frontier labs; coastal hubs pay about 20% more than inland areas. The editor cautions the data covers only the English-language job market and shouldn't be extrapolated to China. The spread across 569 companies and 22 verticals may preview how FDE demand diffuses domestically, and readers are advised to track vertical distribution monthly.

Key points

  • Tracker covers 982 open FDE roles at 462 companies; global estimate 1,000-1,400 positions.
  • Median base: frontier labs ~$250K vs industrial AI $148K; seed $150K vs frontier labs $243K.
  • Coastal hubs pay roughly 20% more than inland locations.
  • Data reflects the English-language market only; not applicable to China job counts.
  • Recommended as a monthly data source, watching vertical distribution trends.

Editor's Note FDEChina Editorial Team

This is currently the most granular data source on the FDE job market, and its value lies in continuous updates plus multi-dimensional breakdowns: by industry vertical (median base of $250K at frontier labs vs. $148K for industrial AI), by funding stage ($150K at seed stage to $243K at frontier labs), and by geography (coastal hubs roughly 20% higher than inland regions). A caveat on misuse: the data covers the English-language job market, so the numbers cannot be extrapolated to job volumes in China; when citing, note the scope and update date.

Compared with salary report data sources, the incremental value of this card lies on the "supply side": it answers how many companies are hiring and in which industries, rather than what individuals can earn. For observers in China, the diffusion trajectory across 569 companies and 22 industry verticals can serve as a forward-looking reference for the domestic market — domestic FDE demand will most likely spread from major tech companies to the industry long tail along a similar path. Recommended action: bookmark the tracker as a monthly data source and focus on trends in the industry vertical distribution.
