Forward Deployed Engineering manager: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-10-31 · 11 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch analyzed an October 24, 2026 snapshot of public job boards, finding 71 management or program titles among 725 FDE-family postings (9.8%) at 26 of 121 employers. Databricks (25) and Accenture (12) account for over half. The piece breaks down title wording, locations, remote share, travel requirements, and posted pay: 27 US postings show a median midpoint of $249,400, above the $216,104 for engineer titles, and stated travel tops out at a 50% median versus 25% for engineers. Posted ranges are not offers.

Key points

  • 71 of 725 FDE-family postings (9.8%) carry management or program titles at 26 employers.
  • Databricks and Accenture post 37 of the 71 management roles (52.1%).
  • US management postings show a median pay midpoint of $249,400 vs $216,104 for engineers.
  • Median stated travel upper bound is 50%, double the 25% for engineer titles.
  • 18 management postings (25.4%) list remote; Databricks accounts for 13 of them.

Published

Forward Deployed Engineering manager seats lead or run teams of Forward Deployed Engineers, and employers post them as Manager, Senior Manager, Engineering Manager, Director, Head or VP of Forward Deployed Engineering, plus FDE engagement and program manager titles. In FDE Dispatch's October 24, 2026 snapshot of public job boards, 71 of 725 FDE-family postings (9.8%) carry a management or program title, at 26 of 121 employers. Databricks (25) and Accenture (12) post 37 of the 71; the other 24 employers post 1 to 3 each. 35 of the 71 titles name the team Forward Deployed Engineering. 27 US-located postings list a USD range, with a median midpoint of $249,400, against $216,104 for US engineer-title postings; posted ranges are not offers. Postings that state travel put the median upper bound at 50%, against 25% for engineer titles. FDE Dispatch is not affiliated with any employer named on this page; the figures come from public job postings.

FDE management titles fall into four groups by title text. Manager, Senior Manager and Engineering Manager titles lead with 39 postings at 16 employers, for example Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering at Anthropic, OpenAI, Databricks, Parloa and Snorkel AI. Director, Head and VP titles add 11 at 10 employers. The other 21 are engagement, delivery, solutions and technical program manager titles, and Databricks posts 16 of those 21.

How much FDE managers travel

Travel is mentioned in 32 of the 71 management postings, and 22 state a percentage. The median stated upper bound is 50%, against 25% for engineer titles (242 stated) and 75% for deployment strategist titles (49 stated), the same figures as how much forward deployed engineers travel.

7 of the 22 are Accenture postings that say "0 to 100%", a range that covers any amount of travel. Leaving those 7 out, the median of the other 15 is still 50%: 8 top out at 50%, 1 at 40%, 2 at 25% and 4 at 20%. Management postings state more travel than engineer postings, so ask how many customer sites the team covers and how many days a month the manager spends at them.

Method and data

Population: the 71 postings with role_family = management among the 725 FDE-family postings from 121 employers counted on the salary, job titles, locations and travel pages. Snapshot pulled October 24, 2026 from public Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday boards. Recruiting agencies and duplicates removed.

Title type and wording: keyword match on the posting title. Title type is the first match in order: program manager; engagement manager, delivery partner manager, solutions manager or delivery & operations; head of, VP or director; every other management title counts as Manager, Senior Manager or Engineering Manager. US-located and remote-listed use the published locations-index definitions: a posting is US-located when every listed location is in the US, and remote-listed when any listed location contains Remote.

Travel: upper bound = largest number in the stated percentage string, the rule on the travel page. US pay: US-only location, USD range posted, first range on the posting. These 71 listings were live on the October 24, 2026 snapshot; counts are open postings on one day and do not measure headcount.

Downloads: FDE management JSON, field notes, and the per-posting postings JSON. All published datasets are listed on FDE Dispatch job-posting data. Built by

Questions people ask

What job titles do Forward Deployed Engineering managers have?

In the October 24, 2026 snapshot, 71 FDE-family postings carry a management or program title. By title text: 39 are Manager, Senior Manager or Engineering Manager titles at 16 employers (for example Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering), 11 are Director, Head or VP titles at 10 employers, 12 are engagement, delivery or solutions manager titles at 3 employers, and 9 are technical program manager titles at 3 employers. 35 titles name the team Forward Deployed Engineering, 24 use the abbreviation FDE, and 7 say Forward Deployed Engineer.

How much do Forward Deployed Engineering managers make?

27 US-located FDE management postings at 14 employers list a USD range. The median midpoint is $249,400, against $216,104 across 255 US engineer-title postings. Manager, Senior Manager and Engineering Manager titles post a median midpoint of $249,400 (13 postings) and Director, Head or VP titles $311,750 (6). The highest manager-title ranges are Anthropic at $320,000 to $405,000 and OpenAI at $302,000 to $385,000, both for Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering in New York. Posted ranges are not offers and exclude equity value.

Which companies hire Forward Deployed Engineering managers?

26 of the 121 employers with FDE-family postings post at least one management or program title. Databricks posts 25 and Accenture 12, together 37 of the 71. OpenAI posts 3, and Anthropic, Cohere, Cursor, NVIDIA, Parloa, PwC, Snorkel AI and Thomson Reuters post 2 each. 15 more employers post 1 each, including Adobe, Cloudflare, Datadog, Notion and Stripe.

Do Forward Deployed Engineering managers travel?

Travel is mentioned in 32 of the 71 management postings, and 22 state a percentage. The median stated upper bound is 50%, against 25% for engineer titles and 75% for deployment strategist titles. 7 of the 22 are Accenture postings that say 0 to 100%; leaving them out, the median of the other 15 is still 50%, and 8 of those 15 top out at 50%.

Are Forward Deployed Engineering manager roles remote?

18 of the 71 management postings (25.4%) list a remote location on the locations index, against 199 of 725 (27.4%) across the full snapshot. Databricks accounts for 13 of the 18. Across the other 25 employers, 5 of 46 management postings (10.9%) list remote: Cohere 2, Cisco 1, Cursor 1 and NVIDIA 1.
