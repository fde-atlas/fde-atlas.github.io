Forward Deployed Engineer Intern | Lio | Munich, Germany

Zero G Talent · 2026-11-19 · 3 min read

Summary

Lio (formerly askLio), an a16z-backed, Y Combinator-built startup building AI agents for enterprise procurement, is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer Intern in Munich. Candidates must be enrolled in CS, Business Informatics, Engineering or a related field, have solid coding fundamentals, use AI coding tools daily, be comfortable with APIs and data, and speak fluent English (German is a plus). The role involves building AI agents from scratch, integrating them with SAP, Oracle, Coupa, Databricks and Snowflake, owning integrations, data pipelines and agent configurations, and joining customer workshops and go-lives with Fortune 500 teams. Lio ships to production weekly or daily, requires five days a week in the office, and explicitly warns the job goes beyond 9 to 5. Perks include an unlimited AI coding budget, and every employee holds equity.

Forward Deployed Engineer Intern (all genders) – Munich

Procurement touches every company, every product, every euro spent, and it’s still one of the most traditional spaces out there. We’re rebuilding it with AI.

At Lio, we’re building the AI workforce for procurement. We lead the largest AI deployments in procurement, at Fortune 500 companies around the globe, and we’re growing like few companies ever do. We need exceptional engineers to build what comes next, and we want you to start with us early.

What this internship really is

  • AI-native, from the ground up. No pre-AI SaaS legacy. You help build AI products and agents from scratch, from agents across the whole procurement process to voice avatars in strategic negotiations.
  • Wired into real enterprise systems. You work on connecting AI agents to the systems that run Fortune 500 companies: SAP, Oracle, Coupa, Databricks, Snowflake. Millions of orders, contracts and suppliers, live.
  • Real responsibility from day one. You take ownership of concrete pieces of real customer projects, from integrations and data pipelines to agent configurations and debugging in live environments, with experienced Forward Deployed Engineers at your side.
  • Built for one customer, live at many. You help design solutions for a customer’s problem and see how they become part of the product everyone runs on.
  • Agentic speed. We ship to production every week, often every day, and fix fast. High intensity, and the steepest learning curve you’ll find.
  • No boundaries. You work with the latest frontier models, without limits, and use any means to reach your goals as fast as possible.
  • More than an engineer. You get a look at the whole picture: customer workshops, demos, go-lives and conversations with Fortune 500 teams. Where you want to grow is up to you.

What you should know before you apply

  • We’re in the office five days a week.
  • This is not a 9 to 5 job. We grow faster than our processes can keep up, and there are weeks when a project needs everything you have. In return, you’ll learn more in a few months than in years anywhere else.

What you bring

  • You build with AI. You use AI coding tools and agents daily, have solid coding fundamentals, and are comfortable with APIs and data. Personal projects, hackathons, open source or previous internships count. Show us what you’ve built.
  • Ownership and curiosity. You grab opportunities instead of waiting for them, teach yourself whatever the problem needs, and don’t give up when something breaks.
  • Customer mindset. You want to understand a problem before you solve it, you enjoy working with people, and you hold your work to a high bar.
  • You’re currently enrolled in Computer Science, Business Informatics, Engineering or a similar field.
  • Fluent English is a must, German is a plus.

Procurement knowledge or experience with ERP systems such as SAP or Oracle is a plus, but not a requirement.

Why Lio

Lio is backed by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) and built at Y Combinator, with offices in Munich and New York. We’re winning, and every employee is a shareholder, so you win with us.

You’ll work with some of the smartest, most driven and unconventional people in Europe, and no two weeks look the same: one week a16z drops by for a lecture, the next you’re on a flight to a client.

On top of that, you get an unlimited AI coding budget, the newest tools within a week of release, free breakfast and dinner, an office gym, a subsidized Wellpass or Deutschlandticket, and events from hackathons and game nights to Wiesn.

Sounds like you? Let’s talk.
