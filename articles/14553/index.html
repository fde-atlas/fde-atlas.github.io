Accenture Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-10-30 · 9 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch analyzes 40 Accenture FDE-family postings from an October 24, 2026 snapshot of 725 postings across 121 employers. Accenture ranks fourth by count, with 12 management titles (30.0% of its postings, the highest share among employers with 10+ postings versus 9.8% overall). Nine titles mention cybersecurity, unique among employers, and four reference other platforms (Palantir, AWS). Only 9 postings are US-located; 60% are classified outside the US. The 9 US postings list pay from $70,350 to $434,000, with unusually wide bands (median top is 3.34x the floor versus 1.38 across all employers). All 12 postings stating travel say a boilerplate "0 to 100%", so no travel median is reported.

Key points

  • Accenture has 40 of 725 FDE-family postings, fourth by count.
  • Management titles are 30.0% of its postings, the highest among large employers.
  • Nine titles name cybersecurity; no other employer's FDE titles do.
  • US pay ranges span $70,350 to $434,000 with very wide bands (3.34x median).
  • All 12 travel-stating postings use a boilerplate 0 to 100% range.

Published

Accenture posts Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) roles on its consulting career ladder, from Consultant and Specialist up to Manager, Senior Manager and Associate Director. In FDE Dispatch's October 24, 2026 snapshot of public job boards, Accenture accounts for 40 of 725 FDE-family postings (5.5%), fourth by count: 28 engineer-family and 12 management. That 30.0% management share is the highest of any employer with 10 or more postings, against 9.8% across all 725. 9 Accenture titles name cybersecurity, and no other employer's FDE titles do. 31 of the 40 list no US office. All 12 postings that state a travel percentage say "0 to 100%", a boilerplate range, so this page reports no travel median. US postings list pay ranges between $70,350 and $434,000; posted ranges are not offers. FDE Dispatch is not affiliated with Accenture; the figures come from Accenture's public job postings.

Accenture puts its consulting career levels on Forward Deployed Engineer titles: a Cybersecurity FDE Consultant, a Forward Deployed Engineer Manager, an AI Engineer FDE Software Engineering Sr. Manager. 28 of its 40 FDE-family postings are engineer-family and 12 are management titles. It posts no deployment strategist titles in this snapshot.

How much Accenture Forward Deployed roles travel

Travel appears in 14 of 40 Accenture postings (35.0%), and 12 state a percentage. All 12 say "0 to 100%": the 9 US-located postings and the 3 Prague cybersecurity postings. No other employer in the snapshot uses that wording.

"0 to 100%" covers any amount of travel, so this page reports no median upper bound for Accenture; a median of 100% would only restate the boilerplate. The all-employer page, how much forward deployed engineers travel, leaves the same 12 rows out of its band table and reports a 30% median stated upper bound across employers. On an Accenture FDE seat, ask the recruiter how many days a week the team spends at the client site.

Office, hybrid or remote

Accenture's workplace field is blank on 31 of its 40 FDE-family postings. Of the rest, 7 say Hybrid, 1 Remote and 1 On-Site, which sums to 40. The 7 Hybrid postings include all 3 Palantir roles in Chicago and the Paris posting. 1 posting lists a remote location on the locations index, the CyberCX Senior Engineer (FDE) role based in Melbourne; across the full snapshot, 199 of 725 postings (27.4%) do.

The 2 Toronto postings list only Canadian offices, and the snapshot records their ranges as USD ($100,300 to $216,700 and $142,050 to $292,000); they are left out of the US table. The other 29 postings with no US office list no pay, and none of the 40 mention equity.

Method and data

Population: 40 Accenture postings with in_page_stats among the 725 FDE-family postings from 121 employers counted on the salary, job titles, locations and travel pages. All 40 come from Accenture's public Workday careers site; snapshot pulled October 24, 2026. Recruiting agencies and duplicates removed.

Level, cybersecurity and platform: keyword match on the posting title. Level is the first match in order Associate Director, Senior Manager, Associate Manager, Manager, Lead, Senior, Consultant, Specialist. US-located and remote-listed use the published locations-index definitions: a posting is US-located when every listed location is in the US, and remote-listed when any listed location contains Remote. Listed-location counts are the entries in the index's location list. Country uses the first listed location.

Travel: stated percentage strings as posted; the "0 to 100%" rows are reported as a count with no median. US pay: US-only location, USD range posted, first range on the posting; band width is the top of the range divided by the floor. These 40 listings were live on the October 24, 2026 snapshot; counts are open postings on one day.

Downloads: Accenture JSON, field notes, and the per-posting postings JSON. All published datasets are listed on FDE Dispatch job-posting data. Built by
