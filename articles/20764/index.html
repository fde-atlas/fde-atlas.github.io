What Is an FDE's Annual Salary? Market Rates in Japan and Overseas (2026) and How to Increase It

Original title: FDEの年収・給料はいくら?日本と海外の相場(2026年)と上げ方

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-07-23 · 20 min read

Summary

This article compiles FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) salary data from job postings, Levels.fyi reports, and private compensation reports as of October 2026. In the US, posted base salaries include OpenAI at $185k–$300k, Anthropic Staff FDE at $280k–$320k, and Palantir at $135k–$200k; Palantir's US median total compensation is about $255k. Private reports estimate frontier AI lab FDEs earn $385k at mid-level and over $1M at principal level, with equity making up 60–70% of total pay. In Japan, full-time FDE offers mostly fall in the ¥10M–20M range, with a median upper limit of ¥15M across 26 Japanese-company postings. The article attributes high pay to supply-demand gaps, direct revenue impact, and equity-heavy structures, and offers tactics for raising compensation.

Key points

  • US posted base pay: OpenAI $185k–$300k, Anthropic Staff FDE $280k–$320k, Palantir $135k–$200k.
  • Palantir US FDE median total compensation is about $255k per Levels.fyi (224 reports).
  • Frontier AI labs' FDE equity share rose to 60–70% of total compensation.
  • Japan FDE full-time offers center on ¥10M–20M; median upper limit ¥15M.
  • High pay stems from talent scarcity, revenue linkage, and equity structures.

Written and reviewed by: FDE Talent Editorial Team / Primary sources such as official job postings and official company announcements are cited

The FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is currently one of the most talked-about engineering roles in terms of salary and compensation. Overseas compensation reports describe it as "the highest-paying generalist role in the AI field," and in Japan, job postings offering annual salaries above 10 million yen are now the norm.

This article organizes FDE salary benchmarks, split between overseas and Japan, based on company job postings, public compensation data such as Levels.fyi, overseas compensation reports, and aggregations of domestic job listings. It also covers the breakdown of monthly and annual pay, why salaries are so high, and how to increase them. Overseas and Japanese job postings and Levels.fyi figures were verified as of October 16, 2026 (the overseas compensation report was published in May 2026). Please note that figures may fluctuate due to exchange rates and market conditions.

Key Takeaways of This Article

  • Annual salaries offered for FDE positions (full-time) in Japan generally fall in the 10-20 million yen band. According to an analysis of 35 domestic job postings published on note (February 2026), the median upper limit of offered annual salaries at the 26 Japanese companies was 15 million yen.
  • Looking at FDE monthly salaries, the lower limits stated in Japanese FDE job postings vary by posting, from 357,143 yen for JAPAN AI's Associate FDE (open to recent graduates) to 1 million yen at SB OAI Japan. SoftBank (seconded to SB OAI Japan) lists a monthly salary of 523,750-999,000 yen, and JAPAN AI lists 500,000-1,428,571 yen (as of October 16, 2026).
  • The breakdown of FDE compensation differs by company; some Japanese job postings' monthly salaries include overtime pay for 40-45 hours, and at overseas frontier AI companies, equity (stock compensation) makes up a large share of total compensation.
  • As of October 16, 2026, the annual salary ranges (mainly base salary) stated in US FDE job postings were $185,000-$300,000 for OpenAI (San Francisco), $280,000-$320,000 for Anthropic's Staff FDE (US), and $135,000-$200,000 for Palantir (New York). In terms of total compensation including stock, the median self-reported total compensation for Palantir FDEs in the US was approximately $255,000 (Levels.fyi).
  • The structural reasons FDE salaries are high are threefold: supply-demand imbalance, direct impact on revenue, and a high equity ratio.

Overseas FDE Salaries: Base Salaries in Job Postings and Total Compensation Including Equity

US FDE job postings list annual salary ranges. As of October 16, 2026, OpenAI's FDE (San Francisco) is $185,000-$300,000, Anthropic's Staff FDE (New York, San Francisco, Seattle) is $280,000-$320,000, and Palantir's FDSE (Forward Deployed Software Engineer, New York), the origin of the FDE role, is $135,000-$200,000. OpenAI states this is the base salary range, with equity granted separately and performance-based bonuses for eligible employees. Palantir also states that RSUs (restricted stock units) and signing bonuses may be added.

In terms of total compensation including equity, the US median for Palantir FDEs, based on 224 reports submitted to Levels.fyi, is $255,000 (US range approx. $201,000–$620,000, as of October 16, 2026). For frontier AI companies such as OpenAI and Anthropic, there are private reports estimating total compensation.

  • A compensation report published in May 2026 by US-based Perspective AI (a synthesis of approximately 1,200 data points from Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, salary ranges in job postings, Blind posts, and reports from its own network) estimates the average total compensation of FDEs at frontier AI companies such as Anthropic and OpenAI at $385,000 at the mid level, $610,000 at the staff level, and over $1 million at the principal level
  • The same report analyzes that FDE compensation at frontier labs is 2 to 3.5 times that of equivalent roles at Palantir, and that the difference is entirely due to equity (stock compensation)
  • According to the same report, at frontier AI companies equity accounts for 60–70% of total compensation, up from 35–45% two years ago

The figures in this report are estimates synthesized from multiple public data sources, not figures published by the companies themselves. Even so, when compared alongside base salaries in job postings, it becomes clear that the reality of high FDE salaries overseas is not "exceptionally high base salaries" but rather a structure in which "total compensation jumps up because of equity at fast-growing AI companies." Since the equity of unlisted companies changes in value depending on future valuations, evaluation must consider not only headline amounts but also vesting conditions and risks.

FDE Salaries in Japan: Offered Salaries Mostly Above ¥10 Million

FDE job postings in Japan are still only in the dozens, but offered salaries sit clearly in a high band among domestic engineering roles. Domestic media outlet BizDev Tech puts the offered salary range for FDE roles at domestic companies at ¥10–20 million. An analysis published on note (February 2026), aggregating 35 domestic job postings, found the median upper limit of offered salaries for 26 Japanese companies to be ¥15 million. Differences by experience also appear in job postings: at SoftBank (seconded to SB OAI Japan), the Software Deployment Engineer (SDE) role requiring 3+ years of experience has a theoretical annual salary of ¥7,124,000–¥14,114,400, while the FDE role requiring 5+ years is ¥8,123,000–¥20,346,200 (as of October 16, 2026).

Also, for Tokyo positions at foreign AI companies (OpenAI has made Tokyo one of its FDE hubs), global-standard compensation structures apply, and offers can far exceed domestic market rates. For contract work, domestic media covering freelance projects list freelance FDE compensation at ¥1.3–2 million per month.

Are FDE salaries higher than those of ordinary engineers?

In the US, there is data showing that FDE salaries exceed those of typical software engineers. According to Lightcast, a labor market data provider, the median advertised annual salary for FDE job postings exceeds $188,000, surpassing the roughly $145,000 for traditional software engineers (Fortune, September 2026).

In Japan, there are no official wage statistics that distinguish FDEs yet. Looking at job postings, some Japanese FDE postings have offered annual incomes exceeding 20 million yen (CADDi's Staff FDE is 30 million yen), a range that can accommodate the high incomes of experienced engineers. On the other hand, there are also postings with lower bounds in the 5 to 8 million yen range and postings with an upper bound of 6.6 million yen (ExaWizards' Junior FDE), so the job title of FDE alone does not mean a higher salary. What determines salary is the structural factors listed below and which type of company you work for.

Why FDE annual incomes and salaries are high: three structural factors

  • 01 Supply-demand gap: According to analyses by Indeed and Financial Times cited by Salesforce, job postings for FDEs grew 800% from January to September 2025, while "people with FDE experience" are almost nonexistent. Talent that combines implementation skills with the ability to face customers is itself scarce, and hiring competition is pushing up compensation
  • 02 Direct impact on revenue: FDE is a role that directly contributes to winning deals, retention, and expansion, so from a company's perspective it is not a labor cost but an "investment that generates revenue." In fact, a ZenML case study compiling an interview with Colin Jarvis, who leads OpenAI's FDE team, notes that the team focuses on "problems worth tens of millions to billions of dollars"
  • 03 Equity structure: Fast-growing AI companies in particular rely heavily on FDEs, and their compensation has a high equity component. The design reflects company growth directly in total compensation

Career strategies for increasing your salary as an FDE

Even among FDEs, compensation structures vary greatly depending on the type of company you join. Deciding which market you will compete in is the first step in your salary strategy.

Summary

FDE salaries at overseas frontier AI companies reach the hundreds of thousands of dollars including equity, and in Japan the typical offered annual salary centers on ¥10–20 million, with monthly salary floors ranging from the ¥300,000s to ¥1 million depending on the posting. This is among the highest levels of engineering roles, driven by structural factors: the supply-demand gap and direct revenue impact. However, whether the monthly salary includes overtime pay and whether there is equity compensation make a big difference even at the same figure. Frequently Asked Questions

Q How much is an FDE's salary (monthly)?

Among Japanese FDE job postings listing monthly salaries (as of October 16, 2026): SoftBank (seconded to SB OAI Japan) offers ¥523,750–¥999,000 per month, JAPAN AI offers ¥500,000–¥1,428,571, KINTO TECHNOLOGIES offers a base monthly salary from ¥526,940, SB OAI Japan's direct hiring starts at ¥1,000,000 per month, and ExaWizards' FDE pays ¥600,000–¥900,000 per month. JAPAN AI's Associate FDE, open to recent graduates, pays ¥357,143–¥825,000 per month, and ExaWizards' Junior FDE in Fukuoka pays ¥420,000–¥550,000 per month. The monthly salaries at SoftBank, JAPAN AI, SB OAI Japan, and ExaWizards include overtime pay for 40–45 hours, and SoftBank, JAPAN AI, and SB OAI Japan also pay bonuses separately. All figures are gross amounts before taxes and social insurance premiums.

Q Is a ¥10 million annual salary realistic for FDE jobs in Japan?

Yes, it is realistic. According to domestic media outlet BizDev Tech, offered annual salaries for FDE positions at Japanese companies generally fall in the ¥10–20 million range, and an analysis published on note aggregating 35 domestic job postings (February 2026) found the median upper limit for Japanese companies was ¥15 million. However, the lower limit varies with experience requirements; for example, in SoftBank's (secondment to SB OAI Japan) job posting, the theoretical annual salary for an SDE requiring 3+ years of experience starts at ¥7,124,000, while an FDE requiring 5+ years starts at ¥8,123,000 (as of October 16, 2026).

Q Which earns more, an FDE or a regular software engineer?

In the US, Lightcast data shows the median advertised annual salary for FDE job postings exceeds $188,000, surpassing the roughly $145,000 for traditional software engineers (Fortune, September 2026). Overseas compensation reports describe FDEs as "the highest-paid generalist role in the AI field." Japan has no official wage statistics distinguishing FDEs, but the upper limits of offered annual salaries in job postings range from ¥6.6 million (ExaWizards' Junior FDE) to ¥30 million (CADDi's Staff FDE) (as of October 16, 2026).

Q Is the "$1 million total compensation" for overseas FDEs real?

This is a figure estimated in a private compensation report for principal-level (top-tier) FDEs at frontier AI companies, as total compensation including equity valuation. Note that it depends not on base salary but on the stock valuation of a private company. Even in the same report, the average for mid-level roles is $385,000, and the base salary listed in job postings for OpenAI's FDE (San Francisco) is $185,000–$300,000 (as of October 16, 2026).

What are effective points for negotiating QFDE compensation?

The three key factors are: ① quantified customer outcomes (cost reductions, contribution to sales), ② a track record in LLM implementation plus evaluation infrastructure (Evals), and ③ competing offers. Since FDEs directly drive revenue, candidates who can demonstrate in monetary terms "how much value my joining will create" have the strongest negotiating position.
