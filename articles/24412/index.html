Generative AI Adoption Support & FDE Implementation Services

Original title: 生成AI導入支援・FDE導入のサービス

FDE Consulting · 2026 · 6 min read

Summary

This is a service page for FDE Consulting, a Japanese firm offering generative AI adoption support staffed by Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs). Unlike traditional consulting that ends with a proposal deck, FDEs embed at the client site, define KPIs, build production AI systems (RAG, LLM apps, AI agents) using Claude Code and Codex, and run weekly improvement loops until metrics move. The page details six service areas, a five-step process, criteria for comparing vendors, and pricing: a free KPI-design phase (LIGHT, capped at three clients monthly), production build and operations (STANDARD) from 1 million yen per month plus performance fees, and a fixed-price AI security diagnostic from 500,000 yen. One-off RAG builds and PoC-only projects are generally declined.

Key points

  • FDEs embed on-site and own delivery through production launch, not just proposals.
  • Six areas: RAG/LLM apps, AI agents, workflow redesign, KPI design, internal enablement, AI governance.
  • Five-step process with weekly reviews tied to business and operational KPIs.
  • Pricing: free LIGHT phase (3 clients/month), STANDARD from ¥1M/month plus performance fees.
  • Declines one-off RAG implementations and projects that stop at PoC.

01 AI Business System Development (RAG & LLM Applications)

Building RAG systems that leverage internal data, and designing and implementing business applications with LLMs integrated. Includes access control and evaluation design, built to withstand production operation.

Service

FDE Consulting's generative AI adoption support is a service in which Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) work on-site at the customer, taking responsibility for everything from defining challenges to implementation and establishing ongoing operations. Unlike development outsourcing (contracted work or SES), FDEs join the customer's team as on-site members. Our clients are DX and business divisions at companies looking to promote the adoption and use of AI. Even small and medium-sized businesses and small teams start with organizing requirements and designing integration with existing systems. This page summarizes our support areas, the process we provide, questions to ask when comparing AI adoption support companies, and our pricing structure.

Service Areas

Support begins by deciding which KPIs to move. Before choosing technology, we look at the business and its KPIs. Our main support areas are the following six.

02 After breaking down the business and designing the division of roles between humans and AI, we integrate AI agents into your workflows to autonomously handle research, drafting, verification, and more. Includes design of approval flows and audit logs.

03 Starting with an inventory of business processes, we separate tasks suited to AI from tasks requiring human judgment and redesign the workflow. We improve it weekly until it takes root in day-to-day operations.

04 We break down management KPIs into business KPIs so the impact of AI adoption can be shown in numbers. We support everything from measuring pre-adoption baselines to building dashboards and calculating return on investment.

05 While building together, we transfer design principles and operational procedures to your team, and hand over in a state where you can continue improving on your own. We also support designing hiring criteria and training plans for AI talent.

06 Establishing usage guidelines, designing permissions, and building audit log and evaluation frameworks. We set up rules and an environment for safe use, mitigating shadow AI risks while expanding internal adoption.

Service Process

At FDE Consulting, Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) work on-site at the customer, taking responsibility from defining challenges through implementation and operation. The process consists of the following five stages.

  • 01 Business Inventory and Issue Structuring We inventory business processes and structure and define priority issues from both management and business KPI perspectives.
  • 02 KPI Design and Human-AI Division of Labor We determine what AI can and cannot do, and translate the division of roles between humans and AI into an implementation plan.
  • 03 Production System Development FDEs work on-site, using Claude Code / Codex to implement AI business systems that can withstand production operation.
  • 04 Launch + Weekly Reviews We observe how the system is used and incorporate fixes every week.
  • 05 KPI Improvement Loop We check KPI numbers every week and keep improving until they move.

We listen to your current business and challenges, and decide together which business processes to prioritize. The first consultation is free.

Project Journey

Differences in Approach

A typical consulting contract ends with the delivery of a proposal document. FDEs run things in production and remain responsible even after operations begin.

Ends with delivery of a proposal; the client handles production implementation

  • 01 Interviews and Current-State Analysis Documentation / interviews / industry research
  • 02 Strategy and Approach Structuring Dozens of slides / decision-making materials
  • 03 Contract ends with delivery of the proposal report Actions go no further than a "next steps" list

After the contract ends (client side)

  • 04 In-house / SIer attempts implementation Time spent aligning specs / ambiguous responsibility
  • 05 PoC works but never reaches production No one takes responsibility for operation and improvement

Result: A proposal exists, but the system is not running in production The money spent does not lead to KPI improvement

From strategy and business structuring through implementation, operation, and moving KPIs

  • 01 Business Inventory and KPI Design Defining priority issues from both management and business KPI perspectives
  • 02 Translating Human-AI Division of Labor into an Implementation Plan Separating tasks suited to AI from tasks requiring human judgment
  • 03 FDEs work on-site to build the production system Developing with Claude Code and Codex
  • 04 Launch and Weekly Retrospectives Observing usage and incorporating improvement points the following week
  • 05 KPI-Driven Improvement Checking and fixing KPI numbers every week

Result: The system is used in production, and KPI changes can be verified in numbers The next investment can be judged based on data
