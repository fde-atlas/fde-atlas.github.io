A Claude Skill

Now sell with it.

Forward Deployed Selling is a Claude skill that advises you on every stage — from first reply to closed deal — using an approach honed selling enterprise AI at AWS.

01 / What it does

01Get an advisor on every deal you're in.

02Build selling agents from your existing stack.

03Get to first revenue, faster.

04Plug it into any agent workflow.

05Sell without learning a methodology.

06Keep all your sales work inside Claude.

02 / Who it's for

03 / How it works

It builds three things every sale needs.

→Your ideal customer profile (ICP).

→Your go-to-market strategy (GTM).

→The messaging that lands both.

The 3x3 is the surface. The method underneath does the work.

04 / The method

Every deal runs on all six — three principles for the seller, three things AI now does.

The three principles

→Make it easy to say yes.Lower the cost of every step the buyer takes — don't maximize the ask.

→Let them see the future.Show the world after the purchase, in their own numbers — not a generic case study.

→Show, don't tell.Demonstrate the outcome with real work, not a deck.

What AI makes possible

→Know more.Research that goes five layers deep on the account, from public data alone.

→Personalize every touch.Every artifact is one-of-one, sized to the situation, with sourced evidence.

→Come with a thesis.Arrive with a point of view the buyer reacts to. Discovery becomes validation.

05 / Inside the skill

Targeting first. Then every task runs against a qualified target, and every output gets checked before it ships.

ICP qualificationMarket × Persona × Channel, locked before anything else runs.

GTM strategyMotion, channel, message, and qualification criteria, derived from the ICP.

Stage diagnosisPlaces any account on the Awareness → Familiarity → Value path from public data.

Thesis generationA one-page point of view on the account, sourced and labeled for your review.

Stakeholder profilingFive layers of context, plus who uses, buys, sponsors, and unblocks the deal.

Value framingCost of inaction and value of action, both sourced, both in the buyer's numbers.

Deal scoringStage, risk, and speed combine into a tier, and it won't let you skip a stage.

Live-deal coachingSpots what's stalling the deal and hands back a recovery plan.

DisqualificationNames a bad fit honestly and tells the buyer what would serve them instead.

Demonstration artifactsBuilds the work you'd otherwise write by hand.

Every output passes three checks: ethics, method, and honesty. Every number cites its source. Try to skip a step, and it warns you first.

06 / Install

Works in Claude Code, Cowork, and Claude chat. Claude auto-loads what it needs per question. You don't manage files.

Claude Code

$mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills

$git clone https://github.com/vonarmen-wq/forward-deployed-selling ~/.claude/skills/forward-deployed-selling

Cowork & Claude chat

  • Download the zip below.
  • In Claude: Settings → Capabilities → Skills → Add.
  • Upload the zip — no unzipping needed.

Once installed, try

  • “Build a go-to-market strategy to sell my product.”
  • “Craft a sales plan to get into (target customer).”
  • “Help develop my product value proposition.”

08 / About

Angel Armendariz is a Principal at AWS in Business Innovation Advisory, the founder of Caerus Alpha, and the author of Forward Deployed Selling. He writes Loaded & Layered on Substack. Personal site at angelarmendariz.com.

09 / The standard

The skill is free. Two paid tracks sit on top: certification, for individuals or teams that want to learn the framework, and the Powered by FDS mark, for products and delivery built on the method.

Certification

Available for individuals or teams that want to learn how to apply the full Forward Deployed Selling framework. Cohort-based curriculum, with a final demonstration on a real or simulated deal.

The Powered by FDS mark

For consultancies and software vendors whose delivery or product is built on the method. Apply, complete an audit, and license the mark for packaging, documentation, and marketing.
