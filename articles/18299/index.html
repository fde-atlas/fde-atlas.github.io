FDE (On-site Resident AI Engineer) | Development up to Internal System Integration

Original title: FDE（現場常駐型AIエンジニア）｜社内システム連携まで開発

株式会社Aiworker · 2025-11-30 · 11 min read

Summary

This is a service page from Japanese company AIworker describing its FDE offering: an engineer embeds at the client site at least one day per week, builds AI workflows connected to internal systems and data, and hands over to employees who can maintain the systems themselves. It cites real results from a holding-company case—attendance data for about 630 employees automatically routed to three subsidiaries, and manual verification of seconded staff reduced from 58 to 36 items. Pricing starts at 350,000 yen per month for one day weekly, up to 1.5 million yen for full-time embedding, with a three-month minimum. The page distinguishes this from outsourced development and staffing by emphasizing in-house maintainability after handover.

Key points

  • AIworker's FDE engineers work on-site at least one day weekly, developing alongside employees.
  • Scope includes integration with internal systems and production operation, not just no-code tools.
  • Case results: ~630 employees' attendance data automated; manual checks cut from 58 to 36 items.
  • Pricing: 350,000 yen/month for one day weekly to 1.5 million yen for full-time, 3-month minimum.
  • Goal is handover: employees can modify and maintain systems without external engineers.

What is happening on the ground

Hiring AI engineers is difficult, and outsourcing development means starting from requirements definition, so it takes time before anything usable appears. Meanwhile, interest on the ground fades. In consultations, we often hear comments like the following.

Rolled out company-wide, but only some employees use it

Even when AI tools are distributed, no one can build usage tailored to the actual work.

A prototype was made, but it never made it into actual operations

The person who built it returned to their regular duties, and the prototype has been left as is.

No one who can connect with internal systems

There are things they want to do, but no one can be entrusted with integration with core systems or data.

Relying on a development company takes half a year just to firm up requirements

Without being able to decide what to build, estimates and requirements definition continue.

Tried on-site support, but it stayed within the limits of no-code tools

It did not reach integration with business systems or operation in production.

If everything is left to external parties, you will be unable to make changes when the work changes. What is needed is a form where the builder enters the workplace and hands things over to employees as they build.

Even if what to build is not yet decided, we will sort out the initial candidates together in 30 minutes.

The 4 things we uphold as an FDE

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is an engineer who embeds themselves in a company's workplace, listens to the issues, and develops AI systems on the spot. This term has started to be used in Japan as well, but how engineers engage with the workplace, the experience of the people assigned, and the scope of development vary greatly by service. For this reason, we provide our service in a form that satisfies all four of the following.

On-site presence at the customer's workplace

At least one day a week, we develop at your workplace

Rather than proceeding through online meetings alone, the basic approach is to develop while observing the work, right beside the employees who will use it.

Handling by professional engineers

From gathering business requirements to development, engineers with development experience take charge

Our project manager is responsible for the working methods and quality.

Development that connects with internal systems and data

We do not stop at what can be built with no-code tools

The scope of development extends to integration with business systems and data, and operation in the production environment.

Handover in a state where employees can make changes themselves

We leave things in a state where modifications and additions can be made in-house, without external engineers

While developing, we teach employees how things are built and create procedure documents.

What we build while on-site

For things that can be done with already deployed tools, such as summarizing meeting minutes or searching documents, we limit ourselves to explaining how to use them. What our engineers develop is work that involves each company's formats, data, and decision-making rules. We do not limit the themes; we prioritize and build based on issues that arise in the field.

Creating documents matched to the other party's format

  • Using core system information and past documents, we create drafts of reports and notifications that differ by business partner

Document cross-checking and error checking

  • We verify purchase orders, contracts, and application documents against set criteria, and show only the parts a person needs to look at (AI-ification of document work)

Aggregation spanning multiple systems

  • We automatically collect from systems the figures that form the basis of meeting materials and reports

Integration of business systems and AI

  • We connect AI with kintone, Salesforce, data platforms, and more, in a form that does not increase manual input

Systematizing the judgment criteria of veterans

  • We listen to and organize the reasons behind judgments, and turn them into a system together with the confirmation procedures (e.g., Estimate AI)

How we choose the AI to use

  • We choose based on the tools already introduced at your company and your internal rules, such as Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, and Claude

Development case studies

These are examples built by working on-site and connecting to internal data and systems. Company names are withheld. Only figures verified with real data are shown; unmeasured items are marked "not measured."

Cross-checking attendance, payroll, and secondment data

ChallengeAttendance, payroll, and secondment data differed by company, and staff verified the connections manually.
Support providedThrough weekly on-site visits, we built systems together with employees for sorting attendance data and pre-checking payroll data. We made thresholds and monitoring items changeable by the HR group themselves and prepared a maintenance manual.
ResultsAutomated the sorting of attendance data for approximately 630 employees across three companies, reducing manually reviewed secondment records from 58 to 36 (real data). The system runs without relying on us.

Creating work reports with different formats per customer

ChallengeStaff manually transcribed work reports, which differ in format by customer, from core system data and past documents.
Support providedWe decided, together with the core system team, on an approach of connecting a standalone system via data integration rather than embedding it in the core system. AI only creates drafts; staff review the content and submit.
StatusUnder development for the first release in December 2026. Effects are not yet measured and will be measured after release.

Pricing and contract structure

Contracts are quasi-delegated, per engineer per month. The minimum period is three months, and all prices exclude tax. With any engagement model, we develop on-site at your workplace, and integration with internal systems and operation in production environments are included in the scope of support.

Two to three days per week¥900,000Developing multiple challenges in parallel
Full-time on-site¥1,500,000Embedding in a department and developing within daily operations

For the one-day-per-week model, pricing is determined after we learn about the target processes and scope of what to build. If the development volume increases, we discuss and adjust the engagement model or number of engineers.

Judge based on the effect

Work time of the target process × number of people involved × hourly wage × duration

We first estimate the effect using this formula. Then, so you can also compare with the cost and time of hiring one AI engineer (salary, social insurance, recruiting costs, time until they can start working, impact of resignation), we present the numbers side by side. That is our approach.

This is not staffing. Our project manager directs how the work proceeds and is responsible for quality.

We explain the numbers side by side, including comparison with hiring.
