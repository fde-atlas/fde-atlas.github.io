FDE Roadmap 2026: How to Become a Forward Deployed Engineer

Top FDE Jobs · 2026-11-27 · 8 min read

Summary

Top FDE Jobs published a 2026 roadmap for becoming a forward deployed engineer, built from 1,238 live job postings and organized into eight camps covering 79 topics. Each topic shows what share of postings request it and median posted base pay. Python appears in 74% of jobs and customer-facing skills in 68%, while AI agents (52%) and LLM fundamentals (51%) are now core requirements. The guide also covers Palantir's Delta/Echo split, a six-level career ladder with pay data, and the four-round interview loop, noting FDE is rarely a first job.

Key points

  • Roadmap tracks 79 topics across 8 camps, based on 1,238 live FDE postings.
  • Python is the top skill at 74% of jobs, with $200k median base pay.
  • AI agents (52%) and LLM fundamentals (51%) are now standard FDE requirements.
  • Working with customers appears in 68% of postings, defining the role.
  • Most FDEs transition from backend, full-stack or data engineering roles.

Every topic on the FDE roadmap, explained

Start here

What an FDE does. A forward deployed engineer is a software engineer who works with a company's customers to get its product running on their real data, systems and workflows, then feeds what they learn back into the product.

  • Deltas and Echos: Palantir, where the role started, called its FDEs Deltas and its customer-facing strategists Echos. Many FDE teams still use that split.
  • From SWE to FDE: Most FDEs come from backend, full-stack or data engineering and add customer-facing work. It is rarely a first job.
  • FDE at labs vs startups vs consultancies: AI labs, startups, SaaS companies and consultancies all hire FDEs, with different pay and travel. See who hires most and what they pay.

Programming foundations

Learn a language. Pick one language to go deep in, then be comfortable reading a second. FDEs write production code, scripts and glue every day.

  • Python: The default language for FDE work: data wrangling, integrations, LLM apps and scripts. (74% of jobs, $200k median base)
  • TypeScript / JavaScript: For web apps, Node services and front ends you build for customers. (32% of jobs, $190k median base)
  • Java / Scala: Common at enterprise customers and in data platforms. (10% of jobs, $183k median base)
  • Go: Used for infrastructure, CLIs and backend services. (8% of jobs, $216k median base)
  • C++ / Rust: Shows up in robotics, defense and performance-critical roles. (9% of jobs, $168k median base)

DSA and system design. Coding rounds still test data structures and algorithms, and senior loops add system design. Pass this gate first.

  • Data structures and algorithms: Arrays, hash maps, trees, graphs, sorting and complexity: the basics of every coding round.
  • System design: Design services that survive real traffic and messy customer environments. (14% of jobs, $225k median base)

Git, Linux and the shell. You will work in customer repos and on customer machines. Be fast with git, the terminal and SSH.

  • Git and GitHub: Branches, rebases, reviews and CI on someone else's codebase. (<1% of jobs)
  • Linux and Bash: Debugging on servers you don't own, often without a GUI. (2% of jobs, $159k median base)

Full-stack delivery

Front end. Enough front end to build internal tools, dashboards and demos customers actually use.

  • React: The most common UI library in FDE postings. (8% of jobs, $182k median base)
  • Full-stack apps: Own a feature end to end: UI, API and data. (6% of jobs, $196k median base)

Back end and APIs. Services and APIs that connect your product to the customer's world.

  • SQL: Query, join and fix customer data. Expect a SQL question in most loops. (14% of jobs, $170k median base)
  • API design (REST, GraphQL): Design clean APIs and read badly documented ones. (14% of jobs, $206k median base)
  • NoSQL databases: MongoDB, DynamoDB and friends at customers that use them.

Integrations and data

Integration engineering. Connect the product to the customer's CRM, ERP, identity provider and internal tools, including systems with no documentation. 41% of live FDE jobs mention it.

  • Enterprise systems (Salesforce, SAP, ServiceNow): The systems most enterprise deployments have to talk to. (3% of jobs, $197k median base)
  • Legacy systems: Mainframes, on-prem databases and old SOAP services still run most enterprises. (<1% of jobs)
  • SSO and identity (SAML, OAuth): Almost every enterprise deployment starts with single sign-on. (1% of jobs, $200k median base)

Data engineering. Pipelines that move, clean and model customer data so the product can use it.

  • Data pipelines and ETL: Batch and streaming jobs that keep data fresh. (8% of jobs, $170k median base)
  • Data modeling: Turn five systems that disagree into one clean view. (<1% of jobs)
  • Warehouses (Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery): Where enterprise data lives. (6% of jobs, $216k median base)
  • Spark, Airflow, Kafka: The heavy tools for big and streaming data. (4% of jobs, $216k median base)
  • Palantir Foundry: Palantir's data platform, also used by its partners and consultancies. (4% of jobs, $156k median base)

AI engineering

LLM fundamentals. How large language models behave: tokens, context windows, cost, latency and failure modes. 51% of live FDE jobs mention it.

  • Prompt and context engineering: Get reliable output by shaping instructions, examples and context. (<1% of jobs)
  • Choosing model providers: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, open models: trade-offs on quality, cost and where data may go. (9% of jobs, $233k median base)
  • Fine-tuning: When prompting and retrieval are not enough. (1% of jobs, $200k median base)

RAG and retrieval. Answer questions over a customer's own documents and data, with the right permissions. 24% of live FDE jobs mention it.

  • Vector databases: Store and search embeddings at scale. (9% of jobs, $200k median base)
  • Document permissions: Users must only see answers from documents they may read.

AI agents. Systems where a model plans, calls tools and acts. Most new FDE roles deploy agents for customers. 52% of live FDE jobs mention it.

  • Agent architectures: Workflows vs agents, planning, retries and human approval steps.
  • Tools and function calling: Give the model well-designed tools with clear inputs.
  • MCP: The Model Context Protocol connects agents to tools and data in a standard way. (8% of jobs, $203k median base)
  • LangChain / LangGraph: Popular frameworks for building agents. (6% of jobs, $203k median base)
  • Voice agents: Phone and voice assistants, a fast-growing FDE niche. (2% of jobs, $194k median base)

Evals. Prove that what you built works before the customer finds out it doesn't. Evals roles pay among the highest on this roadmap. 5% of live FDE jobs mention it.

  • Eval pipelines: Test sets, graders and regression checks for LLM features.
  • Guardrails and failure handling: What happens when the model is confidently wrong in front of a customer. (3% of jobs)

AI coding tools. FDEs ship fast with coding agents. Know at least one well.

  • Claude Code: Anthropic's coding agent in the terminal. (<1% of jobs)
  • Cursor and Copilot: AI-first editors and assistants. (<1% of jobs)
  • Codex and Devin: Coding agents that work on whole tasks. (2% of jobs)

Cloud and deployment

Cloud platforms. Be fluent in at least one major cloud and comfortable in the others. 19% of live FDE jobs mention it.

  • AWS: The most requested cloud in FDE postings. (12% of jobs, $214k median base)
  • Google Cloud: Including Vertex AI for model work. (6% of jobs, $216k median base)
  • Azure: Common at Microsoft-heavy enterprises. (7% of jobs, $216k median base)
  • Terraform and IaC: Deploy the same stack again and again, in each customer's account. (3% of jobs, $219k median base)

Containers. Package your app so it runs the same in every environment. 14% of live FDE jobs mention it.

  • Docker: Build and run containers. (2% of jobs, $215k median base)
  • Kubernetes: Run containers at scale, often the customer's own cluster. (13% of jobs, $200k median base)

CI/CD and observability. Ship safely and see what breaks before the customer calls. 3% of live FDE jobs mention it.

  • Logs, metrics and traces: Debug production at a customer at 2am. (1% of jobs, $218k median base)
  • MLOps and inference: Serve and monitor models in production. (4% of jobs, $216k median base)

Security and compliance. Enterprise buyers send security reviews before anything goes live. Answer them well. 9% of live FDE jobs mention it.

  • Security clearance: Defense and government FDE roles often require one. (12% of jobs, $170k median base)
  • SOC 2, HIPAA, FedRAMP: The compliance frameworks customers ask about. (1% of jobs, $243k median base)
  • AI security: Prompt injection, data leaks and agent permissions. (1% of jobs)

Customer delivery and field skills

Working with customers. Embed with the customer's team, on site or on calls, and become the person they trust to make it work. 68% of live FDE jobs mention it.

  • Discovery and scoping: Find the real problem behind the request and agree on what to build first. (6% of jobs, $225k median base)
  • Prioritizing: The customer wants ten things. Pick the two that matter.
  • Stakeholder management: Executives and end users want different things. Keep both on side. (2% of jobs, $167k median base)
  • Communication and writing: Clear updates, docs and demos for technical and non-technical people. (<1% of jobs)
  • Owning the outcome: Measure value, report ROI and make the deployment stick. (6% of jobs, $193k median base)
  • Product feedback loop: Turn what you learn in the field into product changes. (<1% of jobs)
  • Travel: Many roles travel to customers, often around half the time.

Get hired

The FDE interview. Four rounds in most loops: a customer case, coding, design and behavioral. Practice all four.

  • Know your pay: Median posted base for FDE roles and how it changes by level, city and company type.
  • Apply: Live FDE roles, checked daily on employers' own job boards.

FDE roadmap questions

  • What is the FDE roadmap?
  • A step-by-step guide to becoming a forward deployed engineer: programming foundations, full-stack delivery, integrations and data, AI engineering, cloud and deployment, customer field skills and the interview. Each topic shows how many of the 1,238 live FDE job postings on Top FDE Jobs ask for it, updated daily.
  • What skills do you need to become a forward deployed engineer?
  • The most requested topics in live FDE postings are Python (74% of jobs), Working with customers (68% of jobs), AI agents (52% of jobs), LLM fundamentals (51% of jobs), Integration engineering (41% of jobs), TypeScript / JavaScript (32% of jobs). Most roles combine strong coding with working directly with customers.
  • Do I need AI skills to become an FDE?
  • Mostly yes. 52% of live FDE postings mention AI agents and 51% mention LLMs, because most new FDE roles deploy AI products for customers. Defense and data-platform roles lean more on cloud, security clearance and data engineering.
  • Is FDE a beginner role?
  • Usually not. Most FDEs come from software, data or backend engineering and add customer-facing work. Entry-level FDE roles exist, but they are a minority of postings. See the career path for levels and pay.
