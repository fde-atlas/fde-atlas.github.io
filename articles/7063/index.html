Aced (formerly Exponent) · 2025-12-28 · 1 min read

Summary

Aced (formerly Exponent) lists 12 interview questions for the Forward Deployed Engineer role at ElevenLabs, contributed and verified by hiring managers and candidates. The set mixes behavioral questions—such as building something end-to-end independently, your proudest project, and why you want to join ElevenLabs—with coding problems like reversing a linked list and finding the longest substring without repeating characters. Several questions include video answers, and most show community answers with vote counts and timestamps. The database relies on user contributions, and readers are invited to share their own interview experiences.
