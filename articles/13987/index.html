Forward Deployed Engineering Recruitment

Re:Sourced · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

Recruiter Re:Sourced describes its specialist search service for Forward Deployed Engineers, targeting AI-native and enterprise SaaS companies. It sources alumni from Palantir, Scale AI and Anthropic, distinguishes FDEs from pre-sale solutions engineers, and outlines a four-stage process: a 90-minute briefing, direct outreach to 200-400 named engineers, a shortlist of 3-5 candidates with technical memos, and closing support. Sydney FDEs earn AUD 180-240k base (tech leads AUD 230-270k) for 2026. The talent pool is thin since the title consolidated only around 2023-24, so pay runs 10-20% above comparable software roles and most searches close within a 21-day median.

Key points

  • FDEs embed post-sale in customer environments, writing production code, unlike pre-sale solutions engineers.
  • Talent is sourced from Palantir, Scale AI, Anthropic alumni and data/AI platform solutions teams.
  • Search process: 90-minute brief, 200-400 direct outreaches, 3-5 candidate shortlist with technical memos.
  • Sydney 2026 base pay: AUD 180-240k for FDEs, AUD 230-270k for tech leads.
  • The title only consolidated in 2023-24, making the pool thin and pay 10-20% above software norms.

Common questions

Forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) sit between your product and your customers. They embed into customer environments, ship integrations, and often act as the technical voice in commercial conversations. Common in AI-native and enterprise SaaS deployments.

AI-native startups (think Palantir-style customer engineering), enterprise SaaS with deep technical integrations, and platform companies entering complex enterprise customers all use the FDE pattern.

Solutions and sales engineers are pre-sale; FDEs are post-sale and embedded. FDEs typically write production code, ship customer-facing features, and own technical outcomes inside customer environments. Title varies: forward-deployed engineer, customer engineer, deployment strategist.

FDEs in Sydney sit at AUD 180-240k base for 2026, base only, 25th-75th percentile of accepted offers, with tech leads at AUD 230-270k. We do not publish a principal band for the discipline: it consolidated as a title too recently for us to have enough accepted offers at that level. The market is thin; deep candidate networks matter more than usual.

Yes. Heads of forward-deployed engineering, FDE leads and engineering directors with FDE backgrounds are placed regularly, often for AI-native startups scaling enterprise GTM.

Production engineering depth plus customer-facing judgement. The strongest FDEs we place are former senior software or ML engineers who have run customer-embedded projects, not solutions engineers trying to move post-sale.

Genuinely thin. The title only consolidated around 2023-24, so most qualified candidates carry other titles. Searches rely on network reach and profile translation rather than keyword sourcing - which is why generalist recruiters struggle with FDE briefs.

Yes, typically 10-20 per cent above like-for-like software bands, reflecting the dual skill set and scarcity. AI-native scale-ups also structure meaningful equity into FDE offers because the role directly drives revenue retention.

Most close inside the 21-day median, but the search shape differs: shortlists are smaller (3-4 calibrated profiles rather than 5-6) because the qualifying pool is smaller. The 72-hour shortlist commitment still holds.
