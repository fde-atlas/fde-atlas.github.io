Forward Deployed Engineer Market 2026: 166 Jobs Analysed

Forward Deploying · 2026-11-01 · 3 min read

Summary

The author analysed 166 Forward Deployed Engineer job adverts from 33 companies' own careers pages, collected on November 1, 2026, covering who hires, what skills are demanded and what pay is published. Hiring is heavily concentrated in two large employers, the role is customer-facing with frequent travel, Python dominates skill requirements, and US median published base pay is $239k. UK adverts almost never disclose salaries.

Key points

  • Sample: 166 adverts from 33 companies, collected November 1, 2026.
  • Two largest employers account for 43% of adverts.
  • 97% involve customer work; 72% mention travel, 42% on-site work.
  • Python appears in 72% of adverts, agents in 53%, evals in 45%.
  • US median base midpoint is $239k; only 2 of 40 UK adverts publish pay.

Key findings

  • Hiring is concentrated. The two biggest employers account for 43% of the adverts. For everyone else, the competition for candidates is a handful of very visible companies.
  • It is a customer job first. 97% of adverts describe working with customers or clients, 72% mention travel and 42% mention on-site work.
  • Python is the default. 72% ask for Python and 60% for TypeScript or JavaScript. Agents appear in 53% and evals in 45%.
  • Published pay is high and wide. Across 46 distinct US salary ranges, the median midpoint is $239k and the middle half sits between $210k and $264k.
  • The UK hides its numbers. Only 2 of 40 UK adverts publish a salary.
  • No quotas, some pre-sales. 0% mention a sales quota, but 22% mention demos, proofs of concept or pre-sales work, a reminder that the title covers different jobs.

What it means if you are hiring

  • You are competing with a few loud names. Candidates compare your offer with the biggest FDE employers. Know their published bands before you set yours.
  • Say what the travel really is. Most adverts mention travel; very few say how much. Being specific is an easy way to stand out.
  • Test for production, not demos. With pre-sales language in a fifth of adverts, CVs carrying the title are unreliable. Ask for evidence of systems still running. Our screening is built around this.
  • In the UK, publishing a range is an advantage. Almost nobody does it.
  • Python plus customer judgment is the core. Long framework lists add little; agents and evals experience is the differentiator.

Method

Adverts come from employers' own careers pages through the public job feeds of their applicant tracking systems, collected on November 1, 2026 for the US, UK, Canada, Ireland and Australia. An advert is counted as Forward Deployed Engineer from its title (architects and managers are counted separately). Skills and work features are case-insensitive keyword matches on the full description: a mention is not always a requirement. Salaries are the midpoint of the published range, and the same range from the same company counts once (usually one advert repeated across cities); seniority comes from the title. The figures on this page are frozen at the collection date so they do not change when our live data refreshes.
