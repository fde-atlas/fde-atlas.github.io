LTM to Train 1,000 Claude Engineers Under Expanded Partnership With Anthropic

Full text

LTM, formerly LTIMindtree, has deepened its partnership with Anthropic by integrating the Claude family of AI models into its BlueVerse AI platform and launching a large-scale programme to train thousands of Claude-certified engineers.

The partnership will see Claude, Claude Code and Claude Cowork embedded into LTM's AI Delivery Fabric for software engineering, application modernisation, agent orchestration, site reliability engineering (SRE), observability and chaos engineering, the company said in a statement on July 13.

LTM will also expand its AI1000 initiative to train and deploy thousands of Claude-certified architects and Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), who will work with enterprise clients on AI assessments, implementation and continuous optimisation.

“LTM helps clients accelerate AI adoption and translate AI investments into measurable business outcomes through our partnership with Anthropic. Combining Claude with LTM’s BlueVerse ecosystem, deep domain expertise, technology capabilities, and AI1000 talent initiative creates a powerful foundation for enterprises to embed AI across their business and modernise at scale,” Venu Lambu, CEO and Managing Director of LTM said in a statement.

The company will additionally establish a dedicated Claude Centre of Excellence to develop reusable AI capabilities, reference architectures and governance frameworks around responsible AI, model governance and data privacy.

The announcement comes as Indian IT services companies move beyond simply offering access to large language models and instead position themselves as implementation partners capable of embedding AI into enterprise workflows.

As clients shift from experimentation to production deployments, service providers are investing heavily in specialised engineering talent, industry-specific AI assets and governance capabilities.

The latest partnership also reflects Anthropic's growing enterprise push in India, where the company has increasingly relied on systems integrators to scale adoption of Claude among large enterprises. Other AI developers such as OpenAI, and Mistral have similarly expanded partnerships with global and Indian IT companies to reach enterprise customers.

"LTM brings delivery expertise, trained people, and long-standing client relationships across industries, and their customers want to embed Claude into the systems they rely on. LTM is embedding Claude and Claude Code in BlueVerse, bringing trusted frontier AI technology to the center of how they do what they do best - help their clients build, modernise, and run their software," Chris Ciauri, Managing Director of International, Anthropic, said in a statement.
