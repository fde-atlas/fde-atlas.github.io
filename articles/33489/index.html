Meesho FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) India: Salary & Role

FDE India · 2026-11-11 · 7 min read

Summary

Meesho is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer - II in Bengaluru (on-site) for its Tech – AI Services team, which sells a B2B voicebot platform to enterprises. The FDE owns deployments end to end: discovery, solution design, configuration, UAT, go-live and production support. The role asks for 2–5 years in software or client-facing technical delivery, Python or Go, SQL/NoSQL, cloud basics and voice AI knowledge (ASR, TTS, LLMs). Pay is not posted; Weekday's November 2026 data puts Meesho SDE-2 median fixed pay at ₹33.3 LPA, offered only as a rough benchmark. The article also covers Meesho AI Services' background and interview preparation tips.

Meesho, the Bengaluru e-commerce company, hires a Forward Deployed Engineer - II to take enterprise clients live on its voice AI platform. The role sits in the Tech – AI Services team, is on-site in Bengaluru and asks for 2–5 years of experience, according to Meesho’s Lever listing. In plain terms, you are the engineer who turns a signed contract into a voicebot that handles a client’s live customer calls.

Why Meesho has an FDE team

Meesho first built voice AI for its own customer support. In November 2024 the company said its multilingual voice bot was handling about 60,000 calls a day and resolving 95% of queries without a human, YourStory reported. The bot launched in Hindi and English and was designed for basic smartphones and noisy settings. In March 2026 it added Vaani, a voice shopping assistant, which the company said reached over 1.5 million users in its first phase, per YourStory.

That capability is now being sold outside Meesho. A MAS sales-strategy listing describes Meesho AI Services (MAS) as a 0–1 business that runs repeatable service workflows for other companies: inbound calls, chats and tickets, and outbound work such as lead qualification and verification. Pricing is outcome-based (per-minute, slab-based, pilots and risk-sharing), and the posting names clients in consumer durables, auto and D2C. It calls MAS a technology company, not an AI BPO. A closed GTM Engineer posting from June 2026 adds that its targets include Indian enterprises looking at voice AI, IVR modernisation and agent assist.

Every new client has different call flows, CRMs and telephony. That is why MAS needs forward deployed engineers.

What the FDE does at Meesho

The listing calls the FDE team the technical backbone of client relationships, working with Product, Infra, Business and Platform Engineering. The FDE is the main technical owner of each client deployment:

  • Onboarding, end to end. Requirements, solution design, platform configuration, user acceptance testing and production go-live.
  • Technical discovery. Run sessions with the client to understand their processes and systems.
  • Conversation design. Build custom conversation flows and the integration logic behind them.
  • Prototypes. Build proofs of concept to show what the platform can do for a specific client.
  • Production support. Troubleshoot and fix issues across the stack once calls are live.
  • Integrations. Connect the voicebot platform to client systems.
  • Better tooling. Improve onboarding frameworks and internal FDE tools as the team learns.

Note the communication bar: the listing wants someone who can explain an outage to a CXO and a configuration change to a developer in the same meeting.

Pay

Meesho does not post pay for the FDE role. The listing says only that pay is competitive, with cash and equity, and depends on role and experience.

The nearest public benchmark is engineering pay at a similar level. Weekday reports a median fixed pay of ₹33.3 LPA for Meesho SDE-2 from 60 salaries (updated November 2026). Its middle range is ₹25.8–37.4 LPA. By experience, the median is ₹28.8 LPA at 2–3 years and ₹35 LPA at 4–6 years. That is a different job, and FDE pay may not match it, so treat it as an estimate only. To compare with FDE pay elsewhere in India, use the FDE Salary Estimator.

How to prepare

  • Build a small voice agent. Chain speech-to-text, an LLM and text-to-speech to answer one narrow job, such as “where is my order”, in Hindi and English. Measure latency and how it handles someone saying “haan” or interrupting mid-sentence.
  • Learn telephony basics. Understand SIP, how VoIP calls are routed and what a CPaaS provider does. The listing calls this a strong plus, and few applicants have it.
  • Show one integration you shipped. Have a story about connecting your system to someone else’s (a CRM, an order database, a webhook) and what broke in UAT or after go-live.
  • Practise incident communication. Prepare a two-minute explanation of a real outage for a non-technical leader, then the same incident for an engineer. The interview will likely test both.
  • Sharpen Python or Go debugging. Expect to read unfamiliar code and find the bug, with queries against MySQL, PostgreSQL or MongoDB along the way. Our FDE interview questions guide has practice prompts.

Meesho FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Process for the FDE role: Meesho's FDE listing does not describe the interview rounds, and no public interview reports for this title were found.
  • What to expect from the job description: The role covers client discovery, conversation-flow design, platform configuration, UAT, go-live and production debugging, so expect Python or Go coding, a debugging or incident discussion, a voicebot or integration design problem and a test of how you explain technical issues to client leadership.
