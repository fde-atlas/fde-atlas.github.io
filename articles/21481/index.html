Summary

Japanese proptech startup Canary has posted a job listing for a Forward Deployed Engineer focused on vertical AI agents for the real estate sector. The role involves embedding with major clients, pairing with AI consultants to redesign workflows in an AI-native way, and owning everything from requirements through production deployment, including multi-agent architecture, RAG pipelines, and evals. Insights from client work are expected to feed back into the company's SaaS product, CANARY Cloud, supporting its evolution toward a master property database. Salary ranges from 10 to 15 million yen, based in Tokyo.

Key points

  • Canary is hiring an FDE for vertical AI agents in real estate.
  • The role covers requirements, multi-agent design, RAG, and evals.
  • FDEs pair with AI consultants and work directly at client sites.
  • Client learnings feed into CANARY Cloud's agentic evolution.

  • 【FDE】Forward Deployed Engineer (Real Estate Vertical AI Agent)/468862_20261111
  • Job Description
  • As a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) in our AI Solutions business, you will deeply embed yourself in the operations of major clients, including companies in the industry's largest corporate group, and lead end-to-end transformation (AX) that rebuilds business processes in an AI-native way, from requirements definition through implementation and operation. An FDE is "an engineer who solves problems closest to the customer's frontline." At our company, you will pair with AI consultants from strategy consulting backgrounds, translate clients' management and operational challenges into problems solvable with AI, implement prototypes yourself and deliver them to the field, and grow them into production systems while gathering feedback. However, achieving AX for individual companies is only part of this position's mission. Starting from an understanding of the real domain challenges of the real estate industry—gained precisely because of deep immersion in the field—you will evolve our SaaS product "Canary Cloud" into agentic applications and move closer to realizing the "Master Property DB Vision." The larger mission expected of the FDE is to go beyond implementation for individual companies and contribute to building the foundation for the "vertical AI agent in the real estate domain" that Canary aims for. 【Specifically】 ・Understanding and decomposing clients' business processes, and identifying opportunities for replacement or enhancement with AI agents ・Requirements definition, solution design, and PoC through production implementation in collaboration with AI consultants ・Design and development of multi-agent architectures (agent orchestration, Tool Use / Function Calling, incorporating Human-in-the-Loop, etc.) ・Building RAG pipelines (ingesting business documents and data, designing search infrastructure, context engineering) ・Ensuring the quality and reliability of AI agents (building evaluation frameworks (Evals), guardrail design, production monitoring) ・Designing integration with client environments and data (API integration, data pipelines, security requirements) ・Contributing to the agentic evolution of "Canary Cloud" and the realization of the "Master Property DB Vision" by feeding back domain knowledge gained from individual implementations 【Highlights】Management experience welcome
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • ・Basic knowledge of computers, networks, databases, and security (including protocols such as DNS) ・Practical knowledge and experience in design ・Backend development experience ・Development experience using version control systems (Git, etc.) ・Empathy with the FDE way of working: embedding yourself in clients' operations and handling everything from hearing about challenges to proposing and implementing solutions 【Preferred】 ・Development experience using AI agent frameworks such as LangGraph / ADK / Mastra ・Experience building AI agent evaluation frameworks and designing guardrails ・Experience building systems using GCP (GKE, Cloud Run, Cloud Spanner, etc.)
