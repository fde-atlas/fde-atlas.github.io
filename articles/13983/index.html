Summary

This page is a daily-updated job board aggregating 395 live forward deployed engineer, deployment strategist, and customer-embedded engineering roles from 47 companies' own job boards. Listings are rebuilt each morning, closed roles are removed automatically, and every entry links directly to the employer's application page. Hand-tuned filtering excludes pre-sales and DevOps roles that merely sound similar. Free browsing covers all open roles; membership adds search and filters by region, level, and published pay, plus a 30-day learning roadmap, recruiter placement, live Q&As, and expert sessions. The author also curates a short personal picks list, often unposted roles at small, early-stage companies.
