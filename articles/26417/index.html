Today's Assessment

Jiangsu's call for service providers lists FDE teams as a dedicated reporting category, while a Shanghai manufacturing pricing report offers a task-level efficiency claim. The two respectively reflect service supply organization and business task practice: enterprise AI services require delivery implementation, operations, and governance capabilities, as well as verifiable task evidence.

Key Signals

1. Jiangsu calls for AI application service providers, with a dedicated FDE team option

On October 26, the Jiangsu Provincial Department of Industry and Information Technology issued a notice calling for AI application service providers offering consulting and planning, delivery implementation, operations management, security governance, and supporting services. The filing form includes a dedicated option for "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) teams," with plans to subsequently build a resource pool, service groups, and an FDE team database. The notice is dated October 22, and the online filing window runs from October 22 to November 10.

Terminology note: This site typically uses "Forward Deployed Engineer"; when citing this document, its original term "前线部署工程师" is retained. Translations across different documents should not be described as a single nationwide standard.

Evidence boundary: Applicant organizations must meet conditions such as registration within Jiangsu Province; being selected in the call does not mean inclusion in the database, winning orders, or completing delivery. Specific requirements are subject to the original text and attachments.

Source: Jiangsu Provincial Department of Industry and Information Technology

2. Shanghai manufacturing pricing report: behind the efficiency claim lies years of data accumulation

On October 26, the Shanghai Municipal Science and Technology Commission republished a survey from Yicai (First Financial). The interviewed company Haizhi Intelligent Manufacturing stated that a non-standard manufacturing order containing one hundred drawings typically takes a week to produce a valid quote, while its processing time is about twenty minutes. The company explained that its foundation consists of eleven years of accumulated data on equipment, precision, materials, costs, and delivery feedback, plus a factory capacity map built from over three hundred field labels.

Evidence boundary: This is the interviewed company's own efficiency metric, not an independent government audit. Without data on sample size, quote accuracy, human review, customer adoption, and total cost, it cannot be extrapolated to gains for all manufacturing enterprises.

3. OpenAI GPT-6.1 Terra: visual understanding and computer operation add enterprise pilot options

OpenAI officially introduced GPT-6.1 Terra, emphasizing visual understanding and computer operation, with examples involving technical drawings, forms, and invoice entry. For FDEs, the change in capability supply is worth testing, but whether an operation is actually completed must still be verified against the final state of business systems.

Evidence boundary: Vendor benchmarks and product introductions do not equal enterprise production results. Actual workflows should separately measure accuracy, human takeover, failure recovery, and total cost.

Source: OpenAI official introduction

Implications for FDE practitioners

  • Define deliverables by service stage: diagnosis, implementation, operations, governance, and training evals should each specify responsibilities and acceptance criteria.
  • Incorporate business data, rules, and feedback into solutions; do not equate drawing reading or generation capabilities with completed pricing judgment.
  • For computer operation, measure both action success and correctness of the business end state; when approvals, payments, and write-backs are involved, restrict permissions.
  • Distinguish between call announcements, company self-reports, and customer acceptance, so that conclusions match the strength of the evidence.
