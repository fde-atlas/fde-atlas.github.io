Senior Forward Deployed Incident Response Consultant at Google — FDE Job Description

How to Become an FDE — Interview Kickstart · 2026-07-01 · 2 min read

Summary

This article archives a now-closed Google job posting for a Senior Forward Deployed Incident Response Consultant on Mandiant's National Security team, based in Ottawa, Canada. The role required 5+ years of experience, paid roughly $166K–$171K, and involved embedding with Canadian national security and defence clients to deliver incident response, assessments, managed detection and response, and training. The editor notes the posting's closure doesn't mean Google stopped hiring, and that a single listing should be read alongside broader FDE market data.

Key points

  • Google posted a Senior FDE Incident Response Consultant role in Ottawa, now closed.
  • The role sat within Mandiant's National Security team serving Canadian federal agencies.
  • It required 5+ years of experience and paid about $166K–$171K.
  • Duties included incident response, assessments, managed detection and response, and training.
  • The editor advises treating one posting as a single data point in FDE research.

From the posting

The National Security Solutions team supports and augments Canada’s most critical cyber missions. We enable federal defence, intelligence, cybersecurity, and public safety departments and agencies with Google technologies and Mandiant intelligence and services. As a Senior Forward Deployed Incident Response Consultant within Mandiant's National Security team, you will embed with clients in Canada's national security and defence sector and directly contribute to building a secure and resilient Canada. You will provide industry-leading incident response, assessment, transformation, managed detection and response, and training services with in-depth tactical support. You will help organizations effectively detect and respond to threats and reduce the overall impact of mission risk before, during, and after an incident. …
