Delivering "FDE" — where engineers go on-site and handle everything from DX promotion to AI implementation end-to-end — nationwide through three pillars: information sharing, talent development, and matching

Minna Systems Co., Ltd. (Head office: Sasebo, Nagasaki Prefecture; Representative Director: Yuki Noda) has launched "FDE Japan" (https://fde-japan.jp/), a platform to spread the FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) development approach across Japan.

With the mission of "creating a state where every company in all 47 prefectures can access the latest AI technology through FDE," FDE Japan will deliver FDE nationwide through three functions: information sharing, talent education, and matching between companies and engineers.

What is FDE?

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a development approach in which "engineers go directly to the customer's company, identify on-site challenges, and solve them with the power of AI and IT." It is now spreading rapidly, primarily in the United States.

In traditional contract development, customers typically compile requirements such as "we want a system like this," and engineers take them back to their own company to implement and build. While this method suits development with clear specifications, the situation is somewhat different when a company wants to adopt and utilize AI in its operations.

This is because maximizing AI's benefits requires designing with an understanding of on-site workflows and how to leverage the company's unique data.

FDE starts from the field. Before beginning development, engineers go on-site, observing, listening to, and experiencing actual operations, then determine "what to build for maximum impact" and handle development and deployment through to completion.

FDE Japan defines FDE in two senses.

  • FDE as a development methodology (Forward Deployed Engineering): A new development style for the AI era that starts with engineers going on-site, rather than the traditional "receive requirements and develop" approach.
  • FDE as an engineer job type (Forward Deployed Engineer): An engineer who visits the customer's site, understands the operations, and solves challenges using AI and digital tools. A role requiring not only IT skills but also an understanding of business and organizations.

In 2026, major US AI companies have successively established dedicated FDE organizations, and major SaaS companies have begun fully rolling out FDE partner programs in Japan, showing this approach is spreading rapidly worldwide.

Why launch FDE Japan now?

Once upon a time, PCs entered every company, and then the internet did.

Next, AI will enter every company. AI is a technological change of that magnitude, and it is already available at a cost accessible to everyone.

However, AI does not produce results simply by being introduced. Significant benefits arise only when someone identifies "where in our operations AI should be used" and can actually integrate it into the business. That role is filled by FDE.

Since its founding in 2016, Minna Systems has handled over 100 system development projects, primarily for small and medium-sized enterprises with 5 to 20 employees. Operating from four locations — Tokyo, Sasebo, Oita, and Gifu — we have seen labor shortages become especially severe in regional workplaces, and feel that leveraging AI as a solution has become urgent. Many SMEs lack IT-savvy personnel, don't know where to start, and are unable to fully utilize AI. It is precisely such companies that need the FDE approach, where external engineers go on-site and work together with them.

However, a single company cannot reach every company nationwide. FDE Japan is a mechanism to spread the FDE approach itself and deliver it across Japan.

FDE Japan's Mission, Vision, and Values

FDE Japan operates under the following mission, vision, and values.

[Mission]

AI for all Japan — all 47 prefectures, every company

Create a state where every company in all 47 prefectures can access the latest AI technology through FDE.

[Vision]

Growth from Genba — Creating Japan's growth from the field.

Change starts from the field. When one company changes, the region changes, and that accumulation becomes Japan's growth.

[Values]

  • Start from Genba — Start from the field. Not at a desk, but on-site. Everything starts from the challenges in the field.
  • Respect the as-is, build the to-be — Respect the current state and build the ideal. The current way of doing things has reasons and history behind it. Understand and respect that, then change what needs to change together with the field.
  • Deliver the last 1 mile — See the last mile through. Deliver the latest AI to every field, in every region.
  • Do it together — Let's do it together. Doing what one company cannot do alone, together. We will grow the community circle.

Three things FDE Japan will tackle

FDE is an approach that is easier for companies with ample funds and talent to adopt. But those who truly need it are SMEs without IT-savvy personnel who don't know where to start. To deliver FDE to such companies, FDE Japan will undertake three initiatives.

  • Information sharing: Clearly communicate the FDE approach and expand awareness
  • Talent education: Train engineers who can excel as FDEs
  • Matching: Connect companies that need AI or DX with FDE talent and FDE companies

To make it easy for SMEs to use, we offer a daily-rate pricing structure based on revenue scale. Small businesses with annual sales under 100 million yen can start from 40,000 yen per day with a minimum of 5 days.

The current service areas are three regions: all of Kyushu (excluding Okinawa), the Gifu/Nagoya area, and the Tokyo/Chiba/Saitama area. Together with partner organizations in each region, we will expand to all 47 prefectures.

Future Developments

FDE Japan plans to advance collaborations with various organizations and institutions. By delivering FDE together with locally rooted experts, the company will build a framework that makes AI and DX more accessible to businesses.

In addition, Minna Systems has already begun supporting small and medium-sized enterprises using the FDE approach, with the Murase Group in Sasebo as its first case. Going forward, the company will continue to accumulate practical case studies through FDE Japan and spread the FDE methodology nationwide.
