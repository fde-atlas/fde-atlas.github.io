Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer: Role and Pay

FWD Deployment · 2026-10-11 · 2 min read

Summary

This article outlines Anthropic's forward deployed engineer role and posted pay. FDEs sit on the Applied AI team, work post-sales with strategic customers, and build production applications with Claude, delivering artifacts such as MCP servers, sub-agents and agent skills. They also document repeatable deployment patterns for internal product and engineering teams. Based on postings checked October 11, 2026, a New York manager role pays $320,000–$405,000, while a London FDE role pays £225,000–£255,000. Most individual postings were in London, Munich and Paris. A separate pre-sales Applied AI Architect role handles pre-build integration planning.

Key points

  • Anthropic FDEs embed with strategic customers post-sales and build with Claude.
  • Deliverables include MCP servers, sub-agents, agent skills and deployment support.
  • They write repeatable deployment patterns for Anthropic's internal teams.
  • Posted pay: New York manager $320k–$405k; London FDE £225k–£255k.
  • Most October 2026 postings were in London, Munich and Paris.

The role

What a Anthropic forward deployed engineer does

Anthropic's FDEs sit on its Applied AI team and work with the post-sales side of an account. The posting lists deliverables such as MCP servers, sub-agents and agent skills, plus hands-on deployment support.

They also write up repeatable deployment patterns for Anthropic's product and engineering teams, so what works at one customer can ship to the next. Anthropic's pre-sales Applied AI Architect is a separate job that helps companies plan an integration before the build starts.

Most individual FDE postings on 2026-10-11 were in London, Munich and Paris. The US posting was a New York manager role.

Team: Applied AI, working with the post-sales team. In Anthropic's words: "Deliver technical artifacts for customers like MCP servers, sub-agents, and agent skills" (posting)

Best for: Enterprises deploying Claude in core workflows

  • Applied AI FDEs
  • Claude Partner Network

Anthropic's Applied AI team includes forward deployed engineers who embed with strategic customers and build with Claude, from MCP servers to sub-agents. In May 2026 it formed an AI services company with Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman and Goldman Sachs for mid-sized companies, and its partner network includes Accenture, Deloitte and PwC.
