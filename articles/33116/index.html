Deloitte to put forward deployed engineers on client sprints

Kernavique · 2026-07-22 · 2 min read

Summary

On July 22, 2026, Deloitte announced an expansion of its Industry Solution Studios to six studios, sending forward deployed engineers on client sprints lasting six to eight weeks. The engineers are described as carrying industry, sector, offering and product knowledge, working on clients' existing AI platforms, data platforms and hyperscalers. The release gives no headcount for these engineers, does not name the six industries covered, and notes Deloitte has roughly 470,000 people worldwide. It quotes Kelly Herod, Sundhar Sekhar and Lynne Sterrett. Kernavique's reading: for employers benchmarking teams, the sprint length is the usable fact, not team size.

On July 22, 2026, Deloitte said in a release that it had expanded its Industry Solution Studios, with six studios and sprints of six to eight weeks. Deloitte said forward deployed engineers with specific industry, sector, offerings and product knowledge will be deployed. The release gives no headcount for those engineers and states that Deloitte has approximately 470,000 people worldwide.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: Deloitte.

What happened

Deloitte said the engineers work on the AI platforms clients already use, which the release describes as frontier GenAI collaborators, data platforms and hyperscalers. The six industries covered by the studios were not named in what we read.

The release quotes Kelly Herod, chief client officer at Deloitte Consulting LLP, and Sundhar Sekhar, chief services officer, on the engineers. It also quotes Lynne Sterrett, vice chair and Clients & Industries leader at Deloitte.

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

Deloitte names industry, sector, offerings and product knowledge as what its forward deployed engineers bring. This suggests sector depth matters alongside engineering here. The release gives no number of roles.

If you are hiring them

Deloitte attaches its engineers to fixed sprints of six to eight weeks and does not publish a headcount. If you are sizing your own team, the sprint length is the usable fact here, not the team size.
