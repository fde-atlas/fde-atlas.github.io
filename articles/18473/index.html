Report on the Strengths and Process of In-House FDE Development for All Industries

Original title: 全業種向け 社内FDE育成の強みと進め方レポート

株式会社Aiworker · 2025-11-30 · 1 min read

Summary

A whitepaper from Japanese company AIworker argues that companies should develop FDEs internally rather than relying on outsourced development, training alone, hiring AI talent, or merely deploying tools. It compares these approaches in a table and proposes starting with five task patterns: data transcription/verification, inquiry handling, document creation, aggregation/reporting, and quoting/proposals. For each pattern, it shows results of having AI process fictional business data plus AI-generated checkpoints. The recommended process has four stages: selecting people, choosing topics, building small, then scaling. It includes a topic scoring sheet, effect estimation sheet, and answers to common internal questions.

Key points

  • Argues internal FDE development beats outsourcing, training-only, hiring, or tool-only approaches.
  • Identifies five task patterns as starting topics, e.g., transcription/verification and inquiry handling.
  • Shows AI processing results on fictional data with AI-generated checkpoints per pattern.
  • Recommends four stages: pick people, pick topics, build small, expand.
  • Includes scoring sheet, effect estimation sheet, and FAQ answers for internal buy-in.

Full text

A comparison with outsourced development, training alone, hiring AI talent, and tool implementation alone, and a process that starts with five business patterns such as data transcription, verification, and inquiry handling

This report covers the following topics

  • A comparison table of outsourced development, training alone, hiring AI talent, and tool implementation alone versus in-house FDE development
  • How to choose subject matter based on five business patterns: transcription and verification, inquiry handling, document creation, aggregation and reporting, and quotations and proposals
  • Results of having AI process hypothetical business data for each of the five patterns, and the checkpoints AI identified
  • A four-step process: selecting people, choosing subject matter, building small, and scaling up
  • A scoring sheet for subject matter, an impact estimation sheet, and how to answer questions commonly asked internally
