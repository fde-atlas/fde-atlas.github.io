2 FDE Positions at AI Shift, Inc. | Up to ¥15 Million Annual Salary | FDE board

Original title: 株式会社AI ShiftのFDE求人2件・年収1,500万円まで｜FDE board

株式会社Ai Shift · 2026-08-31 · 2 min read

Summary

FDE board lists two Forward Deployed Engineer openings at AI Shift Inc., a CyberAgent subsidiary of about 75 employees focused on vertical AI products such as AI Worker, agent-building platforms, and voice agents. The roles, based in Tokyo and Osaka with hybrid work, pay 5.04 to 15 million yen and involve embedding AI into customer operations using LLMs, RAG, agents, and voice AI. Stated career paths include Lead FDE, product manager, and engineering manager. The Osaka opening is notable as FDE roles in Kansai remain rare.

Key points

  • Two FDE openings at AI Shift, up to 15 million yen salary.
  • Positions in Tokyo and Osaka, both hybrid work.
  • AI Shift is a wholly owned CyberAgent subsidiary with ~75 staff.
  • FDEs embed AI into customer workflows using LLMs, RAG, and voice AI.
  • Career paths include Lead FDE, PM, and engineering manager.

About AI Shift Inc.

A 100% subsidiary of CyberAgent with the mission of "collaboration between humans and AI." In addition to domain-specific AI products such as the AI Worker series, an AI agent building platform, and voice agents, the company provides custom AI solutions for individual enterprises, reskilling, and consulting. With approximately 75 employees, hires are officially employed by CyberAgent.

Characteristics of FDE Roles at This Company

The company is recruiting FDEs who leverage its own "AI Worker Platform" to implement AI into customer operations, at two locations: Tokyo and Osaka. FDE positions in the Kansai region are still rare, making the Osaka opening particularly valuable. Career paths offered include Lead FDE, Product Manager, and Engineering Manager.
