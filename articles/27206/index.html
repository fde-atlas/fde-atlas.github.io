FDE (Forward Deployed Eng) Meetup + Happy Hour

Rocketlane · 2025-08-28 · 3 min read

Summary

This page is an event listing from Rocketlane promoting an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineering) meetup and happy hour on August 28, 2025, in San Francisco. Most of the page is promotional content for Rocketlane's professional services automation platform, including its Nitro AI agents, project delivery, financial management, and client portal features. It also advertises the Frontline Forum, described as the first community dedicated to Forward Deployed Engineers, alongside customer stories with metrics such as GoCardless delivering 59% faster time-to-value and Gamify cutting implementation timelines from six months to 30 days. The page offers little substantive discussion of FDE roles; its main value is the event announcement and community pointers.

Key points

  • Event: FDE meetup and happy hour, August 28, 2025, San Francisco.
  • Page doubles as promotion for Rocketlane's services delivery platform.
  • Frontline Forum is Rocketlane's community for Forward Deployed Engineers.
  • Customer stories cite metrics like 59% faster time-to-value at GoCardless.
  • Little substantive content on FDE work itself.
