Summary

OpenFDE's signal library indexes 1,251 primary sources on forward deployed engineers, centered on Anthropic's enterprise push. Key items include the joint enterprise AI services company with Blackstone, Goldman Sachs and others, its acquisition of Fractional AI, Claude's rollout to Deloitte's 470,000 employees (described as the largest enterprise deployment), a $30 billion Series G at a $380 billion valuation, and roughly $30 billion annualized revenue with about 80% from enterprise. Anthropic is also hiring many Applied AI Engineers. Sources are searchable by field, type, language and year, with verification marks reflecting status at collection time only.

Key points

  • 1,251 searchable, filterable primary sources on FDEs.
  • Anthropic builds an enterprise AI services venture with Blackstone and Goldman Sachs, acquiring Fractional AI.
  • Claude deployed to Deloitte's 470,000 staff, the largest enterprise rollout.
  • $30B Series G at $380B valuation; ~$30B annualized revenue, ~80% enterprise.
  • Many open Applied AI Engineer roles across industries; verify current info at original sites.
