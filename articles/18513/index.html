How to expense Maven courses

maven.com · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

This Maven help page explains how to get course costs reimbursed through an employer's L&D budget. Students receive a receipt after enrolling and can pull a detailed invoice with their company name from maven.com/me; a certificate of completion is emailed after finishing a course. Maven also supplies a manager-approval email template, advising readers to frame the course as a way to make or save the company money rather than a personal perk. The example is the Forward Deployed Engineering Bootcamp, a live online course taught by Hamza Farooq costing $2,750. Maven says nearly half of its students get reimbursed, and it supports bulk purchases and itemized invoices for teams.

Key points

  • Enrollment generates a receipt you can forward for reimbursement.
  • Detailed invoices with company names are available at maven.com/me.
  • Completion certificates are emailed after finishing a course.
  • Approval requests should emphasize company benefits, not career goals.
  • The example course costs $2,750 and is taught by Hamza Farooq.

Good news: Many companies offer an L&D or education budget that covers the cost of our courses. We provide several ways to help you get your course reimbursed.

Get reimbursed for Forward Deployed Engineering Bootcamp

Maven receipt

After you enroll in a course, you will receive a receipt for your course purchase. You can forward this receipt directly to your team for reimbursement. If you need additional information on the receipt, such as your company name, you can view a more detailed invoice from maven.com/me. Please reach out to Maven support if you need help.

Certificate of completion

Once you complete a course, we will email you a link to claim your certificate. Here is an example:

Email template

If you need manager approval, we also provide a reimbursement template that you can forward. Let your manager know you're interested in a course that will improve your work.

Sure, you could say the course aligns with your career goals, but what your manager REALLY cares about is whether the course will help your company make money or save money. That's why you must frame the course in terms of how it benefits your manager, team, and organization.

This will make the course feel like a no-brainer and create a sense of urgency. Show that your team will fall behind if you don't take this course — or will get ahead if you do.

Managers are more likely to approve purchases that feel like painkillers, not vitamins.

Hey {manager},

There's a course called Forward Deployed Engineering Bootcamp that I'd love to enroll in. It's a live, online course with peers who are in similar roles to me, and it's run on Maven, where 100,000+ professionals from companies like Meta, Google, Airbnb, and OpenAI go to level up when they need to learn quickly and efficiently.

A few highlights:

  • Learning directly from Hamza Farooq, the expert instructor teaching the course
  • Hands-on working sessions to test new tactics and ideas
  • Latest thinking in the space on how to solve problems we're facing

I anticipate being able to put my learnings directly into practice during the course. After the course, I can share a recap with the team.

The course costs $2,750. If you like, you can review course details here, including the instructor’s bio: https://maven.com/boring-bot/ai-system-design

What do you think?

Thanks,
{Your Name}

P.S. Other teams have sent a few people to the course together because it's an efficient way to get shared context that otherwise takes back-and-forth time to build. It might make sense for us to send a few folks to this course.

Enjoy the course!

It's so simple that nearly half our students get their courses reimbursed. Investing in yourself with a Maven course is a powerful way to accelerate your career, and we hope your company agrees. If you need help, please email support@maven.com.

Want to buy Maven courses for your team?

We facilitate bulk purchases, itemized invoices or simply answer questions about Maven for teams.
