Rovo Credits Overage Billing Starts December 3: What Jira Admins Must Configure Before Extra Usage Charges Begin

Traversaal.ai · 2026-11-16 · 7 min read

Summary

Traversaal.ai warns Jira admins that Atlassian starts overage billing for Rovo credits, Automation steps, and AI agent resolutions on December 3, 2026. The 'Enable extra usage' toggle in Atlassian Administration is the only control blocking charges once an org exceeds its included credit pool, which varies by plan and contract. Rovo Dev runs on a separate meter and bills at the org level, with community reports of charges on accounts lacking access. The article recommends verifying the toggle, checking live allowances in the Billing center, auditing automation rules and agent usage, and disabling unauthorized Rovo Dev access before enforcement.

Key points

  • Overage billing for three meters starts December 3, 2026.
  • The extra-usage toggle in Atlassian admin controls all overage charges.
  • Rovo Dev credits are metered separately and billed org-wide.
  • Automation steps draw from the same pooled credit bucket.
  • Audit top consumers and set budget alerts before December 3.

What exactly starts billing on December 3 and which meters are affected?

Three meters become real charges on December 3, 2026, the moment your org exceeds its included pool, if extra usage is enabled.

What changes December 3 is that going over your pool stops being free. Atlassian Cloud AI credits are pooled org-level allowances covering Rovo Chat, agents, Deep features, Automation executions, and AI agent resolutions, all one bucket.

Rovo Dev is an important exception. Its credits run on a completely separate meter used only for Dev features like the CLI and code reviews. That separation creates a real billing risk: community reports confirm charges for Rovo Dev on accounts that cannot access it. An entitlement misconfiguration does not stop the meter, because metering runs at the org level.

How do I find and set the "enable extra usage" overage toggle in Atlassian admin today?

The "Enable extra usage" toggle in Atlassian admin is the only control that blocks or allows Rovo credits overage billing on December 3, so verify its live state in your tenant now, because your org's default may not be what you expect.

Your tenant's default depends on its rollout history, plan tier, and provisioning date. Verify the live setting directly rather than assuming any particular default applies to your org.

Step-by-step to verify and set the toggle:

  • Open admin.atlassian.com and select your org.
  • Navigate to Billing > Usage & limits, or Products > Rovo / AI usage settings if your layout differs.
  • If neither path is visible, search "Usage" in the admin search bar.
  • Find the toggle labeled "Enable extra usage" or "Allow charges beyond included credits."
  • Read the confirmation text beneath it, which will specify when billing applies.
  • Make a deliberate choice: disable to block overage charges entirely, or leave it enabled with budget alerts configured.
  • Save, take a dated screenshot, and log a change record.

Do not rely on any external guide as a substitute for checking your own tenant. If you are in a governance-sensitive environment, calendar a weekly check through January and set a Cloud admin alert for any billing threshold change.

How many Rovo credits do we get by plan, and when will overage kick in for our org?

Your included Rovo credit pool is set by your Atlassian Cloud plan tier and contract, and overage starts the moment pooled consumption exceeds that allowance within your billing cycle, if extra usage is enabled.

Standard-tier orgs should verify their specific allowance in admin, as published figures vary by contract and may not match what any external guide lists. Pools aggregate at the org level across all products. Pull your current allowance directly from admin.atlassian.com > Billing > Usage & limits at the moment you audit rather than relying on published figures, which may not reflect your specific plan configuration.

How do I audit and stop "phantom spend" from Rovo Dev and Automation before charges start?

Use Atlassian's updated automation usage report to identify the top credit-consuming rules, cross-check Rovo Dev entitlements against its separate meter, then disable or scope the offenders before December 3.

Automation audit: Go to Project automation > Usage report, sort by Rovo credit consumption descending, and pause the highest-consuming rules. Review which rule types are driving the most spend and consider narrowing their trigger conditions or scope before re-enabling them.

Rovo agents audit: Export agent usage from the Rovo admin panel. Restrict permissions, narrow trigger scopes, and throttle schedules on the highest consumers.

Rovo Dev entitlement audit: Pull the list of users with Rovo Dev product access and compare against Rovo Dev meter usage. If usage is above zero but no users should have Dev access, disable Rovo Dev at the org level and open a support ticket with log evidence.

As a practical rule of thumb used in this guide: consider a change freeze from November 25 through December 5 to prevent new automation rules or agent deployments from adding unexpected consumption during the enforcement window.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the extra-usage setting enabled by default on my org, and where do I check?

Defaults vary by tenant history, plan tier, and rollout timing, so no external guide can tell you reliably. Open Atlassian admin > Billing > Usage & limits and read the live toggle state directly in your tenant. Take a dated screenshot and log your finding as a change record.

What is the per-credit overage cost for Rovo and where is it officially documented?

Atlassian has not published a single universal per-credit rate that applies across all plans and contracts. Your org-specific overage rate, if any, is defined in your Billing center price card. To find the rate that applies to your account, go to admin.atlassian.com > Billing > Usage & limits and review the price card listed there, or contact your Atlassian account representative for a written confirmation before December 3.

How do I see which automation rules or Rovo agents consume the most credits?

Use Atlassian's automation usage report, which shows per-rule consumption of Automation steps and Rovo credits. Sort by most expensive and pause the top offenders.

Can I disable Rovo or Rovo Dev for specific users to prevent charges?

Yes, remove product access and feature flags at the group or project level. For Rovo Dev, treat this as urgent: it bills from a separate meter and charges at the org level even when individual users lack access.

Conclusion

December 3 turns three live meters into three billing exposures the moment your org crosses its included pool, if extra usage is on.

Set it deliberately, screenshot it, then pull your included pool from the live Billing center and compare against the last 30 days of consumption. If you are above 80% of allowance, treat the automation audit as urgent.

What should Jira admins do before December 3?

Your three actions, in order:

  • Right now: Open Atlassian admin and set the extra-usage toggle intentionally. Screenshot it and log the change.
  • Within 48 hours: Export your usage report, pause top credit consumers, and document your plan allowance from the live Billing center. Confirm your Rovo Dev meter shows zero if no users should have access.
  • Before December 3: Lock entitlements, set budget alerts, disable unauthorized Rovo Dev access, and schedule a post-enforcement review for December 10 to catch any unexpected charges in the first billing cycle.
