Partner Forward Deployed Engineering Manager III, GenAI, Google Cloud at Google — FDE Job Description

How to Become an FDE — Interview Kickstart · 2026-06-06 · 2 min read

Summary

This page archives a since-closed Google Cloud job posting for a Partner Forward Deployed Engineering Manager III (GenAI) based in Singapore. The role targets engineers with 8+ years of experience and leads an FDE team that works hands-on inside customer environments — coding, debugging, and shipping agentic AI solutions with partners rather than advising. It emphasizes solving production blockers such as integration complexity, data readiness, and state management. The posting was first seen on 2026-06-06 and gone by 2026-07-01. The page also links other Google FDE openings in Singapore, India, the Nordics, and New York.

From the posting

As a Manager of a GenAI Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) team, you are an embedded builder who bridges the gap between frontier AI products and production-grade reality within partners for our customers. Unlike traditional advisory roles, you will function as an innovator-builder, moving beyond high-level architecture to code, debug, and jointly ship bespoke and scalable agentic solutions directly with our partners, for and within the customer’s environment. This role is designed for high-agency engineers with a founder’s mindset. You will address blockers to production including solving the integration complexities, data readiness issues, and state-management issues that prevent AI from reaching enterprise-grade maturity. …
