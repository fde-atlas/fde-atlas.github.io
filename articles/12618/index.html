Hong Kong Enterprise AI Services

Original title: 香港企業 AI 服務

Onyx Devs Lab · 2026 · 3 min read

Summary

This is a service page from Onyx Devs Lab, a Hong Kong firm offering enterprise AI consulting and delivery. It outlines how it identifies AI opportunities, builds integrated agents and knowledge systems, and compares vendors and delivery models (consulting, custom development, FDE) using evidence, controls, accountability, and exit terms. The page links to a large set of practical guides: vendor verification, RFPs, launch and handover checklists, RAG and agent security, AI operations, ROI estimation, and sector-specific guides for legal, accounting, manufacturing, and recruiting, plus six delivery cases with metric definitions.

Key points

  • Onyx Devs Lab is a Hong Kong enterprise AI consulting and delivery firm
  • Compares vendors on evidence, production controls, accountability, and exit terms
  • Offers frameworks for choosing consulting, custom development, or FDE delivery
  • Guides cover RAG, agent security, operations, ROI, and sector playbooks
  • Six delivery cases include metric definitions and source pages
