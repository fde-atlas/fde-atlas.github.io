FDE Sprint: your first autopilot, shipped in ten business days

Eupraxic · 2026-11-01 · 5 min read

Summary

Eupraxic's FDE Sprint is a fixed-price, ten-business-day engagement for founder-led B2B SaaS companies. A forward deployed engineer scopes one workflow on Day 1, delivers a prototype on the client's real data by Day 5, and hands over a staging deploy or production-ready pull request by Day 10, with a full refund if either checkpoint is missed. Pricing is $5,000 through the November 17 kickoff and $7,500 from December 1, split between signing and Day 10; a second Sprint within six months costs the original price. Deliverables include an output rubric, a workflow playbook, a 30-day roadmap, and 30 days of async support. Eupraxic declines legal, medical, compliance, payments, or autonomous customer-facing workflows, does not guarantee production deployment, and leaves compliance review to the client's team.

Key points

  • Ten business days from scoping to staging deploy or production-ready PR.
  • Full refund if the Day 5 prototype or Day 10 acceptance criteria are missed.
  • Priced at $5,000, rising to $7,500 from December 1.
  • Includes rubric, playbook, roadmap, and 30 days of async support.
  • Declines high-risk workflows; production deployment is not guaranteed.

What you get

  • Scope and design
  • Use-case selection, workflow scope, the one-sentence Brief, UX flow, architecture, prompt and data design.
  • Build
  • Hands-on implementation in your repo. Working prototype on your data by Day 5.
  • Handoff
  • Staging deployment or production-ready pull request on Day 10, a Loom walkthrough, handoff notes, an output quality rubric with worked examples, a reusable workflow playbook and a 30-day roadmap.
  • Support
  • 30 days of async support after handoff. Something breaks in week three, you write, we fix.
  • Guarantee
  • No prototype on your data by Day 5, full refund. Workflow not running to the Brief's acceptance criteria by Day 10, full refund.
  • Price
  • $5,000 through the November 17 kickoff, $7,500 from December 1. Half on signing, half on Day 10. The price is set against what the workflow replaces: a contractor month, an engineer role you haven't filled, or six weeks of two engineers. A second Sprint booked within six months of handoff runs at the price you paid for the first.

The ten days

Days Phase What happens
1 Scope Workflow, user, data, constraints and success criteria pinned down in one signed sentence.
2 to 3 Design UX flow, architecture, prompt and data structure, and the output rubric.
4 to 5 Prototype First working version on your real data. Day 5 checkpoint.
6 to 9 Implementation Edge cases, error states, prompt tuning against the rubric, handoff preparation.
10 Handoff Staging deploy, production-ready PR or implementation handoff, depending on your infrastructure and access.

The refund guarantee

  • The workflow in the signed Brief doesn't run to its acceptance criteria: full refund.

Payment is half on signing and half on Day 10.

What we take on first

Low risk

Internal drafts and summaries that a person reviews before they are used. Most first Sprints are here.

Medium risk

Workflows that touch customer-facing data, with explicit human review before any output reaches a customer.

High risk

Anything legal, medical, compliance or payments related, or anything acting autonomously toward customers. We decline these as a first Sprint and rescope them to a lower tier.

Is the Sprint for you?

This is for you if

  • You lead product at a founder-led B2B SaaS company.
  • You have one workflow in mind and a person who runs it today.
  • Your data sits in one system you can export.
  • One decision-maker can answer within a business day.
  • You want the code in your repo, not a deck.

This is not for you if

  • You want to "add AI" without a user and a trigger.
  • You need a full AI transformation.
  • Your data is not accessible yet.
  • Nobody on your side can review during the ten days.
  • You need guaranteed production deployment regardless of your infrastructure.

What you need to bring: read-only codebase access, sample data, and one decision-maker who answers within a business day. About five hours of the founder's time across the ten days, most of it on Day 1.

Questions about the Sprint

Is there a refund guarantee?

Yes. A full refund at either of two checkpoints: no working prototype on your data by Day 5, or the workflow in the signed Brief not running to its acceptance criteria by Day 10.

How is this different from an AI consulting firm?

We ship a working workflow into your codebase by Day 10; a consulting firm delivers a roadmap. Same category as the deployment companies OpenAI and Anthropic launched in 2026, at a founder-tier price.

Should we hire a forward deployed engineer, use an agency, or run a Sprint?

Hire when there is a steady pipeline of AI work to justify a full-time salary. Use an agency or consultancy when you want a team embedded for months. Run a Sprint when you have one workflow and want it in your product in ten business days at a fixed price, with your engineers owning the pull request afterwards.

Can you guarantee production deployment?

No. Production depends on access, infrastructure and third parties we do not control. If production is blocked, the deliverable is a staging deploy or a production-ready pull request your team merges.

Do you build agents?

When the workflow needs one: tool calls, lookups in your systems, multi-step outputs, often through MCP servers. Every first Sprint keeps a person approving anything that leaves the company.

Do you handle compliance, security, SOC 2 or GDPR?

No. The workflow is built to pass through your existing compliance posture; the review itself is your team's work and is out of scope.

What happens after Day 10?

You own the pull request, the config, the rubric, and the playbook. We suggest running the workflow for one account first and widening from there. A second workflow is another Sprint at your first price, booked within six months. Help with the rollout is scoped as a separate engagement and is never a condition of the Sprint; there is no retainer required to keep the first one running.

Want one useful AI workflow in your product?

Score your workflow in two minutes, then book a 15-minute Scoping Call. If it fits, you leave the call with a Brief draft and a kickoff date.

Prefer to start smaller? The $500 Discovery Day is two hours with you and the person who does the workflow today. We write the workflow out step by step and mark each step as rule-following or judgement, draft the one-sentence Brief and a first rubric, note what the workflow replaces and what that costs you now, and give a go or no-go. You keep the one-page memo. The $500 is credited to the Sprint.
