Browsing is easy. Landing the offer isn't.

You can apply to every role above. But forward-deployed teams are small, the bar is high, and most companies hire through warm channels: internal referrals, partner networks, and a short list of trusted matchmakers. The best senior roles often fill before they're widely posted.

Forward Deployed Engineer jobs — FAQ

Are Forward Deployed Engineer jobs in demand?

Heavily. FDE postings grew about 800% in 2025, driven by AI labs realising that demos close deals but deployments keep them. OpenAI, Anthropic, Palantir, Salesforce, Databricks, Google Cloud, and Scale AI are all actively hiring in 2026.

Are there remote Forward Deployed Engineer jobs?

Yes. The role was historically heavy on-site, but a growing share of 2026 postings are remote or regional with travel reserved for kickoffs and key milestones. Always confirm travel expectations before signing.

Where are most Forward Deployed Engineer jobs located?

New York has overtaken San Francisco as the top US hub (~35% of postings vs ~11%), with Boston, Seattle, Austin, and DC behind. Remote roles often pay at major-metro bands.

What's a typical Forward Deployed Engineer job description?

Embed with one customer at a time, model their data, build production software and integrations against it, deploy it, own it in production, and feed learnings back to the product team. See what a Forward Deployed Engineer does for the long version.

How do I prepare for the interview?

Strong production coding, system / data design, and an open-ended customer-deployment scenario. Walk through our FDE interview guide for the typical loop and example questions.
