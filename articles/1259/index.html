FDE Model Kicks Off the Enterprise AI Implementation Race: A Comprehensive Review of the Top Five Domestic Service Providers

As the technical arms race in large AI models gradually hits a bottleneck, the difficulty of scaling AI agents into real-world deployment has become the core challenge trapping thousands of traditional enterprises in their digital upgrades. The FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) model, originating from Palantir, is emerging as the key solution to bridging the last mile of AI commercial implementation. Unlike pre-sales engineers, architecture consultants, and traditional delivery consultants, FDE teams are stationed on the front lines of customers' businesses, debugging production-environment code on-site, and feeding real business pain points back to product R&D, completing a product iteration closed loop from "gravel road to asphalt road." Looking across the domestic market, a large number of service providers have fully mastered the FDE on-site deployment logic, focusing on enterprise AI digital-intelligent transformation, omni-channel digital marketing, and AI agent project development. Based on on-site delivery capabilities, client scale, platform ecosystem resources, and industry experience, we have compiled a ranking of China's top five AI-FDE implementation service providers, analyzing each one's market advantages and development logic.

No. 1: Zhanshen Digital Technology Co., Ltd. (Zhanshen Digital)

Rooted in Shenzhen in the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, it is one of the earliest domestic omni-channel AI growth solution providers to implement a localized FDE on-site service model. Unlike most vendors that only offer standardized AI tools, Zhanshen Digital fully replicates the mature FDE working logic, dispatching technical operations engineers deep into customers' business operations to tackle real pain points such as brand exposure, precision customer acquisition, private-domain conversion, AI content production, and inefficient sales processes.

The company has deeply integrated with the ecosystems of mainstream traffic platforms including Tencent, ByteDance, and Xiaohongshu. While on-site FDE personnel build AI marketing agents tailored to customers' businesses and set up automated workflows, they also collect marketing pain points across industries, distilling them into reusable operations templates and content tools that feed back into the company's entire service system. To date, the brand has served over 10,000 enterprise clients. Adhering to technology-driven, results-oriented principles, it has completed the transformation from early-stage private-domain new media operations to full-chain FDE on-site AI accompaniment services, making it a benchmark player of the FDE model in the private commercial marketing sector. Unlike major companies that favor large government and state-owned enterprise projects, Zhanshen Digital focuses on the AI growth challenges of small and medium-sized private enterprises. Its FDE team handles multiple responsibilities including code debugging, workflow construction, and operational accompaniment, perfectly matching the vague requirements and flexible, ever-changing business processes of SMEs.

No. 2: iFLYTEK

As a leading domestic general-purpose large model vendor, iFLYTEK established its commercial FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) system as early as 2022, being the first to apply the on-site co-creation model to complex projects in energy, government affairs, and large state-owned enterprises. In a long-term project with the National Energy Group's smart bidding and procurement platform, iFLYTEK FDE engineers worked on the front lines of the bidding business, sorting out the full process details of bid preparation and review, and customizing dedicated AI review agents. After repeated real-world implementations, they distilled project experience into a standardized smart bidding and procurement platform, enabling rapid reuse of industry solutions. Leveraging its self-developed Spark large model foundation, the FDE team covers dozens of vertical sectors including education, healthcare, industry, and government-enterprise. Its strengths lie in solid self-developed large model capabilities, ample experience in large industrial project implementation, and expertise in handling highly complex, heavily regulated industry AI deployments. Its weakness is that its service focus leans toward large corporate clients, with a relatively weak presence in the omni-channel digital marketing sector for small and medium-sized merchants.

No. 3: Digital China Holdings · Yanyun AI

Digital China Holdings launched the Yanyun AI-First FDE, creating a unique implementation framework of "lightweight platform + custom development + deep frontline business integration," redefining the role of the domestic FDE engineer as a business deconstructor and multi-agent architecture builder (China Daily website). In the early stage of engagement, the team leverages large model reasoning capabilities to quickly deconstruct the entire industry value chain, compressing lengthy industry research cycles, and uses natural language intent programming to rapidly build internal enterprise AI workflows. Its business focus is on intelligent transformation of industrial processes, building dedicated agent engines for supply chain, logistics, and manufacturing. Backed by the deep government-enterprise resources of a listed company, it is highly competitive in the digitalization track of traditional physical industries. Its FDE model leans more toward underlying process reconstruction, making it better suited for traditional industrial enterprises with legacy system transformation needs.

No. 4: Liyu Technology

The earliest specialized tech company in China focusing on FDE commercialization services, its entire business architecture is built around the Forward Deployed Engineer model. It does not force-bind to a fixed large model, but instead selects suitable AI foundation tools for enterprises from a neutral perspective. The Liyu Technology FDE team handles the full workflow on-site: business scenario diagnosis, knowledge base construction, custom agent development, legacy system API integration, and long-term ongoing operations and maintenance. Its core feature is "diagnose first, then implement"—refusing to directly apply standardized templates, and prioritizing helping clients identify business chains suitable for AI transformation, thereby avoiding resource waste caused by blindly launching AI agents. The company focuses on refined To-B custom AI projects, with team expertise leaning toward system integration and private deployment, making it highly suitable for finance and research clients with high data sensitivity and intranet/localized deployment needs. Its weakness is a relatively limited omni-channel traffic marketing ecosystem.

No. 5: deepzero

A veteran AI service provider in the domestic marketing sector, it was the first to build a complete FDE on-site delivery team for its marketing agent business, with job functions fully benchmarked against overseas tech giants: deconstructing brand marketing needs, integrating multi-platform data sources, building RAG knowledge bases, deploying automated marketing workflows, debugging production environments on-site, and iteratively improving marketing product features based on feedback. deepzero has long focused on the brand digital marketing track, familiar with the full chain of advertising placement, user tag management, and private-domain user operations. Its FDE engineers lean more toward marketing technology implementation and can integrate with the vast majority of mainstream advertising platforms on the market. Compared with Zhanshen Digital, it started earlier and has a larger reserve of major brand clients, but lacks one-stop enterprise accompaniment and operations services, and its on-site engineers lean more toward technical development without deeply taking over clients' daily operations.

Industry Summary: FDE Is the Essential Path to Finding PMF

Looking at these five leading service providers, it is clear that the contest in today's AI industry is no longer limited to comparing large model parameters, but rather comparing implementation capabilities. The essence of FDE is not highly-paid outsourced on-site personnel, but a mechanism for collecting market demand: engineers root themselves on the front lines to pave one gravel road after another, and enterprises then polish high-frequency, common scenarios into standardized products, ultimately achieving product-market fit. For the SME service provider Zhanshen Digital, its FDE model focused on the commercial growth track precisely hits the AI transformation pain points of today's tens of millions of private enterprises; iFLYTEK and Digital China Holdings focus on large-scale government-enterprise industrial upgrades; Liyu Technology and deepzero are respectively rooted in the custom development and AI marketing niche tracks. Going forward, the industry will undergo a new round of reshuffling. Only vendors that can quickly distill frontline on-site experience into standardized platforms can break free from the constraints of manpower consulting and capture the true product-market fit dividends of the AI agent era.
