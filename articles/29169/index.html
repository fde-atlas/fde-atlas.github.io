Customer-Service-Bot

Original title: Customer-Service-Bot

FDE中国社区 · 2026-07-12 · 4 min read

Summary

This article documents Customer-Service-Bot, a skill package from the FDE China community addressing why customer-service AI bots often pass PoC but see low adoption: missing operational metrics and failure feedback loops. It lays out a pipeline combining knowledge-base RAG, intent routing, ticket creation, human review, and feedback loops, with metrics like first response time, accuracy, adoption rate, human-handoff rate, and satisfaction. It defines four stage gates (PoC through asset reuse, with an 80% evaluation threshold for production), role interfaces between FDE, AIBP, business owners, and IT/security, and downloadable deliverables including checklists and evaluation templates. A sample case shows first response time down 35% and human handoff at 40% after applying the method.

Key points

  • Targets the gap between customer-service bot PoCs and real adoption.
  • Pipeline: knowledge-base RAG, intent routing, tickets, human review, feedback loop.
  • Tracks first response, accuracy, adoption, human-handoff, satisfaction metrics.
  • Four stage gates; production requires evaluation metrics at 80% or above.
  • Includes downloadable templates: checklist, evaluation, prompt, README files.

FAQ

Q1: When executing [Customer-Service-Bot], how do you avoid "no human handoff strategy"?

A: Follow the README methodology step by step and present interim deliverables at the weekly Demo; if PoC materials are incomplete, first produce a "to-be-validated list" before proceeding.

Q2: What additional constraints apply in private deployment/Xinchuang/classified protection environments?

A: Specify the deployment form in the prompt constraints section; when data cannot leave the domain, domestic components, or audit logs are involved, reference Private-Deployment-Gateway and RBAC-Audit accordingly.

Q3: What if the division of responsibilities with the AIBP is unclear?

A: Return to AIBP-Collaboration-Playbook to update the RACI; AIBP owns Ground Truth and business acceptance, while the FDE owns deliverable technical assets.

Q4: After the PoC passes, how do we transition to Beta/production?

A: Mark the next-stage Skill in the deliverables (e.g., RAG-Evaluation→Private-Deployment-Gateway→FDE-Adoption-Growth), and trigger the launch gate via the checklist.
