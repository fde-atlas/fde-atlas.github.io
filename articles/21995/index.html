Launch of "KUROKO," a Back-End Support Service That Ensures AI Initiative Leads Are Never Left to Struggle Alone

Original title: AI推進担当者を“ひとりで悩ませない”後方支援サービス『KUROKO』提供開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-05-29 · 6 min read

Summary

Neurosphere launched KUROKO, a monthly subscription support service for in-house AI promotion staff and DX teams. Rather than embedding external engineers on-site like its existing Tokumei FDE service, KUROKO backs internal staff from behind, helping them grow into "in-house FDEs" who can make technical decisions, review implementations, handle governance risks, and present results to management. The service addresses a common failure pattern: AI tools get distributed but go unused, and PoCs stall because a single overloaded person must decide themes, tools, automation boundaries, and compliance issues alone. Packages include a first-month onboarding that audits current efforts and builds a 90-day roadmap, plus a standard plan with twice-weekly sessions, up to four hours of monthly implementation review, and chat-based advice.

Key points

  • KUROKO is a monthly support service that trains internal AI staff into in-house FDEs.
  • It covers technical decisions, implementation review, governance, results reporting, and UI/UX advice.
  • It targets the common problem of AI tools going unused and PoCs stalling.
  • Onboarding includes a current-state audit and a 90-day roadmap; the standard plan offers twice-weekly sessions.
  • It complements Tokumei FDE, which embeds external professionals for fastest deployment.

As a new service in the AI implementation support "Special Mission FDE Series," it develops in-house talent into "in-house FDEs," moving AI utilization from "trial" to "implementation and establishment"

Neurosphere Inc. (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director: Minoru Negoro) has launched "KUROKO," a companion-style support service that develops and evolves the staff responsible for AI promotion within companies into "in-house FDEs" who serve as a bridge between business operations and AI, as a new related service in its AI implementation support "Special Mission FDE" series.

"KUROKO" is a monthly subscription service that puts AI promotion staff and DX promotion departments in the leading role, with Neurosphere's expert team providing back-end support in technical decisions, implementation reviews, governance compliance, and results organization.

While "Special Mission FDE" features external professionals stationed on-site to advance AI implementation at the fastest possible pace, "KUROKO" is an insourcing support service that develops a company's AI promotion staff into "in-house FDEs," enabling AI utilization to continue and take root within the company itself.

■ What Is "FDE," Which Is Attracting Growing Attention in the AI Implementation Field?

In recent years, the role of "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" has been attracting attention in the AI and tech industries.

An FDE is not merely a system developer, but a professional who goes deep into the customer's workplace and drives everything end-to-end: identifying business challenges, selecting technologies, implementing solutions, establishing operations, and generating results.

While the adoption of generative AI tools is progressing, many companies are facing challenges such as "AI tools were distributed but are not used in the field," "PoCs never progress to production use," and "they cannot be connected to business workflows or existing systems."

To achieve real results with AI, simply introducing tools is not enough. Companies need people who understand frontline operations, identify the challenges AI should solve, and can integrate AI into existing business processes. The FDE is the one who fulfills this role.

■ Two Approaches to AI Implementation Offered by Neurosphere

Neurosphere offers two approaches to AI implementation, tailored to each customer's phase and needs (budget, speed, and organizational policies).

The first is "resident support by external professionals." Our "Special Mission FDE" service is a high-end approach in which professional consultants and AI engineers are stationed on-site and put a "company-specific AI" into production at overwhelming speed. It is chosen by companies that say, "We lack internal resources, so we want to leave it to professionals and achieve business impact as fast as possible."

The other is "KUROKO," which we are releasing now.

"KUROKO" supports the development of in-house AI promotion staff and DX officers into "in-house FDEs," helping build a framework that allows AI utilization to continue within the company on an ongoing basis.

Companies have people who, over many years, have gained a deep understanding of business processes, commercial practices, internal rules, and frontline decision criteria. When such in-house talent with this "deep business understanding" acquires the ability to make technical decisions about AI agents and drive implementation, they evolve into "in-house FDEs" who form the core of AI transformation within the company.

"KUROKO" is a service that supports this evolution from behind the scenes.

■ Why Is "KUROKO" Needed Now? The Walls Faced by "AI Promotion Staff"

AI utilization has already begun at many companies.

The number of companies using general-purpose AI tools such as Claude Code and ChatGPT for business, establishing AI promotion staff positions, and working on PoCs and automation in the field is growing.

On the other hand, there are still major challenges in the speed of achieving tangible results.

AI promotion staff are required to make decisions like the following on a daily basis.

- Which business themes to start with - Which AI tools or models to use - How far to automate and where to leave human judgment - How to assess risks related to security, copyright, personal information, and governance - How to explain PoC results to management and connect them to production deployment

These are not merely questions of how to use AI tools; they are advanced decisions spanning business design, technical judgment, risk management, and results design.

As a result, it is not uncommon for AI promotion staff to struggle alone and stall at the PoC stage.

"KUROKO" stands behind such AI promotion staff, functioning as an expert team they can consult whenever they are unsure.

It supports everything from technical decisions, implementation reviews, governance checks, and results organization to designing explanations for management, ensuring AI utilization does not end as a "trial" but advances to "implementation and establishment."

■ Main Support Provided by "KUROKO"

Technical decisions: Support for selecting and establishing usage policies for the latest AI models, AI tools, RAG, AI agents, Claude Code, and more.

Implementation reviews: Professional review of prompts, business workflows, design policies, implementation quality, and safety.

Logic design support: Support for breaking down business processes, organizing requirements, and designing decision flows.

Governance compliance: Support for risk decisions concerning personal information, copyright, security, and internal rules.

Results organization: Support for KPIs, monthly reporting, and the creation of presentation materials for management.

UI/UX advice: Support for improving AI utilization screens and business workflows, and enhancing usability.

Through this support, "KUROKO" reduces the decision-making burden on in-house AI promotion staff and transforms AI utilization from individual skill into an internal framework that can be operated continuously.

■ "KUROKO" Service Packages

Depending on each company's situation, we offer first-month onboarding support and monthly continuing support plans.

Onboarding support (first month only): We take stock of existing initiatives, determine the role of the in-house FDE and priority themes, and design a 90-day roadmap (framework, tasks, and schedule). (Note: Companies whose foundations are already organized can start directly with the monthly plan.)

Standard plan: We provide twice-weekly regular support sessions (thinking-partner consultations), up to four hours per month of implementation quality reviews, and everyday consultation via chat tools. We shortcut R&D on technology trends and support always-optimal choices.

■ Toward an All-Around Partner That Addresses Every AI Challenge

Neurosphere offers two support approaches tailored to each company's AI implementation phase and organizational structure.

"Special Mission FDE": external professionals embed on-site to drive AI implementation at top speed.

And "KUROKO": developing internal talent into "in-house FDEs" to support self-sustaining AI initiatives.

By offering "Special Mission FDE" to companies that prioritize speed and "KUROKO" to those that prioritize in-house development and continuous organizational transformation, Neurosphere ensures AI adoption doesn't end at "introduction," but provides end-to-end support through "implementation, establishment, and results generation."

We welcome inquiries from companies whose AI initiatives have stalled, companies looking to move from PoC to production deployment, and companies seeking to develop in-house AI promotion staff.
