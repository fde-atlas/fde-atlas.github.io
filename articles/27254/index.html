Today's Take

Today, the three-piece puzzle of FDE industrialization came together:

  • Government brought FDE training into the enterprise technology center system: Shanghai launched an FDE general education class for enterprise technology centers, with leaders from over 30 national- and municipal-level enterprise technology centers attending.
  • Cloud vendors brought FDE into partner certification and joint delivery: Alibaba Cloud released its public cloud ecosystem partner FDE system, with the first batch of certified service providers signing on.
  • Overseas vendors turned field methodology into reusable delivery assets: Anthropic's Forward Deployed Engineer published a post clearly explaining "what counts as done and how to get into production."

FDE is evolving from a job title into a complete supply system composed of "internal enterprise backbone + external service partners + auditable delivery methodology."

Key Signals

Shanghai enterprise technology center FDE general education class launches: cultivating internal backbones who "understand business, know models, grasp security, and can deliver"

  • Source: Shanghai Municipal Government / Municipal Commission of Economy and Information Technology
  • The first session of Shanghai's enterprise technology center FDE general education class opened on September 22, with leaders from over 30 national- and municipal-level enterprise technology centers attending; the first day featured 5 courses and on-site teaching. The Municipal Commission of Economy and Information Technology's stated goal is to cultivate internal AI backbones who "understand business, know models, grasp security, and can deliver" for enterprises, while also connecting them with AI application service providers who know their industries and excel at delivery. The curriculum unfolds in three stages—business diagnosis, system assessment, and production deployment—emphasizing "identify the right problem → validate in small steps → deploy to production."
  • Why it matters: This extends FDE's institutional entry point from startups and cloud vendors to the R&D-to-production transition systems of traditional enterprises. What's more notable is its division of labor: enterprises cultivate internal "wall-breakers" internally while connecting with specialized service providers externally, with both sides collaborating under the same framework. Here, FDE does not replace enterprise teams but forms a dual-role structure with them. Note: What has been made public so far is only the class launch information and curriculum framework; the list of participating enterprises, completion rates, and real project outcomes have not been disclosed, so "over 30 participants" cannot be written as "over 30 enterprises completed AI deployment."

Alibaba Cloud releases public cloud ecosystem partner FDE system: first batch of certified service providers signs on

  • Source: Alibaba Cloud Developer Community; Beyondsoft repost; iSoftStone disclosure repost
  • At the 2026 Yunqi Conference, Alibaba Cloud and Qianwen Office released the public cloud ecosystem partner FDE system and held a signing ceremony for the first batch of FDE-certified service providers; partners including Dianming Yunsai, Beyondsoft, and iSoftStone announced their signings or certifications. The division of labor is: the cloud vendor provides models, compute, Bailian, and the application toolchain; partners handle scenario definition, ontology modeling, agent development, system integration, and ongoing operations. iSoftStone said it has assembled an FDE consultant resource pool of over 700 people, with its first batch of consultants and engineers passing Alibaba Cloud's FDE certification.
  • Why it matters: Following Tencent Cloud's FDE certification and the Kimi partner program, Alibaba Cloud has also turned FDE into a channel certification and joint delivery interface. The focus of industry competition is shifting from "who defines FDE" to "who owns the partners, certifications, real projects, reusable assets, and recurring operations revenue." For service providers, joining a vendor's system provides access to deal flow, but they must also preserve their ability to work across models and platforms and keep customer assets portable. Note: The 700-person resource pool and scenario samples are partner self-reported; the full certification standards, pass rates, and project-level business results have not been made public.

Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer: turning "correct" into a qualification certificate and launch accountability into a promotion policy

  • Source: Anthropic / Claude official
  • On September 23, in its "Notes from the Field" series, Anthropic's Forward Deployed Engineers summarized a six-step method for AI-driven modernization of mission-critical systems: define goals → establish qualification certificates → set launch promotion policies → prepare environment and organizational prerequisites → build and iterate agent workflows → validate end-to-end on a small scope, then scale. Here, the "qualification certificate" is not a diploma but a set of checkable conditions every change must pass, including tests, coverage, performance boundaries, independent adversarial review, replay/differential testing, pre-production observation, and security scanning; the "promotion policy" pre-agrees how deep human review is required for different risk levels and who bears approval responsibility.
  • Why it matters: Once agents accelerate output, the bottleneck shifts to review, approval, environments, accountability, and launch channels. The value of an FDE lies not in writing more code for customers, but in turning "what counts as done, why it should be trusted, how it gets into production, and who handles failures" into a system. This six-step method is not limited to code modernization; any high-risk agent delivery can apply it. Note: This is a vendor methodology post with no public customer samples or controlled experiments; it is best used as a methodological framework.

Additional signals: On September 24, the Shanghai Financial Regulatory Bureau released measures to promote AI adoption in banking and insurance, specifying directions such as intelligent marketing, credit, claims adjudication, operations, investment research, and risk control, while also requiring attention to data, models, business continuity, and risk management (original text). Jiangsu released the country's first dedicated "AI + e-commerce" policy, proposing to build 300 high-quality e-commerce datasets and 20 specialized parks by 2028 (original text).

Implications for FDE Practitioners

  • Redesign courses around dual roles: internal backbones handle business, data, accountability, and adoption; external FDEs handle diagnosis, engineering, integration, evals, and asset write-back. The completion standard should be delivering a real task package; classroom hours must not serve as the basis for completion.
  • Do due diligence before joining a vendor partner system: clarify item by item before signing—the certifying entity, project sources, deal allocation, on-site responsibilities, acceptance criteria, asset IP ownership, cloud revenue attribution, renewal/upsell revenue sharing, customer data, and exit mechanisms.
  • Upgrade delivery acceptance to a six-stage process: goals → qualification certificate → promotion policy → environment prerequisites → pilot → scale. Every deliverable must be traceable to versions, tests, usage environments, human sign-off, and incident records. Models and agents accelerate output; qualification certificates and promotion policies control "correctness" and "launch."
