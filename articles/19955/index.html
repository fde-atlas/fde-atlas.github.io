Live Q&A with Salesforce FDE Leader

maven.com · 2026-08-13 · 1 min read

Summary

Maven is hosting a free 60-minute live Q&A with Pavan Ghantasala, who leads Salesforce's FDE and customer-facing organizations. The session covers how Salesforce built its FDE function across iterations, where it sits relative to sales, PM, and engineering, which metrics prove FDE impact and which fail, and the traits Salesforce screens for when hiring, including interview questions and profiles that look strong but underperform. The framing: forward deployed engineering is how AI products reach production in enterprises, yet most teams are still improvising org design and metrics.

Key points

  • Live Q&A with Salesforce FDE leader Pavan Ghantasala, 60 minutes.
  • Covers building an FDE team from first hire to full function.
  • Discusses KPIs that tie deployments to revenue and product.
  • Explains hiring traits, interview questions, and failing profiles.
  • Frames FDE as essential for deploying AI products in enterprises.

Why this topic matters

Forward deployed engineering is how AI products reach production inside enterprises: engineers embedded with customers, shipping against messy data and shifting requirements. The function is new enough that most teams are improvising org design and metrics. Pavan Ghantasala leads FDE and customer facing org at Salesforce. This is an open Q&A on how the team built FDE, type of talent they prioritized and more.
