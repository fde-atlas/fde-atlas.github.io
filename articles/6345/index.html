Author, The AI Engineering Bootcamp Book

Author, LLMs in Production

+ Tyler Laughlin

The authors of The AI Engineering Bootcamp Book teamed up with the authors of LLMs in Production to produce this advanced, comprehensive, one-of-a-kind course spanning Q1 2027.

To compete with AI Forward Deployed Engineers at leading AI labs like Anthropic and OpenAI, you need to scope customer problems, build and test AI prototypes with users, deploy prototypes to existing production environments, and secure, operate, and improve production applications at scale that generate real ROI.

This course is for you if:

  • You truly believe that you can be a unicorn AI FDE, or
  • You're part of an FDE team of 2-3 people taking the course, and
  • You have direct support from your company or client to access users, engage with stakeholders, and drive one project from pilot to production in 10 weeks

This course is NOT for you if:

  • You do not work at a company, or
  • You work at a company, but they do not support you driving AI transformation

If you're too early in your AI career journey for this course, check out The AI Engineering Bootcamp as a first step.

The skills you need to succeed as an AI Forward Deployed Engineer in production environments.

  • Learn to ask the right questions and guide your organization through prioritization and problem scoping
  • Learn to move from MVP to PoC to production, and understand the right tools & techniques to get you there
  • Bring the fundamentally valuable token-minimization mindset to your company's leadership table
  • Learn to implement the right retrieval technique and when, from LLM Wikis to dense vector embedding RAG
  • Understand tradeoffs between tools, skills, MCPs, subagents, code mode, Universal Tool Calling Protocol (UTCP)
  • Learn to install guardrails at the right layer of your agentic systems
  • Run local OSS models aligned with your enterprise hardware
  • Bypass sensitive and private data restrictions with local synthetic data generation
  • Use best-practice tools to choose, host, and run local models
  • Understand how to answer the question "How do we know that this works and we can trust it?"
  • Go beyond simple vibe checks and evals with "What if?" scenarios that align directly with your business
  • Understand how to evaluate your agents systematically, no matter how they have been structured
  • Learn to enhance your own prompt injections through well-known prompt optimization techniques
  • Understand how Hugging Face got reward-hacked by OpenAI; learn advanced techniques like context bombs
  • Understand enterprise threats that cannot be solved with root-level access
  • Learn to be guided by wherever the bottleneck is in product development, not by what you like doing better
  • Understand the importance of removing features rather than adding features as you scale
  • Build solutions with performance, scalability, and security that are enough for your current user base
