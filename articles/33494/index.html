Adobe FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer): Role, Skills & Prep

FDE India · 2026-11-08 · 8 min read

Summary

This article profiles Adobe's Forward Deployed Engineer hiring in India, centred on a Sr FDE role in Noida (requisition R172762) within a customer pod for Adobe Experience Platform and Apps. It requires 10+ years in solution architecture, engineering or technical consulting, plus CDP, identity resolution and workflow orchestration experience (Temporal, AWS Step Functions). Duties include reference architectures, fixing production issues across data ingestion and identity resolution, solutions spanning AEP, AJO and Marketo, and AI-assisted prototyping with Claude Code, Cursor and Copilot. Adobe posted no India salary; its US Lead FDE role at Frame.io listed $159,200–$301,600. The Noida role showed as filled in November 2026, but an FDE Manager listing suggests the team may grow. The article closes with prep advice: learn XDM and Real-Time CDP, build an end-to-end customer-data demo, and prepare regulated-industry scenarios.

Adobe hires Forward Deployed Engineers in India to work inside its biggest customers’ marketing and data stacks, getting Adobe Experience Platform (AEP) and its apps running in production. The clearest India listing is a Sr Forward Deployed Engineer role in Noida (requisition R172762). It sits in a customer pod for Experience Platform and Apps and asks for 10+ years of experience. Adobe’s careers page showed it as filled in November 2026, but it shows the kind of FDE Adobe wants in India.

What the FDE does at Adobe

The Noida listing, mirrored on Freehire and 4dayweek, places the FDE in a multi-functional Customer Pod for Adobe Experience Platform and Apps. In its own words, “You’ll work directly with Adobe’s strategic customers.” The work it describes:

  • Customer architecture. Understand each customer’s systems, workflows, security needs and goals. Then turn their problems into scalable designs, reference architectures and prototypes.
  • Production fixes across the data path. Investigate platform issues in data ingestion, identity resolution, journey orchestration and audience activation.
  • Solutions across Adobe’s apps. Design and validate enterprise solutions spanning AEP, Adobe Journey Optimizer (AJO), Marketo and related systems.
  • Workshops and deep-dives. Lead architecture sessions with the customer’s technical teams.
  • Fast, AI-assisted prototyping. The listing names Claude Code, Cursor and GitHub Copilot.
  • Field feedback. Carry what customers need back to Adobe’s product and engineering teams, and own the technical relationship with assigned accounts.

One mirror, Workopia, labels the same Noida role under “Adobe Firefly”, while the other mirrors describe Experience Platform. Its skills list (CDP, identity resolution, Adobe Experience Platform) points to the Experience Cloud side, so prepare for marketing and customer-data work rather than creative tools.

Why Adobe needs FDEs now

Adobe made AI agents for Experience Platform generally available in September 2025, according to a Business Wire release. They included an Audience Agent, Journey Agent, Data Insights Agent and Site Optimization Agent, run by an AEP Agent Orchestrator. The release says over 70% of AEP customers already use Adobe’s AI Assistant. Getting agents like these to work on a large company’s messy customer data is the kind of job forward-deployed teams are built for.

Adobe also has an FDE team in the US. A Lead Forward Deployed Engineer role, posted in July 2026 for San Jose, described “the newly formed Forward Deployment Engineering (FDE) team” within Adobe’s Digital Experience organisation. Instahyre has also carried a “Forward Deployed Engineering Manager” listing for Adobe in Bangalore and Noida, so the India team may extend beyond a single role. What this means for you:

  • This is a senior, data-heavy FDE role. It suits engineers who have shipped customer data platforms, marketing tech or identity systems for large enterprises, rather than pure LLM application builders.
  • Adobe product knowledge is a plus, not a gate. AEP, AJO and Marketo are listed as helpful. A strong CDP background elsewhere (Segment, Salesforce Data Cloud, an in-house platform) can carry you.
  • Regulated-industry work counts. Banks, insurers and hospitals are named, which fits India’s large BFSI technology base.

How to prepare

  • Learn Adobe Experience Platform’s data model. Work through Adobe’s free Experience League material on XDM schemas, datasets, identity graphs and Real-Time CDP. Be able to explain how a customer profile is stitched together from web, app and CRM data.
  • Build one end-to-end customer-data demo. Ingest sample events, resolve identities across two sources, build an audience and trigger a journey. Use AEP if you have access, or open-source tools if not. Then write a one-page architecture note, since the job centres on reference architectures.
  • Show workflow orchestration. Build a small, reliable pipeline in Temporal or AWS Step Functions with retries and monitoring. Both are named in the listing.
  • Prepare a regulated-industry story. Explain how you would roll out a CDP or AI agent for a bank: consent, data residency, PII handling and audit trails. Our FDE interview questions guide has customer-scenario examples.
  • Practise prototyping with AI tools and set alerts. Be ready to build a working prototype live with Cursor, Copilot or Claude Code. Set a job alert for “forward deployed” on Adobe’s careers site, and track Noida and other NCR employers on our Delhi NCR FDE jobs page.
