Forward Deployed Engineering — From Signal to Steady State

Cloud Intelligence™ · 2026-04-16 · 3 min read

Summary

This DoiT marketing page describes its Forward Deployed Engineering model: senior cloud architects embed directly with customer teams to implement, test, and ship changes, rather than just raising alerts or delivering consulting reports. The service spans six disciplines—reliability, security and compliance, Kubernetes optimization, cost and FinOps, incident response, and AI/LLM workload operations—claiming fixes reach production rather than remaining recommendations. It is bundled with a Customer Success Manager and cloud procurement support for AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure.

Key points

  • DoiT's FDEs are senior cloud architects embedded in customer teams.
  • The pitch is shipping fixes in production, not just alerts.
  • Six disciplines span reliability, security, K8s, cost, incidents, and AI ops.
  • GenAI ops cover GPU efficiency, scaling, and FinOps for AI.
  • Includes a Customer Success Manager and cloud procurement support.

Beyond alerting

Cut noise. Ship fixes

We don't send alerts. We help you ship fixes. We cut through noise, map issues to business impact, and work side-by-side with your team to roll out changes at scale.

  • Workload Reliability

    Patterns that keep services up under pressure. HA, failure domains, and graceful degradation built in.

  • Security & Compliance

    Least-privilege, sane defaults, and continuous hygiene. Posture reviews with prioritized fix lists and owners.

  • Kubernetes Optimization

    Healthy clusters, predictable spend. Advanced requests/limits, node pools, autoscalers, and bin-packing.

  • Efficiency Automation

    Policy-driven actions for scheduling, snapshots, TTLs, and tag hygiene. Remove toil, not reliability.

  • Governance & Visibility

    One place to see health, cost, and risk. Clear ownership wired to teams and services.

  • FinOps Alignment

    Operations that respect budgets. Instance types, storage classes, and data paths picked with SLOs and cost in mind.

Real work, not recommendations

Our FDE owns outcomes across six disciplines - embedded with your team, shipping alongside your engineers

  • AI and LLM Optimization

    Fewer tokens, smaller models, right-sized GPUs. Your FDE audits inference pipelines for waste. Same results, fraction of the spend.

  • Cost Optimization

    Finds savings and implements them the same week. Your FDE makes the changes, monitors the impact, and moves to the next one.

  • Incident Response

    Diagnoses root causes without a discovery call. When production breaks, your FDE is already on it. Fix first, post-mortem after.

  • K8s Tuning

    Right-sizes pods, limits, and autoscaling policies based on actual utilization. Less overhead, same reliability, every cluster.

  • Migration Support

    Plans moves and estimates cost impact before you commit. Your FDE handles the complexity across regions, providers, or architectures.

  • Reliability & Performance

    Designs for uptime under real load. Right-sizes and auto-scales for real-world demand, seasons, and traffic spikes. No guesswork.
