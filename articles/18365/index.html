Training the New Key Players of the AI Era: "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" — Green Waters Co., Ltd. Launches Its "AI Business & FDE Training Program"

Original title: AI時代の新たな主役「FDE（Forward Deployed Engineer）」を育成。株式会社グリーンウォーターズ、「AIビジネス・FDE養成プログラム」を提供開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-09-01 · 7 min read

Summary

Japanese education company Green Waters (operator of the GSchool platform) will launch an online training program on September 1, 2026 to cultivate Forward Deployed Engineers among working professionals. The three-month program teaches AI direction, programming and data analysis with Claude Code, and consulting-style problem solving, followed by five optional specialization tracks. Citing METI research that 73% of skills needed by 2030 will be new, the company argues tool-specific skills quickly become obsolete, so it emphasizes durable, self-driven learning habits. A companion platform, GWork, visualizes learners' skills and connects them to employers. A Nikkei article cited notes FDEs in the US can earn around 32 million yen annually.

Key points

  • Online FDE training program launches September 1, 2026, lasting three months.
  • Core curriculum covers AI direction, Claude Code, data analysis, and hypothesis-driven consulting skills.
  • Five specialization tracks follow the core: consulting, data analysis, AI literacy, AI-driven development, infrastructure/security.
  • Program stresses lasting self-learning habits over tool-specific skills that quickly date.
  • GWork platform visualizes skills and links learners to business sites.

Master the essential skills of AI × business in just three months. Hone "universal problem-solving abilities" that are not swayed by technological change, and become a professional who can drive business operations with AI.

Green Waters Co., Ltd. (Head office: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director: Hiroyuki Minakami; hereinafter "the Company"), which operates the educational platform "GSchool," will begin offering "AI Business & FDE Training Program," an online learning program aimed primarily at working professionals to acquire skills for solving business challenges through AI, starting Tuesday, September 1, 2026.

Through its educational platform "GSchool," the Company has provided IT and AI learning services to many students and working professionals. This time, we are significantly updating our services from the new angle of the FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) and launching them as a new program. Through this update, we will further strengthen our support for individual career development in the AI era.

■ Background of the Service Launch: What Are the Truly Necessary "Universal Skills" in an Era of Continuously Evolving AI

In recent years, the evolution and spread of generative AI have reached an astonishing pace. According to a survey by the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry, it is estimated that "73% of the skills required by 2030 will be replaced by new skills due to the impact of generative AI" (*1), making the acquisition of generative AI skills unavoidable for business professionals. However, since major AI models undergo major updates every three to six months (*2), simply learning prompt techniques or operational skills quickly becomes obsolete. In response to the voices of business professionals who say, "I want to become someone who can provide value with AI as a partner, without being swayed by technological change," the Company has launched the "AI Business & FDE Training Program," which cultivates "universal self-driven capability" that goes beyond superficial technical skills.

■ Thoroughly Learn the Skills of the New AI-Era Profession: "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)"

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a role that goes deep into the front lines of business, directs AI, and proactively leads problem-solving. Although this profession and concept was established in the United States, with the spread of generative AI it is rapidly gaining attention in Japan as "the business professional with the highest market value in the AI era" (*3). To date, the Company has supported AI adoption through corporate AX training and has provided more than 100 training programs for everyone from engineers to non-IT professionals, supporting the careers of many individuals. Precisely because the Company is deeply familiar with both practical challenges in the field and learners' needs, we were able to develop a program that enables the acquisition of the skills truly in demand in the workplace today. This program offers working professionals, whose business experience is a strength, practical learning to take the next step and grow into "cutting-edge AI × business professionals (FDEs)."

■ Features of the "AI Business & FDE Training Program"

Rather than simply providing AI learning content, we thoroughly cultivate "reproducible self-driven capability" that enables learners to continue learning autonomously after completion and keep delivering results in the business field.

① Thoroughly master AI-driven learning itself

First, participants thoroughly master "effective learning methods for the AI era" itself. By then proceeding through the subsequent learning process in an AI-driven manner, learning efficiency and speed are dramatically increased.

② Building autonomous learning habits through "study groups"

Instead of passive e-learning, we provide hands-on practice in study groups and an environment where participants can ask instructors questions at any time. Through the experience of resolving their own "unknowns," participants establish habits of independent study.

③ "Practical problem-solving" that ensures reproducibility in the field

Intermediate assignments simulating real work are placed in each unit. By working hands-on along problem-solving frameworks, participants acquire the skills to autonomously drive projects even after returning to the workplace.

■ Overview of the Systematized Curriculum: 3 Months of Foundation Building + 5 Selectable Specialized Areas

We adopt a two-step structure that allows participants to progressively master the essence of business and AI and expand their applied skills.

【STEP 1】"3-Month Core Curriculum" to Establish Core Skills as an FDE

- Month 1: Learn IT fundamentals, security, and AI direction to establish the foundations for mastering AI - Month 2: Learn the basics of programming/data analysis and techniques for using Claude Code, acquiring the skills to drive business improvement with AI - Month 3: Learn hypothesis thinking and problem identification to solve field challenges, acquiring business consulting skills

【STEP 2】"5 Specialized/Enhancement Curricula" to Further Increase Market Value

After building core skills, participants can choose from 5 specialized areas (business consulting, data analysis, AI literacy specialization, AI-driven programming development, and infrastructure/security) to deepen their expertise according to their career vision and their company's business characteristics.

■ "GWork": A Growth Ecosystem Connecting Learning and Career

Participants are also provided with "GWork," a platform that links learning with career development.

With the following features, "GWork" strongly supports participants' career development.

- Visualization of skills and proof of ability

Learning history and acquired skill levels are visualized as data, allowing participants to objectively promote and prove their abilities both inside and outside their organization.

- Continuous learning and connection to the workplace

Classes and events covering the latest industry trends are held regularly, boosting motivation for continuous learning while connecting participants with the business field.

Through the provision of "GWork," we will create an environment where professionals who have mastered the latest AI and business skills can thrive at companies, supporting a future in which individuals and companies grow together.
