ElevenLabs Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Interview Guide | Sample Questions (2026)

Aced (formerly Exponent) · 2026-02-05 · 9 min read

Summary

This Aced guide details ElevenLabs' forward deployed engineer interview loop: recruiter screen, a 60-minute CodeSignal assessment, live coding in a shared Google Doc, a customer case study diagrammed on Excalidraw, and a founder interview with the CTO. The full loop runs 4-5 weeks, or about 2 weeks for referrals. ElevenLabs screens hardest for individual ownership, threading behavioral questions through nearly every round instead of a dedicated behavioral stage. Case study rounds reward customer discovery before solutioning. The guide also advises clarifying role scope and location early and preparing to discuss AI tool usage.

Key points

  • Five-stage loop lasting 4-5 weeks; referrals finish in ~2 weeks.
  • Strongest hiring signal is individual, end-to-end project ownership.
  • Behavioral questions appear in nearly every round, with no dedicated stage.
  • Case study tests customer discovery before proposing solutions.
  • Coding challenges are general pattern-based Python, not product-specific.

ElevenLabs screens forward deployed engineers for individual builders who can embed with customers, a sharper filter than the loops at larger AI labs that keep coding and customer work in separate rounds. The strongest signal is individual ownership, and values and behavioral questions surface inside nearly every round, with no dedicated behavioral stage. This guide breaks down each stage of the ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer interview process, what interviewers look for, and how to prepare with real example questions, tips, and resources.

The ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer interview process

The ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer interview treats customer problem-solving as seriously as technical skill, splitting the technical evaluation across an automated assessment and a live coding round before a customer case study.

Here's what the interview process can look like:

  • Recruiter screen: A 30-minute call covering your background, past projects, and interest in the role
  • Coding assessment: An automated, timed 60-minute set of pattern-based coding challenges on CodeSignal
  • Coding interview: Live coding in a shared Google Doc, opening with a short behavioral discussion
  • Case study: A conversational customer scenario diagrammed on Excalidraw, opening with a behavioral discussion
  • Founder interview: A high-level conversation with the CTO or a senior technical leader on past projects and role fit

The full loop typically runs about 4-5 weeks, though it can compress to roughly 2 weeks for referred candidates. Most interviews are remote, though deployed engineers are sometimes expected to work from a specific office.

Recruiter screen

The ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer recruiter screen is a brief call that examines your background, past projects, and motivation for the role. It goes deeper than a typical recruiter screen, centering on your autonomy and your ability to solve challenges on your own.

Expect the interviewer to ask you to walk through past projects in detail to gauge your skills fit. Lead with examples that combine customer-facing work with hands-on engineering, since the role involves constant interaction with ElevenLabs' customers.

Interviewers look for:

  • Individual ownership: Whether you drove past projects yourself or leaned on a team's structure
  • Customer-facing technical work: Examples that pair direct customer interaction with hands-on engineering
  • Motivation for the role: Why forward deployed engineering at ElevenLabs fits your trajectory
  • Skills fit: Whether your background maps to Python, API integration, and customer solutioning

  • Tell me about a project you worked on.
  • Why do you want to work at ElevenLabs?

Coding assessment

The ElevenLabs FDE coding assessment is an automated, timed 60-minute set of pattern-based coding challenges delivered through CodeSignal. It's the only round without a behavioral discussion, since you complete it on your own.

The challenges center on general problem-solving and aren't tied to ElevenLabs' products. Expect a small set of medium-difficulty questions, and because you work alone, you can run tests to confirm your solutions before you submit them.

Interviewers look for:

  • Pattern-based coding fluency: How efficiently you work through the challenges
  • Correctness: Whether your solutions pass the test cases you can run before submitting
  • Code clarity: How readable and well-structured your solutions are
  • Time management: Whether you complete multiple challenges within the 60-minute window

Coding interview

The ElevenLabs FDE coding interview is a live session in a shared Google Doc that opens with a short behavioral discussion before a hands-on coding task. The behavioral portion covers a topic like a past conflict or a challenging project.

The coding challenge is more involved than the assessment, and it won't necessarily relate to ElevenLabs' products. Expect to write Python functions that account for several dimensions at once, such as a file-system permissions challenge that factors in hierarchy and the timestamps of permission changes.

Interviewers look for:

  • Live coding ability: How you write and reason through code in a shared document
  • Handling complexity: Whether you account for interacting constraints and edge cases
  • Communication: How clearly you explain your approach as you work
  • Behavioral signal: How you describe a past conflict or challenging project in the opening discussion

Case study

The ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer case study is a mostly conversational round where you work through a customer scenario and diagram your solution on Excalidraw. It opens with a brief behavioral discussion, such as cross-functional work, leadership, or conflict resolution.

You're given a scenario, often a company looking to automate an internal or customer-facing process, and you can ask about the customer's specific needs. The interviewer wants to see you lead with customer discovery before proposing a solution.

Interviewers look for:

  • Customer empathy: How quickly you identify the customer's pain points and motivations
  • Discovery questions: Whether you ask how the customer solves the challenge today and what frustrates them most
  • Practical solutioning: Whether you propose a grounded, workable solution the customer could adopt
  • Technical judgment: How you fold technical considerations into a customer-facing recommendation
  • Communication: How clearly you structure the conversation and your diagram

Founder interview

The ElevenLabs FDE founder interview is a high-level conversation with the CTO or a senior technical leader focused on your past projects, technical depth, and fit for the role. It mirrors the recruiter screen's focus on autonomy and results in a flat, fast-moving organization.

Highlight projects you led or completed independently, since ElevenLabs favors self-directed builders. This round can feel loosely defined, and the role's scope may still be taking shape, so use it to ask how ElevenLabs deploys its engineers and how the work splits with solutions architects.

Interviewers look for:

  • Individual track record: Whether you led or independently shipped the projects you describe
  • Fit for a flat, fast organization: How you operate without heavy process or defined structure
  • Technical depth: How you discuss the engineering behind your past work
  • Role curiosity: The questions you ask about how ElevenLabs uses forward deployed engineers

How to prepare for the ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer interview

  • Lead with individual ownership: Choose projects you drove yourself, and be explicit about what you personally built and decided.
  • Prepare customer-solutioning stories: Pair technical work with direct customer interaction, and show how you turned a manual workflow into a working solution.
  • Practice discovery for the case study: Open with how the customer handles the challenge today and what frustrates them most, then propose something they could adopt.
  • Sharpen pattern-based coding: Work through pattern-based coding challenges and practice Python functions that handle hierarchy, timestamps, and edge cases.
  • Expect behavioral questions everywhere: ElevenLabs threads values and behavioral discussion through most rounds, so have conflict and leadership examples ready.
  • Confirm role scope and location early: The role can be loosely defined and hiring location can shift late, so clarify scope, office expectations, and visa considerations up front.
  • Be ready to discuss how you use AI tools: ElevenLabs works AI-first, and some candidates report interview discussion of your AI workflow, so be prepared to explain how you use AI in your engineering process.
  • Run a mock interview: Practice the full loop with peer and AI mock interviews to get feedback before your real interview. For targeted help on the case study or founder round, work with an expert coach.

Common mistakes to avoid in the ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer interview

  • Overpreparing for a dedicated behavioral or culture round, which ElevenLabs doesn't run as a separate stage
  • Focusing only on ElevenLabs' AI audio products, when the coding and case challenges are usually more general
  • Choosing examples built on heavy process or shared credit, when ElevenLabs screens for end-to-end ownership
  • Jumping straight to a solution in the case study before understanding the customer's pain points
  • Treating the founder interview as a formality, when it examines technical depth and role fit
  • Assuming the timeline matches a larger company's, when ElevenLabs moves fast and reuses questions

About the ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer role

ElevenLabs forward deployed engineers work directly with enterprise customers to deploy and customize the company's AI audio and agent products in production. The role sits between engineering and customer work, and the day-to-day varies with the customer you support.

ElevenLabs FDEs typically:

  • Collaborate daily with customers' engineers and executives to get the most from ElevenLabs' tools
  • Build customer- and industry-specific solutions using Python and API integrations
  • Translate customer needs into technical proposals and working prototypes
  • Bring field insights back to help shape ElevenLabs' product roadmap

ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer experience and education requirements

ElevenLabs forward deployed engineers need strong Python skills, software architecture knowledge, API integration experience, and the customer-facing judgment to deploy solutions in production.

ElevenLabs doesn't set official experience or degree requirements for the role, and it's open to candidates from mid- to staff-level.

Customer experience matters, though ElevenLabs notes that working with customers through student clubs or side projects can count. Past projects, including academic ones, are fair game in interviews.

FAQs about the ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer interview

How long is the ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer interview process?

The ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer interview process typically takes about 4-5 weeks from first call to decision. Referred candidates sometimes move through in about 2 weeks, and some loops run closer to 6 weeks.

Do you need AI experience to work at ElevenLabs as a forward deployed engineer?

You don't need formal AI work experience to interview for an ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer role, but you should understand audio AI, API integration, and how AI models work at a working level. Personal projects, hackathons, and student work can demonstrate this.

Is the ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer role remote?

ElevenLabs is a remote-first company, and the forward deployed engineer role can be based globally, though some openings prefer a specific location like San Francisco for close work with customers and the engineering team. Hiring location can shift late in the process, so confirm office and visa expectations early.

How much does an ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer make?

ElevenLabs doesn't publish FDE compensation by level, but according to Levels.fyi, the median total compensation across ElevenLabs is around $128,000 as of mid-2026, with senior software engineering packages reaching significantly higher.

The role is open to mid- to staff-level candidates and pairs a base salary with stock options, so equity is the main variable in most packages. ElevenLabs' valuation has climbed quickly, reaching $11 billion in its February 2026 funding round, and the company discusses specific numbers during the initial call.

Is the ElevenLabs Deployment Strategist or Forward Deployed Creative role the same as forward deployed engineer?

ElevenLabs lists Forward Deployed Engineer, Deployment Strategist, and Forward Deployed Creative as separate roles. Forward Deployed Engineer is their technical, customer-facing engineering position; Deployment Strategist is a go-to-market role that owns commercial deals and pricing; Forward Deployed Creative focuses on creative production with ElevenLabs' tools.

ElevenLabs also posts related titles like Forward Deployed Engineer - Strategist and Deployment Strategist Lead, which lean toward go-to-market and team leadership, so confirm the exact role and function with your recruiter.
