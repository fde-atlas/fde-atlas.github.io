Senior Forward Deployed Developer, Generative AI, Google Cloud at Google — FDE Job Description

How to Become an FDE — Interview Kickstart · 2026-06-06 · 2 min read

Summary

This page archives a since-closed Google Cloud job posting for a Senior Forward Deployed Developer focused on Generative AI, based in Toronto or Montreal. The role targeted senior individual contributors with 5+ years of experience and a pay band of $182K–$187K. Google framed the position as an embedded builder who codes, debugs, and ships bespoke agentic solutions inside customer environments, tackling production blockers like integration complexity, data readiness, and state management rather than doing advisory work. The posting was visible in June 2026 and removed by early July. The page also lists other Google FDE-adjacent openings in Singapore, India, the Nordics, and New York, positioning the archive as one data point among broader market analysis of FDE roles.

Key points

  • Google Cloud hired a Senior GenAI Forward Deployed Developer in Canada.
  • The role required 5+ years of experience and paid $182K–$187K.
  • It emphasized hands-on coding and shipping agentic solutions at customer sites.
  • Key challenges cited: integration complexity, data readiness, state management.

From the posting

As a GenAI Forward Deployed Developer at Google Cloud, you are an embedded builder who bridges the gap between frontier AI products and production-grade reality within customers. Unlike traditional advisory roles, you function as an "innovator-builder," moving beyond high-level architecture to code, debug, and jointly ship bespoke agentic solutions directly within the customer’s environment. This role is designed for high-agency developers with a founder’s mindset. You will address blockers to production including solving the integration complexities, data readiness issues, and state-management challenges that prevent AI from reaching enterprise-grade maturity. …
