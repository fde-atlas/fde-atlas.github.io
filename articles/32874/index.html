Solutions Engineer vs Forward Deployed Engineer Salary in India

FDE India · 2026-11-06 · 9 min read

Summary

Using Levels.fyi, AmbitionBox and Second Talent data, this guide compares solutions engineer and FDE pay in India. Median FDE total pay is about ₹25.4 LPA versus ₹17.5 LPA for sales engineers, a roughly 45% gap that widens at higher percentiles, though samples are small (11 and 26 reports). Employer tier matters as much as title: a solutions engineer at VMware can out-earn an FDE at an IT services firm. The premium reflects ownership of production systems, not pre-sales work. Switching pays off most for solutions engineers with 3–8 years of experience who already write production code.

Key points

  • Levels.fyi medians: FDE ~₹25.4 LPA vs sales engineer ~₹17.5 LPA, about 45% higher.
  • 90th percentile: FDE ~₹56 LPA vs ₹40.9 LPA, but based on only 11 and 26 reports.
  • Employer matters more than title: Zoho solutions engineers earn ₹6.4–11.8 LPA, VMware ₹20.8–36.8 LPA.
  • The FDE premium comes from owning running production systems, not demos.
  • Switching makes most sense for 3–8 year solutions engineers who can already ship production code.

Forward Deployed Engineers in India generally earn more than solutions engineers. On Levels.fyi’s self-reported India data, the median FDE takes home about ₹25.4 LPA in total pay, against about ₹17.5 LPA for a sales engineer (the category Levels.fyi uses for solutions engineering). The gap is real but uneven: it depends heavily on who employs you, and a solutions engineer at a global product company can out-earn an FDE at an IT services firm.

This guide puts the numbers side by side by experience and employer, explains where the gap comes from, and shows when a switch makes financial sense.

Solutions engineer vs FDE salary: the headline numbers

The cleanest like-for-like comparison comes from Levels.fyi, because it reports both roles the same way: total compensation (base plus annualised stock plus bonus), self-reported and, the site says, checked against offer letters.

Measure Sales / solutions engineer (India) Forward Deployed Engineer (India)
Median total pay ~₹17.5 LPA ~₹25.4 LPA
25th percentile ~₹10.0 LPA ~₹16.2 LPA
75th percentile ~₹30.0 LPA ~₹46.8 LPA
90th percentile ~₹40.9 LPA ~₹56.0 LPA
Reports 26 (updated 13 Oct 2026) 11 (updated 4 Nov 2026)

Source: Levels.fyi Sales Engineer India and Levels.fyi Forward Deployed Engineer India.

At the median, FDEs are about 45% ahead. At the 75th and 90th percentiles the gap in rupees grows to ₹15–17 LPA. Treat these as directional: 11 and 26 reports are small samples, and self-reported data may under-represent IT services firms.

FDEs lead at every point of the distribution, and the gap widens at the top. Levels.fyi, October–November 2026.

City matters for solutions engineers too. Levels.fyi’s sales engineer medians range from about ₹49.4 LPA in Delhi and ₹22.4 LPA in Bengaluru down to about ₹8.1 LPA in Chennai, so a Delhi NCR pre-sales role can already sit above the FDE national median.

Pay by experience

Neither role has a large, experience-split dataset in India yet, so the best view comes from combining two sources: AmbitionBox’s self-reported solution engineer salaries, and Second Talent’s FDE rate card.

Experience Solution engineer (AmbitionBox) FDE (Second Talent rate card)
0–2 years ₹2.5–12 LPA (1 yr) · ₹2.9–15 LPA (2 yrs) ₹6–14 LPA
3–6 years ₹3.5–18 LPA (3 yrs) · ₹4–25 LPA (5 yrs) ₹14–28 LPA
Senior ₹5.1–30.5 LPA (senior solution engineer, 7 yrs) ₹28–65 LPA (6–10 yrs)
Lead / principal Highest reported: ₹68.9 LPA (senior solution engineer) ₹55–100 LPA (10+ yrs)

AmbitionBox Solution Engineer (about 3,900 salaries, updated March 2025) and Senior Solution Engineer (about 1,700 salaries, March 2025); Second Talent fixed-pay bands, September 2026.

Two things stand out. First, the floor of the FDE band is much higher. Solution engineer ranges start at ₹2.5–4 LPA because the title covers support-heavy and implementation jobs at IT services firms, while FDE roles are concentrated at product companies, GCCs and AI startups. Second, the ceiling diverges after about six years: Second Talent’s senior FDE band runs to ₹65 LPA, while AmbitionBox’s senior solution engineer ranges mostly top out around ₹30 LPA.

The bands overlap early in a career; after six years the FDE band pulls well clear. AmbitionBox (2025) and Second Talent (Sep 2026).

One caution: the AmbitionBox data is from 2025 and the FDE band is a vendor rate card, not a salary survey. Second Talent says its FDE bands are drawn from its senior AI, platform and full-stack engineer pool. Other published FDE bands run higher: Build Fast with AI, citing OYC Labs, puts FDEs at ₹18–28 LPA at 0–2 years and ₹28–55 LPA at 3–6 years. Employer decides more than title

For solutions engineers, the company you work for moves pay more than years of experience. AmbitionBox’s company pages show how wide the spread is:

A solutions engineer at a global platform company already earns in the mid-level FDE band. AmbitionBox company salary pages, 2025; samples are small (3 to 53 salaries per company).

The same is true for FDEs. Second Talent says GCCs and US product companies sit at the top of every band and domestic services firms at the bottom, and that Bengaluru pays 15–20% above the national average. Build Fast with AI makes the same point: “Forward Deployed Engineer” is a title, not a level, so the employer tier sets the band.

So the honest comparison is within a tier. A solutions engineer at Google (₹19.6–29.3 LPA for 2–6 years on AmbitionBox, from only six salaries) is not far behind the ₹14–28 LPA FDE band for 3–6 years. A solution engineer at an IT services firm moving into an FDE role at a GCC or AI startup could see a much bigger jump.

Why the FDE premium exists

The roles overlap: both sit between the customer and the product, run discovery calls and build things to prove value. Indian employers even advertise FDE-style work under solutions engineer titles, Second Talent notes. The pay gap comes from what each role is accountable for.

Solutions engineer Forward Deployed Engineer
Main phase Before the sale After the sale, through go-live and beyond
Main output Demos, proofs of concept, technical answers for deals Production code inside the customer’s systems
Measured on Deals won, technical wins Systems live, adoption, outcomes delivered
Coding depth Scripts and prototypes Full engineering: tests, integration, monitoring, on-call fixes
Travel Customer meetings Often embedded on-site for weeks

Scaler describes the FDE as a rare mix of strong engineering and client-facing ability, and says the “scarcity drives compensation”. It adds that FDE pay at MNCs and product companies sits above the standard software engineer band because of client-facing scope, travel and delivery responsibility. Build Fast with AI estimates the FDE premium over a regular SDE in India at around 20–40%, though it doesn’t give a source for that figure.

Demand adds to it. Indeed’s India data, as reported in October 2026 and summarised by Masai School, showed FDE postings up nearly 19-fold between June 2025 and June 2026. Solutions engineering is a mature role with a large talent pool; FDE is new and short of experienced people.

When switching makes financial sense

Switching is worth it on money alone in some cases and not in others.

It usually pays off if:

  • You have 3–8 years of experience. This is where the bands diverge most: ₹4–25 LPA for a 5-year solution engineer against ₹14–28 LPA for a 3–6 year FDE, and the senior FDE band runs to ₹65 LPA.
  • You are at an IT services firm or a mid-tier software company where solution engineer pay is at the lower end of the range.
  • You already write real code, beyond demo scripts, and can show it.

It may not pay off if:

  • You are already a solutions engineer at a global platform company on ₹25–35 LPA. A move to a services-firm FDE role could be a pay cut.
  • A large part of your pay is variable or tied to deals. Check how the FDE offer’s variable component compares, and compare total pay, not base salary.
  • You don’t want to be on-site often. FDE roles carry more travel and post-launch responsibility.

How to move from solutions engineer to FDE: step by step

  • Benchmark your current total pay. Add base, variable, stock and bonus. Compare it with the FDE band for your experience and target employer tier in our salary hub, not with the national median.
  • Close the production-code gap. Take one of your proofs of concept and finish it properly: tests, error handling, logging, a CI/CD pipeline and deployment. This is the single biggest difference hiring managers look for. Our FDE skills guide lists the stack Indian listings ask for.
  • Ship one LLM or agent feature. AI deployment is now central to the role: AWS, for example, launched a $1 billion Forward Deployed Engineering unit for agentic AI in June 2026, according to SiliconANGLE. Build a retrieval or agent workflow on real data, measure accuracy, latency and cost, and write up what broke.
  • Reframe your customer experience as delivery. In your CV and interviews, lead with what went live and what changed for the customer, not with deals supported. Discovery and stakeholder handling are strengths most software engineers applying for FDE roles lack.
  • Target the right employers. GCCs, US product companies and funded AI startups pay at the top of FDE bands. Check our FDE career ladder to pitch yourself at the right level.
  • Negotiate on total pay. If you’re giving up commission, ask for a higher base or a sign-on bonus to cover the first year.

Frequently asked questions

Do Forward Deployed Engineers earn more than solutions engineers in India?

On the data available, usually yes. Levels.fyi's India figures put median FDE total pay at about ₹25.4 LPA (11 reports, November 2026) against about ₹17.5 LPA for sales engineers (26 reports, October 2026). The samples are small, and top solutions engineers at product companies can match mid-level FDE pay.

What is the salary of a solutions engineer in India?

AmbitionBox puts solution engineers at ₹3–20 LPA, based on about 3,900 salaries (updated March 2025), with ranges of ₹2.5–12 LPA at 1 year and ₹4–25 LPA at 5 years. Pay at global product companies is much higher: AmbitionBox shows Google solution engineers at ₹19.6–29.3 LPA for 2–6 years.

Why do FDEs get paid more than solutions engineers?

FDEs combine strong engineering with client-facing work and own delivery in production, a mix that is scarce, according to Scaler. Solutions engineers mostly work before the sale, on demos and proofs of concept, while FDEs ship and maintain the system after it.

Should I switch from solutions engineer to FDE for the money?

It makes most sense if you are 3–8 years in, already code beyond demos and are at a services or mid-tier employer. If you are already at a global product company, the cash gain is smaller and you are trading pre-sales work for delivery responsibility and more travel.
