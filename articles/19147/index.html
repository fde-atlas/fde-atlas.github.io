Buyer's guide5 questions

What does AI consulting for a law firm actually include?

On the pages read, four shapes: governance and enablement (Clear Guidance Partners, Affinity, PGH Networks), building and connecting systems (PaperStreet, Lumeor Studio, Helm360), a full strategy to build programme for large firms (Epiq, Kraft Kennedy, Morae), and adoption training (Traveling Coaches). Pick the shape your problem needs before you pick the firm.

How much does AI consulting cost for a law firm?

From the firms' own pages: AI enablement from $3,500 (Clear Guidance Partners), an AI readiness assessment at $2,500 to $7,500 and a custom AI agent at $15,000 to $60,000 (Lumeor Studio), and a free AI and automation audit (PaperStreet). Seven of the ten print nothing.

Should a law firm hire an AI consultant or a legal technology firm?

If the problem is policy, risk and lawyers using personal AI accounts, a governance led consultant is enough. If the problem is three systems that don't share a client record, you need someone who builds integrations. If the AI exists and stalls on real matter data, a forward deployed engineer inside the firm fixes what a roadmap won't.

Do the ABA rules affect which AI consultant we hire?

Yes. ABA Formal Opinion 512, cited on Lumeor Studio's legal page, ties generative AI use to competence, confidentiality, supervision and fees. PGH Networks' write up adds state bar guidance and client outside counsel rules. Ask any consultant how its build keeps privileged data out of public model training.

Which firms here actually build, rather than advise?

Epiq says it develops agentic, RAG and Copilot applications. PaperStreet builds intake and CRM connectors and dashboards with its own developers. Lumeor Studio builds agents and RAG assistants at printed ranges. Helm360 integrates Elite 3E, Aderant and Intapp. The rest lead with strategy, governance, IT or training.

How to choose

  • 1This week, follow one new matter from first call to first invoice and count every time someone types the same client's name, address or matter number into a different system: the website form, the intake sheet, Clio or your practice management system, the conflict check, billing. That count, and the hours behind it, is the number every firm on this page should be judged against.
  • 2Find out what your people already paste into AI. Ask, don't audit. If client documents are going into personal ChatGPT accounts, the first job is a written AI use policy and an approved tool, which Clear Guidance Partners will start from $3,500 and gives a free template for.
  • 3Check what you already own. Microsoft 365 Copilot, Clio's own AI features or iManage's AI may cover the first use case without a new licence. Clear Guidance says it starts from the tools a firm already uses, and Kraft Kennedy's own advice is to choose the lightest tool that solves the problem.
  • 4Decide the shape. Policy and risk: a governance consultant. Retyped data and leads lost between systems: a builder such as PaperStreet or Lumeor Studio. An Am Law sized programme: Epiq, Kraft Kennedy or Morae. Licences nobody uses: Traveling Coaches.
  • 5Ask every firm for the first price in writing before a second meeting, and for the confidentiality design: where the data sits, whether it trains any model, and how a lawyer verifies each citation.
  • 6If the systems still don't share one client record after the roadmap, and nobody in house can join them up, that's when you bring in a forward deployed engineer rather than another consultant. Does a small law firm need an AI consultant?

Not to switch on AI features inside Clio, Microsoft 365 or tools it already pays for. It needs one when client data has to move between two or more systems, when staff are using personal AI accounts on client work, or when a client's outside counsel guidelines demand a written AI policy.

Is AI consulting for law firms worth it?

It is when the first step is priced, the confidentiality design is in writing, and the work moves one number you chose before you signed, such as hours of intake retyping or days from first call to engagement letter. A roadmap with nothing built behind it rarely is.
