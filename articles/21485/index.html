Summary

A job listing on OpenWork for Matsurika's Sozo division recruiting a Forward Deployed Engineer. The company launched Sozo AI Solutions in October 2026 to support enterprise AI transformation end to end, emphasizing business outcomes over AI adoption itself. The FDE embeds with mid-size and large clients, designs and builds AI agents, integrates them with existing tools and data, sets operating rules, and measures impact while feeding lessons back into product design. Requirements include experience building generative AI applications or agents plus three or more years in system implementation at an SIer or consultancy, or technical delivery in IT/SaaS. Salary ranges from 8 to 12 million yen.

Key points

  • Matsurika is hiring an FDE for its Sozo division, with salary of 8-12 million yen.
  • Sozo AI Solutions launched October 2026, covering consulting through adoption.
  • The FDE builds AI agents on-site and embeds them into client operations.
  • Requires generative AI building experience plus 3+ years in SIer, consulting, or IT/SaaS roles.
  • The company also runs the Mazrica business with sales and marketing AI products.

  • Creating an environment where people can work creatively, as if playing
  • We believe that in order to make this world even more filled with human energy, it is important to make "work life"—where people spend much of their lives—shine. When people work proactively as if playing and genuinely enjoy the work itself, creativity and productivity can be maximized. With this in mind, Mazrica has embraced the vision of "creating an environment where people can work creatively, as if playing." By combining the power of people and technology, we aim not merely at efficiency, but at creating a freer, more creative, and more fulfilling work life for the world.
  • Business Overview
  • Through an environment where AI and data circulate naturally, we realize a way of working that lets people focus on the thinking they should truly be engaged in. With the vision of "creating an environment where people can work creatively, as if playing," Mazrica Inc. supports the creation of environments where working people can focus on more creative work through the power of people and technology. Currently, we operate two core businesses: the "Mazrica business," which offers AI products for sales and marketing, and the "Sozo business," which supports corporate AX (AI transformation). ▼Mazrica business We support corporate sales and marketing activities through Mazrica Sales, Mazrica Target, Mazrica Engage, Mazrica Marketing, Mazrica DSR, and more. ▼Sozo business With "Sozo AI Solutions," we do not aim at AI adoption for its own sake, but prioritize driving change in operations and organizations and delivering business results. By combining consulting, AI agent development, contract development, system/AI implementation, education and training, and business process outsourcing, we support clients from conception through to full adoption.
  • Product Details [Mazrica Business]
  • Toward mechanisms that deliver results ■ "Mazrica Sales": a sales support tool anyone can use to achieve results Mazrica Sales is a sales support tool that centrally manages customer information, deal status, and sales activities. Data is naturally accumulated and shared in the field, visualizing deal status in real time. In addition, AI handles company data matching and information summarization, supporting a comfortable sales environment. ■ "Mazrica Target": an AI-powered corporate database specialized for sales Mazrica Target is a sales AI tool that turns company information and data into "valuable sales assets." It integrates a database of 5.6 million companies with external web data and internal SFA/CRM data to automatically identify "companies to approach now" and "proposals that resonate." The per-company proposal stories built by AI can be output to PowerPoint with one click, reducing time spent on company research and document creation so you can focus on what truly matters: dialogue with customers and problem solving. ■ "Mazrica Engage": a marketing AI agent Mazrica Engage is an AI agent that delivers optimal information to each customer across touchpoints such as websites and sales/marketing materials, maximizing lead generation and meeting creation. Customers enjoy an information-gathering experience where they can quickly reach the information they want, while companies gain visibility into customer interests through that process. ■ "Mazrica DSR": a digital sales room Mazrica DSR is a digital sales room where meeting-related information such as proposals, quotes, and minutes can be shared and managed online. Because customers and sales reps share information in the same space, it helps visualize the meeting process while tracking views and interest levels, supporting deal progression.
