Forward Deployed Engineering Firms in the Bay Area

Reveneau · 2026-10-21 · 13 min read

Summary

This editorial guide reviews nine firms claiming a San Francisco Bay Area presence that offer forward deployed engineering. Five publish street addresses: Reveneau, Fluxon (via Stripe's partner directory), Factored in Mountain View, Grid Dynamics in San Ramon, and Tredence in San Jose and Foster City. Distyl AI, Ode with Anthropic, and Tribe AI name San Francisco without addresses. Turing's listed address is a private mailbox, and TechCrunch called it Palo Alto-based in 2025. The article argues that offices matter less than where engineers actually work, since FDEs embed at client sites, and advises buyers to ask about on-site days, time zones, and travel costs.

Key points

  • Five of nine firms publish a verifiable Bay Area street address.
  • Turing's San Francisco address is a private mailbox, not an office.
  • Tribe AI's SF presence rests only on its own website footer.
  • FDEs work at client sites, so office location matters less.
  • Ask firms how often engineers will be on-site and who pays travel.

Why the office matters less for forward deployed work

A forward deployed engineer works inside the client's team, on the client's systems, often at the client's site. The guide to forward deployed engineering explains the role in detail. So for a firm that sells this work, the office is where you meet its leaders, where some of its staff live, and which time zone the firm works in. The engineers on your project may spend most of their time with you.

Two firms say this in their own words. Fluxon writes on its AI development page: "Same channels, same standups. It feels like we sit next to you because, in practice, we do" [16]. A standup is a short daily team meeting. Factored's homepage says its engineers "embed in your environment, work across your stack, and drive critical engineering through production" [17], meaning they work inside your systems and tools until the software is in daily use.

Firms in San Francisco

Reveneau

Reveneau lists an office at 435 Utah Street in San Francisco and a second office on Floor 33, 909 Mainland Street, in Vancouver. It offers forward deployed engineers. By its own account, AI writes 100 percent of Reveneau's code, and every change has to pass a large eval suite before release. Reveneau says the suite is a set of automated tests written from the specification, and that it is graded by Jev, which makes it run ten times faster than its previous grader did. Reveneau says it uses AI in place of hiring more engineers and puts that saving into its clients' prices. It says it takes responsibility for the whole project through production, meaning daily use by real users, and after release. Anthropic's partner directory did not list Reveneau on 20 October 2026 [1]. To meet the team at either office, use the contact page.

Fluxon

Fluxon's listing in Stripe's partner directory gives 236 West Portal Ave, 848, San Francisco [3]. The partner supplies that address itself, so it is Fluxon's own statement. Forbes's company page, with figures as of March 2026, gives Fluxon's headquarters as San Francisco and its staff as 200 employees [2]. Fluxon's homepage shows clocks labelled SF, TOR, WAW and MUM. Its AI development page carries the heading "Forward-deployed by default." [16]. Its enterprise page says its builders "join your team, work your way, and stay for as long as you need them". Anthropic's directory did not list Fluxon on 20 October 2026 [1].

Distyl AI

Distyl's about page lists New York, San Francisco and London under Locations, with no street address [4]. Crunchbase News calls Distyl "the San Francisco-based startup" [5]. Distyl says it places "teams of engineers and researchers who own the outcome" inside large companies, and pairs them with its own products. Anthropic's directory did not list Distyl on 20 October 2026 [1].

Ode with Anthropic

Ode's careers page says it is based in San Francisco, with offices in New York, Raleigh-Durham and Dubai [6]. No street address appears on the pages read. TechCrunch reported in July 2026 that Ode employs 100 engineers and works closely with Anthropic's applied AI team [7]. Anthropic co-owns Ode, and its directory lists Ode at the Select tier [1].

Tribe AI

Tribe's website footer lists offices in New York, San Francisco and Lisbon [8]. TechCrunch called Tribe AI "The Brooklyn-based startup" in July 2024, and reported that it builds client projects with a network of over 500 contractors [9]. So the San Francisco office comes only from Tribe's own site. Ask Tribe for the address if a meeting in the city matters to you. Anthropic's directory lists it as TribeAI at the Preferred tier [1].

Firms elsewhere in the Bay Area

Factored, Mountain View

Factored lists 400 Castro Street, Suite 600, Mountain View, CA 94041 on its contact page [10]. Its about page says it was founded in Palo Alto in 2019. By its own account it has more than 500 engineers [17]. Anthropic's directory lists Factored at the Preferred tier [1].

Grid Dynamics, San Ramon

Grid Dynamics lists its headquarters at 6101 Bollinger Canyon Road, Suite 465, San Ramon, CA 94583 [11]. The same contact page lists US engineering centers in Plano, Texas, Alpharetta, Georgia, Tampa, Florida and Scottsdale, Arizona, and an office in Evanston, Illinois. Grid Dynamics is a public company. Anthropic's directory lists it at the Select tier [1].

Tredence, San Jose and Foster City

Tredence lists two Bay Area offices on its contact page: 1900 Camden Ave, Suite 66, San Jose, CA 95124, and 950 Tower Lane, Suite 940, under the heading Foster City, with no city line after the street [12]. The dateline of its July press release reads "Bengaluru/ San Jose", and the release says its staff work across the San Francisco Bay Area, Chicago, Riyadh, London, Toronto and Bengaluru [13]. This guide describes Tredence as a firm with US offices in four cities. Anthropic's directory lists Tredence at the Select tier [1].

Turing: a San Francisco mailbox

Turing's company page says it is "Headquartered in San Francisco" [14]. The address in its site footer is 548 Market Street, PMB 18282. PMB stands for private mailbox: a mail service rents out box numbers at its own street address. TechCrunch called Turing "Palo Alto-based" in March 2025 [15]. Both can be true, because a company can receive mail in one city and work from another. This guide found no street address for a Turing office, so Turing is not counted as a firm with a confirmed Bay Area office. If you want to meet Turing's team, ask where its Bay Area staff work. Anthropic's directory lists Turing at the Select tier [1].

How to check a Bay Area office before you rely on it

  • Read the address closely. PMB, or a suite number at a mail service, means a mailbox.
  • Look for an outside source. A news report that calls the firm "San Francisco-based" is evidence from outside the firm. An address in a partner directory is usually supplied by the firm itself.
  • Ask which legal entity will sign the contract, and at which address it is registered.
  • Ask who will come to your site, how often, and who pays for travel.
  • Visit once before a large contract, and meet the engineers who would work on your project.

Which Bay Area firm fits which buyer

If you want embedded engineers whose code is written by AI and tested against your own specification before each release, Reveneau is at 435 Utah Street. If you want a team that joins your channels and daily meetings and stays as long as you need, Fluxon describes that offer. If you are one of the largest US companies and will accept the vendor's own products as part of the work, Distyl AI says it pairs its engineers with them. If you build on Anthropic's models, Ode is part-owned by Anthropic. If you want data and AI engineers south of San Francisco, Factored is in Mountain View. If you want a public company with staff in several states, Grid Dynamics is in San Ramon. If you are a retailer or a consumer goods company, Tredence says its FDEs know those industries.

Common questions

Which forward deployed engineering firms have an office in the Bay Area?

Eight firms in this guide name a Bay Area office: Reveneau, Fluxon, Distyl AI, Ode with Anthropic and Tribe AI in San Francisco, Factored in Mountain View, Grid Dynamics in San Ramon, and Tredence in San Jose and Foster City. Turing gives a San Francisco address that is a private mailbox. All were read on 20 October 2026.

Which Bay Area FDE firms publish a street address?

Five do. Reveneau lists 435 Utah Street, San Francisco. Fluxon's Stripe directory listing gives 236 West Portal Ave, 848, San Francisco. Factored lists 400 Castro Street, Mountain View. Grid Dynamics lists 6101 Bollinger Canyon Road, San Ramon. Tredence lists 1900 Camden Ave, San Jose, and 950 Tower Lane in Foster City. Distyl AI, Ode and Tribe AI name the city only.

Why is Turing shown apart from the other Bay Area firms?

Turing's company page says it is headquartered in San Francisco, but the address in its footer is 548 Market Street, PMB 18282, and PMB means private mailbox. TechCrunch called Turing Palo Alto-based in March 2025. This guide found no street address for a Turing office, so it shows the mailbox and the news report side by side.

Where are Tredence's offices in California?

Tredence's contact page lists 1900 Camden Ave, Suite 66, San Jose, and 950 Tower Lane, Suite 940, under the heading Foster City. Its press release dateline reads Bengaluru and San Jose. Tredence also lists US offices in Chicago and in Bentonville, Arkansas. This guide describes Tredence as a firm with US offices.

Who confirms that Tribe AI works from San Francisco?

Only Tribe AI's own website, whose footer lists offices in New York, San Francisco and Lisbon. TechCrunch described it as a Brooklyn-based startup in July 2024. No street address appears on Tribe's site. If a meeting in San Francisco matters to you, ask Tribe for the address and for the names of the people who work there.

Which FDE firms are in the Bay Area but outside San Francisco?

Three firms list their Bay Area office outside the city. Factored is at 400 Castro Street, Suite 600, Mountain View. Grid Dynamics has its headquarters at 6101 Bollinger Canyon Road, Suite 465, San Ramon. Tredence lists offices in San Jose and Foster City. Each address comes from the firm's own contact page, read on 20 October 2026.

Can a Bay Area FDE firm send engineers to a client in another state?

Each firm in this guide says its engineers work inside the client's team, so an office in one state does not stop them from working for a client in another. Grid Dynamics also lists engineering centers in Texas, Georgia, Florida and Arizona. Tredence lists offices in Chicago and Arkansas. Ask each firm how many days its engineers would spend at your site, and who pays for their travel and hotels.

Where does Fluxon list its San Francisco address?

Fluxon's listing in Stripe's partner directory gives 236 West Portal Ave, 848, San Francisco. Fluxon supplies that address itself. Forbes's company page, with figures as of March 2026, gives its headquarters as San Francisco. Fluxon's homepage shows clocks labelled SF, TOR, WAW and MUM, and its AI page says its work is forward deployed by default.

Will the engineers from a Bay Area FDE firm live in the Bay Area?

Not always. An office shows where some of the firm's staff work. TechCrunch reported in 2024 that Tribe AI builds with a network of over 500 contractors, and Grid Dynamics lists engineering centers in four other states. Ask each firm where the engineers named for your project live, which time zone they keep, and how often they will travel.

How can I meet Reveneau's team in San Francisco?

Reveneau's San Francisco office is at 435 Utah Street, and it has a second office on Floor 33, 909 Mainland Street, in Vancouver. You can ask for a meeting through the contact page on reveneau.com.
