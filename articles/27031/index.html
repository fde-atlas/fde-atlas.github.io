Ep 01 — What Is a Forward Deployed Engineer? | FDE Zero to Hero #forwarddeployedengineer

FDE Times · 1 min read

Summary

This is the first episode of FDE Times' "FDE Zero to Hero" video series, aimed at viewers unfamiliar with the role. Presented by Mayank Aggarwal, it explains what a forward deployed engineer is and how the role differs from adjacent positions like sales engineers and solutions engineers, emphasizing on-site, customer-facing delivery of AI products. The accompanying page includes hiring data: 924 open FDE roles (up 33 in a week), 196 companies hiring, a median US pay of $200k, and Databricks as the top hirer with 107 openings. A reasonable starting point for readers new to the FDE concept.

Key points

  • First episode of a video series defining the FDE role.
  • Hosted by Mayank Aggarwal, pitched as introductory content.
  • Contrasts FDEs with sales engineers and solutions engineers.
