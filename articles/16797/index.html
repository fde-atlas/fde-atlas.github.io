Cohere Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Experience (Rejected, Aug 2026)

Top Tech Transition · 2026-10-07 · 1 min read

Summary

A candidate recounts being rejected after interviewing for a Staff-level Forward Deployed Engineer role at Cohere in August 2026. The process had three rounds: a recruiter screen, a phone screen, and a technical system design round. The author says the interviews focused on backend software engineering practices rather than customer empathy or handling difficult clients. Despite being told to prepare for AI system design, the technical round asked a backend question—designing a metrics and logging service—plus a discussion of a product the candidate had built. Each interview involved two interviewers asking questions simultaneously. All interviewers were based in Canada even though the role was on the US west coast.

Key points

  • Three rounds: recruiter screen, phone screen, and technical system design.
  • Phone screen covered a customer scenario about deploying a product in the customer's own environment.
  • Technical round: design a metrics and logging service, plus discuss a product built.
  • Interviews emphasized backend SWE practices, not customer empathy.
  • All interviewers were in Canada for a US west coast role.
