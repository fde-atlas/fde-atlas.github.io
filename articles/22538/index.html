Google Forward Deployed Engineer: Role and Pay

FWD Deployment · 2026-10-11 · 3 min read

Summary

This article outlines the role and compensation of Google's forward deployed engineers. Based in Google Cloud's go-to-market organization and Google Public Sector, FDEs embed with strategic accounts to code, debug, and ship custom AI agent systems built on Gemini, clearing data, integration, and state-management blockers while feeding field lessons back into the product roadmap. The author checked Google's job boards on October 11, 2026: a GenAI FDE IV role lists $207,000–$300,000 plus 20% bonus and equity, while an Applied AI FDE Manager lists $262,000–$364,000 plus 25% bonus. Openings span 25+ US cities plus Singapore, Dublin, São Paulo, Zürich, Sydney, and Tokyo, some requiring a second language. A DeepMind (Kaggle) FDE posting also appeared.

Key points

  • Google Cloud FDEs ship custom agent systems inside customer environments.
  • Google Public Sector runs a separate FDE squad for government clients.
  • GenAI FDE IV pay: $207K–$300K plus 20% bonus and equity.
  • Applied AI FDE Manager pay reaches $364K plus 25% bonus.
  • Postings cover 25+ US cities and six international locations.

The role

What a Google forward deployed engineer does

Google Cloud's GenAI forward deployed engineers move past architecture diagrams to write and ship the agent systems themselves, clearing data, integration and state-management blockers along the way. They also feed what they learn in the field back into Google Cloud's product roadmap.

Google Public Sector runs its own FDE squad that deploys secure AI for federal, state and local government. A DeepMind (Kaggle) FDE posting also appeared on Google's careers site.

Live postings on 2026-10-11 spanned 25+ US cities plus Singapore, Dublin, São Paulo, Zürich, Sydney and Tokyo, several requiring a second language.

Team: Google Cloud go-to-market (GenAI and Applied AI) and Google Public Sector. In Google's words: "moving beyond high-level architecture to code, debug, and jointly ship bespoke agentic solutions" (posting)

Best for: Google Cloud customers building agents on Gemini

  • Google Cloud and Public Sector
  • $207K–$300K + bonus posted
