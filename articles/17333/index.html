FDE vs SDE: 9 Differences in Amazon's Own Job Postings

Hayat Amin · Operator · 2026-10-15 · 9 min read

Summary

Hayat Amin read every US Amazon posting titled forward deployed engineer (14) and software development engineer (919) on 15 October 2026 and derived nine differences from the postings themselves. His core claim: Amazon defines the FDE as an SDE pointed at a customer's business. Base pay bands are identical at each level, so money does not decide the choice. The real differences are travel (12 of 14 FDE postings require 30-50 percent versus almost none for SDEs), success metrics (business outcomes versus service scale and latency), on-call duty (SDEs only), work content (all FDE postings involve AI agents), and preferred backgrounds (13 of 14 FDE postings favor founders or startup builders). AWS Forward Deployed Engineering also hires SDEs who build the internal tools that FDEs deploy at customers.

Key points

  • Amazon pays FDEs and SDEs the same base band at the same level.
  • 12 of 14 FDE postings require 30-50 percent travel; SDE postings almost none.
  • All 14 FDE postings measure success by customer business outcomes.
  • 13 of 14 FDE postings prefer founder or startup-builder backgrounds.
  • Within AWS FDE teams, SDEs build tools and FDEs deploy them at customers.

An SDE, a software development engineer, builds and runs software for their own employer. An FDE, a forward deployed engineer, builds production software inside a customer's company and travels to do it. At Amazon, which posts both titles, the base pay is the same at each level.

I'm Hayat Amin. I spent twenty years as a technology chief financial officer, sold three companies in that seat, and now do forward deployed work myself inside other people's systems. SDE is Amazon's word for a software engineer, so I went to the one employer that uses both titles. This morning, 15 October 2026, I read every US posting on amazon.jobs titled forward deployed engineer, 14 of them, and 919 US postings titled software development engineer. Here are the 9 differences the postings show, and the one place they don't differ at all.

Why people type fde vs sde

Google completes fde vs sde as its own suggestion, then fde vs sde salary. Type it the other way round and you get sde vs fde, sde vs fde salary and sde or fde. I think most of those searches come from software engineers wondering whether to move across, and from founders who've heard the new title and want to know if it's a real job or a rebrand.

Amazon answers the rebrand question in its own words. Its Forward Deployed Engineer II posting says the role combines "the technical rigor of an AWS SDE with the urgency and ownership required to deliver measurable customer business outcomes". The company that made SDE a household title defines the FDE as an SDE who works for a customer. The 9 points below are what that means in practice.

The 9 differences, from Amazon's own postings

1. Where the work sits

The FDE II posting in Arlington, Virginia opens with an FDE who "embeds directly inside a strategic enterprise customer" and builds AI production systems alongside the customer's own engineering team. Compare the Software Development Engineer II posting for Hosted Execution in Seattle. That engineer builds infrastructure for internal Amazon services, the compute behind Stores, Kindle, Alexa, Pharmacy and Payments. One works inside somebody else's company. The other works inside Amazon on something Amazon owns.

2. Travel

All 14 US forward deployed engineer postings mention travel. 12 say 30 to 50 percent. The Principal FDE and the senior US Government FDE say up to 25 percent. Of the 919 SDE postings, 16 contain the word travel at all, and 5 of those are a line about Amazon's products helping you plan travel to exotic places. A handful of data centre roles ask you to visit network sites. If you don't want to be away from home a third of the month or more, this point decides it. I wrote more on that in do forward deployed engineers travel.

3. Base pay, where there is no difference

This is the surprise. Amazon pays the two titles the same base band at the same level. The FDE II posting is $143,700 to $194,400 in Arlington, Boston and Seattle and $158,100 to $213,800 in New York. 71 SDE II postings in Seattle carry the same $143,700 to $194,400. The senior FDE in New York is $184,900 to $250,200, the same band as 17 senior SDE postings in New York, including the Fauna robotics team. The Principal FDE goes to $220,100 to $297,700 in New York. Both sets of postings say the package adds sign-on payments and restricted stock units on top.

4. Years of experience

Same ladder again. The FDE II asks for 3 or more years of non-internship professional software development, and 119 of 132 SDE II postings ask for the same 3. The senior FDE asks for 5 or more, as do 138 of 157 senior SDE postings. Amazon even numbers the FDE the way it numbers the SDE: Forward Deployed Engineer II, and SDE 3 for the senior grade in one of its own postings.

5. What success is measured by

All 14 FDE postings use the phrase business outcome, against 9 of 919 SDE postings. All 14 ask for experience explaining technical ideas to non-technical audiences, against 6 of 919. An SDE's posting talks about the service, its scale, its latency and its cost. An FDE's posting talks about the customer's processes, their constraints and what changes in their business. You're judged on whether the customer's week got better, which is the part I find most interesting.

6. On call

168 of the 919 SDE postings mention on-call, like the Hosted Execution team's on-call rotation or the Fauna senior posting that asks you to join the rotation for the services you own. None of the 14 FDE postings uses the word. The FDE still owns production. The FDE II posting says "own production for your workstream" and troubleshoot incidents across models, distributed systems and application services. The difference is that it's the customer's production, for as long as the engagement runs.

7. What gets built

All 14 FDE postings mention AI agents or agentic work, against 223 of 919 SDE postings. The FDE II lists what it builds: retrieval pipelines, workflow automation, API orchestration layers and data integration, joined to foundation models and the customer's existing applications. An SDE at Amazon builds whatever the team owns, which might be search ranking, storage, robotics telemetry or advertising. The FDE's job is narrower in subject and wider in systems. It's AI work that has to plug into a stack nobody at Amazon designed.

8. Who Amazon wants in the seat

13 of 14 FDE postings list a background "as a founder, engineering lead, or startup builder with high ownership and urgency" as a preferred qualification. One SDE posting of 919 mentions founders at all. On the other side, 868 of 919 SDE postings mention code review, against 4 of 14 FDE postings. Amazon is hiring the FDE for ownership and judgement in a room it doesn't control, and the SDE for engineering discipline inside a team that does.

9. The SDE inside the forward deployed team

AWS Forward Deployed Engineering also hires SDEs, and those postings draw the line for you. A Sr. Software Development Engineer posting in New York says "You'll build, not consult", calls the team a product engineering org inside Professional Services and says "This is not billable-hours consulting". That engineer builds the agent platform Amazon's own consultants use for cloud migrations, travels about 5 percent and writes production code daily. A second senior SDE posting in the same organisation travels about 20 percent. Both pay $184,900 to $250,200 in New York, the senior FDE band. Inside one team, the SDE builds the tools and the FDE takes them into the customer.

Which one should you pick

If you're an engineer choosing, ask two things. How many nights a month do you want in hotels, and do you want to own one service for years or one customer outcome for months? The money won't decide it at Amazon. If you're hiring, I'd say the opposite of what most job boards imply. You don't need a better SDE. Amazon's own definition is an SDE pointed at a customer's business, and the hard part is the customer half. My longer comparison of the two, for founders, is on my firm's site at forward deployed engineer vs software engineer, and do forward deployed engineers code covers how much of the week is code.

Questions people actually ask

FDE vs SDE: what is the difference?

An SDE, a software development engineer, builds and runs software for their own employer's product. An FDE, a forward deployed engineer, embeds inside a customer's company and builds production software there. Amazon's own FDE posting describes the role as combining the technical rigor of an AWS SDE with the urgency and ownership to deliver measurable customer business outcomes. On 15 October 2026 all 14 of Amazon's US FDE postings mentioned travel, 12 of them at 30 to 50 percent, against 16 of 919 US SDE postings.

FDE vs SDE salary: which pays more?

At Amazon, neither. The Forward Deployed Engineer II posting carries $143,700 to $194,400 in Arlington, Boston and Seattle and $158,100 to $213,800 in New York. 71 Software Development Engineer II postings in Seattle carry the same $143,700 to $194,400. The Senior Forward Deployed Engineer in New York is $184,900 to $250,200, the same band as 17 Senior Software Development Engineer postings in New York. Both packages add sign-on payments and restricted stock units, per the postings.

Is FDE better than SDE?

Neither is better. They suit different people. Pick the FDE seat if you want to sit with a customer, learn how their business runs and are happy on the road 30 to 50 percent of the time. Pick the SDE seat if you want to own one service for years, go deep on it and take its on-call rotation. Amazon pays both the same base band at the same level, so the choice is about the work, not the money.

What is an SDE at Amazon?

SDE is Amazon's title for a software engineer, short for software development engineer, and it runs in levels. On 15 October 2026 Amazon had 919 US postings with Software Development Engineer in the title. Most entry level and SDE II postings asked for 3 or more years of non-internship professional software development, and most senior postings asked for 5 or more. One senior posting calls the senior level SDE 3.

Forward deployed engineer vs SDE: do FDEs write code?

Yes. Amazon's FDE II posting says FDEs write production-grade software and own delivery of complete AI workstreams from requirements through production deployment, and asks for 3 or more years of professional software development, the same bar as most SDE II postings. The difference is where the code runs: in the customer's systems, not Amazon's.

Where these numbers come from

Every count comes from Amazon's own job search at amazon.jobs, read on 15 October 2026. A search for forward deployed engineer returned 54 postings worldwide. 19 are titled forward deployed engineer at some level, leaving out managers, security engineers, product managers and SDEs, and 14 of those are in the United States. A search for software development engineer filtered to the United States returned 1,176 postings, 919 with software development engineer in the title and nothing forward deployed in it. Word counts are plain text matches on each posting's description and qualifications, so a posting that says travel once counts the same as one that says it five times. Bands are the base salary ranges each posting publishes by city. A band is a range across a level, not an offer. The autocomplete phrases were pulled from Google the same morning.
