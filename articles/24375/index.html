Analysis of 97 FDE Job Postings in Japan | Offered Salaries and Required Qualifications (November 2026)

Original title: 国内のFDE求人97件を集計｜提示年収と必須要件（2026年11月）

FDE Consulting · 2026-11-05 · 11 min read

Summary

On November 4, 2026, the author reviewed 97 FDE job postings from 65 companies in Japan. Only 53 listed salary figures; among the 48 that stated an upper bound, 34 fell in the 10–19.99 million yen range, with the highest at 47 million yen. Comparisons are complicated: 29 of the 53 include fixed overtime pay or overtime allowances, and some include bonuses or are theoretical annual income. Among 88 engineer-titled roles, 57 require development experience, 49 require direct customer interaction, but only 23 require hands-on AI/LLM experience and 17 require English. The author's takeaway: identical FDE titles cover very different jobs, so applicants should read required qualifications rather than job titles, and engineers without AI experience may find more openings than expected.

Key points

  • 97 FDE postings from 65 Japanese companies were reviewed on Nov 4, 2026.
  • 34 of 48 postings with salary caps fell in the 10–19.99M yen range.
  • 29 of 53 salary-listed postings include fixed overtime or overtime allowances.
  • 57 of 88 engineer roles require development experience; only 23 require AI/LLM experience.
  • Same FDE title means different jobs; read requirements, not titles.

There are three reasons the figures cannot be compared as they are.

  • 29 of the 53 postings include fixed overtime pay or overtime allowances. Of the 28 that state the hours, 20 specify 45 hours, with a range from 30 to 46 hours
  • 14 postings state an amount that includes bonuses or a theoretical annual salary
  • 7 postings have no "annual" wording in the salary field. Based on the size of the figures, I counted them as annual amounts

What we read from the numbers

From here on, these are our views, and should be read separately from the facts written in the job postings.

Even with the same title, the experience sought differs

While 57 postings require development experience, 14 do not. Some postings even state "Professional experience as a software engineer is not required." I do not consider this good or bad. It simply means that some companies attach the FDE name to a role of organizing customer business processes, while others attach it to a role of writing production code.

We describe the FDE as an engineer who builds production systems themselves. Since this tally was counted by job title, it includes postings that do not fit this description. For applicants, reading the required qualifications section rather than the job title is a faster way to grasp the substance of the work. Points to consider when reading are summarized in "FDE Job and Career Change Guide".

Development and customer-facing skills are sought more than AI experience

Postings requiring professional experience building or implementing AI/LLM are 23 out of 88, fewer than the 57 requiring development experience or the 49 requiring customer interaction. Some postings explicitly state "Professional AI/LLM development experience is not required."

The reason cannot be verified from the job postings. In my view, many companies probably believe that if they hire people with development and customer-facing experience, AI knowledge can be acquired on the job. For engineers without professional AI experience, this means the range of places to apply is wider than expected.

Compare salaries on equal terms

Even at the same 10 million yen, the base salary differs greatly between postings that include 45 hours of fixed overtime pay and those that do not. When comparing, you need to check the fixed overtime hours and how bonuses are treated, and align the figures down to the monthly salary level. How salaries are determined is written in "How Much Does an FDE Earn?".

For companies that outsource FDEs or keep them in-house

If you are receiving proposals under the FDE name, you should verify whether the assigned person will do the implementation themselves. As this tally shows, the job title alone does not reveal whether the person will handle development. How to verify this is written in "How to Choose an FDE Support Company".

For companies placing FDEs in-house, the table of requirements and offered salaries serves as material for deciding hiring conditions. Our support for building an in-house FDE team is covered in FDE Team Launch Support.

Methodology and limitations of this survey

  • Date checked: November 4, 2026. Job pages were retrieved and their listings and descriptions verified
  • Jobs counted: Open positions whose job title includes FDE, Forward Deployed, Forward Deployed (Japanese), or Deployment Strategist, located in Japan, with the company name disclosed. Variations in notation, such as the presence or absence of a middle dot, were treated as the same
  • Places searched: Company career pages, HRMOS, HERP, Talentio, Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable, Wantedly, and Green
  • Jobs not counted: Companies' own job postings, postings with the company name withheld, postings listed only on recruiting agency pages, closed postings, and two Palantir positions involving work for the US government that require US citizenship
  • Pages that could not be opened: Job pages on Indeed, doda, Bizreach, and LinkedIn could not be retrieved. Pages requiring login were not accessed
  • Possible undercounting: No postings were found from major SIers or major consulting firms, except for Deloitte Tohmatsu. This does not necessarily mean they have no openings
  • Weighting bias: Each posting was counted as one, so companies posting multiple jobs have a large influence. The maximum was 8 postings from a single company, and none of these 8 listed salary figures
  • Determination of requirements: Includes our own judgment based on reading the job posting text
  • Nature of the salary figures: All are the amounts stated in the job postings, which may differ from actual amounts paid

When citing the figures in this survey, please include the date checked, November 4, 2026, and the URL of this page. If you notice any errors, we would appreciate it if you could let us know via Contact.
