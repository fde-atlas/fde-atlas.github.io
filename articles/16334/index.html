Summary

This piece compares two forward deployed engineering providers from Top 11's 2026 rankings: Databricks' AI Forward Deployed Engineering unit and Ode with Anthropic. Ode ranks higher (#5 vs #7, scoring 7.9 vs 7.1 out of 9.4). Ode is a $1.5 billion services firm built on Fractional AI with about 100 engineers, targeting mid-size US companies with CEO buy-in. Databricks' function is credible but locked to its own platform and best suited to existing enterprise customers. Neither discloses pricing; the rankings target founders and CTOs choosing between boutique retainers, vendor FDE programs, and systems integrators.
