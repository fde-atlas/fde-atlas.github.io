A Deloitte Forward Deployed Engineer is an engineer who sits with a client’s team and takes an AI use case all the way into production, usually on a specific platform such as Palantir, ServiceNow, Salesforce or NVIDIA’s open models. For India, the key fact is recent: in September 2026 Deloitte India launched an Open Model Engineering practice and said it plans to hire, train and certify forward deployed engineers through fiscal year 2027, according to Deloitte India. As of 9 November 2026 we could not find an India-located Deloitte FDE job listing, so this page explains what the role looks like from Deloitte’s own announcements and its US listings, and how to get ready before India openings appear.

Why Deloitte is building forward deployed engineering

Deloitte has moved quickly on FDE over the past year:

  • A named FDE service. Deloitte US sells Forward Deployed Engineering as a consulting service, with small cross-disciplinary teams and time-boxed stages of roughly 3 days to a use case, 3 weeks to proven value and 3 months to production, its service page says.
  • Salesforce. In August 2026 Deloitte said it was the first global system integrator to staff and deliver a Salesforce FDE engagement, embedding FDEs in delivery teams to put Agentforce solutions into production, per its press release. The release did not give a team size or locations.
  • India and open models. The September 2026 India launch, announced by Deloitte India, pairs Deloitte with NVIDIA (Nemotron open models and NIM microservices) and its own Zora AI platform. The release says FDEs certified in open models will form the operational base of the practice and work directly with clients, including in India. It does not name Indian cities or give a hiring number.

What the FDE does at Deloitte

Deloitte’s US listings show that FDE is not one job but a family of platform-specific roles:

  • Platform FDE (Palantir, ServiceNow, Databricks, Microsoft). Build GenAI features such as copilots, agentic workflows and RAG on top of the client’s platform, and integrate them with existing systems through APIs or event-driven designs.
  • Lead FDE. The Palantir lead role runs pods of 2 to 5 engineers and owns a workstream end to end.
  • AI/Agentic FDE (senior). Sets standards for distributed FDE teams, handles identity, data access and enterprise integration in customer deployments, and manages executive stakeholders. It is linked to Deloitte’s Zora AI platform.

Two details matter for Indian engineers. The Palantir and ServiceNow listings name experience with hybrid onshore/offshore teams as a preferred skill, and the ServiceNow listing asks for hands-on use of Claude Code, including writing CLAUDE.md files and MCP integrations, which shows how current the tooling expectations are.

The pattern is consistent: one named platform, at least a year of shipping GenAI to production, and proof you can lead a small team in front of a client. The Palantir and ServiceNow listings also require a bachelor’s degree.

Pay

Deloitte has not published FDE pay for India, and no India listing with pay was found. The US listings carry Deloitte’s own estimated ranges (for example, $134,500–$265,100 for the ServiceNow FDE), but those reflect the US market and say nothing about Indian offers. For India context, Indeed India data cited by Masai School in October 2026 puts average FDE base pay at about ₹12 LPA, within a ₹9–16 LPA range, and reports FDE postings up nearly 19-fold between June 2025 and June 2026. How to prepare

  • Pick one Deloitte platform and go deep. Choose the one closest to your work: ServiceNow (get the CAD certification), Palantir Foundry/AIP, Salesforce Agentforce, Databricks or NVIDIA NIM. Deloitte FDE roles are hired per platform.
  • Ship one production-style GenAI feature. Build an agentic workflow or RAG feature on that platform with evaluation, logging and access control, and write up the before-and-after metric.
  • Learn open models for the India practice. Fine-tune or serve an open model (for example a Nemotron model through NIM) and document cost, latency and data-residency choices. That maps directly to Deloitte India’s stated focus on sovereign AI and open models.
  • Prepare a client-delivery story. Have one example where you led 2–5 people, worked with a senior stakeholder and took something to production in weeks, not quarters.
  • Set alerts on both career sites. Track “Forward Deployed Engineer” on Deloitte India and Deloitte USI careers pages. If you are already at Deloitte, tell your talent team you want the open-model or platform FDE track.

Deloitte FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Application: Deloitte has not published an India FDE listing we could find. Watch Deloitte India and Deloitte USI careers pages for 'Forward Deployed Engineer' titles, often tagged with a platform (Palantir, ServiceNow, Databricks, Microsoft).
  • Technical assessment: Deloitte does not publish its FDE interview format. Its US listings screen on a named platform plus GenAI delivery, so expect questions on that platform, agentic workflows and RAG.
  • What is assessed: The listings stress production delivery in client environments, leading small pods and handling senior client stakeholders. Prepare examples of each.

Frequently asked questions

Does Deloitte hire Forward Deployed Engineers in India?

Deloitte India has said it plans to hire, train and certify forward deployed engineers through fiscal year 2027 for its Open Model Engineering practice, launched in India in September 2026. As of 9 November 2026 we could not find an India-located FDE job listing, so watch the careers pages.

What does a Deloitte Forward Deployed Engineer do?

Deloitte FDEs embed with client teams to move AI use cases into production quickly. Its US FDE page describes stages of about 3 days to a use case, 3 weeks to proven value and 3 months to production, and its listings centre on agentic workflows, RAG and enterprise integration.

What experience does a Deloitte FDE role need?

Deloitte's US listings range from 3+ years of engineering plus 4+ years of ServiceNow work, to 7+ years for a Lead FDE (Palantir), to 10–15+ years for an AI/Agentic FDE role. All ask for at least a year of GenAI or LLM delivery.

What is the Deloitte FDE salary in India?

Deloitte has not published it. As market context, Indeed India data cited by Masai School in October 2026 puts average FDE base pay in India at about ₹12 LPA, within a ₹9–16 LPA range.
