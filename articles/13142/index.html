Forward Deployed Engineer Salary (Nov 2026)

FDE Council · 2026-11-06 · 4 min read

Summary

This monthly report from FDE Council compiles Forward Deployed Engineer pay data from multiple sources. Levels.fyi self-reported data puts the US median total compensation at $225,000 (n=157), with a middle half of $172,000–$292,000. Job-posting studies show lower figures ($176,000–$200,000 midpoints) because they capture base salary only, excluding equity and bonus. The report breaks pay down by level and city, and cites Lightcast counts showing rapid demand growth: roughly 200 US FDE postings in 2024, about 1,200 in 2025, and over 5,200 in January–July 2026. Each figure is linked to its source, with data re-verified on November 6, 2026.

Key points

  • US median FDE total compensation is $225,000 per Levels.fyi (n=157).
  • Posting-based midpoints run $176,000–$200,000, base salary only.
  • Senior/staff posted base midpoint rose to $204,000 in November.
  • Lightcast counts over 5,200 US FDE postings in January–July 2026 alone.
  • Report is updated monthly with fully cited, archived sources.

November 2026 (2026-11)

Highlights from the November 2026 edition

  • Levels.fyi puts the US FDE median total compensation at $225,000 (n=157), up from $220,000 (n=160) in October. The middle half is unchanged at $172,000–$292,000.
  • Posting studies still center lower because they count base salary only. FDE Pulse’s midpoint moved from $186,000 to $176,000 (192 of 1,187 postings). Recruiting from Scratch is unchanged at a $200,000 midpoint.
  • Lightcast counts are unchanged: about 200 US FDE postings in 2024, about 1,200 in 2025, and more than 5,200 in January–July 2026.

Sources and methods are in the report. Data accessed November 6, 2026.

How much does a Forward Deployed Engineer make?

In the US, the most-cited figure is a $225,000 median total compensation (base, annualized stock and bonus) from Levels.fyi. The same page puts the median base salary at $175,000. Studies of job postings, which show base salary only, put the midpoint between $176,000 and $200,000. The gap is mostly equity and bonus, which postings leave out.

What do FDEs earn by level?

Posted base pay now rises more with seniority than it did in the October edition. FDE Pulse posted base midpoints, updated November 6, 2026:

Posted base salary midpoint by level ( FDE Pulse , US, November 6, 2026)
Level Posted base midpoint Postings October edition
Mid-level $160,000 121 $186,000 (n=119)
Senior / staff $204,000 37 $191,000 (n=32)
Manager / lead $202,000 29 $198,000 (n=34)

Junior / entry is $121,000 across 5 postings, too few to treat as a benchmark. Company-level total compensation is in the report and CSV.

How much do FDEs make in San Francisco and New York?

Levels.fyi reports a median total compensation of $225,000 in the New York City area (middle half $190K–$277K, n=58). The San Francisco Bay Area page could not be re-read on November 6; the October edition reported a $230,000 median there. Posted base midpoints are $213,000 in the Bay Area (41 postings) and $190,000 in New York (51 postings), from FDE Pulse.

How fast is demand for Forward Deployed Engineers growing?

Lightcast counts about 200 US FDE job postings in 2024, about 1,200 in 2025, and more than 5,200 in January–July 2026 alone.

Why do FDE salary sources disagree?

  • Posted ranges come from job postings, mostly under state pay-transparency laws; they show base salary at hire, not actual pay.
  • Self-reported data (e.g. Levels.fyi) is pay employees chose to submit; it skews toward larger, better-paying employers.
  • Posting studies aggregate many postings; sample sizes are shown where the source discloses them.

Method and cadence

Updated monthly in the third week. Each edition re-checks every source, keeps local currencies, and never overwrites a prior edition. Values that can’t be verified are left blank and explained.

The State of FDE 2026 survey will add anonymous, first-hand data on roles, scope and pay.
