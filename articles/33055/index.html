Forward Deployed Engineer at Consulting & IT Firms in India

FDE India · 2026-11-13 · 10 min read

Summary

This guide examines the largest forward deployed engineer (FDE) build-outs in India, all at IT services and consulting firms. TCS targets an FDE team of 1–1.5% of its workforce (roughly 5,900–8,900 people), Infosys plans 6,000 hires, LTM's AI 1000 programme aims for 1,000+ AI-certified engineers, and Deloitte will hire and certify FDEs through FY2027. Most seats will likely go to retrained internal staff rather than external hires. Based on six listings opened on 13 November 2026 at Accenture, Infosys and Kyndryl, experience requirements range from 2 to 12+ years and none posts a salary. UnearthInsight estimates 75–80% of jobs labelled FDE are closer to solutions or implementation engineering, so candidates should read the responsibilities, not the title. The article outlines four core KRAs: moving client projects from pilot to production, building agents and RAG on real client systems, owning client conversations, and governance plus mentoring.

Key points

  • TCS plans 5,900–8,900 FDEs; Infosys 6,000; LTM 1,000+.
  • Most seats will be filled via internal reskilling, not external hiring.
  • Listings ask for 2 to 12+ years of experience; none posts salary.
  • 75–80% of FDE-labelled jobs are really solutions or implementation roles.
  • Core duties: pilot-to-production, agents/RAG, client ownership, governance and mentoring.

At consulting and IT services firms in India, a forward deployed engineer is an engineer placed with a client to take an AI or digital project from pilot to production, usually by wiring AI into systems the client already runs. These firms have announced the largest FDE build-outs in the country, but many of the seats will go to existing employees who are retrained, and the experience asked in live listings ranges from 2 to 12 years.

This guide uses company announcements and six India listings from Accenture, Infosys and Kyndryl that we opened on 13 November 2026. It covers what services FDEs are expected to deliver, how much experience they need, what pay data exists, and how to get picked.

How big the services FDE push is

The numbers are large. TCS CEO K Krithivasan wants an FDE team of about 1–1.5% of the workforce, which Business Today worked out at 5,900 to 8,900 people based on the June 2026 headcount. Deccan Herald reports that Infosys will hire 6,000 forward deployed engineers over the next few years. LTM’s AI 1000 programme, announced in June 2026, aims to build a pool of more than 1,000 AI-certified engineers, including FDEs.

TCS’s figure is the top of its 1–1.5% target. Business Today (Jul 2026), Deccan Herald (2026), LTM (Jun 2026).

Others are moving too. HCLTech’s chief people officer Ram Sundararajan told Deccan Herald that elite engineers should join the FDE cohort over the next two to three years. Deloitte’s September 2026 release says it will hire, train and certify forward deployed engineers through fiscal year 2027 for its new Open Model Engineering practice, which covers clients in India and is built first on NVIDIA’s Nemotron models. Deccan Herald also names Wipro among the firms deploying FDEs.

Demand estimates are even bigger than the announced plans. Han Digital Group, quoted by Analytics India Magazine in September 2026, puts the requirement at 25,000 FDE roles across India’s IT-BPM industry in the first two quarters of FY27 alone. Xpheno counts a ready talent pool of only about 2,800.

Most seats will be filled from inside

Business Today notes it is unclear whether TCS will hire new people or retrain and redeploy existing staff. Vijay Vasanth of Gi Group told Deccan Herald that most FDE talent will initially come through internal reskilling. LTM’s programme shows how that works in practice: it finds high-potential engineers with an “AI Readiness Index”, trains them, validates them through hackathons and real use cases, and then places them into AI programmes.

If you already work at a services firm, that is good news. If you are outside, expect fewer external openings than the headline numbers suggest.

What the job titles hide

Read the duties before you trust the title. Gaurav Vasu of UnearthInsight estimates that 75–80% of Indian jobs labelled FDE are closer to solutions or implementation engineering, according to Analytics India Magazine. The services listings we opened show the same spread.

Services FDE titles span junior builders to principal architects. Listings opened 13 November 2026.

Accenture’s Bengaluru role is a good example of title drift. It asks the FDE to define architecture for full-stack enterprise apps with AI copilots, add services such as RAG retrieval into enterprise systems, and act as a link between traditional engineering teams and AI-native squads. The listing states that the role does not train models or design ML architectures. That is senior integration and architecture work, not the customer-embedded role you see at AI startups.

KRA 1: Take client projects from pilot to production

This is the core of the role everywhere. Deccan Herald describes services FDEs as engineers who embed with client teams and move AI projects from pilot to deployment, often onsite. It cites industry estimates that over 70% of emerging FDE demand comes from production AI work: putting AI into ERP, CRM and existing workflows.

Kyndryl’s listings break this into rapid prototyping of agentic AI, end-to-end delivery to production, and performance and reliability tuning. Infosys’ Senior FDE role asks for integration work across APIs, data pipelines and cloud.

KRA 2: Build agents and RAG on the client’s real systems

Services listings name specific tools. Kyndryl’s Gurugram role asked for at least one year of agentic AI work with frameworks such as LangGraph, Semantic Kernel, AutoGen or CrewAI, plus RAG, and listed evaluation platforms such as LangSmith as a plus. Its Principal role wants at least two agentic frameworks, one of them in production, and two or more years with vector databases and RAG.

Backend depth comes first, though. “You can’t do FDE unless you’ve got your backend sorted out first,” HCLTech’s Ashish Kumar Gupta told Analytics India Magazine. Accenture’s listings reflect that, leading with Java or .NET full-stack experience before any AI skills.

KRA 3: Own the client conversation

Senior services FDEs carry the relationship. Infosys asks its Senior FDE to lead workshops, discovery and solutioning with global client engineering teams, and to coordinate delivery across distributed teams. Kyndryl’s Principal FDE owns scoping and escalation across FDE squads and advises C-suite stakeholders. Krithivasan told Business Today that TCS’s edge is “deep knowledge of the customer environment”.

KRA 4: Governance, guardrails and mentoring

Services firms work inside regulated client estates, and their listings stress control. Accenture’s Bengaluru FDE enforces responsible-AI guardrails and architectural integrity. Its Chennai listing included architecture governance, design sessions and code reviews. Infosys and Kyndryl both ask senior FDEs to mentor others, and Kyndryl asks FDEs to feed field learnings back into its AI platforms.

Pay: what is and isn’t public

No services FDE listing we reviewed posts pay, and the firms have not published FDE bands. The available numbers are market-wide estimates:

  • Quess Corp CEO Kapil Joshi, citing industry reports to Deccan Herald: about ₹18–28 LPA at entry level, ₹30–50 LPA at mid level and ₹50 LPA or more for experienced FDEs.
  • Han Digital Group, via Analytics India Magazine: ₹25–50 LPA for 4–8 years of experience and ₹55–95 LPA or more at senior levels. UnearthInsight’s Vasu estimates FDE pay runs 30–60% above comparable engineering bands.

Market estimates across employer types, not services-firm offers. Quess via Deccan Herald (2026); Han Digital via AIM (Sep 2026).

Neither source breaks pay out for services firms, so ask about the band early in any FDE interview. For posted pay from real listings, see the FDE salary hub.

How to get an FDE role at a services or consulting firm

  • Get your backend story straight. Pick the stack your firm or target firm hires for (Java Spring Boot or .NET full stack in Accenture’s listings) and be ready to show production APIs, integrations and testing.
  • Ship one agent into a real system. Build a RAG or agent workflow with LangGraph or a similar framework that reads from and writes to a business system, with an evaluation set. Kyndryl’s listings ask for exactly this.
  • Volunteer for the AI pilot on your account. Internal programmes like LTM’s AI 1000 select engineers who are already delivering. Being the person who took a client pilot to production is the strongest signal you can give.
  • Collect client-facing evidence. Note every workshop you ran, requirement you scoped and escalation you handled. Senior services FDE roles weigh this as heavily as code.
  • Check the KRAs, not the label. Use the FDE Fit Check to see which duties your experience covers, and read each listing’s responsibilities to tell a real FDE role from a renamed implementation one. Our support engineer to FDE guide shows how to use implementation work as a stepping stone.
  • Know the firm’s plan. Read our pages on TCS, Infosys and Accenture before an internal or external interview, and ask how the firm’s FDE programme selects people.

Frequently asked questions

Do consulting and IT services firms in India hire forward deployed engineers?

Yes. TCS, Infosys, LTM, HCLTech, Wipro and Deloitte have all announced FDE teams or programmes, and Accenture, Infosys and Kyndryl have posted Forward Deployed Engineer roles in Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai and Gurugram. Many seats will be filled by retraining existing employees rather than external hiring.

What does a forward deployed engineer do at an IT services firm?

They embed with client teams to take AI and digital projects from pilot to production, usually by integrating AI into systems the client already runs, such as ERP, CRM and internal workflows. Senior roles add client scoping, architecture governance and mentoring other engineers.

What is the salary of an FDE at TCS, Infosys or Accenture in India?

None of the services FDE listings reviewed for this guide posts pay. Industry estimates put entry-level FDEs at ₹18–28 LPA and mid-level at ₹30–50 LPA (Quess, via Deccan Herald), and FDEs with 4–8 years at ₹25–50 LPA (Han Digital, via Analytics India Magazine). Treat these as market bands, not services-firm offers.

Can I move into an FDE role from inside an IT services company?

That is the main route these firms describe. LTM's AI 1000 programme identifies engineers with an AI readiness index, trains them and then places them into AI programmes, and TCS may retrain staff into its FDE team. Shipping a production AI integration on a client project is the strongest internal signal.
