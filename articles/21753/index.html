Is the 4.6x Increase in FDE Job Postings in One Year Real? Interpreting the Tech Job Report with 525 Listings

Original title: FDE求人が1年で4.6倍は本当か|テック求人レポートを525件で読み解く

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-11-11 · 17 min read

Summary

A November 2026 Lenny's Newsletter report, citing TrueUp data, claimed FDE job postings grew 4.6x year-over-year to about 1,500 while overall engineer postings grew only 2.5%. The FDE Talent editorial team cross-checked using 525 FDE postings from 133 company job boards: over six weeks the count stayed flat (526 to 525), but about a third of postings turned over, showing active hiring. Signs of rebranded sales roles were rare—only 7 postings offered OTE pay and 9 had sales-related titles. Remote-eligible postings were 8.2%, below the 14% for tech jobs overall, and 17 postings (9 companies) were based in Japan. The team notes the yearly growth claim and the roughly 1,000 unobserved postings cannot be verified, and postings concentrate heavily in Databricks, Palantir, and OpenAI, so candidates should judge postings by content rather than counts.

Key points

  • Lenny's Newsletter reported FDE postings grew 4.6x to ~1,500, citing TrueUp data.
  • The editorial team's 525 postings stayed flat over six weeks, with ~1/3 turnover.
  • Only 7 postings had OTE pay and 9 had sales titles, weakening the rebranding theory.
  • Remote-eligible FDE postings were 8.2%; 17 postings across 9 companies were Japan-based.
  • Postings concentrate in Databricks, Palantir, and OpenAI (43% of the total).

米国のプロダクト職向けニュースレター、Lenny's Newsletterが2026年11月10日に公開した求人市場レポートは、エンジニアの求人の伸びが止まりつつある中で、FDE(Forward Deployed Engineer/フォワードデプロイドエンジニア)の求人が過去1年で4.6倍に増えたと報じました[1]。同じレポートは、この増加にはセールスエンジニアなどの職種がFDEと名前を変えた分が混ざっている可能性が高い、とも書いています[1]。

People considering a career move to FDE will first want to know: "Are FDE job postings really increasing? Or is the increase just sales roles being rebranded?" In this article, we organize the report's FDE figures and then use the job postings from 133 company job boards that the editorial team has been collecting weekly since September 29, 2026 (75 companies and 525 postings as of November 10) to answer what can and cannot be verified. For an overview of the FDE role, see What is an FDE?.

Did FDE Job Postings Really Grow 4.6x in a Year?

1年で4.6倍という数字は、レポートが使ったTrueUpのデータ(9,000社超のテック企業の求人を集計)によるもので、編集部のデータでは1年間の伸びを確かめられません[1]。編集部が確かめられたのは、FDEの採用で知られた会社に限ると、この6週間は求人の数が横ばいのまま、中身が3分の1ほど入れ替わっていることです。増え方の勢いは数字だけでは判断できませんが、求人の出し入れは活発に続いている、と編集部は読みます。

Where Does the Report Place FDE in the Tech Job Market?

レポートの軸は「エンジニアの需要が頭打ちになった」ことです。TrueUpのデータでは、テック企業のエンジニアの求人の伸びは2024年に29%、2025年に15%、直近1年は2.5%に落ちました[1]。転職の記録を集めるLive Data Technologiesのデータでは、米国でソフトウェアエンジニアとして採用された人は直近12か月(2025年5月〜2026年4月)で約11.3万人と、2022年のピーク(約23.9万人)の半分以下です[1]。

その中でFDEは、エンジニアの職種の中で注目すべき新しい伸びとして取り上げられています。レポートによれば、FDEの求人は長く世界で数十件(主にPalantir)しかなく、2025年に増え始め、過去1年で4.6倍の約1,500件になりました[1]。FDEに次いで伸びているのはセキュリティエンジニアで、反対にモバイルとフロントエンドのエンジニアの需要はこの1年でほぼ半分になったとしています[1]。

What changed in six weeks across the 525 postings tracked by the editorial team?

The editorial team has been counting weekly job postings containing "Forward Deployed," "FDE," or "FDSE" in the job title from the public job boards (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, etc.) of 133 companies, selected mainly from companies with a track record of hiring FDEs. From September 29 to November 10, 2026, the count moved between 503 and 526, remaining essentially flat.

Even though the count stayed the same, the contents changed substantially. Treating postings with the same company, job title, and location as identical (520 postings on both dates under this counting method), of the postings present on September 29, 347 remained through November 10, 173 had ended, and 173 new ones had appeared. The new postings include some where the job title or location was rewritten while keeping the same URL. Comparing by URL, 165 were new and 166 had ended, so about one-third turned over either way. The most new postings came from Databricks (24), OpenAI (16), Cognition (11), and Anduril (10), while the most ended postings came from Databricks (23), Palantir (15), and ElevenLabs (10).

What should you watch out for when reading posting counts?

The number of postings does not match the number of people companies want to hire. Even in the editorial team's 525 postings, the counts are skewed toward companies with few postings.

  • Databricks 117件(22%): 業界別・地域別にSr. FDEの求人を細かく分けて出している。部門名は「Professional Services Operations」で、導入サービスの組織に置かれている[7]
  • Palantir, 73 postings (14%): The company considered the prototype of the FDE, it lists 41 different job titles across combinations of role type (government, commercial, new grad, infrastructure, etc.) and location
  • OpenAI, 38 postings (7%): Splits FDE postings by industry and location

上位3社で525件の43%を占めます。4位のWonderful(37件)は、同じFDEの求人を日本を含む36の国・地域に分けて掲載しています[4]。1つの求人を地域ごとに分けて出す会社が増えれば、採用したい人数が同じでも件数は増えます。レポートの約1,500件や4.6倍も、求人の掲載件数の数字として読むのが安全です。

Where are FDE roles assumed to be based?

レポートは、テック求人がベイエリア(サンフランシスコ周辺)に集まり、リモートの求人が過去最低になったと報じました。テック求人全体のうちベイエリアは15%、エンジニアの求人では5件に1件です。リモート可はテック求人の14%、エンジニアの求人でも14%でした[1]。編集部の525件で同じ項目を数えると、定義の違いを踏まえた目安ですが、FDEはリモートがさらに少ない傾向です。

Of the 43 remote-available postings, 12 were from Databricks and 7 from Cresta; most companies assume FDEs work at customer sites or their own offices. Given the nature of FDE work, which involves going into customer sites, the scarcity of remote roles is natural. The 17 Japan-based postings were 3 each from Databricks, OpenAI, and Palantir, 2 each from Notion and Sakana AI, and 1 each from Cognition, Cursor, Rogo, and Wonderful.

What should Japanese job seekers take away from this report?

FDEという肩書きの求人の数より、求人票の中身で応募先を選ぶことです。エンジニアの採用全体が縮む中でFDEの求人が増えているのはレポートの示すとおりですが[1]、件数は少数の会社に偏り、リモートの求人も1割未満でした。

  • Use job postings to tell whether a role is sales-type: Check whether compensation is OTE (commission included), whether the requirements include experience writing production code, and which department the role is in. Don't judge by the department name alone
  • Look at the number of companies, not postings: A single company may post dozens of openings per region, so your options are not as numerous as the posting count suggests. As of November 10, 2026, Japan-based FDE postings came from 9 companies and totaled 17
  • Don't expect remote work: Remote-available FDE postings are under 10%. If you want to join an overseas company from Japan, you'll need a Japan-based posting or an application that assumes relocation

Requirements and language conditions for Japan-based FDE postings are covered in Comparison of FDE postings in Japan, a list of the latest postings in FDE job postings list, and how to approach a job change in FDE career change guide. Indeed's data on growing FDE demand in India is covered in FDE demand grows 19x in India.

What can't be determined from this data?

The biggest issue is that the editorial team cannot verify the 4.6x one-year growth with its own data. In addition, the following points are outside the scope of this article's tally.

  • One-year growth: The editorial team's observations began on September 29, 2026, so the 4.6x one-year growth cannot be verified. TrueUp's FDE page could not be retrieved in the editorial team's environment, and the definition of FDE used in the report's count (the range of job titles) could not be confirmed either
  • 採用された人数: 求人の件数は、実際に採用された人数とは違う。レポートは、エンジニアやPMでは求人が増えても採用数は減っていると指摘している[1]
  • The latter half of the report: The seventh point, that tech job growth is shifting to roles outside software, is in the paid section and was not verified by the editorial team
  • Companies not observed: Postings from companies not among the editorial team's 133 were not counted. Whether rebranded sales roles are mixed in cannot be determined for those companies

Summary

Lenny's Newsletterの2026年11月のレポートは、エンジニアの求人の伸びが2.5%まで落ちる中で、FDEの求人が過去1年で4.6倍・約1,500件になったと報じました[1]。編集部が観測する133社の525件では、件数はこの6週間ほぼ横ばいで、3分の1が入れ替わりながら採用が続いています。営業の報酬体系をとるFDE求人は少数で、FDEの求人の多くはエンジニアとしての実装の経験を求めていました。一方で、1年の伸びと、編集部が観測していない約1,000件の中身はこの集計からは分かりません。FDEを目指す人は、求人の件数の伸びよりも、応募したい会社の求人票で報酬の形と実装の要件を確かめることをおすすめします。

Frequently asked questions

QAre FDE postings really increasing?

Lenny's Newsletter's November 10, 2026 report, citing TrueUp data, states that FDE postings grew 4.6x over the past year to about 1,500. On the job boards of the 133 companies the editorial team observes, the count stayed around 525 from September 29 to November 10, 2026, but about one-third were replaced with new postings. The one-year growth cannot be verified with the editorial team's data.

QAre FDE postings just a rebranding of sales engineers?

The report notes the possibility that rebranded roles are mixed in, but this is the author's speculation. Of the 525 postings the editorial team counted on November 10, 2026, 7 had commission-included (OTE) salaries and 9 had sales-related terms in the job title; most postings required software development experience or experience building systems that run in production. However, postings from companies the editorial team does not observe are unknown.

QCan FDEs work remotely?

Remote-available postings are not common. Of the 525 FDE postings the editorial team counted on November 10, 2026, 43 (8.2%) included remote as a work location, lower than the 14% remote rate for tech postings overall shown in the report. Most postings assume working at customer sites or the company's own offices.

QHow many FDE postings allow working in Japan?

Of the 525 postings the editorial team counted on November 10, 2026, Japan-based postings came from 9 companies and totaled 17: 3 each from Databricks, OpenAI, and Palantir, 2 each from Notion and Sakana AI, and 1 each from Cognition, Cursor, Rogo, and Wonderful.
