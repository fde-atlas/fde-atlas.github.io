Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs: 260 Roles Analyzed (2026)

Nasser Ghanemzadeh · 2026-10-12 · 7 min read

Summary

Drawing on job-board data from Ashby, Greenhouse, and Lever collected October 12, 2026, the author analyzes 260 distinct forward deployed engineer roles across 44 companies (382 postings). Databricks, Palantir, and OpenAI account for half the roles. The median US posted base salary range is $180,000–$250,208, with a median midpoint of $216,104. Over half of roles mention travel, most commonly up to 50%. Postings emphasize Python, agents, TypeScript, and evals over model training, and 42% of roles are non-engineering positions, showing the title spreading to bankers, lawyers, and strategists.

Key points

  • 44 companies and 260 distinct roles analyzed; Databricks, Palantir, and OpenAI hold half the roles.
  • Median US posted base midpoint is $216,104; 88% of US engineering roles publish salary ranges.
  • 56% of roles mention travel; the most common stated figure is up to 50% of the time.
  • Top skills: Python (67%), agents (55%), TypeScript/JavaScript (54%), evals (48%); fine-tuning only 13%.
  • 42% of roles are non-engineering, extending the model to bankers, lawyers, and product managers.

The numbers

  • 44 companies, 260 distinct roles, 382 postings. Databricks, Palantir, and OpenAI alone account for half of the roles.
  • Median US posted base range: $180,000 to $250,208. Median midpoint $216,104 across 74 engineering roles; $220,552 if each company counts once.
  • 88% of US engineering roles post a salary range, so the pay figures cover nearly the whole US market in this sample.
  • 56% mention travel; 42% put a number on it. The most common figure is up to 50% of the time.
  • 58% of roles are in the US, 40% outside it. London has more than any other city outside the US, with 36.
  • Python (67%), agents (55%), TypeScript or JavaScript (54%), and evals (48%) are what engineering postings ask for most.
  • A quarter of engineering roles mention a security clearance. Government and defense are a large share of the work.
  • 42% of roles are not engineering roles. Companies now hire forward deployed bankers, lawyers, strategists, architects, and product managers.

What it pays

Pay figures cover the 74 distinct engineering roles in the US that post a salary range in US dollars, which is 88% of them. They are base salary ranges as posted, not offers or total compensation; equity and bonus come on top at most of these companies. Where a posting gives one range per location, the first range is used.

  • Median range: $180,000 to $250,208.
  • Median midpoint: $216,104, and the middle half of roles fall between $170,000 and $242,500.
  • Company-weighted median midpoint: $220,552 across 26 companies, so the three biggest hirers don't set the number on their own.
  • By level: staff and principal roles have a median midpoint of $250,000 (5 roles), senior roles $216,104 (15), and roles with no level in the title $215,000 (53). Level barely moves the midpoint below staff.
  • Lowest and highest: $90,000 at the bottom of a Palantir US Government security role, $445,000 at the top of OpenAI's Forward Deployed Security Engineer range.

Where the jobs are

152 roles (58%) are in the US and 103 (40%) are outside it; 5 are remote with no country given. Outside the US, London leads with 36 roles, then Tokyo (9), Singapore (8), Paris (7), and Munich (6). 32% of roles allow remote work in at least one location, which is low for software jobs in 2026 and fits a role defined by being on site.

Palantir accounts for half of the roles that mention a clearance, followed by the government and public-sector teams at Databricks, Cohere, OpenAI, and Vannevar Labs.

The title is spreading

Forward deployed started as an engineering title. In October 2026 it is a way of working that companies attach to other jobs.

Rogo hires forward deployed bankers and investors. Eudia hires forward deployed lawyers. Harvey hires forward deployed finance engineers for private credit, buyouts, and real estate. Cursor hires forward deployed strategists, Notion forward deployed architects, and Scale AI and Cresta forward deployed product managers. The common thread is the one in the original Palantir model: a specialist who sits with the customer and makes the product work inside their business.

Most engineering titles carry no level at all (113 of 151). Of those that do, senior (25) and staff or principal (11) far outnumber junior (2). The entry points are internships and new-grad programs, 19 roles, nearly all at Palantir.

What it means

For founders. A forward deployed engineer is priced by OpenAI, Databricks, and Palantir. A median posted midpoint above $216,000, before equity, is hard to justify for a startup with one or two AI workflows to ship; the three ways to get one compares hiring with contracting and a fixed-scope Sprint.

For engineers. The postings describe the job plainly: Python and TypeScript, agents and evals, a customer you sit with, and a suitcase. How to become a forward deployed engineer turns that list into a path.

Method and limits

  • Source. The public job-board APIs of Ashby, Greenhouse, and Lever, queried on October 12, 2026 for 144 AI and software companies. 100 had a board on one of the three; 44 had at least one matching role.
  • Match. Any title containing "forward deployed" (with or without a hyphen) or "FDE." Postings listed on two boards were counted once.
  • Distinct roles. One title at one company, counted once across locations. Shares are of the 260 distinct roles unless a section says otherwise.
  • Pay. Taken from the board's structured salary field where it exists (Ashby, Lever), otherwise from the first US dollar range in the posting text (Greenhouse). Annual base only; hourly and non-USD figures are excluded.
  • Travel and skills. Keyword matches in the posting text, checked by hand against a sample of postings.
  • What it misses. Companies on other hiring systems, such as Workday at most large enterprises, and roles that do the work without the title. It is a single day's snapshot, and three companies carry half the sample, so treat company-level patterns as the stronger finding.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a forward deployed engineer make in 2026?

In US job postings collected on October 12, 2026, the median posted base salary range for a forward deployed engineer was $180,000 to $250,208, with a median midpoint of $216,104. The middle half of roles had midpoints between $170,000 and $242,500. Posted ranges are base pay only; equity and bonus come on top at most of these companies.

Which companies hire forward deployed engineers?

On October 12, 2026, 44 companies had live forward deployed roles on public job boards. Databricks, Palantir, and OpenAI had the most, and together accounted for half of the 260 distinct roles. Cohere, Scale AI, Cursor, Cresta, Snorkel AI, Notion, Shield AI, Stripe, and Anthropic followed.

How much do forward deployed engineers travel?

56% of the roles mention travel, and 42% state a percentage. Among those, the most common figure is up to 50% of the time, and 57% allow for 50% or more. OpenAI's postings mostly say up to 50%, Databricks' mostly 20%, and Palantir's 25 to 75% within the country.
