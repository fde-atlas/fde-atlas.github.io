Founding Forward-Deployed Engineer | Closure | Remote, US

Zero G Talent · 2026-03-24 · 2 min read

Summary

Closure, a Y Combinator- and CRV-backed startup, builds AI digital analysts for U.S. police departments, prosecutors, and other criminal justice agencies, helping them manage evidence overload in investigations such as cold cases and homicides. It is hiring a founding Forward Deployed Engineer to work directly with users, often in person, build non-scalable one-off analyses and integrations, and translate customer pain into product decisions. Candidates must pass FBI background checks and CJIS clearance. The role prefers NYC or the Bay Area, allows remote work for exceptional candidates, and requires 25-75% travel. Compensation ranges from $130k to $215k plus founding-level equity and performance bonuses.

About Closure

Closure helps law enforcement search evidence and solve crime. We build digital analysts that support public servants at every level of criminal justice.

Public servants across the U.S. use Closure to expedite investigations into cold cases, homicides, gang conflicts, sexual assault, and other serious offenses, as well as critical administrative support.

We’re live with police departments, prosecutors, sheriffs, state investigative agencies, and other criminal justice stakeholders across the country, reducing evidence overload across the criminal justice system.

Closure is an intentionally tight-knit team of ambitious ex-Palantir and ex-IDF engineers and operators. We operate with high speed, high integrity, high autonomy, and low ego (or so we like to think), backed by reputable investors including Y Combinator, CRV, Pathlight, and others.

Read more about our work, here:

  • Axios, AI is reshaping police detective work, starting with cold cases
  • Comstock’s Magazine, “Murder: Solved. Local investigators see new hope with Closure AI platform”
  • Breaking Battlegrounds Podcast, Interview with Aaron Zelinger & DA Dan Dow, San Luis Obispo County
  • Shots Fired Podcast, “Being a Cop in Alaska is Nothing like you think”

Role Description:

  • Live With Users: You are thrilled to apply your engineering skills to solving real-world user problems, directly with users (and frequently in-person).
  • Pre-Scale Problem Solver: You are a strong engineer, but you are comfortable building things that do not “scale”; whether a one-off analysis or an integration.
  • Product Thinker & Tinkerer: Your experience on the frontlines with customers will directly translate into product roadmaps. You are an executor who turns customer pain into PRs, or clear understandings of what/how we must build.

About You

  • Mission-Driven: Excited to contribute to U.S. government competence and public safety.
  • Owner-Builder Mindset: Bias toward action, thrives in extremely fast-paced environments. Looking to do the best work of your life.
  • Comfort with Ambiguity: We are an early-stage startup with many exciting questions to be answered; you run toward unasked and unanswered questions.

Location:

  • New York City (Preferred)
  • Bay Area, California (hybrid work arrangements available)
  • Remote with regular travel for exceptional candidates
  • Travel: 25-75% of the time

Additional Details:

  • Comp
    • Salary: $130k-$215k, with potential for significant adjustments based on experience and ability
    • Equity: Meaningful ownership stake at founding-hire levels
    • Bonuses based on company performance
  • Benefits:
    • We contribute 3% to your 401(k), regardless of your own contribution
    • Unlimited PTO
    • Health, Dental (Direct or Reimbursed)
    • Short- & Long-term Disability Insurance
    • Life Insurance
    • Discounted Gym & Class Membership
  • Criminal Justice:
    • Criminal Justice Ready: Given the sensitivity of our work, we support employees through FBI background checks / CJIS clearance process as needed

Closure is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other legally protected characteristic.
