Building AI that runs inside your company, right in your workplace. A consulting service covering everything from implementation to adoption.

FDE Consulting is a service that supports the introduction of generative AI as a single engagement, from implementation through production operation to adoption. This page explains the target audience, process, team structure, and how fees are determined. For an explanation of the FDE support model itself, see "What is FDE"; for an overview of our business, including case studies and training, see "Our Services".

For Who This Is For

For companies in these situations

Our clients are executives and initiative leaders at companies aiming to move beyond the testing phase of generative AI and reach a state where it can be used in real business operations.

Beyond PoC / From Testing to Production

You have run PoCs or trial implementations but have not yet reached the stage of delivering results in real business operations. You want to leverage the assets from testing and get all the way to operation.

Adoption / Toward Actual Use

You have introduced tools or an internal AI environment, but usage has not taken hold in the field. You want to integrate AI in a way that it is naturally used as part of daily work.

Data / Without Sending Data Outside

Due to the nature of the information you handle, data cannot leave the company. You want to build AI that runs within your own environment and data.

Service What We Provide

Building AI that runs inside your company, right in the field

An FDE embeds themselves in your workplace and consistently handles everything from understanding the business to implementation, operation, and adoption.

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) grasps your business, data, and constraints on-site, identifies application areas directly tied to value, and builds AI agents and workflows in the actual environment. Rather than ending as a concept on paper, our support is premised on reaching from the testing stage all the way to production operation.

"Runs inside your company" carries two meanings. The first is technical: it runs on your data, and we can accommodate configurations that keep data in-house as required. The second is organizational: we transfer operating procedures, evaluation criteria, and improvement processes to your side, and develop your staff, so that improvement continues under your own hands even after the engagement ends. "A good FDE makes themselves unnecessary." This philosophy is built into how we design our support.

Our particular strength is the AI transformation of management and administrative functions such as accounting, management information aggregation, recruiting, M&A, and store operations. Combining practical expertise in management and finance with generative AI implementation capability forms the foundation of our support.

Areas of Service

Designing the Management Agenda

We define application areas for generative AI based on contribution to corporate value and business outcomes. From ROI estimation and prioritization to execution planning. We can also handle issue structuring at the management meeting and board level.

On-Site Implementation

Centered on coding agents such as Claude Code and Codex, we build AI agents and workflows in real environments. We also support low-code development with tools like Dify and n8n, handling everything from testing to production operation.

Adoption and Internalization

From establishing operational structures and governance design such as usage guidelines, to building citizen development environments and developing the people who run them. We create a state where value continues after implementation.

Process How We Work

From the first consultation to adoption

Rather than fixing a grand vision first, we proceed by verifying working artifacts in short cycles.

  • First consultation: In a 30-minute online consultation, we ask about your current situation and challenges. Apply via the contact form.
  • Understanding the field: We grasp the business, data, and constraints, and identify application areas directly tied to value.
  • Proposal and estimate: We propose the scope, duration, team structure, and cost of support individually, tailored to your situation.
  • Implementation and production operation: We build with your actual data and environment, verify as we go, and move into production operation.
  • Adoption and handover: We transfer procedures, evaluation, and improvement processes to your company, and hand over a state where things run in-house.

Team Structure from Consultation to Adoption

Management and implementation, as one team

Professionals with practical experience in management and business operations, together with engineers responsible for implementation, handle everything from understanding the business to implementation and adoption as a single engagement.

In the first consultation, we ask about your current situation and challenges, and share our assessment of potential application areas.

Works Actual Engagements

Examples of implementations running in the field

A Manager AI Supporting Store Operations

For a major hospitality group operating hundreds of stores nationwide, we built a RAG that lets staff pull up operational know-how on the spot. Store operations are gradually being handed over to AI.

Automating M&A-Related Work and Company-Wide Rollout

We implemented research, analysis, and document creation tasks as roughly 100 AI workflows and rolled them out for company-wide operation. The system remains in place even as people change, and improvement continues.

Establishing an Internal Dedicated Generative AI Environment

An internal AI environment that had been introduced but not used was established in the field through training and use-case design. Veteran expertise is being preserved for the company in a form that will not be lost.

Pricing Fees and Contract Models

Designed individually to fit your situation

Our basic model is monthly ongoing support, and we also offer project-based support divided by theme. Fees are quoted individually according to the scope and duration of support; after learning about your situation in the first consultation, we present an estimate together with our assessment of application areas.

The main factors affecting the estimate are as follows.

  • The number and complexity of the target business processes
  • The integration environment for data and internal systems
  • Security requirements (self-hosted configurations, etc.)
  • Team structure and duration of support

Our basic approach is to start small and expand while verifying results.

FAQ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions when considering our service

How much does it cost?

We provide individual estimates according to the scope and duration of support. After learning about your situation in the first consultation, we present an estimate together with our assessment of application areas.

How long does it take?

It depends on the target business processes and scope. Our basic approach is to first show something working in the field within a short period, then proceed to production operation while verifying results.

We cannot send data outside the company. Can you accommodate that?

Yes, we can support that. We have experience building self-hosted configurations that run entirely within your own environment, as well as configurations where all processing is completed within domestic regions. We will start by working with you to organize your data handling requirements.

Can we request your services even if we are stuck at the PoC stage?

Yes. Our support centers on bridging the distance from the validation stage to actually generating value. We design solutions that build on your existing validation assets.

How are you different from strategy consulting firms or development companies?

The endpoint of our responsibility is different. We do not end our support with proposals or deliverables; we take responsibility through implementation, putting the solution into operation, and handing it over in a state where your team can continue improving it on your own.

Where should we start?

Please start with a 30-minute online consultation. It is fine if you simply want help organizing where to begin. We will listen to your current situation and propose an approach that fits your company. To apply, please use our contact form.

Contact: Initial Consultation

The initial consultation is free of charge. It is fine if you simply want help organizing where to begin. Please reach out via the form, and our representative will reply to schedule a date and time.
