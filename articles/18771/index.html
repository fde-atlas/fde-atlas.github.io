Summary

This is the company profile page of Gomumari Inc., a Japanese startup founded in May 2025 in Tokyo that calls itself an "FDE company" — forward deployed engineers embed in customer sites to build AI agents. Its stated vision is a world where humans and AI work together, expanding what people and companies can do rather than headcount. Gomumari commits to repeatedly transforming client organizations as AI evolves, refusing to cap ambitions at human limits and treating AI errors as things to fix permanently. Case results include automating a cost-comparison report from roughly 7 hours to about 40 seconds with around 70,000 cells matching human output exactly, making about 2,000 design-change documents searchable by item, and having an AI agent handle personnel assignment notices with all extracted codes correct. Services span four offerings: specialized-work AI-BPO, AI agent implementation, products, and development/verification/diagnostics. Internally, AI agents handle sales, delivery, back-office, and management dashboards while humans decide what to solve. CEO Yuta Murayama previously worked in ERP, SaaS sales, and generative AI development.

Key points

  • Gomumari Inc. is a Tokyo FDE company founded in May 2025 that builds AI agents on customer sites.
  • Its philosophy: don't limit scope to human capacity, and raise AI like a colleague by permanently fixing errors.
  • A 7-hour cost analysis was cut to ~40 seconds, with ~70,000 cells matching human output exactly.
  • Other cases: ~2,000 design-change documents made searchable; AI agent handled personnel notices with all codes correct.
  • Four services: AI-BPO, AI agent implementation, products, and development/verification/diagnostics.

A company that builds companies that work with AI agents. Gomumari is an FDE company that embeds itself in our clients' operations and builds AI agents.

The World We Aim For

To create a world where humans and AI work together, and where both people and companies can grow bigger.

When humans and AI work together, what each person can do and what a company can do both grow. What grows is not the number of people, but the scale of what can be done.

Our Role

To continually transform companies into companies that work with AI agents.

Every time AI evolves, the scope and ways of working with AI agents change. So transforming a company never ends after just one round. Once one change has been implemented, when AI evolves again, we think through the next change together.

How We Behave

  • We don't set our standards by the limits of human labor. We don't define the scope of work by the amount of human hands available.
  • We raise AI as a colleague. When AI makes a mistake, we fix it on the spot and in a way that prevents it from happening again.

Results

Track Record

B2B Company

A cost comparison document that took about 7 hours to analyze—table creation automated to about 40 seconds

Of a monthly task set that took about 7 hours per analysis cycle, creating the comparison tables now takes about 40 seconds via program execution. Approximately 2,000 rows and 70,000 cells verified across two models matched the human-made finished product perfectly

Large Enterprise

Making years of accumulated design change documents searchable by item

Approximately 2,000 documents were ingested, with all columns verified against the originals for a perfect match. For rewriting into the current format, all approximately 200 files were read back and verified the same way

Large Enterprise

Turning manual data entry for HR personnel appointments into AI agent work

When tested with actual appointment notices (including scans), all department and appointment type codes identified by the AI agent matched the correct codes. Items the agent couldn't determine are returned to the person in charge
