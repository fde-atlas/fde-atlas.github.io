Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (3/5, Sep 13, 2023): Believed in the company, but not so

Taro · 2023-09-13 · 2 min read

Summary

A Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer with under a year at the company rated it 3/5 in an anonymous review. They praise the fast pace, smart colleagues, and growth opportunities, but criticize compensation: no 401k matching, no bonuses, no raises tied to promotions, and long hours. They also found the FDE role technically unfulfilling and noted a company-wide compensation review took two years, with only a fraction of employees getting raises. The page also collects interview experiences: a 2025 process with five rounds including technical and systems design interviews, a 2022 candidate who passed but found the role filled, and a 2017 account of repeated recruiter no-shows. The reviewer warns management that unfair pay is driving talent to competitors.

Key points

  • Current FDE rates Palantir 3/5: great growth, poor pay.
  • No 401k match, no bonuses, no raise on promotion.
  • Comp review took two years; few employees got raises.
  • Interview loop has five rounds, including systems design.
  • Other candidates report filled roles and recruiter no-shows.

3.0

Pros

Fast-paced, very smart coworkers, and overall, the opportunity to grow is quite amazing.

Cons

No 401k matching, no bonuses, and no compensation increase with promotions or title promotions. Long work hours.

Life as a Forward Deployed Engineer can be technically unfulfilling. It took 2 years for a company-wide compensation review to happen, and only a fraction of the company got a raise then.

Advice to Management

Please treat your employees better and fairly compensate them. You are losing great talent to peer companies because of this.
