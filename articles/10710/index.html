Accenture Launches Forward Deployed Engineering Program with Workday to Reinvent Work for the Agentic AI Era

Accenture · 2026-10-13 · 4 min read

Summary

Accenture and Workday announced a joint forward deployed engineering (FDE) program on October 13, 2026, aimed at moving AI agents from pilots into production in HR and finance functions. Accenture engineers will work within clients' actual processes, data, policies, and controls, combining Accenture's industry and engineering expertise with Workday's agentic platform to speed up deployment and ROI. The program also creates a feedback loop: Accenture's experience deploying Workday's agentic capabilities at enterprise scale will inform Workday's product development. Executives from both companies stressed reliability, noting that near-accuracy is insufficient when AI handles payroll and financial closes.

Key points

  • Accenture and Workday launched a joint FDE program
  • Focus: putting AI agents into production in HR and finance

Full text

New joint program combines Accenture’s transformation and engineering capabilities with Workday’s trusted agentic platform to help global organizations put AI agents to work in HR and finance and accelerate measurable value from AI

NEW YORK; Oct. 13, 2026 – Accenture (NYSE: ACN), in collaboration with Workday (NASDAQ: WDAY), the trusted agentic platform for HR, finance, and IT, today announced a joint forward deployed engineering (FDE) program to help organizations reinvent how work gets done in the age of AI.

Through the FDE program, Accenture and Workday will work alongside client HR and finance teams to bring agentic capabilities into production, helping organizations get Workday agents up and running faster and see ROI on their investments sooner. The teams will combine Accenture’s function, industry, and engineering expertise with Workday’s trusted agentic platform capabilities, working within clients’ actual processes, data, policies and controls. The program will benefit from Accenture’s own experience deploying and extending Workday’s agentic capabilities at enterprise scale. These insights will help inform Workday product innovation and the approaches Accenture brings to clients.

“AI is fundamentally changing work, the workforce and how organizations operate,” said Julie Sweet, chair and CEO, Accenture. “Together with Workday, we will help our clients reinvent HR and finance for a future where people and AI agents collaborate in new ways of working that create measurable business outcomes and drive growth."

The collaboration will go beyond client engagements. Accenture engineers will work with Workday’s product and technology teams so they can build new agentic capabilities on the Workday platform, creating a continuous feedback loop between client transformation and product innovation. Accenture and Workday will put these new agentic capabilities to work in real enterprise environments, learn from their use, and bring those insights back into the platform and to clients.

"When AI is helping run payroll and close the books, almost right isn’t good enough,” said Aneel Bhusri, co-founder, CEO and chair, Workday. "Our customers already trust Workday with their people and their money. And now with Accenture, we’ll help them move from experimenting with AI to running their business on it, faster and with greater confidence."

The FDE program expands Accenture and Workday’s longstanding relationship, helping organizations reinvent HR and finance for a world where people and AI agents increasingly work together.
