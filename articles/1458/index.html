ElevenLabs — Meet our Forward Deployed Engineers

ElevenCreative · 2025-11-20 · 2 min read

Summary

In this ElevenLabs post, Alex Holt, who leads enterprise engineering (previously at Palantir), explains how the company's Forward Deployed Engineers help enterprises deploy AI agents at scale. He identifies three barriers: weak post-deployment feedback loops, fragmented enterprise tech stacks, and trust/compliance requirements. FDEs embed with customer teams from day one, handling design, integration, scaling, and compliance, and are accountable for measurable outcomes like resolution speed and containment rate. Deployments follow SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, and HIPAA, with zero-data-retention and regional data residency options. The engagement model spans implementation, stabilization, and ongoing collaboration.

Key points

  • FDEs embed in customer teams from day one, building within customer infrastructure
  • Deployments are tied to measurable KPIs like resolution speed and containment rate
  • Standards include SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, plus zero-data-retention options

Building AI that works at enterprise scale is hard. Most know the potential, but few have the capability to deploy it reliably. Siloed systems, fragmented infrastructure, and compliance requirements slow progress.

Our Forward Deployed Engineers have helped hundreds of enterprises launch AI agents that deliver value faster.

Why enterprises struggle to scale AI

Capability gap

AI models are powerful, but success depends on what happens after deployment. Without tight feedback loops, iteration, and human oversight, even high-performing systems lose accuracy and reliability over time.

Fragmented systems

Enterprises run on complex tech stacks - CRM systems, contact centers, data lakes, APIs. Connecting these reliably is often the hardest part. Disconnected systems limit automation and make it difficult to measure ROI across teams.

Trust and compliance risks

For enterprise AI, trust isn’t optional. Security, data control, and regulatory compliance are mandatory from the start. Without rigorous governance and transparent reporting, projects face approval delays and adoption barriers.

How Forward Deployed Engineers close the gap

Embedded from day one

Our engineers work side-by-side with your technical teams, building directly within your infrastructure. With deep expertise in ElevenLabs Agents and enterprise infrastructure, our engineers manage every part of implementation - from system design and integration to scaling, change management, and compliance.

Measurable accountability

Each deployment is tied to clear outcomes like faster resolution, higher containment rate, or better customer experience. Once live, we stay engaged, continuously monitoring and optimizing until results are proven.

Enterprise-grade standards

Forward Deployed Engineers follow SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, and HIPAA standards. Deployments can be configured with zero-data-retention and regional data residency, ensuring full alignment with your organization’s privacy and security policies.

How we work

  • Implementation - We scope, build, and deploy AI systems with clear governance and KPIs.
  • Stabilization - We monitor performance, fine-tune outcomes, and transfer operational best practices to your teams.
  • Continuous collaboration - We remain a long-term partner, refining models and integrations as your AI footprint expands.

From ambition to measurable impact

Forward Deployed Engineers bridge the gap between research and reality. By embedding engineers directly within enterprise teams, we accelerate deployment, reduce friction, and ensure every system launched drives measurable business outcomes.
