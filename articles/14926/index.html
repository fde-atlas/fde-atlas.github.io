Hiring Insights, Salary Trends & Market Signals

Re:Sourced · 2026-04-08 · 11 min read

Summary

This is a content index from Re:Sourced, an Australian recruitment agency, listing its market-signal reports, salary guides and hiring-process advice for 2026. It covers forward deployed engineers (FDE), AI, data, cloud, security and platform roles, with pay bands broken down by city (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane) and seniority. Key claims: FDE is the breakout role of 2026 that few know how to hire; AI engineer demand rose 34% quarter over quarter; security clearance now commands a premium above seniority in defence-adjacent work; identical titles can vary by AUD 30k depending on the employer's ladder. It also covers equity valuation, contract-versus-permanent maths, interview loop design and offer-decline data. Useful for anyone benchmarking Australian tech hiring or compensation.

Key points

  • FDE is framed as 2026's breakout role that almost nobody knows how to hire for.
  • AI engineer demand rose 34% QoQ; software salaries are stabilising after 18 months of compression.
  • Salary bands are given per city and level for AI, data, cloud, security and software roles.
  • Security clearance (NV1/NV2) is worth more than seniority, with AUKUS driving defence demand.
  • Contractor day rates lose roughly 30% versus permanent once bench time and super are counted.
