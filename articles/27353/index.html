Deployment Strategist | Palantir Technologies

Zero G Talent · 2026-10-23 · 6 min read

Summary

Palantir is hiring a Deployment Strategist to support its US Government work, based in Wiesbaden, Germany. The role involves embedding with customers to understand their workflows, identifying relevant datasets, and working with Forward Deployed Engineers to build data pipelines, plus training users and presenting results from analysts to executives. Requirements include an active Top Secret clearance with SCI eligibility and 25–75% travel. Estimated salary is $110,000–$170,000 per year, with possible RSUs and sign-on bonus. Programming experience (Python, R, SQL) is a plus, and the company emphasizes low ego and comfort with unstructured problems.

Key points

What you need

  • Active Top Secret Clearance with eligibility and willingness to obtain SCI
  • Extraordinary ability to take on open-ended problems in unstructured environments
  • Ability to travel 25 – 75% required
  • Adaptive and introspective; willing to learn, teach, lead and follow
  • Experience with programming, scripting or statistical packages such as Python, R, Matlab, SQL

What you'll do

  • Go onsite and meet with customer analysts to understand critical questions and pain points
  • Identify relevant datasets and integrate data into stable, extensible pipelines
  • Work with customers to build bespoke workflows for new user groups
  • Lead training sessions to ensure product meets user needs and has concrete impact
  • Present results and proposals to audiences ranging from analysts to C-suite executives

NOTE: Although this position is in support of our US Government work, this role is located in Wiesbaden, Germany.

What We Do

Deployment Strategists are do-ers who immerse themselves in our customers' most intricate workflows, partner with customer teams and explore the data, and dive into the product landscape to enable us to scale. A select number of Deployments Strategists may also be deployed to Palantir internal teams and problems. In this role, the problems you'll tackle will require a curious and analytical mindset, a sharp intuition for product, and a strong degree of user empathy to ultimately empower our customers to make better decisions.
