Full text

[Caixin.com] At nine in the evening, 25-year-old Wu Mingze returned to his apartment in Shenzhen after a business dinner. He picked up his phone and, slightly tipsy, said to the screen: "Being an FDE is really hard. If you can't handle this kind of business stuff, you just can't do the job..." He then posted this slice of FDE daily life on his Xiaohongshu account. Over the past month or so, the account has gained more than 8,000 followers—all of which started in early-to-mid May, when he began sharing the day-to-day of a profession known as the FDE.

FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer. The role's main responsibility is to work on-site at customer locations as a technical expert, ensuring that technical solutions are successfully implemented in real-world scenarios. With advances in AI coding capabilities and the rollout of AI agent applications, large AI model and cloud companies have begun a new round of technology deployment aimed at enterprise customers. The FDE role has become a hot profession: overseas AI giants OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google have all increased their FDE hiring or emphasis on the role, and many companies in China have also ramped up related recruitment.
