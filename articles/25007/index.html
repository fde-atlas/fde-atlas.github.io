Salesforce Launches FDE Training in Korea

Original title: Salesforce、韓国でFDE研修を開始

日本Fde協会 · 2026-11-02 · 3 min read

Summary

According to Digital Today, Salesforce has introduced its Partner FDE training program in South Korea for the first time, aiming to develop engineers who specialize in AI agents. The inaugural workshop ran October 13-15 in Seoul, drawing 44 engineers and consultants from 11 local system integration and consulting partners. The three-day curriculum covered business automation using Agentforce and Slack, unstructured data usage, metrics setting, and data security and governance. Participants needed prior certifications such as Agentforce Specialist plus roughly 80 hours of study; passing the final assessment grants the Agentforce FDE Practitioner credential. Over 1,000 people across 140 companies worldwide hold the credential, four Korean firms will join the FDE network, and Salesforce aims to train more than 100 experts by next year.

News Details

According to a report by Digital Today, Salesforce has introduced the "Partner FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" program to Korea, which trains specialist engineers for AI agents. Partner FDEs are positioned as specialists who analyze customers' business environments and support everything from AI agent design to building, deployment, operation, and optimization. The inaugural workshop was held in Seoul from October 13 to 15, with 44 engineers and consultants from 11 local system integration and consulting partner companies participating.

Over the three-day training, participants learned about business automation using the AI agent platform Agentforce and the collaboration tool Slack, leveraging unstructured data, data analysis, and setting metrics. They also reviewed data security and governance, as well as the technical requirements for operating AI agents. Participation required prior acquisition of certifications such as Agentforce Specialist and Data 360 Consultant, along with approximately 80 hours of study, and passing the final assessment grants the Agentforce FDE Practitioner credential.

According to Salesforce, more than 1,000 people across 140 companies worldwide hold the credential. In Korea, four companies are expected to join the FDE network following this workshop.

Background

An FDE refers to a technical professional who works on-site with customers to implement and embed AI and software into their business operations. Park Se-jin, who leads the Korean subsidiary, stated that a company's competitiveness is determined not by how quickly it adopts AI, but by how it integrates AI into its organizational workflows and turns it into results. The company aims to strengthen its partners' technical capabilities and move beyond experimental builds toward practical use and tangible outcomes.

What This Means for FDE Practitioners

The fact that this FDE training covers everything from design to operation and optimization shows that FDEs play a role in organizing on-site operations and driving continuous improvement. The inclusion of data security and governance reviews also indicates that practical implementation is expected to take permission management and operational aspects into account. For more on ensuring adoption sticks after implementation, see Ensuring Operational Adoption After AI Implementation | The Reality of Accompaniment Support.
