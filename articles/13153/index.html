About FDE Council: Mission, Independence and Who Runs It

FDE Council · 2026-10-24 · 2 min read

Summary

FDE Council, founded by Noor Surani, aims to create an open, vendor-neutral definition of competent Forward Deployed Engineering. It defines FDEs as engineers embedded with customers who take AI-based capabilities from unclear problems to production outcomes the customer owns, measured by results rather than demos or hours. The Council has published a v0.3 draft capability framework, plans a State of FDE 2026 practitioner survey and a formal job task analysis, and intends to offer a credential kept separate from training. It sells no training, accepts no vendor money, and membership is free; it is self-funded by the founder, who runs separate paid training businesses. The framework is industry-neutral, with the founding community starting in North America.

Key points

  • The Council sells no training and takes no vendor money; membership is free.

To give practitioners, employers and educators a shared, vendor-neutral definition of competent Forward Deployed Engineering, developed in the open and validated with the people who do the work.

Our mission

What is Forward Deployed Engineering?

A Forward Deployed Engineer embeds with a customer, internal or external, to take a technical capability, now usually AI-based, from an unclear problem to a production outcome the customer owns. The work spans discovery, design, building, deployment, adoption, handover, and feeding what was learned back into the product.

It’s different from pre-sales demonstration, account management and advisory-only consulting. FDEs are measured by production outcomes, not demos or hours. More on the Forward Deployed Engineer role.

What we’re doing

  • Publishing an open capability framework (v0.3 draft now).
  • Running a practitioner survey (State of FDE 2026) to inform it.
  • Validating it through a formal job task analysis with practitioners and subject-matter experts.
  • Working toward a credential that is kept separate from any training.

How we stay independent

  • We don’t sell training, and we won’t require any specific course.
  • Membership and sponsorship never buy influence over framework content or any future assessment.
  • Training providers can say their material is “aligned to the open framework”. They can’t call it official, approved or accredited.
  • Advisors declare conflicts of interest.

Funding

FDE Council is self-funded by its founder. It has no sponsors, takes no vendor money, and membership is free. If that changes, sponsors will be listed on this page, and no sponsor will have any say over the framework.

Industry-neutral, North America first

The framework applies to FDE work in any industry. Industry-specific tracks may come later if the community asks for them. Our founding community is starting in North America (US, Canada and Mexico).

Who’s behind it

FDE Council (Forward Deployed Engineering Council) was founded by Noor Surani. The founder also delivers paid AI and engineering training, including FDE topics, through separate businesses. FDE Council does not sell, endorse or accredit any course, including the founder’s. The Council is being shaped by its founding community and a forming advisory board.
