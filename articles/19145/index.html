Summary

Buyer's guide5 questions

What does AI consulting for a startup actually include?

On the pages read, three shapes: a short paid assessment that ranks what to build and what to skip (Valere, $1,000 to $3,000), a fixed scope MVP or feature build (Digital Scientists, Azumo, Bitcot), or a team or engineer embedded for months (AE Studio, HatchWorks AI, Toptal). Pick the shape before you pick the firm.

How much does AI consulting cost for a startup?

From the firms' own pages: an assessment at $1,000 to $3,000 (Valere), a two week start at $10K and then $20K a month (Digital Scientists), $499 to $899 per accepted feature (Azumo), and a startup MVP at $75K to $150K (Digital Scientists). Bitcot's enquiry form starts at $10,000. Six of the ten print nothing.

Should a startup hire an AI agency or an AI engineer?

An agency if you need a product built and have no one to manage engineers. A freelance engineer from a network like Toptal if you already have a technical lead. A forward deployed engineer if the AI exists but stalls on your real data and needs someone inside the company to join the systems up.

Can I pay an AI firm in equity?

Two firms here say so on their own sites: Digital Scientists offers a reduced rate sprint for equity to funded startups, and AE Studio says it invests alongside you when the fit is right. Neither prints the terms, so get them in writing and run them past your lead investor.

Who owns the code an AI firm builds for my startup?

Ask before you sign. Digital Scientists, HatchWorks AI and Brainforge state you own it, Bitcot hands over the source code and leaves rights to the contract, and Rapid Developers keeps its reusable code. Due diligence at your next round will ask.

How to choose

  • 1This week, write down the one AI feature or workflow that matters to the next board meeting, and the number it should move: support tickets closed without a human, hours of onboarding saved, demo to paid conversion. Every firm should be judged against that number.
  • 2Run the pilot you already have on 50 real customer records, not the demo set. If it breaks, find where: records that don't match between the CRM, billing and the product database are the usual cause, and they need fixing before any firm builds more AI.
  • 3Decide the shape. No technical team: an agency that builds, such as Digital Scientists, AE Studio or Bitcot. A technical lead already in place: one engineer from a network such as Toptal. A working pilot that fails on real data: a forward deployed engineer inside the company.
  • 4Take the cheapest priced first step that fits. Valere's $1,000 to $3,000 assessment and Digital Scientists' $10K two week start are printed; ask every other firm for the first price in writing before a second call.
  • 5Put code and IP ownership, the cancellation terms and any equity in the contract. Your next round's due diligence will ask who owns the model prompts, the code and the data pipelines.
  • 6If the AI still fails on real data after the first build, and nobody in house can join the systems up, that's when you bring in a forward deployed engineer rather than another agency sprint.
