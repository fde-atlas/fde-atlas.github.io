About Classmethod, Inc.

The company provides cloud consulting, development, and operations centered on AWS, as well as data analytics platforms, app development, SaaS implementation support, and generative AI adoption support. It has 8 offices in Japan and 6 overseas, with a track record of more than 5,600 clients. It is also known for its technical blog "DevelopersIO." The group has approximately 820 employees in total. Guided by its mission of "supporting the growth and success of all people through open ideas and actions," the company has a culture of openly publishing the knowledge it gains from its own use. Its client track record exceeds 5,600 companies.

Characteristics of FDE roles at this company

A distinctive feature is that the job posting begins by explaining the definition itself: "An FDE is a job concept created by Palantir Technologies." Using three of the Data Business Division's own products as offerings, the company recruits candidates for a "candidate" position to be developed as FDEs, with a structure that allows them to progressively acquire skills through on-the-job training and real projects after joining.
