What Is the FDE Certification? An Overview of the 5 Grades and Exam Content

Original title: FDE検定とは？5段階の等級と試験内容を解説

日本Fde協会 · 2026-07-18 · 7 min read

Summary

The Japan FDE Association, a private body, is launching the FDE Certification, a five-level exam (grades 5 to 1) that validates the practical ability of forward deployed engineers to solve customer business problems using AI. Written by the association's representative director, the article explains the grade design: a low entry barrier at grade 5 (assistant) up to grade 1 (architect), which covers designing FDE strategy, talent development, and organizational architecture, with grade 3 (practitioner) as the core working level. Exam format, fees, and eligibility are still being finalized; a free two-minute self-assessment is available. The certification is positioned as a complement to existing IT credentials, giving a name to previously unrecognized hybrid engineers and helping companies match talent levels to projects.

Key points

  • Private certification by the Japan FDE Association, not a national qualification.
  • Five grades from 5 (assistant) to 1 (architect); grade 3 is the core practitioner level.
  • Grade 1 covers FDE strategy, talent systems, and business architecture design.
  • Exam details are still in design; a free 2-minute self-diagnostic exists.
  • Complements IT Passport, G Test, PMP, etc., rather than replacing them.

The FDE Certification is a private certification operated by the Japan FDE Association. It certifies practical competence as an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)—the ability to solve customers' business challenges using AI—across five grades, from Grade 5 to Grade 1. Although it is not a national qualification, it is a competency certification system that allows engineers who have been doing "a job with no name" to objectively demonstrate their skills.

As the Representative Director of the Japan FDE Association, I designed this certification system together with Director Mitsunaga. The origin of creating this system lies in my own experience: "I have run both technology and business in the field, yet I had no way to prove it." As a PM, trader, e-commerce entrepreneur, and AI developer, I had been doing FDE-like work throughout my career, but there was no job title that could describe it in a single phrase, nor any qualification to demonstrate that ability. The FDE Certification is a system for such practitioners.

If you would like to learn about the FDE role itself first, please see "What Is an FDE? A Practitioner Explains the Differences from SEs and SIers".

The Five Grades of the FDE Certification

The FDE Certification consists of five grades, from Grade 5 (Assistant) to Grade 1 (Architect). Each grade clearly defines "what level of work the certified person can be entrusted with."

Grade 5AssistantBeginners interested in FDEUnderstands FDE fundamentals and basic concepts of AI utilization
Grade 4OperatorPractitioners who have started using AI and IT tools in their workCan apply AI tools to business operations and carry out routine process improvements
Grade 3PractitionerFDEs who solve problems on-site at customer locationsCan identify customers' business challenges and design and implement solutions using AI and technology
Grade 2SeniorFDEs who lead multiple projectsCan lead complex projects and drive organization-wide business transformation
Grade 1ArchitectTop-level professionals who design FDE organizations and systemsCan formulate FDE strategy, build talent development frameworks, and design the architecture of the entire business

The Philosophy Behind the Grade Design—"Low Barriers to Entry, a High Ceiling"

Grade 5 is designed so that anyone at the "interested in FDE" stage can take it. If the entry barrier is set too high, capable people will be kept away, thinking "I'm not ready yet." On the other hand, Grade 1 certifies the highest level of ability: the capacity to design the architecture of an entire FDE organization. Grade 1 is envisioned for professionals with achievements such as building an organization from zero to 60 people at a startup, or managing both technology and business on a 100-million-yen project.

This structure of "a wide entrance, with higher grades narrowed down through practical work" was something I kept in mind from the system design stage. I wanted the certification to be not merely an exam of "whether you can pass," but something that lets you "know where you currently stand and understand what you should develop next."

What Each Grade Certifies

Grades 5 and 4—Professionals with AI Fundamentals

Grade 5 (Assistant) is the stage of understanding FDE's basic concepts and the fundamentals of AI utilization. It is the level at which one can accurately explain "what AI is" and "what kind of work an FDE does." Grade 4 (Operator) is the stage of actually starting to use AI tools in one's work—for example, someone who can execute routine process improvements using ChatGPT or no-code tools.

For corporate managers, it serves as a decision-making aid: "If we have employees at Grade 4 or above, we can entrust simple AI adoption within the department to them."

Grade 3—Professionals Capable of Hands-On FDE Work

Grade 3 (Practitioner) is the core of the FDE Certification. It certifies the ability to immerse oneself in the customer's site, identify business challenges independently, and design and implement solutions using AI and technology. This "ability to handle everything from problem identification to implementation on one's own" is the defining line for Grade 3.

Grade 3 envisions professionals who can, for example, join a manufacturing client, discover through on-site observation that "data entry takes two hours every day," and build and demonstrate an automation prototype in a single day.

Grades 2 and 1—Professionals Who Can Move Organizations

Grade 2 (Senior) certifies professionals who can lead multiple projects simultaneously and drive organization-wide business transformation. Grade 1 (Architect) is the highest level, certifying the ability to design FDE organizations themselves: formulating FDE strategy for one's own company or client companies, building talent development frameworks, and designing the architecture of the entire business. Just as a director of our association, who manages multiple businesses, firmly states that "you cannot manage without knowing the front lines," Grade 1 certifies professionals who can integrate both hands-on fieldwork and organizational design at a high level.

About the Exam

Exam Format and Eligibility

The exam format of the FDE Certification is currently in final design. Eligibility requirements will be set for each grade, and grade-skipping and multiple applications are also being considered. Exam fees are also being prepared. As soon as the details of the system are finalized, we will announce them on the FDE Certification page.

For those who want to know their current level first, we offer a skill self-assessment that delivers results in about two minutes. Simply answering 10 questions yields a score equivalent to Grade 5 through Grade 1. If you would like to see what Grade 3-level questions look like, please also check the sample questions.

Relationship with Existing IT Certifications

The FDE Certification does not deny or replace existing IT certifications such as IT Passport, the G Test, Registered Information Security Specialist, or PMP. While existing certifications measure "technical knowledge" or "project management," the FDE Certification certifies the practical competency unique to FDEs: "solving problems on-site at customers using AI." Rather, the knowledge built through existing certifications serves as a foundation for FDE activities.

Why the FDE Certification Is Needed Now

Giving a Name to "a Job with No Name"

Like me, there are many engineers in Japan who have run both technology and business in the field. However, this way of working had no name and no system for evaluating it. Such people are often treated as "handy all-rounders" and their expertise goes unrecognized. I created the FDE Certification to give a name and a grade to the abilities of such practitioners.

Making It Easier for Companies to Find FDE Talent

For companies seeking to drive DX, the biggest challenge is "being unable to find people who understand both technology and business." The grades of the FDE Certification serve as a common yardstick for companies to judge "what level of talent this project requires." Speaking from my experience struggling with staffing on a financial institution project, simply being able to say "an FDE at Grade 3 or above" should greatly reduce mismatches in hiring and outsourcing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the FDE Certification a national qualification?

A. No, it is a private certification operated by the Japan FDE Association. While it is not a national qualification, it is designed as a program that certifies practical skills in business improvement through AI utilization.

Q. Which grade should I start with?

A. First, check your current level with the skill self-assessment (about 2 minutes, free of charge). The grade shown in your assessment results will serve as a guide for which exam to take.

Q. Can I take the exam without practical work experience?

A. Grade 5 (Assistant) is designed so that you can take the exam without any practical work experience. It serves as an entry point for those interested in FDE. Grade 3 and above are expected to include questions that assume practical experience.

Q. What do I get if I pass?

A. We are considering issuing digital badges corresponding to each grade for successful candidates. If issued, these badges can be displayed on your portfolio, social media, business cards, and more, objectively demonstrating your level of competence as an FDE. Please note that the certification is an indicator of skill level and does not guarantee the performance of specific tasks or outcomes.

Why not start by checking your level as a first step toward taking the FDE certification exam?

With the free skill self-assessment (about 2 minutes), you can find out your FDE level (equivalent to Grade 5 to Grade 1).

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out via Contact Us
