Summary

Japanese listed company Stamen (operator of the TUNAG engagement platform, used by over 1,500 companies with a 99% retention rate) is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer for a new 0-to-1 business automation unit. The role centers on building n8n-based automation workflows with LLMs for clients heavy in non-desk workers (manufacturing, retail, care, logistics), covering everything from architecture and requirements gathering to internal rollout. Candidates need low-code/iPaaS experience, scripting skills, and API integration experience. Salary ranges from 6.48M to 8.04M yen including 45 hours of fixed overtime, based in Tokyo with limited remote work.

Key points

  • Stamen's TUNAG platform serves 1,500+ companies with 99% retention.
  • The FDE role is a 0-to-1 business using n8n and generative AI for automation.
  • Focus is on AX for non-desk industries like manufacturing and retail.
  • Requires low-code tool, scripting, and API integration experience.
  • Salary is 6.48M-8.04M yen with full-flex hours in Tokyo.

  • Job Title
  • FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)
  • Job Description
  • ■Background Starter has developed a SaaS business connecting organizations and people, centered on the engagement management platform "TUNAG." With the rapid spread of generative AI, the way business processes work is about to change dramatically, even on the front lines centered on non-desk workers who have not fully benefited from DX until now. We are building a new framework to support client companies in business automation and AX (AI Transformation). In this position, armed with an automation platform centered on n8n, you will serve as a "front-line AX companion," replacing clients' routine tasks with AI and automated workflows. As this is a business in the new planning phase, you will be entrusted with everything end-to-end, from technology selection and solution design to aligning requirements with clients and rolling out internally. ■Specific responsibilities [Design and development of business automation solutions for client companies] - Building and operating automated workflows centered on n8n (including cases using LLMs within workflows) - Automating business processes in integration with our own products such as TUNAG [Promoting internal business automation at Starter] - Automating routine tasks in sales, customer success, corporate departments, etc. - Horizontally deploying use cases refined internally into client-facing solutions
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • [Required] *Must meet all three of the following: ・Practical experience in business automation using low-code/iPaaS tools such as n8n, Zapier, or Power Automate ・Experience writing scripts in some programming language (Python, TypeScript, etc.) ・Experience implementing integrations with external services using APIs/Webhooks [Preferred] ・Experience incorporating LLMs such as Amazon Bedrock or the OpenAI API into workflows ・Experience designing and operating in cloud environments such as AWS ・Experience structuring issues through business interviews and translating them into requirements ・Experience leading DX projects at a SaaS company or consulting firm ・Understanding of business operations in the non-desk worker domain (manufacturing, retail, caregiving, transportation, etc.) ・Knowledge of BPR and process design ・Experience launching a 0-to-1 business at a startup [Ideal Candidate] ・Someone with a strong commitment to building things that are "actually used in the field" ・Someone who values both technical elegance and a gritty, hands-on understanding of business operations ・Someone with the ownership to form and test their own hypotheses when facing questions without clear answers ・Someone who empathizes with Stammen's values (Autonomy / Co-creation / Evolution)
  • Appeal of the Position / Career Path
  • ■The cutting edge of "AX" in the non-desk worker domain Stammen's main clients are companies with many non-desk workers in the field, such as manufacturing, retail, caregiving, and transportation. While analog business processes involving paper, phone, fax, and verbal communication remain deeply rooted, we believe this is an area where AX will advance rapidly over the next few years as voice input, image recognition, and natural language instructions come together. This is work at the forefront of that wave, raising productivity across entire industries. ■The hands-on feel of delivering "usable AI" to the field While many AI implementations end at the PoC stage, what we want to provide is "automation that is embedded in daily operations and keeps running." We place n8n at the core because it can be built quickly with no-code/low-code and flexibly adapts to changes in field operations. The appeal of this work lies in designing entire business workflows and building systems that are actually used in the field. ■Experience leading a 0-to-1 business with technology The business automation business is currently in its use case development phase, where we discover issues through interviews with client companies and crystallize them into solutions. You will handle everything from architecture design to packaging solutions, gaining experience that goes beyond engineering to shape the very foundation of the business through technology. ■A dual automation field, both internal and external While client-facing business automation is the main focus, you will also be involved in automating Stammen's internal operations as a place to hone your skills. Pitfalls and improvements that only become visible through internal operation can be fed directly back into client-facing solutions.
