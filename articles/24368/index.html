Full text

US research firm Gartner has revealed a prediction that 70% of AI agents developed by vendors' FDEs (Forward-Deployed Engineers or Forward-Deployed Engineering) will be abandoned unused by enterprises by 2028 (Gartner Predicts 70% of Enterprises Will Abandon Agentic AI Built by Vendor Forward-Deployed Engineering by 2028).

Gartner Newsroom: Gartner Predicts 70% of Enterprises Will Abandon Agentic AI Built by Vendor Forward-Deployed Engineering by 2028 #GartnerNewsroom https://t.co/AWjqBRjt3b— Gartner (@Gartner_inc)September 29, 2026

FDE refers to software engineers, or a development model, in which engineers at a vendor that provides software products embed themselves at customer companies and co-develop AI-powered services or software tailored to each customer's specific challenges.

However, Gartner Senior Director Analyst Mukul Saha points out that FDE is being used with a different meaning than its original one.

Many providers now use 'forward deployed' as a label for implementation, professional services, solution engineering, or AI consulting; many software providers are using the term "forward deployed" as a name for product implementation, professional services, solution engineering offerings, or AI consulting.

He warned that in such a state, the FDE model fails structurally before it fails technically, as it does not meet the three principles of the FDE model as defined by Gartner: "FDE should only be used for problems that require deep product expertise, rapid adaptation, and close integration of the vendor's technology and operational environment," "FDE should be embedded with in-house domain experts, technologists, and end users, sharing knowledge deemed important and reflecting it in the work," and "an exit strategy should be clear from the start."

Gartner also predicts that by 2028, fewer than 20% of vendors will be able to reflect FDE's ongoing customer needs into their core products, and that, along with the prediction that 70% of AI agents developed by vendors' FDEs will be abandoned unused by enterprises by 2028, the risk of "FDE washing," in which consulting services are sold as FDE, will increase.
