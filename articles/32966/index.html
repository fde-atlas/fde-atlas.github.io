FDE for Freshers in India 2026: Roadmap, Pay ₹6–15 LPA & Junior Roles

FDE India · 2026-10-31 · 14 min read

Summary

This guide examines whether fresh graduates in India can land a forward deployed engineer (FDE) role. The answer: yes, but rarely with the title straight out of college. An October 2026 tracker snapshot found only 2 of 43 FDE postings with stated minimums accepted a year or less of experience, with a median of five years; Glassdoor India showed 522 FDE listings but only 5 internships. Pay signals vary widely: ₹6–15 LPA for freshers, about ₹12 lakh average base on Glassdoor, and ₹18–28 lakh for entry-level per Quess. Recommended routes are implementation, solutions or support engineering roles at AI product companies, junior FDE titles at startups like Exotel, or internal moves at IT services firms such as TCS and HCLTech, with the title typically reached in 12–24 months. The article also outlines a six-month plan for building deployed projects, including API integrations and AI agents with evals.

Key points

  • Only 2 of 43 postings with stated minimums accepted a year or less; median was five years.
  • Glassdoor India showed 522 FDE listings but just 5 intern listings in October 2026.
  • Pay estimates range from ₹6–15 LPA (fresher) to ₹18–28 lakh (Quess entry-level).
  • Best entry routes: implementation/solutions roles, junior FDE titles at startups, or IT services internal moves.
  • Build two or three deployed, documented projects tied to real users, including an AI agent with evals.

Yes, a fresher can become a forward deployed engineer (FDE) in India, but rarely straight out of college with that title. Most Indian FDE listings ask for two or more years of production engineering. The realistic path is a junior FDE role at an AI startup, or an implementation, solutions or support engineering job that does the same customer-facing deployment work, followed by a move into the title within 12–24 months.

This guide looks at what the listings actually say, what early-career pay looks like, and what to build in college so you are ready for one of those few openings.

How many FDE jobs in India accept freshers?

Very few. Neurapath’s tracker counted 138 open FDE and related roles across 84 companies in India on 12 October 2026. Only 43 of those postings stated a minimum experience, and of those just 2 asked for a year or less. Fifteen asked for 2–3 years, 13 for 4–6 years and 13 for 7 or more. The median stated minimum was five years. By level, Neurapath classed only 6 of the 138 roles as entry level.

Only 2 of 43 postings with a stated minimum accepted a year or less. Source: Neurapath tracker, 12 October 2026.

Job boards tell the same story. In October 2026, Glassdoor India showed 522 listings for a broad “forward deployed engineer” search across India, but only 5 for “forward deployed engineer intern”.

The wider hiring market also tilts towards experience. Deccan Herald reported Quess data showing about 7,000 openings for candidates with under one year of experience over a 90-day period, against about 1,72,000 for people with 3–5 years. That is for tech hiring broadly, not FDE roles alone, but it explains why campus hiring for new AI roles is expected to stay measured.

Why employers hesitate to hire fresher FDEs

An FDE is often the only engineer a customer sees. They scope a vague problem, write production code inside someone else’s systems, and own the result. That is hard to do without having shipped and supported software before. Analytics India Magazine reported in September 2026 that Han Digital Group sees taking AI from prototype to reliable production — evaluations, observability, guardrails and monitoring — as the hardest capability to find.

There is also a supply squeeze. Xpheno research put India’s potential FDE talent pool at only about 2,800 people, AIM reported. Large employers are responding by retraining existing engineers rather than hiring freshers into the role.

The roles that do accept early-career applicants share a pattern: small or fast-growing AI product companies, a narrow product, and a clear ask for shipped projects rather than years.

What freshers can expect to earn

There is no authoritative salary survey for fresher FDEs in India, and the figures that exist measure different things. Treat them as signals, not promises.

  • Fresher estimate: Corporate Bhaiya puts typical fresher FDE pay (0–1 years) at ₹6–15 LPA, compiled from Glassdoor India, AmbitionBox, Levels.fyi and job postings, and calls ₹12 LPA or more a strong offer.
  • Glassdoor base pay: Business Today reported in October 2026 that Glassdoor India’s FDE salary page shows average base pay of about ₹12 lakh a year, in a ₹9–16 lakh range, from 28 submissions. Individual submissions for 1–3 years of experience showed total pay of ₹22–26 lakh in Hyderabad and ₹23–27 lakh in Bengaluru.
  • Posted range: Neurapath recorded a printed range of ₹14–30 LPA on a Weekday AI FDE role in Bengaluru, with the experience level not stated.
  • Industry band: Quess Corp’s Kapil Joshi, citing industry reports, put entry-level FDEs at about ₹18–28 lakh, Deccan Herald reported.

Each bar is a different measure, so compare with care. The higher bands mostly reflect people with some experience. Sources: Corporate Bhaiya, Glassdoor via Business Today, Neurapath, Quess via Deccan Herald.

The gap between ₹6 LPA and ₹28 LPA is mostly about who the “entry level” person is. Bands such as Quess’s describe engineers early in the FDE role, many of whom already have a few years of software work behind them. A true fresher at a small startup should expect the lower end, and judge the offer on how much real deployment work it gives. For how pay steps up after that, see our guide to FDE experience required in India and the salary hub.

Entry routes that actually work

1. Implementation, solutions and support engineering

These roles do much of the FDE job: configuring a product for a customer, writing integration code, debugging in someone else’s environment and explaining fixes. AIM reported UnearthInsight’s estimate that 75–80% of Indian jobs labelled FDE are closer to solutions or implementation engineering anyway. Neurapath’s fresher guide also points early-career candidates to implementation, solutions and technical support engineer roles at AI product companies. Our comparison of roles similar to forward deployed engineer explains how each one maps to FDE work.

2. Junior FDE and Applied AI Engineer titles at startups

AI agent and voice AI startups are where the lowest experience bars sit — Exotel, Prodigal and NeoAistriq in the table above. Applied AI Engineer roles, such as Composio’s Bengaluru role that Neurapath lists at 2 years, are a close cousin.

3. Join an IT services firm and move internally

Indian IT is building FDE teams mostly by retraining. TCS aims for at least 6,000 FDE-style engineers, about 1% of its workforce, and onboarded 14,000 campus graduates last quarter, Deccan Herald reported. HCLTech added more than 1,050 freshers in the April–June quarter, and its chief people officer said elite engineers should join its FDE cohort over the next two to three years. Joining as a graduate and asking for client-facing AI delivery projects is a slower but well-trodden route.

Job titles to search for

Use all of these on Naukri, LinkedIn, Glassdoor and foundit, then filter by the experience line:

  • FDE titles: Forward Deployed Engineer - 1, Junior Forward Deployed Engineer, FDE Intern
  • Close cousins: Applied AI Engineer, AI Deployment Engineer
  • Adjacent roles at AI and SaaS product companies: Implementation Engineer, Solutions Engineer, Integration Engineer, Technical Support Engineer

4. Internships that convert

Glassdoor India’s 5 FDE intern listings in October 2026 show these exist, but they are scarce. Deployment, solutions or integration internships at AI and SaaS product companies are worth just as much if they put you in front of real customer systems.

What to build in college: a step-by-step plan

Listings repeatedly ask for proof of shipped work. Exotel wants “shipped projects”; Deccan Herald’s recruiters advise freshers to build two or three complete, deployed, documented projects tied to a real business problem. Here is a practical sequence.

  • Pick one core foundation and go deep. Backend in Python or TypeScript is the most common ask in the listings above. Learn APIs, JSON, authentication, SQL and debugging properly before chasing LLM tools.
  • Build an integration service with tests. Connect two real systems — for example a form tool and a CRM or ticketing API — with webhooks, retries and logging. Write a README a stranger could follow. This mirrors Neurapath’s suggested first 30 days.
  • Add an AI agent with retrieval and an evaluation loop. Use RAG over a real document set and write a small test harness that scores answers and catches hallucinations. DevRev and Exotel both name evals; few fresher CVs show them.
  • Deploy it and keep it running. Use Docker, a cloud free tier and basic monitoring. Exotel lists Docker, monitoring, logs and incident handling as bonus skills.
  • Find one real user. A college club, a family business or a local NGO is enough. Scope the problem with them, ship, and write a one-page case study with the before-and-after metric.
  • Practise explaining it. DevRev expects about 30% customer-facing time, and samora ai asks FDEs to explain technical ideas to customers with low technical exposure. Record a five-minute walkthrough for a non-technical listener.

Do you need strong DSA to become an FDE?

Less than for a typical product-company SDE role, but you can’t skip coding practice entirely. FDE coding rounds lean practical: DataInterview describes Databricks’ FDE coding round as 60 minutes in a notebook, and one Statisfy candidate’s technical round covered Python, SQL, JSON handling and data validation. Some companies still ask classic problems: a July 2025 Glassdoor review of DevRev’s FDE loop mentions merge intervals and a rate limiter.

A sensible split for a fresher: practise easy-to-medium problems on arrays, strings and hash maps until they feel routine, then put most of your time into deployed projects and practical API and data tasks. Our guide to the FDE interview process covers each round.

Fresher FDE jobs in Chennai and Tamil Nadu

Chennai is a smaller FDE market than Bengaluru or Hyderabad: Glassdoor India showed about 20 Chennai FDE listings in November 2026. None we found accept true freshers. The lowest ask is 2–4 years at Newpage Solutions, which posts ₹6–14 LPA. For graduates in Tamil Nadu, the practical options are:

  • Remote-India FDE roles such as Exotel’s Forward Deployed Engineer - 1 (0.5–2 years), Peakflo’s Forward Deployed Engineer (remote, 0.5–2 years, on our job board in November 2026) and NeoAistriq’s junior role, which don’t require relocating.
  • Implementation and solutions roles at Chennai SaaS and AI companies, which do much of the same work.
  • Moving into FDE work after a year or two of shipped, customer-facing projects.

How to read a “fresher” FDE listing

A few checks save time:

  • Ignore the title, read the experience line. “Associate” and “FDE - 1” can mean anything from 6 months to 6 years.
  • Ask how much of the week is coding. Neurapath warns some IT-services onsite roles are relabelled FDE without much engineering.
  • Look for posted pay. Many startup listings put the range in the description even when the job board says “not disclosed”, as samora ai’s did.
  • Check the date. Several FDE listings stay online after they close; samora ai’s listing was marked closed when we checked.

The honest answer, then: the FDE title as a first job is possible but rare in India. The work itself is very reachable for a fresher who picks the right adjacent role and builds deployed, customer-facing proof from day one.

Frequently asked questions

Can a fresher get a forward deployed engineer job in India?

Yes, but such roles are a small minority. In an October 2026 snapshot of Indian FDE postings, only 2 of 43 with a stated minimum accepted a year or less. Freshers improve their odds by applying to junior FDE titles at AI startups and to implementation or solutions roles that do the same work.

What is the salary of a fresher forward deployed engineer in India?

There is no authoritative survey. Corporate Bhaiya estimates ₹6–15 LPA for 0–1 years, Glassdoor India's FDE base pay averages about ₹12 lakh with a ₹9–16 lakh range, and Quess cites ₹18–28 lakh for entry-level FDEs. Check the posted range on each listing.

Which roles should freshers apply for if they want to become an FDE?

Implementation engineer, solutions engineer, technical support or integration engineer roles at AI and SaaS product companies, plus Applied AI Engineer and junior or associate FDE titles. Analysts say most Indian jobs labelled FDE are already close to solutions or implementation work.

What should I build in college to become a forward deployed engineer?

Two or three deployed projects tied to a real user or business problem: an API integration with tests, an AI agent with retrieval and an evaluation loop, and a short written case study for each. Listings such as Exotel's ask for shipped projects in place of years of experience.

Can I become a forward deployed engineer without strong DSA skills?

Yes, for most routes, but not with zero coding practice. FDE coding rounds lean practical: data handling, APIs and clean code, such as Databricks' 60-minute notebook round or Python, SQL and JSON tasks. Some companies still ask classic problems: a Glassdoor review of DevRev's FDE loop mentions merge intervals and a rate limiter. Practise easy-to-medium problems on arrays, strings and hash maps, and spend more of your time on deployed projects.

Which job titles should freshers search for to get into FDE work?

Search for Forward Deployed Engineer - 1, Junior FDE, FDE Intern and Applied AI Engineer, plus Implementation Engineer, Solutions Engineer, Integration Engineer and Technical Support Engineer at AI and SaaS product companies. Then read the experience line, not the title: Salesforce's FDE Associate role asks for 4–6 years.

Are there fresher FDE jobs in Chennai or Tamil Nadu?

Very few so far. Chennai is a smaller FDE market (about 20 Glassdoor India listings in November 2026), and the lowest ask we found is 2–4 years at Newpage Solutions, which posts ₹6–14 LPA. Freshers in Tamil Nadu should also apply to remote-India roles such as Exotel's Forward Deployed Engineer - 1 and NeoAistriq's junior role, and to implementation or solutions roles at Chennai SaaS companies.
