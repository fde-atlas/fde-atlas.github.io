About Stockmark Inc.

A company whose strength is its ability to develop domestic LLMs fully from scratch; in 2024 it released "Stockmark-2," a 100-billion-parameter Japanese-specialized LLM. It operates a SaaS business with "Aconnect," an AI agent for the manufacturing industry, and a PaaS business with "SAT," a platform for supporting business AI implementation, and has been adopted by more than 300 companies, including 30% of Nikkei 225 firms.

Characteristics of FDE roles at this company

The FDE role involves deploying the PaaS product SAT into enterprises such as major manufacturers. Joining from the proposal phase, the FDE handles everything end-to-end: assessing technical feasibility, designing architecture, formulating roadmaps, and managing projects from a technical standpoint. On 2026-08-18, the company announced the expansion of its FDE organization, emphasizing an initiative to structure unstructured data—such as CAD drawings, plant piping diagrams, handwritten reports, manuals, and the tacit knowledge of skilled engineers—using its in-house LLM and turn it into assets for companies in core industries. On 2026-01-15, it formed a business alliance with the strategy consulting firm Logos&Pathos Consulting, establishing a system in which consultants identify management challenges and Stockmark's engineers implement its in-house LLM and AI infrastructure tailored to the customer's environment. The role is fully remote in principle, with residence limited to within Japan.
