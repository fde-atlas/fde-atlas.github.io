Forward Deployed Engineer Salary 2026: Pay by Level, City and Company

Nexus It Group · 2026-10-26 · 8 min read

Summary

Based on 804 US job postings with published pay from 139 employers in October 2026, this article maps forward deployed engineer salaries. Typical base pay runs $155,000–$229,000, with entry roles near $135,000 and staff/lead roles up to about $297,000. New York and the Bay Area pay the most; financial services employers offer the highest bases, while consulting firms like Accenture and Deloitte post the most roles at lower pay. FDEs earn only about $4,000 more than software engineers on average. Figures exclude equity, which can be substantial at large AI companies.

Key points

  • Typical US FDE base salary is $155,000–$229,000 across 139 employers.
  • Entry level starts near $135,000; staff and lead roles reach about $297,000.
  • New York and the Bay Area carry the most roles and highest pay.
  • Consulting firms post the most FDE jobs but pay less than software and AI companies.
  • FDEs out-earn software engineers by only about $4,000 at the median.

Forward deployed engineers in the United States are being offered base salaries of roughly $155,000 to $229,000 a year. That range comes from 804 job postings with published pay at 139 employers, collected by nexus IT group in October 2026. Entry level roles start around $135,000, staff and lead engineers are offered up to about $297,000 and the people who manage FDE teams up to about $287,000. Those are base salaries only, and at the large AI companies equity adds a great deal on top.

Searches for this role have grown about fivefold in a year, and most published salary figures for it still date from 2024 or blend forward deployed engineers in with general software engineering. Do Forward Deployed Engineers Earn More Than Software Engineers?

Not by much, and not everywhere. We compared each company’s FDE postings on its own job board with its software engineer postings at the same time. Across the 21 companies that posted both, the midpoint of the FDE range was higher at 11, lower at 8 and identical at 2, and the typical difference was about $4,000 in the FDE’s favor.

The exceptions are telling. OpenAI and Anthropic pay their core software engineers more than their forward deployed engineers, whereas Asana, Harvey, GitLab and Together AI pay their FDEs more than their software engineers. The frontier AI labs treat research-adjacent engineering as the premium role, while application companies pay extra for the engineer who can make the product work inside a customer. Our forward deployed engineer vs software engineer comparison covers solutions, sales and consulting roles as well.

What These Ranges Leave Out

Posted ranges cover base salary. They leave out equity, which at the large AI companies can match or exceed base pay, and any bonus tied to deployment success or account expansion. They also say nothing about travel, and many FDE roles involve 25% to 50% travel or long stretches on site with a customer, which engineers weigh heavily when they compare offers. A cleared FDE role in Washington DC may also post a lower base than a commercial role in New York while being far harder to fill, because the pool of engineers holding an active clearance is small.

How These Figures Were Collected

We combined two sources in October 2026. The first is the public job boards that about 300 technology companies publish through Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever, read on October 26. The second is 727 forward deployed postings with a published pay range that appeared on LinkedIn, Indeed and Google’s job search in the previous 90 days, out of 4,158 FDE listings in that window. We kept postings with “forward deployed”, “FDE” or “FDSE” in the title and a US annual base range, and we removed staffing agencies, job aggregators and non-engineering roles such as deployment strategists, product managers and program managers.

Where an employer posted the same role at the same level and pay in several places, each city was kept once, leaving 432 distinct postings from 139 employers. Level comes from the job title. Employers that do not publish pay are not represented. Treat these as ranges, not a benchmark to offer against.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a forward deployed engineer make?

Most US forward deployed engineer roles post a base salary between about $155,000 and $229,000, based on 804 postings with published pay from 139 employers in October 2026. Entry level roles start around $135,000, and staff level engineers and FDE managers are offered up to about $290,000. Equity and bonuses come on top of those figures and can be substantial at the large AI companies.

How much does a forward deployed engineer make at Palantir?

Palantir’s current US postings offer $135,000 to $200,000 base for a forward deployed software engineer and $135,000 to $145,000 for new graduates, plus equity. Palantir created the title, so its bands are often used as the reference point, although several newer AI companies now post noticeably higher base pay for the same role.

How much does a forward deployed engineer make at C3 AI?

C3 AI posts $120,000 to $158,000 base for its federal forward deployed engineer roles and $120,000 to $150,000 for new graduates. Those are among the lower bands in our data, which fits the wider pattern that government and defense FDE roles pay less in base salary than commercial ones.

What does a forward deployed engineer at OpenAI make?

OpenAI posts $185,000 to $300,000 base for most of its forward deployed engineer roles, including the ones focused on healthcare, legal and financial services customers. Its forward deployed security engineer role is posted at $266,000 to $445,000, and its FDE managers at $302,000 to $385,000. Equity is offered on top in each case.

Do consulting firms pay forward deployed engineers less?

Usually, yes. Consulting and services firms such as Accenture, Deloitte, PwC and Capgemini posted the most FDE roles in our data, at a typical $145,000 to $220,000 base, against $156,000 to $230,000 at software and AI companies. Their bands are also unusually wide because one posting often covers many levels and states, so the top of an Accenture or Deloitte range is reached by few hires.
