Forward Deployed Engineer Salary in Kanpur, in

Salary.com · 2025-04-22 · 2 min read

Summary

According to Salary.com's Global Salary IQ data as of April 22, 2025, the average annual salary for a Forward Deployed Engineer in Kanpur, India is $18,037, or about $9 per hour. Salaries range from $14,481 (25th percentile) to $21,193 (75th percentile), depending on location, education, and experience. The article breaks pay down by experience level: entry level averages $16,445, intermediate $16,710, senior $17,241, specialist $18,979, and expert $19,827. The data comes from Salary.com's CompAnalyst platform, which covers 35 countries and offers compensation benchmarks and salary structure tools.

Key points

  • Average FDE salary in Kanpur is $18,037 per year, roughly $9 hourly.
  • Pay ranges from $14,481 to $21,193 across the 25th to 75th percentiles.
  • Entry level averages $16,445; experts with 8+ years reach $19,827.
  • Salary varies by location, education, and experience level.
  • Data sourced from Salary.com's SalaryIQ covering 35 countries.
