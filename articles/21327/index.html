Relic / Solutions Engineer / FDE — Seeking a Solutions Engineer/FDE to Drive Customer Business Through AI × Business Proposals

Summary

Relic, a Japanese co-creation firm that helps large enterprises turn new-business ideas into revenue, is hiring a Solutions Engineer / FDE via OpenWork. The role goes beyond building to spec: engineers must surface unarticulated customer problems, define requirements without written specs, handle frontend/backend work and cloud infrastructure, and use AI tools like Claude Code and Cursor to boost efficiency. Recent projects include AI platform proposals for large enterprises and shifting clients to outcome-based contracts with in-house development support. Salary ranges from ¥5.4M to ¥11.25M including 45 hours of fixed overtime, with a discretionary work schedule but mandatory afternoon office presence over half the week. Candidates need 3+ years of web development experience and demonstrated AI-driven results.

Key points

  • Relic is a new-business co-creation company serving 5,000+ Japanese firms.
  • The FDE role starts from vague, undocumented customer requirements.
  • Scope spans requirements definition, full-stack coding, cloud infra, and AI proposals.
  • Salary is ¥5.4M–¥11.25M including 45 hours fixed overtime.
  • Requires 3+ years web development and concrete AI usage experience.

That is why we founded Relic: by creating startups, internal ventures, and new businesses/projects and supporting their growth and expansion, we want world-class businesses, services, and products to emerge from Japan one after another. It is extremely difficult to instill will in those who lack it, but we can provide the necessary environment and support to those who have it. Our goal is to create a society where every ambitious challenger can create businesses, realizing the "democratization of innovation." Through democratized challenges, businesses are created, and through that rigorous process, more true managers, business leaders, and entrepreneurs are born. And the industries and businesses born from this are delivered to the world. Relic's businesses exist precisely to make this happen. To create ambitious challenges and deliver and provide diverse value from Japan to the world, we ourselves will continue to challenge ourselves even more than the challengers we support.

  • Job Title
  • Solution Engineer / FDE
  • Job Description
  • You develop systems according to the specifications provided by clients. But it doesn't end there: you draw out problems that clients themselves haven't yet been able to articulate through dialogue, and guide them to a solution. In this position, you will work as an engineer doing exactly that. At client sites, there are many vague issues that haven't yet been organized into specification documents. You will sort out what the problems are together with the client, propose directions for solving them, shape them together with the development team, and see things through to operation and adoption. We consider writing code to be just one of the means to that end. As part of your daily work, you will also use AI to streamline development and related tasks, and devote the time gained to solving client problems. ▼Main Responsibilities ・Understanding and organizing business issues through client interviews ・Proposals and estimates originating from inquiries ・Requirements definition from a state with no specifications, and driving projects forward as one with the client ・Bridging the gap between clients' vague requests and tasks the team can implement ・Proposing solutions, including the use of AI ・Designing proposals including contract structures (fixed-price, quasi-mandate, performance-based, etc.) ・Supporting clients' in-house development and accompanying them beyond delivery ・Frontend and backend implementation, technology research, selection, and introduction ・Infrastructure design, construction, and operation using cloud and hosting services ・Knowledge sharing within the team and member development (teaching is treated as part of the job) ＊You may visit client sites as needed to check on work. [Recent Examples of Initiatives] ・AI platform proposal for a major company: a proposal covering contract structure, non-functional requirements, and policies on AI accuracy ・SaaS provision and in-house development support: accompanying clients in transitioning to performance-based contracts, not just delivering and finishing ＊Details will be discussed in the interview [Technologies and Tools Used] ■ Languages / Frameworks: TypeScript / Next.js, Ruby / Ruby on Rails, PHP / Laravel, Python ■ Infrastructure / Hosting: AWS, GCP, Supabase, Vercel, Netlify ■ Development Environment / Tools: Docker, GitHub, Claude Code, Cursor, Code Rabbit ■ Communication: Slack, GitHub, Zoom, esa.io, Backlog ＊Since technology is selected per project, tech stacks other than those listed above may be adopted ＊Based on our "right person for the right place" policy, we strive to select technologies suited to the nature of each business/product
