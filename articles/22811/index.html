Companies Hiring Forward Deployed Engineers: FDE Interview Questions by Company

Crack ML Interview · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

Summary

Crack ML Interview compiles FDE hiring data across 13 companies, plus 3 more whose reported questions resemble FDE work. Its jobs board lists 517 open forward deployed roles. A table per company counts reported interview questions, rounds that fit FDE interviews, open FDE roles, and candidate experience posts. OpenAI has the most reported questions (82), Palantir the most open FDE roles (56), and Scale AI the most FDE-fitting rounds (7). The article defines each metric: questions come from user uploads and 1Point3Acres posts, and role counts are dated November 11, 2026.

Key points

  • 13 companies with FDE hiring are covered, plus 3 with FDE-like questions.
  • The jobs board lists 517 open forward deployed roles in total.
  • OpenAI leads with 82 reported interview questions.
  • Palantir has the most open FDE roles at 56.
  • Scale AI has 7 interview rounds fitting FDE work.

Full text

13 companies with forward deployed roles on our jobs board or FDE interview reports in our data, and 3 more listed only because some of their reported questions match FDE work. Each links to the company page, whose FDE section separates what candidates reported from our guidance. Our jobs board lists 517 open forward deployed roles across all companies.
