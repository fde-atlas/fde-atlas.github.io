Summary

This is a hub page from FDE总研 (FDE Soken) organizing its articles on FDE careers into five stages: entry points, preparation, hiring/selection, salary, and next career moves. It compares FDE with prior roles such as SWE, AI engineers, consultants, and SIer staff, explains how compensation is structured, and outlines three post-FDE career types (deepening, expanding, pivoting) plus freelance and side-work options. Notable figures include Palantir Japan FDE-level pay of roughly ¥8–12 million for mid-level roles and US Palantir FDSE median total compensation of $211k.

Key points

  • Hub page organizing FDE career articles into five stages: entry, preparation, selection, salary, next steps.
  • Compares FDE with prior roles: SWE, AI engineer, data scientist, consultant, PM, SIer, SES.
  • Post-FDE paths grouped into deepening, expanding, and pivoting types, plus freelance and side jobs.
  • Palantir Japan FDE-equivalent mid-level pay around ¥8–12 million; US FDSE median $211k.
  • Covers interview prep, resume writing, portfolio building, and Palantir certification guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions About the FDE Career

  • What does an FDE career path look like?
  • It is a multi-track path centered on hands-on implementation experience at customer sites, expanding into both technology and business. A single article covers how to proceed from zero experience, salary, and future prospects. (Read more)
  • What types of FDE career paths are there?
  • They can be organized into three types: deepening, expanding, and pivoting. The basic progression is "run projects → turn your approach into a repeatable framework → extend it to others." (Read more)
  • Which roles make it easiest to move into FDE?
  • You can enter from any role, including SWE, AI engineer, solutions engineer, and consultant. However, what you're missing depends on your previous role. First, check the role-by-role comparison tables above to see how your previous role differs. (Read more)
  • Can you become an FDE with no experience or as a new graduate?
  • Yes. However, openings are limited. Given the realities of the hiring market, it's realistic to decide your preparation order before making a move. (Read more)
  • How is FDE salary determined?
  • It is determined by the range stated in the job posting and each company's compensation structure (base salary, equity, and bonuses). Market rates in Japan and the US are summarized in the salary article. (Read more)
