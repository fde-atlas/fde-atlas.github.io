Summary

This page is a speaker lineup for a Rocketlane-hosted event rather than a substantive article. It lists speakers from companies including Chargebee, Contentsquare, Datatonic, Fin, Xceptor, Pendo, and AeroCloud Systems, alongside Rocketlane executives and Milos Mandic, founder of FDE Hub. Topics center on delivery strategies, professional services operating models, and AI deployment in what the event calls the "Outcome Era." Sessions are moderated by Rocketlane staff, including its CEO Srikrishnan Ganesan and co-founder Vignesh Girishankar. Readers seeking detailed arguments or data will need the actual session content.
