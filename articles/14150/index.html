Anthropic AI & Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs (53)

Forward Deploying · 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Summary

Forward Deploying tracks 53 open AI engineering roles published on Anthropic's own careers page, including 2 Forward Deployed Engineer positions, making Anthropic the 42nd of 278 employers it tracks. The US median published base salary is $350k, with 51 of 53 roles listing salary ranges. Machine Learning Engineer roles pay a median of $445k, 83% above the tracked market median, while Forward Deployed Architect roles sit 25% above market. Nearly all roles are hybrid, only 2 are remote-eligible, and all mention equity and visa sponsorship. Notably, 5 of 16 Forward Deployed adverts use pre-sales language, which the site suggests using to distinguish genuine FDE roles from re-titled pre-sales jobs.

What the adverts say

  • 14 of 53 roles were published in the last 30 days.
  • 29 of 53 adverts explicitly ask for production code.
  • 51 of 53 publish a salary range.
  • 2 are open to remote work and 51 are hybrid.
