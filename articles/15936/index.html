What is the role called in China that corresponds to FDE? Identify it by responsibilities rather than by title

This answer addresses the most common practical confusion among domestic job seekers: searching for FDE turns up few openings. The most valuable takeaway is the identification method: don't search by title, search by responsibilities—if all three markers are present (on-site discovery, end-to-end delivery, and accountability for outcomes), it's an FDE role. The article lists common domestic job titles and how closely they relate to FDE, which you can use directly to screen openings. The most common mistakes go in two directions: recognizing only the three letters "FDE" and missing many genuine roles, or trusting any title and mistaking outsourced on-site staffing for FDE. What is the FDE role called in China?

Direct answer: very few companies in China use the FDE title directly, and it often returns no results at all on job platforms. Common job titles covering this scope of responsibilities include: Solutions Engineer, AI Application Engineer, LLM Application Engineer, LLM Delivery Engineer, on-site Algorithm Engineer, AI Implementation Consultant, and pre-sales Delivery Engineer; some companies also use "AI Solutions Expert." The titles differ, but the responsibilities may overlap heavily, so the right approach is not to search for jobs by name but to identify them by responsibilities.

To identify whether a role is FDE, look for three markers. First, does the JD include on-site/customer-side discovery and research responsibilities—a true FDE goes to the customer site to clarify vague requirements, rather than receiving a fully written requirements document. Second, is the deliverable an end-to-end system—from data ingestion and application building to evals and launch, all of it is yours, not just one segment. Third, is there accountability for outcomes—at least one of acceptance metrics, usage rates, or contract renewals is tied to your performance. If all three markers are present, it's an FDE role no matter what it's called; if none are present, be wary even if the title contains "FDE." For more systematic localized background analysis, see FDE China Localization Analysis.

A few common title traps deserve a separate warning. Roles called "Solutions Engineer" are sometimes pre-sales-oriented (producing only proposals, no delivery) and sometimes delivery-oriented (essentially FDE); check the delivery weight in the JD; for how to distinguish them, see The Difference Between FDE and Pre-sales. For roles labeled "on-site," distinguish between outsourced on-site staffing and FDE on-site work with outcome accountability; for how to tell them apart, see The Difference Between Outsourced On-site Work and FDE. For on-site roles called "Algorithm Engineer," if the core work is scenario implementation and business alignment rather than training models, they are in substance closer to FDE.

Practical advice for you: on job platforms, search with combination keywords like "Solutions Engineer AI," "LLM delivery," "on-site LLM," and "AI application customer site," then score the JDs using the three markers above. In interviews, you can also ask two questions directly—"In your last project, who locked down the scope of requirements?" and "When the project hit problems, who was on site to wrap things up?"—the answers will reveal the true nature of the role. For more details on job requirements and interviews, see What FDE Interviews Test.
