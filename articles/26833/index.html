Summary

Responsible AI Practices for Enterprise AI

AI is moving rapidly from chatbots to autonomous agents that can make decisions, access sensitive data, and take actions on our behalf. But as these systems become more capable, questions around safety, bias, privacy, accountability, and human oversight become increasingly important. In this lightning lesson, we'll explore what Responsible AI is and how to make it part of your ecosystem

Build Your AI GTM Dream Team: One Agent to an Entire Team

What if your next GTM team wasn't five new hires, but five AI agents working together? AI is changing how businesses find customers, qualify leads, personalize outreach, and build sales pipelines. But the real opportunity isn't building another chatbot. It's creating coordinated teams of AI agents that can execute entire GTM workflows. In this lightning lesson, we'll explore how to design an AI-native GTM team

Product Manager to Forward Deployed Engineer: The Pivot

The line between Product Management and Engineering is disappearing. With AI coding agents and new development tools, PMs no longer have to stop at writing PRDs, defining roadmaps, or handing requirements to engineers. They can prototype, build, deploy, and own solutions end to end. Forward Deployed Engineering sits at this intersection of product thinking, customer understanding, and hands-on engineering.
