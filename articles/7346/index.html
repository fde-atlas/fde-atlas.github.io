AI Research Papers Explained for Forward Deployed Engineers

FDE Interviews · 2026-10-17 · 31 min read

Summary

FDEInterviews has assembled a research library of about 200 AI papers curated for forward deployed engineers, organized into a guided path from production foundations through retrieval, agents, evaluation, customization, and serving. Each paper gets a short explanation of its mechanism, evidence, and what changes in production. Coverage spans foundational work (Transformer, GPT-3, RAG, LoRA), retrieval and memory systems, agent benchmarks and failure modes, and inference systems like vLLM and FlashAttention, with attention to cost, reliability, and security.

Key points

  • Library of ~200 AI papers curated for FDEs
  • Six-stage path: foundations, retrieval, agents, evals, customization, serving
  • Each paper explained with mechanism, evidence, and production impact
  • Covers RAG, agents, quantization, and serving systems like vLLM
  • Includes agent failure studies and prompt injection security research

    Add controlled actions

    Evaluate reliability

    Understand customization and serving
