ElevenLabs forward deployed engineer interview

open-fde.ai · 2026-11-19 · 2 min read

Summary

This article compiles the interview process for ElevenLabs' Forward Deployed Engineer role, based mainly on one anonymous UK candidate's report plus a third-party guide. The five rounds are a ~30-minute recruiter screen, a 60-minute online coding test, live Python coding in a shared Google Doc, a case study with a role-played airline CTO diagrammed in Excalidraw, and a founder/CTO interview. Coding questions covered folder permissions with inheritance and time-ordered conflicts. Interviewers repeatedly probed individual ownership, behavioral questions appeared across rounds, and the case study rewarded customer discovery before proposing solutions. Notably, the candidate passed every round but was not hired after the role was restricted to San Francisco.

Key points

  • Five rounds: recruiter screen, online coding, live Python, case study, CTO interview.
  • Coding tested folder permissions with inheritance and time-ordered edge cases.
  • Case study required customer discovery before proposing an AI solution.
  • Interviewers probed personal ownership; behavioral questions spanned all rounds.
  • The candidate passed all rounds but lost the offer due to SF-only location limits.

Full text

ElevenLabs interview

Forward Deployed Engineer · Forward Deployed Engineer / Strategist

Process

  • Recruiter screen About 30 min. Assesses: Individual contributions on past projects, motivation. [2]
  • Online coding assessment Timed, about 60 min, three pattern-style problems on a CodeSignal-type platform. Assesses: Baseline coding; candidate called it a sanity check. [2]
  • Live coding interview Python in a shared Google Doc, opens with short behavioral chat. Assesses: Writing correct functions with hierarchy and time-ordered edge cases. [2]
  • Case study Conversational customer scenario with a role-played CTO; diagrammed on Excalidraw. Assesses: Customer discovery before solutioning. [2]
  • Founder / CTO interview High-level conversation. Assesses: Technical depth, ownership, understanding of the FDE role versus solutions architects. [2]

Questions

Coding 1
  • Folders grant or deny users and groups; write functions checking a user's file access, then handle inheritance and conflicting changes over time.
Customer scenario 1
  • An airline CTO says staff reschedule manually when planes break or schedules change. What would you do?
Domain 1
  • How does a forward deployed engineer differ from a solutions architect, and what does an FDE own?
Behavioral 2
  • Which project are you proudest of, and what did you personally build?
  • Tell me about the most complex project you have led.
Motivation 2
  • Why are you motivated by this kind of role?
  • Why ElevenLabs, and how do you view the customer-facing side of the job?

Tips from the sources

  • Be precise about what you personally built and decided; interviewers repeatedly probed individual ownership. [2]
  • Behavioral and values questions appear inside most rounds rather than in one dedicated round. [2]
  • In the case study, ask how the customer handles the problem today and what hurts before proposing anything; avoid jumping straight to an AI answer. [2]
  • Confirm hiring location and role scope early; the candidate passed every round but was not hired after the company limited the role to San Francisco. [2]
  • Be ready to describe your own AI-assisted engineering workflow. [1]

Not found in public sources: Only one detailed candidate account (S2; URL slug says solutions-architect but page labels the role FDE; interview date shown only as roughly a year before posting). No employer-published process or AI policy found.
