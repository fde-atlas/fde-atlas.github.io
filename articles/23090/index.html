Forward Deployed Engineering Intern (AI Agent) | Cresta

Zero G Talent · 2026-03-30 · 4 min read

Summary

Cresta is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineering Intern for its AI Agent team. The company, spun out of the Stanford AI Lab, has raised over $270 million from a16z, Greylock, and Sequoia; CEO Ping Wu previously founded Google's Contact Center AI and Vertex AI. The intern will help configure AI agents, build integrations with external systems, tune and troubleshoot prototypes, and work with customers and internal engineers. Requirements include enrollment in a CS or engineering degree, Python skills, and strong communication; API, cloud, or LLM experience is a plus. Pay is CAD $30-50 per hour at a downtown Toronto office near Union Station. The posting also warns about recruiting impersonation scams, noting official emails come only from @cresta.ai.

About the role:

At Cresta, the AI Agent team is on a mission to create state-of-the-art AI Agents that solve practical problems for our customers. We are focused on leveraging the latest technologies in Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI Agent systems, while ensuring that the solutions we develop are cost-effective, secure, and reliable.

As a Forward Deployed Engineering Intern on the AI Agent team, you’ll support real-world AI deployments and gain hands-on experience with AI systems in customer environments.

What You'll Do :

  • Assist in developing and configuring AI agents using Cresta’s platform tools.
  • Help build and test integrations between AI agents and external systems (like APIs or data sources).
  • Support performance tuning and troubleshooting of AI agent prototypes.
  • Work with customers and internal engineering teams to understand requirements and iterate on solutions.
  • Participate in team demos, feedback sessions, and learning opportunities.
  • This role provides mentorship and exposure to customer-facing technical problem solving in a fast-moving AI/Product environment.

What We're Looking For:

  • Enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree program in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
  • Experience writing code in Python (or another general-purpose language).
  • Basic understanding of programming fundamentals and software development best practices.
  • Strong interest in artificial intelligence, machine learning, or software engineering.
  • Excellent communication skills and willingness to collaborate with teammates and customers.

Nice-to-Haves

  • Experience with APIs, web development, or cloud platforms.
  • Prior coursework or projects involving AI/ML concepts or LLMs.
  • Exposure to prompt engineering or similar experimentation.

Compensation: CAD $30-50/hour
Perks:

  • Lunch and dinner can be expensed (up to $25 each) while working in the office
  • Well-stocked office with snacks and drinks
  • Convenient downtown location near Union Station (public transit accessible)
  • PTO: 4 days
  • Exposure to cutting-edge AI systems in production
