Honshitsu Co., Ltd. Launches "FDE Development Program" for SES Companies and SIers — Transforming Existing Engineers into Talent Who Can Propose and Implement AI Projects

Original title: 株式会社本質、SES・SIer向け「FDE育成プログラム」を提供開始――今いるエンジニアを、AI案件を提案し実装できる人材へ

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-09-08 · 7 min read

Summary

Honshitsu Inc., an AX/DX consulting startup led by Tatsuko Wada, has launched an FDE training program for SES firms and SIers in Japan. It aims to turn existing engineers into forward deployed engineers who can identify client business problems, propose AI solutions, and handle implementation and adoption. The curriculum centers on Claude, Claude Code, and Codex while adapting to tools clients actually use, such as Copilot, Gemini, and ChatGPT. Training is structured in four steps, from company-wide AI onboarding (from 50,000 yen per person) to an FDE course (from 400,000 yen), with a minimum of three participants and online or in-person delivery. The program is designed around Japan's human resource development subsidy, which can cover up to 75% of costs. The launch follows reports that major Japanese IT vendors, including Hitachi, are scaling up FDE hiring—Hitachi plans to grow its domestic FDE headcount from about 300 to roughly 1,000 by the end of fiscal 2026.

Key points

  • Honshitsu launched an FDE training program for SES firms and SIers.
  • It retrains existing engineers to propose and implement AI solutions.
  • Four-step curriculum uses Claude, Claude Code, Codex, plus client tools.
  • Pricing runs from 50,000 to 400,000 yen per person; minimum three participants.
  • Subsidies may cover up to 75%; Hitachi plans ~1,000 FDEs by fiscal 2026.

Practical use of Claude, Claude Code, Codex and more. Develops in-house FDEs who handle everything from proposal to implementation; available online or in person, with a minimum of 3 participants

Honshitsu Inc. (Representative Director: Tatsuko Wada; hereinafter "Honshitsu"), which provides AX and DX support, has begun offering an "FDE Development Program" for SES companies and SIers.

This program develops existing engineers into "FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers)" who can identify customers' business challenges, propose ways to leverage AI, and see projects through implementation and adoption.

The demand is shifting from "the ability to build" to "the ability to propose and implement"

With the spread of AI, the role that client companies expect from SES companies and SIers is changing. Beyond the ability to develop according to specifications, the importance of talent who can identify on-site "where AI should be introduced into business operations," make proposals, and handle implementation is growing.

Against this backdrop, major domestic IT vendors are also stepping up efforts to acquire and develop FDEs. In August 2026, it was reported that four major domestic IT companies are expanding their FDE capabilities. Hitachi, Ltd. has announced a plan to increase its domestic FDE headcount from approximately 300 as of July 2026 to approximately 1,000 by the end of that fiscal year.

This trend shows that talent who can embed themselves in customers' operations and handle everything from identifying challenges to AI design and implementation is becoming a key role in future IT services.

On the other hand, the FDE is still a new profession. It is not easy to secure, through external hiring alone, talent who combine an understanding of customer business, proposal skills, and AI-driven implementation capabilities.

Honshitsu concluded that one solution lies in "investing in the engineers you already have." We developed this program to develop existing engineers into FDEs who can leverage their existing technical skills and experience while handling everything from discovering customer challenges to AI proposal, implementation, and adoption.

Five Features of the "FDE Development Program"

[1] A consistent curriculum from proposal to implementation — not stopping at "being able to use" AI

Rather than just learning how to operate AI tools, participants learn the full process consistently: discovering challenges in customer business, designing AI use cases, proposing, implementing, and establishing operational adoption. Centered on exercises based on actual projects or in-house operations, the program builds skills that can be used in the field even after training ends.

[2] Hands-on training tailored to the client's environment

While focusing on Claude, Claude Code, and Codex, the curriculum is designed around the AI tools adopted by the participating company and its customers, such as Copilot, Gemini, and ChatGPT. The goal is talent who do not depend on a specific product but can choose and implement the appropriate means according to the customer environment.

[3] Development in four stages, from all-hands participation to selecting FDE candidates

In STEP 1, the "AI Setup Training," all engineers build a common foundation for starting to use AI in their work. In STEPs 2 and 3, participants learn practical AI use and business automation, and in STEP 4, selected members are developed as FDEs.

Rather than limiting participants by years of experience alone, we work with the participating company to determine suitable participants for each course through preliminary interviews.

[4] Available both online and in person

The program can be completed entirely online, and can also be held in person upon request. Dates, times, and the number of sessions are flexibly designed according to work arrangements and project circumstances, so even engineers stationed at client sites can attend in line with their on-site workload.

[5] A minimal start from just 3 participants

The program can be introduced with a minimum of 3 participants. You can start with a small group and, while reviewing training outcomes and project strategy, gradually expand the participants and scope.

Four-Stage Course Design and Pricing

We offer four courses, from the introductory stage of "getting everyone to start using AI" to the business-building stage of "proposing and implementing AI projects." How far you aim to go can be chosen according to the participating company's talent composition and project strategy.

STEP 1 | AI Setup Training

Goal: Everyone can start using AI in their work

Price: From 50,000 yen per person (excluding tax; approximate)

STEP 2 | AI Utilization Course

Goal: Able to use AI effectively in practical work

Price: From 200,000 yen per person (excluding tax; approximate)

STEP 3 | AI Agent Development Course

Goal: Able to automate routine tasks

Price: From 300,000 yen per person (excluding tax; approximate)

STEP 4 | FDE Development Course

Goal: Able to propose and implement generative AI projects

Price: From 400,000 yen per person (excluding tax; approximate)

*Prices vary depending on the number of sessions, hours, participants, AI tools covered, and other factors. A detailed quote will be provided after an interview.

Designed in line with the requirements of the Human Resources Development Subsidy Program

This program's course structure and training hours are designed in consideration of the requirements of the Human Resources Development Subsidy Program.

If the requirements of courses such as the "Business Expansion and Other Reskilling Support Course" are met, up to 75% of eligible training expenses may be subsidized. By utilizing this program, it may be possible to reduce the effective cost of adoption.

*Eligibility for subsidies, subsidy rates, and subsidy amounts vary depending on institutional requirements such as company size, training content, and timing, as well as screening results. Subsidy payment is not guaranteed. For the latest program details, please refer to the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's "Human Resources Development Subsidy Program."

Comment from Representative Director Tatsuko Wada

To contribute to Japan's AX/DX transformation, I founded Honshitsu Inc. together with SES engineers.

I deeply respect the fact that SES work has long supported Japan's IT frontline. However, that industry now faces a difficult juncture, with caps on per-person-month rates, an outflow of young talent, and AI transforming the nature of projects. Companies in trouble and highly capable engineers. What connects them is the FDE: someone who listens to the customer's challenges, proposes solutions using AI, and takes on the implementation themselves.

The engineers your company has cultivated are your most valuable resource. Our role is to pave the way to bring out their full potential and support them along the way. We want engineers to become people who can create both their rates and their reasons for being chosen themselves. We aspire to be the force that makes that happen.
