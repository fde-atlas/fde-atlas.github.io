Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs Tracker

MarsLab · 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Summary

MarsLab maintains a tracker counting open roles whose titles contain "forward deployed" on each employer's public job board, last updated 14 November 2026. Databricks leads with 83 openings (9% of its 896 openings), followed by Palantir with 72 (24% of its 302), OpenAI with 36, Scale AI with 12, Anthropic with 8, and Ramp with 2. Common locations include New York, London, and Washington, D.C., with Palantir heavily concentrated in government-related roles. Roles titled differently, such as Applied AI Engineer or Deployment Strategist, are excluded, and multi-city postings count once per city. Counts change daily.

Key points

  • Databricks has the most FDE openings at 83.
  • Palantir's 72 FDE roles are 24% of its openings.
  • OpenAI lists 36; Scale AI 12, Anthropic 8, Ramp 2.
  • Top locations include New York, London, and Washington, D.C.
  • Only titles containing "forward deployed" are counted.

How this is counted

Each number comes from the employer's own public job board. A role counts when its title contains "forward deployed". Roles under other names, such as Applied AI Engineer or Deployment Strategist, are not counted, and the same role posted in several cities counts once per city. Counts change daily. MarsLab is not a job board and does not place candidates.
