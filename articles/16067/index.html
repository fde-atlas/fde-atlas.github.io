Forward Deployed Engineer jobs in Australia (2026)

FDE Times · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Summary

This article is a job board roundup listing 12 open Forward Deployed Engineer roles across 9 companies in Australia, sourced from company careers pages and updated daily. Palantir has the most openings with 3, including two Australia Government roles in Sydney and Canberra plus an internship; Databricks follows with 2, including a manager position. Other employers are OpenAI, Cursor, Cognition, P-1 AI, Rogo, Nash, and Cresta, with one role each. Most positions are Sydney-based, though Cresta's senior role is remote and one Cursor role covers Australia/Singapore. For context, the article compares other markets: New York has 158 open FDE roles and the San Francisco Bay Area 154, far exceeding Australia's total.

Key points

  • 12 open FDE roles at 9 Australian-hiring companies, updated daily.
  • Palantir leads with 3 roles, including two Australia Government positions.
  • Databricks has 2 openings; eight other companies have one each.
  • Most roles are in Sydney; Cresta offers a remote position.
  • New York (158) and SF Bay Area (154) dwarf Australia's FDE market.
