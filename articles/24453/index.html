What is FDE?

Original title: FDEとは？

日本Fde協会 · 2026-05-01 · 2 min read

Summary

An introductory article from the Japan FDE Association explaining the Forward Deployed Engineer role, which originated at Palantir Technologies. FDEs bring products into customer environments, customizing, implementing, and embedding them to solve business problems. The article contrasts FDEs with SIers/SEs, SaaS customer success, and consultants: FDEs combine deep technical and business involvement and work closest to customers, delivering problem-solving itself. Required skills span programming, data analysis, and prototyping; requirements definition and ROI thinking; plus communication and adaptability—skills honed on the job. It argues that in the AI era, integrating AI into workflows, preparing data, and driving adoption still require humans, making the FDE the bridge between AI and the field, with demand rising globally. A career path from junior FDE to lead or founder is outlined.

Key points

  • FDE originated at Palantir: engineers embed products at customer sites.
  • Unlike SIers, SaaS CS, or consultants, FDEs go deep on both tech and business.
  • Required skills are technical, business, and human, learned through practice.
  • In the AI era, FDEs bridge AI capabilities and real-world operations.
  • Career path runs from junior FDE to lead, independence, or founding a company.

A new type of engineer who stands between technology and business

Definition of FDE

Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) is a role that originally emerged at Palantir Technologies in the US. It refers to engineers who bring their company's product to the customer's site and handle everything from customization and implementation to adoption support, tailored to the customer's business challenges. In recent years, the term has expanded beyond Palantir to describe engineers in general who solve problems through technology while working closely with customers. In particular, with the rapid advancement of AI and LLM technologies, demand for FDEs is growing worldwide as specialists who implement and embed AI-driven business improvements on the ground at customer sites.

Skills required for FDEs

Technical skills

Programming, data analysis, cloud infrastructure, API design, prototyping

Business skills

Requirements definition, presentations, client relations, industry knowledge, ROI thinking

Human skills

Problem discovery, communication, adaptability, ability to bring people on board

You don't need to have all of these from the start. FDE skills are honed through practice.

FDEs in demand in the AI era

No matter how much AI performance improves, human involvement is required to integrate it into on-site operations and achieve results. Understanding business workflows, preparing data, and supporting adoption by frontline staff—these cannot be accomplished by AI alone. The role of connecting AI with the field—that is the essence of the FDE in the AI era.
