Summary

This is a job posting from OpenWork for a Forward Deployed Engineer at Treasure Data (Treasure AI) in Tokyo. The role sits in the professional services team and functions as a solutions architect for the company's customer data platform (CDP), covering consulting, architecture design, code reviews, and onboarding for global enterprise clients. Requirements include 2+ years of SQL and ETL experience and a data/technology background; Python, cloud platforms, and English skills are preferred. Compensation is listed at 10-16 million yen annually, including 30 hours of fixed overtime, with a full-flex schedule, on-site work in Azabudai Hills, and benefits such as RSUs and a 401(k)-style pension.

Key points

  • FDE role at Treasure Data, effectively a solutions architect for its CDP.
  • Duties include consulting, architecture design, code reviews, and global deployments.
  • Requires 2+ years of SQL and ETL experience; cloud and English skills preferred.

  • On-site in Tokyo's Azabudai Hills; full-flex hours and RSU benefits offered.
