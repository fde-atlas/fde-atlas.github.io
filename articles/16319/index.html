Summary

This ranking places Google Cloud's Forward Deployed Engineering unit sixth among 2026 FDE service providers and suggests higher-ranked alternatives by use case. Beyond Elevation tops the list with fully published pricing, including fractional engagements from $5,800 per month. HatchWorks AI ranks second with model-neutral FDE work across major AI stacks. Deloitte claims the first Salesforce FDE engagement among GSIs, while Accenture runs named FDE practices for Microsoft, SAP and ServiceNow. Enterprise firms generally keep pricing undisclosed and quote per engagement.
