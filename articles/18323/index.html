Knowledge Communication Launches "FDE-Type AI Implementation Support Service" to Help Embed AI into Business Operations

Original title: ナレッジコミュニケーション、AIの業務現場への定着を支援する「FDE型AI導入支援サービス」を提供開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-10-21 · 5 min read

Summary

Japanese IT firm Knowledge Communication has launched an "FDE-type" AI adoption support service covering the full cycle: identifying business problems, building data and knowledge foundations, developing AI agents, production deployment, and continuous improvement. The company stresses that its FDE approach is not merely about stationing engineers on-site but about working close to the customer while deeply understanding their processes and decision criteria. It selects and builds platforms such as Microsoft Foundry, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Microsoft Copilot Studio, Amazon Bedrock, and Databricks based on each client's environment and security needs. A typical project takes 2–4 weeks for assessment, 1–2 months for prototyping, and roughly 2–4 months to initial production deployment, with pricing quoted individually.

Key points

  • Knowledge Communication launched an end-to-end FDE-type AI adoption service.
  • FDE is defined as a close-accompaniment approach, not just on-site staffing.
  • Supports Microsoft Foundry, Amazon Bedrock, Databricks, and other platforms.
  • Builds a continuous "AI loop" that tunes agents from usage data.
  • Initial production deployment typically takes 2–4 months; pricing is custom.

Full text

— End-to-end support from identifying business challenges to production deployment and continuous improvement of AI agents —

Knowledge Communication Co., Ltd. (Head office: Ichikawa City, Chiba Prefecture; Representative Director: Akira Okusawa; hereinafter "Knowledge Communication") announces the launch of its "Forward Deployed Engineer (hereinafter "FDE")-type AI implementation support service," which works alongside customers' business operations to provide end-to-end support, from identifying challenges to be solved with AI, to building data and knowledge foundations, developing AI agents, production deployment, on-the-ground adoption, and continuous improvement.

In this service, we select and build the optimal AI foundation for each customer's existing environment, security requirements, and target business processes, drawing on a range of platforms including our proprietary AI utilization platform "Narecom AI Platform," Microsoft Foundry, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Microsoft Copilot Studio, Amazon Bedrock, and Databricks.

Rather than simply deploying tools, we rebuild employees' accumulated expertise, decision-making criteria, and operational know-how into a "knowledge foundation that AI can leverage." Furthermore, we support the establishment of a "company-specific AI utilization cycle (AI loop)" in which usage and business outcomes after launch are fed back to continuously evolve the AI agents.

■ Background of the Service

In recent years, while corporate adoption of generative AI and AI agents has been advancing rapidly, a growing number of companies face challenges such as "although we deployed tools, usage remains limited to document creation and information retrieval by a subset of employees" and "adoption has not led to company-wide business efficiency gains or on-the-ground uptake."

To connect AI to real business outcomes (ROI), it is essential to go beyond simply procuring and deploying tools: identifying which business processes to apply it to, organizing and structuring internal data and knowledge, integrating with existing systems, and establishing a continuous improvement process after production deployment.

Leveraging its track record and expertise in supporting cloud, data analytics, and generative AI implementations for numerous companies, Knowledge Communication provides comprehensive support covering not only technical assistance but everything from business design to on-the-ground adoption.

■ Key Features of the FDE-type AI Implementation Support Service

In this service, a dedicated team of AI engineers, data engineers, cloud engineers, and consultants works closely with customers' business stakeholders to drive projects forward.

The "FDE-type (Forward Deployed Engineer)" approach does not simply mean stationing engineers at the customer's site. Regardless of whether engineers are on-site, it is an accompaniment-style approach in which, after deeply understanding the customer's business processes, existing systems, and on-the-ground decision-making criteria, we provide continuous support in the closest proximity to the customer—from identifying the true challenges to be solved through to post-deployment improvement.

■ Main Support Services

1. Business Assessment / Workshops

Based on management and business strategy as well as on-the-ground business challenges, we formulate a company-wide vision for AI utilization. After organizing the desired future state enabled by AI, target departments and processes, required data and knowledge, security and governance policies, and internal promotion structures, we select priority use cases from the perspectives of business impact and feasibility, and define KPIs and a phased implementation roadmap.

2. Building Data and Knowledge Foundations

We collect and organize data, manuals, and operational decision-making know-how that are scattered and buried within the company. We build a knowledge foundation designed for AI utilization, with due consideration for appropriate access permissions and data governance (security).

3. AI Agent Development, PoC, and Production Deployment

We select the optimal AI foundation for the customer and, targeting priority use cases, rapidly develop prototypes to validate business fit, answer accuracy, usability, security, and expected business impact. Even when a PoC is conducted, we do not stop at validation alone; based on the results, we provide consistent support through to migration to the production environment.

4. Operations and Continuous Improvement (Building the AI Loop)

We quantify and evaluate post-deployment usage and business outcomes, and continuously tune and improve data sources, knowledge, prompts, workflows, and AI agent accuracy.

Regarding the delivery timeline for each support service, in a standard project, after an assessment and vision-formulation phase of approximately 2 to 4 weeks, prototype development and impact validation are carried out over about 1 to 2 months. The project then moves on to production deployment, rollout to user departments, and continuous improvement. Initial production deployment is targeted at roughly 2 to 4 months from project start. Pricing for each service is proposed individually after confirming the target business processes and system configuration.

*Timelines vary depending on the target business processes, state of data readiness, system integration, and security requirements.
