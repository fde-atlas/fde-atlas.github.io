Google Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Experience (2026)

Aced (formerly Exponent) · 2026-10-01 · 1 min read

Summary

This is a candidate-reported interview experience for a Google Forward Deployed Engineer role, published on Aced (formerly Exponent). The technical round featured an intentionally vague system description, testing whether the candidate proactively asked clarifying questions — a skill the candidate believes is central to the FDE role. After questioning, they understood the system but ran out of time before completing the design. The post also includes quotes from other Google candidates: one said rounds mapped to rubrics in name only, with a Googliness round turning into Google Photos design; another L6 EM candidate faced a final round requiring manual debugging of 200+ lines of interval logic in a Google Doc with no IDE.

Key points

  • Interviewer gave a deliberately vague system design prompt to test clarifying questions
  • Candidate understood the system after questioning but ran out of time
  • Other candidates report rubrics didn't match actual interview questions
  • L6 EM final round: debugging 200+ lines of code in a Google Doc, no IDE

Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Experience

Interview process

The interviewer gave me a very abstract description of a system, I needed to design, intentionally to let me clarify it through the questions. Most likely because role success depends on how critical it is for you to bother other people with bombarding questions. After all the questions, I clearly understood the system I needed to design, but time was almost up, so I did not have a chance to finish it as I wanted, but maybe someone else will.
