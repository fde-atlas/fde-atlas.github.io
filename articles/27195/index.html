Today's Judgment

There aren't many public signals today, but the direction is consistent: once enterprise AI agents move from "running" to "running continuously," cost and accountability start to become part of delivery.

Tencent Cloud has written the peak/off-peak billing rules for DeepSeek models into both TokenHub and the agent development platform ADP; Wuhan plans to release 55 typical "AI + consumption" application scenarios; and People's Daily's observation from the Digital Trade Expo notes that the industry is shifting from trialing general-purpose models to embedding dedicated models into critical business processes.

All three signals remain at the level of product rules, lists, and observations, but they carry a direct reminder for FDEs: if quotes and demos only discuss model capabilities without specifying running costs and accountable owners, projects can easily lose budget control once they enter continuous operation.

Key Signals

Tencent Cloud adjusts DeepSeek peak/off-peak billing: model unit prices begin to vary by time of day

  • Source: Tencent Cloud announcement
  • On September 24, Tencent Cloud published an announcement adjusting the peak/off-peak billing rules for DeepSeek models on TokenHub and the agent development platform ADP.
  • Why it matters: Billing rules entering agent platforms means the same task may cost differently depending on when it runs. Once an enterprise AI agent runs continuously, model unit price, invocation timing, task routing, and the quality the business can accept all need to enter the ledger of "cost per qualified task." Batch tasks that aren't urgent can be scheduled into off-peak windows—this alone is a cost-reduction design that can be written into proposals. Note: this is a billing change; the announcement does not disclose customer adoption, call volumes, or cost savings, so no enterprise ROI can be inferred from it.

Wuhan plans to release 55 typical "AI + consumption" application scenarios

  • Source: Wuhan Municipal Bureau of Economy and Information Technology
  • The Wuhan Municipal Bureau of Economy and Information Technology has published a list of 55 typical "AI + consumption" application scenarios for planned release.
  • Why it matters: Following Hunan's energy sector, Suzhou's industrial sector, and Nantong's state-owned enterprises, the consumer sector now also has a batch of scenario lists, as local governments roll out scenario supply across more industries. Like the other lists, it is currently at the public-notice stage; project owners, budgets, data authorizations, pilots, and acceptance criteria have not been made public.

People's Daily's Digital Trade Expo observation: shifting from trialing general-purpose models to embedding dedicated models into critical processes

  • Source: People's Daily Online
  • People's Daily's observational coverage during the Digital Trade Expo notes that the industry is moving from the trial phase of general-purpose models to embedding dedicated models into critical business processes.
  • Why it matters: "Embedding into critical processes" means models must engage with real business standards, data permissions, and accountable owners—exactly the parts that can be avoided during general-purpose trials but that FDEs must handle. Note: this is a journalist's observation, with no attributable enterprise performance data.

Takeaways for FDE Practitioners

  • Add running-cost columns to your diagnostic table: task volume, model and tool calls, peak/off-peak pricing, human takeover, failure reruns, cost per qualified output, and budget owner. Any platform recommendation should also include paths for decommissioning, migration, and data exit.
  • Proceed by the "minimal construction blueprint": scenarios and owners → data and permissions → baseline → shadow recommendations → human approval → acceptance and review. Platforms, models, and entry points can all be swapped, but business standards, decision records, and acceptance ledgers cannot be missing.
  • Keep human approval for write operations: For writes to ERP systems, approval workflows, and external communications, default to "AI proposes, human approves, system logs"—only consider delegating authority after enough accuracy data has accumulated.
