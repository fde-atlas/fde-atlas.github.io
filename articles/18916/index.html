In the US, "FDEs" Earn ¥32 Million a Year: The Challenges Reflected by AI's New Star Profession

Original title: 米では年収3200万円の「FDE」、AI時代の新花形職種が映す課題

日本経済新聞社 · 2026-06-15 · 1 min read

Summary

A Nikkei column by editor Okuhei Yukiyama examines the rise of the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), a new high-profile role in the AI era. FDEs embed deeply within customer companies to help them adopt AI, and in the US can earn around 32 million yen annually. The piece cites Google Cloud Japan and Fanuc's AI adoption as examples, and argues that the fate of this role may shape the direction of the AI-driven economy. The full article is behind Nikkei's paywall.

Key points

  • FDEs embed in customer companies to drive AI adoption.
  • US-based FDEs can earn about 32 million yen per year.
  • Google Cloud Japan and Fanuc are cited as AI adoption examples.
  • The author links the FDE role's fate to the AI economy's future.
  • The full text requires a paid Nikkei subscription.

Full text

Editorial Writer, Kazuyuki Okuhira

Considering the news from diverse perspectives

In the IT industry, where new terms are constantly coined and discarded, the one currently drawing attention is the "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)." It refers to an engineer who embeds deeply within client companies to support the adoption of artificial intelligence (AI). The fate of this role could shape the future of an AI-driven economy.
