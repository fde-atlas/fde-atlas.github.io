1-Day AI Assessment | Decide which work to hand over to AI, in just one day.

Original title: 1Day AIアセスメント｜AIに任せる仕事、1日で決まる。

FDX株式会社 · 2026-11-12 · 9 min read

Summary

FDX's 1Day AI Assessment is a fixed-price (¥100,000) diagnostic service for companies with 100+ employees, limited to five clients per month. A consultant interviews departments for one day, inventorying up to 100 business tasks and quantifying their annual hours. Within two business days, clients receive a report estimating reduction rates, methods (generative AI, SaaS, or custom development), costs, and payback periods. The article argues failed AI adoption stems from wrong sequencing: companies deploy tools or training before inventorying and quantifying workloads, citing a survey showing 64.9% leave generative AI use to individuals. Follow-up options include FDX's proprietary AXceed progress-tracking tool and a 1Day FDE retainer from ¥300,000/month.

Key points

  • One-day interview inventories up to 100 tasks; report delivered in two business days.
  • Priced at ¥100,000, limited to five companies monthly, 100+ employees only.
  • Core argument: quantify and inventory work before choosing tools or training.
  • Tasks classified into generative AI, SaaS, or development with reduction estimates.
  • Follow-up via AXceed tracking tool or 1Day FDE retainer from ¥300,000/month.

ROOT CAUSE

What companies struggling with AI adoption have in common is starting with tool implementation or training without inventorying tasks and quantifying time. If you can't see which tasks take how many hours, you can't measure where AI should be applied or its effects.

The 1-Day AI Assessment is a service that uses one day of your time to complete, all at once, the "inventory you should have done first."

The common order: Tool implementation → Training → Wait for organic spread on the ground ✕ Effects can't be measured

The correct order: Task inventory → Time quantification → Decide which tasks to delegate to AI → Tools, training, development ✓ You can speak in reduction amounts

HOW IT WORKS

You fill in your departments and main tasks in a dedicated format. The interview sheet is fully templated, minimizing preparation burden on your side.

A consultant interviews each department. We inventory up to 100 tasks and quantify frequency, hours, and headcount.

The "AI Transfer Diagnostic Report" is delivered 2 business days later. In an online report meeting (60 minutes), we explain priorities and next steps.

All interview and analysis methods are fully templated. That's why it takes just one day and can be offered at this price.

DELIVERABLE

With numbers you can use for investment decisions.

The diagnostic report analyzes all tasks in three steps. Not intuitive AI proposals, but precision you can use directly in internal approval processes.

STEP 1

Quantifies the actual state by department and task, and scores priorities.

  • Target departments/sections, task names, and task details
  • Frequency, hours required, number of occurrences, and number of people involved
  • Monthly/annual required hours (h) calculated per task

→

STEP 2

Assesses AI suitability per task and maps them into 3 categories.

  • AI feasibility and estimated hours reduction (%)
  • AI suitability assessment / conditions, method overview, rough investment estimate
  • Classified into "replace with generative AI," "replace with SaaS," or "automate with development"

STEP 3

You can see at a glance where to start.

  • Implementation cost, annual improvement cost, and estimated payback period
  • Estimated improvement hours
  • Similar cases and results attached (e.g., ◯% reduction)
