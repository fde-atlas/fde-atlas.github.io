Google FDE Interview Prep

Educative · 2026-10-29 · 2 min read

Summary

Educative offers a 48-lesson, 9-module course for preparing for Google Forward Deployed Engineer interviews, created by Educative co-founder and CEO Fahim ul Haq, previously an engineer at Microsoft and Meta. It covers the FDE role versus solutions architects, consultants, and product engineers; building agents on the Gemini stack with function calling, ADK, MCP, and A2A; designing grounded text-to-SQL and retrieval systems on BigQuery with data access enforced in the database; and building evals, guardrails, and observability. It also walks through Google's interview loop, including coding, agentic system design, and Googleyness rounds, plus three case studies spanning retail analytics, EU-regulated customer service agents, and public sector document processing.

Key points

  • 48 lessons across 9 modules targeting Google's FDE interview loop.
  • Explains how FDEs differ from solutions architects, consultants, and product engineers.
  • Teaches agent building on Gemini: function calling, ADK, MCP, A2A.
  • Covers evals, guardrails, and observability beyond demo success.
  • Includes three case studies: retail text-to-SQL, EU AI regulation, public sector documents.

What you'll learn

  • Explain what a Google Forward Deployed Engineer does and how the role differs from solutions architects, consultants, and product engineers.
  • Build agents on the Gemini stack using function calling, ADK, MCP, and A2A, and decide when a managed runtime beats self-hosting.
  • Design grounded text-to-SQL and retrieval systems over BigQuery that enforce data access in the database, not in the prompt.
  • Build evaluation harnesses, guardrails, and observability that separate real readiness from curated demo success.
  • Structure answers for each round of Google’s interview loop, from coding and vibe coding to agentic system design and Googleyness.
  • Scope customer problems, defend architecture trade-offs under challenge, and make only commitments supported by the evidence.
