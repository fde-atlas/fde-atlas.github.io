Summary

This digest from FDE Times tracks a month of forward deployed engineer news. Open FDE roles fluctuated between 625 and 641, with Databricks cutting the most listings while Amazon grew fastest. Under one job title, coding can fill anywhere from 30% to 90% of the week. AI labs now pay premiums for FDEs skilled in evals, with Snorkel AI advertising $320,000. Salary figures diverge sharply depending on methodology: Levels.fyi's median of about $223,000 counts self-reported stock and bonus, while job-post medians count advertised ranges. Clearance-required roles at Palantir, Vannevar Labs and Red Cell face long waits, with Top Secret processing averaging 243 days even in fast cases.

Key points

  • Open FDE roles ranged from 625 to 641; Databricks cut most, Amazon grew fastest.
  • Coding share under the FDE title spans 30% to 90%; verify before signing.
  • AI labs hire eval-skilled FDEs; Snorkel AI offers $320,000.
  • Salary medians differ by methodology; check what a figure measures before negotiating.
  • Clearance roles at Palantir and Vannevar Labs; Top Secret averages 243 days.
