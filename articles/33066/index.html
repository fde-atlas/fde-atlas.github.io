Forward deployed engineer market report, November 2026

Kernavique · 2026-11-26 · 3 min read

Summary

Kernavique's November 2026 report counts US job postings with 'forward deployed' in the title, read daily from employers' own job feeds. As of November 26, 2026, there were 297 open postings at 65 employers, with 259 publishing a salary range from $93,000 (Invisible Technologies) to $445,000 (OpenAI). Databricks led with 63 postings. The report breaks down counts by employer, city, level, industry and skill, and includes no estimates. It excludes same-work roles under other titles and employers without public feeds, and offers no average salary because published ranges differ in composition.

Questions people ask

  • Where do the figures come from?
  • From 76 employers’ own public job feeds, read once a day. A posting is counted when its title contains “forward deployed” and its location is in the United States. This report uses the last reading of the month, on November 26, 2026.
  • Is this the whole market?
  • No. It covers the employers whose feeds we read, and only postings with “forward deployed” in the title. Roles that do the same work under another title are not counted, and neither are employers without a public feed.
  • Why is there no average salary?
  • Because the published ranges are not alike. Some are base salary, some include bonus or equity, and some ads do not say. We give the lowest bottom figure and the highest top figure, and our salary page shows every range with the employer’s own wording.
  • Does the report change after the month ends?
  • The current month is updated with each daily reading until the month ends. After that it stays as it was on its last reading.
