Claude Code for FDE: Architect Track, free

Traversaal · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

Traversaal offers a free course, Claude Code for FDE: Architect Track, taught by founder Hamza Farooq to prepare for Anthropic's Claude Certified Architect exam. The 12-chapter, roughly one-hour track covers all five exam domains with sample questions in exam style, plus rules of thumb and a study plan. A free study kit includes Anthropic's courses, domain cheat sheets, a browser-based practice exam with explanations for wrong answers, and hands-on starter projects. Farooq recommends about a month of hands-on preparation, noting the exam rewards building over memorization.

Key points

  • 12 chapters, 1h 05m total, free to watch.
  • Domain weights: Agentic Architecture 27%, Configuration 20%, Prompt Engineering 20%, Tool Design & MCP 18%, Context Management 15%.
  • Each domain chapter includes a sample exam-style question.
  • Study kit: Anthropic courses, cheat sheets, practice exam, starter projects.
  • Farooq suggests about a month of hands-on prep; building beats memorizing.
