Roles - Brisbane

Re:Sourced runs structured search for forward deployed engineers in Brisbane. Median 21 days from brief to signed offer, 90-day replacement guarantee, calibrated against an active network of senior engineers across the ANZ market.

The role

Customer-embedded engineer who ships production integrations inside client environments post-sale. Common in AI-native and enterprise SaaS. The qualifying pool is thin because the title only consolidated around 2023-24; most strong candidates carry other titles, so searches rely on profile translation rather than keyword sourcing.

In Brisbane, forward deployed engineer hiring is led by the city's enterprise software, govtech and scale-up employers, from TechnologyOne and Go1 through to a tight defence and government-adjacent tail. The local pool is smaller than the southern capitals, so searches frequently widen to remote-Australia candidates to fill senior customer-embedded roles.
