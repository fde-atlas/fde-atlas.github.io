Forward Deployed Engineers for AI Agents and Automation

10Clouds · 2020 · 3 min read

Summary

This is a marketing page for 10Clouds' forward deployed engineer service. Engineers embed with client teams to build and deploy AI agents that automate workflows such as customer service and recruitment, with initial deployment in 2-4 weeks. The page claims adaptation of open-source models like Llama achieves over 90% accuracy on targeted tasks, and that clients save 30-40% on costs. Services span GenAI implementation, backend, and ReactJS development, targeting finance, HR, IT, and e-commerce. All figures are self-reported and unverified.

Key points

  • 10Clouds offers forward deployed engineers who embed with client teams to deploy AI agents.
  • Initial deployment takes 2-4 weeks, covering assessment, design, integration, and team training.
  • Claims Llama-based adaptations reach 90%+ accuracy on targeted tasks.
  • Clients reportedly save 30-40% on costs through AI automation.
  • Content is promotional; all metrics are company-claimed without third-party verification.
