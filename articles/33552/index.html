A Forward Deployed Engineer in London builds and ships software, today mostly AI systems, inside a customer's own environment. On November 26, 2026 our feed held 22 open Forward Deployed Engineer London roles, behind only San Francisco and New York, and public UK ad data puts the median advertised salary at £100,000.

This page is for two readers: engineers looking at forward deployed engineer jobs in London, and hiring managers opening a London seat. We use our own feed, which we read daily from employers' own job boards, and public sources we name and date.

How big is the Forward Deployed Engineer London market?

Small in absolute numbers, and growing fast.

  • Our feed, November 26, 2026: 121 open Forward Deployed Engineer roles across the markets we cover. San Francisco has 43, New York 36 and London 22. No other city has more than 6. You can see the live list on our Forward Deployed Engineer jobs page and every London AI role on /jobs/london/.
  • Public UK data: IT Jobs Watch counted 51 permanent Forward Deployed Engineer adverts in London in the six months to November 26, 2026, against 6 in the same period of 2025.

The two counts measure different things. Ours is a snapshot of roles open today on employer career pages. IT Jobs Watch counts adverts over six months, including those placed by agencies. Both point the same way: a title that barely existed in London two years ago is now a regular hire.

Are forward-deployed engineers in demand in London?

Yes, and the demand comes from three kinds of employer.

  • US AI companies opening or growing a UK team. Replit advertises its London role as its first Forward Deployed Engineer in the UK. The OpenAI Deployment Company is hiring Forward Deployed Engineers for London and Edinburgh.
  • Data and infrastructure vendors with large enterprise customers in Europe. Databricks and n8n both have London forward deployed roles in our feed this month, and Notion has a London Forward Deployed Architect role.
  • UK and European startups selling AI agents to regulated industries. Murphy AI, for example, hires London Forward Deployed Engineers to launch AI collections agents for lenders, telcos and utilities.

Across our whole feed, the companies with the most open Forward Deployed Engineer roles on November 26, 2026 were OpenAI (12), Databricks (7), ServiceNow (6), Celonis (6) and Saronic (6). Not all of those roles are in London, but several of these companies hire there.

The skills London adverts ask for are consistent. In the IT Jobs Watch sample of 51 permanent adverts, 49.0% mention AI, 33.3% Python, 27.5% LLMs and 23.5% retrieval-augmented generation. If you are new to the role, start with what a Forward Deployed Engineer is.

Three things to read from this table.

The public median understates the top of the market. Many of the adverts in public UK data come from agencies and consultancies. The AI companies that post their own ranges go well above £120,000 at the top end, and add equity.

London pays less than the US for the same title. Our feed median of $221,250 is driven by San Francisco and New York. A London offer at the top of a £100K to £180K band is strong for the UK and still below a typical Bay Area base. Our Forward Deployed Engineer salary guide has the full breakdown.

Ranges are wide because the title covers several levels. Murphy AI asks for 1 to 3 years in a technical role. Replit asks for 5 or more years building and deploying production software. Both are called Forward Deployed Engineer.

One caution on the trend: IT Jobs Watch shows the London median moving from £70,000 to £100,000 year on year, but the 2025 figure rests on only 6 quoted salaries. Treat the direction as real and the size of the jump as uncertain.

What London employers expect

We read the live London adverts so you do not have to. The common pattern:

  • Hybrid, not remote. Replit lists its London role as hybrid. Murphy AI states three days a week in its London office. The OpenAI Deployment Company describes hybrid work with customer travel shaped by deployment needs.
  • Production experience with LLM applications. Retrieval, tool calls and evals appear by name in the Replit advert. Python and SQL appear in almost every one.
  • Ownership from prototype to production. The job is to get a system live in the customer's stack, including SSO, data warehouses and internal APIs, and to carry what you learn back to the product team.
  • Comfort in front of senior customers. Several adverts ask for clear communication with executives.

Most adverts do not say whether the employer will sponsor a visa. If you need sponsorship, ask in the first call. The UK government's Skilled Worker visa rules set a minimum of £41,700 per year or the going rate for the occupation, whichever is higher. The salary ranges above clear that floor comfortably, so the open question is whether the company holds a sponsor licence, not whether the pay qualifies.

How to get hired as a Forward Deployed Engineer in London

  • Show shipped work, not a list of tools. One deployment you can explain end to end, with the constraints and what broke, is worth more than ten frameworks on a resume.
  • Prove the customer side. Consulting, solutions engineering, founding a startup or running an internal platform for demanding stakeholders all count. Say who the customer was and what changed for them.
  • Prepare for a practical loop. Expect a build exercise on messy data and a customer-style conversation. Our interview questions guide covers what companies test.
  • Ask about travel and equity early. Travel is rarely quantified in the advert, and equity is a large part of the offer at the AI companies.

Hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer in London

For hiring managers, London has three advantages: a deep pool of engineers from consulting, finance and enterprise software who already work with customers; a time zone that covers European customers and overlaps with the US East Coast; and base salaries below US levels.

It also has three traps.

  • Benchmarking against the wrong data. If you anchor on a £100,000 median and your competitor for the candidate is an AI company posting up to £150,000 or £180,000 plus equity, you will lose late in the process.
  • A vague first hire. The first London Forward Deployed Engineer usually works far from the product team. Write down what they own, who they escalate to and how field feedback reaches engineering. Our job description template helps.
  • A US interview loop run at US hours. Five rounds scheduled for late evening in London loses candidates who have other offers.

FAQ

Are forward-deployed engineers in demand?

Yes. Our feed showed 121 open Forward Deployed Engineer roles on November 26, 2026, 22 of them in London. IT Jobs Watch counted 51 permanent London adverts in the six months to that date, up from 6 a year earlier.

Are forward-deployed engineers paid well?

By UK engineering standards, yes. The public median for London adverts is £100,000, and AI companies that publish their ranges go up to £150,000 or £180,000 plus equity. US roles pay more: the median base in our feed is $221,250.

How much do forward-deployed engineers get paid in London?

Advertised London salaries run from about £80,000 at the 10th percentile to £120,000 at the 90th in IT Jobs Watch data for the six months to November 26, 2026. Employer-posted ranges at AI companies reach £180,000 before equity.

Is being a forward-deployed engineer worth it?

It suits engineers who want to ship quickly, see the result in a real business and spend time with customers. It is a poor fit if you prefer long stretches of uninterrupted work on one codebase, or do not want to travel.

Which companies hire Forward Deployed Engineers in London?

In November 2026, London roles came from AI companies such as Replit and the OpenAI Deployment Company, data and infrastructure vendors such as Databricks and n8n, and startups such as Murphy AI. The list changes weekly, so check our live jobs page.

Can I work as a Forward Deployed Engineer in London remotely?

Rarely fully. The London adverts we read are hybrid, with office days and customer visits. Some EMEA roles are open to remote candidates in the UK, but expect travel to customer sites.
