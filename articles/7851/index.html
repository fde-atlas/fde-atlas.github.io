Enterprise‑Level FDE Knowledge Framework: From Business Insight to AI Engineering Delivery

BestHub · 2026-06-29 · 5 min read

Summary

This BestHub article lays out an enterprise-grade knowledge framework for FDEs, spanning eight modules: the FDE role and its delivery loop (Define, Model, Design, Build, Govern, Consolidate), business scenario diagnosis and ROI estimation, LLM and prompt engineering fundamentals, ontology modeling and knowledge assetization, agent workflows with tool protocols like MCP and A2A, production engineering and integration, quality assurance with evals and security governance, and organizational collaboration with asset reuse. It positions the FDE as distinct from algorithm engineers, pre-sales consultants, and product managers, and argues for turning project delivery into reusable, platform-based assets.

Key points

  • Eight-module FDE framework from business insight to AI delivery
  • Defines a six-step FDE delivery loop ending in asset consolidation
  • Covers ontology modeling, Graph RAG, and MCP/A2A tool protocols
  • Stresses layered evals, BadCase lifecycle, and OWASP LLM security
  • Advocates shifting from project delivery to reusable platform assets
