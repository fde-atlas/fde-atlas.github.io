Forward Deployed Engineer Certification

ADaSci · 2026-05-25 · 2 min read

Summary

ADaSci offers a certification path for forward deployed engineers: a 30-hour CFDE and a 60-hour CFDAS that combines the CFDE with a CAASA curriculum on agentic AI architecture. The article describes the FDE role as working directly with one customer to turn an AI product into a production system—scoping problems, connecting data, building agents, hardening for security and compliance, measuring business impact, and feeding lessons back to product teams. CFDAS spans twelve modules covering agentic AI foundations, multi-agent systems using MCP and A2A protocols, cloud-native architecture, and governance. Assessment is a proctored, two-hour, 120-question multiple-choice exam. Tuition is $375 (discounted for members), credentials are lifetime with a verifiable digital badge, and there are no mandatory prerequisites, though Python, LLM, and cloud knowledge are recommended.

Key points

  • ADaSci offers CFDE (30 hours) and CFDAS (60 hours) FDE certifications.
  • CFDAS combines the CFDE and CAASA curricula into one credential.
  • The FDE role spans scoping, data integration, agent building, and product feedback.
  • Exam format: 120 multiple-choice questions, two hours, proctored online.
  • Tuition is $375 with member discounts; credentials are lifetime with verifiable badges.

01 · The role

What a forward deployed engineer does.

An FDE works directly with one customer to turn an AI product into a system that runs inside their business. They learn how its data, tools and teams work, ship against real constraints, and feed what they learn back to the product team.

The role blends software engineering, applied machine learning, solution architecture and consulting.

  • 1Scope the problemWith the customer's stakeholders
  • 2Connect to their dataInternal systems, permissions, quality
  • 3Build agents and integrationsAgainst real constraints
  • 4Harden itSecurity, compliance, observability
  • 5Measure the resultDid it move a business number?
  • 6Feed back to productWhat to build next
