For Forward Deployed & AI Engineers: Get Introduced

Forward Deploying · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

Forward Deploying is a free candidate-side introduction service for Forward Deployed Engineers, AI Engineers, and ML Engineers. Instead of applying to postings, engineers create a one-minute profile; a human engineer reviews it within two working days and, when a fitting role appears, asks permission before introducing the candidate directly to the hiring manager and helping negotiate the offer. The service tracks 1,907 roles, shares confidential client searches with members first, and never charges candidates—employers pay on successful hires. AI assists matching, but people make every decision, and profiles can be deleted on request.

Key points

  • Free for engineers; employers pay when a hire is made.
  • Every profile is reviewed by an engineer, not a keyword filter.
  • No CV or name goes to an employer without explicit consent per role.
  • Weekly digest of roles above your salary floor, including confidential searches.
  • Referral rewards paid when an introduced engineer is placed.

Full text

Tell us what you want next. An engineer on our team reads your profile and introduces you to the hiring manager when a role fits, including the searches we never advertise. One minute to join, no CV needed to start.

How it works

  • 01

    Join in one minute

    Name, email and LinkedIn. Add your CV and what you want next whenever you like.
  • 02

    An engineer reads your profile

    Within two working days, by someone who has built and deployed AI systems. Not a keyword filter.
  • 03

    We introduce you

    When a role fits, we ask you first, then take your profile to the hiring manager and help you negotiate the offer.

What you get

  • Introductions to hiring managers, not to a résumé filter
  • Confidential client searches, shared with members first
  • A weekly digest of new roles above your salary floor, from the 1907 we track. Nothing else.
  • Salary benchmarks from published ranges, and negotiation help on the offer
  • A referral reward if you introduce an engineer we place

We never send your CV or name to an employer without your yes for that specific role. Ask us to delete your profile at any time.

Create your profile

One minute. Name, email and LinkedIn; the rest is optional.

Is it free for engineers?

Yes, always. Employers pay us when they hire someone we introduce. We never charge candidates, and we never ask for payment to see a role.

Who sees my CV?

Our team only. We never send your CV or name to a company without your explicit yes for that specific role. You can have your profile deleted at any time by emailing us.

Do you use AI to assess me?

We use AI to help find profiles and suggest matches, but an engineer on our team reviews every profile and a person makes every decision. You can ask at any time whether AI was used on your profile and request a human review.

Which roles do you work on?

Forward Deployed Engineers and Architects, AI Engineers building on LLMs, production ML Engineers and the people who lead those teams. If you ship AI into production, we want to meet you.

Can a partner network refer me?

Yes. Many engineers reach us through the technical communities and talent networks we partner with. A referral gets you a faster first conversation; the screening is the same for everyone.

Do you pay for referrals?

Yes. If you introduce an engineer and we place them, we pay you a referral reward, confirmed in writing before we approach them. We only contact people who have agreed to hear from us.

Where do the roles come from?

Directly from employers' own career pages and from searches we run for clients. Some client searches are confidential and only visible to members.
