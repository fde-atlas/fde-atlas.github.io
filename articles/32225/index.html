FDE Hiring Report: weekly data on Forward Deployed Engineer hiring

FDE Times · 2026-10-11 · 2 min read

Summary

FDE Times publishes a weekly (plus monthly) hiring report on Forward Deployed Engineer roles, built solely from companies' official careers boards. Between October and November 2026, headline openings grew from about 440 to 932, but the reports repeatedly show this is mostly wider tracker coverage, not real hiring. Like-for-like, openings often fell: down 60 in the week of 16 November, with Databricks dropping from 124 to 94 roles; Salesforce pulled 22 listings one week after adding 28 the previous week. The methodology counts newly tracked boards as coverage, not growth, and every edition ships as JSON.

Key points

  • Headline FDE openings rose from ~440 to 932 in six weeks.
  • Like-for-like counts often fell, e.g. -60 in mid-November.
  • Databricks shed 30 roles in one week; Salesforce churned listings.
  • Newly tracked boards are counted as coverage, not hiring.
  • All data comes from official careers pages and is published as JSON.

Full text

A weekly report on Forward Deployed Engineer hiring, plus a monthly edition, built only from the FDE Hiring Tracker: open, new and closed roles, who is hiring, where, and which skills job posts ask for.

  • 23 Nov 2026 FDE openings rose to 932, yet real hiring added just four roles Newly tracked job boards supplied 37 of the 41 extra roles, while Amazon grew fastest and Salesforce pulled 22 listings. Data (JSON)
  • 16 Nov 2026 FDE openings hit 865, but on the same boards they fell by 60 Databricks shed the most, from 124 to 94, as companies pulled 113 listings and posted only 53 new ones on boards already tracked. Data (JSON)
  • 9 Nov 2026 Salesforce sheds 13 FDE roles as Databricks extends its lead Open roles fell to 641 in the week to 8 November, and the net fall was 10 once newly tracked boards are excluded. Data (JSON)
  • 2 Nov 2026 FDE openings nearly doubled in October, but like-for-like they fell Strip out 338 roles from 90 newly tracked companies and open posts fell by 25, as 139 closures outpaced 114 new listings. Data (JSON)
  • 2 Nov 2026 New FDE postings nearly triple, yet open roles slip to 631 Salesforce posted 28 of the 66 new roles while 75 came down; strip out newly tracked boards and openings fell by nine. Data (JSON)
  • 26 Oct 2026 FDE job closures nearly double new postings as openings fall by 22 The headline count rose to 625 between 19 and 25 October, but only because 186 roles from 45 newly tracked companies were added. Data (JSON)
  • 19 Oct 2026 Open FDE roles edge up to 461 as Snowflake leads and Palantir churns Strip out one newly tracked company and real growth in the week to 18 October was four roles, with 53 companies hiring at both ends. Data (JSON)
  • 13 Oct 2026 FDE hiring this week: Open FDE roles reach 455 as tracker coverage widens Set aside the 145 roles from newly tracked careers boards, and the count of open Forward Deployed Engineer roles changed by -8 between 7 October and 11 October 2026. Data (JSON)
  • 11 Oct 2026 FDE openings hit 440, but like-for-like they fell by 9 Newly tracked boards at 35 companies added 131 roles; on boards already tracked, 12 roles closed and only 3 new ones appeared. Data (JSON)

Every figure comes from the official careers boards of the companies tracked. Roles from companies whose boards were just added count as wider coverage, never as new hiring, and trends are reported only once there are two comparable periods. Every edition as JSON: /api/hiring-reports.json
