Summary

This OpenWork page is a job posting from Forcia (フォルシア), a Japanese search-technology company, for an AI-driven development director role aligned with its FDE (forward deployed engineer) direction. Forcia's high-speed data search systems are used by 8 of Japan's top 10 travel companies, including ANA, JAL and JTB, and it is building a tourism "OS" combining custom solutions (Spook) and SaaS products (webコネクト, valueコネクト). The role leads requirements definition, service design, and development direction, using generative AI and AI agents rather than writing code. Salary is ¥5–10 million, based in Shinjuku, Tokyo, with roughly four office days per week.

Key points

  • Forcia's search systems are deployed at 8 of Japan's top 10 travel firms.

It automates manual data processing and maximizes the value of the data companies hold.

  • Job Title
  • AI-Driven Development Promotion Director (FDE / Direction)
  • Job Description
  • 【Background】: Forcia is now at a major turning point. We have evolved beyond simply delivering systems and moving on; we have entered a phase where the focus is on "outcomes"—how much value the systems we provide generate for our clients' businesses. To accelerate the introduction of our own products into new domains and clients, beyond evolving the platforms of major clients that underpin Japan's travel infrastructure, including JTB and Nippon Travel Agency, we are seeking someone to drive AI-driven development, the core of our business promotion, and lead everything from service design to development. This role involves identifying issues, building prototypes, and designing services, and delivering services using AI. While the optimal assignment group will be determined based on the individual's aptitude, experience, and the project situation at the time, this is a position where you can work flexibly in both project management and product development. 【Job Description】: Working alongside engineers and sales representatives, you will drive the development and implementation of software that solves the business challenges of major clients as well as industry-wide issues. This is a position that consistently leads product planning, specification design, and business expansion. 【Responsibilities】: ■Issue Identification and Product Proposals: Conduct interviews to uncover clients' latent needs and operational challenges. Devise product architectures leveraging data processing, Forcia's core strength, and carry out service specification proposals and requirements definition. ■Product Design: Define service specifications, screen designs, data structures, and system integrations. Make full use of generative AI and internal tools to validate and visualize data structures, refining the design to a level where AI agents can autonomously proceed with implementation. ■Development Promotion and Direction: Provide precise instructions to internal engineers and development partners, and manage project progress. Communicate with development members as an equal and guide the product to release and growth. This role requires the ability to organize clients' abstract requests and translate them into optimal product specifications from both business value and technical perspectives, as well as an attitude of not merely delegating instructions, but independently thinking through and verifying whether the logic of data structures and system integrations is complete and consistent, and leading the team efficiently and reliably by leveraging tools such as AI.
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • 【Required Skills and Experience】 ■Upstream processes in web application development (requirements definition, specification planning, client negotiations, etc.) 【Preferred Skills and Experience】 ■Experience as a Product Manager (PdM) or Product Director for an in-house product ■Experience with AI-driven development 【Ideal Candidate】 ・Someone who can communicate directly with clients to adjust specifications and build consensus, and who can skillfully use tools such as generative AI themselves to define specifications and drive products forward with a sense of speed (※We are looking for someone who is not a coding engineer, but who understands technology and AI and wants to create value in upstream processes). ・Someone with work experience in development processes using AI agents, or with experience improving development efficiency through the use of AI agents.
  • Forcia's Growth Strategy: Building a "Massive Marketplace" Supporting the Travel and Tourism Industry
  • Forcia aims to be not merely a system provider, but a massive marketplace (shared platform) that supports transactions across the entire industry. We call this the "Tourism OS." 1. Evolution into Infrastructure: Building on search functionality adopted by 8 of the top 10 major travel agencies, we cover everything from reservations and payments to contract management, expanding our domain into "essential infrastructure" for the entire travel industry. 2. Driving New Market Entry: We provide an environment that enables railways, credit card companies, municipalities, and others to launch full-scale travel businesses without enormous costs, leading the revitalization of the tourism MaaS market as a whole. 3. Creating a Massive Distribution Network: We are building a mechanism whereby accommodation providers, activity operators, and others can have their offerings delivered in real time to sales sites around the world simply by placing their information on Forcia's platform, without having to seek out sales channels themselves.
