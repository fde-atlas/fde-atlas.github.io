[ROXX] Announcement of the Launch of an FDE Service Specializing in Talent and Recruitment

Original title: 【ROXX】人材、採用領域に特化したFDEサービス開始に関するお知らせ | ニュースリリース

roxx.co.jp · 2026 · 5 min read

Summary

ROXX, a Tokyo-listed HR tech company, announced an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) service focused on the talent and recruiting domain. Arguing that plain SaaS cannot solve customers' problems, ROXX will decompose AI tools it built and validated in its own operations—such as for its Z-Career platform—into functional modules and recombine them to fit each client's workflows, an approach it calls inside-out FDE. It cites three early cases: a compliance-monitoring AI for sales calls, AI calling embedded in its in-house CRM, and instant AI responses to scout replies. The company plans to expand horizontally within recruiting first, then generalize the resulting products for other industries, and may partner with consulting and custom-development firms.

Key points

  • ROXX launched an FDE service specialized in talent and recruiting.
  • It recombines internally proven AI tools to fit client workflows instead of selling finished SaaS.
  • Three cases: sales compliance AI, AI calling on its in-house CRM, instant scout-reply handling.
  • Plans: expand within recruiting first, then generalize products for other industries.
  • Partnerships with consulting and custom-development firms are under consideration.

Full text

ROXX, Inc. (Head office: Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director and President: Taro Nakajima; TSE Growth: 241A; hereinafter "ROXX") hereby announces that, as communicated in its "Q3 FY September 2026 Earnings Briefing" announced in August 2026, it will launch an FDE service specialized in the talent and recruiting domain.

*Note: FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a delivery model in which engineers embed themselves at the customer's site and take end-to-end responsibility for everything from identifying business challenges to designing and implementing features, as well as embedding and improving solutions in the field. Unlike the model of deploying finished software as-is (SaaS), it assumes building solutions tailored to the customer's business processes.

■Background

To date, in addition to operating 'Z Career', a job-change platform specialized in the non-desk domain, ROXX has provided AI products such as an AI interviewer, AI role-play, and AI feedback, and has improved the recruiting operations of client companies through RPO. It has also accumulated extensive service delivery experience, including meeting the high security standards required for implementations at financial institutions and major enterprise customers.

Recently, in the course of proposing various AI-related tools, ROXX has seen a growing number of consultation opportunities, particularly from major enterprise customers launching AI automation projects, including inquiries about AI adoption within their own companies and requests prompted by learning about ROXX's AI use cases from past earnings materials. Against this backdrop, ROXX concluded that simply providing products under a standard SaaS model could not solve customers' challenges, and decided to launch an FDE service.

In particular, the majority of customers using ROXX's Z Career operate labor-intensive models, which are non-desk domains with significant potential for AI adoption. At the same time, companies with AI expertise remain limited, and ROXX believes that merely providing or implementing AI tools alone will not raise customer satisfaction.

Furthermore, rather than delivering AI tools as finished "products," ROXX takes an FDE approach: it breaks down AI tools that have delivered results in its own operations into operation-level functional components, and recombines and integrates them to fit the customer's on-site challenges and business processes. This enables customization to meet the differing requirements of each company, and by focusing only on solutions that have actually produced results at ROXX, it aims to maximize value delivered to customers.

■Key Features of ROXX's FDE

The FDE offered by ROXX is an "inside-out FDE" that deploys externally the mechanisms proven in its own operations. It is built on AI assets that ROXX originally developed and used to solve on-site challenges in its own service 'Z Career', and takes an approach of embedding them directly into companies' business operations. ROXX believes that the optimal model for FDE in Japan will gradually take shape over the next few years, but it currently views FDE as falling broadly into three categories.

As it expands going forward, ROXX will move faster by also considering partnerships with consulting-type and product-development-type companies.

■Recent Case Examples

ROXX has already begun FDE service engagements with several customers, involving AI-related tools such as AI-powered automated calling, AI role-play, and AI assistants. Some of these case examples are introduced below.

Compliance improvement, AI-powered calling, and streamlining of scout outreach were all achieved with AI tools that ROXX previously learned from, developed in-house, and operated itself, with actual business improvements demonstrated; ROXX is now progressively deploying these strengths externally.

Case 1: AI for Strengthening Governance in Sales Operations Based on ROXX's AI assistant tool, AI analyzes calls to customers and meeting content, detecting statements or meetings that violate the company's compliance standards to prevent misconduct.

Case 2: AI Calling System on an In-House-Developed CRM ROXX will implement its AI calling system on an in-house-developed customer management system that could not be integrated with SaaS. This achieves business automation without changing core systems.

Case 3: Immediate Follow-Up on Scout Replies When a reply is received on a scout service, AI detects it and immediately responds to the applicant. This not only reduces HR workload but also shortens lead times.

■Future Plans

For challenges that are difficult to solve with SaaS alone, such as "strengthening management and talent development" and "embedding operations," ROXX offers its AI tools together with business operations support as a package, aiming for a hybrid approach that truly combines the strengths of SaaS with the strengths of FDE.

First, ROXX will roll out horizontally, specialized in the talent and recruiting domain, providing optimal solutions by performing additional development and AI tuning in line with each customer's distinct business operations and system infrastructure. Building on this, it will refine the new products and know-how generated through this series of processes into more broadly applicable products, expand into other industries, and work to deliver new value to many customers.

Through these initiatives, ROXX will strengthen its FDE service to continuously enhance customer value, going beyond the recruiting and job-change support provided through the operation of 'Z Career', its job-change platform specialized in the traditional non-desk domain.
