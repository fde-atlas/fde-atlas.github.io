Reading Cursor's FDE Job Posting | Rewritten Job Descriptions Under SpaceXAI and New Positions in Japan

Original title: CursorのFDE求人を読む|SpaceXAI傘下で書き換わった職務記述と日本の新ポジション

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-10-10 · 26 min read

Summary

Using job-posting snapshots from late September to October 10, 2026, this article tracks how Cursor's FDE job descriptions changed after SpaceX completed its merger with Anysphere on August 14, 2026, valuing Cursor at $60 billion. Between October 6–9, four of five FDE engineering postings were rewritten around SpaceXAI and Grok, shifting from shipping Cursor workflows to deploying AI agents into customers' business processes and development organizations, with success measured by revenue, cost, time saved and escaped defects—not seat adoption. A Japan-based FDE role posted October 9 requires 5+ years' experience and fluency in Japanese and English, with no stated compensation. Cursor had nine Japan-related openings, and the article compares them with FDE roles at OpenAI, Databricks, Notion and others, noting analyst concerns about model neutrality and data handling.

Key points

  • SpaceX completed the Anysphere merger on August 14, 2026, making Cursor a wholly owned subsidiary.
  • Four of five FDE engineering postings were rewritten around SpaceXAI and Grok in early October.
  • New postings measure success by revenue, cost and time saved, explicitly not seat adoption.
  • The Japan FDE role requires 5+ years' experience and bilingual Japanese-English fluency.
  • Analysts flagged model neutrality and data handling as enterprise concerns after the acquisition.

What does the Japan-based "Forward Deployed Engineer - Japan" posting say?

2026年10月9日(米国時間)に公開された日本勤務のFDE求人は、10月に書き換わった新しい文面とほぼ同じで、必須要件に「日本語と英語の両方の流暢さ」が1行加わっています[1][7]。ジョブボードの勤務地は日本とシンガポールで、報酬、出張の頻度、出社日数、在留資格の支援についての記載はありません(2026年10月10日時点)[1]。

Open Position

Cursor

Forward Deployed Engineer - Japan

  • Location
  • Japan / Singapore
  • Employment Type
  • Full-time
  • Date Posting Retrieved

Responsibilities

  • Scope problems with customers. Understand the codebase and business flows, identify the real bottlenecks and use cases worth building, and define success metrics
  • Design and ship production agents, AI applications, and coding agent workflows using Grok and the company's own models, embedded in customers' business operations and engineering organizations
  • Get a first version running in days, then harden it over weeks based on real usage, with staged rollout, monitoring, and iteration
  • Own production quality. Traces, evals, investigating model and delivery failures, latency and cost trade-offs
  • Build the scaffolding around models (tools, MCP servers, retrieval, rules, skills, CI gates, evals) and integrate it into customers' auth, data, business, and delivery systems
  • Measure results in revenue, cost, time saved, error rates, cycle time, and escaped defects, and hand off what you built in a state the customer's team can operate

Requirements

  • 5+ years of experience in software development, machine learning, or data science
  • Able to write and review production code (Python, JavaScript/TypeScript; other languages welcome)
  • Experience delivering outcomes for customers or operations, not slides or prototypes. Able to turn ambiguous problems into shipped systems with defined scope
  • Experience building and owning AI-native workflows or agents in production, and debugging production failures across models, tools, data, pipelines, and delivery pathways
  • Experience with production reliability (metrics, alerting, safe deployments, incident response) and the ability to build end-to-end, from frontend, backend, and infrastructure to prompt improvements
  • Fluency in both Japanese and English (this ranks alongside other must-haves such as being able to start building before specs are complete, and communicating with both field engineers and VPs)

Preferred qualifications

  • Two or more years in a customer-facing role, with experience leading problem deep-dives and owning outcomes with external stakeholders
  • Hands-on experience with some of the following: agents and tool calling, evaluation frameworks, search and context systems, CI/CD and developer infrastructure, or AI coding in real repositories
  • Experience as an FDE, solutions, or platform engineer working on-site with customers, or as a founding engineer who has owned an AI product end-to-end

Key takeaways from this job posting: The must-have requirements are built around three pillars: 5+ years of experience, production operations, and customer outcomes, while FDE experience itself is only listed as a preferred qualification. This suggests that even without the FDE job title, candidates who can connect their track record in production operations of their own services or agent development to customer outcomes are in scope. On the other hand, experience developing with Cursor is mentioned neither as required nor preferred, and Grok is explicitly named as the model to be used. Since compensation, travel, and office attendance are not mentioned, working arrangements need to be confirmed early in the selection process.

Key points of this job posting: The product is listed as "Grok Build (Cursor)," treating Cursor as one of the products in the "Grok" family. From the way the postings are written, you can read the division of labor: the ADM handles post-deployment adoption, while the FDE builds systems that run on customers' business processes.

For job seekers

  • 扱うモデルが限られる可能性がある。 新しいFDE求人は「Grokと当社のモデル」で作ると書いており、他社のモデルを使う案件があるかは求人票から分かりません[1]。複数のモデルを比べて選ぶ経験を積みたい人は、面接で確かめる論点になります
  • 顧客の懸念に向き合う役割になる。データの行き先やモデルの中立性は、アナリストが買収発表時から挙げていた論点です[16]。日本の顧客のセキュリティ審査でも問われると考えられ、FDEはその説明を現場で担う立場になると読めます
  • 報酬と株式報酬の条件は求人票にない。2026年10月10日時点で、Cursorのジョブボードには報酬の記載がある求人がありませんでした(編集部が集計)[10]。買収の対価はSpaceXの株式で支払われており[12]、株式報酬の有無と内容は選考で確かめる必要があります

For hiring and adopting companies

  • 導入支援の担当が増える。日本で営業・パートナー・FDE・導入後の担当を同時に募っていることから、日本の大口顧客には、契約前の検証から導入後の定着まで担当が付く体制を作りつつあると読めます[10]
  • FDEに頼める範囲が広がる。新しい文面のFDEは、開発の工程だけでなく業務のワークフローにもエージェントを載せると書いています[1]。発注側は、成果指標(売上・コスト・削減時間など)を案件の最初に決めることを求められると考えておくとよいでしょう
  • データの扱いを契約前に確かめる。InfoWorldの記事では、Cursorのゼロデータ保持の方針は単なるセキュリティ機能ではなく、調達と承認の手続きの土台だったという指摘が紹介されています[16]

Summary

SpaceXAI傘下に入ったCursorのFDE求人は、コーディングの道具の導入支援から、顧客の業務にAIシステムを載せて成果で評価される仕事へと書き換わりました[2]。2026年10月10日時点の要点は次のとおりです。

  • CursorはSpaceXの完全子会社になり(2026年8月14日)、FDE求人の文面は10月6〜9日に、Cursorのワークフローから「SpaceXAIのシステム」を作る仕事に書き換わった[12][2]
  • 新しいFDEの文面は、業務のワークフローと開発組織の両方にエージェントを載せ、売上・コスト・削減時間などで成果を測り、顧客のチームが運用できる状態で引き渡すと定めている[1]
  • 日本勤務の「Forward Deployed Engineer - Japan」は、5年以上の経験と日英両方の流暢さが必須で、報酬・出張・出社日数の記載はない(2026年10月10日時点)[1]
  • Cursorの公開ジョブボードには日本を勤務地に含む求人が9件あり(うち3件はシンガポールなどとの兼務)、営業・パートナー・マーケティングと、FDE・Field Engineering・Solutions Architect・ADMが揃いつつある[10]
  • モデルの中立性とデータの扱いは、アナリストが買収の発表時から挙げていた企業側の懸念で[16]、FDEはその説明を現場で担う立場になると考えられる

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Was Cursor acquired by SpaceX?

Yes. According to filings submitted by SpaceX to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, the merger became effective on August 14, 2026, and Anysphere, the developer of Cursor, became a wholly owned subsidiary of SpaceX. The consideration was SpaceX Class A common stock, and under the merger agreement dated June 16, 2026, Cursor's equity value was valued at $60 billion.

Q: Is Cursor hiring FDEs in Japan?

As of October 10, 2026, Cursor's public job board had two such listings: the Japan-based "Forward Deployed Engineer - Japan" (posted October 9, U.S. time) and a "Forward Deployed Engineer" listing Australia, Singapore, and Japan as locations. The Japan-based posting requires fluency in both Japanese and English and 5+ years of experience, with no compensation listed. Listings may change or be closed.

Q: What changed in Cursor's FDE job posting after the acquisition?

Between October 6 and 9, 2026, the wording was rewritten, changing what is shipped from "Cursor workflows" to "SpaceXAI systems." It now includes deploying agents and AI applications into customers' businesses and development organizations using Grok and in-house models, measuring outcomes in terms of revenue, cost, time saved, and similar metrics rather than seat adoption, and requiring 5+ years of experience.

Q: How does a Cursor FDE differ from a Forward Deployed Strategist?

According to the Strategist job posting, they typically work in teams of one Strategist and one or two FDEs. The Strategist owns relationships with customer executives and the scope of engagements, and is responsible for configuration, workflow building, and driving adoption. The FDE joins the customer's team and handles deeper production development. As of October 10, 2026, there is no Japan-based Strategist opening.

Q: What should you verify when pursuing an FDE role at Cursor under SpaceXAI?

Three things not stated in the job posting: the details of compensation and equity, office attendance days and travel frequency, and whether you can use models other than Grok on engagements. It is also worth noting that analysts point out customer companies care about data handling and model neutrality, meaning FDEs will likely be expected to explain these on the ground.
