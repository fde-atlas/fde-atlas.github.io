FDE Times · 2026-11-20 · 7 min read

Summary

A job board roundup listing 154 open Forward Deployed Engineer roles at 81 companies in the San Francisco Bay Area, sourced from company careers pages and updated daily as of 20 Nov 2026. The list spans startups and large firms, with OpenAI (8), Rogo (8), and Databricks (7) hiring the most. Roles include FDEs, Deployment Strategists, research scientists, managers, and new-grad positions, mostly in San Francisco with some in New York, Seattle, and other locations.

Key points

  • Roles range from engineers to Deployment Strategists and directors.
  • Most positions are San Francisco-based; some are in NYC or Seattle.
