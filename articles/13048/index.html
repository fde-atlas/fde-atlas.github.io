Getting Started: What Is an FDE | FDE Learning Center

Original title: 入门篇：什么是 FDE | FDE 学习中心

Summary

This is an introductory module from an FDE learning center aimed at readers with no technical prerequisites. It frames the FDE as the role bridging the engineering gap between model capabilities and production deployment. The module offers a learning map covering a brief AI history, the FDE role definition, an 18-week study roadmap, and a comparison of three FDE types: infrastructure-focused, application deployment, and customer delivery. It also introduces a five-dimension capability model (engineering, AI understanding, operations, learning, collaboration) to help readers pick a path, assess skill gaps, and prepare for interviews.

Key points

  • FDE fills the engineering gap between model APIs and production deployment.
  • The module includes AI history, role definition, an 18-week roadmap, and three FDE types.
  • The three types suit infra, application development, and pre-sales/consulting backgrounds respectively.
  • A five-dimension capability model covers engineering, AI understanding, operations, learning, and collaboration.
  • Goal: build role awareness, choose a learning path, and assess skill gaps.

The FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is the key role in the last mile of AI deployment. This module helps you build a complete understanding of the role, the learning path, and the competency model.

Prerequisites​

No technical prerequisites required—readers from any background can start here. If you are already familiar with the basic concepts of LLMs, you can skip directly

Why Learn This​

The AI industry is developing rapidly, but "knowing how to use an API" is not the same as "being able to deploy." The FDE fills the engineering gap between model capabilities and production services.

This module helps you:

  • Build an understanding of the role: what an FDE is, what they do, and why they matter
  • Choose a learning path: pick the most suitable route based on your background
  • Assess your skill gaps: clearly understand your current level and what you need to improve

Core Concepts at a Glance​

The three types of FDEs:

TypeCore ResponsibilitiesTechnical RequirementsBest Suited For
Infrastructure-focused FDEGPU clusters, inference frameworks, distributed deploymentDepth (GPU/networking/compilers)Those with SRE/Infra experience
Application deployment FDEPrompt engineering, RAG, AI agents, evalsBreadth (full-stack AI applications)Those with backend/application development experience
Customer delivery FDESolution implementation, client liaison, custom developmentCombined (technical + communication)Those with pre-sales/consulting experience

The five-dimension competency model: engineering skills, AI understanding, operational awareness, learning ability, and collaboration—see The Three Types of FDEs for details.

Interview Perspective​

The first 10 minutes of an interview usually test "whether you truly understand what this role does." The documents in this module help you form a clear framework for understanding the role, so the interviewer can see that you:

  • Can clearly describe the core responsibilities of an FDE
  • Know that different types of FDEs correspond to different tech stacks
  • Have a systematic learning path, not scattered bits of knowledge

After Completing This Module, You Should Be Able To...​

  • Clearly describe the definition of the FDE role and its three types
  • Explain the complete engineering pipeline from model to production
  • Create a learning path that suits you
  • Assess your current skill gaps
