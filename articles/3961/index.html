Inside Ode with Anthropic, the private equity-backed startup betting AI services are the future of enterprise

TechCrunch · 2026-07-15 · 1 min read

Summary

TechCrunch's Equity podcast features Chris Taylor and Eddie Siegel of Ode with Anthropic, a joint venture backed by Anthropic, Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman and Goldman Sachs that embeds forward deployed engineers in enterprises. Ode acquired the founders' applied AI services startup Fractional AI earlier this year as its core. The episode covers why enterprise AI pilots often fail to reach production, why Ode hires experienced generalists and former founders rather than AI researchers and trains them into elite FDEs, and whether an AI services firm can scale like software while staying boutique. Taylor argues small applied AI teams can unlock hundreds of millions in value and envisions Ode as a potential trillion-dollar business.

Key points

  • Ode with Anthropic is a PE-backed venture deploying forward deployed engineers into enterprises.
  • It acquired Fractional AI, founded by Taylor and Siegel, as its core earlier this year.
  • The episode examines why many enterprise AI pilots never reach production.

Full text

Can a handful of engineers really do the work of an army of consultants? That’s the bet behind Ode with Anthropic — the joint venture dedicated to embedding forward-deployed engineers in enterprise firms, backed by Anthropic, Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, Goldman Sachs and others.

On this episode of TechCrunch’s Equity podcast, Rebecca Bellan sits down with Ode’s leaders Chris Taylor and Eddie Siegel, who founded Fractional AI, the applied AI services startup that Ode acquired earlier this year to serve as the new venture’s core. The three discuss why so many enterprise AI pilots never make it to production and why they think AI-native services are about to become one of the biggest categories in tech.

Listen to the full episode to hear more about:

  • The case for AI-native services, and why Ode believes small teams of applied AI engineers can unlock hundreds of millions of dollars in business value.
  • Why the company is hiring experienced generalists and former founders instead of AI researchers, and how it’s turning them into elite FDEs.
  • Whether an AI services company can scale like software, yet maintain a boutique feel, and why Taylor believes Ode could one day become a trillion-dollar business.
