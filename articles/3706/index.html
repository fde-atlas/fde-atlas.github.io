The FDE Playbook for AI Startups with Bob McGrew — Y Combinator

Rosetta · 2025-09-08 · 47 min read

Summary

In a Y Combinator podcast, Bob McGrew — early Palantir executive and former OpenAI chief research officer — explains the Forward Deployed Engineer model. FDEs sit at customer sites to close the gap between what a product does and what the customer needs, treating on-site work as product discovery rather than consulting. He describes Palantir's structure of Echo teams (embedded analysts and account managers) and Delta teams (engineers who rapidly build and deploy solutions), and argues the model suits AI startups because the AI agent market has no incumbents. Pricing should reflect delivered outcomes, with contracts growing as solutions generalize into scalable software.

Key points

  • FDEs work inside customer sites to bridge product capability and real needs.
  • The AI agent market has no incumbents, making FDE-driven product discovery valuable.
  • Palantir split roles: Echo teams found use cases; Delta teams built and deployed fast.
  • Unlike consulting, the goal is generalizing custom work into scalable software.
  • Price on delivered outcomes rather than seats, usage, or subscriptions.

Questions answered

What is a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)?

A Forward Deployed Engineer is a technical individual, typically an engineer, who sits at the customer's site to fill the gap between what the product does and what the customer actually needs. They work to solve specific customer problems and adapt the product to deliver valuable outcomes.

Why is the FDE model relevant for AI startups?

The FDE model is highly relevant for AI startups because the AI agent market has no incumbents, requiring extensive product discovery. FDEs can work directly within enterprises to understand needs and drive the development of valuable solutions.

How does the FDE model differ from traditional consulting?

Unlike consulting, the FDE model aims to build a scalable software business. While it may involve custom work initially, the goal is to generalize solutions, increase contract sizes over time, and ultimately deliver repeatable value, potentially turning initial losses into profits.

What are the key roles within Palantir's FDE structure?

Palantir's FDE structure involved 'Echo teams' (embedded analysts and account managers) who identified customer needs and use cases, and 'Delta teams' (Forward Deployed Engineers) who were software engineers responsible for building and deploying solutions.

How should AI startups price their FDE services?

Instead of traditional SaaS pricing (usage, subscription, seats), AI startups using the FDE model should price based on the value of the outcome delivered to the customer. Contracts tend to be larger and more flexible.
