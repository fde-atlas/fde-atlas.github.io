How to Set Up a Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) Team in India

FDE India · 2026-11-02 · 8 min read

Summary

A practical guide for companies building Forward Deployed Engineering teams in India, where FDE job postings grew nearly 19-fold year on year in 2026 per Indeed data. It compares three build paths (hiring, upskilling internal engineers, FDE-as-a-service), recommends starting with two or three FDEs organised in pods per strategic account and reporting into product or engineering rather than sales, and covers India pay benchmarks (₹18–26.5 LPA entry level up to ₹50–80 LPA for 7–10 years), interview design modelled on Google's loop, and metrics like productisation rate and time to production.

Key points

  • Three build options: hire in-house, upskill existing engineers, or use FDE-as-a-service partners.
  • Start with 2–3 FDEs in pods per strategic account, reporting to product or engineering.
  • India pay ranges from ₹18–26.5 LPA entry to ₹50–80 LPA at 7–10 years; Bengaluru leads hiring.
  • Interview loops should test integration coding, system design, problem decomposition and customer skills.
  • Key metric is productisation rate: each engagement should ship a feature to the core product by day 90.

To set up a Forward Deployed Engineering team in India, decide first how you will build the capability (hire, upskill or partner), then start small: two or three FDEs organised around your most strategic customers, reporting into product or engineering, measured on whether their field work makes your product better. This guide walks through each decision with India pay, hiring and interview specifics.

Why companies are building FDE teams in India

Demand has moved fast. FDE job postings in India rose nearly 19-fold year on year in 2026, according to an Indeed report covered by People Matters, with hiring across technology companies, consulting firms, start-ups and AI businesses in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, New Delhi, Mumbai and remote roles. Large employers are building at scale too: TCS has said it wants up to 8,900 forward deployed AI engineers (The Next Web).

For an employer, that means two things: India has a deep pool of engineers who can grow into FDE work, and you are competing with a lot of other companies for the people who already do it well.

Step 1: Choose how to build the capability

Model How it works Best when Watch out for
Hire an in-house FDE team Recruit experienced FDEs, solutions engineers or applied AI engineersFDE work is core to your product and long-term revenueExperienced FDEs are scarce and expensive; hiring takes time
Upskill your own engineers Move strong backend, data or solutions engineers into FDE roles and train themYou already have engineers who know your product and customersCustomer-facing skills take deliberate coaching, not just a course
FDE-as-a-service partner An outside firm embeds engineers who own a production outcome for youYou need results now, or want to test the model before hiringKnowledge stays with the partner unless you plan a handover
Hybrid Start with a partner or a few senior hires, then upskill internallyMost companies starting from zeroNeeds a clear plan for when the work moves in-house

“FDE as a service” is different from staff augmentation: Entrans describes it as outside engineers who own a measurable outcome and deliver working production software, rather than adding developer hours your managers direct. Providers range from large consultancies such as Accenture and Deloitte to specialist engineering firms (Entrans).

Step 2: Make your first hires and set the structure

Start with two or three FDEs. Perspective AI’s playbook argues two gives you shared knowledge and backup cover, and three lets you run two live engagements while the third ramps on a new account.

Organise around pods, not individuals. Insight Partners describes the pod as the unit of delivery: one FDE owns the customer problem, and specialists in data engineering, data science or full-stack work rotate in as needed. Databricks and Wonderful pair each FDE with a deployment strategist who owns strategy and the customer relationship, so the FDE can stay technical. Perspective suggests a pod of one FDE, one product manager and one data or platform engineer per strategic account as the function grows.

Plan the next layers early. Recruiting from Scratch’s stages are a useful map:

Team size What it looks like
1 FDE A generalist doing workshops, code, customer relationships and internal feedback
2–5 FDEs Specialisation by industry or customer size; the founding FDE often becomes the informal lead
5–15 FDEs A formal career ladder, a manager layer and a standard deployment method
15+ FDEs A Director or VP, regional structure, knowledge management and formal onboarding

As a rule of thumb, it suggests about one FDE per major customer early on, two or three for large enterprises with several workstreams, and a dedicated FDE Manager once you have four or five FDEs.

Decide the reporting line deliberately. Tandem’s analysis of FDE job postings found about 45% of roles on a dedicated FDE team and 38% in engineering, with the rest under sales or customer teams. Perspective recommends reporting into product or engineering with a dotted line to sales, because reporting into sales pulls engagements towards closing deals. ServiceNow, for example, places FDEs in product and R&D, explicitly not go-to-market (Insight Partners). For the full ladder, see our guide to the FDE hierarchy.

Step 3: Know the market rate and where to hire

Pay. Published benchmarks put typical FDE pay in India at:

0–2 years ₹18–26.5 LPA
3–6 years ₹30–50 LPA
7–10 years ₹50–80 LPA
10+ years / lead ₹72.5–125 LPA

Typical range = median of published benchmarks (Taggd, Second Talent, Scaler, Quess via Deccan Herald), 2026.

These are medians of published sources; Taggd puts GCCs at ₹35–90 LPA and IT services and consulting at ₹20–50 LPA, and many fresher-tagged listings post far less. Check a specific profile, company type and city with the FDE Salary Estimator.

Cities. Bengaluru is the largest market, with 176 FDE listings on Glassdoor India in October 2026, followed by Hyderabad with 74. See our city guides for Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune.

Who to hire. Strong FDE candidates in India often come from backend and platform engineering, solutions and customer engineering at cloud providers, applied AI and ML engineering, data engineering, and implementation roles at SaaS companies. Many Indian employers advertise the same work under titles such as Applied AI Engineer, AI Solutions Architect or Solutions Engineer, so search beyond the “FDE” title. Our company guides show how employers like Google, Razorpay, Sarvam AI, TCS and Infosys define the role.

Step 4: Design an FDE interview loop

A standard software engineering loop misses half of what makes a good FDE. Candidates for Google’s FDE roles report four kinds of rounds, which make a good template (Scaler):

  • Practical integration coding: messy data, pipelines and APIs rather than puzzles, judged on clean, maintainable code a customer will inherit.
  • System design into an existing system: how your product fits a customer’s legacy data, security and cloud, not greenfield design.
  • Decomposition or case round: an ambiguous customer problem the candidate must break down, scope and prioritise. This round originated at Palantir.
  • Customer-facing and behavioural: explaining technical work to non-technical people, handling pushback and owning outcomes.

Our list of FDE interview questions gives examples for each round.

Step 5: Measure what matters

Perspective AI’s playbook suggests a small set of metrics that keep an FDE team from turning into a services desk:

  • Productisation rate: at least one feature shipped to the core product per engagement, by around day 90. It calls this the master metric.
  • Time to first prototype on real customer data.
  • Time to production: it targets roughly 10 weeks.
  • Handoff completion: engagements handed to customer success or the customer within about 120 days.
  • Roadmap influence: the share of shipped roadmap items traceable to field insight.

It also warns that very high billable utilisation is a red flag: it can mean FDEs are stuck delivering and nothing is flowing back into the product.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Running it as bespoke consulting. Every engagement should leave something reusable behind.
  • Reporting into sales and losing the product feedback loop.
  • Prototyping before discovery. Map the real problem first.
  • Skipping a clean handoff, which traps a small team in maintenance.
  • Hiring for coding alone. Customer discovery and judgement are half the job, so test for them.
  • Ignoring data rules. FDEs work inside customer data and systems, so build access control, audit logging and compliance with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act into your deployment playbook from the start.

Frequently asked questions

How many FDEs should a company hire first?

Two or three. Perspective AI's playbook argues two gives shared knowledge and backup cover, and three lets you run two live customer engagements while the third ramps up on a new account.

Who should an FDE team report to?

Usually product or engineering, with close ties to sales. One job-posting analysis by Tandem found about 45% of FDE roles sit on a dedicated FDE team and 38% in engineering. Reporting into sales tends to pull FDEs towards closing deals instead of improving the product.

What is FDE as a service?

It means hiring an outside firm whose engineers embed with your customers or teams and own a production outcome, rather than just adding developer hours. Providers range from consultancies such as Accenture and Deloitte to specialist engineering firms. It is a fast way to start, but the knowledge stays with the partner unless you plan a handover.

How much does it cost to hire an FDE in India?

Published benchmarks put typical FDE pay at about ₹18–26.5 LPA for 0–2 years, ₹30–50 LPA for 3–6 years and ₹50–80 LPA for 7–10 years, with GCCs and product companies at the top of the range and IT services lower. Use the FDE Salary Estimator for a specific profile and city.
