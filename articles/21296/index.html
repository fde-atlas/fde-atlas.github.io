Summary

Japanese SaaS company RECEPTIONIST is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) with a salary of ¥55–95 million yen (¥5.5–9.5M). The company runs five in-house SaaS products; its cloud receptionist system serves about 5 million users annually and holds the No.1 market share in deployments and revenue, and the company listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth market in November 2026. The FDE role goes beyond coding: structuring customer business problems, designing and implementing solutions on the company's builder platform, taking them from PoC to production, and feeding patterns back into the product. Candidates from solution architecture, technical product management, pre-sales, SIer, and consulting backgrounds are welcome; prior FDE experience is not required.

Key points

  • RECEPTIONIST operates five in-house SaaS products; its reception system serves ~5 million users yearly with No.1 share.
  • The company listed on the TSE Growth market in November 2026.
  • The FDE handles problem structuring, design, implementation, production rollout, and product feedback.
  • Solution architects, technical PdMs, pre-sales, and consultants are welcome; no FDE experience needed.
  • Salary is ¥5.5–9.5M including 40 hours of fixed overtime; remote-friendly work.

  • ◆Background of the Position
  • Our company has a product portfolio covering "physical touchpoints in the field" such as reception, meeting rooms, visitor entry, and outside-line telephony, and we are now entering a phase of taking on business execution itself beyond those touchpoints. By combining workflows, AI prompts/skills, and more, processes can flow automatically beyond reception — extending to approvals, notifications, record-keeping, and core system integration. However, the business operations of mid-sized and larger customers differ from one company to another, and simply applying standard features will not make their operations run. Uncovering customers' business operations and implementing each customer's business on top of our Builder — this is the role of our FDE. Rather than writing separate code for each customer, you will layer customer-specific configurations, data, and logic on top of a shared product.

You can propose what to build next from the front lines of actual projects. - You can work with both physical and software elements. Because devices are placed on-site, there are areas we can reach that pure software cannot, and you can experience design that is not confined to what happens on screen. - You can reach industries that had given up, thinking "our operations are too specialized for SaaS." - Target customers include those in logistics, manufacturing, hospitals, government, and education that standard features alone could not reach. - Unlike contracted development, what you build becomes an asset. Your role extends beyond building for a single customer to generalizing it and leaving it in the product. - With remote work and flex time, you can work efficiently in a flexible environment.
