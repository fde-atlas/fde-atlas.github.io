Is the Market Value of FDEs Rising? Verifying with Sakana AI's FDE Hiring and Job Posting Data

Original title: FDEの市場価値は上がっているのか|Sakana AIのFDE採用と求人データで確かめる

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-10-04 · 30 min read

Summary

The article examines whether FDE market value is genuinely rising, using Sakana AI's three FDE job postings (Applied Team, GTM, Defense) and US job-market data from Lightcast. US FDE postings grew from about 200 in 2024 to over 5,200 in January–July 2026, with advertised median salaries above $188,000 versus roughly $145,000 for traditional software engineers. However, most Tokyo-based FDE postings omit salary figures, and the growth partly reflects the spread of the job title itself. The author advises judging FDE value by the specific experience combinations postings require, not the title.

Key points

  • Sakana AI first posted FDE roles in March 2026; by October it had three: Applied, GTM, and Defense.
  • Lightcast data shows US FDE postings exceeded 5,200 in January–July 2026, over four times all of 2025.
  • Advertised median FDE salary exceeds $188,000, above the ~$145,000 for traditional software engineers.
  • Most Tokyo FDE postings list no salary; SoftBank's range is ¥8.12–20.35 million.
  • Of 509 postings with FDE-related titles, 45 lacked engineer-type titles, showing title inflation.

  • 01 As of October 4, 2026, Sakana AI has three FDE job openings posted: Applied Team, GTM team, and defense sector. The first FDE job posting was published on its careers page in March 2026.
  • 02 According to Lightcast, a labor market data provider, FDE job postings in the US grew from about 200 in 2024 to about 1,200 in 2025, and exceeded 5,200 in just January through July 2026.
  • 03 According to Lightcast data reported by Fortune, the median advertised annual salary for FDE job postings exceeds $188,000, higher than the roughly $145,000 for traditional software engineers (Fortune, September 3, 2026).
  • 04 As of October 4, 2026, the Tokyo-based FDE job postings at Sakana AI, OpenAI, and Databricks do not list salaries, while SoftBank's FDE posting, which does, listed a theoretical annual salary range of ¥8.12 million to ¥20.35 million.

FDE(Forward Deployed Engineer/フォワードデプロイドエンジニア)は「市場価値の高い職種」と紹介されることがあります。2026年には、日本の生成AIスタートアップであるSakana AIもFDEの募集を始め、いまは顧客別のAIソリューション、自社プロダクトの導入、防衛の3つの領域でFDEを募集しています[1][2][3]。では、FDEの市場価値は実際に何に支えられ、どこまで信じてよいのでしょうか。

Is the Market Value of FDEs Rising?

米国の求人データを見る限り、FDEへの需要は急増しており、求人に示される年収もソフトウェアエンジニアより高い水準です。Lightcastによれば、米国のFDE求人は2026年1〜7月に5,200件を超え、2025年通年の4倍を上回りました[12]。日本でも、Sakana AIが2026年3月にFDEの募集を始め、10月4日時点では3つの領域でFDEを募集しています[1][2][3][6]。

ただし、日本で「FDEの値段」を確かめられる材料は多くありません。東京勤務のFDE求人でも、Sakana AI・OpenAI・Databricksの求人票には年収が書かれていません[1][2][3][16][17]。また、求人数の伸びは「Forward Deployed」という職種名が広まった速さも映しています[15]。この記事は、需要・価格・希少性・限界の4つに分けて確かめます。

Why Does Sakana AI Need FDEs?

Sakana AIは、汎用的なAIモデルと実際の業務の間に埋めるべき溝があり、それを埋めるには特定の領域に深く入り込むエンジニアが要る、と公式に説明しています[8]。同社の事業は日本の大企業と防衛分野へのAIプロダクトの提供に重点を置いています[7]。編集部の見方では、顧客の中に飛び込んで課題を解くFDEは、その導入の最前線に立つ職種です。

"To bridge this valley, an approach is essential in which engineers with advanced technical skills at the level of being able to build AI models commit deeply and narrowly to specific domains and design business processes themselves together with AI."

Sakana AI "Announcing Our Series B" (Japanese version)

[8]

同社のApplied Teamの紹介ページは、チームの職種をProject Manager、Technical Project Manager、Forward Deployed Engineer、Applied Research Engineer、Software Engineer、Solution Engineer、Cybersecurity Engineerと並べ、FDEの役割を「顧客の中に飛び込み、LLMやAIエージェントを駆使し、ビジネス課題を解決する」と書いています[7]。開発はProject Manager、FDE、Software Engineer、デザイナー、Security Engineerなどがスクラムを組んで進め、「提示された要件通りに作るのではなく、顧客と深く議論し、課題の根本から一緒に考え」る進め方が大半だとしています[7]。

一方で、プロダクトチームは汎用的なプロダクトを作り、Applied Teamがそれを顧客ごとにさらに作り込む、という分担を目指していると説明しています[9]。GTMチームのFDEはプロダクトの導入を、Applied TeamのFDEは顧客ごとの作り込みを担う、という分担として読めます。

"The setup we're aiming for is one where the Product Team builds general-purpose products, and the Applied Team customizes them further to deliver distinctive value to each client."

Sakana AI "Inside Sakana AI's Product Team" (Japanese version; PD Team refers to the product team, September 17, 2026)

[9]

What determines an FDE's market value?

Sakana AIのGTM、OpenAI、ソフトバンクの求人票に共通して書かれているのは、本番品質のコードを書く力、顧客の業務と既存システムに入り込む力、顧客と技術的な意思決定をする力です[1][16][19]。日本の求人では、これに日本語での顧客折衝が加わります[1][2][3][17]。スキルの全体像はFDEに必要なスキルセットで解説しています。

  • 本番品質の開発:Sakana AIのGTMは本番環境でのソフトウェア開発3年以上、ソフトバンクは本番品質コードの設計・実装経験を必須にしている[1][19]
  • エンタープライズへの導入:Sakana AIのGTMは認証・データ連携・セキュリティ審査対応を含む導入経験を必須にし、ソフトバンクは必須要件にクラウド環境でのシステム構築経験、歓迎要件にエンタープライズ向け大規模システム導入経験を挙げている[1][19]
  • 顧客との意思決定:OpenAIの東京の求人は「活躍できる人」として顧客対応を含む経験5年以上を挙げ、Sakana AIのGTMは顧客対面で技術的な意思決定をリードした経験を必須にしている[16][1]
  • LLMの実装:Sakana AIの3件とソフトバンクの求人は、LLM・生成AIを活用したアプリケーションの開発または導入の経験を必須にしている[1][2][3][19]
  • 言語:Sakana AIの3件は日本語ネイティブ、OpenAIは日英のバイリンガル、Databricksは流暢な日本語と業務レベルの英語を求めている[1][2][3][16][17]

What caveats apply to the market value of FDEs?

FDE求人の伸びは本物ですが、そのすべてが同じ仕事の需要とは限りません。FDEの実務家向けニュースレター「FDE Hub」を運営するMilos Mandic氏は、求人データが測っているのは仕事の中身ではなく「Forward Deployed」という職種名の使われ方だと指摘しています[15]。

"A job posting is a decision to use two words. It is not evidence that the job behind them involves discovery, or production code, or a customer's operators, or anything else the original role was built around."   • Postings coming and going: One Cohere FDE posting for a Tokyo-based role as of the September 30, 2026 compilation had disappeared from the company's board by the October 4 compilation (compiled by the editorial team)

What does this mean for job seekers and employers?

For job seekers, it is important to measure the market value of an FDE not by the "FDE title" but by the combination of experience a posting requires. For employers, given that they will be competing for the same talent as Sakana AI, OpenAI, and SoftBank, they need to think carefully about how they write job postings and present compensation.

What job seekers should verify

  • 01 Determine from the job description whether the role is solutions-type (built per customer) or product-type (deploying the company's own product)
  • 02 Map each required experience to a specific project in your own work history (production development, deployment including authentication and data integration, decision-making with customers, LLM implementation)
  • 03 Present your experience in customer-facing work in Japanese as a track record, even if language requirements are not stated
  • 04年収の記載がない求人では、公開されている他社のレンジ(ソフトバンクの812万〜2,035万円など)を手がかりに、面談で報酬の構成を確かめる[19]

What employers should verify

  • 01自社のFDEがどちらの型かを決め、求める経験を絞る。Sakana AIは型ごとに求人を分けている[1][2]
  • 02FDEの発見をプロダクトに戻す経路を求人票に書く。Sakana AIのGTMの求人は、プロダクト本体のコードへの貢献とロードマップ提案を業務に入れている[1]
  • 03 Consider publishing salary ranges. Many FDE postings in Tokyo do not include them, and postings that do provide a basis for comparison
  • 04経験年数より経験の中身で要件を書く。Sakana AIのApplied Teamの求人は、年数ではなく経験と実績で必須要件を書いている[2]

Summary

The market value of FDEs is backed by both demand and offered salaries in US job posting data, and in Japan it has grown to the point where domestic AI companies like Sakana AI are hiring across three areas. On the other hand, most Japanese postings do not state salaries, so the value must be verified through the substance of the experience that job postings require.

Frequently Asked Questions

QIs Sakana AI recruiting FDEs?

Yes. As of October 4, 2026, Sakana AI has three FDE job postings open: an FDE on the Applied Team, which builds AI solutions for each customer; an FDE on the GTM team, which deploys its own products Sakana Marlin, Namazu, and Fugu to enterprises; and an FDE in the defense domain, targeting the Ministry of Defense and the Self-Defense Forces. All are based in Tokyo and require native-level Japanese.

QWhat is the salary of an FDE at Sakana AI?

As of October 4, 2026, none of Sakana AI's three FDE job postings list a salary, so it cannot be determined from public information. An example of a Tokyo-based FDE posting that does publish a salary is SoftBank's FDE posting (secondment to SB OAI Japan G.K.), with a theoretical annual salary of ¥8,123,000 to ¥20,346,200.

QIs the market value of FDEs really that high?

US data shows demand is surging. According to Lightcast, US FDE job postings exceeded 5,200 between January and July 2026. According to Lightcast data reported by Fortune, the median advertised salary for FDE postings is over $188,000, exceeding the roughly $145,000 for traditional software engineers. However, Milos Mandic, who runs FDE Hub, points out that a considerable number of postings with the FDE job title are simply pre-sales or implementation support roles given a new name. Since most Japanese postings do not publish salaries, the value in Japan needs to be verified through the requirements and published ranges in job postings.

QWhat experience is needed to increase one's market value as an FDE?

Commonly required in Tokyo FDE postings are: production-quality software development (Sakana AI's GTM, SoftBank), enterprise deployment including authentication and data integration (Sakana AI's GTM), experience making technical decisions with customers, and experience developing or deploying LLM/generative AI applications (all three Sakana AI postings, SoftBank). Sakana AI's Applied and Defense FDEs require practical experience in data science and machine learning. In Japan, customer negotiations in Japanese are added to this, and postings at foreign companies also require English. This summary is based on job postings as of October 2026.

QWill demand for FDEs continue going forward?

It is hard to say for certain. Executive search firm Christian & Timbers predicts FDE demand will grow by 2,100% by the end of 2026, but its founder himself has also noted that automation may advance and demand may not persist in two years. Milos Mandic, who runs a newsletter for FDE practitioners, points out that the growth in postings partly measures the spread of the job title.
