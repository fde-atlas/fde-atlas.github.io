Report on the Approach to In-House FDE Development for Real Estate Management Companies

Original title: 不動産管理会社向け 社内FDE育成 進め方レポート

株式会社Aiworker · 2025-11-30 · 1 min read

Summary

Aiworker, a Japanese company, offers a report on training in-house FDEs at real estate management firms. It argues that frontline staff in leasing management, brokerage, and reception roles should be developed into people who can use AI to redesign their own departments' workflows. The report covers selecting candidates, combining two types of internal FDEs, dividing tasks between AI and human judgment (such as disclosure documents, meeting minutes, inquiries, and quotes), a three-month training plus three-month accompaniment process, and how branch offices can sustain the practice afterward.

Key points

  • Targets real estate management firms building in-house FDEs.
  • Frontline staff are trained to redesign their own workflows with AI.
  • Two types of internal FDEs should be combined.
  • Report splits AI tasks from human-judgment tasks.
  • Process: three months training plus three months support.

Full text

A summary of how to select and train front desk, rental management, and brokerage staff over three months so they can use AI to transform their own department's operations

What this report covers

  • Why property management companies should turn employees who know the business into builders of systems
  • In-house FDEs in property management should be developed by combining two types
  • What to delegate to in-house FDEs and what humans should decide (explanations of important matters, meeting minutes, inquiries, and estimates)
  • How to proceed (3 months of training + 3 months of accompaniment)
  • The structure needed to sustain the practice at sales offices after training
