FDE Job Opening at VARIETAS Inc. — 1 Position, Salary up to ¥30 Million | FDE board

Summary

FDE board lists one FDE opening at VARIETAS Inc., a Tokyo-based HR tech startup founded in 2019 that builds an AI interviewer service to structure and standardize corporate hiring. The company has about 13 employees and raised 600 million yen in Series A funding, moving from PMF validation into a growth phase. The role offers 15-30 million yen annually, the top salary range among Japanese FDE job postings at the time of writing. VARIETAS defines the FDE as having a dual identity: the customer's CTO and the platform's CTO, working closely with a Deployment Strategist serving as CPO, in a non-resident, in-and-out engagement model rather than being embedded on-site.

About VARIETAS Inc.

An HR tech company developing an AI interviewer service. By embedding itself into companies' hiring processes and using AI to replace and support the conducting and evaluation of interviews, the company aims to transform the very framework of hiring. With a team of 13 employees, it has raised ¥600 million in Series A funding. It is in the transition phase from validating product-market fit to scaling up. Its business centers on structuring interviews—a person-dependent, low-reproducibility process—into a standardized format, delivering its product by deeply engaging with actual hiring operations.

Characteristics of FDE Roles at This Company

At the time of writing, this company offers the highest salary range among FDE positions in Japan (¥15-30 million). The job posting defines the FDE as a role with a dual nature—"the customer's CTO and also the platform's CTO"—and describes it as a non-resident, in-and-out type position working hand in hand with a Deployment Strategist (serving as CPO).
