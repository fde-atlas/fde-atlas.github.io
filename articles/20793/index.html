Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Jobs for Researchers and ML Engineers | Roles, Requirements, and Compensation Across 10 Companies Including Snorkel

Original title: 研究者・MLエンジニアのFDE求人|Snorkelなど10件で読む仕事・要件・報酬

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-11-13 · 23 min read

Summary

The article analyzes 10 FDE job postings (out of 516 tracked as of November 13, 2026) whose titles explicitly target researchers, data scientists, or ML engineers, spanning six companies: Snorkel AI, Labelbox, Intercom, Modal, Baseten, and Together AI. Researcher-facing roles design training data and evals for frontier AI labs; ML-engineer roles optimize customer inference and post-training; Intercom's data scientist role supports enterprise AI adoption. Labelbox's research role uniquely requires a master's or PhD and pays $200K–$300K base, above its regular FDE range of $140K–$200K. Snorkel AI's researcher sits in a sales and customer success org with OTE-based pay. Experience requirements range from 1–2 years (Baseten) to 5+ years (Together AI), and none of the roles are based in Japan.

Key points

  • Only 10 of 516 tracked FDE postings target research, data science, or ML specialists, across six companies.
  • Researcher roles design training data and evals for frontier labs; ML-engineer roles optimize inference and post-training.
  • Labelbox's research role is the only one requiring a master's or PhD, paying $200K–$300K base versus $140K–$200K for regular FDEs.
  • Snorkel AI's researcher role sits in sales and customer success, with OTE-based pay of $160K–$400K.
  • Experience requirements vary widely (1–2 to 5+ years), and no postings are based in Japan.

FDE(Forward Deployed Engineer/フォワードデプロイドエンジニア)の求人は、ソフトウェアエンジニアを対象にしたものが大半です。その中に、機械学習の研究者、データサイエンティスト、MLエンジニアを職種名で名指しする求人が出ています。Snorkel AIの「Forward Deployed Researcher」[1]、Labelboxの「Forward Deployed Research Scientist」[4]、Intercomの「Forward Deployed Data Scientist」[6]、Modalの「Forward Deployed Engineer - ML」[8]などです。

What people with machine learning backgrounds want to know when they see these postings is: "If I become an FDE while retaining my research or ML expertise, what will I do, what will be expected of me, and how much will I earn?" In this article, we read all 10 such postings published as of November 13, 2026 in full, and compare their counterparts, duties, departments, requirements, and compensation. We also place them side by side with regular FDE postings from the same companies to show the differences unique to research and ML roles. For an overview of the FDE role as a whole, see What is an FDE?.

What Do FDE Roles for Researchers and ML Engineers Involve?

研究者やMLエンジニア向けのFDEは、顧客のAIモデルの性能を上げるために、学習データ・評価・学習や推論の仕組みを、顧客の技術チームと一緒に設計して実装する仕事です。業務システムにAIを導入する通常のFDEと違い、相手は最先端のAI研究所やAI製品を作る会社の研究・技術チームであることが多く、話す内容もモデルの学習方法や評価の設計になります[1][4][8]。

The numbers are not large. Of the 516 FDE postings from the job boards of 133 companies that our editorial team collects weekly, 10 postings, or just under 2%, list research or ML expertise in the job title (as of November 13, 2026, compiled by the editorial team). Counting postings with identical content as one, there are 8 distinct roles.

Can researcher-focused FDEs actually do research?

できますが、会社によって研究に割ける時間の約束が大きく違います。 Labelboxはチームの稼働の25〜30%を研究部門との共同研究に割り当てると明記し、論文を書く機会があるとしています[4]。Snorkel AIの研究職は営業・顧客成功の部門(DaaS Sales & Success)に属し、商談の期間を通じて顧客の研究チームの相談相手になることを職務にしています[1]。

"We protect time for research. 25–30% of team capacity is allocated to research collaboration with Applied Research. This is not aspirational — it is a structural commitment."

Labelbox

Forward Deployed Research Scientist

  • Location
  • San Francisco Bay Area
  • Compensation (as stated in the posting)
  • Annual base salary $200,000–$300,000 USD (US; equity and other benefits not included)
  • Date posting was retrieved

Responsibilities

  • Join scoping meetings with research teams at customers' cutting-edge AI labs as a technical peer, discussing methodology and challenging assumptions about data requirements
  • For each engagement, understand the customer's model architecture, training approach, and target capabilities, and determine which data strategies will work based on experiments
  • Fine-tune open models on customer data (or substitute data) and measure data effectiveness through ablation experiments (removing elements to compare)
  • Work with the data-creation operations team to verify, before execution, that annotation design and quality standards align with the customer's training objectives
  • Collaborate with the research division to contribute to benchmarks, technical documentation, and academic submissions

Requirements

  • Master's or PhD in machine learning, NLP, computer science, or a related field
  • Hands-on experience fine-tuning large language models (open models such as Llama, Mistral, Qwen)
  • Understanding of training pipelines such as pre-training, supervised fine-tuning, and RLHF/DPO, and how data quality and composition affect each stage
  • Experience designing and running experiments involving hypotheses, comparative studies, and statistical analysis
  • Speed: able to go from problem definition to experimental results in days, not months

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience at a cutting-edge AI lab, applied ML startup, or a research role with direct customer interaction
  • Experience designing LLM evaluations or benchmarks
  • Published papers (conferences, journals, or technical reports)

Key takeaways from this posting: The team currently has 5 members, works in two-week cycles, and measures response deadlines in days. Notably, it demands intellectual honesty — clearly stating when experiments show the data isn't working, even if it's inconvenient for the deal. This is the only posting among the 10 that requires a degree.

What makes an FDE for data scientists different?

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat is a Forward Deployed Researcher?

It is a role that works with customers' research teams to design training data, reinforcement learning environments, and evaluation frameworks to improve AI model performance. As of November 2026, Snorkel AI is hiring for this role and defines it as someone who knows where state-of-the-art models fail in their assigned technical domain and what data can improve them. Compensation is listed as $160,000-$400,000 OTE (on-target earnings).

QCan data scientists also become FDEs?

Yes. Intercom is hiring a "Forward Deployed Data Scientist," a role that embeds with large enterprises deploying AI agents for customer support, identifies areas where AI can deliver measurable impact, and implements solutions. It requires excellent SQL and production-minded Python, experience with AI evaluation or conversational AI, and willingness to travel to customer sites. The San Francisco base salary is $206,600-$229,000.

QIs a PhD required for researcher-focused FDE roles?

It depends on the company. Labelbox's Forward Deployed Research Scientist requires a master's or doctoral degree in machine learning or a related field. Snorkel AI's Forward Deployed Researcher lists a bachelor's degree and 4+ years of experience as preferred requirements, with an advanced degree treated as preferred. The ML engineer-focused roles at Modal, Baseten, and Together AI do not require a degree.

QHow many years of experience are required for ML engineer-focused FDE roles?

In postings as of November 2026, Baseten requires a minimum of 1-2 years of software development experience, Modal requires 2+ years as an ML engineer, and Together AI requires 5+ years in a technical role. Many postings weigh deep hands-on experience in either inference engines (vLLM or SGLang) or fine-tuning (SFT or reinforcement learning) more heavily than years of experience.

QAre there FDE job postings for researchers or ML engineers in Japan?

As of November 13, 2026, we found no FDE postings based in Japan with research or ML in the job title. To leverage the same expertise in Japan, the realistic approach is to look among regular FDE postings for ones whose descriptions ask for experience in evaluation design or fine-tuning.
