Roles - Remote Australia

Re:Sourced runs structured search for remote forward deployed engineers across Australia. Median 21 days from brief to signed offer, 90-day replacement guarantee, calibrated against an active national network of senior engineers.

The role

Customer-embedded engineer who ships production integrations inside client environments post-sale. Common in AI-native and enterprise SaaS. The qualifying pool is thin because the title only consolidated around 2023-24; most strong candidates carry other titles, so searches rely on profile translation rather than keyword sourcing.

Remote-first forward deployed engineering suits the role well: the work is customer-embedded and travel-based, so where the engineer lives matters less than the ability to drop into a client environment and ship. Re:Sourced sources remote forward deployed engineers nationwide, reaching strong candidates in cities without a deep local pool.
