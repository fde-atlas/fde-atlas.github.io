From its origins at Palantir to the moves by OpenAI, Anthropic, and Amazon, Salesforce's partner network expansion into Japan, and funding rounds by domestic startups, we organize the publicly available facts with sources, and write about the "criteria for calling yourself an FDE" from the standpoint of a company that actually implements them.

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a role and service area that has been rapidly gaining players in Japan since the start of 2026.

This article organizes, in chronological order and based solely on publicly available facts, how this way of working born at Palantir was turned into a business around the world, and who started what in Japan. Finally, it sets out the criteria for "what should qualify as an FDE," written from the standpoint of someone who actually goes into the field as an FDE.

The intended readers are practitioners and decision-makers preparing to select an AI implementation partner, and those researching FDE as a career.

What is an FDE? (A 30-second definition)

An FDE is an engineer who embeds themselves in the customer's field, implements AI and software into actual business operations, and runs them until results are achieved.

The definition, job description, and differences from ordinary engineers are explained in What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?. This article focuses on "who is moving in the market."

Origins — Palantir's "forward deployment"

The title of FDE was created by Palantir. Engineers embed themselves in the field at government agencies and large enterprises, building the company's platform to fit the customer's challenges. The name comes from the military term "forward deployed," and within the company the team is also called "Delta."

Palantir's official blog explains this role in contrast to ordinary product engineers (Dev). While product engineers build "one feature for many customers," FDEs implement "many capabilities for one customer." It is a role embedded in the customer's field, working to solve that customer's hardest problems.

Source: A Day in the Life of a Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (Palantir official blog, November 2, 2020)

The world — competition shifts from "building models" to "delivering results in the field"

From 2025 onward, the builders of foundation models entered this very domain one after another.

  • In June 2025, a partner at a16z (Andreessen Horowitz) published an essay titled "FDE is the hottest job in startups." Source: Trading Margin for Moat: Why the Forward Deployed Engineer Is the Hottest Job in Startups (a16z, June 4, 2025)
  • OpenAI is expanding its hiring of FDEs across cities and is also recruiting in Tokyo. Source: Forward Deployed Engineer - Tokyo (OpenAI careers page)
  • Anthropic also has FDE positions in its "Applied AI" division. Source: Forward Deployed Engineer, Applied AI (Anthropic careers page)
  • Amazon was reported in June 2026 to have launched a $1 billion-scale FDE organization. The coverage positioned this as "a move following OpenAI and Anthropic." Source: Amazon launches new $1 billion FDE org, following OpenAI and Anthropic (TechCrunch, June 30, 2026)

What this series of moves shows is that the focus of competition has shifted from "building better models" to "getting into the customer's field and turning it into results."

Japan — in the first half of 2026, the category formed in just three months

In Japan, the activity became visible all at once starting in 2026. Here it is in chronological order.

April 2026 — A new job category is created. Findy, an engineer job-change service, added a new job category including FDE. According to the company's announcement, about 50 of the roughly 6,000 listed job postings matched FDE. Six months earlier, it said, the number was nearly zero.

Companies like LayerX are also emerging that have FDE positions. Sources: Addition of new engineer job categories "FDE, SET, CRE" for the AI agent era (Findy, PR TIMES, April 24, 2026), LayerX FDE interview (Findy Media)

June 1, 2026 — Funding for an FDE-dedicated firm. GenerativeX, which positions itself as an "FDE consulting firm," raised a total of approximately 650 million yen in its Series A. Salesforce Ventures was among the investors. Source: GenerativeX raises a total of 650 million yen in Series A funding (PR TIMES, June 1, 2026)

June 8, 2026 — A platformer's partner network. Salesforce announced the full-scale launch in Japan of its "Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) Partner Network." Nine companies, including Accenture, NTT Data, Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu, PwC Consulting, and Fujitsu, joined as initial partners. Source: Salesforce launches "Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) Partner Network" in Japan (Salesforce official, June 8, 2026)

A job category, funding for a dedicated firm, and a major player's partner network. The elements needed to form a category came together in just three months.

There is a gap in this map: the front lines of mid-sized companies.

Foundation vendor direct operations are tied to major customers, partner networks to existing users of specific foundations, and in-house FDEs to their own products. The layer that takes on implementation at the level of "this workflow," without assuming a particular foundation or product and at the scale of mid-sized companies' operations, is still thin.

Criteria for Calling Yourself an FDE — Four Points

When a category emerges, the name spreads before the substance. Speaking from our position working in the field as FDEs, we believe the criteria for this title are the following four. They articulate, in a form the client side can verify, the role Palantir presented as the original model — being embedded in the customer's operations, implementing, and solving that customer's problems. It can also be used as-is as a checklist for evaluating vendors that advertise "FDE."

  • Are you actually in the field? Not just documents and interviews — are you working with real data and real operations?
  • Are you implementing with your own hands? Not stopping at advice, requirements definition, or org charts — are you building something that works?
  • Are you measuring results? Are you verifying time saved and accuracy with numbers on the same basis as before implementation?
  • Do you plan to leave behind a working system? Do you define a state that keeps running even after people leave as the finished form? This is the dividing line from resident man-month contracts.

For anything missing one of these, another name already exists. Missing 1 and 2 is consulting, missing 3 is tool implementation support, and missing 4 is resident SES. That is not to say those are bad — it means the new name "FDE" is unnecessary for them.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How is an FDE different from a consultant or an SIer?

A consultant's deliverables are advice and documents; an SIer's is development to specification. An FDE handles everything from understanding the operations to implementation and measuring results as a single role, and withdraws after leaving behind a working system. The details of the role are explained in What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?.

Can I hire an FDE in-house? Or should I outsource?

There are only about 50 FDE job listings in Japan (April 2026, per Findy), so supply is still thin. For companies with their own foundation or product, in-house hiring is rational. Otherwise, it is realistic to bring in an external FDE starting from a single workflow and leave the system and templates in-house.

How much does it cost to hire an FDE?

It depends on the complexity of the work undertaken, so the mainstream approach is to present prices in advance by phase, with deliverables defined for each. Gomumari's process and pricing are published on AI Implementation FDE (service).
