Solution Architect vs Forward Deployed Engineer: The Real Difference

FDE India · 2026-11-05 · 9 min read

Summary

The article argues that solution architects and forward deployed engineers (FDEs) overlap in discovery, workshops and architecture work, but differ in ownership: architects own the design, while FDEs build, ship and own the running system in production. It offers a four-question self-test based on accountability after go-live. Citing Indeed India data, it notes FDE postings reached nearly 19 times year-ago levels in June 2026. Published FDE pay bands in India (Taggd: ₹50–80 LPA at 7–12 years) generally exceed self-reported solution architect pay (AmbitionBox: ₹13.8–47.9 LPA). Hybrid roles like DevRev's Forward Deployed Architect and Salesforce's FDE Senior Technical Architect are suggested as entry points for architects moving into FDE work, with production coding as the main gap to close.

Key points

  • The key split is ownership: architects own design, FDEs own production systems.
  • A four-question test checks accountability after go-live and production coding.
  • Indeed India counted FDE postings at nearly 19x year-ago levels in June 2026.
  • Indian FDE pay bands (₹50–80 LPA at 7–12 years) exceed typical SA pay.
  • Hybrid titles like Forward Deployed Architect are the natural path from SA to FDE.

Ask a solution architect what a Forward Deployed Engineer does, and a common answer is: “That’s my job already.” They run discovery with customers, design the integration, sit in architecture workshops and handle escalations. All of that is FDE work too.

But the overlap hides one big difference, and it decides your scope, your career options and how fast your pay grows. FullStack puts it in one line: the difference “comes down to who designs the solution, and who builds it, ships it, and owns it in production.”

Where the two roles overlap

Both roles:

  • Run technical discovery with the customer’s engineers and business owners.
  • Map how the product fits the customer’s systems: data, security, integrations.
  • Present architecture and trade-offs to technical and executive audiences.
  • Build proofs of concept to show the solution works.
  • Feed what they learn from customers back to product teams.

That is why so many solution architects feel they are already FDEs. On discovery and design, they are.

FullStack adds that solution architects “aren’t the person accountable for taking a system into production” in most organisations, while an FDE’s typical output is “a deployed system people use every day.” Second Talent found the same in sales tooling: AWS places its solution architects in its global sales organisation and lists Salesforce CRM among their tools, while no FDE posting it read mentioned a CRM.

The test: are you already doing FDE work?

Answer these honestly:

  • After the deal closes, are you still on the account? If your work hands over to a delivery team once the design is signed off, that is SA work.
  • Do you write code that runs in production? Prototypes and demos don’t count. Production means tests, error handling, monitoring and someone depending on it every day.
  • When it breaks after go-live, who gets the call? If it’s you, and you’re expected to fix it in the code, that is FDE work.
  • Do you own the outcome or the document? If the person who designs the solution also builds it and keeps it running in production, the role is FDE work, whatever the title says.

If you answered yes to all four, you may already be doing FDE work under an SA title, and you should position yourself that way. If you mostly answered no, the gap is real but closable.

Why FDE has more scope

Demand is growing faster

Indeed India counted FDE job postings at nearly 19 times the year-ago level in June 2026, 16 times in July and more than 6 times in August. Hiring is spread across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, New Delhi, Mumbai and remote roles, in enterprise AI, data engineering, workflow automation, infrastructure and healthcare.

Indeed India data, reported by PTI in October 2026.

Solution architect roles are not disappearing. Second Talent counted 134 solutions architect and 99 FDE postings at Databricks in October 2026. But, in its words, “the FDE count is the newer story.”

Employers are merging the two titles

Some companies in India now hire for both skill sets in one role:

  • DevRev, Bengaluru: Forward Deployed Architect - Applied AI. Owns technical designs and also builds and launches AI agents, with “hands-on coding to build proofs-of-concept, custom integrations, and solution prototypes” and strong TypeScript and Python. It asks for 5+ years.
  • Salesforce, Hyderabad (also Bengaluru, Mumbai, Gurugram): FDE Senior - Technical Architect. Asks for 10+ years in enterprise architecture and at least two end-to-end Agentforce projects, from discovery to rollout. It combines architecture workshops and pre-sales advisory with RAG, vector databases and acting as the escalation point for delivery issues.

For a solution architect, these hybrid roles are the most natural entry point into FDE work.

The career ladder branches wider

Second Talent describes the two ladders this way: FDE leads to senior FDE, principal FDE and then technical solutions architect, while the architect ladder leads to enterprise architect or pre-sales management. Employers treat the two backgrounds as interchangeable at the top: Anthropic’s FDE manager role accepts 10+ years in “software engineering, solutions architecture, or a technical customer-facing role”, and its architect roles accept experience as a forward deployed engineer.

Beyond that, senior FDEs move into product management, engineering leadership and founding roles, because they know where products fail in production and have owned outcomes, not just designs. Our FDE career ladder guide covers each level.

Notice that the FDE ladder includes the architect role as one of its exits. An FDE can move into solution architecture later; going the other way requires building the production-coding muscle first.

How the pay compares

Self-reported SA pay against published FDE bands. Different sources and methods, so read the chart as direction, not exact numbers.

  • Solution architects (AmbitionBox, March 2025): ₹13.8–47.9 LPA across 7–18 years of experience, from 18,400 salaries. Pay barely moves with experience in these self-reports: 10 and 11 years both show ₹12–45 LPA.
  • FDEs (Taggd, May 2026): ₹30–50 LPA at 3–6 years, ₹50–80 LPA at 7–12 years and ₹90–150 LPA for leadership and AI deployment heads.
  • Top SA roles pay well too: AmbitionBox shows Databricks solution architects at ₹32–54.5 LPA (9 salaries, low confidence), and Levels.fyi shows a ₹55.2 LPA median for Microsoft solution architects in India.

So the bigger pay jump usually comes from moving from a generalist SA role, often in IT services, into an FDE role at a product company, AI start-up or global capability centre. Taggd puts GCC FDE pay at ₹35–90 LPA. The pay structure differs too: FDE packages lean on base and equity, while SA packages often carry a sales-linked component.

In the US, posted ranges are closer than many expect. Second Talent lists OpenAI’s San Francisco FDE at $185,000–300,000 and its AI Architect at $221,000–276,000 base plus commission. Paraform puts total pay for mid-to-senior FDEs at AI labs at $350,000–550,000 including equity, and founding FDE roles pay 29% more than postings that don’t specify a level.

How a solution architect moves into FDE

Second Talent notes that the step from architect to FDE is the harder direction, because roles like OpenAI’s FDE require production code across frontend and backend. So the gap to close is production coding, not design. A practical plan:

  • Take one of your proofs of concept to production standard. Add tests, error handling, logging and documentation, then deploy it. Use it as your main evidence in interviews.
  • Go deep on one stack. FDEs are hired for depth in a product: Salesforce Agentforce and Data Cloud, Databricks, or an LLM stack with RAG, evaluations and agents. See our Salesforce, Databricks and OpenAI guides.
  • Stay on after go-live. In your current role, ask to own one deployment past sign-off: monitoring, fixes, the first month of real users.
  • Lead with your strengths. Discovery, stakeholder management and architecture judgement are what most engineers moving into FDE lack. Put them up front.
  • Target hybrid titles first. Roles like Forward Deployed Architect and FDE Technical Architect value your design background and add hands-on building.

Frequently asked questions

Is a solution architect the same as a Forward Deployed Engineer?

No, though the roles overlap. Both work with customers on how a product fits their systems. A solution architect designs the solution and is usually not accountable for running it in production. An FDE builds the solution inside the customer's environment, ships production code and owns it after go-live.

Can a solution architect become an FDE?

Yes. Employers already treat solution architecture as a feeder: Anthropic's FDE manager role accepts 10+ years in software engineering, solutions architecture or a technical customer-facing role. The main gap is production coding: tests, error handling, monitoring and debugging live systems, not just proofs of concept.

Do FDEs earn more than solution architects in India?

On published bands, usually yes. Taggd puts FDE pay at ₹30–50 LPA for 3–6 years and ₹50–80 LPA for 7–12 years, while AmbitionBox self-reports put solution architects at ₹13.8–47.9 LPA across 7–18 years. Top SA roles at product companies can match FDE pay; Levels.fyi shows a ₹55.2 LPA median for Microsoft solution architects in India.

Which is the better long-term career, solution architect or FDE?

It depends on whether you want to design or to build and own. The FDE path leads to senior and principal FDE, FDE leadership, product management, engineering leadership and founding roles. The SA path leads to enterprise architect, field CTO and pre-sales leadership.
