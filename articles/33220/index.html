Forward deployed engineer career path

Kernavique · 2026-11-21 · 11 min read

Summary

Kernavique argues that no measured career path exists for forward deployed engineers, and separates what is known from what is not. A Bloomberry analysis of 100 LinkedIn profiles found 45% came from software engineering. Kernaverry's own one-day count of 297 US postings across 76 employer job feeds found 40 with leadership titles, only 16 of which manage people. Where FDEs go next is supported only by named examples—founders, investors, product leaders—not by any dataset. No employer publishes promotion levels or timing, though job titles show steps from new grad to senior principal.

Key points

  • No published or measured career path exists for FDEs.
  • Bloomberry's 100-profile sample: 45% were software engineers.

  • Next roles are known only from named examples, not data.

Is there a set career path for forward deployed engineers?

No. Nobody has published one, and nobody has measured one.

There is one sample of who enters the role, our own counts of what employers advertise, and only named examples of where people go next. This briefing says which is which.

Who becomes a forward deployed engineer?

Software engineers are the largest group, in the one sample we could read.

Bloomberry says it “analyzed 100 LinkedIn profiles of current FDEs” in an article marked updated January 25, 2026, read November 20, 2026. It does not say how the 100 were chosen.

Prior role, per Bloomberry Share of 100 profiles
Software engineer (backend or full-stack) 45%
Solutions engineer or solutions architect 22%
Data engineer or data scientist 15%
Technical consultant 10%
Founder or early startup employee 8%

Employers describe a wider mix. Each statement below was read on November 20, 2026 unless another date is given.

  • Founders. Ramp wrote on its engineering blog that 7 of its 16 forward deployed engineers were previous founders. Insight Partners reported on July 21, 2026 that between one in six and one in seven of new hires at Wonderful are former founders.
  • Internal moves. Salesforce wrote in a news story dated March 18, 2026: “Around 40% to 50% of FDE movement at Salesforce is internal.”
  • Consulting. Three named Salesforce directors of forward deployed engineering each said they came from consulting, in a Salesforce post dated October 30, 2025, read November 21, 2026.
  • Non-engineering routes. An AirOps ad asks for “4 to 7 years in Growth, BizOps, Product, Technical Account Management, Analytics”. A Eudia ad asks for “4+ years of practicing law and the ability to manage matters independently.”

What levels do employers advertise?

The titles show steps, though no employer publishes the ladder behind them.

These level words appeared in forward deployed titles in employer job feeds read on November 20, 2026.

  • New grad: Palantir, “Forward Deployed Software Engineer, New Grad - Commercial”.
  • Numbered and junior: Giga, “Forward Deployed Engineer I/II”. Legora, “Junior/Mid-level Forward Deployed Engineer”.
  • Senior and staff: Giga, “Senior / Staff Forward Deployed Engineer”. Anthropic, “Staff Forward Deployed Engineer”.
  • Principal: Snowflake, “Principal Forward Deployed Engineer I” and “Senior Principal Forward Deployed Engineer”. Both Snowflake ads ask for 15+ years of software engineering experience.

No employer handbook or careers page we read on November 21, 2026 sets out levels for the role. Insight Partners reported on July 21, 2026 that Workato has a ladder climbing to an “architect” level.

For the detail, see forward deployed engineer levels.

What leadership roles are advertised?

Leadership titles exist, and fewer than half of them manage people.

On November 20, 2026 we read the public job feeds of 76 employers, mostly AI and tech companies. Of 297 US postings with “forward deployed” in the title, 40 carried a leadership word such as head, director, manager, lead, principal or founding. They sat at 23 employers.

Where do forward deployed engineers go next?

We do not know, and neither does anyone else. We found no dataset or study with a stated sample on next roles. The Bloomberry article has nothing on it.

What follows is what named sources report. We did not check any career history ourselves.

  • Product leadership. Kabir Sial, described by CIO.com as a former Palantir forward deployed engineer and now an investor at Emergence Capital, wrote in an opinion piece for CIO.com dated August 7, 2026 that at Palantir “much of the core product leadership was former FDEs”. Read November 20, 2026.
  • Founding. Jake Stauch, CEO of Serval, told First Round Review that forward deployed engineers are “often former or future founders.” Read November 20, 2026.
  • Entrepreneurship and writing. Lenny’s Podcast show notes dated May 11, 2025 say Nabeel S. Qureshi spent “nearly eight years at Palantir, working as a forward-deployed engineer” and describe him as an entrepreneur and writer. Read November 21, 2026.
  • Advising. Northzone, on June 4, 2026, described Anjor Kanekar as “a former Palantir FDE of seven years” and an advisor. Read November 21, 2026.
  • Back to software engineering. The Pragmatic Engineer reported on August 12, 2025 that after 2016 at Palantir “more FDEs went back to working as software engineers”. Read November 21, 2026.

Employers also describe moves inside the company. A Salesforce post dated October 30, 2025 says its forward deployed engineers can move into leadership, product or customer success roles, and names no one who did. Insight Partners reported on July 21, 2026 that Databricks moves people between the function and product in both directions.

A handful of named people is not a pattern. These examples show the moves happen, not how often.

How do people get in?

Most often from software engineering, going by the one sample above, and for some through a small number of campus programs.

On November 20, 2026, 27 of the 297 US forward deployed postings had an entry-level word in the title: 14 intern, 12 new grad and 1 junior. They sat at 6 employers, and Palantir accounted for 21 of the 27. A further 19 postings at 12 employers were not entry-titled but asked for two years or fewer.

See entry-level forward deployed engineer jobs for the ads, and how to become a forward deployed engineer for the routes.

In practice

If you are an engineer. Ask each employer what the levels are, who was last promoted, and where people who left the team went. Browse open forward deployed engineer jobs, or send us your details and résumé.

If you are hiring. Publish the levels and what moves someone between them. No employer page we read on November 21, 2026 does. If you want help defining the role, you can ask us about hiring forward deployed engineers.

What has nobody measured?

  • Next roles. No source with a sample says what share become founders, managers, product managers or software engineers.
  • Promotion. No employer we read publishes time between levels, or the levels themselves.

What are the limits of this briefing?

Our own counts have limits too. They come from 76 employer job feeds read on one day, November 20, 2026. Leadership and entry-level roles were found by words in the title, so a senior role without one of those words is missed. Role types and requirement lines come from a keyword read of each ad, not an end-to-end read. It is a convenience sample, not a market measure, and shows no trend.

Questions

  • What do forward deployed engineers do next?
  • Nobody has measured it. We found no dataset or study with a stated sample on next roles, in sources read on November 20 and 21, 2026. What exists is named examples and opinion: sources describe former forward deployed engineers who became founders, investors, advisors and product leaders. Treat those as examples, not as odds.
  • What background do forward deployed engineers come from?
  • Software engineering is the largest group, in the one sample we could read. Bloomberry analyzed 100 LinkedIn profiles and reported 45% from software engineering, 22% from solutions engineering or architecture, 15% from data roles, 10% from technical consulting and 8% founders or early startup employees, read November 20, 2026. The article does not say how the 100 were chosen.
  • Is there a promotion ladder for forward deployed engineers?
  • Not a published one. No employer handbook or careers page we read on November 21, 2026 sets out levels for the role. Job titles do show steps: new grad, I/II, senior, staff, principal and senior principal all appeared in employer job feeds read November 20, 2026.
  • Can a forward deployed engineer move into management?
  • The ads show the roles exist. Of 297 US forward deployed postings read on November 20, 2026, 40 had a leadership word in the title and 16 of those were people-manager roles. Nine of the 16 state years of managing or leading people: 2+ years in 6 ads and 5+ years in 3.
  • Do forward deployed engineers become founders?
  • Some do, according to named sources, but no one has counted. Ramp wrote that 7 of its 16 forward deployed engineers were previous founders, read November 20, 2026. That is founders moving into the role. For the reverse direction we found statements from named people and no sample.
  • Can you start as a forward deployed engineer straight from college?
  • A few employers advertise it. On November 20, 2026, 27 of 297 US forward deployed postings had an entry-level word in the title, at 6 employers, and Palantir accounted for 21 of the 27. A further 19 postings at 12 employers asked for two years of experience or fewer.
