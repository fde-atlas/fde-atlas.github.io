AI Reshapes the R&D Paradigm: From Skill-Driven to Intent-Driven

Original title: AI 重塑研发范式，从技能驱动到意图驱动

53AI · 2026-11-02 · 4 min read

Summary

This article argues that AI is shifting software development from a skill-driven to an intent-driven paradigm: since AI can handle most routine coding, human value moves up to defining goals, making key judgments, and controlling quality. Covering coding practices, productivity, organization, management, and individual careers, it claims the real measure is no longer per-stage speed but the throughput of the whole R&D system. Teams are reorganizing around Agents, middle managers who merely relay information are squeezed out, and individual engineers gain the delivery capacity of a former team. The author urges front-end developers to become FDE-style problem solvers who own complete business problems, orchestrating AI across the full delivery chain.

Key points

  • Coding evolves from human-driven to human-AI collaboration with rising AI autonomy.
  • Productivity should be measured by system-wide workflow throughput, not single-stage speed.
  • Agents become the new execution unit; roles expand into end-to-end Builders.
  • Managers shift from relaying information to architecting human-AI production systems.
  • Front-end developers should become FDE problem solvers owning full business problems.
