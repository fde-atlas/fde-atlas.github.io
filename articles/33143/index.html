FDE Jobs in India: DevRev Posts ₹28–36 LPA, Gurugram Heats Up (3 Nov 2026)

FDE India · 2026-11-03 · 8 min read

Summary

India's FDE job market grew modestly in early November 2026: Glassdoor India's broad search reached 557 listings, while exact FDE-titled roles rose from 101 to 119, about 18% in a week. The standout posting is DevRev's Mumbai Forward Deployed Engineer role at ₹28–36 LPA for 5+ years, with 80% customer-facing work and up to 80% travel. Gurugram and Hyderabad are emerging as FDE hubs, with roles at Google Cloud, MongoDB, BCG X, Darwinbox and Arcesium. OpenAI, Anthropic and Palantir list no FDE-titled roles in India; Databricks has 5 of its 112. Qualcomm's performance-based layoffs and Microsoft's exit offer proceed, though neither is linked to FDE hiring.

FDE hiring in India picked up slightly over the weekend. Glassdoor India’s FDE listings rose to 557, and the week’s standout new role is a DevRev Forward Deployed Engineer job in Mumbai with posted pay of ₹28–36 LPA for 5+ years. That is a clear step up from the ₹3.5–14 LPA entry roles we covered on Sunday. On the layoff side, there were no big new India cuts, but Qualcomm’s notifications start this month.

This Pulse follows Sunday’s FDE Jobs in India: Cursor Hires, Pay Starts at ₹3.5 LPA. Here we look at the senior end of the market and at two cities that rarely get attention: Gurugram and Hyderabad.

FDE job listings in India: Glassdoor counts rise again

Both Glassdoor India counts we track went up in early November:

  • The broad forward deployed engineer search showed 557 listings, up from about 522 in late October.
  • The exact Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) title search showed 119, up from 101.

The exact-title count grew faster, up about 18% in roughly a week. Glassdoor India, late Oct and Nov 2026.

The exact-title figure matters more for job seekers, because the broad search also pulls in solutions, support and site reliability roles. A rise of 18 FDE-titled listings in about a week suggests employers are using the title more, not just posting adjacent jobs. Naukri and LinkedIn India totals are still not publicly reported in pages we could access.

DevRev’s Mumbai FDE role: what ₹28–36 LPA buys

DevRev, the AI-native support and product platform, listed a Forward Deployed Engineer role in Mumbai on 1 November 2026, according to JobFound. The details:

Detail What the listing says
Experience 5+ years in software development, systems integration or platform engineering
Posted pay ₹28–36 LPA
Customer time 80% customer-facing, mostly hands-on coding and integration
Travel Up to 80% for on-site implementations and workshops
Stack TypeScript, JavaScript, Python, REST, GraphQL, SQL, AWS Lambda, LLMs

The work is classic FDE: build AI agents, integrations and automations that connect DevRev to a customer’s existing tools through APIs and webhooks, tune agent accuracy with prompt engineering and semantic search, and write SQL and dashboards for customer decisions. Masai School’s check of careers pages on 30 October also found DevRev FDE roles in Delhi NCR and Chennai, plus a Forward Deployed Architect role in Mumbai and Noida.

DevRev’s range starts where the mid-level benchmark ends. JobFound (Nov 2026), Second Talent (Sep 2026), Indeed via IANS (Oct 2026).

For context, Second Talent’s rate card, updated 14 September, puts India FDE fixed pay at ₹14–28 LPA for 3–6 years and ₹28–65 LPA for 6–10 years. Indeed’s figures, relayed by IANS and cited by Masai School, put the average posted FDE base pay in India at about ₹12 lakh, within ₹9–16 lakh. DevRev’s band sits at the bottom of the senior benchmark and well above the Indeed average. The trade-off is the travel: 80% is unusually high, even for an FDE.

Gurugram and Hyderabad: who is posting FDE roles

Gurugram

Indeed India’s Gurugram page says about 50 vacancies for a forward deployed engineer search. On the first page, 10 companies had FDE-titled roles:

  • Google Cloud — Staff Forward Deployed Engineer, Developer AI, listed for Bengaluru, Gurugram and Mumbai. It asks for 8+ years in cloud or customer-facing technical work, plus hands-on experience deploying LLM or agent systems in production. The job covers multi-agent systems, MCP servers, and wiring Google’s agents into tools such as GitLab, ServiceNow and Slack.
  • MongoDB — Forward Deployed Engineer, Industry Solutions.
  • Boston Consulting Group — Lead Forward Deployed AI Engineer, India – BCG X.
  • Coralogix, Convatec (Forward Deployed AI Engineer), Ciroos (FDE – India), Route Mobile (conversational AI), Accenture, Kyndryl and Antino Labs.

Only Route Mobile showed pay, and the figure as displayed was ambiguous, so we have not used it.

Hyderabad

Indeed India’s Hyderabad page for a “forward deployed” search shows about 100 vacancies. Forward-deployed titles on the first page came from Darwinbox, Arcesium (two roles), Amgen, Workato, Crowe Capability Center – India, Redwood Software, Pulsora, Accenture, Inspiredge IT Solutions and Xenon7. None showed pay.

The Hyderabad mix stands out: an Indian HR-tech product company (Darwinbox), a fintech platform (Arcesium), and GCCs such as Amgen and Crowe. Our Hyderabad city guide covers pay and employers there in more depth.

A closed role to note

Cartesia’s Founding Forward Deployed Engineer (India) in Bengaluru, which asked for 4+ years of production systems and voice AI deployment work, was removed on 20 October, according to Built In. Pay was not published for India.

Big AI labs: still no India FDE titles

Masai School counted FDE-titled roles on four careers pages on 30 October:

Company FDE-titled roles In India
Databricks 112 5
Palantir 73 0
OpenAI 32 0
Anthropic 7 0

So the frontier labs are hiring FDEs heavily, but not yet under that title in India. Masai notes that OpenAI and Anthropic list adjacent titles such as Applied AI Architect instead. For Indian engineers, the near-term FDE demand is coming from Databricks, Google Cloud, Cursor and DevRev, plus consulting and services firms. The CapTable reported on 23 October that Indian IT services firms have announced plans for more than 20,000 FDE and related roles, while the specialist talent pool is thin.

Layoffs and FDE hiring this week, side by side

These are listed next to FDE hiring for context. No source links any of these cuts to forward deployed engineer roles.

  • Qualcomm: Business Today, citing a Reuters report of CEO Cristiano Amon’s memo, says affected staff will be notified in November in a “performance-focused reduction”. No number was given, and the report does not mention Hyderabad or Bengaluru.
  • Microsoft: the voluntary exit offer covers about 60,000 staff across India, the US, the UK and Canada. Outlook Money reports eligible staff have until early December to decide, with exits by 31 December; how many of Microsoft’s roughly 23,000 India employees qualify is not disclosed.
  • Innodata: a follow-up to our last two Pulses. A law firm said on 1 November it is investigating possible securities-law issues, according to OffCampusJobs4u, and the company reports results on 5 November.
  • Rehiring: a US–UK survey by SHL, reported by People Matters on 2 November, found nearly 1 in 5 US workers laid off or replaced by AI were later asked to return. It has no India data.

What this means for you this week

  • Price yourself against the senior band. If you have 5+ years and real integration work, DevRev’s ₹28–36 LPA is a useful anchor. Use our salary page to check where your experience sits before you negotiate.
  • Ask about travel early. An 80% travel role is a lifestyle choice. Raise it in the first call, not at offer stage.
  • Search Gurugram and Hyderabad, not just Bengaluru. Google Cloud, MongoDB, BCG X, Darwinbox and Arcesium are all posting FDE titles outside Bengaluru. Set alerts for both cities.
  • For Google-style staff roles, prove agents in production. Prepare one story about an agent or MCP integration you shipped, with metrics. Our interview questions guide has a framework, and the FDE Fit Check shows which gaps to close first.

What does DevRev pay a forward deployed engineer in India?

Are OpenAI, Anthropic and Palantir hiring FDEs in India?
