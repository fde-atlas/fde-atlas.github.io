Summary

This article surveys Salesforce's 2026 Forward Deployed Engineer hiring in India, with roles posted in Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Gurugram, all centered on Agentforce and Data 360. Salesforce hires Forward Deployed Engineers in India to build and ship Agentforce AI agents and Data 360 (formerly Data Cloud) solutions inside customer teams. In 2026 the roles were posted in Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Gurugram. They are closer to the Salesforce platform than most FDE jobs: Apex, Lightning Web Components, Flows and Salesforce certifications appear next to LLM skills in every listing.

What the FDE does at Salesforce

The India postings describe a hands-on engineer who builds AI agents with the customer and stays until they work in production. Across the Hyderabad, Bengaluru and multi-city listings we read, the FDE:

  • Builds agents end to end. Designs Agentforce prompts, topics, actions, multi-agent orchestration and guardrails, from architecture through production handoff.
  • Gets the data ready. Models data and builds pipelines across Data 360, Snowflake, Databricks and the customer’s own platforms. The FDE Data 360 Manager role in Bengaluru focuses on this.
  • Integrates with the enterprise. Connects Salesforce to ERP and other systems through REST/SOAP APIs, middleware and event-driven patterns.
  • Fixes what breaks in the field. Troubleshoots hallucinations, grounding gaps, orchestration failures and integration errors, then refines prompts using usage data.
  • Feeds the product team. The Product FDE roles in Hyderabad stress-test pilot features with customers and report platform gaps back to Product and Engineering.
  • Works in a pair. The June 2026 Mumbai/Bengaluru/Gurugram listing pairs the FDE with a Deployment Strategist, who frames the business problem while the FDE builds. It also asks for about 25% travel.

The bigger picture

Salesforce is expanding the model worldwide. At Dreamforce in September 2026, Salesforce UK said it would double its FDE capacity there to “about 95”, according to ITPro. In April 2026 it launched an FDE Partner Network of 30+ firms, with Accenture, Deloitte, PwC, Slalom and IBM Consulting named as the first partners, Pulse reported. For Indian engineers, that means Agentforce FDE work is also available at partners. See our Accenture FDE page. No source we found gives Salesforce’s FDE headcount in India.

Three patterns stand out:

  • Two entry routes. Most Hyderabad roles want Salesforce developers who have added generative AI. The June multi-city role is open to strong software engineers who know LLMs and data platforms, and Apex is just one of the languages it lists.
  • Certifications matter. Few FDE roles anywhere ask for certificates, but these do. The Agentforce Specialist and Data 360 Consultant certifications come up again and again.
  • Production evidence is required. Every level asks for at least one delivered Agentforce or AI project, not just training.

How to prepare

  • Ship one Agentforce agent. Every level asks for a delivered Agentforce project. Build an agent in a free Developer Edition org with custom actions in Apex or Flow. Ground it on real data, then document what caused hallucinations and how you fixed them.
  • Get the two certifications that keep appearing. Agentforce Specialist and Data Cloud / Data 360 Consultant appear in almost every India listing. Add Platform Developer I, then II, if you’re a Salesforce developer.
  • Learn the data side. Practise ingesting data from Snowflake or S3 into Data 360, resolving identities and using it to ground an agent. This is the core of the Bengaluru and multi-city roles.
  • Prepare an integration story. Be ready to whiteboard Salesforce talking to an ERP through APIs and events, and to explain governor limits and large-data-volume trade-offs.
  • Rehearse customer scenarios. Practise explaining a failed pilot, a scope change and a production incident to a business stakeholder. Our FDE interview questions guide has worked answers.

Hyderabad has the most Salesforce FDE listings we found, so see our FDE jobs in Hyderabad guide. For nearby options, compare roles similar to the FDE.

Salesforce FDE interview: what is publicly known

  • Application: India FDE roles are posted on Salesforce Careers under Customer Success and mirrored on boards such as Indeed India and GetMeReferred. Several 2026 postings have already closed, so set an alert for 'Forward Deployed Engineer' and 'Agentforce'.
  • Screening and technical rounds: Salesforce does not publish an FDE-specific interview format. FDE Academy describes four stages: recruiter screen, hiring-manager round on past AI work, a technical round on Agentforce design and coding, and a customer-scenario panel. Treat this as a third-party account.
  • Certifications check: Unusually, the India listings name required certifications, such as Agentforce Specialist, Data Cloud (Data 360) Consultant and Platform Developer I or II. Expect them to be checked.

Frequently asked questions

Does Salesforce hire Forward Deployed Engineers in India?

Yes. Salesforce posted Agentforce Forward Deployed Engineer roles in Hyderabad in March and April 2026, an FDE Data 360 Manager role in Bengaluru in April 2026, and a Forward Deployed Engineer (Agentforce & Data Cloud) role across Mumbai, Bengaluru and Gurugram in June 2026. Some have closed, so check Salesforce Careers for live ones.

How much experience does a Salesforce FDE role in India need?

It depends on the level. The Agentforce & Data Cloud FDE asks for 3+ years and the FDE Associate for 4–6 years. The Senior Agentforce FDE asks for 5–10 years and two end-to-end Agentforce projects, and the FDE Data 360 Manager asks for 9+ years.

What is the salary of a Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineer in India?

Salesforce does not post pay on its India FDE roles. As an estimate, Levels.fyi shows Salesforce software engineer total pay in India of about ₹26 LPA at Associate MTS, ₹48 LPA at MTS and ₹75 LPA at Senior MTS. Salesforce doesn't say which levels its FDE roles map to.

Do I need Salesforce certifications to become a Salesforce FDE?

The India listings we found name them as requirements. Common ones are Salesforce Certified Agentforce Specialist, Data Cloud or Data 360 Consultant, and Platform Developer I or II. The Senior role also lists the Agentforce Legend badge.
