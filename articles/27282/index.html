Datamarcos · 2026-10-27 · 3 min read

Summary

This page is an index of practical guides from Datamarcos aimed at engineers in India, organized into four areas: forward deployed engineering, agentic AI, generative AI, and data engineering. It compares FDE and AI courses from Indian providers such as IIT Delhi, IIT Hyderabad, Scaler, GUVI, and Simplilearn, explains the FDE role and interview preparation, and covers framework choices like LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, and n8n. Other guides include building an MCP server, RAG versus fine-tuning trade-offs, and 2026 learning roadmaps. Most entries list recent review dates, and Datamarcos promotes its own 22-week live FDE course with four client-style engagements.
