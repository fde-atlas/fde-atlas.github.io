[HCAI Professionals] Hakuhodo DY Group Launches AI Transformation Service Where FDE Specialist Talent Forms Teams to Support Business Growth — 200 Specialist Professionals Support Business Growth Through HCAI Professionals

Summary

Hakuhodo DY Holdings' AI expert group, Human-Centered AI (HCAI) Professionals, has launched an AI transformation service built around teams of FDE-type specialists in marketing, creative, consulting, and technology. Over 200 such professionals work alongside client business units from problem discovery through solution design, implementation, and adoption. The service draws on the group's own AI transformation experience as its first customer, designs AI use around both customer experience (CX) and employee experience (EX), and leverages the CREATIVITY ENGINE BLOOM platform built on consumer data covering roughly 200,000 people. Hakuhodo DY plans to further expand FDE talent and position the service as the core of its AI transformation support.

Key points

  • HCAI Professionals launches an AI transformation service with 200+ FDE-type specialists.
  • Teams are assembled flexibly across marketing, creative, consulting, and technology expertise.
  • The approach follows a Customer Zero model based on the group's own AI transformation.

Full text

Human-Centered AI Professionals, the AI expert group of Hakuhodo DY Holdings Inc. (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; President and CEO: Yasuo Nishiyama; hereinafter Hakuhodo DY Holdings), is launching an AI transformation service to support corporate business growth.

This service brings together "FDE professionals" with diverse expertise across the Hakuhodo DY Group, including marketing, creative, consulting, and technology. These teams work alongside clients' business and functional departments, gaining a deep understanding of their operations and driving everything jointly, from identifying challenges to designing and implementing solutions and embedding them in the workplace. With more than 200 professionals on staff, teams of FDE professionals with the necessary capabilities are assembled according to each client's challenges and desired outcomes. Going beyond specialized knowledge, they support business growth quickly and reliably from multiple angles in response to the challenges clients face.

※FDE professionals (Forward Deployed Engineers): Professionals who work alongside clients in their day-to-day operations, deeply understanding the business while taking end-to-end responsibility from identifying challenges through implementation and adoption.

■ Background In recent years, as companies rapidly adopt AI, they are moving into a phase of pursuing concrete business outcomes such as improved productivity, greater customer value, and stronger competitiveness. The Hakuhodo DY Group has long brought together professionals with different areas of expertise within the Group to implement AI in marketing operations and transform workflows with AI as a given, and has supported clients in creating business outcomes through AI transformation by sharing this know-how. To assemble specialized teams more flexibly and address a wider range of client challenges, HCAI Professionals, equipped with advanced AI-related skills and deep familiarity with the Hakuhodo DY Group's proprietary AI solutions, is now launching its AI transformation service.

■ Overview of the AI Transformation Service This service is delivered by professionals who combine expertise in fields such as marketing, creative, consulting, and technology with FDE-type capabilities, and is structured according to the business goals clients aim for and the challenges identified on the ground.

① Track record as our own first customer ("Customer Zero") Having positioned the Hakuhodo DY Group itself as its first customer, the Group has driven AI transformation in its own business domains, including marketing, creative, consulting, and technology. Based on this unique know-how, we provide practical AI utilization support that clients can also apply.

② "Fully hands-on" problem solving by FDE professionals specialized in each field Professionals deeply versed in their own fields of expertise, such as "FDE-type marketing professionals," "FDE-type creative professionals," and "FDE-type consulting professionals," work alongside clients' business and functional departments, identifying challenges together with client staff and executing and implementing solutions through the development of AI agents and the redesign of business processes. By supporting adoption in the workplace through to problem resolution, they help strengthen clients' AI capabilities and build their creative capital.

③ Experience design and interaction implementation starting from customer experience (CX) and employee experience (EX) The more AI interacts with customers and the more employees use AI daily, the more the quality of those experiences determines outcomes. The Hakuhodo DY Group has cultivated capabilities in consumer-driven experience design over many years. FDE professionals design AI utilization to improve both customer experience (CX) and employee experience (EX), and embed it into actual operations as screen and conversational behaviors. Going beyond concepts to implementation, they connect experience design to results.

④ Integration with the Hakuhodo DY Group's data platform and "CREATIVITY ENGINE BLOOM" FDE professionals leverage "CREATIVITY ENGINE BLOOM", an integrated platform built on consumer data on some 200,000 people held by the Hakuhodo DY Group, to make marketing processes faster and more sophisticated.
