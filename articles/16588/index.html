How We Work: Our Recruiting Process

Forward Deploying · 2026 · 4 min read

Summary

This article outlines Forward Deploying's seven-step recruiting process for Forward Deployed Engineer roles, from a deployment intake with the hiring manager through market mapping, sourcing across three channels, engineer-led validation, and a shortlist of three to five finalists, to interviews, offer, and 30/60/90-day check-ins. The first validated profile usually arrives within a week; most contingent searches close in four to eight weeks. Each finalist comes with a dossier including a scorecard, evidence, risks, and compensation expectations. An illustrative search reviewed 1,200 profiles and produced a shortlist of four. Three engagement models are offered—contingent, retained, and team build—all with a 6-month replacement guarantee and fees agreed in writing.

Key points

  • Seven-step process with defined deliverables at each stage, from intake to onboarding.
  • First validated profile typically within a week; contingent searches close in four to eight weeks.
  • Finalists arrive as engineer-written dossiers with scorecards, evidence, risks, and compensation data.
  • Illustrative search: 1,200 profiles reviewed, 12 engineer deep dives, shortlist of four.
  • Three engagement models, all with a 6-month replacement guarantee and written fee agreements.

The process

What we do, when, and what you get

  • 00Day 0–2

    Deployment intake

    45 minutes with the hiring manager about what this person will deploy: for which customers, on what data, under which security constraints, and what has to be live in 90 days.You get: A written deployment brief, a scorecard weighted to your role, and a salary band checked against live market data.
  • 01Day 3–5

    Market map

    We map who already does this work: the source companies, the teams with Forward Deployed functions, what they pay and how big the realistic pool is.You get: A short market map: 10–20 source companies, the compensation range and the objections to expect.
  • 02Week 1–2

    Sourcing on three channels

    Warm introductions through our partner talent networks, a search across 80M+ profiles on our AI sourcing platform and LinkedIn Recruiter, and the technical footprint: GitHub, Hugging Face, papers and communities.You get: A funnel report with the real numbers of your search: reviewed, contacted, interviewed, shortlisted.
  • 03Week 1–3

    Engineer validation

    Evidence review, engineer-to-engineer deep dive, a deployment case on your domain, a communication check and references from the field.You get: A dossier for every candidate: scorecard, evidence, risks, compensation expectations, motivation and notice period.
  • 04Usually within a week

    Shortlist

    Three to five validated finalists. The first validated profile usually arrives within a week of the intake.You get: An interview guide with questions for each scorecard dimension, so your loop goes deeper than ours.
  • 05Weeks 3–6

    Interviews and offer

    Debrief within 24 hours of every interview, independent references and an offer built on market data, not guesswork.You get: An offer recommendation: base, equity, signing and the terms most likely to close.
  • 06Day 30, 60, 90

    Onboarding and guarantee

    We check in with both sides at 30, 60 and 90 days.You get: A 6-month replacement guarantee: if it does not work out, we run the search again at no extra fee.

What lands in your inbox

Not CVs. Dossiers.

Every finalist comes with a dossier written by the engineer who validated them: the scorecard, the evidence behind it, the risks to probe in your interview and what it will take to close. Your first interview starts where ours ended.

Three ways to work with us

Fees are agreed in writing before we present anyone. Every engagement carries the 6-month replacement guarantee.

Contingent search

One or two senior hires

Paid on hire: a share of first-year base salary, agreed in writing before we present anyone. No retainer.

  • Engineer-validated shortlist
  • Funnel report and market data
  • 6-month replacement guarantee

Retained search

Leaders, founding hires and confidential roles

Paid in three stages: kickoff, shortlist and signature. Every stage payment is credited against the final fee.

  • Full market map before the first call
  • Dedicated researcher
  • Independent referencing

Forward Deployed team build

Four or more hires in a quarter

A monthly fee for an embedded search team plus a reduced success fee per hire.

  • One scorecard for the whole team
  • Weekly pipeline report
  • Interview loop design

Built around your stage

Seed to Series A

Your first Forward Deployed Engineer. The founders are doing every deployment and need someone who can own a customer end to end.

Series B to D

From a few FDEs to a function: leads, architects and a repeatable interview loop that keeps the bar high while you scale.

Enterprise and AI platforms

Applied AI and Forward Deployed teams that work inside large customers, with security reviews, procurement and governance.

Where candidates come from

Three channels, one bar.

Partner talent networks for warm introductions, an AI sourcing platform with 80M+ profiles and LinkedIn Recruiter for reach, and the technical footprint for evidence. Every candidate, however they reached us, goes through the same engineer-led validation.

Compliant by design

Questions about the process

How long does a search take?

The first validated profile usually arrives within a week of the intake. Most contingent searches close in four to eight weeks; leadership searches take longer.

How many candidates will we see?

Three to five per shortlist. We would rather send three people you want to hire than ten you have to filter.

What is the guarantee?

Six months. If the person leaves or is let go in that time, we run the search again at no extra fee.

Do you work exclusively?

Contingent searches do not require exclusivity. Retained and team-build searches are exclusive, which is what lets us map the market properly.

Can we see the screening notes?

Yes. Every profile comes with its dossier and scorecard. Your team starts the first interview where ours ended.
