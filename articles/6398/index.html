Curriculum

AIelsen FDE Academy · 2026 · 3 min read

Summary

AIelsen FDE Academy outlines a twelve-week curriculum for forward deployed engineering, organized into four phases that mirror a real engagement: scope and semantics, data and advisory agents, actions and production, and acceptance with product feedback. Each module specifies concrete deliverables such as a fit memo, metric contracts, evaluation sets, a launch pack, and a retrospective with reusable assets. Weeks five and nine are technical deep dives covering the semantic layer, retrieval, guardrails, and observability. The program stresses that twelve weeks is a learning structure, not a guaranteed deployment timeline, and ends with a capstone defense where candidates must reproduce and justify their work.

Key points

  • Twelve weeks, four phases following a real engagement
  • Every module has defined deliverables like metric contracts and evals
  • Deep dives in weeks five and nine: semantics/retrieval and guardrails/observability
  • Covers production concerns: controlled actions, attribution, handover
  • Ends with a capstone defense testing reproducible competence
