Roles you can target

Six roles. One skill stack.

  • 01GenAI / LLM Application EngineerBuilds copilots, chatbots and generative features on top of foundation models.
  • 02RAG and Knowledge EngineerDesigns retrieval, vector search and graph systems that ground answers in company data.
  • 03AI Agent DeveloperBuilds tool-using and multi-agent workflows with LangGraph, MCP and human approval.
  • 04AI Solutions / Forward Deployed EngineerEmbeds with customers, scopes problems and ships AI inside their environment.
  • 05LLMOps / AI Platform EngineerRuns gateways, tracing, evals, cost control and CI/CD for AI systems.
  • 06AI Security and Governance EngineerRed-teams agents, applies guardrails and maps systems to compliance needs.

Titles vary by company. Role readiness, not a placement, salary or hike guarantee.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

  • “I don’t have AI experience.”You don’t need it. Python and engineering basics are enough; bridge pre-work comes before Day 1.

Ask a counsellor

Do I need prior AI experience?

No. You need Python basics and working engineering fundamentals, or strong shipped projects. Bridge pre-work in Python, Linux, Git and SQL is provided before Day 1.

How much time should I plan for each week?

Plan for about 8 to 12 hours per week: two live sessions, a weekday lab and project work.

How is the program delivered?

It is an 18-week, live, mentor-led program with two live sessions each week, weekly delivery labs, a project studio, scheduled 1:1 mentorship and a final Demo Day.

What projects will I build?

Three client-style projects: a secure Knowledge Copilot, a Multi-Agent Ops Copilot and a Production Hardening project, followed by a domain-specific capstone defended at Demo Day.

Is security covered in depth?

Yes. A full week on AI security and guardrails, governance and compliance essentials (DPDP, GDPR, HIPAA, EU AI Act), plus red-teaming in the projects.

Do you cover Databricks and Snowflake?

Yes. Week 12 covers Databricks, Snowflake and cloud agent platforms such as AWS Bedrock and Azure AI Foundry.

Will I get a job after the program?

No placement, salary or hike is guaranteed. The program includes deployed projects, portfolio support, interview preparation, mock interview loops and a referral and outreach playbook.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. The Datamarcos Executive Certificate in Generative AI and Agentic AI Engineering is awarded on completing the program and defending your capstone.

What is the fee and next cohort date?

The fee, seat booking and EMI options are listed in the Fees & enrolment section on this page. A counsellor can help you choose.

When does the next cohort start?

All current batches start on 5 December 2026. Exact session days and times are confirmed on a counsellor call.

Can my company sponsor me or join as a team?

Yes. Contact admissions to discuss corporate sponsorship and team cohorts.

How is this different from the Forward Deployed AI Engineer program?

This program builds LLM, RAG and agent engineering skills over 18 weeks. The Forward Deployed AI Engineer program assumes engineering experience and adds the customer-facing half of the job: discovery, scoping, SOWs, security reviews, enterprise integration, deployment and handover.
