Expanding an FDE organization that leverages Stockmark's in-house LLM to transform "unstructured data" and "experts' tacit knowledge" into core assets

Original title: ストックマーク自社LLMを活用し「非構造データ」「熟練者の暗黙知」をコア資産へ変革するFDE組織を拡大

ストックマーク株式会社 · 2026-08-18 · 6 min read

Summary

StockMark announced it is expanding its Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) organization to convert unstructured data such as drawings and manuals, plus undocumented tacit knowledge of veteran engineers, into core corporate assets in Japan's core industries. The company has practiced the FDE role since its 2016 founding and now organizes delivery around a triad of FDE (field implementation), FDR (research), and FDS (strategy). A July survey found manufacturers cite organizing proprietary data (60%) and redesigning workflows for AI (57%) as top challenges. The company is recruiting engineers to work with its in-house LLM and data infrastructure, aiming to take projects beyond PoC into production.

Key points

  • StockMark expands its FDE organization targeting tacit knowledge and unstructured data.
  • Japan's core industries face a technology-inheritance gap as veteran engineers retire.
  • Survey: data preparation (60%) and AI-ready workflow redesign (57%) are top challenges.
  • FDE, FDR, and FDS roles work as a triad linking research and field deployment.
  • The company emphasizes running systems in production, not stopping at PoC.

Full text

2026.08.18 Press Release: HR & Recruiting

~Fusing cutting-edge R&D with on-the-ground implementation. Tackling the depths of drawings and specialized documents to contribute to Japan's core industries~

Stockmark Inc. (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director and CEO: Toru Hayashi; hereinafter "Stockmark"), which independently develops a domestically produced generative AI foundation and provides generative AI services for businesses, announces the expansion of its FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)※1 organization and its efforts to turn the "unstructured data (drawings, manuals, etc.)" found in Japan's core industries and the undocumented "tacit knowledge of experts" into core assets. Along with this, the company is formally establishing an organization that connects its own R&D with on-the-ground implementation. Together with the high-class engineers who have led the organization and business to date, and by strengthening the participation of engineers who deeply explore and advance technically challenging challenges such as structuring drawings and converting business-specific tacit knowledge into explicit knowledge, Stockmark will accelerate the social implementation of AI.

・Stockmark recruiting page: https://stockmark.co.jp/recruit/

※1: A new type of engineer role that goes deep into customers' business operations and uses the company's AI and software products to handle everything from problem discovery to implementation, operation, and improvement in an end-to-end manner.

Japan's core industries currently face a serious crisis of "discontinuity in technology succession" due to the successive retirement of veteran engineers. The tacit knowledge of experts—such as the advanced wisdom hidden between the lines of drawings and experimental reports cultivated by the "living encyclopedias" on the front lines—remains undocumented, and the risk of it being lost is growing.

According to a survey conducted by the company in July※2, "organizing proprietary company data and knowledge (60%)" and "redesigning business processes with AI in mind (57%)" were cited as the most important challenges for companies advancing the full-scale use of generative AI and AI agents (※from a survey on the actual state of LLM use in the manufacturing industry).

※2: Survey report on changes in awareness and perception over the four years since the rise of generative AI

On the front lines of Japan's core industries, advanced know-how (tacit knowledge) exists in the "margins" of documents. Unraveling complex unstructured data such as CAD drawings, plant piping diagrams, and handwritten reports, and visualizing the tacit knowledge hidden within them, is a major challenge in the social implementation of AI.

The company's FDEs are an engineering organization that takes on the depths of complex unstructured data. They pursue issues down to the level of data structures and ontologies※3, structuring the tacit knowledge that exists within business operations. By transforming front-line wisdom into core corporate assets, they contribute to value creation in Japan's core industries.

※3: A system that organizes context and background knowledge (relationships between concepts) and structures it into a form that computers can handle.

Even before the concept of "FDE" became widespread, the company had been practicing this role since its founding in 2016, building a solid infrastructure of advanced technology and unstructured data handling capabilities. Leveraging this strength, as an FDE organization that implements advanced technology into customers' business operations, it offers the following three values and a challenging environment.

Centered on engineers who have led complex large-scale system development and DX projects at major SIers and IT consulting firms, the team goes beyond superficial tool adoption and dives deep into customers' unique, complex business workflows. Armed with powerful infrastructure assets such as the company's proprietary data platform, LLMs, and PaaS products, it provides an implementation experience of "seeing things through to actual operation," never stopping at PoC.

The company's FDEs do not stop at mere PoCs or superficial AI adoption; they take on the challenge of building solutions that truly work in the field. High-class engineers and specialist engineers team up with top-level researchers (FDR※4) to elevate R&D outcomes, such as the company's own LLM, into solutions that can be used in the field. It is an environment where engineers can go beyond solving individual companies' problems and enhance their own technical skills and leadership through business results.

FDE (on-the-ground implementation), FDR (cutting-edge research), and FDS※5 (business strategy) work as a trinity to drive customers' business growth. FDEs quickly reflect FDRs' research results into solutions, and FDSs connect business strategy with the field, ensuring that AI is not merely a tool but is put into practical use that enhances corporate competitiveness.

※4: FDR (Forward Deployed Researcher): Talent who can contribute advanced research results to solving customer problems and maximizing value. ※5: FDS (Forward Deployed Strategist): Talent who works on the front lines of customers' businesses to identify essential management challenges and connect business with technology.

Japan's industrial history is long, and there are many core business processes that highly versatile frontier models or SaaS alone cannot fully handle. Aiming at these processes, the company wishes to continue contributing to society by utilizing its proprietary AI model construction technologies accumulated over many years.

Researchers with a track record in image processing and natural language processing research, together with engineers who handle the full stack of system construction, team up on each project to drive technical verification, system construction, and operational improvement to solve business challenges.

■ Recruiting Information

Stockmark is widely recruiting for various engineering positions, including the FDE role that creates new value. We look forward to applications from engineers who want to take on the social implementation of AI from the front lines.

Through its expanded FDE organization, Stockmark will transform front-line wisdom into "core assets" for companies. Going beyond solving immediate problems, the company will create services that become new industry standards together with its customers.

With its unique technical capabilities for unraveling tacit knowledge and complex unstructured data, Stockmark will contribute to the development of Japan's core industries.

Stockmark Inc., with the mission of "reinventing the mechanism of value creation," supports the transformation of many companies using cutting-edge generative AI technology. It operates "Aconnect," an AI agent for the manufacturing industry, and "SAT," which structures all kinds of data and turns it into corporate assets. In addition, it supports the development of company-specific generative AI and the construction of proprietary systems.
