AI & Data Science Certifications

ADaSci · 2026-08-24 · 4 min read

Summary

ADaSci offers nine AI and data science certifications, all earned through proctored multiple-choice exams with verifiable, lifetime-valid certificates. The CFDE targets forward deployed engineers, covering how to turn ambiguous customer needs into production-ready solutions; the advanced CFDAS (60 hours, 120 questions) focuses on architecting and productionizing agentic AI systems. Most credentials involve about 30 hours of self-paced study, with exams of 60–150 questions, pricing from roughly $165, and discounts for members. Enrollment ranges from 359 for the agentic AI architect credential (CAASA) down to single digits for niche ones.

Key points

  • Nine credentials total: eight online plus in-person AIDP.
  • CFDE for FDEs: 30 hours, 60 questions, 168 enrolled.
  • Advanced CFDAS: 60 hours, 120 questions, agentic AI focus.
  • All exams proctored; certificates lifetime-valid and publicly verifiable.
  • Most programs are 30 hours self-paced, finished in 4–8 weeks.
