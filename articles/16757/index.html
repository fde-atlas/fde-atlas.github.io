Try the method (free, right here)

Decomposition drills are how FDE candidates practise. The prompt: "Our support team is drowning. Automate it." The first two moves that score:

Baseline first. Drowning in what metric — volume, first-response time, or resolution rate? Ask for 50 real tickets from last week and which 10 took longest.

Name the guardrail. Speed means nothing if customers keep coming back. Pair first-response time with a 14-day repeat-contact rate before automating anything.

The full drill — five moves, the week-one success definition, and the thing to refuse to build — is Drill 1 of the practice pack and the guide.
