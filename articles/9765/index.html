Compare ADaSci credentials — find the one for your next role

ADaSci · 2026-05-25 · 2 min read

Summary

ADaSci offers nine credentials across four levels — Foundation, Practitioner, Advanced, and Delivery — to match a reader's current or target role. Most exams are 60 questions in one hour online, with 30 hours of recommended training, priced at $249. The Certified Forward Deployed Engineer (CFDE) is a Practitioner-level exam for engineers turning customer problems into production solutions, priced lower at $164.99. Advanced and delivery options demand more: CFDA (120 questions, 2 hours, $375) targets field engineers deploying governed agentic AI, the Chartered Data Scientist runs 150 questions over 3 hours, and the AI Delivery Professional is a 4-hour in-person scenario exam for delivery leads. Members get discounted pricing, and pairing credentials is common.

Key points

  • Nine credentials span four levels: Foundation, Practitioner, Advanced, Delivery
  • Most exams: 60 questions, 1 hour online, 30 training hours, $249
  • CFDE targets FDEs and costs $164.99, cheaper than peers
  • CFDAS: 120 questions, 2 hours, $375, for governed agentic AI deployment
  • AIDP is a 4-hour in-person scenario exam for delivery leads
