Buyer's guide6 questions

What does an AI implementation company do?

It takes an AI idea, such as an agent that answers customer emails or a model that ranks overdue invoices, and builds it into the systems your staff already use, then keeps it working. The good ones end the first step with working software tied to one measure. The weaker ones end it with a report.

How much does AI implementation cost?

On the pages read on 3 November 2026: $8,000 for one workflow at Gigabit, $10,000 for two weeks then $20,000 a month at Digital Scientists, $30,000 for a sprint with a pilot at WorkWise Solutions, and from $100,000 for a production agent across your CRM and ERP at ITRex. Five of the ten print no price.

AI implementation company or forward deployed engineer?

A firm is the right buy when the work is a defined project with a team behind it: a product feature, a new agent, a platform. A forward deployed engineer is the right buy when the problem lives in how the business runs: the pilot needs your CRM, your accounting system and your spreadsheets to agree, and someone has to sit with the people who do the work. Digital Scientists, Brainforge and Tenex now sell a forward deployed model inside a firm.

Why do AI pilots fail to reach production?

Usually the model is fine and the data and the joins are not. The agent was tested on a clean sample, and the real records sit in three systems that disagree. RTS Labs and Brainforge both put a data and integration check before the build for that reason.

How long does an AI implementation take?

On these pages, two to four weeks for a first workflow or a scoping sprint, four to eight weeks to a first usable outcome at Brainforge, 90 days to a measured phase one at RTS Labs, and six to eight weeks for an MVP at WorkWise Solutions.

Should the implementation partner be in the US?

Only if you need it. RTS Labs says all of its staff are US based and Digital Scientists puts a US based architect on each engagement. Gigabit engineers in Dhaka, Leanware in Colombia and Valere partly in India and Peru, and they price accordingly. Ask where the person who writes your code sits.

How to choose

  • 1This week, write one page on the job: the workflow, the systems it touches by name (for example HubSpot, NetSuite and the shared drive), who retypes data between them, and the one number that should move. A firm that cannot price a first step from that page is not ready for you.
  • 2Before any build, export 200 real records from each system the AI will use and check how many disagree on the same customer or order. If more than a handful do, the first job is the data, and Brainforge or RTS Labs, who put that check first, are the closer fit.
  • 3Ask every firm for the price of the first step in writing, and what it ends in: working software or a document. Gigabit ($8,000), Digital Scientists ($10,000), Leanware ($5,000 to $15,000) and WorkWise Solutions ($30,000) print it.
  • 4Ask who builds and where they sit. Digital Scientists and RTS Labs put US based people on the work. Gigabit, Leanware, ITRex and Valere use teams in Dhaka, Colombia, Warsaw, Indore and Lima. Neither answer is wrong, but it should match your budget and your security rules.
  • 5Ask what happens after launch. Models change and accuracy slips without anyone noticing. Gigabit prices operations at $3,000 to $20,000 a month and WorkWise Solutions gives a 60 day warranty. Make sure your company owns every account, model key and line of code from day one.
  • 6Keep the first commitment short: two to four weeks, then a decision. Digital Scientists lets you stop after two weeks and keep everything. Treat any impact total without a method, such as Valere's two different figures, as a vendor claim.
  • 7If the job is bigger than one workflow you can test this month, that is when you bring in a forward deployed engineer. Frequently asked7 answers

What are the top AI implementation companies?

On this page: Digital Scientists, RTS Labs, Gigabit, Brainforge, ITRex, Tribe AI, Tenex, WorkWise Solutions, Valere and Leanware, in that order, read on 3 November 2026.

What is the difference between AI implementation firms and AI implementation partners?

The words are used for the same thing. A partner usually means the firm stays on after launch to run and improve the system, which Gigabit, Brainforge and RTS Labs all say they do.

Is there an AI implementation consultant for small business?

For a company under about 50 people, the cheapest real starts on this page are Gigabit's $8,000 one workflow sprint and Leanware's $5,000 to $15,000 Sprint 0. Most of the other firms are built for larger companies.
