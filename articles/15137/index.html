How to Compare Forward Deployed Engineer Offers

FDEOps · 2026-10-23 · 2 min read

Summary

This FDEOps guide explains how to compare forward deployed engineer offers without giving salary figures, arguing no number holds across employers, levels and locations. It recommends comparing written terms, separating guaranteed, estimated and conditional amounts, and using a field-by-field table covering salary, bonus, sign-on, equity and benefits. It warns against universal percentage conversions between locations, notes the FDE title alone implies no pay premium over product engineering, and lists questions to ask about role level, negotiable components, equity conditions and progression criteria.

Key points

  • No salary table; compare written offer terms instead.
  • Keep guaranteed, estimated and conditional amounts separate.
  • Avoid universal percentages when comparing offers across locations.
  • FDE title alone establishes no pay premium or faster promotion.
  • Track your contributions to support, not guarantee, raises.

Build a like-for-like comparison

Same period, same currency. Annual pay and a multi-year grant never share a column.

Field Record
Role Exact title, level, team and location
Source Official posting or written offer, with date
Salary Amount, currency and pay period
Bonus or variable pay Target, conditions, timing and whether guaranteed
Sign-on Amount, payment date and any repayment terms
Equity Award type, quantity, vesting and valuation assumptions
Other terms Benefits, location, travel, on-call and expenses

Self-reported pay suggests questions to ask. It does not predict your offer.

How FDE offers are structured

Component Questions to ask
Salary What amount is guaranteed, in which currency and for what period?
Variable pay What determines payment, who assesses it and when is it paid?
Sign-on payment Is it one-time, and when could repayment be required?
Equity What is the award type, quantity and vesting schedule? What conditions affect its value or ability to sell?
Benefits and expenses What is included, and how are travel and other work costs handled?

Compare locations carefully

Do

  • Compare the actual role, currency, employment terms, benefits and working arrangements.
  • Write down exchange-rate assumptions when you convert.
  • Get qualified local advice when tax or equity terms could change your decision.

Avoid

  • A universal percentage to translate an offer between countries or cities.

FDE vs product engineering

The title does not establish a pay premium, promotion speed or equity policy. Compare actual roles at the same level and location, and ask about:

  • Ownership
  • Customer time
  • Production support
  • Career progression

Questions for an offer discussion

  • role and level
  • Which responsibilities set the level, and which of your experience counts toward it?
  • cash
  • Which components are open to discussion, and which are fixed by policy? Get revised terms in writing.
  • equity and reviews
  • What does the award represent, what conditions apply, how are future reviews handled?
  • working terms
  • Location, travel, office presence, on-call, expenses, and the support to meet the role's expectations.

Understand progression

Ask how the employer evaluates performance, widens responsibility and decides pay.

Keep a record of your contribution and observed results. It supports the conversation; it does not guarantee the raise. See FDE capabilities.

FAQ

How much does an FDE make?

There is no single reliable figure across employers, levels and locations. Check a current official posting and compare its stated salary separately from other compensation.

Do FDEs earn more than product engineers?

The title alone cannot answer that. Compare specific roles, levels, locations and compensation terms.

Is compensation negotiable?

Policies differ. Ask which parts can be discussed, and weigh responsibilities and working conditions alongside the amounts.

How does pay differ outside the United States?

Use local role-specific sources and the actual offer. A universal ratio to US compensation can hide differences in currency, benefits, employment terms and equity.

What supports compensation growth?

Know the employer's progression criteria and keep a truthful record of your contribution. Pay also depends on factors outside your performance.
