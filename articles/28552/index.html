Today's Judgment

Today's signal comes not from a new product launch, but from changes in the behavior of enterprise buyers and platform providers.

Chinese enterprise customers' AI agenda has shifted from "whether to adopt" to ROI, compliance, reuse of benchmark cases, and independent controllability; cloud vendors have begun measuring organizational adoption through multi-user collaboration and task duration, rather than just counting activated accounts.

For a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), the core deliverables go beyond shipping features and include three types of evidence:

1. Ruling out wrong paths, reducing the sunk costs of enterprises' continued investment.

2. Identifying the process, data, and management root causes behind system problems.

3. Transforming high-risk scenarios from probabilistic generation into verifiable, regression-testable deterministic processes.

Key Signals

CIO on-site agenda shifts from "whether to adopt AI" to "ROI, compliance, and replication"

  • Level/Rating: Weekly signal/A
  • D1Net has released its CIO event schedule for the second half of the year, covering more than 10 cities and industries including manufacturing, automotive, energy, pharmaceuticals, state-owned and central enterprises, and retail, with an expected gathering of over a thousand enterprise decision-makers.
  • The agenda focuses on AI implementation, ROI, independent controllability, intelligent operations, data auditing, and reuse of benchmark experience.
  • Source: Enterprise Network D1Net: Premium CIO Event Series for the Second Half of 2026
  • FDE interpretation: Chinese enterprise demand has shifted from tool awareness to scenario selection, compliance governance, and replication mechanisms, but this source is an event schedule, not enterprise implementation results.

Tencent Cloud begins driving organizational adoption with "sub-accounts + task duration"

  • Level/Rating: Weekly signal/A
  • Tencent Cloud's ADP campaign requires at least 2 sub-accounts within the same organization to complete tasks within one week, with cumulative effective runtime exceeding 120 minutes; platform usage rewards are granted upon meeting the criteria.
  • The page also lists results from Yili's agent matrix: click-through rate up 15.7%, orders up 26%, demand recognition accuracy at 93%, and conversion rate up 39%.
  • Source: Tencent Cloud: ADP Smart Workbench Vitality Season
  • FDE interpretation: Enterprise AI promotion is shifting from "buying packages" to "activating multiple users, running real tasks, and incentivizing based on usage behavior."

Evergreen Methods

The FDE On-Site Convergence Four-Step Method

提出假设 → 同样本对照测试 → 排除不适用路径 → 把根因移交到数据／流程／管理层

Ruling out an option is not failure; it is a deliverable. Only by documenting test samples, tool performance, failure causes, and subsequent responsibility boundaries can enterprises avoid paying repeatedly for the same wrong path.

Implications for FDE Practitioners

  • Include a "ruled-out paths list" as a fixed section in FDE weekly reports, documenting the trial-and-error time saved and the erroneous investments avoided.
  • Change organizational adoption metrics from account activation to: number of active users, real task duration, repeat usage rate, number of tasks completed, and human takeover rate.
  • Pay attention to real implementation cases appearing at CIO events, especially projects with before/after data comparisons.
