Casual Notes - Sharing & Memory

Original title: 随笔记录-分享&记忆

CSDN · 2026-03-22 · 4 min read

Summary

A CSDN blog column by shenhonglei, a solutions director at ChangQing Cloud (under JieChuang Intelligence), collecting essays on AI and cloud computing. Topics include WeChat's ClawBot plugin for running AI assistants in chat, a comparison of OpenClaw versus LangSmith, the rise of Forward Deployed Engineers in enterprise AI, a guide comparing five LLM inference engines (vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, DeepSpeed-MII, LMDeploy), Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash Image (Nano Banana), agent development platforms like Dify and n8n, and DPU technology from Alibaba Cloud and AWS.

Key points

  • Author is a solutions director with 10+ years in cloud computing and Kubernetes.
  • WeChat ClawBot plugin lets users control AI assistants via QR-code setup.
  • OpenClaw is a local personal agent; LangSmith is a cloud developer platform.
  • Argues FDEs bridge technology and business for enterprise AI deployment.
  • Also covers inference engine selection, agent platforms, and DPUs.
