What is a Forward Deployed Expert (FDE)? | Definition, Background, and What It Means as an AX Architect

Original title: Forward Deployed Expert（FDE）とは何か｜定義・背景・AXアーキテクトとしての中身

AX for Revenue Institute · 2026-05-29 · 14 min read

Summary

This article by AlphaDrive's AX for Revenue Institute argues that as AI collapses the cost of building finished products, coding ability alone is no longer scarce. It defines a new role, the Forward Deployed Expert (FDE=Expert): a specialist whose business-development know-how is amplified by AI, embedded beside the client's business owner to drive hypothesis building, full-product launches, and breakthroughs in internal decision-making. The role's substance is AlphaDrive's “AX Architect = BA capability × AI capability” framework. The author positions Engineer and Expert as parallel, complementary roles rather than substitutes, and stresses that FDE engagements aim to transfer AX Architect capabilities into the client organization so it can eventually run its four-step revenue loop autonomously.

Key points

  • Proposes FDE=Expert: AI-amplified business-development expertise stationed at client sites.
  • Rationale: with completion-cost collapse, scarcity shifts from building to defining what to build and breaking through decisions.
  • Core capability is the AX Architect: BA skills plus AI Sprint, AI Orchestration, and Full-Product Launch.
  • Engineer and Expert are parallel roles, not a hierarchy; both are needed on-site.
  • FDE is framed as capability transfer and talent development, not one-off external dispatch.

FDE=Engineer: The World Standard

Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) refers to engineers who work on-site at customers, converting field challenges into products while writing code. Established by Palantir in the 2000s and inherited by cutting-edge AI companies worldwide including Anthropic and OpenAI, this is the core talent profile of business implementation in the AI era.

The hallmark of their work is that, rather than staying at headquarters polishing products as general-purpose goods, they position themselves in the middle of the customer's business workflows and translate the friction and inefficiencies occurring in the field into code on the spot. No requirements documents. No product managers in between. They write code next to the customer, put a working thing in front of them, and rewrite it again. That speed of iteration is what has advanced business implementation in the AI era.

There is a reason the world's cutting-edge AI companies have insisted on this talent profile. The moment a general-purpose AI product touches the real business workflows of a customer company, it is exposed to countless frictions. Solving that friction not in abstracted conference rooms but in the field—that is the essence of Forward Deployed. A solution that is, in a sense, simple—having people who can write code stationed in the field—has carried the core of business implementation in the AI era.

So, is Engineer really the only talent profile that should stand in the field under Forward Deployed?

(Reference: Forward Deployed Engineer)

Why Engineer Alone Is Not Enough—The Shift in Scarcity

In the era of Completion Cost Collapse, where the manufacturing cost of finished products has collapsed, "being able to build" is no longer a rare capability in itself.

This is not a denial of the Engineer's value. It is precisely because of the accumulation of Engineers continuously writing code in the field that the world has reached its current point, where AI writes code. However, the location of scarcity is clearly shifting. Anthropic's internal survey reports that 27% of employees' Claude-assisted work was "work that would not have occurred without Claude" (Book "AI Revenue Evolution Theory," Chapter 3-3). The collapse of manufacturing costs has dramatically broadened the reach of coding.

What is scarce? The power to define what to build, and why, for which customer challenges. The power to read internal decision-making structures and move stakeholders to break through. The power to read the response from finished products released to market and continuously update the business hypothesis itself. These capabilities, in a word, are "practical knowledge of business development."

The methodology of new business itself is also evolving in response to this era. From the paradigm of carefully building and validating with an MVP, to the Full-Product Launch paradigm of launching a finished product with AI all at once and treating market response itself as validation. As the book "AI Revenue Evolution Theory" organizes it, this is not a denial of the MVP. Within a business, there coexist layers that can move with FPL and layers that should proceed carefully with a conventional MVP (Yoichi Aso, "AI Revenue Evolution Theory," Chapter 8-3).

That is precisely why those who should stand in the field under Forward Deployed are not limited to Engineers. Experts who amplify business development practical knowledge with AI should also stand in the field.

Here, one important premise should be added. FDE=Expert is not "someone who only gives instructions without doing hands-on work." Now that AI has broadened the reach of engineering, it has become realistic for business development specialists themselves to build working things with AI as their weapon while standing beside the customer's business owner. Experts also touch code. The difference lies in where the emphasis is: on the side that defines what to build.

What Is a Forward Deployed Expert—The Core Definition

Forward Deployed Expert (FDE=Expert) is an engagement model in which a specialist who amplifies business development practical knowledge with AI works on-site alongside the business owner at the customer company, driving and accompanying everything from hypothesis building to finished-product launch and breaking through internal decision-making.

Engineer and Expert are not opposing concepts. They are two core talent profiles with different roles, standing side by side on the front lines of business implementation in the AI era. If the Engineer takes on "the field of building to completion," the Expert takes on "the field of determining what to build and breaking through internal decision-making." The two are not in a hierarchical relationship but parallel existences with different specializations.

Business implementation in the AI era is completed when both parties are present in the same field, each with their own expertise. The Engineer building things to completion on the ground and the Expert breaking through decision-making on the ground are both indispensable. An Expert alongside the Engineer. This is AlphaDrive's framing.

The substance of the Expert is the "AX Architect = BA capability × AI capability"

The substance of the FDE=Expert's capabilities is the talent profile that AlphaDrive has systematized: the "AX Architect = BA capability × AI capability."

BA capability (Business Architect capability)

Just as METI and IPA positioned the "Business Architect" at the core of transformation-driving talent in DSS v2.0, at the center of business transformation in the digital age lies a talent profile that translates strategy into business structure and moves people to implement transformation. BA capability breaks down into the following three points.

  • The ability to translate corporate strategy into business structure
  • The ability to move stakeholders
  • The ability to build consensus across organizations

The more AI spreads, the more important this BA capability becomes, both relatively and absolutely. This is because it is a domain that AI cannot replace and only humans can carry. In Gartner's 2026 CEO survey, 80% of CEOs responded that "AI will force a high to moderate degree of transformation on their organization's operational capability," and the substance of capability is required to shift toward a "capabilities-first mindset" — the ability to define what to accomplish and how.

AI capability

The AX Architect is BA capability equipped with three capabilities specific to the AI era.

  • AI Sprint: The ability to feed AI large volumes of data and ideas, bringing it to a performance level that can carry out operations end to end
  • AI Orchestration: The ability to bind multiple AIs together under management's will, creating a state where they function as a whole business
  • Full-Product Launch: The ability to use AI as a weapon to bring finished products fully to market

Clarifying the nested structure

Here, let us make the conceptual nesting explicit.

  • Capability profile: AX Architect = BA capability × AI capability
  • Engagement model: The engagement model that deploys this profile embedded at the customer's site = Forward Deployed Expert (FDE)
  • Service: AlphaDrive's service that provides this FDE = Revenue Evolution FDE / AX for Revenue FDE Service

Capability profile, engagement model, and service are concepts at different levels of granularity. The "talent profile" of the AX Architect is deployed at customer sites through the "engagement model" of Forward Deployed. That package launches to market as AlphaDrive's service.

While the world has established "FDE=Engineer" as the standard term, AlphaDrive presents to the market, for the first time in Japan in a business development context, another solution: "embedding AX Architects as Forward Deployed residents."

What the FDE does at the customer's site

The FDE (= an AX Architect embedded on-site) sits beside the customer's business owner and drives all steps of the AX for Revenue Loop together.

To survey what they take on: co-building business hypotheses, executing AI-driven Full-Product Launch, driving the Loop on the ground, accompanying PI Injection, and breaking through commercialization judgments and internal decision-making. All of these are domains that a single business owner cannot carry alone, yet that get severed when outsourced. The Expert standing alongside fills that gap.

The four steps of the AX for Revenue Loop proceed as follows. Step 1 AI Sprint (thorough AI-ization of existing operations) → Step 2 Plateau Detection (detecting the point of limitation) → Step 3 PI Injection (injecting intelligence that only humans can possess) → Step 4 Redesign of revenue structures (scaling seeds into strategy and new business models).

One important point here is that the FDE climbs this Loop continuously without breaking it apart. Starting with accompaniment in the efficiency AI domain (Steps 1 and 2), the moment the Plateau is reached, they continue seamlessly into the revenue evolution phase (Steps 3 and 4). A single FDE climbs both the efficiency AI mountain and the revenue evolution AI mountain in succession. The on-the-ground traction born from the AI Sprint, the resolve of executives facing the Plateau, and the N=1 seeds unearthed through PI Injection can only be connected within this continuous flow.

This article does not go into specific implementation procedures or contract structure details. Conceptually, what the FDE takes on at the customer's site is driving all steps of the Loop together; the substance of its implementation is designed individually according to each customer's situation.

The FDE is not external dispatch — it is the beginning of AX Architect development

AlphaDrive's FDE is not a transient dispatch that departs when the contract ends. The very process of the FDE continuing to sit beside the customer's business owner is a process of transplanting "business development practical knowledge for the AI era = AX Architect capability" into the customer's organization.

For AX Architects, "development" comes first, and external dispatch is positioned as "complementary accompaniment during development." Over the course of one loop of the Loop, business owners, young leaders, and frontline members within the client company equip themselves with AX Architect capabilities alongside the FDE. The goal is to create a state where, after the FDE has fully withdrawn, talent within the client company can run the Loop autonomously. That is the ultimate value delivered.

FDE and existing engagement models such as SES, contracted development, and consulting differ not in superiority but in role. Each model has its own inherent rationale and domain of fit; FDE does not replace them but is positioned as an alternative solution for the specific domain of business development in the AI era.

"AI: from efficiency to revenue creation" — what this brand message means is not a rejection of efficiency-focused AI, but presenting to society a talent profile and engagement model for stepping beyond efficiency AI. FDE is one answer from AlphaDrive for concretizing that philosophy at customer sites.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is Forward Deployed Expert (FDE=Expert)?

A. It is an engagement model in which a specialist who has amplified practical business development knowledge with AI works alongside the client company's business owner, driving and accompanying everything from hypothesis building to launching a finished product and breaking through internal decision-making. The substance of the capability is AX Architect = BA capability × AI capability. It is a talent profile that AlphaDrive was the first in Japan to establish in the business development context.

Q2. How does it differ from Forward Deployed Engineer?

A. The Engineer handles "the front line of building things through to completion," while the Expert handles "the front line of determining what to build and breaking through internal decision-making." The Engineer's primary counterparts are technical leads and frontline staff; the Expert's are business owners and executives. The two are not in a hierarchical relationship but are two core talent profiles with different roles, standing side by side on the front line of business implementation in the AI era.

Q3. Why is the Expert needed now?

A. Because Completion Cost Collapse (the collapse of the cost of building finished products) has made "the ability to build" itself no longer a scarce capability. Scarcity has shifted to the power to define "what to build, for whom, and why" and to break through internal decision-making — that is, practical business development knowledge. This shift is the structural reason why an Expert should stand alongside the Engineer.

Q4. Doesn't FDE=Expert write code?

A. They do. Now that AI has broadened the reach of engineering, it has become realistic for business development specialists themselves to stand on the front line, using AI as a weapon to build working things. The difference from the Engineer is not "whether or not they write code," but that their focus lies on the side of defining what to build.

Q5. How does FDE differ from SES, contracted development, and consulting?

A. They differ in role, not in superiority. SES, contracted development, and consulting each have their own inherent rationale and domain of fit. FDE does not replace them but is positioned as an alternative solution for the specific domain of business development in the AI era. Working alongside the business owner and driving every step of the Loop together is what makes FDE's engagement model unique.

Q6. Does FDE leave anything behind with the client company after the contract ends?

A. Yes. The very process of the FDE sitting alongside the client's business owner is a process of transplanting AX Architect capabilities into the client company. The goal is to create a state where, after the FDE has withdrawn, talent within the client company can run the AX for Revenue Loop autonomously. That is the ultimate value delivered.
