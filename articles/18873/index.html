We prevent "tools that get implemented but never used." Our engineers work on-site alongside your team, covering everything from business analysis to implementation, training, adoption, and internal capability building for generative AI and AI agents.

AI adoption support means support that covers integration into your business and lasting adoption

AI adoption support is a form of support that integrates generative AI and AI agents into your business operations and guides them to a state where they deliver results. The scope covers everything from business analysis to tool selection, implementation, in-house training, operational adoption, and internal capability building. SAI Co., Ltd. provides this with engineers working directly on-site.

What determines the success or failure of AI adoption is not the performance of the tools, but where in your business they are integrated and how adoption is made to stick. The difference from AI consulting is whether implementation is also covered. Unlike vendors that only sell tools or provide training, we continue to take responsibility through restructuring business workflows and measuring adoption. SAI Co., Ltd. takes on everything from implementation to adoption in one continuous effort through FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)-style hands-on support, with engineers embedded in the field.

Do any of these situations sound familiar?

  • You signed up for ChatGPT, but only a few employees use it
  • You don't really know which business tasks AI could be used for
  • You ran a PoC (proof of concept), but actual operations haven't changed
  • Security concerns have prevented you from establishing internal rules
  • You held training, but within a week everyone went back to their old ways

If even one of these applies, what you need is not "a better tool," but the design of integration into your business and lasting adoption.

Covering four areas, from business inventory to training and adoption

AI Utilization Assessment and Business Inventory

We break down your business processes and visualize "tasks that can be delegated to AI" along with cost-effectiveness. We propose priorities for where to start, backed by evidence.

Implementation and Business Integration

We go beyond generic chat tools, embedding AI into everyday workflows such as email, document processing, and CRM. We also build business automation using AI agents.

AI Training and In-house Education

Hands-on training using your actual business tasks as material, not just lectures. We turn effective prompts into shared company assets and develop in-house champions.

Adoption Support and Internal Capability Building

We measure usage, eliminate barriers to adoption, and stay engaged until AI becomes part of your operations. Ultimately, we create a state where usage spreads within your company on its own.

Think about AI adoption costs by structure, not by rate tables

Even when called the same "AI adoption," the scope varies widely, so you cannot judge from a price list alone. When you break costs down into four categories—tool usage fees, build and integration costs, education and adoption costs, and operations and improvement costs—you can see what is missing from the quote in front of you. SAI Co., Ltd. offers a monthly subscription model and proposes a structure matched to the scale of your challenges.

AI adoption fees are monthly and determined by the scale of your challenges and the structure of our involvement

We do not publish a price list. Costs can be broken down into four categories, and the structure we propose changes depending on where the weight falls.

  • How fees are determined
  • It is a monthly subscription model, determined by the scale of your challenges and the structure of our involvement. The overall cost of AI adoption can be broken down into tool usage fees, build and integration costs, education and adoption costs, and operations and improvement costs. Of these, the monthly fee includes build and integration costs, education and adoption costs, and operations and improvement costs, while tool usage fees are charged separately at actual cost.
  • What is included
  • The scope covers everything from business inventory and prioritization to implementation, in-house training, and measuring adoption. We offer an advisory model with unlimited consultations and a hands-on model that includes engineers' actual work. What exactly is included in the monthly fee is confirmed in a checklist before we start.
  • How to think about contract duration
  • Duration is discussed case by case. Our approach assumes starting with a single business process or team, then measuring results and expanding the scope. Whether to expand is decided based on usage data and changes in processing time.
  • Typical timeline to get started
  • We can start as early as the week after your consultation. After kickoff, we first spend time on business inventory. The start date may shift depending on team availability and your company's preparation.
  • Additional costs
  • Usage fees for generative AI and SaaS are not included in the monthly fee. Actual costs such as external service fees are also separate. We reconcile what is and isn't included in the quote before starting work.

Answers on costs, how to start, fit, and common failures

Q. How do you decide what information can be entered into generative AI? +

We classify information by sensitivity and define, for each business process, what may be entered into external services. We choose offering formats with contractual guarantees on how entered data is handled, and establish an internal point of contact for cases where judgment is unclear. Rules are not just distributed and forgotten—we review them while testing them in actual operations.

Q. Where should we start with AI adoption? +

Start with a business inventory. The more routine and repetitive a task is, the easier it is to see results—typical examples include first-line response to inquiries, creation and processing of standardized documents, and drafting materials. Tool selection can wait until after the business processes have been broken down.

Q. How are AI adoption costs determined? +

It is a monthly subscription model, determined by the scale of your challenges and the structure of our involvement. Costs fall into four categories—tool usage fees, build and integration costs, education and adoption costs, and operations and improvement costs—and comparing what is not included in each quote makes comparison possible. A good rule of thumb is the current time and headcount spent on the target business processes.

Q. What are common failures in AI adoption? +

Ending with training sessions and tool distribution without changing business workflows. If tools are distributed without defined use cases, adoption stalls with only a few people. The design includes measuring usage and eliminating, on the spot, the reasons why tools go unused.

Q. What kinds of companies are a good fit, and what kinds are not? +

A good fit is small and mid-sized companies without a dedicated IT department. Since engineers go directly into the field, work can begin even without in-house specialists. A poor fit is work that existing SaaS can handle as-is; in that case, simply selecting and configuring tools is enough.
