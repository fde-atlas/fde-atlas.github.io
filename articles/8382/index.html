Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer Interview (No Offer): The first round with the recruiter

Taro · 2023-11-08 · 3 min read

Summary

A candidate shares a no-offer interview experience for Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer role in New York. The recruiter round went smoothly, but the coding interviewer seemed unenthusiastic, and the technical question was a coding problem modeled on a card game. Aggregated data from 36 interview experiences shows a 14% pass rate, indicating a highly selective process, with 56% positive, 28% neutral, and 17% negative candidate sentiment. The post also compiles employee reviews: strong talent and culture, early exposure to high-impact client work, and flexible growth paths, alongside drawbacks like heavy workloads, intensity depending on team and customer, and little structured career development.

Key points

  • Candidate received no offer; recruiter round went well
  • Coding interviewer seemed uninspired; card-game-style question
  • 14% pass rate across 36 reported interview experiences
  • 56% of candidates rated the process positively
  • Employee reviews praise talent but cite weak growth structure
