Blog — Hold The Churn

Quivly · 2025-07-25 · 26 min read

Summary

This page is the index of Quivly's blog, dominated by a long "Post-Sales Playbook" series by Arushi Jain. The articles are practical how-to guides for customer success and post-sales teams, covering onboarding quality, customer health scoring, renewal forecasting, expansion signals, sales-to-CS handoffs, and AI agents and prompts for CSMs. One piece compares forward deployed engineers, solutions engineers, and CSMs with a RACI for go-live handoffs. Case-study posts reference companies like Upflex, Octolane, and Mycroft. The content doubles as marketing for Quivly's post-sales platform, so readers should treat recommendations as vendor-influenced.

Key points

  • Blog index for Quivly, a post-sales platform vendor.
  • Long how-to series on customer success operations by Arushi Jain.
  • Topics span onboarding, health scores, renewals, and expansion.
  • Includes AI agents, prompts, and MCP integration for CSMs.
  • One article compares FDEs, solutions engineers, and CSMs.
