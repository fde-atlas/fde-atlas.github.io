Stripe Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Questions (Updated 2026)

Aced (formerly Exponent) · 2019-12-29 · 1 min read

Summary

This Aced (formerly Exponent) article compiles 15 interview questions for Stripe's Forward Deployed Engineer role, contributed and verified by hiring managers and candidates. Questions span behavioral topics (self-introduction, past projects, handling difficult customers and negative feedback, client-facing experience) and system design prompts such as building a metrics and logging service or a user login system. Several questions include video answers and community responses, with some listings dating back several years and others added recently. The database relies on user contributions, and readers are invited to share their own interview experiences to earn karma. The page also links to popular roles and trending companies.
