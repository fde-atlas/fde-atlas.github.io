Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (5/5, Oct 18, 2024): Loved The Team And Perks

Taro · 2024-10-18 · 1 min read

Summary

This piece compiles interview experiences from candidates for Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer role. The process is generally light: a recruiter call covering intro and compensation, followed by a roughly 30-minute technical interview featuring an easy LeetCode problem and a code walkthrough. One candidate applied online and finished within four weeks; another was contacted via LinkedIn. Several candidates found the interviews easier than online reports suggested, though one stumbled on the technical round. The post also includes a 5/5 review from a former FDSE in New York who worked there under a year, praising the team, work, location, perks, and hours, with essentially no cons listed.

Key points

  • Palantir FDE interviews typically involve a recruiter call plus a short technical round
  • Technical questions are mostly LeetCode Easy with a code walkthrough
  • One candidate's process took about four weeks from online application
  • Candidates report the interviews were easier than online accounts suggested
  • A former New York FDSE rated Palantir 5/5, citing team, perks, and hours
