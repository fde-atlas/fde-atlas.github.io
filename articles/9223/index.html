Scale AI FDE Salary 2026

FDE Pulse · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

Summary

This article compiles Scale AI's legally posted base-salary bands for FDE-related roles, sourced from its own job board in November 2026. The median base across posted bands is about $241K. Frontier Agents Engineers in San Francisco are banded at $180K–$225K, while a Forward Deployed Software Engineer role in Austin ranges from $184K to $293K. Senior and Staff Frontier Agents Engineer roles reach $270K and $315K respectively. All figures are base salary only; equity and bonus, which most of these roles also offer, would push total compensation higher. The article rates the data confidence as high since it comes directly from employer-posted pay bands, and also lists open forward-deployed product roles in London and enterprise/public-sector positions.

Key points

  • Median posted base salary is about $241K
  • Austin Forward Deployed Software Engineer band: $184K–$293K
  • Staff Frontier Agents Engineer tops out at $315K
  • Figures exclude equity and bonus
  • Data from Scale AI's own postings, November 2026, high confidence
