FDE Interviews: platform overview

InterviewYoda · 2026-10-11 · 1 min read

Summary

This article is an overview of FDE Interviews, a premium interview-prep platform by InterviewYoda aimed at candidates for Forward Deployed Engineer, Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, and adjacent solutions or ML roles. As of 9 November 2026, the library contains 678 worked interview questions, 176 lessons across 5 courses, 210 video lessons, 30 hands-on browser-based FDE Lab missions, 201 explained research papers, and 112 company interview guides. Content spans 11 topics including LLM and RAG fundamentals, agent system design, coding, MLOps, GPU infrastructure, AI security, and behavioral and customer rounds. Some answers and lessons are free; Premium unlocks all videos, lab missions, 12 PDF guides, a practice-test bank, and mastery tracking. FDE Interviews Premium costs $256 per month, while an All-Access Pass covering all 8 InterviewYoda platforms costs $69 for 12 months.

Key points

  • Platform targets FDE, Applied AI Engineer, and transitioning solutions or ML engineers.
  • Library includes 678 worked questions, 210 videos, and 112 company guides.
  • 30 FDE Lab missions run in a browser terminal.
  • Topics span LLMs, RAG, agents, evals, GPUs, MLOps, and AI security.
  • Premium is $256/month standalone; All-Access Pass is $69 for 12 months.
