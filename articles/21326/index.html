Summary

This is a job posting from Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu LLC in Japan for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) focused on financial services (FSI). The role involves working on-site at banks, securities firms, and insurers to solve challenges in operations reform, risk management, credit, and regulatory compliance using code and data, supporting projects from PoC through production deployment. The posting details responsibilities (client-facing problem discovery, AI/data solution development, data platform building), requirements (financial domain knowledge, DX project experience, generative AI development experience, native-level Japanese), and compensation: annual salary of 6.5 to 20 million yen, locations in Tokyo or Osaka, remote work allowed, 40 hours of fixed overtime included, and a 6-month probation period. The page also includes company background, values, an employee interview, and benefits information.

  • Job Description
  • The Financial Services Industry (FSI) practice provides end-to-end support to financial institutions—including banks, securities firms, and insurers—covering everything from strategy development to technology implementation and risk/regulatory response. This position is for a "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" who will play a central role in this practice. The mission of an FSI FDE is to solve the complex challenges financial institutions face—such as operational reform, risk management, credit, and regulatory compliance—using "code and data," while supporting clients from PoC through production operation. The work goes beyond simply implementing AI and other solutions: at the client's business front lines, the FDE identifies issues specific to each company, translates them into implementable requirements, and drives results through rapid build-and-verify cycles. In financial businesses that deal with intangible products in particular, data and algorithms form the very core of the business, so FDEs are expected to drive transformation at the heart of financial business and generate sustained, significant business impact. Specific responsibilities are envisioned as follows: (1) Understanding on-the-ground issues and building client relationships • Interviewing and observing to understand financial institutions' business challenges • Day-to-day collaboration and trust-building with clients' business and IT departments • Translating technical content and supporting decision-making • Adjusting project scope and priorities (together with the client) (2) Solution development • Designing and implementing solutions leveraging AI/data • Designing and executing PoCs using generative AI and machine learning models • Deploying and designing operations for systems that can withstand production use • Building reusable components and practices • Advancing prototyping, implementation, verification, and improvement in short cycles, supporting clients until results are achieved (3) Data platform construction and maintenance • Designing and building pipelines that integrate internal and external structured and unstructured data • Building analytics and AI platforms with data quality, governance, and other safeguards • Building scalable architectures on the cloud. The goal is not merely to implement technology to solve problems, but to shift toward business models with future corporate value enhancement in mind; the FDE provides feedback from an on-the-ground implementation perspective on the transformation scenarios envisioned by Deloitte Strategy (DS) and clients, and drives value creation in collaboration with FDEs in other domains as needed.
