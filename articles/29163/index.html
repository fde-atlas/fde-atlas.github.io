Process-Mapping

Original title: Process-Mapping

FDE中国社区 · 2026-07-12 · 4 min read

Summary

This article documents Process-Mapping, a skill in the FDE China community's methodology library for mapping a client's current business processes and marking where AI should intervene. Its core claim: without understanding real workflows and exception handling, AI only covers the happy path and frontline staff won't adopt it. The method uses on-site shadowing and swimlane diagrams, marking exception branches, time costs, and error rates, producing an As-Is process map, AI intervention points, an exception list, and a non-automatable list. It details execution steps, common pitfalls, stage gates, and role division among FDE, AIBP, and business owners. A finance reimbursement case raised Beta adoption from 12% to 58%. The downloadable package includes checklist, evaluation, and prompt files for Agent Skills-compatible tools.

Key points

  • Unmapped real processes mean AI covers only the happy path and gets rejected by frontline users.
  • Method combines job shadowing with swimlane diagrams, marking exceptions, time costs, and error rates.
  • Deliverables include As-Is process maps, AI intervention points, exception lists, and time baselines.
  • Pitfalls: drawing only the main path, missing human-review points, excluding frontline confirmation.
  • Case study: finance reimbursement automation lifted Beta adoption from 12% to 58%.

Problem definition

Without a clear understanding of the real process and exception handling, AI can only cover the ideal path, and frontline staff won't adopt it.

Methodology framework

Output: As-Is process diagram + pain point annotations + AI intervention points + non-automatable list. Method: on-site job shadowing, swimlane diagrams, exception branches, time-cost/error-rate annotations.

FAQ

Q1: When executing [Process-Mapping], how do you avoid "only drawing the main path and ignoring exceptions"?

A: Follow the README methodology step by step and present interim deliverables in the weekly Demo; if PoC materials are incomplete, first produce a "to-be-validated list" before proceeding.

Q2: What additional constraints apply in private deployment/Xinchuang/MLPS environments?

A: Specify the deployment model in the prompt constraints section; when data cannot leave the domain, domestically produced components, or audit logs are involved, reference Private-Deployment-Gateway and RBAC-Audit accordingly.

Q3: What if the division of responsibilities with the AIBP is unclear?

A: Return to the AIBP-Collaboration-Playbook to update the RACI; the AIBP owns Ground Truth and business acceptance, while the FDE owns deliverable technical assets.

Q4: After the PoC passes, how do you transition to Beta/production?

A: Annotate the next-stage Skill in the deliverables (e.g., RAG-Evaluation→Private-Deployment-Gateway→FDE-Adoption-Growth), and trigger the launch gate via the checklist.
