India FDE Hiring Demand and Entry Barriers

Original title: 印度 FDE 招聘需求与入行门槛

FDE Times · 2026-10-23 · 5 min read

Summary

The article examines India's FDE job market: Indeed data shows job postings grew nearly 19-fold year over year in 2026, peaking in June, while CIEL HR estimates only about 1,200 fully qualified candidates exist. Demand concentrates in Bangalore, Delhi-NCR, and Hyderabad, which together account for nearly 80% of openings. Comparing postings from NTT DATA, GlobalLogic, and HuntingCube, the article shows employers have not uniformly lowered the bar: senior roles require 5-10 years of experience plus client-facing work and production evals, while HuntingCube accepts 1-3 years, counting internships and personal LLM API projects. Salaries range from ₹35-45 lakh for junior roles to ₹70-90 lakh for experts. TCS aims to have 1-1.5% of staff qualify as FDEs, suggesting internal transitions may be the main entry path. The author advises candidates to build LLM API projects with documented eval results as evidence.

Key Takeaways

  • According to Indeed data, FDE job postings in India grew nearly 19x year-over-year in 2026, peaking in June.
  • GlobalLogic requires 5-10 years of experience, NTT DATA typically requires 8+ years; a HuntingCube role in Bangalore asks for only 1-3 years, counting internships and personal LLM projects.
  • Both senior job postings explicitly mention customer-facing work and production evals; newcomers should master these two skills early.

Nearly 19x. According to Indeed data cited by People Matters, that's how much job postings for Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) in India grew in 2026 compared with the same period the previous year. Postings peaked in June and remained well above year-ago levels through August.

Supply is far thinner. CIEL HR research estimates that only about 1,200 people in India can fully perform this role, and even counting those with adjacent skills, the number is only about 6,000.

With demand surging and available talent scarce, the key question for job seekers is whom companies are selecting and on what evidence. A close reading of open positions shows: while this market is short on people, it has not lowered standards across the board. There is a door open for those with fewer years of experience, while senior roles come with very specific requirements.

Demand Is Concentrated in Three Cities

According to Indeed data, FDE jobs in India are spread across six location categories: Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, New Delhi, Mumbai, and remote. The list looks scattered, but CIEL HR data shows demand is far more concentrated. Bangalore accounts for nearly half of annual FDE demand, followed by Delhi-NCR and Hyderabad; together, the three account for nearly 80% of postings.

Specific postings confirm this. NTT DATA is hiring an FDE in Bangalore. GlobalLogic has opened a hybrid role based in Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Noida, with Noida part of Delhi-NCR. The HuntingCube posting is located in East Bangalore.

Job seekers outside India who are eyeing remote roles should read each posting carefully before applying. A role labeled remote may only mean no office attendance is required, not that the company is hiring in all countries. The fastest way to find out is to ask the recruiter directly about residency and contract type requirements in your first message.

Three Postings, Three Bars

Employers cited by People Matters say they need engineers who can sit with customers, clarify real problems, and make technology work in practice. Open roles span multiple domains: enterprise AI, data engineering, process automation, infrastructure, and healthcare.

However, once these needs are written into job postings, the bars set by different companies vary.

NTT DATA's posting leans clearly toward consulting. It wants people accustomed to working with enterprise customers who can carry work from requirements discovery all the way to prototyping. GlobalLogic's posting leans toward platform work and is tied to a specific tech stack: Google Cloud and Vertex AI.

HuntingCube's posting is the most notable exception. It requires only 1-3 years of software engineering experience, recognizes internships at product companies, and counts projects using LLM APIs as experience, even side projects done outside working hours. For those early in their careers, this is a real opportunity.

Senior Roles: Customers and Evals Are Hard Requirements

Both senior postings point to the same thing. NTT DATA requires practical knowledge of generative AI, agent systems, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), plus familiarity with the issues involved in running systems in production, such as evals, security, and observability. GlobalLogic states directly in the job description that FDEs must build automated eval loops measuring model performance, drift, and accuracy.

Why would a customer-facing role list evals among its responsibilities? Because evals are how an FDE proves to customers that a system is working correctly, and they let the FDE catch problems as soon as the system starts to degrade.

Imagine an FDE deploying a RAG assistant at a hospital to answer questions about internal procedures. The engineer writes 50 questions with reference answers, reruns them after every prompt change or model swap, and records how many are answered correctly.

If 41 are answered correctly the first time and only 37 after a prompt change, the engineer immediately knows the system just got worse, before any doctor notices.

Reading the three postings in order of required years of experience reveals a career path. At the 1-3 year stage, projects calling LLM APIs already count as experience; to reach the 5-10 year level, the postings make it clear: candidates must know how to measure and must be able to stand in front of customers and defend those numbers.

Will the Talent Shortage Loosen the Bar?

Not necessarily. CIEL HR says demand is strongest for people with 5-8 years of experience, and both the NTT DATA and GlobalLogic roles fall in or above that range. Salary gaps between levels are also large: junior FDE roles pay ₹3.5-4.5 million (₹35-45 lakh) annually, while experienced specialists can reach ₹7-9 million (₹70-90 lakh).

With so few available candidates, a reasonable option for companies is to develop FDEs from existing staff. The TCS CEO's remarks suggest at least one large company is thinking in this direction.

He said the company wants up to 1%-1.5% of its employees to qualify as FDEs, which by Business Today's estimate means roughly 5,900 to 8,900 people. The outlet also noted it remains unclear whether this refers to new hiring or retraining existing employees.

Either way, the signal for engineers at large services firms is clear: the path to becoming an FDE may be an internal transfer rather than a job hop. If their company is looking to fill this role, engineers should sign up early and apply to join projects with direct customer exposure.

Turning Posting Requirements into Application Materials

This market values evidence, not titles. Engineers with 1-3 years of experience should build a project based on LLM APIs that solves a real problem for real users, pair it with an eval set, and document the before-and-after numbers for every improvement in the README.

The LLM API part maps to the experience explicitly recognized in HuntingCube's posting; the evals part prepares in advance for the requirement GlobalLogic spells out at the 5-10 year level.

For people with 5-8 years of experience, the weak spot is usually on the customer side, not the technical side. Rewrite your resume so that every project makes three things clear: what problem you clarified with the customer, what prototype you built, and how the results were measured.

The "discovery and prototyping" mentioned in the NTT DATA posting should appear on your resume, backed by real examples.

When reading senior job postings, watch for two signals: whether it clearly states who newcomers will collaborate with on the customer side, and whether it mentions evals or observability. A senior role with neither may just be an old engineering position dressed up with a trendy title.

1,200 qualified candidates facing a market that has grown nearly 19-fold—these openings are likely to persist for a while. The people who ultimately fill them won't necessarily be those with the longest years of experience, but those who can produce eval numbers and articulate how they sat down with customers to solve problems.
