What a Forward Deployed Engineer actually does

A Forward Deployed Engineer sits between a platform and the customer deploying it. The work is real engineering — production code, integrations, data modelling — but it happens inside the customer's problem rather than inside a product roadmap. Palantir made the title well known; AI labs and enterprise platforms adopted it as frontier models moved from demonstrations into production systems.

The defining feature is proximity. An FDE is embedded with the account: on their systems, in their meetings, against their deadlines. That produces a much shorter feedback loop than product engineering and a much higher tolerance for ambiguity, because the specification is usually discovered rather than handed over.

It is not a support role and not a pre-sales role, though it borders both. Most FDE postings expect production code and carry no quota — which is the clearest test when a title is ambiguous.

Day to day

  • Building integrations and pipelines against a customer's existing stack
  • Prototyping a workflow with a customer team, then hardening what survives
  • Turning a vague business problem into something the platform can do
  • Feeding recurring customer needs back to product as real requirements
  • Embedded or on-site work, with travel that varies sharply by employer

What hiring teams screen for

  • Python
  • TypeScript or another application language
  • SQL and data modelling
  • API and integration design
  • Distributed systems fundamentals
  • Cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure)
  • LLM application patterns, increasingly
  • Working directly with customer stakeholders

Common questions

  • Is a Forward Deployed Engineer a real engineering job?
  • For most postings, yes — they expect production code, not configuration. The title does get applied loosely, which is the reason this board scores every posting for coding intensity and customer-facing time instead of trusting the title.
  • How much travel does an FDE role involve?
  • It ranges from none to most weeks, and the title tells you nothing. Travel expectations are extracted from each posting into a band, so you can filter to what you will actually accept.
  • What is the difference between an FDE and a Solutions Architect?
  • An FDE builds; a Solutions Architect designs and reviews. In practice the two overlap heavily, and some employers use the titles interchangeably — so the scores on each posting are more reliable than the title on it.
  • Do FDE roles pay like product engineering roles?
  • At most employers they are benchmarked against the equivalent product engineering level, and some add a variable component. Where a posting publishes a range, it is shown on that job's page.
