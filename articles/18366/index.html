Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) and their counterparts, Deployment Strategists (DSs) — a hands-on, five-session program that develops both "wheels" of the FDE model

Foundry Labs, Inc. (Headquarters: Tokyo, CTO: Declan Crowley) has begun offering its hands-on training program "FDE Excellence Program," which supports the launch and development of FDE organizations, starting May 1, 2026. While the establishment of FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer: an engineer who embeds within customer organizations and drives AI through to production deployment) organizations is rapidly advancing among Japanese enterprise AI companies, no established methodology for developing them exists. This program addresses that challenge, and as its first engagement, all five sessions have been completed with the Ai Workforce Division of LayerX, Inc., a pioneer of the FDE model in Japan.

Background: The FDE Model Has "Another Half"

The FDE model — in which engineers embed themselves inside customer organizations and deploy AI all the way to production — is becoming the standard among enterprise AI companies worldwide. Yet most attempts to imitate this model fail for the same reason: the model has a lesser-known "other half." The Deployment Strategist (DS) is responsible for identifying "which problems are worth solving" before any code is written. The FDE Excellence Program is one of the few training programs in Japan that develops both wheels — FDEs and DSs — as a unified whole.

Service Overview

  • Service name: FDE Excellence Program
  • Format: Five workshop sessions in total (in-person or remote / English with simultaneous interpretation, or bilingual)
  • Audience: FDEs, DSs, and customer-facing teams at enterprise AI companies (expected size: 8–15 participants)
  • Content: "Murder board" — a review method that deliberately attacks one's own plan before customer proposals / running steering committees / role design for DSs and FDEs / KPI discovery / knowledge management that compounds as an asset / hiring methods for deployment talent
  • Methodology: Participants' real projects (appropriately anonymized) are used as training material, and each session produces practical deliverables that teams keep
  • Pricing: Please contact us (individual quotes based on team size)
  • Service page: https://foundrylabs.co.jp/fde-excellence

The program's methodology was developed by Crowley based on the experience he gained over six years as a Deployment Strategist at Palantir Technologies, the company where the FDE model originated, working in the field across healthcare, defense, and manufacturing.

First Engagement: LayerX Ai Workforce Division

LayerX was one of the first companies in Japan to advocate for and practice the FDE model. Its Ai Workforce Division, which handles enterprise AI adoption for major corporations, has built a dedicated FDE organization and has actively communicated the model's significance through blog posts, podcasts, and speaking engagements. Adopting this program was a step for a team already on the front lines to further deepen its capabilities in the DS — the model's other half. Members with backgrounds in consulting and data science completed all five sessions. In addition, a course report (note) by participating member Hayato Tajima and a conversation video between Crowley and Nakamura Tatsuya, CEO of the Ai Workforce Division (Executive Officer), titled "How Does the FDE Model Differ Between Japan and Globally?" (YouTube, Japanese subtitles) have been published.

Conversation video (YouTube): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1dGkfuciTw

Comments

Declan Crowley, CTO, Foundry Labs, Inc.: "It is only natural that the FDE model is being imitated around the world. And in Japan, no one has made the case for this model more compellingly than LayerX. But what most imitators overlook is its quiet other half — the Deployment Strategist. In my experience, it is the DS that determines whether you 'deliver the right thing' or merely 'deliver something.' LayerX understood this from the start. It shows in the organization they are building now."

Tatsuya Nakamura, Executive Officer and CEO of the Ai Workforce Division, LayerX, Inc.: While the concept of the FDE has become widely known, its counterpart, the DS, is rarely discussed, and I feel the differences between DSs and consultants or salespeople are still not well understood. However, unless a DS can define the right issues and strategy through dialogue with the customer, even the best implementation will not translate into outcomes. For LayerX as it strives toward this ideal of the DS, being able to learn directly from global cases and experience was an extremely valuable opportunity.

Future Plans

Starting today, Foundry Labs is accepting inquiries both in Japan and overseas from AI companies building deployment organizations. Multiple sessions are planned for 2026.
