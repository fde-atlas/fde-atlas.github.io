Buyer's guide6 questions

What does an AI consulting company do?

It decides with you where AI should do work in your company, then builds it: an agent that reads documents, a model that forecasts demand, an assistant that answers customers. The useful ones put it into production on your real data and stay until your staff use it. The rest hand over a strategy and a slide deck.

How much does AI consulting cost in the USA?

None of the ten ranked firms prints a dollar figure. Provectus prints the structure: a one week fixed fee Sprint, an 8 to 14 week build priced by milestone, then fees tied to outcomes. Ask every firm for the price of the first phase in writing before a second meeting.

Big firm or specialist?

Deloitte, IBM Consulting and Cognizant bring large benches and a name a board recognises. Provectus, 66degrees, Caylent and Pariveda are smaller and closer to the build. A company of 50 to 1,000 people usually needs a handful of people who write code, not a programme office.

Why do AI pilots stall before production?

Usually because the pilot ran on clean sample data and the real data sits in three systems that disagree, or because nobody owns the system after launch. That is why this ranking weights who builds it and what happens at go live above everything else.

Does the cloud platform matter?

Yes. Caylent works on AWS and has a dedicated Anthropic practice, and 66degrees is built around Google Cloud. If you have already chosen a platform, pick a firm that knows it; if you have not, ask the firm to justify the one it recommends.

Should the firm be in my city?

It helps when the work needs people in the room. Slalom lists 54 local offices, Pariveda lists offices and leaders by US city, and Tiger Analytics prints offices in Santa Clara, Jersey City, Chicago and Plano. Ask where the engineers who build your system sit.

How to choose

  • 1This week, write down the one AI result you want live in 90 days, in a sentence a member of staff would understand, such as "quotes drafted from the inbound email and waiting in the CRM by 9am". If you can't write it, you need an audit before a consulting company.
  • 2List the systems that result touches: the CRM, the accounting system, the inbox, the spreadsheet someone keeps. Then check whether they hold the same customer under the same name. If they don't, the first job is joining them up, and any firm that skips it will build on bad data.
  • 3Take a baseline before anyone builds: hours a week, error rate, or time to reply. Provectus builds its whole fee model around that baseline; ask every other firm to measure against one too.
  • 4Ask each firm for the price of the first phase in writing, how long it runs, and what you have at the end of it: working software or a document.
  • 5Ask who owns the code, the prompts, the agents and the cloud accounts when you leave. Provectus commits to handing over every line of code at go live; get the same sentence from whoever you hire.
  • 6Ask who builds it, by name, and where they sit. 66degrees sells forward deployed engineers who work inside your business; at the larger firms, check that the people on the sales call are the people on the build.
  • 7If the pilot already failed once because your systems don't talk to each other, that is when you bring in a forward deployed engineer rather than a consulting team.
