Summary

This OpenWork page is a job posting from Japan's SUPER STUDIO for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE). The company runs an AI commerce platform, ecforce, and pairs it with an FDE model for enterprise DX: FDEs embed in client operations, use ecforce, generative AI tools (ClaudeCode, Cursor), and iPaaS tools (Make, Zapier) to run quick BPR pilots, drive change management, and feed system development needs back to product teams. Requirements include 2-3 years of BPR or IT implementation experience, client-facing work, and hands-on LLM use; programming is not required but API and SQL basics are. Salary ranges from 8 to 20 million yen with 45 hours of fixed overtime pay, flextime, and remote work. The page also covers company values, a founder message, and an interview with a mid-career hire from a system integrator.

Key points

  • SUPER STUDIO is hiring an FDE with salary of 8-20 million yen.
  • The role uses ecforce and generative AI to drive client-side BPR.
  • Requirements: 2-3 years BPR/IT experience; coding not required.
  • The company runs a SaaS plus FDE business model for enterprise DX.
  • The page includes company values and a career-change interview.

  • Maximizing the customer experience for everyone involved in things and experiences
  • SUPER STUDIO Inc. has the mission of "maximizing the customer experience for everyone involved in things and experiences," and develops and provides the AI commerce platform "ecforce" as well as offering consulting services as business support. Since its founding in 2014, the company has applied the expertise gained through its own D2C business to product development, supporting the promotion of commerce DX from both the system and business perspectives. ■About ecforce ecforce aims to optimize the entire business through commerce DX by quickly adopting cutting-edge technological innovations such as AI. In addition to marketing optimization and strengthening sales channels, it offers a wide range of products specialized in commerce business—from AI-driven data utilization to operational improvement and execution—creating an environment where teams can focus on highly specialized decision-making and creative initiatives. ■About Consulting Through "New Normal SI," which combines SaaS and SI, we solve structural challenges in commerce businesses. From upstream strategy design to system implementation and on-site operations, we provide hands-on support with agile processes and reliable know-how based on our own brand management experience.

  • Employee Interview: Mid-Career Hire
  • Why did you choose a SaaS startup over a system integrator? ■About your previous job At a system integrator (Sler), I served as a PG/SE as well as a PM, managing the progress of various projects. ■What prompted your career change My previous workplace had a good environment and was easy to work in, but I couldn't envision continuing the same work for decades. Also, since SIers fundamentally work on client projects, there was the dilemma of being unable to implement better ideas due to deadlines and budgets. ■What convinced you to move from a Sler to SaaS I found the business model of "ecforce" attractive: although it is a to-B product, improving the service enhances the end user's customer experience, and increased sales benefit both clients and SUPER STUDIO. It is a business model with high social value, and I empathized with the "good for all three parties" philosophy. I was also impressed by how energized and motivated everyone seemed in their work. ■About your position when you joined Initially, I was offered a PM position handling data migration and similar work, but as we talked, the conversation turned to whether a PdM position—which had not yet been established at the time—might be a better fit. I was deeply impressed by how quickly they created a position tailored to the skills I wanted to acquire and what I wanted to do, and by their stance of not just seeking immediately productive talent but thinking about growing together, so I decided to take on the PdM role. ■What you feel after actually working here Employees and executives communicate on a flat basis with no barriers between them, showing genuine interest in one another—you can really tell everyone loves people. Since it's a startup, there are still areas where we're finding our way, but there are endless opportunities to make things better, so you can approach your work with your own vision. For those who feel stuck at a system integrator, I recommend this environment—it's full of stimulation.
  • Job Title
  • FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)
  • Job Description
  • ■ Business and Service Overview To establish our position as "the first company that comes to mind for AI × Commerce," we are driving DX support for large enterprises (enterprises) centered on our own AI commerce platform "ecforce" as a pillar of our company-wide strategy. We are promoting a SaaS × FDE business model: advanced commerce operations and AI utilization are realized through ecforce, while for complex internal business challenges that ecforce cannot fully cover, FDE-driven AI × BPR strongly transforms the entire business from the front lines. ■ Job Description You will work embedded in the front lines of large enterprise clients, driving business process reform (BPR) in the commerce domain (supply chain, customer support, marketing, etc.) by leveraging ecforce features, generative AI, and no-code tools. 1. Identifying on-site challenges and visualizing business processes: Deeply understand the client's front-line operations and identify bottlenecks—where inefficiencies exist and how they can be dramatically improved through ecforce adoption and generative AI implementation. 2. Quick BPR validation using generative AI × ecforce: Combine ecforce's standard features and API settings, generative AI tools (ClaudeCode, Cursor), and iPaaS (Make, Zapier, etc.) to build and validate "automation workflows that work immediately in the field" with your own hands. 3. Embedding changes in the field (change management) and feeding back to the product: Provide total support for embedding the AI workflows you create and new ecforce operations, from training to hands-on accompaniment. Furthermore, feed back full-scale system development needs and common issues surfaced through field validation to our in-house product development team and AI specialist team, driving product evolution. ■ What Makes This Position Attractive 1. A hybrid career of "consulting" and "FDE (tech utilization)": You will acquire the skills to master generative AI and SaaS mechanisms and dramatically transform the operations of the clients in front of you. 2. The satisfaction of leading cutting-edge "AI × BPR" without coding: By leveraging the solid foundation of our own products and AI agents, you can turn your ideas into customer value instantly—experience a true sense of speed. 3. Major business impact possible only with SaaS × FDE: Because we have a SaaS foundation, we can boldly and rapidly drive transformations that deliver real value to client companies.
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • ■ Required Qualifications ・Project experience in business process reform (BPR) or IT implementation (approx. 2-3+ years): Practical experience at a consulting firm, SIer, or in the DX promotion/business improvement division of a company ・Client-facing work experience: Practical experience in requirements definition, issue extraction, and proposals/negotiations with clients ・Business experience with or strong interest in generative AI: Experience using and validating LLMs such as ChatGPT in practical work (prompt building, simple automation using iPaaS, etc.) ・Basic IT knowledge: Programming experience is not required, but a basic understanding of IT concepts such as APIs, data structures, and SQL (and no aversion to them) is essential ■ Preferred Qualifications ・Domain knowledge in EC/D2C, retail/distribution, or supply chain, or consulting/business improvement experience in these industries ・Experience implementing and configuring various SaaS tools and improving business efficiency through API integrations ・Facilitation skills to engage multiple stakeholders, such as client front-line teams and internal development teams
