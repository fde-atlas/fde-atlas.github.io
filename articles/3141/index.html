Plank Research

Plank · 2026-06-14 · 1 min read

Summary

Plank Research publishes first-party research on forward-deployed engineering. Its catalog includes dossiers on six major FDE players—OpenAI's Deployment Company, Anthropic's Ode, Microsoft Frontier, AWS FDE, Distyl AI, and Palantir—covering metrics, leadership, and org structure. A 2026 report traces how FDE grew from a Palantir job title into a multi-billion-dollar category in under a year. Other resources include a census of open FDE roles across hundreds of companies, filterable by vertical, funding stage, region, and salary; per-company profiles with tech stacks and hiring signals from job descriptions; and an open-source LangGraph multi-agent template combining memory, vision, planning, and spatial reasoning to solve LS20, the first game in the ARC-AGI-3 benchmark.

Key points

  • Company dossiers cover six leading FDE players, including OpenAI, Anthropic's Ode, and Palantir.
  • A 2026 report says FDE became a multi-billion-dollar category in under a year.
  • A first-party census maps open FDE roles by vertical, funding stage, region, and salary.
  • Per-company profiles mine job descriptions for tech stacks and hiring signals.
  • An open-source LangGraph template targets LS20, the first ARC-AGI-3 game.
