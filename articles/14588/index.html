Do forward deployed engineer jobs sponsor visas?

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-03 · 13 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch hand-classified 651 forward deployed engineer and deployment strategist postings from an October 24, 2026 public-board snapshot to see how many address visa sponsorship. Only 28 postings state a stance: 13 say they sponsor, 14 say they cannot, and 1 (Render) allows transfers only. Another 46 require citizenship, permanent residence, or U.S. Person status for export control, federal, or clearance reasons. The remaining 623 (95.7%) are silent. Employers stating sponsorship include Anthropic, Reflection AI, n8n, Scale AI, and Sim; those declining include Accenture, C3 AI, Runpod, Tagup, Nearform, 84.51°, and Workday. The article advises treating silence as unknown, targeting employers that explicitly sponsor, and asking recruiters early.

Key points

  • 95.7% of 651 postings state no visa sponsorship stance.
  • Of 28 explicit postings: 13 sponsor, 14 do not, 1 transfer-only.
  • 46 postings require citizenship or residency for export control or clearance.
  • Anthropic, Reflection AI, n8n, Scale AI, and Sim say they sponsor.
  • Advice: treat silence as unknown and ask recruiters early.

What the wording means

Sponsors means the posting says the employer sponsors visas. That includes Anthropic's "We do sponsor visas" with a reasonable-effort caveat, Reflection AI's sponsorship-support benefit line, Sim's short "We sponsor visas," n8n's Germany-only sponsorship, and Scale AI Qatar seats that say Scale will support the visa application process (issuance stays with Qatari authorities).

No sponsorship means the posting states sponsorship is not available, or requires work authorization that does not now or in the future need sponsorship. Nearform, 84.51°, Accenture US seats, Workday, Runpod, and Tagup use that pattern. C3 AI federal seats combine it with a U.S. citizenship requirement.

Transfer only or case by case covers wording like Render's: generally not eligible for new visa sponsorship, while existing transfers may be sponsored at discretion. That posting was closed when live-checked for this page; the quoted phrase is from the October text pull.

Citizenship or export control is separate from visa policy. It covers U.S. Person / citizenship lines for export-controlled technology (Cloudflare, CoreWeave, Saronic, Analog Devices, Tomorrow.io), federal or clearance citizenship (Databricks public sector, Salesforce Missionforce, Booz Allen, Palantir green-card-or-citizen, Defense Unicorns), and Together AI's Singapore citizen or permanent resident line. Export-license sponsorship language is counted in this category. It is not visa sponsorship. Clearance sponsorship is also excluded from the visa-sponsorship counts.

What the keyword seed also hits

Of the 154 keyword matches, 89 are noise only: the text matches a seed term, but the posting does not state a visa sponsorship stance or a citizenship / export-control requirement. Common noise in this pull:

  • EEO boilerplate that lists citizenship as a protected class (46 noise-only postings carry that language).
  • Accenture "citizen services" marketing copy (24 noise-only postings).
  • Executive sponsor, CEO-sponsored initiative, or C-suite-sponsored opportunity language (18), including Databricks deployment-strategist seats and Cursor Forward Deployed Strategist seats.
  • Visa the payments company or Visa Ventures in customer or investor lists (5).
  • Nebius work-authorization eligibility lines that do not state a sponsorship stance (5).
  • Parloa E-Verify / I-9 employment-authorization boilerplate (4).
  • Company-sponsored 401(k) or team events, PE deal sponsors, and product "sponsorship measurement" language.

Practical next steps if you need sponsorship

Ask the recruiter early, before a full interview loop. Target employers that state they sponsor in the posting. Treat silence as unknown. A blank posting is not a refusal: 95.7% of postings in this corpus never state a sponsorship stance. Read citizenship and export-control lines carefully; those seats may be closed to most sponsored candidates even when the same employer sponsors other roles. For location context on where seats sit, see Forward Deployed Engineer jobs by location. For how early-career seats read experience, see Entry-level Forward Deployed Engineer jobs.

Official U.S. government overviews of nonimmigrant employment categories are published by USCIS (Working in the United States). Host-country rules outside the U.S. are set by that country's authorities. This page does not interpret either for an individual case.

Method and data

Population: 566 engineer-title posting texts pulled October 25, 2026 and 85 deployment-strategist posting texts pulled October 28, 2026 from the October 24, 2026 public-board FDE snapshot (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday). Management titles from that snapshot are not in this corpus. Keyword seed: visa, immigration, sponsor, work authorization, right to work, authorized to work, citizenship, citizen, green card, permanent resident. Every one of the 154 matches was hand-classified into sponsors, no_sponsorship, transfer_only_or_case_by_case, citizenship_or_export_control, noise, or a combination of non-noise labels.

Limits: keyword matching is approximate; hand review is what produces the published counts. Postings close; check the url before relying on a row. Employers that post only on LinkedIn, Indeed, or other applicant systems are missing. Evidence links were live-checked November 3, 2026; the Render transfer-only posting returned HTTP 404 and is labeled closed.

Questions people ask

Do forward deployed engineer jobs sponsor visas?

Most FDE postings in this corpus say nothing about visa sponsorship. Across 651 engineer and deployment-strategist posting texts, only 28 postings (4.3%) state a sponsorship stance after hand review: 13 say they sponsor (including country-limited or reasonable-effort wording), 14 say they do not sponsor, and 1 says new sponsorship is generally unavailable while transfers may be considered. Silence on a posting is not a no.

How many FDE postings mention visas or work authorization?

A keyword seed for visa, immigration, sponsor, work authorization, right to work, authorized to work, citizenship, citizen, green card, and permanent resident matched 154 of 651 postings (23.7%). After hand classification, 89 of those matches were noise only. 65 postings carried at least one non-noise policy label (sponsorship stance and/or citizenship or export-control wording).

Which employers say they sponsor FDE visas?

In this corpus, Anthropic (4 postings), Reflection AI (4), n8n (2, Germany-only), Scale AI (2 Qatar seats that say Scale will support the visa application process), and Sim (1) state that they sponsor or will support visas. Wording ranges from "We do sponsor visas" with reasonable-effort caveats to country-limited sponsorship.

What does U.S. citizenship or export-control language mean on an FDE posting?

46 postings require citizenship, permanent residence, or U.S. Person status for export-controlled technology, federal contracts, or clearance eligibility. That is a separate category from visa sponsorship. Export-license sponsorship language (for example Cloudflare) is counted as citizenship or export control. It is not visa policy. Clearance sponsorship is excluded from the visa-sponsorship counts.

What should a candidate who needs sponsorship do?

Ask the recruiter early, before investing in a full loop. Target employers that state they sponsor in the posting text. Treat silence as unknown. A blank posting is not a refusal. Read citizenship and export-control lines carefully: those seats may be closed to most sponsored candidates even when the employer sponsors other roles.
