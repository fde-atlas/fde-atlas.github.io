Summary

This is a promotional landing page for Hongshuo AI's FDE bootcamp, which frames the Forward Deployed Engineer as a hybrid business-plus-engineering role responsible for delivering AI systems into enterprises. It cites salary ranges of 20–70K RMB per month and claims 42x demand growth over two to three years, based on public job postings. The curriculum covers five skill areas—business problem decomposition, AI engineering, data integration, delivery management, and ROI measurement—taught by Ma Xinbo and Li Xiao. Training uses anonymized delivery cases from over 50 companies including Jingneng and SABIC, culminating in a sandbox delivery project and a Hongshuo FDE certification with three months of follow-up support. The page also profiles learners from diverse backgrounds and offers an enterprise program for companies to build in-house FDE teams. As marketing material, its claims lack cited sources.

FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer; a more fitting name in China would be "Enterprise AI Implementation Engineer". It's not a pure programmer, nor a pure business role—it's a hybrid role that is half business, half engineering.

FDEs go deep into the customer and business frontline, understand real problems, identify the leverage points where AI can be implemented, then use AI programming and toolchains to quickly build usable systems, complete delivery and iteration, and prove results with quantifiable metrics.

At leading AI companies like OpenAI and Anthropic, the FDE is the core role driving customer success and revenue growth; in China, it is exactly the key person who turns "bought AI but can't use it" into "AI truly running in the business".

01 Understand the business, break down real problems, and find the biggest leverage point for AI implementation.

02 Use AI programming and toolchains to quickly turn solutions into runnable, real systems.

03 Integrate with the enterprise's existing systems and data so AI truly plugs into business workflows.

04 Standardize delivery and control changes to ensure stable, maintainable, and iterable launches.

05 Prove results with quantifiable metrics and clearly communicate AI's real value to management.

Q: How is FDE training different from ordinary "AI programming training"?

AI programming training teaches "tools and skills"; FDE training teaches "role capabilities"—beyond writing code, you also learn to break down business needs, integrate systems, manage delivery, and calculate ROI. It's a complete set of composite skills for actually landing AI in enterprises.

Q: Can I learn this without a technical background / if I'm not a programmer?

Yes. FDE is inherently a "half business, half engineering" role. Business backbone staff, pre-sales, and project managers can fill in the engineering half through AI programming; likewise, developers can fill in the business and delivery half. The course offers paths designed for different starting points.

Q: What can I do after completing the course?

You can independently take on in-house AI implementation projects: running the full process from business research and solution design to system building, delivery, launch, and results reporting—becoming the key person in your company who "can actually put AI to work."

Q: What is the Hongshuo FDE certification for?

The certification is based on "completing the bootcamp + passing a real sandbox delivery," proving genuine delivery capability. It can be used for role transitions, internal promotions, and external credentialing. ⚠️ We only promise capability development, not employment outcomes.

Q: Can a company train its own FDEs in batches?

Yes. Hongshuo offers a B2B "co-building enterprise FDE roles" program—sending employees through the bootcamp plus on-the-job coaching with implementation support, helping enterprises build their own AI delivery teams in a turnkey fashion.
