Summary

A job posting from OpenWork for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role at Upgrade, a Japanese AI startup founded in 2021 by a University of Tokyo student. The company has about 57 employees, runs three businesses (AI consulting, AI products, reskilling), serves mostly large enterprises (over 90% have 1,000+ employees), and is an official Dify and Notion partner. The FDE owns engagements end to end: identifying business problems, designing and prototyping AI agents with Dify, LangGraph, n8n and LLM APIs, deploying to production, and measuring KPIs. Salary ranges from 6 to 11.99 million yen including 45 hours of fixed overtime. The office is in Gotanda, Tokyo, with mostly in-office work, flextime, and company-paid AI tools.

Key points

  • Upgrade is hiring an FDE to own enterprise AI projects from problem definition to production deployment.
  • Founded 2021, ~57 employees, over 90% of clients are enterprises with 1,000+ staff.
  • Stack includes Dify, LangGraph, n8n, LangChain and major LLM APIs; company is a Dify partner.
  • Salary: 6–11.99 million yen including 45 hours fixed overtime; flextime, mostly office-based in Gotanda, Tokyo.
  • Company covers all AI tool costs (Claude Code, ChatGPT, Cursor, etc.) and offers structured onboarding over six months.
