Summary

This is a job posting on OpenWork for a full-time Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) at JDSC, a University of Tokyo-born AI company focused on DX in healthcare, manufacturing, energy, and logistics. The FDE embeds in client worksites, leading problem discovery, design, implementation, and operational improvement of AI/LLM solutions even when requirements are undefined. Requirements are modest: two-plus years of development experience, with no FDE or AI background needed. Salary ranges from 5 to 25 million yen including 45 hours of fixed overtime. Benefits include LLM usage subsidies, Kaggle incentives, certification support, flextime, and remote work. The posting also features an interview with COO Asuka Sato.

Key points

  • JDSC is hiring an FDE to implement AI transformation at client sites.
  • The role leads AI/LLM solutions from vague requirements to production.
  • Only 2+ years of development experience required; AI experience optional.
  • Salary: 5–25 million yen with 45 hours fixed overtime included.
  • Perks include LLM subsidies, Kaggle rewards, flextime, and remote work.

Full text

  • About JDSC
  • We are an AI company spun out of the University of Tokyo with the mission of upgrading Japanese industries. ■ Solving industry-wide challenges with AI and data science JDSC is an AI tech company from the University of Tokyo that drives digital transformation (DX) across various industries, focusing on healthcare, manufacturing, energy, and logistics. We solve not only individual companies' challenges but also productivity issues across entire industries, achieving industrial collaboration. ■ End-to-end delivery from creating quantifiable impact to scaling into a business At JDSC, consultants, data scientists, and engineers work as an integrated team, providing end-to-end services from identifying client challenges to data analysis and AI development. Using AI and data science, we go beyond solving individual companies' problems to "solving industry-wide challenges (SDGs)" and "creating quantifiable impact."
  • Achieving both social and economic value to create new industry standards
  • The consulting market is growing. However, most of its content is aimed at realizing competitive strategies to increase individual companies' market share. JDSC works toward industrial collaboration to "solve social issues," driving industry transformation centered on AI and data science to achieve both social and economic value. A concrete example is the social implementation of AI that detects frailty from electricity data. JDSC holds the patent for this AI, and it is being introduced to municipalities through regional power companies.
  • The "trinity" approach enhances your market value
  • Typical consulting firms operate through "waterfall-style" processes and division of labor. This is because the consulting firms themselves have become enormous, and they aim to maximize the efficiency of their own business to leverage that scale. In contrast, JDSC aims to maximize output by having consultants directly form teams with professionals such as data scientists and engineers to drive transformation. As a result, you will also gain experience in acquiring data science and engineering skills yourself. We also offer robust learning support programs, enabling further career growth.
  • Build solutions and businesses, and create your own signature work
  • Building on Joint R&D with major corporations, JDSC aims to scale across entire industries through solutions and business development. For "frailty detection using electricity data," JDSC has turned its patented technology into an API and is planning to team up with leading companies in the industry to co-develop new services and implement them in society. In addition, seawise, a joint venture with Mitsui & Co., provides a SaaS service aiming to be a "family doctor" for ships in the maritime domain. The appeal of working at JDSC is that you can create solutions and businesses with significant social impact, and that they become your life-defining "signature work."
  • Interview: COO / Asuka Sato
  • Upgrading Japan's core industry, "manufacturing," with the power of data! Driving projects and strengthening the organization daily with expertise honed at a major consulting firm. My name is Asuka Sato. I joined JDSC in January 2020, so I am approaching four and a half years here. I am also apparently the sixth-longest-tenured member of the company. Currently, as an executive officer, I am responsible for the "Sales/Proposal Organization," the "Consulting Organization," and the "Manufacturing Solutions Organization." In addition, I serve as the business-side point of contact for recruiting and PR activities, promoting collaboration with dedicated staff. In 2024, consulting firms rose to such prominence that they dominated the top of employment rankings. I would like to talk about why I, with over 10 years of experience at such a consulting firm, chose JDSC as my place of work, and I hope this helps more people learn about JDSC.
  • Job Title
  • 【Dev】FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)【Full-time】
  • Job Description
  • ■ Your role You will contribute as a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) to implement AX (AI Transformation) not as a "concept" but as a "mechanism that works in the field." The FDE is a position that goes deep into the client's business operations and leads everything from problem discovery to design, implementation, and operational improvement with a high degree of autonomy. Your mission will be to determine "what to build" and "how far to build it" through dialogue with clients, starting from a state where requirements are not fully defined, and to embed AI/LLM-powered solutions into actual business operations. Specifically, you will be involved in tasks such as the following: ・Immersing yourself in the client's business operations to identify issues based on workflows, decision-making processes, and constraints ・Collaborating with business consultants and technology consultants to organize AI/LLM utilization policies and priorities from a field-level perspective ・Leading the conception and PoC development of AI solutions optimized for the client's business ・Quickly bringing prototypes to the field and driving them through to production implementation while incorporating feedback ・Continuing to engage with clients after deployment, leading improvements and enhancements in response to business changes and usage patterns ■ Career development highlights ・By taking on technical decision-making on the client's front lines, you can gain engineering experience that goes beyond just "building things." ・There is a culture that respects field-level judgment in technology selection and architecture design, allowing you to work with a high degree of autonomy. ・We have programs that support self-development investments, such as certification acquisition and book purchases. ・We provide an environment and support for actively using LLMs such as ChatGPT and Claude, as well as AI-driven development tools, in your work. ・We support flexible work styles, including flextime and parental leave systems. ■ Work style At JDSC, each member can realize diverse work styles suited to their lifestyle and values. As current practice shows, many people work in diverse ways tailored to their circumstances, such as primarily working from the office or remotely, taking parental leave, or using the flextime system. Other examples of benefits related to this position include the following. Since our founding, many people have valued the principle of "try it first," and as a result, the following have been realized: ・Book purchase reimbursement ・O'Reilly online learning ・Subsidies for business use of LLMs such as Claude and ChatGPT ・Subsidies for coding agents ・Support for obtaining Google Cloud and AWS certifications ・Support programs for working professionals pursuing master's and doctoral degrees
  • Required Experience and Skills
  • 【Required Skills】 ・At least 2 years of development experience as an engineer ・Experience organizing issues through dialogue with stakeholders and carrying them through to implementation, even when requirements are unclear ※ Experience with FDE or AI projects is not required. 【Preferred Skills】 ・Development experience using Python ・Experience developing and operating a variety of software (e.g., mobile, native, PWA, hardware, data platforms, etc.) ・Experience developing with LLMs and building RAG systems ・Experience developing in modern development environments ・Knowledge of AI agents ・Experience in development and improvement of business systems and business processes ・Experience driving projects while interacting directly with clients ・Experience in design and operation in cloud environments (AWS/GCP, etc.) ※ No specific educational background or certifications required. 【Ideal Candidate Profile】 ・Someone who resonates with our company mission, "Upgrade Japan" ・Someone interested in AX (AI Transformation) of large enterprises ・Someone who finds it exciting to explore, propose, and realize the ideal form of next-generation work powered by AI ・Someone who takes an interest in client companies' business domains and can solve problems from the client's perspective ・Someone who enjoys taking on 0-to-1 challenges
