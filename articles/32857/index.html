Forward deployed engineer pay in the US, by employer

Kernavique · 2026-11-20 · 21 min read

Summary

Kernavique collected 42 US job ads for forward deployed engineers from 31 employers, read on November 20, 2026, and grouped the published salary ranges by pay basis. The article stresses that the basis matters more than the range: 24 ads state the range is base salary, 16 say only "salary" without specifying, and 2 quote on-target earnings (OTE), which cannot be compared with base figures. The sample is a convenience sample, not a market measure. Among base-labeled ranges, the lowest figure is $88,970 (Salesforce) and the highest $376,000 (Figma); across all 42 ads, the lowest is $54,400 (Accenture, Maine) and the highest $405,000 (Anthropic's manager role). The piece also documents how ads disclose equity and bonus differently, and finds that the six ads requiring security clearances state no extra pay for holding one.

Key points

  • 42 ads from 31 employers, grouped into three pay bases that should not be compared across.
  • Base ranges run from $88,970 (Salesforce) to $376,000 (Figma).
  • Anthropic's manager role tops all ads at $320,000 to $405,000.
  • 30 ads mention equity, but none states a dollar figure.
  • Six clearance-requiring ads show no stated pay premium for clearances.

How to read these tables

Every figure below comes from an employer’s own job ad, read on November 20, 2026, on the employer’s careers page or its applicant tracking system. There are 42 ads from 31 employers. We found them by search and by re-checking the ads in our earlier article on how to hire a forward deployed engineer. It is a convenience sample, not a market measure.

The number that matters most is not the range. It is the pay basis, the thing the range counts. The ads use three:

  • Base. The ad itself says the range is base salary. Equity and bonus, where offered, come on top. 24 ads.
  • Salary, not stated as base. The ad says “salary”, “annual salary” or “estimated salary range” and stops there. It may or may not be base. 16 ads.
  • On-target earnings (OTE). The ad says the range is on-target earnings, which includes variable pay that depends on results. 2 ads.

A $270,000 OTE figure and a $270,000 base figure are different offers. So the tables are grouped by basis, and a range in one table should not be set against a range in another.

Three more limits. We read each ad as text, not in a browser. An open ad is not always a recent one: Palantir leaves postings open for years, and Baseten’s ad was first published on March 28, 2024. And two ads, from Accenture and Deloitte, carry an end date that has passed, though both pages still showed an Apply button on November 20, 2026. Treat those two as recently live.

What the ranges show

Everything here is arithmetic on the three tables. Nothing is weighted, and the sample was not drawn to represent the market.

  • Base ranges, 24 ads. The lowest figure is $88,970 (Salesforce, national range). The highest is $376,000 (Figma).
  • Any basis, 42 ads. The lowest figure is $54,400 (Accenture, Maine, not stated as base). The highest is $405,000 (Anthropic, Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering). For a role that is not management, the highest is Figma’s $376,000.
  • Tops and bottoms, 42 ads. The lowest top of any range is $145,000 (Palantir, New Grad). The highest bottom is $320,000 (Anthropic’s manager role). For a role that is not management it is $280,000, in two Anthropic ads.
  • Level, 3 ads. Scale AI publishes three levels of one role: $180,000 to $225,000 at 4+ years, $216,000 to $270,000 at 5+ years and $252,000 to $315,000 at 8+ years, all base.
  • Width, 1 ad. Figma’s $153,000 to $376,000 is the widest single range in the sample. It covers one title with no levels or locations broken out, so the range alone says little about a given offer.
  • Location, 5 ads. Four ads publish more than one US range by location: Scale AI’s Public Sector ad, Salesforce, JLL and Accenture. Cloudflare gives a range for New York City and New Jersey hires only.
  • Travel, 42 ads. 24 ads state travel in some form and 18 do not.

Ranges also hold steady. Eleven ads that we first read on October 11, 2026, showed the same range on November 20, 2026: three at Scale AI, four at Palantir, two at Anthropic, and one each at Databricks and Nebius.

One correction to our own work. Our October article said Anthropic listed the individual forward deployed engineer role only in Munich, Paris and London. That is out of date. Anthropic now advertises a Staff Forward Deployed Engineer in New York, San Francisco and Seattle at $280,000 to $320,000.

What the ads say about equity and bonus

The range is rarely the whole offer, and the ads say so in very different ways.

  • Equity, 42 ads. 30 mention equity, stock or restricted stock units, counting “may be eligible” wording and applicant tracking system labels. 12 do not: Nebius, the four Anthropic ads, Dataminr, JLL, Accenture, Deloitte, Guidehouse, Alation and Carlyle.
  • Bonus or variable pay, 42 ads. 17 mention a performance bonus, incentive or other variable pay. Four more, all Palantir, mention only a sign-on bonus.
  • No sales-linked pay, 1 ad. Buildkite says the role sits on its engineering bands, “base salary plus equity”, with nothing tied to what the customer spends.
  • Sales-style pay, 3 ads. Firecrawl and Together AI quote OTE. Alation publishes a 75/25 split between base and variable pay.

Anduril says equity grants are part of most full-time offers. Anthropic’s ads state no equity for these roles; the only equity reference is donation matching under benefits. In our read, no ad put a dollar figure on equity.

So the structure of the role shows in the pay wording. Where the ad talks about engineering bands, the role is paid like engineering. Where it quotes OTE or a base and variable split, part of the pay depends on results. Ask which one applies before comparing numbers.

Does a security clearance change the published range?

Not in a way these ads show. Six of the 42 ads require a security clearance or eligibility for one: Scale AI’s Public Sector ad, Palantir’s US Government ad, Dataminr and the three Anduril ads. One more, Guidehouse, requires a Federal Public Trust, which is not a security clearance. Uare.ai says an active clearance is a plus.

No ad states an added amount for holding a clearance. Three comparisons from the tables:

  • Palantir publishes $135,000 to $200,000 for its Washington, D.C. government ad and the same $135,000 to $200,000 for its New York commercial ad. Neither is stated as base.
  • Anduril’s Air Defense ad shows $113,000 to $155,000 base, with travel of up to two months at a time and up to 80% of the year. Its senior Strategic Defense ad shows $191,000 to $254,000 base.
  • Scale AI’s Public Sector ad tops out at $292,560 base in San Francisco, New York and Seattle, and at $219,420 in St. Louis.

Six ads are too few to say what clearance is worth. They do show that level and location move the range more visibly than the clearance line does.

Why US ads show a range at all

Several states and cities require it. These are the official pages we could open on November 20, 2026. This is a summary, not legal advice.

  • California. The Labor Commissioner says an employer with 15 or more employees must include the pay scale in any job posting, meaning a good-faith estimate of the salary or hourly wage range it expects to pay on hire (Labor Code section 432.3). The Commissioner reads the rule as covering any position that may be filled in California, including remotely. Bonuses and equity need not be in the posting.
  • New York State. Businesses with four or more employees must list the minimum and maximum annual salary or hourly pay they believe accurate when posting (Labor Law section 194-B).
  • New York City. Since November 1, 2022, employers must include a good-faith pay range in any ad for a job that would be performed in the city.
  • Washington State. Employers with 15 or more employees must include a wage scale or salary range, plus a general description of benefits and other compensation (Equal Pay and Opportunities Act).
  • Illinois. The rule covers an employer with 15 or more employees that makes or republishes a specific job posting after January 1, 2025. It asks for the wage or salary range plus a general description of benefits and other compensation.

The ads show the laws at work. Accenture publishes a separate range for each of 14 jurisdictions. Cloudflare publishes one only for New York City and New Jersey hires. Salesforce adds a second range for California, New York and named metropolitan areas. Those lists show where employers treat disclosure as required. They are not a legal source.

The California rule also explains the gap in the section above. The law asks for the salary range, not the equity or the bonus, so many ads stop at the range.

In practice

If you are an engineer comparing offers. Put each number on the same basis first: base, salary not stated as base, or OTE. Ask the employer which one its range is, and get the equity and bonus terms in writing, since the ad is not required to carry them. Check which location range applies to you; Scale AI’s Public Sector ad differs by $73,140 at the top between its highest and lowest location ranges. Our forward deployed engineer interview guide covers the interviews. If you want to be considered for roles we work on, send your details and résumé.

If you are a company benchmarking an offer. Use the base table, and match on level and location before you match on title. A range like $153,000 to $376,000 tells an engineer little; Scale AI’s three levels tell them a lot. Say in the ad whether the range is base, and say plainly whether variable pay applies. Our forward deployed engineer role page describes the role, and compensation benchmarking is the advisory engagement for building a range. What we left out

  • OpenAI. We could not re-read any of its forward deployed engineer ads on November 20, 2026. The range we quoted in October is unconfirmed until someone opens the ad in a browser, so no OpenAI figure appears here.
  • Baseten’s Forward Deployed Engineer (Training) ad. We cited it in October and could not find it in what we were able to read this time. Its status is unknown.
  • Aggregator and salary-site estimates. No figure here comes from a job aggregator, a salary site or a search-engine summary. Where a summary showed a different range from the ad, as it did for Caylent and Stripe, we used only the ad as read.
  • Ads that have closed or moved. A number of ads now redirect to the employer’s job index or show a closed notice, including a second Stripe ad. Figures a search engine still shows for them are not used.
  • Ads read on Dice in October. The Accenture Federal Services and Booz Allen Hamilton ads were on an aggregator and were not re-checked.
  • Employers we could not read or did not reach. Snowflake and SailPoint ads could not be opened, and a Talkdesk ad could not be found. Several employers that post through Ashby could be read only in part, so more ads may exist there, including at Baseten, Firecrawl and Middesk. We did not search ServiceNow, Rippling, Datadog, Samsara, Shield AI, Vannevar Labs, PwC, KPMG or Booz Allen’s own careers site.
  • Total compensation. No ad gave an equity value, so we publish no total figures.
  • Colorado’s rule and other states. Colorado’s official pages could not be opened. We also leave out the effective dates of the California, New York State and Washington rules, which are not on the pages we read, and the laws of the other jurisdictions Accenture’s ad names.
  • Pay outside the United States. Every range here is a US range.

Questions

  • What does a forward deployed engineer earn in the US?
  • It depends on the employer, the level and the pay basis. In 42 US job ads from 31 employers read on November 20, 2026, the 24 ranges labeled base salary ran from a lowest figure of $88,970 (Salesforce, national range) to a highest of $376,000 (Figma). On any basis, the lowest figure was $54,400 (Accenture, Maine) and the highest $405,000 (Anthropic, a manager role). The sample is not a market measure.
  • What does a forward deployed engineer cost to hire?
  • In published employer pay, the base ranges in the ads we read on November 20, 2026, start as low as $88,970 and reach $376,000, and they move with level and location. Base is rarely the whole offer. 30 of the 42 ads mention equity or stock, and 17 mention a bonus or other variable pay, so plan the offer as base plus those parts. This page covers employer pay only.
  • Which employers publish the highest ranges?
  • Anthropic’s Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering ad shows $320,000 to $405,000, labeled “Annual Salary” (read November 20, 2026). For roles that are not management, the highest top of a range is Figma’s $376,000, labeled base. The highest bottom of a range is $280,000, in two Anthropic ads, Staff Forward Deployed Engineer and Applied AI Engineer, Commercial.
  • Do forward deployed engineers get equity and bonuses?
  • Most ads say they may. Of the 42 ads read on November 20, 2026, 30 mention equity, stock or restricted stock units, and 17 mention a performance bonus, incentive or other variable pay. Four Palantir ads mention only a sign-on bonus. In our read, no ad put a dollar figure on equity, so ask for it in writing.
  • Do roles that need a security clearance pay more?
  • The ads we read do not show it. Six of the 42 ads require a clearance or eligibility for one (read November 20, 2026). Palantir publishes the same $135,000 to $200,000 for its Washington, D.C. government ad and its New York commercial ad. Anduril’s Air Defense ad, which asks for clearance eligibility, shows $113,000 to $155,000 base.
  • Why do US job ads show a pay range?
  • Several states and cities require it. California’s Labor Commissioner says an employer with 15 or more employees must include the pay scale in any job posting. New York State, New York City, Washington State and Illinois publish similar rules on their official pages (all read November 20, 2026). Bonuses and equity need not appear in a California posting, which is one reason the range is rarely the whole offer.
