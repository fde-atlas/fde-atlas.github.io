Today's Take

Today's main storyline is not "a few more agents appeared," but that enterprise AI is beginning to form three on-the-ground mechanisms: the demand side first turns factory pain points into a checklist (the Hangzhou Industrial Intelligence Base publicly listed 12 frontline needs); employees turn job experience into runnable tools (a Changan Automobile frontline engineer compressed two days of work down to 5 minutes); platforms select models at runtime based on quality, cost, and risk (GitHub HydraFusion's multi-model orchestration).

An even more important background signal: the MIIT special action program for cultivating AI application service providers, released on August 31, explicitly encourages service providers to build FDE teams and embed themselves at user sites—this is the first time FDE has appeared in a national ministry policy document.

Key Signals

Hangzhou publishes 12 frontline needs and 50 industrial agents, plans to train on-site delivery engineers

  • Source: Hangzhou Daily | Original link
  • The Hangzhou Industrial Intelligence Innovation Ecosystem Base launched its co-construction on September 4: guided by the Municipal Bureau of Economy and Information Technology, with Zhipu AI, Hangzhou Iron & Steel Cloud, and others as founding members; the first demand list contains 12 real pain points from frontline workshops; the online platform's first batch lists 50 industrial agents, covering R&D design, production scheduling, defect detection, and equipment maintenance; it also plans to dispatch "AI special envoys" to districts and counties and to train on-site delivery engineers in batches who understand both processes and AI.
  • Why it matters: This mechanism design answers three fundamental questions of industrial AI—the demand checklist solves "what to do," the testing base solves "where to validate," and on-site delivery engineers solve "who is accountable for convergence." "On-site delivery engineer" is precisely the local government's phrasing for FDE—after Shanghai Hongkou and Wuhan, Hangzhou has also written this role into its industrial mechanism. Note: the 12 needs and 50 agents are a supply-demand list at launch time, not evidence of being live or producing business results.

Changan Automobile: frontline employees turn job experience into tools, compressing multiple tasks from days to minutes

  • Source: Xinhua | Original link
  • Xinhua reports: Changan Automobile has integrated Qianwen Office across R&D, manufacturing, supply chain, sales, and service scenarios, requiring all employees to use AI agents; many tools were built by frontline employees themselves, not delivered through unified outsourcing. Task results: wire harness selection shrank from about two days by senior engineers to 5 minutes; verification of dozens of parameters in accompanying vehicle documents shrank from over ten minutes to 1-2 minutes; packaging review was split into 7-8 automation scripts, with the company claiming workload reduced by more than 90%.
  • Why it matters: The key to scaling is not having FDEs write tools for everyone, but finding job experts, decomposing tacit judgment into tasks, rules, data, and validation, then having a second unit re-run it. The first "product managers" of enterprise AI are on the frontline—they know the rules best and only lack packaging methods. The horizontal replication described in the report—"multiple production units at Hebei Changan adopting the scanning tool"—is more worth tracking than a single efficiency gain. Note: the figures come from company/vendor case narratives; accuracy, human review hours, and long-term adoption were not disclosed.

GitHub HydraFusion: quality gates, independent review, and multi-model orchestration where failures don't land patches

  • Source: GitHub Blog | Original link
  • GitHub released the Project HydraFusion research preview on September 4: within Copilot CLI, it selects among single-model, cascaded, or "generate—independent review—one revision" execution paths per task, uniformly logging role, result, cost, and latency for each call. Official offline evals claim: compared with Claude Opus 5, quality is 4.9 percentage points higher on TerminalBench 2.1 with an estimated cost 67% lower.
  • Why it matters: Enterprise multi-model routing cannot just be "cheapest model first." The complete control chain consists of six gates: task triage → quality gate → independent read-only review → timeout/budget boundaries → no commit on failure + rollback → full-chain cost and outcome accounting. Business tasks also require permissions, human sign-off, and result write-back. Note: this is a research preview and a controlled offline benchmark; it cannot be extrapolated to Chinese-language business tasks or enterprise ROI.

Policy background: The MIIT special action program for cultivating AI application service providers, released on August 31, targets a national resource pool of over 2,000 providers by the end of 2026 and no fewer than 3,000 by the end of 2027, explicitly encouraging service providers to build FDE teams and embed themselves at user sites, and requiring high-frequency, essential business functions to be packaged as "small, fast, lightweight, precise" product bundles (MIIT original text; the original uses "前线部署工程师," which this site uniformly renders with the standard translation "Forward Deployed Engineer"). This is the first time FDE has entered a national ministry policy document—from career narrative to policy identity, this is a landmark moment for FDE development in China.

Implications for FDE Practitioners

  • Open every project with a "real task checklist": for each item, record the job role, current time cost, data conditions, rule stability, risk, owner, acceptance criteria, and reuse goals—start with only the top 3 items; don't overreach.
  • Find internal co-creators; don't be an all-purpose contractor: prioritize job experts who can already describe rules, are willing to re-run, and can confirm errors; the FDE's role is to turn individual scripts into organizational assets with controlled permissions that are testable and handover-ready.
  • Job-task productization evidence card: job/expert → original task and baseline → rules/data → AI execution steps → quality and exceptions → human sign-off → re-run by a second job role → sustained adoption. A single efficiency gain proves the tool works; only a re-run by a second job role proves an asset has been formed.
  • Watch the MIIT service provider resource pool application: the 2,000-provider resource pool is the policy entry point for FDE service providers; service records, case boundaries, delivery assets, and FDE team descriptions are worth preparing in advance.
