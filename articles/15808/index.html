FDE Forward Deployed Engineer Services

Original title: FDE 前置部署工程师服务｜深云 FDE 模式

深圳市深云科技技术有限公司 · 2026-11-08 · 3 min read

Summary

Shenzhen-based Shenyun Tech describes its Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) service model: engineers embed at the customer site and form a joint team with the client's business and technical staff, taking shared responsibility from problem definition through prototyping, production launch, and ongoing adoption. The service is built on WorkBuddy, ADP, and Tencent Cloud infrastructure, covering business discovery, agent and workflow development, evals and governance, and operations. Deliverables include reusable assets such as connectors, knowledge rules, and eval sets. The article contrasts this with outsourcing, product implementation, and consulting by emphasizing jointly defined problems, real production deployment, and post-launch accountability.

Key points

  • FDEs embed on-site and share accountability for business outcomes with clients.
  • Delivery follows four stages: define, build, launch, and scale, each with defined outputs.
  • Solutions are built on WorkBuddy, ADP, and Tencent Cloud resources.
  • Evals, monitoring, human review, and exception handling are part of the service.
  • Key differentiators: jointly defined problems, production deployment, post-launch ownership.

01 / DEFINITION

Buying models or platforms is easy. The hard part is business judgment, application building, system constraints, and user adoption—someone has to own these together. The FDE is the person who owns them.

FORWARD DEPLOYED ENGINEERFDEForward Deployed Engineer

A Shenyun FDE is not an ordinary Sales Engineer, project manager, on-site developer, or after-sales support. It requires engineering staff to go forward into the business environment, understand processes, users, systems, and metrics, quickly build verifiable applications, and continuously drive production operation.

Customers don't need to coordinate separately with consulting, product, development, implementation, and operations vendors. Shenyun FDEs bring everyone into one joint team accountable for the same business outcome.

02 / METHOD

At each stage, first clarify what conditions trigger the start, what gets delivered, and what justifies continued investment. Otherwise projects easily stall at a demo-able prototype.

Build an operable version within a limited scope, letting real users complete a full work task.

Connect knowledge, systems, and permissions; establish evals, monitoring, human review, and exception handling mechanisms.

Watch usage and business metrics; once connectors, knowledge rules, and eval criteria accumulate, apply them to the next scenario.

06 / FAQ

Helps business, technical, and management teams judge whether this service model fits the current project.

FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer; Shenyun calls it Forward Deployed Engineer. This person goes into the customer's business environment, bridges the business team and the engineering team, and follows through from problem definition to post-launch adoption.

Not the same. Being on-site is just a work location; the key to FDE is taking responsibility for problem definition, cross-team coordination, rapid building, production launch, and sustained adoption.

Projects with clear business value, requirements not yet fixed, multiple systems and teams to connect, and a need for continued operation and scaling after launch suit the FDE model.

Usually requires participation from business owners, frontline users, and technical or data leads. Shenyun FDEs form a joint team with these roles and the Shenyun engineering team.
