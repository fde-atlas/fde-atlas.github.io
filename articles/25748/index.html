Own the implementation — and ongoing adoption

quivly.ai · 2025-12-15 · 4 min read

Summary

Quivly is a workspace for FDE teams that combines implementation rollouts with post-launch adoption monitoring. It consolidates milestones, owners, dependencies, and customer readiness gates into one shared timeline for FDEs and customer success. An AI agent blends CRM, product usage, billing, and market signals to detect integration failures and technical risks early, then routes full technical context to the FDE and a plain-language summary with a suggested customer message to the CSM. The vendor claims a 65% reduction in reactive escalations, 4x faster detection of integration failures, and 3x more accounts covered per FDE.

Key points

  • Single shared timeline covers rollouts from kickoff through cutover.
  • AI agent blends CRM, usage, billing, and market signals to spot risks.
  • Dual alerts give FDEs technical detail and CSMs customer-ready summaries.
  • Claims 65% fewer reactive escalations and 3x account coverage per FDE.
  • Tracks endpoint-level feature adoption to build proactive account agendas.

FAQ

Common questions from FDE teams about Quivly

Quivly can monitor API call volumes and error rates, webhook delivery success and failure, data sync latency and gaps, feature adoption at the endpoint level, SDK and library version currency, and custom technical metrics from your data warehouse. The specific signals depend on what your product emits and which integrations you’ve connected.

Quivly connects to your product analytics layer (Segment, Amplitude, or direct event streams), your observability tools (Datadog, New Relic, or similar), and your data warehouse. It reads the signals you define — Quivly doesn’t need direct access to your production systems. Setup is handled by Quivly’s implementation team with your engineering lead.

Yes, when the data supports it. Quivly can compare error patterns across multiple accounts to detect whether an issue is isolated to one customer (likely their configuration or environment) or affecting a cohort (likely a platform or infrastructure issue). It flags this distinction in the alert so your FDE team knows immediately whether to route to CS or to engineering.

When Quivly detects a technical issue, it simultaneously creates an FDE alert with full technical context and a CS alert with a plain-language summary of what’s happening and a suggested customer message. Both alerts link to the same incident timeline so everyone has the same facts. The CSM can send the customer communication in one click while the FDE works the resolution.

Yes. Beyond error monitoring, Quivly tracks feature adoption at the technical level — which endpoints are being called, which aren’t, which integrations are set up vs. available, and which accounts haven’t enabled capabilities that would drive value. This gives FDEs a proactive agenda for each account: not just ‘what’s broken’ but ‘what’s missing that should be there’.
