What Makes Fourth Age Different?

Fourth Age's Substack · 2025-12-22 · 7 min read

Summary

In this Substack post, Fourth Age explains how it differs from consulting firms and other Palantir ecosystem partners. Founded by four ex-Palantir engineers with over 50 combined years of experience, the company specializes in Palantir Foundry and AIP and runs a lean, builder-first Forward Deployed Engineering model. Its roughly 50 FDEs average 3.5 years of Palantir experience, engineers handle recruiting, sales, and management themselves, and the company grows deliberately while staying flexible with customers, proving value before asking for commitments.

Key points

  • Founding team of four has 50+ combined years as Palantir FDEs.
  • 50 FDEs hold 175+ years of total experience, averaging 3.5 years each.
  • Engineers run recruiting, sales, and management; internal ops run in Foundry.
  • Promotion is based on demonstrated ability, not seniority-based hierarchy.
  • No pre-commitments required; the company proves value before long-term contracts.

At Fourth Age, one of the questions we get most often from potential customers and candidates is: what exactly makes you different from other consulting firms or other partners in the Palantir ecosystem? They may know a few key points, like that some of our engineers are ex-Palantir and that we are specialists in Palantir Foundry and AIP, but this doesn't tell the whole story of what we believe makes our vision and our company unique.

We love this question because it allows us to tell the story of how we are building Fourth Age to be the best place in the world to be an FDE, and the best place in the world to help innovative organizations solve their problems with data and AI. Our customers come to us for our unmatched ability to deploy a differentiated Forward Deployed Engineering model at scale, and our employees come to us for unique, years-long career paths that allow them to learn, grow, develop, and be recognized for their abilities faster than anywhere else — and work on fascinating real-world problems with equally impressive colleagues while they do it.

How exactly are we making this vision a reality, and what gives us the conviction that we are building a unique and scalable business for our customers and employees? A few of the key points follow.

We are Descended from Palantir DNA

Often, one of the first things people know about is that our founders came from Palantir. Palantir pioneered and proved the concept of the Forward Deployed Engineer, and our founding team of four had over 50 combined years of experience working as FDEs and running projects, teams and products at Palantir. From the start, our careers at Palantir gave us a wealth of experience not just in how to wield their products but also in how to build and manage teams of FDEs and deliver outcomes by any means necessary for a wide range of customers across industries, geographies, and stages of maturity.

This foundation has served us well in our journey building the premier services-oriented business for true digital transformation. We were and still are (now more than ever) some of the world’s most experienced specialists in Forward Deployed Engineering in general, the Palantir tech stack in particular, and delivering high-value outcomes for our customers everywhere we go.

We are Builders First, not Salespeople or Managers

No matter how much experience our FDEs bring when they join us, they are joining us to build. This means being hands-on, working with clients to develop and deploy concrete solutions to their problems. We will never compete with the largest firms in selling large teams of consultants, nor will we compete with any other number of tech services firms on delivering engineering hours using the widest breadth of IT point-solutions at the lowest cost. However, we believe we are best-in-class in our ability to deploy lean, hands-on teams that can deliver an unbeatable time-to-value for our customers.

Aside from a small but mighty ops team, our FDEs do our recruiting and interviewing, people management, project management, and sales. We even built many of our internal processes in Foundry so we could be tech- and data-driven from the start and manage our “back office” with the lowest amount of overhead possible. We expect and enable each team of engineers to act as fully atomic units, and this lean staffing structure ensures we can deliver the best results at the most competitive rates for our customers.

Our Senior Engineers are Truly Senior

We aim to hire and provide rewarding career paths for the best and brightest FDEs in the world, whether they have over a decade of experience before joining Fourth Age or if they are new university graduates, or anywhere in between. But no matter where our FDEs are in their career path, we don’t expect our customers to foot the bill for their training or pay a premium for a team of FDEs who are more junior than their own internal resources. As of end 2025/beginning 2026, our 50 FDEs have a total of over 175 years of experience between them, meaning that the average Fourth Age FDE brings 3.5 years of experience with Palantir’s software before they are deployed to one of our customers.

Of course, many of our incredibly high-performing teammates bring something like 6-18 months of experience to their teams, and we recognize and promote employees based on demonstrated ability to work autonomously and take ownership over projects and outcomes. We do not rely on a rigid, seniority-based hierarchy. However, we do find that our ability to provide the highest value outcomes in the shortest possible time requires a range of experience and technical skills, and the depth of experience of our more tenured engineers is a major part of what supercharges our results. Other teams made of solely or primarily junior FDEs would simply lack the foundation of experience necessary to deliver the best results (especially if they come in with a high price point that should match to corresponding levels of experience).

We Keep the Bar High as We Scale

One of the hardest parts of building a differentiated FDE team is to balance two opposing ways of growing. One way is to grow one-by-one, hiring a handful of experienced, proven talent who may be great engineers but are expensive and difficult to scale to meet customer needs. The other path is to lean first into sales to generate business or lean into well-funded recruiting efforts to hire, allowing for headcount to grow rapidly but at the expense of ensuring that each team is made up of sufficiently experienced, consistent talent.

We instead chose a third path, growing carefully first by building up a critical mass of highly differentiated engineers with a breadth of industry exposure, strengths, technical skills and product expertise, and subsequently using that team as the scaffolding to grow from ten to twenty, thirty, forty, and beyond. As we look to scale further in 2026, we believe that the high bar and steady growth we maintained will now be a rock-solid foundation for further growth, and we look forward to continuing to grow around this core team.

We believe with high conviction that for the A+ engineers that we look to recruit and retain, the most compelling thing we can offer is not a high starting salary or a cushy lifestyle billing out hours to customers. Instead, they want to know that each team they work with will be stacked with equally exceptional people that they would have never otherwise had the opportunity to work with and learn from, no matter where they are from in the world or what experience they bring to the table. This uncompromising commitment to maintaining the “A-Team” at scale is our key to keeping our flywheel accelerating by encouraging more top talent to join and grow with us so we can keep delivering transformative outcomes for our customers.

We Bear the Burden of Success for Our Customers

We view ourselves as building a startup rather than a consulting company, meaning we think about how we can build the best company possible on a timeframe of multiple years rather than single quarters. We see our clients as partners rather than customers, meaning that we see every new relationship as a potential start to an enduring, growing collaboration. As a result, we believe it should be as easy as possible for the right partners to jump in and get started with us, without regard for our short-term ability to sell as many billable hours as possible.

In practice, this means we don’t need pre-commitments from our customers, require long lead times, or demand to start with the largest possible team size. We are happy to be as agile as we need to be to meet each customer’s most pressing needs and immediate constraints. We’re selective about which customers we take on, but as long as we believe we can do differentiated work on interesting and valuable problems, we expect to demonstrate that we can deliver outstanding value before asking our customer to make commitments to us.

We’re Just at the Beginning

Over the months and years ahead, software and AI services generally and the Palantir partner ecosystem in particular are only going to become more and more crowded. Meanwhile, we believe these convictions and key differentiators will allow us to continue to build and expand our moat in a hyper-competitive industry. We can’t wait to continue growing Fourth Age in 2026 as we double down on our model for hiring, developing, and deploying talent in order to deliver epic things for our employees and customers.
