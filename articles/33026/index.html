Is forward deployed engineering for you? A self-check

Kernavique · 2026-11-26 · 2 min read

Summary

Kernavique offers a self-check for people considering a forward deployed engineer career. It consists of eight statements drawn from 297 US job ads with “forward deployed” in the title, read on November 26, 2026. Of those ads, 204 require Python, 183 mention travel, and 64 mention a security clearance. Readers answer each statement in the browser and see which requirements they already meet, plus suggested reading on the site. The page stresses it is not an assessment of hiring chances—employers decide that in interviews—and no answers are stored or sent anywhere. Questions people ask

  • Where do the eight statements come from?
  • From the text of the 297 open US ads with “forward deployed” in the title that we read on November 26, 2026, and from our Briefing pieces on the role. Where a statement rests on a count, the count is shown beside it.
  • Is this an assessment?
  • No. It sets your own answers beside what the ads ask for. It cannot tell you whether you would be hired, and it does not try to. Employers decide that in interviews.
  • Are my answers stored?
  • No. They are kept in the page while it is open and are not sent to us. If you want us to know about you, send your details and résumé through the form.
  • What if I answered “not yet” to several?
  • Then you know where the gaps are. Our piece on how to become a forward deployed engineer sets out the routes people take into the role, with sources.
