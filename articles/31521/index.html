Claude Forward Deployed Engineer Learning Path | What's Inside Compared to 412 FDE Job Openings

Original title: Claude Forward Deployed Engineerの学習パス|FDE求人412件と比べた中身

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-11-22 · 15 min read

Summary

Anthropic has released a Claude Forward Deployed Engineer learning path for Claude Partner Network members: 11 self-paced courses totaling about 9.5 hours, nine shared with the developer path, plus two unique courses on delivery methodology and enterprise adoption. The FDE Talent editorial team compared its content against 412 FDE job postings (71 companies) collected on Nov 22, 2026: 36% mention adoption and 33% prototype, but only 5% mention MCP and none mention Claude certification; 54% require years of experience, 55% mention travel, and 60% require Python. The path suits engineers with existing development and customer experience but cannot replace field experience; the authors suggest taking the two unique courses and running a real 30-day pilot.

Key points

  • The learning path has 11 courses (~9.5 hours), restricted to Claude Partner Network members.
  • Two unique courses cover delivery methodology and enterprise adoption, including a 30-day pilot process.
  • Among 412 FDE postings, adoption appears in 36%, MCP in only 5%, Claude certification in 0.
  • 54% of postings require experience years and 55% mention travel; courses cannot replace field experience.
  • Recommended use: experienced engineers take the two unique courses, then run a real 30-day pilot.

Key Takeaways of This Article

  • 01 By November 2026, Anthropic had released a learning path for the "Claude Forward Deployed Engineer," designed to guide customers from their first use case to a working pilot in the field, available to members of the Claude Partner Network.
  • 02 The learning path consists of 11 courses and about 9.5 hours of self-study, of which 9 courses are shared with the developer learning path; the only additions are two courses on delivery methodology and driving company-wide adoption.
  • 03 Of the 412 FDE job postings collected by the editorial team on November 22, 2026, 5% mentioned MCP, and none mentioned Claude certification.
  • 04 Among the same 412 postings, 36% mentioned adoption in the context of customers or implementation, and 33% mentioned prototyping—terms closer to the "delivery" side of the learning path appeared more often than tool names.
  • 05 Of the postings, 54% listed years of experience and 55% listed business travel; the learning path is no substitute for such hands-on experience.

こんにちは、編集部です。今回は、Anthropicが用意した「FDEになるための講座」が、実際のFDE求人の要件とどこまで重なるのかを数えてみました。Anthropicは、パートナー企業の技術者向けの学習サイトで「Claude Forward Deployed Engineer」の学習パスを公開しています[1]。ページに公開日はありませんが、11月20日には海外のニュースレターが「公開されたばかり」と紹介していました[3]。

What SIers and consultancies that want to develop FDEs in-house, and companies responsible for AI implementation, want to know is: "Can this learning path turn our engineers into FDEs? What will they learn, and what's missing?" In this article, we read through the 11 courses and compare them against the full text of 412 FDE job postings from public job boards.

What Does the "Delivery Methodology" Course Teach?

開発者向けの学習パスに無い講座の1つ「Delivery Methodology」が教えるのは、道具の使い方だけでなく、案件をどう成果につなげるかです[2]。この講座は、Claude Codeのパートナー向けバッジの講座も兼ねています[2]。講座のページの最初の一文が、そのことをはっきり書いています。

"The technical deployment is the easier part."

Anthropic "Delivery Methodology" course page (Claude Forward Deployed Engineer learning path, retrieved November 22, 2026)

[2]

  • 改善の種類を分ける: 同じ仕事が速くなる「限界的な改善」と、仕事の単位そのものが変わる「構造的な改善」を分ける。例として、4人で6週間かかった作業が2人で2週間になれば、30日以内に測れて報告会でも説明できるとしている[2]
  • ユースケースの見極め: 量・複雑さ・測りやすさ・開発者の準備の4つの基準で、パイロットに向く題材と、ただの要望を分ける[2]
  • 基準値を先に取る: パイロットの初日より前に基準値を記録する。前のデータが無いと、ROIの計算の分母が無いと書いている[2]
  • 30日の立ち上げ: 始める前の準備期間、毎週の進め方、30日目の発表会、プラグインと文書の引き継ぎまでを通しで扱う[2]
  • チャンピオンを育てる: 顧客側の推進役を決め、CLAUDE.mdや社内の文書を導入の基盤として整え、去った後も推進役が保てるようにする[2]

ここまで具体的に「30日で何を見せるか」を書いた教材は、なかなかありません…！ 一方で、この講座の例はClaude Codeを開発チームに入れる案件が中心です[2]。業務部門にAIのエージェントを入れる案件では、基準値や測り方を自分たちで置き換える必要があります。

Compared with 412 actual FDE job postings, what overlaps and what is missing?

When checked against the job posting texts, the "delivery" side of the learning path is frequently demanded in job postings, while the "tools" side is almost never named. Our editorial team counted word occurrences across 412 FDE job postings (71 companies) from public job boards as of November 22, 2026, excluding duplicates and Anthropic's own postings.

Only 19 of the 412 postings mentioned MCP—a mere 5%...! Although the learning path devotes an entire course to it, few companies explicitly demand it in their postings. Not a single posting mentioned Claude certification, and when we previously counted postings naming FDE-related certifications, the count was also 0.

Conversely, 36% of postings mentioned "adoption" in customer or deployment contexts, and 33% mentioned "prototyping." "Pilots and PoCs" was 14%, and "success metrics and ROI" was 17%—relatively low, but still around three times MCP. The content covered by the two courses absent from the developer learning path overlaps well with the language of job postings. This suggests that what is demanded is not the names of tools, but the ability to carry things through to actual use in customers' environments...

足りないのは、講座では身につかない経験です。412件の54%が経験年数を、55%が出張を書き、60%がPythonを名指ししていました。学習パス自体も、開発と顧客対応の経験を受講の前提にしています[1]。FDEに求められるスキルの全体像はFDEに必要なスキル・資格にまとめています。

How does Anthropic's own FDE job posting position partners?

AnthropicのStaff FDEの求人を読むと、パートナーが作る案件の設計と品質の基準をAnthropicのFDEが決める形が見えます[5]。米国のStaff Forward Deployed Engineerの求人は、職務の1つに「パートナーと届ける案件を率い、自分では作らない仕事の設計と品質の基準を決める」ことを挙げています[5]。

同じ求人は、Pythonの力、本番のシステムを作って運用した豊富な経験、LLMとエージェントを実際に使った経験(副業や試作も数える)、AIのコーディングの道具を日常的に使ってその出力を見極める判断を求め、顧客先への出張は25%としています[5]。基本給の年額(Annual Salary)の幅は28万〜32万ドルです[5]。編集部は、学習パスをこのうちClaudeでの作り方の部分をパートナーの側でそろえるための教材と見ています。

How should companies use the learning path?

Claude Partner Networkの会員の企業なら、開発と顧客対応の経験がある人に、開発者向けの学習パスに無い2講座から受けてもらうのが効率的です[1]。そのうえで、講座で足りない部分は実際の案件で補います。

  • 受講する人を選ぶ: 学習パスの前提は、ソフトウェアを作り、顧客のチームと直接働いた経験[1]。SIerのSEやプリセールスで、顧客の前に立ってきた人が当てはまる
  • 届ける側の2講座を先に: Claude Developerの学習パスの修了者なら、残りは2講座・約100分[1]。導入の方法論と全社への定着の講座を、社内の案件の振り返りと合わせて使う
  • 30日のパイロットを1件やる: 講座の30日の立ち上げの流れを、社内の小さな題材で一度やってみる[2]。基準値を先に取り、30日目に結果を見せる
  • Don't list certification as a hiring requirement: Of 412 job postings, zero mentioned Claude certification. For hiring, asking candidates to show a pilot plan or concrete examples of measured results is a better fit for the requirements

The overall picture of deciding whether to hire or train FDEs, and how to proceed with hiring, is summarized in the FDE Hiring Guide.

What remains unclear from this article?

There are limits to how the learning path pages and job postings can be counted.

  • Course content: Our editorial team is not a partner member and has not taken the courses themselves. This article is based on the public pages of the learning path and courses
  • 公開日: 学習パスのページに公開日の記載はない。11月20日にはニュースレターで紹介されていた[3]
  • かかる時間: 学習パスのページは「Delivery Methodology」を約40分としているが、講座ページは実測の受講時間を1時間13分、演習と成果物づくりを1時間10分と示しており、残り2講座・約100分という目安より時間がかかる可能性がある[1][2]
  • 講座の数: 学習パスのページは11講座、講座「Delivery Methodology」のページは「10講座中の9番目」と書いており、数え方がそろっていない[1][2]
  • How job postings were counted: The count is based on occurrences of terms, without distinguishing whether they are required or merely mentioned in job descriptions. Postings written only in Japanese were not included in the count

Summary

  • Anthropic's Claude Forward Deployed Engineer learning path is a self-study program of 11 courses and about 9.5 hours aimed at enabling learners to write pilot plans, and is available to Claude Partner Network members
  • Of the 11 courses, 9 are shared with the developer path; the two unique ones cover delivery methodology and company-wide adoption
  • Among 412 FDE job postings, 36% mentioned adoption in the context of clients or deployment, while MCP appeared in 5% and Claude certification in zero
  • 54% of postings listed years of experience and 55% listed travel, showing the learning path is no substitute for hands-on experience
  • For companies, the fastest route is to have people with development and client-facing experience take the two delivery-side courses and actually run a 30-day pilot

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat is the Claude Forward Deployed Engineer learning path?

A combination of self-study courses for Claude Partner Network members published by Anthropic by November 2026. It consists of 11 courses and about 9.5 hours, aimed at guiding clients from their first use case to a working pilot in the field.

QCan anyone take the Claude Forward Deployed Engineer learning path?

According to the learning path page, only Claude Partner Network members can enroll. Prerequisites are building software and having experience working directly with client teams.

QDo you get a certification for completing the learning path?

The learning path page points to the exam "Claude Certified Developer - Foundations" as a next step. The learning path itself is a combination of self-study courses. Among the 412 FDE job postings our editorial team collected on November 22, 2026, none mentioned Claude certification.

QHow does it differ from Claude Frontier Academy?

Claude Frontier Academy is a program Anthropic announced on October 2, 2026, investing $100 million to train 10,000 people by the end of 2027. The Claude Forward Deployed Engineer learning path is a separate combination of self-study courses for partners.

QCan you become an FDE with the learning path alone?

The learning path assumes people with development and client-facing experience. Of the 412 FDE job postings our editorial team collected on November 22, 2026, 54% listed years of experience and 55% listed travel, so courses alone are no substitute for hands-on experience.
