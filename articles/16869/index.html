Path to FDE — Become a Forward Deployed Engineer

pathtofde.com · 2026-10-29 · 2 min read

Summary

Path to FDE is a free curriculum site for becoming a Forward Deployed Engineer, built around a fictional project called CityOps. The content spans six stages: calling LLM APIs in production, embeddings and vector search, building a RAG pilot, evals and tracing, deployment with Compose and CI/CD, plus career guidance and interview question banks. Lessons are heavily sequential—most list prerequisites from earlier stages. Career posts argue you should pick a job by its actual day-to-day work rather than its title, and a design lesson frames model autonomy as a budget to spend minimally.

Key points

  • Free FDE curriculum organized around a fictional CityOps project.
  • Six stages: LLM APIs, RAG, evals, deployment, CI/CD, careers.
  • Most lessons require completing earlier stages as prerequisites.
  • Career advice: choose roles by daily work, not job titles.
  • Teaches giving models the least autonomy that solves the problem.
