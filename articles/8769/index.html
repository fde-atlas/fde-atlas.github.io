Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer Interview (No Offer): I was reached out to on LinkedIn

Taro · 2024-03-21 · 3 min read

Summary

A candidate recruited via LinkedIn for Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer role in Washington, D.C. failed the first-round technical assessment, which covered networking, algorithms, and SQL questions, and received no offer. Aggregated data from 16 interview experiences shows a 0% pass rate, indicating an extremely selective and lengthy process. Accompanying employee reviews (rated around 4.0) praise the caliber of colleagues, impactful work, travel opportunities, and compensation, but consistently cite poor work-life balance, unclear internal advancement paths, and a share of tedious technical work.

Key points

  • Candidate contacted on LinkedIn failed the first-round technical assessment, no offer
  • Assessment covered networking, algorithms, and SQL questions

  • Employee reviews praise talented peers, impactful work, and travel
  • Reviews consistently cite poor work-life balance and unclear promotion paths

Forward Deployed Software Engineer • Current Employee

Washington, District of Columbia, United States

November 5, 2014

Pros: The people are great, friendly, and very intelligent. Interesting work that can have a real impact. Awesome travel opportunities, including San Francisco, Los Angeles, New York City, Washington D.C., London, Sydney, and the UAE, plus more. The schedule is up to you. Come in and leave whenever, as long as the work is getting done.

Cons: There is a fair amount of technical grunt work to be done, and if you're not careful, you'll end up on boring projects. You can avoid this by proving yourself valuable in other areas. It's hard to stand out amongst peers when everyone is so talented. There's no clear path to advancement internally. They're working on this. Work/life balance varies across teams. Some people are overworked (not me...).

Cons: There is no expectation of work-life balance. This is a company for people who are serious about what they do and perform accordingly.
