FDE Interview: Interview Rounds, Take-Home Assignments, and How to Prepare

Original title: FDE 面试：面试轮次、带回家作业与准备方法

FDE Times · 2 min read

Summary

This article maps the FDE interview process end to end. Structurally it resembles software engineering interviews, but the decisive round is a non-coding problem decomposition session where candidates spend about 60 minutes working with a deliberately withholding “customer.” Many companies also assign take-home projects, graded on whether each decision maps to customer needs. The piece is organized by interview stage, company-specific processes (Palantir, Databricks, OpenAI), resume screening, prep resources, and post-offer compensation, with concrete numbers throughout.

Key points

  • The core round is problem decomposition with an information-withholding customer, not coding.
  • Take-home assignments include code, a runnable app, and a recorded walkthrough of decisions.
  • Resumes should show verifiable baseline numbers; portfolios should show where agents fail.
  • Palantir runs two rounds judging process over speed, using unfamiliar code and APIs.
  • US FDE median base pay is about $190K; top offers rely on equity.

In terms of process, the FDE interview is similar to the software engineer interview: there's a coding round, a system design or case study round, and a behavioral interview. The difference lies in emphasis: most companies add a round where you break down an open-ended customer problem into concrete steps, and many also assign a take-home assignment that requires you to explain the reasoning behind every decision.

The articles below are grouped by the stages a candidate encounters in order, and the first article in each group is the one you should read first.

What Does the Interview Process Include

  • The Most Critical Round of the FDE Interview Doesn't Involve Writing Code Distyl AI and Databricks still test coding, but the main event is navigating a deliberately information-withholding "customer" for up to an hour.
  • The Most Critical Round of the FDE Interview Is One That Grinding LeetCode Won't Help With At Databricks, the 60-minute decomposition round starts from a vague business goal and is considered the core of the entire interview, with coding relegated to a separate round.
  • FDE Interview Prep: Algorithms Are Enough, Focus on Practicing Problem Decomposition Databricks and OpenAI test practical coding, while Palantir uses 60 minutes to test decomposition and doesn't care whether you can recite Dijkstra from memory.

Take-Home Assignments and Project Experience

  • OpenAI FDE Take-Home Assignment: The Walkthrough May Matter More Than the Code Within a week, you must submit code, a runnable application, and a recorded walkthrough; the scoring focus seems to be whether every choice aligns with customer needs.
  • In the FDE Interview, Trade-off Questions Are the Main Test Interviewers are looking for judgment: a good answer clearly explains the constraints, options, choice, costs, and how to explain it to the customer.

Interview Processes at Different Companies

  • Companies Currently Hiring FDEs All companies with open FDE positions, along with their number of openings and published salaries.
  • Palantir FDSE Two Rounds: They Watch the Process, Not the Speed One round gives you unfamiliar code with up to 1,000 lines of erroneous output, another gives you an API you've never used; set up small exercises on your laptop and practice the methods until they become instinct.

What Recruiters Look for in Resumes

  • Code Gets You the FDE Interview, Customer Skills Determine the Offer Of roughly 1,000 FDE positions, most require Python and full-stack skills, and 47% require direct customer interaction.
  • FDE Resumes Should Include Numbers With Baselines, Not Just a List of Tech Stacks Recruiters want verifiable deployment results, like "six weeks shortened to nine days," not a string of tool names.
  • FDE Portfolio Repos Should Show Where the Agent Fails An agent with 80% single-run accuracy only achieves 51% on three consecutive successes; evals based on real failures beat polished demos.
  • FDE Roles Grew 800% in a Year, but Many Aren't Real FDEs Don't look at the title; look at what you can do and where the code ultimately lands; a five-point test can expose demo and support roles disguised as FDE roles.

Study Materials

  • Alex Xu's 30 Chapters on System Design, Not a Single One on ML The book is still worth a read for FDE candidates: its four-step answer framework is essentially the first working meeting with a customer.
  • Educative's FDE Course Targets Interviews, Not AI Beginners The course teaches how to ask the customer the first question before teaching design, a step that LeetCode-trained candidates often skip.

After You Get the Offer

  • FDE Salaries: What Job Postings Actually Offer Base salary ranges published in US job postings, broken down by level, company, and city, updated daily.
  • One Year of Experience Lands a Palantir FDE Role at a $135K Starting Salary At the other end, OpenAI's FDE manager role requires eight-plus years of experience and pays $345K; from mid-level onward, equity pushes total compensation well above base salary.
  • The $550K FDE Salary Is Just the Ceiling at Two Labs The median base salary for US FDE positions is around $190K, with top salaries coming mainly from equity that vests over years and is hard to value.
