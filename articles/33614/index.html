Cognizant targets 5,000 Frontier Certified Engineers

Kernavique · 2026-07-09 · 2 min read

Summary

Kernavique reports that on July 9, 2026, Cognizant announced targets of 5,000 Frontier Certified Engineers and 10,000 Frontier Business Operators. Some 40,000 associates are already in certification, with the first cohort expected to be deployment-ready in Q4 2026. The company distinguishes these roles from forward deployed engineers by emphasizing accountability after go-live, including monitoring and tuning of agentic systems. It cites a pilot where a two-person pod built 17 production AI agents. Notably, Forrester described the 5,000 figure as an EMEA unit target, while Cognizant's release frames it as company-wide, a discrepancy the article flags. Sourcing includes internal associates, global capability centers, and campus hiring.

On July 9, 2026, Cognizant said in a release that it would scale to 5,000 Frontier Certified Engineers and 10,000 Frontier Business Operators. It said 40,000 associates are in Frontier certification and that the first cohort will be “deployment-ready” by the fourth quarter of 2026. The release says permanence and accountability set these roles apart from forward deployed engineers.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: Cognizant.

What happened

Cognizant describes seven roles across two tracks and six principles. It said Frontier Certified Engineers build agentic systems and stay accountable after go-live, including monitoring and tuning. Sourcing named in the release is internal associates, global capability centers, and campus hiring from American and global universities.

The release cites one pilot in which a two-person pod built 17 production AI agents, which is the company’s own figure. It quotes Thiru Arohi, Chief Learning Officer, Ravi Kumar S, chief executive, and Kathy Diaz, Chief People Officer.

Forrester wrote on July 30, 2026, that Cognizant “stood up an EMEA unit in July 2026 targeting 5,000 certified engineers”. Cognizant’s release gives 5,000 as a company-wide target and does not mention EMEA, so the two sources differ.

“We are developing a new professional identity for the AI era.”

Our reading

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

Cognizant names internal associates, global capability centers and campus hiring as its sources. This suggests the route in is certification, mainly for existing associates and graduates, with responsibility continuing after go-live.

If you are hiring them

Cognizant positions permanence as its difference from forward deployed engineers and cites a two-person pod. If you hire forward deployed engineers, this shows a large services firm building a similar capability under another name.
