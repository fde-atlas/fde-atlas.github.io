Summary

This piece profiles the levels.fyi page aggregating user-submitted pay data for FDE roles, accessed 2026-09-06: US-wide average total compensation around $206K, Seattle around $275K, Tel Aviv around $124K. The editor stresses that figures are self-reported and unverified, with small companies sometimes having single-digit samples, and that equity-versus-cash mix limits cross-company comparisons. For job seekers, the advice is to check the target company's page before negotiating and use the median and distribution range as an anchor.

Editor's Note FDEChina Editorial Team

This card separates "data sources" from "secondhand reports": compensation figures frequently cited by the media can mostly be traced back to levels.fyi or similar aggregation pages, and checking the original page directly lets you see the distribution rather than just the median. Two usage reminders: first, samples are self-reported and unverified, and small companies may have only single-digit samples, so check the sample size before drawing conclusions; second, the composition of total packages varies enormously — the ratio of equity to cash determines comparability of the numbers, so when comparing across companies, look at the composition.

Compared with report-type data sources, this card's added value is "self-service updating": the numbers change over time, so every citation should note the access date. For practitioners in China, the reference value lies in how it slices by region and level — the pay gap between Seattle and Tel Aviv suggests that FDE compensation is highly localized, so when discussing compensation in China, you should also align on a consistent basis by city and company type. Action item: before negotiating an offer, check the target company's page and note the median and distribution range as negotiation anchors.
