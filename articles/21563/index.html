Meet the Forward Deployed Engineers Behind Cloud Intelligence

Cloud Intelligence™ · 2026 · 9 min read

Summary

This is a promotional page from DoiT introducing its Cloud Intelligence team of forward deployed engineers and cloud architects. The team spans nine time zones, with members based in the US, UK, Israel, Germany, Australia, and elsewhere, covering AWS and Google Cloud specialties including databases, Kubernetes, networking, security, analytics, and AI/ML. DoiT emphasizes that these engineers work directly alongside customers' platform engineering and FinOps teams. The page includes customer testimonials about DataHub integration, the PerfectScale Kubernetes optimization tool, and the Attribute™ product for per-customer cost allocation in multi-tenant environments. It also promotes DoiT's presence at re:Invent 2026 (booth #1662).

Key points

  • DoiT showcases its global team of forward deployed engineers and cloud architects.
  • Team covers nine time zones across AWS and Google Cloud specialties.
  • Engineers embed with customers' platform engineering and FinOps teams.
  • Testimonials cover PerfectScale, Attribute™ cost allocation, and DataHub integration.
  • Page promotes DoiT's booth #1662 at re:Invent 2026.
