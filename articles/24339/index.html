Summary

This piece by Shohei Tokumitsu, CEO of DeFactory, promotes a downloadable guidebook on MVP development for software and apps. It cites the claim that roughly 90% of new businesses fail and frames MVP development within a three-stage flow: ideation, validation, and test marketing. The guide covers the development environment, success and failure patterns, and key pre-development checkpoints that apply whether a company builds in-house or outsources. It targets readers with no prior product development experience and those launching new ventures. The resource is free after filling out a form, alongside related materials such as a six-point checklist for vetting FDE consultants.

Key points

  • Guidebook on MVP development for software and apps.
  • Claims about 90% of new businesses fail.
  • Flow: ideation, validation, test marketing.
  • Checkpoints apply to in-house and outsourced builds.
  • Free download; targets first-time product builders.
