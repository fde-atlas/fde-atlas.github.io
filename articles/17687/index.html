Verdict

Thoughtworks writes the promise most buyers on this page want: to help you "move safely from proof-of-concept to production-ready AI". Its AI page backs that with named work, including Médecins Sans Frontières' Antibiogo app, which it says scaled across more than 100 labs with 60% faster build times, and a preclinical data story for Bayer AG.

What customers praise

Named clients with a result next to them, and an AI strategy offer that talks about token costs and reducing vendor dependency, which is the right worry for a company paying a model bill every month.

What customers criticise

No price, office address or ownership terms on the pages read, and the figures carry no method. Several of the results belong to unnamed clients. It is built for engineering organisations, so a company with no developers of its own may find it hard to use.

Best for

A software company or engineering led business whose AI proof of concept works and now needs to be rebuilt to production standard by engineers who write the code alongside its own team.

Red flags

Public risk signals as of November 2026: low. Live firm. AI offer and named client stories read on its own page on 6 November 2026. No adverse public signals found. See the full red-flag report.
