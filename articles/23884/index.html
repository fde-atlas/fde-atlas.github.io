Forward deployed engineers for banks and financial services

FetchSky Technologies · 2026 · 3 min read

Summary

FetchSky argues financial services is where forward deployed engineering delivers most value, since rules-heavy, document-heavy, regulated work defeats pilots built from written briefs. Since 2015 it has run a digital banking platform at 15 banks and shipped AI automations: a three-agent trade confirmation pipeline cutting manual effort 98% (~20 person-hours daily), fund reporting reduced from four days to three minutes across 34 fund managers, compliance and risk tools, and digital onboarding for UBL with 97-98% verification accuracy. Bank controls include least-privilege access, full logging, data residency, no model training on client data, human approval for money-moving actions, and bank-operated kill switches. Engineers need a decision-making sponsor, access to frontline staff and data, and early booking of core banking integration slots.

Key points

  • Embedded engineers build for the real process; brief-based pilots build for an imagined one.
  • Trade confirmation agents cut manual effort 98%, about 20 person-hours a day.
  • Fund reporting across 34 managers went from four days to three minutes.
  • Controls: least privilege, full logging, data stays in-bank, humans approve money-moving actions.
  • Engineers need a named sponsor, frontline access, and core banking slots booked weeks ahead.

In short

Financial services is where forward deployed engineering pays off most, because the work is document-heavy, rules-heavy and regulated — exactly the conditions where a pilot built from a brief fails and an engineer sitting with the operations team succeeds. FetchSky has built for banks, insurers and brokerages since 2015, runs a digital banking platform at 15 banks, and has put AI automations into production for trade confirmations, fund reporting and compliance checks.

Why does financial services need engineers inside the business?

Because the rules live in people’s heads and in month-end exceptions, not in the specification. A trade confirmation process looks simple until the engineer learns that a regulator requires each client to be confirmed individually, that the ledger arrives as one PDF, and that three clients have two phone numbers each. An engineer who has spent a week with the operations team builds for the real process. One working from a document builds for the imagined one.

What we built

What have forward deployed engineers built for financial firms?

  • Trade confirmations

    A three-agent pipeline that reads the end-of-day ledger, matches each trade to the client record, and sends a confirmation on WhatsApp within minutes. 98% less manual effort, about 20 person-hours a day, every message logged for compliance.

  • Fund reporting

    A monthly top-holdings report across 34 fund managers, from four days to three minutes.

  • Research

    A daily market newsletter drafted from international indices, commodity prices, forex rates and the day’s top five scored headlines, 80%+ faster.

  • Compliance and risk

    AI signature verification and multi-portfolio risk monitoring, with published time savings of 95% and 80%.

  • Payments

    POS settlement and merchant analytics, 90% time saved.

  • Onboarding

    Kashf for UBL, a three-step digital identity check with NADRA verification and video liveness, reaching 98% accuracy on Smart CNIC, 92% on Urdu CNIC and 97% on face verification.

How do you handle a bank’s controls?

Every system gets only the access its task needs. Everything it reads, decides and does is logged. Data can stay in the bank’s own cloud or country, and it is never used to train models. Anything that affects a customer or moves money goes through a person. Systems are tested against misuse before launch, monitored with alerts after, and can be switched off by the bank’s own operations team at any time. A free consultation and a six-week pilot come before any larger commitment.

What does the engineer need from the bank?

A named sponsor who can make decisions, access to the people who do the work, and access to the data and systems in scope, production or mirrored. Core banking integration slots usually need booking weeks ahead, so that goes on the list in week one. Historical data for testing is rarely available, so the engineer builds the test set deliberately.
