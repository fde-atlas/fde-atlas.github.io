AI Engineer Demand Jumps Sevenfold, Salaries Top $200,000 as Tech Industry's New Favorite

Business Media | Reported by He Ying-chen from Taipei

While the global tech industry continues to experience waves of layoffs, the artificial intelligence (AI) field is rising against the trend. In particular, demand for the "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" role, which specializes in deploying AI systems, has surged as much as sevenfold over the past year. According to a report by The Economic Times, these roles can command annual salaries of up to $200,000 (about NT$6.5 million), making them the new favorite of the tech talent market.

Job posting data shows that on the well-known job site Indeed, the number of Forward Deployed Engineer openings grew dramatically from 643 in April 2025 to 5,330 in April 2026, reflecting companies' urgent demand for such talent. These engineers are mainly responsible for building, managing, and deploying AI systems, improving model performance, ensuring AI tools can be reliably integrated into existing workflows, and helping teams use AI technology effectively and safely.

The report notes that tech giants and consulting firms including OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe, Google Cloud, Palantir, and McKinsey & Company's AI unit QuantumBlack are all actively recruiting Forward Deployed Engineers. In terms of compensation, companies are offering annual salaries ranging from $170,000 to over $200,000.

This surge in demand is mainly attributed to the rapid adoption of AI tools, which is prompting companies across industries to reassess their workforce needs and invest heavily in roles that help them build, manage, and deploy AI-driven systems. With a shortage of experienced AI talent, companies are generally willing to offer generous compensation to attract professionals with the required skills. This trend shows that the development of AI may not only eliminate some traditional jobs but is also creating entirely new, high-paying employment opportunities.
