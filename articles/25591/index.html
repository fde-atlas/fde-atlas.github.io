Position interviewing for: FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)

Job description (JD):

The FDE role targets candidates with AI technical understanding, business abstraction skills, customer communication skills, and lightweight delivery capabilities.

You will participate in frontline scenario surveys for enterprise AI projects, requirements clarification, business process decomposition, agent solution design, and delivery asset construction, helping the team translate customers' business processes, data structures, and system boundaries into AI application outcomes that are implementable, verifiable, and reusable.

This role focuses on enterprise-grade agents, multi-agent systems, enterprise Copilots, AI workflows, context engineering, model invocation governance, and human-machine collaboration loops. The role concerns not only solution design but also the creation of directly delivered content, including agent configuration, skill orchestration, context templates, knowledge systems, business rule libraries, prompt templates, eval metrics, and customer-facing operational guides, driving AI from proof of concept to real business scenarios.

(I) Job Responsibilities

1. AI scenario surveying and business process decomposition

Participate in customer business research, sorting out business processes, key roles, data sources, system interfaces, rule documents, and manual operation steps, and identify business scenarios suitable for enhancement through AI agents, enterprise Copilots, and automated workflows.

2. Agent solution design and value anchoring

Assist in designing solutions such as single-agent, multi-agent collaboration, context engineering, tool use, and human-in-the-loop, clarifying business value, usage boundaries, ROI calculation criteria, and pilot paths.

3. Direct-delivery agent design and configuration

Participate in agent delivery for customer scenarios, including role definition, task boundaries, input/output specifications, tool invocation lists, context reference rules, exception fallback strategies, and human confirmation node design, enabling agents to run stably on specific business tasks.

4. Skill and workflow delivery asset construction

Abstract high-frequency actions, standard processes, and operational rules in customer businesses into reusable skills, workflow nodes, or task templates, enabling agents to perform business actions such as querying, analyzing, generating, reviewing, assigning, summarizing, and reminding.

5. Context engineering and knowledge system construction

Focusing on enterprise knowledge, business rules, process states, historical records, user roles, permission boundaries, and other elements, participate in designing mechanisms for context collection, organization, compression, injection, updating, and evaluation; assist in building customer-usable knowledge systems, business glossaries, rule libraries, Q&A libraries, and scenario-based knowledge packages.

6. Customer requirement translation and technical feasibility analysis

Translate customer business language into product requirements, data requirements, interface requirements, context requirements, and delivery tasks; assist in assessing scenario feasibility, data readiness, model adaptation approaches, system integration challenges, and security and compliance requirements.

7. Solution materials and customer communication support

Participate in writing solution proposals, technical roadmaps, intelligent transformation white papers, PoC plans, pilot implementation plans, agent user guides, operations manuals, and other materials; support solution presentations to customer business departments, IT departments, and management.

8. Evaluation system and delivery loop design

Assist in defining evaluation metrics for agent systems, including task completion rate, tool invocation accuracy, context hit rate, knowledge coverage rate, manual time saved, invocation cost, exception fallback rate, etc., driving projects from demonstration demos to acceptable pilots.

9. Delivery process support and customer enablement

Participate in customer-side trials, feedback collection, effectiveness verification, and iterative optimization of agent, skill, knowledge system, and workflow assets; assist with customer training, launch preparation, acceptance materials, and delivery retrospectives.

10. Industry asset accumulation and capability feedback

Accumulate business processes, scenario templates, context templates, prompt templates, agent role configurations, skill templates, knowledge system structures, eval metrics, and interface requirements formed in projects into reusable assets, feeding back into industry solutions and delivery system development.
