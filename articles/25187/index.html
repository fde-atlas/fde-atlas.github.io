Summary

This is a coach profile on Prepfully, an interview-prep marketplace. The coach has 14+ years of experience across Oracle, Google, Palantir, Quip, Salesforce, Slack and startups, and is currently a Senior Director of Software Engineering at Meta. The profile lists 160 completed sessions, a 4.8 review score, and an 81.6% re-book rate. Coaching covers Palantir roles including Forward Deployed Software Engineer and Deployment Strategist, plus system design, decomposition (decomp) interviews, behavioral rounds, and SQL/ETL. Reviews repeatedly praise insider insight into Palantir's unusual interview format and the explanation of how FDSE roles differ from traditional SWE roles. Palantir coaches are typically overbooked and need a day or two of notice.
