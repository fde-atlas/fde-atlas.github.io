Field Delivery Engineer (FDE) Work Styles, Jobs, and Careers Based on First-Hand Overseas Information

Original title: FDEブログ|海外の一次情報で読むFDEの働き方・求人・キャリア

FDE Talent · 2026-09-29 · 12 min read

Summary

This blog from FDE Talent offers deep dives into forward deployed engineer (FDE) roles using primary overseas sources, with each post citing original job postings and announcements. Articles analyze hiring criteria and compensation at companies like OpenAI, Palantir, Salesforce, Datadog, and Fujitsu, compare FDE roles with PMs and solutions engineers, examine new FDE teams (Xero's Xforce, NCS's iFDE, Genesys, Lyra), and verify claims such as FDE postings growing 4.6x in a year. It also covers Japan-based openings, salary ranges, and vendor lock-in concerns.

Key points

  • Primary-source analysis of FDE job postings with cited origins.
  • Covers OpenAI, Palantir, Salesforce, Datadog, Fujitsu and more.
  • Compares FDE roles with PMs, solutions engineers, and bankers.
  • Verifies market claims, like 4.6x growth in FDE postings.
  • Tracks Japan-based openings, salaries, and new FDE teams.
