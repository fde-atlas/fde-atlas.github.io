Forward Deployed Engineer | Strac | Remote, US

Zero G Talent · 2026-10-25 · 3 min read

Summary

Strac, a Y Combinator-backed data security company offering DLP and DSPM across AI tools, SaaS, endpoints and browsers, is hiring a remote Forward Deployed Engineer in the US. The role owns each customer technically: winning proofs of concept, handling escalations for signed accounts, building docs, playbooks and AI agents for support, and converting customer feedback into prioritized product requirements. Candidates need 3+ years in customer-facing technical roles at B2B security or infrastructure companies, with DLP, DSPM, CASB/SSE or cloud security experience preferred. Compensation is $100K–$140K base plus variable pay tied to POC conversion and retention, plus equity. The hiring process includes a mock POC working session and moves within days.

Key points

  • Strac protects sensitive data leaking through AI tools, SaaS, endpoints and browsers, with 75+ customers including Databricks and UiPath.
  • Top priority is winning technical POCs, including tuning detection accuracy and running security reviews.
  • The engineer owns signed-customer support and builds AI agents and playbooks to scale it.
  • Requires 3+ years in customer-facing technical roles, comfortable with APIs, OAuth/SSO, logs and scripting.
  • Pay is $100K–$140K base plus variable tied to POC conversion and retention, plus equity.

About Strac

Strac is data security for the AI era. We stop sensitive data (PII, PHI, secrets, confidential files) from leaking through the tools employees actually use: AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot, MCP servers and AI agents, Slack, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, browsers and endpoints. One platform for DLP and DSPM across AI, MCP, SaaS, endpoint and browser, with real remediation (redact, revoke, delete, block) instead of another dashboard.

Five years in, we're Y Combinator-backed and in production at 75+ mid-market and enterprise customers including companies like Databricks, UiPath and Crypto.com. We're an Anthropic partner integrating with the Claude Compliance API, and a Slack, Notion, Zendesk and Intercom partner. The founder is an ex-Amazon Principal Engineer who built Amazon's payment security infrastructure.

The role

You'll be the technical owner of every new Strac customer, from first evaluation through long after signature. Your number-one job is getting proofs of concept to a technical win. Once a customer signs, you own their support. And because you're closest to customers, you'll turn what they need into clear, prioritized asks for our engineering team. You'll work alongside our account executives and directly with the CEO and engineers every day.

In priority order:

  • Win POCs. Qualify them with the AE, translate customer requirements into written success criteria and a time-boxed plan, run setup and onboarding (Slack, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot, MCP, endpoints and browsers), and proactively drive detection accuracy: review findings, dig into logs and model output, tune policies and prompts to fix false positives and negatives, and test the changes. Run demos, security reviews and the final readout to the security team.
  • Own live customers. Be the first and usually last line for escalations, config issues and accuracy problems on signed accounts, so our engineers can stay on the product.
  • Scale support. Build the docs, playbooks, tooling and AI agents that let customers and our support agent handle routine questions themselves.
  • Shape the roadmap. Turn customer feedback, feature asks and recurring issues into well-written, prioritized requirements, and work with engineering on what gets built next.

Who we're looking for

  • 3+ years in a customer-facing technical role (solutions or sales engineering, forward-deployed engineering, technical support or customer success engineering) at a B2B security or infrastructure company. DLP, DSPM, CASB/SSE, email or cloud security experience strongly preferred
  • Genuinely technical: comfortable with APIs, OAuth/SSO, reading logs, and writing scripts or prompts to test and tune detections. Software engineering background is a plus
  • You've run enterprise POCs or deployments and can hold a room with a CISO or a skeptical security engineer
  • You can write a clear requirement and argue for its priority
  • Extreme ownership: a stalled POC or an unhappy customer is your problem to fix, not something to escalate
  • Clear writer, fast mover, uses AI tools aggressively
  • Former startup founder or early team member is a plus

Compensation

$100K–$140K base plus variable tied to POC conversion and customer retention, plus equity. Remote in the US.

Process

One 30-minute call with the CEO, a working session where you run a mock POC with our engineering team and account executive, references, offer. We move in days, not weeks.
