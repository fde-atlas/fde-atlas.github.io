The Complete Tencent Cloud ADP FDE Exam Process: A Module-by-Module Breakdown of the 7 Modules in the Official Exam Syllabus

Original title: 腾讯云 ADP FDE 考试全流程，官方考纲 7 个模块逐条拆解

FDE 指南 · 2026-10-26 · 5 min read

Summary

This article breaks down Tencent Cloud's ADP Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) certification exam based on the official guide. The exam is 60 questions (40 single-choice, 20 multiple-choice) in 90 minutes, offline at test centers, with a 70/100 passing score. The author corrects three widespread errors: the question count (90 is the duration), the claim of three attempts per registration (FDE allows only one), and the belief that it can be taken online. Multiple-choice questions carry no partial credit and are worth 40 points total, making them the biggest scoring risk. The article converts the official syllabus weights into question counts, prioritizes the two largest modules (solution design and core features, together 47%), and suggests a study order: online course first, then hands-on practice with ADP documentation, module self-checks, and mock exams.

Key points

  • Exam format: 60 questions, 90 minutes, offline, passing at 70/100.
  • Multiple-choice questions are worth 40 points with no partial credit.
  • One registration covers only one attempt; retakes require repurchase.
  • Solution design and core features modules account for 47% of questions.
  • Recommended prep: course, hands-on ADP practice, module checks, mock exams.

Official Basic Information

Tencent Cloud ADP FDE is just one of several FDE certifications on the market. Before reading further, first clarify what the other certifications are to avoid mixing up rules from different systems.

The following information comes from the Tencent Cloud "ADP Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Certification Exam Guide", last updated on September 10, 2026.

ItemDetails
Certification nameTencent Cloud ADP Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)
Exam typeTheory exam
Exam formatIn-person (computer-based at domestic test centers)
Question types and count40 single-choice questions + 20 multiple-choice questions, 60 questions in total
Exam duration90 minutes
Passing score / Full score70 / 100 points
Exam languageSimplified Chinese
Score releaseWithin 3 days after the exam, viewable in Tencent Cloud Training and Certification under "Personal Center - Certification Exams"

There are two rules that are easy to overlook but directly affect your score:

For multiple-choice questions, both wrong selections and missing selections receive no points. No partial credit, no half points. This means that whether a multiple-choice question is "choose 2 out of 4" or "choose 3 out of 5", being off by even one selection zeroes out the question.

This is an in-person exam, not online. When registering, you need to book a test center, so plan your travel and time off in advance; don't prepare at the pace of an online exam.

Three common misconceptions online

When searching for FDE exam rules, three sets of numbers are most easily misquoted. All of them directly change your exam preparation plan; here they are aligned with the official wording:

Common claimOfficial statementWhat's wrong / What it affects
90 questions, 120 minutes, 60 to pass60 questions, 90 minutes, 70 to pass"90" is the duration, mistaken for the question count; pacing yourself for 90 questions will underestimate the time pressure of the multiple-choice section
One registration includes 3 exam attemptsFDE allows only 1 attempt; if you fail or miss the exam, you must purchase again, with no free retakeThe 3 attempts belong to a different certification category; budgeting for FDE based on it will miss the retake cost
You can take it online from homeIn-person computer-based exam at a test centerYou need to book a city and seat in advance, check in 30 minutes early, and bring valid original identification; preparing as if it were an online exam will cause you to miss your session entirely

Why the "3 attempts" claim isn't an official typo. Tencent Cloud Training and Certification's registration rules are split into two documents by product line: the "Individual Registration and Exam Guide" states at the beginning that its scope excludes "practitioner certifications" and covers specialized technical certifications, which includes FDE, stating that "one registration includes only one exam attempt"; the "Practitioner Certification Registration and Exam Guide" covers the certifications with 3 attempts. The two documents are complementary, not contradictory. Strictly speaking, this belongs to registration rules, but it often appears alongside exam rules in searches, so it is included here as well.

About the "90 questions". The official exam guide states 40 single-choice + 20 multiple-choice = 60 questions, 90 minutes. Confusing the duration with the question count is a common mistake; at 60 questions × 90 minutes, that's an average of 1.5 minutes per question. If you actually prepared for 90 questions, your strategy for the multiple-choice section would be completely different.

Time limits for booking, refunds, and rescheduling are not covered here; see the full process from registration to certification.

An easily overlooked score structure

Back-calculating from the official question types (40 single-choice + 20 multiple-choice) and a full score of 100, the per-question point values are:

  • Single-choice: 40 questions × 1.5 points = 60 points
  • Multiple-choice: 20 questions × 2 points = 40 points

In other words, the 20 multiple-choice questions account for 40 points. The passing line is 70 points; if you get half of the multiple-choice questions wrong (10 wrong), you lose 20 points directly, and the remaining 40 single-choice questions must be almost all correct to pass.

This is why many people feel after the exam that they "got all the single-choice questions right", yet score below 70 when results come out. No partial credit on multiple-choice questions is the biggest point-loss trap in this exam, and also where you should focus most of your preparation effort.

Preparation priorities by module

Ranked by "question count × risk of losing points", not simply by weighting:

First priority: ADP Core Features and Application Building (13 questions) + Scenario Understanding and Solution Design (15 questions)

Together these are 28 questions, 47% of the exam, and both are comprehension-based questions that require hands-on platform practice to answer correctly. These two areas are the foundation for passing; it's hard to score points by reading documentation alone without hands-on practice.

Second priority: Security, Compliance, and Responsible AI (6 questions) + Delivery Implementation, Evals, and Operations (7 questions)

13 questions, 21% of the exam. The characteristic of these two areas is that the rules are explicit and the boundaries are clear; they are the "memorize it and you score" type, offering the highest return on investment. Security and compliance in particular are well suited to a concentrated cram in the two weeks before the exam.

Third priority: Industry Solutions and Case Implementation (9 questions)

15%, testing the mapping between industry pain points and delivery boundaries. You don't need deep knowledge of any specific industry, but you should know "which solution fits which industry".

Last: AI and Generative AI Fundamentals (6 questions) + ADP Product Positioning and Capability Boundaries (4 questions)

10 questions, 17%. Basic concept and positioning questions are the easiest points to score for anyone with some AI background. But don't skip them just because they "feel easy"—losing points on basics is the most regrettable.

Recommended study order

The official learning resources are two things: the online course and the ADP product documentation. It's safest to proceed in this order:

  • First go through the online course to build an overall framework. The course's purpose is not to memorize details, but to let you know what capabilities ADP has and where its boundaries lie.
  • Read the documentation while practicing hands-on in ADP. How to build a knowledge base, how to configure chunking strategies, how to connect workflow nodes, how to integrate MCP—these only stick if you've done them yourself. Purely memorizing the docs is basically ineffective for this exam.
  • Self-check against the 7 modules in the exam outline. For each module, can you name 3 specific features and 1 typical use case? If not, you haven't mastered it.
  • Use mock exams to verify. Draw 60 questions under the official ratio, 90 minutes, the same scoring rules, then look at your score rate per module and focus on improving modules below 60%.

Registration

Registration and exam scheduling follow the official guidance of Tencent Cloud Training and Certification; the individual registration entry can be found on the Tencent Cloud "Training and Certification" page. Exam fees, exam cities, and available time slots are subject to what is displayed in real time on the registration page—the official exam guide document does not list fees, so for amounts circulating online, defer to the registration page and do not rely on third-party articles.
