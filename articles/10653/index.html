CFDE - FDE Course & Certification

Artificial Intelligence Uni (AIU.ac) · 2026-10-08 · 11 min read

Summary

Artificial Intelligence Uni (AIU.ac) offers the Certified Forward Deployed Engineer (CFDE) programme, a course-plus-exam certification for engineers deploying AI systems at client sites. It has three levels — Foundation (startups/SMEs), Professional (enterprise), and Specialist (six regulated-industry tracks) — each an 18-week online cohort of 15 weekly modules plus a 3-week capstone, at roughly 8 hours per week. The course and exam are separate products; experienced engineers can take the exam directly via a challenge route. Piloted in London in 2025 and opened globally in 2026, the next Foundation cohort starts 14 December 2026, with fees from £549 to £1,099 depending on scholarship tier.

Key points

  • CFDE certifies FDEs at three levels: Foundation, Professional, and Specialist.
  • Each level runs 18 weeks: 15 modules plus a 3-week capstone, ~8 hours weekly.
  • Course and exam are separate; a challenge route lets experienced engineers skip the course.
  • Piloted in London in 2025; next Foundation cohort starts 14 December 2026.
  • Fees range from £549 to £1,099 depending on scholarship tier and application date.

What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

A forward deployed engineer is a technical practitioner who embeds directly with customers: a customer-facing software engineer who embeds with a customer team to build, adapt, deploy and operate AI systems in the customer’s own environment, across the deployment lifecycle: post-sale handoff, discovery, scoping, build, deploy, iterate, hand-back.

Palantir created the role in the early 2010s. They called them Deltas. Until 2016, Palantir employed more FDEs than it employed software engineers. The job: take the company’s product, sit with the client, make it work in their environment, and bring what you learned back to the product team.

The role has since been adopted across the AI industry. OpenAI established an FDE team in early 2025, growing from two engineers to more than ten across eight cities. Anthropic, xAI, Databricks, Scale AI, Ramp, and Salesforce all employ Forward Deployed Engineers.

Palantir’s description remains the clearest: FDE responsibilities look similar to those of a startup CTO. You work in small teams and own end-to-end execution of high-stakes projects.

An FDE embeds with the customer. Understands their data, their constraints, their workflows. Builds a working solution on top of the company’s platform. Feeds improvements back to the core product. A consultant advises. An FDE ships.

How FDEs differ from adjacent roles

Solutions Architect

Advisory, not hands-on

Builds proofs of concept with anonymised data. Rarely writes code on customer infrastructure. Recommends approaches. Does not own the deployment outcome.

Solutions Engineer

Pre-sales, not post-sale

Demonstrates the product during the sales cycle. Hands off to implementation teams after the deal closes.

Technical Consultant

Scoped engagement, then exit

Engaged for a defined scope. Delivers recommendations or a project. Leaves. Does not contribute to the vendor’s product.

Forward Deployed Engineer

Builds, ships, stays

Writes code on customer infrastructure. Embeds with the client team. Owns the deployment outcome. Contributes improvements back to the core product. Stays until the system is in production and the client team can operate it independently.

About CFDE

CFDE is a structured certification for Forward Deployed Engineers, established by Artificial Intelligence Uni, the sole issuer and owner of the CFDE credential specification at Foundation, Professional and Specialist levels. The programme comprises a training course and a separate certification examination. The standard was designed in 2025, piloted in London in 2025, and opened globally in 2026.

AIU developed the curriculum from active deployment practice across regulated and general industries, including energy, legal, tax, healthcare, education and governance. The programme draws on real production systems built and operated in these sectors, not on theoretical case studies. The research arm, Sovereign AI Lab (SAIL), publishes on the governance, evaluation, and deployment frameworks that inform the Professional-level modules. 7 published papers with DOIs. 3 open models on HuggingFace.

The programme was delivered on-premises in 2025. That delivery validated the curriculum and informed the current structure: Foundation for startups and SMEs, Professional for enterprise environments, Specialist for regulated industries. The October 2026 cohort of CFDE Foundation (2026-C3-CFDE-F) reached capacity and closed to new applications on Monday 5 October 2026; it commences Monday 19 October 2026. The December 2026 cohort (2027-C1-CFDE-F) is open to applications and commences Monday 14 December 2026.

That 2025 cohort was delivered and mentored in person by Noman Shah and Hassan Karim, two senior technology leaders with decades of combined experience taking AI and enterprise systems into real client environments. Learners trained directly under practitioners who have built and deployed the systems the curriculum teaches, not classroom theorists.

The course teaches. The exam certifies.

The CFDE credential is earned by passing a standardised examination. There are two routes to the exam.

Course route

Learn, then certify

Complete the CFDE training course at your chosen level. Work through the modules, the labs, and the capstone project. Then sit the CFDE certification exam.

Challenge route

Prove what you know

Engineers already working in forward deployed or closely related roles may sit the certification exam directly without completing the course. The exam assesses the same competencies regardless of route.

The course and the exam are separate products. Examinations open to CFDE Foundation graduates from March 2027.

Certification levels

Each level certifies a distinct scope of practice. Foundation is not a watered-down Professional. It certifies a complete FDE.

Who is this programme for?

The CFDE course is open to anyone, and is designed for experienced software, data, and engineering professionals. We recommend it for software engineers, ML engineers, data engineers, DevOps engineers, and solutions architects who want to move into forward deployed engineering or formalise their existing deployment practice.

The CFDE certification exam has experience prerequisites. Foundation requires 2+ years and Professional requires 4+ years of relevant experience in software engineering, ML engineering, data engineering, DevOps, solutions architecture, or a closely related discipline. Relevant experience means building and deploying software systems, not engineering in unrelated fields.

Working proficiency in Python, Git, Docker and at least one cloud platform is expected; these tools are used throughout and are not taught from scratch.

CFDE FAQs

Who is the CFDE course for?+

Software engineers, ML engineers, data engineers, DevOps engineers, and solutions architects who want to move into forward deployed engineering or formalise their existing deployment practice. The course is open to anyone. The certification exam requires 2+ years relevant experience (Foundation) or 4+ years (Professional) in software engineering or a closely related discipline.

When does the next cohort start?+

The December 2026 cohort of CFDE Foundation (2027-C1-CFDE-F) commences on Monday 14 December 2026. Applications are open now; enrolment closes Monday 30 November 2026. The October 2026 cohort is at capacity and closed to new applications. Professional and Specialist cohort dates will be announced separately.

How is the programme assessed?+

Each level runs for 18 weeks: 15 weekly modules followed by a 3-week capstone. The Foundation capstone is a three-week capstone in which each learner ships a working AI system against a realistic client brief in a simulated engagement, assessed by practitioner reviewers against the programme’s assessment criteria.

What is the time commitment?+

18 weeks in a structured cohort. Suggested commitment of approximately 8 hours per week. Content is delivered through recorded lectures and course materials on the AIU learning platform, with instructor-led discussion and feedback and human-marked assignments.

When can I sit the CFDE certification exam?+

The course and the certification exam are separate products. Certification examinations open to CFDE Foundation graduates from March 2027. Exam registration details, fees and the retake policy will be published to graduates before the first examination window.

How do I apply?+

Applications are made online at apply.aiu.ac and take about ten minutes. There is no application fee; any scholarship band is confirmed before payment is requested.

Is there an application fee?+

No. The programme fee is payable on application, after your scholarship band (if applicable) has been confirmed, and is refunded in full if your application is not accepted.

Is a scholarship available?+

Yes. The AIU Access Scholarship offers a limited number of fee reductions for nationals of economies classified by the World Bank as low income or lower-middle income, and for applicants currently between roles. See the Admissions and Scholarship page for tiers, dates and how to apply.

What will I be able to do after completing the programme?+

Foundation graduates can own an AI deployment end to end at a startup or SME: run customer discovery, design the solution architecture, build on messy client codebases, integrate LLMs and data pipelines, deploy to production, and hand off to the client team. Professional graduates can do the same inside complex enterprise environments with legacy systems, compliance obligations, multi-stakeholder politics, and air-gapped infrastructure.

Do I need to take the course to earn the CFDE credential?+

No. The course and the certification exam are separate products. The course teaches the skills. The exam certifies them. Engineers with relevant professional experience can sit the exam through the challenge route without completing the course. Course graduates and challenge candidates take the same standardised examination.

What experience do I need for the certification exam?+

Foundation requires 2+ years of relevant experience. Professional requires 4+ years plus either CFDE-F or a passing score on the challenge exam. Relevant experience means software engineering, ML engineering, data engineering, DevOps, solutions architecture, or a closely related discipline involving building and deploying software systems. Working proficiency in Python, Git, Docker and at least one cloud platform is expected.

Who teaches the programme?+

The programme is led by Noman Shah, FBCS, who built the deployment practice the curriculum teaches. The academic team includes Dr Atif Naseer (PhD, Senior Lecturer at Umm Al-Qura University, 60+ publications) and Dr Salman Naeem Khan (PhD, Associate Professor at COMSATS University). Guest practitioners from active FDE and AI deployment roles contribute to specific modules. All instructor profiles are verifiable through Google Scholar, ORCID, and LinkedIn.

What is the difference between Foundation and Professional?+

Foundation certifies a complete FDE for startups and SMEs. Professional adds the enterprise layer: legacy system integration, identity management, air-gapped deployments, compliance frameworks, multi-stakeholder navigation, and board-level communication. Both levels run for 18 weeks with the same structure. Foundation is not a watered-down Professional. It is a separate scope of practice.

Can my employer fund this programme?+

Yes. We issue a sponsorship letter and a pro-forma invoice on request before payment. Employers may pay directly or reimburse the applicant. Contact admissions@aiu.ac.

What is your refund policy?+

Applications that are not accepted are refunded in full. Cancellations within 14 days of payment are refunded in full. Cancellations after 14 days and before the cohort commences are refunded less a £77 administration fee. No refunds are made from the commencement date. In cases of hardship, deferral to a later cohort may be requested by the end of week 4, subject to availability. Full details are in the Refund Policy.

What happens after I pass?+

You receive a verifiable digital certificate with a unique credential ID, a digital badge for LinkedIn and professional profiles, and a detailed transcript. Employers can verify your credential status through a public verification page on aiu.ac. Credential holders join the CFDE alumni network for continued professional development and peer connection.

How do I prepare for the programme?+

The course assumes working proficiency in Python, Git, Docker and at least one cloud platform. These tools are used throughout and are not taught from scratch. If you are not sure whether your background is a fit, contact admissions@aiu.ac and we will advise.
