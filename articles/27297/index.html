Forward Deployed Accountant | LiveFlow | New York, NY

Zero G Talent · 2026-11-12 · 2 min read

Summary

LiveFlow is hiring a Forward Deployed Accountant in New York with a base salary of $115,000–$150,000. The company combines AI, automation, and hands-on accounting experts to run month-end close, reconciliations, and reporting for multi-location restaurant and hospitality groups. It has raised over $21M from investors including YC, Valar, and Seedcamp, and its founding team previously worked at Revolut. Candidates must be qualified accountants with 3+ years owning month-end close end-to-end, experience deploying automation and AI in accounting, and willingness to work in person at customer offices.

Key points

  • Requires CPA, ACA, or ACCA qualification and 3+ years owning month-end close end-to-end.
  • Candidates should have deployed automation and AI in accounting work.
  • The role involves traveling to customer offices and being embedded in their workflows.
  • Responsibilities include running close for restaurant and hospitality clients and converting broken workflows into product improvements.
  • LiveFlow has raised over $21M; its founding team came from Revolut.

About LiveFlow

LiveFlow is on a mission to redefine how businesses manage their finances. We combine AI and automation with hands-on accounting experts to run month-end close, reconciliations and reporting for multi-location businesses, especially restaurant and hospitality groups.

We have raised over $21M from top-tier investors including YC, YC Continuity, Valar, Seedcamp, WndrCo, Moonfire, Bradley Horowitz (VP Product, Google) and more.

We help thousands of companies streamline their financial workflows, and we have hundreds of 5/5 ratings on G2.

Our founding team previously worked at one of the fastest-growing fintechs, Revolut.

What you’ll do

  • Run close for our restaurant and hospitality customers with AI and automations, end-to-end: daily entries, weekly reconciliations, period close, reporting.
  • Travel to our customers’ offices to work with them in person and get fully embedded in their workflows.
  • Turn the workflows that break into product improvements.

This job is for you if

  • You are a qualified accountant (CPA, ACA, ACCA). An FP&A background is a plus.
  • You have owned a month-end close start to finish, ideally for a multi-entity company. Hospitality, restaurants, or other multi-location operators are a plus.
  • You are a true builder who has deployed automation and AI in accounting work.
  • You are thorough and meticulous, very hands-on and in the details. You are excited to reconcile to the cent from the original sources.
  • You want to be working on the ground with customers.
  • You ask probing questions and enjoy discovery: understanding how a customer’s business and workflows really run is a big part of this role.
  • Beyond the close, you have a track record of driving projects end-to-end.
  • You are presentable and can build trust with both CFOs and non-accountants: business owners and general managers.
  • You want to work in person in New York, and you’re ready to travel to customer offices as part of the job.

Base salary

$115,000 – $150,000 per year.

What we look for in team members

  • Extreme ambition: You break barriers and redefine what’s possible.
  • Critical thinking: You question assumptions, and you learn independently and quickly.
  • Initiative: You take the initiative and lead.
  • Curiosity: You ask thoughtful, challenging questions.
  • Work ethic and ownership: Extraordinary results are never the product of mediocre effort.
