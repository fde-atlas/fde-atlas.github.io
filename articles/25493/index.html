See who's hiring in tech in 2026

Live Data Technologies · 2026-11-10 · 3 min read

Summary

Live Data Technologies, which tracks job changes for about 124 million professionals, counted US hires into forward deployed engineer roles from other employers between 1 January and 6 November 2026. Palantir led with at least 151 hires, followed by Google with at least 122 and Accenture with 50. Forward deployed engineer is the fastest-growing engineering title in Lenny's State of the tech job market report. Palantir once accounted for roughly half of such hiring, but more than 2,400 FDEs were hired elsewhere in 2026 so far. Because job changes take months to surface in public sources, recent-month counts are a floor.

Key points

  • Palantir hired at least 151 forward deployed engineers in 2026, the most of any company.

Source: Professional histories compiled from public information on the open web, covering about 124 million professionals. Each record has an employer, a job title and start and end dates.

What a hire is: Someone starting a role at an employer they did not work for before. Promotions and internal moves are not hires. Interns, contractors, students and freelancers are left out.

Roles: Matched on job title, from individual contributor to executive. Engineering specialties, such as front-end, platform or forward deployed engineer, are matched on the specialty in the title.

Who and where: People based in the US, hired by employers in any sector, not only tech companies.

Period: Calendar years 2021 to 2025, and 2026 so far. The list on this page runs to 6 November 2026.

Recent months: Job changes take months to appear in public information, so the latest months keep rising after they are pulled.
