Forward Deployed Engineer & Applied AI | IIT Roorkee Course

Futurense · 2026-03-21 · 8 min read

Summary

IIT Roorkee, in partnership with Futurense Technologies, offers a 3-month Post Graduate Certificate in Forward Deployed AI Engineering aimed at fresh graduates and professionals with 0–3+ years of experience. The hybrid, weekend-online program (~85 learning hours) covers FDE mindset, data pipelines, prompt engineering, RAG, agentic AI, and deployment, with hands-on work through the Futurense AI Clinic on real enterprise projects. Learners build 15+ portfolio artefacts and an end-to-end capstone. The fee is ₹49,999 plus 18% GST, with a ₹5,000 non-refundable registration fee, a mandatory pre-screening test, and a sponsored 2-week bridge course. The first cohort is tentatively September 2026.

Key points

  • 3-month IIT Roorkee certificate program (~85 hours) in Forward Deployed AI Engineering, delivered via weekend live online sessions.
  • Curriculum spans FDE problem structuring, prompt engineering, RAG, agentic AI, LLMOps, and client-facing deployment practice.
  • Futurense AI Clinic provides real enterprise projects; learners produce 15+ portfolio artefacts including a capstone.
  • Fee is ₹49,999 plus 18% GST; ₹5,000 non-refundable registration; EMI options available.
  • Applicants need Python basics and must pass a 45-minute pre-screening test; first cohort tentatively September 2026.

The program is the Post Graduate Certificate in Forward Deployed AI Engineering. The certificate is issued by the Continuing Education Centre (CEC), IIT Roorkee.

A Forward Deployed Engineer is a client-facing AI professional who works directly with enterprises to design, prototype, validate, and deploy AI systems that drive measurable business outcomes.

Unlike traditional software engineers who work in linear build cycles, FDEs operate in rapid iteration cycles—Discovery → Prototype → Validate → Ship → Iterate—owning deployment and business impact end-to-end.
