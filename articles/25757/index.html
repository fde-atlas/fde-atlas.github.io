OpenAI Deployment Company vs Ryz Labs (2026): Facts

Ryz Labs · 2026-11-15 · 4 min read

Summary

This vendor-written comparison from Ryz Labs contrasts its AI pod service with the OpenAI Deployment Company (DeployCo), which launched May 11, 2026, majority owned by OpenAI with over $4 billion in initial investment and Goldman Sachs as a founding partner. DeployCo is acquiring Tomoro (about 150 forward deployed engineers) and Northslope, but publishes no pricing; the widely cited $10 million figure comes from 2025 reporting on OpenAI's earlier consulting. Ryz charges $7,000–$15,000 per engineer monthly, builds with both Anthropic and OpenAI models in client clouds, and names engineers before signing. The author discloses the conflict of interest and advises readers to match choice to their scope.

Key points

  • DeployCo launched May 11, 2026, majority owned by OpenAI with over $4 billion in initial investment.
  • Tomoro acquisition adds roughly 150 forward deployed engineers; Northslope acquisition announced July 8, 2026.
  • DeployCo publishes no pricing; the $10 million figure predates it and concerns OpenAI's earlier consulting.
  • Ryz prices $7,000–$15,000 per engineer monthly with written quotes and named engineers before signing.
  • Ryz pods build with Anthropic and OpenAI models in client clouds, starting within two weeks.

Full text

Disclosure: we're Ryz Labs. We wrote this comparison and we are one of the options discussed. Facts about the OpenAI Deployment Company come from its own site and launch release, listed under Sources. Compare both against your real scope before you decide.

The OpenAI Deployment Company, often called DeployCo, launched on May 11, 2026. It is majority owned and controlled by OpenAI and sends forward deployed engineers to build production systems around OpenAI's models. Pick it if you want the model maker's own team, you can wait for an engagement, and you are comfortable building on one model provider.

Pick Ryz if you want a senior AI pod team working in your cloud, repos and standups on US business hours, building with Anthropic and OpenAI models, with a written price and named engineers before you sign. Our public price is $7,000 to $15,000 per engineer per month. DeployCo has not published pricing.

  • Launch and ownership. Announced May 11, 2026, majority owned and controlled by OpenAI, with more than $4 billion of initial investment from a group of investment firms. Goldman Sachs is named as a founding partner.
  • Engineers. OpenAI agreed to acquire Tomoro, which the release says brings about 150 forward deployed engineers and deployment specialists, subject to regulatory approval. On July 8, 2026 DeployCo listed an agreement to acquire Northslope on its news page.
  • How it works. The release describes a focused diagnostic, then a few priority workflows that its engineers design, build, test and deploy as production systems.
  • Who it serves. Organizations with complex problems, and the release points to the more than 2,000 businesses its investors back.
  • How to engage. The site's contact button opens an email to the company. No eligibility rules or minimums are published.
  • Pricing. Not published. A figure of at least $10 million per client circulates online. It was reported by The Decoder, citing The Information, in July 2025, about OpenAI's consulting before DeployCo existed, so it is not a DeployCo price.

Closest possible link to OpenAI. If your plan depends on early access to OpenAI's research, product teams or roadmap, a company OpenAI controls is the shortest path. No independent team can offer that.

Capital and brand. More than $4 billion of initial investment and the OpenAI name carry weight with a board. For some programs, that signal is the point.

Investor networks. The release says DeployCo will reach the businesses its backers support. If your company sits in one of those portfolios, you may get an introduction others can't.

A price you can see today. We publish ours: $7,000 to $15,000 per engineer per month, and an example pod of five senior engineers runs about $50,000 to $75,000 a month. You get it in writing before anything starts.

More than one model provider. Our pods build with Anthropic and OpenAI models in your AWS or Azure cloud, your GitHub and your Postgres. If a different model wins on cost or quality for a workflow, you can switch without changing teams.

Named engineers, a fast start. You meet the engineers before the engagement starts, and teams start in under two weeks once you say go. Most of our engineers ship code in their first week.

Production proof. Our marketing-compliance case covers 8,000+ marketing materials a year for a leading alternative asset manager, with review turnaround cut from days to hours. Our voice-platform case reports one million calls in a single day.

You own everything. The client always owns the code and IP our engineers write, the work happens in your accounts, and you control access.

DeployCo has not published pricing, minimums or contract terms. Ask for them in writing early, along with how long the diagnostic takes and what it costs. Ryz quotes per team: mid-level engineers $7,000 to $10,000 a month and senior engineers $10,000 to $15,000, with no proprietary platform to license.

  • If you need OpenAI's own people and roadmap access, pick DeployCo.
  • If your board wants the OpenAI name on the program, pick DeployCo.
  • If you want to start this quarter with a known price, pick Ryz.
  • If you want to build with more than one model provider, pick Ryz.
  • If you want the team working in your repos and reporting to your leads on US hours, pick Ryz.
  • If you want both, use DeployCo for strategy access and a Ryz pod to build and run the systems day to day.
