20 Free AI Automation Tools (No Credit Card)

AY Automate · 2026-09-20 · 2 min read

Summary

AY Automate lists 20 free AI and automation tools requiring no credit card, spanning three categories: cost calculators, assessments, and generators. Calculators compare n8n, Zapier, and Make subscription pricing, estimate AI agent per-run costs, compute automation payback periods, and weigh building versus buying AI systems or staffing in-house versus outsourcing. Assessments cover AI readiness across five pillars, AI governance maturity on a 0-4 scale, and workflow automation audits. Generators produce AI job descriptions and use cases with ROI projections. A Forward Deployed Engineer salary explorer reports compensation ranges with cited sources.

Key points

  • 20 free tools, no credit card needed
  • Calculators compare n8n, Zapier, Make pricing
  • AI agent cost and payback period estimators included
  • FDE salary explorer cites sources for every figure
  • Assessments score AI readiness and governance maturity
