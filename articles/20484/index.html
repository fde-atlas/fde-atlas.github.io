Founding Forward Deployed Engineer | Revion | Remote, US

Zero G Talent · 2026-03-30 · 1 min read

Summary

Zero G Talent lists a founding Forward Deployed Engineer role at Revion, a voice AI startup backed by Y Combinator and 8VC. Revion's product turns technician speech into structured repair orders, inspections, and time logs for dealerships. The remote (US) role blends customer-facing work with hands-on development, from onboarding through ongoing platform use. Requirements emphasize communication, managing multiple projects under deadlines, and a founder mindset; preferred skills include React, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, AI/ML application building, API integrations, and LLM prompting. Compensation includes salary plus equity.

Key points

  • The product converts technician speech into repair orders, inspections, and time logs for dealerships.

  • Preferred stack: React, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, AI/ML, APIs, and LLM prompting.

What you need

This role requires both strong technical skills and the ability to communicate effectively with a range of stakeholders. You're a versatile engineer who thrives in a role that blends customer engineering with technical development. You will work closely with our customers from onboarding through to their ongoing use of Revion's platform, ensuring they maximize the value of our solutions.

Must Have

  • Ability to prioritize and manage multiple projects under tight deadlines
  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to explain technical concepts clearly
  • Founder mindset: hard work + independence + ownership

Tech Profile - Bonus Points

  • Comfortable Full-Stack: React, TypeScript, Node.js
  • Strong proficiency in Python with hands-on experience
  • Experience building AI/ML applications
  • Manage APIs and other tools for robust integration with various third-party systems
  • LLM prompting and tuning of voices and transcribers — constant iteration to perfect use cases
  • Building new features or entire MVPs

Why Join Us

  • Ground Floor Opportunity — Join early and shape the company, product, and culture from day one
  • Rapidly growing and backed by top investors like Y Combinator and 8VC
  • Ownership & Autonomy — Take full ownership of projects and ship fast
  • Top-Tier Compensation — Competitive salary + equity in a high-growth startup
  • Work With the Best — Join a world-class team of engineers and builders
