The FDE Transition, a curated reading path

Falkster.AI · 2026-11-15 · 4 min read

Summary

Falkster.AI's 17-part reading series maps the forward deployed engineer role: what it is, why it is scarce, how to measure it, and where it should sit in an organization. The author argues FDE is AI's fastest-growing but least-defined seat, that the shortage is a product problem rather than a talent problem, and that FDE output must become roadmap, evals, and handback packets. Later parts cover comp, career exits, contract clauses, and org placement under the CPO.

Key points

  • A 17-part free series, updated weekly, written as a reading path.
  • Only 2,000 of 17,000 US FDEs deliver real ROI; demand is up 2,100%.
  • Gartner predicts 70% of enterprises will abandon FDE-built agentic AI by 2028.
  • The series argues the FDE org belongs under the CPO, not sales.
  • Anthropic plans to train 10,000 FDEs, with AWS credentialing them.

The shift

What changes when AI changes the product?

Forward deployed engineers are the fastest-growing seat in AI and the least defined. This series is written for the people in the seat and the leaders building around it: what the role is, what product, engineering, and sales owe it, what it owes them back, and how the work becomes roadmap. It is the FDE seat of The Product OS. A new part every Wednesday, written against that week's findings. Read in order; part 1 is the thesis.
