Member of Technical Staff, Forward Deployed | Vapi

Zero G Talent · 2026-03-24 · 2 min read

Summary

Vapi, a San Francisco platform for deploying voice agents, is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer. The company claims 600k+ developers after two years, adding 2,000+ daily, with Enterprise ARR growing roughly 5x year over year and customers including NYLife and Intuit. The role's stated ramp: deploy a large enterprise onsite within 30 days, find second and third use cases by 60 days, and build product that makes similar deployments trivial by 90 days. Candidates should have technical experience at early startups or prior FDE roles; founding experience is a plus. Vapi says 70% of staff are former founders, backed by YC, KP, and Bessemer.

Key points

  • Vapi is a voice agent deployment platform with 600k+ developers, growing 2,000+ daily.
  • Enterprise ARR is growing ~5x YoY, with customers like NYLife and Intuit.
  • The FDE role: onsite enterprise deployment in 30 days, expansion by 60, productization by 90.
  • Candidates need early-startup technical experience or prior FDE work; founding a startup is a bonus.
  • 70% of employees are former founders; investors include YC, KP, and Bessemer.
