OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer Interview (2026)

Orava · 2026-10-25 · 8 min read

Summary

This article breaks down OpenAI's forward deployed engineer (FDE) interview process for 2026. The loop follows OpenAI's standard stages: résumé review (~1 week), introductory calls, a skills-based assessment whose format varies by team (pair coding, take-home, or technical test), then 4–6 hours of final interviews with 4–6 people, with a decision in about a week. A May 2026 candidate reports a one-week take-home building semantic search over Amazon products for ChatGPT, a live walkthrough, and an AI-enabled coding screen. The piece includes 10 sample questions across behavioral, product, technical, system design, and case categories, plus preparation tips. Posted pay for FDE roles in San Francisco, Seattle, and New York is $185K–$300K plus equity.

Key points

  • Process: résumé review, intro calls, skills assessment, then 4–6 hours of final interviews.
  • Assessment format varies by team; a May 2026 candidate got a one-week take-home project.
  • Sample questions test end-to-end delivery, full-stack coding, LLM judgment, and evals.
  • FDEs write production frontend and backend code and travel up to 50%.
  • Posted pay is $185K–$300K plus equity, including Healthcare, Legal, and Financial Services teams.

At a glance

  • Process
  • Résumé review, introductory calls, a skills-based assessment, then 4–6 hours of final interviews with 4–6 people over 1–2 days. A decision usually follows within a week.
  • Format
  • Virtual by default, or onsite in San Francisco if you prefer. AI tools are allowed in some interviews and not others; your prep materials say which.
  • Pay
  • $185K–$300K plus equity on the FDE postings in San Francisco, Seattle and New York, including the Healthcare, Legal and Financial Services teams (checked Oct 25, 2026).
  • What it tests
  • Owning deployments from prototype to production, full-stack coding, LLM product judgment, evals, and working directly with customer teams.

The OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Process

What to expect at each stage of the OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer loop.

  • 1

    Application and résumé review

    About 1 weekOpenAI says the recruiting team typically takes one week to review your résumé and email you back.
  • 2

    Introductory calls

    A conversation with the hiring manager or a recruiter about your work, motivations and goals. OpenAI suggests reading its recent updates, especially those related to the team you're interviewing for.
  • 3

    Skills-based assessment

    OpenAI says formats vary by team and may include pair coding, take-home projects or technical tests, and some roles have more than one. One May 2026 FDE candidate reports a one-week take-home, a team discussion of it, and an AI-enabled coding screen.
  • 4

    Final interviews

    4–6 hours, 1–2 daysTypically 4–6 interviewers, focused on your area of expertise. For engineering interviews OpenAI looks for well-designed solutions, high-quality code, optimal performance and good test coverage.
  • 5

    Decision

    About 1 weekOpenAI says to expect a decision within a week of the final interviews. Your recruiter may ask for references at this stage.

Behavioral Questions

3 questions

  • 1

    Tell me about a complex system you took from first prototype to stable production with a customer or partner team.

    What they're checking: End-to-end ownership. The posting asks FDEs to "own technical delivery across multiple deployments from first prototype to stable production."
  • 2

    Tell me about a time you traded off scope, speed and quality to protect a delivery.

    What they're checking: Judgment under delivery pressure. The posting asks you to "make trade-offs between scope, speed, and quality" and adjust plans to protect delivery.
  • 3

    Tell me about a time you spotted a risk early on a project and changed course without slowing the team down.

    What they're checking: Risk sense and calm. The posting looks for people who "spot risks early and adjust without slowing down" and stay calm "when the stakes are high."

Product Questions

1 question

  • 1

    You've built nearly the same integration for three customers. How would you turn it into something other FDEs can reuse?

    What they're checking: Leverage beyond one account. FDEs are asked to "codify working patterns into tools, playbooks, or building blocks that others can use."

Technical Questions

3 questions

  • 1

    In a pair-coding interview, you're asked to build a small service that takes customer documents and returns structured data using a model API. What do you build first, and how do you test it?

    What they're checking: Practical coding against OpenAI's stated bar for engineering interviews: well-designed solutions, high-quality code, optimal performance and good test coverage.
  • 2

    Give an example where how the model behaved changed the product experience you built.

    What they're checking: LLM product judgment. The posting asks for people who have built with LLMs and "understand how model behaviour affects product experience."
  • 3

    How would you use evals to show a deployment is working, and to give feedback that changes OpenAI's product or models?

    What they're checking: The posting measures success through "production adoption, measurable workflow impact, and eval-driven feedback that changes product and model roadmaps."

Preparation Tips for OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer Interviews

  • Ask your recruiter early what the skills-based assessment is for your FDE team, since OpenAI says formats vary by team and can include pair coding, a take-home or a technical test.
  • If you get a take-home, treat it as a customer engagement: one May 2026 candidate says the evaluation came down to explaining decisions and tying them back to customer needs.
  • Prepare one deployment story you owned end to end, with the scoping, the trade-offs, the production rollout and a measured outcome.
  • Practice coding a small full-stack feature against a model API with validation, error handling and tests, since OpenAI looks for high-quality code and good test coverage.
  • Build or rehearse an eval for an LLM feature from real cases, and be ready to explain how it changed a decision.
  • Read OpenAI's recent product and research updates, especially for the team you're interviewing with, as OpenAI recommends.
  • Know OpenAI's published values and operating principles and have an example for at least two of them.

Frequently Asked Questions - OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer

OpenAI's postings say FDEs "lead complex end-to-end deployments of frontier models in production alongside our most strategic customers." They own discovery, technical scoping, system design, the build and production rollout, write production code across frontend and backend, and feed field results back to Product and Research. Success is measured by production adoption, workflow impact and eval-driven feedback.

OpenAI publishes one process for all roles: a résumé review (about a week), introductory calls, a skills-based assessment, then 4–6 hours of final interviews with 4–6 people over 1–2 days, and a decision within about a week. It says the assessment varies by team and may include pair coding, a take-home or a technical test. It doesn't publish an FDE-specific version. One candidate who interviewed in May 2026 reports a one-week take-home to build semantic search over Amazon products for ChatGPT, a live discussion of that solution with the team, and an easy, AI-enabled coding screen, with the process taking about two weeks. They said "the take home was basically the job."

When checked on October 25, 2026, the FDE postings in San Francisco, Seattle and New York, including the Healthcare, Legal and Financial Services teams, listed $185K–$300K plus equity. Forward Deployed Software Engineer postings listed $153K–$325K or $185K–$325K, and Deployment Lead postings $230K–$294K, each with equity. Ask your recruiter where your level sits.

From the postings: FDEs own deployments end to end, from discovery to production. Forward Deployed Software Engineers build custom software on OpenAI's APIs alongside FDEs, design reusable abstractions, and the San Francisco posting asks for 7+ years of full-stack experience. Deployment Leads own the delivery plan across FDEs, researchers and customer engineers, plus value cases and ROI, and the posting asks for 7+ years of customer-facing delivery leadership.

The FDE postings ask for production-grade code across frontend and backend in Python, JavaScript or comparable stacks, experience building or deploying systems powered by LLMs or generative models, and usually 5+ years of engineering or technical deployment experience that includes customer-facing work.
