Summary

Japanese consultancy At Stream Consulting offers a "Direct Build" service in which consultants with Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) skills handle everything from field interviews and requirements gathering to AI/IT implementation and adoption. Instead of the traditional division of labor between consultants and engineers, which causes handover losses and rework, the service runs rapid "listen, build, use" cycles of roughly one to two weeks. Deliverables include AI agents, RAG applications, and data extraction tools, with use cases in sales and planning functions such as proposal drafting, meeting minutes, and budget aggregation.

Key points

  • FDE-skilled consultants cover listening, building, and adoption end to end.
  • Cycles of one to two weeks replace traditional consultant-engineer handovers.
  • Deliverables include AI agents, RAG apps, and data extraction tools.
  • Use cases span sales and planning: proposals, minutes, quotes, budget reports.
  • Goal is sustained field adoption and a self-driven AI culture.

Direct Build — Business Consulting × FDE

Main Service Contents

From hearing on-site challenges and understanding/organizing business operations, to implementing AI/IT tools and ensuring adoption, consultants with "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)※" skills—combining consulting and engineering—handle the entire process end to end.

Starting from "working artifacts" rather than proposals, we rapidly iterate short cycles of "listen, build, use" to support AI adoption that takes root on-site and continuously delivers results.

On-site-driven AI/IT tool development (advancing the direct build cycle)
Service Features: Four Value Propositions

By cycling through "build fast ⇔ understand deeply," we improve the quality and volume of work while keeping investment and on-site burden low, and contribute to cultivating employee mindset and organizational culture.

Service Approach: Advancing the Direct Build Cycle
  • Listen Understand and organize the business and its challenges
  • Build Select technologies and implement working artifacts
  • Use Try them out on-site
  • Listen Improve based on on-site feedback

By running this cycle at high speed and measuring changes and effects, we steadily create results.

Difference Between the Conventional Approach and Direct Build

Conventionally, a "division-of-labor style" was common, where consultants organized requirements and engineers implemented them. However, this approach inevitably involves handovers, often leading to rework due to misinterpretation and long lead times before anything works. With direct build, the consultant handles everything from "hearing" to "implementation" end to end, reflecting on-site intentions and concerns into working artifacts as fast as possible.

Implementation Schedule Image

By repeating multiple cycles of about 1–2 weeks, we develop usage scenarios, and by reviewing results, we ensure continuity and adoption of the activities.

On-Site Adoption and Cultivating an Autonomous AI-Use Culture
Building deliverables and mechanisms that can be used continuously on-site

We provide not just systems, but tools and products built in line with on-site business objectives and decision criteria. At the same time, we establish practical deliverables rooted in business operations and mechanisms for ensuring adoption.

  • Deliverables usable on-site (prototypes and improved versions) e.g., AI agents for specific tasks, RAG applications, data extraction tools, etc.
  • Feedback reflection records: Making it possible to review "why it was built this way." e.g., usage scenario lists, deliberation and change histories, etc.
  • Self-sufficiency support documents: Manuals and procedures from a business process perspective. e.g., operation manuals, modification procedures, etc.
  • Initiative summary materials: Visualizing effects and on-site changes. e.g., lists of initiative effects and changes (workload reduction, business changes, literacy changes), etc.
