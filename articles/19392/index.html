Building a customer onboarding team? Start here

Rocketlane · 2022-11-16 · 3 min read

Summary

This Rocketlane blog post collects advice from practitioners on building a customer onboarding organization from scratch, focusing on how to define and measure KPIs. Common baseline metrics include projects completed per person, post-onboarding CSAT, ACV of onboarded accounts, and average onboarding duration. The author recommends balancing a throughput metric (implementations per month) with a customer sentiment metric like NPS or CSAT, then layering in finer tracking—such as short-term time logging—to answer nuanced questions, like whether high-ARR customers justify longer implementations. An end-of-onboarding customer survey is suggested as a simple starting point for fast iteration.

Key points

  • First onboarding hires struggle to define success metrics.
  • Baseline metrics: projects per person, CSAT, ACV, onboarding time.
  • Balance throughput metrics with customer sentiment metrics.
  • Layer in granular tracking like hours as the org matures.
  • End-of-onboarding surveys enable quick iteration.

Being the first customer onboarding hire can be overwhelming. You are chasing every opportunity to reduce time to value and increase customer adoption. You try playbooks, tailor them to work for your business, and go through many iterations. Soon enough, you will need to answer questions like:

  • What successful approaches can you take to ensure your team is doing what they should be doing?
  • How can we set KPIs that uniquely align with our goals?

Preflighters discuss the foundational building blocks that helped them build a successful customer onboarding org.

We have a lengthy implementation and onboarding phase for our product, and we have been having a lot of discussions around KPIs and how we can measure success with our customer onboarding process. We are currently working on a customer survey to collect feedback that we will send out at the end of onboarding. It is an excellent place to start since it helps you understand what your customers think and iterate very quickly!

When I started at my current company three years ago, I had exactly these questions. Here are a few things that are consistently tracked across most customer onboarding teams:

  • Volume driven per individual on your team (number of customer onboarding projects completed in X time frame)
  • CSAT scores sent out after customer onboarding has been completed
  • ACV of accounts moved through onboarding per individual
  • Time to complete onboarding (on average) per individual, per team, per department

Once you have a baseline of metrics you can track and measure, you can expand from there.

I recommend optimizing for two metrics that create a system for checks and balances. I look at the throughput, i.e., pure output like the number of implementations completed each month + a customer sentiment metric. Post-implementation, it can be Implementation-NPS/CSAT.

As the customer onboarding org matures, you can start to layer in more and dial in on behavior depending on your business needs. Here are really nuanced questions you answer when you layer in more between two metrics:

  • Should implementation for high ARR customers be longer and more in-depth because we can afford that?
  • Having your team track hours, even for a short period, will allow you to get to the granular details and make better-informed decisions.
