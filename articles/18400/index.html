FDE Support | FDEs on the Front Lines, Turning AI into Business Results

Original title: FDE支援｜FDEが現場に入り、AIを業務の成果に変える

Summary

This is a service page from Japan's FDX株式会社 describing its FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) offering. FDX's FDEs embed at customer sites to turn AI into measurable business results, following four steps: diagnose, design, implement, and transfer. Results are shown as measured reductions in processing volume and time. The stated goal is to make the FDE unnecessary: FDEs build first, then shift to reviewing as in-house staff take over, targeting self-sufficiency within roughly 6–12 months. Engagements can start with a 1Day AI assessment or a 1Day FDE plan with weekly on-site visits. The page contrasts FDEs with full-time hires, SES contractors, and consultants, and extends the FDE mindset to non-engineer roles via FDX Training.

Key points

  • FDX's FDEs work on-site through diagnose, design, implement, and transfer steps.
  • Impact is measured by before/after processing volume and time, checked weekly.
  • Exit design phases out FDE involvement over roughly 3–12 months.
  • Positioned against full-time hires, SES resident contractors, and consultants.
  • The FDE approach extends to non-engineers via FDX Training.

Four Steps: Diagnose, Design, Implement, Transfer

FDE — Diagnose, Design, Implement, Transfer

FDX's FDEs start by deciding which operations to apply AI to, and finish with handover to your internal team.

Diagnose

We take inventory of your operations and prioritize which tasks to reduce with which AI, and by how much. Many engagements start with a 1-Day AI Assessment.

Design

We define the division of roles between people and AI, exception-handling paths, and metrics for measuring results.

Implement

We build workflows, automate, and configure tools, working hands-on alongside your team.

Transfer

We hand over prompt design and operating procedures to your team members.

Beyond Engineers

Extending the FDE Mindset Beyond Engineers

FDX extends the FDE mindset to all professions beyond engineering. What's needed is not turning every employee into an engineer, but developing people in each role who can transform operations using AI. Combined with training (FDX Training), we leave implementation capability in-house.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an FDE?

An FDE is a role and organizational model for engineers who go on-site with customers and turn AI and software into business results. Palantir systematized it in the 2010s. The role covers finding problems on-site, implementing with AI, making it stick, and measuring results.

How is it different from on-site staffing (SES)?

Both involve being at the customer's site, but the purpose and responsibility differ. SES primarily provides labor hours. FDEs use improvement of the customer's operational KPIs as their success metric and take responsibility from strategy through implementation to operational handover.

What does FDX's FDE support include?

We proceed through four steps: diagnose, design, implement, and transfer. We prioritize operations, build workflows and automate alongside your team, and hand over operating procedures to your team members.

Does FDX offer its FDEs to external companies?

Yes. FDX Inc. is a Japanese company that offers its own FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) as a corporate service. FDEs go on-site at your company and handle diagnosis, design, implementation, and transfer. You can start with a plan where an FDE is on-site one day a week (1-Day FDE).

Can we request this without full-time on-site presence?

Yes. We offer a plan (1-Day FDE) where an FDE is on-site one day a week, running implementation and training every week. Progress on non-on-site days is recorded in FDX's proprietary progress management tool and shared through regular meetings and monthly reports.

What remains in-house after the contract ends?

The goal is to make the FDE unnecessary. In the first three months, the FDE builds and the team uses; in months three to six, the team builds and the FDE reviews; and in months six to twelve, we aim for a setup that runs entirely in-house.

Can we use this without in-house engineers?

Yes. FDX extends the FDE mindset to all professions. What's needed is not turning every employee into an engineer, but developing employees in each role who can transform operations using AI.

Where should we start?

Many cases start with a "1Day AI Assessment," which takes stock of your business operations and summarizes which tasks can be reduced by which AI, and by how much per year. You can consult with us even if you haven't conducted one yet.
