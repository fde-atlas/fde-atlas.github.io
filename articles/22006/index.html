PERSOL WORKS SWITCH CONSULTING Launches New "AI Consulting" Service Providing End-to-End Support for AI Transformation

Summary

Persol Works Switch Consulting launched an AI consulting service on August 26, 2026, as the core offering of its new "Switch → AI-Native" brand. ~ A "Team-based FDE" model where client companies and our experts work as "one team" to handle everything from AI strategy to implementation, adoption, and internalization ~

Persol Works Switch Consulting Co., Ltd. (Head office: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; President and CEO: Takamasa Ono; hereinafter "Persol Works Switch Consulting"), a company of the Persol Group whose vision is "Hataraite, Haraou." ("Work and Smile"), announced that on August 26, 2026, it began offering a new service, "AI Consulting (Switch → AI-Native with AI Consulting)," designed to elevate corporate AI utilization from "introduction" to "results."

As the core service of the AI transformation support brand "Switch → AI-Native," which launched the same day, this service provides specialized services covering AI strategy, business transformation, AI agents, organizational/talent transformation, AI governance, and more, bundled flexibly across domains according to each client's challenges. As its delivery model, we have established a "Team-based FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" approach in which client companies and our experts form a single team, handling everything from AI strategy formulation through business transformation, AI implementation, and adoption/internalization with the same team, without fragmentation.

Background of the Service Launch — AI use remains "individual and isolated," failing to reach company-wide results

AI utilization in society is beginning to shift from generative AI use to business AI use (AI agents, AI integrated with core systems, etc.). While adoption is spreading rapidly, at many companies AI use and proof-of-concept efforts remain confined to "individuals" or "individual tasks" and do not translate into company-wide results. In our "Survey on the Actual State of Generative AI and RPA Adoption 2025," 52% of companies that introduced AI reported tangible benefits such as reduced workload, faster processing, and fewer errors, while the lack of specialized personnel ranked among the top challenges for company-wide rollout going forward. In the field, we frequently hear comments such as the following:

"We can't decide what to do" — meetings stall when trying to identify target processes "There's no one in-house to consult with" — assigned as a side duty, I'm the most knowledgeable person in the company and have no one to turn to "We're doing it, but it doesn't produce results" — small efficiency gains occur, but they don't add up to company-wide results

To elevate AI utilization toward generating results across entire business operations, we believe it is necessary for personnel capable of handling strategy, business transformation, implementation, and adoption to drive the effort consistently across these continuous phases.

Service Overview — A team structure spanning phases supports business results without losing momentum

Our Technology Consulting Division has supported business process reengineering (BPR) and the introduction and adoption of AI and automation at numerous companies, including major enterprises in finance, manufacturing, and retail. This new "AI Consulting" service is newly launched as a support service specialized in the AI domain, built on this track record and expertise.

The service provides end-to-end support from AI strategy formulation through business transformation, implementation, and adoption/internalization, aiming to transform companies into organizations that can turn AI into organizational capability. It targets companies facing challenges such as: AI use concentrated among certain employees or processes, inability to move from PoC to production, business processes and decisions dependent on specific individuals, and multiple AI and automation products not connected for overall optimization. The delivery format is a consulting service through a "Team-based FDE" execution team that works alongside the client, designed individually according to the client's challenges, target processes, and scope of support.

The service connects AI use that had been limited to individual use and isolated proof-of-concepts to company-wide results, and through our support, decision criteria and knowledge accumulate within the organization, enabling clients themselves to continue operating and improving.

Key Features of the Service

Feature 1: Client and our company work as "one team," accompanying end-to-end from strategy to internalization Our experts work in the same team as the client's AI promotion staff, IT/DX departments, and business departments. By supporting "strategy, business transformation, implementation, and adoption" — which are typically fragmented — as a continuous flow without switching personnel, we prevent handover losses.

Feature 2: Seven roles and 217 specialized professionals (cumulative) Experts in seven roles participate in projects as needed, covering strategy formulation, business design, AI technical design, implementation, adoption and talent development, and industry expertise. Rather than depending on specific individuals, a broad range of expertise is bundled as a team, enabling us to handle large-scale transformations spanning multiple domains. This fiscal year, we aim to develop more than 300 professionals (cumulative).

Feature 3: A multi-vendor structure combining optimal products based on challenges Rather than starting from a specific product, we select and combine optimal products from multiple offerings — including UiPath, Microsoft (Copilot/Copilot Studio), ServiceNow, and Salesforce — starting from the client's business challenges. We also provide feedback to product vendors as appropriate, driving updates and contributing to environment optimization.

Case Studies — A broad track record of support from strategy to adoption

① Strategy — Corporate Planning: AI-Native Strategy Formulation Support With AI use skewed toward efficiency, we conducted executive-participation workshops aimed at rebuilding AI strategy with the AGI/ASI era in mind. Using backcasting, we organized the AI vision, investment strategy, and promotion structure, supported the formulation of medium- to long-term roadmaps and execution plans, strengthened participants' thinking and transformation-driving capabilities, and built the foundation for strategy execution.

② Business Transformation — Telecommunications: Business Improvement and Citizen Developer Adoption Support To achieve employee-driven business improvement, we provided consistent support from process visualization through BPR promotion, RPA introduction, and citizen developer development. By generating 130 improvement proposals and developing 11 citizen developers, we achieved a reduction of over 500 hours per month and built a foundation for continuous business reform.

③ Implementation — Finance: AI and Business Automation Implementation Support We analyzed business challenges in each department and provided accompanying support from target process selection through PoC and business implementation. Using Copilot, Power Automate, RPA, and other tools, we achieved efficiencies such as advanced document search and inquiry handling, automation of monthly report distribution, and creation of stock price simulations, generating an estimated annual reduction of more than 5,000 hours.

④ Retention - Finance: AI/Automation Talent Development and Retention Support In response to the difficulty of sustaining business improvement due to the shortage of digital talent, we provided hands-on, side-by-side training using real work as material, QA support, development of learning content, and establishment of a certification system. To date, we have trained over 300 development professionals as well as in-house trainers for internal capability building, establishing an internal development platform where improvement progresses autonomously.

Comment from the Person in Charge

Toshio Oshima, Partner and General Manager, AI & Business Automation Department, Technology Consulting Division, Persol Workswitch Consulting Co., Ltd.

We have supported the business transformation of many clients through technology consulting. In the age of AI, what matters is not simply introducing AI, but standing on the same team as our clients and carrying initiatives through from concept to implementation and adoption without interruption. In AI consulting, we combine our expertise with our clients' knowledge into a single team, working to ensure that results take root in business operations and that clients can continue improving on their own.
