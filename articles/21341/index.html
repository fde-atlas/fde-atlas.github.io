Joins Salesforce's "Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network" to expand Agentforce success

Original title: Salesforceの「Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network」に参画、Agentforceの成功を拡大

NTTデータグループ · 2026-06-08 · 5 min read

Summary

NTT Data Group has been selected as a partner in Salesforce's Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network, launched in 2026 to speed up production deployment of the Agentforce AI agent platform, and is an initial partner in Japan. The selection recognizes NTT Data's Agentforce implementation track record and enterprise system integration skills. As a certified partner, NTT Data will support clients in four areas: integrating advanced technology (data structuring, context, permission design), meeting security and regulatory requirements including AI guardrails, building orchestration infrastructure such as APIs and data pipelines, and assisting production rollout. The company plans to expand Agentforce deployment support in Japan and global markets.

Key points

  • NTT Data Group joined Salesforce's FDE Partner Network as an initial Japan partner.
  • The network helps move Agentforce from pilots to production use.
  • Support covers data integration, security, compliance, and AI guardrails.
  • NTT Data will also build orchestration infrastructure like APIs and pipelines.
  • The company plans to expand Agentforce support in Japan and globally.

Third-Party Evaluation

June 30, 2026

NTT DATA Group Corporation

NTT DATA Group Corporation, NTT DATA Corporation, and NTT DATA, Inc. (collectively, "NTT DATA Group") have been selected as a partner in Salesforce's "Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network" (hereinafter, "FDE Partner Network"). Partners are selected based on criteria including their track record of Agentforce implementations and technical expertise. NTT DATA Group was selected as an initial partner in Japan, recognized for its knowledge of Salesforce implementation and adoption support developed both in Japan and overseas, as well as its enterprise system implementation capabilities.

About the Salesforce FDE Partner Network

A partner program launched by Salesforce in 2026 to accelerate production deployment of Agentforce, Salesforce's AI agent platform.

Background and Rationale for Joining

As enterprises evolve toward the agentic enterprise (see note), AI adoption is shifting from the trial-implementation stage to a stage where concrete business outcomes are expected. However, many companies remain at the stage of proof-of-concepts or trial point solutions, and challenges remain in moving to production use and enterprise-wide rollout. The Salesforce FDE Partner Network helps resolve these challenges by providing the advanced engineering capabilities required to handle complex security environments and proprietary data environments.

As a certified partner, NTT DATA Group will support customers' use of AI agents by leveraging its technical expertise and production implementation capabilities. Specifically, it will provide support in the following areas.

Advanced Technology Integration

To ensure AI agents function properly in business operations, NTT DATA Group will help organize complex data structures and design the necessary context and permission management for safe operation.

Addressing Regulatory Environments

In industries where high reliability is required, NTT DATA Group will build AI adoption environments that meet security and regulatory requirements. In preparation for production use, it will also build AI guardrails based on strict security standards to support the safe and appropriate operation of AI agents.

System Orchestration

NTT DATA Group will support the building of integration infrastructure—such as system integration, APIs, and data pipelines—needed for AI agents to function across the entire operating environment.

Production Deployment Support

By combining years of industry knowledge with Salesforce's engineering methods and know-how, NTT DATA Group will rapidly support customers with complex data environments and business requirements in adopting AI agents.

This partner certification recognizes NTT DATA Group's technical expertise in Agentforce and its track record of implementation support in its Salesforce business. To date, NTT DATA Group has collaborated with its global locations to share the latest technology trends and knowledge gained from projects with customers in Japan and each country. Through this, it supports the transition from trial implementation to production operation—a key challenge in Agentforce adoption—and contributes to customers' business growth and value creation.

With this certification, NTT DATA Group will provide ongoing specialized knowledge and technology governance to address the complex challenges associated with AI adoption. This will support the advancement of customers' AI agent adoption and sustainable value creation.

Endorsement

Atsushi Urano, Executive Officer and Head of the Alliance Business Headquarters at Salesforce Japan, said: "Customers seeking success in the AI agent era can concretely leverage Salesforce's advantages through our partner ecosystem. The Salesforce Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network extends the deep expertise and specialized training developed within Salesforce to a select network of partners. This gives customers the engineering depth and confidence needed to move from the pilot stage to production and achieve business outcomes at scale."

Looking Ahead

Through the FDE Partner Network, NTT DATA Group will further strengthen its global collaboration with Salesforce. Going forward, it will expand support for production deployment of Agentforce in Japan and global markets, while promoting solutions that combine industry-specific knowledge with AI technology.

NTT DATA Group's Vision for the Future of AI Adoption

NTT DATA Group will drive the transformation of its customers' businesses and its own business through the twin pillars of proactive AI adoption and governance. For customers' businesses, it aims to realize "Smart AI Agent®," in which AI agents respond to instructions and autonomously extract, organize, and execute tasks in target operations. For its own business, it will accelerate significant productivity improvements in software development and the development of practically skilled AI talent. Through these efforts, NTT DATA Group will pursue AI-native business process reform, contribute to solving social issues such as labor shortages, and realize a world where people can focus on high-value-added work.
