Buyer's guide6 questions

What is the best AI automation agency in the USA?

On this page, XRAY: it prints a flat $250 an hour and a $15,000 design sprint, and Zapier's directory lists it Platinum in New York with 111 reviews at 5.0. Flow Digital if you want to start with one $297 session from the most reviewed agency here. Connex Digital if you want to pay $250 an hour and need AI voice agent work.

How much do AI automation agencies charge?

Of the agencies read for this page, the printed hourly rates are $150 at n8n Lab, $250 at XRAY and $250 at Connex Digital. A first session is $297 at Flow Digital. Projects and sprints run from $5,000 at McBride Method to $5,000 to $15,000 at Leanware and $15,000 at XRAY. Monthly plans run from $25 a week for monitoring at Automation Ace to $9,900 a month at n8n Lab.

Is an AI automation agency worth it?

When you can name the workflow and price the hours it wastes, yes, and a 4 to 10 hour block at $250 is a cheap test. When you cannot say what you want automated, an hourly agency will build what you ask for, not what you need, and a fixed price assessment comes first. Census Bureau survey data shows fewer than 20 percent of the smallest United States firms used AI through May 2026, so most owners are starting from zero.

How do I check an agency's reviews?

Read them on the platform's directory, not the agency's homepage. Zapier's Solution Partner Directory shows tier, review count, minimum spend and location, and you can read the one star reviews there. Two of the agencies on this page claim a different tier or location on their own site than Zapier shows.

Zapier, Make or n8n: does the agency's platform matter?

Yes, because you will pay for it every month after the agency leaves. Most agencies here build on Zapier. Connex Digital, Flow Digital, GetUWired and Automation Ace also name Make and n8n. n8n Lab works only on n8n. Ask which platform the agency recommends and why before you sign.

What happens after the agency finishes?

Automations break when an app changes a field or a token expires. Only Automation Ace prints a price for watching them, $25 a week. Ask every agency who notices when a workflow stops, and what that costs.

How to choose

  • 1Write down the one workflow you want fixed and how many hours a week it costs you before you book a call. Then a $250 hour has something to be measured against.
  • 2Read the agency's listing on Zapier's Solution Partner Directory, not only its own site. Check the tier, the review count, the minimum spend and the one star reviews. XRAY, Flow Digital, Connex Digital, GetUWired, MercOlogy and McBride Method are Platinum, and Automation Ace is Gold.
  • 3Ask whether you build with them or they build for you. XRAY's hourly service and Connex Digital's sessions are live work on a call with you. Flow Digital's sessions are similar. If you want it handed back finished, ask for a project price.
  • 4Ask who owns the accounts and the work. Your Zapier, Make or n8n account should be in your company's name, and so should any AI keys.
  • 5Ask what happens the week a workflow stops. Automation Ace prints $25 a week for monitoring. n8n Lab prints support plans from $900 a month. Everyone else should be asked.
  • 6If your problem is that four systems hold four versions of the truth, one more automation will not fix it. That is a job for someone inside the business who owns the joins, and the wildcard entry exists for that case.
