FDE and Enterprise AI Agent Industry Report

Original title: FDE 与企业 AI Agent 行业报告

FDECareer.work · 2026-11-15 · 2 min read

Summary

This FDECareer.work report hub compiles industry research on forward deployed engineers and enterprise AI agents, aimed at delivery engineers and tech leads. It compares 676 verified job postings (as of 2026-11-15) across Databricks, OpenAI, Palantir, Anthropic, and Mistral AI, covering job family naming, seniority thresholds, city distribution, and salary disclosure, plus a separate slice of 177 China-based roles. A broader sample of nearly 700 domestic postings maps the split between big-tech agent platform engineering and private-sector LLM deployment. Additional resources include a whitepaper on AI delivery in classified/air-gapped environments, a hands-on manual built around after-sales ticket workflows, and a ten-point architecture assessment for SaaS-to-AI transitions. All materials come with reproducible CSV templates and offline drills that never touch real models or production systems.

Key points

  • Compares 676 verified job postings across five major AI companies as of November 2026.
  • Includes an independent slice of 177 China-based FDE roles with reproducible CSV data.
  • Maps China's dual-track market: big-tech agent platforms vs. government/enterprise private deployment.
  • Whitepaper defines three isolation levels: offline rooms, data diodes, and VPC deployment.
  • Provides offline drills and CSV templates that never connect to real models or systems.
