Infosys to hire 6k FDE; to roll out wage hike in October & January

Deccan Herald · 2026-07-23 · 1 min read

Summary

Infosys plans to hire 6,000 forward deployed engineers over the next few years. These engineers embed with client teams to move AI and digital projects from pilot to full deployment. CEO Salil Parekh highlighted Topaz Fabric, a platform that lets clients keep data sovereignty, use any foundational model, and optimize token costs. The company recruited 20,000 graduates in FY26 and expects similar hiring through FY27, with 4,000 freshers already added in Q1FY27. Headcount stood at 328,062 as of June 30, 2026, up 4,274 year over year but down 532 sequentially. Wage hikes will roll out in two phases: most employees in October, senior staff in January. Attrition fell to 13% in Q1FY27 from 14.4% a year earlier.

Key points

  • Infosys will hire 6,000 forward deployed engineers over the next few years.
  • Topaz Fabric keeps client data sovereign while allowing any foundational model.
  • 20,000 graduates hired in FY26; similar hiring planned through FY27.
  • Wage hikes come in October for most staff, January for senior employees.
  • Attrition dropped to 13% in Q1FY27 from 14.4% a year ago.

Full text

Bengaluru: Infosys will hire 6,000 forward deployed engineers (FDE) over the next few years. FDEs are the ones that embed with client teams to take AI and digital projects from pilot to deployment. "We have built a platform Topaz Fabric that enables our clients to get the benefits of AI while keeping the sovereignty of their own data within their company. Our clients are able to work with any foundational model. Our clients are able to optimise token cost which has become very critical now," Infosys CEO and MD Salil Parekh said.

He also announced that the company has recruited 20,000 college graduates in FY26 and plans to hire similar numbers through FY27. It has recruited 4,000 freshers in Q1FY27. Though the company's headcount rose by 4,274 YoY as of June 2026, its headcount reduced by 532 employees sequentially. The company had a workforce of 328,062 employees as of June 30, 2026.

The CEO also informed that the company will roll out wage hikes in phases- one in October and another in January. CFO Jayesh Sanghrajka said most of the employees will get it in October and the senior employees will get it in January. The company's attrition rate stood at 13 per cent in Q1FY27 compared to 14.4 per cent in the year-ago period.
