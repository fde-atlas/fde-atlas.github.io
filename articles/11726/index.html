Essential Maths for FDEs: A Practical Mathematics Primer

FDE Interviews · 2026-10-07 · 5 min read

Summary

FDEInterviews.com has published "Essential Maths for FDEs," a 66-page practical mathematics primer for forward deployed engineers who need to justify customer-facing decisions with numbers. It contains 15 core lessons spanning units and ratios, vector retrieval geometry, probability and evaluation uncertainty, attention, floating point, memory budgets, and queues, plus a lesson on chart selection and a worked release-review case. A companion ZIP offers standard-library Python calculators that pass 21 test groups and detect five deliberate defects. All examples are synthetic teaching data, and the full download requires paid Premium access; three sample pages are public.
