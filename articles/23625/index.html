Forward Deployed Engineering Intern | Dime | New York, NY

Zero G Talent · 2026-05-04 · 1 min read

Summary

Dime (DimeHealth AI), a New York startup building software that helps healthcare organizations streamline operations and automate administrative workflows, is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineering Intern. The role focuses on customer onboarding and deployment: configuring API integrations with external systems, troubleshooting deployment issues, customizing workflows for customer needs, and working with engineering and product teams to improve deployment processes and document best practices. Candidates need at least one programming language (Python or JavaScript), familiarity with APIs and system integration, and strong communication skills for working with non-technical stakeholders. Healthcare or regulated-environment experience is a plus. The internship offers hands-on deployment experience and potential for a full-time offer.

Key points

  • Dime is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineering Intern in New York.
  • Work covers onboarding, API integrations, and deployment troubleshooting.
  • Requires Python or JavaScript plus API and integration basics.
  • Healthcare or regulated-environment experience is preferred.
  • Strong performers may receive a full-time offer.

What you need

About DimeHealth AI

DimeHealth AI builds software that helps healthcare organizations streamline operations, improve patient access, and automate manual workflows. Our platform integrates with existing systems and enables teams to move faster with less administrative overhead.

Role Overview

We’re looking for a Forward Deployed Engineering Intern to help bring new customers onto the platform and ensure successful deployments.

This role sits at the intersection of engineering, product, and customer success. You’ll work closely with both internal teams and external stakeholders to implement solutions, troubleshoot issues, and ensure systems are running smoothly in real-world environments.

What You’ll Do

  • Support onboarding and implementation for new customers
  • Configure integrations with external systems and APIs
  • Troubleshoot technical issues across deployments
  • Assist in customizing workflows based on customer needs
  • Collaborate with engineering and product teams to improve deployment processes
  • Help document best practices and implementation patterns

What We’re Looking For

  • Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail
  • Experience with at least one programming language (Python, JavaScript, or similar)
  • Familiarity with APIs and basic system integration concepts
  • Ability to work in ambiguous, fast-paced environments
  • Strong communication skills and willingness to work with non-technical stakeholders

Nice to Have

  • Exposure to healthcare systems or regulated environments
  • Experience working on technical projects end-to-end
  • Interest in applied AI or workflow automation

What You’ll Get

  • Hands-on experience working on real-world deployments
  • Exposure to cross-functional work across engineering and operations
  • Opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing startup environment
  • Potential for a full-time offer
