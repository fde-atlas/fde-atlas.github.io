Full text

When Palantir's forward deployed engineers roll off, BD Emerson's engineers take over the build. We run the pipelines and ontology, ship extensions through reviewed releases, govern access and agents, and keep compute inside the limits your platform owner sets.

Definition

Palantir managed services are the ongoing work of running, extending, and governing a Foundry or AIP deployment after the team that built it leaves. The build is the pipelines, ontology, Workshop applications, and AIP workflows on your enrollment. Running it means keeping those resources healthy, changing them safely, deciding who can see and do what, and keeping compute usage inside budget.

Palantir supports its own platform under your subscription, and its SEC filings describe operations and maintenance services as part of its subscription and license agreements. The pipelines, object types, and applications that your team and Palantir's forward deployed engineers built on top of the platform are yours to run. BD Emerson's engineers take that work over, as your platform team or alongside the people you are hiring and certifying into it.

BD Emerson is an independent Palantir implementation consultancy. We are not Palantir, we sell no Palantir licenses, and we take no margin on your subscription, so a recommendation to retire a dashboard, slow a schedule, or shrink a usage account carries no conflict.

Services

Our approach

01 Before Palantir's FDEs roll off, we run the seven-point handoff checklist with them: ownership, documentation, access, runbooks, cost controls, a named platform team, and a release process. Every gap gets an owner and a date.

02 We move Owner roles from individual accounts to your groups, reassign Upgrade Assistant tasks, route monitoring alerts to your on-call channel, and write runbooks for the failures your build will see.

03 We run the platform with your team alongside us: alerts handled from runbooks, one change shipped through the release process, one access review from group membership, and the first monthly cost review in Resource Management.

04 New use cases enter a ranked backlog. We build each one on a branch, test it against the applications downstream, and merge it under your approval policies, with your engineers pairing on the work.

contact us

Book a handoff audit. We run the seven-point checklist with the outgoing team and give you a dated plan for every gap before the transition window closes.

Our Advantage

We sell no Palantir licenses and take no margin on your subscription. When we recommend retiring a dashboard, slowing a schedule, or shrinking a usage account, the recommendation costs us nothing and saves you compute.

The engineers who run your platform also build on it: pipelines, ontology, Workshop applications, and AIP workflows. A fix comes from someone who can read the transform, change the object type, and ship the change through your release process.

Security is BD Emerson's house specialty. Access reviews, markings, agent scopes, and change records are part of the run, with evidence your security team and auditors can use without a separate project.
