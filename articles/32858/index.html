Forward deployed engineer levels: senior, staff, manager

Kernavique · 2026-11-20 · 16 min read

Summary

Kernavique analyzed 298 US job postings titled "forward deployed" across 177 employer feeds on November 20, 2026. Most (151) carried no seniority word; 89 said senior, with staff, manager, and other levels far rarer. The article argues titles reveal little: senior roles ask anywhere from 3+ to 6+ years, and untitled postings range from 2–4 to 10+ years. Pay comparisons are only meaningful within one employer on one pay basis. Scale AI shows a clean three-step ladder with overlapping base ranges, while at Legora the manager posting pays less than its Senior/Staff role. Readers should check the years requirement and pay label before the title.

Key points

  • 151 of 298 US postings had no seniority word in the title.
  • Databricks alone accounts for 57 of the 89 senior postings.
  • Scale AI's base ranges step up: $180K–$225K, $216K–$270K, $252K–$315K.
  • Senior experience requirements vary from 3+ to 6+ years by employer.
  • Legora's manager range sits below its Senior/Staff posting's range.

A level word tells you less than the ad does. On November 20, 2026, 151 of 298 US postings titled “forward deployed” carried no seniority word; 89 said senior, 9 staff, 7 lead and 9 manager. Where one employer advertises several levels, pay steps up: Scale AI lists base ranges of $180,000 - $225,000, $216,000 - $270,000 and $252,000 - $315,000 for its plain, senior and staff titles, asking 4+, 5+ and 8+ years.

How many postings carry each seniority word

Start with how often each level word appears at all.

On November 20, 2026, we read the public job feeds of 177 mostly AI and tech employers. They held 298 US postings with “forward deployed” in the title. Of those, 151 carried none of the seniority words below. The other 147 carried at least one.

Four cautions before reading anything into the table.

  • The rows overlap. A title with two words, such as “Senior / Staff Forward Deployed Engineer”, is counted under each. Snorkel AI’s “VP, Head of Forward Deployed Engineering” is in two rows. The rows therefore add up to more than 147.
  • One feed entry is one posting. Databricks posts the same senior title once per city and per industry, and accounts for 57 of the 89 senior postings. Take Databricks out and senior appears in 32 postings.
  • “Manager” means people management here. Product, program and engagement manager titles are excluded from that row.
  • “Lead” is a mixed bag. The seven include “Forward Deployed Engineering Lead” at Lovable, a partner program lead at Cursor and a production lead at Anduril.

This is a one-day count of a convenience sample, not a census. Amazon, ServiceNow and Deloitte are not in it. For who the employers are, see who is hiring forward deployed engineers.

What the table shows, employer by employer.

Scale AI is the cleanest ladder we read: three titles, one base-pay label, one set of locations. Each step up raises both ends of the range, and the ranges overlap. The top of the plain title, $225,000, is above the bottom of the senior title, $216,000.

Deloitte shows the same pattern over three titles for Palantir work. The associate ad sits in its government practice and asks for the ability to obtain a clearance, so it is not a pure level comparison.

Anthropic has two US ads. The manager range starts where the staff range ends, at $320,000.

Palantir separates new graduates by the top of the range, not the bottom. Both ads start at $135,000. The new-grad range stops at $145,000 and the experienced one runs to $200,000. Palantir’s ads say the estimate leaves out any sign-on bonus, benefits and long-term incentives.

Datadog’s two figures were matched in the feed by pattern and labeled by the ad’s own sentence. We did not read those two ads in full.

What the ads ask for at each level

Years of experience are the one requirement most ads attach to a level. All of the following were read on November 20, 2026.

Entry. New-grad and intern titles carry a graduation window instead of years. Only one US posting uses a junior word: Legora’s “Junior/Mid-level Forward Deployed Engineer”. Our briefing on how to become a forward deployed engineer covers the early-career ads.

No level word. These ads cover the widest span. Caylent asks for 2–4 years. Scale AI asks for 4+. Turing and JLL each ask for 8+. Cloudflare asks for 10+ years of technical customer-facing experience under a plain title.

Senior. Atoms asks for 3+ years. Scale AI and Deloitte ask for 5+. Reddit asks for 5+, for a role that serves its own internal teams. Databricks asks for 6+.

Staff. Scale AI and Alation each ask for 8+ years. Anthropic’s staff ad gives no number. It says hands-on experience with LLMs and agents is expected, and adds: “You don’t need to have shipped agents to production.”

Combined levels. Some employers do not draw the line in the ad. Three post a “Senior / Staff” or “Senior/Staff” title: Giga, Legora and Snorkel AI. Salesforce’s single ad is titled “(Mid/Senior Level)” and asks for 3+ years, or 6-10 for the senior levels. Glean says its range covers varying levels of the role.

On scope, the ads say less, and what they say does not always follow the title. Deloitte’s plain-titled ad asks the engineer to contribute independently within a pod while mentoring newer team members. Giga’s Senior / Staff ad describes the role as the project leader between Giga and its customers, includes mentoring agent engineers, and asks for 4+ years of managerial experience.

So a senior title can mean 3+ years at one employer and 6+ at another, and an ad with no level word can ask for more than either.

Management roles

Management titles are a small part of the sample. Of 298 US postings, 9 carry manager, 4 director, 2 head and 1 VP, with one Snorkel AI posting counted under both head and VP. The manager postings are at OpenAI (2), Anthropic, Datadog, Labelbox, Legora, Snowflake, Stripe and Tennr.

What a manager ad asks for. Anthropic’s Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering ad asks for 10+ years of experience, including 2+ years of people management. It is not a desk job: the ad asks for travel to customer sites as needed, 25-50%, which is more than the 25% in Anthropic’s staff ad.

What it pays. Anthropic’s manager range, $320,000 - $405,000 labeled “Annual Salary”, is the highest figure on any basis among the 42 employer ads in our pay sample. Cursor’s ads label two figures the same way, “Annual Salary”: $235,000 - $320,000 for its Forward Deployed Engineer and $350,000 - $400,000 for its Regional Director, Forward Deployed Engineering.

Lead is not always management. Lovable’s “Forward Deployed Engineering Lead” shows a higher feed figure than its staff posting. But other lead titles in the count run a partner program or a production line. Read the ad for direct reports.

Management experience can sit inside an engineer title. Giga’s Senior / Staff ad asks for managerial experience without a manager title.

One stated ladder that does not end in management. Insight Partners, an investor in Workato, reports that Workato has a career ladder for the role that climbs to an “architect” level (July 21, 2026). That is one company, reported by an interested party.

What a title does not tell you

  • How many years the employer wants. Senior runs from 3+ to 6+ in the ads above. Plain titles run from 2–4 to 10+.
  • The pay basis. Scale AI says base. Anthropic says annual salary. Palantir says estimated salary range. Deloitte says a reasonable estimate. A feed figure may say nothing. Ask which it is and what sits on top.
  • How wide the range is. Glean’s single range is written to cover several levels, and Salesforce puts mid and senior in one ad with one pair of base ranges.
  • Whether you manage people. Lead, head and even staff are used for both.
  • Whether the company has a ladder at all. “Founding” appears in 3 postings, all at Glean, for the same title in three locations. A first hire into a new function has no rung above or below it yet.
  • What the job is. The same title can describe different work. See forward deployed engineer vs solutions engineer and our forward deployed engineer role page.

In practice

If you are an engineer judging a title. Read the years line and the pay label before the level word. Compare an offer with other levels at the same employer, on the same basis, not with a different employer’s title. Ask whether the role has direct reports, how much travel it carries, and what the next level up is called. For what employers publish about the hiring process, see our interview guide. If you want Kernavique to know about you, you can send us your details and résumé.

If you are a company deciding what level to open. Decide the years and the scope first, then pick the word. Most of the postings we counted, 151 of 298, use no level word at all, and that is a legitimate choice when one range covers several levels. If you publish several levels, keep them on one pay basis and say which it is, as Scale AI does. Say in the ad whether a lead or manager has direct reports. What we left out

  • A market-wide pay figure for each level. The sources we read do not support one. Ranges are labeled differently from employer to employer, and base cannot be compared with figures that are not stated as base.
  • Levels outside the 177 feeds. Amazon, ServiceNow and employers on Workday are not in the title counts. Deloitte appears in the pay table from its own ads, not from the count.
  • Internal level codes. No ad we read publishes a numbered level, so we cannot map senior or staff at one employer to another.
  • Snorkel AI’s director range. Its ad shows two versions of the figure that do not match, so we left it out.
  • Scope by level in detail. Few ads describe what changes between levels beyond years. We quote the two that do and draw no general rule.
  • Equity and bonus amounts. Many ads mention them without a value, so no level comparison is possible.
  • Vendor salary surveys and third-party level guides. We used employer ads and feeds only.

Questions

  • What levels do forward deployed engineer job titles use?
  • On the 177 public job feeds we read on November 20, 2026, 151 of 298 US postings titled “forward deployed” had no seniority word at all. Of the rest, 89 said senior, 14 intern, 12 new grad, 9 staff, 9 manager, 7 lead, 4 director, 3 principal and 3 founding. A title with two words, such as Senior / Staff, is counted under each.
  • How many years of experience does a senior forward deployed engineer need?
  • It depends on the employer. In ads read on November 20, 2026, Atoms asks for 3+ years for its senior title, Scale AI and Reddit ask for 5+, and Databricks asks for 6+. Meanwhile ads with no level word ask for anything from 2–4 years at Caylent to 10+ years at Cloudflare. Read the years line, not the title.
  • What is the difference between senior and staff?
  • Where an employer advertises both, staff asks for more years and pays more. Scale AI asks for 5+ years at senior and 8+ at staff, with base ranges of $216,000 - $270,000 and $252,000 - $315,000 (read November 20, 2026). Alation’s staff ad also asks for 8+ years. Three employers post a combined Senior / Staff title, so the line is not always drawn in the ad.
  • What do forward deployed engineering managers earn?
  • Anthropic’s Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering ad lists an annual salary of $320,000 - $405,000 and asks for 10+ years with 2+ years of people management (read November 20, 2026). Datadog’s manager ad lists an estimated yearly salary of $200,000 — $250,000. The two are labeled differently and neither is stated as base, so do not compare them directly.
  • Does a manager always earn more than a senior engineer?
  • Not in every feed we read. Legora’s feed on November 20, 2026 showed $224.4K – $303.6K for its Engineering Manager posting and $237,150 – $369,150 for its Senior / Staff posting. Those figures come from an unlabeled compensation field, so the pay basis is unknown. At Anthropic, Datadog and OpenAI the manager range sits above the engineer range.
  • What level should a company open for its first forward deployed engineer?
  • The ads do not settle it. Monte Carlo and Turing both advertise for a new function, and Glean uses a Founding title asking for 4+ years, while Turing typically asks for 8+. Decide the years and scope first, then choose the word. Our guide on how to hire a forward deployed engineer covers screening.
