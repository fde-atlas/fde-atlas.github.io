Full text

Explore the full interview loop for Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer roles, including motivation, live coding, system design, values, and technical screens. Learn how each round assesses different job aspects and how preparation across rounds builds a cohesive strategy.

Anthropic’s interview loop for Forward Deployed Engineers tests the role from several directions. One round checks motivation and fit, another checks how we reason about deploying Claude, another checks how we write code under changing requirements, and later rounds probe past projects, system design, and values. Each round looks at a different slice of the same job.

Preparing round by round without the full map tends to produce uneven results. A strong coding session can sit next to a vague answer about why we want the role, or a polished design next to a values conversation we never practiced. Seeing the whole loop first shows how the rounds connect and where one round’s preparation strengthens another.

The map starts with the job itself, because every round tests some part of it.

What the job description asks for

Anthropic describes the FDE as a member of its Applied AI team who embeds with its most strategic customers to ship AI applications that solve real business problems. FDEs build production applications inside customer systems, deliver artifacts such as MCP servers, subagents, and agent skills, provide deployment support, and feed repeatable patterns back to Product and Engineering. Current postings describe frequent travel to customer sites, between 25% and 50% depending on location.

The qualifications describe the candidate the loop is looking for.

  • Experience: Four or more years in a technical, customer-facing role, such as Forward Deployed Engineer, or as a software engineer with consulting or product experience. Former technical founders are encouraged to apply.
