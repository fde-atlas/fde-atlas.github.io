Summary

Drawing on WAIC 2026, the article argues enterprise AI delivery is shifting in three ways: deliverables like MCP, Skills, and prompts are becoming reusable, auditable organizational assets; acceptance criteria are moving from single-point efficiency gains to business-cycle metrics; and standards bodies are building interconnection protocols and evaluation platforms. Using examples from Shenzhou Information, pharmaceutical CSR reporting, and Huawei Cloud's financial ecosystem, the author concludes that FDE compounding value comes from leaving reusable assets after each project, not from doing more projects.

Key points

  • Shenzhou Information cut bank relationship manager onboarding from 6-12 months to 1-3 months.
  • Pharma CSR report drafts compressed from months to hours, over 90% efficiency gain.
  • Huawei Cloud is building a financial ecosystem where MCP and Skills become reusable industry assets.
  • CAICT released five outputs including an interconnection governance initiative, ASL protocol, and evaluation platform.
  • FDEs should leave four reusable assets per project and validate with business-cycle metrics.

Today's Take

As WAIC 2026 winds down, today brought changes more worth capturing than "yet another batch of Agents launched":

1. Delivery assets are starting to be formally productized. MCP, Skills, prompts, semantic layers, and industry rules are no longer just project attachments, but reusable, auditable, continuously iterable organizational assets.

2. Process outcomes are starting to replace single-point efficiency gains. Business metrics such as bank relationship manager onboarding cycles and pharmaceutical CSR reporting cycles are becoming the acceptance language for Agent projects.

3. Standards and interconnection are starting to fill the gap. At WAIC, CAICT simultaneously released an interconnection governance initiative, a case compilation, research reports, a secure and trusted interconnection protocol, and an evals platform, showing the industry is beginning to tackle cross-vendor collaboration and governance at scale.

Taken together:

  • The next stage of high-ticket delivery should not just sell "knowledge base + workflow," but rather industry asset libraries + process redesign + operational governance + business metric acceptance.
  • The compounding returns of an FDE come not from doing more projects, but from gaining one more reusable asset with each project.

Key Signals

SunGard/Insigma... (see below) 神州信息: Bank relationship manager onboarding cycle shortened from 6-12 months to 1-3 months

  • Source: 神州信息 | WAIC Typical Practice Projects; Corporate Intelligent Marketing Assistant Product Page
  • After the WAIC forum on July 19, 神州信息 disclosed that its corporate intelligent marketing assistant has been deployed at a city commercial bank in East China. The product consists of multiple Agents covering customer insight, product matching, and proposal generation, cutting new relationship manager onboarding time by 50%.
  • Why it matters: This is not "faster answers," but turning senior relationship managers' insight, product matching, and proposal structure into a working system that new hires can use. The value of an FDE lies in shortening role competency cycles, not in the number of model calls.

Pharmaceutical CSR reports: A traditional months-long process compressed to hours, with efficiency gains exceeding 90%

  • Source: People's Daily Online | AI Management Innovation Forum Held
  • On July 20, coverage of a case from WAIC's "AI Management Innovation Forum." Using AI for Process to redesign the pharmaceutical CSR reporting process, multiple agents handle data extraction, content generation, collaborative review, and compliance verification, compressing report first drafts from months to hours.
  • Why it matters: True productionization is not adding a writing assistant to the existing process, but redrawing the end-to-end process and bringing review, compliance, and knowledge retention into the system. The smallest unit of an Agent project is not the task, but the process.

Huawei Cloud financial ecosystem: MCP and Skills are becoming accumulable, reusable industry assets

  • Source: Zhidx | Huawei Cloud Breaks Down Steps for Scaling Agent Deployment at WAIC
  • At the WAIC Huawei Cloud Enterprise Agent Industry Forum on July 18, it was stated that a key step to scaling deployment is accumulating and sharing industry assets such as MCP and Skills. Together with ICBC, UnionPay, Postal Savings Bank, and over ten ecosystem companies, a smart finance zone is being jointly built.
  • Postal Savings Bank has achieved an agent execution engine with 7×24-hour coverage and minute-level intelligence acquisition. Tianyancha has pre-integrated over 160 MCP capabilities and converted six categories of risk control capabilities into Skills.
  • Why it matters: After an FDE completes a project, the most valuable thing left behind is not a set of prompts, but turning interfaces, rules, experience, approvals, and exception handling into reusable assets. Delivery organizations will need to manage an MCP asset library + Skills asset library + Eval/AgentOps simultaneously.

CAICT: The agent industry is moving from single-point products to interconnection, protocols, and evals

  • Source: China Daily | WAIC 2026 Forum: From Large Models to Agents
  • The July 18 forum released five outcomes: the Agent Interconnection Governance Initiative, the Agent Economy Practice Compilation, the "Internet Agent Development Research Report," the Agent Secure and Trusted Interconnection Protocol (ASL), and a terminal agent evals platform.
  • A MIIT official made an explicit judgment: domestic agents are moving from single-point validation to system-level coordination, and from pilot demonstrations to large-scale application.
  • Why it matters: When Agents need to work across vendors, platforms, and departments, an FDE must manage not just single-system performance, but also identity, protocols, mutual trust, evals, and boundaries of responsibility. This is an important leading signal for the Chinese version of Agent delivery standardization.

Implications for FDE Practitioners

  • Upgrade delivery into "asset engineering": Don't just organize materials; form callable, authorizable, testable, versionable Skill and workflow assets. Each project should leave at least four reusable assets: a scenario inventory, a Skill/rules library, an acceptance test set, and an exception/human-handoff checklist.
  • Every project must select one business cycle metric: e.g., new hire onboarding cycle, report first-draft cycle, proposal production cycle, response latency, first-pass rate. Accept based on business cycles, not "efficiency improvement percentages."
  • Track the accumulation rate of reusable assets: The compounding returns of an FDE come not from doing more projects, but from gaining one more reusable asset with each project. MCP tools, Skills rules, Eval acceptance sets, and exception handling rules are all assets.
  • Upgrade external messaging: From "helping enterprises build Agents" to "embedding enterprise experience into processes and being accountable for business outcomes."
