Tech Recruitment Specialisms - AI, Software, Data

Re:Sourced · 2026 · 1 min read

Summary

This is a marketing page from Re:Sourced, a tech recruitment firm covering six disciplines including AI, software, data, and Forward Deployed Engineering. Its pitch is that recruiters specialize in one discipline each, letting them screen candidates credibly (e.g., spotting ML engineers who cannot explain transformers or FDE candidates without customer-facing experience) and draw on a warm bench rather than cold sourcing. The firm claims 700+ placements, 100+ clients across APAC, EMEA and the US, senior hires signed within 21 days, and 90% of roles filled with three or fewer CVs. Case studies describe parallel hiring for a European fintech unicorn and a Sydney fintech scaleup, including Senior and Principal FDE roles staffed concurrently. It also cites candidate loyalty, with four in five placements returning for future moves.

Key points

  • Re:Sourced specializes in six tech recruiting disciplines, one recruiter per field.
  • Claims 21-day senior hires; 90% of roles filled with ≤3 CVs.
  • 700+ placements and 100+ clients across APAC, EMEA and the US.
  • Case studies include parallel Senior/Principal FDE hiring in New York.
  • Four in five placed candidates return to the firm for their next move.

Six disciplines. Senior engineers, placed in 21 days.

We don't dabble. Every recruiter on our team works inside a single discipline - that's how we move from brief to signed faster than any generalist shop can.

· 700+ placements · 6 disciplines · 100+ customers across APAC, EMEA & US ·

A generalist recruiter cannot screen out an ML engineer who cannot explain a transformer, or a Forward Deployed candidate who has never owned a customer relationship. Ours can - because our AI recruiter only does AI. Same logic for every other discipline.

02 Faster shortlists from a warm bench.

Specialism means we already know the senior engineers in your discipline before you call. Cold sourcing is slow. Warm reach-out is the difference between 21 days and 60. 90% of roles filled in ≤3 CVs for exactly this reason.

03 Better candidate experience because we are not bluffing.

Senior engineers stop responding to recruiters who cannot explain the role or the team. Ours can - and the candidate-side data shows it. Four in five of our placements return to us for their next move.
