Lovable AI & Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs (4)

Forward Deploying · 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Summary

Forward Deploying tracks 4 open Forward Deployed Engineer roles at Lovable: 2 Lead and 2 Staff positions across San Francisco, New York, and London, all onsite. All four adverts explicitly require production code experience. The two US roles with published salaries have a median base of $289k, about 38% above the $210k market median across the 278 employers tracked. Two roles were posted in the last 30 days, two mention travel, and the London roles do not publish salary ranges.
