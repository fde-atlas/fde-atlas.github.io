Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Manager

Our Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) don't advise from the sidelines. They embed with enterprise customer teams, build production-grade AI coding workflows alongside them, and leave behind habits that keep paying off after the engagement ends. As Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Manager, you'll build and run the partner ecosystem that extends this work through systems integrators (SIs) and consulting firms: recruiting the right partners, enabling and certifying their delivery teams, and co-delivering forward-deployed engagements that put Grok models, Grok Bot, the Grok API, and Cloud Agents into production at enterprise customers. Success is measured by how much more FDE work gets delivered to our quality bar because partners can carry it, not by how many partners sign up.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Co-deliver the first partner-led engagements alongside our FDEs, then turn what worked into the blueprint: engagement model, scoping templates, architecture patterns, eval standards, and rollout and handoff criteria.
  • Set the technical bar for which firms and practices qualify to deliver, own the go/no-go decision on acceptance, and own the technical success of partner-led projects from scoping and architecture review through production launch and handoff, stepping in on at-risk projects.
  • Design and run partner enablement and certification, and coach partner teams through live projects until they deliver on their own.
  • Work with the partnership team to onboard systems integrators and consulting partners with the technical depth to co-deliver forward-deployed engagements.
  • Build and run joint pipelines with partnership leads and Sales, and shape joint or partner-led proposals.
  • Turn partner field learnings and product gaps into reusable playbooks and specific feedback for Product and Engineering.

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

  • Hands-on technical background as a software engineer, solutions engineer, architect, or forward deployed engineer.
  • Experience personally leading complex technical engagements at enterprise customers, from discovery and scoping through production adoption, across complex stakeholder environments.

PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

  • Experience deploying LLM-based applications, evals, or agentic systems for enterprise customers.
  • Experience building delivery capability through engineers who did not report to you, such as a partner practice, an outsourced delivery org, or an enablement function, while holding the quality bar even when commercial pressure pushed the other way.

ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:

  • Must be willing to travel to meet customers.
