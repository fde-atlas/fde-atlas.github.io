TWOSTONE&Sons group company MapleSystems launches its "industry-specialized FDE" service.

Summary

MapleSystems, an SES subsidiary of TWOSTONE&Sons, has launched an "industry-specialized FDE" service applying the Forward Deployed Engineer concept to its staffing business. It defines specialized FDEs as engineers with 5+ cumulative years in one industry plus AI implementation skills, with 3+ year engineers groomed as successors. An internal survey (123 respondents) found roughly 70% hold AI-driven skills combining engineering, business development, and AI implementation, and 82% are willing to join AI projects. Analysis of 135 engineers across ~1,400 projects showed 68 of 128 analyzed have 3+ years industry experience and 34 meet the 5-year bar, concentrated in finance, IT, and manufacturing; engineers average 4+ industries and 9.3 years of experience. The company plans to standardize AI usage and expand industry-specific talent pools.

Full text

Approximately 70% of in-house engineers are AI-driven (engineering + business development + AI implementation capabilities)

MapleSystems Inc. (Head office: Chuo-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director: Yusuke Mochizuki; hereinafter "the Company"), a group company of TWOSTONE&Sons Inc. that provides system engineering services (SES), is pleased to announce the launch of its "industry-specialized FDE" service, in which engineers equipped with industry-specific engineering knowledge as well as business development and AI implementation capabilities embed themselves at customer sites and go as far as solving their challenges. Approximately 70% of the Company's in-house engineers possess "AI-driven" skills spanning engineering, business development, and AI implementation, and the Company will realize the next form of the SES business.

■ What is an FDE — and the "industry-specialized FDE" the Company champions

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a role spreading primarily among US AI companies, referring to engineers who embed themselves at customer sites, deeply understand the business, and solve challenges through technology. With OpenAI recruiting for FDE positions in Tokyo, attention is growing in Japan as well.

The Company has adopted this concept in its SES operations, defining an "industry-specialized FDE" as "an engineer with business knowledge from a cumulative 5+ years of involvement in projects in a given industry, AI implementation capabilities, and a commitment to continuing to master problem-solving in that industry." "Industry mid-level engineers" with 3+ cumulative years of experience will be developed into the next FDEs as they accumulate experience in the same industry. This way of working is supported by the standardization of AI utilization being advanced within the Company — a shift from "building what you're told" to "understanding the business and thinking from the standpoint of what should be built using AI."

■ Value delivered by the "industry-specialized FDE"

Through the "industry-specialized FDE," client companies are assigned engineers who understand industry-specific operations and business practices, supporting a fast start after project onboarding and development and improvements tailored to on-site challenges. Furthermore, by combining AI implementation capabilities, the Company provides talent capable of making proposals that go beyond streamlining development work to improving entire business processes.

For engineers, the Company provides an environment where they can continuously accumulate experience in a specific industry, supporting career development that leads to medium- to long-term growth in expertise and market value by enhancing not only technical skills but also industry knowledge, problem-solving ability, and AI implementation capabilities.

■ Approximately 70% of engineers possess AI-driven skills

Based on a survey of the Company's in-house engineers (123 respondents).

Tools used include GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude. About 82% are positive about participating in AI-related projects. While AI use may be restricted in highly confidential environments such as finance, approximately 70% of the Company's in-house engineers possess AI-driven skills (engineering + business development + AI implementation capabilities).

■ Composition of the industry-specialized FDE team — centered on finance, IT, and manufacturing

According to a career analysis (135 in-house engineers, approximately 1,400 projects participated in), more than half (68) of the 128 engineers analyzed have 3+ cumulative years of industry experience, of which 34 meet the specialization criteria (5+ cumulative years). The composition of the industry-specialized FDE team is centered on finance, IT, and manufacturing, as shown below.

Each engineer has experience in an average of 4+ industries, and cross-industry adaptability is also a strength. Average practical experience is 9.3 years, with 40 engineers having 10+ years of experience.

■ Future developments — as an organization that keeps taking on challenges

Going forward, the Company will advance the development and assignment of engineers centered on industry specialization and expand its team of "industry-specialized FDEs" who combine engineering expertise, business development capabilities, and AI implementation skills. The Company will support client companies with problem-solving grounded in industry understanding and business process improvement through AI implementation, while supporting engineers in career development that enhances their expertise and market value. In addition, the Company will promote the standardization of AI utilization and expand its industry-specific talent lineup, strengthening the value it delivers to both companies and engineers.

A holding company providing a variety of solution services centered on its engineer platform business. On June 1, 2023, it changed its name to "TWOSTONE&Sons Inc." and transitioned to a holding company structure.

The group operates a wide range of businesses and will contribute to society as a group of companies that solves social issues. For more details, please visit the TWOSTONE&Sons corporate website (https://twostone-s.com/).
