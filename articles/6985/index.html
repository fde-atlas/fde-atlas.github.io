Scale AI Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Experience (2025)

Aced (formerly Exponent) · 2026-10-01 · 1 min read

Summary

This article shares a candidate's 2025 interview experience for the Forward Deployed Engineer role at Scale AI, published by Aced (formerly Exponent). The process was long and heavily SWE-oriented: a hard LeetCode-style online assessment, recruiter screen, non-technical phone screen, and coding phone screen, followed by a five-part onsite covering coding, system design, debugging a large repository, and a hiring manager round. The candidate received an offer and notes Scale AI applies the same hiring bar as software engineering, sorting out FDE fit afterward. Other candidates report fast-paced rounds and an interviewer openly describing the culture as close to 996.

Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Experience

The wild part was that they basically put me through the same bar as software engineering, then only later figured out whether I fit FDE.

Interview process

I got in through a referral, and when I went through it the process was long and very SWE-coded. I started with a hard OA, then had a recruiter screen, a non-technical phone screen, and a coding phone screen. After that I did a five-part onsite with credo, coding, system design, debugging a giant repo, and a hiring manager round. I got the offer, and the biggest thing that stood out was that they really seemed to use the same hiring bar for FDE and software engineering, then sort out role fit later.
