Forward Deployed Engineer — The AI Job Every DevOps Pro Should Target in 2026

FDE Times · 2026 · 1 min read

Summary

This article argues that the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role is the AI-era job DevOps professionals should target in 2026, framing it as a hybrid of engineering and hands-on customer deployment work. It cites FDE Times hiring data: 924 open roles, up 33 in a week with 80 new postings, 196 companies hiring, 25% remote-friendly, a median US salary of $200k, and Databricks as the top hirer with 107 openings. The piece positions FDEs as central to deploying AI systems in real customer environments.

Key points

  • FDE is presented as the top AI job target for DevOps pros in 2026.
  • 924 open FDE roles, up 33 week-over-week, with 80 new postings.
  • 196 companies are hiring; Databricks leads with 107 openings.
  • Median US pay is $200k; 25% of roles are remote-friendly.
