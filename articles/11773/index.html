FDE Capacity Planning

FDE Interviews · 2026-10-07 · 1 min read

Summary

This FDE Interviews page covers capacity planning for forward deployed engineers: matching delivery, support, learning, and shared work against qualified people and calendar windows. Its core point is that a healthy average utilization figure can hide a skill bottleneck that causes missed engagement start dates. The page is largely a paywall stub (₹2,000/$25 for six months of Premium access) listing related course lessons, concepts, and practice questions, including behavioral scenarios on prioritizing customer engagements and a free question on sizing Snowflake warehouses.
