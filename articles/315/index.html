Summary

AY Automate offers nearshore AI engineer placement: engineers from its own bench join the client's sprints, repo and standups, orchestrating coding agents with Claude Code, MCP, Cursor, n8n and E2B. Pricing starts at $3,000 per month with no recruitment fee, placement takes 2 to 4 weeks versus a 3 to 6 month US hiring cycle, and a 90-day replacement guarantee covers bad fits. The company claims up to $120,000 in annual savings per engineer compared with a US hire costing $150,000 or more. The service suits teams with a backlog, not fixed handoff projects; the Golden Offer staffs 1 to 5 engineers under one lead.

FAQ

Engineer Placement Questions

Do you place your own engineers?

Yes. We match clients with engineers from our own bench, so the placement is tied to real implementation standards instead of a generic recruiting handoff.

What roles can we request?

The most common requests are AI engineers, automation specialists, full-stack builders, n8n experts, and technical operators who can own implementation work.

Is this different from the Golden Offer?

This page covers the placement process itself: how requests get filled, timelines, and the 90-day guarantee. The Golden Offer is for teams building out multiple roles at once, choose 1 to 5 engineers across our bench (Forward Deployed Engineer, GTM Engineer, AI Automation Architect) in one engagement. If you need one engineer, this page has the detail you want. If you need a small team, start with the Golden Offer.

How much does AI engineer placement cost?

Placement starts from $3K a month per engineer, with no recruitment fee and no employer taxes or benefits on your side. We confirm the exact figure for your role and seniority on the first call. For comparison, a US in-house engineer usually costs $150,000 or more in salary alone, before taxes, benefits and recruiting.

How long does it take to place an AI engineer?

Most placements take 2 to 4 weeks from the first call to the engineer's first day in your sprint. Requirement analysis takes 1 to 2 days, matching takes 1 to 2 days, and closing terms takes about a day. The rest is contract setup and onboarding into your repo, tools and team rituals.

What does the 90-day replacement guarantee cover?

You get 90 days from the start date to confirm the engineer fits your team and your work. If the fit is wrong in that window, we replace the engineer at no extra cost. We run performance check-ins through the whole 90 days, so a fit problem shows up early instead of at the end of the window.

Who is engineer placement not for?

It is not a fit if you want a fixed project built and handed back, or if nobody on your side can set priorities for the engineer. Placement works when there is a team and a backlog for the engineer to join. If you need several roles at once, the Golden Offer staffs 1 to 5 engineers under one accountable lead.

How is placement different from a freelancer, a recruiter or an in-house hire?

A freelancer works alone, and you vet and manage them yourself. A recruiter sends candidates you still interview, and charges a fee. An in-house hire usually takes 3 to 6 months and adds taxes and benefits. We place an engineer from our own bench, already trained on agent tooling, with no recruitment fee and a 90-day replacement guarantee.

Is this a direct placement or staff augmentation?

Both, in different senses. The engineer is placed directly into your team: they join your standups, take tickets from your backlog and push to your repo, with no sourcing or candidate interview loop. Commercially it works like staff augmentation: you pay from $3K a month per engineer, with no recruitment fee and no employer taxes or benefits on your side, and the 90-day replacement guarantee covers the fit.

What tools do your placed engineers use?

Our engineers build with Claude Code as the main coding agent, MCP to connect it to your tools, and E2B sandboxes to run agent actions in isolation. Depending on the role, they also build n8n automations or work across frontend, backend, databases and deployment. They work inside your repo, your ticketing and your code review process.

Which time zones and languages do your engineers work in?

Our engineers are nearshore and keep working hours that overlap with US time zones, so they join your standups and reviews live. They work in English, French and Arabic. Tell us your core hours and working language on the first call, and we match an engineer whose schedule and language fit your team.

How do we start an AI engineer placement?

Book the free 15-minute call on this page. We ask what you are building, your stack and the gap the engineer fills. Within 1 to 2 days we match an engineer from our bench, then agree terms. Most engineers start in your sprint 2 to 4 weeks after that first call, with the 90-day guarantee running from day one.
