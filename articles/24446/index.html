Japan's first new job category and certification system defining the "outcome-driven professional" FDE for the AI era

Summary

Japan's IoT Certification Committee has announced a plan for the country's first FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) certification framework, aimed at defining and evaluating people who can use AI agents to drive business transformation and continuously deliver results—not merely those who can use AI tools. The framework uses a three-tier model (exam, accreditation, certification) covering knowledge, practical implementation, and proven outcomes, with three levels; the highest level, FDE Consultant, covers organizational change and reproducibility. The FDE role spans four phases—DESIGN, BUILD, EMBED, SCALE—with on-site adoption and scaling as mandatory responsibilities. Level 1 alpha and beta exams are planned from July 2026, Level 2 accreditation within the year, and Level 3 certification within a year, alongside partnerships with AI vendors, SaaS firms, SIers, and educational institutions.

Key points

  • IoT Certification Committee unveiled Japan's first FDE certification framework concept.
  • It evaluates people who deliver sustained results with AI agents, not just AI users.
  • Three-tier model: exam for knowledge, accreditation for practice, certification for outcomes.
  • FDE role spans DESIGN, BUILD, EMBED, SCALE; adoption and scaling are mandatory.
  • Level 1 exams start July 2026; Level 2 within the year, Level 3 within a year.

The IoT Certification Committee Announces Japan's First "FDE Certification Framework (Forward Deployed Engineer Certification Framework)" Concept

The FDE Certification Project of the IoT Certification Committee has announced the concept of the "FDE Certification Framework (Forward Deployed Engineer Certification Framework)," Japan's first※1 certification system, which independently defines "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)" as a new professional profile required in the DX and AX era, tailored to Japan's specific circumstances, with the aim of developing and evaluating such professionals.

This system aims to evaluate not "people who can use AI," but "people who can continuously produce business results using AI agents." Through a three-tier evaluation model that progressively certifies knowledge and applied skills, practical abilities, and results, it makes results-driven professionals of the AI era visible.

While DX initiatives and the use of generative AI are rapidly advancing in companies, many organizations face challenges in transitioning from the PoC (proof of concept) stage to production operation, achieving on-the-ground adoption, and generating results.

The FDE Certification System is not a system that certifies people who can use AI.

It is a system that evaluates professionals who can drive business transformation using AI agents and continuously generate results from it.

With the spread of generative AI, many companies have begun adopting AI. However, in reality, only a limited number of organizations achieve post-implementation adoption and actual results.

The FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a new profession defined to solve this challenge.

Designing, implementing, and embedding AI-agent-driven business transformation in the field, and scaling its results across the entire organization.

The FDE Certification System is Japan's first※1 talent evaluation system that assesses such practical capabilities and results-generation ability.

※1 Based on our own research, conducted in May 2026. The survey covered certification systems in Japan whose names include "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" and whose evaluation targets are business transformation and results generation through AI agents.

Challenges in DX and AI Implementation

In recent years, many companies have been investing in AI and DX.

However, in the field,

  • projects end at the PoC stage
  • systems are implemented but not utilized
  • business transformation is never achieved
  • results are not measured
  • post-implementation adoption does not progress

and many such challenges arise.

The IoT Certification Committee believes the root cause is not a shortage of "people who can implement," but a shortage of "professionals who take responsibility for producing results in the field."

The FDE Certification System is also a proposal for a new definition of a profession for the AI era.

Rather than the conventional job categories of "engineer," "consultant," or "project manager," we define the FDE as a new profession responsible for field transformation and results generation while leveraging AI.

What Is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?

An FDE is a professional who designs and implements AI-agent-driven business transformation, embeds it in the field, and takes responsibility all the way through to continuous results generation.

An FDE is not merely someone who uses AI.

Their mission is to root AI agents in the field, build new business processes where humans and AI collaborate, and extend the results across the entire organization.

The FDE Certification System is designed as a new evaluation standard to make such professionals socially visible.

StagePhaseRole
Stage 1DESIGNAnalyze business challenges and redesign operations with AI as the premise
Stage 2BUILDBuild systems leveraging AI and digital technologies
Stage 3EMBEDEmbed solutions in the field and create a state of continuous use
Stage 4SCALEExtend results across the entire organization in a reproducible form, leading to continuous value creation

The defining feature of the FDE is that "EMBED (adoption)" and "SCALE (results expansion and organizational rollout)" are mandatory areas of responsibility.

Many DX and AI projects end at "implementation" or "deployment." The FDE, however, takes responsibility through field adoption and horizontal deployment of results, with the mission of achieving continuous value creation.

The Three-Tier Evaluation Model: "Examination, Accreditation, Certification"

The FDE system introduces a new mechanism that evaluates not only knowledge, but also "the adoption of AI-agent-driven business transformation and the generation of results."

FDE Talent Evaluation Framework

CategoryEvaluation TargetWhat Is Certified
ExaminationKnowledge and understandingMeasures and certifies the skills that underpin implementation ability
AccreditationPractical abilityMeasures and certifies implementation and deployment capability in the field
CertificationTrack record of resultsEvaluates and certifies results generated in the field

Conventional certification systems were mechanisms for proving "possession of knowledge."

The FDE system goes further, evaluating the entire growth process of:

having knowledge ↓ being able to implement ↓ being able to embed ↓ being able to produce results

This design is based on the belief that what companies truly seek is not "certificate holders" but "results generators."

Level Design

The FDE system currently consists of three levels.

Level | Goal

Level 1Shifting to AI-first thinking
Level 2Implementation and deployment
Level 3Results generation, organizational transformation, and reproducibility

A System Designed for the Age of AI Agents

In the FDE system, AI agents are positioned not merely as tools, but

as "a structure for redesigning business operations."

They are treated as a conceptual understanding at Level 1, a core of implementation at Level 2, and a means of results generation at Level 3, aiming to develop practical professionals for the age of AI agents.

Background of the System's Creation

This system was born from the sense of crisis among practitioners who support DX and AI implementation in the field.

Background of the System's Creation

This system was born out of the sense of crisis felt by practitioners who provide on-site support for DX and AI implementation.

Toru Yamaguchi, Mitsuhiro Oishi, and Mitsuru Chikamori, the three members of the DX project on the IoT Certification Committee, had long felt a common challenge through their years of hands-on involvement in corporate transformation.

That challenge was:

"Technology keeps evolving, yet results are not being achieved."

This is the reality they faced.

The emergence of generative AI and AI agents has dramatically expanded the possibilities for companies. However, in many organizations, adoption itself has become the goal, and they never reach the results they should be achieving. On May 22, 2026, during a conversation after the event "Practical Seminar on Generative AI and AI Agent Utilization for Executives," where the three spoke, these practitioners who continue to support the front lines arrived at a shared realization.

"Right now, we must change the very definition of talent and roles."

From there, the project members and generative AI engaged in relentless discussion and design, rapidly advancing the formulation of this certification program's concept and preparations for its announcement. Precisely because we ourselves have struggled on the front lines, suffered there, and challenged ourselves to create results in the field, this certification embodies the passion of practitioners.

The origin of the FDE concept lies in the idea that "companies need professionals who can design and implement optimal AI utilization for the unique challenges of each organization."

An FDE is not a role that simply introduces a specific AI service.

They analyze a company's current state, extract challenges, select the AI models and AI agents best suited to solving them, and take responsibility for everything from implementation in the field to the creation of results.

Moreover, the "Engineer" in FDE does not necessarily mean a technical specialist who writes code.

The essence of an FDE is a practitioner who understands frontline operations and the characteristics of AI while driving operational improvement and transformation.

Comments from Project Members

"At this moment, as generative AI and AI agents are set to transform society, what is truly needed is not people who can use tools, but professionals who can create results in the field.

The FDE certification program is a challenge to make transformation-driving professionals socially visible.

We hope this program will accelerate DX promotion and AI utilization at Japanese companies, leading to new value creation and the resolution of social issues in the Society 5.0 era."

Future Plans

The IoT Certification Committee will promote the following initiatives going forward.

  • Development of the FDE certification syllabus
  • Design of the accreditation system
  • Design of the certification system
  • Implementation of corporate demonstration projects
  • Collaboration with educational institutions
  • Recruitment of partner companies
  • Preparation for launching the Level 1 exam

After conducting alpha and beta tests for the Level 1 exam starting in July 2026, the official exam will begin. We also aim to launch Level 2 accreditation operations within the year. Furthermore, we are considering launching Level 3 certification operations within one year.

In addition, we will advance collaboration with AI vendors, SaaS companies, system integrators, and educational institutions, aiming to build a cross-industry talent development ecosystem.

What the FDE Certification Program Aims to Achieve

The FDE certification program is not merely a qualification system.

It is a new definition of profession for the age of AI agents, and a challenge to new standards of talent evaluation.

We aim for a society where

"what you know"

is not what matters, but rather

"what you have achieved"

is what is evaluated.

The FDE certification program will promote the creation of a new foundation to make visible to society not "people who can use AI," but "people who can deliver results with AI agents."
