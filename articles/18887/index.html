Summary

Aising, a Japanese company, describes its AI implementation support service for manufacturers. It argues that model performance or successful PoCs alone do not produce results, since equipment, data environments, and operating rules differ by site. The company says it practiced a field-embedded approach before the FDE term became widespread. The service covers four phases—site research, feasibility validation, overall planning, and requirements definition—run iteratively so insights feed into the next theme. Aising frames this as three FDE functions: translating problems, implementing results, and feeding knowledge back, detailed in a free Concept Book.

What Is the AI Implementation Companion Service?

The AI Implementation Companion Service is a companion-style service that consistently supports everything from on-site research to requirements definition, connecting AI to tangible business results on the ground. Improving AI model performance or succeeding at a PoC alone cannot produce results in actual operations. Especially in industrial domains such as manufacturing, equipment configurations, data acquisition environments, and operational rules differ from site to site, so a deep understanding of the front lines is essential to turn AI adoption into results. Even before the term FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) became widely known, we had been practicing an approach of deeply engaging with customers' sites and consistently supporting everything from organizing challenges to the design, implementation, and production deployment of AI systems. This aligns with the essence of FDE, which prioritizes connecting AI to actual business results. We also do not believe that adopting AI is a goal in itself. Our role is to envision the "future you want to achieve" together with our customers and walk alongside them to make it a reality. AI is merely a means to realize that future. To steadily shape the future our customers aim for, this service values the following three principles.

1. Start with Understanding the Front Lines

Through on-site research, we identify the points where AI can be most effective.

2. Assess Investment Value

We make judgments based not only on technology, but also on return on investment and feasibility.

3. Walk Alongside Until Results Are Achieved

With implementation and operation in view, we aim for AI that continuously generates value in the field.

Six Values That Turn AI Adoption into Results

In the AI Implementation Companion Service, we consistently support everything from on-site research to requirements definition, working alongside our customers to concretize the decisions needed for AI adoption. To achieve AI adoption that goes beyond a mere PoC and extends through implementation and operation, we provide the following values.

Identifying where AI can be most effective

Designing AI adoption suited to the front lines

Supporting with implementation and operation in view

Achieving AI that takes root in the field and keeps delivering

Turning on-site insights into the next improvement

Generating continuous business results

Four Phases Supporting AI Adoption

Even before the term FDE became widely known, we had been practicing an approach that turns AI adoption into results through four phases: on-site research, feasibility verification, overall planning, and requirements definition.

AI adoption does not end with a single project. Challenges and priorities change from site to site, so it is important to continuously drive improvements while identifying themes each time. In the AI Implementation Companion Service, we repeat the phases from on-site research to requirements definition, concretizing high-value AI adoption themes one by one. Moreover, by applying the insights gained from projectized themes to the next theme, we keep advancing AI adoption continuously.

Through these four phases, we guide AI adoption toward the "future you want to achieve." How the AI Implementation Companion Service Works

STEP.01 On-Site Research Phase

We grasp the actual conditions and constraints of the site and clarify the challenges that serve as the starting point for AI adoption. <Example Activities> ・On-site operation visits ・Process confirmation by workflow step ・Key person interviews ・Review of business flows and documents/forms ・Investigation of data acquisition status, etc.

STEP.02 Feasibility Verification Phase

We evaluate the value of AI adoption and technical feasibility, and organize the materials needed for investment decisions. <Example Activities> ・Business impact assessment ・ROI simulation ・Business bottleneck analysis ・Technical feasibility assessment ・Simple data evaluation, etc.

STEP.03 Overall Planning Phase

For the selected themes, we prepare roadmaps and promotion structures with implementation and operation in view. <Example Activities> ・Building an implementation roadmap ・Setting phased result targets ・External partnerships and tool adoption policies ・System configuration (microcontrollers/PCs, etc.) ・Preparation of executive briefing materials, etc. *Our approach from on-site research through overall planning is explained in detail in the "Manufacturing AI Adoption Guide." If you would like to learn more, please take a look.

STEP.04 Requirements Definition Phase

Toward the evaluation and implementation of AI models, we organize objectives, deliverables, and requirement specifications, and build a shared understanding with our customers. <Example Activities> ・Objectives and deliverables ・Verification scope and AI generalization scope ・Organizing requirements for the AI ・Organizing AI inputs/outputs ・Required AI accuracy, etc.

Turning AI Adoption into Results

The value of AI adoption is realized only by continuously generating results in the field, not at the moment AI is deployed. That is why we do not stop at short-term PoCs or system deployments, but walk alongside our customers with the "future they want to achieve" in view. Taking on challenges no one has yet solved creates new competitive strength. We draw out the potential dormant in people and industry, and shape the answers together with our customers to build future competitiveness. However, those answers cannot be found without knowing the front lines. Each customer's site is different, and so are the futures they aim for and the paths to achieve them. That is why we start by facing our customers' sites and envisioning the first step toward the future together. Please tell us about the future you aim for and the challenges you face in the field. From there, the first step toward turning AI adoption into results begins.
