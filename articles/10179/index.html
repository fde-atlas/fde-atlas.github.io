A10X · 2026-09-02 · 6 min read

Summary

A10X compiles what is publicly known about Wonderful's Forward Deployed Engineer hiring. As of late October 2026, the company has 39 open FDE requisitions across 38 markets, none in the US. Founded in early 2025, Wonderful raised $550M at a $5B valuation in September, partly to expand deployment teams. The dossier details job requirements, language bars, and team structure, but notes the interview loop is undocumented and no pay figures are published anywhere.

Key points

  • Wonderful has 39 open FDE requisitions across 38 markets, none in the US.
  • Founded early 2025; raised $550M at a $5B valuation in September.
  • Requires 5+ years experience in integrations, AI agents, and production systems.
  • Language bars apply in some markets, e.g. C2 German, business-fluent Japanese.

What we know about the loop, tagged by how we know it

  • Wonderful's hiring board runs on Ashby and lists 127 open requisitions as of 2026-10-29. 39 of them are titled Forward Deployed Engineer, across 38 markets (Italy has two): Mexico, Brazil, the Andean region, the Caribbean, the UK, Ireland, most of the EU, Switzerland, Norway, Turkey, Tel Aviv, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, the Philippines and Australia, among others.
  • None of the 39 FDE requisitions is in the US. The only US-located posting on the whole board as of 2026-10-28 is an accounting role in New York.
  • No requisition on the board publishes pay: 0 of 127 as of 2026-10-29, FDE roles included.
  • 38 of the 39 FDE requisitions use the same job description (33 word for word, 5 with only an added header, footer or language line), and Japan's describes the same job with its own list of requirements. The job: embed with customers, turn loosely defined operational problems into technical plans and production systems, design and build AI agents that integrate with customer tools, data and business processes, own deployments from prototype through launch, monitoring and continuous improvement, and turn lessons from one deployment into reusable tools and patterns.
  • The bar, from the shared description: 5+ years building software or solving complex technical problems; software architecture, system design and production engineering; APIs, integrations, data flows and distributed systems; working directly with customers. Nice to have: a forward-deployed, solutions or consulting role, AI agents or conversational systems, LLM evaluation, observability and reliability in production.
  • Some markets add a language bar: German at C2 in Germany, Austria and Zurich, native-level Dutch in the Netherlands, and business-to-fluent Japanese in Japan (fluent to native preferred).
  • All 39 FDE requisitions are filed in the board's S&M department, under a team called FDE. The description itself is an engineering job.
  • Where a posting states a work arrangement, FDE roles are hybrid (UK, Ireland, Germany, Austria, Belgium, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Andean, and Zurich in its description) or on-site (Tel Aviv). 27 of the 39 do not state one.
  • The careers page names three main roles that work together. Deployment Strategists own the deployment and are "measured on customer P&L outcomes". Forward Deployed Engineers are "either generalists across integrations, agents, and data, or specialists such as telephony solution architects". GTM sellers "coordinate a Wonderful pod". The board mirrors it: 26 Deployment Strategist and 26 GTM requisitions (2 of them public sector) sit next to the 39 FDE ones.
  • Wonderful's Candidate Privacy Notice (last updated July 29, 2026), linked from its careers page and from all 127 postings, says the first interview can be with its own AI agent, if you choose: the "first call asking about your professional experience, education, and skill sets will be conducted by the Agent". It also says "all final decisions are made by our human interviewers and evaluators".
  • Four FDE postings (Germany, Austria, Belgium and Zurich) include the line "Apply here. Everyone will receive a response."
  • The Group Deployment Lead posting (Germany or Austria) shows how the delivery org is built: one lead over 10 to 14 Deployment Strategists and FDEs in 2 to 3 pods, carrying 3 to 6 enterprise deployments at a time, reporting to a regional Field CTO, with about 30 to 50% customer travel and pay described as "salary with equity and revenue share".
  • Wonderful's own post on the role, "FDE: The Engineers Owning The Last Mile", says the FDE "is the implementation": not someone who explains the product and hands off to an implementation team, but a senior technical owner embedded with the customer who also manages the relationships and aligns the stakeholders. Its closest analogy is "a founder".
  • The same post names the integrations it means: "WhatsApp and a CRM from the 1990s, or a voice agent and a proprietary banking platform". It also says FDEs are expected to "challenge a customer request when it's not the right thing to build".
  • It says who the role does not fit: engineers "who want stable and well-scoped work that's clear from the outset, who prefer to go deep on a single technical domain, or who find customer interaction draining rather than energizing".
  • The careers page says it hires people who "thrive working on-site with customers" and promises customers "measurable production impact in weeks, not months".
  • TechCrunch (2026-09-02): a $550M Series C at a $5B valuation led by Insight Partners, with Salesforce investing for the first time, about six months after its previous round at a $2B valuation. Wonderful is Israeli-Dutch, founded in early 2025, and says it works in more than 35 countries.
  • Per the same report, it started with customer-service AI agents built for non-English-speaking markets and now sells a broader platform it calls Wonderful AI OS. It said the new money goes partly to expanding its FDE teams.

What nobody outside can know yet

  • Beyond the optional AI first call, nothing Wonderful publishes describes its interview loop: 0 of 127 postings mention interviews (read 2026-10-29), and neither does its careers page. One web search on 2026-10-28, naming Glassdoor, Blind and Reddit, found no candidate report for its FDE role. One site, JobMentis, lists a software-engineer guide for "Wonderful AI" that we could not open. The number of rounds, whether there is a take-home, and the FDE-specific stages are all unknown.
  • No pay figure is published for any role in any market. The only comp language is in words, on 4 postings: the Group Deployment Lead's "salary with equity and revenue share", share options in the AI Enablement Engineer posting (on the FDE team), and commission plus share options in the GTM postings for Austria and Belgium. No posting says whether FDEs get revenue share.
  • Our inference, not a published fact: Wonderful's own words weight three things. An integration between systems that were never meant to talk to each other. Turning a vague operational ask into a scoped plan with a success metric. Telling a customer no. If you walk in with one real story for each, you have prepared for the job as Wonderful describes it.
