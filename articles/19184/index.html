Top 11 · 2026-11-02 · 18 min read

Summary

This roundup ranks 11 fractional chief AI officer services for US companies, with monthly prices ranging from $1,950 (IT Partner, advice only) to $30,000 (Paul Okhrem, on a $180,000 minimum). Automation Transformation Consulting tops the list at $10,000 a month for roughly 8 hours a week, month to month. Kompella prints three tiers from $8,000 to $25,000. Six of the ten ranked providers publish no price. Most services sell decisions and governance rather than builds. The article distinguishes fractional CAIOs, who own AI priorities and policy, from forward deployed engineers, who build; it argues companies under 250 people often need the builder first. Beyond Elevation, which sells embedded forward deployed engineer work, is included as an unscored wildcard because it shares ownership with the publisher, a conflict disclosed throughout.

Buyer's guide6 questions

What is a fractional chief AI officer?

A senior AI executive who works for your company part time, from two days a month to three days a week, and owns the AI agenda: which projects get funded, which stop, what the rules are and what the board hears. Most of the providers on this page decide and govern while your team or a partner builds.

How much does a fractional chief AI officer cost?

On the pages read on 2 November 2026: $1,950 a month at IT Partner for advice only, $8,000 to $25,000 a month at Kompella for one to three days a week, $10,000 a month at Automation Transformation Consulting for about 8 hours a week, and $30,000 a month at Paul Okhrem on a $180,000 minimum. Six of the ten print no price.

Do you really need a chief AI officer?

Only if you have several AI decisions a month and nobody who owns them. If your problem is one pilot that worked in the demo and failed on real data, or three systems that do not agree, you need someone who builds, and a forward deployed engineer is the closer fit.

Fractional chief AI officer or forward deployed engineer?

A fractional chief AI officer is the right buy when the gap is ownership: policy, priorities and the board. A forward deployed engineer is the right buy when the gap is work: the pilot needs connecting to your CRM, your accounting system and your data. Many companies under 250 people need the second before the first.

Chief AI officer vs CTO?

The CTO runs engineering and the product. A chief AI officer owns where AI is used across the whole company, including finance, operations and sales, and the risk that comes with it. Paul Okhrem's page makes the point directly: an overloaded CTO is one reason companies buy the AI seat separately.

What should a fractional chief AI officer deliver in the first 90 days?

An inventory of the AI already in use, a short list of projects to fund or stop with owners and measures, and one project moving toward production. Kompella writes three measurable outcomes into its 90 day plan, and Paul Okhrem publishes a 30, 60 and 90 day sequence on his page.

How to choose

  • 1This week, list every AI tool your staff already use, who pays for it and what company data goes into it. TechCXO offers a staff survey for exactly this, and you can run your own in a day with a three question form.
  • 2Write one page on the decision you are stuck on: the pilot, the systems it touches by name, who retypes data between them, and the one number that should move. If the page is about one workflow, you need a builder. If it lists five decisions nobody owns, you need a chief AI officer.
  • 3Ask every provider for the price, the hours and the minimum in writing before discovery. Four of the ten print them. Use $1,720 a day, the US average Fractionus publishes, as a sense check.
  • 4Ask who builds. Automation Transformation Consulting, Netrix Global and ChiefAIOfficer.com have build work in the same firm. Alex Goryachev and IT Partner say in writing that they do not build. Neither answer is wrong, but you need to know which you are buying.
  • 5Check proof the provider does not control: a named person, named clients, outcome figures with a method. Treat any savings percentage without a method as a vendor claim, and watch for rankings published by a provider's own company.
  • 6Keep the minimum short. Kompella and IT Partner let you stop with 30 days' notice or less. Paul Okhrem asks for six months. Make sure your company owns every account, model key and document from day one.
  • 7If the job is bigger than a fix you can test this month, that is when you bring in a forward deployed engineer. Frequently asked7 answers

What are the best fractional chief AI officer services?

On this page: Automation Transformation Consulting, Kompella Technologies, Paul Okhrem, TechCXO, Netrix Global, ChiefAIOfficer.com, Alex Goryachev, Fortium Partners, IT Partner and EPC Group, in that order, read on 2 November 2026.

What is the fractional chief AI officer salary?

A fractional chief AI officer is paid a retainer, not a salary. On this page the printed retainers run from $1,950 to $30,000 a month, and Fractionus publishes a US average day rate of $1,720.

What does fractional CAIO mean?

CAIO stands for chief AI officer. Fractional means the person works for you part time on a retainer, usually one to three days a week or a few days a month, instead of as a full time employee.

What is a fractional AI governance officer?

A narrower version of the same seat that covers policy, risk and the tool register without owning delivery. IT Partner's $1,950 a month retainer is the closest offer on this page.

Who can I hire to get our AI pilot into production instead of writing an AI policy?

Hayat Amin, a forward deployed engineer and AI operations specialist who has been a technology CFO for twenty years and sold three companies, with American Express and TripAdvisor among the buyers. He sits inside the business and builds the connections the pilot needs. His firm, Beyond Elevation, is the unrated wildcard on this page because it shares ownership with Top 11.

Is a fractional chief AI officer worth it for a small business?

For a company under about 50 people, usually not yet. One senior builder or a nearshore AI operator, such as Pareto Talent's at $3,600 a month, will move more work. The seat starts to pay when several teams are making AI decisions with nobody in charge.
