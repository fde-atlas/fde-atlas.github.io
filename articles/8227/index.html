Organization type is only a preliminary assumption; every candidate should prove its actual team and delivery boundaries.

When you need global scale, broad industry programs, large platform transformations, or multi-workflow implementations, consider evaluating Accenture or Deloitte.

For governance, impact assessments, explainability, privacy, and risk work, consider evaluating PwC; HKPC also publicly offers governance and testing support.

For training, standard modules, public support programs, and custom digital transformation, consider evaluating HKPC.

When your process requires direct access to a lean senior team, custom software, ERP integration, production evals, or on-site FDEs, consider evaluating Onyx.

Making the shortlist does not mean being a fit; project-specific evidence is still required before procurement.

A company's capabilities page cannot tell you who will actually deliver the work, or how many senior people the client can directly access.

General claims and selected case studies cannot replace process-specific baselines, metric definitions, samples, limitations, and client verification.

Verify scope, subcontracting, data location, model and cloud dependencies, intellectual property, acceptance, support, exit, and total cost of operation.

Provide all candidates with the same process, users, baselines, systems, data constraints, error consequences, and unresolved issues.

Ask who is responsible for diagnosis, architecture, integration, evals, launch, support, and upgrades.

Test before scaling the assumptions most likely to invalidate value, safety, integration, or adoption.

Retain sources, gaps, trade-offs, approvals, acceptance gates, and exit clauses; do not substitute branding or demos for evidence.
