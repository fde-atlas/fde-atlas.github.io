Account Executive (AE)

Original title: アカウントエグゼクティブ（AE）

FDX株式会社 · 2026-06-09 · 1 min read

Summary

FDX, a Japanese company, is hiring an Account Executive to take over new accounts won by its BD team and grow them into multi-year engagements rather than one-off PoCs. The AE runs renewals, upsells and parallel projects, working alongside Forward Deployed Engineers to turn field feedback into follow-on deals. First-year targets include managing 5-10 accounts and securing over ¥50 million in new business from existing clients. The role requires 5+ years of enterprise sales experience, executive-level negotiation skills, and ideally a background in IT/SaaS, consulting, generative AI or DX. Work is mostly onsite (3-4 days per week) with flex time and incentives; hiring involves document screening, a casual interview, role-play/case interviews and a final interview with the CEO.

Key points

  • AE role at FDX converts BD-won accounts into multi-year contracts.
  • Targets: 5-10 accounts and ¥50M+ new revenue from existing clients in year one.
  • AEs work alongside Forward Deployed Engineers to translate field results into deals.
  • Requires 5+ years enterprise sales and executive-level negotiation experience.
  • Onsite-focused work (3-4 days/week), flex time, incentives, four-stage hiring process.

Mission

You will take over new accounts acquired by BD and be responsible for contract renewals, proposing additional projects, and managing multiple deals simultaneously. Working alongside the Forward Deployed Engineer team, you will translate on-the-ground traction into the next sales conversations, growing client relationships from "one-off PoCs" into "multi-year partnership contracts."

Expected Outcomes (First 12 Months)

  • Renewals/upsells for 5-10 assigned accounts
  • Acquire over ¥50 million in new deals from existing customers
  • Continuously update proposals and roadmaps in collaboration with the FDE/BPR teams

Required Experience

  • 5+ years of experience in enterprise or B2B sales
  • Experience driving multiple deals simultaneously as an account manager
  • Experience negotiating with executives and department heads
  • A commitment to deeply learning customers' operations and industries and partnering with them

Preferred Experience

  • Experience deepening existing customer relationships in the IT/SaaS/consulting industries
  • Experience proposing and winning deals for generative AI/DX-related services
  • Experience in customer success or project management

Difference from BD

  • BD (Business Development): Handles the first contact with new enterprises through the initial contract. Focused on market development from scratch and partnerships.
  • AE (Account Executive): Takes over customers after contract signing, focusing on deepening relationships, additional proposals, and closing. Responsible for building revenue from the existing portfolio.

Work Style

  • Primarily in-person, office-based (expected 3-4 days in office per week, including client visits and internal meetings)
  • Flextime system
  • Incentive program available

Hiring Process

  • Document screening
  • Casual interview (30 minutes)
  • Role-play interview (60 minutes) / Case interview (60 minutes)
  • Final interview (CEO)
  • Offer discussion
