CollectWise Hires AI Engineers for Debt Recovery

Zero G Talent · 2026-11-20 · 2 min read

Summary

CollectWise is a New York AI startup building debt recovery agents. It is hiring voice AI and forward deployed engineers in NYC.

Full text

CollectWise is building automated AI agents and integrated legal network services to automate consumer debt collection and payment recovery. Founded in 2024 and backed by Y Combinator and Unpopular Ventures, the eight-person New York startup is expanding its engineering team to build out its voice infrastructure and deploy automated agents directly into financial client workflows.

CollectWise builds its system on a tech stack that includes React, Node/Express JS, TypeScript, AWS Lambda, AWS RDS Aurora Postgres, Firestore, and OpenAI LLMs. In a video covering the startup on the Tech Bytes and News channel, CollectWise (YC F24) Is Hiring, commentators noted that consumer debt recovery is a $35 billion market in the United States. The video highlighted that the company's automated agents aim to achieve "double the performance of human collectors at a fraction of the cost" through prompt engineering, fine-tuning, and systematic agent testing.

To expand its core voice technology, CollectWise is hiring an AI Agent Engineer in New York, NY, US ($150,000–$250,000 USD, onsite). The role focuses on building the infrastructure, prompting systems, and core architecture behind voice AI agents using LiveKit to support high-volume consumer interactions.

Integrating Debt Collection Workflows

Beyond voice architecture, CollectWise is filling deployment and operational roles to integrate its software with client backends. A Forward Deployed Engineer ($150,000–$200,000 USD, onsite in New York, NY, US) leads customer implementations end-to-end. The position builds custom client integrations using back-end frameworks and configures AI agents for live production environments.

CollectWise is also adding a Founding Customer Success Engineer ($160,000–$200,000 USD, onsite in New York, NY, US). Working across customer success, engineering, and operations, the engineer manages client communications post-launch, resolves technical issues, and optimizes agent workflows to grow existing customer accounts.

CollectWise was founded by CEO Sean OBrien and former CTO Vivek Isukapalli. OBrien studied computer science and finance at the University of Virginia, served as a portfolio manager at the McIntire Investment Institute, worked as an analyst at Republic, and scaled a digital agency prior to launching CollectWise. Isukapalli studied information systems and computer science at Carnegie Mellon University.
