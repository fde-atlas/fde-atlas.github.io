Your AI expert has never run a company. I have. | Hayat Amin

meethayat.com · 2026-07-19 · 2 min read

Summary

Hayat Amin, a former operator, pitches his fractional "forward deployed AI engineer" service for finance and operations in an FAQ format. He argues one senior technical person embedded in a company can replace a small consulting team, delivering working AI systems rather than roadmaps. Promises include a first system live within eight weeks, full client ownership of code and documentation, data kept inside the client's own accounts, and human-checked agent outputs. Pricing is a fraction of a senior hire, one or two days per week, positioned against agencies and platforms that don't answer for results.

Key points

  • A single embedded operator builds and runs AI inside your business, not a roadmap.
  • First system goes live within eight weeks, with working software in month one.
  • Clients own all code, agents, accounts and documentation; no lock-in.
  • Data stays in client systems; agent outputs are checked before use.
  • Costs a fraction of a senior hire, one or two days per week.

What is a Forward Deployed AI Engineer?

Someone technical who moves into your business and makes the AI work in your world, instead of handing you a roadmap and leaving. I do that job for finance and operations.

One person, really?

One person with AI covers what used to take a small team. You get a single accountable operator instead of a partner, a project manager and two juniors.

Do you replace my team?

No. I take the work nobody wanted off them. Same people, higher value work.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Code, agents, accounts, documentation. No lock-in by design.

How much does a fractional AI executive cost?

A fraction of one senior hire. You get C-suite judgement plus the build, one or two days a week, without the full-time salary, equity or notice period.

How fast do we see something working?

The first system is live inside eight weeks. Week one is scoping, and you see working software from the first month, not a slide deck.

Is our data safe with AI in the loop?

Everything runs inside your own accounts and your own stack. Nothing leaves your control, and every agent's output is checked before it touches a real number.

Why not just hire an agency?

An agency hands you a roadmap and leaves. I do the work, run what I build, and answer for the number it moves. One accountable person, not a rotating team.

What is a forward deployed engineer?

Someone technical who moves into your company and builds the AI inside it, instead of advising from outside and leaving you a roadmap.

How fast does something actually run?

The first system is live inside eight weeks. Week one is spent in your meetings and your systems, and you see working software in the first month rather than a deck.

Does this replace my team?

No. It takes the repetitive work off them. Same people, higher value work.

Who owns what gets built?

You do. Code, agents, accounts, documentation. No lock in by design.

Why not an agency or a platform?

An agency hands over a roadmap. A platform hands over a tool. Neither answers for the number it was supposed to move. This is one named person who does.
