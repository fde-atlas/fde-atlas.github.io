Founding Forward Deployed Engineer | Hyperspell

Zero G Talent · 2026-04-01 · 3 min read

Summary

Hyperspell, a 2024-founded San Francisco startup building a memory and context layer for AI agents, is hiring a founding Forward Deployed Engineer. Backed by Y Combinator, Afore Capital, and others, the company was founded by Conor Brennan-Burke and Manu Ebert. The role combines customer-facing work with full-stack engineering: owning onboarding, improving the self-integrating SDK, embedding with customers, and shipping features end-to-end. It requires 3+ years of product engineering experience, Python and TypeScript fluency, and on-site work in San Francisco. Compensation is $150-220k base plus 0.5-1.5% equity and a $10,000 work-setup budget.

Key points

  • Hyperspell builds a memory and context layer for AI agents.
  • The founding FDE owns customer relationships and onboarding.
  • Role spans SDK work, integrations, dashboards, and analytics.
  • Requires 3+ years product engineering; Python and TypeScript.
  • Pay is $150-220k base plus 0.5-1.5% equity.

What you need

About Hyperspell

Hyperspell is the Memory & Context Layer for AI Agents.

AI agent are clueless geniuses.

AI agents are crushing humans on any standardized test, but wouldn't last a day at a real job. What today's super-intelligent agents are missing is the real world context they are operating in. A context that humans stitch together from hundreds of data points across dozens of interactions and channels. A context that grows with their tasks.

Hyperspell gives AI agents this context by connecting to their user’s workspace data and building a personalized memory and context layer.

Our Values

We believe the future is shaped by those with the boldest ideas. Our mission is to become the default context layer for all organizations that all their AI agents can connect to. We’re not doing this because it’s easy, but because it’s worth it.

We care deeply. We care about our customers and their success, about the future of AI and its contribution to society, and most of all, about each other. Building the frontier of AI, we are de facto one of the most technologically advanced companies in human history — and because of that, not despite that, want to be the most human company too. A place where we can enjoy shaping the future together.

Our Investors

We're backed by incredible investors including Y Combinator, Afore Capital, Scribble Ventures, and Pioneer Fund. Our angel investors include technical staff from OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, and DeepMind, as well as some of the most well-respected founders in Silicon Valley, including Siqi Chen (Runway) and Julian Weisser (On Deck)

Our Founders

Hyperspell was founded in 2024 by Conor Brennan-Burke and Manu Ebert. Conor previously lead growth at Checkr and managed their $30m API product portfolio and is generally known as "the most helpful founder in YC".

Manu has over 15 years of experience in machine learning and AI, sold two prior companies, led high-growth engineering teams at Airbnb, and bought his first .ai domain in 2014 (he had to send a fax (!) to the domain authority in Anguilla to do so).

About the role

As our founding forward deployed engineer, your mission is to set our customers up for success, and work

advance what's possible with AI agents. You'll be working at the frontier of how AI systems understand, remember, and reason about information.

You'll tackle challenges like:

  • Evolving the world’s first self-integrating SDK: Improving how developers connect their apps to Hyperspell, making integration feel like magic
  • Owning relationships: You’ll be the face of the company to many of our customers. You’ll respond to their feature requests and their bug reports, and will fix many yourself before they even hit our ticket system.
  • Customer deep-dives: Embedding with customers to understand their business, their data, their users — then finding opportunities to solve problems they didn't know they had
  • Full-stack feature ownership: Building new integrations, API endpoints, dashboard features, and analytics tools end-to-end.
  • Shaping the product: Translating customer pain points into product direction. Your insights from the field will directly influence our roadmap
  • Technical enablement: Helping customers architect their implementations, debug tricky issues, and get the most out of our platform

What you'll do

  • In the first 30 days, you'll take over new accounts, own onboarding customers to Hyperspell, and build and ship smaller features they request.
  • In 60 days, you'll ship internal tools and dashboards to help us manage workflows. You'll deploy custom AI agents to work alongside you to help our customers succeed.
  • In 90 days, you'll own larger projects that touch all parts of our stack and affect both current and future customers.

Requirements

  • 3+ years of experience product engineering
  • Fluency in Python and Typescript
  • Clarity of thought, excellent communication
  • A genuine joy in helping other engineers succeed
  • Ability to work on location in San Francisco

Compensation

  • $150-220k base salary
  • 0.5-1.5% equity
  • $10,000 to build your dream work setup
  • Health, dental, and vision coverage and generous health benefits
