Turing Pricing 2026: Costs, Rates and Quotes

Ryz Labs · 2026-10-02 · 5 min read

Summary

Turing pricing explained: Turing doesn't publish rates and quotes every engagement. What drives the cost, and how a scoped Ryz Labs quote compares.

Full text

We'll say this up front: Ryz Labs wrote this page, and we think we're the better choice here. We've kept every fact about other companies to what they publish themselves or what public listings show, with sources at the end.

Turing does not publish pricing. Its homepage and enterprise pages list no rates, fees, minimums or trial terms, and every engagement is quoted after a conversation with its team. Its homepage says Turing "drives real economic progress from AI by helping frontier labs improve models and enterprises deploy agentic systems," so the offers you'd be quoted on today are AI-focused: Advise, Augment and Build, plus Turing pods and forward-deployed engineers.

For most US teams, a Ryz Labs quote is the better value. You get a scoped plan, a price, and the names of the people who would do the work before you sign. Those people are top 1% senior engineers, chosen from tens of thousands interviewed, working on US business hours. If you need AI built, you get a dedicated AI pod team that works in your cloud and ships to production. Get a Ryz quote and put it next to anything else you're weighing.

Here is every public price signal we found, checked November 2026. Most firms in this market don't publish a rate card, so we show the hourly band on their Clutch listing and convert it to a monthly figure for one full-time engineer.

Monthly figures are our math at 160 hours a month. Directory bands are entered by the firms themselves and are not quotes, so ask each vendor for a written monthly price for the same roles at the same seniority.

For enterprises, Turing sells "Turing Intelligence" in three parts. All are quoted, not listed.

  • Advise. Strategy and scoping work.
  • Augment. Turing describes this as AI-native talent joining your team.
  • Build. Production systems and agentic workflows. Turing says Build starts with rapid prototyping to test feasibility and ROI, then moves to production engineering with KPIs set on day one.

Turing also sells "Turing pods" that combine AI talent and domain specialists, and forward-deployed AI engineers who own customer outcomes end to end. It says clients keep their code and IP. Talent comes from a remote global network that Turing says has 4M+ engineers and experts. No rate card, minimum or trial is published for any of these offers.

Which offer you buy. Talent embedding (Augment) and Turing-led delivery (Build) are different contracts. One is closer to paying for people, the other closer to paying for an outcome.

Model-level work. Turing's homepage leads with datasets, RL environments and experts for frontier AI labs. Projects that involve fine-tuning, evaluation or post-training are a specialism, and quotes reflect that.

Where the team sits. Turing draws on a remote global network. Public forward-deployed engineer postings include Hyderabad, India. Location affects both the rate and the time-zone overlap with your team.

Duration and scope. A short prototype costs less than a production build with ongoing support. Ask how a quote changes when the prototype becomes a live system.

Who manages the work. In a staff augmentation model your team directs the engineers day to day. In a vendor-led build, the vendor carries more of that load, and the price reflects it.

A practical tip: ask every vendor to quote the same brief. Same roles, same seniority, same hours, same duration, same definition of done. Then ask who exactly would do the work. With Ryz, the names are in the quote.

AI scores are advisory; people decide. See our vetting process.

On New York hours, on your team. Our engineers work on US business hours and join your standups, your repos and your roadmap from week one. Most are based in Latin America.

AI pod teams that ship. A typical pod is about 7 senior engineers, including a tech lead, an ML engineer and backend engineers, building in your cloud (AWS, Azure, GitHub, Postgres, with Anthropic and OpenAI models), with weekly demos. One pod built fraud detection that scored 244K+ invoices in under 30 seconds each and surfaced $5.94M in fraud confirmed by the client's fraud team. Another took marketing compliance review of 8,000+ documents from days to hours for a global capital management firm. See our case studies.

If your project is model training, evaluation or post-training research, Turing's frontier-lab work is built around that.
