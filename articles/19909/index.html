Forward Deployed Engineer | FurtherAI | San Francisco, CA

Zero G Talent · 2026-03-24 · 2 min read

Summary

FurtherAI is hiring recent graduates as Forward Deployed Engineers in San Francisco to build enterprise-grade AI agents for its insurance automation product. The role involves talking to customers, translating their workflows into agentic solutions, and shipping AI capabilities to production, with mentorship from CTO Sashank, formerly of Apple's AI/ML org. Candidates need strong software engineering fundamentals, Python, web frameworks, and cloud experience; LLM or agent architecture background is a plus. FurtherAI's product automates insurance tasks like submission intake, policy comparisons, underwriting audits, and claims validation.

Key points

  • FurtherAI hires new grads as Forward Deployed Engineers in San Francisco.
  • Role: build enterprise AI agents and deploy them in production.
  • Engineers work directly with customers to map workflows to agent solutions.
  • Requires Python, web frameworks, cloud skills; LLM experience preferred.
  • Product automates insurance workflows like underwriting audits and claims validation.

What you need

We're looking for exceptional people to join our engineering team as Forward Deployed Engineers. This is not your typical new grad role - we’re hiring cracked engineers eager to shape the future of agentic AI systems

What you'll do

  • Build enterprise-grade AI agents from the ground up
  • ⁠Work closely with our CTO, Sashank, who brings experience from building speech and language models at Apple’s AI/ML org
  • Talk to customers, understand their flows, and translate those into robust agentic solutions
  • Implement cutting-edge AI capabilities in production
  • Collaborate with a small, elite team of engineers from Google X, Microsoft, Apple, Meta, …

Why This Role

You’ll be at the forefront of AI agent system design, working alongside experienced engineers and contributing meaningfully from day one. This is a rare opportunity to get deep technical mentorship while building frontier tech that solves critical problems for businesses.

Who You Are

  • Recent graduate with a CS/related degree (or equivalent experience)
  • Strong foundation in software engineering principles
  • Passionate about AI, ML, and building intelligent systems
  • Self-directed problem solver who thrives in ambiguity
  • Experience with Python, modern web frameworks, and cloud infrastructure
  • Bonus: Prior work with LLMs, ML frameworks, or agent architectures

Our Culture

We’re a small, driven team that values technical rigor, intellectual curiosity, and speed. Our engineers come from top-tier teams like Microsoft, Apple. We care deeply about ownership and pushing boundaries together.

Location

San Francisco, CA (Remote options within USA available for exceptional candidates)

Compensation

Competitive salary, meaningful equity, comprehensive benefits

FurtherAI is an equal opportunity employer and values diversity at our company.

About FurtherAI

FurtherAI helps insurers by providing AI-powered assistants tailored for insurance operations. It automates repetitive tasks across workflows such as submission intake, policy comparisons, underwriting audits, claims validations, and form processing. Its system ingests unstructured documents, extracts data, flags inconsistencies, and drives interactions across internal systems or external portals.
