Forward Deployed Engineer job titles: what each company calls the role

FDE Dispatch · 2026-10-20 · 10 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch pulled 725 live Forward Deployed Engineer postings from 121 employers on public Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday boards on October 24, 2026, and indexed the job titles used. 78.3% use engineer titles, 11.9% deployment strategist titles, and 9.8% management titles. Databricks (122), Palantir (105) and Salesforce (50) post the most openings; the top five employers account for 48.1%. The article lists posted pay (e.g. OpenAI $185K–$300K, Anthropic Staff FDE $280K–$320K) and travel requirements, and explains that many employers pair the engineer with a business-side Deployment Strategist — the strategist owns business discovery and rollout planning, the engineer owns technical discovery and the build. Where no strategist is named, the engineer owns both.

Key points

  • 725 postings at 121 employers: 78.3% engineer titles, 11.9% strategist titles
  • Databricks (122), Palantir (105), Salesforce (50) lead; top 5 hold 48.1%
  • Forward Deployed Software Engineer appears in 44 non-internship postings at 7 employers
  • Posted pay ranges from OpenAI $185K–$300K to Anthropic Staff FDE $280K–$320K
  • Paired teams split work: strategist does business discovery, engineer does technical build

Method and limits

These counts cover the 121 employers whose public Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or Workday boards were pulled on October 24, 2026. They count open postings: one employer can post the same role in many cities. They are not a census of the whole market. Titles that borrow the phrase for other jobs stay in the file and are left out of every figure here. FDE Dispatch has no affiliation with these employers.

What the title variants signal

Title variants signal different ownership: some postings put the FDE on discovery through production rollout, others pair the engineer with a strategist or file the role under professional services. The posting text, more than the title string, tells you what you will own.

  • OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer. The FDE owns discovery, technical scoping, system design, build, and production rollout. The posting does not name a paired strategist.
  • OpenAI Deployment Lead, Forward Deployed Engineering. The Platform team turns promising use cases into repeatable solutions and partners with FDEs.
  • Ramp Software Engineer, Forward Deployed. Posted under a Core FDE org; the work includes an embedding model inside a core product area and building agents.
  • Databricks Forward Deployed Engineer (L4). Filed under professional services. The posting states FDEs are billable and lists Databricks certification.
  • 84.51° Lead Forward Deployed Engineer. An internal FDE: embedded with internal delivery teams to drive AI-enabled SDLC across Kroger and 84.51°.
  • Scale Frontier Agents Engineer (Forward Deployed Engineering). Centers on production agents, eval harnesses, and guardrails.
  • Anthropic Staff Forward Deployed Engineer. Department listed as Applied AI; the posting places the role as a member of the GTM team and states you do not need to have shipped agents to production.

The paired model: engineer and strategist

Several employers hire the technical FDE next to a business-facing partner. That pairing is the most FDE-specific signal on these listings.

  • Palantir hires Forward Deployed Software Engineers and Deployment Strategists side by side, including New Grad and Internship variants on the Seoul board and US government tracks. For the full Palantir cut of those 105 postings, see Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer.
  • Salesforce puts its Forward Deployed Engineer in a pod with a Deployment Strategist. The posting calls the strategist the Strategic Builder, a customer-facing advisor who shapes AI strategy and drives adoption, and calls the FDE the Technical Builder.
  • Ramp Software Engineer, Forward Deployed AI Solutions co-leads engagements with an AI Solutions Strategist. Per the posting, the strategist owns business discovery, ROI narrative, stakeholder alignment, and rollout planning; the engineer owns technical discovery, solution design, prototyping, implementation, and production readiness. Artifacts named include system context and data flow diagrams, an integration plan, a security model, an evaluation plan, and an operational plan.

In a paired team, the engineer is judged on technical discovery and the build. Where no strategist is named, you own business discovery too, so prepare for both. Rehearse that split with the FDE interview loop guide and the practice questions bank.

What the postings ask for

Experience floors on these listings: 3+ years at Ramp (preferred) and Salesforce; 4+ years at Scale Frontier Agents; 5+ years at OpenAI, Scale Public Sector, and 84.51°; 8+ years at Anthropic Paris; 9+ years for Databricks L4. Salesforce states prior customer-facing technical delivery is required.

Travel on the postings that name it runs from 20% (Databricks) to about 50% (OpenAI and Ramp AI Solutions). Anthropic Staff FDE lists 25%; Anthropic Paris lists 25-50%; Palantir Seoul FDSE prefers 25-50%; Salesforce lists about 25%; Scale Public Sector expects 25% with up to 50% as necessary.

Questions people ask

Is Forward Deployed Engineer the same as Forward Deployed Software Engineer?

Employers use both titles for the same family of work. OpenAI, Anthropic, Salesforce, Databricks, and 84.51° post Forward Deployed Engineer. Palantir and Scale post Forward Deployed Software Engineer. Ramp posts Software Engineer, Forward Deployed. In the October 24, 2026 snapshot, 44 of 725 non-internship postings contain Forward Deployed Software Engineer, at 7 employers: Anduril Industries, Applied Intuition, Hive, OpenAI, Palantir, Saronic and Scale AI. Read the posting for ownership of discovery, build, and travel; the title alone does not fix the split with a Deployment Strategist.

What is a Deployment Strategist?

A Deployment Strategist is the business-side paired role next to the Forward Deployed Engineer. Palantir hires Forward Deployed Software Engineers and Deployment Strategists side by side. Salesforce puts the FDE in a pod with a Deployment Strategist it calls the Strategic Builder, while the FDE is the Technical Builder. Ramp's AI Solutions engineer co-leads with an AI Solutions Strategist: the strategist owns business discovery, ROI narrative, stakeholder alignment, and rollout planning; the engineer owns technical discovery, solution design, prototyping, implementation, and production readiness.

How much do Forward Deployed Engineers earn?

As posted on October 22, 2026 on these employer listings: OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer SF lists $185K – $300K + equity, with the posting stating "base pay offered may vary." OpenAI Deployment Lead, Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) - Platform lists $207K – $294K + equity, with the same base-pay-may-vary note. Anthropic Staff Forward Deployed Engineer lists an annual salary of $280,000–$320,000 USD; the posting adds a standard note that ranges for sales roles are OTE. Ramp Software Engineer, Forward Deployed lists $168K – $285K + equity. Ramp Software Engineer, Forward Deployed AI Solutions lists $189K – $330K + equity. Scale Frontier Agents Engineer lists base $180,000–$225,000 for SF, NY, and Seattle. Scale Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Public Sector lists base ranges by location: $184,000–$292,560 (SF/NY/Seattle); $165,600–$263,304 (Hawaii, Washington DC, Texas, Colorado); $138,000–$219,420 (St. Louis). 84.51° Lead Forward Deployed Engineer lists base $125,000–$207,000 USD and notes eligibility for variable compensation. Several other postings on this page do not list pay. For the fuller pay table, see Forward Deployed Engineer salary. Figures change when postings close; check the linked careers pages.

Which companies hire the most forward deployed engineers?

In the October 24, 2026 snapshot of 725 live FDE postings at 121 employers, the employers with the most open postings are Databricks (122), Palantir (105), Salesforce (50), Accenture (40), OpenAI (32), LangChain (17), Cognition (15), ElevenLabs (12), then Cloudflare, Cohere and Scale AI (11 each). The top 5 account for 349 postings (48.1%) and the top 10 for 415 (57.2%). These are open-posting counts on public boards that day, not headcount, and FDE Dispatch has no affiliation with these employers.
