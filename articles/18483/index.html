[AI Support Series, Part 4] T3 launches its AI transformation companion service "AI BANSOU DRIVE"

Original title: 【AIサポートシリーズ第4弾】ティースリー、AI変革伴走サービス『AI BANSOU DRIVE』を提供開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-07-28 · 5 min read

Summary

Japanese IT services firm T-three (ティースリー) will launch AI BANSOU DRIVE on July 28, 2026, an AI transformation support service modeled on the US-originated Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) concept. A dedicated AI implementation partner embeds with the client company to handle strategy design, implementation, and on-site adoption end to end—positioned between consulting (advice only) and SES-style contracted development. Pricing is 1.65 million yen per month for a full-time plan or from 660,000 yen for a 40% part-time plan, with a three-month minimum term. The offering draws on experience supporting over 100 companies, with monthly strategy meetings led by CAIO Takuya Nemoto. Reported results include cutting proposal writing from three hours to 30 minutes and reducing inquiry workload by 50% via an FAQ chatbot.

Key points

  • T-three launches AI BANSOU DRIVE on July 28, 2026, based on the FDE concept.
  • Dedicated partners handle strategy, implementation, and adoption end to end.
  • Pricing: 1.65M yen/month full-time; from 660,000 yen/month at 40% capacity.
  • Built on knowledge from supporting over 100 companies; CAIO Takuya Nemoto leads monthly strategy meetings.

A dedicated AI implementation partner at your side from ¥660,000 per month—moving from "using AI" to "transforming with AI"

T-Three Inc. (Headquarters: Omiya Ward, Saitama City, Saitama Prefecture; Representative Director: Toshio Enomoto) will launch "AI BANSOU DRIVE," a service in which a dedicated AI implementation partner embeds within a company to provide end-to-end support from strategy design through implementation to on-the-ground adoption, starting July 28, 2026. The service addresses the challenge faced by many companies: the lack of AI talent.

■ Service Overview

The new profession originating in the United States, the "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)," is someone who embeds deeply in the field, understands challenges, and builds solutions themselves.

"AI BANSOU DRIVE" is based on the FDE philosophy and leverages knowledge accumulated through support for over 100 companies, including generative AI training services, "AI coaching" for executives, and generative AI adoption support "AI BANSOU."

It is neither AI consulting that "ends with recommendations," nor SES (System Engineering Service) that "builds what is instructed," but rather support in which experts themselves roll up their sleeves and take responsibility through implementation.

It supports companies in moving from the stage of "using AI effectively" to the stage of "transforming with AI."

■ Background of the Offering

While the internal adoption of generative AI tools is progressing, many companies report that they "have neither the people, the time, nor the know-how to implement."

AI training and tool adoption alone do not lead to on-the-ground adoption, and the burnout of AI promotion staff and the high costs of consulting and outsourced development have become new challenges.

Through supporting over 100 companies, T-Three recognized these challenges and developed "AI BANSOU DRIVE," in which experts themselves take responsibility through implementation.

■ Service Features

1. Proven knowledge from over 100 companies

We provide insights accumulated through various service formats, including generative AI training services, "AI coaching" for executives, and generative AI adoption support "AI BANSOU." Cross-industry, cross-domain knowledge is our strength.

2. Easy to start, with a clear endpoint for peace of mind

We accompany you as a partner that builds "strategy → creation → on-the-ground adoption" end to end. Because the investment model has a clear entry and exit, you can start with confidence.

3. Monthly strategy meetings with CAIO Takuya Nemoto to promote AI use from a management perspective

Through monthly strategy meetings led by Chief AI Officer (CAIO) Takuya Nemoto, we continuously update your AI utilization policy from a management perspective.

4. Reducing person-dependency risk through manual development, automation tool building, and in-house development support

We reduce the risk of AI use becoming person-dependent through the development of business manuals, the building of automation tools, and in-house development support.

■ Pricing and Eligibility

Pricing:

・Full-time plan: ¥1,650,000 (tax included) per month (100% utilization)

・Part-time plan: from ¥660,000 (tax included) per month (40% utilization and up)

※Minimum contract period of 3 months, auto-renewing monthly thereafter

Eligibility:

・Companies struggling to hire or train AI talent

・Companies whose AI promotion staff are isolated or burned out

・Companies lacking the time or know-how to implement

・Companies troubled by the high costs of AI consulting and AI development

Service format:

・On-site or remote (flexible response according to the company's work arrangements)

■ Implementation Results

・Reduced proposal creation time from 3 hours to 30 minutes, while improving the order win rate

・Automated monthly report creation, fully automating report generation (Word/PowerPoint) from Excel data

・Reduced inquiry handling workload by 50% through the introduction of an internal FAQ chatbot

・Improved the order win rate through sales meeting follow-up automation
