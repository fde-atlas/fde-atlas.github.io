Summary

This article presents a 14-18 week learning roadmap for aspiring AI FDEs, moving from fundamentals through Transformer architecture, inference engines (vLLM, TRT-LLM, SGLang), quantization, distributed systems, deployment, and AI engineering, to business workflows, compliance, and change management. It argues that top AI roles now require a three-layer skill moat: deep technical understanding, full-stack production engineering, and business acumen. Candidates should map technical metrics to business value and prepare STAR-format project stories for interviews.

Key points

  • A sequenced 14-18 week roadmap from basics to interview prep.
  • Covers Transformer, inference engines, quantization, distributed training, and deployment.
  • Proposes a three-layer skill moat: depth, full-stack ability, business sense.
  • Stresses explaining AI value via CSAT and cost savings, not just P99/QPS.
  • Recommends practice questions and STAR project stories for interviews.

A complete learning path from zero to interview, recommended 14-18 weeks

Overall Roadmap​

Week 1-2:  入门篇 ────────── 了解 FDE，建立认知
Week 3-4:  Transformer ─── 理解模型架构
Week 5-6:  推理引擎 ────── vLLM / TRT-LLM / SGLang
Week 7:    量化 + GPU ──── 量化方案、显存模型
Week 8:    分布式 ──────── TP / PP / DP
Week 9:    部署架构 ────── K8s、弹性、可观测性
Week 10:   AI 工程 ────── Prompt / RAG / Agent / 评测
Week 11:   成本 + 前沿 ─── 成本优化、前沿跟踪
Week 12-14: 面试 + 管理 ── 答题框架、培养体系
Week 15:   AI 商业工作流 ─ 业务流程编排、企业集成、业务指标
Week 16:   合规与安全 ─── 数据隐私、审计追溯、Prompt 安全
Week 17-18: 变更管理 ──── 灰度上线、ROI 度量、组织采纳

Five-Dimension Skill Growth Goals​

After completing this learning path, you should be able to:

  • Clearly explain the Transformer architecture and the Attention mechanism
  • Compare the strengths, weaknesses, and use cases of different inference engines
  • Choose an appropriate quantization approach and explain the trade-offs
  • Understand GPU memory models and performance bottlenecks
  • Design multi-GPU/multi-node inference deployment architectures
  • Calculate the cost of inference services and provide optimization recommendations
  • Design AI business workflows (Human-in-the-Loop, state machines, enterprise integration)
  • Build business metrics systems (mapping technical metrics to business value)
  • Design data privacy and compliance solutions (PII handling, audit logs, Prompt security)
  • Develop AI gray-release and ROI measurement plans
  • Track the latest model and technology developments
  • Answer interview questions using a structured framework
  • Demonstrate systematic thinking about FDE team building

The "Skill Moat" of the AI FDE​

Top AI positions have moved past the stage where "knowing how to call APIs" could land you a high salary. In 2025-2026, what companies truly lack is talent with composite skills:

三层技能护城河:

第一层：技术深度（别人不会的你会）
  - 不只是"会用 vLLM"，而是理解 PagedAttention 的底层机制
  - 不只是"做了量化"，而是能对比 INT8/FP8/INT4 的精度-速度 trade-off
  - 不只是"跑了模型"，而是能做 kernel-level profiling 和调优
  - 红队测试/AI 安全评估能力（Anthropic 等公司特别看重）

第二层：全栈工程能力（别人做不出来的你能）
  - 能把一个模糊的 AI 想法变成生产级应用
  - 包含：权限控制、仪表盘、容错机制、监控告警、弹性伸缩
  - 不只是"模型能跑"，而是"系统能稳定运行"

第三层：商业嗅觉（别人说不清的价值你能说清）
  - 能用商业语言解释 AI 价值：提升了多少 CSAT、节省了多少成本
  - 不只是堆砌技术指标（P99、QPS），而是讲业务影响
  - 能判断"这个 AI 方案到底值不值得做"

Learning Approach Recommendations​

  • Learn in order — each stage is the foundation for the next
  • Connect with your actual experience — compare your existing engineering experience (backend development, operations, algorithms, etc.) with the new knowledge
  • Do the exercises — each module ends with practice questions and interview simulation questions
  • Organize your project stories — as you learn, organize your real projects into STAR stories
