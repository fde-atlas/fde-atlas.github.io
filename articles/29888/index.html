Forward Deployed Engineer Jobs India: 250-270 Open Roles

Short answer

Forward Deployed Engineer hiring in India runs through AI startups, GCCs, SaaS companies, consulting firms and BFSI enterprises, concentrated in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Gurgaon, Mumbai and Pune. Demand grew roughly 800% across the first three quarters of 2025 according to TeamLease Digital.

But the pool is small: India has only around 250 to 270 open FDE roles at any given moment. This is a high-leverage, targeted search, not a high-volume one — and the biggest practical mistake is searching only for the exact phrase “Forward Deployed Engineer.”

The Indian FDE market in 2026, honestly

The growth numbers are genuinely striking. Indeed listings for the role rose from 643 in April 2025 to roughly 5,330 in April 2026 — a jump near 729% year on year, reported by the Times of India — in a period when global tech layoffs crossed 115,000. Andreessen Horowitz called the FDE the hottest job in startups, and the Financial Times ran a feature on the same trend.

The counter-signal matters just as much. Those global listing figures are dominated by the US market. India's absolute number of open roles remains small. Both facts are true at once, and understanding that is the difference between a smart career move and a wasted six months.

What follows from it: treat this as a targeted search where fit and evidence beat volume of applications, keep an adjacent role as your fallback, and consider the internal transfer route seriously — moving into an FDE seat at your current employer is often faster than competing for one of a few hundred external openings.

Companies hiring Forward Deployed Engineers

Category Who hires and what the work looks like
Frontier AI labs OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, Google DeepMind. Deploying models into large enterprise environments. Highest compensation; smallest number of India-based seats, often global-remote.
Data and AI platforms Palantir (FDSE, Deployment Strategist), Databricks, Snowflake, Scale AI. The original model and the deepest engineering bar.
Enterprise software Microsoft, Google Cloud, Salesforce, Adobe, ServiceNow. Building out embedded delivery arms.
Consulting and services McKinsey QuantumBlack has hired Principal Forward Deployment Engineers in the region. Indian IT services firms are building AI delivery benches.

AI-native startups Series A to C companies where the FDE is frequently the difference between a pilot and a renewal. Fast hiring, wide variance in pay.

The job titles you should actually search

The single most common search mistake is filtering on one exact phrase. The same job carries several names, and in India many of the best roles are never titled “Forward Deployed Engineer” at all. Search all of these:

  • Forward Deployed Engineer / Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE)
  • Applied AI Engineer — Anthropic's name for the function
  • Deployment Strategist — Palantir's variant
  • Customer-Facing AI Engineer / Customer Engineer
  • AI Solutions Engineer / AI Implementation Engineer
  • GenAI Engineer (enterprise delivery)
  • Principal Forward Deployment Engineer — the consulting-firm framing
  • AI Deployment Engineer / Enterprise AI Engineer

Where the roles are

Bengaluru leads by a wide margin, followed by Hyderabad, then Gurgaon and the NCR belt, then Mumbai and Pune. Most roles expect onsite or hybrid presence near the customer, because proximity is the entire point of the role. Genuinely remote openings exist but are the exception and cluster at global companies hiring into India rather than at Indian employers.

In a market with a few hundred open roles, evidence decides the shortlist. The 360DT Forward Deployed Engineer Program ends with eight real deployments, a panel-evaluated capstone, and interview and portfolio preparation built around exactly what these postings screen for.

What Indian FDE job postings ask for

Read across current listings and the same requirements recur, in roughly this order of emphasis:

  • Production Python, plus SQL and data modelling. Often TypeScript or JavaScript as a second language.
  • LLM APIs and orchestration, with RAG pipelines you can explain at the chunking and retrieval level.
  • Evaluation harnesses — increasingly explicit in postings, and a common gap in candidates.
  • Cloud deployment with IAM and cost awareness. Azure appears frequently in Indian enterprise contexts.
  • REST and gRPC integrations, data pipelines, and the ability to work with undocumented enterprise data.
  • Docker and Kubernetes at a working level; fine-tuning listed as medium priority rather than essential.
  • Demonstrable stakeholder communication — the ability to demo to non-technical audiences is named directly in most postings.
  • Domain exposure in BFSI, healthcare, retail or manufacturing, which is a real differentiator in the Indian market.

Experience expectations cluster around two to five years for a pivot from product or backend engineering. FDE is generally not a fresher-default role; candidates without production experience are usually better served entering through a backend or data engineering seat first.

How to actually get one of these roles

Lead your résumé with deployments, not frameworks. A line reading “shipped a retrieval system into a customer's Azure tenant, passed their security review, running with a monitored eval pipeline” outperforms any list of tools.

Apply under all the title variants above. Set alerts on each one. A large share of suitable roles never use the FDE label.

Target the employer category deliberately. GCCs and AI-native startups have the most India-based seats. Frontier labs pay most but hire fewest. Services firms hire more but often at implementation-level pay — ask who owns the code before you invest in the process.

Push the internal transfer first. If your current employer has any customer-facing delivery work, volunteering for it builds precisely the evidence external applications need, on the payroll.

Prepare for a loop that is half non-technical. Roughly half the process is case studies and stakeholder scenarios. Candidates who prepare only on algorithms are the most common rejection.

Frequently asked questions

Which companies hire Forward Deployed Engineers in India?

Hiring runs through six categories: frontier AI labs such as OpenAI and Anthropic, data and AI platforms including Palantir and Databricks, enterprise software companies such as Microsoft and Salesforce, consulting firms including McKinsey QuantumBlack, global capability centres serving BFSI, healthcare and manufacturing parents, and AI-native startups.

How many Forward Deployed Engineer jobs are there in India?

Around 250 to 270 open roles at any given moment, according to TeamLease reporting. Demand growth has been dramatic — roughly 800% across the first three quarters of 2025 — but the absolute pool remains small, so this is a targeted search rather than a volume one.

Which city has the most FDE jobs in India?

Bengaluru by a wide margin, followed by Hyderabad, then Gurgaon and the NCR belt, then Mumbai and Pune. Most roles expect onsite or hybrid presence near the customer, since proximity is the point of the role.

Are Forward Deployed Engineer jobs remote in India?

Mostly not. Most roles expect onsite or hybrid presence near the customer. Genuinely remote openings exist but are the exception, and they cluster at global companies hiring into India rather than at Indian employers.

What experience do I need for an FDE job in India?

Most postings target two to five years of production experience, typically a pivot from backend, product, data or solutions engineering. FDE is generally not a fresher-default role, because the job assumes you can already ship software before adding customer ownership on top.

What should I search for besides 'Forward Deployed Engineer'?

Search all the title variants: Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, Deployment Strategist, Customer-Facing AI Engineer, AI Solutions Engineer, AI Implementation Engineer, GenAI Engineer and Principal Forward Deployment Engineer. A large share of suitable roles in India never carry the FDE label.
