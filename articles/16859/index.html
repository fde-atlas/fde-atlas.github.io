Scale AI Interview Experiences & Questions (2026)

Top Tech Transition · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

This article compiles 10 real Scale AI interview experiences shared by candidates across seven roles, including Product Manager, Software Engineer, BizOps, Deployment Strategist, and Forward Deployed Engineer. The typical process involves about 3 rounds over roughly 4 weeks, with common stages being behavioral, coding, system design, and technical interviews. Candidates rate the difficulty 3.5/5, and only about 12% of those with final results received offers. The piece lists over 40 actual questions with candidate answers, and notes details like an openly intense "996"-style culture and FDE candidates being held to the same bar as software engineers before role fit was assessed.

Key points

  • 10 real interview experiences across 7 role types.
  • Typical process: about 3 rounds, 4 weeks total.
  • Most common rounds: behavioral, coding, system design, technical.
  • Difficulty rated 3.5/5; offer rate around 12%.
  • FDE candidates faced the same bar as software engineers.
