Forward Deployed Engineer compensation in 2026 spans an unusually wide range — wider than almost any other engineering title — because the label covers everything from consulting delivery roles to frontier-lab principal positions.

The ladder

Tier Level Total comp (2026)
Defense & government (Anduril, DoD Tech Force) Entry–Mid $113K–$200K
Consulting (EY, SIs) Entry–Mid $160K–$280K
Enterprise SaaS (Salesforce, Intercom, GitLab) Mid–Senior $180K–$330K
Big tech (Microsoft, Nvidia, Apple) Mid–Senior $120K–$431K
AI-native product cos (Cresta, DevRev, Labelbox) Mid–Senior $190K–$350K
Data & inference infra (Databricks, Snowflake, Anyscale) Senior $230K–$420K
Palantir Mid–Staff $200K–$400K
Frontier labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) Mid–Senior $350K–$550K
Frontier labs Principal $1M+

Across the whole market, the median base salary is around $210K, with median total comp near $238K. At the top of the market, median mid-level TC at frontier labs is $385K, staff-level $610K, and principal FDEs are clearing $1.2M — driven mostly by equity grants that often exceed base salary.

Two bands are worth reading carefully. The $350K–$550K frontier-lab figure is the enterprise/top-of-track number — OpenAI’s standard domestic FDE reqs actually post $162K–$385K depending on city and level, so the lab premium is real but the entry point is lower than the headline suggests. And the new defense and big-tech tiers pull the floor down: Anduril lists $113K–$155K, the DoD’s GS-14 Tech Force tour starts at $126K, and Microsoft’s FDE ceiling ($235K) sits below OpenAI’s floor. Same title, a 3x spread — the premium is proximity to the model, not the work.

The widest single bands now come from smaller companies pricing in uncertainty: Tenex ($240K–$600K) and Coframe ($180K–$500K) post ranges that top most public companies’. The tightest is Anyscale’s near-surgical $267K–$287K — a $20K spread where everyone else posts $200K ranges.

What moves your number

  • Company tier dominates everything else. The same person commands 2x at a lab vs a consultancy.
  • Equity timing: FDE functions at Ramp, Rippling, and DevRev are new — early equity at still-private valuations is the asymmetric bet.
  • Clearance: cleared FDE work (Scale AI’s government practice, Palantir) carries a durable premium.
  • Deployment scar tissue: demonstrated production deployments beat credentials. One quantified customer outcome (“cut handle time 31%”) is worth more than any certification.

Negotiation notes

FDE comp is less standardized than SWE ladders — bands are wide and managers have discretion. Anchor on the customer-facing premium: you’re being hired to own revenue-adjacent outcomes, not tickets. Benchmark against levels.fyi and current postings (most now include bands).
