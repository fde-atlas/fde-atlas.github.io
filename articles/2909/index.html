AWS establishes an FDE organization, dispatching AI engineers to enterprises to deploy agentic AI

Original title: AWS成立FDE組織，派遣AI工程師進駐企業部署代理AI

Full text

AWS

AWS announced on Tuesday (6/30) the establishment of a dedicated Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) organization, and will invest $1 billion in internal resources to expand its AI deployment services, dispatching thousands of AI engineers to embed within enterprise customers to jointly develop and deploy agentic AI systems, joining OpenAI and Anthropic in the race to offer FDE services.

The FDE model originated at Palantir, referring to a vendor dispatching engineers to embed directly within customer organizations, working alongside customer teams to clarify requirements, integrate data, and tune systems, so that technology can truly land in existing processes and operational scenarios. The AWS FDE model has 3 key features: adopting an "Agentic-First" approach, shortening AI deployment timelines from months to days, and enabling customers to independently operate their AI systems after project completion, rather than remaining dependent on AWS.

AWS explained that most FDE engineers were originally developers of AWS AI services, and will collaborate with customers' business, engineering, and information security teams, leveraging customers' own data, governance frameworks, and processes to jointly build and deploy production-ready AI systems. In addition, unlike traditional consulting firms that only provide assessments and recommendations and treat each project as an independent engagement, AWS FDE adopts a long-term partnership model oriented toward shared business goals and outcomes, rather than billing by hours.

AWS FDE employs Agentic Deployment Technology and introduces an AI-Driven Development Lifecycle, allowing AI agents to participate in every stage of software development, while human engineers are responsible for supervising, verifying, and guiding the process.

The ultimate goal of AWS FDE is to cultivate customer self-sufficiency, enabling customer engineers to gradually progress from observers to co-developers, and ultimately to managers capable of independently operating AI systems; upon project completion, customers will receive the fully deployed AI system, a Knowledge Graph, operation manuals, system architecture documentation, and internally trained AI seed talent.

AWS FDE engineers have already embedded at multiple enterprises, including the Allen Institute, Cox Automotive, the National Basketball Association (NBA), the National Football League (NFL), Japan's Ricoh, and Southwest Airlines.
