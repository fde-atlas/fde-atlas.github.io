The A10X field notes

A10X · 2026-10-24 · 3 min read

Summary

A10X's field notes answer common questions about the Forward Deployed Engineer role, with sourced data. It covers what FDEs do, 2026 pay (Levels.fyi median ~$201K; Palantir $240K, Sierra $460K), how to break in via a portfolio of agents, RAG and evals, round-by-round interview questions, and how the role differs from SWE and sales jobs. Analysis of 477 postings shows FDE is an engineering role, not sales. It also revisits a16z's gross-margin-for-moat thesis and Anthropic's plan to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by 2027.

Key points

  • FDEs embed with customers and ship AI systems to production.
  • Median US FDE pay is ~$201K; Sierra pays $460K.
  • Only 4 of 477 FDE postings mention sales quotas.
  • Anthropic's Academy targets 10,000 trained engineers by 2027.
  • Advice: build an agent, eval suite, and shadow rollout.
