Summary

This page is an index of articles from FDE Soken, a Japanese resource on Forward Deployed Engineers. It organizes content into four sections: FDE fundamentals, comparisons with related roles, Palantir and its products, and FDE hiring trends at companies like Anthropic and OpenAI. The comparison articles distinguish FDEs from SWEs, solutions engineers, SIers, data scientists, product managers, and consultants, mainly by scope of responsibility and whether work ends at recommendations or runs through production deployment. Palantir articles cover Gotham, Foundry, AIP, and Apollo, plus competitors such as Databricks and Snowflake.

Key points

  • Defines FDEs as engineers embedded at customer sites from problem discovery through implementation and adoption.
  • Comparison articles cover FDE vs SWE, solutions engineer, SIer, data scientist, PM, consultant, and SES.
  • Palantir section explains Gotham, Foundry, AIP, Apollo and contrasts with Databricks and Snowflake.
  • Related roles include Palantir-origin Deployment Strategist and FDSE (Forward Deployed Software Engineer).
  • Company trends analyze Anthropic and OpenAI FDE job requirements versus the Palantir model.
