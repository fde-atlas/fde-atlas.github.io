How I interview for the hottest tech role right now

The Human Loop · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

Summary

An FDE lead at a YC startup shares the interview questions he uses when hiring forward deployed engineers, including handling customer distrust of AI, churn threats, and a 45-minute live integration exercise with a messy API. He also mentions a dinner conversation with OpenAI's team that changed how he interviews, reflected in the video's first question. The piece cites market data: FDE job postings up over 1,165% in a year and base pay up to $320K at top labs. It ends by promoting his mock interview service at clankerengineer.io.

Key points

  • Written from the interviewer's side at a YC startup.
  • Sample questions cover customer trust, churn threats, and live coding.
  • A conversation with OpenAI's hiring team shaped his approach.
  • FDE postings grew over 1,165% in a year; base pay up to $320K.
  • Ends with a plug for his paid mock interview service.

Full text

As the FDE lead at a YC startup, I sit on the interviewer side of forward deployed engineer interviews. So I know exactly which answers get people rejected.

So I made a video with the questions I ask FDE candidates.

A few you’ll hear:

  • “Your customer’s team doesn’t trust the AI. What do you do?”
  • “The customer says they’ll churn if you don’t build this.”
  • “Here’s a messy API. Build an integration in 45 minutes, and talk me through it.”

I also got invited to a small dinner with OpenAI’s team recently, and asked them how they hire FDEs. One thing they said changed how I interview. It’s the very first question in the video.

Why care? FDE postings are up over 1,165% in a year, and top labs pay up to $320K base. It’s one of the best jobs in tech right now if you like building and talking to customers.
