1-Day FDE | One day per week on-site, running AI implementation and training

Original title: 1Day FDE｜週1日常駐で、AI実装と教育を回す

FDX株式会社 · 6 min read

Summary

FDX, a Japanese company, offers "1Day FDE": a Forward Deployed Engineer on-site one day per week who builds AI workflows in the morning, trains staff via OJT in the afternoon, and records measured time savings each week against predictions from FDX's prior AI assessment report. Pricing is ¥300,000–400,000 per month (excluding tax), with a 3-month minimum and only 3 client slots monthly; assessment clients get priority. Progress is tracked in FDX's in-house tool AXceed, which produces an AX score (0–100), weekly check-ins, and monthly PDF reports for management. The service is explicitly designed to end: over three phases spanning 6–12 months, implementation ownership shifts in-house, after which clients graduate to occasional spot consulting.

Key points

  • FDE works on-site one day weekly: morning implementation, afternoon OJT training.
  • Costs ¥300,000–400,000/month, 3-month minimum, capped at 3 clients monthly.
  • Weekly measured time savings are verified by on-site managers, not self-reported.
  • In-house tool AXceed tracks progress with a 0–100 AX score and monthly reports.
  • Designed to graduate clients to self-sufficiency within 6–12 months.

03 Beyond the impressive recommendations, who builds the workflows that actually run? Only a gap in implementation remains.

EXIT DESIGN

We don't let on-site contracts drag on indefinitely. Once your team can run on its own, 1Day FDE graduates.

PHASE 1 | Up to 3 months

The FDE builds, the team uses. Time savings are measured and accumulated starting from the priority tasks in the diagnostic report.

PHASE 2 | 3 to 6 months

The team builds, the FDE reviews. Implementation leadership gradually shifts in-house.

PHASE 3 | 6 to 12 months

Everything runs in-house. After that, we switch to on-demand consultations only when needed.
