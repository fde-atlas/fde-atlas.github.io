Employer Funding for CFDE Foundation

Artificial Intelligence Uni (AIU.ac) · 2026-10-08 · 3 min read

Summary

This page from Artificial Intelligence Uni (AIU.ac) explains how employers can fund a place on the Certified Forward Deployed Engineer (CFDE)™ Foundation programme, an 18-week online cohort course costing £1,099 with roughly 8 hours of weekly work. It covers two payment routes—reimbursement or direct corporate card payment—and provides an adaptable email template for requesting training budget. The course covers the full AI deployment engagement lifecycle and ends with a three-week capstone.

Key points

  • CFDE Foundation runs 18 weeks at £1,099, about 8 hours per week.
  • Employers pay via reimbursement or corporate card on the learner's payment link.
  • AIU provides sponsorship letters and pro-forma invoices on request.
  • The course covers handoff, discovery, scoping, build, deploy, iterate, and hand-back.
  • Includes an editable email template for asking a manager for funding.

How employer funding works

Your employer can fund your place in either of two ways. You pay and your employer reimburses you, or your employer pays your secure payment link directly by corporate card. A pro-forma invoice addressed to your employer is available on request, and our admissions team can send your employer a sponsorship letter confirming your place, the programme, the dates and the fee.

If your employer is considering places for three or more people, they can contact partnerships@aiu.ac.

Dates for the next cohort, and the application itself, are on the Admissions page.

The letter

Copy the letter into an email, or download it as a Word document you can edit. Fill in the parts in square brackets and change anything you like; it reads best in your own words.

Subject: Request for funding: Certified Forward Deployed Engineer (CFDE)™ Foundation

[Date]

Dear [Manager’s name],

More of our AI work now depends on getting systems out of the demo stage and into everyday use with the people who rely on them. I would like to build that skill properly, and I am writing to ask whether the business would fund my place on a course designed for exactly that work.

The course is CFDE Foundation, run by Artificial Intelligence Uni Ltd. It is delivered online through the AIU learning platform in a structured cohort: recorded lectures and course materials, instructor-led discussion and feedback, and human-marked assignments and capstone.

Programme: CFDE Foundation
Provider: Artificial Intelligence Uni Ltd
Structure: 18 weeks: 15 weekly modules, then Capstone, weeks 16 to 18
Time: Suggested commitment of approximately 8 hours per week.
Starts: [Cohort start date]
Fee: £1,099

CFDE follows the work of deploying an AI system with a client through each stage of the engagement: post-sale handoff, discovery, scoping, build, deploy, iterate, hand-back. It ends with a three-week capstone in which each learner ships a working AI system against a realistic client brief in a simulated engagement, assessed by practitioner reviewers against the programme’s assessment criteria. By the end I will have produced the full set of work an engagement like that calls for: discovery notes, an architecture document, a working deployed prototype, client-facing documentation and a written record of the design decisions behind it.

Could the fee be met from our training and development budget? If that is not the right budget, I would be grateful to know which one could cover it. AIU can issue a sponsorship letter and a pro-forma invoice addressed to the business, and the fee can be paid in either of two ways: I pay and the business reimburses me, or the business pays my secure payment link directly by corporate card.

If you agree, I will bring what I learn back to the team as the course goes on, and share my capstone work once it has been assessed so that others can use it on our own projects.

I would be glad to talk this through whenever suits you. Thank you for considering it.

Kind regards,
[Your name]
[Your role]

After your manager agrees

Step 1

Apply

Complete the application at apply.aiu.ac. It takes about ten minutes and there is no application fee.

Step 2

Ask for the paperwork

When we confirm your fee and send your payment link, email admissions@aiu.ac with your application reference if your employer needs a sponsorship letter or a pro-forma invoice.

Step 3

Payment

Your employer pays your payment link by corporate card, or you pay and claim the fee back. Questions about invoices and receipts go to billing@aiu.ac.
