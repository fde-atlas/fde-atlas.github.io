The Career Path for a Forward Deployed Engineer

Full text

Forward deployed engineering has a hiring boom and, at most companies, no written answer to what the engineer becomes. Rupanjana Bhattacharjee put it plainly in August 2026: "The career ladder is genuinely unsettled."

Companies are responding with a ladder inside the seat. Bhaskar Roy at Workato has one that climbs to an "architect" FDE. That's fine with one rule: define each rung by what the product absorbed from the engineer's deployments. Otherwise the ladder rewards being irreplaceable at one account, which is how you get what Miku Jha at ServiceNow called "the permanent glue between the customer and the product."

The exits matter more. Into product, as the person who answers the handback. Up, running the function without a bookings number. Out, to found a company on a problem seen at three customers. And as of October 2026 founders are coming the other way, into the seat, for paid work.

Part 8 of The FDE Transition, The Career Out of the Seat: Draw the Exits, Not Just the Ladder, makes the case in full, including the checkpoint I'd write into the offer. The handbook chapter on becoming an FDE covers the three doors in.
