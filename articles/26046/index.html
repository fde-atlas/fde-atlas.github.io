Enterprise AI Adoption Daily Briefing — FDE Field Report

Original title: 企业AI落地每日动态

FDE百科 · 2026-06-17 · 13 min read

Summary

This is the daily field log of FDE Baike (FDEBAIKE.COM), a Chinese site tracking enterprise AI deployment. Running for 132 consecutive days, each edition curates verifiable signals about AI adoption, generated by a daily intelligence system and human-reviewed before publishing. Topics span the evolution of the forward deployed engineer role, agent governance, acceptance criteria, industry delivery cases (finance, manufacturing, healthcare), and Chinese policy developments. A recurring theme: going live does not equal ROI—delivery claims require customer-side evidence, and FDE value lies in results plus long-term contracts.

Key points

  • Daily digest running 132 days, human-reviewed, sourced facts.
  • Tracks FDE roles, agent governance, and delivery acceptance.
  • Covers finance, manufacturing, healthcare cases and policy.
  • Recurring theme: launch is not ROI; demand customer-side proof.
