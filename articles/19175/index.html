Buyer's guide3 questions

What is Sales Commission Software?

Sales commission software automates the process of calculating, tracking, and paying commissions to sales teams. It replaces manual spreadsheets by integrating with data sources like your CRM and ERP to apply commission plan rules automatically, providing accurate and timely payout information to both administrators and reps.

When do you need to upgrade from spreadsheets?

You should upgrade from spreadsheets when calculation errors become frequent, you spend more than one day per month on commission processing, or your sales team regularly disputes their payout amounts. Other triggers include having more than 15-20 reps on variable compensation or implementing multi-layered commission plans that are difficult to model in Excel.

How does this software improve sales performance?

This software improves performance by providing reps with real-time visibility into their potential and earned commissions. This direct line of sight between activity and reward acts as a powerful motivator, helps reps forecast their income, and builds trust in the compensation process, reducing time spent on disputes and increasing time spent selling.

How to choose

  • 1Map your most complex commission rule and ask vendors to demonstrate exactly how their platform handles it.
  • 2Verify native, bi-directional integrations for your specific CRM (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot) and ERP/payroll systems.
  • 3Request a sandbox environment to test the admin experience for building a test plan and the rep experience for viewing a statement.
  • 4Ask for references from two customers with a similar team size (e.g., 100 reps) and industry (e.g., B2B SaaS).

Frequently asked4 answers

What is the average cost of sales commission software?

The average cost typically ranges from $25 to $60 per payee per month, with enterprise platforms often requiring a minimum annual contract value of $25,000 to $50,000. Pricing is heavily dependent on the number of commissioned employees, complexity of plans, and the level of implementation support required.

How long does it take to implement commission software?

Implementation time varies from 4 weeks for simple plans and small teams to over 6 months for complex, enterprise-wide deployments. The average for a mid-market company (50-250 reps) is typically 8 to 12 weeks from kickoff to go-live.

Can these tools handle ASC 606 compliance and reporting?

Yes, most leading commission software platforms offer features to help with ASC 606 compliance. They can automate the capitalization and amortization of commission expenses over the life of a contract, generate the necessary reports for finance and audit teams, and reduce the manual work required for compliance.

What is the difference between ICM and commission tracking software?

ICM (Incentive Compensation Management) is the broader category, while commission tracking is a core function within it. ICM platforms usually include commission calculation, tracking, reporting, territory management, quota planning, and performance analytics. Simpler 'commission tracking' tools may focus only on the calculation and reporting aspects.
