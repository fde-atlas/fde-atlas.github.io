Summary

This is a careers page from FDX, a Japanese company that applies the Forward Deployed Engineer model—originating at Palantir—to enterprise AI transformation (AX). It emphasizes end-to-end delivery from strategy and diagnosis through production implementation and handover to client in-house teams, explicitly avoiding support that stops at PoC. Five Tokyo-based roles are listed with salary ranges: FDE (¥8M–18M), Solutions Architect (¥9M–20M), Business Development (¥7M–15M plus incentives), Account Executive (¥8M–16M plus incentives), and Pre-sales Engineer (¥8M–16M). Office attendance is roughly two to three days per week for most roles.

Key points

  • FDX implements the Palantir-origin FDE model for Japanese enterprises.
  • Work spans diagnosis, design, production rollout, and operations handover.
  • Projects end with client self-sufficiency, not vendor dependence.
  • FDE salary is ¥8M–18M; Solutions Architect reaches ¥20M.
  • Five Tokyo-based openings, mostly with 2–3 office days weekly.

Full text

FDX Inc. is a company that implements enterprise AX (AI Transformation) through the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) model. We are looking for teammates to take on everything from strategy planning to operational handover, end to end.

Why FDX

What it means to work at FDX.

"Making the complex simple." Here is the environment where we untangle complex business processes at client sites and reshape them into AI-driven forms.

Standing in the field as Forward Deployed

Embed at client worksites, observe, form hypotheses, and verify them with code. We implement the Palantir-originated FDE model, connecting design, implementation, and field adoption within the same team, for Japanese enterprises.

End to end, from strategy to operations

We don't offer support that ends with a PoC. We connect diagnosis of management challenges, solution design, production implementation, and operational handover within the same team. That is the outline of FDX's work.

Making clients self-sufficient

We don't stop at being an outsourced vendor. At project completion, we hand operations over to the client's in-house team, leaving behind a state where things run without us. The completed form of AX is not dependence, but independence.

Teammates for whom the drive to grow is the default

In an era where AI, business, and even ourselves can transform in six months, we want to work with people who go beyond their current skills, keep learning, test things in the field, and keep reinventing themselves. We invest generously in books, conferences, and internal study sessions.
