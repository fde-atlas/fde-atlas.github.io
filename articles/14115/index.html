Sales engineer

FDE Interview Prep · 2026 · 2 min read

Full text

A sales engineer (SE), also titled presales engineer, is a technical role on or beside a sales team that helps win deals with demos, proofs of concept and technical answers. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics describes sales engineers as selling business products or services that need technical expertise, and says employers usually pay them salary plus commissions or salary plus bonuses. Why it matters in interviews

SE experience is strong customer evidence: you have run discovery, handled objections and demoed to executives. The gap to close is what you built that ran after the deal closed, so bring one story where your code stayed in production and name who used it. Don’t frame the move as leaving sales. Source 6Forward Deployed Engineers (Gergely Orosz)PublisherThe Pragmatic EngineerSource typenews report Frame it as staying on after the sale: you still help win the deal, and you are also the one who ships it. OpenAI’s Financial Services posting in New York, as of September 2026, asks the FDE to lead discovery and scoping from pre-sales through post-production and to own on-call readiness for the AI systems it ships. If an FDE posting mentions commission or quota, check its pay basis; the OTE entry has the questions to ask.

The lesson FDE, software engineer, solutions architect, sales engineer: telling them apart covers quota, and the other lines that decide the role.
