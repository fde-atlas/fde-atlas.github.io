Salesforce Launches "Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) Partner Network" in Japan to Accelerate Agentforce Implementation Together with Partners

Together with nine initial partners in Japan, Salesforce will support customers in moving from pilot deployments of AI agents to production, accelerating the transformation to the agentic enterprise

Salesforce Japan (Headquarters: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; Chairman and CEO: Shinichi Koide; hereinafter "Salesforce") today announced the full-scale launch of the "Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network" (hereinafter "FDE Partner Network") in Japan.

The FDE Partner Network was announced at TDX 2026, held in the United States in April this year. To accelerate the production deployment of Salesforce's AI agent platform "Agentforce," it provides Salesforce's engineering expertise and deep access to the product roadmap to a select group of partner companies that have passed rigorous screening. Nine initial partners have joined in Japan, and the network is now being fully rolled out in the Japanese market. Through this, Salesforce will further strengthen its framework for supporting customers' transformation into agentic enterprises together with its partners.

Background and Challenges

While momentum for AI adoption is growing among Japanese companies, many remain stuck at pilot deployments and struggle to move to full production. The main cause is not AI model performance, but a lack of enterprise-grade implementation know-how, such as data integration with complex legacy systems, establishing governance frameworks, and embedding AI into business processes. To close this "gap between pilot and production," Salesforce will further strengthen its investment in the partner ecosystem.

About the FDE Partner Network

The FDE Partner Network is a select group of partner companies screened based on their Agentforce implementation track record and depth of technical expertise, rather than company size or length of business relationship. Salesforce shares the engineering methodologies and product knowledge it has cultivated internally with certified partners, adopting a collaborative model in which partners' specialized engineers engage deeply in customer projects.

Globally, FDE Partner Network partners already drive one-third of all successful Agentforce implementations, a track record that underpins the credibility of this partner network.

The benefits that Salesforce's FDE Partner Network provides to partners and customers are as follows.

  • For partners: ○Priority access to Salesforce's engineering expertise and product roadmap ○Acquisition of advanced technical skills in Agentforce implementation and differentiation ○Eligibility to join an ecosystem that can successfully lead customers to production deployment
  • For customers: ○Reliable and rapid transition from AI pilot deployments to production that actually generates business value ○Practical, hands-on support from partners trained in Salesforce's engineering methodologies ○Beyond mere configuration and deployment, business transformation through AI agents that fundamentally integrate business processes with AI agents while maintaining governance

The nine initial partners of Salesforce's FDE Partner Network in Japan are as follows. (Listed in Japanese syllabary order, including group companies)

  • Accenture Japan Ltd.
  • NTT Data Corporation
  • Globalway Corporation
  • Terrasky Corporation
  • Deloitte Tohmatsu LLC
  • Dentsu Research Institute Corporation / Dentsu Digital Inc.
  • PwC Consulting LLC
  • Fujitsu Limited
  • Flect Corporation

Salesforce's Perspective

Atsushi Urano, Executive Officer and Head of the Alliance Business Headquarters at Salesforce Japan, said: "For Japanese companies to achieve real results with AI, close collaboration with partners who combine both technical expertise and business understanding is essential. While many Japanese companies recognize the potential of AI agents, they face challenges in moving beyond pilot deployments. The FDE Partner Network was designed to break down this barrier by sharing Salesforce's engineering expertise with partners. Globally, partners in this network account for one-third of successful Agentforce implementations, and I am confident we will achieve the same results in Japan. We offer customers not just implementation support, but a partnership that delivers production deployments that reliably generate business value. Together with the nine partners joining this initiative, we will fully accelerate the AI transformation of Japanese companies and their transformation into agentic enterprises."

Through the FDE Partner Network, Salesforce will further expand its partner ecosystem in Japan, increasing Agentforce production deployments in the Japanese market and strengthening customers' transformation into agentic enterprises.

About Salesforce

Salesforce helps companies of all sizes transform into agentic enterprises. By integrating people, AI agents, applications, and data into a single, trusted platform, it enables unprecedented growth and innovation. For more information, visit salesforce.com/jp.
