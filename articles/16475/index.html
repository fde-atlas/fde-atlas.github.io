Career paths

All careers

  • Forward Deployed Engineer

    Work with a customer, find out what is actually stuck, and build the software that makes their workflow run.

  • AI Engineer

    Build useful software on top of AI models, then find out where it gets things wrong and fix it.

  • Agent Engineer

    Build AI software that can take actions, with clear limits and a way to recover when something fails.

  • GTM Engineer

    Fix the data and automate the handoffs a sales or marketing team depends on.

  • AI Account Executive

    Help a buyer work out whether an AI product solves a real problem, then run the evaluation and the deal.

  • AI SDR / BDR

    Find people with a real reason to talk, start a relevant conversation, and hand a qualified meeting to an AE.
