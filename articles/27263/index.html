Compare forward deployed engineer (FDE) courses in India: IIT Delhi, IIT Hyderabad, Scaler, FDE School, Fireblaze and Datamarcos by format, length and structure.

Short answer: in 2026, FDE programs in India run from 13-week practitioner courses to a 12-month program. Some are offered by IIT units (IIT Delhi's Continuing Education Programme, IIT Hyderabad's AI Launchpad) and others by training providers. The useful ways to compare them are:

  • How much of the program is a client-style engagement, as opposed to lectures.
  • Whether you practise the consulting side: discovery, scoping, SOWs, demos and handover.
  • What experience the program expects you to have already.

What a forward deployed engineer does

A forward deployed engineer works inside a customer's environment. They connect a product or model to that customer's systems, data, security rules and users, and stay until it works in production. In India the role is being hired for by global capability centres, IT-services firms, AI companies, enterprise software firms, cloud platforms and consulting companies (The Hindu BusinessLine, 12 Aug 2026). For how the role compares with solutions engineers, AI engineers and consultants, see FDE vs adjacent roles.

Who each option is for

  • IIT Delhi's CEP certificate suits professionals who want a certificate from IIT Delhi's CEP and a 6-month weekend format. Registration for the current batch was closed when we checked, so look for the next intake.
  • IIT Hyderabad's FDE Practitioner program suits engineers with roughly 2–5 years' experience who want an intensive 13-week program and an optional campus visit. The certificate details are still to be confirmed on the page.
  • Scaler's program suits people who want a long (12-month) live program with many graded sessions.
  • FDE School suits learners who want a short 13-week live program with a published week-by-week structure.
  • Fireblaze suits working IT professionals who want weekend classes and the option of a Nagpur classroom.
  • FDE Academy suits engineers who want a selective, 8-month program.
  • Datamarcos's Forward Deployed AI Engineer program suits working engineers who want 22 weeks of live, engagement-based practice. It covers the consulting side (discovery, SOW, UAT, ROI, executive demo) as well as the build, with FDE-specific interview preparation. It issues a Datamarcos certificate, not an IIT one. If an IIT name on the certificate is your priority, the IIT Delhi or IIT Hyderabad programs fit that better.

Six questions to ask any FDE program

  • How many full client-style engagements will I run end to end? One? Four?
  • Do I write discovery notes, a scope or SOW, and a handover pack, or only code?
  • Is security and governance hands-on? That means SSO and RBAC, PII handling and audit trails, not just a lecture.
  • Who plays the "client", and who reviews my decisions?
  • What interview preparation is FDE-specific? For example ambiguous system design, live debugging and customer role-play.
  • What experience does it assume? Several of the programs above ask for prior engineering experience.

FAQ

Is there a forward deployed engineer course in India?

Yes. As of October 2026 several exist, including programs from IIT Delhi (CEP), IIT Hyderabad AI Launchpad, Scaler, FDE School, Fireblaze AI School, FDE Academy and Datamarcos. They differ a lot in length, format and certificate.

Is there an IIT forward deployed engineer course?

IIT Delhi's Continuing Education Programme runs an Advanced Certificate in AI Forward Deployed Engineering with a CEP, IIT Delhi certificate. IIT Hyderabad's AI Launchpad runs an FDE Practitioner program and lists an IIT Hyderabad certificate, with final certificate details to be confirmed. Check their pages for intake dates and eligibility.

Can a fresher become a forward deployed engineer?

Many FDE programs expect prior engineering experience: for example, IIT Hyderabad's program is aimed at engineers with roughly 2–5 years and Fireblaze's at 1–8 years. A few, such as FDE School, also describe accepting graduates. Expect to build a strong portfolio, and possibly to start in an adjacent role.

How long does an FDE course take?

The programs compared here run from 13 weeks to 12 months.

Do FDE courses guarantee jobs?

Don't assume so; read each provider's terms. Datamarcos doesn't guarantee placement, salary or a hike.
