Written and reviewed by: FDE Talent Editorial Team / Primary sources such as official job postings and official announcements from each company are cited

FDE hiring means recruiting Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) who work on-site with customers to implement your product all the way to production. Since experienced candidates are scarce, the basic approach is not to search by job title, but to define the allocation of responsibilities and hire from adjacent roles. FDE is a new job category, and there are no established best practices yet for requirements definition, candidate pool building, or selection design.

In this article, we explain how to approach FDE hiring from the perspective of hiring companies, based on primary information from Palantir, the originator of the FDE organization, and OpenAI, which launched its FDE division in 2025, as well as data on public job postings compiled by our editorial team. For the FDE role itself, see What is an FDE?; for those looking for FDE jobs, see FDE Job Listings and FDE Jobs and Hiring Companies List.

Key Takeaways

  • FDE hiring means recruiting Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) who work on-site with customers to implement your product all the way to production; as of the editorial team's tally on November 7, 2026, there were 523 FDE job postings from 74 companies on public job boards.
  • The success or failure of FDE hiring is determined by four points: (1) requirements definition with documented allocation of responsibilities, (2) candidate pool building targeting adjacent roles, (3) selection that tests scoping skills through practical exercises, and (4) organizational design with feedback loops into the product and evaluation based on customer outcomes.
  • Since FDE-experienced candidates are virtually nonexistent in the Japanese market, FDE hiring is effectively a potential-based hire from SIer, web engineering, IT consulting, or pre-sales backgrounds.
  • Rather than expecting one person to do everything, a realistic design for an FDE organization is to split roles—implementation (Delta) and problem discovery (Echo), as Palantir does—and launch with two or more people.
  • If you label a role "FDE" when its actual work is closer to customer success or maintenance and operations, mismatches after joining will lead to early turnover.

What is FDE Hiring? Overview of the Process

FDE hiring proceeds through the following seven steps. The biggest difference from typical engineer hiring is that you define the allocation of responsibilities before searching for people.

  • 01 Define the purpose: Is it a condition for winning deals, post-adoption retention, or internal AI adoption?
  • 02 Define the allocation of responsibilities: Write down the percentages of implementation, customer interaction, and business travel as numbers
  • 03 Decide whether to split the role: Separate implementation and problem discovery, or assign both to one person?
  • 04 Set the compensation range: Compare public job postings with your internal standards
  • 05 Write the job posting and reach candidates from adjacent roles
  • 06 Evaluate through practical exercises: Include scoping exercises and time-limited hands-on tasks
  • 07 Define post-hire evaluation: Evaluate based on customer outcomes and contributions back to the product

What is an FDE Professional?

An FDE professional refers to someone who has both the implementation ability to write production-quality code and the ability to define problems from customers' vague requests and build consensus. It is not limited to people whose job title is FDE. In Japan, experienced candidates are still scarce, so people from SIers, consulting, and pre-sales backgrounds are potential candidates.

Why FDE Hiring Now?

The issue that remains at the end of AI product sales conversations is not model performance, but "how to integrate it into the customer's real data, existing systems, and business workflows." The FDE is the role that fills this last mile of adoption at the customer's site. OpenAI established its FDE division in early 2025 and expanded to 8 cities across 3 continents in less than a year precisely because this function directly impacts winning deals and retention in the AI business. According to an analysis by Indeed and the Financial Times, FDE job postings increased by more than 800% from January to September 2025 (as cited in an official Salesforce article), and hiring competition will intensify further going forward.

FDE hiring is particularly effective for the following types of companies:

  • AI startups: The product alone doesn't sell, and per-customer implementation and deployment are conditions for winning deals
  • SaaS companies: Expanding into enterprise markets and needing talent to bridge individual customization and product standardization
  • Enterprises: Advancing internal AI adoption in-house and needing engineers who can embed themselves in business units

Why is FDE Hiring Difficult? Three Reasons

  • 01 Almost no experienced candidates: FDE is a role whose job postings began increasing in Japan after 2025, and people with hands-on FDE experience are virtually absent from the market
  • 02 Complex requirements: Because both production-quality implementation skills and customer-facing abilities are required, there may be many candidates strong in one or the other, but few who meet both
  • 03 Few interested candidates: In a March 2026 article, The Pragmatic Engineer cited recruiters' view that while every company wants FDEs, only about 10% of the market is interested in this role

Growing demand is also intensifying hiring competition. According to Lightcast, a labor market data provider, job postings containing "forward-deployed" increased by more than 1,000% year-over-year from January to August 2026 (Fortune, September 2026). In Japan as well, companies such as SoftBank and LayerX are recruiting, with multiple companies competing for the same candidate pool.

How to Build FDE Hiring Requirements? The Three-Layer Model and Allocation of Responsibilities

The most common failure in FDE hiring is when requirements become "a superman who can do everything," resulting in a candidate pool of zero. A useful reference is Anthropic's job posting. The company describes FDE duties in concrete units: building production applications within customer systems, delivering artifacts such as MCP servers and subagents, and systematizing deployment patterns and feeding them back into the product, while also specifying the percentage of travel to customer sites (around 25%). In Japan, JAPAN AI lists the workload breakdown in its job posting: "customer interaction about 20%, technical design and implementation about 80%." Deciding in advance how much time your FDEs will spend on what makes it possible to prioritize requirements.

Also, as Palantir separates "Delta" (Forward Deployed Software Engineers focused on implementation) and "Echo" (Deployment Strategists focused on problem discovery and adoption), a design that splits the role into two job types rather than demanding everything from one person is effective. Especially when launching an FDE organization with two or more members, hiring a pair—one implementation-focused and one customer-focused—is realistic.

Where are FDE candidates? How to build a candidate pool

People with "FDE experience" barely exist in the Japanese market. FDE hiring is therefore effectively potential-based hiring from adjacent roles. There are four promising candidate pools.

  • SIer and outsourced development engineers: Accustomed to being stationed at customers and developing under constraints, with the lowest adaptation cost in working style. Assessing modern stack and LLM experience is key
  • Web software engineers: Implementation skills are sufficient. Verify motivation and aptitude for customer engagement during selection
  • IT consultants (those who can code): Problem discovery and consensus building make them immediately effective. Hands-on verification of whether they can actually complete code is essential
  • Pre-sales, customer engineers, and solution architects: Translators between technology and customers. Check whether they have the drive to carry things through to production, not just stopping at PoCs

In the job posting, given that awareness of the job title "FDE" is still low, adding a supplementary note like "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)" in the title and stating at the beginning of the body that it is a role originating from Palantir, along with the work allocation, improves applicant quality. Conversely, calling a role "FDE" when it is actually closer to customer success or maintenance operations leads to post-hire mismatches and early turnover.

What to evaluate in FDE selection? Verifying scoping skills through hands-on testing

FDE aptitude cannot be judged from a resume alone. Just as OpenAI runs FDE projects in three phases—"scoping → validation → delivery"—we recommend verifying this process execution ability through practical exercises in the selection process.

  • 01 Technical interview: In addition to a standard coding assessment, give an "incomplete-information design problem" and evaluate the quality of questions asked to clarify assumptions
  • 02 Scoping exercise: Conduct a mock intake session with a setting close to one of your actual customers, and have them reproduce the flow from problem identification → minimal solution proposal → scope agreement
  • 03 Practical assignment: Build a small prototype using an LLM (time-limited). Rather than polish, look at prioritization and the judgment to build a working end-to-end flow first
  • 04 Reference checks: If you can ask people on the customer side "would you want to work with this person again?", it corroborates their customer-facing ability

How should post-hire onboarding and the organization be designed?

Hiring an FDE is not the end; organizational design determines the results. There are three design points that can be read from primary sources.

  • Create a path for feedback into the product: OpenAI has published a case where evaluation metrics an FDE built at a customer site led to improvements in the Realtime API. Establish a regular mechanism to reflect FDE discoveries into the product roadmap from the start
  • Make the on-site ratio explicit: In overseas examples, Palantir is about 25%, and some companies around 50%. Hiring with this left vague causes early turnover
  • Base evaluation on customer outcomes: Evaluating by the number of features shipped distorts FDE behavior. Evaluate based on customer business metrics, retention rates, and contribution to expansion deals

What are common failures in FDE hiring? Four patterns

  • 01 Superman requirements: Making implementation, AI, customer negotiation, and industry knowledge all mandatory results in zero applicants. Decide the division of work first, then narrow the mandatory requirements
  • 02 FDE in name only: Calling a role "FDE" when the reality is customer success, maintenance and operations, or on-site support billed hourly. Post-hire mismatch leads to early turnover
  • 03 Not disclosing the on-site ratio: Hiring while leaving the percentage of time spent working at customer sites vague. Palantir explicitly states the travel ratio in its job postings
  • 04 Evaluating by features shipped: Evaluating with the same metrics as product development makes FDEs prioritize the number of features over customer outcomes

What should FDE salaries be set at?

FDEs are in high demand, so the salary range should be set at or above that of software engineers with similar experience. In addition, given the role's direct contribution to deal closure, retention, and expansion, combining performance-based variable compensation or stock options is common overseas. If you set the salary range below market, you need to compensate with attractive opportunities to feed back into the product and career paths (FDE → product development or business leader).

In public Japanese job postings, SoftBank's FDE (seconded to SB OAI Japan) listed a theoretical annual salary of ¥8.12M–¥20.34M, Money Forward's internal FDE listed ¥7.9M–¥15M per year (posting ended in October of the same year), and JDSC's FDE listed an expected annual salary of ¥5M–¥25M (as of September 2026). In a survey aggregating 35 public domestic job postings, the median upper limit for FDE salaries at Japanese companies was ¥15M.

For overseas levels, in 171 job postings in US dollars aggregated by our editors on November 7, 2026, the median of the compensation fields was a lower bound of about $180K and an upper bound of $250K–$300K. The widely reported "$1.45M per year" is a recruiting firm's estimate for top-tier candidates with team leadership experience and is not representative of the market. Define job levels based on who will be entrusted with what, then set the range.

Summary: FDE hiring is decided by "design"

The success of FDE hiring depends on four points: (1) requirements definition with the division of work documented, (2) building a candidate pool targeting adjacent roles, (3) a selection process that tests scoping ability through practical exercises, and (4) organizational design with product feedback loops and customer-outcome-based evaluation. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q What is FDE hiring?

FDE hiring means recruiting a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) who works on-site with customers and implements your product through to production. Because experienced candidates are scarce, the basic approach is to decide the division of work first and then hire from adjacent roles such as SIers, web engineers, consultants, and pre-sales engineers.

Q Why is FDE hiring difficult?

There are three main reasons: few people in Japan have this experience, requirements tend to be complex because the role demands both production-quality implementation skills and the ability to engage with customers, and the pool of people seeking this role is limited. You need to narrow down requirements and proactively explain the role to candidates from adjacent professions.

How many people should you start with when hiring QFDEs?

We recommend starting with at least two. Just as Palantir divides the work between implementation (Delta) and problem discovery (Echo), starting with a pair—one implementation-oriented and one customer-oriented—avoids unrealistic requirements that expect one person to do everything, and also prevents knowledge from becoming siloed in a single individual.

Can a QFDE also serve as a customer success manager or SE?

Some companies start with a combined role, but the essence of an FDE is "performing production-quality implementations at customer sites." If the role is designed so that demos and adoption support make up most of the work, it can lead to a mismatch with people who joined specifically to become FDEs, so clearly defining the allocation of duties is important.

How does hiring a QFDE differ from outsourcing to an SIer?

With SIer outsourcing, the relationship ends upon delivery and no expertise remains in-house. An FDE is an employee who feeds discoveries made at customer sites back into improving the company's own product—the essential difference is that with each deployment, both the product and the organization grow stronger. OpenAI has publicly shared a case where insights from customer sites led to API improvements.

How much does QFDE hiring cost?

When using a recruitment agency, it is success-fee based, so fees are incurred only when a hire is made and the candidate joins. The fee is calculated by multiplying the theoretical annual salary by a rate; the typical market rate is around 35% (based on explanations from PERSOL TEMPSTAFF and ManpowerGroup). For example, hiring an FDE with an expected annual salary of 12 million yen at a 35% rate would result in a fee of 4.2 million yen (calculation example).

What annual salary should you offer a QFDE?

The baseline is equal to or higher than that of a software engineer with the same years of experience. In published Japanese job listings, salaries range from a lower bound of 5 to 8 million yen to an upper bound of 14 to 25 million yen (as of September 2026). In an aggregation of 35 public job listings in Japan, the median upper-bound salary for FDE positions at Japanese companies was 15 million yen.

Can you develop in-house engineers into FDEs?

Yes. Engineers and pre-sales staff who know your own product well are candidates for FDE roles. SoftBank is recruiting for a position called Software Deployment Engineer as a role that will expand into FDE work over time. Since aptitude for customer engagement depends on the individual's aspirations, recruiting volunteers rather than appointing people is the appropriate approach.

Can I request referrals of FDE talent?
