Japanese company Gomumari (ゴムマリ株式会社) published a recruiting page arguing it will train forward deployed engineers (FDEs) from young people with enterprise sales backgrounds, rather than competing for scarce experienced hires. It notes FDEs, a role originating at Palantir, are now recruited by OpenAI and Anthropic, but no company publishes a systematic training program; the industry default is selecting or poaching. Since October 2024, Gomumari has worked across roughly 50 departments at large enterprises, turning diagnosis, implementation, and verification into repeatable, teachable patterns. Candidates practice first on the company's own operations before facing customer sites; experienced engineers are instead wanted for reviewing deliverables.

  • FDEs are heavily contested; no published training programs exist.
  • Gomumari has practiced across ~50 departments since October 2024.
  • AI lowers technical barriers, shifting difficulty to business understanding.
  • Target hires are young people with enterprise sales experience.

FDE—a role that goes into customers' operations and implements AI into their business. Gomumari develops people into this job, starting from young professionals with sales backgrounds.

Experienced candidates are already being fought over.

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a role that embeds itself in customers' operations, implements AI into their business, and delivers results. The role was created by Palantir, and today both OpenAI and Anthropic are hiring for it. OpenAI is recruiting in Tokyo as well. In the US, a16z called it "the hottest role at startups" (June 2025), and in Japan too, engineer job sites have created a new FDE category. Six months ago, there were zero job postings there.

Reading FDE job postings from around the world, you notice something. Everyone is looking for experienced candidates. No company explains how to develop them. Even Palantir, which created the FDE role, has not published a systematic training program. In other words, the industry's current answer comes down to two options: select the best, or poach experienced people.

Why do we believe we can develop them?

The FDE's work falls into two parts. Understanding the business and pinning down its issues. Implementing with AI and validating the results. The technical barrier of the latter is being lowered by AI itself every year. Code is now written by AI. What remains is the former—the ability to discern which business processes, and what exactly, need to be pinned down to produce results.

Since October 2024, Gomumari has been doing this work on-site at major companies. Taking inventory of operations across roughly 50 departments, implementing, and validating. Repeating the same procedure dozens of times turned the procedure into a template. A diagnostic template for taking inventory of issues and drilling down to their essence. An implementation template for running everything from initial build to monthly operation. A validation discipline of machine-checking every item before delivery. Work that has become a template should be teachable. Proving that for the first time at Gomumari is your job.

We want people who have worked in sales.

The hard part of the FDE's work lies before the technology. Understanding business operations in an unfamiliar industry. Communicating with both on-site staff and executives. Taking ownership of the results you deliver as your own responsibility. All of these are things enterprise salespeople do every day. Our CEO, Murayama, also came to this work from enterprise sales of ERP systems.

There is also the matter of fit with templates. If you first use a template to its fullest and then point out its flaws, this training approach moves fast. For people who want to customize things their own way before using them, the first year will likely feel restrictive.

Let us also write down who this is not for.

It is not for people who want to grow their coding skills, or who expect polished training and materials. This is a method of learning not from materials, but by using templates in real work.

It is not that we don't want experienced engineers—it is that their place is different. Gomumari inspects every deliverable with eyes other than those of its creator before shipping it. For experienced people, we want to rely on those inspecting eyes. Young hands build; experienced people review.

The training ground is our own business.

Gomumari is a company that runs on the very systems it sells. Sales meeting preparation, inquiry handling, accounting checks, website operations—there are real operations running every day. Before standing in a customer's site, you first run the templates here. Mistakes cause no trouble for customers, and slacking off stops our own operations. As a training ground, we know of nothing better.
