What is an FDE: The Forward Deployed Engineer delivery model

Original title: FDE 是什么：前置部署工程师（Forward Deployed Engineer）交付模式

和毅智能 Heyi · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

Heyi Intelligence explains its Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) delivery model: the engineers who write the code sit in the client's meetings and stay from first contact through final delivery, instead of handing work across sales, pre-sales, project managers, developers, and operations. The article argues critical business details—like a 94-column reconciliation sheet or intranet browser restrictions—only exist on-site. It cites delivered projects (warehouse ordering, county-level public-security petition statistics), notes a 2026 Chinese MIIT policy document encouraging FDE teams, and describes Heyi's own four-person, all-FDE team averaging 15+ years of experience each.

Key points

  • FDE means the same people who code also gather requirements and deliver, with no handoffs.
  • Traditional delivery passes through six roles, losing business understanding at each step.
  • Key constraints live only on-site and never appear in requirement documents.
  • A 2026 MIIT notice encourages service providers to build FDE teams.
  • Heyi fields four FDEs with 63 combined years of experience and PMP certifications.

In plain terms: we don't send salespeople to negotiate—what arrives are the people who actually do the work; from the first meeting to final delivery, you deal with the same group of people.

Why someone needs to go on-site

Platforms stay outside; people have to go in.

The technical foundations—compute, data, models—have largely been filled in over the past two years; yet many companies' AI investments still haven't translated into business value. The reason is that what really determines whether a system can be used is written in no requirements document:

  • Why that 94-column reconciliation spreadsheet can't be changed;
  • Who keeps track, in their head, of goods promised but not yet shipped;
  • Which single browser the intranet allows.

These exist only in the business environment, visible only to those who go there. They correspond respectively to our delivered warehouse-managed ordering system and county-level public security intranet petition statistics system.

How FDE differs from traditional delivery

Traditional delivery passes through six pairs of hands; with FDE, it's the same people from start to finish.

传统做法 FDE 做法
Who discusses requirementsSales talks, pre-sales writes the proposalThe people writing code sit in your conference room listening to your business
Number of handoffsSales → pre-sales → project manager → development → implementation → operations, six pairs of handsFrom first meeting to final delivery, it's these same few people
Business understandingEach handoff loses a layer of business understandingNo handoff loss; everyone understands the full picture
AccountabilityFollow the documents, deliver by billable hoursAccountable for results

When customers choose a delivery partner, their common concerns map to the FDE model as follows:

Customer concernThe FDE model's answer
Build your own technical team?High cost, hard to hire, hard to retain, and mismatched with business peaks and troughs
Outsource to a big vendor?Where do you rank in their client list? Which tier of their people do you get?
Hire a small supplier?Usually "just follow the docs"—no business understanding, and they leave once it's done
Choose an FDE squadHigh-experience-density people working directly with your business, accountable for results, not for hours

FDE in Ministry of Industry and Information Technology documents

"Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) teams" have been written into policy documents of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology.

The General Office of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology's "Notice on Launching the Special Action for Cultivating AI Application Service Providers" (MIIT Office of Science and Technology Letter [2026] No. 414, issued August 27, 2026) states under "Strengthening service providers' support and safeguards": "Encourage service providers to build Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) teams, rooted at users' sites, to ensure scenario implementation."

The policy shows this organizational form is encouraged; what should reassure customers is still the actual results delivered.

FDE is the organizational form of the two characters in "Heyi"

We didn't learn FDE and then start working this way—we always worked this way, and later found out it's called FDE abroad.

  • He (connection): engineers embedded in the business environment, no layers, no relaying messages;
  • Yi (execution): from pre-sales to delivery, the same people see it through to the end, delivering results, not excuses.
