Founding Forward Deployed Engineer | Rebolt | Remote, US

Zero G Talent · 2026-03-24 · 1 min read

Summary

Rebolt, a company that builds tailor-made AI software on top of existing systems, is hiring a founding Forward Deployed Engineer for a remote US role. The hire will embed with customers to find problems, automate phone, email, and messaging workflows using Rebolt, and lead technical implementations including custom integrations with freight management systems, CRMs, and databases. Requirements include an engineering background, Python and Typescript proficiency, and API experience with REST, GraphQL, OAuth, and JWT. AI experience and fast-paced adaptability are preferred. The role also involves building the FDE team and feeding customer insights back into product development.

Key points

  • Rebolt is hiring a founding Forward Deployed Engineer, remote in the US.
  • The role automates customer phone, email, and messaging workflows using Rebolt.
  • Candidates need Python, Typescript, and API skills (REST, GraphQL, OAuth, JWT).
  • Duties include custom integrations with freight systems, CRMs, and databases.
  • The hire will build the FDE team and relay customer insights to product.

What you need

In this role you will...

  • Embed yourself into the client workflow to identify problems and oportunites
  • Build the team of forward deployed engineers
  • Develop solutions using Rebolt to automate phone calls, email, and messaging systems
  • Lead technical implementations and ensure smooth integration with customer systems and data sources
  • Build custom integrations connecting our platform to freight management systems, CRMs, and databases
  • Provide technical guidance throughout the customer lifecycle from onboarding to ongoing optimization
  • Collaborate with engineering to feed customer insights back into product development

You will be a perfect fit if you...

  • Have engineering background with strong problem-solving and analytical skills
  • You’re proficient in Python and Typescript
  • Have experience with APIs & integrations including REST, GraphQL, OAuth, and JWT
  • Can translate complex technical concepts into clear language for customers and stakeholders
  • Have a customer-centric mindset

You will be an exceptional fit if you also...

  • Are adaptable and thrive in fast-paced, evolving tech environments
  • Have worked with AI solving company problems
  • Can diagnose technical issues a ship fixes quickly

What it means to join the team:

  • Autonomy to "just ship it"
  • Modern tech stack
  • Honest and low-ego
  • Ownership of problems and solutions
