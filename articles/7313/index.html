What is inside

Six parts, in the order the loop tests them. The detailed chapter breakdown is still being edited, so this is the shape rather than a table of contents.

The role, and the engagement loop

What a forward deployed engineer actually is, how the title fragments across companies, and how a customer engagement runs from first call to handover. The context every later round assumes you already have.

The decomposition round

Palantir's signature round and its imitators: turning a vague business goal into components, interfaces and a plan you can defend. This is the round most candidates fail, and the one that is hardest to practise alone.

Coding and practical builds

The coding an FDE loop actually asks for, which is integration work and multi-part builds under time pressure rather than competitive puzzles. Including what changes when the interviewer expects you to use AI tools.

Applied LLMs, RAG and agents

Retrieval that survives a customer's real documents, evaluation before launch, guardrails that hold, and the judgment to say when an agent is the wrong answer. The part of the job that did not exist five years ago.

Production debugging and deployment

Works in staging, breaks in production. Drift, skew, silent degradation, and taking a pilot that impressed someone to a system that meets a real SLA in an environment you do not control.

The customer rounds

Explaining a tradeoff to someone non-technical, holding the room when a demo breaks, saying no to a customer, and the behavioural rounds that decide more offers than anyone admits.

Who it is for

  • Engineers moving into forward deployed work from backend, data or ML, who can build but have never been graded on a customer.
  • Solutions engineers and technical consultants going the other way, towards a role that expects production code.
  • Anyone with a loop booked at one of the 111 companies we track and three weeks to prepare properly.

When is the FDE book released?

November 2026. Everyone on the early-access list hears first, and gets it before it goes anywhere else.

How much does the book cost?

Nothing, if you are a premium member. It is included in premium at no extra charge, alongside everything else premium unlocks. It is not sold separately.

No. The book is a premium benefit. A free account gets you a larger share of the answer bank than an anonymous visitor, and the early-access list is open to anyone, but the book itself requires premium.

What format is it?
