Guides for Engineering & AI Leaders

Ryz Labs · 2026-10-14 · 17 min read

Summary

This is a library of guides by Ryz Labs for engineering and AI leaders deciding how to staff projects and build AI systems. Each guide leads with a direct answer, then covers trade-offs, cost arithmetic and fit. Topics include engagement models (staff augmentation, outsourcing, nearshore vs offshore), the difference between forward deployed engineers, consultants and solutions engineers, 2026 AI project and AI pod budgets, roughly 12 AI use cases for each of about 20 industries, AI agent patterns by function, and technology choices such as RAG vs fine-tuning, Bedrock vs Azure OpenAI, and vector store options. Statistics pieces cite BLS, Levels.fyi, Stanford and other public sources.

Key points

  • Compares engagement models: staff augmentation, outsourcing, nearshore vs offshore, build vs buy.
  • Explains how FDEs differ from consultants and solutions engineers.
  • Gives 2026 budget examples for AI projects and AI pods with cost comparisons.
  • Lists about 12 AI use cases per industry across roughly 20 sectors.
  • Covers tech choices: RAG vs fine-tuning, Bedrock vs Azure OpenAI, vector stores, agent frameworks.
