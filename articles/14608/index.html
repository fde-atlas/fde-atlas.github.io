Palantir FDSE interview questions

FDE Interview Prep · 2026 · 4 min read

Summary

This article compiles interview questions for Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE) and related roles, drawing on official hiring pages, Lever job postings, and candidate reports from Blind, Reddit, and Glassdoor. A September 2026 count found 110 matching postings on Palantir's Lever board: 75 engineering titles and 35 Deployment Strategist roles. The process starts with one or two phone interviews, then an onsite round in person or virtually. Question types include client scenarios (e.g., an unreliable bus transit agency), messy CSV parsing, debugging unfamiliar code, learning an unseen library, complexity analysis, and behavioral questions. Government-facing roles add clearance questions, and one posting explicitly permits AI tools for the application project.

Key points

  • September 2026 count: 110 Lever postings, 75 engineering and 35 Deployment Strategist.
  • Hiring starts with one or two phone screens, then an onsite round.
  • Questions cover client scenarios, data cleaning, debugging, and learning new libraries.
  • New York FDSE posting requires up to 25% travel, described as flexible.
  • A US Government Deployment Strategist posting encourages AI tools in the application project.
