Industry-Specific FDE Job Openings: How Much Industry Experience Is Required? A Comparison of 146 Listings

Original title: 業界特化のFDE求人とは|業界経験はどこまで要るかを146件で比べる

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-11-10 · 25 min read

Summary

Based on 525 FDE job postings from 75 companies collected on November 10, 2026, this article analyzes the 146 postings whose titles include an industry name—about 28% of the total, with 63% coming from just Databricks and Palantir. OpenAI added cyber and life sciences FDE roles plus a financial services FDE manager on November 5–6, bringing its industry-specific FDE postings to 11 across five industries. The author sorts postings into three types: hiring industry natives (Harvey, Rogo), requiring industry experience among other criteria (OpenAI finance, life sciences), and treating it as a bonus only (OpenAI cyber, Databricks). Notably, industry specialization did not raise listed pay ranges, and among three Tokyo postings only Rogo mandated industry experience.

Key points

  • 146 of 525 FDE postings carried industry names; 63% came from Databricks and Palantir.
  • OpenAI's November postings brought its industry FDEs to 11 roles across five industries.
  • Industry experience requirements fall into three types, from mandatory to bonus-only.
  • OpenAI and Databricks industry FDE pay ranges matched non-industry FDE roles.
  • Of three Tokyo postings, only Rogo required industry work experience.

金融、医療、製薬、セキュリティ、法務などの業界で働いてきた人が、FDE(Forward Deployed Engineer/フォワードデプロイドエンジニア)への転職を考えるとき、まず知りたいのは「業界の経験は武器になるのか、それとも必須なのか」ではないでしょうか。2026年11月5〜6日、OpenAIがサイバーセキュリティとライフサイエンスのFDE、金融のFDEマネージャーを相次いで公開し、職種名に業界名を付けたFDEの募集が目立つようになりました[1][2][4]。

In this article, from the 525 FDE listings across 75 companies that the editorial team collected on November 10, 2026, we count the 146 listings with industry names in the job title by industry, and compare, based on the original text and focusing on OpenAI's listings across 5 industries, how much industry experience is actually required. For an overview of the FDE role, see What is an FDE?; for a general discussion of required skills, see Skills and Qualifications Needed for FDEs.

How Much Is Industry Experience Actually Required in FDE Listings?

It varies greatly by listing. Some listings include an industry name but state no industry experience requirement at all, while others require a set number of years of hands-on industry experience. The editorial team classified the listings we reviewed into three types. We believe that looking at whether the requirements section describes work in that industry is more useful for judging whether to apply than simply checking whether the job title includes an industry name.

How Many Industry-Named FDE Listings Are There?

On the public job boards the editorial team monitors, as of November 10, 2026, 146 of the 525 listings whose job titles included Forward Deployed, FDE, or FDSE (86 distinct roles, 17 companies) had an industry name in the job title. That is about 28% of the total. However, the majority are concentrated in a small number of companies.

What do the Life Sciences and Finance FDE roles require?

ライフサイエンスと金融は、OpenAIの5業界の中で業界経験を最も強く書いている求人です。どちらも、その業界での顧客対応を含む5年以上の経験を最初の項目に置いています[2][3]。ライフサイエンスは博士・修士かそれに相当する応用経験を推奨し、金融は隣接する規制産業の経験でもよいとしています[2][3]。同じ型の医療のFDEは、保険者・医療機関の業務や電子カルテの経験を並べたうえで、過去のFDEの肩書き、臨床の資格、医療のすべての領域の経験は不要と書いています[5]。

What about postings that name an industry but don't require industry experience?

業界名が主に、採用後にどの業界の顧客を担当するかを示している求人です。Databricksの医療・ライフサイエンスのSr. FDEは、要件欄にデータエンジニアリングやソフトウェアエンジニアリング6年以上、PythonやScalaでの開発、2つ以上のクラウドの知識、Apache Sparkの深い経験などを挙げていますが、医療や製薬の語はありません[8]。金融のSr. FDEも同じ構成です[18]。

Summary: What industry-specialized FDE postings tell us

While FDE job postings with industry names appear to be increasing, most of the volume comes from industry-specific placements by a small number of companies. How industry experience is treated varies by posting, so you need to judge from the requirements section rather than the job title.

  • In observations on November 10, 2026, 146 of 525 FDE job postings (17 companies) had industry names in the job title, with Databricks and Palantir accounting for 63% of them (editorial tally).
  • OpenAIは11月5〜6日にサイバーとライフサイエンスのFDE、金融のFDEマネージャーを公開し、業界名つきのFDEは5業界・11件(政府向けを含めると12件)になりました[1][2][4]。
  • 業界経験を最も強く求めていたのは、業界出身者を採るHarveyとRogo、次いでOpenAIのライフサイエンスと金融でした[2][3][9][10]。

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is an industry-specialized FDE?

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) who serves customers in a specific industry, with industry names such as "Financial Services," "Healthcare," "Life Sciences," "Cyber," or "Public Sector" in the job title. Of the 525 FDE postings counted by the editorial team on November 10, 2026, 146 fell into this category, with Databricks, Palantir, and OpenAI posting many of them.

Q: Do you need industry experience to become an FDE?

It depends on the posting. Harvey's credit investing FDE requires 3+ years of hands-on investing experience, and Rogo's Tokyo posting requires 3+ years in front-office roles at investment banks or consulting firms. OpenAI's life sciences and financial services FDEs require 5+ years of experience including customer-facing work in that industry. On the other hand, OpenAI's cyber and legal FDEs and Databricks' industry-specific FDEs do not require industry-specific experience.

Q: Do industry-specialized FDEs earn more?

In the postings read on November 10, 2026, they did not pay more. OpenAI's FDE compensation across the 5 industries was $185,000-$300,000, the same as FDEs without industry names. Databricks' Healthcare/Life Sciences and Financial Services Sr. FDEs were also the same as the Sr. FDE without an industry name, at $182,000-$250,208. Compensation figures are the ranges listed in the postings, not actual amounts paid.

Q: Do you need security experience to apply for OpenAI's cybersecurity FDE?

The posting states that what it primarily seeks is a strong engineering generalist. The core requirements are 5+ years of software engineering and experience protecting production systems in security, infrastructure, SRE, DevOps, or product development, while deep experience in areas such as application security or detection engineering is listed among the bonus qualifications.

Q: Are there FDE postings in Japan where industry experience can be leveraged?

As of November 10, 2026, there were 3 Tokyo-based FDE postings with industry names: Rogo for financial services, Sakana AI for defense, and Palantir for the Japanese government. Only Rogo required hands-on industry experience. OpenAI's Tokyo cyber posting has the job title Applied AI Engineer and requires strong cybersecurity expertise.
