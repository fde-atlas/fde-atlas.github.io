Foundry Labs · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

This page is the official company profile of Foundry Labs K.K., a Tokyo-based firm founded in 2025 that designs, deploys, and scales custom AI systems for enterprises and startups. Its services span LLM development and fine-tuning, robotics and edge AI, GPU optimization, computational biology, computer vision, and quantum chemistry simulation. The company emphasizes a forward deployed engineering model in which engineers embed deeply with client teams and transfer capabilities during delivery. The three founders have complementary backgrounds: CEO Ilya Kulyatin combines business, finance, and machine learning experience and founded the Tokyo AI community; CTO Declan Clowry spent six years at Palantir, rising from Deployment Strategist to Enterprise Lead and now runs an FDE & DS Excellence Programme; COO Cedric Wagrez has over 20 years in Japan's tech sector, including data strategy leadership at Stability AI.

Key points

  • Foundry Labs K.K. was founded in 2025 in Tokyo, offering AI integration, engineering, and consulting.
  • Services cover LLM fine-tuning, robotics, edge AI, GPU optimization, and scientific computing domains.
  • Its FDE model embeds engineers with client teams while transferring capabilities during delivery.
  • CTO Declan Clowry spent six years at Palantir, leading FDE and Deployment Strategist teams in the UK, Germany, and Japan.
  • CEO Ilya Kulyatin founded Tokyo AI; COO Cedric Wagrez previously led data strategy at Stability AI.

Meet the founders

A founder who connects research and business, a practitioner of enterprise deployment, and an operations leader with deep roots in Japan. Their experience comes together at Foundry Labs.

Co-founder & CEO

Ilya Kulyatin

Ilya's background spans business, finance and machine learning, with quantitative research and machine learning work across Europe, the US and Asia. Having built startups in Singapore and New York, he brings experience in company building to Foundry Labs' AI strategy and product development.

He also founded Tokyo AI (TAI), bringing entrepreneurs, researchers, investors and industry practitioners together. His community work connects Japan's AI ecosystem with people and opportunities internationally.

Ilya on LinkedIn

Co-founder & CTO

Declan Clowry

Declan spent six years at Palantir Technologies, progressing from hands-on work as a Deployment Strategist to Enterprise Lead. He led teams of Forward Deployed Engineers and Deployment Strategists in the UK, Germany and Japan, delivering systems across healthcare, defence and manufacturing.

Before Palantir, he worked in digital transformation in the NHS. At Foundry Labs, he leads the FDE & DS Excellence Programme, drawing on experience doing the roles, hiring for them and running deployment teams.

Declan on LinkedIn

Co-founder & COO

Cedric Wagrez

Cedric has spent more than 20 years in Japan's technology sector, with experience across Stability AI, TaskUs and Gengo. His work covers AI services, data strategy and team operations, including leading data strategy and operations at Stability AI.

He brings experience leading cross-functional teams and operating within international AI businesses. That background combines an understanding of technology delivery with the organisational work needed to support it in Japan.
