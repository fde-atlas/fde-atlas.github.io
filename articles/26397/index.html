How Industrial AI Moves from Co-Building to Handover: Four Signals from Manufacturing Practice and FDE Services — 2026-11-19

Today's Judgment

Who explains business rules, who handles failed samples, and who maintains the system after the project ends? Recent manufacturing interviews, a procurement pilot, Fujian's scenario solicitation, and AWS's service update each offer observations from different stages. For FDE teams, these responsibilities can be clarified starting from the first pilot round.

Key Signals

After manufacturing data query integration, business staff continue maintaining the rules

According to a November 17 industry interview, after Yinghe Technology connected its SAP data interface, business consultants organized field meanings and business rules. CITIC Dicastal, meanwhile, assigned business staff to collect failure cases from its materials assistant for future iterations. Both companies described existing practices.

At delivery, you can designate an owner for each key rule and keep confirmed failure cases as regression samples. For example, when the same material name matches multiple codes, business staff should confirm the judgment criteria, then verify whether the rule change affects other queries. Industry Interview, republished with permission by DoNews

Procurement supply assurance AI agent is in a co-built pilot

A November 18 report on the Cloud Bridge Conference introduced the SupplyPulse pilot. The project lead demonstrated a replenishment scenario for factory raw material shortages, where the system combines supplier lead times and purchase prices to propose a replenishment mix that meets production needs. Public materials indicate the project is at the roadshow and pilot stage.

Such pilots can replay historical shortage orders, first checking whether recommendations meet delivery deadlines and procurement constraints, then measuring how long manual review takes. Also clarify who approves purchases and how to handle cases where lead times cannot be confirmed. Cloud Bridge Conference on-site report, republished by Hangzhou CPPCC website

Fujian solicits already-deployed AI scenarios, requiring user organizations to provide results documentation

On November 18, Fujian's Department of Industry and Information Technology publicly announced a solicitation for AI scenarios in the industrial and information technology sector that have been implemented and deployed within the province. Application materials include project contracts, capital investment, and proof of application results issued by user organizations. The notice is dated November 12, and recommending departments in each locality must submit to the provincial department by November 30.

For delivery teams, you can agree with users at project kickoff on how to record baselines, usage results, and acceptance opinions, making it easier later to verify which changes came from this implementation. Fujian Department of Industry and Information Technology solicitation notice

AWS expands FDE services with fixed-price sprint billing

AWS disclosed on November 17 that its FDE services have expanded to 15 countries, with each 45-day sprint priced at a fixed rate set in the service terms and billed through the customer's AWS account. AWS also emphasized co-building with customer engineers and handing over operational capabilities. The public article did not list a uniform amount.

When organizing quotes by phase, you can include the personnel and data the customer must provide, phase acceptance tasks, and handover materials in the same scope statement. Timeline descriptions should also correspond to specific tasks and integration conditions. AWS FDE service update

Takeaways for FDE Practitioners

  • At pilot kickoff, agree with the business lead on data and rule criteria, failure sample collection methods, and acceptance baselines.
  • At handover, use a real task to test whether the customer team can independently modify rules, re-run tests, and take over exception handling.
