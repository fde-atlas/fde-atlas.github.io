Summary

Top 11 compares Sierra Exclusive and Wipfli for small businesses and construction firms weighing AI consulting. Sierra Exclusive ranks #2 (score 6.8) in the small-business AI consulting ranking, targeting US owners and executives of firms with 5–200 staff, with a $500 flat AI Opportunity Audit, a priced menu for larger builds, and one fix built during the initial call. Rankings claim no paid placement.
