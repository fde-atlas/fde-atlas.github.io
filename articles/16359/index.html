Alternatives to Palantir Technologies (2026)

Top 11 · 2026-09-03 · 3 min read

Summary

This ranked list places Palantir Technologies at #5 among US firms for hiring forward deployed engineers in 2026, then presents higher-ranked alternatives by use case. Beyond Elevation tops the list with published pricing (with an ownership disclosure), followed by HatchWorks AI, ISHIR, Deloitte, and Accenture. Platform-tied options include Google Cloud, Databricks, and OpenAI's newly formed Deployment Company, which launched with about 150 FDEs and $4B in backing. Most enterprise vendors keep pricing undisclosed and quote per engagement.

Key points

  • Palantir ranks #5 on the 2026 US FDE hiring list.
  • Beyond Elevation leads with published pricing, disclosed ownership ties.
  • ISHIR offers fractional FDE hours at a published hourly benchmark.
  • Deloitte and Accenture serve enterprise buyers with custom-quoted pricing.
  • Google Cloud, Databricks, and OpenAI tie FDE work to their platforms.
