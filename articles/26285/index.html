Forward Deployed Engineer | MSPilot | Remote, Ukraine

Zero G Talent · 2026-11-20 · 5 min read

Summary

MSPilot, a Y Combinator-backed startup building AI infrastructure for the IT services industry, is hiring a remote Forward Deployed Engineer (Ukraine-based). The role involves direct customer contact via Slack and Teams, end-to-end full-stack delivery, and building AI agents and MCP connectors. Requirements include 3+ years of full-stack experience, production operations skills, PostgreSQL, cloud infrastructure, and LLM API experience. The hiring process includes a paid trial task and completes in under two weeks, starting with a three-month contract before conversion to a permanent role with equity.

Key points

  • MSPilot is a YC-backed platform helping Managed Service Providers deploy, govern, and monetize AI agents.
  • The FDE owns customer problems from first message to production fix, with no ticket queue.
  • Requires 3+ years full-stack experience, Python or TypeScript, PostgreSQL, cloud platforms, and LLM API/MCP skills.
  • Hiring includes a paid 1-2 day trial task simulating a real customer scenario; total process under two weeks.
  • Offer starts as a three-month paid contract, then converts to permanent with backdated equity vesting.

What you need

  • 3+ yrs full-stack engineering with production operations experience
  • Production Python or TypeScript and web framework skills
  • Relational database design and PostgreSQL experience
  • Cloud infrastructure: AWS, GCP, or Azure
  • LLM APIs and AI agent or MCP integration experience

What you'll do

  • Serve as technical point of contact in Slack and Teams
  • Build and ship full-stack features, integrations, and fixes
  • Build and tune AI agents and MCP connectors
  • Investigate and resolve production issues from customer reports
  • Turn customer patterns into platform features and documentation

About us

We're a Y Combinator-backed startup building the AI infrastructure layer for the IT services industry: the platform IT providers use to deliver AI to the regulated industries they manage, and to build and run AI agents for them on the same rails.

The founder previously built an AI coding platform to 2M+ users. You'd join as a Forward Deployed Engineer at the exact moment the category is forming for small and medium businesses.

What you'll do

You'll be the engineer closest to our customers. You work directly with IT providers and the small businesses they manage, in shared Slack and Microsoft Teams channels, and you own their problems from the first message to the fix running in production. There is no ticket queue between you and the customer, and no separate team that ships your work for you.

1. Work with customers directly

  • Be the technical point of contact in shared Slack and Teams channels: answer questions, debug issues live, and explain clearly what is happening and what happens next
  • Turn vague requests ("the agent isn't doing what we expected") into a precise problem statement, a plan, and a shipped change
  • Run calls and write updates that a non-technical business owner and a senior IT admin can both follow

2. Ship to production, end to end

  • Build the features, integrations, and fixes your customers need, across the stack: frontend, backend, database, and deployment
  • Build and tune AI agents and MCP connectors for specific customer workflows, on top of the platform's runtime and governance rails
  • Investigate production issues from the customer report down to logs, traces, and code, then fix the root cause yourself
  • Own what you ship: deploy it, monitor it, and confirm with the customer that it solved their problem

3. Bring the field back into the product

  • Spot the patterns across customers and turn one-off solutions into platform features, templates, and documentation
  • Work directly with the founder and the founding engineer on what to build next, backed by what you hear from customers every day

This is a real engineering role with a customer-facing surface, not support with some scripting. You write production code every week, and customers see the results in days.

What we're looking for

Who you are

  • 3+ years of full-stack engineering experience, with real time spent operating what you built
  • Excellent English, written and spoken. You'll talk to customers every day in Slack, Teams, and on calls, mostly with US-based IT providers. You write clearly, you're concise, and you can explain a technical issue to a non-technical person without losing accuracy
  • Customer-oriented by instinct: you care whether the customer's problem is solved, not whether the ticket is closed. You follow up without being asked, you say plainly when something is broken, and you stay calm when a customer is frustrated
  • You've shipped a side project end to end. A side project, indie product, or freelance build that you personally took from idea to production: architecture, code, deployment, and operations
  • You've worked with production users: real people who relied on what you built, reported bugs, asked for features, and got answers from you directly
  • Daily user of agentic coding tools (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or similar). You build with them and understand how they call models and tools under the hood
  • Comfortable owning problems alone. You take a customer request from first message to shipped fix without waiting for someone to scope it, assign it, or unblock you

Technical requirements

  • Strong full-stack skills: production experience with a web framework (Django, Next.js, React, or similar) on both the frontend and the backend
  • Strong in Python or TypeScript, and able to read and change code in languages you don't use daily
  • System design experience: you've designed systems that ran in production, and you can assess the tradeoffs between different designs (cost, complexity, reliability, speed to ship) and communicate them clearly to both engineers and customers
  • Solid relational database experience (PostgreSQL preferred): schema design, writing and debugging queries, and migrations on live systems
  • Real production experience: deployments, logs and monitoring, incident debugging, and rollbacks. You've been the person who got paged, not only the person who merged
  • Working knowledge of cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, or Azure), containers, and how HTTP traffic gets from a client to your service: DNS, TLS, proxies, and APIs
  • Hands-on experience with LLM APIs (Anthropic, OpenAI) and with building agents or tool integrations; MCP (Model Context Protocol) experience is a strong plus

Nice to have

  • Previous forward deployed, solutions engineering, or customer-facing engineering experience
  • Startup experience: you've worked at an early-stage company (or founded one) and know what shipping with a small team under real constraints feels like
  • Experience with the Microsoft ecosystem: Microsoft 365, Entra ID (Azure AD), Teams, Intune
  • Experience with auth and identity: OAuth2 / OIDC / SAML
  • Background in IT services, or with tooling for IT providers (RMM, PSA, device management)
  • Experience building integrations with business systems (CRMs, ticketing, accounting, document storage)

What we offer

  • Early-stage equity: meaningful ownership, not a token grant
  • Competitive salary, discussed openly at the first call
  • Direct work with the founder and with customers: no layers, no legacy code, no account managers in between
  • A concrete, revenue-backed problem: paying customers and validated demand, not a research project
  • Fast feedback: what you ship on Tuesday, a customer is using on Wednesday and telling you about on Thursday
  • Hardware/co-working budget and paid time off

Hiring process

  • Intro call (30 min): mutual fit, product walkthrough, comp expectations. Held in English
  • Production deep dive (60 min): you walk us through something you've built and shipped to production, your choice: architecture, the decisions behind it, what broke, how you handled your users, what you'd redo
  • Paid trial task (~1-2 days, compensated): a real customer scenario. You get a request the way it arrives in Slack, clarify it, build the change, ship it, and write the update back to the customer
  • Offer: paid full-time contract for the first 3 months, then conversion to the permanent role with equity. This is a mutual trial: you evaluate us as much as we evaluate you. On conversion, equity vesting is backdated to your start date

Total process time: under two weeks.
