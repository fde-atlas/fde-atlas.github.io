The number of engineers considering "I want to change careers to become an FDE" is rapidly increasing against the backdrop of the generative AI boom. Overseas, FDE job openings surged from 2025 to 2026, and in Japan as well, AI startups and SaaS companies have begun recruiting for FDE positions. On the other hand, FDE is still a new profession, and there is almost no information available on "what you need to prepare to pass the selection process."

In this article, from our position as a career support service specializing in FDE, we explain FDE career changes based on primary information, covering market trends, suitable backgrounds, how to write a resume, and selection preparation.

Key Points of This Article

  • Because FDE is a new profession with almost no experienced candidates in the market, companies are hiring career changers from adjacent roles such as SIer engineers, web engineers, IT consultants, and pre-sales.
  • The keys to an FDE career change are threefold: ① a track record of delivering end-to-end, ② LLM implementation experience including evaluation (Evals), and ③ reframing your achievements in terms of customer outcomes.
  • The FDE selection process differs by company; as of October 2026, job postings state that SoftBank includes discussions involving system design and case studies, while JAPAN AI conducts coding tests, so applicants need to prepare to answer "why you chose that problem" and "how you evaluated it" on both the technical and customer-facing fronts.
  • The published annual salary ranges for FDE positions in Japan vary widely by posting—SoftBank at ¥8.12M–¥20.34M, JDSC at ¥5M–¥25M, LayerX from ¥12M (indicative annual salary), among others (as of October 2026).
  • The required years of experience for FDE positions differ by company, from 1+ years for Associate FDE at Palantir (Tokyo) and JAPAN AI to 10+ years for Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer at Databricks (Tokyo), with entry paths suited to people in their 20s, 30s, and 40s (as of October 2026).

Current State of the FDE Career Change Market: Job Openings Surging, Candidates Still Scarce

The FDE job market is in a state where "supply cannot keep up with demand." Fortune (September 3, 2026) reported, based on data from labor market analytics firm Lightcast, that job postings for "forward-deployed" engineers increased by more than 1,000% year-over-year from January to August 2026. When the editorial team tallied 133 public job boards, mainly used by overseas companies, on October 16, 2026, there were 517 postings (344 roles, 79 companies) with Forward Deployed, FDE, or FDSE in the job title. OpenAI has had FDEs in eight cities including Tokyo since 2025 (The Pragmatic Engineer, August 2025), and as of October 16, 2026, its public job board lists 30 postings with Forward Deployed or FDE in the title (3 in Tokyo). Anthropic is also continuing to hire FDEs responsible for enterprise Claude deployments. It also made headlines when US VC a16z called the FDE "the hottest job in tech."

In Japan as well, generative AI startups, SaaS companies incorporating AI, and enterprise AI deployment companies have begun hiring FDEs (or equivalent roles). According to the editorial team's tally above (October 16, 2026), 16 of the 517 postings had Japan as the work location or included Japan in the job title, from 8 companies including OpenAI, Databricks, Palantir, Notion, Sakana AI, and Cursor. This tally does not include postings that appear only on domestic recruitment sites such as SoftBank and LayerX; an analysis of domestic job postings published on note (February 2026) counted 35 postings in total: 26 at Japanese companies and 9 at foreign companies. Because the profession has a short history, "experienced FDEs" are virtually nonexistent in the market, and the reality is that companies are hiring potential career changers from adjacent roles. In other words, at this point in time, FDE is a role that can be targeted even without direct professional experience.

How to Write Your Resume: Reframing Around "Customer Outcomes"

The most important principle for an FDE application resume is to describe your achievements not in terms of features or technologies, but in terms of "what happened to the customer's business." This is because FDE performance is measured by customer impact, not the number of features shipped. Even with the same experience, how you write it can dramatically change how you are evaluated.

  • Weak example: "Developed a report-processing system in Python"
  • Strong example: "Automated the accounting department's monthly report processing, reducing processing time from 40 hours to 2 hours per month. Incorporated exception-handling rules identified through interviews into the design, and expanded coverage to three departments within three months of deployment"
  • 01 Write each project using the structure "customer's problem → what you identified and proposed → what you implemented → business results (with numbers)"
  • 02 Describe at least one experience (small-scale is fine) where you delivered end-to-end, in the most detail
  • 03 For LLM/AI implementation experience (including personal projects), mention your evaluation methods (Evals) to add specificity
  • 04 Have an episode ready about a time you structured an ambiguous situation yourself (no requirements, messy data, many stakeholders)

Interview Preparation: Three Things Evaluated in FDE Interviews

The FDE hiring process differs by company. According to publicly available information as of October 2026, OpenAI uses a process of document screening, introductory meetings, skill assessments, and final interviews (4-6 interviewers, 4-6 hours total); JAPAN AI (Ginie)'s FDE process involves document screening, a coding test, and 4-5 interviews; and SoftBank's job posting states that FDE interviews include discussions covering system design and case studies. Based on these companies' public information and example questions, the points to prepare can be summarized into the following three.

  • 01 Technical interviews: In addition to standard coding, whether you can proceed with system design "under incomplete information." The ability to ask questions that clarify assumptions is itself an evaluation target
  • 02 Case interviews and role-plays: Whether, starting from an ambiguous scenario like "a company is struggling with AI adoption," you can identify problems through interviews and propose a minimal end-to-end solution
  • 03 Demos and practical exercises: Tasks such as building an LLM-integrated prototype in a short time, or walkthroughs of past work. Candidates who can show something that actually runs have a strong advantage

The most effective preparation is to build one LLM application to production-level quality before the interview process and be ready to explain "why you chose this problem" and "how you evaluate it." This serves as a portfolio that demonstrates technical skill, problem-framing ability, and communication skills all at once.

How to Think About Salary and Benefits

Because FDE is a new role with excess demand, compensation ranges tend to be offered at or above the level of software engineers with comparable experience. In particular, since results are directly and visibly tied to customer revenue and costs, this role tends to be designed with upside potential tied to business contribution (performance-linked pay, stock options). For Tokyo positions at foreign AI companies, compensation packages at global standards may also apply. Since market rates vary greatly depending on experience and company stage, I recommend confirming specific job openings in a career consultation.

Regarding work style, you should understand that on-site work at customers and business trips account for a certain percentage (overseas job postings cite up to 25% travel for Palantir's New York posting and up to 50% for OpenAI's San Francisco posting). If you strongly prefer full remote work, this could be a mismatch.

How to Spot "FDE in Name Only" Job Postings and Questions to Ask in Interviews

As the job title FDE has spread, the name "FDE" has also come to be attached to postings whose actual work is closer to full-time on-site support billed hourly or customer support. What separates FDE from SES is whether the company has its own product. Before applying and during the interview, check the following points.

  • 01 Does the company have its own product: Check the job posting and the company's website for what product the FDE brings to customers
  • 02 Is there a mechanism for findings to flow back into the product: Ask, "Through what process do challenges the FDE discovers at customers get reflected in the product?"
  • 03 What are the evaluation metrics: Ask, "Is FDE performance evaluated by hours worked or by customer outcomes?"
  • 04 What percentage of the work involves writing code: Ask, "What percentage of working hours is spent on implementation?"
  • 05 What percentage is on-site: Check the number of days working at customer sites and the frequency of business trips

FDE Career Changes by Age: 20s, 30s, and 40s

FDE job postings do not list age requirements, but required years of experience differ greatly by company. Among public postings, Palantir (Tokyo) requires 1+ years after graduating university, JDSC requires 2+ years of development experience, JAPAN AI requires 3+ years of professional software engineering experience (or equivalent implementation ability; the Associate FDE for early-career candidates requires 1+ year), Anthropic (London) requires 4+ years in technical customer-facing roles, SoftBank and OpenAI (San Francisco) require 5+ years, and Databricks (Tokyo) requires 8+ years (Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer, also in Tokyo, requires 10+ years) (as of October 2026). Common Failure Patterns in FDE Career Changes

Here is a summary of failures I repeatedly see in FDE career change consultations and how to address them. All of them can be avoided with preparation before applying.

  • 01 Falling into FDE roles in name only: At companies with no product of their own, or no mechanism for feeding discoveries back into a product, an "FDE" role often ends up being full-time, hourly-rate on-site support in practice. The Pragmatic Engineer cites the example of an engineer who quit within four weeks of joining, saying it was "typical IT services thinking with no software development element." Countermeasure: In interviews, always ask "How do FDE discoveries get reflected in the product?" and "Is evaluation based on hours worked or customer outcomes?"
  • 02 Applying without proof of implementation skills: Common among people from consulting or pre-sales backgrounds. Job postings require "production-quality code," and experience limited to proposals or PoCs will stall at the technical interview. Countermeasure: Build one complete LLM application with authentication, error handling, and an evaluation framework, and get to a state where you can show the code before applying.
  • 03 Being unable to speak in terms of customer outcomes: Common among those from web engineering backgrounds. They stop at "I developed X" and cannot quantify what happened in the customer's business. Countermeasure: Rewrite each entry in your resume as "Problem → Your proposal → Implementation → Customer metrics."
  • 04 Overlooking language requirements: Databricks (Tokyo) requires fluent Japanese and business-level English, and JAPAN AI requires fluent Japanese sufficient for requirements gathering with executives and frontline staff (as of October 2026). Countermeasure: Check language requirements in the job posting first, and for postings requiring English, prepare for English communication in interviews.
  • 05 Mismatch in working style: Underestimating on-site work at customer locations or business travel (up to 25% in Palantir's New York posting, 25-50% in Anthropic's London posting, and up to 50% in OpenAI's San Francisco posting) and then finding it a burden after joining. Countermeasure: Before applying, ask about the percentage of on-site work and travel frequency, and compare against your own tolerance.

How to Approach an FDE Career Change: Why Use a Specialized Agent

Because FDE is still a new profession, there are many de facto FDE positions that are not labeled "FDE" in job postings (under titles such as Solutions Engineer, AI Implementation Engineer, or Technical Account Engineer). Summary

The FDE job-change market is in a historically favorable situation for career changers: "openings are surging while experienced candidates are almost nonexistent." FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I change careers to FDE without prior experience?

Transitioning from "no hands-on FDE experience" is entirely possible. Because FDE is a new profession, experienced candidates are almost nonexistent, and companies hire on the assumption of transitions from adjacent roles such as SIer, web engineering, consulting, and pre-sales. However, most postings assume hands-on software development experience itself as a prerequisite.

Q: Can I transition to FDE in my 30s or 40s?

Yes. FDE job postings contain no age requirements, and experience requirements range from 1+ years for Associate FDE at Palantir (Tokyo) and JAPAN AI, to 5+ years at SoftBank, and 8+ years at Databricks (Tokyo) (10+ years for Sr. Forward Deployed Engineer) (as of October 2026). Because FDE is a role where experience in customer engagement and project leadership carries value, the depth of your "experience delivering while facing the customer" is evaluated over age. Being able to present management experience as negotiation strength with enterprise customers is actually an advantage for mid-career professionals. If you have been away from implementation for a long time, build an application using LLMs yourself to show that you can still write production code.

Q: How should I search for FDE jobs?

In addition to searching for "FDE" and "Forward Deployed Engineer," it is important to include de facto FDE openings under other names such as Solutions Engineer, AI Implementation Support Engineer, and Technical Account Engineer. Among overseas AI companies, there are examples of recruiting customer-facing technical roles without using the FDE title, such as Applied AI Engineer (OpenAI, Anthropic) and Deployed Engineer (Cognition). Since the scope of work differs by company, verify it in the job description (as of October 2026). Covering everything, including the specialized agents' confidential openings, is the most efficient approach.

Q: Is English necessary for transitioning to FDE?

It depends on the employer. OpenAI's Tokyo FDE posting requires proficiency in both Japanese and English, and Databricks' Tokyo FDE posting requires fluent Japanese and business-level English. Some postings, like Notion's Forward Deployed Architect, list English as a preferred qualification. Among domestic companies, SoftBank lists English communication skills as a preferred qualification, and JAPAN AI conducts customer work mainly in Japanese with English as a preferred condition (all as of October 2026). According to an analysis of 35 domestic job postings published on note (February 2026), only 3 of 26 Japanese company postings (12%) included English ability as a requirement, and all were preferred qualifications. Reading comprehension for primary sources from overseas is useful at any company.

Q: Are there common regrets when transitioning to FDE?

On-site work and business trips at client sites, starting from ambiguous requirements, and priorities shifting due to client circumstances. First Round Review also notes that FDE work involves challenging problems and a heavy workload with travel. If you want to work fully remotely or polish a single product over the long term, please carefully review the job details before applying.

QCan you become an FDE without QA engineer experience?

There are FDE job postings that don't specify years of development experience but require experience in pre-sales, implementation consulting, and client-facing work (Andpad, RevComm, Channel Talk, etc.). However, they do set technical requirements such as the technical literacy to read and write code (Andpad) or experience with APIs and data integration (RevComm). Another option is to choose a role focused primarily on problem discovery, such as Palantir's Deployment Strategist.

QIs there a portfolio I should build before transitioning to an FDE role?

We strongly recommend building one application incorporating an LLM, completed to production-level quality (with authentication, error handling, and an evaluation framework). This lets you simultaneously demonstrate not only technical skill but also problem-framing and communication abilities, and it directly helps you prepare for case interviews and practical assignments.
