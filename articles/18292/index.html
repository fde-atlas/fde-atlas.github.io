Helper FDE | A DX Support Service Where Engineers Experience the Front Lines Firsthand

Original title: 助っ人FDE | エンジニアが現場を体験して行うDX支援サービス

minna-systems.co.jp · 2025 · 2 min read

Summary

This is a service page for “Helper FDE,” a DX support offering by Japan's minna-systems. Its distinguishing approach is that engineers visit the client's workplace and personally experience the work, uncovering inefficiencies invisible from the outside. Based on that firsthand experience, they propose DX improvements achievable with the existing team—without new hires or new systems—and then implement them through systematization and automation. The page cites over 100 development projects, 95% customer satisfaction, and a 93% maintenance contract renewal rate. The process runs from consultation to on-site experience, improvement planning, and implementation. The service currently operates in Tokyo, Nagasaki, Gifu, and Saga, with plans to expand to other regions.

Key points

  • Engineers visit client sites and experience the work firsthand.
  • Improvement plans assume no new hires or new systems.
  • Changes are implemented via systematization and automation.
  • Track record: 100+ projects, 95% satisfaction, 93% renewal rate.
  • Currently available in Tokyo, Nagasaki, Gifu, and Saga.
