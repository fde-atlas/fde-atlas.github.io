Summary

Considering AI adoption? Do any of these challenges sound familiar?

The era of AI adoption is shifting from "experimentation" to "production." Yet many companies face barriers: security concerns, shadow AI, PoCs that never progress, and a lack of internal champions.

Concerns about security

You can't decide whether it's safe to give internal data to AI, so usage approval never comes. With no clarity on how to manage permissions and logs, consideration has stalled.

Shadow AI is spreading

Employees are pasting internal documents into personally contracted generative AI tools. The company can neither track nor control this, and the risk of information leaks keeps piling up.

Stuck at the PoC stage

Proof-of-concept experiments move forward, but deployment to production stalls. Unable to gauge the impact on existing systems, you hesitate—and only technical debt accumulates.

No internal champion

You have no one in-house who understands both tool selection and business integration and can drive the organization forward. The AI tools you adopt go unused in the field, and the investment never pays off.

What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?

ECU's FDEs deploy the integrated AI platform ECU OS into your environment, implement solutions such as ECU Knowledge on top of it, and support you through to full adoption. Rather than simply implementing based on client instructions, they proactively drive strategy and planning, committing from implementation through to embedding knowledge within your organization.

Frequently asked questions

Q. How is an FDE different from traditional IT consultants or SIers?

The fundamental difference lies in "who they are accountable to, and for what." For traditional consultants, the goal is to "build" a system; for an FDE, the goal is to make the business succeed. Rather than developing on instruction, an FDE proactively drives strategy and planning, and works as a "companion" pursuing results end to end, from implementation to embedding knowledge in the organization.

Q. Does implementing ECU OS require modifying existing systems?

No. ECU OS is built in a dedicated environment separated from existing systems, and production data is replicated only to the extent necessary. Existing systems can continue operating as is, without downtime.

Q. How soon can we expect results after an FDE is assigned?

Within the first 2 to 4 weeks, we analyze the current state and identify quick wins, and within 1 to 2 months we deliver the first quantitative results. Because we commit to business KPIs rather than billing for man-hours, there is no risk of "dragging on indefinitely without results."
