Free Forward Deployed Engineer Course

HireAFDE · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

HireAFDE offers a free, interactive online course on becoming a Forward Deployed Engineer. It spans eight modules (roughly 140 minutes total) covering what the role is, a four-part skill stack (technical depth, business acumen, communication under pressure, high agency), a technical foundation in production Python, agents, integration servers and eval suites, a week-by-week deployment playbook, five portfolio artefacts, the interview format, and three transition routes from student, software engineering or consulting backgrounds. Each module includes lessons, a shippable exercise, and a quiz; progress is stored locally with no account required.

Key points

  • Free interactive course with eight modules on becoming an FDE.
  • Covers a four-part skill stack including high agency under ambiguity.
  • Technical foundation: production Python, agents, integration servers, eval suites.
  • Learners build five portfolio artefacts, including an agent and post-mortem.
  • Outlines three transition routes and the FDE interview format.
