FDE Forward Deployed Engineer Practical Training and Competency Assessment

Original title: FDE前线部署工程师实战培训与能力评定

深圳市真实智元科技有限公司 · 2026-11-04 · 6 min read

Summary

Shenzhen-based 真实智元 (Real Zhiyuan) offers a 12-week training and assessment program for forward deployed engineers, built around a five-step delivery framework modeled on OpenAI's practices: Discover, Prototype, Validate, Go live, and Scale. The curriculum spans 33 chapters across 9 sections (8–10 hours per week), requiring a demonstrable artifact at each stage. All 16 assessment questions and grading criteria are public, and outcomes are measured against the vendor-neutral FCB benchmark, a 5-dimension × 3-level capability matrix. The course draws on research into 29 Chinese and 100 global job descriptions, and culminates in a 48-hour simulated delivery project, portfolio, and reusable playbook.

Key points

  • 12-week, 33-chapter curriculum with 8–10 weekly hours.
  • Five-step framework: Discover, Prototype, Validate, Go live, Scale.
  • 16 assessment questions and grading standards are publicly available.
  • FCB benchmark: 5 capability dimensions × 3 levels.
  • Based on 29 Chinese and 100 global JD samples.

Data and Benchmarks

The course draws on job research covering 29 domestic companies and an initial 100 global companies, with training focused on requirements, engineering, evals, and delivery, and learning outcomes assessed against the FCB capability benchmark.

FCB · FDE Capability Benchmark

Capability levels are assessed based on delivery outcomes.

FCB (FDE Capability Benchmark) is a vendor-neutral framework for defining and assessing FDE capabilities: a five-dimension capability model (engineering implementation, scenario understanding, AI orchestration, on-site delivery, and value closure) × three levels of behavioral anchoring, forming a 15-cell assessment matrix, not tied to any model, cloud platform, or tool.

阅读 FCB 能力基准白皮书 →

L1 · Apprentice: Complete one slice with supervision. L2 · Independent: Independently own a complete slice (the direct assessment target of the 48-hour challenge). L3 · Lead: Define the problem and take responsibility for the results.

Want to verify it yourself? The full text of 100 original JDs and 16 assessment questions with scoring criteria are publicly available on the official website.
