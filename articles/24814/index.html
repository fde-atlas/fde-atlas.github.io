What Is a Solution Engineer?

A Solution Engineer (SE) is a bridge between technology and sales who translates customer challenges into technical solutions and handles everything from proposals to PoCs and implementation. With both product knowledge and implementation skills, SEs understand customers' business challenges and design and present optimal solutions. Their core role is to lead the technical aspects of sales negotiations, mainly in the pre-sales domain.

Job Responsibilities

The work of a Solution Engineer centers on technical support before and after sales negotiations, and extends broadly from there.

  • Hearing and organizing customers' business challenges and requirements
  • Designing and proposing solutions using the company's own products
  • Building demos and PoCs (proofs of concept) and demonstrating value
  • Providing technical support during implementation and assisting with initial implementation
  • Collaborating with sales and product teams

Difference from FDE

Solution Engineers and FDEs (Forward Deployed Engineers) are continuous roles with significant overlap. The difference lies in "the depth of responsibility." While Solution Engineers focus mainly on proposals through PoCs and initial implementation, FDEs go deep into production implementation, operational adoption, and delivering results after a deal is won. Career moves between SE and FDE roles are also active. For details, see Differences between FDEs and other roles.

Required Skills and Salary

What is required is an understanding of products and technology, proposal and presentation skills, customer engagement skills, and the ability to translate requirements into designs. In the generative AI domain, knowledge of LLMs/RAG becomes an advantage. While there are no public statistics that can express salary as a single figure, for senior roles at foreign SaaS and AI companies it is important to judge by total compensation including stock-based compensation. For a comparison with FDE salaries, also see Typical FDE Salary Ranges.
