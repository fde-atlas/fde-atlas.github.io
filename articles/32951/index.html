India Q1 2027 Hiring Outlook: Why Prep for FDE & AI Roles Now

FDE India · 2026-11-09 · 8 min read

Summary

This article compiles India hiring data pointing to early 2027, arguing that growth is concentrated in AI roles while generic software and QA postings are nearly flat. India has the world's strongest hiring outlook (Net Employment Outlook 54%, 65% of employers planning to hire). FDE demand is the standout: Indeed India counted FDE postings at nearly 19 times year-ago levels in June 2026, TCS targets roughly 5,900–8,900 AI deployment engineers, and Infosys plans 6,000 FDE hires. The author urges starting preparation right after Diwali, since prep, interviews and 90-day notice periods total four to six months, and lays out an eight-week plan.

Diwali is over, and for many engineers in India the next few weeks are when job-switch plans get made. The hiring data for early 2027 is encouraging, but it is not evenly spread. Almost all of the growth is in AI roles, and one of the fastest-growing of those is the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE). Generic software roles are barely moving.

Here is what the latest numbers say about January–March 2027, why AI and FDE roles lead, and why the next eight weeks matter more than they look.

India’s hiring outlook going into 2027

Employers are the most optimistic in the world. ManpowerGroup’s survey of 3,055 Indian employers found 65% plan to increase hiring in October–December 2026, up from 59% the quarter before. India’s Net Employment Outlook of 54% is the strongest of every country surveyed and 25 points above the global average. The information sector, which includes tech, scored 57%.

ManpowerGroup’s January–March 2027 survey is expected in December. For reference, last year’s January–March outlook was 52%, the second strongest globally.

Actual hiring is up too. Naukri’s JobSpeak index, which tracks new job postings, rose 14% year on year in October 2026. Info Edge CEO Hitesh Oberoi noted that part of that rise reflects a weak festive-season base last year, but said: “What’s encouraging is the kind of hiring we’re seeing.”

GCCs keep expanding. In Taggd’s GCC Talent Lab Survey 2026, 52% of global capability centres plan to increase hiring in FY27, up from 47% last year, adding about 1.5 lakh roles. Professionals with 1–5 years’ experience make up 30% of planned hiring and those with 6–10 years 28%.

The growth is in AI roles, not generic software

Naukri JobSpeak, October 2026.

This is the most important chart in this post. In October 2026:

  • AI/ML roles: up 32%
  • GCCs: up 18%
  • All white-collar jobs: up 14%
  • General software and QA: up 1%

If you are a software engineer without AI skills, the market around you is growing but your segment of it is flat. Taggd’s GCC survey points the same way: nearly half of GCCs said more than 25% of their open roles require AI skills, and one in four said most of their positions are AI-adjacent.

Last year’s post-Diwali window followed the same pattern

Naukri JobSpeak, December 2025, January 2026 and October 2026.

After Diwali 2025, October–December was the strongest hiring quarter of the year (up 9%), and AI/ML hiring rose 53% in December 2025. In January 2026, overall white-collar hiring grew only 3% and IT hiring dipped about 2%, yet AI/ML roles still grew 34%. AI hiring has held up even in months when the wider market was soft.

Why FDE roles stand out

The Forward Deployed Engineer is one of the clearest AI roles to target, because demand is rising fastest and the role rewards experience you already have: building production systems, working with customers and owning outcomes.

  • Fastest-growing title: Indeed India counted FDE postings at nearly 19 times the year-ago level in June 2026, 16 times in July and more than 6 times in August, across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, New Delhi, Mumbai and remote roles.
  • Large employers are committing: TCS CEO K Krithivasan set a target of 1–1.5% of the workforce as forward deployed AI engineers, roughly 5,900 to 8,900 people (TCS guide). Infosys plans to hire 6,000 FDEs over the next few years (Infosys guide).
  • Strong pay: Taggd puts typical FDE pay at ₹30–50 LPA for 3–6 years and ₹50–80 LPA for 7–12 years. Check your own band with the FDE Salary Estimator.

Why start preparing now, right after Diwali

1. The timeline is longer than it looks. Count backwards from a new job:

Step Typical time If you start now
Focused preparation 6–8 weeks Mid-November to early January
Applications and interviews 4–6 weeks January to February
Notice period 30–90 days February to April
Joining April–May 2027

Notice periods are the hidden constraint. Analytics India Magazine found that almost one in three IT roles carries a 90-day notice period, and IT accounts for more 90-day-notice jobs than any other industry. If you start preparing in February instead, you are realistically joining in the second half of 2027.

2. You will not be the only one switching. In foundit’s 2026 appraisal survey, 52% of professionals were actively job-hunting and 96% were open to switching, with 70% expecting a raise of 21% or more when they move. When that many people apply for the same roles, the candidates who stand out are the ones who prepared before they started applying.

3. AI skills take time to show. Companies are hiring for skills they can verify, not just claim. Taggd’s survey flags a gap between claimed and demonstrable AI skills. A project you have actually deployed takes weeks, not a weekend.

An 8-week plan: mid-November to early January

  • Weeks 1–2: know your gaps. Run the free FDE Fit Check on your resume. Read what FDE roles require and pick your two biggest gaps.
  • Weeks 3–5: build one deployed AI project. A retrieval (RAG) app or agent connected to a real data source, with an evaluation set and basic monitoring, deployed where someone can use it. Write it up as a short case study.
  • Weeks 4–6: practise the customer side. Explain a technical trade-off to a non-technical person in five minutes, every week. FDE interviews test this directly.
  • Weeks 6–8: interview prep. Learn the FDE interview process and practise the common FDE interview questions, especially decomposition and deployment scenarios.
  • Early January: apply. Rewrite your resume around deployment outcomes, then apply to the companies on our companies list, starting with GCCs and the IT services firms building FDE teams.

If you want a structured, live path through these steps, see how a Forward Deployed Engineer course in India covers them.

What could change

Hiring outlooks measure employer intentions, not guaranteed jobs. ManpowerGroup’s January–March 2027 survey, due in December, could move either way, and Naukri’s October jump is flattered by a weak base last year. Even so, every source here agrees on the direction: AI roles are where Indian hiring is growing, and generic software roles are not. We will update this outlook when the Q1 2027 survey is published.

Frequently asked questions

What is India's hiring outlook for January–March 2027?

ManpowerGroup's Q1 2027 survey is expected in December; last year's Q1 survey came out on 9 December. The latest data points the same way as last year: India had the world's strongest outlook for October–December 2026 (Net Employment Outlook 54%), and for January–March 2026 it was 52%, the second strongest globally. Naukri's October 2026 data shows white-collar hiring up 14% and AI/ML roles up 32%.

Which tech roles will be in demand in India in 2027?

AI and machine learning roles, AI-adjacent roles at global capability centres (GCCs) and Forward Deployed Engineers. Taggd's GCC survey found nearly half of GCCs say over a quarter of their open roles need AI skills, and Indeed India counted FDE postings at nearly 19 times the year-ago level in June 2026. General software and QA postings grew just 1% in October 2026.

Is November a good time to start preparing for a job switch in India?

Yes. A focused prep of 6–8 weeks takes you to early January. Interviews then run through January and February, and many IT roles carry a 90-day notice period, so starting now puts your joining date around April–May 2027.

How much do Forward Deployed Engineers earn in India?

Taggd puts typical FDE pay at ₹30–50 LPA for 3–6 years of experience and ₹50–80 LPA for 7–12 years. Try the FDE Salary Estimator on this site for your experience, company type and city.
