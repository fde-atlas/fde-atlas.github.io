Summary

FDE board lists three FDE openings at ExaWizards Inc., a listed Japanese AI company behind the exaBase and CareWiz product lines, with salaries up to ¥12.6 million. The company formed a capital and business alliance with Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group in March 2026 and has around 659 consolidated employees. About ExaWizards Inc.

A listed company that develops the "exaBase" series of generative AI business solutions and the "CareWiz" series for nursing care and healthcare, supporting AI adoption by companies and local governments. In March 2026, it formed a capital and business alliance with Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group. The company has approximately 659 employees on a consolidated basis. Founded on the principle of using AI to solve social issues, it is distinguished by a track record of implementing AI across a wide range of industries, from nursing care and healthcare to manufacturing and finance.

Characteristics of FDE Roles at This Company

One of the companies in Japan with the most well-structured FDE career levels. In Tokyo/Osaka, it hires Standard-level (¥7.2M–¥10.8M) and Senior-level (¥9.6M–¥12.6M) FDEs without a Junior equivalent, while its new Fukuoka subsidiary, "ExaWizards Forward Kyushu," recruits Junior-level FDEs (¥5.04M–¥6.6M, no practical AI experience required). The job postings explicitly state, "This is not a position whose purpose is to be stationed at client sites."
