Three job titles often appear side by side: forward deployed engineer (FDE), AI engineer and solutions engineer. They share some tools, but they serve different goals. This post explains what each role centres on, and which one to train for if you are choosing a path.

AI engineer: building the system

An AI engineer designs, builds and runs the AI features of a product or platform. The work centres on the codebase. Typical tasks include model integration, retrieval pipelines, evals in the release process, and monitoring after launch. The people served are usually an internal product team or the end users of the product.

Forward deployed engineer: building it with the customer

A forward deployed engineer works close to one customer or one workflow. They learn the process, identify the problem worth solving, secure access to the data, and build a working system around it. They then hand over the system so the customer team can run it. The customer shapes the design rather than only receiving the output.

Solutions engineer: proving the fit

A solutions engineer is a technical role often placed beside sales. The work centres on understanding a prospect's needs, showing a demonstration, answering technical questions and setting up a proof of concept. The output is a convincing demonstration and a clear account of what the product can and cannot do.

How to choose

Ask what you enjoy most in a typical week. If you like designing the core system and owning its quality over time, the AI engineer path fits. If you like sitting with a customer, working through messy data and shipping something live for them, the FDE path fits. If you like explaining products, demonstrating them and helping a buyer see the value, the solutions engineer path fits.

The three roles share skills. All of them need Python or another language, API work, a sense of what models can and cannot do, and clear writing. Train the shared base first, then branch.

Frequently asked questions

Try it first: turn on practice mode to hide the answers, think through each question, then reveal.

What is the main difference between an FDE and an AI engineer?

What a good answer covers

An AI engineer mainly builds and runs the AI system inside a product. An FDE also works with one customer on a workflow, and hands over a system the customer team can run.

Is a solutions engineer role a good route into FDE work?

What a good answer covers

It can be, if you enjoy technical depth and customer contact. Build a small project with real data as well, because a demonstration alone does not show build skills.

Do all three roles need the same training?

What a good answer covers

They share a base in Python, APIs and testing. Specialise after that, depending on whether you prefer building the system, working with a customer, or proving fit.
