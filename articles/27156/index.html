Summary

This page presents key excerpts from Socket's job description for a Python-focused Forward Deployed Engineer, captured on 2026-11-02. The role is positioned as a technical bridge between enterprise customers and Socket's engineering teams. Requirements include a track record of building integrations, APIs, webhooks, and automation scripts in production environments. Responsibilities cover coordinating technical rollouts in complex enterprise settings, translating customer feedback into actionable insights for product teams, and maintaining relationships with customer engineering and security stakeholders. The page notes the excerpts are for research comparison only and do not constitute a hiring commitment.

Key points

  • Socket is hiring a Python-focused FDE as a technical bridge between enterprise customers and engineering.
  • Candidates need production experience with integrations, APIs, webhooks, and automation scripts.
  • The role coordinates technical rollouts across complex enterprise environments.
  • It also involves relaying customer feedback to product teams and engaging security stakeholders.
  • Excerpts are for research only; the original posting governs.
