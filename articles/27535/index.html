Antai AI Scenario Co-Creation Welcomes First Batch of Results, Launches FDE Talent Development with Datawhale Published: 2026-09-06

On September 6, the 2026 Yangtze River Delta AI Scenario Innovation Forum — the FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) Talent Specialized Development Program — was held at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology recently issued a notice on launching a special initiative to cultivate AI application service providers, proposing to "encourage universities and leading service providers to jointly build training bases and cultivate, at scale, versatile application talent who 'understand business, master models, know security, and can deliver.' Encourage service providers to build Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) teams that take root at customer sites to ensure scenario implementation." Under the theme "Bringing Scenarios to AI Implementation, Bringing Talent to Scenario Implementation," the forum focused on scenario development and talent cultivation in the process of AI moving from technological innovation to industrial application.

At the forum, the first batch of the "Hundred Industries, Thousand Enterprises" AI scenario list and scenario co-building ecosystem partners were officially released; the Antai College of Economics and Management at Shanghai Jiao Tong University reached a strategic partnership with the AI open-source learning community Datawhale; the FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) Talent Specialized Development Program was launched simultaneously, along with the release of the Antai MBA program's "AI+X" practical training courses and selected FDE courses.

On August 16, Shanghai Jiao Tong University initiated the establishment of the AI-Era Business Talent Development Alliance and officially launched the "Hundred Industries, Thousand Enterprises" 10×100 AI Scenario Co-Creation Plan, planning to spend ten years working with various industries to create 1,000 dedicated AI real-world projects, while Antai MBA simultaneously released a series of training reform measures for "Five-Chain Talent in the Intelligent Era." Less than a month later, the first batch of scenarios entered co-creation, with the technology ecosystem, FDE talent, and practical courses further connected. Antai MBA is continuously advancing AI-era business talent development from systemic reform toward industrial practice.

Releasing the First Scenario List, Bringing AI Needs to the Industrial Front Line

At this forum, the first batch of the "Hundred Industries, Thousand Enterprises" AI scenario list was officially released, marking the transition of scenario co-creation work from collection and mechanism building to project co-creation. The first batch of scenarios comes from real business needs on the industrial front line and will subsequently be incorporated into Antai MBA practical teaching, specialized training, and the "AI+X" course series, turning enterprise problems into classroom projects and allowing students to complete requirements analysis, solution design, and industrial validation in real scenarios.

Chen Fangruo, Dean of the Antai College of Economics and Management at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Dean of the Industry Research Institute, noted that the release of the first scenario list is an extension and implementation of the August 16 AI-Era Industrial Innovation Management Forum, moving from systemic reform to industrial practice. Business education for the intelligent era must closely follow real industrial pain points to iterate on training objectives, curricula, and practical training models. Antai's promotion of AI scenario co-creation brings complex, real problems from the enterprise front line into the entire talent development process, allowing students to understand the relationships between technology, organization, and business while solving real problems.

Convening the Scenario Co-Building Ecosystem, Driving Two-Way Implementation of FDE Talent and Cases

For AI scenarios to truly land, enterprises need to raise real problems, and technology communities, professional talent, and university education must all participate. At the forum, the "Hundred Industries, Thousand Enterprises" scenario co-building ecosystem partners were officially released, and the Antai College of Economics and Management reached a strategic partnership with Datawhale. The two parties will collaborate on versatile talent development and practical course construction, collection of real AI scenarios, and industrial case development, launching FDE 100 Pioneers and FDE 100 Cases, releasing selected FDE case courses, and forming an open ecosystem from scenario discovery to talent entry and from practical cases to course transformation.

Fan Jingjing, founder of Datawhale, stated that FDEs must go deep into enterprises' real business sites and transform business problems into deliverable AI solutions; their core characteristics are understanding business, being able to use models, maintaining security, and being accountable for results. FDEs are not trained — they are identified from real projects. The FDE Talent Specialized Development Program identifies people through cases and certifies through results, turning what a few have accomplished into methods that a hundred industries and a thousand enterprises can use.

Releasing "AI+X" Practical Training Courses, Bringing Real Industrial Problems into the MBA Classroom

The "Five-Chain Talent in the Intelligent Era" training system released on August 16 proposed agile training reforms: starting from the class of 2027, implementing a flexible credit system, a two-year program, and practical courses accounting for no less than one quarter of total class hours, relying on real industrial scenarios to hone three core practical capabilities: AI4O industrial operations, FDE engineering implementation, and OPC full-stack business management.

Chen Jianke, Assistant Dean of the Antai College of Economics and Management at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Director of the MBA Center, stated that the "AI+X" practical training courses launched at this forum will continue to strengthen the Antai MBA training reforms, further incorporating real industrial scenarios, enterprise cases, and FDE practical capabilities into the curriculum, allowing students to develop these capabilities in real industrial scenarios.

From the release of the first scenario list and the first batch of "Hundred Industries, Thousand Enterprises" scenario co-building ecosystem partners, to the "AI+X" practical training courses further taking on industrial scenarios, the Antai College of Economics and Management at Shanghai Jiao Tong University is continuously connecting the links between scenarios, talent, cases, and courses, allowing real industrial needs to penetrate more deeply into the entire MBA talent development process.

Bringing scenarios to AI implementation, bringing talent to scenario implementation. In the future, Antai will continue to rely on the AI-Era Business Talent Development Alliance and the "Hundred Industries, Thousand Enterprises" co-creation ecosystem to build an ecosystem-based training paradigm that links intelligence, links science and technology innovation, links industry, links the world, and links SJTU, driving industrial projects into classrooms, technical talent into scenarios, and real cases into courses, empowering the industrialization of AI through talent development.

During the forum, Xu Huan, Professor and Head of the Department of Management Science at the Antai College of Economics and Management at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Director of the AI MBA Program, and former Algorithm Director at Alibaba Group, delivered a keynote speech. Guests from universities, associations, enterprises, AI technology communities, and the industrial front line gave special reports and participated in roundtable discussions on FDE talent capabilities, AI scenario development, and industrial practice. FDE scenario project roadshows, AI scenario project showcases, and matchmaking exchanges were held simultaneously.
