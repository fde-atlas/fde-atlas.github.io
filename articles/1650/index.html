Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-10-29 · 6 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch analyzes Palantir's FDE-family job postings from an October 24, 2026 snapshot of public boards. Palantir accounts for 105 of 725 FDE-family postings (14.5%) across 121 employers: 70 engineer-family roles and 35 Deployment Strategist roles. The article breaks down title families, government vs commercial tracks, new-grad and internship postings, locations, travel requirements, and posted pay, comparing Palantir against all other employers in the dataset.

Key points

  • Palantir posted 105 of 725 FDE-family roles: 70 engineer-family, 35 Deployment Strategist.
  • 54 titles name a government track, 12 commercial, and 39 are unlabeled.
  • No remote postings: 62 onsite, 43 hybrid; 76 of 105 are US-located.
  • 79% of postings mention travel; median stated upper bound is 75% vs 30% all-employer.
  • Common engineer pay range is $135,000-$200,000, below the all-employer median midpoint of $216,104.

Published

Palantir posts Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE) roles and Deployment Strategist roles side by side. In FDE Dispatch's October 24, 2026 snapshot of public job boards, Palantir accounts for 105 of 725 FDE-family postings (14.5%) across 121 employers: 70 engineer-family and 35 deployment strategist. None list remote (62 onsite, 43 hybrid). Travel appears in 83 postings, with a percentage in 67, most often in the 25-75% and 25-50% bands. 76 are US-located. US posted pay repeats a few ranges, for example engineer $135,000 to $200,000 and strategist $110,000 to $170,000; posted ranges are not offers. FDE Dispatch is not affiliated with Palantir; the figures come from Palantir's public job postings.

What Palantir calls the role

Palantir posts Forward Deployed Software Engineer titles and Deployment Strategist titles as a paired hiring track.

Of the 105 Palantir postings in this snapshot, 70 are engineer-family and 35 are deployment strategist. Engineer title families: Forward Deployed Software Engineer (49), Forward Deployed Infrastructure Engineer (11), Forward Deployed Enablement Engineer (3), Forward Deployed Reliability Engineer (3), Forward Deployed AI Engineer (2), Forward Deployed Security Engineer (1), and Forward Deployed Engineer (Mixed Reality) (1). All 35 strategist titles sit under Deployment Strategist, including Build to Apply, internship, new-grad and location suffixes.

Short definitions: Forward Deployed Engineer and deployment strategist in the FDE glossary. Interview-round prep for the engineer track is on the FDE interview loop.

US Government vs Commercial

54 titles name a government track, 12 name Commercial, and 39 leave the track unlabeled in the title.

Classification is from title text only: government when the title matches government, USG or federal; commercial when it matches commercial. Examples include Forward Deployed Software Engineer - US Government, Deployment Strategist - US Government, Forward Deployed Software Engineer, New Grad - Commercial, and Year at Palantir internship titles marked Commercial or USG. Unlabeled titles include location-only suffixes (Japan Forward Deployed, Korea Forward Deployed), Warp Speed, Intel, NATO and plain Deployment Strategist or FDSE titles with no track word.

New grad and internship tracks

Title text marks 17 new-grad postings and 21 internship postings; the dataset intern flag is false for every Palantir row here, so these counts come from titles.

New grad: 14 engineer-family and 3 deployment strategist. Internship: 18 engineer-family and 3 deployment strategist. Both families appear under US Government, Commercial, Warp Speed and country-specific suffixes. Treat the title string as the source of truth for early-career Palantir rows in this file.

How much Palantir Forward Deployed roles travel

Travel appears in 83 of 105 Palantir postings (79.0%), and 67 state a percentage; the median stated upper bound is 75%, against 30% across all employers on how much forward deployed engineers travel.

Normalized bands (en-dash and hyphen variants of the same range counted together): 25-75% on 32 postings, 25-50% on 21, 25% on 10, plus one posting each at 10-20%, 50%, 75% and 70-90%. Palantir's stated travel sits above the all-employer median upper bound of 30% from the same October 24, 2026 snapshot.

How Palantir's mix compares to other employers

Palantir is 105 of 725 in-page-stat postings (14.5%) across 121 employers, with 0 remote-listed against 199 remote-listed (27.4%) across the full snapshot on the locations index.

The other 120 employers account for the remaining 620 postings. Palantir's workplace mix is 62 onsite and 43 hybrid. On travel, Palantir's median stated upper bound (75%) sits above the all-employer median (30%). On pay, Palantir's most common US engineer range ($135,000 to $200,000) sits below the all-employer US engineer median midpoint ($216,104). These counts are open postings on one pull date. FDE Dispatch is not affiliated with Palantir.

Method and data

Population: 105 Palantir postings with in_page_stats among the 725 FDE-family postings from 121 employers counted on the salary, job titles, locations and travel pages. Public Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday boards; snapshot pulled October 24, 2026. Recruiting agencies and duplicates removed.

Title families, government vs commercial, and new grad or internship labels: keyword match on the posting title. The intern column is false for every Palantir row in this snapshot; early-career counts use title text.

Travel: upper bound = largest number in the stated percentage string; bands normalize en-dash and hyphen variants. US pay: US-only location, USD range posted, first range on the posting. Posted ranges are not offers.

These 105 listings were live on the October 24, 2026 snapshot. The ATS posted_or_updated field (especially on Lever) can show dates years earlier for evergreen listings; it is not a posting-date range and should not be read as when the role opened.

Downloads: Palantir JSON, field notes, and the per-posting postings JSON. All published datasets are listed on FDE Dispatch job-posting data. Built by

What does Palantir call the Forward Deployed Engineer role?

Engineer title families include Forward Deployed Software Engineer (49), Infrastructure (11), Enablement (3), Reliability (3), AI (2), Security (1) and Mixed Reality (1).

How much do Palantir Forward Deployed Engineers travel?

Travel is mentioned in 83 of Palantir's 105 postings (79.0%), and 67 state a percentage. The median stated upper bound is 75%, against 30% across all employers on the travel page. Normalized bands: 32 postings at 25-75%, 21 at 25-50%, 10 at 25%, and 4 other values.

What pay does Palantir post for Forward Deployed Engineer roles?

Among US-located USD postings, engineer-family pay appears as repeated ranges: $135,000 to $200,000 (23), $135,000 to $145,000 (10), $110,000 to $147,000 (3), plus two other ranges once each. Deployment strategist pay is $110,000 to $170,000 (20) and $110,000 to $120,000 (1). Posted ranges are not offers. Across all employers, the US engineer median midpoint is $216,104.

Does Palantir hire new grads and interns for Forward Deployed roles?

Yes, by title text: 17 new-grad titles (14 engineer, 3 strategist) and 21 internship titles (18 engineer, 3 strategist). The dataset intern flag is false for every Palantir row in this snapshot, so these counts come from the title string.

Are Palantir Forward Deployed roles remote?

None of Palantir's 105 postings list remote. 62 are onsite and 43 are hybrid. Across the full 725-posting snapshot, 199 postings (27.4%) list remote on the locations index.
