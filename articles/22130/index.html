Professional media for Forward Deployed Engineers (FDE)

Original title: 前线部署工程师（FDE）的专业媒体

FDE Times · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Summary

FDE Times is a Chinese-language publication focused on forward deployed engineers, offering news, analysis, job listings, and a learning roadmap. The page lists open roles at companies including PolyAI, Cursor, Snowflake, Lovable, and AssemblyAI, spanning FDE internships, Deployment Strategist positions, and a regional director role in financial services. Its learning path is organized into eight stages: understanding the role, full-stack engineering, AI applications, customer work, deployment, measuring value, leading projects, and career development, with entry points for newcomers and career changers.

Key points

  • FDE Times is a bilingual-oriented media site for forward deployed engineers.
  • Job board includes PolyAI, Cursor, Snowflake, Lovable, and AssemblyAI roles.
  • Listings cover FDE interns, Deployment Strategists, and a regional director role.
  • Learning roadmap has eight stages from role basics to career development.
  • Separate entry points serve beginners and those switching careers.
