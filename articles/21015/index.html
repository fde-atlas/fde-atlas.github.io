NTT Data and Fujitsu Expand AI Consulting "FDE," Aiming to Shift Billing Models

Original title: NTTデータや富士通、AIコンサル「FDE」拡大 課金モデルの転換狙う

日本経済新聞社 · 2026-09-11 · 1 min read

Summary

Nikkei reports that major Japanese IT outsourcing firms, including NTT Data and Fujitsu, are expanding their teams of forward deployed engineers (FDEs) — engineers embedded at client companies to support AI adoption. The article follows Reo Tagawa of NTT Data, who works on-site at an energy company, identifying problems from an engineer's perspective and proposing improvements as part of the client team. The FDE model has been booming in the US as a new engineering archetype. Japanese firms aim to leverage this trend to shift their billing models away from conventional time-and-materials contracting toward new revenue structures for AI consulting work.

Key points

  • NTT Data and Fujitsu are expanding FDE teams for AI consulting.
  • FDEs embed at client sites, e.g. NTT Data's Reo Tagawa at an energy firm.
  • The FDE model has boomed in the US as a new engineering archetype.
  • The goal is shifting billing away from time-based contracting.
