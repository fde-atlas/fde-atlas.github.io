Forward Deployed Engineer, Enterprise Accounts | Labelbox

Zero G Talent · 2026-11-13 · 5 min read

Summary

Labelbox is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer for enterprise accounts in the San Francisco Bay Area. The role is a technical-commercial front line: mapping customer business processes, defining measurable outcomes, and building solutions on the Labelbox data annotation platform and Recursion Managed Agent, focused on agentic workflows combining AI agents with human oversight. Responsibilities include demos, proofs of concept, executive business cases, and partnering with Account Executives on new business, renewals, and expansion. Candidates need a master's degree in a relevant field, 3+ years of experience, Python and data analysis skills, experience leading LLM or agent projects, and consultative selling ability. Success is measured by enterprise growth, retention, and account expansion.

Key points

  • Role based in the San Francisco Bay Area, focused on large enterprise customers.
  • Solutions built on Labelbox's annotation platform and Recursion Managed Agent.
  • Requires a master's degree, 3+ years experience, and LLM or agent project leadership.
  • Blends engineering with consultative selling to executives and buying committees.
  • Measured on new enterprise growth plus retention and expansion of existing accounts.

About the Role

Forward Deployed Engineers are Labelbox’s technical and commercial front line inside our largest enterprise customers. You work directly with business and technical leaders to understand how their business actually runs: the processes, use cases, and workflows where AI can change outcomes. Then you design and build the solution, using the Labelbox data annotation platform and the Recursion Managed Agent platform, with a focus on agentic workflows that put AI agents and humans in the loop to run real operations.

Your success is measured by business results: new enterprise growth, and retention and expansion within existing accounts. This role blends engineering, product, and sales. You need the technical depth to build credible solutions and the salesmanship to earn trust, shape the vision, and win the deal. Identifying and solving the problem is half the job. Persuading a customer to commit to the solution is the other half.

Your day to day

  • Work side by side with customers to identify and map their business processes, use cases, and workflows, and define the measurable outcomes they want to achieve
  • Propose, demonstrate, and develop solutions built on Labelbox’s products and services, especially the data annotation platform and Recursion Managed Agent, designing agentic workflows that automate and augment core enterprise processes
  • Sell the vision: present compelling solution proposals, run technical demos and proofs of concept, and build the business case for executive sponsors and buying committees
  • Partner with Account Executives to win new enterprise business, identify expansion opportunities, and accurately forecast renewals
  • Build trusted advisor relationships with customer stakeholders and executive sponsors, and tie every engagement to business value and ROI
  • Drive retention and expansion by ensuring customers realize value from deployed solutions and by spotting the next workflow to take on
  • Guide customers through architecture and integration design across SaaS, API, SSO, and security requirements
  • Manage onboarding, implementation, and ongoing delivery for highly technical customers
  • Stay current on the evolving ML and agent development lifecycle, including LLMs, agent architectures, and the role of training and evaluation data
  • Partner with product, engineering, support, and sales to translate customer needs into new solutions, and advocate internally for customer requirements and feature requests
  • Turn successful deployments into customer references and case studies that fuel further enterprise growth

About you

  • Master’s degree or higher in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or an AI-related field
  • 3+ years of professional experience in a related field
  • Proficiency in Python and data analysis
  • Prior experience leading LLM or agent-based projects
  • Strong commercial instincts: comfortable in customer-facing, consultative selling, with a track record of persuading and influencing stakeholders, including executives
  • Ability to understand a customer’s business processes and translate them into a concrete technical solution tied to business outcomes
  • Exceptional communication skills, with the ability to explain complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and business audiences
  • Strong project management and organizational skills
  • Passion for AI and for the intersection of technology, product, and customer needs
