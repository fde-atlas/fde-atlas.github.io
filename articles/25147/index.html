Baseten forward deployed engineer interview

open-fde.ai · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

Summary

This article compiles Baseten's interview process for forward deployed engineer roles, drawing mainly on one third-party guide plus Baseten's blog and job postings. The process has four rounds: a ~30-minute recruiter screen, a ~60-minute practical coding session in a shared editor, a 45-60 minute AI/ML systems and solution design round, and a ~45-minute STAR-style behavioral round on customer focus and ownership. Sample questions cover designing inference services under strict latency and cost constraints, monitoring model quality in production, and end-to-end project ownership. Tips include structuring answers around quality, latency, and cost, and reading Baseten's FDE blog posts beforehand. One employee reportedly got hired after a public blog post on deploying open-source LLMs caught a co-founder's attention. The author notes the sourcing is thin: questions are mostly not tied to named candidate reports.

Key points

  • Four rounds: recruiter screen, practical coding, AI/ML design, behavioral.
  • Design question centers on inference services with strict latency and cost limits.
  • Structure answers around quality, latency, and cost; state assumptions early.
  • Public technical writing helped one candidate get hired at Baseten.
  • Sourcing is limited; questions lack ties to verified candidate reports.

Full text

Baseten interview

Forward Deployed Engineer · Forward Deployed Engineer (Training) · Forward Deployed ML Engineer

Process

  • Recruiter / HR screen About 30 min. Assesses: Background, motivation, interest in FDE work. [1]
  • Practical technical screen About 60 min live coding in a shared editor on a practical scenario. Assesses: Structuring and testing a service, explaining tradeoffs. [1]
  • AI/ML systems and solution design 45-60 min. Assesses: Inference service design, ML lifecycle, quality/latency/cost tradeoffs. [1]
  • Behavioral: customer and ownership About 45 min, STAR-style. Assesses: Ownership across engineering, PM and product work; handling ambiguity. [1]

Questions

System design 1
  • Design an inference service for a customer with strict latency and cost requirements.
Coding 1
  • Walk through how you would structure and test this service; what tradeoffs are you making and why?
Domain 1
  • How would you monitor and evaluate model quality in production?
Behavioral 2
  • Tell me about turning a vague objective into a clear plan and shipping it.
  • Describe a project you owned end to end.
Motivation 1
  • Why Baseten, and why a forward deployed role?

Tips from the sources

  • Structure design answers around quality, latency and cost, stating assumptions early. [1]
  • Read Baseten's FDE blog posts (e.g., Whisper transcription, model servers, ComfyUI) before interviewing. [1]
  • A Baseten FDE describes his path in: a public blog post on deploying open-source LLMs caught a co-founder's attention, followed by in-office projects and an offer. Public technical writing can open the door. [2]

Not found in public sources: Only one third-party guide describes rounds, and its questions are mostly not tied to named candidate reports. The only first-hand interview account surfaced is on Glassdoor (excluded). No employer statement on process or AI use.
