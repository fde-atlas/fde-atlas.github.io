Summary

This article by Ningbo Youdao Network Technology describes its Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) model for vertical-industry AI solutions. It defines FDEs as engineers embedded at customer sites who turn vague business requirements into deliverable, verifiable AI Agent workflows, and contrasts this role with traditional R&D and pre-sales positions. It outlines the full delivery chain—planning, skill encapsulation, memory and tool integration, orchestration, monitoring and evals—and presents deployments such as a freight-forwarding AI platform covering quoting, shipment tracking, documentation and multilingual customer service, plus a legal AI work platform, with customization offered for other industries.

Key points

  • FDEs are embedded on-site to deliver end-to-end AI Agent workflows.
  • Their work spans planning, skills, memory, tools, orchestration and evals.
  • Unlike platform engineers, FDEs handle non-standard, on-site business needs.
  • Deployed cases include a freight-forwarding AI platform and a legal AI platform.
  • The freight platform automates quoting, tracking, documents and 24/7 support.

Full text

FDE · Forward Deployed Engineer

End-to-end AI agent implementation expert on the business front line

FDE = Forward Deployed Engineer, often translated in China as Forward Deployed Engineer or on-site AI implementation engineer. Not backend, not an algorithm researcher, not pre-sales — but someone who dives straight into the business front line, turning vague business requirements into agent workflows that are deliverable, verifiable, and able to accumulate lasting value.

POSITIONING

Traditional developers build generic products inside the company; an FDE is deployed directly to business/customer sites to solve vague business requirements and build customized agent workflows from scratch.

01 Polishing platform-level generic products inside the company, far from front-line business details.

02 Diving into business/customer sites, understanding real processes and handling vague requirements.

03 Encapsulating business know-how into AI agents that are runnable, iterable, and able to accumulate lasting value.

CORE WORK

From requirements to delivery, the FDE covers the complete chain of AI agent implementation

Planning layer: breaking down vague, high-level business goals with HTN hierarchical decomposition, and sorting out available skills, tool permissions, and execution constraints.

Profile / Memory / Tools / Action: encapsulating business skills, integrating vector memory, connecting database/API tools, and writing execution logic.

Orchestration: implementing agent loops, exception circuit breakers, replanning, and safety guardrails so the agent runs reliably in real business settings.

04 Engineering extension layer: building metric monitoring and automated evals to verify the agent's business value, then feeding generic capabilities back into the company's product line.

DIFFERENCE

Delivers mature product modules following standard processes, without touching underlying logic or handling non-standard front-line business requirements.

Builds platforms and writes generic features inside the company, focusing on architecture and performance, usually without going deep into front-line business details.

Customizes agents on site, delivers business results end to end, and feeds validated generic capabilities back to the team and products.

NOTE

Some self-media also refer to engineers who develop underlying agent infrastructure as FDEs — essentially the same track, but focused on low-level agent framework development.

We stick to the original meaning of FDE as "forward deployed": people are deployed on site, results must be deliverable, and capabilities must be fed back into the enterprise.

It's not about reading the product manual to customers, but about helping them turn pain points in real business scenarios into runnable agent workflows.

FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer (on-site AI implementation engineer). Ningbo Youdao Network deploys AI agents directly to business sites such as freight forwarding and legal practices, breaking down vague business requirements and building them into deliverable, runnable agent workflows that accumulate lasting value — rather than just selling generic tools.

Currently, implementations focus on vertical industries with highly process-driven workflows and clear pain points; solutions already launched include a freight forwarding AI platform and a legal AI work platform. Industries such as manufacturing, foreign trade, retail, and education can also have dedicated agents customized to their actual business scenarios on demand.

The freight forwarding AI platform connects the entire workflow of quotation, order follow-up, documentation, and customer service: unified freight rate management with one-click quotations, automatic tracking and reminders for booking, trucking, and customs clearance milestones, automatic generation and validation of bills of lading and packing lists, and multilingual customer service available 7×24 — reducing manual errors and response times.
