Summary

AY Automate's 2026 report triangulates Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer pay across named sources rather than asserting single figures. Three sources yield three different medians: $150,000 base salary from 21 legally filed H1B disclosures, $211,000 total compensation from self-reported Levels.fyi data, and $177,381 average total pay from Glassdoor (429 entries). The report argues these measure different things and should not be compared directly. Location shifts Glassdoor averages from $154,686 (NYC) to $190,706 (Washington, DC). The widely cited $150K–$1M range is attributed to Varick Agents as an unverified claim. India, Singapore, and frontier labs like OpenAI and Anthropic were excluded due to insufficient data.

Key points

  • H1B filings (n=21) show a $150,000 median base salary.
  • Levels.fyi self-reported median total compensation is $211,000.
  • Glassdoor's average total pay is $177,381 across 429 entries.
  • Location matters: DC averages $190,706 vs NYC's $154,686.
  • The $150K–$1M range is Varick Agents' claim, not audited data.

Executive summary

Three credible, named sources report three different numbers for Forward Deployed Engineer compensation at Palantir, the role's originator and the only company with enough public data to triangulate: a $150,000 base-salary median from legally filed H1B disclosure (n=21), a $211,000 total-compensation median from Levels.fyi (self-reported), and a $177,381 total-pay median from Glassdoor (self-reported, n=429). None of these numbers is wrong. They measure different things.

Location moves the Glassdoor average from $154,686 (New York City) to $190,706 (Washington, DC). Seniority moves it further, though we do not have a separately sourced figure per level, only our own disclosed partition of the national H1B range. Widely cited round numbers like "$150K to $1M" trace to a single named source (Varick Agents) making its own claim, not an independently audited fact, and we present it as exactly that below.

Comp by source, US national

Three sources, three different measurements of the same role. Reading them as competing claims about one number is the mistake; reading them as three real measurements of three different things is the honest way in.

Glassdoor Total pay $191,000 - $292,000 $177,381 (avg) 429 self-reported entries, pulled 2026-07-21

H1B/LCA disclosure (h1bdata.info): Legally required disclosure for H1B-sponsored hires. Real filed data, not self-reported, but covers only base salary and only H1B-sponsored roles.

Levels.fyi: Directional, not audited. Self-reported data tends to skew toward people motivated enough to submit a figure.

Glassdoor: Largest sample size of the three. This total-pay range is Glassdoor's own estimate, separate from its base-pay-only 25th-75th percentile band of $124,513 to $198,096. The $177,381 figure is Glassdoor's separately published average total pay, which sits below this range's low end because it blends a wider set of levels/locations than the range calculation.

Methodology, and what we left out

Every figure on this page comes from app/tools/fde-salary-explorer/data.ts, the same dataset that powers our live FDE salary explorer, sourced from four places: H1B/LCA disclosure data (h1bdata.info, 21 filings for the exact job title "Forward Deployed Engineer," 2024), Levels.fyi (self-reported), Glassdoor (6 separate location pulls, 2026-07-21), and AY Automate's own published pricing.

We deliberately left three things out rather than guess: India (Glassdoor shows a small, 12-entry sample with currency formatting we could not verify against a second source), Singapore (no aggregator returned a usable sample for this exact job title), and OpenAI, Anthropic, or other frontier labs beyond Palantir (none appear to have a separately aggregated "Forward Deployed Engineer" compensation entry distinct from general Software Engineer titles as of this writing).

We also cite, without asserting, Varick Agents' widely referenced $150,000 to $1,000,000 range from their FDE-in-30-Days guide. It is the most detailed public breakdown of the role's methodology we have found, but the compensation figure itself is their stated claim, not independently audited data, and we are not repeating any of the unsourced, oddly-precise figures that a wave of newly published low-authority sites publish with no visible methodology.

Frequently asked questions

What is the real median salary for a forward deployed engineer?

There is no single verified, audited median we would assert as fact. The three sources we triangulate report different medians because they measure different things: H1B/LCA disclosure reports a $150,000 base-salary median (legally filed, n=21), Levels.fyi reports a $211,000 total-compensation median (self-reported), and Glassdoor reports a $177,381 total-pay median (self-reported, n=429). All three are real, cited, and correct for what they measure.

Why do the three sources disagree so much?

Mostly because they measure different things from different sample types. H1B/LCA data is legally filed base salary for H1B-sponsored hires only. Levels.fyi and Glassdoor are self-reported total compensation (or total pay) across a broader, unverified sample. Comparing a base-salary median to a total-compensation median as if they were the same number is the single most common error in FDE salary content online.

Is the $150K-$1M range some sites cite accurate?

That range comes from Varick Agents' FDE-in-30-Days guide, and it is their stated claim, not independently audited data and not a number we assert ourselves. We cite it because it is a named, linkable source, not because we have verified it against payroll records. See our forward deployed engineer salary guide for the full context on that figure.

Does experience level change FDE compensation?

Almost certainly, but we do not have a separately sourced figure per experience band. What we show on this page is our own partition of the sourced national H1B range (n=21) into four experience bands by percentile position, clearly labeled as our estimate, not a separately verified per-level figure. Treat it as a reasoned partition of real data, not a new data point.

Where does this report get its numbers, and what did you leave out?

Every figure traces to one of four sources: H1B/LCA disclosure data (h1bdata.info), Levels.fyi, Glassdoor (6 separate pulls by location), and AY Automate's own published pricing. We deliberately excluded India (unclear currency formatting we could not verify against a second source), Singapore (no usable aggregator sample), and frontier labs beyond Palantir like OpenAI or Anthropic (no separately aggregated FDE-title compensation data exists for them yet).
