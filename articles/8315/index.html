Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer (5/5): Long hours and hard work, but worth the pay

Taro · 2023-02-26 · 2 min read

Summary

A Taro review from a Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer with under a year at the company gives a 5.0 rating. The author praises high pay, smart colleagues, and prestige, but notes long hours, a ban on side projects and investments, and argues that founding a company would yield higher returns. The page also aggregates several interview experiences: a recruiter call, HackerRank assessment, LeetCode Medium questions, a decomposition/system-design round, and a hiring manager round. One 2026 candidate found the process easier than online reports suggested.

Key points

  • Current FDE rates Palantir 5.0 overall
  • Pros: great salary, smart peers, prestige
  • Cons: long hours, no side projects allowed
  • Interviews include recruiter call, HackerRank, LeetCode Medium
  • One candidate found interviews easier than expected
