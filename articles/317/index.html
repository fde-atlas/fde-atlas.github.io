FDE Salary: Forward Deployed Engineer Pay Ranges (2026)

AY Automate · 2026-06-23 · 5 min read

Summary

AY Automate compiles Forward Deployed Engineer pay data, citing a source for every figure and omitting unverifiable numbers. For Palantir, the role's originator, US H1B/LCA filings show base salaries of $135K–$250K (median ~$150K), while self-reported total compensation ranges up to $295K. Glassdoor's London figure (£36K median, n=59) looks low and is flagged as such. The article attributes wide pay variance to seniority, company type, and the scarcity of engineers with both technical and commercial judgment. It also notes gaps: no usable Singapore data, and frontier-lab FDE figures circulating online lack sources. AY Automate's own $3,000/month placement price is listed separately as a company cost, not a salary.

Key points

  • Palantir US H1B base salaries: $135K–$250K, median ~$150K
  • Self-reported Palantir total comp reaches $295K
  • London Glassdoor median £36K flagged as a low outlier
  • Pay variance driven by seniority, company, and dual judgment
  • Singapore and frontier-lab FDE data deemed unreliable and excluded

Where the public data runs out

Glassdoor shows a small sample (12 entries) with currency formatting that was unclear enough that we could not verify it cleanly against a second source. Rather than guess at a conversion, we left it out.

No aggregator returned a usable sample size for this exact job title in Singapore as of this writing.

These companies do not appear to have a separately aggregated "Forward Deployed Engineer" compensation entry on Levels.fyi or Glassdoor distinct from general Software Engineer or Member of Technical Staff titles. Several newly-published sites claim specific frontier-lab FDE figures with no visible source; we are not repeating those numbers here.

Why FDE comp varies this much

The gap between a $150,000 median H1B filing and a $295,000 reported total comp figure is not a contradiction. It is three real variables stacking on top of each other, plus a sourcing problem the rest of the search results have not solved.

Seniority

The role spans someone doing their first embedded deployment to someone running a seven-figure account. That is a wide band in any engineering discipline, not unique to FDE.

Company and scope

Palantir originated the model, embedding roughly 120 forward deployed engineers inside JPMorgan starting in 2009. Compensation at the role's originator with venture-scale backing is a different market than the same title at a startup, an agency, or a mid-market vendor.

The two-judgments premium

The role requires technical judgment (agent loops, tool use, evaluation-driven development) and commercial judgment (mapping a real workflow, earning trust, explaining outcomes to a non-technical stakeholder) in the same person. Engineers strong on both are rarer than engineers strong on either alone, and that scarcity is a real driver at the top of the range.
