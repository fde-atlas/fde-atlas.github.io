【Digitalize】Digitalize, with a track record of supporting AI adoption at a cumulative total of 560 companies, officially launches its FDE-style AI in-house development support service "Digitalize FDE"

Original title: 【デジライズ】累計560社のAI導入支援実績をもつデジライズ、FDE型AI内製化支援「デジライズFDE」を本格始動

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-06-25 · 10 min read

Summary

Japanese company Digirize has launched "Digirize FDE", a Forward Deployed Engineer-style service supporting AI in-house development. Drawing on experience with over 560 client companies and 40,000 people trained, the company positions the service as the step beyond AI training, which it says only gets firms to routine task automation. Dedicated engineers work on-site one to five days a week, covering AI diagnosis, PoC, full system development, and company-wide adoption across three phases. The service uses tools like Claude Code, Cursor, and Dify, remains vendor-neutral, and helps clients apply for subsidies. Examples include a large manufacturer where a three-month on-site engagement produced a knowledge AI agent automating about 55% of internal inquiries. Digirize is also recruiting partner companies to supply FDE resources.

Key points

  • Digirize launched an FDE-style AI in-house development support service.
  • It builds on training experience with 560+ companies and 40,000+ trainees.
  • Dedicated engineers stay on-site from diagnosis through PoC to full development.
  • A manufacturer case automated ~55% of internal inquiries via a knowledge AI agent.
  • The company is recruiting partner firms to provide FDE engineers.

DejiRise Co., Ltd. (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo / Representative Director: Masahiro Chien; hereinafter "the Company") has officially launched "DejiRise FDE," an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)-model AI development and insourcing support service, as a new offering of its Consulting Division. A dedicated AI engineer embeds within the client company's operations, providing end-to-end support from AI diagnostics and PoC through full-scale system development and company-wide adoption. At the same time, the Company is now recruiting FDE partner companies to collaborate on this service.

Background: Companies that have built a foundation through AI training are increasingly demanding "what comes next"

To date, through corporate AI reskilling and AI utilization consulting, the Company has supported the development of AI talent at a cumulative total of more than 560 companies and more than 40,000 participants, and in May 2026 it earned the No.1 ranking for number of corporate clients among AI reskilling services (according to a survey by Tokyo Shoko Research). Companies that have built this foundation through training are now clearly raising expectations for "the next stage": "We want to rebuild our business processes themselves with AI."

Companies that have adopted generative AI, trained employees to use it proficiently, and automated routine tasks increasingly want to go a step beyond using a "smart Google" or "time-saving tools" and "own in-house" genuine AI systems that drive business productivity. DejiRise FDE is what makes that "next step" a reality, working hands-on alongside teams in the field.

In fact, worldwide, in May 2026 Fujitsu partnered with US-based Anthropic and OpenAI to launch an initiative dispatching AI engineers to client companies to guide them through to problem resolution—FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)—and the FDE model is rapidly spreading to major Japanese companies as a "standard model" among AI-leading enterprises. Meanwhile, the reality is that mid-sized companies, which find it hardest to secure specialized talent, are the least able to benefit from it.

Building on know-how that the Company has systematized through training and consulting engagements with 560 companies, it offers "DejiRise FDE" as a service to close this implementation gap.

DejiRise's "5-Level AI Maturity Model"

The Company defines corporate AI adoption maturity in five levels. Levels 1–2, which AI training raises, are the solid foundation that underpins all further development. On top of that foundation, specialized engineers who embed on-site and work hands-on with teams drive progress to Levels 3–5.

  • Lv.1 General AI Mastery: Employees use generative AI proficiently in daily work (delivered by: AI training)
  • Lv.2 Routine Automation: Routine and repetitive tasks are automated with AI (delivered by: AI training)
  • Lv.3 Workflow Building: Cross-departmental business workflows are redesigned with AI (delivered by: DejiRise FDE)
  • Lv.4 Lightweight Coding: AI apps and automation are insourced, led by frontline teams (delivered by: DejiRise FDE)
  • Lv.5 Full-Scale Development: AI systems integrated into core business operations are fully developed (delivered by: DejiRise FDE)

DejiRise FDE builds on the "Level 2" foundation established through training, lifting companies to genuine productivity improvement.

Three Key Features of "DejiRise FDE"

FEATURE 01 | A dedicated AI engineer stationed at your site The FDE works on-site 1 to 5 days a week and provides daily support via Slack/Teams. With a proximity that lets "quick questions" be answered immediately, the FDE embeds deeply into frontline operations.

FEATURE 02 | Training × Consulting × Development, end to end We don't stop at proposals. Using Claude Code, Cursor, Dify, and more, we implement "working AI," and include employee education, operational rules, and governance setup within the scope.

FEATURE 03 | Vendor-neutral / subsidy utilization support We design the optimal AI stack without being tied to specific SaaS or models. We also support cost-effective adoption through the use of various government subsidies.

How We Support You (3 Phases)

  • Phase 1: AI Diagnosis & Interviews (1–2 weeks): Department-by-department interviews / issue organization / narrowing down priority initiatives / action plan development
  • Phase 2: Individual Consulting & PoC (3 months+): Priority theme selection / prompt and workflow design / PoC and impact verification / KPI design
  • Phase 3: Full-Scale AI System Development (3 months+): Full MVP development / system integration / permissions and logging setup / operational framework building / maintenance and improvement

DejiRise FDE Implementation Examples

In the implementation phase of DejiRise FDE, we combine our "Claude Code Corporate Adoption Support" (approximately 60 video lessons × hands-on sessions × FDE accompaniment support) to create a state where even non-engineers can insource "working solutions." Examples of tasks actually streamlined with Claude Code:

  • Issue inventory → on-the-spot mockups: Business issues are listed and prioritized, with the first one immediately turned into a prototype.
  • In-house development of internal dashboards and business apps: Inquiry management, KPI visualization, and more built no-code/low-code.
  • Automation of Excel and PowerPoint tasks: Routine reports, aggregations, document creation, and manuscript checks generated automatically.
  • Automation of meeting minutes, email, and inquiry handling: Draft generation, summarization, and FAQ creation shorten daily work.
  • Prototype → production implementation: Working with an FDE, teams move from prototype to production systems in as little as two weeks.
  • Quality assurance with E2E testing: A single phrase—"run E2E tests"—raises the operational accuracy of deliverables.

Case Studies (Selected Excerpts)

The Company's development and consulting support spans a wide range of industries, including wholesale, retail, e-commerce, professional services, food service, manufacturing, real estate, IT, finance, and elder care. Below is a selection, with company names withheld.

  • Major internet group: The Company served as the group's AI advisor, driving generative AI adoption company-wide, contributing to the creation of approximately 6,000 hours of work time per month across the group.
  • Law firm (marketing support for professional services): Built an AI advisor plus automated email drafting workflow, reducing email writing by approximately 85% and document creation by approximately 75%, creating approximately 90 hours per month (including a case where 3 hours of daily work was reduced to under 30 minutes).
  • Major retail/e-commerce company (approx. 1,600 employees): Supported company-wide AI adoption through "7 business functions × biweekly workshops." Automated meeting minutes, survey tabulation, and schedule registration, and reported results at a company-wide meeting of 800 attendees.
  • Tax accounting firm / M&A brokerage: Developed an AI-powered M&A brokerage platform on contract. Implemented an end-to-end solution covering client and negotiation management, automated generation of business valuations, business plans, acquisition and PMI plans, and a tax advisory chatbot supervised by tax accountants.
  • Food service and hospitality: Developed an AI system handling core kitchen and store operations on contract, with delivery and acceptance completed. This evolved into a maintenance contract, and requirements definition for the next core system has begun.
  • Real estate: In addition to adding AI features to a business system (Kintone), expanded multifaceted engagement with a single client—AI advisory, company-wide AI reskilling training, and AI co-development with a subsidiary—now moving into the maintenance phase.
  • IT and telecommunications: Built and delivered business automation workflows using Dify + n8n. A publicly disclosed track record, including publication of a case study interview article and permission to display the client's logo.
  • Wholesale and distribution (business-use liquor wholesaler): Through Claude Code consulting, cataloged and prioritized 18 business challenges, and as a first step built a mock dashboard for managing LINE inquiry progress.
  • Major manufacturer: An FDE was on-site full-time for about 3 months and implemented an internal knowledge AI agent through full development. Automated approximately 55% of internal inquiry handling.

*These results reflect performance at the time of support and do not guarantee effectiveness. In this release, all client names are withheld and described by industry.

Comment from Masahiro Chaen, Representative Director and CEO

"The true value of AI is determined not by 'adoption' or 'training,' but by the 'productivity' that follows. What we have seen through working alongside over 560 companies is a clear sense that many companies have steadily built the foundation to 'use AI effectively' through training, and are now beginning to move to the next stage of wanting to 'rebuild their business processes with AI.' Beyond that point, true productivity can only emerge when there are engineers who embed themselves in the field and work hands-on alongside teams. Digirise FDE is an initiative to deliver Japanese companies' AI internalization not just as 'proposals' but all the way to 'results.' We sincerely hope to work together with companies serious about transforming their business with AI, and with partner companies willing to help spread this new profession."
