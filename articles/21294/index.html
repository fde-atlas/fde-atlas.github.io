Summary

This is a job posting on OpenWork for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) at RevComm, a Japanese SaaS company offering AI, voice, and cloud products such as MiiTel, an AI-powered IP phone with over 50,000 users. The role involves structuring undefined customer problems into feasible technical designs, integrating MiiTel into client systems via APIs, webhooks, and MCP, and abstracting field learnings back into the product. Requirements include BtoB SaaS client-facing experience, API or data integration skills, and SQL proficiency. Salary ranges from 10 to 15 million yen annually, with remote work available. The company was the only Asian firm on Forbes AI 50 2023 and won a CES 2025 Innovation Award.

Key points

  • RevComm is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer with a 10-15 million yen salary.
  • The FDE structures undefined customer problems and designs API, data, and MCP integrations.
  • The role feeds individual project learnings back into the product and shapes the FDE role itself.
  • Required: BtoB SaaS client experience, API or data integration, and SQL skills.
  • RevComm's MiiTel has 50,000+ users; the company made Forbes AI 50 2023.
