Forward Deployed Engineer Academy — College to Job

Vibe Engines · 2026-10-28 · 2 min read

Summary

Vibe Engines offers a free, browser-saved study route into forward deployed engineering, aimed at college students and experienced builders alike. The curriculum has seven phases covering engineering foundations, production systems, AI workflows with evals, customer engagement, and interview preparation. Each phase requires shipping a real artifact and passing scored gates with questions and project evidence. The central argument: employers expect production judgment, so learners must build verifiable evidence rather than skip prerequisites. The capstone is a nine-artifact portfolio case study following an insurance-claims team from ambiguous request to handed-off deployment, ending with mock interview rounds and a readiness rubric.

Key points

  • Free route, saved in-browser, no account needed
  • Seven phases, two scored gates, nine capstone artifacts
  • Standard: ship for a real user and measure correctness
  • Capstone follows an insurance-claims team to handoff
  • Ends with mock interview loops and readiness rubric

THE HONEST START

Many employers hire for production judgment associated with experienced engineers. That does not mean “wait two years.” It means your route must build the missing evidence instead of pretending the title has no prerequisites.

College learners begin with engineering foundations. Experienced builders skip them. Both converge on the same standard: ship for a real user, measure correctness, handle failure and explain the outcome.
