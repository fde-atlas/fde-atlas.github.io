FDE Companion Development (FDE Support) | Embedding in the Field to "Build It Through to Completion"

Original title: FDE伴走開発（FDE支援）| 現場に入り込み“作り切る”

株式会社Sai · 2026-09-01 · 7 min read

Summary

This is a service page from Japan's SAI describing its monthly-subscription FDE support offering, where engineers embed in client sites to handle everything from problem discovery through implementation, operational adoption, and internal capability transfer. The page contrasts this with contracted development, SES staffing, and consulting, outlines a four-step process (consultation, site observation, minimal implementation, adoption and handover), and details staffing, pricing, and common failure modes.

Key points

  • The FDE model originated at Palantir and has spread to AI companies including OpenAI.
  • Unlike contracted development, FDE support starts before requirements are fixed, identifying what to build.
  • Work covers observation, implementation, adoption, and in-housing, with engineers of 3–5 years' experience on site 1–3 days weekly.
  • Pricing is monthly, split into advisory-only and hands-on tiers, with no published price list.
  • The most common failure is abandoning a tool after it works; SAI predefines adoption metrics like processing time and usage rate.

Engineers embed themselves on-site and "see it through" — from identifying challenges to implementation, adoption, and internalization. This is a hands-on, companion-style support service (FDE support) that puts the FDE model, which originated at Palantir, into practice for companies in Japan.

What is FDE Companion Development? Support that handles everything from discovering challenges to embedding solutions, on-site

FDE Companion Development is a form of support in which engineers embed themselves in the customer's workplace and handle everything in one continuous flow — from identifying challenges to implementation, operational adoption, and internalization. FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) refers to engineers deployed to the customer's "front line." SAI Inc. offers this as FDE support on a monthly subscription basis.

Unlike consulting that only offers strategic proposals, or contract development that simply builds to spec, we start by identifying "what should be built." It is best suited to challenges that arise before requirements are fixed; for large-scale development with finalized specifications or cases where off-the-shelf SaaS suffices, conventional options are a better fit. We propose arrangements ranging from an advisory model with unlimited consultations to a hands-on companion model with actual engineering work, depending on the scale of the challenge.

FDE is a way of working established by Palantir in the US and now spreading across AI companies including OpenAI. Now that generative AI has widened the gap between "what technology makes possible" and "what actually delivers results on the ground," FDEs are gaining worldwide attention as the ones who bridge that gap (see our explainer article for details).

How FDE support differs from contract development, SES, and consulting

The differences lie in who decides what to build, and where the engagement ends.

Engagement modelScope of workWho decides what to buildEndpoint
Contract developmentWe develop according to finalized requirements.The client defines the requirements and hands them over.Delivery
SESWe provide engineers' working hours.Stays with the client.End of the contracted engagement period
ConsultingWe organize the challenges and make proposals. Implementation is usually handled by another company.The client decides based on the proposal.Proposal / report
FDE support (SAI Inc.)We handle everything in one continuous flow, from observing the workplace to implementation, operational adoption, and internalization.We decide together while observing the workplace and showing working solutions.The solution is in daily use and improvements run on their own within your company

Contract development suits development with finalized requirements; introducing off-the-shelf SaaS suits challenges it can already cover. If you are still at the stage of deciding what to build, FDE support is the right fit.

We handle four phases, from observing challenges to internalization

Business observation and structuring of challenges

We embed ourselves on-site and observe the actual work and data. We identify the real bottlenecks that never surface in interviews, and agree with management and the front line on the order in which to solve them.

Implementation of systems and automation

From business systems and embedding generative AI and AI agents into workflows, to data platforms and dashboards. We place "working solutions" on-site within weeks and refine them through actual use.

Companion support for operational adoption

We eliminate the reasons solutions go unused, on the spot, and stay until the solution becomes part of daily work. We define adoption criteria upfront (processing time, usage rate, etc.) and proceed while measuring against them.

Internalization and technology transfer

We build alongside your team members and transfer skills in the process. We prepare documentation and an operating structure, with the goal of reaching a "self-sustaining" state where improvements run on their own within your company.

The process follows four steps: consultation, on-site observation, minimal implementation, and adoption

No requirements specification is needed. The early phase is centered on on-site visits; once operations are being embedded, we shift to a remote-centered approach.

Step 01

Start with a consultation

We listen to your challenges and give you an honest assessment of whether FDE support is a good fit

Step 02

On-site observation

Over several days to two weeks, we observe the work and data to identify challenges

Step 03

Minimal implementation

Within a few weeks, we build a working solution and validate it on-site

Step 04

Adoption and handover

We embed the solution into operations and support you through internalization

Team and workload guidelines: engineers with 3–5 years of experience, 1–3 days per week, on-site visits centered in the early phase

  • Working days
  • Typically 1–3 days per week, decided according to the scale of the challenge.
  • On-site visits and remote work
  • From identifying challenges through minimal implementation, we work mainly on-site. From the operational adoption phase onward, we shift to a remote-centered approach. The ratio is tailored to your company.
  • Engineers who embed on-site
  • We assign engineers with 3–5 years of experience, matched to the challenge at hand.

What's harder than building is what comes after. We start with a single workflow and a single team, and expand the scope while observing how the solution is actually used.

FDE Companion Development pricing is monthly, based on the scale of the challenge and the team involved

We do not publish a price list. Whether you start with the advisory model or the companion model is the starting point for how pricing is determined.

  • How pricing is determined
  • Pricing is monthly. The starting point is whether you begin with the advisory model, which includes unlimited consultations, or the companion model, which includes actual engineering work. The team is determined by the scale of the challenge you want to solve and the number of engineers involved. We ask how much time and how many people the target workflow currently consumes, and assess the necessary team together with you.
  • What's included
  • The advisory model includes unlimited consultations. The companion model adds actual engineering work on top of that. Support covers everything from observing workflows and identifying challenges, through implementation, to operational adoption and technology transfer for internalization.
  • How to think about contract duration
  • Duration is discussed case by case. Our approach assumes starting with a single workflow and a single team, then expanding the scope while observing actual usage. Defining adoption metrics upfront makes it easier to decide whether to continue or expand.
  • Time to get started
  • We can begin as early as the week after your consultation. The first few days to two weeks after kickoff are devoted to observing the workplace. The exact start date depends on team availability and your company's preparations.
  • Additional costs
  • Actual expenses such as cloud and external service usage fees are billed separately. Fees for AI tools and SaaS are also not included in the monthly fee. What is and isn't included is confirmed in writing at the time of contract.

Answering questions on pricing, team structure, differences from contract development, fit and misfit, and failure cases

Q. Can we request this without a requirements specification? ＋

Yes, we can. Even when it's not yet decided what to build, we start by looking at on-site operations and data. The first few days to two weeks are devoted to observation, and together we put into words the problems that need to be solved. Rather than fixing specifications upfront, we proceed by deciding as we show working prototypes.

Q. How does this differ from contract development, SES, or consulting? +

Contract development takes fixed requirements, builds them, and ends with delivery. SES is a contract that provides engineers' working hours, leaving the decision of what to build to the client. Consulting typically covers only the proposal stage, with implementation handled by another company. FDE support starts by identifying "what should be built" and stays alongside you until it is actually used in the field and delivers results.

Q. What kinds of companies is this suited for, and which is it not suited for? +

It suits companies whose operations are inefficient but who haven't decided how to systematize them. The same applies to companies that want to embed generative AI into their operations, break out of PoC (proof of concept via prototyping) stagnation, or reduce dependence on outsourcing. It does not suit large-scale development with fully fixed requirements, or problems that off-the-shelf SaaS can already solve. In those cases, we recommend conventional options.

Q. How are fees determined? +

It's a monthly subscription, determined by the scale of the problem and the level of involvement. It ranges from an advisory model with unlimited consultations to a hands-on model that includes actual engineer work hours. Cost-effectiveness is estimated together based on the time and number of people currently spent on the target operations.

Q. What is the team structure, and how many days per week do you come on-site? +

The engineers who go on-site have 3 to 5 years of experience, with a typical commitment of 1 to 3 days per week. From problem identification to minimal implementation, visits are the focus; from the operational adoption stage onward, we shift to a remote-centered approach. The ratio of on-site visits to remote work is adjusted to fit your company.

Q. What are common failures in FDE support? +

Letting go once something that works has been built. The reasons something goes unused exist only in the field, and if left alone, people revert to the old ways. SAI Co., Ltd. first defines adoption metrics such as processing time and usage rate, and expands the scope of application while measuring them.
