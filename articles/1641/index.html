Forward Deployed Engineer resume: what employers screen for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-10-27 · 6 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch analyzes 563 live engineer-title FDE postings from 115 employers (October 25, 2026) to show what resume screeners look for. Python appears in 76.2% of postings, customer-facing work in 74.1%, APIs or integrations in 69.8%, travel in 58.8%, and TypeScript/JavaScript in 56.5%. The article advises mapping each resume bullet to a named posting requirement, leading with measured customer deployments, shipped integrations, and post-launch ownership. It also contrasts FDE resumes with software engineer and solutions engineer resumes, and notes 58.3% of postings state a years minimum (median 5).

Key points

  • Based on 563 live FDE postings from 115 employers.
  • Top requirements: Python 76.2%, customer-facing 74.1%, APIs/integrations 69.8%.
  • 58.3% of postings state a years minimum; median is 5 years.
  • Databricks and Palantir account for 157 of the 563 postings.
  • Resumes should show measured customer deployments and post-launch ownership.

How an FDE resume differs from a software engineer resume

Keep the production engineering proof. Lead with customer-facing delivery, named integrations into an external stack, and deployments you owned through go-live and after. A software engineer resume that stops at internal services and peer review leaves the screeners without the customer and travel evidence FDE postings name.

The full role split is on Forward Deployed Engineer vs. software engineer. On the resume, that split shows up as: who the user was (external customer vs. internal team), where the system ran (customer stack vs. your company's services), and what you measured after launch (customer workflow metric vs. service-level metrics alone).

The resume opens the screen. The interview loop tests the same deployment story in person.

How a solutions engineer resume becomes an FDE resume

Keep the customer discovery and stakeholder lines. Add production build ownership: the system you designed, coded, evaluated, and left running, with evals and fallbacks you owned after launch. Demo, configuration, and deal support alone do not read as FDE ownership on a screen.

See FDE vs. solutions engineer for the ownership line. On the resume, name the artifact you shipped, the stack it integrated into, and the measured result after go-live.

From resume screen to FDE interview

Ship one measured customer deployment, put that story on the resume with the stack and the outcome, then apply with evidence that matches the posting. The resume gets you into the loop; the rounds test the same story under time pressure.

Map each resume bullet to a round on the FDE interview loop overview, then rehearse with the FDE interview practice questions. Decomposition and work-trial rounds ask for the customer outcome; deployment deep dives ask for what broke after go-live.

Questions people ask

What should a Forward Deployed Engineer resume show?

Show customer-facing delivery with a named outcome, a shipped API or integration into someone else's stack, and production ownership you carried end to end. Ground those lines in the requirements index: across 563 live engineer-title FDE postings from 115 employers (October 25, 2026), Python appears in 76.2%, customer-facing phrasing in 74.1%, APIs or integrations in 69.8%, travel in 58.8%, and TypeScript or JavaScript in 56.5%.

How do I rewrite a software engineer resume for Forward Deployed Engineer roles?

Keep the production engineering proof. Lead with customer-facing delivery, named integrations into an external stack, and deployments you owned through go-live and after. A software engineer resume that stops at internal services and peer review leaves the screeners without the customer and travel evidence FDE postings name. How do I get an FDE interview as a software engineer?
