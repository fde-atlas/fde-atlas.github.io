FDE jobs at Accenture and Deloitte: what the work involves

FDE Interview Prep · 2026-09-25 · 17 min read

Summary

This article analyzes what "Forward Deployed Engineer" actually means at Accenture and Deloitte, based on a September 2026 count of their job boards. It finds the title covers very different work: 66 of Accenture's 78 India FDE postings are ServiceNow application support roles, while Deloitte's 61 US postings are tied to partner platforms like Databricks, Palantir and Anthropic, often requiring 50% travel and US government clearances. Consulting FDEs work in pods on client engagements, cannot modify the partner platform, and differ from product-company FDEs. The piece ends with a checklist for reading such postings.

Key points

  • 66 of Accenture's 78 India FDE postings are actually ServiceNow application support roles.
  • Deloitte's 61 US FDE postings name partner platforms: Databricks (8), Palantir (7), plus Anthropic and OpenAI.
  • 43 of Deloitte's postings require 50% travel; 22 mention a US government clearance.
  • Accenture launched FDE programs with Google Cloud, ServiceNow and SAP; Salesforce named both firms as launch partners.
  • Consulting FDEs work in pods on 6-8 week sprints and can only configure, not fix, partner platforms.

The partner platform decides the job

At a product company, the platform is the employer’s own. At these firms it often belongs to a partner, and the posting names it: in our count, Deloitte titles alone name Databricks 8 times and Palantir 7. Here is how we read a consulting-firm posting, in order. It is our checklist, not the firms’.

Read a consulting-firm FDE posting

  • Which partner platform is named in the title or must-have skills?
  • How many years with that platform does it ask for?
  • What does the summary call the job (FDE, or something else, such as application support)?
  • Which practice is hiring: government, cyber, a product studio, or a platform team?
  • What travel figure does it state?
  • Does it ask for an active clearance, the ability to obtain one, or neither?
  • Does it accept visa sponsorship?

How the work differs from a product company’s FDE role

The postings point to four differences. Each is a trade, not a verdict.

You work in a pod

Deloitte’s postings describe contributing “independently within an FDE pod while mentoring newer team members”, and in our count 31 of its 61 FDE postings use that framing. Source 17Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer - GPSPublisherDeloitte USSource typecompany job postingSource 20Forward Deployed Engineer, Frontier GenAIPublisherDeloitte USSource typecompany job posting Accenture’s template calls FDEs the execution spine of its deployment pods. Source 1Accenture Workday job detail records, India FDE-titled postingsPublisherAccenture (Workday)Source typeour analysis of public dataHow we countedSource 15Forward Deployed Engineer (London, R00339251)PublisherAccenture careers (Workday)Source typecompany job posting Expect to be one of several engineers on the client, though not always: EY’s AFE cell lead posting says the role is often solo at a client site. Source 21AFE Cell Lead - AI Advanced Forward Engineering - ManagerPublisherEY CareersSource typecompany job posting

The engagement has a clock

Deloitte describes six- to eight-week sprints. Source 9Deloitte Drives Agentic Industry Transformation at the Speed of AI - Press releasePublisherDeloitte USSource typecompany website If you’re asked “how would you run a six-week engagement?”, answer three things:

  • What you cut in week one: “I’d pick the one workflow that costs the client most and park everything else in a written list.”
  • What done means by week six: “Real users running it on their own data, with one number we agreed on at the start.”
  • What you hand over: “The running system, a runbook their team has used once without me, and a short list of what comes next.”

You cannot change the platform

At a product company, you can file a bug with your own engineering team and sometimes ship the fix. On a partner platform, the fix has to live on top of it: configuration, integration code and workarounds. Your lever is the vendor, not a code change: in the ServiceNow program, ServiceNow’s own FDEs work in the same customer environments. Source 6ServiceNow and Accenture Launch Forward Deployed Engineering Program to Scale Agentic AI Across the EnterprisePublisherAccenture NewsroomSource typecompany blog Say in the interview how you would escalate a platform gap through them while shipping a workaround.

The customer may be a government

Deloitte’s GPS postings, such as its Anthropic FDE role, and Accenture Federal Services’ APX program both point at government clients. So prepare from the posting. Here is how we would do it for these firms.

Have the logistics answers ready

Travel, clearance and sponsorship are stated requirements, so a recruiter can check them early. Write one-breath answers before the first call. For travel:

“I’ve read the travel line on the posting and I can commit to it.”

For clearance, check the citizenship line first. Accenture Federal Services’ FDE postings require US citizenship and the ability to obtain a federal clearance. Prepare for platform depth

Deloitte’s Anthropic posting asks for 1+ years with the Claude API or related tools. Source 17Anthropic Forward Deployed Engineer - GPSPublisherDeloitte USSource typecompany job posting We would prepare for two follow-ups: what you built with it, and what you found limiting. Our question which of our APIs have you built with? drills that answer. A strong version names one thing you built, one limit you hit, and what you did about it. An example to adapt, not a real result:

“I built a document triage tool on the Claude API using tool use. The limit I hit was latency on long documents, so I split intake: a small, fast model classified the first page and routed the document within seconds, and full extraction ran in the background on the larger model. Caseworkers got a routing decision right away instead of waiting for the whole document.”

Follow the AI rules

Both firms publish company-wide rules, and both are strict.

  • Accenture. Its candidate AI policy forbids using AI to complete assessments such as coding challenges unless explicitly stated otherwise, forbids using AI products during interviews to generate answers, and says Accenture uses proctoring tools and AI-detection methods. Is a consulting-firm FDE role right for you?

It fits well if:

  • you have shipped on one of the named platforms and want to go deeper on it;
  • you like variety across clients and time-boxed work with a clear end;
  • you want a team around you, with people to learn from and people to mentor;
  • you can travel, and, for government work, meet the citizenship and clearance lines.

Think twice if:

  • you want to shape the product itself, not build on top of someone else’s;
  • you cannot travel much, or need visa sponsorship on a posting that rules it out;
  • the posting turns out to be application support, and you wanted to build AI systems.

Coming from consulting and heading the other way? Moving from consultant to forward deployed engineer covers that move.

Whichever way you go, the skills under the title are the same: read the customer’s problem, cut it down, and ship something that works in their environment.
