The market in 2026

The FDE role is widely described as 2026's hottest job in AI. Reported demand grew roughly 10x in about 18 months, and most billion-dollar AI companies now run an FDE function ranging from 20 to a couple hundred engineers. Tracking aggregators counted well over two hundred open roles across dozens of companies in 2026.

The companies named most often as running large FDE teams: Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Scale AI, Databricks, Cohere, Harvey, Mistral, and Glean — with Palantir and OpenAI consistently the largest by open headcount.

The big 2026 developments

OpenAI launched a dedicated, multi-billion-dollar deployment company whose engineers embed inside enterprises to build the systems that make its software produce business outcomes — and seeded it by acquiring an applied-AI consulting firm to bring roughly 150 experienced FDEs from day one.

Anthropic hires the role as "Applied AI Engineer" (and "Forward Deployed Engineer, Applied AI"), embedding with strategic customers to ship production Claude applications. Google Cloud has been hiring forward deployed engineers in volume to help enterprises adopt Gemini and Vertex AI, often paired with large system integrators.

Beyond the labs

Plenty of other companies hire the same role under varying titles: Databricks (often "AI Engineer" or customer-facing engineer for lakehouse and GenAI work), Cohere (enterprise LLMs in regulated industries), Scale AI (defense, government, large enterprise), Microsoft, and agent-focused companies like Sierra and Glean. The titles vary; the mandate — embed, build, ship, own the outcome — is consistent.

Also search forward deployed software engineer or FDSE, applied AI engineer, deployed engineer, solutions engineer, solutions architect, field engineer, customer engineer and implementation engineer. Read the responsibilities rather than the title: if the job says you embed with a customer and ship in their environment, it is this role.

Travel varies widely, from effectively remote with occasional customer visits to substantial on-site time; one Shield AI posting states roughly 30 percent. Clearances apply only to defense and government work at companies like Palantir, Shield AI and Anduril, some of which will sponsor the process. Commercial AI roles do not require one.

Palantir and OpenAI are consistently the largest by open roles, followed by Anthropic, Databricks, Scale AI, Cohere, Mistral, Harvey, and Glean. Google Cloud is also hiring FDEs in volume for Gemini and Vertex AI.
