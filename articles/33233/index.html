Dreamforce 2026 - partnering to provide Foward Deployment Engineer capabilities to the Salesforce customer base

Diginomica · 2026-09-17 · 5 min read

Summary

This Diginomica piece from Dreamforce 2026 examines how Nagarro, one of roughly 80 Salesforce FDE partners, builds Forward Deployed Engineer capabilities. Its Salesforce practice has about 800 consultants and 2,500+ certifications, with Data Cloud certification treated as critical. Through its Fluidic Intelligence approach (Advisory, Forge, Teams), Nagarro trains multi-skilled FDEs and delivers Agentforce results: 30% faster dealer responses for a manufacturer, 200 man-days saved monthly at a financial firm, and up to 10% higher member conversions for a fitness company. MD Viyom Jain argues many AI pilots fail because they lack production plans, and partners must first fix business friction. Persistent Systems is acquiring Nagarro for about $1.45 billion.

  • Nagarro is one of ~80 Salesforce FDE partners, being acquired by Persistent Systems for ~$1.45 billion.
  • Its Salesforce practice has ~800 consultants and 2,500+ certifications; Data Cloud certification is key for FDE work.

  • Jain says failed AI pilots stem from no production roadmap; partners should fix business friction first.

When you attend Dreamforce, you get to meet a wide range of Salesforce partners who, once the jamboree of main stage announcements is over, will be tasked with working alongside customers to implement all the new technologies and products.

One such partner is Nagarro, an Indian heritage software development and consulting company, set up in 1996. In 2011 the company was acquired by a German holding company and then in 2020 gained independent listing. In June this year Indian digital engineering firm Persistent Systems announced a voluntary public takeover bid to acquire Nagarro for roughly $1.45 billion, an offer that will likely be approved next month.

Nagarro is one of around 80 firms that are Salesforce FDE (Forward Deployment Engineers) partners. I took the opportunity to speak with Viyom Jain MD and Global Business Head, Enterprise Products @ Nagarro to find out how it approached this type of partnership.

Data Cloud certifications are critical

Nagarro’s Salesforce practice was set up in 2012 as a result of work undertaken for a manufacturing client, and then expanded by serving energy and utilities companies. This practice currently employs around 800 consultants globally and addresses five main geos: US (its biggest market), UK and Ireland, Germany and the Nordics, Japan and South Asia.

Alongside horizontal technology practices such as the Salesforce one, the company is structured in practices across different vertical sectors of which Banking, Financial Services and Insurance, along with manufacturing are the biggest revenue earners The Salesforce practice works with the different vertical business units as it has developed expertise in Salesforce industry-specific clouds such as Government Cloud, Financial Services Cloud and Life Sciences Cloud.

One other area of Salesforce specialization that the Nagarro practice has, which is very important for FDE deployment, lies with its 100 consultants that work in Data Management, with Nagarro holding 2,500+ Salesforce certifications. The company is also incentivising staff to gain AI and Agentforce certifications as part of the FDE partner certification program in Europe, US and India.

Nagarro has closely aligned its strategy with Salesforce and its AI and agentic solutions, creating its own agents.

Jain is particularly proud of the company’s Retail Assist solution which was the result of a hackathon in India in which 400 teams took part. This provides intelligent visit planning, store insights, shelf compliance analysis to make sure the right products are on the right shelves. As a hands-free, voice assisted agent it provides real-time recommendations.

Aside from creating its own Agentforce solutions, Nagarro works alongside clients to help them develop their solutions. For example, with a manufacturing company Nagarro deployed Agentforce to enable 30% faster response times to dealer queries about product information, setup and installations.

It has implemented Agentforce with a global financial firm, saving 200 man-days per month on support requests by auto-classifying emails and preparing pre-drafted responses for human review, validating loan details and payments across systems.

It has also helped a fitness company use Agentforce to enable up to 10% higher member conversions by providing a web concierge to greet prospects with context aware guidance to clubs and amenities, and a member-centric concierge in the mobile app that resolves account, membership and facility queries.

Nagarro Fluidic Intelligence nurtures its FDE capability

Jain explains to me that Nagarro could see that with AI projects customers are looking for people who are more than just developers or project managers and this, of course, is where the Forward Deployed Engineers come into play. I asked Jain how Nagarro addresses this requirement:

Nagarro has developed its Fluidic Intelligence approach which provides organisations with ways to move from a state of friction to a state of flow. The approach has several different components. There is Fluidic Advisory which is a broad AI capability that works alongside domain-specific consultants. There is Fluidic Forge, where artifacts and frameworks created by different teams for different projects are stored to stop different business units building everything from scratch. And then we also have Fluidic Teams which are AI-native developers who apply AI technology to solve business problems.

This Fluidic Intelligence approach develops multiple skills in one particular person – a forward deployed engineer. Although Nagarro does provide FDEs beyond the Salesforce practice, it has created special teams for Salesforce FDE deployment. Salesforce is investing in some of these teams to help them run workshops. As Data Cloud certification is a pre-requisite to taking the workshops, Salesforce provides some investment for this.

I asked Jain why he thinks customers are getting stuck with their AI implementations. His response:

The problem with AI is that the technology was greeted with so much enthusiasm that pilots were not calculated to be taken to production. The upfront investments were made and the pilots were often successful but there was never any long-term perspective as to what was being enabled. Implementation partners are in a better position to advise customers as to what makes sense, because you need to look at sorting out the business friction. Fix those problems first.

Jain says that customers are at different stages of their journeys:

Some customers are not even aware of AI, so we do pilots with them, showing our existing agentic solutions to demonstrate the art of the possible. A few customers are further ahead and may have created agents but then not been successful, so we look at the state of the agents and what has been implemented, using our AI Maturity Framework. If clients are at stage one or two then it is not a good idea to try to jump straight to stage four. In this way we are a sort of sparring partner for clients and enable them to do stuff for themselves, taking a train the trainer approach to AI implementation.

He concludes:

The FDE partners basically do a lot of handholding. With AIforce, that has just been announced, the Nagarro architectural team will create proofs of concept and then we will go to customers and explain the new features. Most of our customers have been with us for many years and see this as part of our role in the relationship.

My take

Jain is very complimentary about the FDE partner enablement sessions that Salesforce has been running. He hopes that sessions like this are currently being set up to support partners who will be asked by their clients to help them apply the new slew of new Dreamforce announcements within their different business contexts. I sincerely hope so too as, pardon my metaphor, but the Salesforce swan can only glide through agentic waters because its partners are paddling like crazy beneath the water line.
