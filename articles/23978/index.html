Staff Forward Deployed Engineer, Google Cloud Consulting (Multiple Languages) at Google — FDE Job Description

How to Become an FDE — Interview Kickstart · 2026-07-27 · 1 min read

Summary

This article examines a Google job posting for a Staff Forward Deployed Engineer in Google Cloud Consulting, based in Dublin, Ireland. The senior IC role requires 8+ years of experience; pay is not disclosed. Unlike advisory positions, the FDE works embedded at customer sites, writing code, debugging, and co-shipping bespoke agentic AI solutions. The role focuses on clearing production blockers such as integration complexity, data readiness, and state management that keep AI from reaching enterprise-grade maturity. The piece also lists other Google FDE openings in Singapore, India, the US, and the Nordics, and cautions that one posting is only a single data point for assessing fit.

Key points

  • Google is hiring a Staff FDE in Dublin for Google Cloud Consulting, requiring 8+ years of experience.
  • The role is an embedded builder, not an advisor: coding, debugging, and shipping agentic solutions on-site.
  • Key focus is solving production blockers like integration, data readiness, and state management.
  • Other Google FDE roles are open in Singapore, Mumbai, New York, and the Nordics.
  • The author advises treating one posting as one data point and consulting broader market analysis.

From the posting

As a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) in the Google Cloud Consulting organization, you will be an embedded builder who bridges the gap between frontier AI products and production-grade reality within customers. Unlike traditional advisory roles, you will function as an "innovator-builder," moving beyond high-level architecture to code, debug, and jointly ship bespoke agentic solutions directly within the customer’s environment. You will address blockers to production including solving the integration complexities, data readiness issues, and state-management issues that prevent AI from reaching enterprise-grade maturity. …
