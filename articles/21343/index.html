Hitachi and Google Cloud Expand Strategic Alliance in Physical AI Social Implementation via FDE and in the Security Domain

Original title: 日立とGoogle Cloud、FDEによるフィジカルAIの社会実装とセキュリティ領域での戦略的アライアンスを拡大

Hitachi Global · 2026-06-06 · 10 min read

Summary

Hitachi is expanding its strategic alliance with Google Cloud, first formed in May 2024, to accelerate real-world deployment of physical AI and strengthen cybersecurity. The partnership centers on a Forward Deployed Engineer model that combines Hitachi's Lumada-based co-creation approach and GlobalLogic's engineering talent with Google Cloud's AI. The companies will enhance Hitachi's HMAX platform using Gemini Enterprise and multimodal Gemini models to automate complex frontline operations in manufacturing and infrastructure, while turning FDE domain knowledge into reusable assets. On security, citing Mandiant M-Trends 2026 findings that AI shortens attack cycles, they will combine Wiz, Mandiant, and Google SecOps with Hitachi's mission-critical SI expertise. A customer-zero approach, validated at Hitachi Power Solutions, underpins the rollout.

Key points

  • Hitachi expands its 2024 Google Cloud alliance around an FDE model.
  • HMAX will be enhanced with Gemini Enterprise and Gemini models.
  • FDE domain knowledge will be assetized for scalable customer reuse.
  • Security offering combines Wiz, Mandiant, Google SecOps with Hitachi SI expertise.
  • Customer-zero validation was done at Hitachi Power Solutions.

Full text

June 9, 2026

  • By combining the co-creation approach and digital engineering capabilities cultivated through Lumada across the Hitachi Group with Google Cloud's advanced AI, Hitachi will strengthen the capabilities of its FDEs and accelerate the creation of value through physical AI globally.
  • Through the strengthening of FDEs, Hitachi will advance HMAX while leveraging Gemini Enterprise, supporting frontline workers by autonomizing operations in complex environments. By horizontally deploying FDE knowledge, Hitachi will deliver value to a broader range of customers.
  • The two companies will expand their collaboration and provide solutions that support customers in strengthening their security operations against the threat of AI-driven cyberattacks.

Hitachi, Ltd. (hereinafter, "Hitachi") today announced the expansion of its strategic alliance*1 with Google Cloud to accelerate the social implementation of physical AI and to deploy cybersecurity solutions against the threat of AI-driven cyberattacks, thereby supporting customers in solving their challenges. Physical AI, which is currently attracting significant attention, is a technology that connects AI analysis and judgments derived from field data to concrete actions such as the autonomous control and operation of various machines and equipment. By repeating this cycle, AI continuously learns, enabling optimal judgment and execution suited to the situation. Through the social implementation of physical AI, Hitachi is working on the transformation of social infrastructure.

To realize this important mission of Hitachi, the company will leverage the strengths in IT, OT, and products cultivated through Lumada, combining its co-creation approach to solving customer challenges with Google Cloud's advanced AI to establish a Forward Deployed Engineers (hereinafter, "FDE") model and deploy it globally.

FDEs are a group of specialists who work directly at customers' business sites, handling everything end-to-end from identifying management challenges to early value validation through PoCs and agile implementation into actual operations. Hitachi has a long history of "field-oriented engineering," staying close to customers' sites and accompanying them from problem discovery through resolution, which is highly compatible with the FDE model. By fusing this Hitachi DNA with Google Cloud's advanced capabilities, Hitachi's consultants and AI Transformation (AX) experts with deep expertise across a wide range of industries, together with AI-native software engineers from GlobalLogic, Hitachi's U.S. subsidiary, will collaborate with Google Cloud's top engineers to provide powerful support to customers.

The two companies will work closely with Hitachi's "Frontier AI Deployment Center" to advance "HMAX by Hitachi" (hereinafter, "HMAX"), a suite of next-generation solutions that uses AI to innovate social infrastructure, through Gemini Enterprise, an agent platform that can leverage Google's high-performance AI technologies. Centered on this enhanced HMAX, the two companies will drive the resolution of frontline workers' challenges through the autonomization of complex operations. Furthermore, through a data platform that makes the domain knowledge gained from FDE activities reusable as assets, enabling scalable deployment, they will deliver value to a broad range of customers.

In the field of physical AI, where AI connects directly with the real world, even more advanced security is essential. AI is also transforming the cybersecurity landscape: attackers are actively leveraging AI models to accelerate vulnerability discovery at an unprecedented scale and to automate the generation of exploits (attack methods). In addition, according to "Mandiant M-Trends 2026"*2 published by Google Cloud, it has become clear that the evolution of AI technology has significantly shortened the cyberattack process. To address these risks, Hitachi's dedicated security organization, "Cyber CoE," will collaborate with Google Cloud, combining Google Security technologies such as Google AI Threat Defense*3 with the mission-critical SI expertise Hitachi has cultivated through social infrastructure to support customers' security operations.

Through these efforts, the two companies will accelerate the creation of value through physical AI safely and globally.

Since their partnership began in May 2024, the two companies have promoted advanced initiatives to create value through the use of AI. As part of this, based on a "Customer Zero" approach of first practicing thoroughly within Hitachi itself, Hitachi Power Solutions Co., Ltd., which handles maintenance businesses in the power and industrial sectors, conducted technical validation of maintenance and inspection work using Gemini Enterprise, confirming the feasibility of improving quality and efficiency*4. In this trial, Gemini Enterprise was proven to have high affinity with the OT domain, such as manufacturing and infrastructure. The two companies also shared the common understanding that field-oriented, accompanying support that unravels field constraints and agilely creates and implements value is effective for safely connecting advanced AI with field operational equipment, business systems, and the tacit knowledge of experienced workers.

Currently, at Hitachi, with a view to leveraging AI for company-wide business reform, the Digital Systems & Services sector, which leads AX, has taken the lead in introducing Gemini Enterprise, and by advancing numerous pilot projects in which frontline employees themselves use AI, Hitachi is strongly promoting the raising of AI literacy across the organization.

Building on these success stories across the Hitachi Group, the two companies are now expanding their strategic alliance to safely scale physical AI to customers' sites.

(1) Accelerating the implementation of physical AI through strengthened FDE capabilities

GlobalLogic's AI-native software engineers, along with Hitachi's consultants and AX experts, will collaborate with Google Cloud's top engineers to further develop and strengthen FDEs. To utilize continuously evolving AI technology in business and in the field, an agile approach that rapidly validates practicality and adapts to change is essential. Unlike traditional SI, which builds systems according to customers' individual requirements, the FDE model is characterized by a process of validating effectiveness in actual operations. This enables customers to visualize return on investment in advance and create business value through AI at overwhelming speed while minimizing risk.

(2) Promoting the resolution of field challenges through the advancement of HMAX

The two companies will embed and enhance HMAX with Gemini Enterprise's autonomous AI and multimodal Gemini models. Gemini Enterprise, which can simultaneously process and understand a wide variety of information such as on-site camera footage and vast amounts of sensor data, has an extremely high affinity with physical AI that autonomously operates while grasping real-world conditions. Leveraging these technical characteristics, the companies will also incorporate use cases such as maintenance inspection checks through image comparison using Gemini Enterprise, promoting the automation of complex operations in manufacturing and social infrastructure sites. To realize this, Hitachi's proprietary domain knowledge and Google Cloud's Agent Ready data utilization technologies will be incorporated into Hitachi's data platform. This will create scalable business outcomes that go beyond simply providing AI services, aiming to establish new business models in the infrastructure field.

(3) Next-generation cybersecurity against security threats in the AI era

To respond to evolving threats in cybersecurity, the two companies will provide autonomous next-generation security solutions to the market. This solution combines Google Cloud's security technologies, including Wiz, which delivers comprehensive visibility into cloud and AI risks and automatic risk reduction, and Mandiant, which possesses advanced expertise in cyber threats, with Hitachi's mission-critical SI experience that has supported social infrastructure such as railways, power, and finance, as well as its global OT knowledge. Its effectiveness has already been demonstrated through customer zero initiatives, such as the adoption of Google SecOps at Hitachi group companies worldwide, and the companies will continue working to expand proposals to customers going forward.

In addition, Hitachi will consolidate the practical AI implementation knowledge and technologies cultivated on the front lines into the "Frontier AI Deployment Center." By broadly channeling this cutting-edge knowledge back to systems engineers in Japan, the Frontier AI Deployment Center will strengthen FDE across the entire group. Through these efforts, Hitachi will accelerate the rollout of the FDE model and further accelerate innovation through the use of AI globally.

Combining Hitachi's deep domain knowledge, GlobalLogic's engineering capabilities, and Google Cloud's advanced AI and engineering resources is an innovative approach to accelerating real-world AI transformation. With Gemini Enterprise's powerful multimodal AI, we will deliver leading examples of AI innovation globally. Furthermore, by integrating Google Security's advanced solutions with Hitachi's expertise in mission-critical domains, we will support customers in driving innovation in a safe and trusted environment in the AI era.

Hitachi has been actively implementing and practicing Gemini Enterprise as a customer zero, experiencing significant benefits in operational efficiency and productivity improvement. The expansion of this strategic alliance is intended to build on the strengthening of FDE development, enabling both companies to take on the challenge of solving more difficult problems. By combining Google Cloud's world-leading AI implementation approaches cultivated on the front lines with Hitachi's customer co-creation approach and GlobalLogic's outstanding digital engineering capabilities, we aim to create the value of physical AI in customers' business operations. In addition, to deliver this value safely, Hitachi will link its mission-critical system building expertise with Google Security's advanced solutions to protect social infrastructure from threats in the AI era and lead the realization of a sustainable society.
