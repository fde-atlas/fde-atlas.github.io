Is the $1.45M FDE Salary Real? What 171 Job Postings and a Viral Survey Reveal About Typical Pay

Original title: FDE年収145万ドルは本当か|話題の調査と求人票171件で見る普通の水準

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-11-07 · 15 min read

Summary

This article fact-checks the widely reported claim that FDEs can earn $1.45 million a year. That figure is the top of an estimate range for "elite" FDEs by recruiting firm Christian & Timbers, covering people with 7–15 years of experience who led teams and delivered $10M+ results—not a market average. The editors analyzed 171 US-dollar job postings: median salary ranges were roughly $180k (lower) to $250k–300k (upper), with the highest cap at $445k, and equity rarely disclosed. The article cautions that the study comes from a firm with recruiting incentives and advises verifying equity, bonuses, and vesting when comparing offers.

Key points

  • The $1.45M figure is an estimate for elite FDEs, not an average.
  • Median job-posting ranges: ~$180k lower, $250k–300k upper bound.
  • 52–66% of elite pay is bonus and equity, mostly undisclosed in postings.
  • Nearly 80% of elite FDEs reportedly come from Palantir backgrounds.
  • Compare offers by checking equity valuation, vesting, and bonus targets.

Key Points of This Article

  • 01 The figure of $586,000–$1.45M for FDE salaries is an estimate of the top tier published by recruiting firm Christian & Timbers in November 2026, and is not the average for all FDEs.
  • 02 The top tier the firm describes consists of people with 7–15 years of experience, a track record of over $10M in measurable results, and experience leading FDE teams; in a separate August estimate, the firm put the number of proven professionals in the US at about 2,000.
  • 03 Among 171 USD-denominated job postings on November 7, 2026, the median of the compensation fields was roughly $180,000 at the lower bound and $250,000–$300,000 at the upper bound depending on how the data is aggregated, with the highest upper bound among the postings being $445,000. Equity is separate, and total compensation cannot be determined from job postings.
  • 04 Back-calculating from the firm's ratios, the portion of top-tier compensation excluding bonus and equity is roughly $280,000–$490,000; 66 postings had upper bounds reaching the lower end of the range, but not a single one of the 171 reached the upper end.
  • 05 Because many postings do not state equity amounts, when comparing offers you need to verify the equity valuation, vesting period, bonus target, and when shares can be sold.

FDE(Forward Deployed Engineer/フォワードデプロイドエンジニア)になれば、本当に年収が2億円を超えるのでしょうか。結論から言うと、それは一部の上位層についての推定で[1]、普通のFDEの水準ではありません。2026年11月7日に編集部が集計した米ドル建て(主に米国)の求人票171件では、報酬欄の中央値は下限が約18万ドル(約2,840万円)、上限が集計の仕方により25万〜30万ドル(約3,940万〜4,730万円)でした。最も高い上限でも44万5,000ドル(約7,020万円)です[6]。株式は別に付くことが多く、総報酬は求人票からは分かりません。換算は2026年11月6日の仲値によります[10]。

話題の発端は、人材紹介会社Christian & Timbersの調査と、それを取り上げたWall Street Journalの記事です[1][4]。この記事では、調査の発表文と公開されている概要、報道、求人票171件を比べ、145万ドルが誰の数字で、自分の報酬を考えるときにどう読めばよいかを整理します。FDEの年収の全体像はFDEの年収・給料を、会社別の求人票の比較はFDEの年収を求人票272件で比べるをご覧ください。

Where Does the Gap with Job Postings Come From?

差の理由は2つあります。対象者の違いと、求人票に金額が出にくい株式と賞与です[1]。同社によれば、エリートFDEの報酬の52〜66%が賞与と株式で、この比率はレンジの下端と上端にあたります[1]。52%を下端、66%を上端に対応させると、残りの部分は下端で約28万1,000ドル、上端で約49万3,000ドルです。これは原文の書き方に基づく編集部の逆算で、調査の全文にある基本給の数字は確認できていません。

How reliable is this survey?

上位層の報酬が高いこと自体は十分ありえますが、145万ドルは検証された統計ではなく、人材紹介会社の推定として読むのが妥当です。同社も、報酬のレンジは自社の推定で、統計上の平均ではないと書いています[1]。

  • 調査の材料: 2026年1〜6月の550件超の面談に、進行中の案件や成立したオファーを合わせたものです[1]。概要のページは180社のデータとしています[2]。回答者の選ばれ方は分かりません。
  • 発信者の立場: 同社はFDEの採用を支援する会社で、発表文は企業に社内でFDEを持つよう勧めています[1]。高い報酬と人材不足の数字は、同社の事業の追い風になりえます(編集部の見方)。
  • 偏りの可能性: 同社の社長は、上位層の80%近くがPalantir出身だと述べています[3]。特定の出身者に偏った層の数字で、市場全体には広げられません。
  • 批判の声: ニュースレターの筆者は、古くからの導入の仕事に特殊部隊のような名前を付け、希少性の物語が報酬を押し上げたと批判しています[5]。これは数字の検証ではなく、1つの意見です。

事業会社の採用が広がっているという報道も、職種名だけで判断しないほうが安全です。報道はTarget、Barclays、Bausch and Lomb、American ExpressをFDEの募集企業に挙げています[4]。Barclaysの公式の採用検索では2026年11月7日時点でFDE関連の2件(プネー、グラスゴー)が見つかりました[11]。転載サイトに残るVP職の求人は基本給17万〜23万ドルで、開発者向けの普及活動に近い内容でした。掲載が終わっている可能性があると転載サイトは表示しています[12]。同じ「FDE」でも、中身と報酬は大きく違います。

How should people working in Japan read these figures?

日本の求人の相場として使うことはできません。2026年11月7日の集計で、日本関連のFDE求人17件のうち報酬の金額を読み取れたのは、米国政府の業務を支える東京勤務のPalantirの2件だけで、エンジニア職は13万5,000〜20万ドルです[9]。今回の数字は米国の、しかも上位層の推定です。

What should you check in an offer?

株式を含む提示を比べるときは、総額ではなく中身に分けて確かめます。同社が調べた公開求人10件には、株式の金額を書いたものが1件もありませんでした[2]。求人票だけでは比較できないので、次の点は選考の中で確かめる必要があります。

  • 01 What is the base salary? Does the offered amount include incentives?
  • 02 What is the bonus target percentage, and how much has actually been paid in the past?
  • 03 What are the type and valuation of the equity based on? If the company is private, when might shares become sellable?
  • 04 What is the vesting period, and how is the annualized value calculated?
  • 05 Does compensation change based on work location or travel requirements?

Summary: What to take away from the $1.45 million headline

145万ドルは上位層についての推定で[1]、普通のFDEの年収ではありません。差には対象者の違いと、求人票に金額が出にくい株式と賞与が関わります[1]。普通のFDEの総報酬は、公開情報からは確かめられません。

Frequently Asked Questions

Q Do FDEs really earn over 200 million yen a year?

Christian & Timbers estimates total compensation for the top tier, those with 7-15 years of experience who have led teams, at $586,000-$1.45 million (approximately 92.5-229 million yen). This is an estimate including equity and bonuses, and does not reflect the level of an average FDE.

Q How much does an average FDE earn?

Among 171 job postings in US dollars compiled by the editorial team on November 7, 2026, the median of the compensation ranges was approximately $180,000 at the lower end and $250,000-$300,000 at the upper end, depending on how the figures were aggregated. Since compensation ranges typically do not include equity, total compensation may be higher, but this cannot be verified from public information. Levels in Japan need to be assessed separately.

Q What is an elite FDE?

By Christian & Timbers' definition, an elite FDE is someone with roughly 7-15 years of experience, at least 2 years of experience running agentic AI in production, documentable results of $10 million or more, and experience founding and leading an FDE team. As a rough headcount, the company estimated in a separate August analysis that about 2,000 people in the US have such track records.

Q Why do job posting figures differ from media-reported figures?

Because the media figures are estimates of total compensation for the top tier, adding bonuses and annualized equity to base salary, while most job postings only show salary ranges. The subjects also differ: media reports cover senior people with team leadership experience, while job postings include general roles as well.

Q Can the figures in this study be trusted?

The company itself states that the compensation ranges are estimates, not statistical averages. The company is a recruiting firm that supports FDE hiring, so it is also in a position where these figures could benefit its business. Use them only as a directional reference, and rely on job postings and actual offers for individual decisions.
