Shanghai Enterprise Technology Center FDE General Education Class (Session 1) Opens, with 5 Courses Delivered on the First Day in Xuhui West Bund 2026-09-24 Source: Municipal Commission of Economy and Informatization

On September 22, the "Shanghai Enterprise Technology Center FDE General Education Class (Session 1)", jointly organized by the Shanghai Industrial Technology Innovation Promotion Association and the Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Industry Association, officially opened. Pan Yan, Deputy Director of the Municipal Commission of Economy and Informatization, attended and delivered opening remarks. Representatives from more than 30 national-level and municipal-level enterprise technology centers in the city participated in this training.

Pan Yan pointed out that to deeply implement the national "AI+" initiative and vigorously grow the FDE talent pool, Shanghai is advancing the "Modeling Shanghai" project in depth, accelerating the landing and transformation of large model innovations in key industries. After more than thirty years of dedicated development, Shanghai has built a three-tier enterprise technology center innovation system covering the national, municipal, and district levels, which has become the "pillar" of industrial technology innovation and the "converter" for the industrialization of scientific and technological achievements. Through the FDE general education training class, the bridging role of technology centers connecting R&D and production should be leveraged to cultivate in-house AI backbone talent for enterprises who "understand business, know models, are aware of security, and can deliver", while precisely connecting enterprises with AI application service providers who understand industries and excel at delivery, embedding AI capabilities into the "core competencies" that enterprises have accumulated over the long term, so that dormant data and experience can truly be transformed into intelligent productivity. He expressed the hope that all enterprise technology centers would accurately grasp development trends and deeply recognize the urgency and strategic importance of FDE talent cultivation; accelerate AI-driven empowerment to forge enterprises' innovation advantages into winning momentum for intelligent transformation; and adhere to combining learning with application to effectively ensure that intelligent transformation takes root.

In the on-site practical teaching session, all trainees first visited the CEC GienTech Innovation Experience Center under China Electronics Corporation. Combined with the Forward Deployed Engineer methodology, the session explained how CEC GienTech relies on products such as the CEC Yuanyuan AI-native software factory and the agent factory to go deep into the business frontline, transforming AI technology into actionable industry solutions, helping trainees intuitively experience the practical application of the FDE concept in enterprise-level AI implementation and building a concrete understanding for subsequent course learning.

The general education course in the morning was jointly taught by Sun Chongyan, Assistant Professor at the Shanghai International CTO School and Asia Europe Business School of East China Normal University and Mingyuan Chenhui Scholar, and AI practical teaching instructor Li Yi, focusing on "Generative AI Applications and Introduction to FDE: From AI Capabilities to Real Business Problems". By breaking down key stages such as business diagnosis, system evaluation, and production deployment, the course guided trainees to break out of the habitual "technology-first" mindset and establish an AI implementation cognitive framework of "identify the right problem—validate in small steps—deploy to production", enabling a more systematic understanding of the complete path from AI capabilities to business applications and from prototype validation to actual value creation, and providing practical insights on how to make AI truly enter the business and continuously create value.

In the first professional course in the afternoon, Zhang Wei, Deputy General Manager of the Data Intelligence Department of Shanghai Telecom's Cloud Middle Platform, gave a special presentation on the theme "Transformation of Production Methods in the AI Era and Upgrading of Frontline Combat Capabilities", focusing on the building of the Telecom Smart Engineer (FDE) team. He interpreted the role positioning and team structure of Smart Engineers, introduced a four-pool collaborative construction system covering talent, projects, capabilities, and tools, and proposed implementation methodologies such as capability front-loading and on-site validation, providing practical reference for trainees from enterprise technology centers in building AI talent teams and bridging the "last mile" of implementation.

The second professional course was delivered by Yu Jiayao, Head of AI Solutions at the CEC GienTech Research Institute, on "The Path to Enterprise Large Model Implementation and Application Practices in Typical Scenarios". Starting from AI development trends, the course deeply dissected the core challenges in enterprises' large model implementation and proposed the "three carriages" methodology of the agent factory, knowledge engineering, and the AI intelligent foundation. Combined with rich business cases and typical scenarios such as intelligent writing, forgery detection, and marketing, the course helped trainees master the complete path from cognition to implementation and understand the underlying logic of how agents, knowledge engineering, and the AI foundation jointly empower business, providing practical reference for trainees to advance enterprise large model implementation in business scenarios and build enterprise AI capability systems.

The third professional course was taught by Wei Shicheng, Founder and CEO of Shanghai Tawai Intelligence and enterprise AI application implementation consultant, on "From Scenarios to Value: Practical Paths and Case Studies of Enterprise-Level AI Implementation". Drawing on real cases accumulated by Shanghai Tawai Intelligence in its enterprise services, the course focused on application methods of AI in scenarios such as manufacturing, R&D, operations, and organizational management, and provided systematic analysis from the perspectives of AI-native organization building, enterprise knowledge and context management, human-machine collaboration, and agent-based ways of working. Through case explanations and sharing of practical experience, the course helped trainees further understand the implementation path of AI moving from "tool application" to "business implementation" and "organizational transformation", enhancing enterprises' AI application and innovation capabilities.

This Shanghai Enterprise Technology Center FDE General Education Class (Session 1) adhered to learning for practical use and the unity of knowledge and action. Through general and professional courses, combined with diverse training formats such as on-site enterprise teaching and group co-creation, it expanded practical scenarios and enriched learning dimensions, aiming to cultivate FDE backbone talent for enterprise technology centers at all levels across the city. Going forward, the Municipal Commission of Economy and Informatization will regularly conduct a series of trainings for the city's enterprise technology centers, fully leveraging their advantages in technology aggregation and achievement transformation, driving learning through real business scenario problems, promoting the deep integration of AI and industrial innovation, and helping Shanghai build an AI hub with global influence.
