On June 30, 2026, The Logic reported that Canadian employers are building forward deployed teams, and that job postings use modified wording because Canada restricts the title “engineer”. The article, by Anita Balakrishnan, says Manik Sharma, chief of agentic solutions at Kinaxis in Ottawa, is preparing the company to build its own teams. It says these engineers are often deployed in “pods”.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: The Logic (reported).

What happened

The Logic reported that Manik Sharma first met the model at Palantir. Daniel Hoadley, senior product director at Clio in B.C., describes the ideal hire as a “unicorn”. Annie Veillet, who leads PwC Canada’s AI offerings, is also quoted.

Krish Banerjee, managing director of Accenture Canada’s AI team, compares traditional consultants to contractors who measure a home and return with a design. The article says the engineers are often deployed in pods with product managers, consultants or subject-matter experts.

The Logic reports OpenAI’s deployment company as US$4 billion, backed by Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, SoftBank, McKinsey and 15 other partners. It says Brookfield’s private-equity arm plans to send OpenAI engineers into its portfolio companies. The page is subscriber-gated.

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

If you are looking in Canada, this suggests searching beyond the word “engineer”, since postings use modified wording. The report also describes pod working alongside product managers, consultants or subject-matter experts.

If you are hiring them

For employers in Canada, the report points to two practical matters: the job title has to be adapted, and teams are often built as pods that mix engineers with product managers and domain experts.
