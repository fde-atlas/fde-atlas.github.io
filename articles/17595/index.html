2026 FDE Salaries: Salary Ranges from Real Job Postings

Original title: 2026 年 FDE 薪资：来自真实职位的薪资范围

FDE Times · 2026-11-15 · 2 min read

Summary

FDE Times compiled base salary ranges from 168 US forward deployed engineer postings published by 60 companies on official job boards. The median midpoint is $200k, with the common published range spanning $154k–$236k and the middle half of postings at $167k–$225k. Senior/Staff/Lead roles center around $216k versus $140k for entry level. New York has the most postings (67), while Washington DC pays noticeably less ($168k midpoint). Figures cover base salary only—equity and bonuses are excluded—and Palantir and Databricks together account for 29% of postings, skewing aggregate numbers.

Key points

  • Based on 168 US postings with disclosed salary ranges from 60 companies.
  • Median base salary midpoint is $200k; middle half falls between $167k and $225k.
  • Senior/Staff/Lead roles median $216k midpoint; entry level $140k.
  • Washington DC postings pay lowest at a $168k midpoint; New York has 67 postings.
  • Palantir (31) and Databricks (18) postings make up 29% of the dataset.

What these numbers are

Base salary only, in US dollars, as published by employers. Excludes equity, bonuses, and benefits; at AI companies, equity can add a substantial amount on top of these figures.

US postings only (228 of 475 open US FDE postings published salary ranges; laws in California, New York, Washington, Colorado, and other states require publication). Few postings outside the US publish salary ranges, so we do not estimate them.

The salary range a posting publishes is the span a company is willing to pay for that role. Where a final offer lands within the range depends on level, location, and negotiation.

A large share of salary ranges comes from two employers: Palantir (31) and Databricks (18), which together account for 29%. As a result, the overall figures and per-city figures are skewed toward these two companies' pay levels; the by-company list shows each employer separately.

Methodology

Every open Forward Deployed Engineer position on the official job boards we track (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workday, plus the official career sites of Amazon, Microsoft, ByteDance, and Tencent) is read multiple times each day. For each US position, we take the first annual base salary range and exclude figures that are not annual salaries. Level is inferred from the job title. When the same company posts multiple positions with identical titles and salary ranges across different cities, it is counted only once (per-city figures still use all postings). When a posting changes, the page is automatically regenerated.
