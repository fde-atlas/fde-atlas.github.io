In-house FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)

Original title: 社内FDE（Forward Deployed Engineer）

株式会社Stract · 2026-08-19 · 7 min read

Summary

STRACT, a 19-person Japanese startup behind the EC-focused AI agent DONDO (formerly the shopping app PLUG, with 3 million downloads and over ¥10 billion in annual transaction flow), is hiring an in-house FDE. Unlike product-focused engineers, this role embeds directly in business and corporate teams, learns their workflows hands-on, and builds AI-powered automation using LLM APIs, agents, and no-code tools. The goal is to absorb routine operations, freeing staff for more aggressive client-facing proposals. Salary is ¥10–15 million, full-time, based in Tokyo with mostly in-office work.

Key points

  • STRACT created an in-house FDE role for business and corporate departments.
  • The role embeds in daily operations, then designs and builds AI-driven automation.
  • Tech stack includes LLM APIs, AI agents, RPA, and no-code tools like Zapier and n8n.
  • Salary ranges from ¥10M to ¥15M; the position is office-based in Tokyo.
  • Freed-up staff time is reinvested into bolder customer proposals.

STRACT, Inc.

Description

[Business Overview] STRACT provides DONDO, a personal AI agent specialized for e-commerce. We previously operated PLUG, an AI shopping app. By partnering with and aggregating over 2,200 e-commerce sites in Japan, it enabled automatic lowest-price comparison, automatic coupon and cashback offers, and more, growing into a product that lets anyone easily enjoy a "safe and smart online shopping experience." Starting from the unique technology of a Safari extension for iPhone, rare even globally, the business expanded to 3 million cumulative downloads (as of April 2026) and over 10 billion yen in annual transaction volume. Then, in November 2026, PLUG was renewed as DONDO, a personal AI agent specialized for e-commerce. We are expanding the product and business with the goal of establishing an AI x commerce platform covering the entire purchase funnel, from search and recommendation to purchase and payment. Pitch video: https://youtu.be/OZ4qlcfH9tM Service site: https://dondoapp.jp/ [Why We Are Hiring] In proportion to the speed of business growth, the volume and complexity of work handled by the business and corporate departments are increasing. Until now, much of this has been covered by the ingenuity and individual efforts of existing members, but to scale further, it is essential to have engineers embed themselves in frontline business processes and improve and streamline operations using AI. Therefore, we have decided to create a new position, "In-house FDE," where you will actually work within the business and corporate departments, deepen your understanding of the work by working hands-on alongside existing members, and develop improvement and automation mechanisms based on the insights gained there. Unlike the product development team's role of "how to evolve the product with AI," the distinctive feature of the In-house FDE is its focus on "embedding into the work right in front of you and reliably driving business growth with the power of AI." There is also a deeper aim behind creating this position. Currently, the business department spends much of its time on daily operations, and there is a challenge that proposals to customers tend to remain conservative to minimize risk. To further accelerate business growth, it is essential to use AI to take over operational tasks that existing members cannot fully handle, and to create a state where the time freed up can be reinvested in more substantive proposal activities. At the same time, we also expect the In-house FDE to engage in communication with customers as needed, supporting proposals with technical backing from a position deeply familiar with frontline operations, thereby building an environment where more aggressive proposals directly linked to business growth can be made with confidence. [Specific Responsibilities] - Work alongside members of the business department (teams facing toB clients) and corporate department to deeply understand workflows, issues, and bottlenecks - Based on input from the frontline, design and build mechanisms for operational improvement and efficiency (tools, automation, internal systems, etc.) - Use generative AI, LLMs, etc. to plan, implement, and operate automation and labor-saving of routine tasks and information processing end-to-end - Stay close to the frontline even after release, running improvement cycles based on actual usage and reactions (not just "build and done") - Systematize and document the knowledge gained through development, avoiding personal dependency, and share it with other members - Collaborate continuously with the product development team and feed back generalizable elements to the product side - Use AI to take over and reduce the operational workload of business and corporate department members, creating a state where the time saved can be reinvested in proposal activities more directly tied to business growth - Engage in communication with customers as needed, supporting proposal design technically from a position deeply familiar with frontline operations, building an environment where the business department can confidently make more aggressive proposals [Examples of Technical Challenges] - Balancing business understanding and implementation: Break down the tacit knowledge and manual workflows of non-engineer frontline members, articulate them as requirements, and translate them into working solutions - Speed-focused development: Iterate and improve quickly while gathering frontline feedback - Implementation skill in AI utilization: Use LLM APIs, AI agents, no-code/low-code tools, etc. appropriately to achieve business improvement by the shortest path

Requirements

- Experience developing web applications or business systems (language and framework not specified) - Business understanding and communication skills to interview frontline members, break down and organize their work, and translate it into development requirements - Proactiveness to design solutions from the business challenges themselves, rather than just "building what you're told" - Strong interest in, or implementation experience with, product development and business efficiency using generative AI and LLMs

Preferred Experiences

- Development experience using generative AI / LLM APIs (RAG construction, AI agent development, etc.) - Experience implementing business automation using RPA or no-code/low-code tools (GAS, Zapier, n8n, etc.) - Experience driving business improvement or DX projects at a company - Requirements definition experience at a consulting firm, SIer/SES, etc. - Understanding of business department domains such as toB business, sales operations, and customer success - Experience negotiating and proposing to customers (client-facing experience in BtoB sales, customer success, consulting, etc.) - Understanding of corporate department (back office, HR) business domains

We are looking for

- Empathy with STRACT's mission, vision, and values - A proactive attitude of embedding into the frontline and persistently seeking to understand the work - Someone who avoids perfectionism, quickly builds something that works first, and refines it based on frontline feedback - Communication skills to build trust with business and corporate department members and draw out candid information - A willingness to engage in communication with customers without hesitation when necessary, not just internally - A growth-oriented mindset of supporting "offensive" rather than "defensive" proposals - Someone who avoids personal dependency and is mindful of systematization and knowledge sharing - Someone sensitive to new technology trends (generative AI and LLM related) who learns on their own and applies it in practice - Someone positive about change and motivated to contribute to organizational and team growth
