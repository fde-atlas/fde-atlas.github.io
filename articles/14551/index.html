Cohere Forward Deployed Engineer: what the postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-11-01 · 10 min read

Summary

An FDE Dispatch snapshot of Cohere's public Ashby board (October 24, 2026) counts 11 open FDE-family postings: 9 engineer seats across three tracks (Agentic Platform, Infrastructure Specialist, Sovereign AI) and 2 Engineering Manager, FDE seats. All 9 engineer postings mention AI agents and customer-facing work, well above the 41.0% and 74.1% rates across 563 engineer postings in the requirements index; 6 mention Python and RAG, 3 Kubernetes or Docker. No posting is US-only; 8 are outside the US and 6 of 11 list remote. Every engineer seat states 20-40% travel, above the 30% all-employer median. Six postings list pay, all in CAD or KRW, none in USD; engineer ranges reach CAD 385,000. The author cautions the sample is small.

Key points

  • Cohere had 11 FDE-family postings: 9 engineer seats in three tracks plus 2 Engineering Manager roles.
  • All 9 engineer postings mention AI agents and customer-facing work, far above index averages.
  • No posting is US-only; 8 of 11 are outside the US and 6 list remote.
  • All engineer seats state 20-40% travel, above the 30% all-employer median.
  • Posted pay is only CAD or KRW, with engineer ranges up to CAD 385,000.

Published

Cohere posts Forward Deployed Engineer seats under Agentic Platform, Infrastructure Specialist and Sovereign AI titles, next to Engineering Manager, FDE seats. In FDE Dispatch's October 24, 2026 snapshot of public job boards it has 11 open, tied with Cloudflare and Scale AI for ninth of 121 employers. 9 are engineer titles: 5 Agentic Platform (United States-named/Canada mixed, Middle East, Europe, Korea, UK Public Sector), 3 Infrastructure Specialist (Middle East, Ottawa Public Sector, UK Public Sector) and 1 Sovereign AI in Ottawa. The other 2 are Engineering Manager, FDE titles (Agentic Platform; Infrastructure NORAM). None are US-only; 8 are outside the US and 3 are mixed US/Canada. 6 list remote (workplace Remote); 5 are Hybrid. All 9 engineer seats state 20-40% travel. 6 of 11 list pay in the Ashby field (5 CAD, 1 KRW); none list USD. 6 of 11 mention equity. Eleven postings is a small sample. FDE Dispatch is not affiliated with Cohere; the figures come from Cohere's public job postings.

What Cohere calls the role

Cohere posts under four title tracks in this snapshot: Forward Deployed Engineer, Agentic Platform; Forward Deployed Engineer, Infrastructure Specialist; Forward Deployed Engineer, Sovereign AI; and Engineering Manager, FDE. Public-sector wording appears in the UK Public Sector Agentic and Infrastructure titles and in the Ottawa Infrastructure Specialist (Public Sector) and Sovereign AI seats. See Forward Deployed Engineering manager for how management titles compare across employers.

Agentic Platform texts describe designing, building and deploying agentic workflows with customers. Infrastructure Specialist texts describe private-cloud and on-premises deployment of North, with Kubernetes and cloud infrastructure. Sovereign AI sits in Ottawa with government-customer proximity language.

Where the roles are, and how many are remote

None of the 11 postings are US-only on the published locations index. 8 (72.7%) are outside the US, against 40.1% across all 725 postings: Europe, Korea, the Middle East, the United Kingdom and Ottawa. 3 (27.3%) are mixed US and Canada: both Engineering Manager seats and the United States-named Agentic Platform seat (which also lists Canadian cities).

6 of the 11 list remote (54.5%), against 27.4% across the snapshot. Workplace is Remote on those 6 and Hybrid on the other 5 (Middle East Agentic and Infrastructure, Europe Agentic, Ottawa Infrastructure Public Sector, Sovereign AI).

Metro and remote figures for every employer are on Forward Deployed Engineer jobs by location.

Cohere's 11 FDE-family postings split across Agentic Platform, Infrastructure Specialist and Sovereign AI tracks, with public-sector seats in the UK and Ottawa plus seats in Korea, the Middle East and Europe. FDE Dispatch trains engineers for the customer-deployment rounds those hiring loops use.

How much Cohere Forward Deployed Engineers travel

All 11 postings mention travel. 9 of 11 state a percentage: every engineer-title seat says 20-40% (upper bound 40). Both Engineering Manager seats mention travel without a percentage. The median stated upper bound among those 9 is 40%, against the 30% all-employer median on how much forward deployed engineers travel.

What Cohere posts for pay

6 of the 11 postings list a pay range in the Ashby pay field: 5 in CAD and 1 in KRW. None list USD. The United States-named Agentic Platform seat is mixed US/Canada on the locations index and CAD in the ATS field; the posting body does not state a salary range or currency (only stipend amounts in $ / £). Posted ranges are not offers and exclude equity value. Non-USD pay is not compared to the USD engineer median of $216,104 across 255 US engineer-title postings on Forward Deployed Engineer salary. What this means if you are preparing for a Cohere loop

  • Track. Agentic Platform seats center on LLM-powered agent workflows with customers. Infrastructure Specialist seats center on private-cloud and on-premises deployment with Kubernetes. Sovereign AI is the Ottawa government-proximity seat. Read the track in the title before you decide which seat fits.
  • Public sector and geography. UK Public Sector and Ottawa Public Sector titles name the sector. Seats also sit in Korea, the Middle East and Europe. Have a worked example of shipping with a public-sector or regulated customer when those titles apply.
  • Travel and workplace. Every engineer seat states 20-40% travel. 6 of 11 list remote; the other 5 are Hybrid.

Round formats for FDE loops are on the FDE interview loop, explained, and practice prompts on FDE interview practice questions.

Method and data

Population: the 11 Cohere rows with in_page_stats = true among the 725 FDE-family postings from 121 employers counted on the salary, job titles, locations and travel pages. Snapshot pulled October 24, 2026 from Cohere's public Ashby board. Requirements come from the October 25, 2026 requirements index, which re-fetched the posting text; the 9 engineer-title Cohere postings are in it. The 2 Engineering Manager texts were absent from that re-fetch.

Track: title text (Agentic Platform, Infrastructure Specialist, Sovereign AI, Engineering Manager FDE). US-located, mixed and remote-listed use the published locations-index definitions: a posting is US-located when every listed location is in the US, mixed when US and non-US locations are listed together, and remote-listed when the board marks it remote or a listed location contains Remote. Travel upper bound = largest number in the stated percentage string, the rule on the travel page. Pay: report the ATS currency as stated; never convert; never compare non-USD pay to the USD engineer median. These 11 listings were live on the October 24, 2026 snapshot; counts are open postings on one day and do not measure headcount. Eleven postings is a small sample.

Does Cohere hire Forward Deployed Engineers?

Yes. Eleven postings is a small sample.

How much does a Cohere Forward Deployed Engineer make?

6 of the 11 Cohere postings list a pay range in the Ashby pay field: 5 in CAD and 1 in KRW. None list USD. Engineer CAD ranges include CAD 175,000 to 385,000 (Agentic Platform United States-named seat and Sovereign AI), CAD 215,000 to 385,000 (Infrastructure Specialist, Public Sector in Ottawa), and both Engineering Manager seats at CAD 320,000 to 385,000. The Korea Agentic Platform seat lists KRW 230,000,000 to 315,000,000. Posted ranges are not offers and exclude equity value. Non-USD pay is not compared to the USD engineer median of $216,104 across 255 US engineer-title postings.

What skills do Cohere Forward Deployed Engineer postings ask for?

In the requirements index (posting text re-fetched October 25, 2026; 9 Cohere engineer-title rows), all 9 mention AI agents or agentic systems and customer-facing work, against 41.0% and 74.1% of 563 engineer-title FDE postings. 6 mention Python and RAG or retrieval, 5 mention APIs, 3 mention Kubernetes or Docker, and 1 mentions TypeScript or JavaScript. The llm_genai, evals and cloud keyword flags undercount relative to the posting texts, so those flags are not used as Cohere comparisons. None of the 9 state a years-of-experience figure. Are Cohere Forward Deployed Engineer jobs remote?

Where are Cohere Forward Deployed Engineer jobs located?
