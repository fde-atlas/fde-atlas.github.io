FDE India · 2026-10-29 · 9 min read

FDE listings in India ticked up on Glassdoor this week, to 522, but fresh postings on employer career pages slowed to a trickle. The most useful signal for job seekers is at the junior end: two new fresher FDE roles pay ₹4–13 LPA, well below the ₹18–25 LPA entry-level figure often quoted for the role.

This Pulse follows Tuesday’s FDE Jobs in India: Infosys and TCS Build FDE Teams. There was little big FDE news in the past two days, so we go deeper on one question many readers ask: what does an entry-level FDE job in India actually pay?

Neurapath flagged only 2 new roles this week, after 7 in the previous week and 11 the week before. Its count has now fallen in each of our last two Pulses, from 139 to 126. Glassdoor’s broad search, which pulls in related titles, rose instead.

Our reading: the market is not shrinking, but the flow of new FDE-titled postings on career pages has slowed. That fits the longer trend in Indeed data, reported by People Matters on 15 October: FDE postings in India were about 19 times higher year on year in June 2026, but only more than 6 times higher in August. Naukri and LinkedIn India totals are still not publicly reported in pages we could access.

Fresher FDE jobs in India: what they really pay

Two fresher-tagged FDE roles appeared on JobFound in the past week:

  • Neurasity, Bengaluru (posted 25 October) — a startup building autonomous AI workers. Pay is listed at ₹4–6 LPA base plus deployment bonuses. The work is wiring AI agents and APIs into client systems, turning one-off code into a reusable stack, and keeping live client workflows running. The stack is Python, TypeScript, Next.js, AWS and Claude. Applicants are asked to send a link to a live system they have built.
  • Peakflo, remote India (posted 23 October) — a Y Combinator-backed agentic AI company for finance teams. Pay is listed at ₹10–13 LPA for 6 months to 2 years of experience in ML, NLP and LLM work. Tasks include voice prompt flows with telephony, LLM evaluation loops that catch hallucinations, multi-agent finance workflows with RAG, and Python and SQL reporting.

Compare these with the benchmark. TeamLease Digital put entry-level FDE pay at ₹18–25 lakh a year and senior FDE pay at up to ₹85 lakh or more, Analytics India Magazine reported in July. The new fresher listings sit well below that band.

Fresher FDE listings this week paid ₹4–13 LPA, below TeamLease’s ₹18–25 LPA entry-level band. Source: JobFound listings (October 2026); TeamLease Digital via AIM (July 2026).

Why the gap? The benchmark likely reflects people who already have a few years of engineering behind them. True fresher roles are mostly at small startups, where the job is closer to an implementation engineer with a title upgrade. That is still a good first step: you get customer-facing deployment work early, which is the hardest part of an FDE profile to fake later. Senior FDE roles: global product firms keep posting in India

At the senior end, Neurapath’s newest listings are dominated by product companies asking 5+ years:

  • Blitzy — Forward Deployed Engineer, India, senior, posted 4 days ago.
  • Databricks — Senior FDE (L5), remote, posted 8 days ago.
  • Adobe — Sr Forward Deployed Engineer, Noida, posted 14 days ago.
  • Xactly — Lead FDE, Bengaluru.

Mid-level (2–5 years) roles also appeared from SailPoint (Pune, an agentic operations FDE), Axtria (Gurugram, generative AI), 66degrees (Pune, multi-cloud), F22 Labs (Chennai) and Summit Consulting Services (Hyderabad, a GCC-facing role). None of these listed pay on Neurapath.

The spread across Pune, Gurugram, Noida, Chennai and Hyderabad matters. Bengaluru still leads, but the newest mid-level roles are not concentrated there. For how these roles differ from solutions or applied AI jobs, see roles similar to forward deployed engineer.

Indian IT’s FDE headcount plans: the full picture

Tuesday’s Pulse covered Infosys and TCS. Analytics India Magazine’s July round-up shows a wider set of targets. TCS aims for about 1% of its workforce, roughly 6,000 people, in what it calls “rapid build engineer” roles. Infosys plans 6,000 frontier engineers over the next few years. Cognizant is building 5,000 Frontier Certified Engineers, and LTM’s AI1000 programme targets 1,000 FDEs. HCLTech is building a pipeline through internal skilling and selective lateral hiring, without a number.

Large IT firms plan thousands of FDE-style roles, mostly filled internally. Source: Analytics India Magazine, July 2026.

Set against that, Xpheno estimated India’s potential FDE talent pool at about 2,800 people, AIM reported. Targets of this size can only be met by retraining existing staff. That is why internal moves remain the biggest route into FDE work at IT services firms.

Net hiring at large IT firms was positive last quarter. NewsBytes reported on 18 October that HCLTech added a net 6,440 people in the July–September quarter, Tech Mahindra 5,446 and TCS 4,258.

Layoffs and FDE hiring this week, side by side

Innodata: About 550 employees in India lost system access on Monday, 26 October, Business Today reported, citing IndiaToday.in. Cuts were also reported in the Philippines. Termination emails cited a ramp-down of a business assignment, not performance, per HRKatha. Innodata, a data engineering and AI services firm, had not confirmed the figures. Its shares fell about 25% on 27 October, American Bazaar reported.

FDE hiring: Glassdoor India listings rose to 522 in the same week, and new fresher and senior FDE roles were posted.

No source links the Innodata cuts to FDE hiring, and this post makes no such claim. One practical point does connect them: AI data and annotation work can be cut quickly when a client project winds down. Engineers in that segment who can also build and run deployments have more options.

Where FDE demand is heading for India

The AI labs are still building their own deployment arms. OpenAI’s Deployment Company launched in May with more than $4 billion in backing and about 150 engineers from its Tomoro acquisition, Kotak Neo reported. It noted that this moves into work Indian IT firms have typically handled. In India, Anthropic’s openings reported by DQ India in September were Applied AI Architect roles in Bengaluru and Mumbai, not FDE-titled jobs.

GCCs are a second engine. Careernet said in August that GCCs are emerging as larger FDE recruiters than IT services firms. Its CBO Neelabh Shukla described FDEs as “translators between what the technology can do and what the business needs.”

What this means for you this week

  • Freshers: apply with a live system, not a CV. Neurasity asks for a link to something you built. Deploy one small agent that calls a real API and keep it running.
  • Learn evaluation, not just prompting. Peakflo’s listing asks for LLM evaluation loops that catch hallucinations. A simple test harness for your project is a strong signal. The FDE Fit Check shows which other gaps to close.
  • Don’t anchor on ₹18–25 LPA as a fresher. This week’s fresher roles paid ₹4–13 LPA. Judge offers on the deployment experience you will gain.
  • Mid-level engineers: widen your city filter. New 2–5 year roles are in Pune, Gurugram, Chennai and Hyderabad. Our FDE skills guide shows what these listings test for.

Frequently asked questions

Can a fresher get a forward deployed engineer job in India?

Yes, but such roles are few. In October 2026, Neurasity in Bengaluru and Peakflo (remote) posted fresher-tagged FDE roles paying ₹4–6 LPA plus bonuses and ₹10–13 LPA respectively. Both ask for proof of real systems you have built, not just a degree.

What is the entry-level FDE salary in India?

TeamLease Digital put entry-level FDE pay at ₹18–25 lakh a year, as reported by Analytics India Magazine in July 2026. Fresher listings seen in October 2026 were lower, at ₹4–13 LPA, so the benchmark mostly reflects people with a few years of experience.

How many FDE jobs are open in India this week?

Glassdoor India showed 522 listings for a broad forward deployed engineer search and 101 with the exact 'Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)' title in late October 2026. Neurapath's tracker of employer career pages counted 126 roles from 73 companies.

Were the Innodata layoffs in India linked to FDE hiring?

No source says so. Reports say about 550 India employees lost access on 26 October 2026, and termination emails cited a ramp-down of a business assignment. Innodata had not confirmed the numbers publicly.
