Atlassian has 46 forward deployed engineers in three regions

Kernavique · 2026-10-07 · 1 min read

Summary

In an October 7, 2026 blog post titled Meet the forward deployed engineer, Atlassian states it has 46 forward deployed engineers across the US, EMEA and India, and is moving toward 100. The program is limited to a select group of enterprise Cloud customers using Rovo. Kernavique's reading: this shows a company scaling the role across multiple regions, and offers an example of how to choose which customers get engineers while the team is still small. The post describes the program and is not a list of open roles.

Key points

  • Atlassian reports 46 forward deployed engineers today.
  • The team spans the US, EMEA and India.
  • The company is moving toward 100 engineers.
  • Access is limited to select enterprise Cloud customers using Rovo.
  • The post describes the program, not open roles.
