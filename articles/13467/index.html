03 · Then, every week

One decision, one mental model

The letter is written for founders who invest and investors who build, and it has one job: better judgment. Each week takes one real decision and runs it through one mental model, the same tools Daily Wisdom works through a page at a time. No frameworks borrowed from Twitter threads. No promises of overnight scale. Just the decision, the model, and what the model says to do.

  • One real decision. A hire, a raise, a price, a term sheet, a pivot. Something with money or trust on the line, not a hypothetical.
  • One mental model. Base rates, incentives, inversion, margin of safety. One per letter, in plain words, from the latticework Daily Wisdom builds over a year.
  • Where the judgment slips. The misjudgment the model catches, and why it is so easy to miss in the moment.
  • What to do on Monday. The same decision, re-run with the model in hand. Short enough to read in one sitting.
