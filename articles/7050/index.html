Palantir Interview Process (2026)

Aced (formerly Exponent) · 2026-10-01 · 1 min read

Summary

Aced (formerly Exponent) offers a 2026 guide to Palantir's interview process, with two interview guides for Deployment Strategist and Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) roles, 39 interview questions, and 9 candidate experiences, updated October 2026. Candidates report a recruiter screen, technical coding rounds, and behavioral/problem-solving stages. A key insight from a Deployment Strategist: the decomposition round is not a consulting case—Palantir wants candidates to turn messy problems into data and logic while narrating their reasoning. Reviewers praise collaborative interviewers but flag time management in unfamiliar-codebase rounds as a common weakness.

Key points

  • Covers Deployment Strategist and FDE interview guides.
  • Includes 39 questions and 9 candidate experiences.
  • Decomposition round tests structuring messy problems, not consulting cases.
  • Process: recruiter screen, coding, behavioral rounds.
  • Common weakness: time management in unfamiliar-codebase rounds.

Deployment Strategist

Sep 2025

“I went into the decomposition round expecting a consulting case, and that was the exact wrong frame. What they really cared about was whether I could take a huge messy problem, turn it into data and logic, and bring them along for the ride.”

Software Engineer

Jul 2026

“My interview process included a recruiter screen, technical coding interviews, and a final behavioral and problem-solving round. What went well was that the interviewers were clear, collaborative, and gave me opportunities to explain my thinking.
