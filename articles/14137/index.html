Lesson 0.4: Who hires FDEs, and under which titles

FDE Interview Prep · 2026 · 15 min read

Summary

This job-search lesson explains how to actually find forward deployed engineer (FDE) roles. An exact-phrase search fails both ways: it misses Sierra's "Software Engineer, Agent" and Palantir's "Forward Deployed Software Engineer," while surfacing Anduril's FDE, which requires climbing towers to repair sensors. Drawing on a September 2026 census of job boards, the author gives four search rules (search partial phrases and the abbreviation FDE, read what title matches return, and include titles without "forward"), an employer-by-employer title table, and three questions to judge any posting: what you'd build, where it runs, and who you answer to.

Key points

  • Exact-phrase searches miss reversed titles like Ramp's "Software Engineer, Forward Deployed"; searching "FDE" alone caught 37 extra postings.
  • Cognition, Decagon, Sierra and LangChain use titles with no "forward" at all, such as Deployed Engineer and Agent Deployment Engineer.
  • Title suffixes matter: Palantir's suffixes signal clearance levels; Snowflake's separate AI evaluation from Spark data engineering.
  • LangChain's board shows one title, two tracks: a Bay Area role owning pre-sales technical wins versus a Professional Services variant.
  • Beware mismatches: Anduril's FDE is field hardware repair, and 66 of Accenture India's 78 FDE postings are really ServiceNow application support roles.

When a title search comes back empty or unclear

Harvey’s board had no title containing “Forward Deployed” in September 2026, yet its Staff Product Manager posting mentions working with “forward deployed engineering and customer teams”. Source 56Staff Product Manager, New Verticals @ HarveyPublisherHarvey (Ashby job board)Source typecompany job posting The team exists and no open title names it. At xAI in September 2026, no open job said “forward deployed” anywhere, title or description. Source 57Greenhouse job board - xaiPublisherSpaceXAI (Greenhouse)Source typecompany job board

Then ask the recruiter, in words like these:

  • No title for the team: “Your Staff Product Manager posting mentions forward deployed engineering. Which title does that team hire engineers under, and is one open now?”
  • An ambiguous title, such as Modal’s: “The posting mentions working with Account Executives. Before I prepare, can you tell me what someone in this role builds in the first quarter, where that code runs, and who they report to?”

Search descriptions too, since careers-page search boxes may match titles only. Each board’s JSON carries the full posting; Greenhouse includes it only with

?content=true

, per its

Job Board API documentation

.

# Ashby: postings whose description mentions the work, with department and team
curl -fsS https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/sierra \
  | jq -r '.jobs[] | select((.descriptionPlain // "") | test("forward[- ]deployed|\\bFDE\\b|embed"; "i")) | [.title, .department, .team] | @tsv'

# Greenhouse: the same search on a duty phrase
curl -fsS 'https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/anthropic/jobs?content=true' \
  | jq -r '.jobs[] | select(.content | test("embeds? directly"; "i")) | [.title, .departments[0].name] | @tsv'

Product, security and sales roles will match too; drop them by title. The Sierra run also surfaces Deployed Infrastructure Engineer, which calls itself a Forward Deployed Infrastructure Engineer; no title search here catches it. Source 69Deployed Infrastructure Engineer (San Francisco)PublisherSierra (Ashby)Source typecompany job posting

Your search list

  • Search every pattern: “forward deployed”, “deployed engineer”, “deployment strategist”, “agent deployment engineer”, “FDE” as a whole word, and titles the patterns miss, such as “Software Engineer, Agent”.
  • Run the description search on each target board, with a duty phrase from its own postings.
  • Pull departments from each board’s JSON with the commands in telling FDE from its neighbors. Treat a department as a lead, not a filter.
  • Fill each row, including any platform or clearance gate and the level.
  • Drop postings whose duties are field hardware, application support or pre-sales only, unless that is the job you want.
