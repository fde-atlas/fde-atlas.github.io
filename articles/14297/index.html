Forward Deployed Engineer salary 2026: real pay ranges

FDE Times · 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Summary

FDE Times compiled base salary ranges from 168 US job posts by 59 companies for Forward Deployed Engineer roles, read from official careers pages. Median posted ranges are $182k–$266k for senior/staff/lead roles, $150k–$206k for mid-level, and $120k–$145k for early-career. Figures cover base pay only, excluding equity and bonus. Palantir (31 posts) and Databricks (18) account for 29% of listings, skewing aggregate numbers. New York and Washington DC post lower ranges than the Bay Area; the highest ranges, up to $407k, come from Splunk, Anthropic, Snorkel AI, and Cursor.

Key points

  • Based on 168 US job posts from 59 companies.
  • Senior roles: median range $182k–$266k; mid-level $150k–$206k.
  • Base pay only; equity and bonuses excluded.
  • Palantir and Databricks supply 29% of posts, skewing averages.
  • Top posted ranges reach $321k–$407k at Splunk and Anthropic.

What these numbers are

Base salary only, in US dollars, as the employer published it. Equity, bonus and benefits are not included; at AI companies equity can add a large share on top.

US roles only (228 of 465 open US FDE roles state a range; states such as California, New York, Washington and Colorado require it). Outside the US, posts rarely state pay, so we do not estimate it.

A posted range is what the company is prepared to pay for that role. Where an offer lands inside it depends on level, location and negotiation.

Two employers post many of the ranges: Palantir (31) and Databricks (18), 29% of the posts between them. The overall and per-city figures lean towards their pay; the company list shows each employer on its own.

How we count

Every open Forward Deployed Engineer role on the official careers boards (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workday, and the career sites of Amazon, Microsoft, ByteDance and Tencent) we track is read several times a day. We take the first annual base-pay range in each US post and drop anything that is not an annual salary. Seniority is read from the job title. When a company posts the same title with the same range in several cities, it counts once (city figures still use every listing). The page rebuilds itself when the roles change.
