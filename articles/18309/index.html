Lightup Launches FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) Specialized Recruitment Service on August 1

Original title: ライトアップ、FDE（Forward Deployed Engineer）特化の人材紹介サービスを8月1日提供開始

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-07-16 · 6 min read

Summary

Japanese listed AI company Writeup will launch a recruiting service specialized in Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) on August 1, 2026. The service is AI-driven: AI agents handle nearly the entire matching process, from structuring job requirements and evaluating candidates' skills to selecting and recommending matches, with humans limited to final confirmation under a licensed placement officer. The launch responds to rapid market growth: FDE-related postings on Indeed in the US reportedly grew from 643 in April 2025 to 5,330 in April 2026, roughly 8.3 times, and companies like OpenAI are building large FDE organizations. Writeup argues the market lacks expertise on both sides—companies cannot define FDE requirements, and engineers struggle to spot nominal FDE roles that are actually conventional development work. Leveraging its own experience deploying hundreds of AI agents monthly, the company offers success-based fees and plans future FDE training programs.

Key points

  • Writeup launches an FDE-specialized recruiting service on August 1, 2026.
  • AI agents handle nearly all matching steps; humans do final checks only.
  • US Indeed FDE postings grew ~8.3x year-over-year to 5,330.
  • The service helps define FDE requirements and screen out nominal FDE jobs.
  • Success-based pricing; FDE training programs are planned next.

Writeup Inc. (Securities Code: 6580) will launch a recruitment service specializing in the new job category "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)," which is in growing global demand due to the rapid expansion of AI adoption, starting August 1, 2026.

This service is an "AI-driven" recruitment service in which AI agents handle nearly the entire placement process, from designing job requirements to evaluating candidates' skills and matching. Humans focus solely on final confirmation under a recruitment manager, achieving both matching accuracy and speed (Paid Employment Placement Business License No.: 13-Yu-305366).

■ What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is an engineering role that works deeply on-site at client companies and handles everything end-to-end, from AI system implementation to embedding it into business operations.

It is a job model established by the US data analytics company Palantir, and is described as a hybrid of "engineer" and "consultant."

While the performance of generative AI is improving rapidly, in the field, companies face

- Deployed solutions that fail to take root

- Projects that end at the PoC (proof of concept) stage

and such implementation gaps are widening.

Demand for FDEs, who fill this "last mile of AI implementation," is surging worldwide.

■ Background: Rapid Expansion of the FDE Market — Job Postings Up Roughly 8x in One Year

- FDE-related job postings on US Indeed reportedly grew from 643 in April 2025 to 5,330 in April 2026, a 729% year-over-year increase (roughly 8.3x).

- Since May 2026, major overseas AI companies have successively announced the launch of FDE organizations on the scale of billions of dollars. US-based OpenAI is reported to be building an FDE organization of approximately 150 people through a new company that raised over $4 billion.

- In Japan as well, joint ventures of major telecom groups, AI startups, and SaaS companies have successively begun hiring for FDE positions. Job postings with annual salaries of 12 million yen or more have also been published, and compensation levels continue to rise sharply.

■ Challenge: No "Discerning Eye" in a Rapidly Growing Market

While the market is expanding rapidly, FDE is a job category that has only just emerged. As a result, the following challenges exist.

- For hiring companies: FDE-experienced professionals barely exist in the market, and defining "the FDE requirements our company needs" is itself difficult

- For engineers: It is hard to distinguish postings that are "FDE in name only" (actually conventional outsourced or on-site development)

Neither hiring companies nor engineers have a "discerning eye." This is the bottleneck of the FDE market.

In other words, what is needed is not mere intermediation between job postings and people, but matching based on an understanding of the FDE role itself.

■ Solution: Service Overview

This service is a recruitment service specialized in the FDE domain, supporting both hiring companies and engineers.

[An "AI-driven" recruitment service where AI agents handle nearly the entire matching process]

AI agents handle everything from structuring job requirements to evaluating candidates' skills and implementation experience, and selecting and recommending matching candidates, while humans focus solely on final confirmation under a recruitment manager. Compared with conventional recruitment operations, the service is designed to achieve both accuracy and speed.

[For Hiring Companies]

- Support for articulating and designing FDE job requirements based on hands-on AI implementation expertise

- Candidate introductions via AI matching against requirements

[For Engineers]

- Career transition support to FDE based on AI evaluation of skills and implementation experience

- Support for vetting job postings (confirming scope of work, discretion, and implementation workload)

[Fees]

Success-fee based (fees are incurred only upon a successful hire, a design that minimizes fixed costs).

■ What This Service Achieves

[For Hiring Companies]

- From "we want to hire an FDE but can't write the requirements" to being able to entrust the entire process, including requirements definition

- AI matching shortens lead time to candidate proposals

[For Engineers]

- Understand how your skills and implementation experience are valued in the FDE market

- Avoid "FDE in name only" positions and transition to FDE roles with real substance

■ Why Writeup

[1. We are a "practitioner" implementing hundreds of AI agents per month]

We are an AI company listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth Market (Securities Code: 6580) that implements hundreds of AI agents per month for small and medium-sized businesses. We ourselves practice, as a business, the "last mile of AI implementation" work that FDEs handle. Unlike recruitment-specialized firms, the foundation of this service is our ability to define and evaluate FDE job and skill requirements with the resolution of a hands-on implementer.

[2. AI-driven recruitment operations themselves]

As a company that develops its own AI SaaS, we have rebuilt the recruitment process itself with AI agents. This structure enables us to offer the service on a success-fee basis with low fixed costs while improving matching accuracy and speed.

■ Future Outlook

Going forward, we will pursue developments beyond recruitment, including building FDE talent development programs and supporting the transition of in-house engineers into FDE-type talent.

Under its vision of "making every small and medium-sized business in Japan profitable," Writeup will build a supply network of talent responsible for AI implementation at SMBs, accelerating the social implementation of AI from a talent perspective as well.
