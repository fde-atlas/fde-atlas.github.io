DXC had 86 Claude-certified forward deployed engineers

Kernavique · 2026-10-01 · 2 min read

Summary

Kernavique summarizes a CIO Dive report from October 1, 2026, attributing to DXC CEO Raul Fernandez the figure of 86 Claude-certified forward deployed engineers as of July 31, 2026, working across 57 customer environments. This contrasts with DXC's June 11, 2026 announcement of plans to train tens of thousands. Kernavique's reading: certification at scale takes time, announced plans can far exceed present capacity, and buyers of forward deployed work should ask how many engineers are actually deployed in customer environments today.

  • Those engineers operated in 57 customer environments.

CIO Dive reported on October 1, 2026, that DXC had 86 Claude-certified forward deployed engineers as of July 31, 2026. CIO Dive attributed the count to DXC’s chief executive, Raul Fernandez, who said the engineers were operating in 57 customer environments. DXC’s own release of June 11, 2026, stated a plan to train tens of thousands.

Happened . Written up by Kernavique on . Source: CIO Dive (reported).

What happened

The report is by Matt Ashare of CIO Dive and covers Gartner’s forecast for vendor forward deployed engineering. We read it on November 20, 2026. The count of 86 is a figure CIO Dive attributes to DXC’s chief executive, so we give it as reported.

Our reading

What it means

The facts above are the source’s. What follows is Kernavique’s reading of them.

If you are a forward deployed engineer

A reported count of 86 certified engineers, against a stated plan to train tens of thousands, suggests certification at scale takes time. If you already hold such a certificate, DXC’s reported count suggests few colleagues there did by July 31, 2026.

If you are hiring them

The gap between 86 certified engineers and a plan for tens of thousands suggests announced plans and present capacity can differ widely. If you are buying forward deployed work, ask how many engineers are in customer environments today.
