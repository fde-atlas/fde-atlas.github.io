Forward Deployed Engineer jobs in India (2026)

FDE Times · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Summary

FDE Times maintains a daily-updated listing of Forward Deployed Engineer openings in India, sourced directly from company careers pages. As of 20 Nov 2026, it shows 27 open roles across 15 companies. Databricks leads with 8 openings, followed by Handshake with 3, and Cisco, Turing, and MongoDB with 2 each. Roles are concentrated in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and Gurugram, spanning AI agents, generative AI, industry solutions, and public sector work, plus Deployment Strategist and management positions. The article also compares other markets, with New York (158) and the San Francisco Bay Area (154) offering the most FDE roles globally.
