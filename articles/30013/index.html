What is CFDE? Eligibility requirements, exam format, and target audience for the TIIDA Level 3 certification

First, let's clarify which certificate CFDE actually is

There is now more than one "FDE certificate," and CFDE is the one with the most complete system. Its full name is TIIDA Certified Forward Deployed Engineer, established by the International Institute of Data and Artificial Intelligence (TIIDA). After DAMA Greater China signed a strategic cooperation agreement with TIIDA on August 30, 2026, the two organizations jointly promote the training and certification.

First, separate it from the other certificates on the table: the two MIIT certificates and the Tencent Cloud one come from different organizations with different natures; see How Many Types of FDE Certification Are There. CFDE's distinguishing feature is that it is a three-tier progressive international professional certification—it is neither a national vocational qualification nor an ecosystem certification from a cloud vendor.

Three levels: you must go through them one by one

CFDE has three levels, covering different stages from mastering basic skills to coordinating complex projects and building organization-level capabilities:

LevelFull namePositioning
CFDE-AAssociateNo entry threshold; master basic skills
CFDE-PProfessionalIndependently take on FDE projects
CFDE-MMasterCoordinate complex projects and organization-level capabilities

One key point: the three levels are not "three exams taken at once" but a progressive path taken level by level. You cannot jump straight to the Professional level—the hard requirement in the public information is that you must first pass CFDE-A and hold a valid certificate.

Eligibility requirements for each level

CFDE-A (Associate): the lowest threshold; current university students, or anyone with an associate degree or above, may apply.

CFDE-P (Professional): two conditions must be met simultaneously—

  • You have passed the CFDE-A certification exam and hold a valid certificate;
  • Education and work experience, meeting one of the following: a doctoral degree; or a master's degree plus at least 1 year of IT-related work after graduation; or a bachelor's degree plus at least 3 years; or an associate degree plus at least 5 years.

The second condition is a hard threshold—there is no way to "offset years of experience with training hours." So between A and P there is usually a stretch of real work time, which is also the point most often glossed over in marketing pitches.

CFDE-M (Master): public information says very little about the eligibility requirements for this level, mostly describing it only as positioned for "coordinating complex projects and building organization-level capabilities." Before applying, verify the current specific requirements with the certifying body or training provider; do not rely on secondhand articles.

Course modules: what's tested is a complete set of capabilities

The public course syllabus spans four days, and its structure maps clearly onto the actual workflow of the FDE role:

  • Business side: business discovery (strategy, architecture, requirements mining, value analysis), solution design (PoC, MVP, ROI, value assessment)
  • Data side: enterprise data foundations (ontology modeling, data architecture, governance, master data, metadata, quality, security)
  • AI side: large model principles, Embedding, RAG, tool calling, model selection; agent engineering (single/multi-agent, workflows, MCP, memory, planning)
  • Delivery side: enterprise delivery (project implementation, integration, change management, launch and operations, continuous optimization), governance and trustworthy AI, professional skills (consulting methods, workshop design, demos and presentations, project management)

This structure corresponds closely to the three-tier FDE capability model broken down on this site—all three tiers of production engineering, AI implementation, and on-site and organizational work are covered. This is why CFDE is called "relatively complete" as a system: it attempts to cover the entire chain rather than just testing platform operations.

Who should take CFDE

Match yourself against the positioning and thresholds:

  • Those who want to systematically build a complete capability for enterprise AI implementation: CFDE's three-tier progression offers a clear path—starting from A, you don't have to agonize over which certificate to take first.
  • Business managers and technical leaders (CIO / CDO / CTO / heads of digital transformation and AI): the solution design, ROI assessment, governance, and organizational capability parts of the course are close to real decision-making scenarios.
  • Engineers transitioning from technical backgrounds (AI / data / solutions / architects): the course can fill in the business discovery and delivery sides, but note—a certificate cannot replace real project experience; hiring looks at whether you can be accountable for outcomes.

If your goal is simply "delivering agents within the Tencent Cloud ecosystem," the better fit is Tencent Cloud ADP FDE; see the full exam process for the syllabus and fees. If your goal is a capability credential for government or state-owned enterprise settings, first understand the difference between the two MIIT certificates before deciding.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

What is the relationship between CFDE and the FDE people usually talk about?

FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is a job title; CFDE is the name of one certification for it. Holding CFDE does not mean you have worked as an FDE—it is simply a standardized certification of this body of knowledge and skills.

Can I take the Professional level of CFDE directly?

No. The published eligibility requirements state that you must first pass CFDE-A and hold a valid certificate, then meet the education and work experience requirements. The three levels are a step-by-step path, not levels you can choose from freely.

Is CFDE a national vocational qualification in China?

No. CFDE is an international professional certification launched by TIIDA and jointly promoted by DAMA Greater China and TIIDA. It is not a national vocational qualification, a professional license, or a professional title. Its purpose is to serve as evidence of capability; it has no entry-qualification nature.

Does CFDE test the same content as Tencent Cloud ADP FDE?

The emphases differ. CFDE covers the full chain—business discovery, data foundations, AI, agent engineering, enterprise delivery, governance—and is a systematic capability certification; Tencent Cloud ADP FDE focuses on agent delivery capabilities on the Tencent Cloud ADP platform and is more ecosystem-oriented. The two do not conflict in positioning.

Are CFDE's fees and exam schedule fixed?

No. The fees in public information (RMB 980 for Level A, RMB 1980 for Level P) and the "once per quarter" schedule come from information published by training providers and may change over time. Before applying, rely on the latest announcements from the certifying body or officially authorized channels; do not treat figures from secondhand articles as quotes.
