Kimi Enters the Enterprise Business Space, Partnering with IT Service Providers to Promote Forward Deployed AI

Full text

[Caixin Global] After Kimi K3 achieved a technical breakthrough, Moonshot AI (Kimi) is pushing into the enterprise business. On September 10, Moonshot AI announced the launch of the "Kimi Enterprise Partner Program," teaming up with IT service providers and integrators across different industries to build a team of Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) to help enterprises deploy AI into their core business operations.

The first batch of partner companies includes Teamsun (600410.SH), Kingsoft Cloud (03896.HK), Yakun Technology (301085.SZ), AsiaInfo Technologies (01675.HK), and ChinaSoft International (00354.HK). Kimi is also recruiting more partner companies in the announcement.
