Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Experiences & Questions (2026)

Top Tech Transition · 2026 · 1 min read

Summary

This article compiles three firsthand Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer interview accounts shared up to May 2026, detailing each round and outcome. The typical process has about three rounds (recruiter screen, phone screen, technical, final/onsite), most commonly featuring behavioral, coding, and decomposition rounds, plus one debugging round. Candidates rated difficulty 3.3/5 (medium), and the process took roughly three weeks from first contact to decision. Candidates note the technical rounds favor practical feature-building over LeetCode-style puzzles, rewarding thinking aloud and delivering working solutions. Notably, none of the three candidates with final results received an offer.

Key points

  • Based on 3 real candidate interviews, latest from May 2026.
  • Typical process: recruiter screen, phone screen, technical, and final rounds.
  • Common rounds: behavioral, coding, decomposition, plus debugging.
  • Difficulty rated 3.3/5; hiring takes about 3 weeks.
  • Technical rounds favor practical coding over LeetCode-style problems; 0 of 3 candidates got offers.
