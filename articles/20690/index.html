68.3% of IT Engineers Are Interested in the New AI-Era Job Role "FDE," with Over 70% Predicting That Demand Will Increase

Original title: AI時代の新職種「FDE」にITエンジニアの68.3％が関心、7割以上が「今後需要が増える」と予測

プレスリリース・ニュースリリース配信シェアNo.1｜PR TIMES · 2026-06-30 · 6 min read

Summary

Levtech, a Japanese IT staffing company, surveyed 243 IT engineers about the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role. 68.3% expressed interest in the position, and 72.0% predicted demand for FDEs will grow, with only 4.5% expecting no growth. The top reason given (61.1%) was that applying AI to work requires understanding specific business operations and on-site contexts; 55.4% cited rising value of engineers who can handle everything from understanding client problems to implementation, and 48.0% pointed to AI replacing some coding, raising the importance of upstream work. President Masahiro Izusawa commented that engineers combining technical skill with business understanding will be key to corporate AI adoption and DX efforts. The online survey ran in late May 2026 via GMO Research & AI.

Key points

  • 68.3% of surveyed IT engineers are interested in the FDE role.
  • 72.0% expect FDE demand to increase; only 4.5% disagree.
  • 61.1% say AI adoption requires understanding specific operations and field contexts.
  • 48.0% believe AI-assisted coding raises the importance of upstream engineering.
  • Survey of 243 engineers, conducted May 2026 by Levtech via GMO Research & AI.

Awareness Survey on "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)"

Levertech Inc. ( https://levtech.jp/ ), whose vision is "Making Japan an IT Advanced Nation," conducted an awareness survey on the "Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)," a new profession gaining attention in the AI era, targeting 243 IT engineers.

<Survey Summary>

  • 68.3% of IT engineers answered that they are interested in "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)"
  • Over 70% of IT engineers predict that "demand for FDEs will increase going forward"
  • As a reason for increased FDE demand, over 60% answered "understanding individual operations and on-site conditions is important for AI utilization"

1. 68.3% of IT engineers answered that they are interested in "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)"

When asked about their interest in the profession of "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)," which works on-site with customers to handle problem-solving and technical implementation, 68.3% answered that they are interested, combining "very interested (21.4%)" and "somewhat interested (46.9%)."

2. Over 70% of IT engineers predict that "demand for FDEs will increase going forward"

To the question "Do you think demand for FDEs will increase in the future?", 72.0% of IT engineers answered that demand for FDEs will increase, combining "I think it will increase (25.1%)" and "I think it will somewhat increase (46.9%)."

On the other hand, the combined answers of "I don't think it will increase much (3.7%)" and "I don't think it will increase (0.8%)" were only 4.5%, suggesting that many IT engineers recognize that FDEs will come to play an important role in the future, against the backdrop of advancing AI utilization and corporate digital transformation.

3. As a reason for increased FDE demand, over 60% answered "understanding individual operations and on-site conditions is important for AI utilization"

When asked why they think FDEs will increase in the future, the most common answer was "Because I think understanding individual operations and on-site conditions will be important to apply AI to business operations (61.1%)." This was followed by "Because I think the value of personnel who can understand customer issues and handle everything from start to finish through technical implementation will increase (55.4%)" and "Because I think the importance of upstream processes will increase as some coding is replaced by AI (48.0%)."

While the spread of AI tools is lowering the barrier to writing code itself, expectations are rising for personnel who possess not only development skills but also a combination of business understanding and technical expertise, and who can handle everything from problem organization to implementation.

<Comment from Masahiro Izumisawa, Representative Executive Officer and President, Levertech Inc.>

This survey revealed that approximately 70% of IT engineers are interested in FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer), and that over 70% predict that demand for FDEs will expand in the future.

While the evolution of AI is driving efficiency and automation of development work, for companies to achieve results using AI, they are required to deeply understand their own operations and challenges and implement technology in ways suited to each. In this survey as well, the most common answer was "understanding on-site conditions is important to apply AI to business operations," suggesting that in the AI era, the importance of personnel who possess not only advanced technical skills but also deep understanding of business and operations is increasing.

Going forward, I believe personnel who deeply understand customers' businesses and operations and can handle everything from problem identification to implementation and operation will be increasingly sought after as supporters of corporate AI utilization and DX promotion. FDE is one of the representative professions for this, and its importance will likely continue to grow in Japan as well.
