FDE Jobs in India: Postings Cool After 19x Surge (25 Oct 2026)

FDE India · 2026-10-25 · 8 min read

Summary

This first FDE Pulse report tracks India's forward deployed engineer job market as of October 2026. Indeed data shows year-on-year posting growth cooling from roughly 19x in June 2026 to over 6x in August, though from a very small base. Glassdoor India lists about 504 FDE jobs, 176 in Bengaluru, while an employer-page aggregator counts 139 roles across 84 companies. Employer-stated pay ranges from ₹7–10 LPA at a services firm to ₹70–90 LPA at AI startup Cartesia; Glassdoor's average base is about ₹12 lakh. The piece also covers AWS's $1 billion FDE unit, OpenAI's Deployment Company, and notes that OpenAI and Anthropic use titles like Applied AI Engineer in India rather than FDE. Barclays' 5,000 job cuts are reported separately, with no evidence linking them to FDE hiring.

Key points

  • Indeed: FDE posting growth fell from ~19x YoY in June 2026 to over 6x in August, from a tiny base.
  • Glassdoor India shows ~504 FDE listings in October 2026, with 176 in Bengaluru.

  • Barclays announced 5,000 cuts on 22 October; no source connects this to FDE hiring.

FDE hiring in India is still growing, but the pace is cooling. Indeed data shows postings were about 19x higher year on year in June 2026, then only over 6x higher by August. Glassdoor India still lists roughly 500 forward deployed engineer jobs, most of them in Bengaluru.

This is the first FDE Pulse, so the numbers below set the baseline. If you are new to the role, start with what an FDE engineer does.

How many FDE jobs are there in India? (October 2026 counts)

According to Indeed data, FDE postings in India grew nearly 19x between June 2025 and June 2026, up 1,807% year on year in June. They were nearly 16x higher in July and more than 6x higher in August. That is a cooling from the peak, though still far above a year earlier. One report adds a caveat: the growth is from a very small base, and it counts postings on one platform, not hires.

What do FDE jobs in India pay?

Pay data is thin. Glassdoor reported an average base of about ₹12 lakh, within a ₹9–16 lakh range, from 28 submissions (January 2026 update). Individual submissions showed total pay of ₹22–26 lakh for 1–3 years’ experience in Hyderabad and ₹23–27 lakh in Bengaluru. Indeed said its own sample is too small for a reliable median.

Listings show a much wider spread. Look at employer-provided figures, not Glassdoor’s auto-estimates, which for some listings look implausibly low. Examples: PinnacleU HR in Bengaluru offers ₹7–10 LPA, Thrifty AI in Noida posts ₹15 LPA and Kuber and Company in Mumbai shows ₹20–50 LPA. A Weekday listing for a remote role asks 2–5 years and shows ₹5–20 LPA.

Layoffs and FDE hiring: this week’s picture

The biggest India-relevant cut this week was at Barclays. On 22 October it announced plans to shed 5,000 jobs, about 5% of its global workforce. Around 1,000 have gone, 4,000 more are expected by year-end, and many back-office functions sit in India. About 60% of the cuts will hit support roles, including software developers.

The bank’s own framing is mixed. The CEO did not say AI was behind the cuts and pointed to technology investment and simplification. He also said Barclays will prioritise internal moves over outside hiring.

Elsewhere, Amazon reportedly laid off fewer than 1,000 white-collar workers in its stores division, with staff in the US, India and the UK affected. In the wider tally, TrueUp counts 665 tech layoffs affecting 196,349 people so far in 2026.

Set side by side: while Barclays announced 5,000 cuts, FDE listings in India stayed in the hundreds across Glassdoor. We found no source linking any of these layoffs to FDE hiring, and this post does not claim a link. Citi’s Pune FDE-titled role shows GCCs are posting such jobs, but that says nothing about who was let go elsewhere.

Where FDE demand is heading and what it means for India

AWS: It announced a $1 billion Forward Deployed Engineering unit, seeded with “thousands” of FDEs. AWS said it will hire externally for some roles and move others internally. The announcement was on 30 June 2026. No India hiring numbers were published in the sources we found.

OpenAI and Anthropic: OpenAI launched its Deployment Company on 11 May 2026 with more than $4 billion of initial investment. On the training side, Anthropic announced Claude Frontier Academy on 2 October 2026, with $100 million to train 10,000 engineers by end-2027.

The India gap: the labs’ India roles mostly do not carry the FDE title. On 2 October, none of OpenAI’s 24 FDE-titled postings was in India, though an Applied AI Engineer role covered Delhi, Mumbai and Bangalore. Anthropic had not posted an FDE role in Bengaluru as of mid-September; its listings there included Applied AI Architect. The practical read: lab-grade FDE work in India currently sits under different titles, while the FDE-titled volume comes from startups, consultancies, IT services and GCCs.

Related roles also hiring in India

  • Applied AI Engineer: OpenAI’s Delhi posting lists forward-deployed, customer engineering, solutions architecture and technical consulting as relevant backgrounds.
  • Applied AI Architect and Partner AI Deployment Engineer (AWS): OpenAI lists these across Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru. The AWS-partner role suits senior solution architects and consultants.
  • Solutions Engineer: OpenAI also has a Delhi Technical Success listing for this title.

What this means for you this week

  • Search every title variant. Use “forward deployed engineer”, “forward deployment engineer”, “applied AI engineer” and “AI deployment engineer”. Glassdoor counts varied from 97 to 586 by title, and OpenAI’s India roles use other names.
  • Target the 2–5 year band if you are mid-level. Several named roles (TCS, Google Cloud, Axtria, Fico, Cyble) are marked 2–5 years. Cities to filter: Bengaluru, Pune, Gurugram, Noida.
  • Show a deployed project, not just a model. Indeed’s skills list covers Python or Java, data engineering, generative AI, RAG, AI agents, cloud, APIs, integrations and full-stack work. Build one RAG or agent workflow against a real API and document the customer problem. The FDE Fit Check can show which gaps to close first.
  • Apply on employer pages and read pay carefully. Take employer-provided pay as the signal and ignore Glassdoor estimates. If you work in GCC support or dev roles, ask internally about deployment-style openings, since Barclays’ CEO has promised internal moves a priority.

Frequently asked questions

How many forward deployed engineer jobs are there in India right now?

Glassdoor India pages showed about 504 forward deployed engineer listings in October 2026, and an aggregator of employer career pages counted 139 roles from 84 companies. Counts differ by job title and platform, so treat them as indicative.

What is the typical forward deployed engineer salary in India?

Glassdoor reported an average base pay of about ₹12 lakh a year, in a ₹9–16 lakh range, from only 28 submissions as of its January 2026 update. Individual listings vary much more, from single-digit LPA to ₹70–90 LPA at a funded AI startup.

Which Indian cities have the most FDE openings?

Bengaluru leads, with 176 listings on Glassdoor India in October 2026. Indeed's report also names Hyderabad, Chennai, Delhi, Mumbai and remote roles, and listings appear in Pune and Noida too.

Do OpenAI and Anthropic hire FDEs in India?

Not under the FDE title, based on third-party checks. OpenAI's India openings include Applied AI Engineer and Applied AI Architect roles, and Anthropic's Bengaluru listings included Applied AI Architect roles, not FDE, as of mid-September.
