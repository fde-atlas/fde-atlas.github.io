AI Pods: 6 Best AI Pod Team Providers (2026)

Ryz Labs · 2026-10-02 · 6 min read

Summary

What AI pods are and who sells them: six AI pod team providers compared on what a pod is, who is in it and how it is priced, with sourced costs.

Full text

Disclosure: we're Ryz Labs, and we compete with the companies on this page. Read it with that in mind. We've flagged every case where we'd send you elsewhere.

"Pod" has become a sales word. For some vendors it is a metered subscription of AI agents. For others it is a few individual hires, or a team run by the vendor's project manager. The question that decides it: are you buying named engineers who work in your systems, or capacity measured some other way?

  • Best overall for a dedicated senior team in your stack: Ryz Labs (us)
  • Best for a consumption-priced agent pod you can buy like a SKU: Globant
  • Best for AI talent plus domain specialists from a large network: Turing
  • Best for vendor-managed pods with their own PMs: BairesDev

Here is every public price signal we found, checked October 2026. Most firms in this market don't publish a rate card, so we show the hourly band on their Clutch listing and convert it to a monthly figure for one full-time engineer.

Monthly figures are our math at 160 hours a month. Directory bands are entered by the firms themselves and are not quotes, so ask each vendor for a written monthly price for the same roles at the same seniority.

Procurement wants something it can compare line by line. Engineering wants people who can ship in its systems. Pod offerings try to satisfy both, and the definitions drift as a result. Two vendors can quote "a pod" and mean completely different things.

Before you compare prices, compare what is within the box: who the people are, who manages them, where the code lives, and what you are actually billed for (hours, tokens, outcomes or a team). Then compare prices.

  • Agent subscription: AI agents do the work, people supervise, you pay for usage.
  • Vendor-managed team: engineers plus the vendor's PM deliver a scoped build.
  • Dedicated team: named engineers join your repos and rituals and own an outcome with you.

Inside a pod, roles vary by the work: AI agent developers, AI voice agent developers, LlamaIndex developers and engineers who handle MCP server development. To price the people, see AI engineer rates and data scientist rates.

We compared every company on the same six things buyers ask us about: published pricing, or the clearest public rate we could find; seniority and how candidates are vetted; time-zone overlap with US teams; whether people work on your team or inside a vendor-managed project; depth in the specific work this list covers; and public evidence such as case studies, client reviews and filings. Every fact about another company comes from its own site, its public filings or a review platform, and is linked in the Sources section at the end. We update this list regularly and correct anything a listed company shows us is wrong: email info@ryzlabs.com.

We're trusted by Fortune 500 engineering teams, and our AI pod teams are the third shape. A typical pod is about seven senior engineers, including a tech lead, an ML engineer and backend engineers, working as one team in your time zone. The pod works in your repos, CI and standups, runs weekly demos, and builds on your stack (AWS, Azure, GitHub, Postgres, Anthropic and OpenAI models). As the work grows, you can add people or disciplines; when it's done, the pod can hand the whole system over. Before you sign, you get a scoped plan, a price and the names of the people who would do the work.

Our pods have production systems behind them: a fraud detection system for a global fleet company ($5.94M in confirmed fraud, 244K+ invoices scored), a marketing-compliance system for a global capital management firm (8,000+ documents, days to hours), and an AI real-estate agent that answers around the clock. See our case studies.

Where we're not the right fit: if procurement needs a consumption-priced SKU, we can't offer that: we price per engineer, typically $7,000 to $15,000 per engineer per month. If you need engineers in Europe or Asia time zones or follow-the-sun coverage, a global network fits better. If you want a management-consulting engagement or a proprietary AI platform, look at the big consultancies and platform vendors. And we have fewer analyst ratings than the big names.

Globant launched "AI Pods" in June 2025: a monthly subscription with token-metered capacity, with agents orchestrated on its GEAI platform and supervised by Globant experts. Since August 2026 they are sold through Glob.AI in three models: Software, Operations and Catalyst.

Where it beats us: a catalog procurement can buy from, platform partnerships including Anthropic, AWS, OpenAI and Google Cloud, and a global, consumption-priced pod platform. The catch: you are buying agent output, not a named team, and AI Pods were $20.6M ARR against about $2.45B in revenue in FY2025. See Ryz Labs vs Globant.

"Turing pods" combine AI talent with domain specialists embedded in client workflows, under its Advise, Augment and Build offerings. Its core business supplies data and evaluations to frontier AI labs.

Where it beats us: model-level depth, for teams that need evaluation and post-training work. The catch: enterprise build is its newer line, and continuity depends on who is staffed.

BairesDev describes its Dedicated Teams as "focused, delivery-ready pods to handle full builds or workstreams," led by BairesDev tech PMs. It also offers staff augmentation and outsourced projects, and says it has 4,000+ Latin American engineers.

Where it beats us: a vendor-managed model with its own PMs and a strong Clutch record (4.9/5 across 63 reviews). The catch: it is a generalist vendor; AI is one of many technologies rather than a dedicated practice.

EPAM's AI/Run.Transform, launched in October 2025, is consulting built around blueprints for AI-native teams, alongside its open-source DIAL platform and managed delivery teams.

Where it beats us: if you want help redesigning your whole engineering organization, not one pod. The catch: a large-firm engagement floor.

Tribe AI staffs small forward-deployed squads from a network of senior AI practitioners (500+ as of 2024, per TechCrunch) and adds strategy and adoption phases.

Where it beats us: strategy and adoption coverage. The catch: contracted talent can affect continuity on long work.

If you want a pod that is a real team in your systems: Ryz Labs. Named senior engineers, New York hours, your cloud and repos, weekly demos, and a system you own at the end. Our pods have shipped production work like the $5.94M fraud detection system and the 8,000+ document compliance system described above.

If procurement needs a metered SKU: Globant.

If you need a vendor PM to run a full build for you: BairesDev.

If you are redesigning your whole engineering org: EPAM.

  • Write one sentence defining "pod" for your purchase: people, agents, or both.
  • Ask each vendor what exactly you are billed for and what happens to unused capacity.
  • Ask for the names of the people in the pod and who manages them day to day.
  • Confirm the code and models will live in your repos and cloud.

An AI pod is a small, cross-functional engineering team that owns one AI workstream end to end, usually a tech lead plus ML and backend engineers. There is no standard definition, though: vendors also use "pod" for a metered agent subscription or a vendor-managed team. Ask which one you are buying.

It varies by vendor. Our typical pod is about seven senior engineers. Agent-based pods are sized by usage rather than headcount.

With a dedicated pod is one form of it. See forward-deployed engineering partners.

Normalize to what you get: named people and hours, or tokens and outcomes. Few vendors publish prices, so ask for written quotes. Our guide to dedicated AI teams vs staff augmentation covers the team-versus-individuals side.

Last checked October 2026. Pricing, trials and services move fast in this market, so verify the details with each vendor.
