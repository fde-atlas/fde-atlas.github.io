Forward-Deployed Engineer | zudo.work | Remote, US

Zero G Talent · 2026-10-14 · 7 min read

Summary

Zudo, a startup building AI workers for the extended service contract industry (automotive and home protection), is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer, remote in the US or Canada. The role requires 2-4 years of software engineering or technical consulting experience, Python/TypeScript skills, and familiarity with LLMs, AI agents, and cloud security. The engineer independently owns the full lifecycle: customer discovery, solution design, building and deploying AI agents, secure cloud deployment, evaluation, and ongoing improvement. Candidates start with a four-month internship before a full-time offer, and the company aims to complete interviews within two weeks.

Key points

  • Zudo builds production AI agents for claims, coverage, renewals, and sales workflows.
  • Requires 2-4 years experience with Python/TypeScript, APIs, databases, and cloud infrastructure.
  • The FDE owns the entire deployment lifecycle, including enterprise security reviews.
  • Remote role (US/Canada) with direct collaboration with founders and high autonomy.
  • Hiring starts with a four-month internship; interviews targeted within two weeks.

About Zudo

At Zudo, we're building AI workers for the extended service contract industry, starting with automotive and home protection.

This is a massive industry where businesses still rely on manual processes, phone calls, and fragmented software to run critical operations. We're changing that by building AI agents that execute real workflows — from claims initiation, coverage inquiries, and adjudication to parts research, renewals, and sales.

We're not interested in building AI demos. We're building production-ready AI systems that get real work done and create measurable business value.

The Role

We're looking for a Forward-Deployed Engineer who can combine strong technical skills, exceptional problem-solving, and an ability to work directly with customers.

You'll work with customers to understand how their businesses operate, identify opportunities for AI automation, and independently design, build, deploy, and continuously improve AI-powered solutions.

You'll own the entire lifecycle: customer discovery, solution architecture, engineering, deployment, security, evaluation, and iteration.

This isn't a role where someone hands you a detailed specification and tells you what to build. We expect you to figure out what matters, make decisions, move quickly, and take ownership of the outcome.

You'll work directly with our founders and customers, with significant autonomy over how you solve problems.

What You'll Do

Understand customer problems and design solutions

  • Work directly with enterprise customers to understand their workflows, operational bottlenecks, and business priorities.
  • Break down ambiguous problems and translate them into practical AI-powered solutions.
  • Identify opportunities where AI can improve efficiency, customer experience, and revenue.

Build and deploy AI systems

  • Design, build, and deploy AI agents that execute real business workflows.
  • Develop integrations with APIs, databases, and existing enterprise software.
  • Build and optimize LLM-powered applications, including agentic workflows and voice AI.
  • Evaluate models, develop testing frameworks, and improve reliability, latency, and performance.
  • Apply prompting, retrieval, and LLM fine-tuning when appropriate to improve performance on specific tasks.

Own secure, enterprise-ready deployments

  • Architect, deploy, and manage AI applications on cloud infrastructure such as AWS, GCP, or Azure.
  • Implement appropriate security practices, including encryption, access controls, secrets management, and secure handling of customer data.
  • Work with customers' IT and security teams to understand and satisfy security requirements.
  • Complete technical security questionnaires, prepare supporting documentation, and help customers navigate vendor security reviews.
  • Ensure deployments are reliable, secure, scalable, and suitable for production use.

Own customer outcomes

  • Take responsibility for the success of each deployment, not just shipping code.
  • Monitor performance, troubleshoot issues, and continuously improve solutions.
  • Build strong relationships with customers through clear communication, responsiveness, and execution.
  • Proactively identify opportunities to solve additional customer problems and expand the value Zudo delivers.

Who We're Looking For

Technical foundation

  • Approximately 2–4 years of professional experience in software engineering, technical consulting, solutions engineering, or a similarly technical role.
  • Strong programming skills, preferably Python, TypeScript, or equivalent.
  • Solid understanding of software engineering, APIs, databases, and cloud infrastructure.
  • Familiarity with LLMs, AI agents, model evaluation, and approaches to model customization, including fine-tuning.
  • Understanding of secure cloud deployment and fundamental enterprise security principles.
  • Ability to independently learn unfamiliar technologies and deploy working solutions.

How you operate

  • You think critically. You're excellent at breaking down complex, ambiguous problems and finding practical solutions.
  • You take ownership. You don't wait for instructions. You identify what needs to happen and make it happen.
  • You move with urgency. You act quickly, prioritize what matters, and maintain a strong bias toward execution.
  • You're AI-native. You naturally use AI to accelerate how you build, learn, investigate, and solve problems.
  • You're an excellent communicator. You can work with technical and nontechnical stakeholders, understand their needs, and build trust.
  • You're resourceful. You're comfortable figuring things out without having all the answers upfront.

You might be a software engineer who enjoys working with customers, a technically strong consultant who loves building things, or a solutions engineer looking for more engineering ownership.

We care more about demonstrated ability, intellectual curiosity, and execution than specific job titles or credentials.

Nice to have, not required

  • Experience building production AI agents or voice AI systems.
  • Experience with LLM fine-tuning or model adaptation.
  • Experience with AWS, GCP, or Azure in enterprise environments.
  • Experience responding to enterprise security reviews or questionnaires.
  • Background in B2B SaaS, consulting, insurance, warranty administration, or early-stage startups.

How We Work

Our values aren't just words on a wall. They're how we expect everyone at Zudo to operate.

1. Urgency

Speed matters. We move quickly, make decisions with imperfect information, and favor action over prolonged discussion. We believe the fastest way to learn is often to build, ship, and iterate.

2. High Ownership, High Decentralization

We give people significant autonomy and expect them to use it. You won't need permission for every decision or a manager telling you what to do next. We trust capable people to exercise judgment, take initiative, and drive projects forward.

3. Own the Outcome

We don't measure success by tickets closed, hours worked, or meetings attended. We care about results. You're responsible for ensuring what you build actually works and delivers meaningful value to the customer.

4. Be AI-Native

We believe AI is fundamentally changing how work gets done. We expect everyone to experiment with, embrace, and deeply integrate AI into their workflows. We're constantly looking for ways to accomplish more with better tools, better thinking, and smaller teams.

5. Work With Exceptional People

We want to build a team of extraordinarily capable, curious, and ambitious people who challenge one another, share what they learn, and raise the standard of what great work looks like.

Why Join Zudo?

Learn what it takes to build a company

If you think you might want to become a founder someday, this role offers exposure to many of the skills you'll need: understanding customers, identifying valuable problems, building products, selling solutions, navigating enterprise relationships, and delivering business outcomes.

You'll experience both the technical and commercial sides of building a startup.

Real ownership, not just responsibility

You'll own projects end-to-end, make consequential decisions, and directly influence what we build and how we serve customers. This is an opportunity to operate with the autonomy and accountability of a founder.

Remote-first, results-first

Work remotely from the US or Canada. We care about what you accomplish, not how many hours you're visibly online.

We keep internal meetings to a minimum, communicate directly, and protect time for deep work. We believe highly capable people do their best work when trusted to manage their own time.

Work with exceptional people

Join a small, ambitious team where you'll work directly with founders and people who care deeply about solving hard problems, moving quickly, and doing exceptional work.

Build at the frontier of applied AI

Work on real-world AI agents, LLMs, voice AI, secure cloud infrastructure, and challenging enterprise integrations. You'll solve problems where the technology is evolving quickly and the business impact is immediate.

Grow as fast as your capabilities

We value initiative and results over hierarchy or tenure. As Zudo grows, the scope of your responsibility can grow with it.

Who Will Thrive Here?

Someone who loves building, thinks independently, moves with urgency, and wants to own meaningful outcomes.

Someone who can talk to a customer, understand an unfamiliar problem, design a technical solution, and independently bring it to life.

Someone who wants the freedom to work like a founder, alongside ambitious people, without unnecessary bureaucracy.

If that sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you.

Interview Process

At Zudo, we value urgency, ownership, exceptional problem-solving, and the ability to get things done. Our interview process is designed to be fast, practical, and focused on how you think, build, and operate.

Stage 1: Founder Intro (20–30 minutes)

A conversation about your background, projects you've owned, technical experience, and what motivates you. We'll share more about Zudo, our culture, and the problems we're solving.

Stage 2: Technical Deep Dive (45–60 minutes)

A practical discussion about systems you've built and how you approach engineering challenges. We'll explore software development, AI agents, APIs, cloud infrastructure, LLM evaluation and optimization, security, and debugging. We're more interested in how you reason through real problems than memorized technical answers.

Stage 3: Real-World Customer Scenario (45–60 minutes)

We'll present a realistic customer workflow and ask you to think through how you'd understand the business problem, design an AI solution, deploy it securely, and measure its success. We're evaluating your critical thinking, technical judgment, communication, and ability to take ownership.

Stage 4: Final Founder Conversation (20–30 minutes)

A final conversation about working style, autonomy, initiative, our values, and expectations. You'll have the opportunity to ask questions and understand what success at Zudo looks like.

Initial 4-Month Internship → Full-Time Opportunity

We'll begin with a four-month internship, with the intention of transitioning successful candidates into full-time roles.

During this period, you'll work on real customer projects, own meaningful technical challenges, and operate with the same autonomy and responsibility as the rest of our team. We'll evaluate mutual fit based on your technical execution, initiative, customer impact, and ability to independently own outcomes.

Our goal is to build long-term relationships with exceptional people, and we'll aim to make a full-time offer at the end of the internship based on performance, mutual fit, and business needs.

Our Hiring Philosophy

We aim to complete the interview process within two weeks. No unnecessarily lengthy take-home assignments, no endless interview rounds — just meaningful conversations and practical problem-solving with the people you'll work alongside.
