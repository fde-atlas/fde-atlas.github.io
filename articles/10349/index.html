Enterprise AI startup Wonderful plans to hire up to 1,000 forward-deployed engineers in India

The Economic Times · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

Summary

Enterprise AI startup Wonderful plans to grow its India team to as many as 1,000 forward deployed engineers within 10 to 12 months, cofounder and CTO Roey Lalazar told The Economic Times, betting on India becoming one of its largest markets. Founded in 2025 by Lalazar and Bar Winkler, the company builds AI-native apps and agents for enterprises in telecom, finance and other sectors. It is approaching $100 million in annualised revenue, employs staff across more than 35 countries, and expects global headcount to exceed 1,000 by year-end. Wonderful has raised about $280 million from investors including Index Ventures, Insight Partners, IVP, Bessemer and Vine Ventures.
