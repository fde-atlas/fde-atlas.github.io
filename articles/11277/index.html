AIU and ACM Agile sign MoU to bring CFDE to Türkiye

Artificial Intelligence Uni (AIU.ac) · 2026-10-14 · 1 min read

Summary

Artificial Intelligence Uni (London) and İstanbul-based ACM Agile have signed an MoU to bring the Certified Forward Deployed Engineer (CFDE) programme to Türkiye. AIU introduced the CFDE standard and certification in 2025. Under the agreement, ACM will deliver AIU-vetted CFDE Foundation training locally, and candidates can earn AIU's CFDE credential by passing the examination. AIU has named ACM its Preferred Authorised CFDE Training Provider for Türkiye and the South Caucasus. The article also invites other consultancies to partner with AIU to offer the certification in their markets.

This week, Artificial Intelligence Uni (London) and ACM Agile Danışmanlık A.Ş. (İstanbul) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to bring the Certified Forward Deployed Engineer (CFDE)™ programme to engineers in Türkiye.

The MoU was signed by Noman Shah FBCS, Director, for AIU, and Mehmet Yitmen, Managing Partner, for ACM.

Why it matters

Forward Deployed Engineers build and deploy AI systems inside client organisations. It is the role that turns an AI pilot into a system that runs in production, and demand for engineers who can do it is growing across the AI industry.

AIU is the home of the CFDE™ programme. It introduced the standard and the certification in 2025, and delivers the training online and on-site for organisations.

About ACM

ACM Agile Danışmanlık A.Ş. is a transformation and technology company based in İstanbul. It designs business processes from the ground up around AI agents, and delivers training in Turkish and English.

What the MoU sets out

The MoU is an important first step in establishing a collaborative relationship between the two organisations in the region. Under it, AIU and ACM intend to:

  • deliver CFDE Foundation training in Türkiye through ACM, vetted by AIU
  • certify candidates through the CFDE examination, leading to AIU’s CFDE credential

AIU has designated ACM as Preferred Authorised CFDE Training Provider for Türkiye and the South Caucasus.

Bring CFDE to your market

If you run a consultancy or training organisation and want to bring CFDE to your market, write to partnerships@aiu.ac.
