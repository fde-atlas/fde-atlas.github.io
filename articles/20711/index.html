Comparing 16 FDE Job Postings in Japan in Full | Requirements, Languages, and Compensation Across 8 Companies Including OpenAI and Databricks (September 2026)

Original title: 日本のFDE求人16件を全文比較|OpenAI・Databricksなど8社の要件・言語・報酬(2026年9月)

FDE Talent編集部 · 2026-09-29 · 32 min read

Summary

The FDE Talent editorial team reviewed all Japan-related FDE job postings visible on 132 public job boards as of September 29, 2026, finding 16 postings from 8 companies (Databricks 4, OpenAI and Palantir 3 each, Notion 2, plus Cohere, Cursor, Rogo, Wonderful). Comparing responsibilities, experience requirements, language needs, travel, and pay, the article shows that identical titles hide very different roles. Only 9 of 16 are hands-on engineering roles serving Japanese customers; 3 are manager/lead roles, 2 are non-engineering, and 2 support US government work. Eleven postings require Japanese fluency; 10 state years of experience (5+ at OpenAI, Notion, Wonderful; 8–10+ at Databricks). Only Palantir's two US-government postings list salary ($110K–$200K), and the article also catalogs six adjacent roles like Applied AI Engineer at OpenAI and Anthropic.

Key points

  • 16 Japan-related FDE postings from 8 companies as of September 29, 2026.
  • Only 9 of 16 are hands-on engineering roles for the Japanese market.
  • 11 postings require Japanese; English is usually business level.
  • Experience bars: 5+ years (OpenAI, Notion, Wonderful), 8–10+ (Databricks).
  • Only Palantir's two US-government roles disclose pay; 14 postings list none.

  • 01 As of September 29, 2026, Japan-related FDE job postings (postings with "Forward Deployed" etc. in the job title) listed on the public job boards of 132 companies totaled 16 postings from 8 companies.

Palantir's "Japan Forward Deployed" postings are for US Government work

Palantirの2件は、ジョブボード上の勤務地が Washington, D.C. ですが、本文の冒頭に、米国政府の業務を支える職で勤務地は東京だという注記があります[11][12]。必須要件の欄(What We Require)にあるのは、有効な Top Secret クリアランス(SCI の取得資格と意思を含む)の1項目だけで、日本語の要件は書かれていません[11][12]。エンジニア職の歓迎要件には、兵站、資材、維持整備、航空、即応性の分析に関する経験が挙げられ、福利厚生の欄には、SOFA を通じて日本に配属される場合の転居支援があります[11]。

Banker, Architect, and Strategist roles have different requirements from engineering roles

Rogoの Forward Deployed Banker は、元バンカーや元投資家で構成されるチームの職で、金融のフロントオフィス職での4年以上の経験を求めています[15]。求人票は、ソリューションアーキテクトが技術面の合意を取りつけるのに対し、この職は金融面の合意(financial win)を取りつけると説明しています[15]。Notionの Forward Deployed Architect は、高度な技術が要る場面では Forward Deployed Engineering と組むと書かれた職です[9]。

"You do not need to write production code. However, you must be able to reason through technical constraints in a structured way and effectively isolate problems." What should job seekers and hiring companies read into these job postings?

For job seekers, the most reliable way to evaluate a posting is not the job title but the four points in the body text: who the customer is, whether you write code yourself, the language requirement, and the travel percentage. For hiring companies, the companies posting these jobs are the ones competing for talent in Japan, and being specific about compensation, travel, and language is a way to differentiate. Below is our editorial take.

需要の見通しには慎重な声もあります。TechCrunchは2026年7月、エグゼクティブサーチ会社 Christian & Timbers の調査をもとに、FDEの需要が急速に高まっていると報じました[33]。同社の創業者 Jeff Christian 氏は同じ記事で、2年後にはすべてが自動化されているかもしれず、それは起こりうることだとも述べています[33]。

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhich companies are posting FDE jobs that allow working in Japan?

As of September 29, 2026, the public job boards of the 132 companies we track contained 16 FDE postings based in or covering Japan. The companies are OpenAI, Databricks, Notion, Palantir, Cohere, Cursor, Rogo, and Wonderful (8 companies). We counted only postings with "Forward Deployed" or similar in the job title, and excluded postings from domestic Japanese companies.

QHow much English is required to apply for FDE jobs in Japan?

Of the 16 postings as of September 29, 2026, many required business-level English or English usable in professional work; Databricks, Cohere, and Wonderful fall into this category. OpenAI requires fluency in both Japanese and English, stating that resumes should be in English and interviews conducted in both languages. Notion's Forward Deployed Architect listed English as a preferred qualification.

QIs OpenAI hiring FDEs in Tokyo?

As of September 29, 2026, OpenAI's public job board had 3 FDE-related postings based in Tokyo: Forward Deployed Engineer, Deployment Lead, and Manager. The Forward Deployed Engineer role requires 5+ years of experience and Japanese-English bilingual skills, is hybrid with 3 days per week in the office, and states that travel is mainly within Japan. No compensation is listed.

QDo FDE jobs in Japan list salaries?

Of the 16 postings as of September 29, 2026, 14 had no compensation listed. The only ones that did were Palantir's 2 US government roles, at $135,000-$200,000 and $110,000-$170,000 per year. OpenAI and Notion list salary ranges for the same roles based in the US, but not for their Tokyo postings.

QWhat is Palantir's Japan Forward Deployed role like?

It is a role supporting US government work, with the posting noting that the work location is Tokyo. The location on the job board is Washington, D.C. The required qualification is an active Top Secret clearance, and no Japanese language requirement is stated. Because it differs in nature from roles serving Japanese customer companies, we treated it as a separate category in this article. Information as of September 29, 2026.

QIs Anthropic hiring FDEs in Japan?

According to data collected by the editorial team as of September 29, 2026, Anthropic's public job board listed 7 job postings with "Forward Deployed" in the job title, but none were based in Japan. However, an Applied AI Engineer position based in Tokyo was posted, a role that develops LLM solutions for Japanese companies and also supports their deployment. It requires native-level Japanese and business-level English. This article treats it as an adjacent role and does not count it among FDE postings.
