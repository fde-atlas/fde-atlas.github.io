Author: Lu Hanzhi Editor: Li Na

Currently, tech companies are in a frenzy to snatch up FDE talent.

On June 30 local time, Amazon announced an investment of $1 billion to build an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) team, allowing AI engineers to work directly with customers and help them build and deploy AI systems.

Amazon said that FDE embedded teams will work closely with customers' business, engineering, and security teams to shorten deployment cycles from months to days. In addition, after customers complete an AWS FDE deployment, they not only gain new solutions but also acquire new engineering capabilities. Currently, companies such as the Allen Institute, NBA, NFL, and Ricoh have already partnered with the AWS FDE team.

The FDE concept was introduced to the business world by big data company Palantir, referring to engineers who have both programming skills and an understanding of business scenarios. Their responsibilities include being on-site with customers and building solutions in real data and business environments.

Currently, tech companies are in a frenzy to snatch up FDE talent. Data released by recruiting platform Paraform in April showed that from January to September 2025, postings for FDE-related positions surged by more than 800%. On the Paraform platform alone, FDE job postings in the first quarter of 2026 grew 350% year over year.

Paraform also observed that demand is not concentrated in any particular sector. Among companies recruiting FDEs on the Paraform platform, 59% are at the seed to Series A funding stage, 27% are AI-native companies whose company names or products are related to artificial intelligence, and 35% are at Series B or later funding stages.

Based on the above data, Paraform concluded that this is a structural shift: as AI products move from research labs to enterprise deployment, someone must ensure they work properly, which highlights the importance of FDEs.

Industry giants have chosen to create companies to provide FDE services to customers.

On May 4, Anthropic announced a partnership with Blackstone, Goldman Sachs, and other companies to establish an AI enterprise services company, aimed at integrating Claude into customers' core business operations.

On May 11, OpenAI announced the establishment of OpenAI Deployment Company, aimed at helping enterprises build and deploy AI systems that can run reliably in important daily work. To quickly build service capabilities, OpenAI acquired Tomoro, an AI consulting and engineering company. The acquisition brought OpenAI Deployment Company approximately 150 experienced Forward Deployed Engineers and deployment experts, ready for use from the day the company was founded.

Regarding the reason for establishing OpenAI Deployment Company, OpenAI stated: "We firmly believe that building powerful AI models is only part of the job. Real impact comes from helping individuals and organizations use these systems safely, efficiently, and at scale."

Some companies are offering high salaries for FDE positions. A Google job posting shows that the base annual salary for a Google Cloud Forward Deployed Engineer IV is $207,000 to $301,000, with the job described as "requiring frequent travel, with the main responsibility being the technical delivery of conversational AI pilot projects."

Meanwhile, FDE positions posted on the Paraform platform have base salaries ranging from $150,000 to $217,000, with a median of $183,000.
