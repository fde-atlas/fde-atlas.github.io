FREE Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Interview Prep

maven.com · 2026-09-15 · 1 min read

Summary

A free 60-minute Maven session on preparing for Forward Deployed Engineer interviews. It argues that FDE loops differ from standard SWE interviews: instead of LeetCode-style coding, they test building production AI systems (tools, MCP, evals), scoping messy customer problems, and judgment under ambiguity. The session covers 7+ interview stages, including applied AI design, customer discovery, case and behavioral rounds, and mission fit. It notes FDE roles command $1M+ packages at Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google, and that strong engineers often fail by preparing for the wrong interview format.

Key points

  • Free 60-minute virtual session on FDE interview prep.
  • FDE loops have 7+ stages beyond coding, including customer discovery.
  • Covers building AI systems with tools, MCP, and evals.
  • Behavioral prep uses STAR stories and discovery-first thinking.
  • FDE packages exceed $1M at Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google.

Why this topic matters

FDE roles are among the hottest AI jobs right now $1M+ packages at Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, but also the easiest to prep for wrong. Most candidates walk in ready for a typical SWE loop: LeetCode, system design, the usual. FDE interviews test something different can you build AI systems for the real world, scope messy customer problems, and make judgment calls under ambiguity. Miss that, and even strong engineers fail.
