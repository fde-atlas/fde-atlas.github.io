Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Guides by Company

The Forward Deployed · 2026-09-28 · 1 min read

Summary

This article compiles Forward Deployed Engineer interview guides for OpenAI, Anthropic, Palantir, Google Cloud, and startups. Each guide outlines the interview stages, what each round tests, representative questions, and a two-week prep plan. OpenAI's loop includes a take-home on its APIs with a recorded walkthrough plus an open customer solution-design round. Anthropic features a customer-conversation simulation, a Claude deployment design round, and a values interview reported as the hardest stage. Palantir has the best-documented loop, centered on a decomposition round. Google Cloud combines standard engineering interviews with integration-heavy system design and a hiring committee. Startup loops are less structured, broader, and include a founder round. Where available, question banks collect reported system-design questions with full solutions—17 for OpenAI and 7 for Anthropic.

Key points

  • OpenAI's loop: API take-home, technical deep dive, and open customer design round.
  • Anthropic adds a values interview widely reported as the hardest stage.
  • Palantir's loop is the best documented, built around decomposition rounds.
  • Google Cloud pairs standard engineering screens with integration-heavy system design.
  • Startup loops are less structured, broader, and include a founder round.
