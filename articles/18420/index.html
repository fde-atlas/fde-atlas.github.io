Official definition: What is an FDE

An FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is an engineering role that deploys AI applications directly at customer sites and is end-to-end accountable for delivery outcomes.

Palantir gave the earliest public definition in its 2020 IPO prospectus (S-1): FDEs are "the front line for identifying R&D opportunities"—they don't simply "hand over" R&D results to customers; instead, they work on-site at the customer to discover "what is worth building," while also taking on the work of "enabling multiple capabilities for a single customer" (source: Palantir's 2020 IPO prospectus, verifiable on your own).

OpenAI's official Forward Deployed Engineering page describes how the role works: Map your processes, Define success, Prove it in production, Scale what works. This course's five-step delivery framework (Discover → Prototype → Validate → Go live → Scale) is a course framework synthesized from official practices and the publicly documented approaches of multiple FDE teams, not an official formulation. The official page also uses the "deployment gap" to explain the primary reason the role exists—between model capability and actual enterprise output lies a gap in data, processes, permissions, organization, and compliance (source: OpenAI official page, as of 2026-10, verifiable on your own).

The minimal common core of an FDE is five links: discover problems on-site → build quickly → validate with quantifiable metrics → deliver to production → consolidate learnings. Remove any one link and the role drifts toward an adjacent one: without "on-site," it leans toward pure R&D; without "quantifiable validation," it leans toward demo engineer; without "consolidating learnings," it leans toward on-site outsourcing.

Why this role is exploding in 2026

As of 2026-11, multiple public data sources cross-confirm the same conclusion: FDE is currently the fastest-growing engineering role, and the primary driver of growth is the "deployment gap."

  • US hiring data: Lightcast data shows fewer than 200 FDE job postings in all of 2023, but over 7,300 in the first nine months of 2026, with a median advertised salary of about $195K (reported by CNBC, as of 2026-10).
  • Global data: LinkedIn Economic Graph shows global FDE job postings grew 42x from 2023 to 2025, versus 13x for AI engineers over the same period (source: LinkedIn Economic Graph public data, verifiable on your own).
  • China data: Liepin Big Data Research Institute reports an average annual salary of RMB 408,000 for domestic FDEs and a supply-demand ratio of 0.28 (roughly 3.5 openings competing for 1 job seeker); the report was published in 2026-09.

For the full frequency table of this initial sample of 100 global company JDs, the recalculation methodology, and notes on "how to read this data and what it cannot be used for," see FDE Job Research: Initial Dual Samples from China × Global (2026-11 Snapshot).
