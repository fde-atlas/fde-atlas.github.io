FDE Times · 2026 · 4 min read

Summary

This FDE Times skills guide aggregates hands-on articles across categories such as customer work, full-stack engineering, deployment, AI applications, value measurement, and careers. Topics include converting HL7 to FHIR in Python, automatic rollback with Docker Compose, schema-based testing with Schemathesis, MCP pagination limits, filtering LLM-generated eval cases, and analyzing job postings from Anthropic and Databricks. It cites MIT NANDA research that 95% of 300 enterprise AI projects produced no measurable profit, explaining why AI labs compete for forward deployed engineers.

Key points

  • Organizes FDE skill articles by category with steps and exercises.
  • Covers HL7-to-FHIR conversion, Docker Compose rollback, and OpenAPI testing.
  • AI topics include MCP pagination, debugging agents, and eval case filtering.
  • Cites MIT NANDA: 95% of 300 enterprise AI projects lacked measurable profit.
  • Career sections analyze Anthropic and Databricks hiring and interview prep.
