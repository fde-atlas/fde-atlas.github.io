FDE Services for Small and Medium Enterprises

Original title: 中小企業向けFDEサービス

株式会社Walkers · 2026 · 2 min read

Summary

This is a service page from Japanese company Walkers promoting an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) offering for small and mid-sized businesses. AI-skilled personnel are dispatched into client organizations to deliver solutions, from market research and new business creation to process automation and internal capability building. The page highlights fast placement (as soon as the same day), end-to-end support across planning, development, operations, and improvement, and a six-step engagement flow ending with knowledge transfer so clients can sustain improvements independently.

Key points

  • Walkers offers a dispatch-style FDE service for SMBs.
  • AI talent embeds in client organizations to drive change.
  • Covers both new business creation and operational efficiency.
  • Claims placement in as little as one day.
  • Six-step flow ends with internalization and self-sufficiency.
