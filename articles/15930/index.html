Job Profile: OpenAI FDE — Ownership from Prototype to Production Written on the First Line of Responsibilities

Original title: 岗位档案：OpenAI FDE——从原型到生产的所有权写在职责第一行

FDE中国 · 2026-09-04 · 1 min read

Summary

OpenAI is hiring Forward Deployed Engineers (SF), with the job description's first line assigning end-to-end ownership of technical delivery from first prototype to stable production. Roles are split by industry: Healthcare (SF/NYC/Seattle), Legal (NYC), and Gov (Washington DC), with the Gov role requiring government-domain experience and possibly a security clearance. The editor reads this as evidence that FDE at OpenAI is industry-specialized and institutionalized rather than a generalist pool, aligned with the company's broader deployment strategy. Third-party estimates put total compensation at $350K–$550K, though unofficial. Practical advice: put industry delivery track record ahead of tech stack on your resume.

Editor's Note FDEChina Editorial Team

The signals in this JD come down to two words: ownership and industry specialization. "Own… technical delivery" writes end-to-end responsibility into the first line — no handoffs, no fragmentation from prototype to production. Positions are set up separately for Healthcare, Legal, and Gov, with the Gov role requiring government-domain experience and possibly a security clearance, showing that FDE at OpenAI has already become industry-specialized and institutionalized, not a general pool. Third-party estimates of total compensation at $350K–$550K are unofficial figures and should be noted as such when cited.

For job seekers, the incremental value is "targeted application": industry segmentation means your past industry experience itself is your application qualification — the Healthcare role wants a track record of healthcare-domain delivery, not general LLM tuning experience. For ecosystem observers, OpenAI simultaneously built out its Deployment Company to expand its delivery system; the FDE role definition and the company's delivery strategy are one and the same. Action recommendation: rearrange the first screen of your resume, putting industry delivery experience before your tech stack.
