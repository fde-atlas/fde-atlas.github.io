Degirize, with a track record of supporting AI adoption at a cumulative total of 560 companies, officially launches its FDE-type AI in-house development support service "Degirize FDE"

Original title: 累計560社のAI導入支援実績をもつデジライズ、FDE型AI内製化支援「デジライズFDE」を本格始動

デジライズ（Digirise） · 2026-06-25 · 10 min read

Summary

Japanese AI consulting firm Digirise has launched "Digirise FDE," a Forward Deployed Engineer-style service supporting AI development and in-house capability building. Drawing on its track record of supporting over 560 companies and roughly 40,000 trainees, the service assigns dedicated AI engineers who embed at client sites, covering AI diagnostics, PoC, full system development, and company-wide adoption. Digirise frames this with a five-level AI maturity model, with FDEs handling levels 3-5, and is also recruiting partner firms to supply FDE resources. Reported cases include a manufacturer where an on-site FDE built a knowledge AI agent automating about 55% of internal inquiries.

Key points

  • Digirise launched "Digirise FDE," an FDE-style AI in-house development support service.
  • The company has supported 560+ companies and ~40,000 trainees in AI adoption.
  • Dedicated engineers work on-site 1-5 days weekly, from diagnosis through full development.
  • A five-level AI maturity model assigns FDEs to levels 3-5 (workflow, coding, full development).
  • A manufacturing client automated ~55% of internal inquiries with an FDE-built knowledge AI agent.

Full text

Digirize Inc. (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo / Representative Director: Masahiro Chayen; hereinafter "the Company") has officially launched "Digirize FDE," an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)-type AI development and in-house capability building support service, as a new offering from its Consulting Division. A dedicated AI engineer embeds themselves in the client company's workplace and provides end-to-end support, from AI diagnostics and PoC through full-scale system development and company-wide adoption. At the same time, the Company has begun recruiting FDE partner companies to collaborate on this service.

■Background: Companies that built a foundation through AI training are increasingly demanding "what comes next" Through corporate AI reskilling and AI utilization consulting, the Company has supported the development of AI talent at a cumulative total of more than 560 companies and over 40,000 participants, and in May 2026 achieved the No.1 number of corporate clients for AI reskilling services (according to a survey by Tokyo Shoko Research). Companies that built a foundation through training are now clearly raising expectations for the "next stage": "We want to rebuild our business processes themselves with AI."

Companies that have introduced generative AI, trained employees to use it effectively, and automated routine tasks increasingly want to go a step beyond using a "smart Google" or "time-saving tools" and "own in-house" full-scale AI systems that drive business productivity. Digirize FDE is what makes that "next step" happen, working hands-on alongside teams in the field.

In fact, worldwide, in May 2026 Fujitsu partnered with US-based Anthropic and OpenAI to launch an initiative to dispatch AI engineers to client companies and guide them through to problem resolution—an approach known as "FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)"—and the FDE model is rapidly spreading to major Japanese companies as a "standard model" among AI-leading enterprises. However, the reality is that mid-sized companies, which find it hardest to secure specialized talent, are the least able to benefit from it.

Building on know-how systematized through training and consulting engagements with 560 companies, the Company offers "Digirize FDE" as a service to close this "implementation gap."

■Digirize's "5-Level AI Maturity Model"

The Company defines corporate AI adoption maturity in five levels. Levels 1-2, which AI training raises, are the solid foundation on which all further development depends. On top of that foundation, specialized engineers who embed themselves in the field and work hands-on with teams drive progress to Levels 3-5.

●Lv.1 General AI Mastery: Employees use generative AI effectively in daily work (delivered by: AI training) ●Lv.2 Routine Automation: Routine and repetitive tasks are automated with AI (delivered by: AI training) ●Lv.3 Workflow Design: Cross-departmental business processes are redesigned with AI (delivered by: Digirize FDE) ●Lv.4 Lightweight Coding: AI apps and automation are built in-house, led by the field (delivered by: Digirize FDE) ●Lv.5 Full-Scale Development: AI systems integrated into core business operations are fully developed (delivered by: Digirize FDE)

Digirize FDE builds on the "Level 2" foundation established through training and lifts companies toward full-scale productivity gains.

■Three Key Features of "Digirize FDE"

FEATURE 01 | A dedicated AI engineer, on-site at your company The FDE works on-site 1 to 5 days a week and provides daily support via Slack/Teams. With a proximity that lets "quick questions" be answered immediately, the FDE becomes deeply embedded in day-to-day operations.

FEATURE 02 | Training × Consulting × Development, end to end We don't stop at a proposal document. Using Claude Code, Cursor, Dify, and other tools, we implement "working AI" and include employee education, operational rules, and governance setup within the scope.

FEATURE 03 | Vendor-neutral / support for subsidy utilization We design the optimal AI stack without being tied to specific SaaS or models. We also support cost-effective adoption through the use of various government subsidies.

■How Our Support Works (3 Phases)

●Phase 1: AI Diagnosis & Interviews (1-2 weeks): Department-by-department interviews / issue mapping / prioritization of initiatives / execution plan development ●Phase 2: Individual Consulting & PoC (3 months and up): Priority theme selection / prompt and workflow design / PoC and impact validation / KPI design ●Phase 3: Full-Scale AI System Development (3 months and up): Full MVP development / system integration / permissions and logging setup / operational framework building / maintenance and improvement

■Implementation Examples of Digirize FDE

In the implementation phase of Digirize FDE, we combine our "Claude Code Corporate Adoption Support" (approximately 60 video lessons × hands-on sessions × FDE accompaniment) to create a state where even non-engineers can build "working things" in-house. Examples of tasks actually streamlined with Claude Code: ●Issue inventory → instant mockups: Business issues are listed and prioritized, and the first one is immediately turned into a prototype. ●In-house development of internal dashboards and business apps: Inquiry management, KPI visualization, and more built with no-code/low-code. ●Automation of Excel and PowerPoint tasks: Automatic generation of routine reports, aggregations, documents, and manuscript checks. ●Automation of meeting minutes, email, and inquiry handling: Draft generation, summarization, and FAQ creation shorten daily work. ●Prototype → production implementation: Working with an FDE, projects move from prototype to production systems in as little as two weeks. ●Quality assurance with E2E testing: A single phrase—"run E2E tests"—raises the operational accuracy of deliverables.

■Client Case Studies (Selected Excerpts)

The Company's development and consulting support spans a wide range of industries, including wholesale, retail, e-commerce, professional services, food service, manufacturing, real estate, IT, finance, and elder care. Below is a selection, with company names withheld.

- Major internet group: The Company served as the group's AI advisor, driving generative AI adoption company-wide, contributing to the creation of approximately 6,000 hours of work time per month across the group.

- Law firm (marketing support for professional services): Built an AI advisor plus automated email drafting workflow, reducing email writing by approximately 85% and document creation by approximately 75%, and creating approximately 90 hours per month (in one case, 3 hours of daily work was reduced to under 30 minutes).

- Major retailer and e-commerce company (approximately 1,600 employees): Supported company-wide AI adoption through "7 workstreams × biweekly working groups." Automated meeting minutes, survey aggregation, and schedule registration, and reported results at a company-wide meeting of 800 employees.

・Tax Accounting Firm / M&A Brokerage: Contract development of an AI-powered M&A brokerage platform. Implemented end-to-end, from client and negotiation management to corporate valuation, automatic generation of business plans, acquisitions, and PMI plans, and a tax advisory chatbot supervised by tax accountants.

・Food Service and Hospitality: Contract development of an AI system handling core operations for kitchens and stores, with delivery and acceptance completed. This developed into a maintenance contract, and requirements definition for the next core system has also begun.

・Real Estate: In addition to adding AI features to a business system (Kintone), the engagement expanded multifacetedly from a single client—including an AI advisor, company-wide AI reskilling training, and AI co-development with a subsidiary—and has moved into the maintenance phase.

・IT and Telecommunications: Built and delivered business automation workflows using Dify + n8n. A publicly announced track record, including publication of a case study interview article and permission to display the client's logo.

・Wholesale and Distribution (Business-use Alcohol Wholesale): Through Claude Code consulting, identified and prioritized 18 business challenges, and as a first step, built a mock-up of a dashboard for tracking the progress of LINE inquiries.

・Major Manufacturer: An FDE was on-site full-time for about 3 months and implemented an internal knowledge AI agent through full development. Automated approximately 55% of internal inquiry handling.

※These results are achievements as of the time of support and do not guarantee effectiveness. In this release, all client names are withheld and described by industry.
