Salesforce Announces "Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network" to Accelerate Agentforce Deployment

Original title: Salesforce、Agentforceの展開を加速する「Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network」を発表

Salesforce · 2026-04-20 · 12 min read

Summary

Salesforce has launched the Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network, a selective certification program that gives vetted global partners access to internal engineering knowledge and product roadmap insights to help enterprises move Agentforce from pilots to production. Launch partners include Accenture and Deloitte, plus over 30 firms such as PwC, Slalom, and IBM Consulting; Japanese members include TerraSky, Flect, and Globalway. Members must have proven Agentforce deployments and already account for roughly a third of successful implementations. The delivery model covers solution architecture, AI agent design, post-launch hypercare, and change management, with partner incentives tied to production outcomes.

Salesforce Japan (Headquarters: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; Chairman and CEO: Shinichi Koide; hereinafter "Salesforce") today announced the "Forward Deployed Engineering Partner Network," a new partner program designed to accelerate the rollout of Agentforce. By providing advanced engineering expertise and product roadmap insights to a select group of global partners, the network supports enterprises in deploying and scaling AI agents in production, helping drive measurable business outcomes.

Closing the Gap Between High Ambitions and Real Results

For executives, the experimentation phase of AI is over, and delivering concrete business results is now the expectation. Yet a significant execution gap remains. According to an IDC FutureScape* report, "More than one-third of organizations are stuck in the trial phase of AI experimentation or in one-off solution deployments, and need to shift their focus to enterprise use cases to achieve ROI." While many companies can build initial proofs of concept (PoCs), few have the technical foundation required to scale those pilot projects into systems that can actually run the business.

Transforming into an agentic enterprise requires more than simply deploying models. It demands systems that can analyze proprietary data, operate within complex security environments, and deliver consistent, trustworthy results. Salesforce is meeting this demand by expanding access to its specialized Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) knowledge to customers. This gives enterprises more ways to close the gap between high AI ambitions and realistic outcomes.

Scaling Elite Engineering to Meet Global Demand

For years, Salesforce has supported customers through a portfolio of proven, success-driving solutions, including Professional Services, implementation partners, and Success Plans. With [Agentforce](1] growing faster than anything in Salesforce's history, Salesforce is expanding its investment in these solutions to support tens of thousands of customers worldwide.

With the launch of the Salesforce FDE Partner Network, enterprises can now tap into the advanced engineering know-how needed to achieve large-scale, measurable results. By sharing internal expertise and specialized training with a select group of partners, Salesforce helps customers turn their own business insights into production-ready solutions.

Salesforce President and Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) Miguel Milano said: "Our partner ecosystem is a major competitive advantage, and the Salesforce FDE Partner Network operationalizes that advantage for the 'age of AI agents.' The most successful organizations don't just invest in Agentforce. They partner with firms that combine engineering and deep industry knowledge to transform the technology into an 'agentic enterprise.' This network gives partner firms a direct connection to our internal product teams, reducing deployment risk and solving complex data challenges through specialized support that keeps pace with Salesforce's speed of innovation. By also tying partner incentives to AI agents reaching production, Salesforce helps ensure every engagement delivers measurable business value."

Salesforce President of Global Professional Services Lori Steele said: "Bringing AI to production is fundamentally an engineering discipline. We know this because we have done it at enterprise scale. The Salesforce FDE Partner Network extends the skills, capabilities, and high standards we have built internally, scaling talent and partnerships through a select network of partners. For customers, this means faster time to value and absolute confidence in deployments proven in enterprise environments."

Designed for Success, Backed by Proven Expertise

The Salesforce FDE Partner Network is an exclusive certification program that prioritizes technical rigor and proven track records over company size or years in business. Every member is a rigorously vetted partner with demonstrated Agentforce implementation success. To date, companies in the FDE Partner Network have been responsible for one-third of all successful Agentforce implementations. By combining this technical expertise with decades of knowledge across industries such as financial services, healthcare, and manufacturing, these companies ensure that enterprises can build digital workforces with the industry knowledge needed to succeed in complex regulatory environments.

In addition to launch partners Accenture and Deloitte, the network includes global leaders such as Capgemini, Cognizant, IBM Consulting, KPMG, PwC, Slalom, and Tata Consultancy Services, along with local specialists such as InspireAI, OSF Digital, Rosetree Solutions, Twoday, and Oy, as well as more than 20 other partner companies (English). Japanese partner companies include TerraSky, Flect, and Globalway. This global network gives customers access to engineering expertise regardless of location or business model, paving a secure and compliant path to building digital workforces tailored to specific industry requirements.

Manish Sharma, Chief Strategy and Services Officer at Accenture, said: "The Salesforce FDE Partner Network is a critical milestone in our mission to transform enterprises in the age of agentic AI. By embedding Salesforce-trained engineers directly into our delivery teams, we are moving beyond traditional implementation work toward true ecosystem orchestration. For our clients, this means not just 'setting up' Agentforce, but modernizing their digital core so they can operate an integrated agentic workforce at a scale that delivers measurable business value. This production-first model blends human ingenuity with high-velocity engineering, translating business intent into high-performing AI agents in production faster than ever before."

Karl Rupilius, US Salesforce Alliance Lead at Deloitte Consulting LLP, said: "At Deloitte Digital, we believe the agentic front office only creates value when it is trusted from day one, governed, and built for production. The Salesforce FDE Partner Network reflects a critical shift from traditional implementation to embedded engineering. By integrating deep technical knowledge, rigorous data architecture, and compliance from the design stage onward, it gives leadership the confidence to move from pilot to production faster." It is not merely a matter of implementation; the significance lies in how these systems are built from the ground up." Specialized product development outsourcers (PDOs) (English) such as Appiphony, Aquiva Labs, and Bridgenext bring these same high engineering standards to developers building apps for AgentExchange (English). This ensures that the solutions customers purchase are as robust as those they would build themselves, with every application designed to support enterprise-grade production environments from day one. By combining global scale with specialized development expertise, the network offers an unmatched breadth of capabilities to support the most complex business objectives in the era of AI agents.

Methodology: How FDEs embody success

Whether through Salesforce Professional Services or the FDE Partner Network, the delivery model is powered by knowledgeable, outcome-driven teams. These teams work closely with customers to drive everything from high-priority use cases to the realization of AI agent environments that are live in production and compliant.

Examples of the FDE delivery model include the following elements:

  • Solution architecture design: Before a single line of code is written, FDEs define how AI agents will connect to enterprise data, what permissions govern their behavior, and where compliance controls will be placed. This is done in line with Salesforce best practices from the outset, rather than as an afterthought.
  • AI agent design: A detailed blueprint for how AI agents will behave in production. Every decision, action, and escalation path is mapped, with trust guardrails and compliance checks built in before the build phase begins. This eliminates surprises that could derail enterprise deployments at the most critical moments.
  • Production go-live and post-launch hypercare: FDEs remain engaged after launch, monitoring AI agent behavior, resolving issues that only surface in production, and fine-tuning for sustained performance. Launch is not the finish line; it is only the starting line.
  • Adoption and change management: Even a technically flawless AI agent will fail if users do not trust it. FDEs develop and execute adoption plans for readiness, onboarding, and long-term usage.

Conclusion

In the era of AI agents, success is measured by systems running in production, and ultimately by the business value they deliver. Every company depends on its own data and workflows, but the organizations leading the pack are those that have established an engineering discipline to embed that uniqueness directly into their agentic workforce.

Through a combined solution of the Success Plan, Professional Services, and the newly launched FDE Partner Network, Salesforce offers a proven path from AI investment to lasting results. By combining expert support with in-house technical rigor, Salesforce helps ensure every AI agent deployment is high-performing and built for long-term stability.
