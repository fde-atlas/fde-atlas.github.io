Entry-level forward deployed engineer jobs: how much experience FDE postings ask for

FDE Dispatch · 2026-10-27 · 6 min read

Summary

FDE Dispatch analyzed 563 engineer-family FDE postings from 115 employers, pulled October 25, 2026. Of the 328 postings that state a years requirement (58.3%), the median minimum is 5 years, and only 36 (11.0%) ask for two or fewer. 27 postings at 10 employers use early-career titles like New Grad or Junior, with Palantir accounting for 14. The article lists 24 employers with low-experience postings, led by Palantir, Applied Intuition, and C3 AI, and offers advice for early-career engineers: build a measured customer deployment, tailor resumes to top requirements like Python and customer-facing work, and target seats matching their experience floor. Note that 235 postings omit a years figure, which does not mean no experience is expected.

Key points

  • Median stated experience minimum is 5 years across 328 postings that state years.

How many years of experience FDE jobs ask for

What an early-career engineer can do to close the gap

Match the evidence employers already name, then put that evidence on the resume and into the interview loop. No step here promises an offer.

  • Ship one measured customer deployment. Own discovery, an integration into someone else's stack, go-live, and what you measured after launch. That story maps to the top of the skills requirements index: Python (76.2%), customer-facing work (74.1%), and APIs or integrations (69.8%).
  • Rewrite the resume against those lines. Lead with customer-facing delivery, named integrations, and production ownership. The Forward Deployed Engineer resume guide maps each top requirement to resume evidence.
  • Target seats that fit your floor. Filter for early-career titles and for employers that ask for 2 or fewer years. Postings that state no years figure still need a close read: a missing years figure does not mean no experience is expected. Use the how to become an FDE path for the build-and-reposition sequence.
  • Rehearse FDE-specific rounds. Decomposition, messy debugging, constrained system design, work trials, deployment retrospectives, and role-play test the same deployment story. Start with the interview loop overview.

Method and data

Population: 563 engineer-family FDE postings from 115 employers. Posting text pulled October 25, 2026 from the October 24, 2026 snapshot (same population as the skills and requirements index). Internships excluded.

Years = first 'N+ years ... experience' phrase in the posting text (lower bound of a range). That phrase can occasionally name a sub-skill floor, so treat bands as approximate.

Early-career = title contains New Grad, Early Career, Junior, Associate, or level I / I-II (Principal ... I excluded).

122 postings mention a computer science degree in this index. Limits: these are open postings on one pull date, not headcount, and keyword and phrase matching is approximate.

Downloads: experience JSON, field notes, and the per-posting skills JSON. All published datasets are listed on FDE Dispatch job-posting data.

Questions people ask

Can I become a Forward Deployed Engineer without experience?

Sometimes. Of 563 engineer-family FDE postings from 115 employers (October 25, 2026), 36 of the 328 that state years ask for 2 or fewer, and 27 postings at 10 employers use early-career titles such as New Grad, Early Career, Junior, Associate, or level I. 235 postings state no years figure; a missing years figure does not mean no experience is expected. The median stated floor among postings that name years is still 5.

How many years of experience do Forward Deployed Engineer jobs require?

Where a posting states years, the median first stated minimum is 5 years, with the middle half between 4 and 6. 328 of 563 engineer-family FDE postings (58.3%) state a years figure in the October 25, 2026 pull. 163 of those 328 (49.7%) sit in the 5-6 band.

Are there new grad Forward Deployed Engineer roles?

Yes. 27 postings at 10 employers carry early-career titles (New Grad, Early Career, Junior, Associate, or level I). Palantir accounts for 14 of them. Examples include Applied Intuition Forward Deployed Engineer - New Grad (2027), C3 AI Forward Deployed Engineer new-grad seats, Databricks Associate Forward Deployed Engineer, and LangChain Deployed Engineer (Early Career).

Which companies hire entry-level Forward Deployed Engineers?

24 employers have at least one posting asking for 2 or fewer years, led by Palantir (6), Applied Intuition (3), C3 AI (3), Baseten (2), LangChain (2), and Tailscale (2). Employers with early-career titles in this index are Palantir, Applied Intuition, C3 AI, Databricks, DevRev, Giga, Handshake, LangChain, Legora, and Salesforce.

Do Forward Deployed Engineer jobs require a computer science degree?

Most postings in this index don't mention one: 122 of 563 (21.7%) do. The skills index still leads with Python, customer-facing work, and APIs or integrations. Full shares are on Forward Deployed Engineer skills.
