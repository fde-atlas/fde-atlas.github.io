CFDE Programme Now Open for Global Online Enrolment

Artificial Intelligence Uni (AIU.ac) · 2026-06-22 · 3 min read

Summary

Artificial Intelligence Uni (AIU.ac) has opened global online enrolment for its Certified Forward Deployed Engineer (CFDE) programme. Designed in June 2025 and piloted on-premises from August to October 2025, the programme was refined over twelve months using cohort feedback. It cites Live Data Technologies data showing FDE job postings grew 1,165% year-on-year through 2025 as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Databricks and consultancies built dedicated teams. The programme has three levels—Foundation, Professional, Specialist—each 18 weeks (15 modules plus a three-week capstone), with Professional assessed by oral defence. Training and certification exams are separate products, and experienced engineers can take the exam directly via a challenge route. Curriculum draws on real deployments such as PULVINIR Energy, a compliance platform for UK energy operators, and research from the SAIL joint initiative with XEROTECH LTD. Foundation October 2026 cohort applications open 19 August 2026.

Full text

Forward Deployed Engineer job postings grew 1,165% year-on-year through 2025, according to Live Data Technologies, as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Databricks, and the major consulting firms built dedicated teams to embed engineers inside client organisations. The role now has a formal certification pathway in the UK, and today we are opening it for global online enrolment.

We have opened global online enrolment for our Certified Forward Deployed Engineer (CFDE) programme. We designed it in June 2025, delivered it on-premises to the pilot cohort (2025-C0) between August and October 2025, and refined it over twelve months based on cohort feedback and evolving industry demand before opening it for global online delivery.

What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

A Forward Deployed Engineer works inside client organisations to deploy AI systems across the full deployment lifecycle, from post-sale handoff to hand-back. The role originated at Palantir in the early 2010s and is now a standard hiring track at AI-native companies and enterprise technology firms. FDEs write production code, own customer outcomes, and bridge the gap between a vendor’s platform and a client’s operating environment.

“Most of what gets called AI deployment training is really API integration with a certificate at the end,” said Noman Shah, FBCS, our Founder and Programme Lead. “We built this programme around what actually happens when you sit with a client, inherit their broken data pipeline, and have to ship something that works in their environment, not yours. That is a different discipline, and it deserves a serious assessment.”

We offer the programme at three levels. Foundation covers AI deployment at startups and SMEs. Professional covers enterprise environments with legacy infrastructure, compliance frameworks, and multi-stakeholder requirements. Specialist covers regulated industries including financial services, healthcare, government, legal, tax, and education. Each level runs for 18 weeks: 15 weekly modules followed by a three-week capstone, assessed at Professional level through oral defence by a practitioner panel and at Foundation and Specialist level by practitioner reviewers against the programme’s assessment criteria.

We keep the training course and the certification examination as separate products. The course prepares candidates. The exam certifies them. Engineers with relevant professional experience can sit the exam directly through a challenge route without completing the course.

We developed the curriculum from active deployment practice across regulated and general industries, including energy, legal, tax, healthcare, education and governance, and it draws on published research from our Sovereign AI Lab (SAIL), a joint research initiative between AIU and XEROTECH LTD. SAIL has published seven published papers with DOIs, released three open models on HuggingFace, and published one open dataset. The governance and evaluation frameworks from this research inform our Professional-level modules directly.

Participants study real-world deployment case studies including PULVINIR Energy, an AI-powered compliance intelligence platform built for UK energy operators under Ofgem’s NIS assurance framework. The platform combines deterministic rules engines with AI-generated regulatory narrative, and we use it in the curriculum to illustrate AI deployment inside a regulated sector with audit, traceability, and board-level reporting requirements. Participants also work in CallGPT 6X, a privacy-focused multi-model AI workspace that provides unified access to providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google without exposing learner data to third-party servers.

Our academic team includes Dr Atif Naseer, Senior Lecturer at Umm Al-Qura University with over 60 publications, and Dr Ajaz Hussain Hashmi, a dual PhD holder and EU Marie Sklodowska-Curie Fellow. Faculty hold sitting positions at established universities; these are personnel affiliations, not institutional partnerships.

XEROTECH LTD, operates SAIL with us as a joint research initiative, is a partner in Anthropic’s Claude Partner Network.

If you are interested in the programme, you can register your interest and access the prospectus at aiu.ac.
