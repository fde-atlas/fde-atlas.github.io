The China Electronics Standardization Institute of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and Lenovo jointly launch FDE certification, becoming the industry's first joint certification

Original title: 工业和信息化部电子工业标准化研究院与联想集团联合推出FDE认证 成为行业首个联合认证-品玩

Full text

Recently, Lenovo Group announced that the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) training and certification program, jointly launched by the company and the China Electronics Standardization Institute of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, has been officially released. According to reports, this is the industry's first FDE certification jointly and authoritatively recognized by both an enterprise and a professional talent standardization institution, marking a shift in the cultivation and evaluation of AI engineering delivery talent from spontaneous corporate exploration toward industry standardization and normalization, and providing a reusable reference benchmark for talent development and professional competency evaluation in FDE roles.

Caption: Certificate sample; the actual certificate issued shall prevail

Institutions and enterprises jointly building a standardized training path

As artificial intelligence accelerates its integration into enterprise business scenarios, the market is placing higher demands on versatile talent who understand both business needs and possess technical implementation and engineering delivery capabilities. In the view of industry insiders, the barrier to enterprise AI no longer lies in models, but in delivery. Enterprise customers' concerns have shifted from model selection to engineering implementation—how agents can be embedded into existing business processes, how to integrate with legacy systems and private data, how to run stably under security and compliance requirements, and who will operate and how to iterate after delivery.

Dai Wei, Senior Vice President of Lenovo Group and General Manager of China Solutions and Services Group, said that the core competitiveness of enterprise AI implementation is actually people. FDE is not an optional new title, but a verifiable delivery capability line.

Against this backdrop, FDE is gradually becoming a key role connecting AI technology with industry applications. How to establish standardized training and evaluation mechanisms to objectively assess engineers' professional knowledge and actual delivery capabilities has become an important issue in industry talent development.

Unlike training that focuses solely on product knowledge and tool operation, the joint certification launched by the China Electronics Standardization Institute of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and Lenovo Group places greater emphasis on the comprehensive capabilities of enterprise AI engineering delivery. Through professional assessments combining theoretical exams and hands-on engineering practice, it systematically examines FDE personnel's professional knowledge, scenario understanding, and actual delivery capabilities. The deep integration of professional industry evaluation and enterprise practical experience makes this certification both reflective of professional technical requirements and grounded in standardized, professional evaluation, providing a more credible reference for different enterprises and industries to identify talent capabilities.

Industry observers believe this collaboration fully leverages the complementary strengths of both parties: on one hand, Lenovo Group has accumulated deep experience in enterprise AI engineering practice; on the other, the China Electronics Standardization Institute of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology possesses professional capabilities in industry standard research and professional examination evaluation. Based on the standard requirements relevant to the talent role and combined with Lenovo's practical experience in enterprise AI solution design, project implementation, and continuous operation, the certification builds a training path covering course learning, engineering practical training, and professional assessment. Those who pass the training and examination evaluation will receive a joint certification certificate for AI professional technical personnel, supporting the standardized cultivation and professional competency evaluation of FDE talent.

Three capability series, approximately 120 hours of investment, hands-on training spanning the full delivery chain

Around FDE capability requirements, the two parties jointly built an end-to-end training system oriented toward the full delivery chain. The courses are organized into three series—enterprise AI design methodology, agent workshops and AI foundational capabilities, and project delivery, implementation, and operations—with a total investment of approximately 120 hours, spanning the complete chain from business scenario identification, solution design, on-site delivery, to continuous operation.

As the concrete implementer and resource provider of this certification, Lenovo Solutions and Services has systematically injected practical experience into course design based on delivery methodologies and cases accumulated in frontline project practice, making the training content closer to real delivery scenarios.

The training investment is clearly tilted toward practice, with most of the time devoted to after-class exercises and integrated comprehensive practical training, all completed in pre-configured environments. Learners receive accounts, pre-configured projects, data, templates, and authorized interfaces, and independently complete building, debugging, and evidence submission, rather than remaining at the level of demonstrations and observation. Five types of industry solutions—intelligent supply chain, production operations, education, smart cultural tourism, and the Leda agent—run through the teaching and practical training; learners study all of them and choose one to complete the integrated practical training.

The training path forms a closed loop of four stages: online theoretical learning, phase assessments, workshops with mentor-led practice, theoretical and hands-on exams, and certification. The assessment includes both a theoretical exam and a hands-on exam, the latter requiring completion of designated tasks in a pre-configured environment with submission of process and result evidence—meaning that passing the exam is based not on how many questions were answered correctly, but on whether the delivery actions were genuinely completed and can withstand review.

Notably, the FDE training system lists the security and compliance capabilities unique to enterprise agents as mandatory content. The courses cover risks such as prompt injection, knowledge base poisoning, tool misuse, multi-user memory isolation, credential management, and human confirmation for high-impact operations, and the delivery practice segment includes security and compliance checks with release evidence requirements. This means the delivery party not only promises the system "can run," but can also clearly explain how to degrade and roll back when anomalies occur, and where evidence is retained. As enterprise AI enters core business processes, this capability often determines project success more than a feature checklist.

Lenovo Solutions and Services stated that it will continue to center on the goal of "delivering visible AI value," accumulating courses, cases, mentors, and practical training resources, promoting the transformation of individual experience into organizational capability assets, and forming an FDE talent development path that is learnable, practicable, evaluable, certifiable, and reusable, accompanying customers through the complete cycle from planning and construction to operation and optimization.
