Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer Interview (No Offer): Very smooth and quick process

Taro · 2018-03-24 · 3 min read

Summary

This article compiles interview and employee reviews for Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer role in New York. One candidate, rejected but positive, describes a smooth, fast process: a responsive recruiter, two phone interviews, and fair questions and coding exercises. Based on 36 interview experiences, the pass rate is only 14%, with 56% positive and 17% negative experiences. Current employees rate the company highly, citing smart colleagues, quick promotion to Lead within one to two years, heavy responsibility from day one, and self-directed growth paths. Drawbacks include high autonomy demands, long hours, and restrictions on side projects.

Key points

  • A rejected candidate still praised the smooth, quick interview process
  • Two phone interviews with fair questions and coding exercises
  • Only a 14% pass rate across 36 interview experiences
  • 56% of candidates reported positive interview experiences
  • Employees cite fast growth to Lead but long hours and high autonomy

A great company to grow and learn

Forward Deployed Software Engineer • Current Employee

New York, New York, United States

November 3, 2020

Pros: People are very smart. There is room to grow. It's not uncommon to see people who have been in the company for 1-2 years becoming Leads. Huge responsibilities from day 1. "Choose your own adventure" -> You can create your own growth path in the company if you see the need for it. For example, if you work on X product/customer but you see that there is a need to develop another product, you can start working on that without an issue (as long as you don't jeopardize your other role). D&I is big here. You can get as involved as you want with this, and that's awesome! Overall, this is an amazing company. I love it.

Cons: Too much autonomy at times. This is also a pro, but being independent and figuring out things on your own is not for everyone.

Long hours and hard work, but worth the pay

February 26, 2023

Pros: Great salary. Working with some of the smartest people. Prestige.

Cons: Still a job. Could earn a lot more working a few years and then starting your own company. Long hours. Can't work on side projects or investments.

Great company and culture

August 16, 2024

Pros: Loved the people and the environment.

Cons: More busy at times, but it's the only real con.
