Forward Deployed Engineer jobs in Canada (2026)

FDE Times · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Summary

This is a job-board style roundup of Forward Deployed Engineer openings in Canada, updated 20 Nov 2026. It lists 10 open roles across 7 companies—Cohere (3), Cresta (2), and one each at Sardine, MongoDB, Tailscale, Artefact, and Stripe—sourced directly from company careers pages. Roles span Toronto, Ottawa, Montréal, and remote, covering infrastructure, integrations, industry solutions, sovereign AI, and AI agents, including one internship. The page also compares market sizes elsewhere, such as New York (158) and the San Francisco Bay Area (154).

Key points

  • 10 open FDE roles in Canada across 7 companies.
  • Cohere has the most openings with 3, in Toronto and Ottawa.
  • Roles cover infrastructure, sovereign AI, integrations, and AI agents.
  • Cresta offers both a senior AI agent role and an internship.
  • Listings are pulled from careers pages and updated daily.
