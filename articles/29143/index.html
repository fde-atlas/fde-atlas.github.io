China-FDE-Consulting-Pattern

Original title: China-FDE-Consulting-Pattern

FDE中国社区 · 2026-07-12 · 4 min read

Summary

This article from the FDE China community presents a master playbook for consultative FDE delivery in China, built around a five-ring framework: embedding, consulting, building, operating, and asset consolidation. It specifies a meeting cadence from kickoff to launch gates, four-stage entry/exit criteria (production requires evaluation.md metrics of at least 80%), and role interfaces among FDEs, AIBPs, business owners, and IT/security. It flags three common pitfalls: demo-only delivery, no asset consolidation, and neglecting adoption operations. Positioned as the hub of the community's methodology library, it links to related skills and ships as a downloadable skill package (checklist, prompt, workflow files) importable into Claude, Cursor, or Codex.

Key points

  • Five-ring framework: embed, consult, build, operate, consolidate assets.
  • Four-stage gates; production launch requires evaluation.md metrics ≥80%.
  • Defines handoffs across FDE, AIBP, business owner, and IT/security roles.
  • Three pitfalls: demo-only work, no asset consolidation, ignored adoption.
  • Ships as a skill package importable into Claude, Cursor, or Codex.

Problem definition

Methodology framework

The Five Rings of China FDE

  • Embedding: onboarding, stakeholders, AIBP team formation
  • Consulting: Issue Tree + DIVE + scenario cards
  • Building: three-layer PoC + EDD + private deployment
  • Operating: weekly Demo + adoption growth + customer success
  • Assetization: templates/eval sets/Prompts/Skills accumulation

Meeting cadence

Kickoff → scenario review → weekly Demo → failure retrospective → launch gate → phase retrospective

FAQ

Q1: When executing [China-FDE-Consulting-Pattern], how do you avoid "doing demos without consulting"?

A: Follow the README methodology step by step and present interim deliverables at weekly Demos; if PoC materials are incomplete, first produce a "to-be-validated list" before proceeding.

Q2: What additional constraints apply in private deployment/Xinchuang/MLPS environments?

A: Specify the deployment model in the prompt constraints section; when data cannot leave the domain, domestic components, or audit logging are involved, reference Private-Deployment-Gateway and RBAC-Audit accordingly.

Q3: What if the division of responsibilities with AIBP is unclear?

A: Go back to the AIBP-Collaboration-Playbook to update the RACI; AIBP is responsible for Ground Truth and business acceptance, while the FDE is responsible for deliverable technical assets.

Q4: After the PoC passes, how do we transition to Beta/production?

A: Mark the next-stage Skill in the deliverables (e.g., RAG-Evaluation→Private-Deployment-Gateway→FDE-Adoption-Growth), and trigger the launch gate via the checklist.
