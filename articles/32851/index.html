FDE Hierarchy in India: Career Ladder, Levels, Roles & Pay (2026)

FDE India · 2026-10-30 · 12 min read

Summary

This article maps out the Forward Deployed Engineer career ladder in India for 2026: five levels from Associate FDE (0–2 years) to Director/VP (12+), where each step means owning more of the customer's outcome. It roughly aligns FDE levels with SDE levels, compares titles at Palantir, Databricks, Google and Indian firms, and warns that Indian titles are inconsistent, so responsibilities matter more than names. Published pay benchmarks range from ₹18–26.5 LPA at entry to ₹50–80 LPA for 7–10 years, though fresher listings often pay ₹4–15 LPA. It also covers pod structures, reporting lines (about 45% of roles sit in dedicated FDE teams) and promotion criteria.

Key points

  • The FDE ladder has five levels, each adding ownership of customer outcomes.
  • India pay: roughly ₹18–26.5 LPA entry, ₹50–80 LPA at 7–10 years.
  • Fresher listings post ₹4–15 LPA, well below published benchmarks.
  • Palantir pairs FDSEs (Deltas) with deployment strategists (Echos).
  • About 45% of FDE roles report into dedicated FDE teams, 38% into engineering.

The Forward Deployed Engineer career ladder looks a lot like a normal software engineering ladder, with one difference at every step: how much of the customer’s outcome you own. An Associate FDE delivers pieces of a deployment, a mid-level FDE runs one, a Senior FDE runs the hardest accounts, and a Staff or Principal FDE decides how the whole team deploys.

Here is the full hierarchy: the ladder and what each level does, how it maps to SDE levels, the titles different companies use, how FDE teams and reporting lines are organised, what gets you promoted, and what each step pays in India.

The FDE seniority ladder

Year bands follow FDE Pulse’s level guide; companies use different titles for the same steps.

The year bands below follow FDE Pulse’s level guide. Other sources draw the lines slightly differently: Recruiting from Scratch puts its first two FDE levels at about 1–3 years and Senior FDE at about 3–6 years, so treat the years as a guide, not a rule.

Level Typical experience What you own
Associate / Junior FDE (often “FDE I”) 0–2 yearsSpecific technical tasks inside a larger deployment, under a senior FDE. You are building both your engineering and your customer-communication skills.
FDE / Mid-level (often “FDE II”) 2–5 yearsA full customer deployment on your own: scoping, design, build and managing the customer, typically two or three engagements a year.
Senior FDE 5–8 yearsThe most complex engagements and enterprise accounts, escalations and executive relationships. You mentor junior FDEs and influence the product roadmap.
Staff / Principal / Lead FDE 8–12+ yearsThe FDE playbook itself: deployment methods, tools that make other FDEs faster, patterns across accounts, and technical leadership on flagship customers.
Director / VP of Forward Deployed Engineering 12+ yearsThe whole FDE organisation: hiring, team structure, methodology and business metrics such as deployment speed, retention and expansion.

Two details are worth knowing:

  • Where the IC track ends. Recruiting from Scratch describes the FDE Lead or Principal FDE as the highest pure individual-contributor level. Beyond it, the path moves into management: an FDE Manager who still stays close to customer work, then Director or VP.
  • When pay changes shape. Rocketlane’s blueprint says FDE I and II are usually paid at parity with equivalent engineering levels, while Senior and above often add a variable component tied to the FDE team’s main business metric.

FDE levels vs SDE levels

Indian engineers usually ask “which level would I join at?” There is no universal mapping, but by experience the steps line up roughly like this:

FDE level Typical years Rough SDE equivalent Google-style level
Associate FDE / FDE I 0–2 SDE-1 L3
FDE / FDE II 2–5 SDE-2 L4
Senior FDE 5–8 SDE-3 / Senior L5
Staff / Principal FDE 8–12+ Staff engineer L6+

This mapping is our reading of the year bands, not a published standard. What sources do say: Scaler maps Google’s FDE roles to its L4 and L5 engineering bands, Rocketlane says FDE I and II are paid at parity with equivalent engineering levels, and Insight Partners reports that ServiceNow calibrates FDE pay to the top of its engineering ladder. Databricks blends FDE pay across software engineering, product management and consulting benchmarks. In practice, many companies treat the customer-facing load as a premium on top of the matching SDE level.

FDE titles at different companies

The same rung can carry very different names:

  • Palantir, which created the role, splits it in two: forward deployed software engineers (internally “Deltas”) build and ship the code, while deployment strategists (“Echos”) own the customer problem, adoption and results, according to Second Talent’s analysis of Palantir’s postings. Palantir hires FDSEs from about one year out of college.
  • Databricks and Wonderful use a similar pair: an FDE as the technical lead and a deployment strategist who owns strategy and the customer relationship (Insight Partners).
  • Workato’s FDE ladder tops out at an “architect” FDE, supported by a hybrid delivery team (Insight Partners).
  • Rocketlane describes five FDE “shades” by deal size, from deployment strategists on smaller accounts to deployment engineers, innovation FDEs, implementation FDEs and agent-PM pods on the largest ones.
  • Google uses numbered titles such as Forward Deployed Engineer II and III alongside its L-levels.

In India, titles are less settled. Razorpay hires a single “Forward Deployed Engineer” title across 3–10 years of experience. Sarvam AI uses “Forward Deployed Software Engineer”. IT services firms use FDE titles inside large delivery organisations: an Infosys “Senior FDE” listing on Foundit was tagged 0–1 years and fresher, despite the senior title and client-leadership duties, and TCS talks about “forward-deployed AI engineers”. So read the responsibilities and experience asked, not the title, to work out the real level.

How FDE teams work day to day

On a single deployment, the hierarchy is flatter and more practical. Across the sources, a typical engagement looks like this:

  • Lead / Principal or Senior FDE: runs discovery and scoping with the customer, sets the technical approach and architecture, defines success metrics and owns the handover.
  • FDE (builder): writes the core code, custom integrations and agent workflows, and iterates with the customer’s users.
  • Deployment strategist (at some companies): owns the customer relationship, adoption and business results, so the FDE can stay technical. Palantir, Databricks and Wonderful all use this pairing.
  • Specialists who rotate in: Insight Partners describes the pod, not the single engineer, as the unit of delivery: one FDE owns the problem, and data science, data engineering and full-stack specialists join as the problem needs.
    • Data FDEs build the pipelines that move customer data into the product. Razorpay’s FDE listing, for example, asks for data pipelines alongside APIs and webhooks.
    • ML-leaning FDEs tune models, prompts and evaluations. Google Cloud’s Applied AI FDE role in Bengaluru asks for experience in speech, reinforcement learning or ML infrastructure (our Google guide).
    • Platform and infrastructure FDEs run agents and services inside the customer’s cloud. Sarvam AI matches some FDEs to a team building MCP servers, multi-agent systems and event-driven pipelines (our Sarvam AI guide).
    These are specialisations within FDE teams, not separate standard job titles. Most listings still use one FDE title and describe the speciality in the job description.

How big these teams get depends on the company’s stage. Recruiting from Scratch suggests about one FDE per major customer early on, rising to two or three for large enterprises with several workstreams, and adding a dedicated FDE Manager once a team reaches four or five FDEs. Rocketlane describes FDE Managers running pods of four to eight FDEs. Recruiting from Scratch’s 12-week delivery model (discovery, foundation, value delivery, handoff) is a good picture of what one engagement feels like from the inside.

Who FDEs report to

Where the FDE team sits shapes your growth:

  • A dedicated FDE team is the most common home. Tandem’s analysis of FDE job postings found about 45% of roles on a dedicated FDE team and 38% in engineering, with the rest under sales or customer teams.
  • Product or engineering: ServiceNow puts FDEs in product and R&D, explicitly not go-to-market; Workato places them in product so field learnings reach the roadmap; Databricks keeps them close to product, with engineers rotating both ways (Insight Partners). Recruiting from Scratch also recommends reporting into engineering to protect technical quality.
  • Sales or services: some companies run FDEs inside go-to-market or professional services teams.

What it means for you: under engineering or product, promotion tends to follow the engineering ladder and your work feeds the product. Under sales or services, expect more variable pay tied to revenue and a stronger pull towards solutions architecture. Ask “who does this team report to?” in every FDE interview.

What gets you promoted at each level

Move What the next level expects
Associate → FDE Working independently on build workstreams (Rocketlane expects this within about six months), then running a full engagement from discovery to handoff
FDE → Senior FDE Handling complex, multi-stakeholder accounts, escalations and executive relationships; documenting playbooks; mentoring junior FDEs
Senior → Staff / Principal Spotting architectural patterns across accounts, influencing the product roadmap, building tools that make other FDEs faster, and leading flagship accounts technically
Senior → FDE Manager Coaching and hiring, owning a pod’s results; Rocketlane says FDE Managers are measured on revenue per FDE or platform contributions, plus retention

FDE pay in India by level

Typical range = median of published benchmarks (Taggd, Second Talent, Scaler, Quess via Deccan Herald), 2026. India bands use slightly different year cut-offs from the global ladder above.

Indian salary sources use their own experience bands, so the pay table uses those:

Level (India bands) Typical range What individual sources say
Entry (0–2 years) ₹18–26.5 LPA Taggd ₹18–28 · Second Talent ₹6–14 · Scaler ₹18–25 · Quess (via Deccan Herald) ₹18–28
Mid (3–6 years) ₹30–50 LPA Taggd ₹30–50 · Second Talent ₹14–28 · Scaler ₹30–65 · Quess ₹30–50
Senior (7–10 years) ₹50–80 LPA Taggd ₹50–80 · Second Talent ₹28–65 · Scaler ₹65–120
Staff / Lead (10+ years) ₹72.5–125 LPA Taggd ₹90–150 (leadership) · Second Talent ₹55–100

Three things keep these numbers honest:

  • Entry level is overstated for most freshers. Fresher-tagged FDE listings we tracked in October 2026 posted ₹4–15 LPA, far below the benchmark band. The benchmark reflects strong candidates at well-funded companies.
  • Employer type matters as much as level. As one example, Scaler estimates Google FDE pay in India at ₹35–55 LPA at L4 and ₹55–90+ LPA at L5.
  • Global roles pay in a different league. FDE Pulse lists base pay of about $130,000–165,000 for associate FDEs, rising to $240,000–300,000+ at staff level. Those figures appear to be US numbers, not India pay.

Your roadmap up the ladder

  • Associate → FDE: own one deployment end to end. Ship a project where you did discovery, built it, put it in front of real users and supported it after launch. Rocketlane expects FDE I engineers to work independently within about six months.
  • FDE → Senior FDE: own the hard account. Volunteer for the messy customer: legacy systems, strict security, unhappy stakeholders. Senior FDEs are promoted for handling escalations and executive relationships, not just code.
  • Senior → Staff / Principal: make other FDEs faster. Write the playbook, build the reusable tools, spot patterns across accounts and turn them into product requests. Your impact is now measured across deployments, not inside one.
  • Or switch to management. If you enjoy hiring, coaching and owning team metrics, the FDE Manager role keeps you near customers while you lead a pod.
  • Know your exits. Scaler and FDE Academy list staff or principal engineering, product management, solutions architecture, engineering leadership and founding a company as common next steps. Scaler also notes that some firms run flatter promotion tracks for FDEs than for core engineers, so ask how promotion works in your interviews.

Frequently asked questions

What SDE level is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

Roughly, an Associate FDE lines up with SDE-1, a mid-level FDE with SDE-2 and a Senior FDE with SDE-3 by years of experience, but companies set this differently. Scaler maps Google FDE roles to Google's L4 and L5 engineering levels, and ServiceNow pays FDEs at the top of its engineering ladder, according to Insight Partners.

What is the difference between an FDE and a deployment strategist?

At Palantir, the forward deployed software engineer builds and ships the production code, while the deployment strategist owns the customer problem, adoption and results. Databricks and Wonderful use a similar pairing, with the FDE as technical lead and the strategist owning strategy and the customer relationship.

What are the levels of a Forward Deployed Engineer?

A common ladder is Associate FDE (0–2 years), FDE or mid-level (2–5), Senior FDE (5–8), Staff or Principal FDE (8–12+), then Director or VP of Forward Deployed Engineering (12+). Some companies use FDE I and FDE II instead of Associate and mid-level, and titles vary.

What is the highest individual contributor level for an FDE?

Usually Staff, Principal or Lead FDE. Recruiting from Scratch describes the FDE Lead or Principal FDE as the highest pure individual contributor level; above it, the path moves into FDE Manager and Director roles.

How much does a senior FDE earn in India?

Published benchmarks put FDEs with 7–10 years in India at about ₹50–80 LPA as a typical range, with individual sources ranging from ₹28 LPA to ₹120 LPA. Pay depends heavily on whether the employer is a global platform, GCC, AI startup or IT services firm.

Is FDE a dead-end career?

No. Senior FDEs move into staff or principal engineering, product management, solutions architecture, engineering leadership or founding companies, according to Scaler and FDE Academy. Some firms do have flatter promotion tracks for FDEs than for core engineers, so ask how promotion works before you join.
