What exactly is included?

Twelve months of the Premium tier on the eight platforms listed here. That includes their current Premium libraries and new material added to those tiers during your access period. AI Agent Interviews and Cloud Interview Prep are not part of this pass. Separate services and future products are not automatically included.

How does the learning stay current?

Our team reviews and updates the material as AI and engineering evolve, adding relevant developments alongside the fundamentals. Structured learning paths, worked explanations and practical exercises help you build understanding step by step. New material added to the included Premium tiers is part of your pass during your access period.

Is this useful if I’m still a student?

Yes. You can use the platforms to explore different areas of AI before choosing a specialism, or to build depth alongside your studies. Start with a learning path, work through the explanations, and put the ideas into practice with exercises and labs where available. You don’t need an interview lined up to begin.

Which email should I use?

Use the email of the Google account you’ll sign in with. We verify it before payment. The pass follows that exact address across the eight platforms; a different Google account or email alias will not match.

I already have Premium on one of the platforms.

You keep your account, progress and remaining paid time. On that platform, the pass adds a full year after your existing Premium term. Other included platforms start with your pass. A platform can therefore show a later expiry than your pass receipt.

Do I need to create eight accounts first?

No. Buy once, then visit the platforms when you need them. Sign in with the verified Google account. Existing profiles are kept; a new profile is created on your first sign-in to a platform. Your year starts when payment is confirmed, not when you first visit.

What if I’m already signed in when I buy?

Your pass is linked to your email, so you don’t need to buy again or create another account. Refresh the platform or use its Refresh access option. If Premium hasn’t appeared after a few minutes, contact us with your payment reference.

Does it renew automatically?

No. One payment covers 12 calendar months. We never charge you again automatically. If you buy another pass while yours is active, we ask you to confirm that you want to add another year.

Can I buy Premium on just one platform?

Yes. Individual Premium remains available on each platform’s pricing page. The comparison here uses $25 per 6-month term, as checked on 9 November 2026. Individual prices may change.

What if payment succeeds but access hasn’t appeared?

Payment confirmation can take a few minutes. Keep your payment reference and check the email you verified, including spam. Do not pay again to fix an access delay. Write to support@interviewyoda.com and we’ll help trace the payment.

Can I get a refund?

Please review the All-Access Pass terms before paying. Change-of-mind refunds are not generally offered after activation, subject to rights required by law. For a duplicate charge, incorrect access or another payment issue, contact support@interviewyoda.com with your payment reference.

Who handles payment and my data?

Dodo Payments is our merchant of record and handles payment details and tax invoices. InterviewYoda records your verified email and pass entitlement. Each platform operates under its own terms, additional service conditions and privacy policy.
