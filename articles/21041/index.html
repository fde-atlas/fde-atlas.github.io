Forward Deployed Engineer (Resident)

Original title: Forward Deployed Engineer 常駐

株式会社Tufe Company · 2026 · 4 min read

Summary

Tufe Company, a Japanese firm, offers an on-site Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) service for mid-sized Japanese companies. A senior engineer familiar with Claude, Bedrock, MCP, and Agent SDK embeds at the client for 3-12 months, reporting directly to executives and handling design through implementation and operations for company-wide AI adoption. The pitch stresses no multi-layer subcontracting, client ownership of all code and IP, and contractual knowledge transfer so in-house engineers can operate independently after the FDE leaves. The article cites Pragmatic Engineer's report of a major 2025 uptick in FDE hiring at Anthropic, OpenAI, and others, traces the model to Palantir, and lists industry examples of 6-12 month deployments plus pricing at roughly 1.3-1.5x a senior SI consultant's monthly rate.

Key points

  • Senior engineers embed at clients for 3-12 months to drive company-wide AI adoption.
  • No subcontracting layers; clients own all code, documentation, and IP.
  • Knowledge transfer to in-house engineers is built into the contract.
  • Cites Pragmatic Engineer's report of a major 2025 uptick in FDE hiring.
  • Pricing is roughly 1.3-1.5x a senior SI consultant's monthly rate.

Q. How is an FDE different from a typical SI engagement?

With an FDE, the engineer doing the implementation communicates directly with the person responsible on the ground. This shortens the distance between decision-making and implementation, making it easy to reflect frontline feedback. We will explain the specific team size and roles before contracting.

Q. What is the cost?

Monthly pricing is standard (custom quote). Costs vary with the engagement period, scope of commitment, and depth of in-house development. As a guideline, expect 1.3-1.5x the monthly rate of a senior consultant at a mid-sized SI firm, but ROI tends to be high given the absence of multi-layer subcontracting and full IP ownership.

Q. What if the FDE leaves? How is continuity ensured?

A two-person handover structure within Tufe, documentation, and in-house engineer development are built into the contract. We create a structure that does not depend on a single FDE.

Q. Can we engage for a short period (1-2 months)?

We recommend a minimum of 3 months for an FDE. In 1-2 months, you would only get through relationship building and design. For short-term needs, please consider our other capabilities (e.g., PoC Escape Sprint).

Q. Do we need an internal structure to receive the skills transferred from Tufe?

Ideally, having at least one person in your company to pair with the Tufe FDE accelerates knowledge transfer. If not, we can include recruiting support as well.
