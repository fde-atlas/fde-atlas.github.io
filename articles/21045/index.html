FDE Delivery in Practice: Phased Engineering Drills and Implementation Notes

Original title: FDE 交付实战专题：分期工程演练与落地手记

FDECareer.work · 2026-10-26 · 2 min read

Summary

FDECareer has published a hands-on series on FDE delivery, released in installments, covering field-tested methods for enterprise AI deployment. Articles break down a five-layer architecture for integrating WeCom and DingTalk with legacy ERP systems, multi-agent orchestration guarded by deterministic state machines with checkpoint recovery, and a three-phase delivery lifecycle from scoping to production handover. A separate guide explains how to build verifiable FDE portfolios around engineering evidence rather than tech-stack lists. The series also includes a browser-based sandbox for approval workflows, a hybrid RAG design with row-level RBAC filtering, plus test cases, field worksheets, and offline Python packages.

Key points

  • Installment series of practical FDE delivery methods, sandboxes, and portfolio guidance.
  • Five-layer architecture links WeCom and DingTalk to legacy ERP with 5-second async ACK and RBAC identity mapping.
  • Multi-agent workflows guarded by deterministic state machines with checkpoint recovery and idempotent loop prevention.
  • Standard delivery spans project scoping, high-availability prototyping, production launch, and ops handover.
  • Portfolio guide stresses reproducible evidence: incident reviews, regression tests, and handover assets.
