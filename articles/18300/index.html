Launch of "Results-Driven FDE Service" Supporting Everything from AI Adoption to Business Integration

TECOTEC Co., Ltd. is pleased to announce the launch of its "Results-Driven FDE Service," which provides small and mid-sized enterprises considering AI adoption and DX initiatives with engineers who work alongside their teams to support everything from AI implementation to business improvement and operational integration as a unified service.

This service incorporates the concept of the FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer), an engineering role gaining attention at Silicon Valley tech companies and financial institutions, in which engineers embed themselves in the customer's workplace and provide integrated support from business issue analysis through AI adoption, system development, and operational integration.

In addition, TECOTEC has standardized AI-driven development, a methodology that leverages generative AI and coding agents as the core of design, implementation, and testing to maximize development speed and quality. As a result, there are cases where implementation speed from prototype development to production operation has improved approximately threefold compared to conventional development projects, enabling a development framework that lets customers realize return on investment sooner.

Background of the Service

In recent years, while the need for DX promotion and AI adoption has grown, many companies face challenges such as "AI and systems were introduced but are not used in the field," "return on investment is hard to see," and "operations do not take hold due to a shortage of IT talent." In particular, at small and mid-sized enterprises, the roles of consulting, which handles strategy planning, and engineering, which handles implementation, are often separated, and the situation of "having a plan but implementation not progressing" has become a barrier to DX promotion.

To address this challenge, we focused on the concept of the aforementioned role, the FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer), as a key to the solution.

At TECOTEC, we have built systems supporting advanced data processing in the financial domain through the development and operation of the stock investment management and analysis app "CabuU." In addition, with the ticket sales management system for leisure facilities "RAP" and the direct ticket sales management system "ChikeChoku," we support both the improvement of on-site operations and increased sales in an integrated manner.

By combining our proven track record of launching and growing these services as businesses with the expertise of our BPR (business process reform) division, newly established in July 2025, we have launched the "Results-Driven FDE Service" as a new engineering service that connects technology to business outcomes.

Key Features of the "Results-Driven FDE Service"

Agile development based on "profit" rather than "specifications"

In conventional on-site SES (System Engineering Services), the criteria for results are typically the contracted working hours and whether implementation matches the specification documents. In contrast, TECOTEC's "Results-Driven FDE Service" advances development using the impact on the customer's P&L (profit and loss statement) as the key metric, rather than working hours or adherence to specifications. Based on the analysis of on-site data, we prioritize the design and implementation of features expected to directly contribute to profit, and by flexibly revising the direction of development while regularly reviewing results with the customer, we achieve optimization of development investment and faster revenue generation.

On-site support through to "integration"

TECOTEC goes beyond API integration and tool adoption, embedding itself in the customer's on-site environment and going as far as improving business workflows and integrating with existing systems. By digitizing operations that previously depended on tacit knowledge and shaping them into forms that can actually be operated in the field, we support post-implementation integration and business efficiency. Knowledge and know-how gained during development are shared with the customer's internal members, so we also aim for a framework in which the customer can eventually maintain and extend the system themselves—in other words, in-house development of the system.

Integrated support from prototype to production operation

Rather than ending with the delivery of a consulting report, we provide a working prototype at an early stage. By organizing requirements while operating actual screens and data, we prevent gaps in understanding and achieve a smooth production rollout. Furthermore, by continuing to support post-rollout data analysis and improvement measures, we support continuous business improvement (BPR) and the strengthening of corporate competitiveness.

Fast implementation and improvement cycles through AI-driven development

TECOTEC has standardized AI-driven development methodologies that leverage AI-based code generation, automated testing, and more. By engineers highly skilled in AI tools incorporating AI into the development process, we streamline work that would previously have required multiple engineers, dramatically shortening the period from prototype creation to production operation. As a result, there are cases where system implementation and modification have been improved to approximately three times the speed of conventional development projects, making it possible to provide a development framework that lets customers realize return on investment sooner. By combining AI's automatic generation capabilities with the technical skill of engineers who use it accurately, we achieve a fast, high-quality development cycle.

Solving these business challenges through AI adoption and DX promotion

We support AI adoption and DX promotion tailored to each company's challenges, including the following.

  • Sales improvement through sales data analysis
  • AI automation of inquiry handling
  • AI assistant adoption for internal operations
  • Automation of Excel-based tasks
  • Building data analysis platforms

We design optimal AI adoption and system development aligned with each company's business processes.

Service Implementation Process

We support AI adoption and DX promotion step by step according to each company's challenges. We work alongside you in an integrated manner, from organizing business issues through system implementation and operational integration.

  • Issue Interviews and Business Analysis First, we interview the company about its operations and challenges, and organize on-site workflows and data usage. We identify areas that can be improved through AI adoption or systemization and design the direction of DX promotion.
  • Design of AI Adoption and DX Measures Based on the interview content, we formulate a policy for AI adoption and system development tailored to each company's business challenges. We design measures with prioritization, taking into account return on investment and business impact.
  • Prototype Development and Implementation Leveraging AI-driven development, we develop working prototypes in a short period. By testing in the field and iterating on improvements, we build systems designed with production operation in mind.
  • Operational Integration and Continuous Improvement Even after system rollout, we support operations while monitoring how they are used in the field. We continuously conduct data analysis and business improvement, supporting the company's DX promotion and business efficiency.

If your company has concerns such as the following regarding AI adoption or DX promotion, please feel free to contact us.

  • We are considering adopting AI, but don't know where to start
  • We want to advance DX, but lack IT personnel
  • Even after implementing systems, they are not used in the field

In the initial consultation, we will propose an approach to AI utilization and DX promotion tailored to each company's challenges.
