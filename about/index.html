About FDE Atlas

FDE Atlas collects public information about the forward deployed engineer role and organizes it so it is easy to browse. It is updated every few hours; this version was published on 2026-11-14.

Sources

  • Jobs and salaries: the official job boards of companies hiring FDEs (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and others). A role is removed here once it is taken down from its board.
  • Articles: published blogs, newsletters, news and reports.
  • Open source: GitHub repositories about the role.
  • Discussions: Hacker News.

What is shown

Each article has a page with its title, source, date, a summary and the full text, linking to the original. Chinese articles come with English translations, and appear in the English edition once translated. List pages use a short excerpt. Job postings are summarized as role, location and disclosed pay range, linking to the company's own posting. Sections and topics may contain the occasional misfiled entry; corrections are welcome.

Corrections and removals

If you are an author or employer and want an entry corrected or removed, please open an issue on GitHub.
