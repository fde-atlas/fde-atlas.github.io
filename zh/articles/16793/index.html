Labelbox 前沿部署工程师面试经历（拿到 Offer，2025 年 8 月）

原标题：Labelbox Forward Deployed Engineer Interview Experience (Offer, Aug 2025)

Top Tech Transition · 2026-08-18 · 约 5 分钟读完

摘要

作者分享 2025 年 8 月拿到 Labelbox 前沿部署工程师 offer 的完整面试经历。流程共四轮：招聘官初筛、RLHF 风格代码数据管线的 take-home 作业、与 FDE 和经理的复盘答辩，以及看似轻松但实为考察态度的使命价值观终面。公司最看重的是对数据集设计端到端的真实理解，任何像 AI 生成或照搬材料的内容都会被识破；同时从第一轮起就明确筛选能否接受长时间、周末加班和高压客户环境。作者认为流程组织欠佳、沟通不畅，并建议案例展示保持简洁、能从第一性原理解释每个细节。

要点

  • 面试共四轮，最终拿到 offer。
  • Take-home 要求设计 RLHF 风格的代码数据管线。
  • 公司严查 AI 生成或照搬、缺乏真实理解的内容。
  • 从初筛起就考察加班与高压工作的接受度。
  • 作者认为流程混乱、沟通不畅。

面试流程

流程包括招聘人员初筛、一份相当有分量的带回家作业——设计一个 RLHF 风格的编程数据流水线、一次针对该作业的现场复盘，以及最后一轮看起来轻松但依然非常认真的面试，围绕使命、价值观和同事契合度展开。

最难的部分是展示我真正理解如何端到端地采集、构建和质检一个编程数据集。他们对任何看起来像是从 AI 复制、从 PDF 粘贴、或没有真正理解就照搬重复的内容都非常敏感。

后面的轮次还会考察我是否适应这种高强度苦干的服务文化，而且即使到了后期，候选人仍可能因为自负或态度问题被淘汰。整个流程本身感觉不太一致，组织得也不算好。

面试轮次 · 4

  • 1

    招聘人员初筛

    BehavioralThe intro screen felt normal at first, but it was really a gut check on whether I was willing to sign up for a very intense setup around hours, weekends, and being thrown straight in front of high-pressure clients or labs.
    • Q1. Are you okay with long hours, weekend work, and being put immediately in front of top research labs?How they answeredI made it clear I was comfortable being hands-on with demanding work and owning messy projects end to end. What stood out was that this was not a throwaway culture question. They were very explicitly screening for whether I would flinch at long hours, weekend work, and immediate pressure.Follow-up questions
      • 你能接受长期项目以及从第一天起就承担那种程度的责任吗？
  • 2

    带回家作业

    Case StudyArtificial IntelligenceTechnicalData Pipeline DesignThe take-home was the real filter. I had to design an end-to-end RLHF-style data curation plan for code in a slide deck and with supporting code.
    • Q1. How would you design an end-to-end plan to create a coding dataset that helps improve a research lab's model on benchmarks like SWE-bench?How they answeredI centered the plan on pulling bugs or PRs from public repos, then filtering for issues that were clean, fixable, worthwhile, and hard enough to matter. I mapped out how I'd recruit SME engineers to write fixes, how those fixes would be tested, and how I'd store the before and after as Python files or JSON traces. The core idea was that the expert-written fix becomes the ground-truth trace the lab can score the model against.Follow-up questions
      • 你会从开源或公开代码仓库中收集哪些数据？
      • 你会从专业人士那里收集哪些标注、修正或轨迹？
      • 你会如何挑选专家或众包贡献者？
      • 你会如何对数据集进行质量评估？
    • Q2. What quality metrics or statistics would you use to know the dataset is actually good?How they answeredI focused on measurability more than jargon. I wanted clear QA around whether a candidate bug was valid, whether the engineer fix passed the right tests, and what the delta was between a model-generated solution and the expert solution. If I mentioned stats, I needed to actually explain drift, agreement, and loss in the context of code, not just name-drop something I copied from a paper.Follow-up questions
      • 你会如何思考不同可能答案之间的漂移或分歧？
      • 找到的解决方案与工程师的解决方案之间的 Delta 是什么？
      • 你的测试会如何融入质检环节？
  • 3

    技术轮

    Case StudyPresentationTechnicalArtificial IntelligenceThe next step after the take-home was an in-person retro with an FDE and the manager. They pushed on whether I really understood the value, tradeoffs, and weak spots in my proposal.
    • Q1. What value did your proposed workflow create, what would you change, and what would you do better?How they answeredI treated the retro as a real debrief. I walked through what value the workflow created for a lab, where the data selection could still be noisy, and how I'd tighten QA or workforce design next time. The interviewers were interactive, and the whole point was showing there was an actual voice behind the work. If I was just reading slides or hiding behind polished language, it would have fallen apart fast.Follow-up questions
      • 你的数据选择中哪里存在噪声？
      • 你会如何收紧质检流程？
      • 事后来看，你从这个练习中学到了什么？
  • 4

    最终轮 / 现场轮

    BehavioralCross-FunctionalThe last round sounded lightweight on paper, usually a peer coffee chat plus manager and mission-values conversations, but it was not a freebie. They used it to check for ego, attitude, cooperation, and whether I would buy into a very intense service culture.
    • Q1. Do you have the work ethic for long projects, complete ownership, and complete transparency? Are you ready to work till midnight?How they answeredI answered it by emphasizing ownership, transparency, and the fact that I can operate in ambiguous client work. But it was obvious they were not asking this as a generic culture question. They wanted to know whether I would normalize long hours, late nights, and a very intense pace without blinking.Follow-up questions
      • 你害怕长时间工作或要求非常高的项目吗？
    • Q2. How willing are you to work hard with other FDEs and push projects that seem impossible over the line?How they answeredI framed myself as collaborative and service-minded, because they cared a lot about whether I would work well with other FDEs and take delivery seriously. Even the informal chat had teeth. People can get cut late if they come off ego-driven or like they think the offer is already locked.Follow-up questions
      • 你有多重视交付以及与同事的合作？

候选人的建议

不要把案例研究搞得太复杂。练习从第一性原理出发解释微调数据集与代码 RLHF 数据集的区别、你会如何采集干净的 bug 或 PR、你会如何挑选领域专家，以及你会如何证明这些修复是可测试、可衡量且真正属于标准答案的。演示文稿要足够简洁，让你能为每一行内容辩护。

公司文化

他们对耐力和苦干意愿的筛选非常严格，有时早在招聘人员电话中就开始了，而后期的行为面试轮次拥有真正的否决权——如果候选人显得自以为是或对交付态度过于随意就会被刷掉。沟通很薄弱，招聘流程感觉就是公司本身的写照：高强度、运转不太顺畅，比起精致的面试表现，他们更看重原始的工作意愿。
