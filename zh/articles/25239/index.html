Anthropic 前沿部署工程师面试

原标题：Anthropic forward deployed engineer interview

open-fde.ai · 2026-11-11 · 约 3 分钟读完

摘要

本文汇总 Anthropic 前沿部署工程师及相关岗位（Applied AI 工程师、解决方案架构师等）的面试流程与题目。流程约五到七轮：申请需写 200-400 字的“Why Anthropic”，随后是招聘官筛选、Claude 部署场景技术面、CodeSignal 式分阶段编程、用人经理深挖、企业客户方案设计终面，以及几乎必有的价值观面试。价值观轮被报道为后期最大淘汰点，考察诚实、敢于异议与 AI 风险观点。AI 使用规则明确：申请自写后可用 Claude 润色，备考鼓励用 Claude，但笔试与现场面试禁用。文中列出 LRU 缓存扩展、安全合规设计等真题，并指出公开渠道缺乏一手 FDE 候选人记录，各指南对轮次数量说法不一。

要点

  • 流程含申请短文、技术面、编程、终面与价值观面试，轮次说法不一。
  • “Why Anthropic”短文 200-400 字，官方称权重很高。
  • 价值观面试是后期主要淘汰点，考察诚实与异议意愿。
  • 申请可自写后用 Claude 润色，现场面试禁用 AI。
  • 真题包括 LRU 缓存扩展与受监管客户的合规部署设计。

全文

Anthropic 面试

前沿部署工程师，应用 AI · 前沿部署工程经理 · 解决方案架构师，应用 AI · 应用 AI 工程师

流程

  • Application Resume plus written answers, including a 'Why Anthropic?' response; candidates must confirm they have read the AI guidance. Assesses: Motivation and written communication; posting says the Why Anthropic answer is weighted highly (often 200-400 words). [2]
  • Recruiter screen About 30 min. Assesses: Background, motivation, level fit. [4]
  • Technical use-case screen Practical Claude deployment scenario (MCP tooling, long-context reliability). Assesses: How you reason about deploying Claude for a customer. [4]
  • Coding exercise Multi-stage incremental problem on a CodeSignal-style platform. Assesses: Writing code as requirements change across stages. [4]
  • Hiring manager round Technical and behavioral deep dive on past projects. Assesses: Ownership, judgment on when an LLM fits, adoption outcomes. [4]
  • Solution design / final panel Architect a Claude deployment for an enterprise customer, plus behavioral. Assesses: Scoping, failure modes, security/compliance trade-offs. [4]
  • Values / culture interview Non-technical round in the onsite for nearly every role. Assesses: Ethics, honesty, willingness to disagree, views on AI risk; reported as a major late-stage rejection point. [6]
  • Solutions Architect loop (candidate account) Recruiter, hiring manager, panel presentation; each 45+ min; 2-3 weeks. Assesses: Motivation and role fit; presentation to a panel. [7]

AI tools in interviews: Anthropic's candidate guidance (updated July 2025): write application drafts yourself, then Claude may help refine them; Claude is encouraged for interview prep; take-homes and live interviews are without AI unless Anthropic says a specific task allows it. Applications require confirming you have read this guidance. [1]

面试问题

系统设计 2
  • 设计一个让用户从大模型中采样的 API，然后为其设计批量优化方案。据描述整理，非原话
  • 为受监管客户（如政府承包商）的 Claude 部署处理安全与合规问题。据描述整理，非原话
编程 3
  • 实现一个 LRU 缓存，然后在后续阶段根据新出现的约束条件进行扩展。
  • 将采样得到的栈数据转换为函数调用的执行轨迹。
  • 读取一组文件并去除重复项。
客户场景 1
  • 扮演买家：说服我 Claude 能做到 ChatGPT 做不到的事情。厂商博客；仅笼统引用候选人反馈
领域知识 1
  • 你为什么在那个问题中使用机器学习或 LLM？你如何判断它适用？
行为面试 3
  • 详细介绍你最重要的项目，并论证其中的关键技术决策。
  • 采用情况如何？解决方案需要多长时间才能达到生产就绪状态？
  • 描述一个你持有的不寻常信念，以及你如何为它辩护。Daniela Amodei 对文化测试的描述，来自 Bloomberg 摘要
动机 2
  • 为什么选择 Anthropic？（书面申请答案，通常 200-400 字）
  • 请介绍一下你自己，为什么选择 Anthropic，以及为什么选择这个职位。

来自各来源的建议

  • Draft application answers yourself and use Claude only to refine; never let AI describe experience you do not have. [1]
  • Using Claude to research Anthropic, rehearse answers, and prepare questions for interviewers is explicitly encouraged. [1]
  • Invest in the 'Why Anthropic?' answer; the posting says it is valued highly. [2]
  • In the values round, honest skepticism and a real example of a troubling decision land better than rehearsed enthusiasm; recruiters say strong technical candidates still fail here. [6]
  • In customer discovery role-plays, ask and reflect back rather than pitch, and give concrete deployment-risk reasoning instead of generic responsible-AI language. [5]
  • In solution design, drive the conversation: scope first, name failure modes early, and state trade-offs explicitly. [4]
  • Expect little feedback between rounds; one Solutions Architect candidate reported minimal feedback after each interview. [7]

公开来源中未找到： 在 Reddit、博客、Taro 或 Aced 上未找到第一手的 FDE 候选人经历（Aced 的 Anthropic FDE 经历标签页为空）；编程和设计问题来自第三方指南。Glassdoor/Blind 上有 FDE 相关报告，但按政策不予采用。各指南对轮次数量的说法不一（五轮还是六轮；是否存在带回家作业）。没有雇主对 FDE 面试流程本身的描述。
